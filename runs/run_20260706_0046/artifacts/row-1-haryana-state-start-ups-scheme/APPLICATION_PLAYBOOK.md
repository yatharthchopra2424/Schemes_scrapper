# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Company Age: Period of existence and operations should not exceed 10 years from the Date of Incorporation.
- [ ] Company Type: Incorporated as a Private Limited Company, Registered Partnership Firm, or Limited Liability Partnership.
- [ ] Annual Turnover: Should not exceed ₹100 crores for any financial year since incorporation.
- [ ] Original Entity: Should not have been formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business.
- [ ] Innovative & Scalable: Must work towards development or improvement of a product, process, or service with a scalable business model and high potential for wealth and employment creation.
- [ ] Category 1 Startups: Must be DPIIT-registered and have a registered office in Haryana.
- [ ] Category 2 Startups: Must be DPIIT-registered, have a registered office outside Haryana, but operate through government-owned or supported operational incubators located in Haryana.

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Copy of Incorporation / Registration Certificate of entity
- Copy of PAN & TAN Number of Entity
- Copy of Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Articles of the Association (AoA) or Partnership Deed (as applicable)
- Copy of the Balance Sheet of the Entity since Incorporation / Registration (if applicable)
- Copy of DPIIT Certificate of Recognition as Startup
- Aadhar Card & Passport size Colored Photograph of the Authorized Representative
- Undertaking (As per Annexure-III)
- Certificate from Incubator (As per Annexure-IV)
- Copy of ID Proof of each Director / Designated Partner/ Partner
- Copy of Residential Proof of each Director / Designated Partner/ Partner
- Copy of DIN/DPIN / Unique ID of Partners in case of Partnership Firm
- Copy of PAN Card & Aadhar Card of each Director / Designated Partner/ Partner
- Passport size Colored Photograph of the Director/ Designated Partner / Partner
- Detailed Project Report w.r.t. product(s)/ process(s) / service(s) covering Description, Business Model, Features, Technology & Infrastructure Architecture, IPR, Innovativeness, scalability, potential employment etc.

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Letter of Authorization (As per Annexure-II)
- Copy of Haryana Udhyam Memorandum (HUM) (for Seed Grant applications)
- Cancelled cheque of the bank account of Startup Entity (for Seed Grant applications)
- Pitch deck of the startup entity (for Seed Grant applications)
- CA certified Utilization Certificate for 1st / 2nd Tranche (for Seed Grant applications)
- Copy of invoices against each expense head (for Seed Grant applications)
- Copy of tickets/boarding pass (in case of travel) (for Seed Grant applications)
- Progress report of the previous tranche (for Seed Grant applications)
- Updated Pitch deck of the startup entity (for Seed Grant applications)
- 3-5 Photographs showing the Space taken on lease/rent by the Startup Entity (for Lease Rental Subsidy)
- Proof of monthly payment towards Lease rental or Bank Account Statement showing remittance of lease rental money (for Lease Rental Subsidy)
- Copy of latest audited financial statement (for Lease Rental Subsidy)
- CA certificate for amount of rent paid – Annexure IV (for Lease Rental Subsidy)
- Cancelled cheque of the bank account of Startup Entity (for Patent Cost Reimbursement)
- CA-certified Utilization Certificate (for Patent Cost Reimbursement)
- Invoices, tickets/boarding passes (if travel) (for Patent Cost Reimbursement)
- Progress reports (for Patent Cost Reimbursement)

### Document Quality Check
- File size must not exceed 2 MB per document.
- File names should clearly indicate document type (e.g., "PAN_Card.pdf", "Balance_Sheet_2023.pdf").
- All documents must be legible, scanned in color where applicable (photographs, ID proofs).
- Authorized representative's Aadhar and photograph must be valid and match the authorization letter.
- Undertaking and Letter of Authorization must be signed and stamped as per annexure formats.
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, provide certified translation.
- Ensure all director/partner details (ID, residential proof, PAN, Aadhar) are consistent across documents.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required. It must cover: Description of product/process/service, Business Model, Features, Technology & Infrastructure Architecture, IPR, Innovativeness, scalability, and potential employment generation.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the portal at https://startupharyana.gov.in and log in via email/password, Gmail, or Startup India credentials.
2. Select 'Startup' as the registration type during signup.
3. Ensure DPIIT recognition is obtained prior to application; if not, secure it via DPIIT first.
4. Complete the 4-step application form in sequence:
   - (a) Details of the entity (Startup Details, Authorized Representative Details, Incubation Details, Upload Documents)
   - (b) Details of Directors/Designated Partners
   - (c) Details of Business
   - (d) Preview and Final Submit
5. Upload all required documents, ensuring each file is ≤ 2 MB.
6. Accept the terms and conditions before final submission.
7. Save draft periodically using the "Exit" option to avoid data loss; resume via "My Applications" → "Complete Application".

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All application form fields are complete and accurately filled.
- [ ] All required documents are uploaded and meet quality checks (size, naming, legibility).
- [ ] Letter of Authorization (Annexure-II) and Undertaking (Annexure-III) are signed and stamped.
- [ ] Certificate from Incubator (Annexure-IV) is obtained if applicable.
- [ ] Detailed Project Report is comprehensive and covers all mandated sections.
- [ ] Declarations and consent clauses are accepted.
- [ ] Application is free of spelling or formatting errors.
- [ ] Authorized representative details match across documents and form.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the portal after final review.
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation page showing the application number and timestamp.
- Store the application reference number securely in the case file.
- Set calendar reminders for follow-up at 25 days (to check status) and 35 days (if no update, initiate deficiency resolution).

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Download the registration certificate from the 'My Applications' section on https://startupharyana.gov.in.
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount and benefits approved (e.g., Seed Grant up to INR 10 Lakh, Lease Rental Subsidy, etc.).
- [ ] Initiate utilization of funds as per the approved purpose and scheme guidelines.
- [ ] Prepare and submit Utilization Certificate (CA-certified where required, e.g., for Seed Grant, Patent Cost Reimbursement).
- [ ] Maintain records of all invoices, bills, and proof of expenditure for audit purposes.
- [ ] Track milestone-based disbursements (e.g., quarterly for Seed Grant).
- [ ] Ensure compliance with benefit-specific conditions (e.g., Lease Rental Subsidy max 1 year, Cloud Storage limited to Haryana-based Data Centres).
