## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the tangible financial benefits delivered to clients under the Haryana State Start-ups Scheme, ensuring clients retain the majority of the grant value.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees are calibrated to the scheme’s moderate risk profile — while processing is standardized (30-day approval timeline with 10-day deficiency window), the quarterly evaluation cycle for Seed Grant introduces variability in approval timing, warranting mid-tier success fees.
3. **Accessibility for Target Segment**: The Growth tier pricing is specifically calibrated for DPIIT-recognized startups with turnover ≤ ₹100 Cr, aligning with the scheme’s primary eligibility criteria and ensuring services remain accessible to early-stage ventures.
4. **Transparency & Predictability**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover initial eligibility assessment and documentation preparation, while success fees are contingent only upon successful disbursement of benefits, reducing client financial risk.
