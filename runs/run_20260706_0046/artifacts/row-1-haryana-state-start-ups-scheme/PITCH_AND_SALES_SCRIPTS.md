# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping Haryana-based startups access government funding and incentives through the Haryana State Start-ups Scheme. Many founders I speak with are unaware they qualify for up to INR 10 Lakh in seed grants, 45% lease rental subsidies for women-led startups, or 100% patent cost reimbursement—all without giving up equity. Before we explore whether this fits your startup, may I ask a few quick questions to understand your current stage and needs?

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your company incorporated? (To verify <10 years eligibility)
2. What is your current legal structure? (Private Limited, LLP, or Partnership Firm?)
3. What has been your annual turnover in any financial year since incorporation? (Must be ≤₹100 crores)
4. Are you DPIIT-recognized as a startup? (Mandatory for application)
5. Where is your registered office located? (Must be in Haryana for Category 1 benefits)
6. Are you developing a product, process, or service with a scalable business model and high potential for wealth/employment creation?
7. Has your entity been formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business? (Must be original entity)
8. Which specific benefits are you most interested in—seed grant, lease subsidy, patent reimbursement, cloud storage, acceleration programs, or SGST reimbursement?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Company age ≤10 years from Date of Incorporation  
- [ ] Incorporated as Private Limited Company, Registered Partnership Firm, or LLP  
- [ ] Annual turnover not exceeding ₹100 crores in any financial year since incorporation  
- [ ] Original entity (not formed by splitting/reconstructing existing business)  
- [ ] Working towards development/improvement of product, process, or service with scalable business model and high potential for wealth/employment creation  
- [ ] DPIIT-registered startup  
- [ ] Registered office in Haryana (Category 1) OR registered office outside Haryana but operating through Govt.-supported incubator in Haryana (Category 2)  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you meet the core eligibility criteria for the Haryana State Start-ups Scheme—particularly being DPIIT-registered, under 10 years old, and focused on innovation. This scheme offers startups like yours up to INR 10 Lakh in seed grants, 45% lease rental subsidies for women-led teams, and full patent cost reimbursement—all non-dilutive funding. Would you like me to walk you through how it works and what’s required to apply?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Early-stage startups in Haryana struggle with high costs for prototype development, patent filings, office leases, and cloud infrastructure—especially when trying to validate ideas without diluting equity or taking on debt. Many miss out on available state support simply because they don’t know they qualify or find the application process overwhelming.

**Scheme intro:**
The Haryana State Start-ups Scheme, administered by the Department of Industries and Commerce, Government of Haryana, provides a suite of non-dilutive financial incentives including: Seed Grant of up to INR 10 Lakh; Lease Rental Subsidy of 30% (45% for women-led startups) up to INR 5 Lakh for 1 year; 100% Patent Cost Reimbursement up to INR 25 Lakh; 75% Cloud Storage Reimbursement (max INR 2.5 Lakh/year for 5 years on Haryana-based data centres); Reimbursement for Acceleration Programs (up to INR 2.5 Lakh/year); and 50% Net SGST Reimbursement for 7 years (capped at 100% of Fixed Capital Investment). Applications are processed via the portal at https://startupharyana.gov.in on a rolling basis with no fixed deadline.

- Your startup is DPIIT-registered, meeting the mandatory recognition requirement  
- Incorporated within the last 10 years, satisfying the company age criterion  
- Operating as a Private Limited Company/LLP/Partnership Firm, an eligible entity type
