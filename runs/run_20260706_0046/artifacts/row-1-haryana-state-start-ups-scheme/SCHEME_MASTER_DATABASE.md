### Haryana State Start-ups Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Haryana only
- **Administering Body:** Department of Industries and Commerce, Government of Haryana
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://startupharyana.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Incorporated as a Private Limited Company, Registered Partnership Firm, or Limited Liability Partnership
- Age of business: Period of existence and operations should not exceed 10 years from the Date of Incorporation
- Sector: Must work towards development or improvement of a product, process, or service with a scalable business model and high potential for wealth and employment creation
- Revenue cap: Should not exceed ₹100 crores for any financial year since incorporation
- Employee count: 
- Other: 
  - Must be DPIIT-registered
  - Should not have been formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business
  - Category 1 Startups: Must have a registered office in Haryana
  - Category 2 Startups: Must have a registered office outside Haryana but operate through government-owned or supported operational incubators located in Haryana

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - Seed Grant: up to INR 10 Lakh per startup
  - Lease Rental Subsidy: 30% (4% for women-led startups, max INR 5 Lakh for 1 year
  - Patent Cost Reimbursement: 100% of actual expenses, max INR 25 Lakh
  - Reimbursement for Cloud Storage: 75% of expenses, max INR 2.5 Lakh per annum for 5 years
  - Reimbursement for Acceleration Programs: up to INR 2.5 Lakh per year
  - Reimbursement of Net SGST: 50% for 7 years, capped at 100% of Fixed Capital Investment
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement / Direct credit (upon submission of required documents and approval by the Evaluation & Approval Committee)
- Duration of benefit: Varies by scheme (One-time for Seed Grant, Annual for Lease Rental Subsidy, etc.)

#### Documents Required
1. Copy of Incorporation / Registration Certificate of entity
2. Copy of PAN & TAN Number of Entity
3. Copy of Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Articles of the Association (AoA) or Partnership Deed (as applicable)
4. Copy of the Balance Sheet of the Entity since Incorporation / Registration (if applicable)
5. Copy of DPIIT Certificate of Recognition as Startup
6. Letter of Authorization (As per Annexure-II)
7. Aadhar Card & Passport size Colored Photograph of the Authorized Representative
8. Undertaking (As per Annexure-III)
9. Certificate from Incubator (As per Annexure-IV)
10. Copy of ID Proof of each Director / Designated Partner/ Partner
11. Copy of Residential Proof of each Director / Designated Partner/ Partner
12. Copy of DIN/DPIN / Unique ID of Partners in case of Partnership Firm
13. Copy of PAN Card & Aadhar Card of each Director / Designated Partner/ Partner
14. Passport size Colored Photograph of the Director/ Designated Partner / Partner
15. Detailed Project Report w.r.t. product(s)/ process(s) / service(s) covering Description, Business Model, Features, Technology & Infrastructure Architecture, IPR, Innovativeness, scalability, potential employment etc.

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register on the Startup Haryana portal at https://startupharyana.gov.in
2. Log in using email/password, Gmail, or Startup India login details
3. Select 'Startup' as the registration type
4. Ensure DPIIT recognition is obtained prior to application (if not, if not already recognized, get recognized via DPIIT first
5. Complete the 4-step application form:
   - (a) Details of the entity (Startup Details, Authorized Representative Details, Incubation Details, Upload Documents)
   - (b) Details of Directors/Designated Partners
   - (c) Details of Business
   - (d) Preview and Final Submit
6. Upload all required documents (file size ≤ 2 MB)
7. Accept terms and conditions and submit the application
8. Application is processed within 30 days if no deficiencies; if deficiencies are found, applicant has 10 days to resubmit
9. Upon approval, download the registration certificate from 'My Applications' section
