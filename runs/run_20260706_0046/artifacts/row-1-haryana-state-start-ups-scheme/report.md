## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Haryana State Start-ups Scheme** is a state-level initiative administered by the **Department of Industries and Commerce, Government of Haryana**, designed to foster innovation, entrepreneurship, and job creation within the state. Launched under the **Haryana State Startup Policy 2022**, the scheme aims to establish Haryana as a preferred destination for startups by providing financial incentives, regulatory simplifications, mentorship, incubation support, and access to funding and networking opportunities. The scheme operates on a **rolling basis** with **no fixed deadline**, accepting applications year-round via the portal: **https://startupharyana.gov.in**.

### Objectives
- Foster innovation & entrepreneurship  
- Create jobs and stimulate economic growth  
- Encourage investment in startups  
- Develop a robust startup ecosystem  
- Establish Haryana as a preferred destination for startups  
- Support 5000 startups in the next 5 years  
- Attract major investment  
- Provide financial incentives including grants, subsidies, and tax benefits  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Criteria | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| **Company Age** | Period of existence and operations should not exceed 10 years from the Date of Incorporation |
| **Company Type** | Incorporated as a Private Limited Company, Registered Partnership Firm, or Limited Liability Partnership |
| **Annual Turnover** | Should not exceed ₹100 crores for any financial year since incorporation |
| **Original Entity** | Should not have been formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business |
| **Innovative & Scalable** | Must work towards development or improvement of a product, process, or service with a scalable business model and high potential for wealth and employment creation |
| **DPIIT Recognition** | Must be DPIIT-registered (mandatory for all applicants) |
| **Category 1 Startups** | Must be DPIIT-registered and have a registered office in Haryana |
| **Category 2 Startups** | Must be DPIIT-registered, have a registered office outside Haryana, but operate through government-owned or supported operational incubators located in Haryana |
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Startups; incubators; women entrepreneurs |

> **Note**: Benefits/Incentives can be availed under only one policy in the State (viz. Haryana State Startup Policy 2022 or HEEP 2020 or MSME or IT & ESDM Policy or any other State Policy).

### Benefits & Financial Support
The scheme provides multiple fiscal incentives, disbursed upon submission of required documents and approval by the Evaluation & Approval Committee.

| Benefit | Details | Maximum Amount | Duration/Conditions |
|--------|--------|----------------|---------------------|
| **Seed Grant** | Financial support for idea validation, prototype development, proof of concept, and initial activities | Up to INR 10 Lakh per startup | Disbursed in tranches; applications evaluated quarterly (received in a quarter considered in subsequent quarter) |
| **Lease Rental Subsidy** | Reimbursement of lease rental for general startups (30%) and women-led startups (45%) | Max INR 5 Lakh for 1 year | Available for maximum 1 year only; requires 3-5 photographs of leased space, proof of monthly payment, CA certificate for rent paid |
| **Patent Cost Reimbursement** | 100% reimbursement of actual expenses (filing, consultancy, search, maintenance, publishing fees) | Up to INR 25 Lakh for domestic and international patents | Requires CA-certified Utilization Certificate, invoices, tickets/boarding passes (if travel), progress reports |
| **Cloud Storage Reimbursement** | 75% reimbursement of expenses | Max INR 2.5 Lakh per annum for 5 years | Limited to Haryana-based Data Centres only |
| **Acceleration Programs Reimbursement** | Reimbursement for participation in acceleration programs | Up to INR 2.5 Lakh per year | — |
| **Net SGST Reimbursement** | 50% reimbursement of Net SGST | Capped at 100% of Fixed Capital Investment | Available for 7 years |

### Non-Monetary Benefits

- Access to Events/Workshops/Startup-Specific Programs  
- Mentorship  
- Networking with investors and industry experts  
- Access to funding and investment opportunities

### Required Documents
1. Copy of Incorporation / Registration Certificate of entity  
2. Copy of PAN & TAN Number of Entity  
3. Copy of Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Articles of Association (AoA) or Partnership Deed  
4. Copy of Balance Sheet since Incorporation/Registration (if applicable)  
5. Copy of DPIIT Certificate of Recognition as Startup  
6. Letter of Authorization (As per Annexure-II)  
7. Aadhar Card & Passport size Colored Photograph of Authorized Representative  
8. Undertaking (As per Annexure-III)  
9. Certificate from Incubator (As per Annexure-IV)  
10. Copy of ID Proof of each Director / Designated Partner/ Partner  
11. Copy of Residential Proof of each Director / Designated Partner/ Partner  
12. Copy of DIN/DPIN / Unique ID of Partners (for Partnership Firm)  
13. Copy of PAN Card & Aadhar Card of each Director / Designated Partner/ Partner  
14. Passport size Colored Photograph of each Director/ Designated Partner / Partner  
15. Detailed Project Report covering: Description, Business Model, Features, Technology & Infrastructure Architecture, IPR, Innovativeness, scalability, potential employment  

### Key Caveats
- Benefits/Incentives can be availed under only one policy in the State (viz. Haryana State Startup Policy 2022 or HEEP 2020 or MSME or IT & ESDM Policy or any other State Policy)  
- All Government-owned/Supported Incubators in Haryana must register with Startup Haryana Cell under DITECH to be eligible for benefits  
- Private incubators must have at least 10,000 Sq. ft. of built-up space, owned or leased, with lease period minimum 5 years if leased  
- Seed Grant applications are evaluated quarterly; applications received in a quarter are considered in the subsequent quarter by the Evaluation & Approval Committee  
- Lease Rental Subsidy is available for a maximum of 1 year only  
- Patent Cost Reimbursement requires submission of CA-certified Utilization Certificate, invoices, tickets/boarding passes (if travel), and progress reports  
- Cloud Storage Reimbursement is limited to Haryana-based Data Centres only  
- Net SGST Reimbursement is capped at 100% of Fixed Capital Investment and available for 7 years
