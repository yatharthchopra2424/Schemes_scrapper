# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Business is an informal micro-entrepreneur or micro-enterprise (IME) for livelihood enterprise promotion  
- [ ] Business is located in PAN India (operational in 30 States/UTs as of December 2023)  
- [ ] Business is approached through a registered Partner Institution (Financial Intermediary or Non-Financial Intermediary) empanelled with SIDBI under the Prayaas Scheme  
- [ ] Business falls under target beneficiary categories: MSME, startup, women entrepreneur, or SC/ST (note: focus on women and socially backward sections is indicative but not a strict eligibility criterion)  
- [ ] Business is not applying directly to SIDBI (assistance is provided only through empanelled Partner Institutions)  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Completed application form  
- PAN card  
- Aadhaar card (for identity and address proof)  
- Bank account details (cancelled cheque or bank statement)  
- Udyam Registration Certificate or Udyam Assist Certificate  
- GST registration certificate (if applicable)  
- Latest ITR (Income Tax Return)  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- KYC documents (Identity and Address proof)  
- Business proof / enterprise details  
- Any additional documents as required by the Partner Institution  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be clear, legible scans or photographs  
- File size should not exceed 5 MB per document  
- File names must match the client’s name as per KYC (e.g., `Rajesh_Kumar_PAN.pdf`)  
- All documents must be self-attested by the applicant  
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, a certified translation must be attached  
- Photographs must be recent (not older than 3 months)  
- Bank statements must show the account holder’s name matching KYC

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for this scheme. The SIDBI SMILE Scheme (Prayaas) does not mandate a Detailed Project Report (DPR). However, if the Partner Institution requests additional business details for credit assessment, verify with the nodal officer at the Partner Institution level.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Ensure all fields in the Partner Institution’s application form are fully completed; leave no blanks.  
2. Use exact names as per PAN and Aadhaar; avoid nicknames or abbreviations.  
3. Enter accurate business address matching proof of address documents.  
4. Specify correct business type (e.g., sole proprietorship, partnership) and activity.  
5. Provide truthful turnover and employee count estimates; do not inflate figures.  
6. Attach all required documents in the specified format (PDF/JPEG, <5 MB each).  
7. Double-check mobile number and email for OTP and communication.  
8. Confirm that the applicant is applying through a registered Partner Institution (not directly to SIDBI).  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Application form is 100% complete with no missing fields  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached and clearly legible  
- [ ] KYC documents match the applicant’s name and details in the form  
- [ ] Bank account details are validated (name matches KYC, account is active)  
- [ ] Declarations and consent forms are signed and dated  
- [ ] Application is being routed through a verified Partner Institution (confirm empanelled status via SIDBI website or Partner Institution list)  
- [ ] No false or exaggerated claims about turnover, employment, or business vintage

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed application and documents to the Partner Institution (in person or via their digital portal/app)  
- Obtain and screenshot the submission acknowledgment (including timestamp and reference number if provided)  
- Store the Partner Institution’s application reference number securely  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 7 days after submission to check status  
- Advise the client to monitor the Prayaas App/portal for updates (if applicable)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval/sanction letter from the Partner Institution or SIDBI  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned loan amount and disbursement schedule  
- [ ] Ensure the loan is disbursed to the bank account provided in the application  
- [ ] Guide the client on utilization of funds for livelihood/enterprise promotion as per scheme objectives  
- [ ] Remind the client about repayment schedule and due dates (to be confirmed with Partner Institution)  
- [ ] Utilization Certificate: Not explicitly required by the scheme, but maintain records of expenditure for internal tracking and potential audit
