## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client.
2. Transparent fee structure with no hidden charges.
3. Success fee contingent on successful credit sanction and disbursement through Partner Institutions.
4. Scheme-specific note: The SIDBI SMILE Scheme has a low to moderate risk profile due to its rolling basis status, quick and paperless sanction process via digital platforms (Prayaas App), and high beneficiary volume (over 2,94,000 assisted as of December 2023), indicating strong operational maturity and predictable outcomes.
