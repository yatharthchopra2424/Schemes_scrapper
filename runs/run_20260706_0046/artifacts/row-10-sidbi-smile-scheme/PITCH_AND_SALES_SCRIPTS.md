# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping informal micro-entrepreneurs and micro-enterprises access affordable credit through government-backed schemes like the SIDBI SMILE Scheme. This scheme is designed specifically for livelihood enterprise promotion, with a strong focus on supporting women entrepreneurs and socially backward sections across PAN India. I’d like to understand your business journey to see if this could be a fit for your growth needs.

### Discovery Questions
1. How long has your business been operational, and what type of enterprise do you run (e.g., trading, manufacturing, services)?
2. Are you currently seeking working capital or term loan support for livelihood or enterprise promotion activities?
3. Do you operate as an informal micro-enterprise, or are you registered under Udyam or another formal framework?
4. What percentage of your ownership or leadership is held by women entrepreneurs or individuals from socially backward sections (SC/ST/OBC)?
5. Have you previously approached banks or financial institutions for credit, and what challenges did you face in the application or sanction process?
6. Are you located in one of the 30 States/UTs where SIDBI’s Partner Institutions are operational under the SMILE Scheme?
7. How do you currently manage the loan lifecycle?
7. Would you prefer doorstep assistance with digital documentation and application support through a local Partner Institution?
8. What is your expected loan amount requirement, and are you looking for quick, paperless sanction processes?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is an informal micro-enterprise/micro-enterprise (IME) focused on livelihood promotion  
- [ ] Located in PAN India (operational in 30 States/UTs as of Dec 2023)  
- [ ] Seeking credit access for enterprise promotion or livelihood activities  
- [ ] Open to assistance from Partner Institutions (Financial or Non-Financial Intermediaries)  
- [ ] Willing to use digital platforms like Prayaas App for application tracking  
- [ ] Not applying directly to SIDBI (understands assistance is only via empanelled Partner Institutions)  
- [ ] Business aligns with scheme’s indicative focus on women entrepreneurs (>85% beneficiaries) or socially backward sections (>70% beneficiaries)  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like the SIDBI SMILE Scheme could be a strong fit—especially given its focus on informal micro-entrepreneurs like you, doorstep support via Partner Institutions, and quick paperless sanctions. Let me walk you through how it works and the specific benefits it offers.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Informal micro-entrepreneurs often struggle to access timely, affordable credit due to complex documentation, lack of collateral, and limited digital access—hindering their ability to scale from sustenance to enterprise level.

**Scheme intro:**
The SIDBI SMILE Scheme (Small Industries Development Bank of India) is a loan facilitation program implemented through empanelled Partner Institutions. It does not lend directly but enables credit delivery via Financial Intermediaries (NBFCs, MFIs, FinTechs) and Non-Financial Intermediaries (Corporates, Aggregators). The scheme provides doorstep assistance for application processing, digital documentation, and collections, with seamless digital integration between Partner Institutions and SIDBI through the Prayaas App/portal. Over 2,94,000 beneficiaries have been assisted as of December 2023.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are an informal micro-enterprise engaged in livelihood enterprise promotion — the core target of the scheme  
- You are open to working with a Partner Institution for application support and digital documentation  
- Your business operates in PAN India, where the scheme is active across 30 States/UTs  
- You align with the scheme’s focus on women entrepreneurs or socially backward sections (based on your earlier input)
