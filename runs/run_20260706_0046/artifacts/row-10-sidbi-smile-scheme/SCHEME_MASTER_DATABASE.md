### SIDBI SMILE Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://sidbi.in/smile
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Informal micro-entrepreneurs/ micro-enterprises (IMEs)
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Not specified
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Targeted at livelihood enterprise promotion; implemented through Partner Institutions including Financial Intermediaries (BC/ MFI/ SHPI/ NBFC/ FinTech’s, etc.) and Non-Financial Intermediaries (Corporates, Aggregators, etc.); operational in PAN India covering 30 States/UTs; more than 85% of beneficiaries are women entrepreneurs; more than 70% belong to socially backward sections

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Facilitates access to affordable credit through Partner Institutions; SIDBI provides refinance and credit facilitation (does not directly lend)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (via Partner Institutions)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (per loan sanction)

#### Documents Required
1. Application form
2. KYC documents (Identity and Address proof)
3. Business proof / enterprise details
4. Bank account details
5. Any additional documents as required by the Partner Institution

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Approach a registered Partner Institution (Financial Intermediary or Non-Financial Intermediary) empanelled with SIDBI under the Prayaas Scheme.
2. Submit application details including business information and KYC documents at the Partner Institution level.
3. Partner Institution assists in collection of application information, digital documentation, and verification.
4. Application is processed through seamless digital integration between the Partner Institution and SIDBI.
5. Credit sanction and disbursement are coordinated between the Partner Institution and SIDBI based on eligibility and credit assessment.
6. Utilize the Prayaas App/ portal for tracking and managing the loan lifecycle where available.
