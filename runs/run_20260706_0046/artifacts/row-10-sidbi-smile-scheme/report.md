# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **SIDBI SMILE Scheme** (also known as the **PRAYAAS Scheme**) is a loan facilitation initiative implemented by the **Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI)** under the **MSME & Finance** category. It operates on a **rolling basis** with **Pan-India** geographic coverage and is currently **active** as of the last update in **2026**. The scheme does not involve direct lending by SIDBI but instead enables credit delivery through a network of **Partner Institutions** (both financial and non-financial intermediaries). Its core mechanism relies on digital integration via the **Prayaas App/portal** to ensure quick, paperless sanction and disbursement processes.

### Objectives
The scheme is designed to achieve the following specific objectives:
- Facilitate access to **affordable credit** for informal micro-entrepreneurs/micro-enterprises (IMEs) for livelihood enterprise promotion.
- Provide **doorstep assistance** through Partner Institutions in credit processes including application information collection, digital documentation, and collections.
- Ensure **faster credit delivery** via seamless digital integration between the Originating Partner Institution (OPI) and SIDBI, supported by the **Prayaas App/portal**.
- Support **women entrepreneurs** and **socially backward sections** of society.
- Promote enterprise development and scaling from sustenance to enterprise level.

### Benefits & Financial Support
The scheme does not provide direct subsidies or grants but facilitates access to **affordable credit** through refinancing and credit facilitation by SIDBI to Partner Institutions, which then lend to end beneficiaries.

> **> Key Takeaway:** The scheme’s primary benefit is **enabling access to credit** that might otherwise be inaccessible due to lack of collateral, credit history, or formal documentation — particularly for underserved segments like women and marginalized communities — by leveraging SIDBI’s refinance capacity and Partner Institutions’ last-mile reach.

### Application Process
The application process is entirely mediated through Partner Institutions. There is no direct application to SIDBI. The process is designed to be digital-first and paperless where possible, leveraging the Prayaas App.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Beneficiary approaches a registered Partner Institution<br/>(Financial or Non-Financial Intermediary empanelled with SIDBI under Prayaas)] --> B[Submit application details including business info and KYC documents at Partner Institution level]
    B --> C[Partner Institution assists in collection of application information, digital documentation, and verification]
    C --> D[Application processed through seamless digital integration between Partner Institution and SIDBI]
    D --> E[Credit sanction and disbursement coordinated between Partner Institution and SIDBI based on eligibility and credit assessment]
    E --> F[Utilize Prayaas App/portal for tracking and managing loan lifecycle where available]
    F --> G[Loan disbursement to beneficiary]
    style A fill:#e3f2fd,stroke:#1565c0
    style G fill:#c8e6c9,stroke:#2e7d32
```

> **> Process Warning:** Applicants **must not** attempt to apply directly to SIDBI. All applications must originate at a **registered Partner Institution**. The Partner Institution is responsible for initial screening, documentation, and acting as the interface with SIDBI for sanction and disbursement.
