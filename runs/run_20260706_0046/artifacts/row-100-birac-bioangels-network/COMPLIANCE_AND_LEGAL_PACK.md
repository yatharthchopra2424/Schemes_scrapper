## 8D. Internal Data Handling Policy

(Standard data handling policy — encrypted cloud storage, 2FA, limited access, no personal devices,
2-year retention post case closure, 24-hour breach notification.)

**Data Storage:** All client data is stored in encrypted cloud storage with AES-256 encryption.
Access is protected by multi-factor authentication (2FA). Access is limited to authorized personnel
only. No personal devices are permitted for accessing or storing client data.

**Access Control:** Role-based access controls ensure that only consultants directly involved in a
client's BIRAC BioAngels Network application can access relevant files. Audit logs track all access
and modifications.

**Retention:** Client data is retained for a period of two (2) years post case closure, after which
it is securely deleted unless longer retention is required for legal or regulatory compliance.

**Breach Notification:** In the event of a data breach, affected clients and relevant authorities
will be notified within 24 hours of discovery.

**Scheme-specific note:** All documents submitted to Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council (BIRAC)
under BIRAC BioAngels Network must be retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit purposes.
