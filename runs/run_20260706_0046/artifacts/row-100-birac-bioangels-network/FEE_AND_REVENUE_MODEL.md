## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual grant amount disbursed under BIRAC BioAngels Network, ensuring clients pay only for realized value.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Retainers**: Retainer amounts reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile — while BIRAC is a credible government-backed entity under DBT, the scheme has low confidence in public data availability and limited transparency on disbursement volumes, increasing client acquisition and documentation effort.
3. **Transparency in Lag**: Processing time for BIRAC BioAngels Network is not explicitly documented in crawled evidence; however, typical biotech grant cycles (including due diligence, angel network matching, and disbursement) range from 4–6 months, justifying a 3-month approval lag in revenue projections.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The BIRAC BioAngels Network operates through a niche angel investor network for biotech startups, which introduces variability in funding timing and amount. Success fees are therefore structured to reflect uncertainty in final grant size, though historical disbursements (where available) suggest typical support in the ₹10L–₹1Cr range for early validation and prototype development.
