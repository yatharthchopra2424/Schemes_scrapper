## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping early-stage biotechnology and life sciences startups access non-dilutive funding to accelerate their R&D and commercialisation journey. The BIRAC BioAngels Network is designed specifically for innovative bio-startups like yours that are working on breakthrough solutions in healthcare, agriculture, or industrial biotech and need early-stage investment to validate and scale their ideas.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your biotech or life sciences startup incorporated, and what stage of development are you currently at (idea, prototype, pre-revenue, early revenue)?
2. What specific problem in healthcare, agriculture, or industrial biotech is your innovation solving, and do you have a working prototype or proof of concept?
3. Are you seeking early-stage funding to support product development, clinical validation, or market piloting, and have you considered angel investment or grant options?
4. Is your startup registered as a private limited company or LLP in India, and are the founders Indian nationals or OCI/PIO cardholders?
5. Do you have a clear roadmap for how the funding will be used to achieve key milestones over the next 12–18 months?
6. Are you open to mentorship and strategic guidance from experienced biotech investors and industry experts as part of the funding process?
7. Have you previously applied for any BIRAC or government innovation grants, and if so, what was the outcome?
8. Are you looking for funding that does not require immediate equity dilution, allowing you to retain greater control over your startup’s direction?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup incorporated in India as a private limited company or LLP  
- [ ] At least one founder is an Indian national or holds OCI/PIO status  
- [ ] Operating in biotechnology, life sciences, agriculture, healthcare, or industrial biotech sectors  
- [ ] Has a working prototype, proof of concept, or early-stage product with clear innovation  
- [ ] Seeking early-stage funding for product development, validation, or market piloting  
- [ ] Not yet received significant institutional VC funding (early-stage preference)  
- [ ] Committed to using funds for R&D, prototyping, clinical trials, or scale-up activities  
- [ ] Open to receiving mentorship and guidance from BioAngels Network mentors  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like your startup aligns well with the BIRAC BioAngels Network’s focus on early-stage bio-innovators. This scheme offers early-stage funding and mentorship support specifically for startups like yours working on impactful biotech solutions. Let me share how it works and how we’ve helped similar ventures secure approval.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Early-stage biotech startups in India often struggle to access patient capital that understands the long R&D cycles and regulatory hurdles of the sector. Traditional investors may be hesitant due to high risk and long timelines, leaving promising innovations stuck at the prototype stage without the funding needed to validate, scale, or attract follow-on investment.

**Scheme intro:**
The BIRAC BioAngels Network is an initiative by the Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council (BIRAC), a Public Sector Undertaking under the Department of Biotechnology, Ministry of Science and Technology, Government of India. It connects early-stage biotech startups with accredited angel investors who provide early-stage funding (typically ranging from ₹25 lakhs to ₹2 crores) in the form of equity or convertible instruments, along with mentorship, strategic guidance, and access to BIRAC’s ecosystem of resources, including regulatory support, infrastructure, and follow-on funding opportunities.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your startup is incorporated in India as a private limited company or LLP with at least one Indian national/OCI/PIO founder  
- You are working on an innovative solution in biotechnology, life sciences, agriculture, healthcare, or industrial biotech with a working prototype or proof of concept  
- You are seeking early-stage funding to support product development, validation, or market piloting  
- You are open to mentorship and strategic guidance from experienced biotech investors and industry experts
