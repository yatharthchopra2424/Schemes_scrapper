### Checklist

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation/Registration Certificate of the educational institution
- PAN Card of the institution
- Audited Financial Statements / ITR of the institution (last 2 years)
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable as MSME, though not typical for educational institutions)
- Authorization letter from the head of the institution

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Proposal document detailing objectives, target audience, duration, and budget
- List of resource persons with their credentials
- Budget estimate for the camp
- Infrastructure and faculty details of the institution
- Undertaking from the institution head

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and in PDF format (scanned copies acceptable if clear)
- File names must match the document type (e.g., "Proposal_NSTEDB.pdf", "Resource_Persons_List.pdf")
- File size should not exceed 10 MB per document
- Undertaking and proposal must be signed and stamped by the head of the institution
- Budget estimate must be itemized and realistic
- All documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, provide certified translation

### Client Communication Template
**Subject:** Application Support for NSTEDB Entrepreneurship Awareness Camps – Proposal Submission Guidance  

Dear [Client Name],  

We are assisting your institution in applying for the **NSTEDB Entrepreneurship Awareness Camps** scheme, implemented by the National Science and Technology Entrepreneurship Development Board (NSTEDB), Department of Science and Technology (DST), Ministry of Science and Technology.  

This scheme provides grant-in-aid support to educational institutions for organizing Entrepreneurship Awareness Camps targeting students and youth. The grant covers expenses such as honorarium for resource persons and coordinators, TA/DA for participants, printing of study materials, venue expenses, and other incidental costs.  

**Key Points:**  
- **Eligibility:** Universities, colleges, polytechnics, and technical institutions with necessary infrastructure and faculty are eligible. Preference may be given to institutions in backward regions or serving underrepresented groups.  
- **Required Documents:** Proposal, list of resource persons, budget estimate, infrastructure/faculty details, and undertaking from the head of the institution.  
- **Application Process:** Proposals must be submitted directly to NSTEDB, DST. There is no fixed deadline as applications are invited throughout the year (refer to DST’s “Applications Invited Throughout the Year” page: https://dst.gov.in/announcement/applications-invited-throughout-year).

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A formal Detailed Project Report (DPR) is not explicitly required. However, the proposal document must include:  
- Objectives of the camp  
- Target audience (students/youth)  
- Duration and schedule  
- Detailed budget estimate  
- List of resource persons with credentials  
- Expected outcomes  
- Alignment with scheme objectives (creating entrepreneurship awareness, promoting innovation, encouraging startups, providing info on govt schemes, fostering academia-industry linkages)  
If uncertain about depth, **verify with nodal officer.**

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the application form from the DST portal if available; otherwise, prepare a proposal as per scheme guidelines.  
2. Ensure all sections of the proposal are completed: objectives, target audience, duration, resource persons, budget, expected outcomes.  
3. Attach all scheme-specific documents as annexures.  
4. The undertaking from the head of the institution must be on official letterhead and duly signed.  
5. Budget must be itemized and justify each expense head (honorarium, TA/DA, printing, venue, etc.).  
6. Clearly state how the camp aligns with NSTEDB’s objectives: entrepreneurship awareness, innovation promotion, startup encouragement, govt scheme exposure, academia-industry linkages.  
7. Do not leave any mandatory field blank; if not applicable, write “Not Applicable” with justification.  
8. Before submission, verify that the proposal is addressed to NSTEDB, DST and includes all required enclosures.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Proposal document is complete and signed  
- [ ] List of resource persons includes names, designations, and relevant credentials  
- [ ] Budget estimate is itemized and matches proposed activities  
- [ ] Infrastructure and faculty details are provided  
- [ ] Undertaking from institution head is attached and signed  
- [ ] All documents are in correct format and within size limits  
- [ ] Proposal clearly links to scheme objectives  
- [ ] Application is addressed to the correct authority (NSTEDB, DST)

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the proposal via email or post to NSTEDB, DST (exact address/email to be confirmed from nodal officer or official DST communications)  
- Obtain and save proof of submission (email receipt, courier acknowledgment, or postal tracking)  
- Record any application/reference number provided by NSTEDB  
- Set follow-up reminders for 15 days post-submission if no acknowledgment received

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and review approval letter/sanction order from NSTEDB  
- [ ] Verify sanctioned amount and duration of utilization  
- [ ] Ensure funds are used strictly for approved camp expenses (honorarium, TA/DA, printing, venue, incidental costs)  
- [ ] Maintain auditable records of all expenditures  
- [ ] Submit utilization certificate and audited statement of expenditure to NSTEDB post-camp completion  
- [ ] Submit camp report including outcomes, attendance, feedback, and photographs  
- [ ] Coordinate with client to calculate and collect success fee as per engagement agreement  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with caveats: no recurring activities supported, funds used within stipulated time, utilization reports submitted on time
