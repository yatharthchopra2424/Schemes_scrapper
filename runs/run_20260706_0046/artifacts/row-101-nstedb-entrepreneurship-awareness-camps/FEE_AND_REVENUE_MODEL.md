## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client.
2. Transparent fee structure with clear milestones for retainer and success fees.
3. Risk-adjusted pricing reflecting the scheme’s processing time and approval uncertainty.
4. Scheme-specific note: This scheme targets educational institutions (not commercial entities) for organizing Entrepreneurship Awareness Camps, with financial support varying based on scale and duration. Success is contingent on proposal quality, institutional capacity, and adherence to NSTEDB guidelines, introducing moderate risk due to subjective evaluation and fund availability constraints.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description:** Retainer fee for consulting services related to the NSTEDB Entrepreneurship Awareness Camps scheme, including proposal preparation, eligibility assessment, documentation support, and liaison with NSTEDB, DST.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description:** Success fee for securing financial sanction under the NSTEDB Entrepreneurship Awareness Camps scheme, implemented by the National Science and Technology Entrepreneurship Development Board (NSTEDB), Department of Science and Technology (DST), Ministry of Science and Technology.
