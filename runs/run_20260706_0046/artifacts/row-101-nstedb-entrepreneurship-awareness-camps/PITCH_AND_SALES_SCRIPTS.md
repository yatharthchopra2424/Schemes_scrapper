# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping educational institutions like universities, colleges, polytechnics, and technical institutes access government grants for entrepreneurship awareness initiatives. The NSTEDB Entrepreneurship Awareness Camps scheme is designed specifically to support institutions in fostering entrepreneurial mindsets among students and youth. I’d like to understand your institution’s goals and see if this aligns with your mission.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of educational institution are you (university, college, polytechnic, technical institute)?
2. Do you currently organize programs focused on entrepreneurship awareness or innovation for students?
3. What is your target audience for such programs (e.g., undergraduate students, youth from specific regions)?
4. Do you have the necessary infrastructure (venue, facilities) and faculty to host a multi-day camp?
5. Are you located in or serving students from backward regions or underrepresented groups?
6. What is your typical budget range for organizing student-focused events or workshops?
7. Have you previously applied for or received grants from DST/NSTEDB for similar initiatives?
8. What outcomes are you aiming to achieve (e.g., startup creation, innovation mindset, industry linkages)?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is an educational institution (university, college, polytechnic, or technical institute)
- [ ] Institution has necessary infrastructure to conduct Entrepreneurship Awareness Camps
- [ ] Institution has faculty capable of organizing and delivering entrepreneurship programs
- [ ] Target beneficiaries include students and/or youth
- [ ] Preference may be given to institutions in backward regions or serving underrepresented groups (if applicable)
- [ ] Proposal must include objectives, target audience, duration, resource persons, budget estimate, and expected outcomes
- [ ] Institution undertakes to submit utilization certificates and audited expenditure reports post-camp
- [ ] Funds will be used strictly for the approved camp purpose and within the stipulated time frame
- [ ] Application is for a one-time awareness camp (not for recurring entrepreneurial activities)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your institution is well-positioned to benefit from the NSTEDB Entrepreneurship Awareness Camps scheme, which provides grant-in-aid support to cover all camp-related expenses—including honorariums, TA/DA, materials, and venue costs—to foster entrepreneurship among students. Shall I walk you through how this works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many educational institutions struggle to organize impactful entrepreneurship awareness programs due to limited budgets for expert honorariums, participant logistics, and study materials. This limits students’ exposure to entrepreneurship as a career path and hinders innovation ecosystem growth, especially in underserved regions.

**Scheme intro:**
The NSTEDB Entrepreneurship Awareness Camps, administered by the National Science and Technology Entrepreneurship Development Board (NSTEDB) under the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Ministry of Science and Technology, provides grant-in-aid financial support to educational institutions for organizing Entrepreneurship Awareness Camps. The grant covers honorariums for resource persons and coordinators, TA/DA for participants, printing of study materials, venue expenses, and other incidental costs, with the exact amount varying based on camp scale and duration per NSTEDB guidelines.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are an educational institution (university/college/polytechnic/technical institute) with the infrastructure to host such programs  
- You have faculty capable of organizing entrepreneurship-focused activities  
- Your target beneficiaries include students and youth, aligning with the scheme’s focus  
- [If applicable] Your location/service to backward or underrepresented groups may receive preference under the scheme

**Proof:**

**Next step:**
We offer a free, no-obligation eligibility check and document review. If you qualify, we assist with proposal development and submission on a success-fee-only basis—you pay only upon approval.
