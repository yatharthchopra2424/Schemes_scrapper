### NSTEDB Entrepreneurship Awareness Camps
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Science and Technology Entrepreneurship Development Board (NSTEDB), Department of Science and Technology (DST), Ministry of Science and Technology
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dst.gov.in/nstedb
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Applications Invited Throughout The Year

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Educational institutions such as universities, colleges, polytechnics, and technical institutions
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Education
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Institutions must have the necessary infrastructure and faculty to conduct such programs. Preference may be given to institutions in backward regions or those serving underrepresented groups.

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support covers expenses such as honorarium for resource persons and coordinators, TA/DA for participants, printing of study materials, venue expenses, and other incidental costs. The exact amount varies based on the scale and duration of the camp, subject to NSTEDB guidelines.
- Disbursement mode: Grant-in-aid
- Duration of benefit: One-time per approved camp

#### Documents Required
1. Proposal document detailing objectives, target audience, duration, and budget
2. List of resource persons with their credentials
3. Budget estimate for the camp
4. Infrastructure and faculty details of the institution
5. Undertaking from the institution head

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Educational institutions interested in organizing Entrepreneurship Awareness Camps must submit a proposal to NSTEDB, DST.
2. The proposal should include details such as objectives, target audience, duration, resource persons, budget estimate, and expected outcomes.
3. Proposals are evaluated by NSTEDB based on feasibility, relevance, and alignment with scheme objectives.
4. Approved proposals receive financial sanction and guidelines for implementation.
5. Institutions conduct the camp as per the approved plan and submit utilization certificates and reports to NSTEDB post-completion.
