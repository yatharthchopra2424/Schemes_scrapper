## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-based pricing**: Fees aligned with the ₹20 lakh maximum grant value per school, reflecting the substantial financial benefit delivered.
2. **Risk-adjusted success fees**: Success fee range (6–8% for applicable tier) calibrated to moderate risk profile due to milestone-based disbursement and utilization reporting requirements.
3. **Retainer for pre-approval work**: Covers application preparation, documentation, and portal navigation given the structured but competitive selection process.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: Processing time involves evaluation cycles and milestone verification, introducing a 3–6 month lag from approval to full disbursement; success is contingent on ongoing compliance with operational commitments (3–4 hours/week lab usage).

**Description**: Success fee for securing Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL) grant from Atal Innovation Mission (AIM), NITI Aayog – covering the full grant value of up to ₹20 lakhs (₹10 lakhs establishment + ₹10 lakhs operational over 5 years) upon milestone-based disbursement and utilization compliance.
