# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping schools access government innovation grants like the Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL) scheme, which provides up to INR 20 lakhs to establish hands-on STEM learning spaces for students. I’d like to understand your school’s current infrastructure and innovation goals to see if this could be a strong fit — no obligation, just a diagnostic conversation.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of school is yours (Government, Government-aided, or private trust/society-managed), and do you have a valid UDISE code?
- When was your school established or last recognised by the education authority?
- Do you currently have a dedicated space (minimum 20–25 sq. ft.) that could be converted into a tinkering lab, with reliable electricity and internet?
- Are you able to commit to running the lab for at least 3–4 hours per week beyond regular school hours for student activities?
- What is your current capacity to offer hands-on STEM, robotics, or coding activities for students in grades 6–12?
- Does your school/trust have a valid PAN card and audited financial statements for the last financial year?
- Are you open to participating in national-level innovation challenges, exhibitions, and mentorship programs if selected?
- Have you previously applied for any central or state government education or innovation grants?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] School is Government, Government-aided, or managed by a private trust/society
- [ ] School has a valid UDISE code
- [ ] School has a valid PAN card (in school/trust name)
- [ ] School has minimum required infrastructure (space, electricity, internet, computers)
- [ ] School commits to running ATL for minimum 3–4 hours per week beyond school hours
- [ ] School serves students in grades 6–12 (target beneficiary alignment)
- [ ] School is willing to sign MoU and submit utilization reports/milestones
- [ ] School has not been blacklisted from central government grants

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, your school appears to meet several key criteria for the Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL) scheme — particularly your infrastructure readiness and commitment to extended-hour student engagement. The ATL scheme offers up to INR 20 lakhs (INR 10 lakh for setup, INR 10 lakh for operations over 5 years) to establish such labs, and I believe we have a strong case to explore eligibility further.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many schools lack dedicated spaces and resources for hands-on STEM education, limiting students’ exposure to innovation, problem-solving, and future-ready skills like coding, robotics, and design thinking — despite growing emphasis on experiential learning in national education policy.

**Scheme intro:**
The Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL) scheme, implemented by the Atal Innovation Mission (AIM) under NITI Aayog, is a pan-India grant programme that provides selected schools with a one-time establishment grant of up to INR 10,00,000 and operational expenses of up to INR 10,00,000 over five years (INR 2 lakh per annum) to set up and run innovation labs. The grant supports equipment, tools, consumables, and mentorship access. Applications are accepted year-round via the official portal: https://aim.gov.in/atl.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your school is managed by a Government/Government-aided/private trust/society — an eligible entity type
- You possess a valid UDISE code and PAN card — mandatory documentation
- You have or can allocate space with electricity and internet — meeting infrastructure prerequisites
- You commit to running the lab 3–4 hours weekly beyond school hours — a core eligibility condition
