### Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Atal Innovation Mission (AIM), NITI Aayog
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://aim.gov.in/atl
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Schools (Government, Government-aided, or private)
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Education
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have minimum required infrastructure and commit to running the lab for at least 3-4 hours per week beyond regular school hours; managed by Government, local bodies, or private trusts/societies

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to INR 20 Lakhs (INR 10 lakh establishment + INR 10 lakh operational over 5 years)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (in installments based on milestones and utilization reports)
- Duration of benefit: One-time establishment grant + operational support over maximum 5 years

#### Documents Required
1. School registration certificate
2. UDISE code of the school
3. PAN card of the school/trust
4. Photographs of the proposed lab space
5. Details of infrastructure available (computers, internet, electricity)
6. Undertaking to run the lab for minimum 3-4 hours per week beyond school hours

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the official ATL application portal at https://aim.gov.in/atl
2. Register the school by filling in basic details such as school name, address, contact information, and UDISE code
3. Fill out the online application form with details about infrastructure, faculty, and commitment to run the lab
4. Upload required documents including school registration certificate, UDISE details, and photographs of proposed lab space
5. Submit the application and await evaluation by the AIM selection committee
6. If selected, sign the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) and receive grant disbursement in phases
