## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL)** scheme is a flagship initiative under the **Atal Innovation Mission (AIM)**, NITI Aayog, designed to foster innovation, creativity, and STEM education among school students across India. It provides financial and infrastructural support to schools to establish dedicated innovation workspaces where students can engage in hands-on learning, experimentation, and problem-solving activities.

### Objectives
The core objectives of the ATL scheme are:
- To create workspaces where young minds can learn innovation skills  
- To foster curiosity, creativity, and imagination in students  
- To inculcate skills such as design mindset, computational thinking, adaptive learning, and physical computing  
- To provide a platform for students to develop solutions to local problems  
- To promote STEM education through experiential learning

The application process for ATL is entirely online and follows a structured sequence:

#### Detailed Steps:
1. **Portal Access**: Visit the official ATL application portal at [https://aim.gov.in/atl](https://aim.gov.in/atl)  
2. **School Registration**: Fill in basic details including school name, address, contact information, and UDISE code  
3. **Application Form Completion**: Provide details about existing infrastructure, faculty availability, and explicit commitment to run the lab for 3-4 hours per week beyond regular school hours  
4. **Document Upload**: Upload scanned copies of:
   - School registration certificate  
   - UDISE code verification  
   - PAN card of the school or managing trust/society  
   - Photographs of the proposed lab space  
   - Details of available infrastructure (computers, internet, electricity)  
   - Signed undertaking to operate the lab for minimum 3-4 hours per week beyond school hours  
5. **Submission & Evaluation**: Submit the application and await review by the AIM Selection Committee (evaluations occur in periodic cycles despite rolling basis)  
6. **Post-Selection**: If selected, sign the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with AIM  
7. **Grant Disbursement**: Receive funds in installments contingent upon:
   - Achievement of predefined milestones (e.g., lab setup completion)  
   - Submission of utilization certificates and progress reports  
8. **Ongoing Compliance**: Maintain lab operations for 3-4 hours/week beyond school hours and submit periodic reports to continue receiving operational grant  

> **> 📌 Important Notes**:
> - Applications are accepted on a **rolling basis** throughout the year, subject to fund availability and periodic review cycles  
> - There is **no fixed deadline**; however, selection depends on budget cycles and evaluation schedules  
> - The grant is **non-transferable** and must be used exclusively for ATL activities  
> - Schools must maintain proper accounting and submit audited utilization certificates for fund release
