## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is not explicitly mentioned, but a **project proposal detailing objectives, methodology, and expected outcomes** is required.  
If a DPR is expected by the nodal officer, standard sections should include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Objectives and Alignment with National Clean Energy Goals  
- Methodology and Work Plan  
- Technical Feasibility and Innovation Quotient  
- Budget Breakdown and Justification  
- Implementation Timeline (Milestone-based)  
- Expected Outcomes and Scalability Plan  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Strategy  
- IP Management Plan  
- Team Credentials and Collaborator Details  
- Utilization Plan and Sustainability Model  

> **Verify with nodal officer** whether a full DPR is required or if the project proposal suffices.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal via the Department of Science and Technology (DST) website or designated Mission Innovation India portal.  
2. Select “Apply for Mission Innovation India” or equivalent grant call.  
3. Fill in applicant organization details exactly as per registration documents (name, PAN, address).  
4. Clearly articulate how the project aligns with national clean energy goals and Mission Innovation India’s thematic areas.  
5. Provide a detailed budget with item-wise justification; ensure totals match funding request.  
6. Upload all scheme-specific documents in the prescribed format and size limits.  
7. Declare IP status and management plan explicitly; note any government-use clauses if applicable.  
8. Confirm compliance with environmental and safety regulations in the declaration section.  
9. Double-check that all mandatory fields are marked and documents are attached before final submit.  
10. Do not refresh or navigate away during final submission to avoid session timeout.
