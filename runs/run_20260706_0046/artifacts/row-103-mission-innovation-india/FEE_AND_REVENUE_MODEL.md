## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the financial benefit delivered, with success fees reflecting the scheme’s grant quantum (up to 100% for academic institutions, 50% for industry partners).
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees account for the scheme’s competitive review process and milestone-based disbursement, which introduces uncertainty in approval timing and funding continuity.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainer structures are designed to match client capacity — startups and MSMEs (primary beneficiaries) align with Growth tier, while larger industry partners and institutions fit Scale or Enterprise.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Given the scheme’s reliance on budgetary allocations and mandatory progress reporting, the risk profile is medium. Approvals are not guaranteed, and funding is released in stages, increasing the importance of retainer stability for consulting continuity.
