## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping clean energy innovators access government funding for research, development, and pilot projects. Mission Innovation India supports Indian startups, MSMEs, academic institutions, and industry partners working on scalable clean energy technologies with grants covering up to 50% of project costs for industry partners. I’d like to understand your current innovation goals and current clean energy innovation work to see if this could be a fit.

### Discovery Questions
- What clean energy technology or innovation are you currently developing or planning to develop?
- When was your organisation incorporated or registered (to confirm eligibility as a startup/MSME/academic/industry entity)?
- What stage is your project at — idea, lab-scale, pilot, or ready for demonstration?
- Are you seeking funding to cover research, development, testing, or demonstration costs?
- Do you have collaborators such as research institutions, other industry partners, or international organisations involved?
- What is your estimated project budget, and how much of it are you looking to fund through non-dilutive grants?
- Are you aligned with national clean energy goals such as those under the National Solar Mission, National Hydrogen Mission, or similar initiatives?
- Have you previously applied for government innovation grants, and if so, what was the outcome?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is an Indian startup, MSME, academic institution, research organization, or industry partner
- [ ] Project focuses on innovative clean energy technologies
- [ ] Project aligns with national clean energy goals (e.g., solar, hydrogen, bioenergy, grid storage)
- [ ] Project demonstrates potential for scalability and impact
- [ ] Applicant has or can provide: project proposal, budget breakdown, technical feasibility report, organisational details, collaborator information, IP management plan
- [ ] Applicant is willing to comply with environmental, safety regulations and provide progress reports/utilization certificates
- [ ] Applicant accepts that IP may be subject to government-use clauses
- [ ] Project cost does not exceed available budgetary allocations (to be assessed during review)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, your work in [specific technology area mentioned] appears to align well with Mission Innovation India’s focus on scalable clean energy innovation. This scheme offers grants covering up to 50% of project costs for industry partners, with funding disbursed in milestones for R&D and pilot projects. Let me explain how it works and whether you might qualify.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Problem framing:
Clean energy innovators often struggle to secure early-stage, non-dilutive funding for research, development, and pilot-scale demonstration — especially when projects are capital-intensive, involve long timelines, or require access to testing facilities and expert mentorship. Traditional financing may not support high-risk, high-impact clean energy R&D, leaving promising innovations stuck in the lab.

### Scheme intro:
Mission Innovation India, implemented by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), is a pan-India innovation grant scheme that provides financial support for research, development, and demonstration projects in clean energy. Funding is provided as grants, covering up to 100% of project costs for academic and research institutions, and up to 50% for industry partners, disbursed in milestones based on project progress.

### Why they qualify (personalised):
- You are developing an innovative clean energy technology aligned with national goals (e.g., solar, hydrogen, storage)
- Your project is at a stage suitable for R&D, pilot, or demonstration funding
- You are an Indian startup, MSME, academic institution, or industry partner eligible to apply
- You are willing to submit a detailed project proposal, budget, technical feasibility report, and IP management plan
