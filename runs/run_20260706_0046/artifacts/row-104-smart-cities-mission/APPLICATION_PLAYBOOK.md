## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Client is an Urban Local Body (ULB), Municipal Corporation, or State Government entity  
- [ ] Client is located in India (Pan-India geographic scope applies)  
- [ ] Client has been nominated by its State Government for participation in the Smart Cities Challenge  
- [ ] Client meets the nomination criteria: urban population threshold, presence of municipal corporation, and demonstrated readiness for reform (as defined by MoHUA for each challenge round)  
- [ ] Client has not already been selected in a previous Smart Cities Mission round (unless applying for a new challenge cycle)  
- [ ] Client understands the requirement to incorporate a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) if selected  
- [ ] Client commits to providing matching funds equal to the central assistance (INR 100 crore) from State Government and ULB combined

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Government Order establishing the ULB or Municipal Corporation  
- PAN Card of the ULB / Municipal Corporation / State Government entity  
- Audited Financial Statements (last 3 years)  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- Authorization Letter from competent authority (Mayor, Commissioner, or Principal Secretary)  
- Board/Council Resolution authorizing application and commitment of matching funds  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Smart City Proposal (SCP) – detailing vision, area-based development plan, pan-city initiatives, and financial sustainability  
- Certificate of Incorporation of Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) – *required post-selection, but draft SPV structure may be included in SCP*  
- Board Resolution of the SPV – *to be obtained post-incorporation; include proposed SPV governance structure in SCP*  
- Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between State Government, ULB, and proposed SPV  
- Utilization Certificate template (for future fund release claims)  
- Project Reports and Progress Updates framework (to be submitted during implementation)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (minimum 300 DPI)  
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ClientName_Date.pdf` (e.g., `SmartCitiesMission_SCP_AhmedabadMunicipalCorporation_20240515May2024.pdf`)  
- All certificates must be valid and not expired (where applicable)  
- Financial documents must be audited and certified by a Chartered Accountant  
- Resolutions must be signed, sealed, and dated by authorized signatory  
- SCP must be in English or Hindi; if in regional language, certified translation must be attached  
- MoU must be on non-judicial stamp paper of appropriate value and notarized

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Smart City Proposal (SCP) is required and serves as the detailed project report. It must include:  
- Vision and goals for smart city development  
- Area-Based Development (ABD) plan covering a specific geographic area  
- Pan-city initiatives leveraging technology for city-wide service improvement  
- Financial plan detailing funding sources, including matching contributions from State and ULB  
- Implementation timeline and milestones  
- Institutional framework (including SPV structure)  
- Expected outcomes and impact on quality of life, sustainability, and inclusivity  
- Risk assessment and mitigation strategy  

> **Note**: The SCP is evaluated competitively by a national-level committee. Quality and innovation are critical selection criteria.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official portal: https://smartcities.gov.in  
2. Download the latest Smart City Proposal (SCP) template and guidelines from the "Challenge" or "Guidelines" section  
3. Ensure all data entered is consistent across documents (e.g., population figures, financials)  
4. Clearly demarcate Area-Based Development (ABD) and Pan-City initiatives in the proposal  
5. Provide realistic, milestone-based financial projections tied to fund release tranches  
6. Demonstrate convergence with other central and state schemes (e.g., AMRUT, Swachh Bharat, Digital India)  
7. Include stakeholder consultation reports and citizen feedback summaries  
8. Attach all mandatory annexures: SPV incorporation details, MoU draft, board resolutions  
9. Validate that the proposal emphasizes sustainability, inclusivity, and private sector participation (PPP)  
10. Save draft frequently and generate PDF for internal review before final submission  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Smart City Proposal (SCP) is complete and aligned with MoHUA guidelines  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached and properly formatted  
- [ ] Financial plan clearly shows INR 100 crore central assistance + INR 100 crore matching share (State + ULB)  
- [ ] Milestones are specific, measurable, and time-bound  
- [ ] SPV structure and governance framework are clearly defined  
- [ ] MoU between State, ULB, and SPV is drafted and ready for execution  
- [ ] Declarations and undertakings are signed by authorized signatory  
- [ ] File size of final SCP does not exceed portal upload limit (verify on portal)  
- [ ] Application is submitted before the challenge round deadline

- [ ] Submit quarterly progress reports and utilization certificates for subsequent fund tranches  
- [ ] Ensure matching contribution (INR 100 crore) from State Government and ULB is deposited in proportion to central fund release  
- [ ] Prepare for internal and third-party audits as required by MoHUA  
- [ ] Coordinate with consultant for success fee invoicing upon milestone-linked fund disbursement
