## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial benefit (up to ₹100 Cr central assistance per city) and the strategic urban transformation outcomes delivered.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the competitive, challenge-based selection process and milestone-dependent fund releases, which introduce execution risk despite high scheme credibility.
3. **Transparency & Trust**: Clear retainer for proposal development (SCP, SPV setup, MoU) and success fee only upon central fund sanction and release, aligned with MoHUA’s utilization certificate requirements.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Given the 5-year staggered disbursement (₹50 Cr Year 1, ₹10 Cr/Year for Years 2–5) and competitive selection with periodic challenge rounds, the firm applies a conservative success fee range (2–4%) and structures retainers to cover intensive proposal and SPV formation work. Processing timelines (typically 6–12 months from proposal to first tranche) necessitate phased billing.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for securing central financial assistance under the Smart Cities Mission, administered by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), upon sanction and release of the first installment of central funds (₹50 Cr) to the city’s SPV.
