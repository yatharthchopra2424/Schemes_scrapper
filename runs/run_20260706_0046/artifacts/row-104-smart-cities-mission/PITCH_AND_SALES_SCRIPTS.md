## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping urban local bodies and municipal corporations access central government funding for urban transformation initiatives. The Smart Cities Mission is designed to support cities receive INR 100 crore in central assistance over five years, matched by state and ULB contributions for a total of INR 200 crore per city to improve infrastructure and quality of life. I’d like to understand your city’s current urban development priorities and see if this mission aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
- What is the current population and administrative status of your urban local body (e.g., municipal corporation, municipality)?
- Has your state nominated your city for participation in the Smart Cities Challenge in previous or current rounds?
- Are you currently preparing or planning to prepare a Smart City Proposal (SCP) for area-based development and pan-city initiatives?
- Do you have a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) incorporated or planned for implementing smart city projects?
- What is the status of your memorandum of understanding (MoU) between the state government, ULB, and SPV?
- What urban infrastructure challenges (water supply, sanitation, mobility, etc.) are you aiming to address through smart solutions?
- Are you able to commit to matching the central assistance of INR 100 crore with equal contributions from the state and ULB?
- What is your timeline for submitting utilization certificates and progress reports for fund release?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Urban local body, municipal corporation, or state government entity
- [ ] Nominated by state government for Smart Cities Challenge
- [ ] Preparing or willing to prepare a Smart City Proposal (SCP)
- [ ] Willing to incorporate a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) for implementation
- [ ] Able to secure matching funds from state government and ULB (equal to central assistance)
- [ ] Commitment to submit utilization certificates and progress reports and utilization certificates
- [ ] Alignment with mission objectives: core infrastructure, quality of life, smart solutions, sustainability, inclusive development

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your city may be well-positioned to participate in the Smart Cities Mission. The scheme provides INR 100 crore in central assistance per selected city over five years, matched by state and ULB for a total of INR 200 crore to drive urban transformation. Let me share how we’ve supported similar urban bodies in securing this funding.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Urban local bodies face significant challenges in funding large-scale infrastructure upgrades, adopting smart technologies, and delivering improved services due to limited municipal budgets and complex project implementation requirements. The Smart Cities Mission addresses this by providing substantial central funding to enable comprehensive urban renewal.

**Scheme intro:**
The Smart Cities Mission, administered by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), is a central sector grant scheme that provides INR 100 crore in central financial assistance per selected smart city over five years (INR 50 crore in the first year and INR 10 crore annually for the next four years). This must be matched by an equal contribution from the state government and urban local body (ULB), resulting in a total of INR 200 crore per city for area-based development and pan-city smart initiatives.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your urban local body is eligible as a municipal corporation or similar entity under the mission’s target beneficiaries
- You have expressed willingness or ability to prepare a Smart City Proposal (SCP) detailing vision, area-based development, and pan-city initiatives
- You are positioned to incorporate a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) and establish the required MoU between state, ULB, and SPV
- You acknowledge the need for matching funds from state and ULB to access the central assistance

**Proof:**
"We've helped [N] similar urban local bodies get approved for the Smart Cities Mission. [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]"

**Next step:**
We offer a free, no-obligation eligibility check to assess your city’s readiness for the Smart Cities Challenge. If there’s a fit, we support end-to-end application preparation—from SCP development to SPV setup and MoU drafting—on a success-fee-only basis. You pay only if your city is selected and funds are approved.
