### Smart Cities Mission
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://smartcities.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Challenge-based selection with periodic rounds; no rolling deadline. Specific timelines announced for each challenge round.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Urban local bodies; state governments; municipal corporations
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Urban infrastructure and development
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Cities must be nominated by states based on urban population, presence of municipal corporation, and readiness for reform; selected cities must prepare a Smart City Proposal (SCP) detailing vision, plan for area-based development and pan-city initiatives, and financial sustainability.

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: INR 100 crore central assistance per selected city over five years (matched by equal contribution from state government and ULB for total INR 200 crore per city)
- Disbursement mode: Installments based on achievement of milestones and submission of utilization certificates
- Duration of benefit: Five years (INR 50 crore in first year, INR 10 crore each year for next four years)

#### Documents Required
1. Smart City Proposal (SCP)
2. Certificate of incorporation of Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV)
3. Board resolution of SPV
4. Utilization certificates
5. Project reports and progress updates
6. Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between state, ULB, and SPV

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. States nominate cities for participation in the Smart Cities Challenge based on set criteria.
2. Nominated cities prepare Smart City Proposals (SCPs) detailing vision, area-based development, area-based development, and financial plan.
3. SCPs are-based development, pan-city initiatives, and financial plan.
3. SCPs are evaluated by a national-level committee through a competitive challenge process.
4. Selected cities are announced and must incorporate a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) for implementation.
5. SPVs implement projects as per the SCP and submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates for fund release.
