## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant must be a registered Indian organisation.
- [ ] The applicant must have demonstrable competency in undertaking science, technology, innovation based documentation and/or dissemination or any other activity of similar nature.
- [ ] The applicant must have experience in scouting and documentation of grassroots technological innovations, innovative agricultural practices, and outstanding herbal knowledge in the field of Agriculture, Veterinary or Human Health.
- [ ] The applicant must have projects undertaken and credentials of core team members that reflect relevant experience.
- [ ] The applicant must not have shared incomplete or incorrect information.
- [ ] The applicant must have demonstrable output as reflected in documents shared.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate
- PAN Card
- Audited Financial Statements / ITR
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration Certificate

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Expression of Interest (EOI) with suitability note and work done in the field of innovation
- Examples of scouted innovations and traditional knowledge practices (if available)
- Project Proposal (as per format specified by NIF)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and in PDF format (unless otherwise specified)
- File names must match the document type and applicant name
- Scanned copies must be certified true copies where applicable
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, a certified translation must be attached
- File size must not exceed 10 MB per document

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not explicitly mentioned as required for EOI submission. However, a detailed Project Proposal is required as part of the EOI format. Verify with the nodal officer whether a full DPR is needed post-EOI shortlisting.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the EOI format from the NIF website or request it via email to vivekkumar@nifindia.org.
2. Fill in all fields in the Project Proposal format as specified by NIF.
3. Attach the suitability note detailing the applicant’s experience and objectives.
4. Include examples of scouted innovations and traditional knowledge practices, if available.
5. Ensure the proposal has clear objectives, deliverables, and a commensurate budget.
6. Double-check that all information is accurate and complete to avoid rejection due to incomplete or incorrect data.
7. Submit the EOI via email to vivekkumar@nifindia.org and campaign@nifindia.org or by post to the specified address.
8. Keep a copy of the submitted EOI and proof of submission (email sent receipt or postal tracking).

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All sections of the EOI and Project Proposal are completed.
- [ ] Suitability note is included and aligned with NIF’s scouting and documentation goals.
- [ ] Examples of scouted innovations (if available) are attached.
- [ ] Budget is realistic and commensurate with proposed activities.
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached and verified.
- [ ] Declarations and undertakings are signed where required.
- [ ] File names and formats comply with quality check standards.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and review the approval letter from NIF.
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount and duration of support.
- [ ] Sign any required agreements or MoUs with NIF.
- [ ] Utilize the institutional support for scouting, documentation, filing patents, prototyping, value addition, validation, and diffusion as per the approved proposal.
- [ ] Submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates as required by NIF.
- [ ] Coordinate with NIF for technology transfer, commercialization, and social diffusion activities.
- [ ] Ensure compliance with Prior Informed Consent (PIC) and benefit-sharing norms.
- [ ] Prepare for final submission and impact assessment.
- [ ] Invoice success fee as per consultancy agreement (to be filled by consultant).
