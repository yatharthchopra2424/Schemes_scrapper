## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the tangible innovation outcomes delivered to grassroots innovators and traditional knowledge holders.
2. Transparent fee structures with clear milestones for scouting, documentation, validation, and dissemination support.
3. Risk-sharing model where success fees are contingent upon measurable outcomes such as patent grants, technology transfer, or commercialization.
4. Scheme-specific note: The National Innovation Foundation (NIF) Grants scheme operates on a non-monetary recognition and support model (e.g., prototyping, IP support, dissemination) rather than direct cash grants. Processing timelines are long (e.g., biennial campaigns open for years), and success is measured through technology transfer, patents, and incubation outcomes. This reduces immediate financial risk but increases engagement duration, requiring consultants to manage long-term client relationships with phased deliverables.
