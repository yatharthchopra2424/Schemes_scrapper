# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping grassroots innovators, artisans, farmers, and local communities access government support for their technological innovations and traditional knowledge. The National Innovation Foundation (NIF) Grants are designed specifically for individuals and communities developing solutions at the grassroots level—whether in agriculture, engineering, health, or other fields—without reliance on formal sector support. I’d like to understand if your work aligns with this mission and explore whether NIF support could help scale your innovation.

### Discovery Questions
1. Can you describe the innovation or traditional knowledge practice you’ve developed, and the problem it solves in your community?
2. Are you an individual innovator, part of a local community, or representing an institution/organisation working at the grassroots level?
3. Has your innovation been developed without significant reliance on formal sector support or external funding?
4. Is your innovation focused on areas like agriculture, engineering, human health, veterinary science, or traditional knowledge?
5. Have you already documented your innovation through prototypes, field trials, or community validation?
6. Are you seeking support for prototyping, value addition, intellectual property protection, or diffusion of your innovation?
7. Is your innovation intended to generate material or non-material incentives for yourself and others in the value chain?
8. Are you based in India and working within rural, urban fringe, slum, or tribal communities?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Individual innovator, local community, or institution/organisation
- [ ] Grassroots technological innovation or outstanding traditional knowledge
- [ ] Developed without reliance on formal sector support
- [ ] Focused on solving local problems in agriculture, engineering, health, or traditional knowledge
- [ ] Innovation has been prototyped, validated, or field-tested
- [ ] Seeking support for prototyping, value addition, IP protection, or diffusion
- [ ] Innovation aims to generate incentives for innovators and value chain participants
- [ ] Based in India, working in rural, urban, or tribal areas

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, your work in [mention specific area, e.g., sustainable agriculture or herbal innovation] appears to align closely with the National Innovation Foundation (NIF) Grants, which support grassroots innovators with funding for prototyping, value addition, and technology diffusion. Let me explain how this scheme could help you scale your impact.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Grassroots innovators often lack access to resources for prototyping, validation, intellectual property protection, and scaling their solutions—despite creating effective, low-cost innovations that address real community needs in agriculture, health, and traditional knowledge.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Innovation Foundation (NIF) Grants, administered by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India, provide financial and technical support for grassroots technological innovations and outstanding traditional knowledge. Benefits include support for prototyping, value addition, validation, patent filing, and diffusion through commercial or non-commercial channels—tailored to the innovator’s needs.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are an individual innovator, local community member, or institution working at the grassroots level  
- Your innovation addresses a local problem without reliance on formal sector support  
- Your work falls within sectors like agriculture, engineering, human health, or traditional knowledge  
- You have already begun documenting or prototyping your innovation
