### National Innovation Foundation (NIF) Grants
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Innovation Foundation (NIF) - India, an autonomous body of Department of Science and Technology, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nif.org.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Individuals and local communities (grassroots innovators, farmers, artisans, mechanics, etc.)
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Any technological field (agriculture, engineering, human health & veterinary, etc.)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Innovations must be grassroots technological innovations or outstanding traditional knowledge; applicants must be individuals or local communities developing solutions without help from the formal sector

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Varies (includes prototyping support, value addition, validation, IP filing, technology transfer, business incubation, and dissemination)
- Disbursement mode: Direct support (technical and financial assistance), reimbursement for certain activities, and in-kind support
- Duration of benefit: Project-based (varies by activity: prototyping, validation, diffusion, etc.)

#### Documents Required
1. Expression of Interest (EOI) with suitability note and work done in the field of innovation
2. Examples of scouted innovations and traditional knowledge practices (if available)
3. Comprehensive resume/CV (for internship programs)
4. Project proposal (for institutional EOI)
5. Prior Informed Consent (PIC) from knowledge providers
6. Documentation of innovations (photographs, videos, interviews in prescribed NIF format)
7. Monthly report (for internship programs)
8. Application via online form (https://forms.gle/sbkYtzhAVzfVVrA5A for internship)
9. Submission via email (vivekkumar@nifindia.org, campaign@nifindia.org) or post to NIF address

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Submit Expression of Interest (EOI) along with suitability note and work done in the field of innovation
2. Attach examples of scouted innovations and traditional knowledge practices (if available)
3. Submit via email to vivekkumar@nifindia.org or campaign@nifindia.org, or by post to NIF address
4. Proposals reviewed by external Experts/Project Review Committee
5. Selection based on clear objectives, deliverables, and commensurate budget
6. For internship: Submit resume/CV via https://forms.gle/sbkYtzhAVzfVVrA5A
7. Initial screening based on qualifications, area of scouting, and alignment with program objectives
8. Shortlisted candidates invited for online interview
9. Final selection based on interview performance and overall fit
10. Upon selection, undergo orientation (online/offline, 2 days)
11. Conduct innovation scouting via field visits and community engagement
12. Document innovations through interviews, photographs, videos in NIF format
13. Submit monthly report with documented innovations
14. Receive monthly stipend (for internship) or institutional support (for EOI)
15. Upon completion, receive certificate (for internship) or project closure (for EOI)
