# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is a State Forest Department or a designated agency under the Green India Mission (GIM).
- [ ] The applicant is a Joint Forest Management Committee (JFMC), Village Forest Committee (VFC), Panchayati Raj Institution (PRI), NGO, or local community engaged in afforestation, reforestation, or forest conservation activities.
- [ ] The proposed activities are on forest or non-forest land as per approved plans under GIM.
- [ ] The project conforms to the approved Perspective Plan and Annual Plan of Operations (APO) under GIM.
- [ ] Afforestation activities use native and ecologically appropriate species.
- [ ] Land tenure and community rights are respected; no activities on disputed or private land without consent.
- [ ] The project encourages convergence with other schemes (e.g., MGNREGA, CAMPA) for optimal resource use.
- [ ] The applicant has prepared an Annual Plan of Operations (APO) based on the Perspective Plan under GIM.

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation/Registration Certificate
- PAN Card
- Audited Financial Statements / ITR
- GST Registration (if applicable)
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs, if applicable)
- Authorization Letter from the Applicant Organization

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Annual Plan of Operations (APO)
- Perspective Plan for the state
- Utilization Certificates (UCs) for previous fund releases
- Audited Statements of Expenditure
- Progress Reports (physical and financial)
- Geo-tagged photographs of plantation sites
- Minutes of JFMC/VFC meetings
- Land records and community consent documents

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format, and clearly named (e.g., APO_FY2024-25_StateName.pdf).
- File size should not exceed 10 MB per document; compress if necessary.
- Names on documents must match the applicant’s official records.
- Documents must be self-attested or certified by a gazetted officer where required.
- All documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, provide a certified translation.
- Utilization Certificates must be in the prescribed format and signed by the competent authority.
- Geo-tagged photographs must include latitude, longitude, date, and time stamps.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is not explicitly required for GIM APO submission. However, the APO must include detailed physical and financial targets, species-wise plantation plans, budget breakdown, and implementation arrangements. If a DPR is requested during appraisal, verify with the Nodal Officer (National Mission Authority, GIM) for required sections.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the GIM application portal via the MoEF&CC website (https://moef.gov.in) – note that the direct GIM page may redirect; use the scheme guidelines section or contact the National Mission Authority.
2. Fill the APO form with accurate financial and physical targets as per the Perspective Plan.
3. Ensure all figures are consistent across documents (APO, UC, progress reports).
4. Clearly specify the species to be planted, area (in hectares), and maintenance plan for 5 years.
5. Include a convergence plan with MGNREGA, CAMPA, or other relevant schemes.
6. Attach all mandatory documents in the prescribed order and format.
7. Declarations must be signed by the authorized signatory (e.g., PCCF or Nodal Officer) and stamped.
8. Double-check that land details and community consent are explicitly mentioned to avoid rejection on tenure grounds.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All sections of the APO form are completed accurately.
- [ ] Financial and physical targets match the Perspective Plan and previous UCs.
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents are attached and clearly labeled.
- [ ] Utilization Certificates are for the immediately preceding installment and show satisfactory utilization.
- [ ] Geo-tagged photographs are recent and show clear site identification.
- [ ] Minutes of JFMC/VFC meetings reflect approval of the APO and budget.
- [ ] Declarations are signed and stamped by the competent authority.
- [ ] No activities are proposed on disputed or private land without consent.
- [ ] Species listed are native and ecologically appropriate for the region.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the APO package via the designated channel (typically email to the National Mission Authority, GIM, or through the state forest department’s nodal officer).
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation or save the sent email with timestamp.
- Store the reference number or diary number provided upon receipt.
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days after submission to check on appraisal status.
- Mark the calendar for the next financial year’s APO preparation cycle (typically January–March).

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and securely store the approval letter or sanction order from the National Mission Authority, GIM.
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount against the APO request and note any reductions or conditions.
- [ ] Confirm the installment release schedule (typically 25% advance, then subsequent releases upon 75% utilization and UC submission).
- [ ] Prepare and submit the first Utilization Certificate (UC) and audited expenditure statement after utilizing 75% of the first installment.
- [ ] Ensure all expenditure is supported by original bills and vouchers as per PFMS guidelines.
- [ ] Track utilization in the Public Financial Management System (PFMS) under Scheme Code for GIM (to be confirmed with nodal officer).
- [ ] Submit periodic progress reports (physical and financial) as stipulated in the sanction letter.
- [ ] Upon project completion, submit the Final Technical Report (FTR), Consolidated Utilization Certificate, and audited statements for final settlement and release of the withheld 10% grant.
- [ ] Coordinate with the client to deduct the success fee from the sanctioned amount as per the retainer agreement. [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]
