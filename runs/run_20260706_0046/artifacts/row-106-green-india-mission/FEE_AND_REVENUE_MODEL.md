## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client, which for the Green India Mission is a grant-based scheme with no fixed upper limit on funding, though typical project values for state-level implementing agencies range from ₹1Cr to ₹10Cr+ based on Annual Plans of Operations (APOs).
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the moderate risk profile of this scheme — while funding is assured upon APO approval and utilization certificate submission, delays can occur due to annual planning cycles and monitoring requirements, justifying mid-to-low success fee ranges.
3. **Transparency & Predictability**: Retainer fees cover advisory work for APO preparation, documentation, and liaison with MoEF&CC, ensuring clients understand costs upfront despite the scheme’s annual planning cycle.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Green India Mission has an annual planning cycle with APOs submitted before each financial year, and funds are released in installments based on utilization certificates. This creates a predictable but staggered revenue lag, supporting the use of retainer fees to smooth cash flow during approval cycles.
