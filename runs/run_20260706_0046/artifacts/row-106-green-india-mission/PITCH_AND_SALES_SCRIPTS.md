# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because the Green India Mission (GIM) is specifically designed to support forest-dependent communities, local institutions like Joint Forest Management Committees (JFMCs), Village Forest Committees (VFCs), Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs), and NGOs engaged in afforestation, reforestation, and eco-restoration across India. I’d like to understand your current work in forest conservation or community-based natural resource management to see if GIM’s grant support could align with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is the legal status of your organization or committee (e.g., registered NGO, JFMC, VFC, PRI, or local community group)?
2. Are you currently engaged in afforestation, reforestation, or forest conservation activities on forest or non-forest land?
3. Do you have an approved Annual Plan of Operations (APO) or Perspective Plan) for your state under the Green India Mission framework?
4. Is your project located on land where community consent and land tenure rights are clearly established and not disputed?
5. Are you planning to use native and ecologically appropriate species for plantation activities?
6. Do you have systems in place to submit Utilization Certificates (UCs), audited expenditure statements, and progress reports (physical and financial)?
7. Are you able to provide geo-tagged photographs of plantation sites and minutes of JFMC/VFC meetings as part of reporting?
8. Is your project aligned with the perspective plan of your state forest department and does it converge with other schemes like MGNREGA or CAMPA?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Implemented through State Forest Departments or designated agencies  
- [ ] Eligible entity: JFMC, VFC, PRI, NGO, or local community  
- [ ] Engaged in afforestation, reforestation, or eco-restoration on forest/non-forest land  
- [ ] Has approved Annual Plan of Operations (APO) and Perspective Plan  
- [ ] Uses native and ecologically appropriate species  
- [ ] Respects land tenure and community rights; no activity on disputed/private land without consent  
- [ ] Projects conform to approved perspective plan and APOs  
- [ ] Willing to submit UCs, audited statements, progress reports, and geo-tagged photos  
- [ ] Open to convergence with MGNREGA, CAMPA, or other schemes  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your work in [mention specific activity, e.g., community-led afforestation using native species] aligns well with the Green India Mission’s objectives. The mission provides grants to support exactly these kinds of activities — covering plantation, maintenance, soil and moisture conservation, and community mobilization — with funds released based on approved APOs. Shall I walk you through how this could work for your initiative?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Forest-dependent communities and local institutions often lack the financial resources to scale up afforestation, reforestation, and eco-restoration efforts, despite having the willingness and local knowledge. Without sustained funding for plantation maintenance, soil conservation, and community engagement, these initiatives struggle to deliver long-term ecosystem benefits like carbon sequestration, water conservation, and livelihood improvement.

**Scheme intro:**
The Green India Mission (GIM), implemented by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEF&CC), is a central government grant scheme that provides financial assistance for afforestation, reforestation, and eco-restoration activities. Funds are released in installments based on approved Annual Plans of Operations (APOs) and utilization certificates, covering costs related to plantation, maintenance, soil and moisture conservation, and community mobilization.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a [JFMC/VFC/PRI/NGO/local community] engaged in afforestation/reforestation on forest or non-forest land — an eligible entity under GIM  
- You have or are preparing an Annual Plan of Operations (APO) aligned with your state’s perspective plan  
- You commit to using native and ecologically appropriate species and respecting land tenure and community rights  
- Your project supports convergence with schemes like MGNREGA or CAMPA for optimal resource use
