### Green India Mission
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEF&CC)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://moef.gov.in/green-india-mission
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Annual planning cycle — APOs are typically submitted before the start of each financial year for approval.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: State Forest Departments and other designated agencies
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Afforestation, reforestation, and forest conservation
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Eligible entities include Joint Forest Management Committees (JFMCs), Village Forest Committees (VFCs), Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs), NGOs, and local communities engaged in afforestation, reforestation, and forest conservation activities on forest and non-forest lands as per approved plans under the mission.

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance in the form of grants for afforestation, reforestation, and eco-restoration activities; funds released based on approved Annual Plans of Operations (APOs) and utilization certificates; covers costs related to plantation, maintenance, soil and moisture conservation, and community mobilization.
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (funds released in installments to states upon approval of APOs)
- Duration of benefit: Annual (based on approved Annual Plans of Operations)

#### Documents Required
1. Annual Plan of Operations (APO)
2. Perspective Plan for the state
3. Utilization Certificates (UCs)
4. Audited Statements of Expenditure
5. Progress Reports (physical and financial)
6. Geo-tagged photographs of plantation sites
7. Minutes of JFMC/VFC meetings
8. Land records and community consent documents

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. State Forest Departments prepare Annual Plans of Operations (APOs) based on the perspective plan under GIM.
2. APOs are submitted to the National Mission Authority for appraisal and approval.
3. Upon approval, funds are released in installments to the states.
4. States implement the projects through Joint Forest Management Committees (JFMCs), Village Forest Committees (VFCs), and other local institutions.
5. Progress is monitored through periodic reporting and field visits.
6. Utilization certificates and audited statements are submitted for subsequent fund releases.
