## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Manufacturer is registered under FAME-II with the Department of Heavy Industries, Ministry of Heavy Industries and Public Enterprises  
- [ ] Vehicle type approval certificate from ARAI or other designated testing agency is obtained for the vehicle model
- [ ] Vehicle model is on the approved list of eligible models under FAME-II
- [ ] For electric two-wheelers: battery capacity does not exceed 15 kWh; incentive capped at INR 10,000 per kWh
- [ ] For electric three-wheelers: battery capacity does not exceed 10 kWh; incentive capped at INR 20,000 per kWh
- [ ] For electric four-wheelers: battery capacity does not exceed 15 kWh; incentive capped at INR 10,000 per kWh
- [ ] For electric buses: vehicle length exceeds 12 meters; incentive capped at up to INR 20 lakhs per bus
- [ ] Vehicle is intended for public transport or commercial use (for buses and trucks)
- [ ] OEM is enrolled under FAME-II and authorized to claim incentives
- [ ] End consumer purchases the vehicle from an OEM enrolled under the scheme
- [ ] Application is submitted before March 31, 2024, or before fund exhaustion, whichever occurs earlier

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for FAME-II subsidy claims. The scheme operates on a monthly reimbursement model based on vehicle sales. However, verify with the nodal officer if any additional project details are needed for first-time OEM registration or model approval.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the FAME-II portal at https://fame2.heavyindustries.gov.in using OEM login credentials.
2. Navigate to the 'Claims Submission' section for monthly incentive claims.
3. Enter accurate OEM details (name, FAME-II registration number, GSTIN) as per registration.
4. Select the correct vehicle category (two-wheeler, three-wheeler, four-wheeler, bus) and model from the approved list.
5. Input vehicle-specific data: battery capacity (kWh), quantity sold, chassis numbers, and date of sale.
6. Calculate incentive amount based on applicable rate per kWh and vehicle category (respecting caps: 15 kWh for 2W/4W, 10 kWh for 3W, up to INR 20 lakhs/bus for >12m buses).
7. Attach all scheme-specific and universal documents in the prescribed format and size.
8. Declare that the subsidy has been or will be passed on to the end consumer and that the vehicle is used for eligible purposes (public/commercial for buses/trucks).
9. Preview the form thoroughly before final submission to avoid errors.
10. Submit only after internal review gate clearance.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All fields in the application form are filled accurately (OEM details, vehicle data, incentive calculation)
- [ ] Vehicle model is confirmed on the FAME-II approved list
- [ ] Battery capacity does not exceed category-specific limits (15 kWh for 2W/4W, 10 kWh for 3W)
- [ ] Incentive amount calculated correctly per kWh rate and category cap
- [ ] All required documents are attached and meet quality checks
- [ ] Proof of subsidy pass-through to consumer is clearly demonstrated
- [ ] Declarations regarding end-use and OEM enrollment are signed and attached
- [ ] Reference to monthly sales period and claim frequency is correct
- [ ] Application is submitted via the official FAME-II portal (https://fame2.heavyindustries.gov.in)
- [ ] Copy of submitted application and acknowledgment is saved for records
