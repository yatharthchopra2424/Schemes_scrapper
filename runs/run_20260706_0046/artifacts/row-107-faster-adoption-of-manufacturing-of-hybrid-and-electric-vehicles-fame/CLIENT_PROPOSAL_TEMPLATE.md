## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified Faster Adoption of Manufacturing of Hybrid and Electric Vehicles (FAME) — a subsidy scheme administered by the Department of Heavy Industry, Ministry of Heavy Industries and Public Enterprises — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is up to ₹20 lakhs per electric bus (for buses above 12 meters) or based on battery capacity for other vehicle categories, as per the scheme's incentive structure.

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment and vehicle/model classification under FAME-II guidelines  
2. Assistance with OEM registration on the FAME-II portal (https://fame2.heavyindustries.gov.in)  
3. Compilation and verification of required documents as per scheme guidelines  
4. Preparation and submission of monthly incentive claims  
5. Liaison with the Department of Heavy Industry for claim verification and approval  
6. Monitoring of fund disbursement and ensuring subsidy is passed to end consumers  
7. Monthly reporting on claim status and compliance, and support for DPR/project report requirements (not required for vehicle incentives under FAME-II; applicable only for charging infrastructure under separate guidelines)

## 4. What We Need From You
1. Documents as per the attached checklist, including vehicle type approval certificate from ARAI or designated agency, invoice of sale, registration certificate, proof of subsidy passing to end consumer, bank account details of the OEM, and monthly sales report of eligible vehicles  
2. A designated point of contact within your organization for coordination and document submission  
3. A response within 2 business days to queries to ensure timely claim processing
