## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial benefit delivered (up to ₹20 lakhs per electric bus or ₹10,000–20,000 per kWh for other EVs), ensuring consultant compensation scales with client savings.
2. **Risk Sharing**: Success fees are contingent on successful subsidy disbursement, aligning incentives with outcomes given the scheme’s reimbursement model and verification process.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainers are adjusted to client size and capacity to navigate the OEM-focused application process, with lower barriers for MSMEs and startups seeking to enroll as OEMs or access benefits.
4. **Scheme-Specific Risk Note**: Processing involves OEM registration, monthly claims, and verification by the Department of Heavy Industry, with typical approval lags of 2–3 months; funds are subject to availability and may exhaust before the March 31, 2024 deadline, increasing timing risk.
