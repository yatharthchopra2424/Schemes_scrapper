## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good [morning/afternoon], I'm calling from [Your Firm Name]. We specialise in helping businesses in the electric mobility and clean transport sector access government incentives like the Faster Adoption of Manufacturing of Hybrid and Electric Vehicles (FAME) scheme. I’d like to understand your current vehicle procurement or fleet electrification plans to see if FAME support could reduce your upfront costs — no obligation, just a quick diagnostic.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of vehicles are you currently procuring or planning to procure for your operations — two-wheelers, three-wheelers, four-wheelers, buses, or trucks?
2. Are you purchasing these vehicles for personal use, public transport, commercial fleet operations, or institutional deployment?
3. Have you checked whether the vehicle models you’re considering are on the FAME-II approved list and ARAI-certified?
4. Are you buying directly from an OEM, or are you procuring through dealers or third-party vendors?
5. What is the approximate battery capacity (in kWh) of the electric vehicles you’re evaluating?
6. Are you a manufacturer looking to claim incentives on vehicles sold, or an end-user beneficiary seeking lower upfront costs?
7. Do you have the required documentation ready, such as vehicle type approval certificate, sale invoice, and registration certificate?
8. Are you aware that FAME incentives are passed on to consumers via reduced ex-showroom prices and cannot be claimed directly by end users?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Vehicle type: Electric two-wheeler, three-wheeler, four-wheeler, bus, or bus/trucks, commercial, institutional, or individual consumer use
- [ ] Vehicle has valid ARAI or designated testing agency type approval certificate
- [ ] OEM is registered under FAME-II and model is on the approved list
- [ ] For buses/trucks: used for public transport or commercial purposes (not private use)
- [ ] Battery capacity within eligible limits: up to 15 kWh (2W/4W), up to 10 kWh (3W)
- [ ] End consumer benefit is passed through reduced ex-showroom price (not direct claim)
- [ ] Required documents available or obtainable: type approval, invoice, RC, bank details, sales report
- [ ] Application timeline aligns with scheme validity (open until fund exhaustion or March 31, 2024)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like your [vehicle type] procurement for [use case] could qualify for upfront incentives under the FAME-II scheme — potentially reducing your vehicle cost by lakhs per unit, depending on battery capacity and category. Let me walk you through how it works and what’s needed to access it.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Businesses and consumers face high upfront costs when switching to electric vehicles, which slows adoption despite lower operating expenses. Many are unaware that government subsidies can significantly reduce this barrier at the point of purchase — if they buy the right model from an enrolled OEM.

**Scheme intro:**
The Faster Adoption of Manufacturing of Hybrid and Electric Vehicles (FAME) scheme, administered by the Department of Heavy Industry under the Ministry of Heavy Industries and Public Enterprises, provides direct subsidies to manufacturers of eligible electric vehicles. These subsidies are passed on to buyers as reduced ex-showroom prices. Incentives range from INR 10,000 per kWh (up to INR 1.5 lakhs for two-wheelers) to INR 20 lakhs per electric bus (for buses above 12 meters), depending on vehicle category and battery capacity. The scheme is valid until fund exhaustion or March 31, 2024, whichever comes first. More details are available at the official portal: https://fame2.heavyindustries.gov.in

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are procuring [specific vehicle type, e.g., electric three-wheelers] for [use case, e.g., last-mile delivery or public transport]
- The vehicle model you’re considering is ARAI-certified and on the FAME-II approved list (as confirmed in discovery)
- You are purchasing from an OEM enrolled under FAME-II, enabling the subsidy to be passed on to you
- Your vehicle’s battery capacity ([e.g., 8 kWh]) falls within the eligible range for your vehicle category
