### Faster Adoption of Manufacturing of Hybrid and Electric Vehicles (FAME)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Heavy Industry, Ministry of Heavy Industries and Public Enterprises
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://fame2.heavyindustries.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Closed
- **Window:** Valid until March 31, 2024, or until exhaustion of funds, whichever occurs earlier

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: OEMs (Original Equipment Manufacturers) registered under FAME-II; public transport operators; fleet operators; institutional buyers; individual consumers
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Electric and hybrid vehicle manufacturing, public transport, fleet operations
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Vehicles must be approved by ARAI or designated testing agencies and meet performance and localization criteria; only vehicles manufactured by OEMs enrolled under FAME-II are eligible; end consumers must purchase from OEMs enrolled under the scheme

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Incentives range from INR 10,000 per kWh for two-wheelers (up to 15 kWh), INR 20,000 per kWh for three-wheelers (up to 10 kWh), INR 10,000 per kWh for four-wheelers (up to 15 kWh), and up to INR 20 lakhs per electric bus (for buses above 12 meters)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (reimbursement model: OEMs claim incentives after selling eligible vehicles; funds disbursed to manufacturer’s account)
- Duration of benefit: One-time per eligible vehicle sold

#### Documents Required
1. Vehicle type approval certificate from ARAI or designated agency
2. Invoice of sale
3. Registration certificate of the vehicle
4. Proof of subsidy passing to end consumer
5. Bank account details of the OEM for fund transfer
6. Monthly sales report of eligible vehicles

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. OEMs must register their eligible vehicle models on the FAME-II portal after obtaining ARAI certification.
2. Manufacturers submit monthly claims for incentives based on vehicles sold.
3. Claims are verified by the implementing agency.
4. Upon approval, funds are disbursed to the manufacturer’s account.
5. End consumers benefit through reduced ex-showroom prices at the time of purchase.
