# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is either a Public Sector Undertaking (PSU), private sector company, joint venture (JV), or consortium (not exceeding 3 companies)  
- [ ] Applicant will undertake to set up a new surface coal/lignite gasification project in India  
- [ ] Minimum plant capacity of the project, in terms of clean syngas, is 0.5 MTPA (calculated based on annual plant availability of 330 days at an average clean syngas density of 1.1 kg/Nm³)  
- [ ] Net worth of the applicant is more than 20% of the total project cost  
- [ ] No expenditure made prior to the approval of the Scheme is considered for incentive  
- [ ] Applicant is a company incorporated in India under the Companies Act, 2013 or incorporated outside India in accordance with the laws of its jurisdiction of incorporation  
- [ ] No insolvency proceedings have been admitted against the Applicant by the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) or any court of competent jurisdiction  
- [ ] Applicant has not been blacklisted by Central or State Government (or its instrumentalities)  
- [ ] Promoters or directors of the Applicant have not been convicted of an offence relating to coal block allocation or sentenced with imprisonment for more than 3 years  
- [ ] Applicant is in compliance of Applicable Laws  
- [ ] If consortium: comprises not more than three companies; members have nominated a lead member holding not less than 51% of paid-up and subscribed equity share capital; Joint Application Agreement executed; no change in consortium permitted during selection process without prior written approval of MoC; each member satisfies eligibility requirements  

**Sources:**  
- Scheme Key Facts: Eligibility criteria  
- RFP Document (04-06-2026b-wn-20.pdf), Section 1.3: Eligibility Parameters

- PDF file size <1 MB per file  
- Documents must be accompanied by English translations, duly authenticated and certified by the Applicant  
- Physical submission) original, sealed envelope addressed to Sh. Alok Verma, Under Secretary (Coal)")  
- File size must not exceed 10 MB per document

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Board-approved Pre-Feasibility Report (PFR) or Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required.  
**Standard Sections (as per Appendix B of RFP):**  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Description  
- Technology Description  
- Market Analysis  
- Technical Feasibility  
- Financial Feasibility  
- Environmental Impact Assessment  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation  
- Implementation Schedule  
- Project Cost Breakdown  
- Financing Plan  
- Economic Analysis  
- Conclusions and Recommendations  

**Sources:**  
- RFP Document (04-06-2026b-wn-20.pdf), Appendix B: Contents for PFR/DPR  
- Annexure IIA: Particulars of the Project  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Ensure all fields in the Application are filled completely; no blank fields allowed  
2. Use only the formats prescribed in the RFP annexures (II to X)  
3. Submit Application both on the E-Portal and in physical original form  
4. All pages must be serially numbered  
5. Authorised Signatory must sign all documents where required  
6. No conditions or qualifications may be attached to the Application  
7. Physical documents must be submitted in a sealed envelope superscribed:  
   “Enclosures for the Application for Setting up Surface Coal/Lignite Gasification Projects under the ₹37,500 crore Scheme of Ministry of Coal”  
8. Application Security (Bank Guarantee of ₹50,00,000) must be included and valid for ≥9 months from Application Due Date  

**Sources:**  
- RFP Document (04-06-2026b-wn-20.pdf), Section 2.3: Preparation and Submission of Application  
- Section 2.4: Tests of Responsiveness

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify approval letter (Letter of Award) from MoC  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount of Financial Incentive (up to 20% of P&M, capped at ₹5,000 crore/project)  
- [ ] Arrange for disbursement of 1st instalment (25%) upon completion of pre-implementation activities:  
    - Land acquisition  
    - Grant of Environment Clearance  
    - Coal/lignite tie-up  
    - Finalization of technology licensor  
    - Finalization of EPC contractor  
    - Achievement of Financial Closure  
- [ ] Submit Insurance Surety Bond or Bank Guarantee (25% of 1st instalment amount) prior to 1st instalment disbursement  
- [ ] Track utilization of funds against approved milestones for 2nd, 3rd, and 4th instalments  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with all Applicable Laws and obtain requisite clearances for subcontractors/technology partners  
- [ ] Prepare Utilization Certificate and submit to MoC as per disbursement schedule  
- [ ] Monitor for any change in consortium/project structure – requires prior written approval of MoC  
- [ ] Plan for project commissioning and achievement of 50% peak annual production capacity for 4th instalment  
- [ ] Ensure Financial Incentive is not treated as equity contribution for debt financing  
- [ ] Verify that no prior expenditure (before Scheme approval) is claimed
