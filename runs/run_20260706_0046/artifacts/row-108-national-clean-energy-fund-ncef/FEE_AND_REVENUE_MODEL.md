## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, documentation preparation, and application submission support.  
2. Success fee is contingent on financial incentive approval and disbursement, aligning consultant incentives with client outcomes.  
3. Tiered pricing reflects client scale and scheme value, with Enterprise tier tailored for large PSUs and private entities undertaking high-capacity gasification projects.  
4. Scheme-specific note: This scheme involves high-value projects (typical incentive up to ₹5,000 crore per project) with a structured, milestone-based disbursement over 3–5 years. The risk profile is moderate due to the competitive bidding process and clear eligibility criteria, but revenue realization may lag due to lengthy approval and commissioning timelines.
