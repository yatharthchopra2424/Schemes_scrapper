# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping energy and industrial companies access government incentives for clean coal and lignite gasification projects. The National Clean Energy Fund (NCEF) supports projects that reduce import dependence on chemicals and fuels while utilizing domestic coal resources. I’d like to understand your current initiatives in this space to see if this scheme aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you planning to set up a new surface coal/lignite gasification project in India?
2. What is the proposed annual clean syngas capacity of your project (in MTPA)?
3. What downstream product(s) are you targeting (e.g., ammonia, methanol, DRI, SNG, urea)?
4. Is your entity a PSU, private company, joint venture, or consortium (max 3 companies)?
5. What is the net worth of your applicant entity compared to the total project cost?
6. Have you incurred any expenditure on plant and machinery prior to scheme approval?
7. Do you have a technology tie-up with a gasification provider, and is it indigenous or international?
8. What is your estimated project cost per million tonne of coal/lignite utilization?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is a PSU, private company, JV, or consortium (≤3 companies)
- [ ] Project is a new surface coal/lignite gasification project
- [ ] Minimum plant capacity ≥ 0.5 MTPA clean syngas (330 days availability, 1.1 kg/Nm³ density)
- [ ] Applicant net worth > 20% of total project cost
- [ ] No expenditure on plant and machinery prior to scheme approval
- [ ] Entity incorporated in India under Companies Act, 2013 or equivalent foreign law
- [ ] No insolvency proceedings admitted by NCLT or competent court
- [ ] Not blacklisted by Central/State Government
- [ ] Promoters/directors not convicted of coal block allocation offence or imprisoned >3 years
- [ ] Compliant with applicable laws
- [ ] If consortium: lead member holds ≥51% equity, Joint Application Agreement in place, no change in consortium during selection
- [ ] Each consortium member meets individual eligibility criteria

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, if you meet the criteria for a new gasification project with net worth exceeding 20% of project cost and targeting products like ammonia or methanol, the National Clean Energy Fund (NCEF) could provide up to 20% of your plant and machinery cost, capped at ₹5,000 crore per project. Let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
India imports 89% of crude oil, 50% of LNG, 25% of urea, and 35% of ammonium nitrate, creating a strategic vulnerability. Domestic coal and lignite reserves (~401 Bt coal, ~47 Bt lignite) remain underutilized for high-value chemical and fuel production, forcing industries to rely on expensive imports despite available feedstock.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Clean Energy Fund (NCEF), administered by the Ministry of Coal, offers a financial incentive of up to 20% of the cost of plant and machinery (P&M) for new surface coal/lignite gasification projects. The incentive is capped at ₹5,000 crore per project, ₹9,000 crore per product (except urea/SNG), and ₹12,000 crore per entity across all projects. Disbursed in four equal instalments tied to milestones: pre-implementation activities, loan disbursement and equity infusion, commissioning, and achieving 50% peak annual capacity.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are undertaking a **new** surface coal/lignite gasification project (expenditure pre-approval not counted)
- Your applicant entity’s **net worth exceeds 20%** of the total project cost
- Minimum plant capacity of **0.5 MTPA clean syngas** (based on 330 days availability and 1.1 kg/Nm³ density)
- Targeting eligible downstream products such as **ammonia, methanol, DRI, or SNG** (as per Annexure I)
