### National Clean Energy Fund (NCEF)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Coal
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://coal.nic.in/
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs); private sector companies; joint ventures (JVs); or consortiums (not exceeding 3 companies)
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Surface coal/lignite gasification projects
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: 
  - Applicant must undertake to set up new surface coal/lignite gasification projects
  - Expenditure made prior to Scheme approval not considered for incentive
  - Net worth of applicant must be more than 20% of total project cost
  - Minimum plant capacity: 0.5 MTPA clean syngas (based on 330 days annual availability at 1.1 kg/Nm³ density)
  - Applicant must be a company incorporated in India under Companies Act, 2013 or incorporated outside per jurisdiction laws
  - No insolvency proceedings admitted against applicant by NCLT or competent court
  - Applicant not blacklisted by Central or State Government
  - Promoters/directors not convicted of coal block allocation offence or sentenced to >3 years imprisonment
  - Applicant must comply with Applicable Laws
  - For consortium: max 3 companies; lead member must hold ≥51% equity; Joint Application Agreement required; no consortium changes during selection without MoC approval; each member must meet individual eligibility criteria

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Maximum 20% of Plant and Machinery (P&M) cost; single project capped at ₹5,000 crore; product-specific cap (except urea/SNG) at ₹9,000 crore; entity cap at ₹12,000 crore across all projects
- Disbursement mode: Four equal instalments (25% each) linked to milestones
- Duration of benefit: One-time (disbursed over project lifecycle)
