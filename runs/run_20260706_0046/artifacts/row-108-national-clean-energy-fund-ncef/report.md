# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **National Clean Energy Fund (NCEF)**, officially titled the **Scheme for Promotion of Surface Coal/Lignite Gasification Projects**, is a flagship initiative by the **Ministry of Coal**, Government of India, with a total financial outlay of **₹37,500 crore**. The scheme aims to accelerate the implementation of surface coal/lignite gasification projects through targeted financial incentives, promote **Atmanirbharta** (self-reliance) by reducing import dependence on chemicals and petrochemicals, enable value-added utilization of domestic coal resources, and contribute to achieving the national target of **100 Million Tonnes (MT)** of coal gasification by 2030.

### Objectives
- Accelerate implementation of surface coal/lignite gasification projects via financial incentives.
- Promote Atmanirbharta by reducing import dependence on:
  - Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG)
  - Urea
  - Ammonium Nitrate
  - Ammonia
  - Coking Coal (via Direct Reduced Iron)
  - Methanol
  - Di-methyl Ether (DME)
- Enable value-added utilization of domestic coal resources.
- Contribute to the national target of 100 MT coal gasification by 2030.

### Application Process
The selection process is conducted through transparent and competitive bidding via Request for Proposal (RFP) issued by the Ministry of Coal. Evaluation is based on a pre-defined scoring framework (total 100 marks), with selection subject to a minimum qualifying score of 40 and availability of funds.

#### Evaluation Framework

|----------|-----------|----------|--------|
| **Viability Amount Score** | 30 | Calculated as: (Lowest Financial Incentive as % of P&M amongst all bidders ÷ Applicant’s proposed Financial Incentive as % of P&M) × 30 | Key Facts, RFP Section 1.4.3(a), Appendix A, 25-06-2026a-wn.pdf, 29-05-2026a-wn.pdf |
| **Plant Capacity** | 20 | Based on syngas output (Raw + Clean):<br>- Clean Syngas: 2 pts per 40,000 NM3/hr increment >60,000 up to >400,000 NM3/hr (max 10)<br>- Raw Syngas: same scale (max 10) | Key Facts, RFP Section 1.4.3(b), Schedule II, 25-06-2026a-wn.pdf, 29-05-2026a-wn.pdf |

#### Selection Criteria
- Projects ranked in descending order of total score (out of 100).
- Subject to:
  - Minimum qualifying score of **40 marks**.
  - Availability of funds under the overall Scheme outlay of **₹37,500 crore**.
- Tie-breaking (if same score):
  1. Project with firm tie-up to indigenous technology or acquisition of international technology.
  2. Project having Urea or Synthetic Natural Gas (SNG) as product.
  3. Higher score under "Project Cost per MT of Coal".
  4. Draw of lots by MoC with prior notice.

#### Post-Selection Process
- **Letter of Award (LoA)**: Issued by MoC upon receipt of applicable clearances/approvals (if any). Selected Applicant must sign and return acknowledged copy within **7 working days**; failure may lead to disqualification and LOA cancellation.  
> Source: RFP Section 1.8.2-1.8.3, 25-06-2026a-wn.pdf.

- **Project Agreement**:  
  - Selected Applicant must incorporate a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) under Companies Act, 2013.  
  - SPV shareholding pattern must match Application submission (if JV/consortium).  
  - Prior to execution, SPV must furnish **Performance Security** of **3% of Cost of Plant and Machinery** (cash transfer via RTGS/NEFT or irrevocable/unconditional bank guarantee from Scheduled Commercial Bank).  
  - Upon execution, SPV referred to as "Project Developer".  
> Source: RFP Section 1.9.1-1.9.5, 25-06-2026a-wn.pdf.

- **Disbursement of Financial Incentive**:  
  - Four equal instalments (25% each) linked to milestones, verified by MoC.  
  - 1st instalment: Pre-implementation activities (land acquisition, EC, coal/lignite tie-up, tech licensor, EPC contractor, financial closure).  
    - Requires Insurance Surety Bond (25% of 1st instalment) or Bank Guarantee (initially 1 year, extendable), released upon 2nd instalment.  
  - 2nd instalment: After loan disbursal by Bank/FI, 30% equity infusion, and ≥50% actual expenditure of approved incentive on P&M (reimbursement).  
  - 3rd instalment: On project commissioning.  
  - 4th instalment: On achieving ≥50% of peak annual production capacity in previous 365 days.  
  - If commissioning >5 years post-Agreement (excluding Force Majeure):  
    - 50% if 5–6 years,  
    - 25% if 6–7 years,  
    - NIL if beyond 7 years.  
> Source: RFP Section 1.10.1-1.10.2, 25-06-2026a-wn.pdf.

#### Additional Provisions
- Under commercial coal block auction, coal utilisation for gasification is permitted; Scheme does not debar/restrict from availing other benefits under commercial mining regime.  
> Source: RFP Section 1.2.7, 1.2.8, 25-06-2026a-wn.pdf.

- No separate financial incentive of any nature considered in addition to Scheme incentive, except incentives for coal utilisation from commercial coal auctions.  
> Source: RFP Section 1.2.9, 25-06-2026a-wn.pdf.

- Applicants encouraged to set up projects at **pithead** (criteria same as Ministry of Power for coal-based thermal power plants).  
> Source: RFP Section 1.2.10, 25-06-2026a-wn.pdf.

- Project Developer may use own coal/lignite; MoC facilitates coal linkage via NRS linkage sub-sector under 'Production of Syn-Gas leading to coal gasification'.  
> Source: RFP Section 1.2.11, 25-06-2026a-wn.pdf.

- Responsibility for offtake rests entirely with Project Developer.  
> Source: RFP Section 1.2.12, 25-06-2026a-wn.pdf.

- Adoption of indigenous surface coal/lignite gasification technologies encouraged.  
> Source: RFP Section 1.2.13, 25-06-2026a-wn.pdf.

- Project implemented under Scheme cannot be transferred, sold, or disposed of without prior MoC approval during implementation period (Lock-in Period).  
  - During Lock-in: Project Developer must hold ≥51% subscribed/paid-up equity in SPV.  
  - Transfer/sale permitted only with prior MoC approval, unchanged Ultimate Beneficial Ownership, and management control with same Group Companies.  
  - Post Lock-in: Transfer/sale requires written intimation to MoC.  
> Source: RFP Section 3.3.2, 25-06-2026a-wn.pdf.

- Consequences of default (transfer/sale/disposal/cessation/change in Control in breach):  
  - Financial Incentive disbursed recoverable by MoC with interest at **twice the 3-year SBI MCLR** (compounded annually).  
  - Project Agreement terminated.  
  - Project Developer ineligible for future Financial Incentive under Scheme.  
> Source: RFP Section 3.3.3(a), 25-06-2026a-wn.pdf.

### Key Takeaways
> **Blockquote**:  
> The Scheme offers substantial financial support (up to 20% of P&M cost, capped at ₹5,000 crore per project) but imposes strict eligibility (net worth >20% of project cost, new projects only, 0.5 MTPA minimum capacity) and a rigorous competitive selection process based on a 100-point scoring framework. Disbursement is milestone-linked over four instalments, with significant compliance obligations including Application Security, Performance Security, Integrity Pact, and Lock-in Period restrictions. Success requires meticulous preparation, adherence to timelines, and ongoing compliance monitoring.
