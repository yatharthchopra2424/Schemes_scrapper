## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client under the MNRE Off-grid Solar Power Schemes.
2. Transparent fee structure with clear milestones tied to scheme approval and subsidy disbursement.
3. Risk-adjusted pricing reflecting the scheme’s moderate processing time (typically 3–6 months from application to subsidy disbursement) and dependency on authorized channel partners and inspection.
4. Scalable model accommodating diverse beneficiary categories (individuals, farmers, cooperatives, government institutions) and system types (solar home lights, street lights, pumps, mini-grids).

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description:** Retainer fee for consulting services related to the MNRE Off-grid Solar Power Schemes, including eligibility assessment, documentation support, and liaison with State Nodal Agencies or authorized channel partners.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description:** Success fee for securing subsidy approval under the MNRE Off-grid Solar Power Schemes, implemented by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) through State Nodal Agencies.
