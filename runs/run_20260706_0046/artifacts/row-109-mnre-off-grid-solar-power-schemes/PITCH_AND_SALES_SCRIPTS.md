# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because I specialize in helping rural households, farmers, and remote communities access government support for off-grid solar solutions—like solar home lights, street lights, pumps, and mini-grids—through the MNRE Off-grid Solar Power Schemes. Many of the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy runs. These schemes provide substantial subsidies to bring reliable, clean power to areas still dependent on diesel or without grid access. I’d like to understand your current energy setup and see if this could be a fit for your needs.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your current source of electricity for lighting, water pumping, or community facilities—grid, diesel generator, or none?
2. Are you located in an off-grid or poorly electrified area, such as a remote village, farm, or hamlet?
3. What type of solar system are you considering—solar home lights, street lights, solar pumps, or a mini-grid?
4. Do you have proof of identity (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN) and address for the primary beneficiary?
5. Do you have land ownership or consent documents for the proposed installation site?
6. Are you willing to work with an MNRE-authorized channel partner for installation and compliance with technical standards?
7. Have you previously received a subsidy for the same solar purpose under any other government scheme?
8. Are you or your community part of a priority group—such as SC/ST, women-led, or located in a border, NE, hilly, or island region?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Individual, farmer, cooperative, panchayat, or government institution
- [ ] Located in off-grid or poorly electrified area
- [ ] System type: solar home light, street light, pump, or mini-grid
- [ ] Proof of identity (Aadhaar/Voter ID/PAN) available
- [ ] Proof of address available
- [ ] Land ownership or consent document available
- [ ] Technical specification of proposed system ready
- [ ] Bank account details for subsidy transfer available
- [ ] Willing to use MNRE-authorized channel partner
- [ ] No prior subsidy for same purpose under another scheme
- [ ] Priority beneficiary (SC/ST/women/border/NE/hilly/island) — *preferred but not mandatory*

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it looks like you’re in an off-grid area with a need for reliable solar power—and you meet several key criteria for the MNRE Off-grid Solar Power Schemes. This scheme offers central financial assistance covering 30% to 90% of the benchmark cost, depending on your category and region. Would you like me to walk you through how it works and what support you could qualify for?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Rural households, farmers, and remote communities often face unreliable or no grid electricity, forcing dependence on expensive, polluting diesel generators or going without power for lighting, water pumping, or essential services. This limits productivity, increases costs, and harms the environment—especially in underserved regions.

**Scheme intro:**
The MNRE Off-grid Solar Power Schemes, administered by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), provide Central Financial Assistance (CFA) as a subsidy ranging from 30% to 90% of the benchmark cost for solar home lights, solar street lights, solar pumps, and mini-grids. The subsidy is higher for NE/hilly/island and SC/ST/women beneficiaries. Funds are disbursed through State Nodal Agencies after installation and inspection by authorized channel partners.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are located in an off-grid or poorly electrified area — a core eligibility requirement
- You plan to install an MNRE-approved system (home light, street light, pump, or mini-grid) through an authorized channel partner
- You have or can obtain the required documents: ID proof, address proof, land consent, technical specs, and bank details
- [If applicable] You belong to a priority group (SC/ST/women/border/NE/hilly/island), which may qualify you for a higher subsidy rate
