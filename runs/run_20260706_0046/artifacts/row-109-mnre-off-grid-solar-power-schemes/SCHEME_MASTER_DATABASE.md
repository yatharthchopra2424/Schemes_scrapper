#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Individuals, farmers, cooperatives, panchayats, government institutions
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Off-grid solar (solar home lights, solar street lights, solar pumps, mini-grids)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must comply with MNRE technical standards; installed by authorized channel partners; priority to SC/ST, women, border regions; beneficiary must not have availed subsidy for same purpose under any other government scheme

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Central Financial Assistance (CFA) as subsidy ranging from 30% to 90% of benchmark cost, depending on beneficiary category and region (higher for NE/hilly/island areas)
- Disbursement mode: Disbursed through State Nodal Agencies or implementing agencies after installation and inspection
- Duration of benefit: One-time (after installation and inspection)

#### Documents Required
1. Proof of identity (Aadhaar/Voter ID/PAN)
2. Proof of address
3. Land ownership or consent document (for community/installation site)
4. Technical specification of the proposed system
5. Bank account details for subsidy transfer
6. Photographs of installation site (before and after)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify eligible off-grid solar system (home light, street light, pump, etc.)
2. Contact State Nodal Agency or authorized channel partner
3. Submit application with required documents
4. System installed by authorized vendor
5. Inspection by concerned authority
6. Subsidy disbursed to beneficiary or vendor
