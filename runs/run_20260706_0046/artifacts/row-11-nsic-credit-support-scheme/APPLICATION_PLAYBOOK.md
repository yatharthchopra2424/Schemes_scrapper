# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] MSME unit with valid Udyam Registration  
- [ ] Unit engaged in manufacturing or services (traders not eligible)  
- [ ] Unit has commenced commercial production  
- [ ] Unit may not have completed one year of existence (eligible for provisional registration with monetary limit of Rs. 5.00 Lacs)  
- [ ] Unit not engaged in manufacture of medicine and drugs except Ayurvedic, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathic medicines  
- [ ] Unit not engaged in wholesale trading, retail trading, or commission agency  
- [ ] Unit not blacklisted  
- [ ] Proprietor/partner/director/Karta not convicted of any criminal offence  
- [ ] Unit possesses valid Bank Guarantee from an approved bank (mandatory for assistance)  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN card (Self-attested)  
- Udyam Registration (Self-attested)  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) for last 3 years  
- GST Registration Certificate  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Audited Financial Statements (Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss Account) for last 3 years  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Details of Plant & Machinery (Self-attested, Annexure B-1)  
- Copy of ownership document of premises or Lease/Rent Deed (Self-attested)  
- List of quality control equipment and testing facility available in factory (Self-attested)  
- Latest Electricity Bill Copy (Self-attested)  
- Face of Audited Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss A/cs, Schedule of Fixed Assets, Schedule of Revenue from Operations (last 3 years, duly signed by authorized person under seal, Self-attested)  
- Statement showing Results of Operation for last 3 years duly signed by Chartered Accountant with UDIN (Annexure C-1, Self-attested)  
- Bankers’ Report giving details of financial status as per Performa (Draft at Annexure E) (Self-attested, Annexure E-1)  
- Declaration signed by applicant MSE Unit accepting conditions of registration as per Annexure D (Self-attested)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be self-attested where required  
- File size should not exceed 5 MB per document  
- File names must match the document description (e.g., "PAN_Card_SelfAttested.pdf")  
- All declarations and reports must be signed and dated  
- UDIN must be present on CA-signed documents  
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in regional language, certified translation required  
- Bankers’ Report must be on bank’s letterhead

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for this scheme. The application is form-based with supporting documents as listed. However, verify with the nodal officer if any additional project details are needed for limit sanction.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the prescribed application form from the NSIC website: https://nsic.co.in/Schemes/RawMaterialAgainstBG  
2. Fill the form in block letters; avoid overwriting or correction fluid  
3. Ensure all fields are completed; write "NA" if not applicable  
4. Attach self-attested copies of all required documents as per checklist  
5. Sign the declaration (Annexure D) and affix seal/stamp if applicable  
6. Submit the form along with documents to the nearest NSIC Branch Office (not online)  
7. Obtain an acknowledgment receipt with date and inward number  
8. Retain a photocopy of the entire submission for your records  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Application form completely filled with no blank mandatory fields  
- [ ] All 10 scheme-specific documents attached and self-attested  
- [ ] Universal documents (PAN, Udyam, ITR, GST, bank statements) verified  
- [ ] Declarations signed and dated  
- [ ] UDIN present on CA-signed financial statements  
- [ ] Bankers’ Report on bank letterhead  
- [ ] Copy of submission retained for office records  
- [ ] Acknowledgment receipt obtained from NSIC Branch

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application package to the nearest NSIC Branch Office  
- Obtain a signed acknowledgment receipt with Inward Number and Date  
- Store the reference number (Inward No.) in the case file  
- Take a clear photograph of the submitted documents and acknowledgment  
- Set follow-up reminders for Day 25 and Day 35

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify approval letter specifying sanctioned limit and validity  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount and interest rate applicable based on SME rating  
- [ ] Sign the agreement between NSIC and the unit  
- [ ] Ensure Bank Guarantee is furnished from an approved bank as per sanction terms  
- [ ] Monitor disbursement of assistance to the unit’s account  
- [ ] Track utilization for raw material procurement within 180-day period  
- [ ] Ensure timely repayment to avail concessional interest rates; otherwise normal rates apply  
- [ ] Prepare Utilization Certificate after end of assistance period  
- [ ] Follow up for success fee invoice (if applicable as per engagement terms)  
- [ ] Set reminders for renewal processing (processing fee: 0.5% p.a. for Micro, 1.0% p.a. for Small & Medium on renewal)  
- [ ] Monitor for penal charges: 1.25% per quarter on delayed payment beyond 180 days over normal rate
