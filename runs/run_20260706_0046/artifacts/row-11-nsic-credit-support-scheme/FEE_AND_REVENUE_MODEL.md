## Pricing Principles

1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the financial benefit delivered—NSIC’s Raw Material Assistance Scheme provides credit support for raw material procurement up to 180 days, enabling MSMEs to avail bulk purchase discounts and cash benefits, which consultants help unlock.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile—processing involves bank guarantee requirements, NSIC inspection, and agreement signing, but approvals are routine for eligible MSMEs with Udyam registration and valid financials.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainer fees scale with client complexity; Starter tier suits micro units seeking initial limit sanction, while Growth/Scale tiers match MSMEs needing ongoing raw material financing support.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The scheme’s reliance on timely repayment to retain concessional interest rates (8.75–10.25% p.a.) introduces performance risk; success fees are structured to incentivize consultants to ensure clients meet repayment terms to avoid penal charges (1.25% per quarter over normal rate).
