## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping manufacturing and service-based MSMEs access government-backed credit support for raw material procurement. The NSIC Credit Support Scheme (Raw Material Assistance against Bank Guarantee) is designed specifically for units like yours that need working capital to buy raw materials without straining cash flow. I’d like to understand your current procurement challenges and see if this scheme aligns with your needs.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your Udyam Registration number, and when was your unit incorporated?  
2. Are you engaged in manufacturing or services? (Traders are not eligible.)  
3. Have you commenced commercial production, and how long have you been operational?  
4. What is your typical raw material procurement cycle, and do you face cash flow gaps when purchasing in bulk?  
5. Do you currently have a Bank Guarantee facility from an approved bank, or would you need assistance arranging one?  
6. What is your SME rating status (SME 1, SME 2, or unrated), as it affects interest rates under this scheme?  
7. Are you looking to finance raw material purchases for up to 180 days to avail bulk purchase discounts or cash benefits?  
8. Are you involved in any excluded activities such as medicine manufacturing (non-AYUSH), wholesale trading, retail trading, or commission agency work?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Unit has valid Udyam Registration  
- [ ] Engaged in manufacturing or services (not trading)  
- [ ] Has commenced commercial production  
- [ ] May not have completed one year of existence (eligible for provisional registration with limit up to Rs. 5.00 Lacs)  
- [ ] Not engaged in manufacture of medicine and drugs except Ayurvedic, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathic  
- [ ] Not a wholesale trader, retail trader, or commission agent  
- [ ] Not blacklisted; no proprietor/partner/director/Karta convicted of criminal offence  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you may qualify for the NSIC Credit Support Scheme, which provides credit support for raw material procurement against a Bank Guarantee, with interest rates as low as 8.75% p.a. for rated units and repayment terms up to 180 days. Let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
MSMEs often face working capital constraints when procuring raw materials, limiting their ability to benefit from bulk purchase discounts, cash payments, or timely production. This strains operations and affects competitiveness, especially when suppliers demand upfront payment or offer better terms for immediate settlement.

**Scheme intro:**
The NSIC Credit Support Scheme (Raw Material Assistance against Bank Guarantee), administered by the National Small Industries Corporation Ltd. (NSIC) under the Ministry of MSME, provides credit support for raw material procurement (both indigenous and imported) against the security of a Bank Guarantee from approved banks. The scheme offers financing for up to 180 days, with interest rates varying based on SME rating and unit type.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You have a valid Udyam Registration, which is mandatory for eligibility  
- You are engaged in manufacturing or services (traders are excluded)  
- You have commenced commercial production (eligible even if less than one year old, with provisional limits up to Rs. 5.00 Lacs)  
- You are not involved in excluded activities such as medicine manufacturing (non-AYUSH), wholesale, retail trading, or commission agency work
