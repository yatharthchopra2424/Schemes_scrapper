### NSIC Credit Support Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Small Industries Corporation Ltd. (NSIC)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nsic.co.in/Schemes/RawMaterialAgainstBG
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: MSME units with Udyam Registration engaged in manufacturing and services; traders not eligible
- Age of business: Units must have commenced commercial production but may not have completed one year of existence for provisional registration
- Sector: Manufacturing and services
- Revenue cap: Monetary limit of Rs. 5.00 Lacs for provisional registration
- Employee count: 
- Other: Units engaged in manufacture of medicine and drugs except Ayurvedic, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathic medicines are not eligible; wholesale trading, retail trading, or commission agents are not eligible; units blacklisted or with proprietor/partner/director/Karta convicted of criminal offence are not eligible

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Credit support for raw material procurement up to 180 days; interest rates vary based on SME rating and unit type: For units with valid SME 1 rating: 8.75% p.a. for Micro, 9.25% p.a. for Small & Medium (upto 180 days); For units with valid SME 2 rating: 9.25% p.a. for Micro, 9.75% p.a. for Small & Medium; For other units: 9.75% p.a. for Micro, 10.25% p.a. for Small & Medium; Processing fee is 1.0% p.a. on new sanctions for both Micro and Small & Medium, and 0.5% p.a. on renewal for Micro and 1.0% p.a. for Small & Medium; Penal charge on delayed payment beyond 180 days is 1.25% per quarter over and above the normal rate of interest
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (against Bank Guarantee security)
- Duration of benefit: Up to 180 days

#### Documents Required
1. PAN card (Self-attested)
2. Udyam Registration (Self-attested)
3. Details of Plant & Machinery (Self-attested, Annexure B-1)
4. Copy of ownership document of premises or Lease/Rent Deed (Self-attested)
5. List of quality control equipment and testing facility available in factory (Self-attested)
6. Latest Electricity Bill Copy (Self-attested)
7. Face of Audited Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss A/cs, Schedule of Fixed Assets, Schedule of Revenue from Operations (last 3 years, duly signed by authorized person under seal, Self-attested)
8. Statement showing Results of Operation for last 3 years duly signed by Chartered Accountant with UDIN (Annexure C-1, Self-attested)
9. Bankers’ Report giving details of financial status as per Performa (Draft at Annexure E) (Self-attested, Annexure E-1)
10. Declaration signed by applicant MSE Unit accepting conditions of registration as per Annexure D (Self-attested)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Apply for Raw Material Assistance only on the prescribed application forms.
2. Download the application forms from the link provided on the NSIC website.
3. Fill and submit the forms to the nearest NSIC Branch Office.
4. Submit the application form along with required documents.
5. Preliminary appraisal and unit inspection are carried out by NSIC.
6. Sanction of limit to the unit.
7. Signing of agreement between NSIC and the unit.
8. Disbursement of assistance to the unit.
