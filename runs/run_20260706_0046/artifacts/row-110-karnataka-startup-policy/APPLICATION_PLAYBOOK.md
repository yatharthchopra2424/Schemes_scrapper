## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not explicitly required as a formal DPR. However, a detailed **business plan and pitch deck** are mandatory and must cover:  
- Innovation and uniqueness of the product/service/process  
- Scalability and employment generation potential  
- Financial projections and use of funds  
- Current traction and customer validation  
- Team background and expertise  
- Karnataka operations and impact  

> **Note**: Verify with the nodal officer (Karnataka State Startup Committee) if a formal Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required for specific benefit categories (e.g., seed funding vs. marketing assistance).  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Register first** on the Karnataka Startup Cell portal (startup.karnataka.gov.in) before applying for any benefit.  
2. **Select the correct benefit type** (seed funding, marketing assistance, patent reimbursement, certification reimbursement, or monthly operational support) based on client need.  
3. **Ensure DPIIT/Karnataka State Startup Committee certification is uploaded and valid** — applications without this will be rejected.  
4. **Fill in the primary place of business address accurately** and match it with proof documents (utility bill/rental agreement).  
5. **Declare that the startup is not formed by splitting up or reconstruction of an existing business** — this is a mandatory declaration.  
6. **Provide realistic financial projections** — overoptimistic projections may trigger scrutiny during due diligence.  
7. **Specify the exact amount sought** under each benefit category (e.g., INR 50 lakhs for seed support, INR 2 lakhs for patent reimbursement).  
8. **Save draft frequently** — the portal may time out; do not rely on auto-save.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All form fields completed accurately and consistently  
- [ ] DPIIT or Karnataka State Startup Committee certificate uploaded and valid  
- [ ] Business plan and pitch deck uploaded and aligned with innovation and scalability claims

- [ ] Bank account details are correct and in the startup’s name

- [ ] Declarations (no splitting/reconstruction, Karnataka operations) are explicitly checked  
- [ ] No duplicate or conflicting information across documents  
- [ ] Application saved as draft and reviewed before final submission
