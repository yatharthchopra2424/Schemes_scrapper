## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the tangible financial benefit delivered (up to ₹50 lakhs seed support plus additional reimbursements and support), ensuring client success drives consultant compensation.
2. **Risk Mitigation**: Success fees are contingent on approved disbursement, reducing client risk; the scheme’s rolling application basis and clear milestones lower uncertainty compared to lump-sum grant programs.
3. **Market Competitiveness**: Pricing tiers align with standard consulting rates for startup advisory services in India, adjusted for the scheme’s non-dilutive funding nature and administrative complexity.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Karnataka Startup Policy has a moderate risk profile due to its rolling application basis and tranche-based disbursement tied to milestones. While fund availability is subject to annual budget (₹2000 Cr corpus), the clear eligibility criteria and documented approval process (via nodal agencies/incubators) support predictable outcomes. Processing typically involves multiple stages (portal registration, DPIIT recognition, benefit-specific application, review, agreement), which may extend timelines — success fees should account for this lag in revenue recognition.
