## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping Karnataka-based startups access government funding for innovation and growth. The Karnataka Startup Policy offers up to INR 50 lakhs in seed support plus additional benefits like patent reimbursement and marketing assistance. I’d like to understand your startup’s current stage and goals to see if this scheme aligns with your needs.

### Discovery Questions
- When was your startup incorporated, and what is your legal structure (Private Limited, LLP, or Partnership)?
- Where is your primary place of business located, and do you have proof such as a utility bill or rental agreement in Karnataka?
- Are you recognised by DPIIT or certified by the Karnataka State Startup Committee?
- What innovative product, service, or process are you developing, and how does it have potential for scalability and employment generation?
- Have you received any prior funding, and if so, what amount and from which sources?
- Are you planning to apply for seed funding, patent/certification reimbursement, marketing assistance, or operational support under this scheme?
- Do you have a business plan and pitch deck ready for submission?
- Are your promoters women, SC/ST, differently abled, rural, or social entrepreneurs? (This may enhance eligibility under priority categories.)

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Incorporated as Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm under Indian laws
- [ ] Primary place of business in Karnataka
- [ ] Recognised by DPIIT or certified by Karnataka State Startup Committee
- [ ] Working on innovative products, services, or processes with scalability and employment generation potential
- [ ] Not formed by splitting up or reconstruction of an existing business
- [ ] Able to provide Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, DPIIT/Karnataka certification, audited financials (if applicable), business plan, pitch deck, funding details, bank account details, authorization letter, and proof of Karnataka location

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your startup may meet the core eligibility criteria for the Karnataka Startup Policy. This scheme provides up to INR 50 lakhs in seed support along with additional grants for patent filing, certification, and marketing. Let me outline how it works and how we can assist.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many early-stage startups in Karnataka struggle to secure seed funding for product development, face high costs for patent and certification filing, and lack access to markets and mentorship needed to scale. The Karnataka Startup Policy directly addresses these gaps by offering structured financial and non-financial support.

**Scheme intro:**
The Karnataka Startup Policy, administered by the Department of IT, BT and S&T, Government of Karnataka, provides seed support of up to INR 50 lakhs per startup as grant or convertible debt, reimbursement of up to INR 2 lakhs for patent filing and INR 1 lakh for certification costs, marketing assistance of up to INR 5 lakhs for approved events, and monthly operational support of INR 1 lakh for up to 12 months for startups in recognized incubators. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis via the portal: https://startup.karnataka.gov.in.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm under Indian laws
- Primary place of business located in Karnataka with verifiable proof
- Recognised by DPIIT or certified by the Karnataka State Startup Committee
- Developing an innovative product, service, or process with scalability and employment generation potential
