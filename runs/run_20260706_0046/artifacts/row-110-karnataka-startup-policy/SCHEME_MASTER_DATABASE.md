### Karnataka Startup Policy
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Karnataka
- **Administering Body:** Department of IT, BT and S&T, Government of Karnataka
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://startup.karnataka.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Innovative products, services, or processes with potential for scalability and employment generation
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: 
  - Primary place of business in Karnataka
  - Recognized by DPIIT or certified by the Karnataka State Startup Committee
  - Not formed by splitting up or reconstruction of an existing business
  - Target beneficiaries include startups, women entrepreneurs, SC/ST entrepreneurs, differently abled entrepreneurs, rural startups, and social enterprises

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - Up to INR 50 lakhs as seed support per startup (grant or convertible debt)
  - Reimbursement of up to INR 2 lakhs for patent filing
  - Reimbursement of up to INR 1 lakh for certification costs
  - Marketing assistance of up to INR 5 lakhs for participation in approved events
  - Monthly operational support of INR 1 lakh per month for up to 12 months for startups in recognized incubators
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement (for patent, certification, marketing) and Direct credit (seed funding, operational support) through nodal agencies and incubators
- Duration of benefit: 
  - Seed support: One-time (up to INR 50 lakhs)
  - Operational support: Annual (up to 12 months)
  - Patent/certification/marketing: One-time reimbursement

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. DPIIT recognition certificate or Karnataka State Startup Committee certification
4. Audited financial statements (if applicable)
5. Business plan and pitch deck
6. Details of funding received (if any)
7. Bank account details of the startup
8. Authorization letter from authorized signatory
9. Proof of primary place of business in Karnataka (e.g., utility bill, rental agreement)
10. Patent or certification invoices (for reimbursement claims)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register the startup on the Karnataka Startup Cell portal (startup.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Obtain DPIIT recognition or certification from the Karnataka State Startup Committee
3. Apply for specific benefits (seed funding, marketing, patent reimbursement, etc.) through the online portal
4. Submit required documents including incorporation certificate, DPIIT recognition, business plan, and financial projections
5. Application is reviewed by the Karnataka State Startup Committee or designated nodal agency
6. Upon approval, enter into an agreement and submit bank details for disbursement
7. Funds are released in tranches based on milestones or as per the scheme guidelines
