## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Karnataka Startup Policy** is a state-level initiative launched by the Government of Karnataka to foster innovation and entrepreneurship within the state. Administered by the **Department of IT, BT and S&T**, the policy operates through the **Karnataka Startup Cell** portal and provides comprehensive support to eligible startups across multiple dimensions including financial assistance, infrastructure access, mentorship, and regulatory facilitation.

### Objectives
The policy aims to achieve the following strategic goals by 2025:
- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship across Karnataka  
- Create **20,000 startups**  
- Generate **6 lakh (600,000) direct and indirect employment opportunities**  
- Achieve **INR 50,000 Crore** in economic output from startups  
- Establish at least **one startup incubator in each district** of Karnataka  
- Promote entrepreneurship among **women, SC/ST, differently abled, and rural** communities  
- Develop **sector-specific startup parks and innovation zones**  
- Simplify regulatory compliance via **single-window clearance**  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Criterion | Requirement | Details |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Legal Structure** | Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm | Must be incorporated under Indian laws (Companies Act, 2013; LLP Act, 2008; Partnership Act, 1932) |
| **Primary Place of Business** | Karnataka | Must be located and operating primarily in Karnataka; proof required (utility bill, rental agreement, etc.) |
| **Recognition Status** | DPIIT recognized **OR** certified by Karnataka State Startup Committee | Mandatory for application; certificate must be valid at time of submission |
| **Innovation & Scalability** | Working on innovative products, services, or processes | Must demonstrate scalability and employment generation |
| **Exclusion | Not formed by splitting up or reconstruction of an existing business |
| **Target Beneficiaries (Priority)** | Startups led by: Women, SC/ST, Differently Abled, Rural Entrepreneurs, Social Enterprises | Eligible for focused outreach and potential preference in evaluation |

> **Key Caveat**: Startups must maintain primary operations in Karnataka for a **minimum of 3 years** after receiving financial support under the policy.

### Benefits & Financial Support
The policy offers a mix of financial and non-financial benefits. Financial support is disbursed through **nodal agencies and recognized incubators** after due diligence.

#### Financial Support Breakdown
| Benefit Type | Maximum Amount | Form / Conditions | Notes |
|--------------|----------------|-------------------|-------|
| **Seed Support** | Up to **INR 50 lakhs** | Grant or convertible debt | Per startup; disbursed in tranches based on milestones |
| **Patent Filing Reimbursement** | Up to **INR 2 lakhs** | Reimbursement | Against valid invoices; requires submission of patent/application details |
| **Certification Cost Reimbursement** | Up to **INR 1 lakh** | Reimbursement | For ISO, CE, BIS, or other approved certifications; invoice proof required |
| **Marketing Assistance** | Up to **INR 5 lakhs** | Reimbursement / Support | For participation in **approved national/international events** (e.g., Slush, Web Summit, CES) |
| **Monthly Operational Support** | INR 1 lakh/month | For up to **12 months** | Only for startups in **recognized incubators**; subject to incubator recommendation and compliance |

#### Non-Financial Benefits
- Access to **incubation centers** and shared infrastructure/labs  
- **Mentorship** from industry experts and domain specialists  
- Facilitation for participation in **national and international startup events**  
- **Priority in government procurement** tenders and contracts  
- **Regulatory facilitation** via single-window clearance for approvals, licenses, and clearances  
- Assistance in **fundraising and investor connect** through demo days, pitch events, and VC introductions  
- Access to **sector-specific startup parks and innovation zones** (as developed under the policy)  

> **Important**: Financial support is **subject to availability of funds** under the annual budget allocation. The total fund size is **INR 2,000 Crore**. Benefits **cannot be combined** with similar state-level incentives for the same expenditure (anti-duplication clause).

### Application Process
The application process is conducted entirely online via the Karnataka Startup Cell portal. It is a **rolling basis** system with no fixed deadlines, subject to fund availability.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Startup Registers on Portal<br/>https://startup.karnataka.gov.in] --> B[Obtain DPIIT Recognition<br/>or Karnataka State Startup Committee Certification]
    B --> C[Apply for Specific Benefits<br/>(Seed Funding, Marketing, Patent Reimbursement, etc.)]
    C --> D[Submit Required Documents<br/>(See Checklist Below)]
    D --> E[Application Reviewed by<br/>Karnataka State Startup Committee<br/>or Designated Nodal Agency]
    E --> F{Approval?}
    F -->|Yes| G[Enter into Agreement<br/>Submit Bank Details]
    G --> H[Funds Released in Tranches<br/>Based on Milestones<br/>or Audit Compliance]
    F -->|No| I[Application Rejected<br/>Feedback Provided<br/>Re-application Possible<br/>After Addressing Gaps]
    style I fill:#f9f,stroke:#333
    style H fill:#9f9,stroke:#333
```

#### Required Documents Checklist
| S.No | Document | Purpose / Notes |
|------|---------|----------------|
| 1 | Certificate of Incorporation / Registration | Proof of legal entity status |
| 2 | PAN of the Entity | For tax and financial tracking |
| 3 | DPIIT Recognition Certificate **OR** Karnataka State Startup Committee Certification | Mandatory eligibility proof |
| 4 | Audited Financial Statements | If applicable (usually for startups >1 year old) |
| 5 | Business Plan and Pitch Deck | To assess innovation, scalability, employment potential |
| 6 | Details of Funding Received (if any) | Equity, debt, grants; to avoid duplication |
| 7 | Bank Account Details of the Startup | For disbursement of funds |
| 8 | Authorization Letter from Authorized Signatory | For application submission and agreements |
| 9 | Proof of Primary Place of Business in Karnataka | Utility bill, rental agreement, property tax receipt, etc. |
| 10 | Patent or Certification Invoices | **Only** for reimbursement claims under those specific heads |

#### Key Process Notes
- Applications are accepted **throughout the year** on a rolling basis  
- Review and approval timeline varies based on completeness and nodal agency workload  
- Disbursement occurs in **tranches** linked to milestone achievement or audit compliance  
- Startups must enter into a formal **agreement** with the implementing agency/nodal agency before fund release  
- Contact for queries: **Email: startupcell@karnataka.gov.in** \| **Phone: 080-2235 3575**  
- Policy valid until **2025** unless extended by the Government of Karnataka
