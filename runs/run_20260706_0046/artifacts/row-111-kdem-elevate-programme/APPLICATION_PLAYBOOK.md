## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Startup is registered in Karnataka (Certificate of Incorporation/Registration shows Karnataka address)
- [ ] Startup has a minimum viable product (MVP) or prototype (Product description or prototype details submitted)
- [ ] Startup is incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm (Certificate of Incorporation/Registration confirms entity type)
- [ ] Startup has not received more than INR 50 lakh in funding prior to application (Declaration of compliance and funding history to be verified)
- [ ] Startup is working on an innovative product, service, or process with scalability potential (Product description and team profiles to demonstrate innovation and scalability)
- [ ] Startup is not a sole proprietorship or unregistered entity (Only Private Limited, LLP, or Partnership Firm eligible)
- [ ] Startup has not previously received a grant under KDEM Elevate Programme (Declaration of compliance to confirm no prior benefit)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required. The KDEM Elevate Programme application is based on an online form and document upload; no detailed project report (DPR) is mandated in the evidence. However, applicants should be prepared to provide a clear product description, prototype details, and team background as part of the form. **Verify with nodal officer if a detailed project report is requested during screening.**

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the portal at **https://elevate.karnataka.gov.in** and register using a valid email and mobile number.  
2. Use the startup’s official name exactly as per the Certificate of Incorporation/Registration.  
3. Fill in founder/director details (name, Aadhaar, PAN, contact) matching uploaded documents.  
4. Describe the product/service clearly, emphasizing innovation, MVP/prototype status, and scalability potential.  
5. Specify the exact amount of grant sought (up to INR 50 lakh) and break down intended use (product development, market validation, team building, operational expenses).  
6. Disclose total funding received prior to application; ensure it does not exceed INR 50 lakh.  
7. Upload all documents in the specified format (PDF/JPEG, under 5 MB each) with correct file names.  
8. Review the declaration of compliance carefully before signing and uploading; ensure it is dated and signed by an authorized signatory.  
9. Double-check all entries for accuracy before final submission — edits may not be allowed after submission.  
10. Take a screenshot of the confirmation page after submission and save the application reference number.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All mandatory fields in the online form are completed  
- [ ] Startup registration details match the Certificate of Incorporation/Registration
