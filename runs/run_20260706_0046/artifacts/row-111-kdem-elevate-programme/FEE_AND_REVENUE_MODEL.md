## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the ₹50 lakh grant value, ensuring consultant compensation scales with client benefit.
2. **Risk Mitigation**: Success fees are contingent on grant approval, reducing upfront client risk; processing time of 3–6 months (based on periodic application cycles and evaluation stages) justifies staggered fee triggers.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainers are scaled to client stage — early startups (Growth tier) pay lower fixed costs to access support, aligning with their limited pre-revenue capacity.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The KDEM Elevate Programme has a moderate risk profile due to competitive selection and milestone-based tranche disbursement; success fees are structured at 6–8% (Growth tier) to reflect effort required for application strength, milestone planning, and compliance tracking, supported by evidence of multi-stage evaluation (pitch/interview) and tranche release conditions.
