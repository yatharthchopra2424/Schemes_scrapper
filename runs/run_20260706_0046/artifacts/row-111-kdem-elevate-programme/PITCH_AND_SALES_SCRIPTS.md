## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I'm calling from [Your Firm Name]. We specialise in helping early-stage technology startups in Karnataka access non-dilutive government funding to accelerate product development and market validation. The KDEM Elevate Programme is designed specifically for startups like yours that have built an MVP and are ready to scale — offering up to INR 50 lakh in grant funding with no equity taken. I’d like to understand where you are in your journey to see if this could be a fit.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated, and what is your current legal structure (Private Limited, LLP, or Partnership)?
2. Are you currently registered and operating primarily in Karnataka?
3. Do you have a minimum viable product (MVP) or working prototype that you’ve tested with users?
4. What stage of funding have you reached so far — have you raised more than INR 50 lakh in prior investments?
5. Is your product, service, or process based on an innovative idea with clear scalability potential in Karnataka or beyond?
6. Are you planning to use funds for product development, market validation, team building, or initial operational expenses?
7. Do you have your incorporation certificate, PAN, founder Aadhaar/PAN, and product details ready for documentation?
8. Are you open to participating in mentorship, investor meets, and demo days as part of the programme?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup is registered in Karnataka  
- [ ] Incorporated as Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm  
- [ ] Has a minimum viable product (MVP) or prototype  
- [ ] Has not received more than INR 50 lakh in prior funding  
- [ ] Working on an innovative, scalable product/service/process  
- [ ] Founders have valid Aadhaar and PAN  
- [ ] Can provide bank account details of the startup  
- [ ] Willing to comply with milestone-based disbursement and usage terms  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you meet several key criteria for the KDEM Elevate Programme — particularly being a Karnataka-registered startup with an MVP and under the INR 50 lakh prior funding threshold. This programme offers exactly what you’re looking for: up to INR 50 lakh in grant funding to scale your innovation, without giving up equity. Let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many early-stage startups in Karnataka struggle to bridge the gap between prototype and market-ready product due to limited access to early-stage capital. Traditional funding often comes with equity dilution or debt pressure, forcing founders to compromise on vision or timing. The KDEM Elevate Programme addresses this critical gap by providing non-dilutive grant funding to validate and scale innovative ideas.

**Scheme intro:**
The KDEM Elevate Programme, administered by the Karnataka Digital Economy Mission (KDEM), is a state-level grant initiative offering a one-time grant of up to INR 50 lakh per selected startup. The funds are disbursed in tranches based on milestone achievement and are intended for product development, market validation, team building, and initial operational expenses. There is no equity dilution or repayment obligation.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your startup is registered and operating in Karnataka  
- You have incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm  
- You possess a minimum viable product (MVP) or prototype  
- You have not exceeded INR 50 lakh in prior funding
