### KDEM Elevate Programme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Karnataka
- **Administering Body:** Karnataka Digital Economy Mission (KDEM)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://elevate.karnataka.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Applications are accepted in periodic cycles; specific dates are announced on the portal

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Innovative product, service, or process with scalability potential
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be registered in Karnataka, have a minimum viable product (MVP) or prototype, and not have received more than INR 50 lakh in funding prior to application

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to INR 50 lakh
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit in tranches based on milestone achievement
- Duration of benefit: One-time grant disbursed in tranches

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN card of the startup
3. Aadhaar and PAN of founders/directors
4. Product description or prototype details
5. Team profiles with LinkedIn or professional backgrounds
6. Bank account details of the startup
7. Declaration of compliance with eligibility criteria

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the official Elevate Karnataka portal at https://elevate.karnataka.gov.in
2. Register on the portal by creating a login using email and mobile number
3. Fill in the online application form with details about the startup, founders, product, and traction
4. Upload required documents including incorporation certificate, product details, and team profiles
5. Submit the application and await screening by the evaluation committee
6. Shortlisted startups will be invited for a pitch presentation or interview
7. Final selection is based on innovation, scalability, team strength, and market potential
8. Selected startups will receive a grant disbursement letter and further instructions for fund utilization
