## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) or business plan is required for all MSInS schemes as per the evidence.  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
1. Executive Summary  
2. Problem Statement and Innovation Aspect  
3. Solution Description (technology, product, or service)  
4. Market Analysis (target market, size, competition)  
5. Business Model and Revenue Strategy  
6. Implementation Plan (timeline, milestones, resources)  
7. Financial Projections (5-year, including funding requirement and utilization)  
8. Team Background and Expertise  
9. Risk Assessment and Mitigation Strategy  
10. Annexures: Prototypes, test results, IP details (if any), partnership letters  

> **Note**: For proof-of-concept or prototype-specific schemes, emphasize technical feasibility and development stages. Verify exact DPR requirements with the nodal officer for the selected program, as some innovation challenges may have tailored formats.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Access the portal only via the official website**: https://maha-innovation.in (verify SSL certificate and domain)  
2. **Select the correct scheme**: Navigate to 'Schemes' or 'Apply' section and choose the specific program matching your innovation domain and eligibility  
3. **Read guidelines thoroughly**: Download and review the detailed scheme guidelines before starting the form  
4. **Fill in all mandatory fields**: Do not leave any asterisk-marked (*) fields blank; use "NA" only if explicitly allowed  
5. **Match document details exactly**: Ensure name, PAN, Aadhaar, bank account number, and IFSC match across form and uploaded documents  
6. **Describe innovation clearly**: In the innovation section, specify what is novel, how it solves the problem, and its stage (idea, PoC, prototype, market-ready)  
7. **Declare originality**: Explicitly confirm in the declaration field that the work is original and does not infringe third-party IP  
8. **Review before submission**: Preview the entire form, check for spelling/errors, and confirm all documents are uploaded in correct format (PDF/JPG as specified)  
9. **Note application ID**: Immediately save and record the application ID generated upon submission for tracking  
10. **Portal-specific tip**: The portal may time out after inactivity; draft responses offline and copy-paste to avoid data loss  

> **Sources**: [Maharashtra State Innovation Society Key Facts](https://maha-innovation.in), Application Process section (steps 4–8)
