## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit received by the client, ensuring our compensation scales with the value delivered.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Retainers**: Retainers reflect the effort required to navigate scheme-specific requirements, including document preparation and portal navigation.
3. **Transparency**: All fees are disclosed upfront; clients receive detailed invoices with scheme-specific references and implementing agency details.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Given the rolling application basis and competitive evaluation process for Maharashtra State Innovation Society schemes, success carries moderate risk. While funding up to INR 50 lakhs is available per the evidence, approval is not guaranteed and depends on fund availability and evaluation outcomes. This warrants a success fee in the mid-range (6–8%) for eligible startups and MSMEs, reflecting both the potential reward and the uncertainty in selection.
