## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/We work with startups and innovators ecosystem, and MSInS)
Good morning/afternoon, I’m [Your Name] from [Your Firm]. We specialise in helping Maharashtra-based startups, MSMEs, and innovators access government innovation support — particularly through the Maharashtra State Innovation Society (MSInS). I’ve helped several early-stage ventures in Pune, Nagpur, and Mumbai secure seed funding and incubation support. Before we explore whether MSInS could be a fit for you, may I ask a few quick questions to understand your current stage and innovation goals?

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your entity incorporated or registered, and is it based in Maharashtra?  
2. Are you currently working on an innovative product, process, or technology solution — ideally at proof-of-concept or prototype stage?  
3. What is your primary innovation domain (e.g., agritech, healthtech, clean energy, IoT, AI/ML)?  
4. Are you seeking financial support for early-stage development, such as seed funding or grants for prototyping?  
5. Do you have a detailed project report or business plan outlining your innovation, milestones, and budget?  
6. Are you looking for additional support beyond funding — such as mentorship, IPR assistance, or market linkages?  
7. Have you previously applied for any state or central government innovation schemes?  
8. Are you open to participating in investor meets, demo days, or corporate partnership facilitation programs?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity is registered in Maharashtra (startup, MSME, individual innovator, academic institution, or industry partner)  
- [ ] Engaged in innovative activities as defined by MSInS scheme guidelines  
- [ ] Has or is developing a proof of concept, prototype, or innovative project  
- [ ] Possesses Certificate of Incorporation/Registration, PAN, and Aadhaar of authorized signatory  
- [ ] Can provide detailed project report/business plan and bank account details  
- [ ] Willing to submit declaration of originality and innovation and authorization letter  
- [ ] Not currently restricted by sector-specific caps (if applicable to chosen sub-some schemes have domain limits)  
- [ ] Seeking support aligned with MSInS objectives: funding, mentorship, infrastructure, market linkage, or IPR assistance  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like you’re working on an innovative solution at an early stage and are based in Maharashtra — which aligns well with the Maharashtra State Innovation Society’s support for startups and innovators. MSInS offers financial support up to INR 50 lakhs, along with incubation, mentorship, and market linkage — all of which could help accelerate your progress. Would you like me to walk you through how it works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many early-stage innovators in Maharashtra struggle to access seed funding and technical support to move from idea to prototype. They often lack access to mentorship, IPR guidance, or investor networks — critical resources that are either too expensive or difficult to navigate without structured support.

**Scheme intro:**
The Maharashtra State Innovation Society (MSInS), implemented by the Directorate of Industries, Government of Maharashtra, offers financial support through grants and seed funding of up to INR 50 lakhs for early-stage startups and innovators. Beyond funding, it provides access to incubation infrastructure, mentorship from industry experts, assistance with IPR filing, market linkage, and facilitation of participation in investor meets and demo days. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis via the portal: https://maha-innovation.in.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your entity is registered in Maharashtra — a core eligibility requirement  
- You are engaged in innovative activities with a proof of concept or prototype in development  
- You have or can prepare a detailed project report and necessary documents (PAN, Aadhaar, bank details)  
- You are seeking early-stage financial and non-financial support — exactly what MSInS designs its programs to deliver
