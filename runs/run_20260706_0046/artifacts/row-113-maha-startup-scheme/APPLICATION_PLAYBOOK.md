## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Startup is registered in Maharashtra as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm under the Companies Act, 2013 or LLP Act, 2008  
- [ ] Startup is incorporated not more than 5 years ago  
- [ ] Annual turnover does not exceed INR 25 Crore in any preceding financial year  
- [ ] Startup is working on innovative products, services, or processes with potential for commercialization  
- [ ] At least one founder is a resident of Maharashtra  
- [ ] Preference given to startups led by women, SC/ST, or differently abled individuals (if applicable)  
- [ ] Startup has not received similar grants from other central or state government schemes (verify)  
- [ ] Startup operates within Maharashtra (geographic scope)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not explicitly required as a formal DPR. However, a **pitch deck or business plan (max 10 slides)** is mandatory and must cover innovation, business model, financials, and utilization plan.  
→ **Verify with nodal officer** if a detailed project report is needed alongside the pitch deck for high-value asks.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Register on the portal https://startup.maharashtra.gov.in as a new user using startup and founder details  
2. Log in and complete the online application form in one session to avoid timeouts  
3. Fill all fields accurately: innovation description, business model, financials, founder details, and fund utilization plan  
4. Upload documents in the specified format (PDF/JPEG, <5 MB each)  
5. Ensure pitch deck does not exceed 10 slides and is clear, concise, and innovation-focused  
6. Tick all declarations and confirm compliance with scheme guidelines  
7. Double-check turnover, incorporation date, and Maharashtra residency of at least one founder  
8. Preview the entire form before final submission — no edits allowed after submit  

> **Sources**: Application Process from Maha Startup Scheme Key Facts; Portal: https://startup.maharashtra.gov.in

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and sanction letter from Directorate of Industries, Government of Maharashtra  
- [ ] Verify sanctioned amount (up to INR 20 Lakhs) and disbursement tranche schedule  
- [ ] Sign grant agreement and submit utilization plan as per milestone requirements  
- [ ] Utilize funds strictly for approved purpose (prototype development, validation, etc.) within 12 months of sanction  
- [ ] Submit utilization certificates and progress reports for each tranche before next disbursement  
- [ ] Maintain records of all expenditures, invoices, and bills for audit  
- [ ] Coordinate with state-approved incubation centers, knowledge partners, and IPR support services as offered  
- [ ] Prepare for participation in national/international startup events if facilitated under the scheme  
- [ ] Track job creation and other outcomes for final reporting  
- [ ] Submit final utilization certificate and project completion report to enable closure  
- [ ] Follow up on success fee invoice as per consultancy agreement
