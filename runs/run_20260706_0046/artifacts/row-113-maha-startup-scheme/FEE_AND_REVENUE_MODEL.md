## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual grant amount (₹20 Lakhs maximum per entity) under the Maha Startup Scheme, ensuring clients retain the majority of the financial benefit.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s rolling basis and milestone-based disbursement (with processing time typically taking 6–9 months from application to full grant release), success fees reflect the implementation risk and effort required for utilization reporting and compliance.
3. **Accessibility for Early-Stage Firms**: The retainer structure is designed to be accessible to startups with turnover under ₹25 Crore, aligning with the scheme’s eligibility criteria and avoiding prohibitive upfront costs.
4. **Transparency and Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; success fees are contingent only on successful sanction and disbursement of the grant, with no hidden charges.
