## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

I’d like to understand your current stage and goals to see if this could be a fit.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm in Maharashtra?
2. What is your annual turnover in the last financial year, and has it exceeded INR 25 Crore at any point?
3. Are you currently working on an innovative product, service, or process with clear potential for commercialization?
4. Is at least one founder a resident of Maharashtra?
5. Have you received any prior grants or funding from central or state government schemes for similar purposes (e.g., prototype development, innovation)?
6. Do you have a pitch deck or business plan (max 10 slides) ready that outlines your innovation and market validation plans?
7. Are you looking for non-dilutive funding to support early-stage validation, prototyping, or market readiness without giving up equity?
8. Would access to state-approved incubation centers, shared labs, mentorship, and IPR filing support be valuable for your current goals?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Early-stage startups in Maharashtra often struggle to secure non-dilutive funding for prototype development, market validation, and early product iteration. Traditional financing options like bank loans require collateral or revenue history, while equity funding dilutes founder ownership too early. This gap leaves many innovative ideas stuck at the idea or MVP stage without the resources to test, refine, or scale.

**Scheme intro:**
The Maha Startup Scheme, administered by the Directorate of Industries, Government of Maharashtra, is a state-level grant program offering a one-time grant of up to INR 20 Lakhs per eligible startup. The grant supports early-stage validation, prototype development, and market readiness, disbursed in tranches based on milestone achievement after technical and financial appraisal. Funds are released directly to the startup’s bank account upon approval of utilization reports, with no equity taken by the government. More details are available at the official portal: https://startup.maharashtra.gov.in

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Incorporated in Maharashtra as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm within the last 5 years  
- Annual turnover does not exceed INR 25 Crore in any preceding financial year  
- Working on an innovative product, service, or process with potential for commercialization  
- At least one founder is a resident of Maharashtra
