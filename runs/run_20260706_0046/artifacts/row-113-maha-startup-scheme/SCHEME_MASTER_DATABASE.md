### Maha Startup Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Maharashtra
- **Administering Body:** Directorate of Industries, Government of Maharashtra
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://startup.maharashtra.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Always Open (Rolling basis)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm
- Age of business: Incorporated not more than 5 years ago
- Sector: Innovative products, services, or processes with potential for commercialization (technology, manufacturing, and service sectors)
- Revenue cap: Annual turnover should not exceed INR 25 Crore in any preceding financial year
- Employee count: 
- Other: 
  - Must be registered in Maharashtra
  - At least one founder must be a resident of Maharashtra
  - Working on innovative products, services, or processes with potential for commercialization
  - Preference given to startups led by women, SC/ST, or differently abled individuals

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: One-time grant of up to INR 20 Lakhs per eligible startup
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (released directly to the startup’s bank account after due diligence and approval of utilization reports)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (disbursed in tranches based on milestone achievement)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN Card of the startup
3. Aadhaar and PAN of all founders
4. Pitch deck or business plan (max 10 slides)
5. Bank account details of the startup
6. Declaration of innovation and compliance with scheme guidelines
7. Proof of registration in Maharashtra (address proof of registered office)
8. Details of any prior funding or grants received

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the official Maha Startup Scheme portal at https://startup.maharashtra.gov.in
2. Register as a new user by providing basic details of the startup and founders
3. Log in and fill the online application form with information on innovation, business model, and financials
4. Upload required documents including Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, pitch deck, and founder details
5. Submit the application for preliminary screening
6. Shortlisted applicants will be invited for a pitch presentation before the evaluation committee
7. Based on the evaluation, approved startups will receive a sanction letter
8. Grant disbursement will commence after submission of utilization plan and execution of agreement
