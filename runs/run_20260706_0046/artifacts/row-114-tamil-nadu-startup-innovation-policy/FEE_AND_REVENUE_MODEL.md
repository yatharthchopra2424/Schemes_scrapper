## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-based alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the tangible benefit delivered to the client, with success fees tied to actual fund disbursement or benefit realization.
2. **Risk sharing**: Success fees ensure consultant compensation is partially contingent on successful outcomes, aligning incentives with client success.
3. **Transparency**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover initial assessment and application preparation, success fees only payable upon approval.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: Given the scheme is currently undergoing website maintenance and key details (financial support, eligibility, processing time) are unavailable, the risk profile is elevated. Consultants should advise clients of potential delays and uncertainty in benefit realization until the portal is restored and official guidelines are republished.
