## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping Tamil Nadu-based startups access government innovation support. The Tamil Nadu Startup & Innovation Policy is designed to foster entrepreneurship and provide growth resources for early-stage ventures in the state. I’d like to understand your startup’s current stage and goals to see if this policy aligns with your needs.

### Discovery Questions
- When was your startup incorporated, and is it registered in Tamil Nadu?
- What stage is your startup at (idea, prototype, early revenue, scaling)?
- Are you seeking support for product development, market access, or funding readiness?
- Have you previously applied for any state or central government startup schemes?
- What are your primary challenges in accessing mentorship, infrastructure, or funding?
- Are you looking to scale operations within Tamil Nadu or expand nationally/internationally?
- Does your startup focus on innovation in sectors like manufacturing, IT, biotech, or social impact?
- Are you interested in policy-driven support such as incubation, seed funding, or tax incentives?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup is incorporated and operating in Tamil Nadu
- [ ] Startup is in early growth stage (typically <10 years old, though policy does not specify exact age limit)
- [ ] Focus on innovation and scalable business model
- [ ] Founders are residents of Tamil Nadu or startup is registered in the state
- [ ] Seeking support aligned with policy objectives: funding, mentorship, infrastructure, or market access
- [ ] Not currently under legal dispute or insolvency proceedings
- [ ] Willing to comply with reporting and utilization requirements if selected
- [ ] Open to engaging with Tamil Nadu’s startup ecosystem (incubators, mentors, investors)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, your startup appears to be in a position where the Tamil Nadu Startup & Innovation Policy could provide meaningful support—particularly in accessing funding, mentorship, and infrastructure. Let me outline how this policy works and what benefits it may offer ventures like yours.
