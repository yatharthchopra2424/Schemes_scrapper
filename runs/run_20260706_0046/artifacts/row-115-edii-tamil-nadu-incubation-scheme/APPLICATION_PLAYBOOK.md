### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official portal: https://edii.org.in and navigate to the incubation section or application page.  
2. Fill in all startup details accurately: name, incorporation date, TN address, contact information, and founder details.  
3. Clearly describe the innovative idea or prototype, emphasizing novelty and scalability.  
4. Specify the current stage (pre-revenue, early revenue, prototype development, etc.) with supporting evidence.  
5. Upload all required documents in the correct format (PDF preferred) and ensure file sizes are within limits.  
6. Double-check that founder names and details match across incorporation documents, PAN, and resumes.  
7. Declare any existing funding or support received – transparency is critical for credibility.  
8. Review the entire form before submission; incomplete or inconsistent applications may be screened out.
