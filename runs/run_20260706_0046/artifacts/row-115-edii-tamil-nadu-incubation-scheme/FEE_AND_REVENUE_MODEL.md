## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with client outcomes (e.g., accelerated product development, investor readiness, market validation).
2. Transparent retainer covering initial assessment, application support, and documentation preparation.
3. Success fee contingent on measurable milestones achieved post-incubation (e.g., prototype completion, fundraising traction, revenue generation).
4. Scheme-specific note: The EDII Tamil Nadu Incubation Scheme carries **low financial risk** for clients as it provides no direct funding, but **moderate operational risk** due to time-bound support, progress reviews, and milestone adherence requirements. Success is highly dependent on founder commitment and alignment with Tamil Nadu’s priority sectors. Processing time is not specified in evidence, but rolling applications suggest variable intake cycles; revenue lag assumptions should account for application screening, pitch/interview rounds, and onboarding timelines.
