## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping early-stage startups in Tamil Nadu access structured incubation support through government-backed programmes like the EDII Tamil Nadu Incubation Scheme. Many founders I speak with are building innovative products but struggle to find mentorship, lab space, or investor connections — especially in the pre-revenue phase. I’d like to understand where you are in your journey and see if this scheme could be a fit for your venture.

### Discovery Questions
- When was your startup incorporated, and is it registered in Tamil Nadu?
- Are you currently pre-revenue or in the early revenue stage?
- Do you have a working prototype or a validated innovative idea?
- Are the founders based in Tamil Nadu and committed to building a scalable venture?
- Have you already received any form of government or private incubation support?
- Are you looking for mentorship, infrastructure, or guidance on fundraising and compliance?
- Is your idea aligned with any of Tamil Nadu’s priority sectors (e.g., manufacturing, agritech, healthcare, or ICT)?
- What stage are you at in terms of business model validation or market testing?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup is incorporated in Tamil Nadu  
- [ ] Viable innovative idea or prototype exists  
- [ ] Preferably pre-revenue or early revenue stage  
- [ ] Founders are committed to building scalable ventures  
- [ ] Proof of address/domicile in Tamil Nadu available  
- [ ] Pitch deck or business plan prepared  
- [ ] Founder profiles and resumes ready  
- [ ] PAN card of startup or founders available  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re building an early-stage venture in Tamil Nadu with a real innovation — exactly the kind of startup the **EDII Tamil Nadu Incubation Scheme** is designed to support. This scheme doesn’t offer direct funding, but it provides high-value incubation support: mentorship, infrastructure, and investor access — all critical for de-risking early growth. Let me walk you through how it works and whether you’d qualify.

Early-stage startups in Tamil Nadu often struggle to access mentorship, technical infrastructure, and investor networks — especially before revenue. Without structured support, many fail to validate their models or scale, even with promising ideas.

**Scheme intro:**
The **EDII Tamil Nadu Incubation Scheme**, administered by the Entrepreneurship Development Institute of India (EDII), Tamil Nadu Chapter, offers non-financial incubation support including access to incubation infrastructure, mentorship from industry experts and academics, networking with investors and peers, guidance on business modeling, legal compliance, fundraising, and prototype development and market validation.
