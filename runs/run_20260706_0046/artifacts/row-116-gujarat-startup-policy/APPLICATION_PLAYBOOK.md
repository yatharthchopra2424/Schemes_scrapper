# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Startup incorporated as a Private Limited Company, Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), or Registered Partnership Firm in Gujarat  
- [ ] Innovative business model with scalable potential  
- [ ] Working towards development or improvement of products, processes, or services  
- [ ] Registered with DPIIT under the Startup India initiative  
- [ ] In operation for not more than 10 years from the date of incorporation  
- [ ] Located within the geographic scope of Gujarat only  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration  
- PAN of the entity  
- ITR (Income Tax Returns)  
- GST registration certificate  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration Certificate  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Pitch deck or business description  
- Authorization letter from authorized signatory  
- Details of funding received (if any)  
- DPIIT recognition certificate  
- Proof of innovation or prototype  
- Bank account details  

### Document Quality Check
- File size: Each document must be under 5 MB  
- File naming: Must match the exact document name as listed (e.g., "Certificate_of_Incorporation.pdf")  
- Certification: All certificates must be clear, legible, and issued by competent authorities  
- Language: Documents must be in English or accompanied by a certified translation  
- Validity: Udyam, DPIIT recognition, and incorporation certificates must be valid and not expired

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not explicitly required per evidence. However, a pitch deck or business description is mandatory. If a detailed project report is requested during evaluation, verify with the nodal officer (Industries and Mines Department) for required sections.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Register the startup on the Startup India portal (startupindia.gov.in) to obtain DPIIT recognition first.  
2. Ensure all entity details (name, address, incorporation date) match exactly across all documents.  
3. Clearly describe the innovative aspect of the product/process/service in the pitch deck.  
4. Specify the stage of development (idea, prototype, validation, early revenue) and fund utilization plan.  
5. Disclose any prior funding received (amount, source, equity diluted if any).  
6. Attach proof of innovation (prototype photos, test reports, user feedback, IPR filings if applicable).  
7. Confirm that the startup is incorporated in Gujarat and has been operational for ≤10 years.  
8. Authorized signatory must sign the authorization letter and application form.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All application form fields completed accurately  
- [ ] Universal and scheme-specific documents attached and validated  
- [ ] DPIIT recognition certificate verified and valid  
- [ ] Authorization letter signed by authorized signatory  
- [ ] Pitch deck clearly outlines innovation, scalability, and fund utilization  
- [ ] Bank account details match the entity name  
- [ ] No discrepancies in incorporation date, PAN, or Udyam number  

---

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the designated state portal or nodal agency after obtaining DPIIT recognition  
- Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing submission timestamp and application ID  
- Store the application reference number securely in the case file  
- Set follow-up reminders for Day 25 and Day 35 post-submission

### Escalation Path
- Level 1: Portal helpdesk – Startup India portal support (via https://startupindia.gov.in/)  
- Level 2: Nodal officer – Industries and Mines Department, Government of Gujarat (Email: icmsme@gujarat.gov.in | Phone: 079-232-52532/58378)  
- Level 3: RTI filing if no response within statutory SLA (typically 30 days for government schemes)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter sanctioning benefits under the Gujarat Startup Policy  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned amount (up to INR 25 lakhs) and form of support (seed funding, equity, credit guarantee, etc.)  
- [ ] Sign the agreement with the nodal agency or approved incubator as per the approved plan  
- [ ] Utilize funds strictly for the approved purpose (prototype development, validation, working capital, or capex as per agreement)  
- [ ] Maintain records of utilization for submission of utilization certificate  
- [ ] Track milestone-based disbursements if applicable  
- [ ] Prepare for post-approval compliance reporting, IPR filing or market access support as offered under the scheme  
- [ ] Invoice success fee as per consultancy agreement (to be filled by consultant)
