## 8D. Internal Data Handling Policy

All client data shall be handled in accordance with the following standards:

- **Encrypted Cloud Storage:** All files stored in encrypted cloud environments with access controls.
- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** Mandatory for all system access involving client data.
- **Limited Access:** Access to client data restricted to authorized personnel only on a need-to-know basis.
- **No Personal Devices:** Prohibition on storing or processing client data on personal laptops, phones, or removable media.
- **Retention Period:** Client data retained for a minimum of 2 years post case closure.
- **Breach Notification:** Any suspected or confirmed data breach must be reported to the affected client within 24 hours of discovery.

**Scheme-specific note:** All documents submitted to Industries and Mines Department, Government of Gujarat under Gujarat Startup Policy must be retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit purposes.
