## Pricing Principles
1. Value-Based Pricing: Fees aligned with the financial benefit secured (up to ₹25 lakhs seed funding under Gujarat Startup Policy).
2. Risk-Adjusted Fees: Success fees reflect moderate risk due to rolling application basis and committee approval dependency.
3. Transparency: Clear retainer for advisory work; success fee contingent on fund disbursement.
4. Scheme-Specific Note: Processing time varies (evaluation by committee post-submission); success fee lag of 3 months assumed based on typical review cycles for state startup schemes.
