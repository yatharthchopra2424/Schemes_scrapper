# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping early-stage startups in Gujarat access government-backed funding and support. The Gujarat Startup Policy is designed specifically for innovative ventures like yours—offering seed funding, incubation access, and mentorship to help you validate and scale your idea. I’d love to learn about your startup and see if this scheme aligns with your current stage and goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Registered Partnership Firm?
2. Is your startup registered with DPIIT under the Startup India initiative?
3. How long has your startup been in operation since incorporation?
4. What innovative product, process, or service are you developing or improving?
5. Does your business model demonstrate scalability and potential for growth?
6. Are you currently seeking funding for prototype development or validation?
7. Have you accessed any incubation centers, co-working spaces, or mentorship programs yet?
8. Are you based in Gujarat, or do you plan to establish your primary operations here?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Incorporated as Private Limited Company, LLP, or Registered Partnership Firm in Gujarat  
- [ ] Registered with DPIIT under Startup India initiative  
- [ ] In operation for not more than 10 years from date of incorporation  
- [ ] Innovative business model with scalable potential  
- [ ] Working towards development or improvement of products, processes, or services  
- [ ] Target beneficiary: startup, entrepreneur, innovator  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like your startup meets the core eligibility criteria for the Gujarat Startup Policy—particularly being DPIIT-recognized, under 10 years old, and focused on innovation. This scheme offers up to INR 25 lakhs in seed funding along with incubation support and mentorship. Let me walk you through how it works and how we’ve helped similar startups secure approval.

**Problem framing:**
Early-stage startups in Gujarat often struggle to access early-stage funding for prototype development, lack mentorship and incubation support, and face regulatory hurdles that slow down product validation and market entry—especially when operating with limited capital and no prior track record.

**Scheme intro:**
The Gujarat Startup Policy, administered by the Industries and Mines Department, Government of Gujarat, provides financial support of up to INR 25 lakhs for seed funding, prototype development, and validation. It also offers equity-based investments through GVFL or state-funded AIFs, credit guarantee support for bank loans, access to incubation centers, mentorship, IPR assistance, fast-tracked approvals, and participation in investor meets and startup fairs.
