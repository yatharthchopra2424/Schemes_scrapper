### Gujarat Startup Policy
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Incubation
- **Geography:** Gujarat only
- **Administering Body:** Industries and Mines Department, Government of Gujarat
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://startupindia.gov.in/
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Private Limited Company, Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), or Registered Partnership Firm
- Age of business: Not more than 10 years from the date of incorporation
- Sector: Innovative business model with scalable potential, working towards development or improvement of products, processes, or services
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be registered with DPIIT under the Startup India initiative

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to INR 25 Lakhs
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (seed funding), equity-based investments, credit guarantee support
- Duration of benefit: One-time (seed funding), ongoing (mentorship, incubation access)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. Pitch deck or business description
4. Authorization letter from authorized signatory
5. Details of funding received (if any)
6. DPIIT recognition certificate
7. Proof of innovation or prototype
8. Bank account details

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register the startup on the Startup India portal (startupindia.gov.in) to obtain DPIIT recognition.
2. Apply for recognition under the Gujarat Startup Policy via the designated state portal or nodal agency.
3. Submit the application form along with required documents such as Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, pitch deck, and details of innovation.
4. Await evaluation by the appointed committee or incubator.
5. Upon approval, sign the agreement and disbursement of benefits as per the approved plan.
