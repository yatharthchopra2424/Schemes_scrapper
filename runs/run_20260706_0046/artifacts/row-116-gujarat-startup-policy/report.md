# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Gujarat Startup Policy** is a state-level incubation scheme implemented by the Industries and Mines Department, Government of Gujarat. It aims to promote entrepreneurship and innovation in Gujarat by providing financial and non-financial support to early-stage startups. The policy is aligned with the Startup India initiative and offers rolling admissions with no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round via the Startup India portal.

**Key Details:**
- **Scheme Name:** Gujarat Startup Policy  
- **Scheme ID:** row-116  
- **Ministry / Category:** State Level (Gujarat)  
- **Scheme Type:** incubation  
- **Geographic Scope:** Gujarat only  
- **Implementing Agency:** Industries and Mines Department, Government of Gujarat  
- **Application Portal:** https://startupindia.gov.in/  
- **Status / Deadlines:** Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.  
- **Last Updated:** 2020  
- **Max Per Entity:** Up to INR 25 Lakhs  
- **Contact Details:** Email: icmsme@gujarat.gov.in | Phone: 079-232-52532/58378  
- **Confidence:** medium  

### Objectives
The policy seeks to:
- Promote entrepreneurship and innovation in the state  
- Strengthen the startup ecosystem through incubation and mentorship  
- Provide financial and non-financial support to early-stage startups  
- Encourage innovation in thrust sectors and technology-driven ventures  
- Facilitate access to funding, mentorship, and market linkages  
- Support women, SC/ST, and young entrepreneurs through inclusive provisions  
- Enhance ease of doing startups via regulatory simplification and single-window clearance  
- Promote research, development, and innovation in collaboration with academic and R&D institutions  

### Eligibility Matrix
Startups must meet the following criteria to be eligible:

| Criteria | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| **Legal Structure** | Private Limited Company, Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), or Registered Partnership Firm |
| **Geographic Scope** | Incorporated in Gujarat |
| **Innovation** | Innovative business model with scalable potential |
| **Stage of Development** | Working towards development or improvement of products, processes, or services |
| **DPIIT Recognition** | Must be registered with DPIIT under the Startup India initiative |
| **Age Limit** | Not more than 10 years from the date of incorporation |
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Startups, entrepreneurs, innovators |

> **Warning:** Startups must maintain DPIIT recognition to continue availing benefits. Misrepresentation of facts may lead to rejection and blacklisting.

### Benefits & Financial Support
The policy provides a mix of grants, equity funding, loan facilitation, and non-financial support.

#### Financial Support Breakdown
| Type of Support | Details |
|----------------|---------|
| **Seed Funding** | Up to INR 25 lakhs for prototype development and validation |
| **Equity-Based Investments** | Through Gujarat Venture Finance Limited (GVFL) or state-funded Alternate Investment Funds (AIFs) |
| **Credit Guarantee Support** | For bank loans |
| **Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS)** | Access facilitated |
| **Interest Subvention** | On loans taken by recognized startups for working capital and capital expenditure |

#### Non-Financial Benefits
| Benefit | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Prototype Development Support** | Assistance in building and validating prototypes |
| **Incubation Centers & Co-working Spaces** | Access to state-supported facilities |
| **Mentorship** | From industry experts |
| **IPR Filing Assistance** | Support in intellectual property rights filing |
| **Fast-Tracked Approvals** | Expedited regulatory clearances |
| **Regulatory Exemptions** | Exemption from certain compliances |
| **Startup Fairs & Investor Meets** | Participation in networking and pitching events |
| **Market Access & Scaling Support** | Guidance for market entry and growth |

> **Note:** Financial support includes grants, equity funding, and loan facilitation through state-backed funds. Equity-based support may involve dilution as per term sheet. Funds must be utilized only for the approved purpose as per the agreement.

### Application Process
The application process involves registration on the Startup India portal, followed by state-level recognition and evaluation.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Start] --> B[Register on Startup India Portal<br/>(startupindia.gov.in) to obtain DPIIT recognition]
    B --> C[Apply for recognition under Gujarat Startup Policy<br/>via designated state portal or nodal agency]
    C --> D[Submit application form with required documents:<br/>• Certificate of Incorporation/Registration<br/>• PAN of the entity<br/>• Pitch deck or business description<br/>• Authorization letter<br/>• Details of funding received (if any)<br/>• DPIIT recognition certificate<br/>• Proof of innovation or prototype<br/>• Bank account details]
    D --> E[Await evaluation by appointed committee or incubator]
    E --> F{Approved?}
    F -->|Yes| G[Sign agreement and receive disbursement of benefits<br/>as per approved plan]
    F -->|No| H[Application rejected; may reapply after addressing gaps]
    G --> I[End]
    H --> I
```

> **Important:** Benefits are subject to availability of funds and approval by the empowered committee. The process is rolling, with no fixed deadlines.

### Key Caveats
> - Benefits are subject to availability of funds and approval by the empowered committee  
> - Startups must maintain DPIIT recognition to continue availing benefits  
> - Misrepresentation of facts may lead to rejection and blacklisting  
> - Equity-based support may involve dilution as per term sheet  
> - Funds must be utilized only for the approved purpose as per the agreement
