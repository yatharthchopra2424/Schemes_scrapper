# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is an individual innovator, student, or startup (Indian citizen)  
- [ ] Project is in Idea-to-PoC development stage or has a PoC ready  
- [ ] Project relates to hardware or software-based technology innovation  
- [ ] For Government of Gujarat grants: Startup registered as LLP or private limited company in Gujarat  
- [ ] For Spark-up Idea Fund: Applicant is a student or team from any discipline and college  
- [ ] For NIDHI-SSS: Startup is 1–3 years old, has advanced prototyping, revenue generation, bootstrapped funding, and is incorporated as a Private Limited Company or capable of incorporation within 45 days  
- [ ] For iCreate Idea Catalyst (iIC): Applicant is an entrepreneur, team, university/college student, inventor, researcher, or individual passionate about solving problems with innovative ideas  
- [ ] Idea is implementable, commercially viable, and new or a better alternative (for Spark-up Idea Fund)  
- [ ] For NIDHI-EIR: Applicant is a graduating student or budding entrepreneur with promising technology business idea  
- [ ] For TEC grant: Project has an element of technology innovation at its core  
- [ ] For NIDHI-PRAYAS: Project involves physical product development (not software or pure academic research)  
- [ ] For TIDE 2.0: Startup has a definite PoC to be developed into MVP  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration  
- PAN of the entity  
- ITR (last 2–3 years)  
- GST registration certificate  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (if applicable)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Pitch deck or business description  
- Authorization letter from authorized signatory  
- Details of funding received (if any)  
- Proof of student status (for Spark-up Idea Fund)  
- Business plan  
- Proof of concept or prototype details  
- Incorporation documents (for Gujarat-based startups under Government of Gujarat grants)  
- Project report and roadmap  

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible and in PDF format (scanned copies acceptable)  
- [ ] File names match the document type (e.g., "PAN_Card.pdf", "Incorporation_Certificate.pdf")  
- [ ] File size does not exceed 5 MB per document  
- [ ] All certificates are valid and not expired  
- [ ] Documents are in English or Hindi; if in another language, certified translation is attached  
- [ ] Authorization letter is on company letterhead and signed by authorized signatory  
- [ ] Bank statements show consistent transactions and are stamped by the bank  
- [ ] ITR copies are complete with acknowledgment from income tax department

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A detailed Project Report is required for most programs (especially NIDHI-PRAYAS, TIDE 2.0, Startup Gujarat, NIDHI-SSS). If not explicitly mentioned for a specific program (e.g., Spark-up Idea Fund, TEC grant), **verify with the nodal officer** whether a DPR is needed.  

**Standard DPR Sections (if required):**  
- Executive Summary  
- Problem Statement and Innovation  
- Market Analysis and Customer Validation  
- Technical Description and Methodology  
- Business Model and Revenue Projections  
- Implementation Timeline and Milestones  
- Team Background and Roles  
- Budget Utilization Plan  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Strategy  
- Sustainability and Scalability Plan  
- Annexures (prototype photos, test results, letters of support)  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Read all instructions carefully** before entering any data—each program has unique fields.  
2. **Use exact names** as per official documents (e.g., PAN, Certificate of Incorporation).  
3. **Upload documents in the specified format** (PDF/JPEG) and size limits (typically <5 MB).  
4. **Ensure all mandatory fields are filled**—incomplete forms are auto-rejected.  
5. **Double-check numeric entries** (e.g., funding amount requested, turnover, employee count).  
6. **Save draft frequently** to avoid data loss due to session timeout.  
7. **Preview the entire application** before final submission to verify attachments and declarations.  
8. **Refer to program-specific timelines** (e.g., Spark-up Idea Fund: apply between 1 Jul–31 Jul) to avoid missing windows.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Take a **screenshot of the submission confirmation page** showing application ID, timestamp, and program name.  
- Store the **application reference number** in the case file and CRM.  
- Set **follow-up reminders** for:  
  - Shortlisting notification date (e.g., 10 Aug for Spark-up Idea Fund)  
  - Final pitch date (e.g., 16 Aug for Spark-up Idea Fund)  
  - Result announcement (e.g., 20 Aug for Spark-up Idea Fund)  
  - Onboarding date (e.g., 26 Aug for Spark-up Idea Fund)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the **approval letter** specifying sanctioned amount and program terms  
- [ ] Confirm **disbursement schedule** (tranches, milestones) as per scheme (e.g., NIDHI-PRAYAS: 4 tranches; TEC grant: 4 tranches)  
- [ ] Sign **NDA and other legal agreements** (if required) before accessing funds or campus facilities  
- [ ] Prepare **utilization certificate** format as per iCreate/auditor requirements  
- [ ] Track **milestone completion** for tranche release (e.g., prototype development, market validation)  
- [ ] Maintain **audit-ready records** of all expenditures (bills, invoices, bank statements)  
- [ ] Submit **progress reports** as per schedule (monthly/quarterly)  
- [ ] Ensure **timely submission of utilization certificate** post-project completion  
- [ ] Follow up on **success fee invoice** (if applicable per consultancy agreement)  
- [ ] Explore **additional support** from iCreate (mentorship, market connects, investor pitch events)  
- [ ] Plan for **exit or scaling** post-incubation (e.g., apply for follow-on funding like NIDHI-SSS, SISF)
