## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the tangible financial benefit delivered—typically up to ₹30 lakhs under the Government of Gujarat grant or ₹10 lakhs under NIDHI-PRAYAS—ensuring client ROI exceeds consulting costs.
2. **Risk Mitigation**: Success fees are contingent on grant approval, reducing upfront client risk; processing times vary by program (e.g., Spark-up Idea Fund: ~2 months from application to onboarding), so retainers cover initial advisory work.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Starter and Growth tiers align with the scheme’s focus on students, early-stage startups, and MSMEs, particularly those eligible for Spark-up Idea Fund (students only) or NIDHI-PRAYAS/TIDE 2.0 (hardware-focused prototyping).
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: This scheme has a moderate risk profile due to competitive selection and tranche-based disbursement (e.g., NIDHI-PRAYAS, TEC grant), but high success rates for well-prepared applications, as evidenced by iCreate’s 912+ supported startups and structured mentorship.
