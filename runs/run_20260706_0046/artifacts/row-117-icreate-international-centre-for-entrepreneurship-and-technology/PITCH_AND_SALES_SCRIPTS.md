# Pitch & Sales Scripts for iCreate – Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I’m [Your Name] from [Your Firm]. I specialise in helping early‑stage innovators and student entrepreneurs in Gujarat and across India access non‑dilutive funding and incubation support through government‑backed programmes like iCreate’s Spark‑up Idea Fund, which offers up to ₹50,000 for student‑led tech ideas, along with mentorship, prototyping lab access, and a pathway to larger grants. I’d love to understand where you are in your journey and see if iCreate’s ecosystem could be a fit for you.

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you currently a student (any discipline) or part of a student team from a recognised college/university?  
2. What stage is your idea at – pure concept, sketch/prototype, or have you built a basic proof‑of‑concept?  
3. Is your innovation technology‑based (hardware, software, IoT, EV, agritech, healthtech, etc.)?  
4. Have you validated the idea with any potential users or customers, even informally?  
5. Are you looking for funding to develop a prototype or to take an existing PoC toward an MVP?  
6. Do you intend to incorporate a company (LLP or Private Limited) in Gujarat, or are you open to applying as an individual/team?  
7. What is your timeline for building and testing the prototype – are you able to commit to a 3‑day bootcamp and subsequent 3‑month incubation if selected?  
8. Have you previously applied for any government startup grants or incubation programmes?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is a student or team of students from any discipline/college  
- [ ] Idea is technology‑based and innovative  
- [ ] Idea is implementable, commercially viable, and either entirely new or a better alternative  
- [ ] Applicant can commit to the programme timeline (application 1 Jul – 31 Jul, shortlist 10 Aug, final pitch 16 Aug, result 20 Aug, onboarding 26 Aug)  
- [ ] Willing to submit required documents: proof of student status, pitch deck/business description, authorisation letter, PAN, incorporation certificate (if applicable), funding details, business plan, PoC/prototype details  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your answers, it sounds like you’re a student with a tech‑based idea ready for prototyping – exactly who iCreate’s Spark‑up Idea Fund is designed for. This programme can provide up to ₹50,000 in grant funding, intensive mentorship, and access to iLab@iCreate’s rapid prototyping facilities to turn your concept into a working product.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**  
Students with innovative tech ideas often struggle to access early‑stage funding, mentorship, and prototyping resources needed to validate concepts and build minimum viable products. Without support, many promising projects stall at the idea stage due to lack of capital, expertise, or lab access.

**Scheme intro:**  
iCreate Spark‑up Idea Fund – a Government of Gujarat‑supported initiative implemented by the International Centre for Entrepreneurship and Technology (iCreate). It offers a grant of up to ₹50,000 for student innovators, coupled with a 3‑day bootcamp, expert mentorship, access to iLab@iCreate prototyping facilities, and a pathway to larger incubation programmes.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**  
- You are a student or team from a recognised college/university (eligibility open to any discipline)  
- Your idea is technology‑based and aims to be implementable and commercially viable  
- You are prepared to engage in a structured timeline: apply by 31 Jul, attend bootcamp, pitch on 16 Aug, and onboard by 26 Aug if selected
