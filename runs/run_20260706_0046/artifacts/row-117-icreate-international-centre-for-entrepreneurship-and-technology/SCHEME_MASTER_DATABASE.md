### iCreate – International Centre for Entrepreneurship and Technology
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Incubation
- **Geography:** Gujarat and Pan-India (with international collaborations)
- **Administering Body:** International Centre for Entrepreneurship and Technology (iCreate), supported by the Government of Gujarat and the Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://icreate.org.in/program_listing/spark-up-idea-fund/
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Rolling basis for some programs; specific windows for others (e.g., Spark-up Idea Fund: Application Open 1 Jul, Close 31 Jul, Announcing Shortlisted Startups 10 Aug, Final Pitch 16 Aug, Announcing Final Result 20 Aug, Onboarding 26 Aug)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Startups, entrepreneurs, students, MSME, agri-startups, women entrepreneurs
- Age of business: Varies by program (e.g., Spark-up Idea Fund: any student or team; NIDHI-SSS: 1-3 years old; Government of Gujarat grants: registered as LLP or private limited company in Gujarat)
- Sector: Agritech, Healthtech, Electric Vehicle, Renewable Energy, Fintech, Internet of Things, Watertech, and other technology-driven sectors
- Revenue cap: Not explicitly mentioned for general scheme; varies by program (e.g., NIDHI-SSS prefers revenue-generating startups)
- Employee count: Not mentioned
- Other: 
  - For Spark-up Idea Fund: any student or team from any discipline and college can apply if the idea is implementable, commercially viable, and new or a better alternative
  - For NIDHI-SSS: applicants must be slightly mature startups (1-3 years old) with advanced prototyping, revenue generation, bootstrapped funding, and incorporated as a Private Limited Company or capable of incorporation within 45 days
  - For iCreate Pro-Fund Gateway: applicants must be individual innovators, students, or startups, Indian citizens, in Idea-to-PoC or PoC stage, with hardware/software tech innovation
  - For Government of Gujarat grants: startups must be registered as LLP or private limited company in Gujarat
  - For iCreate Idea Catalyst (iIC): applicants include entrepreneurs, teams, university/college students, inventors, researchers, and individuals passionate about solving problems with innovative ideas

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support varies by program: Spark-up Idea Fund offers up to INR 50,000 for students; NIDHI-PRAYAS provides up to Rs 10 lakhs for prototype development over 18 months (extendable by 6 months); TIDE 2.0 offers Rs 7 lakhs per startup for nascent startups with PoC to develop MVP; Government of Gujarat Startup Gujarat offers grants up to Rs 30 lakhs for product development and go-to-market over 12 months; TEC grant provides up to Rs 2 lakhs for PoC development in 90 days; NIDHI-EIR offers a monthly stipend of Rs 10,000–30,000 for up to 12 months; NIDHI-SSS provides seed support between Rs 25 lakhs to Rs 45 lakhs (up to Rs 100 lakhs in exceptional cases) as interest-bearing debt or equity
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement / Direct credit (varies by program; e.g., funds disbursed in tranches subject to progress review for NIDHI-PRAYAS, TEC grant)
- Duration of benefit: Varies by program (e.g., One-time for Spark-up Idea Fund; Annual for NIDHI-EIR; 3 years for some programs)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. Pitch deck or business description
4. Authorization letter from authorized signatory
5. Details of funding received (if any)
6. Proof of student status (for Spark-up Idea Fund)
7. Business plan
8. Proof of concept or prototype details
9. Incorporation documents (for Gujarat-based startups under Government of Gujarat grants)
10. Project report and roadmap

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the relevant program (e.g., Spark-up Idea Fund, NIDHI-SSS, iCreate Pro-Fund Gateway, iCreate Idea Catalyst)
2. Check eligibility criteria for the specific program
3. Apply during the application window (dates vary by program; e.g., Spark-up Idea Fund: open 1 Jul, close 31 Jul)
4. Submit required documents via the application portal or email
5. Await shortlisting notification (e.g., 10 Aug for Spark-up Idea Fund)
6. Attend final pitch or evaluation session (e.g., 16 Aug for Spark-up Idea Fund)
7. Receive final result and begin onboarding (e.g., 20 Aug for Spark-up Idea Fund)
8. Sign NDA and start working from iCreate Campus (e.g., 26 Aug for Spark-up Idea Fund)
