## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Entity is registered in Uttar Pradesh (Certificate of Incorporation/Registration shows UP address)
- [ ] Entity is a startup recognized by DPIIT **OR** registered under the UP Startup Policy
- [ ] Entity is an MSME, artisan unit, self-help group, or individual engaged in ODOP-identified products
- [ ] Business plan/project report demonstrates linkage to an ODOP product or service (Proof of linkage required if applicable)
- [ ] Applicant meets preference criteria (if applicable): woman entrepreneur, SC/ST, or rural entrepreneur
- [ ] Entity has a viable business plan related to ODOP products/services
- [ ] Entity complies with state government guidelines for the UP Startup Policy & ODOP initiative
- [ ] No double benefit claimed from other central/state schemes for the same activity
- [ ] Entity agrees to utilize funds only for approved purpose and submit utilization certificates
- [ ] Entity agrees to comply with project timelines to avoid sanction cancellation

> **Note**: Applications are accepted on a rolling basis throughout the year subject to fund availability. No fixed turnover, employee count, or age-of-business thresholds are specified in the evidence.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
- PAN card of the entity or applicant
- Bank account details (cancelled cheque or bank statement)
- Udyam Registration Certificate (for MSMEs)
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)
- Latest ITR (if applicable, for entities have filed returns)
- Audited financial statements (if applicable)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Aadhaar card of the applicant
- Business plan or project report (must demonstrate ODOP linkage)
- Proof of linkage to ODOP product (if applicable — e.g., product certification, sourcing proof, or district-specific ODOP list alignment)
- Photographs of product or unit (clear images showing product, branding, and operational unit)
- Caste certificate (for SC/ST applicants seeking special benefits)
- Gender certificate (for women applicants seeking special benefits — e.g., self-declaration or official document)
- Quotations for machinery or equipment (if seeking loan for capital expenditure under interest-free loan component)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document unless otherwise specified on portal)
- File names must match document type (e.g., "PAN_Card.pdf", "Aadhaar_Applicant.pdf")
- Certificates (caste, gender) must be issued by competent authority and valid at time of application
- Business plan must include: executive summary, ODOP product linkage, market analysis, financial projections, utilization plan, and milestones
- Photographs must be in JPEG/PNG format, clearly showing product and unit (minimum 1080p resolution recommended)
- All declarations and forms must be signed and dated by authorized signatory
- Documents must be in Hindi or English; if in another language, certified translation required

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a **business plan or project report** is required as per scheme evidence.  
**Standard sections to include**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Promoter Details & Background  
- Product/Service Description (explicit linkage to ODOP product of the district)  
- Market Analysis (demand, competition, target customers)  
- ODOP Justification (how the product aligns with district’s ODOP list)  
- Technical Details (production process, technology used)  
- Financial Projections (3–5 years: revenue, profit, break-even)  
- Utilization Plan (how grant/loan/seed funds will be used across milestones)  
- Implementation Timeline (Gantt chart)  
- Risk Assessment & Mitigation  
- Annexures: Quotations, photographs, ODOP proof, certificates  

> **Note**: Verify exact DPR format expectations with the nodal officer or UPTPA if unsure.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the portal at **https://startup.up.gov.in** and register as a beneficiary using valid email and mobile number.  
2. Select “Apply for UP Startup Policy & ODOP Scheme” from the available schemes.  
3. Fill all applicant details exactly as per PAN and Aadhaar (name, DOB, address).  
4. Clearly state the ODOP product linkage in the designated field — upload supporting proof if prompted.  
5. In the business plan section, summarize key points; attach full DPR as a separate document.  
6. Specify the type of financial support sought (grant up to ₹10L, interest-free loan up to ₹25L, seed funding up to ₹5L, or certification reimbursement).  
7. Ensure all declarations (no double benefit, fund utilization compliance, timeline adherence) are checked and signed.  
8. Preview the entire form before submission — once submitted, edits may not be allowed.  
9. Keep digital copies of all uploaded documents and the submitted application PDF.  
10. Note: The portal may time out during long sessions — save progress frequently if draft-saving is available.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All form fields filled accurately and match supporting documents  
- [ ] Business plan/uploaded DPR clearly demonstrates ODOP product linkage  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents attached in correct format  
- [ ] Declarations signed: no double benefit, proper utilization, timeline compliance  
- [ ] Financial support type and amount requested are clearly specified and within limits (grant ≤ ₹10L, loan ≤ ₹25L, etc.)  
- [ ] Application preview reviewed for errors before final submission  
- [ ] Reference number or application ID generated and recorded upon submission

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and sanitize approval letter/sanction order from UPTPA or designated nodal agency  
- [ ] Verify sanctioned amount, type of support (grant/loan/seed funding), and disbursement schedule  
- [ ] Review and acknowledge terms and conditions in sanction letter (utilization purpose, timelines, reporting)  
- [ ] Prepare utilization plan aligned with approved milestones  
- [ ] Submit Utilization Certificate (UC) and expenditure reports as per milestone completion  
- [ ] Maintain all bills, vouchers, and audit-ready records for fund usage  
- [ ] Track disbursement of funds against submitted UCs  
- [ ] Ensure no diversion of funds; use strictly for approved purpose (product dev, certification, marketing, etc.)  
- [ ] Prepare and submit final project completion report  
- [ ] Coordinate with client to claim success fee upon verified fund utilization and approval closure  
- [ ] Advise client on compliance with post-approval audits or monitoring visits by UPTPA
