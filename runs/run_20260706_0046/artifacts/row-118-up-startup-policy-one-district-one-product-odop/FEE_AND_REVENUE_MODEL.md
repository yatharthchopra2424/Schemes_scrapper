## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-aligned pricing**: Fees are structured as a percentage of the financial benefit secured, ensuring our success is tied to client outcomes.
2. **Risk-adjusted retainers**: Retainer amounts reflect the complexity of documentation and coordination required with UPTPA and district-level committees.
3. **Transparency**: All fee structures are disclosed upfront, with clear milestones for retainer and success and success and success fee triggers.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: The UP Startup Policy & ODOP scheme has a moderate risk profile due to its rolling application basis and dependency on fund availability. While processing times are not explicitly stated in the evidence, the multi-stage approval process (district screening → state-level committee) suggests a typical lag of 3–4 months from application to sanction, which should be factored into revenue recognition and client expectations.

## Invoice Templates

**Description**: Retainer fee for UP Startup Policy & ODOP scheme application support, including eligibility assessment, business plan refinement, documentation compilation, and liaison with district-level committees for preliminary screening.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for securing financial support under the UP Startup Policy & ODOP scheme, implemented by the Uttar Pradesh Trade Promotion Authority (UPTPA), under the Department of Infrastructure and Industrial Development, Government of Uttar Pradesh. Fee is contingent upon sanction letter issuance and fund disbursement approval.
