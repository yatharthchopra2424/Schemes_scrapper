## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because I specialise in helping startups and MSMEs in Uttar Pradesh access government support for product development and market expansion—particularly through the UP Startup Policy & One District One Product (ODOP) scheme. I’ve worked with several entrepreneurs in your space who’ve secured grants up to INR 10 lakhs for certification, prototyping, and trade fair participation. Before I share how this might apply to you, I’d like to understand your current stage and goals to see if this is a relevant fit.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your business registered or incorporated in Uttar Pradesh?
2. Are you recognised by DPIIT as a startup, or registered under the UP Startup Policy?
3. Is your product or service linked to any of the One District One Product (ODOP) items identified for your district?
4. What stage are you at—idea validation, product development, or ready for market scaling?
5. Are you seeking support for activities like ISO/FSSAI certification, machinery, working capital, or e-commerce integration?
6. Are you a woman entrepreneur, from an SC/ST background, or based in a rural area of UP? (This helps us prioritise eligibility pathways.)
7. Do you have a detailed business plan or project report outlining your ODOP-linked product development plan?
8. Are you currently receiving similar financial support from another central or state scheme for the same activity?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is registered in Uttar Pradesh  
- [ ] Entity is a startup recognised by DPIIT OR registered under UP Startup Policy  
- [ ] Product/service is linked to an ODOP-identified item (or applicant is willing to establish linkage)  
- [ ] Applicant is an MSME, artisan, self-help group, or individual engaged in ODOP products  
- [ ] Preference given if applicant is woman, SC/ST, or rural entrepreneur (not mandatory but strengthens case)  
- [ ] Viable business plan related to ODOP products/services exists or can be developed  
- [ ] No double benefit claimed from other central/state schemes for same activity  
- [ ] Willing to comply with utilization reporting and project timelines  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you’re aligned with several key criteria for the UP Startup Policy & ODOP scheme—particularly your ODOP-linked product and registration in UP. This scheme offers grants up to INR 10 lakhs for product development, certification, and market promotion, along with interest-free loans and seed funding. Let me walk you through how it works and whether it’s the right fit for your current needs.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many startups and artisans in Uttar Pradesh struggle to move beyond the idea stage due to lack of funds for prototyping, certification, or market access. Even when they have a viable ODOP-linked product, costs for ISO/FSSAI certification, machinery, packaging, or trade fair participation can be prohibitive—especially for women, SC/ST, or rural entrepreneurs with limited collateral or credit history.

**Scheme intro:**
The UP Startup Policy & One District One Product (ODOP) scheme, administered by the Uttar Pradesh Trade Promotion Authority (UPTPA) under the Department of Infrastructure and Industrial Development, Government of Uttar Pradesh, offers a comprehensive support package. This includes grants of up to INR 10 lakhs per entity for product development, certification, and market promotion; interest-free loans up to INR 25 lakhs for working capital and machinery; seed funding of up to INR 5 lakhs for idea validation; and reimbursement of up to 50% of costs for ISO, FSSAI, or other certifications. Additional benefits include incubation support, mentorship, IPR assistance, trade fair participation, e-commerce integration, and access to raw material banks.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your business is registered in Uttar Pradesh — a core eligibility requirement  
- You are either DPIIT-recognised or registered under the UP Startup Policy — meeting the startup recognition criterion  
- Your product is linked to an ODOP-identified item — directly aligning with the scheme’s focus  
- [If applicable] You are a woman/SC/ST/rural entrepreneur — qualifying for preference under the scheme
