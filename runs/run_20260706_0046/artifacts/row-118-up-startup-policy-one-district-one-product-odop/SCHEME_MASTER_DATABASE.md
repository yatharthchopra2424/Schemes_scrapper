### UP Startup Policy & One District One Product (ODOP)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Uttar Pradesh
- **Administering Body:** Uttar Pradesh Trade Promotion Authority (UPTPA), under the Department of Infrastructure and Industrial Development, Government of Uttar Pradesh
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://startup.up.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (applications accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Startups registered in Uttar Pradesh, MSMEs, artisans, self-help groups, individuals engaged in ODOP-identified products
- Age of business: Not specified (must be recognized by DPIIT or registered under UP Startup Policy)
- Sector: ODOP-identified products or services (agri-startups, artisans, etc.)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Preference given to women, SC/ST, and rural entrepreneurs; entities must have a viable business plan related to ODOP products or services and comply with state government guidelines

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to INR 10 lakhs grant for product development, certification, and market promotion; interest-free loans up to INR 25 lakhs for working capital and machinery; seed funding up to INR 5 lakhs for idea validation; reimbursement of up to 50% of costs for ISO, FSSAI, or other certifications
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit through Uttar Pradesh Trade Promotion Authority (UPTPA) or designated nodal agencies after project appraisal
- Duration of benefit: One-time (subject to project milestones and utilization)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN card of the entity or applicant
3. Aadhaar card of the applicant
4. Business plan or project report
5. Proof of linkage to ODOP product (if applicable)
6. Bank account details
7. Photographs of product or unit
8. Caste certificate (for SC/ST applicants)
9. Gender certificate (for women applicants, if seeking special benefits)
10. Quotations for machinery or equipment (if seeking loan for capital expenditure)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the official UP Startup Policy portal at startup.up.gov.in
2. Register as a beneficiary by filling the online application form
3. Submit details of the business idea, ODOP product linkage, and required documents
4. Await preliminary screening by the district-level committee
5. Present the business plan before the state-level evaluation committee
6. Receive approval and sanction letter
7. Submit utilization reports and claim disbursement of funds as per milestones
