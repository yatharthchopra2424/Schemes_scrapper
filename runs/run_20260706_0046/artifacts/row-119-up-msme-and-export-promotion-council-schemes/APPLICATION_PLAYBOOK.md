## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Enterprise is classified as Micro, Small, or Medium as per MSMED Act, 2006  
- [ ] Registered office or operational unit is located in Uttar Pradesh  
- [ ] Engaged in manufacturing or service activities  
- [ ] Possesses valid Udyam Registration Certificate  
- [ ] Not availed similar benefits from other government sources for the same purpose (to avoid double dipping)  
- [ ] For export promotion sub-schemes: Has export realization or export-oriented business plan  
- [ ] For capital subsidy sub-schemes: Has investment in plant and machinery or plans for technology upgradation  
- [ ] For interest subsidy sub-schemes: Has sanctioned term loan from a bank or financial institution  
- [ ] For quality certification reimbursement: Has obtained or plans to obtain ISO or other recognized certification  
- [ ] For trade fair participation: Has plan to participate in domestic or international trade fair/exhibition  
- [ ] For patent registration support: Has invention or innovation eligible for patent filing  
- [ ] For skill training: Has workforce requiring upskilling or new hiring plans  
- [ ] For infrastructure/cluster support: Part of an MSME cluster or willing to join cluster development program  

> **Note**: Specific sub-schemes may have additional criteria (e.g., export turnover threshold, investment ceiling, sector focus). Verify with nodal officer or sub-scheme guidelines before proceeding.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Udyam Registration Certificate  
- Aadhaar and PAN of proprietor/partners/directors  
- Certificate of Incorporation (for companies) / Partnership Deed (for partnerships) / Proprietorship affidavit  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- ITR of business and promoters (last 2 years)  
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- Address proof of business unit (electricity bill, rent agreement, or property tax receipt)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Project Report or Business Plan *(required for capital subsidy, technology upgradation, and general support sub-schemes)*  
- Quotations for plant and machinery *(for capital subsidy claims)*  
- Bank sanction letter for term loan *(for interest subsidy claims)*  
- Proof of export realization (shipping bills, bank realization certificate, FOB value proof) *(for export promotion sub-schemes)*  
- ISO or other certification certificates *(for reimbursement of certification expenses)*  
- Details of bank account (cancelled cheque or bank passbook)  
- Patent application/registration documents *(for patent support sub-schemes)*  
- Trade fair participation invitation or registration proof *(for market development assistance)*  
- Skill training proposal or MOU with training provider *(for skill development sub-schemes)*  
- Cluster participation certificate or DPR for CFC *(for infrastructure/cluster development sub-schemes)*  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)  
- Name on documents must match exactly with Udyam Registration and PAN  
- Certificates (Udyam, ISO, etc.) must be valid and not expired  
- Bank sanction letter must be on bank letterhead, signed, and sanctioned within last 6 months  
- Export realization documents must show FOB value and realization in INR  
- Quotations must be itemized, with vendor details, validity period, and GST breakdown  
- Project Report must include promoter details, product/process description, market analysis, financial projections, and employment generation  
- All documents must be in English or Hindi; vernacular documents require certified translation  
- Self-attested copies are acceptable unless notarization is specifically required

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a **Project Report or Business Plan** is required for most sub-schemes under this council, particularly for capital subsidy, technology upgradation, and general support.  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Promoter(s) Profile (experience, qualifications)  
- Product/Service Description and USP  
- Manufacturing Process or Service Delivery Model  
- Market Analysis (demand, competition, target audience)  
- Marketing and Sales Strategy  
- Organizational Structure and HR Plan  
- Technology Details (existing vs. proposed upgradation)  
- Implementation Timeline (Gantt chart)  
- Financial Projections (5-year):  
  - Capital Cost Breakdown  
  - Working Capital Requirements  
  - Revenue Projections  
  - Profitability Statement  
  - Break-even Analysis  
  - Cash Flow Statement  
- Employment Generation (direct and indirect)  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
- Sustainability and Compliance (environmental, safety)  
- Annexures: Quotations, land documents, NOCs, promoter affidavit  

> **Note**: For interest subsidy or reimbursement-based sub-schemes, a detailed Project Report may not be mandatory; verify with nodal officer.  
> **Source**: Application Process, Key Facts  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Select correct sub-scheme**: Clearly identify the relevant sub-scheme (e.g., capital subsidy, interest subsidy, export promotion) based on client need before starting the form.  
2. **Use Udyam Registration Number as primary identifier**: Ensure it matches exactly in all fields and documents.  
3. **Fill all mandatory fields**: Do not leave any asterisk-marked (*) fields blank; if not applicable, write “NA” or “Not Applicable” with justification.  
4. **Enter financial figures accurately**: Investment, loan amount, turnover, and subsidy claimed must match bank statements, ITR, and quotations.  
5. **Upload documents in correct format**: Only PDF/JPEG allowed; ensure file names are clear (e.g., “Udyam_Certificate.pdf”, “Quotation_P&M.pdf”).  
6. **Declare no double benefit**: Explicitly confirm in declaration that applicant has not availed similar central/state benefit for same purpose.  
7. **Provide bank details correctly**: Account number, IFSC, and bank name must match cancelled cheque or passbook; errors cause disbursement failure.  
8. **Review before final submit**: Use preview option; once submitted, edits may not be allowed. Save draft regularly.  
9. **Note application reference number**: Auto-generated upon submission; critical for tracking.  
10. **Follow sub-scheme-specific guidelines**: Some sub-schemes (e.g., export promotion) require additional fields like IEC code, export turnover, or country-wise shipment details.  

> **Portal Note**: Application portal is https://upmsme.in; ensure stable internet and browser compatibility (Chrome/Firefox recommended).

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify approval letter from UP MSME and Export Promotion Council  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount matches eligibility and claim  
- [ ] Verify mode of disbursement: direct to beneficiary or via lending institution (for interest subsidy)  
- [ ] For capital subsidy: Ensure funds are utilized only for approved plant and machinery within validity period  
- [ ] For interest subsidy: Confirm periodic disbursement to bank/lender as per sanction  
- [ ] For reimbursement claims (ISO, patent, trade fair): Submit original invoices and payment proof within stipulated time (usually 30–60 days post-expenditure)  
- [ ] Utilization Certificate (UC) to be submitted within timeframe specified in approval letter (if required)  
- [ ] Maintain audit trail: bills, vouchers, bank statements showing utilization  
- [ ] Inform client of success fee invoice schedule as per engagement agreement  
- [ ] Track post-disbursement compliance: some sub-schemes require performance reporting (e.g., export growth, employment retention)  
- [ ] Notify client of any post-approval conditions (e.g., no transfer of assets for X years, inspection rights)  

> **Note**: Disbursement is typically made after verification of claims by designated nodal agencies or banks.
