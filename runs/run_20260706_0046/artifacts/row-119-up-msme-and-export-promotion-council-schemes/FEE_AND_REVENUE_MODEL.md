## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are tied to the actual financial benefit secured (capital subsidy, interest subsidy, or reimbursement), ensuring clients pay only for realized outcomes.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the medium confidence in scheme availability and the dependency on fund availability and timely claim submission, success fees reflect the moderate risk of delays or non-disbursement.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainers are scaled to client size, ensuring MSMEs (the primary beneficiaries) can access support without prohibitive upfront costs.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; clients receive itemized invoices linking payments to specific scheme milestones (application submission, approval, disbursement).

*Note: This scheme involves multiple sub-schemes with varying benefit mechanisms (capital subsidy, interest subsidy, reimbursement). Processing times vary by sub-scheme and nodal agency, but disbursement typically occurs after verification of claims, introducing a 3–6 month lag from approval to payment. Success fee calculations are based on the verified amount disbursed, not the claimed amount.*
