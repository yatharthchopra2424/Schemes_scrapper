## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping MSMEs in Uttar Pradesh access state government support for growth, technology upgradation, and export promotion through the UP MSME and Export Promotion Council Schemes. Many businesses in manufacturing and services miss out on capital subsidies, interest support, and certification reimbursements simply because they don’t know which sub-scheme fits their needs or how to apply correctly. I’d like to understand your current operations and growth plans to see if any of these UP-specific incentives could support your next phase of development.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your business incorporated or registered under Udyam, and where is your primary operational unit located in Uttar Pradesh?
2. Are you currently engaged in manufacturing or service activities, and what is your sector of operation (e.g., agro-processing, textiles, engineering, IT services)?
3. Have you made recent investments in plant and machinery, or are you planning such upgrades in the next 6–12 months?
4. Are you availing of a term loan from a bank for business expansion, and if so, have you received a sanction letter?
5. Do you export goods or services, or are you planning to enter international markets and participate in trade fairs?
6. Have you obtained ISO or other quality certifications, or are you considering certification to improve market access?
7. Are you looking for support in skill development for your workforce or infrastructure upgrades through cluster-based initiatives?
8. What is your current annual turnover or investment in plant and machinery, as this helps determine eligibility under specific MSME categories and sub-schemes?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is registered as an MSME under Udyam Registration (valid certificate)
- [ ] Registered office or operational unit is located in Uttar Pradesh
- [ ] Engaged in manufacturing or service activities as per MSMED Act, 2006
- [ ] Not availing double benefits for the same expenditure under central and state schemes
- [ ] Has not previously claimed similar benefits from other government sources for the same purpose
- [ ] For export promotion: proof of export realization available or planned
- [ ] For interest subsidy: bank sanction letter for term loan available
- [ ] For capital subsidy: quotations for plant and machinery obtained
- [ ] For certification reimbursement: ISO or other certification certificate in hand or applied for

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your business may qualify for one or more sub-schemes under the **UP MSME and Export Promotion Council Schemes**, which offer financial support such as capital subsidy on plant and machinery, interest subsidy on bank loans, and reimbursement of ISO certification costs. These are designed specifically for UP-based MSMEs like yours to reduce operational costs and enhance competitiveness. Would you like me to outline how these schemes work and what support you could potentially access?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many MSMEs in Uttar Pradesh face high upfront costs for technology upgradation, struggle with interest burdens on term loans, and lack resources to pursue quality certifications or export market development — limiting their ability to scale, compete, and access international markets despite strong growth potential.

**Scheme intro:**
The **UP MSME and Export Promotion Council Schemes**, administered by the Uttar Pradesh MSME and Export Promotion Council, provide a range of financial incentives including capital subsidy (up to a certain percentage of investment in plant and machinery), interest subsidy on bank loans, and reimbursement of expenses for ISO certification, patent registration, and participation in domestic and international trade fairs. Support is disbursed through designated nodal agencies or banks after claim verification, with applications processed on a rolling basis via the portal: https://upmsme.in.
