### UP MSME and Export Promotion Council Schemes
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Uttar Pradesh
- **Administering Body:** Uttar Pradesh MSME and Export Promotion Council
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://upmsme.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Micro, small, and medium enterprises as defined under the MSMED Act, 2006
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Manufacturing or service activities
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have registered office or operational unit in Uttar Pradesh; must possess valid Udyam Registration; specific sub-schemes may have additional criteria related to export turnover, investment in plant and machinery, or sector-specific focus

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support varies across sub-schemes; includes capital subsidy up to a certain percentage of investment in plant and machinery, interest subsidy on bank loans, and reimbursement of expenses for quality certification and export promotion activities
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit through designated nodal agencies or banks after verification of claims
- Duration of benefit: One-time (based on incurring expenditure and claim submission)

#### Documents Required
1. Udyam Registration Certificate
2. Aadhaar and PAN of the proprietor/partners/directors
3. Certificate of Incorporation / Partnership Deed / MSME Registration
4. Project Report or Business Plan
5. Quotations for plant and machinery (if applicable)
6. Bank sanction letter for term loan (for interest subsidy claims)
7. Proof of export realization (for export promotion schemes)
8. ISO or other certification certificates (for reimbursement claims)
9. Details of bank account
10. Address proof of the business unit

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the relevant sub-scheme under UP MSME and Export Promotion Council based on enterprise needs.
2. Ensure eligibility and obtain Udyam Registration.
3. Prepare the required documents as per the sub-scheme guidelines.
4. Submit the application either online through the designated portal or offline to the concerned district industry center or nodal agency.
5. Application undergoes scrutiny and verification by the implementing agency.
6. Upon approval, financial assistance is disbursed as per the scheme norms, either directly to the beneficiary or through the lending institution.
