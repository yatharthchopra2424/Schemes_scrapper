# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Manufacturing or Service MSME (as per MSMED Act)
- [ ] Valid Udyam Registration Certificate
- [ ] Not engaged in trading activities (trading not allowed under this scheme)
- [ ] Maximum exposure limits: Manufacturing unit ≤ Rs. 1000 Lakhs; Service unit ≤ Rs. 600 Lakhs; Infrastructure unit ≤ Rs. 800 Lakhs (individual) or ≤ Rs. 1000 Lakhs (group of 2+)
- [ ] Group exposure ceiling: Manufacturing ≤ Rs. 2000 Lakhs; Service ≤ Rs. 1500 Lakhs; Infrastructure ≤ Rs. 1000 Lakhs for associate/sister concerns
- [ ] Unit must not be in CIBIL/RBI defaulters list (undertaking required)
- [ ] Bank Guarantee from approved bank must be available as security
- [ ] Credit period: 180 days (extendable on request with Zonal Head consent, subject to conditions)
- [ ] Valid GST Registration Certificate
- [ ] Audited/Provisional financial statements (last year audited, provisional current year, or current year estimates for startups)
- [ ] Bank statement of unit for last six months
- [ ] Copy of latest Electricity Bill
- [ ] Conduct Report of Account with Banks (other than BG issuing Bank) and Financial Institutions
- [ ] Copy of orders in hand (if enhancement of limit beyond Rs. 5 crores is sought)

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN Card (self-attested)
- Udyam Registration Certificate (self-attested)
- GST Registration Certificate (self-attested)
- Audited/Provisional financial statements (last year audited, provisional current year, or current year estimates for startups)
- Bank statement of unit for last six months
- Latest Electricity Bill (self-attested copy)
- Passport size photograph of each Proprietor/Director/Partner/Society office bearer
- Valid self-attested Officially Valid Documents (OVD) for identity and address proof (PAN, Aadhar, Passport, Driving License, Voter ID, etc.)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Self-attested statement of personal assets and liabilities along with residential address of Proprietor/Directors/Partners/Society office bearers
- Copy of Board Resolution (for Pvt./Public Ltd. Co.), Power of Attorney (for partnership), or Governing Body Resolution (for Society)
- Specimen signatures of authorized signatory attested by bank
- Copy of sanction letter for credit limit sanctioned by FIs/banks
- Conduct Report of Account of the unit with Banks (other than BG issuing Bank) and Financial Institutions
- Certificate/undertaking that the unit’s name(s) do not fall in CIBIL/RBI defaulters list
- Copy of orders in hand (if enhancement of limit beyond five crores is sought)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be self-attested where required
- File size: Each document < 5 MB (recommended for portal upload)
- File naming: Use format `[DocumentType]_[ApplicantName]_[Date].pdf` (e.g., `UdyamCert_ABCPvtLtd_20250405.pdf`)
- Language: English or Hindi; if regional language, attach certified translation
- Photographs: Passport size, clear background, no borders
- Bank attestation: Specimen signatures must be attested by the bank with seal and signature
- Financial statements: Must be signed by authorized person with seal; for CA-certified documents, UDIN must be visible
- Electricity Bill: Latest copy, not older than 3 months
- Bank statements: Last six months, clearly showing account details and transactions

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for this scheme. The application form captures all necessary details. However, if enhancement of limit beyond Rs. 5 crores is sought, a copy of orders in hand must be submitted. Verify with nodal officer if additional project details are needed for high-value cases.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the application form from NSIC’s website (https://nsic.co.in/Schemes/RawMaterialAgainstBG) or obtain it from the nearest branch office.  
2. Fill **all fields** – no column/field should be left blank (as per NSIC instructions).  
3. Use **Capital Letters** for the name of the signatory.  
4. Provide **complete bank details**: Name of bank, address, account number, telephone number for all banker accounts.  
5. Clearly specify **Amount of assistance sought (Rs.)** in the designated field.  
6. Under “Details of Security proposed”, specify **Bank Guarantee** value, issuing bank name, address, and telephone number.  
7. For “Personal Guarantees”, provide name, address, net worth, and e-filing acknowledgement number of guarantors.  
8. Declare whether unit is located in a backward/hilly region and whether it belongs to SC/ST/Women entrepreneur/Physically Handicapped/Ex-Servicemen/Technocrat (tick marks).  
9. List **Names and addresses of Sister/Associate/Group Account Concerns** if applicable.  
10. Provide **Particulars of Proprietor/Partners/Directors/society office bearers** including name, father’s/husband’s name, address, age, qualification, business experience, PAN, Aadhar number, telephone/mobile, email, net worth, and IP address of ITR filed.  
11. Specify **Line of Activity** and **Nature of Project** with products being manufactured.  
12. State **Annual Requirement of Raw Materials** with material name, manufacturer/supplier, quantity, and value (Rs.).  
13. Detail **Particulars of financial assistance already received or likely to be received from Banks/FIs**, including institution name, amount, reference number, sanction date, repayment position, and any defaults/overdues.  
14. Answer B1 and B2: Whether you/your sister/associate concern have availed/availing assistance from any NSIC branch/NTSC under RMA against BG; if yes, provide complete details (branch/NTSC name, unit name, scheme, sanctioned limit, date of sanction, present status w.r.t. outstanding dues).  
15. State **Employment generated by the unit** (Total nos. of person): Existing, Additional, Total.  
16. Provide **Any other information** in the final field.  
17. Sign and date the declaration with official stamp of authorized signatory.  
18. Submit **two copies** of the completed application form (one for acknowledgement).

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the duly filled application form (two copies) along with all prescribed documents to the **nearest NSIC branch office**.  
- Obtain an **acknowledgement receipt** for one copy of the application form (to be returned by NSIC).  
- **Screenshot** the acknowledgement receipt (if digital) or scan/photograph the physical receipt.  
- Store the **application reference number** (if provided) or acknowledgement details securely.  
- Set follow-up reminders:  
  - Day 25: Check status if no update received  
  - Day 30: Escalate if no response (Level 1)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the **approval letter** from NSIC specifying the sanctioned limit  
- [ ] Confirm the **sanctioned amount** matches the requested assistance (up to 95% of BG value)  
- [ ] Sign the **agreement** between NSIC and the unit as per protocol  
- [ ] Ensure **Bank Guarantee** remains valid for the sanctioned period (180 days, extendable)  
- [ ] Track **utilization** of the credit limit for raw material procurement  
- [ ] Maintain records of **invoices** submitted to NSIC for payment release to suppliers  
- [ ] Monitor repayment schedule to avoid penal charges (1.25% per quarter beyond 180 days)  
- [ ] Apply for **renewal** one year before expiry if conduct of account was satisfactory (requires renewal documents: request letter, latest bank sanction letter, audited annual accounts, etc.)  
- [ ] Coordinate with NSIC for **disbursement** to suppliers as requested by the MSME  
- [ ] Verify that **processing fee** (1.0% p.a. on new sanctions, 0.5%/1.0% p.a. on renewal) is accounted for  
- [ ] Ensure **interest is serviced** during the credit period to retain concessional rates (if applicable based on SME rating)  
- [ ] Retain all correspondence and documents for audit and compliance purposes  
- [ ] Inform client of **success fee** invoice from consultancy firm (as per engagement terms)
