## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees aligned with the financial benefit delivered—NSIC Raw Material Assistance Scheme provides credit support up to 95% of Bank Guarantee value, with maximum exposure of ₹1000 lakhs for manufacturing units and ₹600 lakhs for service units.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect processing risk; scheme has rolling basis approvals, 180-day credit period, and documented success in facilitating raw material procurement against BG, reducing non-payment risk.
3. **Transparency**: All fees disclosed upfront; retainer covers application processing, document preparation, and liaison with NSIC branches.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The scheme’s processing involves preliminary appraisal, unit inspection, and sanction—typically 4–6 weeks based on NSIC’s standard workflow—supporting a 3-month lag in revenue recognition for success fees.
