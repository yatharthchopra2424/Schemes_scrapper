### NSIC Raw Material Assistance Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Small Industries Corporation Ltd. (NSIC)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nsic.co.in/Schemes/RawMaterialAgainstBG
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Manufacturing/Service MSME
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Manufacturing/Service
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have Udyam Registration Certificate

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Maximum assistance up to 95% of the Bank Guarantee (BG) value; maximum exposure to a single unit is Rs. 1000 Lakhs for manufacturing units and Rs. 600 lakhs for service units
- Disbursement mode: Payment released to the supplier as requested by the MSME (not directly to the MSME)
- Duration of benefit: Credit period of 180 days, extendable on request with consent of Zonal Head

#### Documents Required
1. Passport size photograph of each Proprietor/Director/Partner/Society office bearer
2. Valid self-attested Officially Valid Documents (OVD) for identity and address proof
3. Self-attested copies of Udyam Registration Certificate
4. Self-attested copy of GST Registration Certificate
5. Self-attested statement of personal assets and liabilities along with residential address
6. Copy of Board Resolution (for Pvt./Public Ltd. Co.), Power of Attorney (for partnership), or Governing Body Resolution (for Society)
7. Specimen signatures of authorized signatory attested by bank
8. Copy of sanction letter for credit limit sanctioned by FIs/banks
9. Audited/Provisional financial statements of the unit (last year audited, provisional current year, or current year estimates for startups)
10. Bank statement of the unit for the last six months
11. Copy of the latest Electricity Bill
12. Conduct Report of Account of the unit with Banks (other than BG issuing Bank) and Financial Institutions
13. Certificate/undertaking that the unit’s name(s) do not fall in CIBIL/RBI defaulters list
14. Copy of orders in hand (if enhancement of limit beyond five crores is sought)

#### Application Process
1. Download the application form from NSIC’s website or obtain it from the nearest branch office.
2. Fill the application form with required details.
3. Submit the duly filled application form along with prescribed documents to the nearest NSIC branch office.
4. Preliminary appraisal and unit inspection are carried out by NSIC.
5. Sanction of limit to the unit is granted.
6. Agreement is signed between NSIC and the unit.
7. Assistance is disbursed to the unit.
