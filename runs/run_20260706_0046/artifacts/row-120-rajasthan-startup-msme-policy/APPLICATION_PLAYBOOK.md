## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate / Partnership Deed / LLP Agreement  
- PAN Card of the business entity  
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- Udyam Registration Certificate  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the business for the last 2 financial years  
- Aadhaar and PAN of authorized signatory/promoters  
- Address proof of business premises (electricity bill, rent agreement, or property tax receipt)  
- Canceled cheque or bank passbook copy for account verification  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining business model, innovation, scalability, and job creation potential  
- Proof of innovation or technology adoption (patents, prototypes, software registration, etc.)  
- Certificate of recognition from an approved incubator or accelerator (if applicable)  
- Audited financial statements or CA-certified turnover and employment details  
- Declaration of non-availment of similar capital or interest subsidy from other state/central schemes  
- Board resolution authorizing application for the Rajasthan Startup & MSME Policy benefits  
- Sector-specific licenses or certifications (e.g., FSSAI for food, PCB clearance for manufacturing)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (minimum 300 DPI)  
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ClientName_Date.pdf` (e.g., `Rajasthan_Startup_Policy_Udyam_ClientX_20240510.pdf`)  
- Scanned copies must be certified true copies by a gazetted officer or CA/CS (where required)  
- Documents in languages other than English or Hindi must be accompanied by a notarized translation  
- No document should exceed 5 MB in size; compress if necessary without losing clarity  
- Dates on documents must be current (e.g., bank statements not older than 30 days)  
- Udyam, PAN, and GST details must match exactly across all submissions

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
✅ **Required**  
The Detailed Project Report (DPR) is a mandatory scheme-specific document for the Rajasthan Startup & MSME Policy.  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
1. Executive Summary  
2. Promoter Profile & Background  
3. Business Model & Value Proposition  
4. Product/Service Description & Innovation Quotient  
5. Market Analysis & Go-to-Market Strategy  
6. Technology Used & Intellectual Property Status (if any)  
7. Operational Plan (manufacturing/service delivery)  
8. Organizational Structure & Team  
9. Financial Projections (5-year): Revenue, Expenses, Profitability, Break-even  
10. Funding Requirements & Utilization Plan  
11. Employment Generation Plan (direct & indirect)  
12. Risk Analysis & Mitigation Strategy  
13. Social Impact & Sustainability Aspects  
14. Timeline for Implementation (Gantt Chart)  
15. Annexures: Certificates, licenses, prototype images, letters of intent  

> **Note**: The DPR must emphasize innovation, scalability, and job creation — key focus areas of the Rajasthan Startup & MSME Policy.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official portal via Rajasthan Single Sign-On (SSO) or the Department of Industries website (verify current URL)  
2. Select “Rajasthan Startup & MSME Policy” from the list of active schemes  
3. Fill all fields in English; avoid abbreviations unless officially recognized  
4. Ensure Udyam, PAN, and GST numbers match exactly across form and documents  
5. In the “Innovation Details” section, clearly describe the novel aspect of your product/service/process  
6. Specify employment generation numbers (current and projected) with justification  
7. Declare any availed benefits from other central/state schemes to avoid duplication  
8. Attach all documents in the prescribed order and format as per portal instructions  
9. Preview the application before final submission to check for errors or missing fields  
10. Do not submit until all scheme-specific documents (especially DPR and innovation proof) are attached
