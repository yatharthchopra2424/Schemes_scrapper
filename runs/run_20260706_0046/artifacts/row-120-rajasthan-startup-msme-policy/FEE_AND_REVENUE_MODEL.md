1. **Value-based alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the financial benefit delivered to the client, ensuring fairness and transparency.
2. **Risk-adjusted pricing**: Success fees account for scheme-specific risks, including application complexity and approval uncertainty.
3. **Tiered accessibility**: Retainer pricing scales with client size and capacity to pay, enabling broad market coverage.
