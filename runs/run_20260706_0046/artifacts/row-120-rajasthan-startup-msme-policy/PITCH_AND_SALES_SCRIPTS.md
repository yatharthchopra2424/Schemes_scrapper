## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping startups and MSMEs in Rajasthan access government support designed to accelerate early-stage growth. The Rajasthan Startup & MSME Policy offers targeted incentives for new and scaling businesses in the state, and I’d like to understand if your venture might qualify for this support.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your business incorporated or registered in Rajasthan?
2. Are you currently operating as a startup or MSME within Rajasthan?
3. What sector or industry is your business active in (e.g., manufacturing, services, IT, agro-processing)?
4. Have you previously applied for any state-level startup or MSME incentives in Rajasthan?
5. What are your current growth objectives over the next 12–24 months (e.g., scaling operations, hiring, R&D, market expansion)?
6. Are you seeking financial support, infrastructure access, or policy-based incentives to achieve these goals?
7. Do you have audited financial statements or Udyam/MSME registration documents available?
8. Are you looking for non-dilutive funding or subsidies that do not require equity dilution?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is incorporated or registered in Rajasthan  
- [ ] Entity qualifies as a startup or MSME under state definitions  
- [ ] Operational for less than 10 years (if startup criteria apply)  
- [ ] Engaged in eligible sectors (manufacturing, services, IT, agro-processing, etc.)  
- [ ] Possesses Udyam/MSME registration or equivalent  
- [ ] Has not exceeded applicable turnover or investment thresholds for MSME/startup status  
- [ ] Seeking support for growth, innovation, or job creation  
- [ ] Capable of providing required documentation (incorporation, financials, project report)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your venture may align with the Rajasthan Startup & MSME Policy’s target beneficiaries. This scheme provides financial and infrastructural support to eligible startups and MSMEs in Rajasthan to help scale operations and innovation. I can walk you through how it works in just a few minutes.
