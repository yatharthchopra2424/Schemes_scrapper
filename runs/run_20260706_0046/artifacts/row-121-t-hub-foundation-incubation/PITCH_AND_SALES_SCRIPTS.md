# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
I specialize in helping technology startups in Telangana access world-class incubation support, mentorship, and funding pathways through T-Hub Foundation—the world’s largest home for startups. I’d like to understand your startup’s current stage and goals to see if T-Hub’s programs align with your needs.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup legally incorporated? (T-Hub requires incorporation ≤ 10 years)
2. Is your startup developing a technology-based product, platform, or IP-led service? (Core eligibility: must be tech-driven, not pure service-based)
3. Are you recognized as a DPIIT-registered startup or eligible for such recognition?
4. What proof of traction do you currently have? (e.g., MVP/prototype, paying customers/revenue, external funding, active pilots)
5. Which sector does your startup operate in? (T-Hub has sector-specific programs: semiconductor, space-tech, healthcare, mobility, AI/ML, sustainability, etc.)
6. Are you seeking access to mentorship, investor networks, or co-working infrastructure?
7. Have you explored central or state government funding schemes like SISFS, T-Fund, or T-Spark?
8. What is your primary goal for the next 6–12 months? (e.g., product development, fundraising, market access)

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Legally incorporated entity developing and commercializing technology-based products, platforms, or IP-led services
- [ ] Recognized as DPIIT-registered startup or eligible for such recognition
- [ ] Core offering is technology-based (software, hardware, deep-tech, or IP-led platform) — not pure service-based
- [ ] Demonstrated proof of traction: MVP/prototype, paying customers/revenue, external funding, or active pilots
- [ ] Incorporated ≤ 10 years from date of registration
- [ ] Operating in a technology sector aligned with T-Hub’s focus (e.g., AI/ML, semiconductor, healthcare, mobility, space-tech)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like T-Hub Foundation’s incubation programs could be a strong fit—particularly given their access to ₹25 lakhs to ₹1 crore in funding via T-Fund, SISFS support, and sector-specific accelerators. Let me walk you through how it works.
