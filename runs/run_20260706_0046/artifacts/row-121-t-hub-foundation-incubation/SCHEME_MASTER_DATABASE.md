### T-Hub Foundation Incubation
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Incubation
- **Geography:** Telangana (with national and international reach)
- **Administering Body:** T-Hub Foundation (a Section 8 not-for-profit organization)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://t-hub.co/programs
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Varies by program (e.g., AIC Semiconductor: applications closure in Jan 2024; Healthcare: applications closure 28th Feb 2026; Mobility: last date of application 7th June 2025)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Legally incorporated entity developing and commercializing technology-based products, platforms, or IP-led services that drive innovation, scalability, and impact
- Age of business: Incorporated ≤ 10 years from the date of registration
- Sector: Technology-based (software, hardware, deep-tech, or IP-led platform); sector-specific programs available in semiconductor, space-tech, healthcare, mobility, sustainability, AI/ML, and more
- Revenue cap: Not explicitly mentioned in evidence
- Employee count: Not mentioned
- Other: Must be recognised as a DPIIT-registered startup or eligible for such recognition; must demonstrate proof of traction in at least one of the following forms: MVP or functional prototype, paying customers or revenue, external funding (angel, VC, or grant), active pilots with institutions, corporates, or Government; not a pure service-based entity (e.g., consulting, outsourcing, staffing, training, or agency-type business)

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Access to 290+ mentors, 80+ value partners, 200+ MNCs and corporates, 20+ academic partners, 46+ international partners, 100+ investors, angels, and HNIs; community access to 50+ state and Govt of India institutions; access to T-Hub Value Providers Desk, all T-Hub events and conferences, T-Hub and Atal Innovation Mission network, tech and research facilities from global universities and partners; information regarding govt grants, funds, and incentives; network with investment networks, HNIs, and banks; sector-specific programs in semiconductor, space-tech, healthcare, mobility, sustainability, AI/ML, and more; access to co-working spaces, legal, financial, IPR, procurement, and HR support via T-Edge; mentorship, investor networking, market access, and global partnership opportunities; T-Fund provides ₹25 lakhs to ₹1 crore in funding for early-stage tech startups; Startup India Seed Fund (SISF) supports young tech startups in their first two years with funding for PoC, prototyping, and early market entry; SIDBI IDEX Fund managed by T-Hub
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement / Direct credit (varies by scheme; T-Hub facilitates access to central and state government schemes)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing (program-based; e.g., program fee for sector-specific programs is one-time)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. Proof of traction (MVP, prototype, revenue, funding, or pilot details)
4. Founder details and team background
5. Business model or pitch deck
6. Authorization letter from authorized signatory
7. Details of funding received (if any)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the relevant program (e.g., Foundation, AIC Semiconductor, SpaceTech, Healthcare, Mobility, etc.) based on startup stage and sector
2. Visit the specific program page on T-Hub website (e.g., https://t-hub.co/aic-semiconductors)
3. Check eligibility criteria and program schedule
4. Submit application via the provided form or contact email (e.g., aic@t-hub.co)
5. Await selection communication from T-Hub team
6. Upon selection, complete onboarding and pay applicable program fee (if any)
7. Begin participation in the program with access to mentorship, resources, and ecosystem benefits
