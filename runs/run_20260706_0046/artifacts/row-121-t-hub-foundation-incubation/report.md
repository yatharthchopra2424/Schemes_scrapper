# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
T-Hub Foundation Incubation is a state-level incubation scheme implemented by the T-Hub Foundation, a Section 8 not-for-profit organization based in Telangana. It serves as the world's largest home for startups, democratizing access for 10,000+ startups and providing comprehensive support from ideation to scale through programs, partnerships, and global access. The scheme anchors several national initiatives including the AIC T-Hub Foundation (India's leading Atal Incubation Centre), MATH (Centre of Excellence for AI/ML), India's first Google for Startups Hub, and the largest incubator for the defence open-innovation platform iDEX.

### Objectives
- Empower founders with access, resources, and allies to build world-class startups from India for the World
- Support founders from ideation to scale through programs, partnerships, and global access
- Anchor the AIC T-Hub Foundation, India’s leading Atal Incubation Centre within a national network of 70+ AICs
- Host India’s largest incubator for the defence open-innovation platform iDEX
- Host MATH, India’s leading Centre of Excellence for AI and ML supported by the Department of Science and Technology
- Host India’s first Google for Startups Hub
- Provide access to world-class infrastructure spanning over 572,000 square feet
- Strengthen founders to build and scale startups from Telangana for India and the world

### Critical Caveats & Warnings
> **Program Fees Apply**: Sector-specific programs (Semiconductor, SpaceTech, Healthcare, Mobility) charge INR 50,000 + GST but take **zero equity** in startups.
> 
> **Physical Presence Required**: Some programs require physical attendance for launch and closure events (hybrid model).
> 
> **Eligibility Varies by Program**: Healthcare program requires minimum MRR of ₹1 lakh and growth/acceleration stage; Semiconductor prefers MVP/POC with traction.
> 
> **Google for Startups Hub Pass**: Valid for six months, subject to renewal based on engagement; strictly for AI-first founders/co-founders only.
> 
> **Value Partner Offers**: Subject to terms like one-time use per startup and eligibility verification (e.g., Abda Digital: 10 free smart cards max per startup).
> 
> **No Equity Taken**: T-Hub does not take any stake/equity in startups under any program.
> 
> **Documentation Required**: Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, proof of traction, founder details, business model/pitch deck, authorization letter, funding details (if any).
