# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Women entrepreneur(s) hold at least 51% stake in the business  
- [ ] Business is legally registered in India (Certificate of Incorporation/Registration available)  
- [ ] For **WE Reach**: Applicant is a woman or group of 2-3 women from Tier 2/Tier 3 regions of Telangana seeking to set up a for-profit enterprise or formalize an informal enterprise  
- [ ] For **Project Inclusion**: Registered enterprise in operation for at least 2 years; woman holds ≥51% stake; annual turnover between INR 5 lakhs – 10 lakhs (relaxable for Handicrafts sector); operates from commercial premises (home-based units not encouraged); full-time engagement by woman entrepreneur  
- [ ] For **WE Leads**: Woman entrepreneur owns ≥51% of a registered business operational for at least 2 years; serves as executive decision-maker; demonstrates scalability and growth potential; works ≥3 days/week for the business  
- [ ] Business falls within target sectors: Manufacturing, Food Processing, FMCG, Textiles & Handlooms, Handicrafts (for Project Inclusion); or other sectors as per program focus (e.g., tech, services for Urban Innovation programs)  
- [ ] Applicant is not engaged only part-time in the enterprise (disqualifying for incubation/acceleration programs)  
- [ ] Applicant has access to required documents (see Stage 2)  
- [ ] Applicant understands that WE Hub facilitates funding linkages but does not directly disburse grants or loans  

> **Sources**:  
> - Eligibility criteria from KEY FACTS and SIE program pages (WE Reach, Project Inclusion, WE Leads)  
> - Geographic scope: Telangana (KEY FACTS)  
> - Program-specific details: https://wehub.telangana.gov.in/sie/

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation / Business Registration  
- [ ] PAN Card of the business entity  
- [ ] GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- [ ] Udyam Registration / MSME Certificate  
- [ ] Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- [ ] Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the business (last 2 years, if applicable)  
- [ ] Aadhaar and PAN of authorized signatory  
- [ ] Passport-sized photographs of applicant(s)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- [ ] Pitch deck or detailed business description (mandatory for all programs)  
- [ ] Details of shareholding pattern to confirm ≥51% women ownership  
- [ ] Proof of turnover (audited financial statements, GST returns, or sales invoices) — required for Project Inclusion and WE Leads  
- [ ] Proof of business operation duration (e.g., old invoices, registration certificate) — required for Project Inclusion (≥2 years) and WE Leads (≥2 years)  
- [ ] Authorization letter from authorized signatory  
- [ ] Details of prior funding received (if any)  
- [ ] For Project Inclusion: Confirmation of commercial premises (rent agreement/ownership documents)  
- [ ] For WE Reach: Self-declaration of residence in Tier 2/Tier 3 Telangana region  
- [ ] For WE Leads: Evidence of scalability (e.g., growth projections, market analysis, team structure)  

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are clear, legible scans (PDF preferred)  
- [ ] File size < 5 MB per document  
- [ ] Document names match content (e.g., "PAN_Card_ABC_Private_Limited.pdf")  
- [ ] All certificates are valid and not expired  
- [ ] English language preferred; if in regional language, attach certified translation  
- [ ] PAN, GST, Udyam numbers match across documents  
- [ ] Bank statements show consistent business transactions  
- [ ] Authorization letter is on company letterhead and signed

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- **DPR Required?** Not explicitly required for WE Hub programs.  
- However, a **detailed pitch deck or business description** is mandatory for all programs (see Stage 2).  
- If applying for funding linkages (e.g., via PMEGP or other schemes supported by WE Hub), a DPR may be needed — **verify with nodal officer** based on target funding source.  

> **Sources**:  
> - Required documents list (KEY FACTS and SIE page) specifies pitch deck/business description, not DPR  
> - Examples of funded ventures (e.g., PMEGP loan) suggest DPR may be needed for external loans  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Download or request the application form** from the program coordinator via email (forms are not publicly hosted; issued upon inquiry).  
2. **Fill all fields in block letters** to avoid misinterpretation.  
3. **Ensure woman-owned stakeholding is clearly stated** (≥51%) in the ownership details section.  
4. **Match turnover and operation duration exactly** with supporting documents (e.g., if claiming INR 7.5 lakhs turnover, attach audited proof).  
5. **Specify the exact program** you are applying for (WE Reach / Project Inclusion / WE Leads) in the application header.  
6. **Declare any prior funding received** transparently; non-disclosure may lead to rejection.  
7. **Authorized signatory must sign and date** the form; attach authorization letter if signing on behalf of a company.  
8. **Double-check email address and contact details** before submission to ensure communication is not lost.  

> **Sources**:  
> - Application process: “Apply via the designated email address for the program” (KEY FACTS)  
> - Form-filling best practices adapted from government scheme standards  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All personal and business details filled correctly  
- [ ] Woman’s stakeholding ≥51% clearly documented and declared  
- [ ] Selected program (WE Reach / Project Inclusion / WE Leads) matches eligibility  
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents attached (pitch deck, stakeholding proof, turnover proof, etc.)  
- [ ] Authorization letter attached if applicant is not the signatory  
- [ ] No blank mandatory fields  
- [ ] Declarations signed (if applicable)  
- [ ] File names are professional and consistent  
- [ ] Application emailed to correct program-specific ID (fo-sie-wehub@telangana.gov.in or pm-wehub@telangana.gov.in)  
- [ ] Read receipt or delivery confirmation requested

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Take a **screenshot of the sent email** showing:  
  - Recipient (correct program email)  
  - Subject line (e.g., “Application for Project Inclusion – [Business Name]”)  
  - Attachments list  
  - Timestamp  
- Save the **sent email** in a dedicated folder labeled:  
  `[Case ID]_WEHub_[Program]_Submission_[YYYYMMDD]`  
- Note any **auto-reply or acknowledgment** from WE Hub as the application reference  
- If no acknowledgment within 3 working days, follow up via phone or email  
- Set a **follow-up reminder for Day 25** to check on application status

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and save the **approval letter or selection email** from WE Hub  
- [ ] Confirm **program start date, duration, and mode** (online/offline/hybrid)  
- [ ] Attend **orientation or onboarding session** as scheduled  
- [ ] If seeking funding linkages (e.g., PMEGP, angel investment):  
  - [ ] Work with WE Hub mentors to refine pitch deck and business plan  
  - [ ] Apply for external grants/loans with WE Hub’s recommendation letter  
  - [ ] Track disbursement of funds (if any)  
- [ ] Maintain **attendance and participation records** for internal reporting  
- [ ] Prepare for **mid-term and final presentations** (if applicable)  
- [ ] Upon completion, obtain **program completion certificate**  
- [ ] Submit **utilization certificate or outcome report** if required by WE Hub or funding partners  
- [ ] Provide **feedback or testimonial** to WE Hub for their records  
- [ ] Follow up on **post-program support** (alumni network, mentorship, networking events)  
- [ ] If applicable, coordinate with WE Hub for **success fee invoicing** (only if pre-agreed in consulting engagement)  

> **Sources**:  
> - Program details (duration, outcomes): SIE page (WE Reach: 3 months, Project Inclusion: 6 months, WE Leads: 4 months)  
> - Post-approval actions: Standard incubation/acceleration program flow  
> - Examples of outcomes: PMEGP loan facilitation, awards, market readiness (from SIE page testimonials)  
> - Funding facilitation: KEY FACTS (access to grants, seed funding, VC, recoverable grants)
