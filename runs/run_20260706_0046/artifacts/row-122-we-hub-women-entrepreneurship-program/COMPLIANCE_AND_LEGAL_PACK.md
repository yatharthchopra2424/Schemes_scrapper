## 8D. Internal Data Handling Policy

All client data shall be handled in accordance with the following standards:
- Encrypted cloud storage with AES-256 encryption
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) required for all system access
- Access limited to authorized personnel only on a need-to-know basis
- Prohibition on use of personal devices for storing or processing client data
- Data retention for 2 years post case closure
- 24-hour breach notification protocol to affected clients and relevant authorities

**Scheme-specific note:** All documents submitted to Government of Telangana under WE-Hub Women Entrepreneurship Program must be retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit purposes.
