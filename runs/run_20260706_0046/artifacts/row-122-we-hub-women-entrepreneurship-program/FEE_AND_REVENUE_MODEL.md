## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit delivered — for WE-Hub Women Entrepreneurship Program, this includes facilitation of external funding such as PMEGP loans (e.g., ₹3.10 lakh) and linkages to grants, seed funding, angel investors, venture capitalists, and recoverable grants like the USD 2.5 crores from USAID via Samridh Healthcare Blended Finance Facility.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s facilitative (non-disbursing) nature — WE Hub does not directly provide grants or loans but enables access through partnerships, introducing moderate risk in outcome realization; thus, success fees are set in the mid-range (6–8% for Growth tier) to balance effort and uncertainty.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Pricing scales with client capacity — the scheme primarily targets women entrepreneurs with turnover between INR 5 lakhs – 10 lakhs per annum (Project Inclusion) or early-stage ventures (WE Reach), aligning with the Growth tier (MSME/Startup) for retainer and success fee applicability.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainer covers advisory work (eligibility assessment, documentation, application support), while success fee is contingent on measurable outcomes like loan sanction or funding linkage, verified via client disclosures or program reports.
