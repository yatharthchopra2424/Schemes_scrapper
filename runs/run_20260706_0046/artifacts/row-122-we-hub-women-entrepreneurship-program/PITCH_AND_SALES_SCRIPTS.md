# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping women entrepreneurs in Telangana access government-backed support programs like the WE-Hub Women Entrepreneurship Program. I’d love to understand your current stage and goals to see if WE Hub’s incubation, pre-incubation, or acceleration programs could be a fit for you — all designed to provide mentorship, funding linkages, and compliance support without requiring you to navigate it alone.

### Discovery Questions
- What stage is your business at — idea, early-stage, or already operating for 2+ years?
- Are you based in a Tier 2 or Tier 3 region of Telangana, or are you open to relocating or operating from there?
- Do you currently hold at least 51% ownership in your business, and is it legally registered in India?
- Are you looking for support with mentorship, funding access, compliance, or market linkages?
- For existing businesses: what is your current annual turnover, and how long have you been operational?
- Are you able to commit to a full-time engagement in your business during the program period?
- Have you previously applied to any WE Hub programs (e.g., WE Reach, Project Inclusion, WE Leads)?
- What kind of support would move the needle most for your business right now — skill development, investor access, or formalization (Udyam/MSME)?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Woman entrepreneur holding ≥51% stake in the business  
- [ ] Business legally registered in India  
- [ ] For WE Reach: applicant is a woman or group of 2–3 women from Tier 2/Tier 3 Telangana seeking to formalize or launch a for-profit enterprise  
- [ ] For Project Inclusion: registered enterprise operational for ≥2 years, turnover ₹5–10 lakhs p.a. (relaxable for Handicrafts), commercial premises, full-time engagement  
- [ ] For WE Leads: business operational ≥2 years, woman as executive decision-maker, scalable model, commitment to innovation  
- [ ] Not a home-based unit (for Project Inclusion eligibility)  
- [ ] Able to provide required documents: incorporation, PAN, pitch deck, stakeholding proof, turnover proof, authorization letter  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like the WE-Hub Women Entrepreneurship Program — specifically [WE Reach/Project Inclusion/WE Leads, as applicable] — could be a strong fit. This Telangana government initiative offers tailored incubation, mentorship, and funding linkages to help women-led ventures like yours start, scale, and sustain — without upfront costs for most programs.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Women entrepreneurs in Telangana often struggle to access structured mentorship, formalize their businesses, connect with investors, or navigate compliance — especially when operating from Tier 2/Tier 3 regions or informal setups. These barriers limit growth, sustainability, and access to formal funding channels like PMEGP or angel investment.

**Scheme intro:**
The WE-Hub Women Entrepreneurship Program, implemented by the Government of Telangana, offers tiered support through programs like WE Reach (pre-incubation), Project Inclusion (incubation), and WE Leads (acceleration). It provides business mentorship, compliance and tax assistance, funding linkages, sector-specific guidance, and networking opportunities — all designed to de-risk the entrepreneurial journey for women. While WE Hub does not directly disburse grants, it facilitates access to recoverable grants (e.g., USD 2.5 crores via USAID), seed funding, angel investors, venture capitalists, and PMEGP loans (e.g., ₹3.10 lakh secured by past participants).

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a woman entrepreneur holding at least 51% stake in your business  
- Your venture is legally registered in India  
- [Depending on program: e.g., for Project Inclusion] You’ve been operational for 2+ years with turnover between ₹5–10 lakhs p.a. and operate from commercial premises  
- You are based in or willing to operate from Tier 2/Tier 3 regions of Telangana (for WE Reach/Project Inclusion)
