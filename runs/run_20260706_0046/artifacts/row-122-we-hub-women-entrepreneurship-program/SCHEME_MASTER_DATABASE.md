### WE-Hub Women Entrepreneurship Program
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Incubation
- **Geography:** Telangana
- **Administering Body:** Government of Telangana
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://wehub.telangana.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Programs operate in cohorts (e.g., 4 cohorts per year for WE Reach) with specific intake windows; applicants advised to check with program coordinators for upcoming cycles.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: For-profit enterprise or informal enterprise seeking formalization
- Age of business: Varies by program (e.g., pre-incubation: idea stage; incubation: 2+ years operation; acceleration: 2+ years operation)
- Sector: Varies by program (e.g., Project Inclusion focuses on Manufacturing, Food Processing, FMCG, Textiles & Handlooms, Handicrafts)
- Revenue cap: Varies by program (e.g., Project Inclusion: turnover between INR 5 lakhs – 10 lakhs per annum)
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Women entrepreneurs must hold at least 51% stake in their business; business must be legally registered in India; program-specific criteria apply (e.g., geographic restrictions to Tier 2/Tier 3 regions for WE Reach; home-based units not encouraged for certain programs; part-time engagement may disqualify applicants)

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Access to business consultation, entrepreneurial and management courses, business mentorship, marketing assistance, compliance and tax assistance, funding and financial assistance guidance, community and networking opportunities, sector-specific mentoring, skill enhancement programs, internship and live project opportunities, patenting and IP support, credit linkage, Udyam and MSME registration support, participation in hackathons and exposure visits; facilitation of external funding (e.g., PMEGP loans, recoverable grants such as USD 2.5 crores from USAID via Samridh Healthcare Blended Finance Facility)
- Disbursement mode: Facilitative (guidance and linkages to funding sources; no direct disbursement of grants or loans by WE Hub)
- Duration of benefit: Program-based (e.g., WE Reach: 3 months, Project Inclusion: 6 months, WE Leads: 4 months)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. Pitch deck or business description
4. Details of stakeholding (to confirm ≥51% women ownership)
5. Proof of turnover or business operation duration (if applicable)
6. Authorization letter from authorized signatory
7. Details of prior funding received (if any)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the suitable program based on business stage (e.g., WE Reach for pre-incubation, Project Inclusion for incubation, WE Leads for acceleration).
2. Check eligibility criteria specific to the program (e.g., geographic, turnover, stakeholding requirements).
3. Prepare required documents including business registration, PAN, pitch deck, and stakeholder details.
4. Apply via the designated email address for the program (e.g., fo-sie-wehub@telangana.gov.in for SIE programs, pm-wehub@telangana.gov.in for Project Ujaagar).
5. Await confirmation and further instructions from the WE Hub team regarding selection and onboarding.
