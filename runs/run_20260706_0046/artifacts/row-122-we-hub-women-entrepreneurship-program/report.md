# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The WE-Hub Women Entrepreneurship Program is a state-level initiative by the Government of Telangana designed to empower women entrepreneurs through incubation, acceleration, and skill development programs. It operates under the Social Impact Entrepreneurship (SIE) vertical and offers tiered programs tailored to different business stages: pre-incubation (WE Reach), incubation (Project Inclusion), and acceleration (WE Leads). The scheme does not directly disburse grants or loans but facilitates access to funding through partnerships, including recoverable grants, angel investors, venture capitalists, and government-linked schemes like PMEGP.

### Objectives
- Build a powerful community of 1 million women entrepreneurs across India  
- Transform the entrepreneurial landscape by fostering innovation and leadership among women  
- Counter biases and stereotypes faced by women entrepreneurs  
- Enable access to technical, financial, government, and policy support  
- Support women to start, scale, sustain, and accelerate their businesses  
- Promote social empowerment through economic enablement  
- Engage with communities and institutions to advance 12 of the 17 UN SDGs  
- Provide skill development and entrepreneurial training for students  

### Eligibility Matrix
Eligibility varies by program. The following table summarizes the core criteria:

| Program | Minimum Women Ownership | Business Registration | Operational Duration | Turnover Requirement | Geographic Focus | Other Key Criteria |
|--------|--------------------------|------------------------|------------------------|------------------------|------------------|--------------------|
| **WE Reach** | ≥51% | Legally registered in India | Informal or for-profit enterprise (no minimum duration) | Not specified | Tier 2/Tier 3 regions of Telangana | Woman or group of 2-3 women; wants to set up for-profit enterprise or formalize informal enterprise |
| **Project Inclusion** | ≥51% | Registered enterprise | Minimum 2 years in operation | INR 5–10 lakhs per annum (relaxable for Handicrafts) | Telangana (no explicit geographic restriction stated) | Must operate from commercial premises (home-based not encouraged); full-time engagement; potential for employment generation |
| **WE Leads** | ≥51% | Registered business | Minimum 2 years operational | Not specified (scalability & growth potential required) | Urban India (for WE Engage sub-programs; WE Leads itself is Telangana-focused) | Executive decision-maker; works ≥3 days/week; evidence of past growth; scalable business model; committed to innovation |
| **Project Ujaagar** | ≥51% (implied) | Microbusiness (home-based acceptable) | Willing to formalize & scale | Not specified | Urban & rural Telangana | Belongs to marginalized minority communities; sector-agnostic |

> **Note**: Program-specific eligibility criteria apply. For example, WE Reach is restricted to Tier 2/Tier 3 regions, while Project Inclusion discourages home-based units and part-time engagement.

### Benefits & Financial Support
WE Hub does not provide direct funding but offers comprehensive non-financial support and facilitates linkages to external funding sources.

| Support Type | Details |
|-------------|--------|
| **Non-Financial Benefits** | Business consultation, entrepreneurial & management courses, mentorship, marketing assistance, compliance & tax assistance, community & networking opportunities, sector-specific mentoring, skill enhancement, internships & live projects, patenting & IP support, credit linkage, Udyam & MSME registration support, hackathons, exposure visits |
| **Funding Facilitation** | Access to grants, seed funding, angel investors, venture capitalists, corporate partners, and recoverable grants (e.g., USD 2.5 crores from USAID via Samridh Healthcare Blended Finance Facility); guidance for PMEGP loans (e.g., ₹3.10 lakh secured by alumni); CSR-funded programs (e.g., Project Inclusion by Re-Sustainability Limited) |
| **Program Fee Structure** | WE Reach: Free<br>Project Inclusion: CSR-funded (free for participants)<br>WE Leads: Paid program (exact fee not specified in evidence)<br>WE Enable, WE Engage, WE Elevate: Fee structure not detailed; some may be free or subsidized |

> **Important**: WE Hub has supported ventures in securing external funding, including PMEGP loans (e.g., ₹3.10 lakh) through its programs. The recoverable grant of USD 2.5 crores from USAID is facilitated via partnerships, not direct disbursement.

### Timelines & Cohort Structure
- **Status**: Rolling basis  
- **Cohort Frequency**: Programs operate in cohorts (e.g., 4 cohorts per year for WE Reach)  
- **Intake Windows**: Specific to each program; applicants advised to check with program coordinators  
- **Program Duration**:  
  - WE Reach: 3 months (pre-incubation)  
  - Project Inclusion: 6 months (incubation)  
  - WE Leads: 4 months (acceleration)  
  - Project Ujaagar: 3 months  
  - WE Enable: 12 months  
  - WE Engage: 6–12 weeks  
  - WE Elevate: 3–6 months  
- **Last Updated**: 2025

### Key Caveats
> - Program-specific eligibility criteria apply (e.g., geographic restrictions to Tier 2/Tier 3 regions for WE Reach)  
> - Some programs are paid (e.g., WE Leads), while others are free or CSR-funded  
> - Home-based units are not encouraged for certain programs like Project Inclusion  
> - Part-time engagement in the enterprise may disqualify applicants for incubation and acceleration programs  
> - Funding support is facilitative; WE Hub does not directly provide grants or loans  
> - Specific programs may have sectoral focus (e.g., Project Inclusion focuses on Manufacturing, Food Processing, FMCG, Textiles & Handlooms, Handicrafts)
