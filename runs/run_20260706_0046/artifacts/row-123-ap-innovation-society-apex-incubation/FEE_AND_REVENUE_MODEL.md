## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-based pricing**: Fees are linked to the tangible outcomes delivered (e.g., securing incubation support, mentorship, prototype grants).
2. **Risk-sharing model**: Success fees reflect the uncertainty in fund disbursement, which is discretionary and subject to fund availability and committee evaluation.
3. **Transparency**: Clients are informed that financial support (seed funding, grants, stipends) is not guaranteed and depends on proposal evaluation and fund availability.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: Given the rolling application basis and discretionary nature of financial support, revenue realization may lag; success fees are contingent on actual disbursement of seed funding or grants, not just selection.
