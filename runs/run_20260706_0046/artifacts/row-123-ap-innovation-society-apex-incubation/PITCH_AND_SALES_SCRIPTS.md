# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping early-stage startups and innovators in Andhra Pradesh access government-backed incubation support through the AP Innovation Society & APEX Incubation scheme. Many founders I speak with are building innovative products but aren’t aware of the infrastructure, mentorship, and funding support available right here in the state. I’d like to understand your venture’s stage and goals to see if this scheme could be a fit — no obligation, just a diagnostic conversation.

### Discovery Questions
1. Where is your startup or venture currently based, and are you incorporated or planning to incorporate in Andhra Pradesh?  
2. What stage is your innovation at — idea, prototype, early product, or ready for market validation?  
3. Are you working on a scalable solution in sectors like agriculture, healthcare, manufacturing, or another high-impact area?  
4. Do you have a founding team in place, and are the founders residents of Andhra Pradesh with valid Aadhaar and PAN?  
5. Have you prepared a business plan or project proposal outlining your innovation, technology, and go-to-market strategy?  
6. Are you seeking support with infrastructure (office/labs), mentorship, IPR guidance, or access to funding and investor networks?  
7. Are you currently receiving any other government or private incubation support that might conflict with joining a state-run program?  
8. What are your immediate milestones for the next 3–6 months — prototype development, customer validation, or fundraising?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup, innovator, or entrepreneur based in Andhra Pradesh  
- [ ] Working on an innovative idea, product, or service with scalability and social impact potential  
- [ ] Preference given to ventures registered in Andhra Pradesh or committing to operate within the state  
- [ ] Founders possess valid Aadhaar and PAN cards  
- [ ] Proof of residence or office address in Andhra Pradesh available  
- [ ] Business plan or project proposal prepared  
- [ ] Team profile and qualifications documented  
- [ ] Details of innovation or technology being developed provided  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re building an innovative venture in Andhra Pradesh with clear development goals — exactly the kind of applicant the AP Innovation Society & APEX Incubation scheme is designed to support. This scheme offers selected incubatees access to incubation center infrastructure, mentorship, prototyping support, and potential seed funding or grants (subject to evaluation), and help accessing further funding — all at no cost if selected. Would you like me to walk you through how it works and whether you’d qualify?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Early-stage startups in Andhra Pradesh often struggle to access affordable office/lab space, expert mentorship, prototyping funds, and investor networks — critical resources needed to validate ideas and scale. Without this support, many promising innovations stall due to infrastructure gaps or lack of guidance, especially in sectors like agriculture, healthcare, and manufacturing where domain expertise is essential.

**Scheme intro:**
The AP Innovation Society & APEX Incubation scheme, implemented by the AP Innovation Society under the Government of Andhra Pradesh, provides incubation support to early-stage startups, innovators, and entrepreneurs. Selected incubatees receive access to incubation center infrastructure (office space, labs, internet), mentorship from industry experts and academics, guidance on product development and business modeling, assistance in IPR filing, networking with investors and industry partners, and support in accessing government schemes and funding programs. Financial support — including seed funding, grants, or stipends for prototype development and early-stage validation — may be awarded based on project evaluation and fund availability, with the exact amount determined by the incubation committee.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your venture is based in Andhra Pradesh and working on an innovative, scalable idea with social impact potential  
- You have (or can provide) a business plan, incorporation proof, founder PAN and Aadhaar, and proof of local office/residence  
- Your innovation aligns with priority sectors such as agriculture, healthcare, or manufacturing, which the scheme explicitly supports  
- You are seeking the exact type of support the scheme offers: infrastructure, mentorship, prototyping help, and funding access
