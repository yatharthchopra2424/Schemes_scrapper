### AP Innovation Society & APEX Incubation
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Incubation
- **Geography:** Andhra Pradesh
- **Administering Body:** AP Innovation Society, Government of Andhra Pradesh
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://apinnovation.ap.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Startups, innovators, entrepreneurs
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Not restricted; preference for scalable and socially impactful innovations (e.g., agriculture, healthcare, manufacturing)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be based in Andhra Pradesh; preference for ventures registered in the state or committing to operate within Andhra Pradesh

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Seed funding, grants, or stipends for prototype development and early-stage validation (exact quantum based on proposal evaluation); access to incubation infrastructure (office space, labs, internet); mentorship; IPR filing assistance; networking with investors; support in accessing other government schemes
- Disbursement mode: Discretionary financial support (if any) based on fund availability and project evaluation; non-financial benefits provided directly
- Duration of benefit: Subject to periodic performance review and availability of resources/slots

#### Documents Required
1. Business plan or project proposal
2. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration (if applicable)
3. PAN card of the applicant or entity
4. Aadhaar card of founders
5. Proof of residence or office address in Andhra Pradesh
6. Details of innovation or technology being developed
7. Team profile and qualifications

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the official AP Innovation Society website at https://apinnovation.ap.gov.in
2. Navigate to the incubation or startup support section
3. Fill out the online application form with details of the venture, innovation, and team
4. Upload required documents such as business plan, incorporation proof, and identity documents
5. Submit the application for review
6. Await evaluation by the selection committee
7. If shortlisted, attend an interview or pitch session
8. Receive incubation offer upon selection
