# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Startup incorporated in Kerala as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or OPC with active MCA status  
- [ ] DPIIT recognition obtained  
- [ ] Valid KSUM Unique ID certificate (mandatory for fund disbursement)  
- [ ] Startup working on an innovative product or technology (not a service startup or SME in trade and commerce)  
- [ ] No pending dues with Government agencies, KSUM, or other incubators  
- [ ] Not blacklisted by any Government agency in India  
- [ ] Founder(s) have a good CIBIL score (preferably >750 for loan schemes)  
- [ ] For Idea Grant: Startup at ideation, designing, or Proof of Concept (PoC) stage  
- [ ] For Productization Grant: Startup in product development stage with innovative technology product  
- [ ] For Market Acceleration Grant: Startup in early revenue stage  
- [ ] For Scale-up Grant: ₹10 lakhs revenue in last 6 months OR ₹30 lakhs external equity raised  
- [ ] For Women/Transgender Productization Grant: Women/transgender founders holding ≥51% shareholding  
- [ ] For Seed Fund: LLP or Pvt. Ltd with active KSUM Unique ID and DPIIT registration  
- [ ] For NIDHI Seed Support: DPIIT-recognized startup with ≥3 months association in incubator, ≥51% Indian promoter shareholding  
- [ ] For Innovation Grant: Innovator based in/out of Kerala, company incorporation and KSUM Unique ID mandatory for disbursement  
- [ ] For Women Soft Loan: Registered company in Kerala as LLP or Pvt. Ltd, active KSUM Unique ID, DPIIT registration, innovative product/technology, no pending dues, CIBIL score >750 preferred

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Company Incorporation Certificate (COI)  
- Company PAN card  
- Latest ITR (Income Tax Return)  
- GST registration certificate  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration certificate  
- KSUM Unique ID certificate  
- ID proof of founders (Aadhaar/Passport/Voter ID)  
- Company Bank Account details (cancelled cheque/bank letter)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Rs.200 worth Stamp paper in the name of the company  
- CA certified No Dues Certificate  
- Utilisation certificate (if previously availed grants/loans)  
- Documents in connection with Revenue generation/Investment raised  
- Goodwill certificate  
- Company Balance sheet for the last two years  
- Project plan with milestones and time frame  
- Fund Utilization Plan  
- Progress report and utilisation certificate for subsequent tranches  
- Partnership deed (for LLPs)  
- MoA/Deed (Company Incorporation details)  
- CA-certified No Dues & Margin Money Certificate with UDIN (for specific grants like Scale-up, Market Acceleration)  
- Revenue/investment proof  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible scans or clear photographs  
- File size: Each document < 5 MB; total upload < 25 MB  
- File names must match the document type (e.g., "PAN_Certificate")  
- All certificates or English  
- Stamp paper.pdf", "Balance_Sheet_FY2023-24.pdf")  
- All certificates must be self-attested by the authorized signatory  
- Documents in regional languages must be accompanied by certified English translations  
- Financial documents (Balance Sheet, ITR, Bank Statements) must be for the most recent financial year  
- CA certificates must include UDIN and be valid at time of submission

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Project Report is required for most KSUM schemes. Standard sections include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Problem Statement and Solution  
- Product/Technology Description  
- Market Analysis and Go-to-Market Strategy  
- Business Model and Revenue Projections  
- Technical Feasibility and Development Plan  
- Team Background and Roles  
- Financial Projections (5-year)  
- Fund Utilization Plan with Milestones  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation  
- Implementation Timeline  
- Annexures (documents, certificates, etc.)  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the KSUM portal at https://startupmission.kerala.gov.in and navigate to the 'Schemes' section.  
2. Select the relevant scheme and download the guideline document for detailed eligibility and process.  
3. Check for open application calls published on the website, social media, and print media.  
4. Fill the application form online during the application window.  
5. Upload all required documents in the specified format (PDF/JPEG, <5 MB each).  
6. Ensure the KSUM Unique ID is entered correctly (if applicable at application stage).  
7. Double-check that all fields are complete before submission; incomplete forms are rejected.  
8. Retain a screenshot of the submitted application and note the application reference number.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All application form fields completed accurately  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents uploaded and legible  
- [ ] File sizes within limits (<5 MB per file, total <25 MB)  
- [ ] Documents named appropriately and match the checklist  
- [ ] Declarations and undertakings signed (where applicable)  
- [ ] Project plan includes clear milestones, timelines, and fund utilization  
- [ ] Financial projections are realistic and aligned with the proposed activities  
- [ ] No discrepancies between information in form and uploaded documents  
- [ ] Application submitted within the announced window  
- [ ] Screenshot of submission confirmation saved with reference number

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the KSUM portal: https://startupmission.kerala.gov.in  
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation page showing the application reference number and timestamp  
- Store the reference number securely in the case file  
- Set calendar reminders for follow-up at 15 days and 30 days post-submission  
- Monitor the portal and registered email for updates from KSUM

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify the approval letter from KSUM (check sanctioned amount, scheme name, and validity period)  
- [ ] Execute the agreement with KSUM at designated offices before fund release  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount matches the approved grant/loan value  
- [ ] Plan utilization of funds as per the approved Fund Utilization Plan and milestones  
- [ ] Submit interim progress reports and utilisation certificates for subsequent tranche releases (generally 50% first tranche, balance after progress review and utilisation certificate)  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with usage restrictions (e.g., no founder salaries, no fixed assets >₹2 lakhs, no rent, no major infrastructure setup)  
- [ ] Maintain records of all expenditures, invoices, and bills for audit purposes  
- [ ] Submit final Utilisation Certificate and Project Completion Report upon full utilization  
- [ ] Follow up on invoice for success fee (if applicable per engagement terms)  
- [ ] Monitor for any post-approval compliance requirements or reporting deadlines
