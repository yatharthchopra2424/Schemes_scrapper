## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-based pricing**: Fees aligned with the financial benefit delivered (grants up to ₹30 lakhs for R&D Grant, ₹20 lakhs for women-led Innovation Grant, etc.).
2. **Risk-sharing model**: Success fees only payable upon fund disbursement, reducing client upfront burden.
3. **Tiered accessibility**: Starter and Growth tiers designed for early-stage Kerala startups eligible for KSUM grants (idea to scale-up stage).
4. **Scheme-specific note**: KSUM schemes have moderate risk due to structured milestone-based disbursement and high success rate for compliant applications; processing time averages 3–6 months from application to first tranche, requiring revenue lag planning.
