# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I’m reaching out because Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM) is the nodal agency of the Government of Kerala dedicated to fostering innovation and supporting entrepreneurs across all stages of their journey—from idea to scale-up. I’d love to understand where your startup is today and see if KSUM’s grants, loans, or incubation support could be a fit for your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your company incorporated, and is it registered as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or OPC in Kerala?  
2. Do you have DPIIT recognition and an active KSUM Unique ID?  
3. What stage is your startup at—ideation, product development, early revenue, or scaling?  
4. Are you working on an innovative technology-based product or service (not pure trading or services)?  
5. Have you generated any revenue in the last 6 months, or raised external equity?  
6. Are the founders women, transgender, or from SC/ST/OBC communities seeking inclusive grants?  
7. Do you have any pending dues with government agencies or KSUM?  
8. What specific funding need are you looking to address—prototype development, market acceleration, R&D, market expansion, or seed funding?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Incorporated in Kerala as Private Limited, LLP, or OPC  
- [ ] Active MCA status  
- [ ] DPIIT recognized  
- [ ] Valid KSUM Unique ID  
- [ ] Innovative technology-based product/service  
- [ ] No pending dues with government agencies/KSUM  
- [ ] Not blacklisted by any Govt. agency  
- [ ] Founder(s) meet shareholding criteria for women/transgender/OBC grants (if applicable)  
- [ ] Revenue/investment thresholds met for scale-up or market acceleration grants (if applicable)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like KSUM’s [specific grant/loan, e.g., Scale-up Grant up to ₹15 lakhs or R&D Grant up to ₹30 lakhs] could align well with your stage and goals. Let me walk you through how it works and whether you’d qualify.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Startups in Kerala often struggle to access non-dilutive funding for critical stages like prototype development, market entry, or scaling—especially when they lack collateral or face high interest rates on traditional loans. Many also miss out on grants due to complex eligibility or documentation requirements.

**Scheme intro:**
Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM) offers a range of grants and loans tailored to startup stages. For example, the **R&D Grant** provides up to **₹30 lakhs** for research and development, the **Innovation Grant** offers up to **₹15 lakhs** (₹20 lakhs for women startups), and the **Seed Fund** provides a loan of up to **₹15 lakhs** at 6% simple interest with a 12-month moratorium. All are administered by KSUM, the Government of Kerala’s nodal agency for entrepreneurship.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Incorporated in Kerala as a Private Limited Company/LLP/OPC with active MCA status  
- DPIIT recognized and holds a valid KSUM Unique ID  
- Working on an innovative technology-based product (not pure trading/services)  
- [If applicable: Woman/transgender founder with ≥51% shareholding for Women/Transgender Productization Grant up to ₹12 lakhs]  
- [If applicable: Generated ₹10 lakhs revenue in last 6 months or raised ₹30 lakhs external equity for Scale-up Grant]

| "I'll try it myself first" | Common mistakes: applying for grants for service-based startups (not allowed), or trying to use grant funds for founder salaries or fixed assets above ₹2 lakhs (prohibited under most KSUM grants). We avoid these pitfalls. |
| "We're too small / too big" | KSUM supports startups from ideation (Idea Grant: ₹3 lakhs) to scaling (Scale-up Grant: ₹15 lakhs or R&D Grant: ₹30 lakhs). There’s no revenue floor for early grants—only for later-stage ones like Market Acceleration (early revenue) or Scale-up (₹10 lakhs revenue in 6 months or ₹30 lakhs external equity). |
| "I don't have the documents ready" | The 3 most commonly missing documents: CA-certified No Dues Certificate, KSUM Unique ID certificate, and audited balance sheets for the last two years. We’ll give you a checklist and help you prepare them. |
| "We already work with a CA" | Great — we work alongside CAs. They handle your accounts; we handle the scheme strategy, eligibility mapping, and application submission. |
