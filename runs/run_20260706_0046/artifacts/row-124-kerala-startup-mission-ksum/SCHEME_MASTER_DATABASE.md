### Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Incubation
- **Geography:** Kerala
- **Administering Body:** Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM), nodal agency of the Government of Kerala for entrepreneurship development and incubation activities
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://startupmission.kerala.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Application windows are announced periodically through KSUM’s website, social media, and print media. Specific schemes like Innovation Grant are conducted under 'Kerala Innovation Drive’YY’ annually or as per demand. EOIs and tenders have fixed closing dates (e.g., EOI for Startup Space Allotment: 22 June 2026 at 5:00 PM; Tender for AutoCAD: 13 July 2026 at 5:00 PM).

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Startup incorporated in Kerala as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or OPC with active MCA status, DPIIT recognition, and a valid KSUM Unique ID
- Age of business: Varies by scheme (e.g., Idea Grant – startups at ideation, designing, or PoC stage; Productization Grant – startups in product development stage; Market Acceleration Grant – startups in early revenue stage; Scale-up Grant – startups with ₹10 lakhs revenue in last 6 months or ₹30 lakhs external equity)
- Sector: Technology-driven and innovative solutions with strong commercialization potential; inclusive focus on women entrepreneurs, student innovators, SC/ST, MSME, agri-startups, transgender-led startups
- Revenue cap: Varies by scheme (e.g., Scale-up Grant requires ₹10 lakhs revenue in last 6 months or ₹30 lakhs external equity; Market Acceleration Grant for early revenue stage)
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: Must not have pending dues with Government agencies, KSUM, or other incubators; must not be blacklisted by any Govt. agency in India; only one idea can be applied for at a time; two grants will not be sanctioned simultaneously; if a startup has availed prior grants (Idea, Productization, Market Acceleration), only the remaining eligible amount can be claimed for subsequent grants; KSUM Unique ID is mandatory for fund disbursement; company incorporation and active MCA status are mandatory before fund disbursal

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support includes grants, loans, and equity-linked instruments. Grants range from ₹2 lakhs (Student Idea Grant) to ₹30 lakhs (R&D Grant). Loans include Seed Fund (up to ₹15 lakhs at 6% simple interest with 12-month moratorium and 36-month repayment), Women Soft Loan (up to ₹15 lakhs at 6% simple interest repayable in 1 year or project completion), and Scale-up Seed Fund (collateral-free debt or CCD/CCPS up to ₹25 lakhs at 6% simple interest with 12-month moratorium and 36-month repayment). The Fund of Funds deploys capital as a Limited Partner in SEBI-registered AIFs that invest in Kerala-based startups. The Innovation Grant provides up to ₹15 lakhs for startups and ₹20 lakhs for women startups. Specific grants: Idea Grant (₹3 lakhs), Productization Grant (₹7 lakhs), Market Acceleration Grant (₹10 lakhs), Scale-up Grant (₹15 lakhs), Women/Transgender Productization Grant (₹12 lakhs), NIDHI Prayas Grant (₹10 lakhs), NIDHI Seed Support (₹25 lakhs–1 Crore), R&D Grant (₹30 lakhs).
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (grant disbursement in milestone-based tranches; loan disbursement in installments)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (grants); 3 years (loan repayment period)

#### Documents Required
1. Company Incorporation details (COI, MoA/Deed)
2. Company PAN card
3. KSUM Unique ID certificate
4. ID proof of the founders
5. Company Bank Account details
6. Rs.200 worth Stamp paper in the name of the company
7. CA certified No Dues Certificate
8. Utilisation certificate (if previously availed grants/loans)
9. Documents in connection with Revenue generation/Investment raised
10. Goodwill certificate
11. Company Balance sheet for the last two years
12. Project plan with milestones and time frame
13. Fund Utilization Plan
14. Progress report and utilisation certificate for subsequent tranches
15. Partnership deed (for LLPs)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the KSUM website at https://startupmission.kerala.gov.in
2. Navigate to the 'Schemes' section and select the relevant scheme
3. Download the guideline document for detailed eligibility and process
4. Check for open application calls published on the website, social media, and print media
5. Submit application online through the KSUM portal during the application window
6. Fill the application form and upload required documents (incorporation, PAN, KSUM Unique ID, bank details, fund utilization plan, etc.)
7. Application scrutiny by KSUM officials
8. Shortlisting based on eligibility and proposal merit
9. Pitch presentation before the Expert Committee
10. Evaluation based on team, product innovation, marketing, financials, and fund utilization plan
11. Approval by CEO/Chairman, KSUM
12. Agreement execution with KSUM
13. Fund disbursement in milestone-based tranches (typically 50% first tranche, balance after progress review and utilization certificate)
