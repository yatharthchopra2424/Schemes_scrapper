## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Startup incorporated as a Private Limited Company, Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), or Registered Partnership Firm in Delhi  
- [ ] At least one founder domiciled in Delhi  
- [ ] Working on innovative products, services, or processes  
- [ ] Not older than 7 years from date of incorporation  
- [ ] Annual turnover not exceeding INR 25 Crore in any preceding financial year  
- [ ] Registered office address located in Delhi  
- [ ] Not receiving duplicate benefits under other central or state government schemes for the same purpose  

> **Sources**: Eligibility criteria from KEY FACTS block; Application Portal: https://investdelhionline.com

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration  
- PAN of the entity  
- GST registration certificate  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (if applicable)  
- ITR of the entity (last 2 financial years)  
- Address proof of registered office (utility bill, rental agreement, or property tax receipt)  
- Aadhaar and PAN of at least one founder  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Pitch deck or business plan  
- Details of prototype or product under development (including specifications, stage of development, and innovation claim)  
- Declaration of compliance with eligibility criteria (signed by authorized signatory)  
- Utilization plan (to be submitted post-approval, but draft recommended during application)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)  
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ClientID_Date.pdf` (e.g., `DelhiStartupPolicy_IncorporationCert_CASE001_20240615.pdf`)  
- Certificates must be valid and not expired (especially GST, Udyam, address proof)  
- Aadhaar numbers must be masked (showing only last 4 digits) as per data privacy norms  
- Declaration must be on company letterhead, signed, and stamped  
- Pitch deck must not exceed 15 slides and must clearly highlight innovation, market potential, and fund utilization

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not explicitly required as a formal DPR. However, a detailed pitch deck or business plan is mandatory, covering innovation, prototype details, market analysis, and fund utilization plan. If the evaluation committee requests deeper technical validation, be prepared to provide a DPR. **Verify with nodal officer if DPR is expected for high-value asks (near INR 10 Lakhs).**

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Register on the portal (https://investdelhionline.com) using the founder’s email and mobile number.  
2. Select “Delhi Startup Policy” from the list of available schemes.  
3. Fill in startup details exactly as per Certificate of Incorporation (name, CIN, date of incorporation, registered address).  
4. Ensure founder details (Aadhaar, PAN, domicile) match uploaded documents.  
5. Clearly describe the innovation: problem statement, solution, uniqueness, and stage of development (idea/prototype/MVP).  
6. Specify the amount sought: up to INR 10 Lakhs for product development OR up to INR 2 Lakhs for patent reimbursement (not both unless explicitly allowed).  
7. Upload documents in the correct sections; do not combine multiple files into one unless permitted.  
8. Review all entries before final submission — edits may not be allowed after submission.  
9. Keep a copy of the submitted application and acknowledgment.  
10. Note: The portal may time out during upload; draft responses offline and upload during low-traffic hours.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify approval letter specifying sanctioned amount (up to INR 10 Lakhs for product development or INR 2 Lakhs for patent reimbursement)  
- [ ] Confirm disbursement tranches linked to milestone completion as per approval terms  
- [ ] Utilization Certificate (UC) format: obtain from portal or nodal officer; ensure it aligns with GFR 2017 standards  
- [ ] Maintain detailed records of expenditure (invoices, bills, bank statements) against approved heads  
- [ ] Submit UC and progress report before each tranche release  
- [ ] Track milestone achievement and ensure timely reporting to avoid fund recovery  
- [ ] Coordinate with client for success fee invoicing upon receipt of each tranche  
- [ ] Archive all approvals, UCs, and correspondence for audit and future reference  

> **Note**: Funds are disbursed in tranches upon milestone achievement and UC submission. Failure to meet milestones or submit UCs may lead to recovery of granted amount.  
> **Sources**: Financial Support and Key Caveats from KEY FACTS block; Application Portal: https://investdelhionline.com
