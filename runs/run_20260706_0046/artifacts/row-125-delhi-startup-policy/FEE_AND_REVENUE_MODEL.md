## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual grant amount (up to ₹10 Lakhs for product development under the Delhi Startup Policy), ensuring clients retain the majority of the financial benefit.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the rolling application basis and milestone-based disbursement (evidence shows funds released upon achievement of predefined milestones and utilization certificates), success fees are structured to reflect implementation risk and processing lag.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The Growth tier pricing aligns with the scheme’s eligibility criteria (startups with turnover ≤ ₹25 Cr, not older than 7 years), making our services accessible to the target beneficiary segment.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover initial eligibility assessment and application preparation, while success fees are contingent on approved grant disbursement, reducing client financial risk.
