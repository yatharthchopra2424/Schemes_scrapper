## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping early-stage startups in Delhi access government grants designed to support innovation and product development. The Delhi Startup Policy offers up to INR 10 Lakhs for product development and prototype creation, along with patent reimbursement and incubation support—specifically for Delhi-based startups working on innovative solutions. I’d like to understand your startup’s current stage and goals to see if this could be a good fit.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated, and what type of legal entity is it (Private Limited, LLP, or Partnership Firm)?
2. Is at least one of your founders domiciled in Delhi, and is your registered office located in Delhi?
3. What innovative product, service, or process are you currently developing or planning to develop?
4. How old is your startup from the date of incorporation, and has it been operating for less than 7 years?
5. What was your annual turnover in the last financial year, and has it exceeded INR 25 Crore in any preceding year?
6. Are you currently receiving funding or benefits from any other central or state government scheme for the same purpose (e.g., product development or patent filing)?
7. Do you have a pitch deck, business plan, or prototype details ready to share?
8. Have you already applied for or received support under the Delhi Startup Seed Fund Scheme (DSSFS)?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Registered Partnership Firm
- [ ] Registered office in Delhi with valid address proof
- [ ] At least one founder domiciled in Delhi (Aadhaar and PAN available)
- [ ] Working on an innovative product, service, or process
- [ ] Not older than 7 years from date of incorporation
- [ ] Annual turnover not exceeding INR 25 Crore in any preceding financial year
- [ ] Not receiving duplicate benefits for the same purpose under other central/state schemes
- [ ] Able to provide required documents (Inc certificate, PAN, GST, founder IDs, pitch deck/prototype details, bank details, declaration)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your startup may meet the core eligibility criteria for the Delhi Startup Policy. If approved, you could access up to INR 10 Lakhs in grant funding for product development and up to INR 2 Lakhs for patent reimbursement—disbursed upon milestone completion. Let me walk you through how the scheme works and how we’ve helped similar startups secure approval.
