### Delhi Startup Policy
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Delhi
- **Administering Body:** Department of Industries, Government of National Capital Territory of Delhi
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://investdelhionline.com
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Private Limited Company, Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), or Registered Partnership Firm
- Age of business: Not older than 7 years from date of incorporation
- Sector: Innovative products, services, or processes (no specific sector restriction)
- Revenue cap: Annual turnover not exceeding INR 25 Crore in any preceding financial year
- Employee count: [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]
- Other: At least one founder domiciled in Delhi; working on innovative products, services, or processes; not receiving duplicate benefits for the same purpose under other central or state government schemes

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to INR 10 Lakhs for product development; up to INR 2 Lakhs for patent filing and certification costs reimbursement; access to seed fund support through Delhi Startup Seed Fund Scheme (DSSFS); subsidized incubation and co-working spaces; mentorship; market access and government procurement assistance; funding linkages; regulatory handholding
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (grant disbursed in tranches upon milestone achievement and submission of utilization certificates)
- Duration of benefit: One-time grant (disbursed in tranches based on milestones)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. GST registration certificate
4. Address proof of the registered office in Delhi
5. Aadhaar and PAN of at least one founder
6. Pitch deck or business plan
7. Details of prototype or product under development
8. Bank account details of the startup
9. Declaration of compliance with eligibility criteria

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register on the Delhi Startup Policy portal (investdelhionline.com)
2. Fill the online application form with startup details and innovation description
3. Upload required documents including Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, GST registration, and address proof
4. Submit pitch deck or prototype details
5. Await preliminary screening by the Delhi Startup Policy Committee
6. Present before the evaluation committee for final assessment
7. Upon approval, sign agreement and submit utilization plan
8. Receive grant in tranches based on milestone completion
