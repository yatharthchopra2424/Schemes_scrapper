## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Delhi Startup Policy** is a state-level grant scheme administered by the **Department of Industries, Government of National Capital Territory of Delhi**. It aims to foster innovation and entrepreneurship within Delhi by providing financial and non-financial support to early-stage startups. The scheme operates on a **rolling basis** with applications accepted throughout the year, subject to fund availability. The total fund size is **INR 200 Crore**, with a maximum grant of **INR 10 Lakhs** for product development and up to **INR 2 Lakhs** for patent/reimbursement costs.

### Objectives
The policy seeks to:
- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship in Delhi  
- Provide financial and non-financial support to early-stage startups  
- Create incubation and co-working infrastructure  
- Simplify regulatory compliance for startups  
- Enhance access to markets and mentorship  
- Support underrepresented groups including women and SC/ST entrepreneurs  
- Strengthen industry-academia collaboration  
- Position Delhi as a preferred startup destination in India
