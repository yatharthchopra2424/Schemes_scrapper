# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is an existing producer of specific pathogen free (SPF) broodstock or involved in domestication and breeding programmes of any potential candidate species for coastal aquaculture from India or overseas.
- [ ] Applicant may apply individually or as lead partner of a consortium.
- [ ] Applicant has successfully designed and operated a Nucleus Breeding Centre (NBC) or Broodstock Multiplication Centre (BMC) for Litopenaeus vannamei or any other fish/shrimp species, maintaining a minimum of 5 generations.
- [ ] Applicant has successfully developed and managed a minimum of 50 families of Litopenaeus vannamei or any other fish/shrimp species through its own R&D activities in the last ten years.
- [ ] Applicant has been engaged in the establishment and operation of at least one NBC or BMC assignment within the last five years preceding the date of application.
- [ ] Applicant possesses the requisite experience and capabilities required for establishment and operation of NBCs or BMCs.
- [ ] No aquaculture activities, Fish Landing Centres, or other sources of direct contamination within a 1000-meter radius of the selected site.
- [ ] NBCs and BMCs are in separate locations; if co-located, adequate physical separation, isolation, independent water supply, and discharge are ensured.
- [ ] Adequate biosecurity measures are in place to analyze and manage risks to life and health of candidate species, including environmental risks, for disease prevention.
- [ ] Compliance with biosecurity requirements, in-house biosecurity audit, in-house health monitoring, disease surveillance, and chain of custody sampling/testing as per Guidelines for Health Monitoring, Disease Surveillance, and SPF Certification of Coastal Aquaculture Units and Stocks in India and Aquatic Animal Health Code of WOAH.

**Portal Reference**: Application Portal: http://dof.gov.in  
**Source**: Scheme Key Facts (Eligibility section)

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate
- PAN Card
- ITR (Last 3 years)
- GST Registration
- Bank Statements (Last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Company background
- Qualifications
- Methodology
- Technology details
- Disease-free certification
- Details of Board of Directors and Managing Director with copy of Memorandum of Association or Articles of Association
- Certified audited financial statement of the firm for the last three years
- Statement of qualification including number of years of experience, number of NBCs/BMCs designed or operated, candidate species, and number of families developed and maintained in the last ten years
- Number of families developed and maintained through own research and development activities in the last ten years
- Details of source material including name of overseas Specific Pathogen Free Facility, address, country of registration, location of facility, terms and conditions of agreement with Indian firms, extent of commercial supply of SPF broodstock, details of any other NBC or BMC operated in another country, reproductive performance of SPF broodstock

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, certified (where required), and in English or Hindi.
- File size for each document should not exceed 5 MB.
- File names must match the document description (e.g., "Audited_Financial_Statement_2023.pdf").
- Scanned copies must be in PDF format with minimum 300 DPI resolution.
- Declarations and undertakings must be signed and stamped by authorized signatory.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is not explicitly mentioned as mandatory in the EOI stage. However, shortlisted proponents must provide a 60-minute presentation covering technical and financial aspects. It is advised to prepare a DPR in anticipation of further evaluation.  
**Status**: Verify with nodal officer whether DPR is required post-EOI shortlisting.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use Form-F-1 as specified in the scheme guidelines for EOI submission.
2. Ensure all sections are completed; leave no field blank unless explicitly marked as optional.
3. Attach all scheme-specific documents as per the checklist.
4. Clearly mention “Expression of Interest for establishing Nucleus Breeding Centres (NBC) for L. vannamei in India” on the top of the sealed envelope.
5. Submit the proposal in hard copy (physical submission only) as per the rolling EOI process.
6. Include a covering letter on company letterhead detailing the purpose of submission.
7. Verify that the applicant’s name, address, and contact details match across all documents.
8. Retain a copy of the submitted application and obtain proof of submission (acknowledgment/receipt).

**Portal Reference**: Application Portal: http://dof.gov.in (Note: Submission is physical, not online)  
**Source**: Scheme Key Facts (Application Process)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify approval letter from Department of Fisheries.
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount, interest subvention (up to 3% p.a.), lending rate (not lower than 5% p.a.), and repayment terms (12 years with 2-year moratorium).
- [ ] Initiate utilization of funds as per approved project plan.
- [ ] Prepare and submit utilization certificates and progress reports as required.
- [ ] Coordinate with lending institution for disbursement and credit guarantee activation (25% facility via IDF of DAHD).
- [ ] Ensure compliance with biosecurity and site isolation norms during implementation.
- [ ] Track milestone achievement for internal review and fee invoicing.
