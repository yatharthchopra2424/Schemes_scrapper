## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, documentation preparation, and application submission support.
2. Success fee is contingent on sanction of FIDF loan/credit guarantee, aligning consultant incentives with client outcomes.
3. Tiered pricing reflects client capacity to pay, with higher-value projects accessing concessional long-term finance.
4. Scheme-specific note: FIDF involves technical appraisal and government approvals with a 90-day PSC review period; success is high for eligible applicants with documented NBC/BMC experience, reducing risk for consultants.
