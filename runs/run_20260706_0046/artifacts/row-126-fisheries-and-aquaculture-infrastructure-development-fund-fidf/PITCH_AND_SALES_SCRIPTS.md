# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping fisheries and aquaculture businesses access government funding for infrastructure development. Given your work in coastal aquaculture or seed production, I wanted to explore whether the Fisheries and Aquaculture Infrastructure Development Fund (FIDF) could support your plans for establishing or modernizing Nucleus Breeding Centres (NBCs) or Broodstock Multiplication Centres (BMCs).

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your current involvement in the production or supply of specific pathogen free (SPF) broodstock for Litopenaeus vannamei or other coastal aquaculture species?
2. Have you successfully designed and operated a Nucleus Breeding Centre or Broodstock Multiplication Centre for at least 5 generations of any candidate species?
3. How many families of Litopenaeus vannamei or other species have you developed and maintained through your own R&D activities in the last ten years?
4. Have you been engaged in the establishment or operation of at least one NBC or BMC in the last five years?
5. Do you have access to overseas SPF broodstock sources, and if so, what are the terms of your agreement with Indian firms?
6. Is your proposed NBC/BMC site located at least 1 km away from any aquaculture activity, fish landing centre, or potential contamination source?
7. Are you applying as an individual entity, or as a lead partner in a consortium with defined roles and documentation?
8. What is your timeline for submitting the Expression of Interest (EOI) under the current rolling window?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is engaged in SPF broodstock production or domestication/breeding programmes for coastal aquaculture species (India or overseas)
- [ ] Has successfully designed and operated an NBC/BMC for minimum 5 generations of any candidate species
- [ ] Has developed and managed minimum 50 families of Litopenaeus vannamei or other species through own R&D in last 10 years
- [ ] Has been engaged in establishment/operation of at least one NBC/BMC in last 5 years
- [ ] Possesses technical capacity in disease-free certification, technology details, and germplasm sourcing
- [ ] Can provide audited financial statements for last 3 years
- [ ] Proposed site maintains 1 km isolation from contamination sources
- [ ] NBC and BMC (if both) are in separate locations or adequately isolated with independent water supply
- [ ] Commitment to biosecurity measures as per Guidelines for Health Monitoring and Disease Surveillance
- [ ] Applying via Form-F-1 as sole organisation or lead partner in consortium

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you meet several key criteria for the Fisheries and Aquaculture Infrastructure Development Fund (FIDF), which offers long-term finance at concessional rates with up to 3% interest subvention and a 25% credit guarantee for eligible NBC/BMC projects. Shall I walk you through how this scheme could specifically support your infrastructure plans?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Establishing Nucleus Breeding Centres (NBCs) and Broodstock Multiplication Centres (BMCs) requires significant upfront investment in biosecure facilities, specialized equipment, and R&D infrastructure. Many entrepreneurs and cooperatives in the coastal aquaculture sector face challenges accessing long-term, affordable finance to modernize or create such critical seed production infrastructure, limiting their ability to scale sustainable broodstock supply.

**Scheme intro:**
The Fisheries and Aquaculture Infrastructure Development Fund (FIDF), administered by the Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India, provides long-term loans at concessional rates for the creation and modernization of fisheries and aquaculture infrastructure. It offers interest subvention of up to 3% per annum, a lending rate not lower than 5% per annum, a repayment period of 12 years with a 2-year moratorium on principal, and a 25% credit guarantee facility for eligible entrepreneurs, individual farmers, and cooperatives — all accessible via the application portal at http://dof.gov.in.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You have successfully designed and operated an NBC/BMC for minimum 5 generations of a candidate species  
- You have developed and maintained at least 50 families of Litopenaeus vannamei or other species through your own R&D in the last ten years  
- You have been engaged in the establishment or operation of at least one NBC/BMC assignment within the last five years  
- You can provide disease-free certification, technology details, and audited financial statements for the last three years
