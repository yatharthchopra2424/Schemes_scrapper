### Fisheries and Aquaculture Infrastructure Development Fund (FIDF)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** http://dof.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Existing producers of specific pathogen free broodstock, those involved in domestication and breeding programmes of any potential candidate species for coastal aquaculture either from India or from overseas, either independently or through a joint venture with a local partner from India
- Age of business: Applicants must have been engaged in the establishment and operation of at least one Nucleus Breeding Centres or Broodstock Multiplication Centres assignment within the last five years preceding the date of application
- Sector: Fisheries and aquaculture infrastructure development
- Revenue cap: Not mentioned in evidence
- Employee count: Not mentioned in evidence
- Other: Applicant organization must possess requisite experience and capabilities for establishment and operation of Nucleus Breeding Centres or Broodstock Multiplication Centres; must have successfully designed and operated a Nucleus Breeding Centres or Broodstock Multiplication Centres for Litopenaeus vannamei or any such other fish or shrimp species which has developed and maintained a minimum of 5 generations; must have successfully developed and managed a minimum of 50 families of Litopenaeus vannamei or any such other fish or shrimp species through its own research and development activities in the last ten years

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Long-term finance at concessional rates; interest subvention up to 3% per annum; 25% credit guarantee facility; lending rate of interest not lower than 5% per annum; repayment period of 12 years with moratorium of 2 years for repayment of principle amount
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (long-term finance)
- Duration of benefit: 12 years repayment period with 2 years moratorium

#### Documents Required
1. Company background
2. Qualifications
3. Methodology
4. Technology details
5. Disease-free certification
6. Details of Board of Directors and Managing Director with copy of Memorandum of Association or Memorandum of Article
7. Certified audited financial statement of the firm for the last three years
8. Statement of qualification including number of years of experience, number of NBCs/BMCs designed or operated, candidate species and number of families developed and maintained in the last ten years
9. Number of families developed and maintained through own research and development activities in the last ten years
10. Details of source material including name of overseas Specific Pathogen Free Facility, address, country of registration, location of facility, terms and conditions of agreement with Indian firms, extent of commercial supply of SPF broodstock, details of any other NBC or BMC operated in another country, reproductive performance of SPF broodstock

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Submit Expression of Interest (EOI) for establishing Nucleus Breeding Centres (NBCs) or Broodstock Multiplication Centres (BMCs) as per Form-F-1
2. Provide company background, qualifications, methodology, technology details, and disease-free certification
3. Shortlisted proponents must provide a 60-minute presentation covering background and experience, presence in India and seed supply chain, available germplasm, conceptual plans, layout, site selection, selective breeding strategies, biosecurity measures, technological advantages, projected economic benefits, and training plans
4. Project Screening Committee (PSC) evaluates and recommends potential applicants
5. Prior permission is granted by the Central Government in the Department of Fisheries based on recommendations of the Project Screening Committee within ninety days from the date of receipt of application
