# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
The **Fisheries and Aquaculture Infrastructure Development Fund (FIDF)** is a loan-based scheme launched by the Government of India in 2018 under the Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying. It aims to promote sustainable growth of the fisheries sector by providing long-term finance at concessional rates for the creation and modernization of fisheries and aquaculture infrastructure. The scheme has a pan-India geographic scope and is implemented by the Department of Fisheries. The application portal is [http://dof.gov.in](http://dof.gov.in). The fund size is ₹7,522.48 crore, and as of 2024, it has sanctioned 132 projects worth ₹5,794.09 crore. The scheme operates on a rolling Expression of Interest (EOI) basis with no fixed deadline.

### Objectives
- Promote sustainable growth of the fisheries sector  
- Provide long-term finance at concessional rates for fisheries and aquaculture infrastructure  
- Support creation and modernization of fisheries infrastructure  
- Strengthen essential infrastructure such as fishing harbours, processing plants, cold storage, and fish seed farms  
- Enhance value-chain efficiencies in fisheries  
- Support fisheries startups and Fish Farmer Producer Organizations (FFPOs)  
- Foster innovation and growth within the fisheries sector  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Eligibility Criteria | Details |
|----------------------|---------|
| **Applicant Type** | Existing producers of specific pathogen free (SPF) broodstock, those involved in domestication and breeding programmes of candidate species for coastal aquaculture (from India or overseas), either independently or through a joint venture with an Indian partner |
| **Technical Capacity & Experience** | (i) Successfully designed and operated a Nucleus Breeding Centre (NBC) or Broodstock Multiplication Centre (BMC) for *Litopenaeus vannamei* or any other fish/shrimp species with a minimum of 5 generations maintained; (ii) Successfully developed and managed a minimum of 50 families of *L. vannamei* or any other fish/shrimp species through own R&D activities in the last ten years |
| **Recent Experience** | Must have been engaged in the establishment and operation of at least one NBC or BMC assignment within the last five years preceding the application date |
| **Application Mode** | Can apply individually (as sole organisation) or as lead partner of a consortium via Form-F-1 |
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Fishers, fish farmers, stakeholders in fisheries sector, entrepreneurs, cooperatives |

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Benefit Type | Details |
|--------------|---------|

| **Lending Rate of Interest** | Not lower than 5% per annum for all EEs for development of identified fisheries-based infrastructure facilities |

| **Fund Utilization** | Long-term finance for creation and modernization of fisheries and aquaculture infrastructure |

### Required Documents
1. Company background  
2. Qualifications  
3. Methodology  
4. Technology details  
5. Disease-free certification  
6. Details of Board of Directors and Managing Director with copy of Memorandum of Association or Memorandum of Article  
7. Certified audited financial statement of the firm for the last three years  
8. Statement of qualification including number of years of experience, number of NBCs/BMCs designed or operated, candidate species and number of families developed and maintained in the last ten years  
9. Number of families developed and maintained through own research and development activities in the last ten years  
10. Details of source material including name of overseas Specific Pathogen Free Facility, address, country of registration, location of facility, terms and conditions of agreement with Indian firms, extent of commercial supply of SPF broodstock, details of any other NBC or BMC operated in another country, reproductive performance of SPF broodstock

### Key Caveats
> **Biosecurity & Site Requirements**  
> - No aquaculture activities, Fish Landing Centres, or any other source of direct contamination within a 1000-meter (1 km) radius of the NBC/BMC site  
> - NBCs and BMCs must be in separate locations; if substantial cause exists, they may coexist on the same site with adequate physical separation, isolation, independent water supply, and discharge  
> - Adequate biosecurity measures must be in place to analyze and manage risks to life and health of candidate species, including associated environmental risks for disease prevention  
> - NBCs and BMCs must comply with biosecurity requirements, including in-house biosecurity audit, in-house health monitoring, disease surveillance, and chain of custody sampling and testing as per Guidelines for Health Monitoring, Disease Surveillance, and Specific Pathogen Free Certification of Coastal Aquaculture Units and Stocks in India and Aquatic Animal Health Code of World Organisation for Animal Health
