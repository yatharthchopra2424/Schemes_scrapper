## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the scale of fisheries infrastructure projects supported under the scheme.
2. Retainer covers initial project scoping, DPR preparation, and liaison with State Fisheries Departments.
3. Success fee is contingent upon fund sanction and disbursement milestones.
4. Scheme-specific note: The Blue Revolution Scheme operated from 2015-16 to 2019-20 and is now closed; however, similar projects are now routed through PMMSY. Processing time for approvals was typically 3–6 months based on annual budget cycles and State-level proposal reviews, indicating moderate risk due to dependency on fund availability and State prioritization.
