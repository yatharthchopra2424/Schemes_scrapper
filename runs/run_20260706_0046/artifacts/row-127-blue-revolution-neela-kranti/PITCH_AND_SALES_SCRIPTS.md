# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping fisheries and aquaculture businesses access government support for infrastructure and technology upgrades. The Blue Revolution – Neela Kranti scheme was designed specifically to strengthen the fisheries value chain through subsidies for hatcheries, farms, and post-harvest facilities. I’d like to understand your current operations to see if this scheme aligns with your growth plans.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of fisheries or aquaculture activity are you currently engaged in (e.g., hatchery, fish farm, seaweed cultivation, post-harvest processing)?
2. When was your business incorporated or registered?
3. Are you operating as an individual entrepreneur, fisheries cooperative, fish farmer, or MSME in the fisheries sector?
4. Do you have a Detailed Project Report (DPR) prepared for your proposed project?
5. Have you secured land ownership or lease documentation do you have for the project site?
6. Have you obtained technical feasibility reports or environmental clearances for your project?
7. Are you seeking financial support for inputs like seed, feed, or equipment, or for infrastructure development?
8. What is your estimated project cost and funding requirement?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Beneficiary type: State Government, Union Territory, fisheries cooperative, fish farmer, entrepreneur, or other stakeholder in fisheries/aquaculture
- [ ] Project proposal submitted through State Fisheries Department for approval under CSS framework
- [ ] Project adheres to scheme guidelines, technical feasibility, and financial viability
- [ ] Project component falls under eligible areas: fishing harbours, fish landing centres, aquaculture farms, hatcheries, post-harvest facilities, technological upgradation, capacity building, training, credit access, input subsidies, market linkages, value addition, export-oriented activities
- [ ] Beneficiary is from target group: fishers, fish farmers, coastal communities, entrepreneurs, or MSME

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, the Blue Revolution – Neela Kranti scheme appears to be a strong fit. This centrally sponsored scheme offered subsidies of up to 60% (Central:State) for general States and 90:10 for Himalayan and North-Eastern States, with 100% central funding for Union Territories, supporting fisheries infrastructure development with a total mobilized investment of ₹5,000 crore. Shall I outline how it could work for your specific project?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Fisheries and aquaculture businesses in India face high capital costs for infrastructure development, technological upgradation, and access to quality inputs like seed and feed. Limited access to finance and subsidies hinders scaling operations, adopting sustainable practices, and improving post-harvest handling, which affects productivity, livelihoods, and export potential—particularly for coastal and inland fishing communities.

**Scheme intro:**
The Blue Revolution – Neela Kranti scheme, administered by the Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India, was a Centrally Sponsored Scheme (CSS) providing financial assistance as a subsidy for fisheries infrastructure development. It had a central outlay of ₹3,000 crore, mobilizing a total investment of ₹5,000 crore. Funding followed a 60:40 (Central:State) ratio for general States, 90:10 for Himalayan and North-Eastern States, and 100% central funding for Union Territories, released in installments based on project progress and utilization certificates.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in an eligible fisheries or aquaculture activity (e.g., hatchery, fish farm, post-harvest facility) as confirmed in discovery
- Your business is registered as an entrepreneur, cooperative, fish farmer, or MSME in the fisheries sector
- You are prepared to submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR) through your State Fisheries Department for appraisal and approval
- Your project demonstrates technical feasibility and financial viability, meeting scheme guidelines

**Next step:**
We offer a free, no-obligation eligibility check. If you qualify, we assist with DPR preparation, State Fisheries Department submission, and end-to-end application support on a success-fee-only model—you pay only upon approval.
