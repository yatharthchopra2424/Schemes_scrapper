### Blue Revolution – Neela Kranti
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dof.gov.in/blue-revolution
- **Current Status:** Closed
- **Window:** The scheme operated from 2015-16 to 2019-20. Applications were accepted during the scheme period based on annual budget allocations and State-level proposals. There was no fixed rolling deadline; submissions were invited periodically as per scheme guidelines and fund availability.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: State Governments, Union Territories, fisheries cooperatives, fish farmers, entrepreneurs, and other stakeholders involved in fisheries and aquaculture activities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Fisheries and aquaculture
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Beneficiaries had to submit project proposals through the respective State Fisheries Departments for approval and funding under the Centrally Sponsored Scheme framework. Specific eligibility criteria varied by component but generally required adherence to scheme guidelines, technical feasibility, and financial viability of proposed projects.

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: The Blue Revolution Scheme had a central outlay of ₹3,000 crore, which helped mobilize a total investment of ₹5,000 crore.
- Disbursement mode: Financial support was provided as a subsidy through the Centrally Sponsored Scheme (CSS) mechanism, where the Government of India provided grant-in-aid to State Governments and Union Territories for implementing approved projects.
- Duration of benefit: Funds were released in installments based on project progress and utilization certificates.

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
2. Land ownership/lease documents
3. Project cost estimates
4. Technical feasibility report
5. Environmental clearance (if applicable)
6. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from relevant authorities
7. Bank appraisal note (for loan linkage)
8. Implementation schedule
9. Undertaking from the beneficiary
10. Registration/incorporation certificate (for companies, cooperatives, etc.)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the project component under Blue Revolution Scheme (e.g., aquaculture development, infrastructure, post-harvest).
2. Prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR) as per scheme guidelines.
3. Submit the DPR to the respective State Fisheries Department.
4. State Fisheries Department forwards the proposal to the Department of Fisheries, Government of India for appraisal and approval.
5. Upon approval, obtain sanction letter and proceed with project implementation.
6. Utilize funds as per approved plan and submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates.
7. Claim reimbursement or receive funds in installments based on milestone achievement.
