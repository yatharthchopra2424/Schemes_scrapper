# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Blue Revolution – Neela Kranti** (Scheme ID: row-127) was a centrally sponsored subsidy scheme implemented by the **Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India**. It operated pan-India from **2015-16 to 2019-20** with a central outlay of **₹3,000 crore**, mobilizing a total investment of **₹5,000 crore**. The scheme has been subsumed under the **Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)** launched in **2020-21** and is **no longer accepting new applications**.

### Objectives
The scheme aimed to:
- Increase fish production and productivity through technological upgradation and expansion of aquaculture
- Enhance socio-economic conditions of fishers and fish farmers
- Promote sustainable and responsible utilization of fisheries resources
- Develop fisheries infrastructure (fishing harbours, fish landing centres, aquaculture facilities)
- Strengthen the fisheries value chain via post-harvest infrastructure and market linkages
- Encourage private investment and entrepreneurship in fisheries
- Improve fish quality and safety through better handling and processing
- Generate employment in fisheries and aquaculture sectors

### Eligibility Matrix
| **Beneficiary Type**       | **Eligibility Criteria**                                                                 | **Application Channel**                              |
|----------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| State Governments          | Submit project proposals through State Fisheries Departments                             | State Fisheries Department → Department of Fisheries |
| Union Territories          | Submit project proposals through UT Fisheries Departments                                | UT Fisheries Department → Department of Fisheries    |
| Fisheries Cooperatives     | Must submit project proposals via State Fisheries Departments                            | State Fisheries Department                           |
| Fish Farmers               | Must submit project proposals via State Fisheries Departments                            | State Fisheries Department                           |
| Entrepreneurs              | Must submit project proposals via State Fisheries Departments                            | State Fisheries Department                           |
| Other Stakeholders         | Involved in fisheries/aquaculture; submit proposals via State Fisheries Departments      | State Fisheries Department                           |

> **Note**: Specific eligibility varied by component (e.g., infrastructure, aquaculture, post-harvest), but all required adherence to scheme guidelines, technical feasibility, and financial viability.

### Benefits & Financial Support
| **Aspect**                 | **Details**                                                                                                                                 |
|----------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Fund Size**              | Central outlay: **₹3,000 crore**; Total investment mobilized: **₹5,000 crore**                                                              |
| **Funding Pattern**        | - General States: **60:40** (Central:State)<br>- Himalayan & North-Eastern States: **90:10** (Central:State)<br>- Union Territories: **100% central funding** |
| **Disbursement Mechanism** | Funds released in installments based on project progress and utilization certificates (UCs)                                                  |
| **Subsidy Type**           | Grant-in-aid to State Governments/UTs under Centrally Sponsored Scheme (CSS) mechanism                                                      |
| **Supported Activities**   | - Fisheries infrastructure (fishing harbours, fish landing centres)<br>- Aquaculture farms, hatcheries<br>- Post-harvest facilities<br>- Technological upgradation<br>- Capacity building & training<br>- Input subsidies (seed, feed, equipment)<br>- Credit facilitation<br>- Market linkages, value addition, export-oriented activities<br>- Employment generation & livelihood diversification |

### Application Process
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Identify project component<br>(e.g., aquaculture, infrastructure, post-harvest)] --> B[Prepare Detailed Project Report (DPR)<br>as per scheme guidelines]
    B --> C[Submit DPR to respective State Fisheries Department]
    C --> D[State Fisheries Department forwards proposal<br>to Department of Fisheries, GoI<br>for appraisal and approval]
    D --> E{Approval?}
    E -->|Yes| F[Obtain sanction letter<br>and proceed with implementation]
    E -->|No| G[Revise and resubmit DPR<br>based on feedback]
    F --> H[Utilize funds per approved plan]
    H --> I[Submit periodic progress reports<br>and utilization certificates]
    I --> J{Claim reimbursement?}
    J -->|Yes| K[Receive funds in installments<br>based on milestone achievement]
    J -->|No| L[Continue implementation<br>pending next milestone]
```

### Key Caveats
- **Scheme Period**: Limited to **2015-16 to 2019-20**; no longer accepting new applications
- **Funding Dependency**: Subject to budget availability and approval of project proposals by Department of Fisheries
- **Compliance Requirements**: Beneficiaries must adhere to scheme-specific guidelines and submit utilization certificates for fund release
- **Timeline Adherence**: Projects must be completed within stipulated timelines; delays may affect fund disbursement
- **Scheme Subsumption**: Replaced by **Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)** from **2020-21**

### Required Documents
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
2. Land ownership/lease documents
3. Project cost estimates
4. Technical feasibility report
5. Environmental clearance (if applicable)
6. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from relevant authorities
7. Bank appraisal note (for loan linkage)
8. Implementation schedule
9. Undertaking from the beneficiary
10. Registration/incorporation certificate (for companies, cooperatives, etc.)
