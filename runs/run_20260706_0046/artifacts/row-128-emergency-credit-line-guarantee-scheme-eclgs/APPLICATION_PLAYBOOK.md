# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Borrower is a Business Enterprise or MSME (as per Udyam Registration or equivalent)
- [ ] Loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020 exists with the Member Lending Institution (MLI)
- [ ] MLI is registered with NCGTC as a Member Lending Institution (MLI) by submitting a signed and stamped undertaking on stamp paper and a copy of Board Resolution
- [ ] Loan being applied for is an additional term loan or working capital loan (not for refinancing existing debt)
- [ ] Loan is extended to meet liquidity needs arising from pandemic-induced distress or other crises like the West Asia situation
- [ ] MLI has login credentials on the NCGTC portal after successful registration
- [ ] Guarantee fee will be paid monthly by the MLI to NCGTC Trust by the 7th day of each month for the outstanding loan amount on the closing day of the previous month

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN card of the borrowing entity
- GST registration certificate
- Audited financial statements (ITR) for last 2 years
- Bank statements of the borrowing entity for last 6 months
- Udyam Registration Certificate (for MSMEs)
- Incorporation certificate / Partnership deed / LLP agreement
- Board resolution authorizing the loan application
- KYC documents of authorized signatories

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Signed and stamped undertaking on stamp paper of requisite value (to be stamped as an Agreement under the local Stamp Act)
- Copy of Board Resolution authorizing the undertaking
- Loan agreement with the borrower
- Documents evidencing loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020
- Proof of borrower eligibility (Business Enterprise/MSME status via Udyam or equivalent)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible scans or clear photographs
- File names must match the document type (e.g., "Udyam_Registration.pdf", "Board_Resolution.pdf")
- PDF file size should not exceed 5 MB per document
- Undertaking and Board Resolution must be on stamp paper of appropriate value and duly notarized if required
- All documents must be in English or accompanied by a certified translation
- Signatures and seals must be visible and authentic

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for ECLGS. However, verify with \ nodal \ officer \ if \ the \ MLI \ requires \ additional \ justification \ for \ the \ loan \ purpose \ (e.g., \ working \ capital \ or \ term \ loan \ details).  
### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Ensure the MLI is logged into the NCGTC portal using credentials created post-registration.  
2. Select “Apply for Guarantee Cover” under the ECLGS section.  
3. Enter borrower details accurately as per Udyam/MSME registration.  
4. Input loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020, and the additional loan amount being sought.  
5. Specify loan purpose as “additional term loan” or “working capital loan” only.  
6. Upload all scheme-specific and universal documents in the prescribed format.  
7. Confirm that the undertaking and Board Resolution are uploaded as per Annexure II format.  
8. Double-check all entries before final submission; no edits allowed post-submission.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All fields in the application form are complete and accurate  
- [ ] Scheme-specific documents (undertaking, Board Resolution, loan agreement, Feb 29, 2020 outstanding proof) are attached  
- [ ] Universal documents (PAN, GST, ITR, bank statements, Udyam) are attached  
- [ ] File sizes are within limits and names are standardized  
- [ ] Declarations and undertakings are signed and stamped  
- [ ] Loan purpose is correctly classified as additional term loan or working capital loan  
- [ ] MLI confirms guarantee fee payment readiness (monthly by 7th)  
- [ ] Application is reviewed by a second consultant before submission

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation page showing the application reference number and timestamp.  
- Store the reference number securely in the case file.  
- Set an automated follow-up reminder for 7 days post-submission to check application status.  
- Log the submission date and time in the case tracker.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from NCGTC specifying the sanctioned guarantee amount  
- [ ] Confirm that the guarantee cover is 100% of the eligible loan amount (based on outstanding as of Feb 29, 2020)  
- [ ] Ensure the MLI sets up monthly guarantee fee payment (by 7th of each month)  
- [ ] Monitor loan disbursement to the borrower and ensure end-use for additional term loan or working capital  
- [ ] Prepare utilization certificate post-loan disbursement for internal records  
- [ ] Track invoice for success fee payable to the consulting firm upon successful approval and disbursement  
- [ ] Set up reminders for monthly guarantee fee payments and annual compliance checks  
- [ ] Archive all approval documents, correspondence, and submission records for audit purposes
