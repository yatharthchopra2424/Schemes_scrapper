## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit delivered under ECLGS, which provides 100% guarantee coverage on eligible loans based on loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020, enabling collateral-free lending for business continuity.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: The scheme’s processing time involves MLI registration, loan disbursement, and claim submission, with guarantee fees payable monthly by MLIs; success fee revenue recognition assumes a 3-month lag from client engagement to approval, reflecting operational timelines observed in ECLGS 5.0 (launched May 2026) and earlier versions.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Pricing aligns with client scale — Starter tier excluded as ECLGS targets MSMEs and Business Enterprises with loan outstanding as of Feb 29, 2020, typically exceeding micro-enterprise thresholds.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: Fees are structured to reflect the scheme’s low risk profile for lenders (100% guarantee via NCGTC) and high impact for borrowers (access to working capital/term loans without collateral), supported by testimonials showing business revival and salary retention.
