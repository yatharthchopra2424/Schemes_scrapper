# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/We specialize in helping MSMEs and business enterprises access government-backed liquidity support during periods of distress. The Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (ECLGS) is designed specifically for businesses like yours that need collateral-free working capital or term loans based on their pre-pandemic financial strength. I’d like to understand your current situation to see if this scheme could be a fit.)

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your business incorporated or registered as an MSME/Business Enterprise?
2. What was your total loan outstanding with banks or NBFCs as of February 29, 2020?
3. Are you currently seeking additional term loan or working capital support due to liquidity constraints?
4. Has your business been adversely impacted by external crises such as the pandemic or geopolitical situations like the West Asia situation?
5. Are you looking for collateral-free lending options to avoid pledging additional assets?
6. Are you open to working through your existing bank or NBFC to access this guarantee-backed facility?
7. Do you have documentation to prove your loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020, and your MSME/Business Enterprise status?
8. Are you open to a no-upfront-cost eligibility check with a success-fee-only model?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is an MSME or Business Enterprise  
- [ ] Has loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020  
- [ ] Seeking additional term loan or working capital support  
- [ ] Impacted by pandemic or distress situations (e.g., West Asia crisis)  
- [ ] Willing to apply through a Member Lending Institution (MLI)  
- [ ] Can provide documents: loan outstanding proof as of Feb 29, 2020, MSME status, board resolution, undertaking on stamp paper  
- [ ] Not applying directly — understands scheme works via banks/NBFCs  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it looks like your business may qualify for the Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (ECLGS), which offers 100% guarantee coverage to banks and NBFCs on loans extended to eligible MSMEs based on their February 29, 2020 outstanding. This could help you access collateral-free working capital or term loans to stabilize operations. May I share how it works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many MSMEs and business enterprises continue to face liquidity pressures due to past disruptions like the pandemic or ongoing crises such as the West Asia situation. Banks often hesitate to lend without collateral, leaving businesses unable to access the working capital or term loans needed for continuity and growth.

**Scheme intro:**
The Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (ECLGS), administered by the National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company Ltd (NCGTC) under the Ministry of Finance, provides 100% guarantee coverage to Banks and NBFCs on credit extended to eligible Business Enterprises/MSMEs based on their loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020. The scheme supports additional term loans or working capital loans to meet liquidity needs arising from distress.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your business is an MSME or Business Enterprise — a core eligibility criterion  
- You have loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020 — the basis for calculating eligible loan amount  
- You are seeking additional term loan or working capital support — the exact purpose the scheme covers  
- You’ve been impacted by distress (pandemic/West Asia) — aligning with the scheme’s objective to support business continuity
