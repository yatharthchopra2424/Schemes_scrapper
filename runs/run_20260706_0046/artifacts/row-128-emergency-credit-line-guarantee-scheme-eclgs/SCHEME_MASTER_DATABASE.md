### Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (ECLGS)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company Ltd (NCGTC)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.ncgtc.in/
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** ECLGS 5.0 launched in May 2026; specific end date not mentioned in evidence. Earlier versions had end dates (e.g., ECLGS 1.0-4.0 loans to be sanctioned by 30.09.2021 and disbursed by 31.12.2021). Current operational status should be verified via official channels.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Business Enterprises/MSMEs
- Age of business: Not specified in evidence
- Sector: Not specified in evidence
- Revenue cap: Not specified in evidence
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: Eligible borrowers include Business Enterprises and MSMEs seeking additional term loan or working capital support; Member Lending Institutions (MLIs) must register with NCGTC by submitting an undertaking on stamp paper and a board resolution to avail guarantee cover under the scheme; guarantee coverage is subject to defined criteria and loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 100% guarantee coverage on eligible loans extended by Member Lending Institutions (MLIs) to eligible borrowers
- Disbursement mode: Through banks and NBFCs (financial support channeled through banks and NBFCs)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (per loan application)

#### Documents Required
1. Signed and stamped undertaking on stamp paper of requisite value
2. Copy of Board Resolution
3. Loan agreement with borrower
4. Documents evidencing loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020
5. Proof of borrower eligibility (Business Enterprise/MSME status)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Eligible Lending Institutions (ELIs) must register with NCGTC as a Member Lending Institution (MLI) by submitting a signed and stamped undertaking on stamp paper of requisite value (to be stamped as an Agreement under the local Stamp Act) along with a copy of Board Resolution.
2. Submit the undertaking and documents to the Registered Office of NCGTC in Mumbai.
3. Upon successful registration, login credentials are created for the MLI on the NCGTC portal.
4. The MLI can then apply for guarantee cover on loans extended by it to eligible borrowers through the NCGTC portal.
5. After disbursement of the loan to the borrower, the MLI must submit a claim for guarantee cover to NCGTC.
6. Guarantee fee is payable by the MLI to the Trust by the 7th day of each month for the outstanding loan amount on the closing day of the previous month.
