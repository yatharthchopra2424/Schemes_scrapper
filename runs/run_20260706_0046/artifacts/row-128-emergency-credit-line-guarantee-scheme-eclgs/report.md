# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
The **Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (ECLGS)** is a loan-type scheme administered by the **National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company Ltd (NCGTC)**, a wholly owned company of the Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India. Launched in May 2026 as **ECLGS 5.0**, it operates on a **Pan-India** geographic scope. The scheme is designed to provide liquidity support to businesses adversely impacted by lockdowns due to the COVID-19 pandemic and exceptional circumstances such as the West Asia crisis. It enables Banks & NBFCs to extend collateral-free loans to Business Enterprises/MSMEs by providing **100% guarantee coverage** on eligible loans. The scheme is administered through Member Lending Institutions (MLIs); direct borrower application is not permitted.

### Objectives
- Provide liquidity support to businesses adversely impacted by lockdown due to COVID-19 pandemic  
- Enable Banks & NBFCs to extend collateral-free loans to Business Enterprises/MSMEs  
- Meet additional term loan or additional working capital requirements of eligible borrowers  
- Support borrowers by encouraging MLIs to provide additional working capital term loans for business continuity and stability  
- Address exceptional circumstances caused by the West Asia crisis through ECLGS 5.0  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Criteria | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Eligible Borrowers** | Business Enterprises and MSMEs seeking additional term loan or working capital support. Must have loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020. |
| **Eligible Lending Institutions (MLIs)** | Banks & NBFCs that register with NCGTC by submitting a signed and stamped undertaking on stamp paper and a copy of Board Resolution. |
| **Loan Purpose** | Additional term loan or working capital only (to meet liquidity needs from pandemic-induced distress or crises like West Asia situation). |
| **Guarantee Coverage** | 100% guarantee coverage on eligible loans extended by MLIs to eligible borrowers. |
| **Key Restriction** | Direct borrower application not permitted; scheme administered exclusively through MLIs. |
| **Basis for Eligibility** | Loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020, subject to defined criteria. |

> **> Warning:** Guarantee coverage is subject to defined criteria and loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020. Loans must be for additional term loan or working capital purposes only. Guarantee fee must be paid monthly by MLIs to NCGTC Trust. Earlier versions (ECLGS 1.0-4.0) had sanction deadline of 30.09.2021 and disbursement by 31.12.2021; current operational status of ECLGS 5.0 should be verified via official channels.

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Benefit | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **100% Guarantee Coverage** | NCGTC bears default risk up to the guaranteed amount for loans extended by MLIs to eligible borrowers. |
| **Collateral-Free Lending** | Facilitates lending without requiring additional collateral from borrowers. |
| **Business Continuity & Stability** | Supports business operations during periods of distress (pandemic, West Asia crisis). |
| **Access to Working Capital & Term Loans** | Enables access to funds without additional collateral requirements. |
| **Backstop for Lenders** | Reduces risk perception for lenders extending credit to stressed sectors. |

**Financial Support Mechanism:**  
The scheme provides 100% guarantee coverage to Banks and NBFCs on credit extended to eligible borrowers (Business Enterprises/MSMEs) based on their loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020. The guarantee covers additional term loans or working capital loans extended by MLIs to meet liquidity needs arising from pandemic-induced distress or other crises like the West Asia situation. Financial support is channeled through banks and NBFCs, with NCGTC bearing the default risk up to the guaranteed amount.

**Guarantee Fee:** Payable by the MLI to the Trust by the 7th day of each month for the outstanding loan amount on the closing day of the previous month.

### Required Documents
1. Signed and stamped undertaking on stamp paper of requisite value (to be stamped as an Agreement under the local Stamp Act)  
2. Copy of Board Resolution  
3. Loan agreement with borrower  
4. Documents evidencing loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020  
5. Proof of borrower eligibility (Business Enterprise/MSME status)  

### Application Process (Mermaid Flowchart)
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Eligible Lending Institution (ELI)] -->|1. Register with NCGTC| B[Submit Undertaking on Stamp Paper + Board Resolution]
    B -->|2. Submit to NCGTC Registered Office (Mumbai)| C[NCGTC Processes Registration]
    C -->|3. Successful Registration| D[Login Credentials Created for MLI on NCGTC Portal]
    D -->|4. Apply for Guarantee Cover| E[MLI Applies for Guarantee on Loans to Eligible Borrowers via NCGTC Portal]
    E -->|5. Disburse Loan to Borrower| F[MLI Disburses Loan to Borrower]
    F -->|6. Submit Claim for Guarantee Cover| G[MLI Submits Claim to NCGTC Post-Disbursement]
    G -->|7. Guarantee Fee Payment| H[MLI Pays Guarantee Fee by 7th of Each Month for Previous Month's Outstanding Amount]
    H -->|8. Trust Processes Claim| I[NCGTC Reviews and Settles Claim]
    I -->|9. Claim Settlement| J[Trust Pays Guarantee Cover to MLI]
```
