# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is a micro unit seeking a loan from a Bank / NBFC / MFI / other financial intermediary engaged in providing credit facilities.
- [ ] The loan amount does not exceed Rs. 10 lakh (for standard loans under CGFMU).
- [ ] For PMJDY overdraft loans, the amount does not exceed Rs. 10,000.
- [ ] The lending institution (Bank / NBFC / MFI / other financial intermediary) is eligible to extend credit under the scheme.
- [ ] The micro unit is engaged in any sector (no sector-specific restrictions mentioned).
- [ ] No minimum turnover, employee count, or age of business specified for the micro unit borrower.
- [ ] The lending institution must be registered with NCGTC as an Eligible Lending Institution (ELI) to apply for guarantee cover.

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Business registration proof (Udyam Registration Certificate)
- PAN Card of the business/entity
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) for last 2 financial years
- Audited financial statements (if applicable)
- KYC documents of proprietor/partners/directors (Aadhaar, PAN, photograph)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Signed and stamped undertaking (from the lending institution to NCGTC)
- Board Resolution (from the lending institution authorizing the undertaking)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and in PDF or clear scanned format.
- File size for each document should not exceed 5 MB.
- Document names must match the checklist (e.g., "Udyam_Registration.pdf", "Board_Resolution.pdf").
- Undertaking and Board Resolution must be on stamp paper of requisite value and duly notarized/stamped as per local Stamp Act.
- All documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, a certified translation must be attached.
- Signatures on undertaking and Board Resolution must be of an authorized official of the lending institution.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- **Not required** for CGFMU guarantee cover application. The scheme does not mandate a Detailed Project Report (DPR) for eligibility or guarantee approval. However, the underlying loan application to the lending institution may require a project report as per their internal policies.  
- **Verify with nodal officer** if the lending institution requires a DPR for loan sanction.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Ensure the lending institution is already registered with NCGTC as an Eligible Lending Institution (ELI); if not, complete ELI registration first (undertaking + Board Resolution).  
2. Log in to the NCGTC portal using ELI credentials.  
3. Select "Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU)" from the product list.  
4. Fill in borrower details: name, Udyam number, loan amount, purpose, and tenure.  
5. Enter loan sanction details: sanction date, loan account number, interest rate.  
6. Upload the loan sanction letter and disbursement proof (if already disbursed).  
7. Confirm that the loan is collateral-free and extended to an eligible micro unit.  
8. Declare that the loan meets all eligibility criteria under CGFMU (amount ≤ Rs. 10 lakh or PMJDY OD ≤ Rs. 10,000).  
9. Review all entries before final submission; incorrect data may lead to rejection.  
10. Submit the application and note the application reference number.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All borrower and loan details correctly filled in the application form.  
- [ ] Loan amount ≤ Rs. 10 lakh (or PMJDY OD ≤ Rs. 10,000).  
- [ ] Lending institution is a registered ELI with NCGTC.  
- [ ] Undertaking and Board Resolution are uploaded and valid.  
- [ ] Loan sanction letter and disbursement proof (if applicable) are attached.  
- [ ] Declarations regarding collateral-free nature and eligibility are signed.  
- [ ] Application reference number generated and recorded.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the NCGTC portal (https://www.ncgtc.in) after internal review.  
- Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing the application reference number and timestamp.  
- Store the reference number in the case file and set a follow-up reminder for 7 working days post-submission.  
- Save all submitted documents and screenshots in a dedicated folder labeled with the Case ID.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from NCGTC confirming guarantee cover.  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned guarantee amount matches the loan amount (up to Rs. 10 lakh or Rs. 10,000 for PMJDY OD).  
- [ ] Track monthly guarantee fee payment (due by the 7th of each month) as per scheme requirements.  
- [ ] Ensure the lending institution recovers the loan from the borrower and shares recoveries with NCGTC proportionately after legal cost adjustment.  
- [ ] Utilization certificate: Not required under CGFMU; guarantee is tied to loan default, not end-use monitoring.  
- [ ] Invoice success fee: [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT] (as per firm’s internal policy).  
- [ ] Maintain records of all correspondence, approvals, and fee payments for audit and renewal purposes.
