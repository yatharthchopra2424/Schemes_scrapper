## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit delivered—here, guarantee coverage for loans up to ₹10 lakh for micro units, ensuring consultant compensation reflects the tangible credit access enabled.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect scheme risk profile; CGFMU has a rolling application basis with no fixed deadline, but monthly guarantee fee compliance (payable by 7th each month) introduces administrative overhead, justifying moderate success fees.
3. **Accessibility for Target Segment**: Starter tier pricing aligns with scheme eligibility for micro units (turnover typically < ₹10 Cr, though scheme focuses on loan size, not turnover), making services accessible to the intended beneficiary base.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; the scheme’s implementing agency (NCGTC) is a government trustee company, reducing perceived risk and supporting clear, ethical pricing.
