# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping micro units access collateral-free credit through government-backed guarantee schemes. The Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU) is designed specifically for small businesses like yours that struggle to get loans due to lack of collateral. I’d like to understand your current financing needs and see if this scheme could support your growth.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of business do you run, and when was it incorporated or registered?
- Are you currently seeking a loan from a bank, NBFC, or MFI for working capital or equipment?
- Have you faced challenges in getting loan approval due to lack of collateral or credit history?
- Are you looking for a loan amount up to Rs.10 lakh, or do you have a PMJDY account with an overdraft facility?
- Are you engaged in any income-generating activity as a micro unit (e.g., manufacturing, trading, services)?
- Do you have access to a bank or financial institution that is registered with NCGTC as an Eligible Lending Institution (ELI)?
- Are you prepared to provide a signed undertaking and board resolution if required by the lending institution?
- Are you aware of the monthly guarantee fee obligation that the lending institution must pay to NCGTC?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is classified as a micro unit (as per MSME definition)
- [ ] Seeking loan from a Bank/NBFC/MFI/other financial intermediary
- [ ] Loan amount does not exceed Rs.10 lakh (or Rs.10,000 for PMJDY overdraft)
- [ ] Business is engaged in legitimate credit-seeking activity
- [ ] Applicant is willing to work with an ELI registered with NCGTC
- [ ] Can provide signed undertaking and board resolution (via ELI)
- [ ] Understands that guarantee fee is paid monthly by the ELI (not the borrower)
- [ ] Not currently an NPA with any lender (if applicable)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re a micro unit looking for collateral-free credit up to Rs.10 lakh — which is exactly what the Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU) was created to support. Let me explain how this scheme works and how it could help you secure the funding you need.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Micro units in India often struggle to access formal credit because they lack collateral or have limited credit history. Banks and NBFCs perceive them as high-risk, leading to loan rejections or unfavorable terms. This limits their ability to invest in working capital, equipment, or business expansion — especially for underserved entrepreneurs.

**Scheme intro:**
The Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU), administered by the National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company Ltd (NCGTC) under the Ministry of Finance, provides guarantee coverage for loans up to Rs.10 lakh sanctioned by Banks, NBFCs, MFIs, or other financial intermediaries to eligible micro units. It also covers overdraft loans of up to Rs.10,000 under PMJDY accounts. The scheme reduces lending risk for financial institutions, enabling collateral-free loan access for micro enterprises.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a micro unit seeking credit from a bank/NBFC/MFI — core eligibility criterion
- Your loan requirement is within the Rs.10 lakh limit (or Rs.10,000 for PMJDY overdraft)
- You are engaged in a legitimate income-generating activity as a micro enterprise
- You can work with an Eligible Lending Institution (ELI) registered with NCGTC

Our fee is success-based — you only pay if we help you secure the guarantee cover. Shall we proceed with a quick document review to confirm your eligibility?

We’ve helped many micro units secure loans under this scheme because it reduces the lender’s risk. |
| "How much do you charge?" | [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT] |
| "I'll try it myself first" | Common mistakes include applying through a non-registered lending institution (ELI not on NCGTC’s portal) and failing to ensure the ELI submits the signed undertaking and board resolution. | "We're too small / too big" | The scheme is specifically for micro units — there’s no minimum size, but the loan limit is Rs.10 lakh (or Rs.10,000 for PMJDY overdraft). If you need more than that, we can explore other NCGTC schemes like CGSSI or MCGS-MSME. |
| "I don't have the documents ready" | The three most commonly missing documents are: (1) signed and stamped undertaking from the ELI, (2) board resolution of the ELI authorizing the application, and (3) loan sanction letter from the bank/NBFC/MFI.
