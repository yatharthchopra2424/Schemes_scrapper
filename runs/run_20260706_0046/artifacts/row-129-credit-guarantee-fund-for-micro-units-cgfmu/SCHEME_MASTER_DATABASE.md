### Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company Ltd (NCGTC)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.ncgtc.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Micro units seeking loans from Banks / NBFCs / MFIs / other financial intermediaries
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Not specified
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Overdraft loan amount of Rs.10,000 sanctioned under PMJDY accounts is also eligible for coverage

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Guarantee coverage for loans up to Rs.10 Lakh; overdraft loan amount of Rs.10,000 under PMJDY accounts also eligible
- Disbursement mode: Guarantee coverage (indirect benefit via lending institutions)
- Duration of benefit: As long as the loan is outstanding and guarantee fee is paid

#### Documents Required
1. Signed and stamped undertaking
2. Board Resolution

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Eligible Lending Institutions (ELIs) must register with NCGTC by submitting a signed and stamped undertaking along with Board Resolution.
2. Upon successful registration, login credentials are created for the ELI on NCGTC’s portal.
3. The ELI can then apply for guarantee cover on loans extended to eligible micro units via the NCGTC portal.
4. Guarantee fee is payable monthly by the 7th day of each month for the outstanding loan amount.
