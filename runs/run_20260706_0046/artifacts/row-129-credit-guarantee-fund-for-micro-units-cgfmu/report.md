# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU)** is a Central Sector Scheme administered by the **National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company Ltd (NCGTC)**, a wholly owned company of the Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India. Established in 2014 under the Companies Act, NCGTC acts, 1956, NCGTC acts as a unified mechanism for administering various credit guarantee schemes across the country. The scheme operates on a **rolling basis** with no fixed deadline, last updated in 2026.

### Objectives
- Provide guarantee coverage for loans up to Rs.10 lakh to eligible micro units  
- Cover overdraft loan amounts of Rs.10,000 under PMJDY accounts  
- Facilitate collateral-free credit access for micro enterprises  
- Strengthen flow of institutional credit to micro units  
- Reduce barriers to credit access for underserved sections

### Key Caveats & Operational Rules
- **Guarantee Fee Payment**: Must be paid monthly by the **7th day** of each month for the outstanding loan amount  
- **Recovery Sharing**: Recovery from borrowers requires proportionate sharing with NCGTC after legal cost adjustment  
- **Administering Agency**: Scheme is administered by NCGTC as a trustee company for the Credit Guarantee Fund  
- **Portal**: Applications processed via [https://www.ncgtc.in](https://www.ncgtc.in)  
- **Documentation Required**:  
  1. Signed and stamped undertaking  
  2. Board Resolution  

> **Warning**: Failure to pay guarantee fee by the 7th of each month may result in penalties or suspension of guarantee coverage.

### Supporting Evidence Summary
- NCGTC manages multiple credit guarantee funds including CGFMU, ECLGS, CGSMFI-2.0, etc.  
- The scheme is part of NCGTC’s portfolio of 20 dedicated credit guarantee trusts with a committed corpus of ₹97,777.50 crore as of March 31, 2026  
- NCGTC’s vision: “To be India’s trusted institution fostering inclusive growth and economic empowerment for all”  
- Mission: “To enable banks and financial institutions to extend collateral-free loans across sectors & sections deficient in credit”
