# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The enterprise is a Micro or Small Enterprise (MSE) as per MSMED Act criteria  
- [ ] The enterprise is engaged in manufacturing or services (trading activities without value addition, packing, or branding are not eligible)  
- [ ] The enterprise has Udyam Registration  
- [ ] The enterprise is applying for or already registered under NSIC’s Single Point Registration Scheme (SPRS)  
- [ ] If applying for SPRS registration, the enterprise’s factory inspection must be completed before empanelment under the Marketing Facilitation Scheme  
- [ ] The enterprise is not engaged in trading activities without value addition, packing, or branding  
- [ ] The enterprise is not involved in manufacture of medicine and drugs except Ayurvedic, Unani, Siddha, or Homeopathic medicines  
- [ ] The enterprise is not engaged in wholesale trading, retail trading, or commission agency  
- [ ] The proprietor/partner/director/Karta has not been convicted for any criminal offence  
- [ ] The enterprise has not been blacklisted till expiry of such period

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN Card (self-attested)  
- Udyam Registration Certificate (self-attested)  
- Latest Electricity Bill Copy (self-attested)  
- Audited Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss Account, Schedule of Fixed Assets, Schedule of Revenue from Operations (for last 3 years, duly signed by authorized person under seal)  
- Statement showing Results of Operation for last 3 years duly signed by Chartered Accountant with UDIN (Annexure ‘C’)  
- Bankers’ Report giving details of financial status as per Performa (Draft at Annexure ‘E’)  
- Declaration signed by applicant MSE Unit accepting conditions of registration (Annexure D)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Application form in prescribed format (Annexures – A)  
- Duly signed by authorized signatory (Proprietor/Partner(s)/Director(s))  
- Documents mentioned in the application form (as specified in Annexures – A)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be self-attested where required  
- File names must match the document type and applicant name  
- File size should be within portal limits (typically <5 MB per file)  
- Scanned copies must be legible and in PDF or JPEG format  
- Declarations and forms must be signed and dated  
- UDIN must be present on CA-signed documents  
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in regional language, certified translation required  

### Client Communication Template

**Key Points:**  
- Scheme operates on a **rolling basis** with no fixed deadline; applications accepted year-round  
- Eligibility requires registration or application for NSIC’s **Single Point Registration Scheme (SPRS)** with completed factory inspection

- Annual enlistment fee based on SPRS monetary limit:

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for this scheme. The application is based on the prescribed form (Annexures – A) and supporting documents. If in doubt, verify with the nodal officer at the nearest NSIC branch office.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use only the prescribed format (Annexures – A) available on the NSIC SPRS portal  
2. Fill all fields accurately; leave no blank sections unless marked optional  
3. Ensure the form is signed by the authorized signatory (Proprietor/Partner/Director) with company seal (if applicable)  
4. Attach all documents listed in the application form; missing documents will lead to rejection  
5. Declare acceptance of registration conditions as per Annexure D  
6. For manufacturing units, provide complete details of plant & machinery, quality control equipment, and testing facilities  
7. For service units, detail service activities, capacity, and supporting infrastructure  
8. Double-check Udyam Registration number and PAN match across all documents  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Application form (Annexures – A) is completely filled and signed  
- [ ] All mandatory documents are attached and legible  
- [ ] Declarations (Annexure D) are signed and dated  
- [ ] Udyam Registration number is consistent across documents  
- [ ] PAN is valid and matches entity name  
- [ ] Audited financials are for the last 3 years and signed by authorized person  
- [ ] CA-signed statements include UDIN  
- [ ] Bankers’ report is on bank letterhead and signed  
- [ ] Factory inspection report is attached (if applying for fresh SPRS registration)  
- [ ] For renewal: Original SPRS Enlistment Certificate is included for return  
- [ ] All documents are self-attested where required  
- [ ] Application is ready for upload on https://nsicspronline.com

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed application and documents via the online portal: https://nsicspronline.com  
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation page showing the application/reference number  
- Store the reference number securely in the case file  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days post-submission to check application status  
- Monitor email and portal for requests for additional information or inspection scheduling

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and store the approval letter/empanelment certificate from NSIC  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned monetary limit and enlistment validity period (2 years)  
- [ ] Note the annual enlistment fee based on SPRS monetary limit (Rs. 1000/2500/5000 + GST) and mark renewal date  
- [ ] For SC/ST-owned units, confirm exemption from enlistment fee  
- [ ] Upon participation in a tender facilitation process, ensure NSIC’s facilitation charges (5% of award value, inclusive of GST) are understood and payable in milestones:  
  - 50% upon acceptance of award  
  - 25% at total execution  
  - 25% upon receipt of payment  
- [ ] Maintain records of all tenders participated in via NSIC facilitation  
- [ ] Prepare for renewal 6 months before certificate expiry to avail 50% fee discount  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with SPRS conditions: furnish performance statement every two years, inform NSIC of product/process changes within 15 days, maintain integrity in business practices  
- [ ] Verify that the unit does not engage in prohibited activities (trading without value addition, etc.) to avoid delisting
