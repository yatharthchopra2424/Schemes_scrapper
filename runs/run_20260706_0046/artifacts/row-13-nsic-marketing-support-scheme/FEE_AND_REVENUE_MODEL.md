## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the non-financial facilitation nature of the NSIC Marketing Support Scheme, where fees are tied to successful contract facilitation rather than upfront capital.
2. Transparent milestone-based success fees mirroring NSIC’s 5% facilitation charge structure (50% on award acceptance, 25% on execution, 25% on payment receipt).
3. Risk-adjusted retainers reflecting the scheme’s rolling application basis and dependency on client SPRS registration status and factory inspection completion.
4. Scheme-specific note: Moderate risk profile due to NSIC’s facilitative role (no invoicing/payment involvement) and dependency on buyer acceptance of L1 recommendation; success hinges on tender participation and MSE competitiveness, not credit risk.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description:** Retainer fee for NSIC Marketing Support Scheme facilitation services, including SPRS registration support, tender marketing guidance, and consortium formation assistance under the National Small Industries Corporation Ltd. scheme.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description:** Success fee for NSIC Marketing Support Scheme facilitation services, where NSIC acts as facilitator to recommend L1 bidders to Central Government & PSU buyers, charging 5% of award value as facilitation fee (inclusive of GST) upon milestone achievement. Implementing Agency: National Small Industries Corporation Ltd. (NSIC).
