# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) access government procurement opportunities through NSIC’s Marketing Facilitation Scheme. Many manufacturers and service providers struggle to connect with government buyers despite having quality offerings. I’d like to understand your current market access challenges and see if this scheme aligns with your growth goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What products or services does your business currently offer to government or PSU buyers?
2. Are you currently registered under NSIC’s Single Point Registration Scheme (SPRS), or planning to apply?
3. What is your Udyam registration number and when was your enterprise incorporated?
4. Do you engage in pure trading activities, or do you add value through manufacturing, packing, or branding?
5. What is your annual turnover based on the last three years’ audited balance sheets?
6. Are you an SC/ST-owned enterprise, as this affects fee exemptions under the scheme?
7. Have you participated in government tenders before, and what challenges did you face in the process?
8. Are you open to forming or joining a consortium with other MSEs to bid for larger government contracts?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is a Micro or Small Enterprise (MSE) as per MSMED Act
- [ ] Engaged in manufacturing or services (not pure trading without value addition)
- [ ] Has Udyam Registration
- [ ] Factory inspection completed or willing to undergo inspection for SPRS
- [ ] Not engaged in prohibited activities (e.g., medicine/drugs manufacturing except AYUSH)
- [ ] Willing to register or already registered under SPRS
- [ ] Located in India (Pan-India scheme)
- [ ] Open to participating in government tender facilitation process

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your business could benefit from NSIC’s Marketing Facilitation Scheme, which helps MSEs like yours access government tenders through facilitation services—without requiring loans or grants. The scheme enables you to compete for government contracts by positioning you as the L1 bidder, with NSIC charging only a 5% facilitation fee on awarded contract value.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many MSEs struggle to access government procurement due to complex tender processes, lack of visibility, and inability to compete on price with larger firms. Despite quality offerings, they miss out on opportunities because they aren’t enlisted in government supplier databases or face barriers like EMD payments and limited tender access.

**Scheme intro:**
The **NSIC Marketing Facilitation Scheme (MFS)**, administered by the National Small Industries Corporation Ltd. (NSIC), a Government of India Enterprise under the Ministry of MSME, facilitates MSE participation in government purchases. It does not provide direct financial support but offers non-financial facilitation services including tender marketing, consortium formation, and recommendation of the L1 (lowest) bidder to buyers. NSIC charges a facilitation fee of **5% of the award value** (inclusive of GST), payable in milestones: 50% upon award acceptance, 25% at total execution, and 25% upon receipt of payment. No credit, loan, or subsidy is extended by NSIC.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your business is engaged in manufacturing/services with value addition (not pure trading)
- You have or are willing to obtain Udyam Registration
- You are prepared for or have completed factory inspection for SPRS empanelment
- You operate within India and target government/PSU buyers

We offer a free, no-obligation eligibility check. If you qualify, we assist with SPRS registration, document preparation, and enlistment under MFS—charging only a success fee upon contract award. Shall we proceed with a quick document review to confirm your eligibility?
