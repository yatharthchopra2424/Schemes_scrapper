### NSIC Marketing Support Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Small Industries Corporation Ltd. (NSIC)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nsic.co.in/Schemes/MarketingFacilitation
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Micro & Small Enterprises (MSEs)
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Manufacturing & Services (traders not eligible)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be registered or applying for SPRS registration with factory inspection completed; units engaged in trading activities without value addition, packing, or branding are not eligible

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Non-financial facilitation services (tender marketing, consortium formation, recommendation of L1 bidders); NSIC charges a facilitation fee of 5% of the award value, payable in milestones
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (facilitation charges received in phases: 50% upon first milestone, 25% at second milestone, 25% upon final milestone)
- Duration of benefit: One-time per contract award

#### Documents Required
1. Application form in prescribed format (Annexures – A)
2. Duly signed by authorized signatory (Proprietor/Partner(s)/Director(s))
3. Documents mentioned in the application form

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Submit application form in prescribed format (Annexures – A) duly signed by authorized signatory (Proprietor/Partner(s)/Director(s)).
2. Include required documents as mentioned in the application form.
3. Apply for enlistment under the Tender Marketing Scheme.
4. Ensure factory inspection is completed before empanelment.
5. Pay applicable annual fee based on SPRS monetary limit: Rs. 1000+GST (up to Rs.100 lacs), Rs. 2500+GST (Rs.100-500 lacs), Rs. 5000+GST (above Rs.500 lacs).
6. SC/ST-owned units are exempt from enlistment fee.
7. Await empanelment approval from NSIC.
8. Upon empanelment, participate in tender facilitation process as per scheme guidelines.
