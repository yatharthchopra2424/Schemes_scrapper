# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The NSIC Marketing Support Scheme (also known as the Marketing Facilitation Scheme or MFS) is a non-financial facilitation scheme implemented by the National Small Industries Corporation Ltd. (NSIC) under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME). It aims to promote the products of Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) by facilitating their participation in government procurement processes through tender marketing, consortium formation, and recommendation of L1 bidders. The scheme does not provide direct financial support such as grants, loans, or subsidies. Instead, it offers facilitation services where NSIC acts as a facilitator (not an intermediary) to recommend the L1 bidder (lowest bidder) to the buyer, who then awards the contract. NSIC receives facilitation charges in phases based on project milestones.

### Objectives
- Promote the products of Micro and Small Entrepreneurs
- Facilitate MSEs to market goods/services individually or collectively through consortium
- Review and update the scheme to enhance effectiveness (last reviewed in 2024)
- Facilitate tender participation for MSEs with government and PSU buyers
- Ensure streamlined and transparent procurement process for buyers
- Support MSEs in accessing government supply chains without acting as an intermediary
- Enable MSEs to supply up to 25% of requirement by matching L1 price under price band policy
- Reserve 358 items for exclusive purchase from MSE sector

### Eligibility Matrix
| Eligibility Criteria | Details |
|----------------------|---------|
| Enterprise Type | Only Micro & Small Enterprises (MSEs) registered with NSIC under its Single Point Registration Scheme (SPRS) are eligible. Also covers MSEs who apply for SPRS registration along with all required documents and whose factory is inspected before empanelment under MFS. |
| Business Activity | Units engaged in manufacturing & services are eligible. Units engaging in trading activities without value addition, packing, or branding are **not eligible**. |
| Registration Requirement | Must be registered or applying for SPRS with factory inspection completed before empanelment. |
| Special Provision | Units owned by SC/ST entrepreneurs are exempted from the enlistment fee. |
| Consortia | No fee for formation or renewal of consortia, but enlistment fee applies during participation in any enquiry by consortia members. |

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Benefit Type | Details |
|--------------|---------|
| **Facilitation Services** | NSIC offers facilitation services to:<br>- Central Governments & their Departments<br>- PSUs<br>- State/UT Governments & their Departments<br>- Local bodies<br>- Public/reputed Private Sector Companies |
| **Role of NSIC** | Limited to recommending the L1 bidder (lowest bidder) to the buyer. NSIC is **not involved in invoicing or payment**. The buyer awards the contract directly to the MSE supplier. |
| **Facilitation Fee** | NSIC charges **5% (inclusive of GST)** of the award value as facilitation fee/commission. Financial transactions occur directly between the buyer and the MSE supplier. |
| **Fee Payment Schedule** | Facilitation charges are received in phases linked to project milestones:<br>- **50%** upon first milestone (acceptance of award)<br>- **25%** at second milestone (total execution)<br>- **25%** upon final milestone (receipt of payment) |
| **SC/ST Exemption** | Units owned by SC/ST entrepreneurs are exempted from the enlistment fee. |
| **Consortia Fee** | No fee for formation or renewal of consortia. However, enlistment fee (based on SPRS monetary limit) applies when consortia members participate in any enquiry. |
| **Annual Enlistment Fee** | Based on SPRS monetary limit:<br>- Up to Rs. 100 lacs: Rs. 1000 + GST<br>- Rs. 100–500 lacs: Rs. 2500 + GST<br>- Above Rs. 500 lacs: Rs. 5000 + GST |
| **Price Band Policy** | MSEs quoting price within L1+15% band are allowed to supply up to 25% of requirement by bringing down their price to L1 price (where L1 is non-MSE). |
| **Reserved Items** | 358 items are reserved for exclusive purchase from the MSE sector. |
| **Tender Benefits** | SPRS-registered units get:<br>- Issue of tender sets free of cost<br>- Exemption from payment of Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) |

> **> Important Note:** The scheme does **not** provide direct financial support. NSIC does not extend credit, loans, or subsidies. All financial transactions are between the buyer and the MSE supplier. NSIC’s role is strictly facilitative.

### Application Process
The following Mermaid flowchart illustrates the step-by-step application process for the NSIC Marketing Support Scheme:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Start: MSE decides to apply] --> B[Submit application form in prescribed format (Annexures – A)]
    B --> C[Include required documents as mentioned in the application form]
    C --> D[Apply for enlistment under the Tender Marketing Scheme]
    D --> E[Ensure factory inspection is completed before empanelment]
    E --> F[Pay applicable annual fee based on SPRS monetary limit:<br/>- Up to Rs.100 lacs: Rs. 1000+GST<br/>- Rs.100-500 lacs: Rs. 2500+GST<br/>- Above Rs.500 lacs: Rs. 5000+GST<br/>- SC/ST-owned units: Exempt from enlistment fee]
    F --> G[Await empanelment approval from NSIC]
    G --> H[Upon empanelment, participate in tender facilitation process as per scheme guidelines]
    H --> I[NSIC recommends L1 bidder to buyer
    I --> J[Buyer awards contract to MSE supplier]
    J --> K[NSIC receives facilitation fee: 5% of award value in milestones:<br/>- 50% on award acceptance<br/>- 25% on total execution<br/>- 25% on payment receipt]
    K --> End[End: Process complete]
```

**Application Portal:** Applications are submitted online via the NSIC SPRS portal: [www.nsicspronline.com](https://www.nsicspronline.com)
