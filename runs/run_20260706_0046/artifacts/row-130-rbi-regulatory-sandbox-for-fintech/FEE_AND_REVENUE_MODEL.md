## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are tied to the tangible outcome of sandbox approval, which enables regulatory pathway and market access — not direct funding.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Retainers**: Reflect the effort required to assess innovation, prepare detailed documentation (product description, business plan, data security, exit strategy), and liaise with RBI.
3. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The RBI Regulatory Sandbox has a **medium processing time** (rolling applications, evaluation phase, agreement signing, testing period, final assessment). Success is not guaranteed — participation does not ensure full regulatory approval. This increases engagement risk, justifying retainer coverage for due diligence and proposal strength.
4. **Client Success Focus**: Fees structured to incentivize high-quality applications that demonstrate genuine innovation, customer protection, and scalability — key RBI evaluation criteria.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Retainer fee for RBI Regulatory Sandbox application support — includes eligibility assessment, innovation positioning, documentation preparation (Certificate of Incorporation, audited balance sheet, product description, business plan, technology details, data security measures, exit strategy), and portal submission guidance.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for securing approval into the RBI Regulatory Sandbox for Fintech, implemented by the Reserve Bank of India, upon successful entry into the sandbox agreement and commencement of live testing under RBI supervision.
