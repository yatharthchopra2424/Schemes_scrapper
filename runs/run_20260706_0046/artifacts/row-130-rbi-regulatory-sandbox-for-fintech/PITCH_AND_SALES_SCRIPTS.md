# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I'm calling from [Your Firm Name]. We specialize in helping fintech startups navigate regulatory pathways like the RBI Regulatory Sandbox for Fintech, which offers a controlled environment to test innovative financial products with real customers under RBI supervision. I'd like to understand your current innovation goals and see if this sandbox could be a strategic fit for your next phase of development.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your company incorporated, and what is your current net worth as per the latest audited balance sheet?
2. What specific fintech product or service are you looking to test, and how is it innovative or not yet widely available in the market?
3. Do you have a clear exit strategy and scalability plan for the product post-testing?
4. What customer protection and data security measures have you implemented for your product?
5. Are you seeking regulatory facilitation and guidance rather than direct funding or grants?
6. Have you prepared a detailed business plan and financial projections for the sandbox application?
7. What technology stack or platform will you use to deliver your product/service during testing?
8. Are you incorporated in India and able to provide your Certificate of Incorporation and latest audited balance sheet?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Incorporated in India
- [ ] Net worth of at least INR 25 lakh as per last audited balance sheet
- [ ] Demonstrates genuine need for testing in the sandbox
- [ ] Has a clear exit strategy
- [ ] Product/service is innovative and not already widely available in the market
- [ ] Can provide Certificate of Incorporation/Registration
- [ ] Can provide latest audited balance sheet
- [ ] Can provide detailed description of innovative product/service
- [ ] Can provide business plan and financial projections
- [ ] Can provide details of technology to be used
- [ ] Can provide customer protection and data security measures
- [ ] Can provide exit strategy and scalability plan

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like your [product name] innovation aligns well with the RBI Regulatory Sandbox for Fintech’s goal of fostering responsible innovation through live testing. The sandbox provides regulatory relaxations and RBI guidance during testing—without offering direct funding—which seems to match what you’re looking for. Shall I walk you through how it works and what the next steps would be?

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Fintech startups often face regulatory uncertainty when launching innovative products, making it difficult to test with real customers at scale. Without a controlled environment, companies risk non-compliance, delayed launches, or investing in products that may not meet future regulatory standards.

**Scheme intro:**
The RBI Regulatory Sandbox for Fintech, administered by the Reserve Bank of India, offers regulatory relaxations, access to RBI guidance, and permission to test with real customers in a controlled environment.
