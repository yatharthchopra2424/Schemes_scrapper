# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Entity is incorporated in India (Certificate of Incorporation required)
- [ ] Minimum net worth as specified by SEBI (verify current threshold from SEBI guidelines)
- [ ] Proposed innovation is related to securities markets (product, service, or business model)
- [ ] Innovation addresses a genuine market need in the securities sector
- [ ] Includes safeguards for investor protection and risk management
- [ ] Entity is a startup, fintech company, or innovator (target beneficiary)
- [ ] No direct financial support expected (scheme provides regulatory relief only)
- [ ] Willingness to bear all costs associated with innovation and testing
- [ ] Understanding that participation is time-limited and subject to SEBI approval
- [ ] Acceptance that relaxed regulations apply only to approved scope of testing
- [ ] Acknowledgment that successful testing does not guarantee future regulatory approval

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation
- PAN Card of the entity
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (for MSME classification, if applicable)
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the entity for last 2 financial years
- Audited financial statements (Balance Sheet, P&L) for last 2 years

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed description of the innovative product, service, or business model
- Business plan and financial projections (3-5 year outlook)
- Risk assessment and mitigation plan
- Investor protection measures document
- Details of promoters and key personnel (CVs, background, roles)
- Any existing regulatory approvals or licenses (if applicable)
- Technology architecture details (if tech-based innovation)
- Target user identification and market analysis
- Compliance framework proposal for the sandbox testing phase

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible scans or certified true copies
- File names must match document type (e.g., "Certificate_of_Incorporation.pdf")
- PDF format preferred; individual file size < 10 MB
- Documents in English or accompanied by certified English translation
- Financial documents must be audited and signed by CA
- Promoter details must include DIN/PAN where applicable
- Business plan must clearly link innovation to securities market improvement
- Risk assessment must identify specific risks and quantifiable mitigation steps

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A formal Detailed Project Report (DPR) is not explicitly mandated, but a comprehensive business plan and innovation description are required. Include:  
- Executive summary  
- Innovation description and securities market relevance  
- Market need and target users  
- Technology/product architecture  
- Business model and revenue projections  
- Risk assessment and mitigation  
- Investor protection mechanisms  
- Implementation timeline  
- Resource requirements  
- Compliance approach during sandbox testing  
*Verify with nodal officer if SEBI expects a specific DPR format.*

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal at https://www.sebi.gov.in/innovation-sandbox  
2. Create an account or log in using valid credentials  
3. Fill all mandatory fields accurately—do not leave any required section blank  
4. Describe the innovation clearly, emphasizing its relevance to securities markets  
5. Explicitly detail investor protection measures and risk mitigation strategies  
6. Provide realistic financial projections—avoid overly optimistic assumptions  
7. Ensure promoter details match KYC documents (PAN, DIN, address)  
8. Declare that the entity will bear all costs of testing and innovation  
9. Confirm understanding that relaxed regulations apply only to the approved scope  
10. Upload all documents in correct format and size as specified  
11. Review the entire application before final submission  
12. Do not submit if any document is missing or unclear  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All application form fields completed accurately  
- [ ] Innovation description clearly tied to securities market improvement  
- [ ] Investor protection and risk mitigation plans are specific and actionable  
- [ ] Financial projections are realistic and based on sound assumptions  
- [ ] Promoter details match submitted KYC documents  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached  
- [ ] Documents are named correctly, in PDF, <10 MB, and legible  
- [ ] Declarations regarding cost-bearing and scope understanding are included  
- [ ] Application reviewed for consistency and completeness  
- [ ] Final sign-off by senior consultant before submission

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via https://www.sebi.gov.in/innovation-sandbox  
- Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing submission timestamp and reference number  
- Save the reference number in the case file and set automated reminders for follow-up at 15, 30, and 45 days  
- Store all submission evidence (screenshots, PDFs) in the secure client folder  
- Notify the client of successful submission with reference number and next steps

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and review the sandbox approval letter outlining scope, duration, and conditions  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned testing period and any regulatory relaxations granted  
- [ ] Ensure all participants understand that no financial grant is provided—only regulatory facilitation  
- [ ] Develop a detailed testing plan aligned with sandbox agreement terms  
- [ ] Set up internal reporting mechanism for periodic updates to SEBI (as required)  
- [ ] Monitor compliance with investor protection and risk mitigation measures throughout testing  
- [ ] Prepare and submit periodic progress reports to SEBI as per agreement  
- [ ] Conduct final testing and compile outcomes, lessons learned, and performance metrics  
- [ ] Submit final report to SEBI on outcomes and recommendations  
- [ ] Utilize SEBI association and credibility for market access, partnerships, or future funding pursuits  
- [ ] Prepare utilization certificate (if required for any associated costs, though scheme is non-financial)  
- [ ] Discuss invoice for success fee with client based on value delivered (regulatory approval, market access, etc.)  
- [ ] Guide client on next steps: seeking full regulatory authorization, scaling, or pivoting based on sandbox results
