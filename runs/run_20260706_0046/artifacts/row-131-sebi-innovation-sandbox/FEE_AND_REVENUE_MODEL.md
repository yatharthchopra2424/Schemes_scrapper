## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the non-financial, strategic value of the SEBI Innovation Sandbox (regulatory mentorship, market access, credibility) rather than direct monetary grants, as the scheme provides no financial support.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees account for the scheme’s rolling application basis and time-limited participation, with outcomes dependent on SEBI approval and ongoing compliance; no guaranteed financial return exists.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Pricing scales with client capacity to engage in innovation testing, targeting fintech startups and innovators (per eligibility) who can bear associated costs of innovation and testing.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: Clear communication that success fees are contingent on achieving sandbox approval and meaningful testing outcomes, not financial disbursement, managing client expectations per scheme caveats.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Retainer fee for SEBI Innovation Sandbox consulting services, including application preparation, eligibility assessment, documentation support (Certificate of Incorporation, business plan, risk assessment, investor protection measures), and liaison with Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) for sandbox entry.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for securing approval into the SEBI Innovation Sandbox, administered by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), contingent on successful application review and entry into a sandbox agreement for testing innovative securities market products, services, or business models.
