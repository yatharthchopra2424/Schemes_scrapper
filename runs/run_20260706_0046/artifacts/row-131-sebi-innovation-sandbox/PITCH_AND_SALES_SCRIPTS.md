## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good [morning/afternoon], I'm calling from [Your Firm Name]. We specialise in helping fintech innovators and securities market startups navigate regulatory pathways to bring their ideas to market. The SEBI Innovation Sandbox is designed specifically for entities like yours—those developing novel products or services in the securities space who need a safe, supervised environment to test and scale. I’d like to understand your current innovation and see if this sandbox could be a strategic fit for your next phase of growth.

### Discovery Questions
- What is the innovative product, service, or business model you are looking to test in the securities market?
- When was your company incorporated in India, and what is your current net worth position?
- Who are your target users or customers for this innovation, and what specific market need does it address?
- What investor protection measures and risk mitigation strategies have you built into your model?
- Have you already obtained any regulatory approvals or licenses from SEBI or other financial regulators?
- Are you seeking a time-bound, supervised testing environment to validate your innovation before full-scale launch?
- What kind of regulatory guidance or mentorship would be most valuable to you during the testing phase?
- Are you prepared to bear all costs associated with the innovation and testing, as the sandbox does not provide direct funding?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity is incorporated in India  
- [ ] Minimum net worth as specified by SEBI is met  
- [ ] Proposes an innovative product, service, or business model related to securities markets  
- [ ] Innovation addresses a genuine market need  
- [ ] Includes safeguards for investor protection and risk management  
- [ ] Target beneficiaries align with startups, fintech companies, or innovators  
- [ ] Willing to participate in a time-limited, supervised testing environment  
- [ ] Accepts that SEBI may terminate participation if risks to investors or market integrity are identified  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your innovation in [specific area mentioned] aligns well with what the SEBI Innovation Sandbox supports. The sandbox offers regulatory relief, direct access to SEBI officials, and a credible pathway to authorization—without requiring you to give up equity or take on debt. Let me walk you through how it works and how we’ve helped similar innovators navigate the process successfully.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Fintech startups and securities market innovators often face lengthy, complex regulatory approval processes that delay time-to-market, increase compliance costs, and create uncertainty—especially when testing novel technologies or business models. Without a supervised testing environment, innovators risk either operating in regulatory grey areas or investing heavily before gaining clarity on compliance requirements.

**Scheme intro:**
The SEBI Innovation Sandbox, administered by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), is a regulatory sandbox that provides eligible entities with access to a controlled testing environment. It offers relaxed compliance requirements for approved innovations, guidance from SEBI officials, and a potential pathway to full regulatory authorization. The sandbox does not provide direct financial support, grants, or funding—its value lies in regulatory facilitation, mentorship, and enhanced credibility through SEBI association.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your entity is incorporated in India, meeting the jurisdictional requirement  
- You have proposed an innovative product/service/model related to securities markets, as evidenced by [specific innovation mentioned]  
- Your design includes investor protection measures and risk mitigation strategies, addressing SEBI’s core safeguards  
- You are seeking a time-bound testing phase, which aligns with the sandbox’s supervised, limited-duration framework
