### SEBI Innovation Sandbox
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.sebi.gov.in/innovation-sandbox
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Incorporated entities in India
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Securities markets (fintech, startups, innovators proposing innovative products/services/business models related to securities markets)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have minimum net worth as specified by SEBI; innovation must address a genuine market need; must include safeguards for investor protection and risk management

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Non-financial benefits only — access to regulated testing environment with relaxed compliance requirements, guidance from SEBI officials, potential pathway to full regulatory authorization, enhanced credibility through SEBI association, regulatory mentorship, and market access opportunities
- Disbursement mode: Not applicable (non-financial support)
- Duration of benefit: Time-limited (subject to SEBI approval; participation duration defined in sandbox agreement)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation
2. Detailed description of the innovative product/service/model
3. Business plan and financial projections
4. Risk assessment and mitigation plan
5. Investor protection measures
6. Details of promoters and key personnel
7. Any existing regulatory approvals or licenses

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Submit an application detailing the innovative product, service, or business model to be tested.
2. Include information on the technology, target users, risk mitigation measures, and investor protection mechanisms.
3. SEBI reviews the application for eligibility and potential risks.
4. If approved, enter into a sandbox agreement outlining the scope, duration, and conditions of testing.
5. Conduct the test under SEBI supervision with periodic reporting.
6. Submit a final report on outcomes and lessons learned.
