# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Entity type: MSME or service enterprise engaged in manufacturing or service activities  
- [ ] Legal structure: Sole proprietorship, partnership, cooperative society, private or public limited company  
- [ ] Sector: Micro and small sector (as per MSME classification)  
- [ ] Project type: New project, expansion, modernization, diversification, technology upgradation, or rehabilitation of sick units  
- [ ] Priority consideration: Women entrepreneurs or units adopting state-of-the-art technology (if applicable)  
- [ ] Business vintage: Existing businesses must have audited financials and ITRs; new units must submit project report  
- [ ] Geographic scope: Pan-India (no location restrictions)  
- [ ] Fund availability: Applications accepted on rolling basis subject to fund availability and project viability  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN card of promoter(s) and business  
- Aadhaar card of promoter(s)  
- Business registration proof (Partnership deed, Certificate of Incorporation, etc.)  
- Udyam Registration Certificate  
- GSTIN certificate (if applicable)  
- Bank account statement (last 6 months)  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of last 2 years (for existing businesses)  
- Audited financial statements (last 2 years)  
- Passport-sized photographs of promoter(s)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed project report covering project cost, means of financing, and projected financials  
- Proof of identity and address of promoter(s) (Aadhaar, passport, voter ID, driving license)  
- Proof of business constitution (Partnership deed, Certificate of Incorporation, LLP agreement, etc.)  
- Land documents (ownership/lease agreement) and building plans  
- Quotations for plant and machinery and other assets  
- Sanction letters from other lenders (if any)  
- Project approvals and NOCs from relevant authorities (if applicable)  
- Details of collateral security offered  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format  
- File size per document: < 5 MB  
- File names must match document type (e.g., "PAN_Promoter1.pdf", "Udyam_Certificate.pdf")  
- Photographs must be clear and recent (within last 6 months)  
- Financial documents must be certified by a Chartered Accountant (if applicable)  
- All documents must be in English or Hindi; vernacular documents require certified translation  
- Bank statements must show active account with consistent transactions

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
✅ **Required**  
Standard sections to include:  
- Executive summary  
- Project description and objectives  
- Project cost breakdown (land, building, plant & machinery, technical know-how, pre-operative expenses, working capital margin)  
- Means of financing (promoter’s contribution, term loan, unsecured loan, etc.)  
- Projected financials (profit & loss statement, balance sheet, cash flow statement for 5 years)  
- Break-even analysis  
- Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR)  
- Sensitivity analysis  
- Implementation schedule (Gantt chart)  
- Management profile and experience  
- Market analysis and demand assessment  
- Raw material and utility requirements  
- Pollution control measures (if applicable)  
- Conclusion and recommendations  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the application form only from the official SIDBI website or obtain it from the nearest SIDBI branch.  
2. Fill all fields in block letters; do not leave any mandatory field blank.  
3. Ensure promoter’s name matches exactly as per PAN and Aadhaar across all documents.  
4. Project cost and loan amount must be consistent with the project report and quotations.  
5. Declare all existing loans and liabilities accurately in the indebtedness section.  
6. Specify the exact purpose of the loan (e.g., “for procurement of CNC machines and installation”) as per sanction letter expectations.  
7. Attach self-attested copies of all supporting documents; do not submit originals unless requested.  
8. Sign the declaration section in the presence of a witness if required by the branch official.  
9. Verify that the Udyam Registration Number is correctly entered in the form.  
10. Retain a photocopy of the fully filled application form before submission.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All sections of the application form are completely filled  
- [ ] Project report is complete and financially vetted  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached  
- [ ] Document file names match the checklist  
- [ ] Declarations are signed and dated  
- [ ] Udyam Registration Number is valid and verified on https://udyamregistration.gov.in  
- [ ] Bank statements show sufficient average balance to support repayment capacity  
- [ ] No discrepancies in promoter details across PAN, Aadhaar, and application form  
- [ ] Loan amount requested aligns with project cost and promoter’s contribution (minimum 10%)  
- [ ] Contact details (mobile, email) are correct and functional

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Visit the nearest SIDBI branch or office with the completed application and document set  
- Obtain an acknowledgment receipt with inward number, date, and officer’s signature  
- Take a clear photograph or screenshot of the acknowledgment for record-keeping  
- Store the application reference number securely in the case file  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days post-submission if no communication is received  
- Do not submit original documents unless explicitly requested by the SIDBI officer

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the sanction letter for loan amount, interest rate, repayment schedule, and moratorium period  
- [ ] Confirm that the loan is covered under CGTMSE or other credit guarantee schemes (if applicable)  
- [ ] Ensure all post-sanction documentation (loan agreement, disbursement request forms) is completed and signed  
- [ ] Track disbursement in stages as per project progress and submission of utilization certificates  
- [ ] Monitor utilization of funds strictly for the purpose specified in the sanction letter  
- [ ] Prepare and submit utilization certificates and progress reports as required during the loan tenure  
- [ ] Set up auto-debit or ECS mandate for timely repayment to avoid penalties  
- [ ] Retain copies of all correspondence, sanction letter, loan agreement, and disbursement records for audit and compliance  
- [ ] Calculate and confirm the success fee as per the engagement letter with the client
