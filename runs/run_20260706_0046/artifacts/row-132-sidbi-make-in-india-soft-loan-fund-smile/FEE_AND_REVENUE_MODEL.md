## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the financial benefit delivered (soft loan amount and concessional terms), ensuring clients pay only for measurable outcomes.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile — approval depends on project viability, credit appraisal, and fund availability, with processing timelines typically 2–4 months based on SIDBI’s due diligence and disbursement in stages.
3. **Transparency**: All fees and payment terms are disclosed upfront in the engagement letter, with no hidden charges; retainer covers initial project assessment and documentation support.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The SMILE scheme offers need-based soft loans with concessional interest, extended repayment tenors, and moratorium periods, reducing financial burden on MSMEs. However, approval is subject to credit appraisal, project viability, and fund availability, introducing moderate execution risk. Success fees are structured to reflect this, with lower percentages for larger clients where loan sizes are higher but approval certainty increases with stronger financials.
