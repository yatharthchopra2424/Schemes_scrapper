# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping manufacturing and service MSMEs access tailored financing solutions like the SIDBI Make in India Soft Loan Fund (SMILE) for projects involving plant and machinery, technology upgradation, or expansion. I’d like to understand your current growth plans and see if this scheme aligns with your needs.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is the nature of your business—manufacturing or services—and how long have you been operational?
2. Are you planning a new project, expansion, modernization, diversification, technology upgradation, or rehabilitation of a sick unit?
3. What is your current incorporation status (sole proprietorship, partnership, private limited, etc.) and are you classified as micro or small enterprise under Udyam?
4. Do you have a detailed project report outlining project cost, means of financing, and projected financials?
5. What specific assets are you looking to finance—plant and machinery, technical know-how, or other project components?
6. Are you availing or planning to avail credit from other lenders for this project?
7. Do you have land/building ownership or lease agreements and related documents ready?
8. Are you a woman-led enterprise or adopting state-of-the-art technology, as these may receive priority under the scheme?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity is an MSME or service enterprise (manufacturing or service sector)
- [ ] Engaged in new project, expansion, modernization, diversification, technology upgradation, or rehabilitation of sick unit
- [ ] Constituted as sole proprietorship, partnership, cooperative society, private/public limited company (micro/small sector)
- [ ] Has or can prepare a detailed project report with cost, financing, and financial projections
- [ ] Possesses or can obtain land documents, building plans, and machinery quotations
- [ ] Can provide identity/address proof of promoters, business constitution proof, and financial statements
- [ ] Project involves capital expenditure for plant and machinery, technical know-how, or related components
- [ ] Not excluded by fund unavailability or non-viability (subject to SIDBI appraisal)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you’re planning a [new project/expansion/technology upgradation] requiring financing for plant and machinery—exactly what the SIDBI Make in India Soft Loan Fund (SMILE) supports with soft loans at concessional interest rates and flexible repayment. Let me share how this scheme can specifically benefit your situation.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
MSMEs undertaking long gestation projects or technology upgradation often face high financing costs, stringent collateral requirements, and rigid repayment terms that strain cash flows, especially during early project phases when revenue generation is delayed.

**Scheme intro:**
The SIDBI Make in India Soft Loan Fund (SMILE), administered by the Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI), provides soft loans for project financing covering plant and machinery, technical know-how, and other project components. The loan amount is need-based, aligned with project cost and repayment capacity, featuring concessional interest rates, extended repayment tenors, and often includes a moratorium period. Loans may be covered under credit guarantee schemes like CGTMSE to reduce collateral needs.
