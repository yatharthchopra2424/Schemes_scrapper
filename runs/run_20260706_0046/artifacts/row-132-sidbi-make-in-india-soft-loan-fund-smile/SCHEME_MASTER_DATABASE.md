### SIDBI Make in India Soft Loan Fund (SMILE)
- **ILE)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://sidbi.in/en/products/smile
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: MSMEs and service enterprises engaged in manufacturing or service activities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Manufacturing and service sectors
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Eligible entities include sole proprietorships, partnerships, cooperative societies, private and public limited companies in the micro and small sector. Units undertaking new projects, expansion, modernization, diversification, technology upgradation, or rehabilitation of sick units are eligible. Priority may be given to women entrepreneurs and units adopting state-of-the-art technology.

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Need-based loan amount subject to project cost and borrower's repayment capacity
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit
- Duration of benefit: Structured to align with project cash flows, often including a moratorium period

#### Documents Required
1. Project report detailing project cost, means of financing, and projected financials
2. Proof of identity and address of the promoter(s)
3. Proof of business constitution (Partnership deed, Certificate of Incorporation, etc.)
4. Land documents (ownership/lease agreement) and building plans
5. Quotations for plant and machinery and other assets
6. Sanction letters from other lenders (if any)
7. Income tax returns and audited financial statements for existing businesses
8. Bank account statements
9. Project approvals and NOCs from relevant authorities (if applicable)
10. Details of collateral security offered

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the project requirement and prepare a detailed project report including project cost, means of financing, and projected financials.
2. Approach the nearest SIDBI branch or office with the project proposal and required documents.
3. Submit the loan application along with the project report and supporting documents.
4. SIDBI officials will conduct due diligence, including technical and financial appraisal of the project.
5. Upon approval, a sanction letter is issued outlining the loan amount, interest rate, repayment schedule, and other terms.
6. Complete documentation and execution of loan agreement.
7. Disbursement of loan amount in stages as per project progress and submission of utilization certificates.
