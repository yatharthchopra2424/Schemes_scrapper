# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- ✅ The applicant must be a Micro, Small & Medium Enterprise (MSME) engaged in manufacturing or service activities (traders are excluded).
- ✅ The unit must mandatorily have Udyam Registration.
- ✅ The applicant must be supplying goods/services to reputed Public Limited Companies, State and Central Govt. Departments/Undertakings, or Private Limited Companies (not traders), engaged in manufacturing/service activities.
- ✅ Bills (Bill of exchange) must be drawn by the MSME for supplies made and duly accepted by the purchaser.
- ✅ The maximum usance period of the bills shall not exceed 180 days.
- ✅ Security must be provided in the form of Bank Guarantee issued by approved banks, equivalent to the value of assistance, or personal guarantee of proprietor, partners, or directors.
- ✅ Annual limits can be fixed for the unit based on information provided in the prescribed application form.
- ✅ The unit must not have outstanding dues beyond the usance period to retain concessional discounting rates (if applicable based on SME 1 or SME 2 rating).

**Sources**:  
- Scheme Key Facts (Eligibility, Security, Benefits, Key Caveats)  
- Bill Discounting Scheme portal: https://nsic.co.in/Schemes/BillDiscountingAgainstBG

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Udyam Registration Certificate  
- PAN Card  
- GST Registration Certificate  
- Audited Financial Statements (Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss Account) for the last 3 years  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- Latest Electricity Bill  
- Banker’s Report (as per Annexure E format)  
- Declaration accepting conditions of registration (Annexure D)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Application Form for Bill Discounting Scheme (downloadable from NSIC portal)  
- List of documents to be enclosed with Application Form (as specified by NSIC)  
- Details of Bank Guarantee (value, issuing bank, validity)  
- Details of Personal Guarantees (if applicable)  
- Proforma invoice or original invoice/bill of exchange to be discounted  
- Purchase order or contract with the buyer (Public Ltd Company, Govt. Dept, or Private Ltd Company)  
- Details of sister/associate/group concerns (if any)  
- Employment generation details  
- Any other information as deemed necessary by NSIC  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be self-attested where required.  
- File size should be optimized for upload (preferably under 5 MB per file).  
- Document names must match the applicant’s legal name as per Udyam/PAN.  
- Scanned copies must be legible, with all seals and signatures visible.  
- Financial statements must be duly signed by authorized person and Chartered Accountant (with UDIN where applicable).  
- Banker’s Report must be on the bank’s letterhead, signed and sealed.  
- All documents must be in English or Hindi; vernacular documents require certified translation.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for the Bill Discounting Scheme. The application is based on the prescribed form and supporting documents. However, if additional financial justification is needed, verify with the nodal officer at the NSIC Branch Office.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the official Application Form from: https://nsic.co.in/Schemes/BillDiscountingAgainstBG  
2. Fill in **all** fields; leave no blank columns.  
3. Ensure the Name of the Applicant Unit matches exactly with Udyam and PAN records.  
4. Provide accurate Udyam Registration Number and date.  
5. Enter correct GST Registration number and date.  
6. PAN details must be complete, including e-filing acknowledgement number for the latest ITR.  
7. Banker details: list all banks, account numbers, IFSC codes, and telephone numbers.  
8. Clearly specify whether the unit is located in a backward/hilly region or not.  
9. Tick applicable boxes for SC/ST, Women entrepreneur, Physically Handicapped, Ex-Serviceman, Technocrat status.  
10. Provide complete details of sister/associate/group concerns (names, addresses, common proprietors).  
11. List all Partners/Directors with full details (name, father’s/husband’s name, address, age, qualification, business experience, PAN, Aadhar, contact details, net worth).  
12. Clearly state the Line of Activity (manufacturing/service).  
13. Specify Annual Requirement of Raw Materials (if applicable to the business).  
14. Disclose any financial assistance already received or likely from Banks/FIs.  
15. Answer B1 and B2 regarding prior assistance from NSIC under RMA against BG or other schemes.  
16. Details of Security proposed:  
    - Bank Guarantee: value, issuing bank, address, telephone number  
    - Personal Guarantees: names, addresses, net worth, e-filing acknowledgement for last ITR  
17. State total employment generated by the unit (existing + additional).  
18. Provide any other relevant information.  
19. Sign the declaration with official stamp, place, and date.  
20. Submit **two copies** of the completed application form (one for acknowledgment).  

**Portal Note**: Forms are also available free of charge at NSIC Branch Offices.  

### Internal Review Gate
- ✅ All fields in the application form are filled; no blanks.  
- ✅ Udyam, PAN, GST numbers are valid and match across documents.  
- ✅ Financial statements are audited, signed, and include UDIN (if CA-signed).  
- ✅ Banker’s Report is on letterhead, signed, sealed, and includes fund/non-fund base limits.  
- ✅ Declaration (Annexure D) is signed and dated.  
- ✅ Application form is signed with official stamp.  
- ✅ Two copies of the application form are prepared.  
- ✅ All supporting documents are self-attested where required.  
- ✅ Bill of Exchange is duly accepted by the buyer and within 180-day usance.  
- ✅ Buyer is verified as Public Ltd Company, Govt. Dept, or Private Ltd Company (not a trader).  
- ✅ Bank Guarantee details are complete and from an approved bank.  
- ✅ Personal Guarantee details (if any) include net worth and documentation.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the duly filled application form along with all supporting documents to the **nearest NSIC Branch Office**.  
- Obtain a signed acknowledgment receipt (one copy of the application form returned by NSIC).  
- Take a clear screenshot or scan of the acknowledgment for records.  
- Store the application reference number (if provided) or acknowledgment details.  
- Set follow-up reminders for Day 25 and Day 30 if no update is received.  
- Preliminary appraisal and unit inspection will be carried out by NSIC post-submission.  

**Sources**:  
- Application Process: Step 2 (submit to nearest Branch Office), Step 5 (Preliminary appraisal and Unit inspection)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- ✅ Obtain and verify the approval letter from NSIC specifying the sanctioned limit.  
- ✅ Confirm the sanctioned amount and usance period (up to 180 days).  
- ✅ Sign the agreement between NSIC and the Unit as per Step 7 of the application process.  
- ✅ Ensure disbursement of assistance to the unit as per Step 8.  
- ✅ Monitor utilization of the discounted bill proceeds for genuine trade transactions only.  
- ✅ Ensure timely repayment of NSIC’s dues to retain concessional discounting rates (if eligible based on SME 1/SME 2 rating).  
- ✅ Prepare and submit Utilization Certificate as required by NSIC.  
- ✅ Track processing fees: 1.00% p.a. for Micro and Small & Medium on new sanctions; 0.50% p.a. for Micro and 1.00% p.a. for Small & Medium on renewal.  
- ✅ Verify that concessional rates (7.75–8.75% p.a. for Micro, 8.25–9.25% p.a. for Small & Medium) apply only if repayments are made within usance period and rating is valid.  
- ✅ Notify client of penalty charges: 1.25% per quarter on overdue amounts beyond usance period.  
- ✅ Assist in renewal process after one year (if conduct of account was satisfactory).  
- ✅ Invoice success fee as per consultancy agreement.
