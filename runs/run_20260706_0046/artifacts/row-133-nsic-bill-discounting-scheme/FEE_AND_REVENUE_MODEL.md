## Pricing Principles
1. Value-Based Pricing: Fees aligned with the financial benefit delivered (bill discounting up to 180 days usance, improving MSME cash flow).
2. Risk-Adjusted Fees: Success fees reflect scheme risk profile — moderate due to 180-day usance limit, bank guarantee security, and processing fees (1.00% p.a. for Micro/SME on new sanctions, 0.50% p.a. for Micro on renewal).
3. Transparency: Clear disclosure of retainer and success fee structures; no hidden charges.
4. Scheme-Specific Note: The NSIC Bill Discounting Scheme has a low-to-moderate risk profile due to bank guarantee security, concessional rates for timely repayment, and high success likelihood for eligible MSMEs supplying to reputed buyers (PSUs, PLCs, Pvt Ltd cos). Processing fees apply on sanctions/renewals, but no upfront grant — revenue is derived from facilitation of discounting limits.
