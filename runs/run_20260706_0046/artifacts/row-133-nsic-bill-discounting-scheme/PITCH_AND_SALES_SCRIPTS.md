# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping MSMEs access government-backed financing schemes like the NSIC Bill Discounting Scheme. This scheme is designed specifically for manufacturers and service providers who supply goods to reputable buyers like government departments, PSUs, or private limited companies—and need working capital to bridge the gap between delivery and payment. I’d like to understand your current receivables situation to see if this could be a fit for your business.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your Udyam Registration number, and when was your enterprise registered?  
2. Are you primarily engaged in manufacturing or service activities (trading activities are excluded from this scheme)?  
3. Who are your typical buyers—are they Public Limited Companies, State/Central Government Departments, Undertakings, or Private Limited Companies (not traders)?  
4. What is the average usance period (credit period) you currently offer on your invoices/bills of exchange?  
5. Do you currently have bank guarantees in place, or are you able to obtain one from an approved bank to secure financing?  
6. Are your bills arising from genuine trade transactions for supplies made by your unit?  
7. What is your annual turnover range, and are you classified as Micro, Small, or Medium Enterprise under Udyam?  
8. Have you previously availed of any bill discounting or working capital assistance from NSIC or other financial institutions?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Udyam Registration Certificate is valid and mandatory  
- [ ] Entity is engaged in manufacturing or service activities (traders excluded)  
- [ ] Buyers are reputed Public Ltd Companies / State/Central/Undertakings / Private Ltd (not traders)  
- [ ] Bills are drawn for genuine trade transactions (supplies made by MSMEs)  
- [ ] Maximum usance period of bills does not exceed 180 days  
- [ ] Security can be provided via Bank Guarantee from approved banks (by buyer or seller)  
- [ ] Personal guarantee of proprietor/partners/directors is available  
- [ ] Unit is not a trader  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you meet the core eligibility for the **NSIC Bill Discounting Scheme**—especially with your Udyam registration, manufacturing/service focus, and sales to government or private limited companies. This scheme can help you unlock working capital by discounting your accepted bills against a bank guarantee, giving you credit support of up to 180 days. Would you like me to walk you through how it works and the specific benefits it could offer your business?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
MSMEs often face cash flow gaps when they supply goods to government departments or large private companies but have to wait 30–180 days for payment. This blocks working capital, making it difficult to pay for raw materials, wages, or take on new orders—especially when buyers insist on credit terms.

**Scheme intro:**
The **NSIC Bill Discounting Scheme**, administered by the National Small Industries Corporation Ltd. (NSIC) under the Ministry of MSME, provides financial assistance by discounting bills of exchange arising from genuine trade transactions. MSMEs can avail credit support up to 100% of the bill value, with a maximum usance period of 180 days, secured by a bank guarantee from an approved bank (either buyer or seller). Processing fees are 1.00% p.a. for Micro and Small & Medium Enterprises on new sanctions, and 0.50% p.a. for Micro and 1.00% p.a. for Small & Medium on renewal. Concessional discounting rates are available for units with valid SME 1 or SME 2 ratings, subject to timely repayment.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You have a valid Udyam Registration Certificate (mandatory for eligibility)  
- Your business is engaged in manufacturing or service activities (traders are excluded)  
- You supply to reputed buyers such as Public Limited Companies, Government Departments, or Private Limited Companies (not traders)  
- Your bills are drawn for genuine trade transactions and can be discounted against bank guarantee security
