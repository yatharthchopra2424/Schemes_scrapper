### NSIC Bill Discounting Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Small Industries Corporation Ltd. (NSIC)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nsic.co.in/Schemes/BillDiscountingAgainstBG
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises engaged in manufacturing / service activities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Manufacturing / service activities (traders excluded)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must mandatorily have Udyam Registration; supplies must be made to reputed Public Limited Companies / State and Central Govt. Departments / Undertakings / Private Limited Companies (not traders), engaged in manufacturing / service activities

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance against Bank Guarantee security; maximum usance period of bills not exceeding 180 days
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (discounting of bills)
- Duration of benefit: One-time per bill discounting transaction (up to 180 days usance)

#### Documents Required
1. Application Form
2. List of documents to be enclosed with Application Form

#### Application Process
1. The Entrepreneurs are required to apply for Bill Discounting only on the prescribed application forms.
2. The application forms downloaded from the link given below may be filled and can be submitted to the nearest Branch Office.
3. The blank forms are also available free of charge from the Branch offices.
4. Duly filled application form is to be submitted along with the Application.
5. Preliminary appraisal and Unit inspection is carried out by NSIC.
6. Sanction of Limit to the Unit.
7. Signing of agreement between NSIC and Unit.
8. Disbursement of assistance to the unit.
