## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees aligned with the financial benefit delivered (e.g., loan amount, interest subvention, guarantee coverage).
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect scheme risk; JanSamarth schemes have moderate risk due to bank-led disbursement and government backing, but processing involves eligibility checks and lender coordination.
3. **Transparency**: All fees disclosed upfront; no hidden charges, consistent with JanSamarth’s no-fee-to-applicants policy.
4. **Scalability**: Tiered structure accommodates diverse beneficiary profiles from street vendors to large corporates.
   - *Scheme-Specific Note*: Processing time varies by scheme (e.g., PM SVANidhi approvals in <30 days, ECLGS may take longer due to bank verification), but JanSamarth’s digital portal reduces lag via real-time tracking and auto-fill, supporting predictable revenue recognition.
