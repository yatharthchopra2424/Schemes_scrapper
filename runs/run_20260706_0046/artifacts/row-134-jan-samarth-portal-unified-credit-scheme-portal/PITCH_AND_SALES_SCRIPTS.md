# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping businesses and entrepreneurs access government-backed credit through the JanSamarth Portal—a one-stop digital platform linking 16 central government schemes. Whether you're a startup, MSME, woman entrepreneur, farmer, or street vendor, this portal simplifies eligibility checking and loan applications across sectors. I’d like to understand your current financing needs and see if any of these schemes align with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of business or activity are you engaged in (e.g., manufacturing, services, trading, agriculture, handicrafts)?
2. When was your business incorporated or registered (to check if under 10 years for startup schemes)?
3. Are you registered under Udyam (for MSME benefits) or DPIIT (for startup recognition)?
4. What is your approximate annual turnover or annual income (to assess eligibility for income-based schemes)?
5. Do you belong to any priority category such as woman entrepreneur, SC/ST, or operate in a rural area?
6. What kind of financing are you seeking—working capital, term loan, equipment purchase, or infrastructure development?
7. Do you have your Aadhaar, PAN, and bank statements ready for digital verification?
8. Are you currently availing credit from any bank, or are you a first-time borrower?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Indian resident with valid Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account  
- [ ] Business falls under one of 8 loan categories on JanSamarth (e.g., Business Activity Loan, Agri Loan, Home Loan)  
- [ ] Meets scheme-specific criteria (e.g., DPIIT recognition for startups, Udyam registration for MSMEs, income ≤ ₹9 lakh for home loans)  
- [ ] Not defaulter to any bank or financial institution  
- [ ] Seeking credit for eligible purpose (working capital, term loan, infrastructure, etc.)  
- [ ] Belongs to target beneficiary group (startup, MSME, woman entrepreneur, SC/ST, farmer, agri-startup, street vendor, SHG, handloom weaver)  
- [ ] Loan amount sought aligns with scheme limits (e.g., up to ₹20 crore for startups under CGSS, ₹10,000 for PM SVANidhi)  
- [ ] No payment made to third parties for scheme access (JanSamarth does not charge applicants)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you may qualify for one or more schemes on the JanSamarth Portal—such as the Loan for Startups (START) or Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY)—which offer collateral-free loans with credit guarantee coverage. Let me walk you through how this portal works and how it can help you secure financing quickly and transparently.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many entrepreneurs struggle to access timely credit due to complex eligibility checks, fragmented schemes, lack of digital integration, and fear of hidden charges or collateral requirements. This delays business growth, especially for first-time borrowers and underserved groups.

**Scheme intro:**
The JanSamarth Portal is a national digital platform launched by the Department of Financial Services (DFS), Ministry of Finance, Government of India. It links 16 central government credit-linked schemes across 8 loan categories, offering access to term loans, working capital, cash credit, overdraft, and composite loans. Financial support ranges from ₹10,000 (e.g., PM SVANidhi) to ₹1,500 crore (e.g., ECLGS for airlines), delivered through 250+ participating banks and financial institutions. Benefits include digital eligibility checking, auto-filling of details, real-time tracking, collateral-free loans under certain schemes, interest subvention, and credit guarantee coverage—all with no payment required from applicants to the portal.

- You meet turnover or incorporation criteria for schemes like Loan for Startups (START) or PMMY (to be confirmed post-eligibility check)  
- You are not a defaulter, making you eligible for standard credit facility consideration by lenders
