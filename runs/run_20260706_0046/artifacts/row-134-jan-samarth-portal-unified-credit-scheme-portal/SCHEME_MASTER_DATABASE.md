### Jan Samarth Portal – Unified Credit Scheme Portal
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Financial Services (DFS), Ministry of Finance, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.jansamarth.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Varies by scheme (startups, MSMEs, women entrepreneurs, SC/ST, farmers, agri-startups, street vendors, self-help groups, micro enterprises, handloom weavers)
- Age of business: Varies by scheme (e.g., startups ≤10 years, no specific age for others)
- Sector: Varies by scheme (agriculture, manufacturing, services, trading, etc.)
- Revenue cap: Varies by scheme (e.g., startup turnover ≤ ₹100 Crore, PMMY loans up to ₹20 lakhs)
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: Must be Indian resident with valid Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account; must meet scheme-specific criteria such as DPIIT recognition for startups, Udyam registration for micro enterprises, or income limits for housing loans

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support varies by scheme; quantum ranges from ₹10,000 (PM SVANidhi) to ₹1,500 crore (ECLGS for airlines)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit through participating banks and financial institutions
- Duration of benefit: One-time (varies by scheme; e.g., term loans 3-7 years, working capital renewed annually)

#### Documents Required
1. Aadhaar Number
2. Voter Id
3. PAN
4. Bank Statements
5. Basic details on the portal

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the JanSamarth portal and select your loan category.
2. Check eligibility by answering a few simple questions.
3. If eligible, proceed to apply online.
4. Provide basic details and upload required documents.
5. The portal’s smart analytics will auto-fill required details.
6. View offers from 250+ lenders and select a bank.
7. Receive digital approval from the selected bank.
8. Track application status in real time via the portal.
