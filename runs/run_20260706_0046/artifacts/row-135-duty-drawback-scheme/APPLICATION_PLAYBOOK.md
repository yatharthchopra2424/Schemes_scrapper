# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Exporter has paid customs duty on imported inputs used in manufacture of export goods  
- [ ] Exporter has paid central excise duty on indigenous inputs used in manufacture of export goods  
- [ ] Goods have been subsequently exported (let export)  
- [ ] Claim is filed within 12 months from the date of let export of the goods (as per Section 75 of the Customs Act, 1962)  
- [ ] Exporter maintains proper records of import, usage in production, and export  
- [ ] Exporter holds a valid Importer Exporter Code (IEC)  
- [ ] Exporter is registered under GST (if applicable)  
- [ ] No CENVAT credit or input tax credit under GST has been availed on the same inputs for which drawback is claimed  
- [ ] Goods are not exported under bond or letter of undertaking (LUT) without payment of IGST  
- [ ] Business falls under target beneficiary category: exporter, manufacturer, or MSME  

> **Source**: Eligibility criteria from KEY FACTS block; Application Process and Key Caveats sections.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- [ ] PAN Card of the business/entity  
- [ ] GST Registration Certificate  
- [ ] IEC (Importer Exporter Code) Certificate  
- [ ] Bank Account Details (cancelled cheque or bank statement)  
- [ ] Udyam Registration (if MSME)  
- [ ] ITR (Income Tax Return) of last 2–3 years  
- [ ] Audited Financial Statements (Balance Sheet, P&L)  
- [ ] Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- [ ] Business Registration Proof (Partnership Deed, Incorporation Certificate, etc.)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- [ ] Shipping Bill (proof of export)  
- [ ] Bill of Entry for imported inputs  
- [ ] Bank Realization Certificate (BRC) or Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC)  
- [ ] Proof of payment of customs duty (e.g., payment challan, TR6 copy)  
- [ ] Proof of payment of central excise duty on indigenous inputs (if applicable)  
- [ ] Consent letter from the manufacturer (if exporter is not the manufacturer)  
- [ ] Declaration regarding non-availment of CENVAT credit or other exemptions (self-declaration on letterhead)  
- [ ] Copy of IEC (Importer Exporter Code)  
- [ ] Bank account details for refund credit (to be verified against submitted proof)  

> **Source**: Required Documents list in KEY FACTS block.

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible and in PDF format (scanned copies preferred)  
- [ ] File size of each document does not exceed 5 MB  
- [ ] Name on documents matches exactly with PAN/IEC/GST records  
- [ ] Shipping Bill and Bill of Entry are dated and correspond to the same export transaction  
- [ ] BRC/FIRC shows realization of export proceeds against the relevant shipping bill  
- [ ] Proof of duty payment clearly indicates amount, date, and challan/TR6 number  
- [ ] Declaration on non-availment of CENVAT/GST credit is signed and stamped by authorized signatory  
- [ ] Consent letter (if applicable) is on manufacturer’s letterhead, signed, and specifies goods and duty details  
- [ ] All documents are in English or accompanied by certified translation  
- [ ] Bank account details match the name on PAN and IEC; IFSC code is valid

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- **Not required**. The Duty Drawback Scheme does not require a Detailed Project Report (DPR) or project report for claim submission.  
- **Note**: Verify with the jurisdictional Customs Officer or nodal officer if any additional project-specific documentation is requested during scrutiny (though not standard per evidence).  

> **Source**: Application Process in KEY FACTS block lists only shipping bill, BRC/FIRC, proof of duty payment, etc. No mention of DPR.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the ICEGATE portal at https://www.icegate.gov.in/ and log in using IEC-based credentials.  
2. Navigate to the **Drawback Claim** module under the Exports section.  
3. Select the correct **Shipping Bill** corresponding to the export transaction for which drawback is claimed.  
4. Enter the **FOB value of exports** as per the shipping bill (drawback rates are based on FOB value or fixed unit rates).  
5. Specify the **type of duty** being claimed: customs duty on imported inputs, central excise duty on indigenous inputs, or both.  
6. Upload all scheme-specific documents in the prescribed order and format (PDF, <5 MB each).  
7. In the declaration section, explicitly state that **no CENVAT credit or GST input tax credit** has been availed on the inputs for which drawback is claimed.  
8. Double-check bank account details (account number, IFSC, name) for refund credit — errors here cause delays or rejections.  
9. Submit the application and **save the acknowledgment number** immediately.  
10. Monitor the application status regularly via the ICEGATE portal using the acknowledgment number.  

> **Source**: Application Process and portal details from KEY FACTS block; portal-specific notes inferred from standard ICEGATE workflow.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All fields in the ICEGATE drawback claim form are filled accurately  
- [ ] Shipping Bill is selected and matches the export transaction  
- [ ] FOB value entered matches the Shipping Bill  
- [ ] Duty type (customs/excise/both) correctly specified  
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents are uploaded and legible  
- [ ] Declaration on non-availment of CENVAT/GST credit is included and signed  
- [ ] Bank account details for refund are correct and match PAN/IEC  
- [ ] Application is submitted within 12 months of let export date  
- [ ] Acknowledgment number is saved and communicated to the client  
- [ ] Follow-up reminder is set for 15 days post-submission

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the ICEGATE portal (https://www.icegate.gov.in/) after internal review  
- Immediately save and screenshot the **acknowledgment receipt** with application number and timestamp  
- Store the acknowledgment number in the case file and share it with the client  
- Set an automated follow-up reminder for **15 days** after submission to check status  
- If no update after 15 days, initiate follow-up via email or portal helpdesk

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify approval letter/intimation from Customs Office via ICEGATE portal or email  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned drawback amount matches eligibility calculation (based on input-output norms or fixed rates)  
- [ ] Verify refund credit has been received in the designated bank account (track UTR number)  
- [ ] Ensure utilization certificate is not required (drawback is a direct refund; no end-use tracking)  
- [ ] Inform client of successful credit and provide transaction details  
- [ ] Follow up for any TDS certificate if applicable (though drawback is typically exempt)  
- [ ] Close case and obtain client feedback  
- [ ] Retain all documents for 6 years as per audit requirements under Customs Act, 1962  

> **Source**: Financial Support section in KEY FACTS block states refunds are credited directly to bank account after verification; no mention of utilization certificate. Standard audit retention applies.
