## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified the Duty Drawback Scheme — a tax benefit scheme administered by the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance, Government of India — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is a refund of customs and central excise duties paid on inputs used in export production, calculated as a percentage of the FOB value of exports or a fixed amount per unit based on notified input-output norms.

## 2. Recommended Schemes

| Scheme | Potential Benefit | Est. Timeline | Confidence Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duty Drawback Scheme | Refund of customs and central excise duties on inputs used in export goods (amount varies per FOB value and notified rates) | 3–6 months | Medium |

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment based on import of inputs and subsequent export of finished goods  
2. Guidance on maintaining records of bills of entry, warehouse documents, production records, and shipping bills  
3. Preparation and electronic filing of drawback claim through the ICEGATE portal (https://www.icegate.gov.in/) along with shipping bill and proof of export  
4. Compilation and submission of supporting documents including bills of entry, bank realization certificate, and consent letter if applicable  
5. Liaison with jurisdictional Customs Office for verification of documents and adherence to time limits  
6. Monitoring claim status and responding to any queries from customs authorities  
7. Confirmation of drawback amount credit to exporter's bank account upon approval  

## 4. What We Need From You
1. Documents as per the scheme checklist: Shipping Bill, Bill of Entry for imported inputs, Bank Realization Certificate (BRC) or Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC), proof of payment of customs duty, consent letter from manufacturer (if exporter is not the manufacturer), declaration regarding non-availment of CENVAT credit or other exemptions, copy of IEC (Importer Exporter Code), and bank account details for refund credit  
2. Designated point of contact within your organization for coordination and document submission  
3. Response to queries or document requests within 2 working days to ensure timely processing
