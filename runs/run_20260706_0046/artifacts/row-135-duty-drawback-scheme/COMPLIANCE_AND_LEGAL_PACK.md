## 8D. Internal Data Handling Policy

All client data shall be handled in accordance with the following standards:
- Data is stored in encrypted cloud storage with access restricted to authorized personnel only.
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for all system access.
- Access to client data is limited to consultants directly engaged on the specific matter.
- No personal devices may be used to store or process client data without explicit encryption and security approval.
- Data retention: All case files are retained for a minimum of two (2) years post case closure, after which they are securely deleted unless longer retention is required by law or regulation.
- In the event of a data breach, notification will be made to affected clients and relevant authorities within 24 hours of discovery.

**Scheme-specific note:** All documents submitted to Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance, Government of India under Duty Drawback Scheme must be retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit purposes.
