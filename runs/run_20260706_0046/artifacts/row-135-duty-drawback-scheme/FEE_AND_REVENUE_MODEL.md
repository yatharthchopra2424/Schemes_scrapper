## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are tied to the actual drawback amount recovered, ensuring client interests are prioritized.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the 12-month filing window and document-intensive nature of Duty Drawback claims, retainers reflect upfront effort in eligibility assessment and documentation preparation.
3. **Transparency**: All fee structures are disclosed upfront, with success fees calculated as a percentage of the verified drawback amount credited to the client’s account.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Duty Drawback Scheme has a moderate risk profile due to its reliance on accurate documentation (shipping bills, bills of entry, BRC/FIRC) and strict 12-month deadline from export date. However, CBIC’s electronic processing via ICEGATE and direct bank credit reduce delays, supporting predictable revenue realization post-approval.
