## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping exporters and manufacturers recover customs and central excise duties paid on inputs used in export production through government schemes like the Duty Drawback Scheme. Many businesses leave significant refunds unclaimed simply because they’re unaware of the eligibility or find the process complex. I’d like to understand your export operations to see if this scheme could benefit you—no obligation, just a quick diagnostic.

### Discovery Questions
1. What goods do you manufacture and export, and what is your approximate annual export value (FOB)?
2. Do you import raw materials, components, or intermediates for use in your export production?
3. Have you paid customs duty on imported inputs or central excise duty on indigenous inputs used in your exported goods?
4. Are you currently claiming any duty refunds or duty exemptions on your exports?
5. What is your Importer Exporter Code (IEC) number, and is it active and linked to your bank account?
6. How do you currently maintain records of import (Bill of Entry), usage in production, and export (Shipping Bill)?
7. Have you filed a drawback claim in the past 12 months for any of your exports?
8. Are you availing CENVAT credit or input tax credit under GST on the same inputs for which you might seek drawback?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Exporter who has paid customs duty on imported inputs or central excise duty on indigenous inputs
- [ ] Inputs used in the manufacture of goods subsequently exported
- [ ] Claim filed within 12 months from date of let export (shipping bill date)
- [ ] Proper records maintained of import, usage in production, and export
- [ ] No CENVAT credit or GST input tax credit availed on the same inputs
- [ ] Goods not exported under bond or LUT without payment of IGST
- [ ] Active IEC (Importer Exporter Code) held
- [ ] Bank account details available for electronic refund credit

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you may be eligible for the Duty Drawback Scheme administered by CBIC, which provides refunds of customs and central excise duties paid on inputs used in export production. Let me explain how this through—this could mean a direct refund to your bank account, improving your export competitiveness.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Exporters often bear the cost of customs and central excise duties on inputs used in export production, which increases their cost base and reduces price competitiveness in international markets. These duties, if not refunded, effectively tax exports and erode margins—especially for MSMEs and manufacturers reliant on imported inputs.

**Scheme intro:**
The Duty Drawback Scheme, administered by the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance, Government of India, provides a refund of customs duty paid on imported inputs and central excise duty paid on indigenous inputs used in the manufacture of export goods. The drawback amount is calculated as a percentage of the FOB value of exports or a fixed amount per unit, based on input-output norms notified by CBIC, and is credited directly to the exporter’s bank account via electronic transfer.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You have paid customs duty on imported inputs or central excise duty on indigenous inputs used in your export production  
- Your goods are subsequently exported, and you maintain records of import, usage, and export  
- You have not availed CENVAT credit or GST input tax credit on the same inputs  
- Your claim can be filed within 12 months from the date of let export (shipping bill)
