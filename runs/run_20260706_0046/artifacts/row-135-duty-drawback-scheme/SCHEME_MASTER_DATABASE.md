### Duty Drawback Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Tax
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.icegate.gov.in/
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Claims must be filed within 12 months from the date of let export of the goods

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Exporters; manufacturers; MSME
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: All sectors engaged in manufacturing export goods using imported or indigenous inputs
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have paid customs duty on imported inputs or central excise duty on indigenous inputs used in the manufacture of goods that are subsequently exported; claim must be filed within 12 months from date of export; must maintain proper records of import, usage in production, and export; drawback not available if CENVAT credit or input tax credit under GST has been availed on the same inputs; no drawback allowed on goods exported under bond or LUT without payment of IGST

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Refund of customs duty paid on imported inputs and central excise duty paid on indigenous inputs used in manufacture of export goods; drawback rate fixed as percentage of FOB value of exports or fixed amount per unit based on input-output norms notified by CBIC
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit to exporter's bank account via electronic transfer
- Duration of benefit: One-time per eligible export shipment

#### Documents Required
1. Shipping Bill
2. Bill of Entry for imported inputs
3. Bank Realization Certificate (BRC) or Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC)
4. Proof of payment of customs duty
5. Consent letter from the manufacturer (if exporter is not the manufacturer)
6. Declaration regarding non-availment of CENVAT credit or other exemptions
7. Copy of IEC (Importer Exporter Code)
8. Bank account details for refund credit

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Determine eligibility based on import of inputs and subsequent export of finished goods.
2. Maintain records of bills of entry, warehouse documents, production records, and shipping bills.
3. File drawback claim electronically through the ICEGATE portal along with shipping bill and proof of export.
4. Submit supporting documents such as bills of entry, bank realization certificate, and consent letter if applicable.
5. Claim is processed by the jurisdictional Customs Office after verification of documents and adherence to time limits.
6. Upon approval, drawback amount is credited to the exporter's bank account.
