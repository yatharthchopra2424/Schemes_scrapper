## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment and scheme suitability analysis  
2. Document preparation and verification as per scheme requirements  
3. Online application submission via the DGFT portal  
4. Liaison with Regional Authority for application scrutiny and approval  
5. Post-approval support for import and export obligation tracking  
6. Assistance with redemption process after export realization  
7. Scheme-specific item: Portal registration and application submission on [https://dgft.gov.in/CP/?opt=advance-authorization](https://dgft.gov.in/CP/?opt=advance-authorization); no DPR/project report required based on evidence.

3. Timely responses within 2 business days to keep the application process on track
