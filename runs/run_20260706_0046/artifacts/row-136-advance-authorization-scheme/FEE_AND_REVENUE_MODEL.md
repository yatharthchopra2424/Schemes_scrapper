## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the indirect financial benefit derived from duty exemption on imported inputs, which reduces production costs and enhances export competitiveness.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile — approval is routine for eligible exporters, but export obligation non-compliance triggers duty recovery with interest, requiring careful client guidance.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainer structures ensure affordability for MSMEs while capturing higher value from mid-market and large exporters benefiting from significant duty savings.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Advance Authorization Scheme offers high financial impact through duty exemption (no direct cash outflow), but success depends on timely fulfillment of export obligation within 12 months. Processing time is typically 2–4 weeks post-submission, supporting a 3-month lag in revenue recognition for success fees.
