## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping manufacturers and exporters reduce their input costs through government schemes like the Advance Authorization Scheme, which allows duty-free import of inputs physically incorporated into export products. Since you're engaged in manufacturing and export, I’d like to understand your current input sourcing and export workflow to see if this scheme could benefit you.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your Importer Exporter Code (IEC) status, and is it currently active and linked to your PAN?
2. Are you currently importing raw materials, components, or inputs that are physically incorporated into your exported goods?
3. What is the HS code of your primary export product, and do you have export orders or self-declaration of export capability?
4. Are you registered for GST and do you have a valid GST registration certificate?
5. What is your typical export obligation timeline—do you usually fulfill export orders within 12 months of receiving inputs?
6. Are you importing any inputs that are consumed in manufacturing but not part of the final product (e.g., fuels, lubricants, catalysts)?
7. Do you have a bank certificate or cancelled cheque ready for submission as part of the application?
8. Are you applying for authorization for a specific export product, or do you export multiple products under different HS codes?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Manufacturers and exporters face high input costs due to customs duties on imported raw materials, components, and consumables. These duties increase production costs, reduce competitiveness in global markets, and create cash flow strain—especially when duties must be paid upfront before export realization.

**Scheme intro:**
The Advance Authorization Scheme, administered by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, provides duty exemption on imported inputs that are physically incorporated into export products. There is no direct cash subsidy; instead, the scheme exempts basic customs duty, additional customs duty, anti-dumping duty, safeguard duty, and social welfare surcharge on eligible inputs. The authorization is valid for 12 months from the date of issue, and the benefit is realized only upon fulfillment of the export obligation.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You have a valid IEC and are engaged in manufacture and export of goods  
- You import inputs that are physically incorporated into your exported product (not fuels or capital goods)  
- You can provide export orders or self-declaration of export capability  
- You are able to fulfill export obligation within the 12-month validity period
