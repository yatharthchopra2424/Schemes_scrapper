### Advance Authorization Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Directorate General of Foreign Trade, Ministry of Commerce and Industry
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dgft.gov.in/CP/?opt=advance-authorization
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Manufacturer exporter or merchant exporter tied to a supporting manufacturer
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Not specified
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have a valid Importer Exporter Code (IEC) and be engaged in the manufacture and export of goods; applicable to exports under the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Duty exemption on imported inputs (basic customs duty, additional customs duty, anti-dumping duty, safeguard duty, social welfare surcharge exempted); indirect financial support via reduced production cost
- Disbursement mode: Tax offset (foregone customs duties)
- Duration of benefit: One-time per authorization (valid for 12 months from date of issue)

#### Documents Required
1. PAN card of the applicant
2. Importer Exporter Code (IEC) certificate
3. GST registration certificate
4. Bank certificate or cancelled cheque
5. Details of the export product and its HS code
6. List of inputs required with their HS codes and quantities
7. Export order or self-declaration of export capability
8. Undertaking to fulfill export obligation
9. Copy of IEC holder's digital signature certificate (if applicable)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register on the DGFT website with a valid IEC.
2. Log in to the DGFT portal and navigate to the Advance Authorisation module.
3. Fill out the online application form with details of the export product, inputs required, and export obligation.
4. Upload required documents including PAN, IEC, GST certificate, and details of the export order or self-declaration of export capability.
5. Submit the application and pay the applicable application fee online.
6. Await scrutiny and approval by the concerned Regional Authority.
7. Upon approval, the Advance Authorization is issued electronically.
8. Import inputs as per the authorization.
9. Fulfill the export obligation within the validity period.
10. Apply for redemption of the authorization after export realization.
