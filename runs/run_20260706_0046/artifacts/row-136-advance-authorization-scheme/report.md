# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Advance Authorization Scheme** (Scheme ID: row-136) is a key export promotion initiative under the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, administered by the **Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT)**, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India. It operates on a **pan-India** geographic scope with **rolling basis** applications accepted throughout the year. The scheme was last updated in 2026 and remains active with high confidence in data accuracy.

### Objectives
The scheme aims to:
- Facilitate duty-free import of inputs required for export production
- Enhance competitiveness of Indian exports by reducing input costs
- Promote value addition and export-oriented manufacturing
- Support exporters in meeting international quality and delivery standards
- Encourage import of high-quality inputs for superior export products

> **Warning**: Only inputs **physically incorporated** into the export product are eligible. Inputs consumed in manufacturing but not part of the final product (e.g., fuel, lubricants, catalysts) are **excluded**. Authorization is **product-specific** and **non-transferable**.

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Benefit Type | Description | Financial Impact | Source |
|--------------|-------------|------------------|--------|
| Duty Exemption | Full exemption on basic customs duty, additional customs duty, anti-dumping duty, safeguard duty, and social welfare surcharge on imported inputs | Indirect financial support via reduced production cost; no direct cash subsidy | Key Facts |
| Cash Flow Benefit | Ability to import inputs without upfront duty payment | Eliminates working capital blockage for duties | Key Facts |
| Sourcing Flexibility | Global sourcing of inputs permitted | Enables access to high-quality inputs worldwide | Key Facts |
| Authorization Validity | 12 months from date of issue | Provides ample time for import, production, and export | Key Facts |
| Redemption Facility | Apply for redemption after export realization | Formal closure of obligation | Key Facts |

> **Note**: The scheme provides **no direct grant or cash subsidy**. The benefit is purely in the form of **foregone customs duties** on inputs physically incorporated into the export product, contingent upon fulfillment of export obligation.

### Critical Caveats & Compliance Requirements
> **Blockquote**:  
> - Export obligation **must** be fulfilled within the authorization’s validity period (typically 12 months).  
> - Failure to meet export obligation results in **withdrawal of duty exemption**, recovery of duties **plus interest**.  
> - Only inputs **physically incorporated** into the final export product qualify.  
> - Inputs used as **fuel, lubricants, or consumed in process** (not in final product) are **ineligible**.  
> - Scheme **does not cover** capital goods, fuel, or lubricants.  
> - Authorization is **specific to one export product** and **cannot be transferred**.  
> - All authorizations are subject to **audit and verification** by DGFT.  
> - Redemption must be applied for post-export; failure to redeem may lead to compliance issues.  
> - Digital signature may be required for certain submissions.
