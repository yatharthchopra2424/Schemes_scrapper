## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a person (company, partnership, proprietorship, or cooperative society)  
- [ ] Applicant is a resident or non-resident as per FEMA regulations  
- [ ] Applicant complies with the SEZ Act, 2005 and SEZ Rules, 2006  
- [ ] For sector-specific SEZ: Minimum land area of 50 hectares  
- [ ] For multi-product SEZ: Minimum land area of 1000 hectares  
- [ ] Proposed SEZ does not fall under restricted sectors (arms, ammunition, explosives, atomic substances, narcotics)  
- [ ] Applicant has financial capability to develop and operate the SEZ  
- [ ] Environmental clearance obtained (if applicable based on project location and size)  
- [ ] Undertaking regarding compliance with SEZ Act and Rules can be provided  
- [ ] Banker's certificate regarding financial capability can be secured

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
✅ **Required** — The SEZ Scheme mandates a project report as part of the application (Form-A).  
**Standard Sections to Include**:
- Executive Summary  
- Details of the applicant (promoters, directors, financial capability)  
- Description of the SEZ (sector-specific or multi-product, location, area)  
- Land details (title/lease, zoning, possession status)  
- Infrastructure development plan (internal roads, water, power, effluent treatment)  
- Proposed units and their activities (with export projections)  
- Employment generation plan (direct and indirect)  
- Foreign exchange earnings projection (to achieve positive NFE within 3 years)  
- Environmental management plan (if clearance obtained or pending)  
- Compliance statement with SEZ Act, 2005 and SEZ Rules, 2006  
- Implementation timeline (development to be completed within 5 years of LoA)  
- Financial projections and means of finance  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use only the **prescribed Form-A** available at https://www.sezindia.nic.in  
2. Fill all fields in block letters; do not leave any mandatory field blank  
3. Ensure consistency of applicant name, PAN, and address across all documents and the form  
4. Clearly specify whether the application is for a **developer**, **co-developer**, or **unit**  
5. Indicate SEZ type: sector-specific or multi-product, and confirm minimum land area compliance  
6. Declare that the applicant is a resident/non-resident as per FEMA and complies with SEZ Act/Rules  
7. Attach all documents as per the checklist; label annexures clearly  
8. Sign the declaration and get it notarized if required by the State Govt. or BoA

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the **Letter of Approval (LoA)** issued by the Board of Approval (BoA)  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned benefits: tax exemptions, duty concessions, procedural simplifications (as LoA is not a financial grant)  
- [ ] Ensure the developer executes a lease deed and begins development within the stipulated time  
- [ ] Monitor that the SEZ is developed within **5 years from the date of LoA** (Key Caveat)  
- [ ] Verify that units within the SEZ achieve **positive net foreign exchange earnings within 3 years of commencement of operations** (Key Caveat)  
- [ ] Guide client on utilization of benefits: duty-free import/domestic procurement, tax exemptions, external commercial borrowing (up to USD 500 million/year)  
- [ ] Assist in filing periodic compliance reports as required by the BoA  
- [ ] Coordinate with the client’s finance team to ensure invoice success fee is processed upon realization of benefits
