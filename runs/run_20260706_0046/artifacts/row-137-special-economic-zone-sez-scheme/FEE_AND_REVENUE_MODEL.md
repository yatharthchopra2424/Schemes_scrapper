## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the indirect financial benefits derived from tax exemptions, duty concessions, and operational savings under the SEZ Scheme, which accrue over time and are not direct cash grants.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Reflects the scheme’s multi-stage approval process (State Government → Inter-Ministerial Committee → Board of Approval) and typical 90-day timeline for Letter of Approval, with additional time required for land development and compliance.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainer structures are scaled to client capacity, recognizing that SEZ development requires significant capital, land, and regulatory expertise — typically accessible only to mid-market and larger entities.
4. **Outcome Partnership**: Success fees are contingent on securing the Letter of Approval (LoA) from the Board of Approval, ensuring alignment between consultant effort and client outcome.

> *Scheme-Specific Note*: The SEZ Scheme involves a high regulatory burden and long development horizon (minimum 5 years to develop SEZ from LoA date). Success is not immediate; revenue recognition lags approval by 3–6 months due to post-approval development milestones. Evidence shows approvals are granted on a rolling basis, but timelines depend on State Government responsiveness (45-day recommendation window) and BoA review (45-day decision window post-recommendation).
