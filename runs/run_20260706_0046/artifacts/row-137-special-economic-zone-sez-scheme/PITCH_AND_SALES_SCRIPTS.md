## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping businesses in manufacturing, services, and infrastructure sectors access government incentives that reduce operational costs through tax and duty exemptions. The Special Economic Zone (SEZ) Scheme is designed for entities looking to set up or operate within SEZs to boost exports and attract investment. I’d like to understand your expansion or export plans to see if this scheme aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
- What is your current or planned annual export turnover, and are you aiming to increase it over the next 3–5 years?
- Are you considering setting up a new manufacturing or service unit, or expanding an existing one, with a focus on export-oriented production?
- What is the legal structure of your business (e.g., company, partnership, proprietorship), and when was it incorporated or registered?
- Do you have access to land or are you evaluating locations for a new facility, preferably with at least 50 hectares for sector-specific or 1000 hectares for multi-product SEZs?
- Are you involved in any restricted sectors such as arms, ammunition, explosives, atomic substances, or narcotics?
- Have you obtained or are you planning to obtain environmental clearance for your proposed project?
- What is your estimated project cost, and do you have a banker’s certificate confirming your financial capability to undertake the project?
- Are you looking for single window clearance and procedural simplifications to accelerate your project approvals?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is a person (company, partnership, proprietorship, or cooperative society)
- [ ] Applicant is a resident or non-resident as per FEMA regulations
- [ ] Business operates or plans to operate in permitted sectors (not arms, ammunition, explosives, atomic substances, or narcotics)
- [ ] Project involves setting up an SEZ or developing infrastructure facilities within an SEZ
- [ ] Minimum land requirement met: 50 hectares for sector-specific SEZ or 1000 hectares for multi-product SEZ
- [ ] Environmental clearance obtained or in process (if applicable)
- [ ] Commitment to be a positive net foreign exchange earner within 3 years of commencement of operations
- [ ] Required documents can be compiled: project report, incorporation certificate, PAN, board resolution, promoter details, land documents, undertaking, banker’s certificate

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your project may align with the SEZ Scheme’s objectives of promoting exports and attracting investment through fiscal incentives. The scheme offers significant indirect financial benefits via tax exemptions, duty concessions, and procedural simplifications—without direct grants but with substantial cost savings. Would you like me to outline how this works for businesses like yours?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Businesses engaged in export-oriented manufacturing or services face high operational costs due to duties on imports, multiple tax levies, and complex approval processes, which reduce competitiveness in global markets.

**Scheme intro:**
The Special Economic Zone (SEZ) Scheme, administered by the Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, is a tax_benefit scheme that provides fiscal incentives including duty-free import/domestic procurement, exemption from central and state taxes and levies, exemption from minimum alternate tax (MAT) under Section 115JB of the Income Tax Act, external commercial borrowing up to USD 500 million per year, single window clearance, and exemption from industrial licensing and service tax. While it does not offer direct financial grants, these incentives significantly reduce capital and operational costs.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a [company/partnership/proprietorship/cooperative society] eligible to apply as a developer, co-developer, unit, or service provider under the SEZ Act, 2005
- Your project involves setting up or developing infrastructure in an SEZ, meeting the minimum land requirement of 50 hectares (sector-specific) or 1000 hectares (multi-product)
- You are not engaged in any restricted sectors such as arms, ammunition, explosives, atomic substances, or narcotics
- You can commit to becoming a positive net foreign exchange earner within three years of commencing operations
