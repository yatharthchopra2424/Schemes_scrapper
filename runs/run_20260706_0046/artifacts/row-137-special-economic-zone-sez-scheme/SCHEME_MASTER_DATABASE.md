### Special Economic Zone (SEZ) Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Tax incentives
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.sezindia.nic.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Any person, including a company, partnership, proprietorship, or cooperative society
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Developers; co-developers; units; manufacturers; service providers
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Applicant must be a resident or non-resident as per FEMA regulations; must comply with the SEZ Act, 2005 and SEZ Rules, 2006; minimum land area requirements: 50 hectares for sector-specific SEZs and 1000 hectares for multi-product SEZs; units must be positive net foreign exchange earners within 3 years of commencement of operations; restricted sectors (arms, ammunition, explosives, atomic substances, narcotics) not permitted

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Fiscal incentives including duty-free import/domestic procurement; exemption from central and state taxes and levies; exemption from minimum alternate tax under Section 115JB of the Income Tax Act; external commercial borrowing up to USD 500 million per year without maturity restriction; single window clearance; exemption from industrial licensing; exemption from service tax
- Disbursement mode: Tax offset / Duty concession
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing during compliance period (subject to conditions)

#### Documents Required
1. Project report detailing the SEZ proposal
2. Certificate of incorporation or registration of the applicant
3. PAN card of the applicant
4. Board resolution authorizing the application
5. Details of promoters and directors
6. Land title documents or lease agreement
7. Environmental clearance (if applicable)
8. Undertaking regarding compliance with SEZ Act and Rules
9. Banker's certificate regarding financial capability

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Submit an application in the prescribed format (Form-A) to the concerned State Government or directly to the Board of Approval (BoA)
2. The State Government forwards its recommendations within 45 days
3. The application is reviewed by the Inter-Ministerial Committee (IMC)
4. The Board of Approval (BoA) takes a final decision within 45 days of receiving the State Government's recommendation
5. Upon approval, a Letter of Approval (LoA) is issued
6. After obtaining LoA, the developer must execute a lease deed and develop the SEZ within the stipulated time
