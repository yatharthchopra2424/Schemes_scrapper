## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The Special Economic Zone (SEZ) Scheme is a pan-India initiative administered by the Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, designed to promote exports, attract investment, generate employment, develop infrastructure, and enhance foreign exchange earnings through fiscal incentives and procedural simplifications. Unlike direct grant schemes, the SEZ Scheme provides indirect financial benefits via tax exemptions, duty concessions, and regulatory relaxations, with benefits accruing through reduced operational and capital costs.

**Application Portal:** [https://www.sezindia.nic.in](https://www.sezindia.nic.in)  
**Implementing Agency:** Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry  
**Scheme Type:** Tax Benefit  
**Geographic Scope:** Pan-India  
**Status / Deadlines:** Rolling basis — applications accepted throughout the year  
**Confidence Level:** Medium  

---

### Objectives
The SEZ Scheme aims to achieve the following strategic objectives:
- Promote exports of goods and services  
- Attract domestic and foreign investment  
- Generate employment opportunities  
- Develop infrastructure facilities  
- Enhance foreign exchange earnings  

These objectives are pursued through a combination of fiscal incentives, single-window clearance, and regulatory exemptions designed to make SEZs globally competitive hubs for manufacturing and services.

### Benefits & Financial Support
The SEZ Scheme does **not** provide direct financial grants or subsidies. Instead, it offers fiscal incentives that reduce operational and capital costs, leading to indirect financial benefits.

|----------------------|------------------------|-----------|----------|

### Application Process
The application process involves multiple stages, including state-level recommendation, central review, and final approval by the Board of Approval (BoA). The process is time-bound at each stage but operates on a rolling basis overall.

#### Detailed Step-by-Step Process
1. **Application Submission:**  
   - Submit Form-A (prescribed format) to the concerned State Government **or** directly to the Board of Approval (BoA).  
   - Portal: [https://www.sezindia.nic.in](https://www.sezindia.nic.in)  
   - Contact: Email: sez@sezindia.nic.in | Phone: +91-11-23063444  

2. **State Government Review (if applicable):**  
   - State Government forwards recommendations within **45 days** of receipt.  

3. **Inter-Ministerial Committee (IMC) Review:**  
   - Application reviewed by IMC for technical, fiscal, and strategic viability.  

4. **Board of Approval (BoA) Decision:**  
   - BoA takes final decision within **45 days** of receiving the State Government’s recommendation.  
   - If applied directly to BoA, the 45-day clock starts from application receipt.  

5. **Letter of Approval (LoA):**  
   - Issued upon approval.  
   - Enables execution of lease deed and commencement of development.  

6. **Development & Compliance:**  
   - SEZ must be developed within **5 years** from LoA date.  
   - Units must achieve positive net foreign exchange earnings within **3 years** of commencement of operations.  
   - Ongoing compliance with SEZ Act, 2005 and SEZ Rules, 2006 required.  

#### Required Documents
Applicants must submit the following with Form-A:
1. Project report detailing the SEZ proposal  
2. Certificate of incorporation or registration of the applicant  
3. PAN card of the applicant  
4. Board resolution authorizing the application  
5. Details of promoters and directors  
6. Land title documents or lease agreement  
7. Environmental clearance (if applicable)  
8. Undertaking regarding compliance with SEZ Act and Rules  
9. Banker's certificate regarding financial capability  

> **Caveat:** Incomplete documentation will delay processing. Ensure all documents are attested and up-to-date.
