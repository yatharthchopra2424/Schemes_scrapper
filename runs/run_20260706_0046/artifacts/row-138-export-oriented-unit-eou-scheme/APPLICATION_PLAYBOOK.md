# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Unit undertakes to export entire production of goods and services (except permissible sales in Domestic Tariff Area)
- [ ] Unit is engaged in manufacturing, services, software development, agriculture, aquaculture, animal husbandry, floriculture, horticulture, pisciculture, poultry, sericulture, viticulture, or mining
- [ ] Unit must achieve positive net foreign exchange earnings cumulatively over a period of five years from commencement of production
- [ ] Unit is not engaged in any activity listed in the negative list of exports as per the Foreign Trade Policy
- [ ] Unit has valid registration (Certificate of Incorporation/Registration, Memorandum and Articles of Association/Partnership Deed)
- [ ] Unit has prepared a detailed Project Report covering investment, employment, export projections, and foreign exchange earnings
- [ ] Unit possesses Bank Solvency Certificate
- [ ] Unit has Income Tax Returns of promoters for last 3 years
- [ ] Unit has List of Plant and Machinery with CIF value
- [ ] Unit has Details of Technical Know-how or Collaboration Agreement (if applicable)
- [ ] Unit has Undertaking to achieve positive net foreign exchange earnings
- [ ] Unit has Letter of Authorization for signatory
- [ ] For companies: Board Resolution authorizing the application

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
- Memorandum and Articles of Association / Partnership Deed
- PAN Card
- GST Registration Certificate
- Income Tax Returns (last 3 years)
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable)
- IEC (Importer Exporter Code) Certificate

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Project Report detailing investment, employment, export projections, and foreign exchange earnings
- Bank Solvency Certificate
- List of Plant and Machinery with CIF value
- Details of Technical Know-how or Collaboration Agreement (if any)
- Undertaking to achieve positive net foreign exchange earnings
- Letter of Authorization for signatory
- Board Resolution authorizing the application (for companies)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible scans or certified copies
- File names must match document type (e.g., "Project_Report_EOU.pdf")
- File size must not exceed 10 MB per document
- All documents must be in English or accompanied by certified translation
- Signatures and seals must be visible and valid
- Dates on documents must be current (within last 6 months for financials, unless specified otherwise)
- Undertaking and Board Resolution must be on company letterhead and duly signed

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Required.** The Project Report must include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Details of promoters and their background  
- Description of proposed project (manufacturing/service activity)  
- Investment plan (capital goods, raw materials, working capital)  
- Employment generation plan  
- Export projections (year-wise for 5 years)  
- Foreign exchange earnings projections (cumulative over 5 years)  
- Raw material sourcing plan (imported vs indigenous)  
- Marketing and export strategy  
- Risk analysis and mitigation measures  
- Implementation timeline  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download Form F from the DGFT EOU portal: https://www.dgft.gov.in/CP/?opt=EOU  
2. Fill all fields in block letters; do not leave any mandatory field blank  
3. Ensure consistency between data in the form and supporting documents (e.g., investment amount, export projections)  
4. Use the same authorized signatory name as in the Letter of Authorization and Board Resolution  
5. Mention the IEC number correctly in the application form  
6. Specify the exact manufacturing/service activity as per eligibility list  
7. Declare commitment to achieve positive net foreign exchange earnings over five years  
8. Undertake not to engage in any activity from the negative list of exports  
9. Attach all supporting documents as per the checklist  
10. Review the form thoroughly before submission; verify arithmetic totals  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All sections of Form F are completed accurately  
- [ ] Project Report is attached and aligns with form data  
- [ ] Bank Solvency Certificate is valid and issued by a scheduled bank  
- [ ] Income Tax Returns of promoters (last 3 years) are included  
- [ ] List of Plant and Machinery with CIF value is detailed  
- [ ] Undertaking to achieve positive net foreign exchange earnings is signed  
- [ ] Letter of Authorization and Board Resolution (for companies) are attached  
- [ ] All declarations are signed and dated  
- [ ] No document exceeds 10 MB; file names are appropriate  
- [ ] Application is ready for submission via DGFT portal

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed Form F and supporting documents via the DGFT EOU portal: https://www.dgft.gov.in/CP/?opt=EOU  
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation page showing the application number and timestamp  
- Store the application reference number securely in the case file  
- Set calendar reminders for follow-up at 15 days and 30 days post-submission  
- Notify the client of successful submission with the reference number

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from DGFT/UAC  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned benefits: duty-free import eligibility, excise/service tax exemption, duty drawback entitlement  
- [ ] Execute Legal Undertaking (LUT) or bond with customs authorities as required  
- [ ] Commence operations only after approval and LUT execution  
- [ ] Maintain records of all imports/utilization under the scheme for audit purposes  
- [ ] File periodic reports as required by DGFT (if any)  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with DTA sales limit (up to 50% of annual production on payment of applicable duties)  
- [ ] Monitor cumulative net foreign exchange earnings to ensure positivity over five years  
- [ ] Prepare for utilization certificate submission if required for benefit continuation  
- [ ] Coordinate with client to claim duty drawback on exported goods  
- [ ] Invoice success fee as per engagement terms
