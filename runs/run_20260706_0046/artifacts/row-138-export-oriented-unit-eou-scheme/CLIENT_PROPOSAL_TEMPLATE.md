## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified the Export Oriented Unit (EOU) Scheme — a tax benefit scheme administered by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT), Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry — for which your business appears eligible. The scheme offers fiscal incentives through duty-free import of inputs, exemption from central excise and service tax, and eligibility for duty drawback, resulting in indirect financial benefits via reduced input costs and tax liabilities.

1. Initial eligibility assessment and scheme suitability analysis  
2. Documentation review and gap identification  
3. Preparation of project report detailing investment, employment, export projections, and foreign exchange earnings
