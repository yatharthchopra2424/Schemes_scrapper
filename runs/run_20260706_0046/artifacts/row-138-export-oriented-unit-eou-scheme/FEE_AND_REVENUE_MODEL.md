## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the indirect financial benefits derived from duty exemptions, tax concessions, and improved export competitiveness under the EOU Scheme, which enhance profitability through reduced input costs.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile due to the 5-year net foreign exchange earnings requirement and export obligation compliance; processing times are typically 3–6 months for approval, introducing revenue lag.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainer structures ensure affordability for MSMEs and startups (primary beneficiaries), while scaled success fees accommodate larger enterprises with higher benefit potential.
4. **Transparency & Compliance**: All fees are clearly communicated and tied to measurable outcomes, such as approval status and benefit realization, adhering to DGFT guidelines and ethical consulting standards.
