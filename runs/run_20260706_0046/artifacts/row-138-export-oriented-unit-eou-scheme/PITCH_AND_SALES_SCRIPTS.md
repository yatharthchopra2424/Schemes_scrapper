## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping manufacturers and exporters reduce their input costs and tax liabilities through government schemes like the Export Oriented Unit (EOU) Scheme. Many businesses in your sector are unaware they can import raw materials and capital goods duty-free while exporting. I’d like to understand your current export operations to see if this scheme could improve your competitiveness and profitability.

### Discovery Questions
1. What year was your unit incorporated or commenced commercial production?
2. Are you engaged in manufacturing, services, software development, agriculture, aquaculture, or another eligible activity under the EOU Scheme?
3. Do you undertake to export your entire production of goods and services, except for permissible sales in the Domestic Tariff Area (DTA)?
4. What are your projected annual export earnings and foreign exchange earnings over the next five years?
5. Are you currently importing inputs, raw materials, or capital goods for your export production?
6. Do you pay central excise duty or service tax on goods/services used in your production process?
7. Have you achieved positive net foreign exchange earnings cumulatively since commencement of production?
8. Are you involved in any activity listed in the negative list of exports under the Foreign Trade Policy?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Unit undertakes to export entire production (except permissible DTA sales)
- [ ] Engaged in eligible activity: manufacturing, services, software, agriculture, aquaculture, animal husbandry, floriculture, horticulture, pisciculture, poultry, sericulture, viticulture, or mining
- [ ] Commenced production and committed to achieve positive net foreign exchange earnings over five years
- [ ] Not engaged in any activity in the negative list of exports
- [ ] Has or will submit: Certificate of Incorporation, MOA/AOA, Project Report, Bank Solvency Certificate, IT Returns of promoters (last 3 years), Plant & Machinery list, Technical Know-how details (if any), Undertaking for NFEE, Authorization Letter, Board Resolution (if company)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your unit may meet the core eligibility criteria for the Export Oriented Unit (EOU) Scheme administered by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT). This scheme allows duty-free import of inputs and exemptions from central excise and service tax, directly reducing your input costs and improving export competitiveness. Shall I walk you through how it works and the specific benefits for your unit?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Manufacturers and exporters face high input costs due to customs duties on raw materials and capital goods, plus central excise and service tax on production inputs. These costs reduce price competitiveness in international markets and squeeze profit margins, especially for units committed to exporting their entire production.

**Scheme intro:**
The Export Oriented Unit (EOU) Scheme, administered by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, is a tax benefit scheme that provides fiscal incentives through exemptions and concessions. It does not offer direct financial subsidies but enables duty-free import of capital goods, raw materials, components, consumables, spares, and packaging materials required for export production. Units are exempt from paying central excise duty on goods manufactured and service tax on services utilized in the production process. The financial benefit arises from reduced input costs and tax liabilities, improving competitiveness and profitability.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your unit is engaged in [manufacturing/services/etc.], an eligible activity under the EOU Scheme  
- You undertake to export your entire production (except up to 50% DTA sales on payment of duties)  
- You have committed to achieve positive net foreign exchange earnings cumulatively over five years from commencement of production  
- You are not involved in any prohibited activity under the negative list of exports
