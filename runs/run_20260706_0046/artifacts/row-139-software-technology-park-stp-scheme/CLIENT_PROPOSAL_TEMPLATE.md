## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified the Software Technology Park (STP) Scheme — a fiscal benefit scheme administered by Software Technology Parks of India (STPI), Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) — for which your business appears eligible. The scheme offers benefits including duty-free imports, GST refunds on capital goods purchased from Domestic Tariff Area (DTA), and 100% depreciation on computers and peripherals over five years, with no direct financial grant amount specified.
