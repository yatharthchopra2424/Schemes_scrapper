## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees aligned with the fiscal benefits delivered (duty-free imports, GST refunds, 100% depreciation), which can yield substantial savings over 5+ years.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Success Fee**: Success fee reflects moderate risk due to compliance requirements (100% export orientation, GST refund eligibility tied to DTA purchases, and donation restrictions after two years).
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainer structure scaled to client size; STP Scheme benefits are most relevant to software exporters with turnover ≥ ₹10L, making Growth tier the typical entry point.
4. **Processing Time Efficiency**: Standard approvals within 10–15 days (Letter of Permission: 10 days, Legal Undertaking: 3 days, Green Card: 2 days) enable faster revenue realization vs. grant-based schemes.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Retainer fee for Software Technology Park (STP) Scheme advisory services  
**Scope**: Includes eligibility assessment, document preparation (Letter of Permission, Legal Undertaking-cum-endorsement, Green Card application), liaison with STPI authorities, and compliance guidance for duty-free imports and GST refunds.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for Software Technology Park (STP) Scheme benefits realization  
**Implementing Agency**: Software Technology Parks of India (STPI), Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)  
**Basis**: Calculated on the quantified value of fiscal benefits availed (e.g., customs duty saved, GST refund claimed, depreciation benefits) over the first 12–24 months post-approval.
