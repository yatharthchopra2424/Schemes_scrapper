## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)

Before we explore whether this fits your business, I’d like to understand your current operations, export activities, and growth plans to see if we can genuinely help you reduce costs and improve compliance.

### Discovery Questions
1. What year was your company incorporated, and are you primarily engaged in the development and export of computer software or professional services via communication links or physical media?
2. What percentage of your revenue comes from exports of software or software services, and do you maintain 100% export orientation for these activities?
3. Are you planning to import hardware, software, or second-hand capital goods for your software development or export operations?
4. Do you currently incur GST on capital goods purchased from the Domestic Tariff Area (DTA) that you wish to reclaim?
5. Have you considered setting up operations anywhere in India, or are you looking for a single-window clearance system to avoid multiple regulatory approvals?
6. Are you interested in availing 100% depreciation on computers and peripherals over five years for tax optimization?
7. Do you plan to donate computers or peripherals to recognized educational or charitable institutions after two years of import without duty payment?
8. Is foreign equity involved in your company, or are you considering 100% foreign investment for your software export unit?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Software exporters face high upfront costs due to customs duties on imported hardware and software, GST liabilities on capital goods purchased from DTA, and complex regulatory approvals across multiple agencies. These factors reduce competitiveness, delay project timelines, and increase compliance burden—especially for firms aiming to scale exports or reinvest in technology.

**Scheme intro:**
The Software Technology Park (STP) Scheme, administered by Software Technology Parks of India (STPI) under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), is a 100% export-oriented scheme that provides fiscal benefits including duty-free import of hardware and software, permission to import second-hand capital goods, GST refund on capital goods purchased from the Domestic Tariff Area (DTA), and 100% depreciation on computers and peripherals over five years. It offers single-window clearance for setting up STP units anywhere in India, with approvals from jurisdictional STPI authorities within 10 days for projects under Rs.100 million with Indian Investment. 100% foreign equity is permitted, and capital, know-how fees, royalties, and dividends can be freely repatriated after income tax payment.

- You intend to import hardware, software, or second-hand capital goods for your export operations  
- You are eligible for single-window clearance through STPI, with project approval possible within 10 days if under Rs.100 million with Indian Investment
