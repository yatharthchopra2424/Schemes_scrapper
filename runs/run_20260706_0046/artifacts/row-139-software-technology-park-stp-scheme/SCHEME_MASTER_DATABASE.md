### Software Technology Park (STP) Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Software Technology Parks of India (STPI), Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://stpi.in/en/stp-scheme
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Any entity engaged in the development and export of computer software, including professional services via communication links or physical media
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Computer software
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Jurisdictional STPI authorities can clear projects costing less than Rs.100 million with Indian Investment; 100% foreign equity is permitted

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Fiscal benefits including duty-free imports, GST refunds on capital goods purchased from DTA, and 100% depreciation on computers and peripherals over five years
- Disbursement mode: Tax offset / Duty exemption
- Duration of benefit: One-time for setup; ongoing benefits (e.g., depreciation over 5 years, duty-free imports as long as compliant)

#### Documents Required
1. Letter of Permission application
2. Legal Undertaking-cum-endorsement of Capital Goods (CG)
3. Green Card application
4. Project details for clearance (if cost < Rs.100 million with Indian Investment)
5. Details of foreign equity (if applicable)
6. Import/export documentation for hardware and software
7. Proof of donation eligibility for computers after two years (if applicable)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Apply for registration under the STP Scheme through STPI
2. Obtain Letter of Permission from jurisdictional STPI authorities (within 10 days)
3. Submit Legal Undertaking-cum-endorsement of Capital Goods (CG) (within 3 days)
4. Receive Green Card (within 2 days)
5. For online registration, click 'For Online Registration Click Here' on the STP Scheme page
