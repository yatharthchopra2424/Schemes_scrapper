# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The Software Technology Park (STP) Scheme is a 100% export-oriented scheme administered by Software Technology Parks of India (STPI) under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY). It focuses exclusively on the development and export of computer software, including professional services delivered via communication links or physical media. The scheme integrates the government concepts of 100% Export Oriented Units (EOU) and Export Processing Zones (EPZ) with the Science Parks/Technology Parks model. Its unique feature is the provision of single-point contact services for member units, enabling export operations at a pace commensurate with international practices.

### Objectives
- To promote the development and export of computer software  
- To provide single-window clearance and statutory services to exporters  
- To enable duty-free import of hardware and software for STP units  
- To allow 100% foreign equity and free repatriation of capital and profits  
- To support re-export of capital goods and donation of computers after two years  
- To provide 100% depreciation on computers and peripherals over five years  
- To permit sales in Domestic Tariff Area (DTA) with GST refund on capital goods  
- To simplify export performance norms to 'Positive Net Foreign Exchange Earnings'

### Eligibility Matrix
| Criteria | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Entity Type** | Any entity engaged in the development and export of computer software, including professional services via communication links or physical media |
| **Geographic Scope** | Pan-India; STP units may be set up anywhere in India |
| **Foreign Equity** | 100% foreign equity permitted |
| **Project Cost Threshold** | Jurisdictional STPI authorities can clear projects costing less than Rs.100 million with Indian Investment |
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Software exporters; IT/ITES companies; computer software developers |
| **Export Orientation** | Must maintain 100% export orientation for software and professional services |
| **Minimum Export Performance** | Simplified norms: "Positive Net Foreign Exchange Earnings" |

### Benefits & Financial Support
The STP Scheme does **not** provide direct financial support such as grants, loans, or subsidies. Instead, it offers fiscal benefits:

| Benefit Category | Specific Benefits |
|------------------|-------------------|
| **Import Benefits** | Duty-free import of hardware and software; permission to import second-hand capital goods |
| **Export Benefits** | Re-export of capital goods allowed; simplified minimum export performance norms (Positive Net Foreign Exchange Earnings) |
| **Domestic Sales** | Sales in Domestic Tariff Area (DTA) allowed with GST refund on capital goods purchased from DTA |
| **Tax & Depreciation** | 100% depreciation on computers and computer peripherals over five years |
| **Repatriation** | Free repatriation of capital invested by foreign entrepreneurs, know-how fees, royalty, dividend after payment of Income Taxes |
| **Donation** | Donation of computers and peripherals to recognized non-commercial educational institutions, charitable hospitals, public libraries, funded R&D establishments, or Govt. of India/State/UT after two years without duty payment |
| **Administrative** | Single window clearance system; permission to set up STP units anywhere in India; use of computer systems for commercial training permissible if no terminals outside STP premises |

### Required Documents
1. Letter of Permission application  
2. Legal Undertaking-cum-endorsement of Capital Goods (CG)  
3. Green Card application  
4. Project details for clearance (if cost < Rs.100 million with Indian Investment)  
5. Details of foreign equity (if applicable)  
6. Import/export documentation for hardware and software  
7. Proof of donation eligibility for computers after two years (if applicable)

### Key Timelines & Deadlines
- **Application Status**: Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications accepted year-round.  
- **Letter of Permission**: Issued within 10 days of application  
- **Legal Undertaking-cum-endorsement of CG**: Submitted within 3 days of Letter of Permission  
- **Green Card**: Issued within 2 days of Legal Undertaking submission  
- **Last Updated**: 2021 (per KEY FACTS); portal last updated 23-01-2021 (per crawled page)  
- **Contact Details**:  
  - Email: sachin[at]stpi[dot]in (Chief Finance Officer, STPI HQ)  
  - Phone: +91-11-24628081  

### Key Caveats & Warnings
> **Warning**: Units must maintain 100% export orientation for software and professional services. Benefits like duty-free import and GST refund are contingent on compliance with export performance norms.  
>  
> **Warning**: Donation of computers and peripherals is permitted only after two years of import.  
>  
> **Warning**: 100% depreciation benefit is spread over five years.  
>  
> **Warning**: Sales in Domestic Tariff Area (DTA) are permitted but capital goods purchased from DTA are eligible for GST refund only.  
>  
> **Warning**: Re-export of capital goods is permitted under specified conditions.
