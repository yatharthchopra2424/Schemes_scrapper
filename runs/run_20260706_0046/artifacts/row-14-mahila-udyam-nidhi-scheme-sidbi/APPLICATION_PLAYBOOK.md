# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a woman entrepreneur (sole proprietor, partner in partnership firm, or director of women-owned private limited company with women holding ≥51% shareholding)
- [ ] Enterprise is engaged in manufacturing, service, or agro-processing sector
- [ ] Applicant is not a defaulter to any bank or financial institution
- [ ] For existing enterprises: unit has been in operation for at least two years
- [ ] For new projects: eligible (no minimum operational period required)
- [ ] Udyam Registration Certificate is obtained and valid
- [ ] Business registration documents are in place (proprietorship/partnership deed/incorporation certificate)
- [ ] Project is viable and assessed for creditworthiness by SIDBI

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation certificate / Partnership deed / Proprietorship proof
- PAN card of the woman entrepreneur
- Aadhaar card of the woman entrepreneur
- Udyam Registration Certificate
- GST registration certificate (if applicable)
- Bank account statement (last 6 months)
- Income Tax Returns (last 2 years, if applicable)
- Audited financial statements (last 2 years for existing units)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) including technical, financial, and economic aspects
- Land and building documents (ownership or lease agreement)
- Machinery/equipment quotations and invoices (for new projects or expansion)
- No dues certificate from existing bankers (if any)
- Details of promoters and their background

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format, and clearly named (e.g., "PAN_WomanEntrepreneur.pdf")
- File size for each document should not exceed 5 MB
- Names on documents must match exactly with the application form
- KYC documents (PAN, Aadhaar) must be self-attested and valid
- Financial statements must be audited and certified by a Chartered Accountant
- Udyam Registration Certificate must be downloaded from the official Udyam portal and be current
- Project Report must be comprehensive and signed by the applicant

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Required.** The Project Report must include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Promoter Background  
- Project Description (product/service, location, technology)  
- Market Analysis  
- Technical Feasibility (machinery, process, utilities)  
- Financial Projections (cost of project, means of financing, profitability, break-even, cash flow)  
- Economic and Social Impact (employment generation, women empowerment)  
- Risk Analysis and Mitigation  
- Implementation Schedule  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the application form from the SIDBI website or collect it from the nearest SIDBI branch.  
2. Fill all fields in **block letters** using black ink; do not leave any mandatory field blank.  
3. Ensure the woman entrepreneur’s name matches exactly across PAN, Aadhaar, Udyam, and application form.  
4. Clearly state the loan amount requested under “Financial Assistance Required” based on project cost and working capital needs.  
5. In the “Constitution of Applicant” section, select “Women-owned Enterprise” and specify shareholding ≥51%.  
6. Under “Existing Loans,” declare “No” if there are no defaults; otherwise, disclose details transparently.  
7. Attach a self-declaration confirming compliance with eligibility criteria (non-defaulter, women entrepreneur, etc.).  
8. Verify that the Udyam Registration Number is correctly entered and matches the certificate.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All sections of the application form are completed and legible  
- [ ] Photograph of the applicant is affixed (if required)  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached and labeled  
- [ ] Declarations are signed and dated by the applicant  
- [ ] Project Report is bound and includes all required sections  
- [ ] Loan amount requested is justified by the DPR  
- [ ] No defaults declared; if any, disclosed with explanation  
- [ ] Woman entrepreneur’s shareholding ≥51% is verified and documented  
- [ ] Udyam Registration Number is valid and matches records

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed application and documents in person at the **nearest SIDBI branch** or through an authorized channel.  
- Obtain a **signed acknowledgment receipt** with Inward Number and date.  
- Take a clear photograph of the submitted application stack and the acknowledgment for records.  
- Store the reference number (Inward No.) securely and set follow-up reminders at 15 and 25 days.  
- Do not submit incomplete applications; ensure all checklists are cleared before submission.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the **approval letter** from SIDBI specifying sanctioned loan amount, interest rate, and tenure  
- [ ] Confirm that the loan is covered under **CGTMSE** (if applicable) for collateral-free lending  
- [ ] Ensure disbursement is made in stages as per project progress (as per SIDBI’s standard process)  
- [ ] Track utilization of funds against the approved project cost and working capital requirements  
- [ ] Prepare Utilization Certificate (UC) and submit to SIDBI upon completion of each disbursement tranche  
- [ ] Monitor repayment schedule and ensure timely EMI payments post-moratorium (if any)  
- [ ] Follow up with client for success fee invoice upon successful disbursement and utilization  
- [ ] Maintain all records (approval, disbursement letters, UC, repayment schedule) for audit and future reference
