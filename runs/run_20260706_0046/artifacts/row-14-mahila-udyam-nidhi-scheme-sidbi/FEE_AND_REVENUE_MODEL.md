## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Pricing**: Fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit delivered — in this scheme, need-based term loans and working capital loans sanctioned by SIDBI based on project viability and repayment capacity.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s risk profile — as a pure loan product (no subsidy/grant), success depends on credit appraisal and project viability; processing involves technical and financial appraisal by SIDBI with disbursement in stages, introducing moderate execution risk.
3. **Transparency & Predictability**: Retainer covers advisory work (project report preparation, eligibility validation, documentation); success fee is contingent on loan sanction, aligning consultant incentives with client outcomes.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Mahila Udyam Nidhi Scheme has a medium confidence rating in eligibility data and involves a multi-step application process (technical/financial appraisal, staged disbursement). Processing time is not explicitly stated but implies a lag due to SIDBI’s appraisal and disbursement in stages, supporting a 3-month approval lag assumption in revenue projections.
