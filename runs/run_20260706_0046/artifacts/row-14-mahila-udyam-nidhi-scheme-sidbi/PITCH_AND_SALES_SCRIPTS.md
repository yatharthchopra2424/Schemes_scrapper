# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping women entrepreneurs access tailored financial solutions through government-backed schemes. The Mahila Udyam Nidhi Scheme by SIDBI is designed specifically for women like you who are looking to start, expand, or modernize their enterprises in manufacturing, services, or agro-processing. I’d love to understand your learn about 20 minutes to see if this could be a good fit for your business goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. Can you share when your business was incorporated or started operations?  
2. What is your current ownership structure, and what percentage of the business is held by women?  
3. Are you engaged in manufacturing, services, or agro-processing activities?  
4. Has your business been operational for at least two years (if existing), or are you planning a new project?  
5. Do you have any outstanding defaults with banks or financial institutions?  
6. Is your business registered under Udyam?  
7. What is the primary purpose for which you’re seeking financing—new project setup, expansion, modernization, or working capital?  
8. Have you previously applied for any SIDBI or government loan schemes?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Woman entrepreneur (sole proprietor, partner in partnership firm, or director of women-owned private limited company with ≥51% women shareholding)  
- [ ] Business engaged in manufacturing, service, or agro-processing  
- [ ] Not a defaulter to any bank or financial institution  
- [ ] Existing unit: in operation for at least two years; New project: eligible  
- [ ] Udyam]  
- [ ] Udyam Registration under Udyam  
- [ ] Project Report, financial needs viability  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like the Mahila Udyam Nidhi Scheme could be a strong match—especially since it offers need-based term and working capital loans for women entrepreneurs like yourself, with collateral-free options under CGTMSE. Let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Women entrepreneurs often face challenges in accessing timely and affordable credit due to lack of collateral, limited credit history, or perceived higher risk by lenders—hindering their ability to start, expand, or modernize their businesses.

**Scheme intro:**
The Mahila Udyam Nidhi Scheme, offered by the Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI), provides term loans for project cost and working capital loans for operational needs. The quantum of assistance is need-based and determined by project viability and repayment capacity. Loans may be covered under the Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) for collateral-free lending, with interest rates set per SIDBI’s internal risk assessment model—competitive for the MSME segment.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a woman entrepreneur with ≥51% shareholding in your enterprise  
- Your business operates in manufacturing, services, or agro-processing  
- You have been in operation for at least two years (or are launching a new project)  
- You have no defaults with banks or financial institutions
