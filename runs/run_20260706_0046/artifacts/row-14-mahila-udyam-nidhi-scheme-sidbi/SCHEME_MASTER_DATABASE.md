### Mahila Udyam Nidhi Scheme (SIDBI)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://sidbi.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Sole proprietorship, partnership firm, or women-owned private limited company
- Age of business: At least two years for existing enterprises; new projects also eligible
- Sector: Manufacturing, service, or agro-processing
- Revenue cap: Not specified in evidence
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: Women entrepreneurs with not less than 51% shareholding in the enterprise; applicant must not be a defaulter to any bank or financial institution

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Need-based term loans for project cost and working capital loans for operational needs; quantum subject to project viability and repayment capacity
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (in stages as per project progress)
- Duration of benefit: Flexible repayment tenure based on project viability

#### Documents Required
1. Project Report
2. KYC documents of the woman entrepreneur (PAN, Aadhaar, etc.)
3. Proof of business registration/proprietorship/partnership deed/incorporation certificate
4. Udyam Registration Certificate
5. Land and building documents (ownership/lease agreement)
6. Machinery/equipment quotations and invoices
7. Last two years' audited financial statements (for existing units)
8. Bank account statement
9. Income tax returns (if applicable)
10. No dues certificate from existing bankers (if any)
11. Details of promoters and their background

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the project and prepare a detailed project report including technical, financial, and economic aspects.
2. Ensure compliance with eligibility criteria, including women entrepreneurship status and Udyam registration.
3. Approach the nearest SIDBI branch or authorized channel with the project report and required documents.
4. Submit the application along with KYC documents, business registration proof, and financial statements.
5. SIDBI conducts technical and financial appraisal of the project.
6. Upon approval, loan sanction is communicated, and disbursement is made in stages as per project progress.
7. Complete documentation and create security as per loan terms.
8. Begin repayment as per the agreed schedule after moratorium, if any.
