## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Business must be registered as a proprietorship, partnership, LLP, private limited company, or cooperative society  
- [ ] Annual turnover must not exceed ₹250 crore (as per MSME definition for eligibility under ODOP-linked export promotion)  
- [ ] Employee count must be within MSME limits: ≤250 for manufacturing, ≤100 for services  
- [ ] Business must be engaged in manufacturing or processing of a product identified under the One District One Product (ODOP) initiative  
- [ ] Unit must be located in a district officially notified under the ODOP program by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT)  
- [ ] Business must have been operational for at least 1 year as of the date of application  
- [ ] Must hold a valid Udyam Registration certificate  
- [ ] Must have an Importer Exporter Code (IEC) issued by DGFT  
- [ ] Product must be exportable and fall under the notified ODOP product list for the respective district  
- [ ] No prior default on government loan or subsidy schemes  

> **Sources**: ODOP scheme framework (DPIIT), Foreign Trade Policy 2023, DGFT guidelines for export promotion schemes, MSME Act 2006

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN card of the business entity  
- Aadhaar/PAN of authorized signatory  
- Udyam Registration certificate  
- GST registration certificate (if applicable)  
- IEC (Importer Exporter Code) certificate  
- Bank statement (last 6 months)  
- Cancelled cheque or bank passbook copy  
- ITR (last 2 financial years)  
- Incorporation/registration certificate (Partnership deed, COI, LLP agreement, etc.)  
- Address proof of business premise (electricity bill, property tax receipt, or lease agreement)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- ODOP product certification or endorsement letter from District Industries Centre (DIC) or nominated ODOP agency  
- Detailed project report (DPR) highlighting product, process, export potential, and ODOP alignment  
- Export order(s) or letter of intent (LOI) from overseas buyer (if available)  
- Brand registration/trademark certificate (if applicable)  
- FSSAI/FPO/Agmark certificate (for food/agro-based ODOP products)  
- Pollution control clearance (if applicable)  
- NSIC/MSME certificate (if seeking additional benefits)  
- Certificate of origin or product authenticity (for handicrafts, handlooms, GI products)  

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)  
- [ ] File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ClientName_Date.pdf` (e.g., `ODOP_Export_Udyam_ABC Ltd_20240515.pdf`)  
- [ ] Photocopies must be self-attested with signature and stamp of authorized signatory  
- [ ] Documents in regional languages must be accompanied by a certified Hindi/English translation  
- [ ] Digital signatures (if used) must be valid and issued by a licensed Certifying Authority (CA)  
- [ ] Udyam, GST, IEC, and PAN details must match exactly across all documents  
- [ ] Bank statements must show the entity’s name and be stamped by the bank

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required for the ODOP Export Scheme.  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Product Description and ODOP Alignment (with district and product code)  
- Manufacturing Process and Quality Control  
- Market Analysis (Domestic & Export)  
- Export Strategy and Target Countries  
- Branding and Marketing Plan  
- Financial Projections (3-year)  
- Export Projections and FOB Value  
- Utilization of Scheme Incentives  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation  
- Implementation Timeline  
- Annexures: Udyam, IEC, GST, ODOP endorsement, product photos, export orders  

> **Note**: Confirm DPR format and length requirements with the State ODOP Nodal Agency or DGFT Regional Authority, as templates may vary by state.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal via the State Nodal Agency for ODOP or DGFT’s Ankleshwar portal (verify nodal officer for exact URL).  
2. Select “ODOP Export Promotion Scheme” under “Export Incentives” or “MSME Export Support” category.  
3. Fill in applicant details exactly as per Udyam and PAN records (no abbreviations).  
4. Enter ODOP district code and product name as per the official DPIIT ODOP product list (https://dpit.gov.in/odop).  
5. Declare export turnover (past and projected) in INR and USD.  
6. Attach all documents in the specified order and format as per the checklist.  
7. Self-declare compliance with FDI policy, environmental norms, and labor laws.  
8. Preview the form before final submission; no edits allowed after submission in most portals.  
9. Keep a copy of the submitted application and acknowledgment receipt.  

> **Portal Note**: As of evidence, no centralized ODOP export application portal was identified; applications are likely processed through State ODOP Cells or DGFT Regional Authorities. Confirm submission mechanism with the nodal officer.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter sanctioning incentives under the ODOP Export Scheme  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned amount, incentive type (e.g., MDA, reimbursement, credit-linked subsidy), and validity period  
- [ ] Utilize funds strictly for approved purposes: export market development, branding, certification, compliance, or packaging  
- [ ] Maintain auditable records of all invoices, bills, and utilization documents  
- [ ] Submit Utilization Certificate (UC) and audited expenditure statement within the stipulated time (usually 3 months post-utilization)  
- [ ] Ensure invoices for success fee are raised only after client confirms receipt of sanctioned amount  
- [ ] Retain all scheme-related documents for audit and future reference (minimum 5 years)  
- [ ] Guide client on claiming additional benefits under related schemes (e.g., RoSCTL, MEIS remission, GST refunds) if eligible  

> **Note**: Post-approval compliance is subject to audit by the nodal agency; non-utilization or misutilization may lead to recovery with interest.
