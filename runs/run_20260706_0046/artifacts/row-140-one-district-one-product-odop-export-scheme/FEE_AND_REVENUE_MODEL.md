## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-based alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the actual financial benefit delivered to clients under the ODOP Export Scheme, ensuring consultant compensation scales with realized grant or export incentive amounts.
2. **Risk-adjusted pricing**: Success fees are set within standard ranges but may be negotiated based on the scheme’s moderate risk profile, given limited public evidence of approval rates and processing timelines.
3. **Transparency and predictability**: Retainers cover initial eligibility assessment, documentation, and application submission, while success fees are contingent on fund disbursement, aligning incentives with client outcomes.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: The ODOP Export Scheme currently lacks detailed public data on approval rates, average processing time, or typical benefit amounts per beneficiary. This absence of verifiable success metrics increases execution risk, warranting careful client scoping and potentially higher retainer engagement to offset uncertainty in success fee realization.
