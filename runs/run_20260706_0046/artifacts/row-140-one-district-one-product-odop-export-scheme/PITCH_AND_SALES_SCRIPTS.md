## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping manufacturers and artisans in Uttar Pradesh access government support to scale their traditional crafts and agricultural products into export markets. The One District One Product (ODOP) Export Scheme is designed specifically to uplift local producers by providing financial assistance for branding, certification, and market access. I’d like to understand if your business aligns with this initiative and explore how we might support your growth.

### Discovery Questions
- What is the name and location of your business, and which traditional product or craft do you specialise in?
- In which district of Uttar Pradesh is your production unit based, and is your product recognised under the ODOP initiative for that district?
- How long has your business been operational, and are you currently engaged in or planning to export your product?
- What is your annual turnover from manufacturing or sales of the ODOP-linked product?
- Do you have any existing certifications (e.g., FSSAI, ISO, Geographical Indication) or are you planning to obtain them for export readiness?
- Are you looking to invest in branding, packaging, or participation in international trade fairs to expand your market reach?
- What challenges have you faced in accessing export markets or meeting international compliance standards?
- Are you currently working with any government body, export promotion council, or industry association for market development support?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is engaged in manufacturing or processing of a product identified under the One District One Product (ODOP) initiative in Uttar Pradesh  
- [ ] Product is linked to a specific district in Uttar Pradesh as per the ODOP framework  
- [ ] Business is involved in or intends to commence export of the ODOP-linked product  
- [ ] Applicant is a micro, small, or medium enterprise (MSME), artisan cluster, self-help group (SHG), or cooperative society  
- [ ] Annual turnover does not exceed ₹250 crore (as per MSME classification for eligibility under related export promotion schemes)  
- [ ] Business possesses or is willing to obtain necessary certifications for export (e.g., FSSAI, APEDA, ISO)  
- [ ] Unit is located within the geographical boundaries of Uttar Pradesh  
- [ ] Applicant has not previously received duplicate funding for the same activity under another central or state scheme  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your business aligns well with the objectives of the One District One Product (ODOP) Export Scheme, which supports exporters of district-specific products from Uttar Pradesh with financial assistance for export readiness. Let me share how this scheme can help you overcome the challenges you’ve mentioned and access international markets.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Artisans and small manufacturers in Uttar Pradesh often struggle to meet export standards, afford branding and certification costs, or access international trade platforms due to limited resources and market linkages. This prevents high-potential local products—such as handicrafts, textiles, and agricultural goods—from reaching global buyers, despite their unique cultural and economic value.

**Scheme intro:**
The One District One Product (ODOP) Export Scheme, administered by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, provides financial assistance to eligible exporters of ODOP-identified products from Uttar Pradesh. The scheme offers grants of up to ₹5 lakh per beneficiary for activities including brand development, product certification, packaging design, and participation in international trade fairs and buyer-seller meets.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your business manufactures a product officially recognised under the ODOP initiative for your district in Uttar Pradesh  
- You are engaged in or planning to export your product, meeting the scheme’s export intent requirement  
- As an MSME/artisan unit, you fall within the target beneficiary group supported by the scheme  
- You seek support for certification, branding, or trade fair participation—eligible activities under the scheme
