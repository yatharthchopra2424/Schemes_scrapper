## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, documentation preparation, and application submission support.  
2. Success fee is contingent upon successful disbursement of interest subvention, aligning consultant incentives with client outcomes.  
3. Tiered pricing reflects client complexity and potential benefit size, with MSME exporters falling into the Growth tier.  
4. Scheme-specific note: This scheme has a moderate risk profile due to its reliance on export realization timelines and interest payment verification; processing typically takes 3–6 months post-application, based on evidence of document submission and verification steps.
