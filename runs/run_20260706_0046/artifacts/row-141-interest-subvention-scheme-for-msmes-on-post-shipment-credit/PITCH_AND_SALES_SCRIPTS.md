# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping MSME exporters reduce their financing costs through government schemes. The Interest Subvention Scheme for MSMEs on Post Shipment Credit is designed specifically to lower the interest burden on post-shipment export credit, improving liquidity and competitiveness in international markets. I’d like to understand your export operations to see if this scheme aligns with your needs.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your Udyam Registration number, and when was your MSME registered?
2. Do you currently avail post-shipment credit from a scheduled commercial bank or eligible financial institution for your export transactions?
3. What is the typical timeline for realization of your export proceeds post-shipment, and are they realized within RBI stipulated periods?
4. Have you availed any other interest subvention or subsidy on the same post-shipment credit transaction?
5. What is the interest rate you are currently paying on your post-shipment credit, and is it linked to a benchmark like repo rate or LIBOR?
6. Do you have documentation such as interest payment certificates, loan sanction letters, and bank realization certificates for your export transactions?
7. Are you exporting goods or services, and do you hold a valid Import Export Code (IEC)?
8. What is the primary challenge you face in managing working capital post-shipment?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Valid Udyam Registration Certificate (URC)
- [ ] Availing post-shipment credit from scheduled commercial bank or eligible financial institution
- [ ] Export of goods or services
- [ ] Export proceeds realized within RBI stipulated time period
- [ ] Valid Import Export Code (IEC)
- [ ] Interest paid on post-shipment credit
- [ ] No prior interest subvention availed on the same transaction
- [ ] MSME as per MSMED Act, 2006

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you meet the core eligibility for the Interest Subvention Scheme for MSMEs on Post Shipment Credit. This scheme can reduce your effective interest cost by subsidizing the difference between your actual interest rate and a benchmark rate, directly improving your working capital. Shall I walk you through how it works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
MSME exporters often face high interest costs on post-shipment credit, which strains working capital and reduces competitiveness in global markets due to delayed realization of export proceeds and financing burdens.

**Scheme intro:**
The Interest Subvention Scheme for MSMEs on Post Shipment Credit, administered by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, provides interest subvention on post-shipment credit availed by MSME exporters. The benefit is the difference between the actual interest rate charged by the lending institution and a benchmark rate (typically linked to repo rate or LIBOR plus spread), subject to a cap, disbursed through the lending institution or directly to the beneficiary upon submission of required documents.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You have a valid Udyam Registration Certificate as an MSME exporter
- You avail post-shipment credit from a scheduled commercial bank or eligible financial institution
- Your export proceeds are realized within the stipulated time as per RBI guidelines
- You hold a valid Import Export Code (IEC) for your export transactions

Many MSME exporters have successfully claimed subvention by following this process. |

| "We're too small / too big" | The scheme applies to all MSME exporters as per MSMED Act, 2006 with valid Udyam Registration — there are no revenue or employee thresholds beyond the MSME definition. Size does not disqualify you if you meet the MSME criteria. |
| "I don't have the documents ready" | The three most commonly missing documents are: interest payment certificate from the lending institution, bank realization certificate (BRC) or foreign inward remittance certificate (FIRC), and loan sanction letter for post-shipment credit. |
