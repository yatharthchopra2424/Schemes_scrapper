### Interest Subvention Scheme for MSMEs on Post Shipment Credit
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises
- **Portal / Application URL:** [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: MSME exporters
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Export of goods or services
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have valid Udyam Registration (UR) Certificate; must have availed post-shipment credit from scheduled commercial banks or other eligible financial institutions; export proceeds must be realized within stipulated time as per RBI guidelines; must not have availed similar interest subvention from any other government scheme for the same transaction

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Difference between actual interest rate charged by lending institution and a benchmark rate (typically linked to repo rate or LIBOR plus spread), subject to a cap
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement or Direct credit (through lending institution or directly to beneficiary MSME upon submission of required documents)
- Duration of benefit: One-time per transaction

#### Documents Required
1. Udyam Registration Certificate (URC)
2. Import Export Code (IEC)
3. Proof of export realization (e.g., bank realization certificate, shipping bill)
4. Interest payment certificate from the lending institution
5. Loan sanction letter for post-shipment credit
6. Bank statement showing interest payment
7. Shipping bill and bill of lading
8. Bank realization certificate (BRC) or foreign inward remittance certificate (FIRC)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Avail post-shipment credit from a scheduled commercial bank or eligible financial institution for export of goods/services.
2. Ensure realization of export proceeds within the stipulated time as per RBI guidelines.
3. Pay the interest on the post-shipment credit to the lending institution.
4. Submit application for interest subvention along with required documents to the nodal agency or designated portal.
5. Submit proof of export realization, interest payment certificate, Udyam Registration Certificate, and IEC code.
6. Await verification and approval from the implementing agency.
7. Receive interest subvention amount as per scheme guidelines.
