# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Indian exporter (particularly MSME)  
- [ ] Has suffered losses due to non-payment by overseas buyers  
- [ ] Loss is due to buyer insolvency, bankruptcy, or geopolitical disruptions in regions such as West Asia  
- [ ] Holds a valid export credit insurance policy issued by ECGC  
- [ ] Claim is filed under RELIEF scheme components I, II, or III  
- [ ] Claim is filed within stipulated time frame after occurrence of loss  
- [ ] Loss is within the insured amount under the ECGC policy  
- [ ] Geopolitical support is applicable only during declared periods of disruption (if claiming under time-bound support)  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN card of the exporter  
- GST registration certificate  
- ITR (last 2–3 years)  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration Certificate (for MSMEs)  
- IEC (Import Export Code) certificate  
- Authorization letter from exporter  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Export invoice  
- Shipping bill  
- Proof of export (Bill of Lading or Airway Bill)  
- Proof of non-payment by buyer  
- Buyer insolvency or bankruptcy certificate  
- ECGC policy copy  
- Claim form as per RELIEF scheme  
- Bank account details for settlement  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and in PDF or clear scanned format  
- File size should not exceed 5 MB per document  
- Name on documents must match the exporter’s name as per PAN and bank account  
- Documents must be self-attested or notarized where required  
- Language: Hindi or English; if in another language, certified translation must be attached  
- Claim form must be filled completely and signed

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for claim submission under RELIEF scheme. However, if additional context is needed for complex cases, verify with nodal officer.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the claim form from the ECGC portal under RELIEF scheme section  
2. Fill in exporter details exactly as per PAN and bank records  
3. Provide accurate details of the export transaction (invoice no., date, value, destination)  
4. Clearly state the cause of loss: buyer insolvency, bankruptcy, or geopolitical disruption (specify country and event)  
5. Attach proof of non-payment and buyer insolvency documentation  
6. Specify the claim amount, ensuring it does not exceed the insured value under ECGC policy  
7. Declare that the loss is genuine and not due to commercial dispute  
8. Sign and date the form; ensure authorized signatory signs  
9. Double-check all attachments are referenced in the form  
10. Save a copy before submission  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All fields in claim form are complete and accurate  
- [ ] Supporting documents are attached and clearly labeled  
- [ ] Claim amount matches insured value and loss incurred  
- [ ] Authorization letter is signed and dated  
- [ ] Bank details are correct and verified  
- [ ] Declarations are signed  
- [ ] No discrepancies between form and documents  
- [ ] Final PDF compiled and ready for submission

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Log in to ECGC client portal at https://www.ecgcltd.in  
- Navigate to RELIEF scheme section and select “File Claim”  
- Upload completed claim form and all supporting documents  
- Submit and capture screenshot of confirmation page  
- Save application/reference number securely  
- Set follow-up reminders for Day 25 and Day 35  

### Follow-Up Script (to nodal officer or portal helpdesk)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify approval letter from ECGC  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount matches claimed amount (within policy limits)  
- [ ] Monitor bank account for disbursement of claim amount  
- [ ] Utilization certificate not required (claim is compensation for loss)  
- [ ] Invoice success fee as per consultancy agreement  
- [ ] Close case and archive all documents
