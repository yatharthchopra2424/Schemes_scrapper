## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit delivered under the ECGC MSME Export Scheme, with claim settlements ranging from ₹13.5 lakh to over ₹10.72 crore as evidenced in multiple disbursements.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile — processing involves verification of buyer default, insolvency, or geopolitical events under RELIEF Components I–III, with typical approval timelines of 3–6 months post-submission.
3. **Accessibility for MSMEs**: Tiered retainer structures ensure affordability for micro and small exporters, aligning with the scheme’s focus on MSME beneficiaries and RELIEF’s partial reimbursement (up to 50%) for non-ECGC-insured MSMEs.
4. **Transparency and Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; no hidden charges, consistent with ECGC’s zero-fee claim processing policy under RELIEF (no processing or administrative fees charged to exporters).
