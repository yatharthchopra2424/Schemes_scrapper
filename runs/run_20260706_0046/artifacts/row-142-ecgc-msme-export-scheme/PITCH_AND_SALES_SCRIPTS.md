# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping Indian exporters, particularly MSMEs, recover losses from overseas buyer defaults through government-backed schemes like the ECGC MSME Export Scheme under the RELIEF initiative. Many exporters face unexpected non-payment due to buyer insolvency or geopolitical disruptions in regions like West Asia, and I help them determine if they qualify for financial compensation without upfront cost. Could I ask a few quick questions to see if this might be relevant for your export business?

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you an Indian exporter registered as an MSME?
2. Do you hold a valid export credit insurance policy issued by ECGC for your overseas shipments?
3. Have you experienced non-payment from an overseas buyer due to their insolvency, bankruptcy, or geopolitical disruptions in regions such as West Asia (e.g., UAE, Saudi Arabia, Israel, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Iraq, Iran, Yemen)?
4. Was the loss incurred during the eligible periods under RELIEF Components I, II, or III (e.g., shipments leaving between February 14, 2026 and June 15, 2026)?
5. Do you have documentation such as export invoices, shipping bills, proof of export (bill of lading/airway bill), and evidence of non-payment or buyer insolvency?
6. Is the claim amount within the insured value under your ECGC policy?
7. Have you already filed a claim with ECGC for this loss, or is this a new potential claim?
8. Are you seeking financial assistance to recover dues from defaulting overseas buyers without impacting your working capital?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Indian exporter
- [ ] MSME classification (as per MSMED Act)
- [ ] Holds a valid ECGC export credit insurance policy
- [ ] Loss due to buyer default, insolvency, or bankruptcy
- [ ] Loss occurred due to geopolitical disruptions in West Asia or covered regions (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Israel, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Iraq, Iran, Yemen)
- [ ] Shipment occurred during RELIEF eligibility window (Feb 14, 2026 – Jun 15, 2026 for Components I & II; Feb 14 – Mar 15, 2026 for Component III)
- [ ] Claim amount does not exceed insured value under ECGC policy
- [ ] Required documents available or can be obtained: export invoice, shipping bill, proof of export, proof of non-payment, buyer insolvency/bankruptcy certificate, ECGC policy copy, claim form, bank details, authorization letter

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you may qualify for the ECGC MSME Export Scheme under the RELIEF initiative, which provides financial compensation for export losses due to buyer default — with settlements ranging from ₹13.5 lakh to over ₹10.72 crore. Would you like me to explain how this works and what the next steps would be?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Indian exporters, especially MSMEs, face significant financial strain when overseas buyers default due to insolvency, bankruptcy, or geopolitical disruptions — particularly in volatile regions like West Asia. These losses can disrupt cash flow, strain working capital, and deter future international trade, yet many exporters are unaware they can claim compensation if they hold ECGC insurance.

**Scheme intro:**
The ECGC MSME Export Scheme, administered by the Export Credit Guarantee Corporation of India (ECGC) under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, provides financial compensation for export losses arising from buyer default, insolvency, or geopolitical risks under the government-backed RELIEF initiative. Claim amounts are based on the insured value of the export transaction and extent of loss, with verified settlements ranging from ₹13.5 lakh to over ₹10.72 crore, disbursed directly to the exporter’s bank account after verification.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are an Indian exporter classified as an MSME  
- You hold a valid ECGC export credit insurance policy  
- Your loss resulted from buyer default or insolvency in a RELIEF-covered region (e.g., West Asia)  
- The shipment occurred during the eligible timeframe under RELIEF Components I, II, or III
