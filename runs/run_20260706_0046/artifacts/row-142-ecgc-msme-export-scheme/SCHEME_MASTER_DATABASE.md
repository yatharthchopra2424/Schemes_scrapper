### ECGC MSME Export Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Export Credit Guarantee Corporation of India (ECGC), Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.ecgcltd.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (claims can be submitted as and when buyer default occurs, subject to policy terms and RELIEF scheme guidelines)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Indian exporters, particularly MSMEs
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Export sector (all sectors engaged in international trade)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must hold a valid ECGC export credit insurance policy; loss must be due to buyer default, insolvency, or geopolitical events covered under RELIEF Components I, II, and III; claims must be filed within the stipulated time frame after loss occurrence; support limited to insured amount under ECGC policy; geopolitical support time-bound and applicable only during declared periods of disruption

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial compensation for export losses; claim amounts range from ₹13.5 lakh to over ₹10.72 crore (based on insured value and extent of loss)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit to exporter’s bank account after verification and approval
- Duration of benefit: One-time per claim

#### Documents Required
1. Export invoice
2. Shipping bill
3. Proof of export (bill of lading, airway bill)
4. Proof of non-payment by buyer
5. Buyer insolvency or bankruptcy certificate
6. ECGC policy copy
7. Claim form as per RELIEF scheme
8. Bank account details for settlement
9. Authorization letter from exporter

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the ECGC website and navigate to the RELIEF scheme section.
2. Click on the ‘Standard Operating Procedure for filing claims under RELIEF Components I, II and III’ link.
3. Download the claim form and required documents checklist.
4. Fill out the claim form with details of the export transaction, buyer default, and loss amount.
5. Attach supporting documents such as invoice, shipping bill, proof of non-payment, and buyer insolvency proof.
6. Submit the completed application via the ECGC client portal or designated email.
7. Await verification and processing by ECGC officials.
8. Upon approval, the claim amount is disbursed to the exporter’s account.
