# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
The **ECGC MSME Export Scheme**, officially known as **RELIEF (Resilience & Logistics Intervention for Export Facilitation)**, is a Government of India initiative under the Export Promotion Mission (EPM) implemented by the **Export Credit Guarantee Corporation of India (ECGC)**, Ministry of Commerce and Industry. It provides financial support to Indian exporters, particularly MSMEs, affected by buyer default, insolvency, or geopolitical disruptions in West Asia and the Gulf region. The scheme operates on a **rolling basis**—claims can be submitted as and when buyer default occurs, subject to policy terms and RELIEF scheme guidelines. The application portal is **[https://www.ecgcltd.in](https://www.ecgcltd.in)**.

### Objectives
- Provide financial assistance to Indian exporters facing non-payment by overseas buyers  
- Support MSMEs in mitigating export credit risks due to buyer insolvency or political disruptions  
- Facilitate timely settlement of export claims under the RELIEF scheme  
- Strengthen export competitiveness by reducing financial vulnerability of exporters  
- Ensure quick disbursal of claim amounts to affected exporters  
- Promote confidence in international trade by backing exporters with government-guaranteed support  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Criteria | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Indian exporters, particularly MSMEs |
| **Eligible Entities** | Indian exporters who have suffered losses due to non-payment by overseas buyers on account of buyer insolvency, bankruptcy, or geopolitical disruptions in regions such as West Asia |
| **Policy Requirement** | Must hold a valid export credit insurance policy issued by ECGC |
| **Loss Cause** | Loss must be due to buyer default, insolvency, or geopolitical events covered under RELIEF Components I, II, and III |
| **Geographic Scope** | Pan-India (exporters based in India) |
| **Transaction Coverage** | Export transactions to countries in the Gulf and West Asia: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Israel, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Iraq, Iran, Yemen |
| **Non-ECGC Insured MSMEs** | Eligible under Component III only (non-ECGC-insured MSME exporters facing extraordinary freight and insurance surcharge burdens) |
| **Non-Eligible** | Non-MSME exporters under Component III; back-to-back or re-import cargo; losses due to marine perils or inherent vice of goods |

### Required Documents
1. Export invoice  
2. Shipping bill  
3. Proof of export (bill of lading, airway bill)  
4. Proof of non-payment by buyer  
5. Buyer insolvency or bankruptcy certificate  
6. ECGC policy copy  
7. Claim form as per RELIEF scheme  
8. Bank account details for settlement  
9. Authorization letter from exporter  

### Key Caveats
> - Claims are admissible only if the exporter holds a valid ECGC export credit insurance policy (except Component III for non-ECGC-insured MSMEs)  
> - Loss must be due to buyer default, insolvency, or geopolitical events covered under RELIEF Components I, II, and III  
> - Claims must be filed within the stipulated time frame after the occurrence of the loss  
> - Support is limited to the insured amount under the ECGC policy (Components I & II) or ₹50 lakhs per exporter (Component III)  
> - Geopolitical support is time-bound and applicable only during declared periods of disruption (Component I: Feb 14–Mar 15, 2026; Component II: Mar 16–Jun 15, 2026; Component III: Feb 14–Mar 15, 2026 for non-ECGC-insured MSMEs)  
> - No processing fees charged by ECGC for claim settlement under RELIEF  
> - Marine perils and inherent vice of goods are not covered  

### Timelines & Deadlines
| Component | Eligibility Period | Coverage Details |
|----------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Component I** | Feb 14, 2026 – Mar 15, 2026 | Up to 100% risk coverage over existing ECGC cover for exporters with prior ECGC insurance |
| **Component II** | Mar 16, 2026 – Jun 15, 2026 | Up to 95% risk coverage over existing ECGC cover for exporters planning upcoming consignments |
| **Component III** | Feb 14, 2026 – Mar 15, 2026 | Up to 50% reimbursement of eligible costs (max ₹50 lakhs/exporter) for non-ECGC-insured MSMEs facing extraordinary freight/insurance and freight costs |
