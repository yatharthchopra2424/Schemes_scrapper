## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, documentation support, and application submission facilitation for PMFBY enrollment.
2. Success fee is contingent upon successful claim settlement or policy issuance under the scheme, aligning consultant incentives with client outcomes.
3. Tiered pricing reflects the scale of farming operations and sum insured, with higher tiers applicable to mid-market and enterprise-level agribusinesses, cooperatives, or FPOs managing large acreage.
4. Scheme-specific note: PMFBY involves moderate risk due to seasonal cut-off dates (31st July for Kharif, 31st Dec for Rabi) and dependency on timely submission of declarations and yield data; however, high enrollment volumes and government backing reduce payment risk, supporting predictable revenue cycles.
