# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping farmers like you access government crop insurance schemes that protect your harvest and income. The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) is designed specifically for farmers growing notified crops who face risks from unpredictable weather, pests, or natural calamities. I’d like to understand your farming situation to see if this scheme can provide you with the financial safety net you need.

### Discovery Questions
1. What crops are you currently growing or planning to grow in the upcoming Kharif or Rabi season?
2. Do you have a Crop Loan or Kisan Credit Card (KCC) account for which credit limit has been sanctioned/renewed for the notified crop this season?
3. What is the total area (in hectares) under cultivation for the notified crop you wish to insure?
4. Are you located in a notified area where PMFBY is implemented for your crop this season?
5. Do you have insurable interest in the crop (e.g., ownership, tenancy, or cultivation rights)?
6. Have you previously faced crop loss due to unforeseen events like drought, flood, hailstorm, or pest outbreak?
7. Are you aware of the cut-off dates for enrollment (31st July for Kharif, 31st December for Rabi)?
8. Do you currently have any other crop insurance coverage, or are you relying solely on savings or loans to manage risk?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Farmer grows notified crops in a notified area during the season  
- [ ] Has insurable interest in the crop  
- [ ] Loanee farmer: Has Crop Loan/KCC account with credit limit sanctioned/renewed for notified crop (compulsory coverage)  
- [ ] Non-loanee farmer: Not covered under compulsory component but has insurable interest (voluntary coverage)  
- [ ] Season: Kharif (cut-off 31st July) or Rabi (cut-off 31st December)  
- [ ] Farmer is an individual (not a company or institution)  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you grow [crop] in [area] and have a KCC/loan account — which means you’re likely eligible for PMFBY. This scheme offers premium subsidies of up to 50% and pays claims directly to your bank account if your crop suffers loss. Let me explain how it works and how we’ve helped farmers like you get approved.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Farmers face unpredictable losses from droughts, floods, hailstorms, pests, or failed sowing — events that can destroy an entire season’s investment and leave them unable to repay loans or reinvest. Without insurance, recovery depends on savings are wiped out, and access to future credit becomes difficult.

**Scheme intro:**
Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY), administered by the Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India, provides crop insurance with financial support for crop loss/damage due to unforeseen events. Farmers pay only a heavily subsidized premium: 2.0% of Sum Insured for Kharif foodgrain/oilseeds, 1.5% for Rabi foodgrain/oilseeds, and 5% for annual commercial/horticultural crops — or the actuarial rate, whichever is less. The difference is subsidized equally by Centre and State.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You grow notified crops in a notified area during the season (confirmed from your response)  
- You have insurable interest in the crop (ownership/tenancy/cultivation rights)  
- You hold a Crop Loan/KCC account with sanctioned credit limit (making you eligible for compulsory coverage)  
- You’re enrolling before the cut-off date (31st July for Kharif / 31st December for Rabi)
