### PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://pmfby.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Cut-off date for receipt of Proposals of farmers (loanee & non-loanee) is 31st July for Kharif and 31st December for Rabi

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: All farmers growing notified crops in a notified area during the season who have insurable interest in the crop
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Agriculture
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Compulsory coverage applies to farmers in the notified area who possess a Crop Loan account/KCC account (Loanee Farmers) to whom credit limit is sanctioned/renewed for the notified crop during the crop season, and such other farmers whom the Government may decide to include from time to time. Voluntary coverage may be obtained by all farmers not covered in the compulsory component, including Crop KCC/Crop Loan Account holders whose credit limit is not renewed.

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support for crop loss/damage due to unforeseen events; on-account payment of claims up to 25% of likely claims if expected yield during the season is likely to be less than 50% of normal yield; claim amount credited electronically to the individual insured bank account
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit
- Duration of benefit: One-time per crop season

#### Documents Required
1. Land identification number in the proposal
2. Documentary evidence with regard to possession of cultivable land
3. Area sown confirmation certificate
4. Bank account details (for loanee farmers, financed by loan disbursing office of the Bank)
5. Proposal form (for non-loanee farmers submitting directly to insurance company)
6. Documentary proof of insurable interest (for non-loanee farmers submitting proposals personally/post to insurance company)
7. Sowing certificate issued by concerned village/sub-district level official of the State (if changing crop planned earlier)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the PMFBY portal at https://pmfby.gov.in and click on 'Farmer Corner' then 'Enroll Now' to apply for crop insurance.
2. Use the Insurance Premium Calculator to know the insurance premium before applying.
3. After enrollment, use the Application Status feature to check your application status on every step.
4. For grievances or to report crop loss, call the Krishi Rakshak Portal & Helpline (KRPH) at 14447.
5. Use the Policy ID to check policy status via the Application Status feature.
6. Access digital tools like Farmer App, WhatsApp Chatbot, AIDE App, CCE App, CLAP App, and WINDS App for application, management, and grievance redressal.
