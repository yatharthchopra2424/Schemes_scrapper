# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
**Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)** is a yield-guarantee crop insurance scheme launched by the Government of India under the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. It aims to provide simple and affordable crop insurance to farmers against crop loss due to unforeseen and non-preventable natural calamities. The scheme operates on an "Area Approach" basis, where insurance units are defined geographical areas (typically village/panchayat level) for major crops, and yield data is based on Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs). PMFBY subsumes earlier schemes like the National Agricultural Insurance Scheme (NAIS), Modified National Agricultural Insurance Scheme (MNAIS), and Weather Based Crop Insurance Scheme (WBCIS) into a single framework. It is implemented insurance companies empanelled by the Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (NAIS, MNAIS, WBCIS) and integrates multiple stakeholders including farmers, to some extent, the Modified National Agricultural Insurance Scheme (MNAIS) and Weather Based Crop Insurance Scheme (WBCIS). The scheme is administered through a web-based ecosystem (https://pmfby.gov.in) that integrates stakeholders—farmers, insurance companies, banks, and government agencies—on a single IT platform for transparency, real-time monitoring, and efficient service delivery.

### Objectives
- Providing financial support to farmers suffering crop loss/damage arising out of unforeseen events  
- Stabilizing the income of farmers to ensure their continuance in farming  
- Encouraging farmers to adopt innovative and modern agricultural practices  
- Ensuring credit worthiness of farmers, crop diversification and enhancing growth and competitiveness of agriculture sector  
- Promoting innovation & research of insurance and allied products to offer expanded choice to farmers and State Governments/UT Administrations  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Criteria | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Farmer Type** | All farmers growing notified crops in a notified area during the season who have insurable interest in the crop are eligible. This includes sharecroppers, tenant farmers, and landless laborers with cultivation rights. |
| **Compulsory Coverage** | Applies to farmers in the notified area who possess a Crop Loan account/KCC account (Loanee Farmers) to whom credit limit is sanctioned/renewed for the notified crop during the crop season, and such other farmers whom the Government may decide to include from time to time. |
| **Voluntary Coverage** | May be obtained by all farmers not covered in the compulsory component, including Crop KCC/Crop Loan Account holders whose credit limit is not renewed. |
| **Crop Type** | Notified crops include: Food crops (Cereals, Millets and Pulses), Oilseeds, Annual Commercial / Annual Horticultural crops. |
| **Geographic Scope** | Pan-India; implemented in all States and Union Territories. |
| **Insurable Interest** | Farmers must have insurable interest in the insured crop(s). Non-loanee farmers are required to submit necessary documentary evidence of land records and/or applicable contract/agreement details (in case of sharecroppers/tenant farmers). |
| **Exclusions** | Farmers without insurable interest, those growing non-notified crops, or those in non-notified areas are not eligible. Double insurance is not allowed. |

### Benefits & Financial Support
#### Premium Rates & Farmer Contribution
| Season | Crop Type | Maximum Insurance Charges Payable by Farmer (% of Sum Insured) |
|--------|-----------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| Kharif | All foodgrain and Oilseeds crops (all Cereals, Millets, Pulses and Oilseeds crops) | 2.0% of Sum Insured or Actuarial rate, whichever is less |
| Rabi | All foodgrain and Oilseeds crops (all Cereals, Millets, Pulses and Oilseeds crops) | 1.5% of Sum Insured or Actuarial rate, whichever is less |
| Kharif & Rabi | Annual Commercial / Annual Horticultural crops | 5.0% of Sum Insured or Actuarial rate, whichever is less |

> **Note**: The Actuarial Premium Rate (APR) is charged by the Implementing Agency (IA). The difference between the premium rate and the rate of insurance charges payable by farmers is treated as the Rate of Normal Premium Subsidy, which is shared equally by the Centre and State.

#### Sum Insured
- Sum Insured per hectare for both loanee and non-loanee farmers is the same and equal to the Scale of Finance as decided by the District Level Technical Committee (DLTC), and would be pre-declared by the State Level Coordination Committee on Crop Insurance (SLCCCI) and notified.
- Sum Insured for individual farmer = (Sum Insured per hectare) × (Area under cultivation in hectare).
- 'Area under cultivation' shall always be expressed in 'hectare'.
- For loanee farmers under compulsory component, sum insured shall be at least equal to the amount of crop loan sanctioned/advanced, which may be extended up to the value of the threshold yield of the insured crop at the option of insured farmers.
- Where value of the threshold yield is lower than the loan amount per unit area, the higher of the two is the sum insured.
- Non-loanee farmers can insure up to 150% of the value of average yield (based on which Notional Threshold Yield is worked out), but premium subsidy is available only up to 100% of threshold yield; coverage beyond 100% threshold yield and up to 150% average yield is without premium subsidy.

#### Claims & Payouts
- **Yield-Based Claims**: If 'Actual Yield' (AY) per hectare falls short of specified 'Threshold Yield' (TY), all insured farmers growing that crop in the defined area are deemed to have suffered a shortfall.  
  **Claim Formula**:  
  `(Threshold Yield - Actual Yield) × Sum Insured / Threshold Yield`  
  Where Threshold Yield = (Average yield of past seven years, excluding calamity year(s)) × Applicable indemnity level (80% or 90%).
- **On-Account Payment**: Up to 25% of likely claims if expected yield during the season is likely to be less than 50% of normal yield. Claim amount is credited electronically to the individual insured bank account.
- **Prevented Sowing**: Eligible up to a maximum of 25% of the sum-insured.
- **Post-Harvest Losses**: Coverage available up to a maximum period of 14 days from harvesting for crops kept in 'cut & spread' condition to dry in the field after harvesting, against specific perils (cyclone/cyclonic rain & unseasonal rains).
- **Localized Calamities**: Assessed at the affected insured field of the individual insured farmer (hailstorm, landslide, inundation).
- **Claim Settlement**: Claims are released only through electronic transfer to individual nodal banks, followed by hard copy containing claim particulars. Banks credit accounts of individual farmers within a week of receipt of funds and provide a certificate to the insurance company along with a list of farmers benefited.

### Key Caveats
> - The scheme is compulsory for loanee farmers obtaining Crop Loan/KCC account for notified crops, but voluntary for other/non-loanee farmers who have insurable interest in the insured crop(s).  
> - Claims for prevented sowing are eligible up to a maximum of 25% of the sum-insured.  
> - Post-harvest losses coverage is available up to a maximum period of 14 days from harvesting for crops kept in 'cut & spread' condition to dry in the field after harvesting.  
> - Localized calamities (hailstorm, landslide, inundation) and post-harvest losses due to specified perils (cyclone/cyclonic rain & unseasonal rains) are assessed at the affected insured field of the individual insured farmer.  
> - The sum insured per hectare for both loanee and non-loanee farmers is the same and equal to the Scale of Finance as decided by the District Level Technical Committee, and would be pre-declared by SLCCCI and notified.  
> - Cut-off date for receipt of Proposals of farmers (loanee & non-loanee) is 31st July for Kharif and 31st December for Rabi.  
> - Cut-off date for receipt of Declarations of Loanee farmers covered on compulsory basis & non-loanee farmers covered on voluntary basis from Bank branches to Respective Nodal Offices is within 15 working days for loanee farmers and seven working days for non-loanee farmers after cut-off date.  
> - Cut-off date for receipt of Declarations of farmers covered on Voluntary basis from designated Insurance Agent(s) to Insurance Companies is within two working days after receiving declaration/premium.
