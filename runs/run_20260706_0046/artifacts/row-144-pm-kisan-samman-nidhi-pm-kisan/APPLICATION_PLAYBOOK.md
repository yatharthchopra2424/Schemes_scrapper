# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is a landholding farmer family having cultivable land in their name as per land records of the concerned State/UT.
- [ ] The family comprises husband, wife, and minor children who collectively own cultivable land.
- [ ] The landholding is for actual cultivation (applies to both urban and rural areas if under cultivation).
- [ ] The land is not used for non-agricultural purposes.
- [ ] The landholding is not a micro land holding (non-cultivable).
- [ ] The family does not include any member holding constitutional posts (former or present).
- [ ] The family does not include any member who is a former or present Minister/State Minister or Member of Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha/State Legislative Assemblies/State Legislative Councils, or former/ppresent Mayor of Municipal Corporations, or Chairperson of District Panchayats.
- [ ] The family does not include any serving or retired officer/employee of Central/State Government Ministries/Offices/Departments, field units, Central or State PSEs, Attached offices/Autonomous Institutions under Government, or regular employees of Local Bodies (excluding Multi Tasking Staff/Class IV/Group D).
- [ ] The family does not include any superannuated/retired pensioner with a monthly pension of Rs. 10,000 or more (excluding Multi Tasking Staff/Class IV/Group D).
- [ ] No member of the family paid income tax in the last assessment year.
- [ ] The family does not include any professional (Doctor, Engineer, Lawyer, Chartered Accountant, Architect) registered with a professional body and carrying out profession.
- [ ] The family is not an institutional land holder.
- [ ] The family is not a Non-Resident Indian (NRI) as per Income Tax Act, 1961 (if applicable for new beneficiaries).
- [ ] For land transfer via succession (inheritance) before 01.12.2018, land records have been updated to reflect the successor’s name.
- [ ] For land transfer between 01.12.2018 and 31.01.2019, proportionate benefit may apply (verify with nodal officer).
- [ ] Cut-off date for eligibility is 01.02.2019 (land ownership must be as of this date, except for succession due to death).
- [ ] Aadhaar number is available (or Aadhaar Enrollment Number with alternate identity documents in Assam, Meghalaya, and J&K till 31.03.2020; exemption extended till 31.03.2021 for these States/UTs).
- [ ] Bank account number and IFSC code are available.
- [ ] Mobile number is advised but not mandatory.

**Sources**:  
- Eligibility criteria from PM-KISAN Scheme Key Facts and Operational Guidelines (revised as on 29.03.2020)  
- Exclusion criteria: Institutional land holders, constitutional post holders, government employees (excluding MTS/Class IV/Group D), pensioners >Rs.10,000/month, income tax payees, professionals (doctors, engineers, etc.)  
- Land must be in own name; tenant farmers not eligible unless via succession  
- Cut-off date: 01.02.2019; no changes for 5 years except succession  
- Aadhaar mandatory from second installment onwards (except Assam, Meghalaya, J&K till 31.03.2020, extended to 31.03.2021)  
- Bank account and IFSC required  
- Portal: http://pmkisan.gov.in

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN Card of the beneficiary
- Aadhaar Number (or Aadhaar Enrollment Number for Assam, Meghalaya, J&K till 31.03.2020/2021)
- Bank Account Number
- IFSC Code of the bank branch
- Udyam Registration (if applicable for any agri-business activity; not mandatory for pure farming)
- Latest ITR (Income Tax Return) – to verify non-taxpayer status
- GST Certificate (if applicable; not mandatory for pure farming)
- Scanned copy of bank passbook or cancelled cheque (for bank detail verification)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Land ownership documents (7/12 extract, Patta, Khatauni, or equivalent State/UT land record) showing cultivable land in the name of the applicant/family
- Self-declaration form (as per scheme guidelines) confirming:
  - Family composition (husband, wife, minor children)
  - No exclusion criteria apply (govt. employee, income tax payer, professional, etc.)
  - Consent to use Aadhaar for verification with concerned agencies
- Alternate identity documents (if Aadhaar not available): Driving Licence, Voter ID Card, NREGA Job Card, etc. (for Assam, Meghalaya, J&K)
- Age proof (if minor children status needs verification): Birth certificate or school record
- Category proof (SC/ST) if applicable: Caste certificate issued by competent authority

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible and in PDF or JPEG format
- [ ] File size does not exceed 2 MB per document
- [ ] Name on all documents matches exactly as per land records and bank account
- [ ] Aadhaar number is valid and seeded with bank account (where mandatory)
- [ ] Bank account number and IFSC code are correct and active
- [ ] Land records are updated and reflect current ownership
- [ ] Self-declaration is signed and dated by the applicant
- [ ] Documents are in English or Hindi; if in regional language, certified translation attached
- [ ] No overwriting or tampering visible on scanned documents

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required. The PM-KISAN scheme is a direct income transfer scheme based on landholding and self-declaration. No project report or DPR is needed for application.  
**Note**: Verify with nodal officer if any State/UT has additional requirements (though not mentioned in national guidelines).

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Ensure all beneficiary details (Name, Age, Gender, Category) match exactly across land records, Aadhaar, and bank documents  
2. Enter Aadhaar number correctly; if not available, use Enrollment Number with alternate ID (only for Assam, Meghalaya, J&K till permitted date)  
3. Bank account number and IFSC must be accurate and active; verify with bank  
4. Mobile number is optional but recommended for SMS alerts  
5. Clearly state “No” to all exclusion criteria in self-declaration (govt. job, income tax, professional, etc.)  
6. For joint landholding, benefit will be paid to the family member with highest landholding; if equal, to the eldest  
7. If land transferred via succession before 01.12.2018, ensure records updated; if between 01.12.2018–31.01.2019, prepare for proportionate benefit calculation  
8. Double-check that land is under actual cultivation and not used for non-agricultural purposes  

**Portal-specific note**: Data must be uploaded by State Nodal Officers (SNOs) via the PM-KISAN portal (http://pmkisan.gov.in) in the prescribed format; individual farmers cannot apply directly unless through CSC or SNO.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All eligibility criteria verified and documented  
- [ ] Land ownership documents show cultivable land in applicant’s name  
- [ ] Aadhaar/Enrollment number and alternate IDs (if applicable) are valid  
- [ ] Bank account number and IFSC are correct and match beneficiary name  
- [ ] Self-declaration form is signed and covers all exclusion criteria  
- [ ] No duplicate entries detected (verify with State/UT if possible)  
- [ ] Documents are within size limit and properly named  
- [ ] Application ready for submission to State Nodal Officer (SNO)

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the complete document package to the designated State Nodal Officer (SNO) or through the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC)  
- Obtain a receipt or acknowledgment of submission  
- Ensure the SNO uploads the verified beneficiary list on the PM-KISAN portal (http://pmkisan.gov.in) in the prescribed format  
- SNO must e-sign the list before upload  
- After upload, note the batch/reference number and date of submission  
- Set follow-up reminders for 15 days and 30 days post-submission  
- Screenshot the submission acknowledgment and store securely

### Escalation Path
- **Level 1**: PM-KISAN Portal Helpdesk – Available via the “Contact us” section on http://pmkisan.gov.in or email: pmkisan@gov.in (as per portal)  
- **Level 2**: State Nodal Officer (SNO) – Details available at: https://pmkisan.gov.in/SearchSNODetails.aspx  
- **Level 3**: RTI filing if no response within statutory SLA (typically 30 days for grievance redressal; refer to District Level Grievance Redressal Monitoring Committee timelines)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Confirm receipt of SMS alert from PM-KISAN regarding credit of installment  
- [ ] Verify bank account for receipt of Rs. 2,000 (first installment) or subsequent installments  
- [ ] Download and store sanction order or benefit transfer confirmation from PFMS portal (if accessible)  
- [ ] Ensure no duplicate payments have been made (check with SNO if needed)  
- [ ] Prepare for utilization certificate if required for any linked scheme (not mandatory for PM-KISAN as it’s unconditional income support)  
- [ ] Track next installment due date (every 4 months: April-July, August-November, December-March)  
- [ ] Inform client to report any grievances via the PM-KISAN portal’s “Grievance” section or District Level Grievance Redressal Monitoring Committee  
- [ ] Follow up for any failed transactions and coordinate with SNO for reprocessing  
- [ ] Document successful receipt for fee invoicing and client records  

**Note**: PM-KISAN does not require utilization certificates as it is a direct income transfer with no conditional use of funds.  

**Sources**:  
- Application process: States/UTs identify beneficiaries → prepare database → upload certified lists on PM-KISAN portal → SNO authenticates → NIC/PFMS/bank verification → RFT signed → FTO issued → sanction order → amount transferred via PFMS  
- Grievance redressal: District Level Grievance Redressal Monitoring Committees; beneficiaries can approach for inclusion if name missing  
- Benefit status check: Available via PM-KISAN portal → “Beneficiary Status” or “Know Your Status” links  
- Contact: http://pmkisan.gov.in/Contacts.aspx  
- SNO details: https://pmkisan.gov.in/SearchSNODetails.aspx
