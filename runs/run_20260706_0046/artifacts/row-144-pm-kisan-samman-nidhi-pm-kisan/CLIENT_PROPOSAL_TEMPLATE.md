## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment based on landholding and exclusion criteria.
2. Document collection and verification as per scheme requirements.
3. Preparation of beneficiary database with required fields: Name, Age, Gender, Category (SC/ST), Aadhaar Number (or Enrollment Number with alternate documents in Assam, Meghalaya, J&K), Bank Account Number, IFSC Code, Mobile Number (optional).
4. Ensuring no duplication of payments and reconciliation of wrong/incomplete bank details.
5. Uploading certified beneficiary lists on PM-Kisan portal (http://pmkisan.gov.in) in batches/phases.
6. Coordinating with State Nodal Officers for authentication and data submission.
7. Facilitating multi-level verification by NIC, PFMS, and banks, and signing Request For Transfer (RFT).

1. 2. A designated point of contact within your organization for coordination and queries.
3. Timely responses within a 2-day SLA to keep the application process on track.
