## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the scheme’s fixed annual benefit of ₹6,000 per eligible farmer family, ensuring consulting costs remain proportionate to the tangible income support delivered.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s high certainty of benefit delivery (direct DBT via PFMS) but incorporate a lag due to the 3-month approval cycle from state data submission to fund transfer, as evidenced in the operational guidelines.
3. **Volume Incentives**: Lower success fees at higher tiers acknowledge the administrative efficiency gains when processing bulk beneficiary lists uploaded by State Nodal Officers, reducing per-client effort.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Given the scheme’s rolling basis, pan-India scope, and mandatory Aadhaar/Bank details (with state-level exemptions for Assam/Meghalaya/J&K), the primary risk lies in documentation completeness and state-level data upload timelines—not benefit denial—warranting moderate success fees tied to verified installation receipt.
