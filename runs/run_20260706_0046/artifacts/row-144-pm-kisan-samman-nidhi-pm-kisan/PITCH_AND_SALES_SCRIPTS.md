# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping farming families access government income support schemes like PM Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN), which provides direct financial assistance to eligible landholding farmer families across India. Before we explore whether this scheme fits your situation, I’d like to understand your farming background and current needs to ensure we’re offering relevant guidance.

### Discovery Questions
1. Do you or your family own cultivable agricultural land in your name as per state land records?
2. What is the total size of your cultivable landholding (in hectares)?
3. Are you, your spouse, or any minor children in your household listed as joint owners of the land in official records?
4. Has anyone in your family held a constitutional post, served as a government employee (excluding Class IV/MTS), or paid income tax in the last assessment year?
5. Are you or any family member a registered professional (e.g., doctor, engineer, lawyer, CA, architect) with a professional body?
6. Is your monthly pension (if retired) above Rs. 10,000?
7. Do you currently receive any direct benefit transfers into your bank account for farming support?
8. Have you previously applied for PM-KISAN or faced issues with bank/Aadhaar linkage for benefit transfer?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant owns cultivable land in their name (per state land records)
- [ ] Family unit includes husband, wife, and minor children (if applicable)
- [ ] No family member holds constitutional posts
- [ ] No serving/retired government employee (excluding MTS/Class IV/Group D)
- [ ] No superannuated pensioner with monthly pension ≥ Rs. 10,000
- [ ] No income tax payer in last assessment year
- [ ] No registered professional (doctor, engineer, lawyer, CA, architect)
- [ ] Land is not institutional holding
- [ ] Land is not used for non-agricultural purposes
- [ ] Landholding is not micro/non-cultivable
- [ ] Cut-off date eligibility met (land owned as of 01.02.2019, or transferred via succession)
- [ ] Aadhaar number available (or enrollment number with alternate docs in Assam/Meghalaya/J&K)
- [ ] Bank account and IFSC details available
- [ ] No duplicate payments already received

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you may meet the core eligibility criteria for PM Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN), which provides Rs. 6000 per year in three installments directly to your bank account. Let me explain how this scheme works and how we can assist you with the application process.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step →**
Many small and marginal farming families struggle to afford essential agricultural inputs like seeds, fertilizers, and pesticides due to limited and irregular income, often forcing them to rely on high-interest loans from moneylenders, which threatens their financial stability and continuity in farming.

### Scheme intro:
Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a Central Sector Scheme launched by the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare, Government of India. It provides a direct income support of Rs. 6000 per annum per eligible farmer family, paid in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 every four months, transferred directly into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts via the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mechanism. The entire financial liability is borne by the Government of India.

### Why they qualify (personalised):
- You own cultivable land in your name as per state land records  
- Your family comprises husband, wife, and minor children (or applicable structure)  
- No family member is a government employee (excluding MTS/Class IV), income tax payer, or registered professional  
- Your land is not institutional, non-agricultural, or micro holding
