### PM Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Agriculture, Cooperation & Farmers Welfare (DAC&FW), Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare
- **Portal / Application URL:** http://pmkisan.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Landholding farmer families
- Age of business: Not applicable (scheme targets individuals/families, not businesses)
- Sector: Agriculture
- Revenue cap: Not mentioned
- Employee count: Not mentioned
- Other: 
  - Family must comprise husband, wife, and minor children who collectively own cultivable land as per land records
  - Landholding must be cultivable and in the beneficiary's own name
  - Cut-off date for eligibility: 01.02.2019 (no changes for next 5 years except land transfer via succession)
  - Exclusions: 
    - Institutional land holders
    - Farmer families with members holding constitutional posts
    - Serving/retired government employees (excluding Multi Tasking Staff/Class IV/Group D)
    - Superannuated/retired pensioners with monthly pension of Rs. 10,000 or more
    - Income tax payees in the last assessment year
    - Professionals like doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants, and architects registered with professional bodies
    - Micro land holdings (non-cultivable)
    - Agricultural land used for non-agricultural purposes

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Rs. 6000 per annum per family
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (Direct Benefit Transfer - DBT)
- Duration of benefit: Annual (paid in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each every four months)

#### Documents Required
1. Name, Age, Gender, Category (SC/ST)
2. Aadhaar Number (or Aadhaar Enrollment Number in Assam, Meghalaya, J&K till 31.03.2020)
3. Alternate identity documents (Driving Licence, Voter ID Card, NREGA Job Card, etc.) where Aadhaar not available
4. Bank Account Number
5. IFSC Code
6. Mobile Number (advised but not mandatory)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. States/UTs identify eligible beneficiary landholding farmer families using land records
2. Prepare database capturing Name, Age, Gender, Category (SC/ST), Aadhaar Number (or Enrollment Number with alternate documents in Assam, Meghalaya, J&K), Bank Account Number, IFSC Code, and Mobile Number (optional)
3. Ensure no duplication of payments and speedy reconciliation for wrong/incomplete bank details
4. Upload certified beneficiary lists on PM-Kisan portal in batches/phases
5. State Nodal Officers authenticate and upload data on the portal
6. Data undergoes multi-level verification by NIC, PFMS, and banks
7. State Nodal Officers sign Request For Transfer (RFT) containing beneficiary count and total amount
8. PFMS issues Fund Transfer Order (FTO) based on RFT
9. Department of Agriculture, Cooperation & Farmers Welfare issues sanction order
10. Amount transferred to beneficiaries' bank accounts via accredited and sponsoring banks using PFMS portal, managed by NPCI
