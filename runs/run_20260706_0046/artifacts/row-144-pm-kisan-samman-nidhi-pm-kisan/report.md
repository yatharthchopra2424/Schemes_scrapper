# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
**Scheme Name:** PM Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)  
**Scheme ID:** row-144  
**Ministry / Category:** Agriculture  
**Scheme Type:** subsidy  
**Geographic Scope:** Pan-India  
**Implementing Agency:** Department of Agriculture, Cooperation & Farmers Welfare (DAC&FW), Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare  
**Application Portal:** [http://pmkisan.gov.in](http://pmkisan.gov.in)  
**Status / Deadlines:** Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round based on State/UT submission of beneficiary lists.  
**Last Updated:** 2020  
**Fund Size (Cr):** 75000  
**Max Per Entity:** Rs. 6000 per annum per family  

### Objectives
- To augment the income of Small and Marginal Farmers (SMFs)  
- To supplement the financial needs of SMFs in procuring various inputs for proper crop health and appropriate yields  
- To protect farmers from falling into the clutches of moneylenders for meeting agricultural expenses  
- To ensure continuance of farmers in farming activities  
- To provide income support to all landholding farmer families in the country  
- To transfer financial benefit directly into bank accounts of eligible farmers under Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mode  

### Eligibility Matrix
| **Eligibility Criteria** | **Details** | **Source** |
|--------------------------|-------------|------------|
| **Landholding Requirement** | All landholding farmer families having cultivable land in their names are eligible. The landholder farmer's family is defined as 'a family comprising of husband, wife and minor children who collectively own cultivable land as per land records of the concerned State/UT'. | Key Facts, Operational Guidelines |
| **Land Size Limit** | Initially limited to Small and Marginal Farmers (SMFs) with cultivable landholding up to 2 hectares. Revised w.e.f. 1.6.2019 to extend to all farmer families irrespective of landholding size. | Revised FAQ |
| **Cut-off Date** | 01.02.2019 for determination of eligibility. No changes thereafter for next 5 years except transfer of land on succession due to death of landholder. | Key Facts, Operational Guidelines, Revised FAQ |
| **Joint Holding** | If land is owned by multiple family members, benefit is transferred to the bank account of the person with highest quantum of landholding. If equal, to the eldest member. | Operational Guidelines, Clarification on OGs |
| **Succession** | Benefit allowed in cases of land transfer due to succession after cut-off date. Proportionate benefit for transfer between 01.12.2018 and 31.01.2019. | Key Facts, Operational Guidelines, Clarifications Relating to Clauses |
| **Exclusions** | Institutional land holders; farmer families with members holding constitutional posts; serving/retired government employees (excluding Multi Tasking Staff/Class IV/Group D); superannuated/retired pensioners with monthly pension ≥ Rs. 10,000; income tax payees in last assessment year; professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs, architects) registered with professional bodies. | Key Facts, Operational Guidelines, FAQ |
| **Special Cases** | - Micro land holdings (non-cultivable) excluded<br>- Agricultural land used for non-agricultural purposes not covered<br>- Tenant farmers not eligible unless land transferred via succession<br>- No distinction between urban/rural cultivable land if under actual cultivation | Key Facts, Additional FAQ, Revised FAQ |

### Benefits & Financial Support

|------------------|-------------|----------------------|------------|
| **Annual Financial Benefit** | Direct payment transferred in three equal installments | Rs. 6000 per annum per family | Key Facts, Operational Guidelines, FAQ |

| **Aadhaar Requirement** | Mandatory for second installment onwards (from 01.04.2019), except in Assam, Meghalaya, and J&K where exemption till 31.03.2020. > **Key Process Notes:**  
> - States/UTs can upload beneficiary lists in phases/batches as they are finalized.  
> - First installment for period 01.12.2018–31.03.2019 transferred immediately on identification.  
> - For transfer of ownership between 01.12.2018–31.01.2019, proportionate benefit from date of transfer to 31.03.2019.  
> - Benefit allowed for succession (inheritance) post cut-off date; transferees via sale/gift not eligible unless transferor retains cultivable land.  
> - States must ensure no duplicate payments and reconcile wrong/incomplete bank details speedily.  
> - Portal URL: [http://pmkisan.gov.in](http://pmkisan.gov.in)

### Important Warnings & Caveats
> **Incorrect Declaration Penalty:** Beneficiary liable for recovery of transferred benefit and penal actions as per law for incorrect self-declaration.  
> **Aadhaar Mandate:** From second installment onwards (01.04.2019), Aadhaar number mandatory except in Assam, Meghalaya, J&K (exempted till 31.03.2020).  
> **Land Must Be in Own Name:** Tenant farmers ineligible unless land transferred via succession.  
> **No Benefit for Non-Agricultural Use:** Agricultural land used for non-agricultural purposes excluded.  
> **Micro Holdings Excluded:** Non-cultivable micro land holdings not eligible.  
> **Cut-off Date Rigidity:** 01.02.2019 fixed for 5 years; only succession triggers eligibility change.  
> **Duplicate Payment Prevention:** States/UTs must ensure no duplication and reconcile bank details speedily.  
> **Exemption Extension:** Assam, Meghalaya, J&K exempted from Aadhaar requirement till 31.03.2021 (per revised guidelines 29.03.2020).

### 2. PITCH_AND_SALES_SCRIPTS.md
**Real-time Usage:** Open this file 5 minutes before your first Discovery Call with a lead. Read the "Problem Framing" out loud to hook them, then use the Qualification Checklist to interrogate their eligibility live on the phone. Keep the Objection Handlers table visible so you can immediately counter when they say "We're too small for this."  
**Specific Scenarios:**  
- **Problem Framing:** Open with: "Many farmers struggle to afford quality seeds and fertilizers between harvests—would access to Rs. 6000 yearly in three installments help ease that cash flow gap?"  
- **Qualification Checklist:** Ask sequentially:  
  1. "Do you or family members own cultivable land in your name?"  
  2. "Is anyone in your family a government employee (beyond Class IV), income tax payer, or receiving pension over Rs. 10,000/month?"  
  3. "Has your land changed hands since 01.02.2019 via sale or gift?" (If yes, explain succession exception)  
- **Objection Handling:** If client says "We’re too small," respond: "Actually, the scheme now covers *all* landholding families regardless of size—your eligibility depends on land ownership, not acreage." Use the revised FAQ excerpt as proof.  

### 3. APPLICATION_PLAYBOOK.md
**Real-time Usage:** Print this out or pin it to your desktop once the client signs the retainer. Check off each box in "Stage 1" before moving to "Stage 2". Use the "Client Communication Template" to copy-paste directly into your email when chasing them for pending documents.  
**Specific Scenarios:**  
- **Stage 1 (Eligibility Confirmation):** Use the checklist to verify land records show ownership as of 01.02.2019. If land transferred between 01.12.2018–31.01.2019, calculate proportionate benefit using the formula: (Days from transfer to 31.03.2019 ÷ 120) × 2000.  
- **Stage 2 (Document Collection):** When client provides Aadhaar, use the template to request: "Please share a clear scan of your Aadhaar card, bank passbook showing account number and IFSC, and your mobile number (optional but helpful for SMS alerts)."  
- **Stage 3 (Upload & Follow-up):** After uploading to portal, use the template to inform client: "Your details have been submitted to the State Nodal Officer. Expect SMS confirmation within 7–10 days. Check status at [pmkisan.gov.in](http://pmkisan.gov.in) using your Aadhaar or bank details."  
- **Stage 4 (Payment Tracking):** If client misses an installment, use the escalation path: Contact District Level Grievance Redressal Monitoring Committee with application reference.
