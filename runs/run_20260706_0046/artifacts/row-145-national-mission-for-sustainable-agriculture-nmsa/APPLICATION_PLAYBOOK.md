# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- ✅ The applicant is a State Government or its subordinate office/institution (State Department of Agriculture, State Agricultural University, Krishi Vigyan Kendra, ICAR Centre, or reputed NGO engaged by State Government for cluster/village development plans).
- ✅ The applicant operates in an area with limited government infrastructure (if NGO is the implementing agency).
- ✅ The applicant is preparing an Annual Action Plan (AAP) based on fund availability and past performance.
- ✅ The AAP includes unfinished/spill-over activities and committed liabilities from the previous year.
- ✅ The applicant agrees to submit Utilization Certificates and progress reports for fund release in installments.
- ✅ The applicant commits to remitting any interest or earnings against grants-in-aid to the Consolidated Fund of India.
- ✅ For North-Eastern and hilly states, the funding pattern is 90:10 (Centre:State); for general states, 60:40; for UTs and National Level Agencies, 100% central funding.
- ✅ The applicant has liberalized transit regulations for transport of timber (precondition for availing benefits under SMAF sub-mission).
- ✅ The applicant ensures at least 50% of allocation is utilized for small and marginal farmers, of which at least 30% are women beneficiaries.
- ✅ The applicant allocates 16% and 8% of total allocation for Special Component Plan (SCP) and Tribal Sub Plan (TSP) respectively, or in proportion to SC/ST population in the district.
- ✅ The applicant agrees to involve Panchayati Raj Institutions and Farmers’ participatory approach in beneficiary selection and location-specific activities.
- ✅ The applicant commits to displaying beneficiary names and assistance details on Panchayat/Block office notice boards and departmental website.
- ✅ The applicant ensures submission of detailed Quarterly Progress Reports by the 10th of the first month of the next quarter and Annual Progress Report within three months after financial year closure.
- ✅ The applicant agrees to maintain digital location of project areas, physical assets, beneficiary names, and assistance provided, uploaded on digital map of district/state and kept in public domain.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation/Registration Certificate  
- PAN Card  
- Audited Financial Statements (Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss)  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of last 3 years  
- GST Registration Certificate  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs, if applicable)  
- Authorization Letter from State Government (if applying as NGO or implementing agency)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Utilization Certificates (GFR 19 A format)  
- Balance Sheet  
- Audited Statement of Accounts  
- Progress Reports (including soft copy, photographs/video)  
- Location details of nursery and plantation  
- Training records  
- Number of saplings/seedlings in nurseries  
- Annual Action Plan (AAP) for the current financial year  
- Project Sanctioning Committee (PSC) approval copy (if available)  
- Soil Health Cards (for agroforestry beneficiaries)  
- Verification Certificate for Plantation (Annexure-IX format)  
- Transit regulation exemption proof (from State Forest Department)  
- List of beneficiaries with Aadhaar linkage (for DBT)  
- Minutes of State Level Committee (SLC) meetings approving AAP  

### Document Quality Check
- ✅ All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)  
- ✅ Document names must match the checklist (e.g., "Utilization_Certificate_FY2023-24.pdf")  
- ✅ All certificates must be issued by competent authorities (CA, Gazetted Officer, etc.)  
- ✅ Photographs must be timestamped and geo-tagged where applicable  
- ✅ Videos must be in MP4 format, max duration 5 minutes, max size 50 MB  
- ✅ All financial documents must be audited and signed by Chartered Accountant  
- ✅ Declarations and undertakings must be on stamp paper where required  
- ✅ Language: English or Hindi; if regional language, certified translation must be attached  
- ✅ Utilization Certificates must reflect separate utilization of central and state shares  
- ✅ Progress reports must be submitted in prescribed format (Annexure-VI)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- DPR is not explicitly required for NMSA AAP submission. However, States must prepare a Mission Implementation Plan (MIP) with a 5–7 year horizon and component-wise Annual Action Plans (AAPs).  
- If detailed project reports are needed for specific interventions (e.g., nursery development, plantation models), they should include:  
  - Executive Summary  
  - Intervention Description (e.g., NDQPM, PBP, LDPFL, HDBP)  
  - Objectives and Expected Outcomes  
  - Implementation Strategy and Timeline  
  - Budget Breakdown (cost norms as per Annexures I–IV)  
  - Monitoring and Evaluation Plan  
  - Risk Assessment and Mitigation Measures  
  - Sustainability Plan  
  - Annexures: Maps, Beneficiary Lists, Cost Estimates  
- **Note**: Verify with the Nodal Officer or Project Sanctioning Committee (PSC) whether a DPR is required for specific components under NMSA.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Use the official AAP format** provided in Appendix-II A of the NMSA Operational Guidelines.  
2. **Ensure all fields are filled** – leave no blank sections; if not applicable, write “Not Applicable” or “NA”.  
3. **Component-wise breakdown** – prepare separate tables for each of the four NMSA components: RAD, SHM, OFWM, and CCSAMMN.  
4. **Financial targets must match** – the sum of central and state share must equal the total project cost.  
5. **Physical targets must be realistic** – based on past performance, fund availability, and agro-climatic suitability.  
6. **Include spill-over activities** – AAP must compulsorily include unfinished/spill-over activities and committed liabilities from the previous year.  
7. **Beneficiary details** – ensure at least 50% of allocation is for small/marginal farmers, with 30% women; include SC/ST breakdown for SCP/TSP.  
8. **Attach all mandatory documents** – Utilization Certificates, progress reports, location details, training records, nursery stock details.  
9. **Declare compliance** – include a signed undertaking that interest/earnings on grants-in-aid will be remitted to the Consolidated Fund of India.  
10. **Portal submission** – submit AAP via https://nmsa.dac.gov.in/RptAAPState.aspx or similar reporting portals; take screenshot of successful submission and save reference number.  

**Portal Notes**:  
- AAP submission portals: https://nmsa.dac.gov.in/RptAAPState.aspx, https://nmsa.dac.gov.in/RptAAPStateNBM.aspx, https://nmsa.dac.gov.in/RptAAPStateAgro.aspx  
- Document upload: https://nmsa.dac.gov.in/RptDocUpload.aspx  
- Progress reporting: https://nmsa.dac.gov.in/RptAchievement.aspx, https://nmsa.dac.gov.in/RptAchievementNBM.aspx  

### Internal Review Gate
- ✅ All sections of the AAP form are completed as per Appendix-II A  
- ✅ Financial and physical targets are consistent across components  
- ✅ Utilization Certificates and progress reports from previous year are attached  
- ✅ Location details of nurseries and plantations are provided with maps/geo-tags  
- ✅ Training records and sapling/seedling counts are documented  
- ✅ Declarations on remittance of interest/earnings to Consolidated Fund of India are signed  
- ✅ Proof of liberalized transit regulations (for SMAF) is attached  
- ✅ Beneficiary list includes Aadhaar numbers, category (small/marginal, women, SC/ST)  
- ✅ AAP includes unfinished/spill-over activities and committed liabilities from previous year  
- ✅ All documents are in PDF format, file size <5 MB, named appropriately  
- ✅ Soft copy of progress reports and photographs/video are included  
- ✅ Application is submitted via the correct portal before the deadline (March end)  
- ✅ Reference number and submission screenshot are saved for follow-up

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed AAP via the official NMSA portal: https://nmsa.dac.gov.in  
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation page showing the reference number and timestamp  
- Save the reference number in the case file and set automated reminders for:  
  - Follow-up on Utilization Certificate submission (quarterly)  
  - Progress report submission (monthly/quarterly)  
  - Fund release tracking (installment-based)  
- Store all submitted documents in a secure, indexed folder (digital and physical backup)  
- Notify the client of successful submission with reference number and next steps

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- ✅ Obtain and save the approval letter from the Project Sanctioning Committee (PSC)  
- ✅ Verify the sanctioned amount against the AAP submission (central and state share breakdown)  
- ✅ Confirm the fund release schedule (installment-based) as per Utilization Certificate submission  
- ✅ Ensure Utilization Certificates (GFR 19 A) are prepared and submitted in the prescribed format for each installment  
- ✅ Monitor submission of detailed progress reports (soft copy, photographs/video) as per Annexure-VI  
- ✅ Verify that unspent balances from previous year are adjusted against current year’s first/second installment (if revalidation claimed)  
- ✅ Confirm that interest or earnings against grants-in-aid are remitted to the Consolidated Fund of India  
- ✅ Ensure beneficiary details are uploaded on departmental website and Panchayat/Block notice boards  
- ✅ Confirm that at least 50% of allocation reaches small/marginal farmers, with 30% women beneficiaries  
- ✅ Validate that SCP/TSP allocations are 16% and 8% respectively (or proportional to SC/ST population)  
- ✅ Prepare and submit the Utilization Certificate and final progress report for financial closure  
- ✅ Coordinate with the client for success fee invoicing upon satisfactory fund utilization and reporting
