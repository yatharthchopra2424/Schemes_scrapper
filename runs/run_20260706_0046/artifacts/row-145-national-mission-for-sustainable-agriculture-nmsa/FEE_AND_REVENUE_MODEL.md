## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client, leveraging the scheme’s grant structure (e.g., 50% cost subsidy for agroforestry interventions under SMAF/NMSA).
2. **Risk-Sharing Model**: Success fees are contingent on fund approval and utilization, reflecting the scheme’s AAP-driven release mechanism and Utilization Certificate requirements.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Pricing scales with client capacity; State Governments and implementing agencies (e.g., SAUs, KVKs, ICAR Centres) typically fall into the Enterprise tier due to scheme eligibility and fund scale.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: NMSA/SMAF involves moderate risk due to annual AAP submission cycles, dependency on Utilization Certificates and progress reports for fund release, and revalidation rules for unspent balances. Processing timelines (typically 3–6 months for AAP approval and installment release) support a success fee lag assumption of 3 months in revenue projections.
