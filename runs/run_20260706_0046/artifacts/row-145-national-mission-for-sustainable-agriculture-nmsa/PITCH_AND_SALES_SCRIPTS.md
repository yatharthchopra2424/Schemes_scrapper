## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. We work with farmers, agri-entrepreneurs, and rural households across India who are looking to adopt sustainable agricultural practices—especially in rainfed areas—to improve productivity, conserve natural resources, and build climate resilience. The National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA) supports exactly these goals through financial and technical assistance for integrated farming, water conservation, soil health, and agroforestry. I’d like to understand your current farming operations and challenges to see if NMSA could be a good fit for you.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is the primary type of farming or agricultural activity you are currently engaged in (e.g., crop cultivation, livestock, horticulture, agroforestry)?
2. How large is your landholding, and is it primarily rainfed or irrigated?
3. Are you located in a state that has liberalized transit regulations for timber transport (e.g., Andhra Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Telangana, etc.)?
4. Have you previously accessed any government agricultural schemes (e.g., RKVY, NFSM, MIDH, MGNREGS)?
5. What are your main challenges related to water availability, soil health, or climate variability on your farm?
6. Are you interested in adopting practices like integrated farming systems, agroforestry models, or water conservation technologies (e.g., farm ponds, drip irrigation)?
7. Do you have access to support from Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), State Agricultural Universities (SAUs), or ICAR institutes in your region?
8. Are you part of a farmer group, cooperative, or FPO, or do you operate individually?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Farmer, agri-entrepreneur, or rural household (primary beneficiary)
- [ ] Engaged in farming activities suitable for NMSA interventions (integrated farming, water management, soil health, agroforestry)
- [ ] Located in a state with liberalized transit regulations for timber (if pursuing agroforestry under SMAF)
- [ ] Willing to participate through State-level implementation structures (Department of Agriculture, SAU, KVK, ICAR, or NGO)
- [ ] Small or marginal farmer preferred (though not strictly excluded)
- [ ] Interest in sustainable, climate-resilient, or resource-conserving agricultural practices
- [ ] Access to or willingness to engage with district/state-level agricultural agencies

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re facing challenges with [water/soil/climate] and looking to improve sustainability and income resilience. The National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA) offers financial support and technical guidance for farmers like you to adopt integrated farming systems, water conservation, and agroforestry—especially if you're in a rainfed area. Would you like me to walk you through how it works and what support you could access?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many farmers in rainfed areas face declining productivity due to erratic rainfall, soil degradation, water scarcity, and climate volatility. Traditional farming systems often lack resilience, leading to income instability and increased vulnerability to droughts, floods, and market fluctuations. Without access to modern, sustainable practices and financial support, farmers struggle to invest in long-term solutions like agroforestry, efficient irrigation, or soil health improvement.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA), implemented by the Department of Agriculture & Cooperation, Ministry of Agriculture, Government of India, is a centrally sponsored scheme that provides financial assistance as grants-in-aid to State Governments. It promotes sustainable agricultural practices through four key interventions: Rainfed Area Development (RAD), Soil Health Management (SHM), On Farm Water Management (OFWM), and Climate Change and Sustainable Agriculture Modeling & Networking (CCSAMMN). Financial support follows a 60:40 funding pattern (Centre:State) for general states, 90:10 for North-Eastern and hilly states, and 100% central funding for Union Territories and National Level Agencies. Assistance is delivered via State Governments and their agencies (e.g., Departments of Agriculture, SAUs, KVKs, ICAR Centres) to beneficiary farmers.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in farming activities aligned with NMSA’s focus areas (e.g., integrated farming, water conservation, soil health, or agroforestry).
- Your landholding is suitable for interventions like RAD, SHM, or OFWM, with support limited to 2–5 hectares per beneficiary depending on the component.
- You are located in a state that participates in NMSA and has liberalized transit regulations (if applicable for agroforestry under SMAF), enabling access to State-level implementation.
- You are willing to work through State Government structures (e.g., Department of Agriculture, SAU, KVK) to access benefits.
