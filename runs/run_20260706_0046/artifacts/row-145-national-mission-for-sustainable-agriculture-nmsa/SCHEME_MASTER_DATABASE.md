### National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Agriculture & Cooperation, Ministry of Agriculture, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nmsa.dac.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Annual Action Plan (AAP) submission is required yearly; funds are released based on AAP approval and utilization of previous funds. Revalidation of unspent funds is permitted subject to conditions.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: State Governments and their subordinate offices/institutions
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Agriculture
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Eligible implementing agencies include State Departments of Agriculture, State Agricultural Universities (SAUs), Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) Centres, and reputed NGOs engaged by State Governments for cluster/village development plans in areas with limited government infrastructure. Beneficiaries are primarily farmers, especially small and marginal farmers in rainfed areas, who participate through State-level implementation structures.

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support is provided as grants-in-aid to State Governments based on Annual Action Plans (AAPs), following a 60:40 funding pattern between Centre and State for general states, 90:10 for North-Eastern and hilly states, and 100% central funding for Union Territories and National Level Agencies. Funds are released in installments upon submission of Utilization Certificates and progress reports.
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (grants-in-aid released in installments to States)
- Duration of benefit: Annual (based on Annual Action Plan submission and approval)

#### Documents Required
1. Utilization Certificates
2. Balance Sheet
3. Audited Statement of Accounts
4. Progress Reports (including soft copy, photographs/video)
5. Location details of nursery and plantation
6. Training records
7. Number of saplings/seedlings in nurseries

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. State Governments prepare Annual Action Plans (AAPs) based on fund availability and past performance.
2. AAPs are submitted to the Government of India for approval by the Project Sanctioning Committee.
3. Funds are released in installments to States upon receipt of Utilization Certificates and detailed progress reports.
4. States implement components through district and village-level agencies.
5. Beneficiaries (farmers) access benefits via State-level implementing departments/agencies.
6. Monitoring and evaluation are conducted quarterly by technical personnel and reported to the National Advisory Committee.
