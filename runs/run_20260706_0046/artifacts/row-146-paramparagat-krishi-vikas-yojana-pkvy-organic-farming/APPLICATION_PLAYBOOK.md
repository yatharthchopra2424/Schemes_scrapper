# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Farmer group, self-help group, producer collective, or individual farmer willing to adopt organic farming under PGS-India  
- [ ] Minimum 5 members in the group (for group registration)  
- [ ] Members belong to the same village or close-by villages with continuous territory  
- [ ] Willingness to adopt organic farming practices and adhere to PGS-India standards  
- [ ] Preference given to small and marginal farmers, women farmers, and those from SC/ST communities  
- [ ] Individual farmers can participate through group formation (maximum 2 years to form a group)  
- [ ] Land holding of any single member does not exceed 50% of total group land  
- [ ] At least 25% of group members are well versed in PGS organic guarantee systems or have undergone training  
- [ ] All members have signed the PGS pledge and group agreement  
- [ ] Access to PGS documents and preferably computer/internet for online data upload (optional)  
- [ ] Registered with the concerned Regional Council and obtained user ID/password for PGS-India portal  

> **Sources**:  
> - Eligibility criteria from KEY FACTS: Farmer groups, SHGs, producers, collectives engaged in agriculture willing to adopt organic farming under PGS-India; individual farmers benefit via group participation; preference for small/marginal, women, SC/ST farmers.  
> - Detailed eligibility from supporting evidence: Minimum 5 members, same/close-by villages, 25% trained members, signed pledge/agreement, land holding cap (<50%), registration with RC, user ID/password for portal.  
> - Application Portal: https://pgsindia-ncof.gov.in/

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN card of group/representative  
- Aadhar number of all members (mandatory)  
- Bank account details (optional but recommended for DBT)  
- Mobile number of all members (mandatory)  
- Udyam registration (if applicable for processing/handling units)  
- ITR of previous year (if applicable for income verification)  
- GST registration (if applicable for commercial entities)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Application form (Annexure I)  
- Farm history sheet (Annexure II)  
- Farmers pledge (Annexure III)  
- Group agreement and terms of reference (ToR)  
- Endorsement letter from local authority or existing local group (mandatory for registration)  
- Farm map with GPS coordinates showing locations of each member’s farm  
- Input usage and management practices record for last one year  
- Attendance and proceeding register for group meetings  
- Attendance register for trainings  
- Peer appraisal summary sheet (season-wise)  
- Individual peer appraisal sheets for last four seasons (24 months)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, signed, and dated  
- File size: PDFs under 5 MB; images under 2 MB  
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_MemberName_Date.pdf`  
- Aadhar and mobile numbers must match across all documents  
- Bank account details (if provided) must be in name of group or authorized representative  
- All declarations must be self-attested or notarized where required  
- Documents must be in Hindi or English; vernacular languages require certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for PKVY under PGS-India. The scheme focuses on group registration, pledge, farm history, and peer appraisal rather than a detailed project report. However, maintain a farm history sheet and input usage records as part of documentation.  
> **Verify with nodal officer** if any additional project details are sought during verification.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use only the official application form from the PGS-India portal (https://pgsindia-ncof.gov.in/PGSI/LocalGroup/registration) or authorized Regional Council.  
2. Fill all fields in block letters; avoid overwriting or correction fluid.  
3. Ensure Aadhar and mobile numbers are entered exactly as per UIDAI records.  
4. Group name must be unique and not already registered on the portal.  
5. Clearly mention the village, block, district, and state for all member farms.  
6. Declare willingness to adopt organic farming and adhere to PGS-India standards in the pledge section.  
7. Attach scanned copies of all supporting documents in the prescribed order.  
8. Authorized signatory must sign and date the form; group leader’s signature is mandatory.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All application form fields completed and legible  
- [ ] Farmers pledge signed by all members  
- [ ] Farm history sheet filled for each member  
- [ ] Group agreement and ToR signed and dated  
- [ ] Endorsement letter attached (if required by RC)  
- [ ] Aadhar and mobile numbers verified for all members  
- [ ] Bank account details (if provided) validated  
- [ ] All documents uploaded in correct format and size  
- [ ] Declarations signed and dated  
- [ ] Application submitted via online portal or hard copy to Regional Council

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit application online via https://pgsindia-ncof.gov.in/PGSI/LocalGroup/registration or in hard copy to the concerned Regional Council  
- Take screenshot of online submission confirmation or obtain acknowledgment receipt for hard copy  
- Store application reference number (e.g., Registration ID, Application No.)  
- Set follow-up reminders for Day 25 and Day 35  
- Monitor portal for updates on verification status

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Download and verify approval letter/user ID and password from PGS-India portal  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount: Rs. 50,000 per hectare for 3 years (62% incentive, 38% mobilization/certification)  
- [ ] Begin organic farming practices and maintain records for peer review  
- [ ] Upload peer appraisal summary sheet 7 days before each harvest  
- [ ] Upload actual yields within 60 days of harvest  
- [ ] Prepare for PGS certification after completion of conversion period (24 months for annual crops, 36 months for perennials)  
- [ ] Utilize financial assistance for input production, labeling, packaging, transportation, marketing, training, exposure visits, documentation, and certification  
- [ ] Submit utilization certificate and expenditure proof as per installment release schedule  
- [ ] Ensure active participation in group activities and adherence to PGS-India standards to avoid sanctions  
- [ ] Monitor for Transaction Certificate (TC) generation post-harvest for market linkage  

> **Sources**:  
> - Financial support: Rs. 50,000/ha for 3 years (62% = Rs. 31,000 incentive, 38% = Rs. 19,000 for mobilization/certification) – from KEY FACTS  
> - Support released in installments based on progress and verification  
> - Benefits: organic input production, certification, labeling, packaging, marketing, capacity building, training, exposure visits, market linkage  
> - Application process: Form group → Register with State Nodal Agency/Regional Council under PGS-India → Submit documents → Verification → Get user ID/password → Upload data → Maintain records → Apply for PGS certification after conversion  
> - Portal: https://pgsindia-ncof.gov.in/  
> - Contact: nbdc[AT]nic[DOT]in | +91 120-2764906 / +91 120-2764212  
> - Status: Rolling basis, no fixed deadline, applications accepted year-round  
> - Last Updated: 2025  
> - Implementing Agency: Department of Agriculture, Cooperation and Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India  
> - Key Caveats: Support only to organized farmer groups (not individuals directly); benefits contingent on PGS-India standards adherence; certification subject to verification; max 3 years per group; subject to verification and compliance
