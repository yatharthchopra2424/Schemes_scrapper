## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to farmer groups under PKVY.
2. Success fees contingent on verified certification and fund disbursement through the PGS-India portal.
3. Retainers cover initial group mobilization, documentation, and application submission efforts.
4. Scheme-specific note: PKVY has a moderate risk profile due to its 3-year conversion period and dependency on group compliance with PGS-India standards; however, the rolling application basis and clear financial structure (₹50,000/ha for 3 years) support predictable revenue modeling once groups are formed and registered.
