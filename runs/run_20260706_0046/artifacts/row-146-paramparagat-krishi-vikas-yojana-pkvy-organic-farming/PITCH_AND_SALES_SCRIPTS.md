# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping farmer groups and collectives access government support for transitioning to organic farming under the Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY). This scheme provides financial assistance for organic certification, input production, and market linkage—specifically designed for groups willing to adopt PGS-India standards. I’d like to understand your group’s current farming practices and goals to see if PKVY aligns with your needs.

### Discovery Questions
1. How many farmers are in your group, and are you all located in the same or nearby villages?  
2. Has your group already registered with a Regional Council under PGS-India, or are you in the process of forming?  
3. What is the total land area (in hectares) your group cultivates, and are you currently practicing or planning to adopt organic farming?  
4. Do at least 25% of your members have training or knowledge of PGS-India standards or organic farming practices?  
5. Have all members signed the PGS pledge and group agreement to follow organic practices collectively?  
6. Does any single member’s landholding exceed 50% of the group’s total land?  
7. Are you able to maintain records of input usage and farm management practices for at least one year?  
8. Are you interested in accessing financial support for organic conversion, certification, and market development over three years?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Group has minimum 5 members  
- [ ] Members belong to same or nearby villages with continuous territory  
- [ ] Group is willing to adopt PGS-India organic farming standards  
- [ ] At least 25% of members are trained in PGS-India/NPOP standards  
- [ ] All members have signed PGS pledge and group agreement  
- [ ] No single member’s landholding exceeds 50% of group total  
- [ ] Group commits to bringing entire farm/livestock under organic management  
- [ ] Group can maintain and upload farm records (input usage, management)  
- [ ] Group is registered or willing to register with concerned Regional Council  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, your group appears well-positioned to benefit from the Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY), which provides up to Rs. 50,000 per hectare for three years to support organic conversion, certification, and market access. Let me explain how this works for groups like yours.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Farmer groups face high costs and technical barriers when transitioning to organic farming—especially for certification, input production, labeling, and market access. Without support, many struggle to meet PGS-India standards or access premium organic markets, limiting income growth despite sustainable practices.

**Scheme intro:**
The Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY), administered by the Department of Agriculture, Cooperation and Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India, provides financial assistance of Rs. 50,000 per hectare for three years. Of this, 62% (Rs. 31,000/ha) supports organic conversion, input production, labeling, packaging, transportation, and marketing, while 38% (Rs. 19,000/ha) covers mobilization, training, exposure visits, documentation, and certification expenses under the PGS-India system.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your group has 5+ members from the same or nearby villages  
- At least 25% of members are trained in PGS-India/NPOP standards  
- All members have signed the PGS pledge and group agreement  
- You are committed to adopting organic practices across your entire landholding

## 3. Objection Handlers

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I've heard these schemes never get approved" | PKVY has a rolling, year-round application process with no fixed deadline. Over 2.4 million farmers and 79,000 groups are already registered under PGS-India, with more than 2.6 lakh certificates issued—demonstrating consistent approvals for compliant groups. |
| "How much do you charge?" | [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT] |
| "I'll try it myself first" | Common mistakes include groups forming with fewer than 5 members or failing to ensure all members sign the PGS pledge and group agreement—both mandatory for registration. We help avoid these pitfalls from the start. |
| "We're too small / too big" | PKVY targets farmer groups with a minimum of 5 members (no upper limit per group, though Regional Councils may cap size based on local context). There is no minimum or maximum landholding for the group, but no single member’s holding should exceed 50% of the total. |
| "I don't have the documents ready" | The three most commonly missing documents are: (1) signed PGS pledge from all members, (2) farm history sheet for each member covering last year’s input usage, and (3) group agreement adhering to collective responsibility and vision. |
| "We already work with a CA" | "Great — we work alongside CAs. They handle your accounts; we handle the scheme strategy and applications." |
