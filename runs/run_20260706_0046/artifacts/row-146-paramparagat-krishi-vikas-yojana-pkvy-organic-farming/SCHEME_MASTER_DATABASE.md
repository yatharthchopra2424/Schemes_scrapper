### Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY) – Organic Farming
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Agriculture, Cooperation and Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://pgsindia-ncof.gov.in/
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Farmer groups, self-help groups, producers, and other collectives engaged in agriculture and willing to adopt organic farming practices under the Participatory Guarantee System (PGS-India); individual farmers can also benefit through group participation
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Agriculture (organic farming)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Preference is given to small and marginal farmers, women farmers, and those from SC/ST communities; financial support is provided only to organized farmer groups, not individuals directly; benefits are contingent upon adherence to PGS-India standards and active participation in group activities

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Rs. 50,000 per hectare for a period of three years, out of which 62% (Rs. 31,000) is given as incentive for organic conversion, input production, labeling, packaging, transportation, and marketing, and the remaining 38% (Rs. 19,000) is allocated for mobilization, capacity building, training, exposure visits, documentation, and certification expenses
- Disbursement mode: Installments based on progress and verification
- Duration of benefit: Three years

#### Documents Required
1. Application form
2. Farm history sheet
3. Farmers pledge
4. Mobile number
5. Aadhar number
6. Bank account details (optional)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Form a farmer group or self-help group with minimum 5 members practicing or willing to adopt organic farming.
2. Register the group with the concerned State Nodal Agency or Regional Council under PGS-India.
3. Submit application along with required documents including group details, farm history, and pledge.
4. Undergo verification and approval by the Regional Council.
5. Upon approval, receive user ID and password to upload data on the PGS-India portal.
6. Begin organic farming practices and maintain records for peer review and certification.
7. After completion of conversion period, apply for PGS certification through the group.
