# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping farmers, entrepreneurs, and agri-businesses access government subsidies for agricultural mechanisation under the Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanisation (SMAM). This scheme supports the purchase of machinery and establishment of Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs) through direct benefit transfer. Before we explore whether this fits your needs, I’d like to understand your current farming or agri-business operations to see if SMAM can help you reduce machinery costs and improve productivity.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of agricultural machinery are you currently using or planning to purchase for your farm or agri-business?
2. Are you an individual farmer, part of a society/SHG/FPO, an entrepreneur, or a manufacturer/dealer in the agriculture machinery space?
3. What is your landholding size (in hectares), and have you updated your land records recently?
4. Do you have your Aadhaar number, bank account details, and identity proof ready for registration?
5. Are you considering setting up a Custom Hiring Centre (CHC) or Farm Machinery Bank to rent out equipment to other farmers?
6. If applying for a CHC, do you have an agriculture graduate certificate, domicile proof, and date of birth documentation?
7. For machinery purchases over ₹25 lakh, are you planning to avail a bank loan, and do you have loan sanction documents ready?
8. Have you previously applied for any agricultural subsidy, and what challenges did you face in the application or verification process?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is an individual farmer, entrepreneur, society/SHG/FPO, manufacturer, or dealer
- [ ] Farmer has provided Aadhaar number, land records, bank details, and identity proof
- [ ] For CHC projects: agriculture graduate certificate, domicile, and date of birth proof are available
- [ ] Manufacturer has GST certificate and product test reports
- [ ] Dealer is registered through a manufacturer
- [ ] Correct state, district, block, and village selected during registration
- [ ] All details furnished accurately to avoid debarment during physical verification
- [ ] For CHC projects with loan over ₹25 lakh: bank loan documents are available for upload
- [ ] Applicant understands that subsidy is disbursed only after physical verification by government officers

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you may be eligible for the Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanisation (SMAM), which offers central and state government subsidies for purchasing agricultural machinery or establishing Custom Hiring Centres, with funds transferred directly to your bank account. Let me explain how this scheme works and how we can support your application.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Farmers and agri-entrepreneurs face high upfront costs when purchasing modern agricultural machinery or setting up Custom Hiring Centres, limiting productivity and income diversification. Many struggle with complex subsidy applications, documentation requirements, and delays in fund disbursement due to verification bottlenecks.

**Scheme intro:**
The Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanisation (SMAM), administered by the Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (DA&FW), Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, provides financial subsidies for purchasing agricultural machinery and establishing Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs). The subsidy is shared between central and state governments, calculated based on the final negotiated price of machinery, and disbursed via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the beneficiary’s bank account after physical verification. For CHC projects involving loans over ₹25 lakh, bank loan documents must be uploaded. The scheme also offers non-financial benefits like access to performance-tested machinery, technical support through FMTTIs, training, a multi-lingual mobile app (FARMS), and SMS alerts at key stages.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in farming or agri-business and registered or registerable on the agrimachinery.nic.in portal as a farmer, entrepreneur, society/SHG/FPO, manufacturer, or dealer
- You possess or can obtain Aadhaar, land records, bank details, and identity proof (essential for farmer registration)
- For CHC aspirations: you have or can secure agriculture graduate, domicile, and date of birth certificates
- Your machinery purchase or CHC project aligns with state-specific targets under SMAM’s Annual Action Plan
