### Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanisation (SMAM)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (DA&FW), Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://agrimachinery.nic.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (applications accepted throughout the year as per state-specific timelines and Annual Action Plan submission)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Individual farmers, entrepreneurs, societies/SHGs/FPOs, manufacturers, dealers
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Agriculture
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: 
  - Farmers must provide Aadhaar number, land records, bank details, and identity proof
  - For CHC projects: agriculture graduate certificate, domicile, and date of birth proof required
  - Manufacturers must register with GST certificate and product test reports
  - Dealers must be registered through manufacturers
  - All applicants must select correct state, district, block, and village during registration
  - Accurate details must be furnished to avoid debarment during physical verification

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Shared subsidy between Centre and State governments; amount calculated based on final negotiated price of machinery with centre and state/top-up limits applying
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (via RTGS/NEFT after physical verification and approval)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (per approved application)

#### Documents Required
1. Aadhaar card
2. Record-of-right of land
3. Passport size photograph
4. Copy of bank passbook
5. Copy of ID proof (except Aadhaar)
6. Caste category certificate (for SC/ST/OBC)
7. GST certificate (for Manufacturer/Entrepreneur)
8. PAN (for Entrepreneur/Society/Manufacturer)
9. Product test report (for Manufacturer)
10. Bank loan documents and account details (for back-ended subsidy in CHC)
11. Bill/invoice of machinery
12. Machine details (make, model, serial no., engine no., chassis no.)
13. Joint photographs (farmer, implement, dealer)
14. Agriculture graduate certificate (for CHC applicant)
15. Domicile certificate (for CHC applicant)

#### Application Process
1. Visit https://agrimachinery.nic.in and click on 'Registration' from the navigation bar
2. Select beneficiary type: Farmer, Entrepreneur, Society/SHG/FPO, or Manufacturer
3. Enter required details: For Farmer – Aadhaar/mobile/name, state, district, block, village, land area, address, pincode, bank details, and identity proof; for Entrepreneur/Society – GSTN/PAN, contact details, address; for Manufacturer – GST certificate, product details, test report, bank details
4. Accept terms and conditions and submit to receive User ID and password (sent via SMS)
5. Login to the portal using credentials
6. For machinery purchase: Select implement, check availability, add application, generate PIN, select dealer, negotiate price, upload bill and joint photographs
7. For CHC project: Apply for CHC establishment, upload project details (agriculture graduate, domicile, DOB certificate), await government officer approval, print permit, upload implement bill and loan documents (if applicable)
8. After physical verification by government officer, subsidy is approved and payment file generated for disbursement
9. Track application status via Application Tracking system using reference number
