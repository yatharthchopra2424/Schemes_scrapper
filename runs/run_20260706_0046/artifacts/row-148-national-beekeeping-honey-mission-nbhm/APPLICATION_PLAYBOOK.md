## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a **Project Report is required** as per scheme evidence.  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
- Executive Summary: Applicant profile and beekeeping intent  
- Current Status: Existing colonies, equipment, and skills (if any)  
- Proposed Activity: Number of beekeeping colonies to be established, type (Apis mellifera/Apis cerana), timeline  
- Equipment List: Hives, extractors, processing units, protective gear (with specifications)  
- Empaneled Supplier Details: Names and quotes for all proposed purchases  
- Training & Capacity Building: Details of any prior/relevant training  
- Budget Breakdown: Total cost, applicant share, subsidy sought (50% or 90% as applicable)  
- Timeline: Month-wise implementation plan (procurement, colony setup, first harvest)  
- Sustainability Plan: Maintenance of colonies for minimum 3 years, market linkage strategy  
- Declaration: Statement that no duplicate benefit is claimed and applicant agrees to terms  

> **Note**: Format may vary by district committee; verify with local NACFED office if template is prescribed.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the latest application form from https://nbhm.dac.gov.in or collect from nearest NACFED office  
2. Fill in **block letters** using black or blue ink (if physical) or PDF editor (if digital)  
3. Ensure **name matches exactly** as per PAN/Aadhaar across all fields  
4. Clearly specify beneficiary category (individual/SHG/FPO/etc.) and special category (SC/ST/woman/NE/Himalayan) if applicable for higher subsidy  
5. Provide complete bank account details (account number, IFSC, bank name, branch) for DBT  
6. Describe beekeeping activity in detail: current status, proposed scale, and timeline  
7. Attach **all required documents** in the specified order; use checklist to avoid omissions  
8. Sign and date the form; get authorization signed if applying on behalf of an entity (SHG, FPO, cooperative)  
9. Retain a photocopy/scanned copy of the submitted application for your records  
10. Submit to the designated NACFED office or district-level committee as advised  

> **Sources**: Application process and document requirements from NBHM Key Facts; portal: https://nbhm.dac.gov.in

### Submission Protocol

- Obtain a **signed receipt or acknowledgment** with date, time, and application/reference number  
- Take a **clear screenshot or photo** of the submitted application and acknowledgment for records  
- Store the application reference number securely in the case file  
- Set calendar reminders for follow-up:  
  - Initial follow-up: 15 days after submission  
  - Second follow-up: 30 days after submission  
  - Escalation trigger: 45 days with no response

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify approval letter from NACFED/district committee (check sanctioned subsidy % and amount)  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount matches eligibility (50% general / 90% for SC/ST/women/NE/Himalayan)  
- [ ] Procure approved items **only from NACFED-empaneled suppliers** as per quotations submitted  
- [ ] Maintain detailed records of all procurement bills, vouchers, and payment proofs  
- [ ] Ensure beekeeping colonies are established and maintained for **minimum 3 years** post-subsidy  
- [ ] Submit bills and vouchers for reimbursement to the NACFED office within validity period  
- [ ] Follow up for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) of subsidy to beneficiary’s bank account  
- [ ] Utilization Certificate (UC) may be required; prepare if requested (format to be confirmed with NACFED)  
- [ ] Invoice success fee as per consultancy agreement  
- [ ] Advise beneficiary on record-keeping for future audits and scheme compliance  
- [ ] Inform beneficiary about prohibition of duplicate benefits for same activity under other central/state schemes
