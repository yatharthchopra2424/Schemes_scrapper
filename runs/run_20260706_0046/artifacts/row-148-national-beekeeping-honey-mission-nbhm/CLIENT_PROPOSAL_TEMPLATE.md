## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified the National Beekeeping & Honey Mission (NBHM) — a subsidy scheme administered by the National Agricultural Cooperative Marketing Federation of India Ltd. (NACFED) — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is up to 50% subsidy for general category beneficiaries and up to 90% subsidy for SC/ST, women, and beneficiaries in North Eastern and Himalayan states for beekeeping colonies, equipment, and processing units.
