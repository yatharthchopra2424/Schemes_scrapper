## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual subsidy amount disbursed under NBHM, ensuring clients pay only for realized benefits.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the reimbursement-based model and dependency on NACFED-empaneled suppliers, success fees reflect moderate execution risk; retainers cover documentation and compliance efforts.
3. **Accessibility for Target Beneficiaries**: Tiered pricing aligns with NBHM’s focus on small/marginal farmers, SHGs, FPOs, and tribal communities — primarily falling under Starter and Growth tiers.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: Clear fee structure disclosed upfront, with success fees contingent on successful subsidy disbursement via DBT, reducing client financial risk.
