We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping beekeepers, farmers, and agri-groups access government support for scientific beekeeping and honey production. The National Beekeeping & Honey Mission (NBHM) offers substantial subsidies for setting up colonies, equipment, and processing—especially for small and marginal farmers, women, SC/ST, and those in Northeastern and Himalayan states. I’d like to understand your current beekeeping or agri activities to see if this scheme could support your growth.

### Discovery Questions
- Are you currently engaged in beekeeping, or planning to start beekeeping activities?
- What is your legal structure—individual farmer, SHG, FPO, cooperative, or tribal group?
- Are you based in a Northeastern or Himalayan state, or do you belong to SC/ST or women’s category?
- Do you have proof of identity (Aadhaar/PAN/Voter ID) and address ready?
- Do you own or lease land where beekeeping activities will be undertaken?
- Have you prepared a project report outlining your beekeeping activity plan?
- Are you willing to procure equipment only from NACFED-empaneled suppliers?
- Can you commit to maintaining beekeeping colonies for at least 3 years after receiving subsidy?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Individual farmer, beekeeper, SHG, JLG, FPO, cooperative, or tribal community
- [ ] Engaged in or willing to take up beekeeping activities
- [ ] Preference given to small/marginal farmers, SC/ST, women, or beneficiaries in NE/Himalayan states
- [ ] Proof of identity (Aadhaar/Voter ID/PAN) available
- [ ] Proof of address available
- [ ] Bank account details (passbook/cancelled cheque) available
- [ ] Land ownership/lease document (if applicable)
- [ ] Project report detailing beekeeping activity plan prepared
- [ ] Willing to procure from NACFED-empaneled suppliers
- [ ] Able to maintain colonies for minimum 3 years post-subsidy
- [ ] Not availing duplicate benefits under other central/state schemes for same activity

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you may be eligible for the National Beekeeping & Honey Mission (NBHM), which offers up to 50% subsidy (up to 90% for SC/ST, women, and NE/Himalayan beneficiaries) for beekeeping colonies, equipment, and processing units. Let me share how this scheme works and how we’ve helped similar beneficiaries access this support.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many beekeepers and farmers struggle with the high upfront costs of setting up scientific beekeeping colonies, purchasing equipment like hives and extractors, and establishing processing units for honey and beehive products. Without financial support, scaling operations or adopting improved technologies remains challenging, especially for small and marginal producers.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Beekeeping & Honey Mission (NBHM), implemented by the National Agricultural Cooperative Marketing Federation of India Ltd. (NACFED) under the Ministry of Agriculture, provides subsidy-based financial assistance of up to 50% for general category beneficiaries and up to 90% for SC/ST, women, and beneficiaries in Northeastern and Himalayan states. Support covers beekeeping colonies, equipment (hives, extractors, processing units), training, infrastructure, market development, branding, and quality certification—disbursed via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) after procurement and bill submission.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in or willing to take up beekeeping activities (eligible beneficiary type)
- You belong to [SC/ST/women/NE/Himalayan state/small/marginal farmer] category, qualifying for higher subsidy tiers
- You have or can prepare a project report and procure from NACFED-empaneled suppliers
- You can maintain colonies for minimum 3 years post-subsidy (as required)
- You are not accessing duplicate benefits for the same activity under other schemes

**Proof:**
"We've helped [N] similar beekeepers, SHGs, and FPOs get approved for NBHM. [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]"

**Next step:**
We offer a free, no-obligation eligibility check. If you’re eligible, we’ll guide you through document preparation and application—charging only a success fee upon approval. Shall we schedule a 30-minute document review to get started?
