### National Beekeeping & Honey Mission (NBHM)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Agricultural Cooperative Marketing Federation of India Ltd. (NACFED)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nbhm.dac.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Individual farmers, beekeepers, self-help groups (SHGs), joint liability groups (JLGs), farmer producer organizations (FPOs), cooperatives, tribal communities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Beekeeping and honey production
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Preference given to small and marginal farmers, SC/ST, and women beneficiaries; beneficiaries must maintain beekeeping colonies for minimum 3 years post-subsidy; duplicate benefits under other central/state schemes for same activity not permitted

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to 50% subsidy for general category beneficiaries; up to 90% subsidy for SC/ST, women, and beneficiaries in North Eastern and Himalayan states
- Disbursement mode: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) after reimbursement
- Duration of benefit: One-time (subject to fund availability)

#### Documents Required
1. Application form (available at NACFED offices or website)
2. Proof of identity (Aadhaar/Voter ID/PAN)
3. Proof of address
4. Bank account details (passbook/cancelled cheque)
5. Land ownership/lease document (if applicable)
6. Project report detailing beekeeping activity plan
7. Quotations from empaneled suppliers for equipment/colonies
8. Training completion certificate (if training was attended)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the nearest NACFED office or designated implementing agency in the state.
2. Submit application form along with required documents.
3. Application is scrutinized by the district-level committee.
4. Upon approval, financial assistance is sanctioned.
5. Beneficiary procures approved items from empaneled suppliers.
6. Submit bills and vouchers for reimbursement.
7. Subsidy amount is transferred directly to beneficiary's bank account via DBT.
