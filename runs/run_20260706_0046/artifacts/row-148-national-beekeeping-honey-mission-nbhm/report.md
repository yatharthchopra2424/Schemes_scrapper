## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **National Beekeeping & Honey Mission (NBHM)** is a centrally sponsored subsidy scheme implemented by the **National Agricultural Cooperative Marketing Federation of India Ltd. (NACFED)** under the Ministry of Agriculture. With a total fund size of **₹500 Crore**, the scheme operates on a **rolling basis** with applications accepted throughout the year subject to fund availability. The primary objective is to promote holistic growth of the beekeeping sector for income and employment generation, with special focus on small and marginal farmers, tribals, women, and SC/ST beneficiaries through scientific beekeeping practices.

### Objectives
The scheme aims to achieve the following objectives:
- Promote holistic growth of beekeeping sector for income and employment generation
- Support small and marginal farmers, tribals, and women through scientific beekeeping
- Enhance honey production and productivity through adoption of improved technologies
- Develop infrastructure for collection, processing, storage, and marketing of honey and beehive products
- Promote value addition and diversification of beehive products (wax, pollen, propolis, royal jelly, venom)
- Strengthen market linkages and brand promotion for domestic and export markets
- Provide training and capacity building to beekeepers and stakeholders
- Encourage pollination support services to enhance agricultural and horticultural crop yields

| Tribal communities | ✅ Yes | High preference | Especially in NE and Himalayan states |
| Women beneficiaries | ✅ Yes | High preference | Individual or group-based |

> **Key Eligibility Notes**: Preference is explicitly given to small and marginal farmers, SC/ST categories, women beneficiaries, and those located in North Eastern and Himalayan states. Duplicate benefits under other central or state schemes for the same beekeeping activity are not permitted.

### Financial Support & Benefits
| **Beneficiary Category** | **Subsidy Rate** | **Maximum Assistance** | **Coverage** |
|--------------------------|------------------|------------------------|--------------|
| General Category | Up to 50% | Not specified (50% of project cost) | Beekeeping colonies, equipment, processing units |
| SC/ST Beneficiaries | Up to 90% | Not specified (90% of project cost) | Beekeeping colonies, equipment, processing units |
| Women Beneficiaries | Up to 90% | Not specified (90% of project cost) | Beekeeping colonies, equipment, processing units |
| NE & Himalayan States | Up to 90% | Not specified (90% of project cost) | Beekeeping colonies, equipment, processing units |

**Comprehensive Benefits Covered**:
- Financial assistance for setting up beekeeping colonies
- Procurement of equipment (hives, extractors, processing units)
- Training and capacity building programs
- Infrastructure support for honey processing and storage
- Market development support
- Branding and packaging assistance
- Quality certification support
- Technical guidance on scientific beekeeping practices

> **Critical Financial Caveats**:
> - Subsidy is **reimbursement-based** – beneficiaries must procure items first and submit bills for reimbursement
> - Assistance is subject to availability of funds under the scheme (₹500 Cr total allocation)
> - Beneficiaries must procure items **only from NACFED-empaneled suppliers**
> - Beneficiaries must maintain beekeeping colonies for a **minimum of 3 years post-subsidy**
> - Duplicate benefits under other central/state schemes for the same activity are **not permitted**

### Geographic Scope & Implementation
- **Geographic Coverage**: Pan-India
- **Implementing Agency**: NACFED (National Agricultural Cooperative Marketing Federation of India Ltd.)
- **Nodal Agency Role**: NACFED provides assistance via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mode
- **State-Level Implementation**: Through designated NACFED offices or state-level implementing agencies
- **Special Focus**: North Eastern and Himalayan states receive enhanced subsidy rates (up to 90%)
