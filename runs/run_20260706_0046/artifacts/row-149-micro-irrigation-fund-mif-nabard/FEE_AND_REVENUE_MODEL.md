## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial outlay and long-term impact of MIF-supported micro-irrigation projects, ensuring consultant compensation aligns with client outcomes.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Returns**: Success fees account for the scheme’s moderate risk profile, including variable processing times (typically 6–12 months based on NABARD project cycles) and dependency on technical feasibility and state-level coordination.
3. **Transparency & Predictability**: Retainers cover initial project assessment, DPR preparation, and submission support, providing predictable cash flow during the lengthy approval lag.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Micro Irrigation Fund (MIF) involves high-value, infrastructure-intensive projects with extended timelines due to technical appraisal, environmental clearances, and multi-stakeholder coordination. Success is evidenced by NABARD’s disbursement of over ₹10,000 Cr since inception, indicating strong eventual approval rates for well-prepared proposals, but necessitating a longer revenue lag.
