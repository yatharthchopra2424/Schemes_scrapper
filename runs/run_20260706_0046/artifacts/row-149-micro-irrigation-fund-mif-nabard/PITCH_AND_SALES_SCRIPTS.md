## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping agribusinesses and farmers access government support for modern irrigation solutions. The Micro Irrigation Fund (MIF) – NABARD is designed specifically to promote water-use efficiency in agriculture by providing financial assistance for drip and sprinkler irrigation systems. I’d like to understand your current farming operations to see if this scheme could help reduce your water costs and improve yield sustainability.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of crops do you cultivate, and what is the total area under irrigation?
- When was your farming enterprise or agribusiness registered or incorporated?
- Are you currently using any micro-irrigation systems (drip or sprinkler), or relying on traditional flood irrigation?
- What is your primary source of water for irrigation (e.g., groundwater, canal, rainwater harvesting)?
- Have you previously applied for any NABARD or central/state government agriculture subsidy schemes?
- Are you an individual farmer, part of a Farmer Producer Organisation (FPO), cooperative, or agri-entrepreneur?
- What are your main challenges related to water availability, irrigation costs, or crop yield consistency?
- Are you planning to expand or upgrade your irrigation infrastructure in the next 6–12 months?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Beneficiary is an individual farmer, Farmer Producer Organisation (FPO), cooperative society, or agri-entrepreneur  
- [ ] Entity is engaged in agriculture and allied activities  
- [ ] Proposed project involves installation of drip or sprinkler irrigation systems  
- [ ] Landholding or operational area is within eligible limits (as per NABARD guidelines)  
- [ ] Beneficiary has not exceeded subsidy caps under similar central/state schemes for the same purpose  
- [ ] Project is technically feasible and recommended by the State Agriculture/Horticulture Department or accredited agency  
- [ ] Beneficiary possesses clear land title or lease agreement for the proposed area (if applicable)  
- [ ] Project cost falls within the permissible unit cost norms for micro-irrigation components

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Problem framing:
Farmers across India face declining groundwater levels, high irrigation costs, and inconsistent crop yields due to reliance on flood irrigation. Water scarcity is increasing input costs and reducing profitability, especially for small and marginal farmers who lack access to modern irrigation technology.

### Scheme intro:
The Micro Irrigation Fund (MIF) – NABARD is a dedicated fund created by NABARD (National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development) to provide concessional loans to state governments for promoting micro-irrigation (drip and sprinkler systems) across the country. While the fund is routed through state agencies, individual farmers, FPOs, cooperatives, and agri-entrepreneurs benefit from subsidised installation costs, with effective financial assistance ranging from 55% to 95% of the unit cost, depending on beneficiary category and state-specific top-ups.

### Why they qualify (personalised):
- You are engaged in agriculture and cultivate crops suitable for drip or sprinkler irrigation (e.g., vegetables, fruits, sugarcane, cotton)  
- Your farming entity (individual/FPO/cooperative/agri-business) is eligible to receive support under MIF guidelines  
- You are planning to install or upgrade to micro-irrigation systems on your farmland  
- Your project will be implemented through the State Agriculture or Horticulture Department, which channels MIF funds
