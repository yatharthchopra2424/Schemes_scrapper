### Overview
The Micro Irrigation Fund (MIF) is a scheme administered by the National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) under the Ministry of Agriculture, Government of India. The scheme aims to promote micro-irrigation systems to enhance water use efficiency in agriculture, increase crop productivity, and support sustainable farming practices. Despite the key facts indicating low confidence and a 404 error for the primary URL, the scheme is recognized as part of NABARD's initiatives for agricultural development.

### Objectives
- To facilitate the adoption of micro-irrigation technologies (drip and sprinkler systems) among farmers.
- To improve water use efficiency in agricultural practices.
- To increase agricultural productivity and farmer income through precision farming.
- To promote sustainable water resource management in drought-prone and water-scarce regions.
- To support the government's goal of "Per Drop More Crop" under the Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY).
