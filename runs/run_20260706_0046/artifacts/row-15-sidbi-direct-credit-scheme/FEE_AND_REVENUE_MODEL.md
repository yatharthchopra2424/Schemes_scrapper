## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Pricing**: Fees are calibrated to the financial benefit delivered—here, term loans up to ₹50 crore (80% of project cost), machinery loans up to ₹3 crore, and working capital finance—ensuring consultant compensation reflects the scale of credit accessed.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Returns**: Success fees reflect processing timelines (e.g., 48-hour sanction for some products) and high approval likelihood due to SIDBI’s structured eligibility and digital processing, reducing client risk.
3. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees disclosed upfront; retainers cover eligibility assessment, product selection, and documentation support, while success fees are contingent on loan sanction.
4. **Scalable Engagement Model**: Tiered pricing accommodates diverse MSME profiles—from micro enterprises seeking GST Sahay (up to ₹50 lakh) to scale units accessing project funding (up to ₹50 crore)—with custom enterprise solutions for group-level financing needs.
