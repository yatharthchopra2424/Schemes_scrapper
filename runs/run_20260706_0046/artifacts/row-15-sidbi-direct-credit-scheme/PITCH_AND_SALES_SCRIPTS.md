# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping MSMEs access tailored financing solutions from SIDBI, India’s principal financial institution for micro, small, and medium enterprises. Whether you're looking to expand operations, upgrade machinery, or manage working capital, I’d like to understand your current needs to see if SIDBI’s Direct Credit Scheme could be a strategic fit for your business.

### Discovery Questions
1. How long has your business been in operation?  
2. Are you registered under Udyam and GST?  
3. What is the primary purpose for seeking financing—project funding, machinery purchase, working capital, or green finance?  
4. Do you have audited financial statements and cash profit in recent years?  
5. Have you had any defaults to banks or financial institutions in the past?  
6. What is your approximate annual turnover or project cost for which you need funding?  
7. Are you seeking collateral-free financing or willing to provide security like a fixed deposit?  
8. Do you have a project report, machinery quotation, or invoices ready for submission?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business operational for minimum 2 years (project funding) or 3 years (machinery loan)  
- [ ] Udyam Registration Certificate available  
- [ ] GST Registration active  
- [ ] No defaults to banks/FIs  
- [ ] Audited accounts available  
- [ ] Cash profit in recent financial years  
- [ ] CIBIL score available (if applicable)  
- [ ] Required documents identifiable (e.g., project report, invoices, quotations)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like you may be eligible for SIDBI’s Direct Credit Scheme, which offers financing up to Rs. 50 crore for project funding or up to Rs. 3 crore for machinery loans with flexible tenures. Let me walk you through how this scheme works and how it could support your growth.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**  
Many MSMEs struggle to access timely, affordable credit for expansion, technology upgradation, or working capital due to stringent bank requirements, long processing times, or lack of collateral—hindering growth and competitiveness.

**Scheme intro:**  
The SIDBI Direct Credit Scheme, offered by the Small Industries Development Bank of India, provides term loans for project funding up to Rs. 50 crore (80% of project cost), machinery loans up to Rs. 3 crore (up to 100% financing with 25% FD security), working capital loans, and green finance options. Repayment tenures range from 30–60 days (Gig Flexi) to up to 7 years, with moratoriums up to 2 years. Interest rates are MCLR-linked and competitive.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**  
- Your business has been operational for over 2–3 years, meeting the minimum vintage requirement  
- You hold valid Udyam and GST registration, which are mandatory  
- You have audited accounts and no defaults to banks/FIs, satisfying key eligibility criteria  
- Depending on your need, you may qualify for project funding, machinery finance, or invoice-based financing
