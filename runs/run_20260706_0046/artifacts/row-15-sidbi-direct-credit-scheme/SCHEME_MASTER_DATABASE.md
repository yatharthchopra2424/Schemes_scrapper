### SIDBI Direct Credit Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://sidbi.in/home-product
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: MSME units
- Age of business: Minimum 2 years for project funding, 3 years for machinery loan (varies by product)
- Sector: Manufacturing or service sector
- Revenue cap: Not explicitly stated; eligibility based on audited accounts and cash profit
- Employee count: Not mentioned
- Other: Mandatory Udyam & GST registration; no defaults to banks/FIs; audited accounts; cash profit in recent financial years; specific products may require validated defence orders, GST returns, ITRs, bank statements, and CIBIL scores

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support includes term loans for project funding up to Rs. 50 crore (80% of project cost), machinery loans up to Rs. 3 crore (up to 100% financing with 25% FD security), working capital loans, green finance loans, invoice-based financing up to Rs. 50 lakh (GST Sahay) or Rs. 2 lakh (FPS Sahay, JAKs), venture debt financing Rs. 10-15 crore, seed funding through incubators (equity investment)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit to bank account
- Duration of benefit: One-time loan with repayment tenures ranging from 30 to 60 days (Gig Flexi) to up to 7 years (project funding) with moratoriums up to 2 years

#### Documents Required
1. Udyam Registration Certificate
2. GST Registration
3. Bank Statements
4. ITR (Income Tax Returns)
5. GST Returns
6. CIBIL Score
7. Audited Accounts
8. Project Report (for project funding)
9. Purchase/Sales Invoice (for invoice-based financing)
10. Quotation for machinery (for machinery loan)
11. Land and building documents (if applicable)
12. Details of collateral (if required)
13. Authorization letter from signatory
14. NACH mandate
15. Digital document execution

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the suitable loan product (e.g., Project Loan, Machinery Loan, Working Capital, Green Finance)
2. Check eligibility criteria specific to the product
3. Apply online via the SIDBI portal or through referral partners
4. Complete registration and KYC process
5. Submit required documents (e.g., GST returns, ITRs, bank statements, Udyam registration, project details)
6. Undergo sanction and acceptance of loan terms
7. Execute digital documents and NACH mandate
8. Receive disbursement directly to bank account
9. Repay principal along with applicable interest on due date
