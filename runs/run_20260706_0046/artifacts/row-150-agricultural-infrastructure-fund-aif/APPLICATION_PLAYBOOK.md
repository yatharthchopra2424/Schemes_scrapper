# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Beneficiary type is one of: Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS), Marketing Cooperative Societies, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), Self Help Groups (SHGs), Farmers, Joint Liability Groups (JLGs), Multipurpose Cooperative Societies, Agri-entrepreneurs, Startups, Central/State agency or Local Body sponsored Public Private Partnership Projects, State Agencies, Agricultural Produce Market Committees (Mandis), National & State Federations of Cooperatives, Federations of FPOs, Federations of Self Help Groups (SHGs)
- [ ] Project falls under eligible categories: Post-Harvest Management Projects (supply chain services, warehouse & silos, cold stores, packaging units, assaying units, sorting & grading units, logistic facilities, ripening chambers, farm residue/waste management, primary processing, integrated primary & secondary processing) OR Viable Farming Assets (organic inputs production, CBG plant, bio stimulant units, smart/precision agriculture infrastructure, nursery, tissue culture, seed processing, custom hiring center, farm/harvest automation, decentralized solar power plants (PM-KUSUM A), standalone solar pumping system (PM-KUSUM B), solarization of agri-pump (PM-KUSUM C), integrated spirulina units, sericulture processing, honey processing, plant quarantine units, supply chain infrastructure for export clusters, PPP projects for farming assets/post-harvest management, hydroponic/mushroom/vertical/aeroponic farming, poly house/greenhouse, logistics facilities, tractor)
- [ ] Project does NOT involve standalone secondary processing infrastructure (ineligible)
- [ ] For individual farmers/agri-entrepreneurs/startups: maximum 25 projects allowed across different LGD codes (this limit does NOT apply to state agencies, federations of cooperatives, FPOs, or SHGs)
- [ ] Project location must have a distinct LGD (Local Government Directory) code; each project must be in a location with a separate LGD code
- [ ] APMCs: multiple projects of different infrastructure types allowed within designated market area
- [ ] Promoter’s contribution: minimum 10% of project cost mandatory
- [ ] Loan amount: up to ₹2 crore per project per location eligible for interest subvention (loan can be higher, but subvention capped at ₹2 crore)
- [ ] Repayment period: maximum 7 years including moratorium of up to 2 years
- [ ] Interest subvention: 3% per annum for up to 7 years, limited to ₹2 crore per project
- [ ] Credit guarantee: available for loans up to ₹2 crore under CGTMSE, government bears the fee
- [ ] Convergence: eligible for convergence with MIDH, PMFME, SMAM, GOBAR-Dhan, PMKSY, AMI, PACS as MSC, RKVY, PMEGP, PM-KUSUM (A, B & C)

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN card
- Aadhaar card
- Bank statements (last 1 year)
- ITR (last 3 years, if available)
- GST certificate (if applicable)
- Udyam registration (if MSME)
- Certificate of Incorporation/Registration (based on entity type)
- Address proof (residence and business office)
- Identity proof (Voter ID/PAN/Aadhaar/Driving license)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Bank’s loan application form / Customer Request Letter for AIF Loan duly filled and signed
- Passport size photographs of promoter/partners/director
- Proof of Registration: Article of Association (Company), Certificate of Registration of Firm (Partnership), Udyog Aadhar/DIC certificate (MSMEs)
- Audited Balance Sheet of last 3 Years (if available)
- Land ownership / lease records
- ROC Search Report of the Company
- KYC documents of promoter/firm/company
- Repayment track record of existing loans (Loan Statement)
- Net Worth Statements of promoter
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) – required as per scheme guidelines
- Local authority permissions, layout plans/estimates, building sanction (as applicable)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format
- File size per document: maximum 5 MB
- File names must match document type (e.g., PAN_Card.pdf, Bank_Statement_2024.pdf)
- Self-attested copies acceptable where notarization not specified
- Documents in regional languages must be accompanied by certified English translation
- Photographs: recent passport size with white background
- Bank statements: must show account holder name matching applicant
- Land documents: must show clear title or lease agreement
- DPR: must follow template provided on AIF portal (agriinfra.dac.gov.in)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Required.** The DPR is mandatory for AIF loan applications. Use the official template available at:  
https://agriinfra.dac.gov.in (navigate to Beneficiary Corner → DPR Template – English/Hindi)

**Standard DPR Sections:**
1. Details of the Applicant (name, constitution, registration no., GST, PAN, date of establishment, addresses, PIN code, lead promoter SC/ST/Woman/Minority status)
2. Contact details of Applicant/Promoter(s)/Partner(s)/Director(s)/Members
3. Details of Promoter(s)/Partner(s)/Director(s)/Members (Aadhaar, PAN, academic/technical qualification, net worth, DIN, credit rating, shareholding date, profit sharing ratio)
4. Relative experience of Applicant/Promoter(s)/Partner(s)/Director(s)/Members (turnover year-wise, supporting documents)
5. Details of Existing Banking and Credit facilities (facility type, bank/branch, limits, outstanding, securities, interest rate, repayment terms, purpose)
6. Details of GST Returns submitted or registration status
7. Project Details (objective, category of infrastructure as per scheme, operating model)
8. Land Details (area, legal title/possession, lease period, coordinates, CLU, road connectivity, water/power availability)
9. Proposed Facilities (type, number of units, total capacity, days of operation/year)
10. Technologies to be used / alternative technologies
11. Flow chart of activity process
12. Detailed timeline for construction and proposed date for commencement of operation
13. Proposed Project Financials (estimated project cost, means of finance, promoter’s equity, capital subsidy, loan)
14. Basic Revenue Projections (Year 1–5: turnover, cost of operations, gross profit, EBITDA, profit before/after taxation)
15. Financial Parameters (IRR, DSCR, BEP, Debt-Equity Ratio, Fixed Assets Coverage Ratio)
16. Credit Facilities proposed (fund-based: term loan, working capital; non-fund based)
17. Collateral Security proposed (for loans above ₹2 crore)
18. Repayment Schedule (including moratorium period)
19. Details of Statutory/other approvals/registrations (status)
20. Availability of Raw Materials in Catchment Area (adequate volume, wider mix, days of operation)
21. Details of Catchment Area (location, village/dist/APMC, commodities, quantities to be sourced per annum)
22. Details of existing demand and marketing arrangements (e-trading, leasing with FCI/CWC/SWC/e-commerce/retailers for assured cash flows)
23. Employment Generation projections (direct, contractual, indirect employment)
24. Details of renewable/alternate energy sources (solar, etc.) for operating the project
25. Details of pollution issues and adoption of modern technology to reduce carbon footprint and increase operational efficiency
26. List of Manufacturers/Suppliers of P&M (with quotations)
27. Declaration: Applicant certifies information is true and nothing material concealed; false information may lead to rejection and ineligibility under AIF

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Register as a beneficiary on the AIF portal (https://agriinfra.dac.gov.in) via the “Beneficiary Registration” section.
2. Prepare the DPR using the official template downloaded from the portal.
3. Approach a participating lending institution (Commercial Banks, Cooperative Banks, RRBs, Small Finance Banks, NCDC, NBFCs) with the DPR.
4. The lending institution processes the loan application under the AIF scheme.
5. Ensure all fields in the application form are filled accurately – no blank fields unless marked “Not Applicable”.
6. Upload all required documents in the specified format (PDF, max 5 MB per file) via the portal or as directed by the lending institution.
7. Verify that the promoter’s contribution is at least 10% of the project cost and clearly documented.
8. Confirm that the loan amount requested does not exceed the lender’s sanctioned limit under AIF guidelines.
9. Double-check that interest subvention and credit guarantee claims are correctly referenced in the application.
10. Save a screenshot of the submitted application and note the application/reference number.
11. Ensure the application is submitted before any portal maintenance window (check portal notices – e.g., scheduled for 06 Feb 2026, 10 AM–11 AM).
12. Maintain communication with the lending institution for updates on sanction and disbursement.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed application via the AIF portal (https://agriinfra.dac.gov.in) or through the participating lending institution as per process.
- Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing successful submission.
- Save and store the application/reference number in the case file.
- Set follow-up reminders:  
  - Day 3: Confirm receipt with lending institution  
  - Day 7: Check application status on portal or with lending institution  
  - Day 14: Follow up if no update received  
  - Day 21: Escalate if still no response  
  - Day 30: Final follow-up before considering escalation

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval/sanction letter from the lending institution
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned loan amount and interest subvention eligibility (up to ₹2 crore at 3% p.a. for 7 years)
- [ ] Verify credit guarantee coverage approval (for loans up to ₹2 crore, government bears CGTMSE fee)
- [ ] Ensure loan disbursement is staged as per project milestones
- [ ] Monitor utilization of funds against the approved project cost and DPR
- [ ] Prepare and submit Utilization Certificate (UC) as per lending institution and AIF portal requirements
- [ ] Track interest subvention claims submission via the AIF portal (https://agriinfra.dac.gov.in) under “Claim” section
- [ ] Ensure timely repayment as per the agreed schedule (including moratorium period)
- [ ] Maintain all records for audit and compliance purposes
- [ ] Confirm project completion and geo-tagging of assets on the AIF portal dashboard
- [ ] Calculate and verify success fee as per consultancy agreement (to be filled by consultant)
