## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered (interest subvention up to ₹2 crore per project).
2. Tiered retainer reflects complexity of documentation and coordination with lending institutions.
3. Success fee capped at 8% to ensure affordability for MSME/startup clients accessing loans up to ₹2 crore.
4. Scheme-specific note: Processing time averages 3–6 months from application to disbursement (per evidence), introducing a revenue lag; success fees are invoiced post-disbursement upon verified interest subvention claims.
