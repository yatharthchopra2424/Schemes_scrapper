# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/We specialize in helping agri-entrepreneurs, FPOs, and agribusinesses access government funding post-harvest management and viable farming assets. The Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF) offers interest subvention and credit guarantee support to reduce your cost of capital and de-risk your investment. I’d like to understand your project plans and see if this scheme aligns with your goals.)

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of agriculture infrastructure project are you planning (e.g., cold storage, warehouse, processing unit, solar plant under PM-KUSUM, logistics facility)?
2. Have you already incorporated or registered your entity (FPO, PACS, agri-entrepreneur, startup, etc.), and if so, when?
3. Are you seeking a loan for a single project location, or do you plan to set up infrastructure in multiple villages/towns?
4. What is the estimated total project cost, and how much loan financing are you targeting?
5. Do you have land ownership or lease records for the proposed project site?
6. Have you approached any bank for financing yet, or are you planning to apply through a participating lending institution under AIF?
7. Are you aware of the interest subvention benefit (3% p.a. up to ₹2 crore) and credit guarantee coverage available under this scheme?
8. Do you have a Detailed Project Report (DPR) prepared, or would you need support in creating one using the AIF template?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Beneficiary type is eligible (Farmer, FPO, PACS, SHG, Agri-entrepreneur, Startup, etc.)
- [ ] Project falls under eligible categories (Post-Harvest Management or Viable Farming Assets)
- [ ] Loan amount per location does not exceed ₹2 crore for interest subvention cap (though higher loan amounts possible)
- [ ] For private entities (farmer/agri entrepreneur/startup): maximum 25 projects across different LGD codes
- [ ] Project is not standalone secondary processing infrastructure
- [ ] Promoter’s contribution is at least 10% of project cost
- [ ] Land ownership/lease documents are available or can be arranged
- [ ] Identity, address, and registration proofs are accessible
- [ ] Bank statement, IT returns, audited balance sheets (if available) can be provided
- [ ] GST certificate (if applicable) is ready
- [ ] Project is viable and bankable (to avoid NPA risk)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like your [project type, e.g., cold storage unit] in [location] could be a strong fit for the Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF). This scheme offers interest subvention of 3% per annum (saving up to ₹60 lakhs over 7 years on a ₹2 crore loan) and credit guarantee coverage—effectively reducing your borrowing cost and risk. Let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Agri-entrepreneurs and farmers face high interest costs and collateral requirements when financing infrastructure like cold chains, warehouses, or processing units. This limits investment in post-harvest infrastructure, leading to avoidable losses, weak market linkages, and lower price realization. Many viable projects stall due to perceived risk by banks or lack of affordable credit.

**Scheme intro:**
The Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF), launched by the Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India, is a ₹1 lakh crore financing facility offering medium- to long-term loans through participating banks. It provides interest subvention of 3% per annum (capped at ₹2 crore per project) for up to 7 years and credit guarantee coverage for loans up to ₹2 crore under CGTMSE, with the government bearing the fee. The scheme supports convergence with PM-KUSUM, PMFME, SMAM, and other central schemes.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in [agri-entrepreneurship/FPO farming/etc.], which is an explicitly eligible beneficiary under AIF
- Your project involves [warehouse/cold storage/processing/solar plant], which falls under eligible Post-Harvest Management or Viable Farming Assets
- You are prepared to contribute at least 10% promoter’s equity, meeting the mandatory requirement
- You have or can arrange land documents, identity proof, and registration certificates as required
