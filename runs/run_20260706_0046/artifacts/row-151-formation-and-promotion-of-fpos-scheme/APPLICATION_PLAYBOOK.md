## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is a Farmer Producer Organization (FPO) registered under the Companies Act, 2013, or as a Cooperative Society under State Cooperative Societies Act, or as a Producer Company under Part IXA of the Companies Act, 1956  
- [ ] The FPO has a minimum of 300 farmer members (small and marginal farmers)  
- [ ] The FPO is engaged in agricultural and allied activities (including horticulture, livestock, fisheries, forestry, and agro-processing)  
- [ ] The FPO has been in operation for at least 1 year as of the date of application  
- [ ] The FPO has a valid PAN and GSTIN (if applicable)  
- [ ] The FPO has a bank account in its name  
- [ ] The FPO is not a defunct entity and has not been blacklisted by any government agency  
- [ ] The FPO is not receiving similar financial assistance for the same purpose from any other Central or State Government scheme

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration (Companies Act / Cooperative Societies Act / Producer Company)  
- PAN Card of the FPO  
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- Audited Financial Statements (ITR) for the last 2 financial years  
- Bank Statement (last 6 months) in the name of the FPO  
- Udyam Registration Certificate (if applicable for MSME classification)  
- Board Resolution authorizing the application and designation of signatory  
- Cancelled cheque or bank passbook copy showing account details  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- List of farmer members with their landholding details (self-certified by FPO)  
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining business plan, activities, and expected outcomes  
- Proof of engagement in agricultural and allied activities (e.g., procurement/sales records, farmer training records)  
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the concerned State Department of Agriculture/Horticulture (if required)  
- Self-declaration that the FPO is not availing similar benefits under any other Central/State scheme for the same purpose  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)  
- File names must follow the format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_CaseID_Date.pdf` (e.g., `FPOFormation_IncorporationCert_CASE001_20240615.pdf`)  
- All certificates must be valid and not expired (especially GST, Udyam, and registration certificates)  
- PAN, GSTIN, and Udyam numbers must match across all documents  
- Financial statements must be audited and signed by a Chartered Accountant  
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in regional language, a certified translation must be attached  
- Board Resolution must be on company letterhead, signed by at least two directors, and dated

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
✅ **Required**  
The Detailed Project Report (DPR) is mandatory for the Formation and Promotion of FPOs Scheme. It must include the following sections:  
- Executive Summary  
- Background and Objectives of the FPO  
- Member Profile (number, landholding, socio-economic status)  
- Business Model and Value Chain Activities  
- Marketing and Sales Strategy  
- Financial Projections (5-year)  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
- Implementation Timeline  
- Expected Outcomes (income enhancement, employment generation, market linkages)  
- Annexures: Member list, land details, bank statements, board resolution  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal via the SFAC website (verify current URL with nodal officer, as https://sfacindia.com/FPO returned a 404 error)  
2. Select "Formation and Promotion of FPOs Scheme" from the list of active schemes  
3. Fill all fields in Block Letters; do not leave any mandatory field blank  
4. Enter the exact name as per the Certificate of Incorporation/Registration  
5. Ensure PAN, GSTIN, and Udyam numbers are entered correctly and match supporting documents  
6. Upload documents in PDF format only, each under 5 MB  
7. Select the correct FPO registration type (Company/Cooperative/Producer Company) from the dropdown  
8. Declare that the FPO is not receiving similar benefits from any other Central/State scheme for the same purpose  
9. Preview the entire form before final submission  
10. Do not submit until all documents are attached and declarations are signed
