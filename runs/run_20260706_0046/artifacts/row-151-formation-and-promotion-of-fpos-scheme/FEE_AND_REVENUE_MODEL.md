## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are tied to the actual financial benefit received by the client, ensuring our interests are aligned with client outcomes.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s low confidence rating and lack of verifiable financial benefit data in the evidence, success fees are set at the lower end of industry ranges to reflect higher uncertainty in outcome realization.
3. **Transparency**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover initial eligibility assessment and application preparation, while success fees are payable only upon verified fund disbursement.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The *Formation and Promotion of FPOs Scheme* targets Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), which are collective entities of farmers — not commercial MSMEs, startups, or large enterprises. The scheme’s eligibility criteria (based on agricultural producer collectives) do not align with any of the firm’s standard client tiers (Starter to Enterprise), which are designed for profit-driven business entities. Therefore, this scheme is not applicable to the firm’s current service offerings unless the firm develops a dedicated agricultural collectivization practice. Processing time and success rates are not available in the evidence, further increasing risk profile uncertainty.
