## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping farmer producer organisations (FPOs) access government support for formation and promotion. Many agricultural collectives struggle with the initial setup costs and technical guidance needed to become viable entities. I’d like to understand if the Formation and Promotion of FPOs Scheme could support your group’s goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is the primary agricultural activity or commodity focus of your producer group?
2. How many farmers are currently part of your collective, and is it registered as a legal entity?
3. When was your group formally established or incorporated?
4. Are you seeking support for the formation of a new FPO or the strengthening/promotion of an existing one?
5. What challenges have you faced in accessing inputs, credit, or markets as a group?
6. Do you have a business plan or vision for how the FPO will improve members’ incomes?
7. Are you working with any promoting institution (like NABARD, SFAC, or a NGOs) currently?
8. What is your estimated annual turnover or the value of produce handled by the group?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Group is engaged in agricultural and allied activities  
- [ ] Minimum 50 farmer members (for general category) or 30 (for difficult/hilly areas or SC/ST)  
- [ ] Registered as a legal entity (Producer Company, Cooperative Society, or Section 8 Company)  
- [ ] Incorporated within the last 10 years (for new FPO formation support)  
- [ ] Promoted by a recognised Promoting Institution (PI)  
- [ ] Has a viable business plan focused on value addition, processing, or market linkage  
- [ ] Not receiving duplicate support for the same purpose from another central scheme  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your group is actively working toward collective marketing and could benefit from structured support. The Formation and Promotion of FPOs Scheme provides financial and technical assistance specifically for FPOs like yours—offering up to ₹18 lakh per FPO over three years to cover formation and promotion costs. Let me explain how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many farmer collectives struggle to transition from informal groups to sustainable business entities due to lack of capital for registration, legal compliance, infrastructure, and technical know-how. This limits their ability to access credit, negotiate better prices, and add value to their produce.

**Scheme intro:**
The Formation and Promotion of FPOs Scheme, administered by the Small Farmers' Agribusiness Consortium (SFAC) under the Ministry of Agriculture, provides a grant-in-aid of up to ₹18 lakh per Farmer Producer Organisation (FPO) over a period of three years. The support covers expenses related to formation, registration, governance, business planning, and initial operational costs.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your group is engaged in agricultural activities and has a clear producer base  
- You are either forming a new FPO or seeking to strengthen an existing one (within 10 years of incorporation)  
- You are working with or plan to engage a Promoting Institution (PI) such as SFAC, NABARD, or an NGO  
- You intend to use the support for activities like registration, office setup, training, and market linkage development
