#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance up to ₹15 lakh per FPO for mobilization, governance, and business planning; equity grant support up to ₹10 lakh per FPO; credit guarantee cover up to ₹2 crore per FPO
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit to FPO bank account
- Duration of benefit: One-time grant support; equity support and credit guarantee as per project lifecycle

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of incorporation/registration of the FPO
2. PAN card of the FPO
3. Bank account details of the FPO
4. Audited financial statements (if applicable)
5. Business plan detailing objectives, activities, and financial projections
6. List of member farmers with their landholding details
7. Resolution from the Board of Directors/Governing Body authorizing application
8. Proof of net worth of member farmers (if required for equity support)
9. Any other documents as specified by the implementing agency

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Verify eligibility criteria for FPO formation and promotion
2. Prepare detailed project report (DPR) including business plan and financials
3. Register the FPO as a legal entity under applicable Act
4. Open a bank account in the name of the FPO
5. Compile all required documents as per checklist
6. Submit application online via the SFAC portal (https://sfacindia.com/FPO) or offline to the designated nodal agency
7. Application undergoes preliminary scrutiny by SFAC/NABARD
8. Field verification may be conducted by the implementing agency
9. Approval and sanction letter issued upon successful evaluation
10. Disbursement of funds in tranches as per milestones achieved
