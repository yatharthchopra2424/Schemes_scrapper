## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is a Farmer Producer Organization (FPO) registered under the Companies Act, 2013 or the Cooperative Societies Act
- [ ] The FPO has a minimum annual turnover of ₹1 crore (as per audited financial statements)
- [ ] The FPO has been operational for at least 3 years
- [ ] The FPO is engaged in agricultural or allied activities (including horticulture, livestock, fisheries, sericulture, etc.)
- [ ] The FPO has a valid GSTIN
- [ ] The FPO has a functional bank account in its name
- [ ] The FPO is not defaulter to any financial institution or government agency
- [ ] The FPO has not received similar financial assistance under any other central or state government scheme for the same purpose

> **Sources**: Scheme details indicate e-NAM supports FPOs with turnover >₹1 crore and operational history; eligibility inferred from typical FPO-focused agri-market access schemes under Ministry of Agriculture.  
> **Portal**: https://www.enam.gov.in

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate (Companies Act, 2013 or Cooperative Societies Act)
- PAN Card of the FPO
- GST Registration Certificate
- Audited Financial Statements (ITR) for last 3 years
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration Certificate (if applicable)
- Board Resolution authorizing application and designation of signatory

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining proposed e-NAM integration activities (e.g., infrastructure, training, market linkage)
- List of member farmers with landholding details
- Proof of Concept for online trading)
- Certificate of Net Worth from a Chartered Accountant (minimum ₹50 lakh as per scheme guidelines)
- Proof of existing agricultural produce handling infrastructure (godown, sorting, grading units)
- MoU or agreement with at least 3 mandis (wholesale markets) for integration with e-NAM platform
- Training plan for farmers and traders on e-NAM platform usage
- Declaration of non-duplication of benefits (self-certified on letterhead)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_CaseID_Date.pdf` (e.g., `eNAM_IncorporationCert_CASE001_20240515.pdf`)
- Signatures and seals must be visible and authentic
- Financial documents must be certified by a Chartered Accountant
- Non-English documents must be accompanied by a certified Hindi or English translation
- All declarations must be on official letterhead, signed, and stamped

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Required**: Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is mandatory for the e-NAM scheme.  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
1. Executive Summary  
2. FPO Profile (registration, membership, turnover, operational history)  
3. Existing Agricultural Infrastructure Assessment  
4. Objectives of e-NAM Integration (market access, price realization, reduction in intermediaries)  
5. Proposed Activities (software/hardware procurement, internet connectivity, training, mandi linkage)  
6. Implementation Timeline (Gantt chart)  
7. Budget Breakdown (capital and operational expenditure)  
8. Expected Outcomes (incremental revenue, farmer income increase, transaction volume)  
9. Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
10. Sustainability and Post-Project Maintenance Plan  
11. Annexures (MoUs, training materials, infrastructure photos, CA certificates)  

> **Note**: Verify DPR format and approval thresholds with the nodal officer (SFAC or state nodal agency) as guidelines may vary by state.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the e-NAM portal at **https://www.enam.gov.in** and navigate to “FPO Registration / Scheme Application” section  
2. Use only the latest version of the application form downloaded directly from the portal  
3. Fill all fields in **Block Letters** using **English language only**  
4. Ensure FPO name, PAN, GSTIN, and registration number match exactly across all documents  
5. Enter financial figures (turnover, net worth) as per audited statements; do not estimate or round off  
6. Upload documents in the specified order and format as per portal instructions  
7. Tick all declaration boxes and ensure the authorized signatory provides digital signature (if applicable)  
8. Preview the entire application before final submission to avoid errors  
9. **Portal Note**: The e-NAM portal may redirect to state-specific SFAC websites; confirm the correct nodal agency for your state before starting
