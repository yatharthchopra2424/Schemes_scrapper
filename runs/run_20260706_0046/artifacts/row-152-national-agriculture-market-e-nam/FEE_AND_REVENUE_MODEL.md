## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-aligned pricing**: Fees are structured as a percentage of the realized benefit, ensuring consultant incentives align with client outcomes.
2. **Risk-adjusted rates**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile — while e-NAM offers significant market access benefits, its low confidence rating and dependence on state-level implementation and platform adoption introduce variability in realization timelines and outcomes.
3. **Tiered accessibility across different retainer fees are scaled to client’s of engagement, and expected scheme value.
4. **Transparency**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover initial eligibility assessment, documentation, and application submission, while success fees are payable only upon verified benefit realization.
