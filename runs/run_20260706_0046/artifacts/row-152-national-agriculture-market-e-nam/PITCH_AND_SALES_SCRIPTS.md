## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I’m reaching out because I specialise in helping agribusinesses, farmers, and agricultural traders access government support to improve market access and realise better prices for their produce. The National Agriculture Market (e-NAM) initiative is designed specifically to create a unified national market for agricultural commodities, and I’d like to understand if your operations could benefit from participating in this digital trading platform.

### Discovery Questions
- Are you currently involved in the sale or trade of agricultural commodities such as grains, pulses, fruits, vegetables, or oilseeds?
- Is your farming or agribusiness entity registered and operating within India?
- Do you currently sell your produce through local mandis (wholesale markets), or are you looking to access broader national markets?
- Are you interested in accessing real-time price information and bidding opportunities across multiple states?
- Do you have the basic digital infrastructure (such as internet access and a bank account) to participate in an online trading platform?
- Are you looking to reduce intermediaries and improve price realisation for your agricultural output?
- Is your produce among the notified commodities covered under e-NAM (e.g., cereals, pulses, vegetables, fruits, etc.)?
- Are you willing to comply with standardised quality parameters and assaying requirements for online trading?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Farmers and agribusinesses often face fragmented markets, limited price transparency, and dependence on local mandis, which can lead to lower price realisation and exploitation by intermediaries. The lack of a unified national market prevents producers from accessing better prices across states.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Agriculture Market (e-NAM) is a pan-India electronic trading portal launched by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. It networks existing APMC mandis to create a unified national market for agricultural commodities. The scheme facilitates online trading, transparent price discovery, and seamless inter-state trade without changing the existing mandi structure. There is no direct financial subsidy or cash benefit; instead, the value lies in improved market access, reduced transaction costs, and better price realisation through competitive bidding.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in the sale of notified agricultural commodities (e.g., grains, pulses, vegetables)  
- Your entity is based in India and operates within the agricultural value chain  
- You are seeking broader market access and improved price discovery for your produce  
- You have or can arrange basic digital access to participate in online trading
