### National Agriculture Market (e-NAM)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://enam.gov.in/
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), Traders, Commission Agents, Warehouse Operators, and other stakeholders in agricultural marketing
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Agriculture
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be registered participants in the e-NAM platform; eligibility may vary by state-specific mandi integration and local APMC rules

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Access to a unified national market for agricultural commodities; reduced transaction costs; transparent price discovery; wider buyer access; logistics and warehousing support through integrated services
- Disbursement mode: Not applicable (platform-based service access)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing as long as user remains active on the platform

#### Documents Required
1. Aadhaar card of the applicant
2. PAN card of the applicant or entity
3. Bank account details (cancelled cheque or bank passbook)
4. Proof of business address (utility bill, rent agreement, or property tax receipt)
5. Registration certificate (for FPOs, companies, or partnerships)
6. KYC documents of authorized signatory (if applicable)
7. Land ownership or lease documents (for farmers/traders using warehouse or mandi facilities)
8. GSTIN (if applicable)
9. Mobile number and email ID for OTP verification and communication

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the e-NAM portal at https://enam.gov.in/
2. Click on 'Register' or 'Login' based on user type (farmer, trader, etc.)
3. Select the appropriate registration category (Individual Farmer, FPO, Trader, Commission Agent, etc.)
4. Fill in the online registration form with personal and business details
5. Upload required documents as per the checklist
6. Submit the form for verification
7. Await OTP or verification call from the designated mandi or state nodal agency
8. Upon verification, receive login credentials to access the e-NAM platform
9. Start listing produce, bidding, or trading on the national market
