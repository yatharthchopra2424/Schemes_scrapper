## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Farmer is selling a notified agricultural commodity under MSP as declared by the Government of India  
- [ ] Farmer is selling through an authorized procurement agency (for PSS component)  
- [ ] Farmer is selling through a registered private trader (for PPPS component)  
- [ ] Farmer is registering sale and submitting claim for price difference (for PDPS component)  
- [ ] Farmer has valid identity proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID)  
- [ ] Farmer has land ownership or tenancy documents  
- [ ] Farmer has an active bank account for DBT  
- [ ] Farmer possesses sale receipt or procurement slip  
- [ ] Farmer can provide crop details and quantity sold  
- [ ] Sale occurs during the notified harvest season and market arrival period (varies by state and commodity)  

> **Note**: Eligibility is contingent on state/UT participation and readiness. PPPS is currently under pilot in select districts. Verify crop notification and state implementation status via [PM-AASHA Portal](https://agricoop.nic.in/pm-aasha).

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Identity proof (Aadhaar card or Voter ID)  
- PAN card  
- Bank account details (cancelled cheque or passbook copy)  
- Land ownership/tenancy documents (7/12 extract, mutation copy, lease agreement)  
- Recent passport-sized photographs  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Sale receipt or procurement slip from authorized agency or registered private trader  
- Crop details and quantity sold (including variety, grade, and weight)  
- For PDPS claims: Proof of sale at modal price (mandi slip or trader receipt)  
- For PPPS: Procurement bill submitted by private trader for service charge reimbursement  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 2 MB per file)  
- Names on identity and land documents must match exactly  
- Bank account details must be IFSC-enabled and in the farmer’s name  
- Sale receipt must include date, crop name, quantity, price, and agency/trader stamp  
- Documents must be in Hindi or English; vernacular documents require certified translation  
- Self-attestation required on all copies; gazetted officer attestation preferred for land documents

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required. PM-AASHA operates through direct procurement, price deficiency payments, or service charge reimbursement — no project report or DPR is needed for individual farmer applications.  
**Verify with nodal officer** if applying under PPPS as a private trader (service charge reimbursement may require additional documentation).

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application via the State/UT nodal agency portal (link varies by state; central guidance at [PM-AASHA Portal](https://agricoop.nic.in/pm-aasha))  
2. Select the correct component: PSS (direct procurement), PDPS (price deficiency), or PPPS (private trader service charge)  
3. Enter farmer’s personal details exactly as per Aadhaar (name, DOB, address)  
4. Provide accurate bank account number and IFSC code for DBT  
5. Select the notified crop from the dropdown (verify current MSP list)  
6. Enter sale date, quantity sold, and price received (mandi/modal price)  
7. Upload all scheme-specific and universal documents in prescribed format  
8. Declaration: Confirm sale was made through authorized channel and produce is of notified variety  
9. Preview all entries before final submission  
10. Save application reference number immediately after submission  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Farmer’s name matches across Aadhaar, land documents, and bank account  
- [ ] Bank account is active and enabled for DBT  
- [ ] Sale receipt includes agency/trader name, stamp, date, crop, quantity, and price  
- [ ] Crop is listed under current MSP notifications for the state  
- [ ] Quantity sold matches across sale receipt and application form  
- [ ] All documents are self-attested and in PDF format (≤2 MB)  
- [ ] Declarations are signed and dated  
- [ ] Application submitted via correct channel (PSS/PDPS/PPPS) as per sale modality

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Collect approval letter or SMS confirmation of DBT initiation  
- [ ] Verify sanctioned amount matches eligible quantity × (MSP – modal price) for PDPS or MSP × quantity for PSS/PPPS  
- [ ] Confirm DBT credit in farmer’s bank account (check UTR number and date)  
- [ ] Advise farmer to retain utilization certificate (if applicable for traders under PPPS)  
- [ ] Document invoice for success fee (as per consultancy agreement)  
- [ ] File closure report with lessons learned for future cases  
- [ ] Notify client of next harvest cycle and re-enrollment procedure  

> **Note**: Under PDPS, payment is the difference between MSP and actual selling/modal price. Under PSS, payment is at MSP via direct procurement. Under PPPS, private traders receive service charges. All payments are made via DBT.
