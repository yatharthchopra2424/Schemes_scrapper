| 10 - Rejected | Application declined | Analyze, re-apply or alternate scheme |

> **Note:** While PM-AASHA does not have a centralized standalone portal, applications and claims are processed through state/UT nodal agencies and linked systems such as PM-KISAN for DBT infrastructure. The portal URL referenced ([https://pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)) is used for beneficiary verification and payment tracking, though actual claim submission occurs via state-level procurement or PDPS/PPPS mechanisms. Always verify current process with respective state agriculture department.
