## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are tied to the actual financial benefit realized by the farmer (e.g., MSP payment or price deficiency amount), ensuring consultant incentives align with client outcomes.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s dependence on state-level implementation, crop notification, and market price volatility, success fees reflect moderate execution risk; retainer covers initial eligibility and documentation work.
3. **Transparency**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover advisory and documentation, success fees are calculated only upon verified benefit disbursement via DBT.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: PM-AASHA involves multi-stage verification (sale registration, MSP eligibility, price difference calculation, DBT processing) with processing times varying by state and commodity. Evidence indicates delays are common due to modal price verification and nodal agency readiness, justifying a 3-month lag in revenue recognition for success fees.
