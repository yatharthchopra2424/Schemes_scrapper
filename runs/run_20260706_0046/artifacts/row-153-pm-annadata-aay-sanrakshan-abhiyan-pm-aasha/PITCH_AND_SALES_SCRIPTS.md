## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I’m calling from [Your Firm Name]. We specialise in helping farmers access government support schemes that ensure remunerative prices for their produce. Given your involvement in agriculture, I wanted to understand your current crop sales experience and see if the PM Annadata Aay Sanrakshan Abhiyan (PM-AASHA) could support you in securing Minimum Support Price (MSP) benefits for your notified crops.

### Discovery Questions
- Which crops do you primarily grow, and are they covered under the Government of India’s Minimum Support Price (MSP) list?
- Have you sold your produce through authorized procurement agencies or registered private traders in the past season?
- What challenges have you faced in receiving timely payments or achieving MSP for your crops?
- Are you currently selling your notified crops through channels that qualify for PM-AASHA benefits (e.g., government procurement centres or registered private traders)?
- Do you have your land records, identity proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID), and bank account details readily available for benefit disbursement?
- Have you previously applied for or received benefits under any MSP-related scheme like PSS, PDPS, or PPPS?
- What is your typical harvest season and market arrival period for your main crops?
- Are you aware of whether your state is currently participating in PM-AASHA for your notified crops?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Farmer grows notified agricultural commodities under MSP as declared by Government of India
- [ ] Farmer sells produce through authorized agencies (PSS) or registered private traders (PPPS)
- [ ] Farmer has identity proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID)
- [ ] Farmer has land ownership or tenancy documents
- [ ] Farmer has bank account details for DBT
- [ ] Farmer can provide sale receipt or procurement slip
- [ ] Farmer can provide crop details and quantity sold
- [ ] Farmer’s state/UT is participating in PM-AASHA for the relevant crop and season
- [ ] Farmer sells during the notified harvest season and market arrival period

Based on your responses, it appears you grow [crop] and sell through [channel], which may make you eligible for PM-AASHA. ## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many farmers struggle to get remunerative prices for their produce due to market fluctuations, distress sales, and delayed payments. Even when MSP is declared, access is limited by lack of awareness, complex procedures, or selling through unauthorized channels, leaving farmers vulnerable to income instability and post-harvest losses.

**Scheme intro:**
The PM Annadata Aay Sanrakshan Abhiyan (PM-AASHA), administered by the Department of Agriculture, Cooperation & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, is a pan-India subsidy scheme that provides financial support through three components: Price Support Scheme (PSS) for direct procurement at MSP, Price Deficiency Payment Scheme (PDPS) for direct payment of the difference between MSP and actual selling/modal price, and Private Procurement & Stockist Scheme (PPPS) where private players procure at MSP and receive service charges. Benefits are transferred directly to farmers’ bank accounts via DBT.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You grow [specific crop], which is covered under MSP as declared by the Government of India
- You sell your produce through [authorized agency/registered private trader], meeting the channel requirement for benefit access
- You possess the required documents: identity proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID), land records, and bank account details
- Your state is currently participating in PM-AASHA for [crop] during the [season] harvest period
