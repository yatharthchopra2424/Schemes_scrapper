### PM Annadata Aay Sanrakshan Abhiyan (PM-AASHA)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Agriculture, Cooperation & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
- **Portal / Application URL:** [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Based on harvest season and market arrival of notified crops; varies by state and commodity

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Farmers
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Agriculture
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: All farmers selling notified agricultural commodities under the scheme are eligible; scheme applies to crops covered under MSP as declared by the Government of India from time to time; farmers must sell their produce through authorized agencies or registered private traders under the PPPS component to avail benefits

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support through direct procurement at MSP (PSS), direct payment of price deficiency (PDPS), and service charges to private players (PPPS); exact quantum varies based on market prices, production, and MSP declarations
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (via DBT)
- Duration of benefit: One-time per sale/transaction

#### Documents Required
1. Farmer’s identity proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID)
2. Land ownership or tenancy documents
3. Bank account details (for DBT)
4. Sale receipt or procurement slip
5. Crop details and quantity sold

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Farmer sells notified crop to authorized procurement agency (PSS) or registered private trader (PPPS)
2. For PDPS, farmer registers sale and submits claim for price difference
3. State/UT nodal agency verifies sale and MSP eligibility
4. Payment is made directly to farmer’s bank account via DBT
5. Private traders under PPPS submit procurement bills for service charge reimbursement
