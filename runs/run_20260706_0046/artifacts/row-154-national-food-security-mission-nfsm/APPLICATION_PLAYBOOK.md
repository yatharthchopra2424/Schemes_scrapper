# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- ✅ Farmer cultivating rice, wheat, pulses, coarse cereals (maize & barley), commercial crops (cotton, jute, sugarcane), or nutri-cereals (shree anna) in identified districts of the country  
- ✅ Farm located in districts prioritized by the mission (low productivity yet high potential, especially rain-fed regions for food-grain crops)  
- ✅ Farmer willing to cooperate and contribute resources for demonstrations or interventions  
- ✅ Farmer has not received benefits for the same intervention under other schemes or missions  
- ✅ Farmer has a Farmer ID (in states where farmer registry work has been initiated)  
- ✅ Land area for demonstration does not exceed 2.00 hectare per farmer in a year  
- ✅ For General States: cluster size for demonstrations is 10 hectares; for Hilly States, North-Eastern States, and UTs: cluster size is 2 hectares  
- ✅ Soil fertility status verified via Soil Health Card for input decisions  
- ✅ Farmer belongs to target beneficiary group: small & marginal farmers, women farmers, SC/ST farmers (allocation proportionate to population), or FRA Patta Holders

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, etc.)  
- PAN Card  
- Bank Account Details (with IFSC code)  
- Land Ownership/Lease Documents (7/12 extract, mutation entry, etc.)  
- Caste Certificate (if applicable for SC/ST benefits)  
- Gender Declaration (for women farmer benefits)  
- Udyam Registration (if applicable for agro-processing linkage)  
- Passport-sized Photographs  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Soil Health Card (mandatory for deciding fertilizer and soil ameliorant use in demonstrations)  
- Farmer ID (from State Farmers Registry developed under Digital Agriculture Mission, or alternative state farmer database)  
- Annual Action Plan (AAP) aligned with Mission’s goals and objectives (prepared by State/UT/District Level Executive Committee)  
- Utilization Certificate (for fund release, audited annually by Designated Authority/Agency of State/UT)  
- PFMS Scheme Account Details (designated scheme accounts through Public Financial Management System)  
- Seed Variety Details (notified/approved varieties/hybrids including notified/approved Bt Cotton)  
- Input Supply Records (seeds, plant growth regulators, micro-nutrients, bio-fertilizers, bio-agents, plant protection chemicals, pheromone traps, intercrop seeds)  
- Display Board Photograph (with details: farmer name, village, crop/variety, technology demonstrated, date of sowing, seed rate/spacing, fertilizers/bio-fertilizers/micronutrients/PGRs/bio-agents/PP chemicals applied)  
- Field Day Attendance Sheet (minimum 30 farmers)  
- Training Attendance Sheet (for cropping system trainings: 30 farmers per session, two sessions per training)  
- Geo-tagged Polygon of Demonstration Field (via Krishi Mapper application)  
- Crop Stage Photograph (with growth stage mentioned)  
- Outcome Report (demonstration yield, average village yield, probable harvest date)  
- Minutes of District Level Executive Committee (DLEC) meeting approving beneficiary selection  
- Minutes of State Level Executive Committee (SLEC) approving Annual Action Plan  
- Minutes of Central Committee for Component-I (B) (if applicable for national-level oversight)  

### Document Quality Check
- ✅ File size: PDFs under 10 MB; images under 5 MB  
- ✅ File naming: Clear, standardized format (e.g., `State_District_BeneficiaryName_DocumentType_Date.pdf`)  
- ✅ Name matching: Beneficiary name matches across all documents and bank account  
- ✅ Certification: Documents self-attested or gazetted officer-attested where required  
- ✅ Language: Hindi or English; vernacular documents accompanied by certified translation  
- ✅ Validity: Certificates (caste, land, etc.) within validity period  
- ✅ Geo-tagging: Polygon clearly demarcates demonstration field via Krishi Mapper  
- ✅ Photographs: Clear, timestamped, showing crop stage and inputs used

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for individual farmer beneficiaries under NFSM. The scheme operates through State/UT Annual Action Plans (AAPs) and district-level implementation. Individual beneficiaries apply through State-driven processes (e.g., beneficiary selection via Gram Panchayat, ATMA, or State Agriculture Department).  
**Note**: Verify with nodal officer (State Mission Director or District Agriculture Officer) if a DPR is needed for any special intervention (e.g., Flexi Interventions, Local Initiatives, or FPO promotion).  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Access the portal**: Visit https://nfsm.gov.in and navigate to the beneficiary login or State/UT login section.  
2. **Authenticate**: Use assigned User ID and Password (provided by State Agriculture Department or NFSM nodal officer).  
3. **Select Scheme**: Choose “National Food Security Mission (NFSM)” and relevant component (e.g., NFSM-Cotton under Component-I(B) of Mission for Cotton Productivity).  
4. **Enter Beneficiary Details**: Fill in personal information (name, Aadhaar, bank details, land details) exactly as per documents.  
5. **Specify Intervention**: Select the specific intervention (e.g., Closer Spacing Technology, HDPS, ELS Cotton, ICM, Cropping System Training, Flexi Intervention).  
6. **Upload Documents**: Attach all scheme-specific and universal documents in the prescribed format.  
7. **Declare Eligibility**: Confirm cultivation of eligible crop in identified district, land ceiling (≤2 ha per farmer per year for demonstrations), and non-duplication of benefits.  
8. **Submit and Save**: Click submit, save the application reference number, and take a screenshot of the confirmation page.  
9. **Follow Protocol**: Do not exceed prescribed input assistance limits; ensure beneficiary arranges recommended chemical fertilizers at own cost.  
10. **Verify DBT Readiness**: Ensure Aadhaar is linked to bank account for Direct Benefit Transfer.  
11. **Retain Copy**: Keep a copy of the submitted application for internal review and audit.  
12. **Confirm Receipt**: Wait for auto-generated SMS/email acknowledgment from portal; if not received within 24 hours, follow up via helpdesk.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit application via https://nfsm.gov.in using assigned credentials  
- Capture screenshot of submission confirmation page showing Application Reference Number and timestamp  
- Store reference number in case file and digital tracker  
- Set automated follow-up reminders:  
  - Day 20: Check portal for status update  
  - Day 25: Escalate if no movement  
  - Day 30: Trigger Level 1 escalation if no acknowledgment

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- ✅ Receive and verify approval letter/sanction order from State Agriculture Department via PFMS portal  
- ✅ Confirm sanctioned amount matches approved intervention norms (e.g., Rs. 14,000/Ha for Closer Spacing, Rs. 18,500/Ha for HDPS)  
- ✅ Ensure Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) is initiated to beneficiary’s Aadhaar-linked bank account  
- ✅ Monitor utilization of funds via PFMS and scheme account  
- ✅ Prepare Utilization Certificate (UC) for submission to State Level Executive Committee (SLEC)  
- ✅ Verify annual audit of scheme accounts by Designated Authority/Agency of State/UT  
- ✅ Track invoice for success fee (as per consultancy agreement)  
- ✅ Document success story: yield increase, technology adoption, farmer testimonial (with photos)  
- ✅ Submit progress report to State/UT as per quarterly reporting requirements
