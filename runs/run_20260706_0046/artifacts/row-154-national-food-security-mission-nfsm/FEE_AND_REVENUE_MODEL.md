## Pricing Principles
1. Fees are structured to align with the scheme’s financial assistance magnitude and implementation complexity.
2. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, documentation, and application preparation.
3. Success fee is contingent upon successful fund disbursement via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) by States.
4. Scheme-specific note: The National Food Security Mission (NFSM) involves multi-year implementation with fund releases in installments based on Annual Action Plans and utilization certificates. Processing timelines are moderate to long due to State-level approvals, PFMS compliance, and audit requirements, resulting in a revenue lag of 3–6 months. Success is evidenced by structured fund flow mechanisms and DBT disbursements, reducing payment risk but extending realization.
