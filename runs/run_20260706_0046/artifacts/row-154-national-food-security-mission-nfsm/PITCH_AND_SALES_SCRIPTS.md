# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because the National Food Security Mission (NFSM) is specifically designed to support farmers like you who cultivate rice, wheat, pulses, coarse cereals, commercial crops, or nutri-cereals in identified districts across India. The mission focuses on boosting productivity and income through science-backed interventions and direct benefit transfers. I’d like to understand your current farming setup to see if NFSM aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What crops do you currently cultivate (rice, wheat, pulses, maize, barley, cotton, sugarcane, nutri-cereals like shree anna)?
2. In which district and state is your farm located? (To verify if it’s an NFSM-identified district)
3. What is the size of your landholding in hectares? (NFSM allows assistance up to 5 hectares per farmer per season)
4. Are you a small or marginal farmer, or do you belong to a Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, or women farmer category? (NFSM prioritizes 33% allocation for small/marginal and 30% for women farmers)
5. What are your main challenges in farming—low yield, poor soil fertility, lack of access to quality seeds, or high input costs?
6. Have you previously accessed any government subsidy or scheme for seeds, fertilizers, or farm machinery?
7. Are you part of a Farmer Producer Organization (FPO), self-help group, or cooperative? (NFSM converges with such groups)
8. What improvements are you seeking—higher yield, better seed replacement rate, access to training, or post-harvest value addition?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Farmer cultivates eligible crops: rice, wheat, pulses, coarse cereals (maize & barley), commercial crops (cotton, jute, sugarcane), or nutri-cereals (shree anna)
- [ ] Farm is located in an NFSM-identified district (low productivity, high potential, rain-fed focus)
- [ ] Landholding ≤ 5 hectares per season (eligibility cap)
- [ ] Farmer belongs to prioritized group: small/marginal, women, SC/ST (if applicable)
- [ ] Faces challenges NFSM addresses: low productivity, poor soil, seed access, input costs
- [ ] Interested in productivity enhancement, training, or input assistance
- [ ] Willing to maintain records and participate in demonstrations/trainings

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you grow [crop] in [district], which is an NFSM-targeted area, and you’re facing [specific challenge]. The National Food Security Mission offers direct financial assistance through DBT for seeds, inputs, trainings, and demonstrations—exactly what could help you improve yield and income. Let me share how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Indian farmers, especially in rain-fed and low-productivity districts, struggle with stagnant yields due to limited access to quality seeds, modern farming techniques, soil health management, and training. Despite hard work, many cannot afford inputs like hybrid seeds, micronutrients, or farm machinery, leading to low income and vulnerability to climate risks.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Food Security Mission (NFSM), implemented by the Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India, is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme providing financial assistance through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) by States. It supports demonstrations, need-based inputs (seeds, INM, IPM), cropping system trainings, seed hubs, infrastructure development, and more—aimed at increasing production and farmer income sustainably.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You cultivate [eligible crop from discovery], which is covered under NFSM for rice, wheat, pulses, coarse cereals, commercial crops, or nutri-cereals
- Your farm is in [district], an NFSM-identified district prioritized for low productivity and high potential, especially in rain-fed regions
- Your landholding is within the 5-hectare limit per season for availing assistance
- You fall under [small/marginal/women/SC/ST if disclosed], a group NFSM prioritizes for 33% (small/marginal) and 30% (women) fund allocation

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|

States release funds based on approved Annual Action Plans, and beneficiary data is tracked via MIS. Many farmers in your district have already benefited—we can share anonymized success stories upon request. |
| "How much do you charge?" | [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT] |
| "I'll try it myself first" | Common mistakes include applying for crops not covered in your district (e.g., sugarcane in a pulses-only zone) or exceeding the 5-hectare limit per season. NFSM requires accurate land records and crop-specific documentation—we help avoid these pitfalls. |
| "We're too small / too big" | NFSM explicitly supports small and marginal farmers—33% of funds are reserved for them. There’s no minimum land size, but assistance is capped at 5 hectares per farmer per season. Whether you have 0.5 ha or 4.5 ha, you’re eligible to apply. |
| "I don't have the documents ready" | The three most commonly missing documents are: 1) Updated land ownership or tenancy records, 2) Seed purchase invoices or DBT receipts for past inputs (if claiming history), and 3) Bank account linked to Aadhaar for DBT readiness. We’ll guide you on obtaining these. |
| "We already work with a CA" | "Great — we work alongside CAs. They handle your accounts; we handle the scheme strategy and applications." |
