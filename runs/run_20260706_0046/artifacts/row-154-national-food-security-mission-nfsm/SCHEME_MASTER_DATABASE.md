### National Food Security Mission (NFSM)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nfsm.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Farmers
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Agriculture (cultivating rice, wheat, pulses, coarse cereals, commercial crops, nutri-cereals)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Farmers cultivating rice, wheat, pulses, coarse cereals (maize & barley), commercial crops (cotton, jute, sugarcane), and nutri-cereals (shree annawri) in identified districts; mission prioritizes districts with low productivity yet high potential, especially rain-fed regions

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance provided through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) by States; benefits include demonstrations, need-based inputs (seed, INM & IPM), cropping system trainings, flexi interventions, seed hubs, strategic adaptive research support, exposure visits to international organizations, assistance for seed transportation, fostering seeds of new varieties, creation & modernization of seed infrastructure, boosting seed production of traditional varieties, strengthening seed production infrastructure in potato, seed processing and storage units at gram panchayat level, strengthening seed quality control components, national seed reserve, and assistance for boosting seed production in private sector
- Disbursement mode: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
- Duration of benefit: Annual (aligned with Annual Action Plan)

#### Documents Required
1. Annual Action Plan aligned with the Mission’s goals and objectives
2. Designated scheme accounts maintained through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS)
3. Utilization certificates
4. Progress reports (quarterly/annual)
5. Beneficiary details (Farmer ID where applicable)
6. Soil Health Cards (for soil analysis)
7. Input supply details (seeds, fertilizers, bio-agents, etc.)
8. Field day participation records
9. Geo-plotting data (polygon of farmer’s field)
10. Crop stage photographs and details
11. Outcome details (demonstration yield, average village yield, probable harvest date)
12. Seed variety details (if adding missing variety)
13. User ID and Password for application login
14. Captcha for login
15. Excel format for beneficiary details (block-wise)
16. Block code for beneficiary upload

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Prepare Annual Action Plan aligned with the Mission’s goals and objectives
2. Execute the Approved Action Plan using funds provided by Government of India, along with matching State Share
3. Maintain designated scheme accounts through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS)
4. Release of Central Share to States shall be in accordance with prevailing guidelines of the Department of Expenditure, Ministry of Finance, Government of India
5. States/UTs should adopt the fund flow mechanism as communicated by the Government of India and ensure that the accounts thus maintained are audited annually by the Designated Authority/Agency of the State/UT
