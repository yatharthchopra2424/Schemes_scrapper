# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The National Food Security Mission (NFSM) is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme (CSS) implemented by the Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (DA&FW), Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India. It operates across all states and union territories (Pan-India) with the primary goal of increasing production of rice, wheat, pulses, coarse cereals (maize & barley), commercial crops (cotton, jute, sugarcane), and nutri-cereals (shree anna) through area expansion, productivity enhancement, and sustainable practices. The mission also focuses on restoring soil fertility, enhancing farm-level economy, improving post-harvest value addition, increasing Seed Replacement Rate (SRR) and Varietal Replacement Rate (VRR), and strengthening seed sector infrastructure. Financial assistance is provided through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) by states. The application portal is https://nfsm.gov.in, last updated for 2025-26.

### Objectives
- Increasing production of Rice, Wheat, Pulses, Coarse Cereals (Maize & Barley), Commercial Crops (Cotton, Jute & Sugarcane) and Nutri-Cereals (Shree Anna) through area expansion, adoption of different cropping patterns and productivity enhancement in a sustainable manner.
- Restoring soil fertility and productivity at farm level.
- Enhancing farm level economy to enhance farmer’s income.
- Enhancing post-harvest value addition at the farm gate for better price realization to farmers through efficient market linkages.
- Enhancing the Seed Replacement Rate (SRR) and Varietal Replacement Rate (VRR).
- Improving the infrastructure of the Seed Sector in the country.

### Eligibility Matrix
Farmers cultivating specific crops in identified districts are eligible. The mission prioritizes districts with low productivity yet high potential, especially rain-fed regions for food-grain crops.

| Crop Category | Specific Crops | Eligibility Criteria |
|---------------|----------------|----------------------|
| Food Grains | Rice, Wheat, Pulses, Coarse Cereals (Maize & Barley) | Cultivated in identified districts; priority to low productivity, high potential districts, especially rain-fed areas |
| Commercial Crops | Cotton, Jute, Sugarcane | Cultivated in identified districts |
| Nutri-Cereals | Shree Anna (Millets) | Cultivated in identified districts |
| Beneficiary Type | Farmers | All farmer categories (small, marginal, women, SC/ST) eligible with specific allocations |

### Benefits & Financial Support
Financial assistance is provided through DBT by states. Interventions include demonstrations, need-based inputs, trainings, flexi interventions, seed hubs, research support, exposure visits, seed transportation assistance, fostering new varieties, seed infrastructure creation/modernization, traditional variety seed production, potato seed infrastructure strengthening, seed processing/storage units at gram panchayat level, seed quality control strengthening, national seed reserve, and private sector seed production boosting.

### Application Process
The application process is state-driven. States/UTs prepare Annual Action Plans (AAPs) aligned with mission goals, execute approved plans using central and state matching funds, maintain scheme accounts via PFMS, release central share per Department of Expenditure guidelines, and ensure annual audits by designated authorities.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[State/UT Prepares Annual Action Plan (AAP)] --> B[AAP Submitted to State Level Sanctioning Committee (SLSC)]
    B --> C[SLSC Approves AAP (Chaired by Chief Secretary)]
    C --> D[Approved AAP Sent to DA&FW for Approval]
    D --> E[DA&FW Approves AAP]
    E --> F[Release of Central Share to States/UTs]
    F --> G[State/UT Releases Funds to District Level Agency via PFMS/Treasury]
    G --> H[District Level Agency Releases Funds to Nodal Departments for Input Procurement]
    H --> I[Nodal Departments Submit Utilization Certificate to District Level Agency]
    I --> J[Consolidated Utilization Certificate Authenticated by DFSMEC and SFSMEC]
    J --> K[Submitted to Ministry of Agriculture, GoI for Further Fund Release]
    L[State/UT Maintains Designated Scheme Accounts via PFMS] --> G
    M[Release of Central Share Follows Prevailing Guidelines of Department of Expenditure, MoF, GoI] --> F
    N[State/UT Adopts Fund Flow Mechanism as Communicated by GoI] --> G
    O[Accounts Maintained Are Audited Annually by Designated Authority/Agency of State/UT] --> J
```

**Key Caveats:**
- The mission is implemented through States/UTs, and States/UTs shall maintain designated scheme accounts through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS).
- The release of the Central Share to States shall be in accordance with prevailing guidelines of the Department of Expenditure, Ministry of Finance, Government of India.
- States/UTs should adopt the fund flow mechanism as communicated by the Government of India and ensure that the accounts thus maintained are audited annually by the Designated Authority/Agency of the State/UT.

> **Warning:** Failure to maintain PFMS accounts or adhere to fund flow guidelines will result in delayed or withheld central fund releases. Annual audit by designated authority is mandatory.

> **Key Takeaway:** The NFSM operates on a centrally sponsored, state-implemented model with strict financial controls via PFMS and DBT for beneficiary payments. Success depends on timely AAP preparation, SLSC approval, and compliance with expenditure guidelines.
