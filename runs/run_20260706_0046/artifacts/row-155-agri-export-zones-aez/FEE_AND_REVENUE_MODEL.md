## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the financial benefit delivered under the Agri Export Zones (AEZ) scheme, which provides variable subsidies for infrastructure, quality upgradation, and market promotion based on project milestones.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s reimbursement-based disbursement model and moderate processing time (typically 6–12 months for approval and fund release), requiring utilization certificates and milestone verification.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The Growth and Scale tiers are most applicable, as the scheme targets farmer producer companies, agro-processing units, exporters, and MSMEs with export potential — aligning with APEDA’s focus on cluster-based agri-value chains.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: Retainers cover initial project assessment, consortium formation, DPR preparation, and RCMC/IEC compliance; success fees are contingent on fund disbursement, reducing client risk.
