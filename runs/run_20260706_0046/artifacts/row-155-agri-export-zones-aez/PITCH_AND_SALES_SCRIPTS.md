# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping agri-exporters, farmer producer companies, and agro-processing units access government support for export-oriented infrastructure and value addition. The Agri Export Zones (AEZ) scheme, administered by APEDAgriculture Development Authority (APEDA), provides financial assistance for infrastructure like packhouses, processing units, cold chains, and market promotion activities. I’d like to understand your current export activities and see if this scheme aligns with your growth plans.

### Discovery Questions
1. What agricultural products are you currently producing, processing, or exporting?
2. Are you engaged in activities related to APEDA-scheduled products (e.g., fruits, vegetables, processed foods, dairy, meat, etc.)?
3. Do you have an existing export track record or demonstrable export potential for your products?
4. Are you operating as an individual farmer, farmer producer company (FPC), agro-processing unit, or exporter?
5. Have you obtained or are you willing to obtain a Registration-cum-Membership Certificate (RCMC) from APEDA?
6. Do you have an Import Export Code (IEC) from DGFT?
7. Are you part of or willing to form a consortium with other stakeholders (farmers, processors, exporters) in a geographic cluster for export-oriented value chain development?
8. What is your approximate annual turnover or investment capacity for infrastructure projects like packhouses or cold storage?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Engaged in production, processing, or export of APEDA-scheduled agricultural products
- [ ] Demonstrates export potential or existing export activity
- [ ] Willing to obtain or already has APEDA RCMC
- [ ] Has or can obtain Import Export Code (IEC) from DGFT
- [ ] Open to cluster-based participation with other stakeholders (farmers, processors, exporters)
- [ ] Has access to land (ownership or lease) for infrastructure development
- [ ] Capable of preparing a Detailed Project Report (DPR)
- [ ] Prepared to submit financial statements and bank appraisal

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you’re involved in [mention specific product/activity from discovery] with export potential — exactly the kind of beneficiary the Agri Export Zones (AEZ) scheme supports. This scheme provides financial assistance for infrastructure and market development through APEDA’s Financial Assistance Scheme, with grants disbursed in installments based on project milestones. Shall I walk you through how it works and whether it fits your needs?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Agri-exporters and processors face high upfront costs for setting up export-grade infrastructure like packhouses, cold storage, and testing facilities. They also struggle with quality certification, branding, and accessing international markets due to limited resources and fragmented value chains. Without support, many cannot meet international SPS standards or compete effectively in global markets.

**Scheme intro:**
The Agri Export Zones (AEZ) scheme, administered by the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA), provides financial assistance as a subsidy for infrastructure development, quality upgradation, and market promotion activities. Support is delivered through APEDA’s Financial Assistance Scheme (FAS), where eligible entities receive grants for setting up packhouses, processing units, cold chains, testing laboratories, and for activities like quality certification, branding, and participation in international trade fairs. The quantum of support varies by project nature and component, with specific ceilings for infrastructure, certification, and market development, disbursed in installments based on milestones and utilization certificates.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in the production, processing, or export of APEDA-scheduled products (confirmed in discovery)
- You have or are willing to obtain APEDA Registration-cum-Membership Certificate (RCMC), a mandatory requirement
- You possess or can obtain an Import Export Code (IEC) from DGFT, required for application
- You are open to or already part of a cluster-based approach involving farmers, processors, and exporters — a core eligibility criterion
