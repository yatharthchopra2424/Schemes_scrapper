### Agri Export Zones (AEZ)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://apeda.gov.in/apedawebsite/aez
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Farmers, farmer producer companies, agro-processing units, exporters, and other stakeholders engaged in the production, processing, and export of agricultural products
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Agriculture and processed food products (scheduled products under APEDA)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be engaged in activities related to scheduled products under APEDA and must demonstrate export potential or existing export activity; encourages cluster-based participation where multiple stakeholders in a geographic region collaborate to develop an export-oriented agri-value chain; Registration with APEDA as an exporter (RCMC) is typically required for availing benefits under the scheme

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support provided as a subsidy for infrastructure development, quality upgradation, and market promotion activities; quantum of support varies based on the nature of the project, with specific ceilings for different components such as infrastructure, certification, and market development
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement (disbursed in installments based on project milestones and utilization certificates)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (based on project completion and milestones)

#### Documents Required
1. Project Report / Detailed Project Report (DPR)
2. Registration-cum-Membership Certificate (RCMC) from APEDA
3. Import Export Code (IEC) from DGFT
4. Proof of land ownership or lease agreement for infrastructure
5. Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) among consortium members
6. Financial statements and bank appraisal of the project
7. Environmental and statutory clearances (if applicable)
8. Details of proposed machinery, technology, and processing capacity
9. Marketing and export strategy document
10. Bank account details and PAN of the applicant entity

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the agri-product and geographic cluster for export potential.
2. Form a consortium of farmers, processors, exporters, and other stakeholders.
3. Develop a detailed project report (DPR) outlining infrastructure, value addition, and export plan.
4. Apply for registration with APEDA to obtain RCMC (Registration-cum-Membership Certificate).
5. Submit the DPR and application through the APEDA portal or designated channel for evaluation.
6. Upon approval, enter into an agreement with APEDA for financial and technical support.
7. Implement the project as per the approved plan and submit periodic progress reports.
8. Claim financial assistance upon submission of utilization certificates and completion of milestones.
