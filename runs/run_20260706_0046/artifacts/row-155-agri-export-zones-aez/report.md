# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The Agri Export Zones (AEZ) scheme is a subsidy-based initiative implemented by the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA) under the Ministry of Agriculture. It operates on a pan-India geographic scope with a rolling basis application process and was last updated in 2024. The scheme aims to promote export-oriented production and processing of agricultural products through cluster-based development, infrastructure creation, quality enhancement, and market promotion.

### Objectives
- To promote export-oriented production and processing of agricultural products  
- To develop infrastructure for post-harvest management and value addition  
- To integrate farmers, processors, and exporters in a cluster-based approach  
- To enhance quality and compliance with international standards  
- To reduce transaction costs and improve supply chain efficiency  
- To encourage private investment and public-private partnerships in agri-export  
- To increase India's share in global agricultural exports through brand promotion  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Eligibility Criteria | Details |
|----------------------|---------|
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Farmers, agro-processors, exporters, MSMEs, farmer producer companies (FPCs) |
| **Core Requirement** | Engagement in production, processing, and export of agricultural products; must be related to scheduled products under APEDA |
| **Export Potential** | Must demonstrate export potential or existing export activity |
| **Participation Model** | Encourages cluster-based participation where multiple stakeholders in a geographic region collaborate to develop an export-oriented agri-value chain |
| **Mandatory Registration** | Registration with APEDA as an exporter (RCMC) is typically required for availing benefits |
| **Additional Notes** | Scheme supports stakeholders across the agri-export value chain including farmers, FPCs, agro-processing units, and exporters |

### Benefits & Financial Support
Financial support under AEZ is provided as a subsidy for infrastructure development, quality upgradation, and market promotion activities, typically extended through APEDA's Financial Assistance Scheme (FAS). The quantum varies based on project nature, with specific ceilings for different components.

| Support Category | Details |
|------------------|---------|
| **Infrastructure Development** | Grants for setting up packhouses, processing units, cold chains, and testing facilities |
| **Quality Upgradation** | Support for quality certification, branding, and market development activities |
| **Market Promotion** | Facilitation of access to export promotion initiatives, participation in international trade fairs, and buyer-seller meets |
| **Technology & Skill Development** | Support for technology upgradation, skill development, and training for stakeholders in the agri-export value chain |
| **Logistics & Compliance** | Assistance for logistics, packaging, and compliance with international sanitary and phytosanitary (SPS) standards |
| **Disbursement Mechanism** | Funds disbursed in installments based on project milestones and utilization certificates; subsidy is disbursed on reimbursement basis after verification of expenditure |
| **Key Caveats** | <ul><li>Benefits subject to availability of funds under APEDA's Financial Assistance Scheme</li><li>Projects must demonstrate clear export linkage and value addition</li><li>Failure to achieve export targets may result in recovery of funds</li><li>Scheme subject to periodic review and modification by the government</li></ul> |

### Application Process
The application process for AEZ involves consortium formation, detailed project reporting, APEDA registration, and milestone-based fund claims.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Identify agri-product and geographic cluster for export potential] --> B[Form consortium of farmers, processors, exporters, and other stakeholders]
    B --> C[Develop Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining infrastructure, value addition, and export plan]
    C --> D[Apply for registration with APEDA to obtain RCMC]
    D --> E[Submit DPR and application through APEDA portal or designated channel for evaluation]
    E --> F[Upon approval, enter into agreement with APEDA for financial and technical support]
    F --> G[Implement project as per approved plan and submit periodic progress reports]
    G --> H[Claim financial assistance upon submission of utilization certificates and completion of milestones]
```

### Required Documents
1. Project Report / Detailed Project Report (DPR)  
2. Registration-cum-Membership Certificate (RCMC) from APEDA  
3. Import Export Code (IEC) from DGFT  
4. Proof of land ownership or lease agreement for infrastructure  
5. Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) among consortium members  
6. Financial statements and bank appraisal of the project  
7. Environmental and statutory clearances (if applicable)  
8. Details of proposed machinery, technology, and processing capacity  
9. Marketing and export strategy document  
10. Bank account details and PAN of the applicant entity
