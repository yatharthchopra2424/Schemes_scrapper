## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a farmer (individual cultivator) engaged in agricultural activities  
- [ ] Applicant is availing short-term crop loan from a scheduled commercial bank, regional rural bank, or cooperative bank  
- [ ] Loan is for agricultural purposes and classified as short-term credit (typically up to 12 months)  
- [ ] Loan is taken during the kharif or rabi season for notified crops  
- [ ] Applicant has not defaulted on previous agricultural loans  
- [ ] Applicant possesses valid land ownership or cultivation rights (owned, leased, or tenant farmer)  
- [ ] Applicant’s Kisan Credit Card (KCC) is active and in good standing (if applicable)  
- [ ] Applicant is not availing similar interest subvention from any other central or state government scheme for the same loan  
- [ ] Applicant’s bank is a participating institution under the Interest Subvention Scheme for Short Term Credit to Farmers  

> **Note**: Eligibility is primarily bank-mediated; farmers apply through their lending institution. Verification of landholding, crop intent, and loan purpose is conducted by the bank.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, or Passport)  
- Address Proof (Aadhaar Card, utility bill, or ration card)  
- PAN Card of the applicant  
- Bank account details (passbook or cancelled cheque)  
- Latest Income Tax Return (ITR) – if applicable (many farmers may be exempt; self-declaration of income may suffice)  
- GST Registration Certificate – if applicable (not mandatory for most small farmers)  
- Udyam Registration – if applicable (for agri-entrepreneurs or FPOs; not required for individual farmers)  
- Bank statement of the last 6 months  
- Passport-sized photographs  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Kisan Credit Card (KCC) – if availing loan via KCC  
- Loan application/sanction letter from the bank for short-term crop loan  
- Land ownership documents (7/12 extract, patta, title deed, or lease agreement)  
- Crop sowing declaration or acreage certificate (issued by village revenue officer or agriculture department)  
- Season and crop details (kharif/rabi, notified crop under scheme)  
- Declaration form stating no duplicate benefit availed under any other government interest subvention scheme  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF or clear JPEG/PNG format  
- File size per document should not exceed 2 MB  
- Name on all documents must match exactly as per bank records and ID proof  
- Land documents must be clear, certified copies (if not original) and show cultivable area  
- KCC must be active and show recent transactions  
- Declarations must be self-attested and dated  
- Language: Documents in Hindi or English; regional language documents must be accompanied by a certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required. This scheme is interest subvention on existing short-term crop loans; no project appraisal or DPR is needed.  
**Flag**: Verify with nodal officer if any state-specific add-on requires documentation (though central scheme does not mandate DPR).

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. The application is **bank-mediated**; farmers do not apply directly via a portal. The bank submits claims on behalf of eligible farmers.  
2. Ensure the loan is classified as a **short-term crop loan** (not term loan or allied activity loan) in the bank’s system.  
3. Verify that the loan is for a **notified crop** under the scheme for the current season (kharif/rabi).  
4. Confirm that the **KCC is tagged** to the loan account if availed via KCC.  
5. Enter the **correct loan amount, date of disbursement, and applicable interest rate** in the bank’s claim format.  
6. Attach **self-declaration** from the farmer stating no duplicate benefit availed.  
7. Ensure **land details and cultivable area** match the loan purpose of the bank’s claim.  
8. Submit claim through the bank’s nodal officer to the sponsor bank or NABARD (as per routing instructions).  

> **Portal Note**: While there is no public-facing application portal for farmers, banks use the **NABARD portal** or ** sponsor bank’s system** for claim submission. Refer to: [https://www.nabard.org](https://www.nabard.org) for guidelines.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Farmer eligibility verified (land, crop, loan type, KCC status)  
- [ ] Loan confirmed as short-term crop credit for notified crop

- [ ] Bank loan sanction/disbursement details match claim requirements  
- [ ] Documents are clear, named correctly, and within size limits  
- [ ] Application ready for bank submission; coordinator liaised with bank branch

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- The bank branch submits the claim on behalf of the farmer to its regional office or sponsor bank.  
- Obtain **acknowledgment receipt** from the bank for submission of documents.  
- Track the **Unique Transaction Reference (UTR)** or claim ID generated by the bank.  
- Store all submission screenshots, acknowledgments, and reference numbers in the case file.  
- Set follow-up reminders at 15, 30, and 45 days post-submission to check claim status with the bank.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Confirm receipt of interest subvention credit in the farmer’s loan account (reduce effective interest rate to 2% per annum, if eligible)  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount matches eligibility (up to ₹3 lakh loan limit; subvention covers difference between actual rate and 7%, capped at 5% subvention)  
- [ ] Obtain/utilize certificate not required; benefit is adjusted directly in loan account  
- [ ] Advise farmer to monitor loan statement for interest debit reflecting subvention  
- [ ] Confirm no further action needed from farmer unless loan is renewed or enhanced  
- [ ] Discuss success fee invoicing with client (if applicable per engagement terms)  
- [ ] Archive case documents and update case status to “Closed – Benefit Availed”  

> **Note**: The subvention is credited periodically by the bank. Farmers should verify their loan statements to ensure the benefit is applied correctly.
