## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the actual financial benefit delivered to clients, ensuring affordability for individual farmers while allowing scalable pricing for institutional clients.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile — processing times are typically 30–60 days with high approval rates for eligible KCC holders, but dependent on timely bank submission and documentation completeness.
3. **Volume Incentives**: Lower success fee percentages at higher tiers encourage engagement with FPOs, cooperatives, and state agencies that can aggregate multiple farmer applications.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Interest Subvention Scheme for Short Term Credit to Farmers has a low to moderate risk profile due to its well-established implementation via NABARD and public sector banks. Evidence shows high uptake among KCC holders, with subvention of 2% per annum on loans up to ₹3 lakh. Processing delays are uncommon if documentation is complete, supporting a 3-month revenue lag assumption in projections.
