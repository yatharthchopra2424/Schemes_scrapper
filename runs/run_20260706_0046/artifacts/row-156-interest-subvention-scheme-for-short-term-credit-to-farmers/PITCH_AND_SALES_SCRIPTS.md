## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I hope you're doing well. I specialise in helping farmers and agri-businesses access government support for short-term credit needs—particularly the Interest Subvention Scheme for Short Term Credit to Farmers, which reduces the cost of borrowing for working capital in agriculture. I’d like to understand your current financing situation to see if this could be relevant for you.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of farming or agri activity are you currently engaged in (e.g., crop cultivation, dairy, fisheries, horticulture)?
- When was your farming entity or agri-business established or registered?
- Do you currently avail short-term crop loans from banks or cooperative societies for purposes like seeds, fertilisers, or pesticides?
- What is the typical loan amount you seek for a single cropping season?
- Are you currently repaying any short-term agricultural loans at an interest rate above 7% per annum?
- Do you have Kisan Credit Card (KCC) facility or are you eligible to obtain one?
- Have you previously applied for or received interest subvention under any government agricultural credit scheme?
- What is the primary challenge you face in managing working capital costs for your farming operations?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Farmer engaged in crop cultivation, horticulture, fisheries, dairy, or other allied agricultural activities  
- [ ] Entity is an individual farmer, joint liability group, or farmer producer organisation (FPO)  
- [ ] Availing short-term crop loan (up to 1 year) from scheduled commercial banks, RRBs, or cooperatives  
- [ ] Loan purpose is for agriculture and allied activities (seeds, fertilisers, pesticides, etc.)  
- [ ] Loan amount does not exceed ₹3 lakh per farmer per season  
- [ ] Borrowing entity is eligible for Kisan Credit Card (KCC) or already holds one  
- [ ] Loan is sourced from a bank participating in the Interest Subvention Scheme  
- [ ] Farmer has not defaulted on previous agricultural loans

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Farmers often face high working capital costs due to interest on short-term crop loans, which can strain cash flow during critical farming cycles. Even with access to credit, effective interest rates frequently exceed 7%, reducing profitability and limiting reinvestment in farm inputs.

**Scheme intro:**
The Interest Subvention Scheme for Short Term Credit to Farmers is a central government initiative administered by the Department of Agriculture, Cooperation & Farmers Welfare under the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. It provides an interest subvention of 2% per annum on short-term crop loans up to ₹3 lakh per farmer, reducing the effective interest rate to as low as 4% per annum for prompt repaying farmers. An additional 3% subvention is available for timely repayment, bringing the effective rate down to 0% for those who repay on time.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in an eligible agricultural activity (e.g., crop cultivation/allied sector)  
- You are availing or planning to avail a short-term crop loan from a participating bank  
- Your loan amount is within the ₹3 lakh limit per season  
- You are eligible for or already hold a Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
