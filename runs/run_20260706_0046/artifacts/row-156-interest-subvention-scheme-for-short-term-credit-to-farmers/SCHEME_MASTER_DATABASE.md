### Interest Subvention Scheme for Short Term Credit to Farmers
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
- **Portal / Application URL:** [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]
- **Current Status:** Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Farmers (individual farmers, joint liability groups, self-help groups, tenant farmers, sharecroppers, oral lessees)
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Agriculture
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be engaged in agricultural activities; short-term crop loans up to ₹3 lakh per farmer per season; loans must be for crop production and post-harvest activities; must be availed through scheduled commercial banks, regional rural banks, or cooperative banks

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 2% interest subvention on short-term crop loans up to ₹3 lakh per farmer per season
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit to loan account (via lending institution)
- Duration of benefit: Annual (per crop season)

#### Documents Required
1. Loan application form
2. Identity proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN, etc.)
3. Address proof
4. Land ownership/tenancy documents (if applicable)
5. Crop details and proposed area of cultivation
6. Bank account details
7. Any other documents as required by the lending institution

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Farmer approaches a participating bank (scheduled commercial bank, regional rural bank, or cooperative bank) for a short-term crop loan
2. Bank verifies eligibility and processes the loan application
3. Upon sanction, bank disburses the loan amount to the farmer
4. Bank claims the interest subvention from the government through the Nodal Agency (NABARD)
5. Government releases the subvention amount to the bank
6. Bank adjusts the subvention against the farmer's loan account, reducing the effective interest rate
