## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
The **Interest Subvention Scheme for Short Term Credit to Farmers** is a government initiative administered by the Ministry of Agriculture. It provides financial support to farmers by subsidizing the interest rate on short-term agricultural loans. The scheme aims to reduce the cost of credit for farmers, thereby enhancing agricultural productivity and ensuring timely access to working capital for cultivation and allied activities.

### Objectives
- To provide affordable short-term credit to farmers for agricultural and allied activities.
- To reduce the effective interest burden on farmers by subvention of interest charged by lending institutions.
- To ensure timely availability of credit during critical agricultural seasons (Kharif and Rabi).
- To promote financial inclusion of small and marginal farmers in the formal banking system.
- To stabilize farm incomes by lowering input financing costs.

### Eligibility Matrix
| Eligibility Criteria | Details | Source |
|----------------------|---------|--------|
| **Beneficiary Category** | Farmers (including individual farmers, joint liability groups, tenant farmers, oral lessees, and sharecroppers) engaged in agricultural and allied activities. | Scheme Design |
| **Landholding Status** | No restriction based on land ownership; tenant farmers and oral lessees are explicitly eligible. | Scheme Guidelines |
| **Loan Type** | Short-term crop loans (up to 12 months) for agricultural and allied activities (e.g., seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, labor, irrigation, storage, marketing). | Scheme Design |
| **Loan Amount Limit** | Maximum eligible loan amount: ₹3 lakh per farmer per annum. | Scheme Guidelines |
| **Geographic Coverage** | All states and union territories of India. | Scheme Design |
| **Lending Institutions** | Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs), Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), Cooperative Banks (including State Cooperative Banks and District Central Cooperative Banks), and Small Finance Banks (SFBs) that are part of the scheme. | Scheme Guidelines |
| **Seasonal Availability** | Available for both Kharif and Rabi seasons; loans must be disbursed within the stipulated cut-off dates for each season. | Scheme Guidelines |
| **Ineligibility** | Loans for non-agricultural purposes, long-term loans (>12 months), loans to non-farmers, defaulters of previous scheme benefits, and loans from non-participating institutions. | Scheme Guidelines |

> **Important Note**: The scheme operates on an annual basis, and eligibility must be re-established each financial year. Farmers must avail loans through participating banks during the notified window for each season.

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Benefit Component | Details | Amount / Rate | Source |
|-------------------|---------|---------------|--------|
| **Interest Subvention Rate** | 2% per annum subvention on the interest rate charged by banks on short-term crop loans. | 2% p.a. | Scheme Guidelines |
| **Effective Interest Rate for Farmer** | If bank interest rate is 7%, farmer pays only 5% after subvention. | Bank rate minus 2% | Scheme Design |
| **Prompt Repayment Incentive** | Additional 3% subvention (total 5%) for timely repayment of loan within the due date. | 3% additional (total 5% p.a.) | Scheme Guidelines |
| **Maximum Subvention Amount** | Calculated on loan amount up to ₹3 lakh; no subvention on amount exceeding ₹3 lakh. | Max ₹6,000 per annum (2% of ₹3 lakh) | Scheme Guidelines |
| **Additional Benefit for Timely Repayment** | Max ₹15,000 per annum (5% of ₹3 lakh) if repaid on time. | Max ₹15,000 per annum | Scheme Guidelines |
| **Funding Mechanism** | 100% central sector scheme; funds released by Ministry of Agriculture to NABARD for onward disbursement to lending institutions. | Central Government Funded | Scheme Design |
| **Claim Process** | Lending institutions submit claims to NABARD after loan disbursement and repayment verification; NABARD settles claims with banks. | Via NABARD | Scheme Guidelines |

> **Warning**: The interest subvention is credited directly to the loan account of the farmer by the bank. Farmers do not receive cash; the benefit reduces their outstanding loan liability.

### Application Process Flowchart
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Farmer approaches participating bank for short-term crop loan] --> B[Bank verifies eligibility: farmer status, loan purpose, amount ≤₹3 lakh, seasonality]
    B --> C{Loan approved?}
    C -->|No| D[Loan application rejected; farmer informed]
    C -->|Yes| E[Bank disburses loan; applies applicable interest rate]
    E --> F[Farmer uses loan for agricultural/allied activities]
    F --> G{Loan repaid within due date?}
    G -->|Yes| H[Bank claims 5% subvention (2% regular + 3% prompt repayment) from NABARD]
    G -->|No| I[Bank claims only 2% regular subvention from NABARD]
    H --> J[NABARD verifies claim and settles amount to bank]
    I --> J
    J --> K[Bank credits subvention amount to farmer's loan account]
    K --> L[Effective interest rate reduced: 5% if timely, 7% if delayed (assuming base rate 7%)]
    L --> M[Farmer benefits from reduced cost of credit]
```

### Key Timelines & Deadlines
| Activity | Timeline | Details |
|---------|----------|---------|
| **Loan Disbursement Window (Kharif)** | April 1 – September 30 | Loans must be disbursed within this period to be eligible for Kharif season subvention. |
| **Loan Disbursement Window (Rabi)** | October 1 – March 31 | Loans must be disbursed within this period to be eligible for Rabi season subvention. |
| **Repayment Due Date** | As per loan agreement (typically harvest-linked) | To avail additional 3% subvention, repayment must occur on or before the due date. |
| **Claim Submission by Banks** | Within 30 days of loan disbursement (initial claim); final claim after repayment verification | Banks submit claims to NABARD in prescribed format. |
| **NABARD Settlement** | Within 15 days of claim receipt | Subject to completeness and verification of documents. |
| **Annual Closure** | March 31 | All claims for the financial year must be submitted by this date; no carry-forward. |

> **Critical Reminder**: Banks must maintain separate records for loans availed under this scheme and submit audited statements to NABARD annually. Non-compliance may lead to recovery of subvention amount.
