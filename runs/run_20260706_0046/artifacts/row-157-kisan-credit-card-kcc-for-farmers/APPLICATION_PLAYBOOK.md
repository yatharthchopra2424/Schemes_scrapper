- [ ] Applicant possesses valid land ownership/tenancy documents or cultivation rights

- [ ] Applicant is willing to undertake crop insurance under PMFBY or WBCIS where applicable

- [ ] Applicant is not availing similar credit facility from another institution for the same purpose

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- **DPR Required?** ❌ No  
  The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme does not require a Detailed Project Report (DPR) for standard crop loans or working capital support.  
  However, for higher loan amounts or term loans under allied activities (e.g., dairy unit setup, fisheries infrastructure), banks may ask for a basic project note.  
  **Action**: Verify with the nodal officer at the respective bank if the loan amount exceeds ₹3 lakhs or involves capital investment.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
Follow these 8 critical rules when filling the KCC application form via the bank’s portal:

1. **Use the correct bank-specific KCC form** – Do not use a generic form; each bank (SBI, PNB, Cooperative Bank, etc.) has its own version.
2. **Enter name exactly as per PAN and Aadhaar** – Any mismatch will trigger rejection during KYC verification.
3. **Specify correct land details** – Include survey number, area (in acres/hectares), and type of ownership (owned/leased/tenant).
4. **Declare all existing loans** – Omission of current agricultural or KCC dues from other banks leads to disqualification.
5. **Select appropriate activity** – Clearly mark primary activity (crops, dairy, fisheries, etc.) and crops to be grown (season-wise).
6. **Mention crop insurance coverage** – Explicitly state willingness to cover loans under PMFBY/WBCIS where applicable.
7. **Set realistic credit limit** – Base the requested limit on scale of finance (SoF) for the crop and area, as notified by DLTC.
8. **Review declarations carefully** – Ensure all undertakings (no default, insurance, end-use) are signed and dated.

> **Portal Note**: Some banks allow online application via their internet banking or mobile app (e.g., YONO SBI, PNB One). Others require physical submission at the branch. Confirm mode with the bank branch.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Collect and verify the approval letter or sanction certificate from the bank
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned credit limit matches the requested amount (or note any reduction with justification)
- [ ] Ensure the Kisan Credit Card is issued and activated (if physical card is provided)
- [ ] Verify that the credit limit is reflected in the client’s account or card statement
- [ ] Advise client to use the KCC only for approved purposes: seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, labor, irrigation, repair of farm equipment, post-harvest storage, and allied activities
- [ ] Monitor utilization and remind client to submit bills/invoices for internal tracking (if required by firm)
- [ ] Ensure timely repayment to avoid interest penalties and maintain creditworthiness
- [ ] Guide client on renewal process (KCC is valid for 5 years with annual review)
- [ ] Assist in obtaining Utilization Certificate (UC) if required for subsidy linkage or audit purposes
- [ ] Follow up for success fee invoicing upon successful sanction and disbursement
- [ ] Maintain all records for audit and future renewal cycles

> **Note**: KCC scheme does not typically require a Utilization Certificate (UC) for routine operations, but UCs may be asked for if the loan is linked to a subsidy scheme (e.g., AMI, RKVY). Confirm with bank branch.
