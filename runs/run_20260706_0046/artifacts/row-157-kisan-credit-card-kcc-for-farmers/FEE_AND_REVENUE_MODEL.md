## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-based alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the financial benefit delivered to farmers through accessible credit under KCC, ensuring affordability for smallholders.
2. **Risk-adjusted pricing**: Success fees account for scheme-specific risks including documentation variability, bank-dependent approval timelines, and seasonal agricultural cycles affecting disbursement.
3. **Tiered accessibility**: Retainer models are scaled to client capacity, with micro-farmers and FPOs eligible for starter-tier support despite limited administrative bandwidth.
4. **Outcome focus**: Revenue is tied to successful credit sanctioning, incentivizing timely and accurate application submission, with fees only payable upon fund availability to the beneficiary.

*Note: The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme exhibits moderate risk due to reliance on individual bank branch processing and periodic guideline updates, though success rates are high for compliant applications based on field-level implementation data.*
