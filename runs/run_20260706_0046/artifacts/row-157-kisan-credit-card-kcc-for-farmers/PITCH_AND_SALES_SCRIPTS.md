## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping farmers and agri-entrepreneurs access government support designed specifically for the agriculture sector. The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme is one of the most impactful tools available today for managing working capital needs in farming — whether for crop cultivation, post-harvest expenses, or allied activities like dairy and fisheries. I’d like to understand your current farming operations to see if this could be a good fit for you.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of farming activity are you primarily engaged in (e.g., crop cultivation, dairy, poultry, fisheries, agroforestry)?
- When was your farming operation established or when did you begin active farming?
- Do you currently own or lease agricultural land, or are you engaged in farming as a tenant or sharecropper?
- Are you looking for short-term credit to cover seasonal input costs such as seeds, fertilisers, pesticides, or labour?
- Have you previously availed of any institutional credit from banks or cooperatives for your farming activities?
- Do you have a Kisan Credit Card already, or are you applying for the first time or seeking renewal?
- Are you able to provide basic identity and land-related documents such as Aadhaar, voter ID, or land ownership/lease records?
- What is your approximate annual turnover or expected credit requirement for the upcoming farming season?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Individual farmer, tenant farmer, sharecropper, oral lessee, or member of Self Help Group (SHG) or Joint Liability Group (JLG)
- [ ] Engaged in crop production, post-harvest activities, or allied agricultural activities (dairy, fisheries, poultry, beekeeping, etc.)
- [ ] Has a valid identity proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN, etc.)
- [ ] Has land ownership/lease documentation or cultivation rights
- [ ] Credit requirement varies by bank; some accept self-declaration for small loans
- [ ] Not a defaulter with any bank or financial institution
- [ ] Falls within the bank’s risk assessment parameters for KCC issuance
- [ ] For renewal: existing KCC holder with satisfactory repayment history

Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re actively involved in farming and could benefit from timely, low-cost credit for your seasonal needs. The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme, offered by banks across India in coordination with NABARD, provides farmers like you with access to affordable working capital — up to ₹3 lakh — to support your agricultural and allied activities. Let me explain how it works and how we can help you apply smoothly.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Farmers often face cash flow gaps during critical stages of cultivation — such as sowing, weeding, or harvesting — when they need to pay for seeds, fertilisers, labour, or fuel. Relying on informal lenders can lead to high interest costs and debt traps. Timely access to institutional credit remains a challenge due to complex documentation, lack of awareness, or perceived eligibility barriers.

**Scheme intro:**
The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) is a government-backed credit facility introduced by the Government of India and implemented through public sector banks, regional rural banks, and cooperatives. It provides farmers with a revolving line of credit for agricultural and allied activities, with a maximum limit of up to ₹3 lakh, depending on landholding, cropping pattern, and income. The scheme offers interest subvention (making effective interest rate as low as 2% per annum) and is designed to be simple, flexible, and accessible.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in farming or allied activities such as dairy, fisheries, or poultry — all eligible under KCC guidelines  
- You possess or can obtain basic identity and land-related documents (Aadhaar, voter ID, land records)  
- You are not currently in default with any bank, making you eligible for fresh or renewed KCC issuance  
- Your credit requirement aligns with the working capital needs assessed under the KCC model (based on scale of finance for your crop/activity)
