### Kisan Credit Card (KCC) for Farmers
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** NABARD (National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nabard.org/auth/writereaddata/tender/1806180417Revised%20KCC%20guidelines.pdf
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Individual farmers, tenant farmers, oral lessees, self-help groups, joint liability groups
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Agriculture, fisheries, animal husbandry
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be engaged in crop production or allied agricultural activities; landholding or cultivation rights required

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Short-term credit limit up to ₹3 lakh (varies by landholding and crop pattern); interest subvention of 2% per annum (effective rate 4% for prompt repayment)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit to KCC-linked account
- Duration of benefit: Annual renewal; valid for 5 years with yearly review

#### Documents Required
1. Duly filled application form
2. Proof of identity (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, etc.)
3. Proof of address (utility bill, ration card, etc.)
4. Land ownership documents or lease agreement
5. Crop details and cultivation plan
6. Passport-sized photographs
7. Bank account details (if applicable)
8. Previous KCC statement (for renewal)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the nearest branch of a participating bank (Cooperative Bank, Regional Rural Bank, or Commercial Bank)
2. Obtain and fill the Kisan Credit Card application form
3. Attach required documents as per checklist
4. Submit the completed application form along with documents
5. Bank verifies the documents and conducts field verification if required
6. Upon approval, bank sanctions the credit limit and issues the Kisan Credit Card
7. Cardholder can withdraw funds using the card at ATMs or through POS terminals for agricultural expenses
8. Repay the utilized amount as per the repayment schedule (usually post-harvest)
9. Renew the card annually by submitting updated documents and crop details
