## Scheme Deep Dive

### Basic Overview
The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme is a Government of India initiative designed to provide timely and adequate credit support to farmers for their agricultural and allied activities. It aims to free farmers from the clutches of informal money lenders by offering institutional credit at affordable rates. The scheme is implemented through a wide network of commercial banks, regional rural banks (RRBs), and cooperative banks across the country.

### Objectives
- To provide short-term credit for cultivation of crops, post-harvest expenses, produce marketing loan, consumption needs of farmer household, working capital for maintenance of farm assets and activities allied to agriculture.
- To provide investment credit for agriculture and allied activities like purchase of equipment such as pumpsets, sprayers, dairy animals, etc.
- To enable farmers to avail revolving cash credit facility involving any number of drawals and repayments within the limit.
- To simplify procedures for availing credit and ensure timely and adequate credit support to farmers.
- To bring all farmers under the institutional credit fold so as to free them from dependence on non-institutional sources of credit.

### Key Takeaways & Warnings
> **WARNING**: The KCC scheme requires mandatory linkage with Aadhaar for DBT of interest subvention benefits. Farmers must ensure their Aadhaar is seeded with their bank account to avail the 4% concessional interest rate.
>
> **KEY TAKEAWAY**: The scheme offers a revolving credit facility, allowing farmers to withdraw and repay multiple times within the sanctioned limit, similar to an overdraft facility, which provides flexibility for managing seasonal cash flows.
>
> **CAVEAT**: While the scheme covers allied activities like dairy, poultry, and fisheries, the documentation and appraisal process may vary significantly between banks for non-crop activities. Farmers should confirm specific requirements with their branch.
