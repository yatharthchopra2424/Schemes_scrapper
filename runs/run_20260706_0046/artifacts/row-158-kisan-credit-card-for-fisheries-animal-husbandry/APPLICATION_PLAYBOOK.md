# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a cultivator, livestock farmer, or fisher engaged in agricultural and allied activities  
- [ ] Applicant requires credit for crop production, fisheries, animal husbandry, or household needs  
- [ ] Applicant is an individual farmer (no specific registration type mandated, but must be engaged in eligible activities)  
- [ ] No explicit turnover threshold mentioned in evidence  
- [ ] No explicit employee count requirement  
- [ ] Sector: Agriculture and allied activities (fisheries, animal husbandry, crop production)  
- [ ] No explicit age of business requirement  
- [ ] Applicant must approach a bank offering Kisan Credit Card facility  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Proof of identity (Aadhaar, PAN, passport, etc.)  
- Proof of address (utility bill, ration card, etc.)  
- Bank account details (passbook, cancelled cheque)  
- PAN card  
- Latest ITR (if applicable)  
- GSTIN (if registered)  
- Udyam Registration (if applicable)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Application form (provided by the bank)  
- Details of fisheries or animal husbandry activities (e.g., farm records, activity description)  
- Land ownership or lease documents (for crop farmers)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and clearly scanned  
- File size should not exceed 5 MB per document  
- Names on documents must match exactly as per application form  
- Self-attested copies acceptable unless notarization is specifically requested by the bank  
- Documents must be in Hindi or English; vernacular languages require certified translation  

### Client Communication Template
**Subject:** Application for Kisan Credit Card (KCC) for Fisheries & Animal Husbandry Activities  

Dear [Client Name],  

This is to inform you that you are eligible to apply for the Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme under the Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying. The KCC provides timely and affordable credit support for crop production, fisheries, animal husbandry, and household requirements through a credit card linked to your bank account.  

To proceed, please approach any bank offering KCC facility and submit the following:  
- Completed application form  
- Proof of identity and address  
- Bank account details  
- Details of your fisheries or animal husbandry activities

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for Kisan Credit Card application. The scheme does not mandate a Detailed Project Report (DPR). However, applicants may be asked to provide details of their fisheries or animal husbandry activities as part of the application form. Verify with the nodal officer or bank if any additional project details are needed for credit limit assessment.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Fill all fields in the application form accurately; leave no blank sections unless marked optional  
2. Ensure name, address, and bank details match exactly with supporting documents  
3. Clearly specify the type of activity: fisheries, animal husbandry, or crop production  
4. Provide accurate details of landholding or water area used for fisheries/animal husbandry (if applicable)  
5. Disclose any existing loans or credit facilities to avoid over-leveraging  
6. State the purpose of credit: short-term loans for production, allied activities, or household needs  
7. Sign and date the declaration section affirming use of funds only for approved purposes  
8. Attach all required documents in the order specified by the bank  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Application form is completely filled and signed  
- [ ] Proof of identity and address are attached and valid  
- [ ] Bank account details are correct and active  
- [ ] Details of fisheries/animal husbandry activities are provided  
- [ ] Land documents attached (if applicant is a crop farmer)  
- [ ] Declarations regarding end-use of credit are signed  
- [ ] All documents are legible, properly named, and within size limits

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed application form and documents to the bank branch offering KCC facility  
- Obtain an acknowledgment receipt with date, time, and token number  
- Take a clear screenshot or scan of the acknowledgment for records  
- Store the application reference number (if provided by the bank)  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 7–10 working days after submission

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Collect the approval letter or KCC issuance confirmation from the bank  
- [ ] Verify that the KCC is linked to the correct bank account  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned credit limit (based on landholding, cropping pattern, and scale of fisheries/animal husbandry activities)  
- [ ] Ensure the card is activated and ready for use  
- [ ] Maintain records of all withdrawals and repayments for utilization certificate (if required by bank)  
- [ ] Track usage to ensure funds are used only for approved purposes: crop production, fisheries, animal husbandry, and household needs  
- [ ] Monitor repayment schedule to maintain creditworthiness and continue availing benefits  
- [ ] Inform client about the need for timely repayment to avoid suspension or cancellation of KCC privileges  
- [ ] Follow up with client after 30 days to confirm successful utilization and repayment behavior  
- [ ] Prepare for renewal process (KCC is typically renewed annually subject to satisfactory performance)
