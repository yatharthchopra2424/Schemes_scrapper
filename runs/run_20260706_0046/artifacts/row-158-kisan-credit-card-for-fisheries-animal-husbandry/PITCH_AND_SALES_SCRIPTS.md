## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialise in helping fishers, fish farmers, livestock farmers, and cultivators access government credit schemes like the Kisan Credit Card for Fisheries & Animal Husbandry. This scheme provides timely and affordable short-term loans for crop production, fisheries, animal husbandry, and household needs through a credit card linked to your bank account. I’d like to understand your current credit needs and see if this could be a good fit for your operations.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of activity are you primarily engaged in—crop farming, livestock rearing, fisheries, or a combination?
2. How long have you been actively involved in this activity?
3. Do you currently have a bank account where you receive income or manage transactions?
4. Are you looking for short-term credit to cover input costs like seed, feed, or maintenance during production cycles?
5. Do you need flexible access to funds as and when required, rather than a lump-sum loan?
6. Are you able to provide proof of your activity—such as land records, livestock details, or fisheries operation details?
7. Have you previously accessed any government credit or Kisan Credit Card facility?
8. What is your primary challenge in accessing timely credit for your operations?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Engaged in cultivation, livestock farming, or fisheries (agricultural and allied activities)
- [ ] Requires credit for crop production, fisheries, animal husbandry, or household needs
- [ ] Has or can open a bank account for KCC linkage
- [ ] Can provide proof of identity and address
- [ ] Can provide details of fisheries or animal husbandry activities (if applicable)
- [ ] Can provide land ownership/lease documents (if crop farmer)
- [ ] Intends to use credit only for approved purposes (crop, fisheries, animal husbandry, household)
- [ ] Understands repayment is required to maintain creditworthiness

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re engaged in [livestock/fisheries/crop] activities and need flexible, short-term credit for inputs and household needs. The Kisan Credit Card for Fisheries & Animal Husbandry is designed exactly for this—it provides a credit card linked to your bank account, letting you withdraw funds as needed for approved purposes. Would you like me to walk you through how it works and what you’d be eligible?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Fishers, livestock farmers, and cultivators often face delays in accessing credit for seeds, feed, maintenance, or household needs. Traditional loans can be slow, inflexible, and require lump-sum disbursement, making it hard to manage working capital efficiently during production cycles.

**Scheme intro:**
The Kisan Credit Card for Fisheries & Animal Husbandry**, administered by the Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying. It is a loan-based scheme where eligible farmers are issued a credit card linked to their bank account, enabling flexible withdrawal of short-term funds for crop production, fisheries, animal husbandry, and household requirements. The credit limit is based on landholding, cropping pattern, and scale of fisheries/animal husbandry activities.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in [livestock/fisheries/crop] farming, which falls under agricultural and allied activities
- You require credit for [fisheries/animal husbandry/crop production] and household needs
- You have or can open a bank account to link the KCC
- You can provide proof of identity, address, and details of your activity
