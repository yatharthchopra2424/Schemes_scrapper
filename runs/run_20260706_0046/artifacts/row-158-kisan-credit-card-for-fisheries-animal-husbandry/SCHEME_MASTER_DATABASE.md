### Kisan Credit Card for Fisheries & Animal Husbandry

- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Cultivators, livestock farmers, and fishers engaged in agricultural and allied activities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Agriculture and allied activities (crop production, fisheries, animal husbandry)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Requires credit for crop production, fisheries, animal husbandry, and household needs

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Short-term loans for crop production, allied activities, and household requirements; credit limit based on landholding, cropping pattern, and scale of fisheries/animal husbandry activities
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (credit card linked to bank account for flexible withdrawal of funds)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (renewable based on repayment and eligibility)

#### Documents Required
1. Application form
2. Proof of identity
3. Proof of address
4. Land ownership or lease documents (for crop farmers)
5. Details of fisheries or animal husbandry activities
6. Bank account details

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Approach a bank offering Kisan Credit Card facility.
2. Submit application form along with required documents.
3. Bank verifies eligibility and land/crop/fisheries/animal husbandry details.
4. Upon approval, KCC is issued linked to the bank account.
5. Cardholder can withdraw funds as needed for approved purposes.
