## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the scheme’s actual financial benefit—central share grants-in-aid released to State Governments/UTs for fisher welfare activities (housing, sanitation, livelihood support, etc.), with no fixed upper limit per beneficiary but subject to fund availability and budget allocations.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: The scheme operates on a rolling basis with proposals appraised for technical and financial feasibility; funds released in installments based on utilization certificates and progress reports, introducing moderate administrative and compliance risk.
3. **Transparency & Compliance**: Success fees are contingent on submission of utilization certificates and progress reports, aligning consultant incentives with scheme milestones and reducing risk of non-payment.
4. **Scalable Engagement**: Retainer covers proposal preparation, liaison with State Governments/UTs, and documentation support; success fee scales with the central grant amount sanctioned, reflecting the scheme’s reliance on State-level implementation and central fund release.
