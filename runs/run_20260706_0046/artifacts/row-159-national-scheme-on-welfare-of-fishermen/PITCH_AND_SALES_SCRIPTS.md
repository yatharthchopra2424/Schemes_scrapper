# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping traditional and small-scale fishermen, fish farmers, and members of fisher communities access government welfare schemes. The National Scheme on Welfare of Fishermen provides financial assistance for housing, sanitation, drinking water, community assets, livelihood support, accident insurance, health coverage, and skill development. I’d like to understand your work and see if this scheme could support your community’s needs.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your primary activity—capture fisheries, aquaculture, or allied activities?  
2. Are you a citizen of India and part of a fisher community identified by your State Government/UT as economically weaker section?  
3. Are you engaged in traditional or small-scale fishing or fish farming?  
4. Has your State Government/UT previously submitted project proposals for fisher welfare activities under this scheme?  
5. Do you have access to basic amenities like housing, sanitation, and drinking water in your community?  
6. Are you part of a fisher cooperative, self-help group, or interested in forming one?  
7. Have you received any skill development or training support for fisheries-related activities?  
8. Do you currently have access to accident insurance or health coverage through any government program?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Citizen of India  
- [ ] Member of a fisher community (traditional/small-scale fishermen, fish farmers, or allied activities)  
- [ ] Identified by State Government/UT as economically weaker section based on socio-economic criteria  
- [ ] Engaged in capture fisheries, aquaculture, or allied activities  
- [ ] Requires support for housing, sanitation, drinking water, community assets, livelihood, insurance, health, cooperatives, skill development, or equipment subsidies  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you may qualify for the National Scheme on Welfare of Fishermen, which provides central financial assistance to State Governments/UTs for welfare activities including livelihood support, insurance, and infrastructure—delivered as grants-in-aid based on approved proposals. Shall I outline how this works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Traditional and small-scale fisher communities often lack access to basic welfare amenities like housing, sanitation, drinking water, accident insurance, health coverage, and livelihood support, which limits their socio-economic development and resilience.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Scheme on Welfare of Fishermen, administered by the Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, provides central share financial assistance to State Governments/UTs for implementation of welfare activities. Support includes financial aid for housing, sanitation, drinking water, community assets, livelihood support, accident insurance, health coverage, fisher cooperatives/self-help groups, skill development, and subsidies for fisheries-related equipment and infrastructure—released as grants-in-aid based on approved project proposals and utilization certificates.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a traditional or small-scale fisher/fish farmer engaged in capture fisheries or aquaculture  
- You are a citizen of India belonging to an economically weaker section as identified by your State Government/UT  
- You require support in areas such as housing, sanitation, drinking water, livelihood, insurance, or skill development

**Next step:**
We offer a free eligibility check with no commitment. If you qualify, we assist with documentation and coordination with your State Government/UT on a success fee basis only.

## 3. Objection Handlers

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I've heard these schemes never get approved" | The scheme operates on a rolling basis with proposals accepted throughout the year as per budget cycles. Approvals are based on technical and financial feasibility appraisal by the Department of Fisheries, and funds are released in installments upon submission of utilization certificates and progress reports—ensuring accountability and steady disbursement. |
| "How much do you charge?" | [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT] |
| "I'll try it myself first" | Common mistakes include submitting proposals without proper beneficiary lists and selection criteria, and failing to provide utilization certificates for previous funds—both mandatory for fund release. |
| "We're too small / too big" | The scheme targets traditional and small-scale fishermen, fish farmers, and fisher communities—there are no strict revenue or employee thresholds, but eligibility is based on socio-economic criteria defined by State Governments/UTs for economically weaker sections. |
| "I don't have the documents ready" | The three most commonly missing documents are: beneficiary list and selection criteria, utilization certificates of previous funds, and detailed project proposal from the State Government/UT. |
| "We already work with a CA" | "Great — we work alongside CAs. They handle your accounts; we handle the scheme strategy and applications." |
