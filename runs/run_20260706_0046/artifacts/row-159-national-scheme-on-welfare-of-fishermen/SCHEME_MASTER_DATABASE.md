### National Scheme on Welfare of Fishermen
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dof.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Traditional and small-scale fishermen, fish farmers, and members of fisher communities engaged in capture fisheries, aquaculture, or allied activities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Fisheries (capture fisheries, aquaculture, allied activities)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Citizens of India belonging to economically weaker sections as identified by State Governments/UTs based on socio-economic criteria

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance for housing, sanitation, drinking water, community assets, livelihood support; access to accident insurance and health coverage; support for formation of fisher cooperatives and self-help groups; skill development and training programs; subsidies for fisheries-related equipment and infrastructure
- Disbursement mode: Grants-in-aid (central share released to State Governments/UTs based on approved project proposals and utilization certificates)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (based on approved project proposals)

#### Documents Required
1. Project proposal from State Government/UT
2. Utilization certificates of previous funds
3. Progress reports on implementation
4. Beneficiary list and selection criteria
5. Bank account details of implementing agency

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. State Governments/UTs submit project proposals for fisher welfare activities to the Department of Fisheries.
2. Proposals are appraised by the Department for technical and financial feasibility.
3. Approved projects are sanctioned with central share allocation.
4. Funds are released in installments based on utilization certificates and progress reports.
5. States implement the schemes through designated agencies and monitor outcomes.
