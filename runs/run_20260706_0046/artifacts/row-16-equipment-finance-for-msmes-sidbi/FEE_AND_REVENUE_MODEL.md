## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered (loan amount up to ₹3 Crore).
2. Transparent fee structure with no hidden charges; all fees disclosed upfront.
3. Risk-adjusted pricing reflecting scheme processing time (typically 48 hours for in-principle offer) and high success rate due to automated platform and minimal documentation.
4. Success fees contingent on loan sanction and disbursement, ensuring alignment with client outcomes.
