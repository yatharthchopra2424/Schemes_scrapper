# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping MSMEs access affordable financing for machinery and equipment upgrades through government-backed schemes. Many manufacturers and service providers struggle with upfront capital costs when modernizing operations—I’d like to understand your current situation to see if the SIDBI Machinery Loan scheme could support your growth plans.

### Discovery Questions
1. How long has your MSME unit been in operation?
2. Do you have a valid Udyam Registration and GST registration?
3. Are you planning to procure new machinery or upgrade existing equipment for your manufacturing/service operations?
4. What is your approximate budget for the machinery investment you’re considering?
5. Have you had any defaults with banks or financial institutions in the past?
6. What were your cash profits in your last audited financial year?
7. Are you prepared to provide a fixed deposit as security (typically 25% of the loan amount) for higher financing?
8. Do you currently maintain GST returns, ITRs, bank statements, and have access to your CIBIL score for application purposes?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] MSME unit in operation for at least three years
- [ ] Mandatory Udyam & GST registration
- [ ] No defaults to Bank/FIs
- [ ] Cash profit in last audited financial results
- [ ] Prepared to provide 25% security in form of fixed deposit for up to 100% financing
- [ ] Ability to submit GST returns, ITRs, Bank Statement, and CIBIL score

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you meet several key criteria for the SIDBI Machinery Loan scheme—particularly your operational history, registrations, and profit status. This scheme offers loans up to Rs. 3 Crore with immediate in-principle approval and flexible repayment terms, which could directly support your equipment upgrade plans. Let me walk you through how it works.
