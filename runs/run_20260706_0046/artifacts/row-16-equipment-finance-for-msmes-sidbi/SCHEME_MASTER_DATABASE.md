### Equipment Finance for MSMEs (SIDBI)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://sidbi.in/machinery-loan
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: MSME units
- Age of business: At least three years in operation
- Sector: Manufacturing or service sector
- Revenue cap: Not specified in evidence
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: Mandatory Udyam & GST registration; No defaults to Bank/FIs; Cash profit in last audited financial results

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Loan amount up to Rs. 3 Crore
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit
- Duration of benefit: Repayment upto 60 months

#### Documents Required
1. GST returns
2. ITRs
3. Bank Statement
4. CIBIL score

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Apply Now through the SIDBI portal
2. Submit GST returns, ITRs, Bank Statement and CIBIL score
3. Complete registration and KYC
4. Receive sanction and acceptance of loan limit
5. Execute digital documents and NACH mandate
6. Receive disbursement
