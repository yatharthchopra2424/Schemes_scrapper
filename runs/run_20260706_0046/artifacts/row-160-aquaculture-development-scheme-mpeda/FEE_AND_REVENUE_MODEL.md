## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client under the Aquaculture Development Scheme (MPEDA).
2. Retainer covers proposal preparation, documentation, and liaison with MPEDA/NaCSA/RGCA.
3. Success fee is contingent on subsidy approval and disbursement, reducing client financial risk.
4. Scheme-specific note: The Aquaculture Development Scheme has a moderate risk profile due to its demand-driven nature and annual budget dependency; processing times range from 4–6 months for mini labs and 6 months to 2 years for certification support, which affects revenue realization timing but not eligibility or subsidy amounts.
