# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping aquaculture farmers, exporters, MSMEs, and fisheries cooperatives access government support for infrastructure, technology upgradation, and value addition in the marine products sector. The Aquaculture Development Scheme (MPEDA) is designed specifically for beneficiaries like you who are engaged in sustainable aquaculture practices and looking to enhance export potential through better management practices and value addition initiatives.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of aquaculture activity are you currently engaged in (e.g., shrimp farming, fish hatchery, mud crab culture)?
2. Are you registered with MPEDA or its affiliated societies like NaCSA or RGCA?
3. What is the size of your farm or facility in hectares or acres?
4. Are you practicing cluster-based farming or Better Management Practices (BMPs)?
5. Do you have export potential or are you already exporting marine products?
6. Are you planning to invest in infrastructure such as cold storage, processing machinery, or hatchery development?
7. Have you obtained environmental and pollution clearance certificates for your operations?
8. Do you have training completion certificates for capacity building components you’ve undertaken?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Registered with MPEDA, NaCSA, or RGCA  
- [ ] Engaged in aquaculture farming, export, MSME, or fisheries cooperative activities  
- [ ] Priority given to small-scale and marginal farmers adopting cluster-based farming and BMPs  
- [ ] Exporters must demonstrate export potential and compliance with quality standards for value addition, cold storage, or processing infrastructure  
- [ ] Project proposal must include objectives, methodology, budget, and expected outcomes  
- [ ] Proof of land ownership or lease agreement available  
- [ ] Financial solvency certificate or bank statement available  
- [ ] Quotations for machinery/equipment to be procured  
- [ ] Environmental and pollution clearance certificates (if applicable)  
- [ ] Training completion certificates (for capacity building components)  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you may be eligible for the Aquaculture Development Scheme (MPEDA), which provides financial assistance as a subsidy for eligible components like cold storage, processing machinery, hatchery development, and farm infrastructure. The support is disbursed through NaCSA and RGCA after verification, and the amount is based on the scope of your project, with higher support for value addition and technology adoption initiatives.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Aquaculture farmers and exporters face high capital costs for infrastructure, technology upgradation, and value addition units, which limits their ability to adopt sustainable practices, diversify species, and meet international quality standards for export markets.

**Scheme intro:**
The Aquaculture Development Scheme (MPEDA), implemented by the Marine Products Export Development Authority (MPEDA) under the Ministry of Commerce & Industry, Government of India, provides financial assistance as a subsidy as a subsidy for eligible components such as cold storage, processing machinery, hatchery development, and farm infrastructure. The scheme operates on a demand-driven basis with budgetary support allocated annually, and assistance is provided based on the scope of the project, with higher support for value addition and technology adoption initiatives.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are registered with MPEDA or its affiliated societies (NaCSA/RGCA)  
- You are engaged in aquaculture farming, export, MSME, or fisheries cooperative activities  
- You are adopting or planning to adopt cluster-based farming and Better Management Practices (BMPs)  
- You have export potential or are already exporting, which qualifies you for prioritized support for value addition, cold storage, or processing infrastructure
