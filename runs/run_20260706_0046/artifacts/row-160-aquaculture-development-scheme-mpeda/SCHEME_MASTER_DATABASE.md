### Aquaculture Development Scheme (MPEDA)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Marine Products Export Development Authority (MPEDA), Ministry of Commerce & Industry, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://e-mpeda.nic.in/registration/Reg_login.aspx
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (applications accepted throughout the year subject to budget availability)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Aquaculture farmers, exporters, fisheries cooperatives, MSMEs engaged in marine products sector
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Marine products / Aquaculture
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be registered with MPEDA or its affiliated societies (NaCSA/RGCA); priority to small-scale and marginal farmers adopting cluster-based farming and Better Management Practices (BMPs); exporters seeking support for value addition, cold storage, or processing infrastructure must demonstrate export potential and compliance with quality standards

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance as a subsidy for eligible components (cold storage, processing machinery, hatchery development, farm infrastructure); assistance based on project scope with higher support for value addition and technology adoption; disbursed through NaCSA and RGCA after verification
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (through NaCSA and RGCA)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (project-based, demand-driven)

#### Documents Required
1. Registration certificate with MPEDA or affiliated societies (NaCSA/RGCA)
2. Project report detailing objectives, methodology, and budget
3. Proof of land ownership or lease agreement for farm/infrastructure
4. Bank statement or financial solvency certificate
5. Quotations for machinery/equipment to be procured
6. Environmental and pollution clearance certificates (if applicable)
7. Training completion certificates (for capacity building components)
8. Utilization certificates and progress reports (for subsequent installments)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the relevant component of the scheme (value addition, infrastructure, training, etc.)
2. Prepare a detailed project proposal including objectives, budget, and expected outcomes
3. Submit the proposal through the respective Regional/Sub-Regional Office of MPEDA or via NaCSA/RGCA for aquaculture-specific interventions
4. Upload required documents such as registration certificates, project reports, and financial statements
5. Await technical and financial appraisal by MPEDA officials
6. Upon approval, receive sanction letter and proceed with implementation
7. Submit utilization certificates and progress reports for fund release
