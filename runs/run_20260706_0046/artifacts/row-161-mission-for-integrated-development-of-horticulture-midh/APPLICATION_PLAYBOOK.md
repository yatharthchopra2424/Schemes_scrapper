# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Farmer / beneficiary (individual or group)  
- [ ] Registered as farmer, farmer group, FIG/FPO, FPC, or rural youth  
- [ ] Located in selected districts of 18 States and 6 Union Territories (for NHM) OR in North Eastern States and Himalayan States (for HMNEH)  
- [ ] Engaged in horticulture sector (fruits, vegetables, root & tuber crops, mushrooms, spices, flowers, aromatic plants, coconut, cashew, cocoa, bamboo)  
- [ ] Willing to contact the Horticulture Officer of the concerned district for availing benefits  
- [ ] For aggregation benefits: willingness to form or join farmer groups (FIGs/FPOs/FPCs)  
- [ ] For skill development: rural youth interested in horticulture and post-harvest management, especially cold chain sector  
- [ ] For micro-irrigation: intent to improve water use efficiency  
- [ ] For planting material: access to quality germplasm  

*Source: MIDH Key Facts – Eligibility and Target Beneficiaries; MIDH Suraksha portal (https://midh.gov.in) – Workflow and contact details*

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card)  
- Address Proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, Utility Bill)  
- Bank Account Details (cancelled cheque or bank passbook)  
- PAN Card  
- GST Registration (if applicable)  
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs/FPOs/FPCs)  
- Income Tax Returns (last 2 years, if applicable)  
- Land ownership/lease documents (7/12 extract, patta, lease agreement)  
- Caste Certificate (if claiming SC/ST benefits)  
- Passport-sized photographs  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Farmer registration certificate from State Horticulture Mission (SHM) or District Horticulture Office  
- Project proposal detailing horticulture activity (area, crop, technology)  
- Certificate of aggregation (for FIGs/FPOs/FPCs) from registering authority  
- Training completion certificate (for skill development under rural youth)  
- Quotation from supplier for micro-irrigation, planting material, or cold chain equipment  
- Land use conversion certificate (if applicable for protected cultivation)  
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) from Pollution Control Board (for processing units)  
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) for post-harvest management, processing, or cold chain projects  

*Source: MIDH Suraksha portal (https://midh.gov.in) – Workflow and contact details; Key Facts – Application Process and Target Beneficiaries*

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)  
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ClientName_Date.pdf` (e.g., `MIDH_LandProof_RajuSharma_20240515.pdf`)  
- Aadhaar and PAN numbers must match across all documents  
- Land documents must be clearly visible, with survey number and owner name  
- Bank details must include IFSC, account number, and account holder name matching PAN/Aadhaar  
- Certificates must be issued by competent authority and bear official seal/signature  
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in regional language, attach certified translation  
- Udyam/FPO registration must be active and verifiable on respective portals

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required for projects involving post-harvest management, processing, cold chain infrastructure, or value addition under MIDH.  

**Standard DPR Sections:**  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Background and Rationale  
- Objectives and Expected Outcomes  
- Technical Details (crop, area, technology, layout)  
- Financial Analysis (cost estimates, funding pattern, profitability)  
- Implementation Schedule  
- Management Structure  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation  
- Environmental and Social Impact  
- Sustainability Plan  
- Annexures (quotations, land documents, approvals)  

*Note: For simple area expansion or planting material support, a DPR may not be mandatory. Verify with the District Horticulture Officer or nodal officer.*  

*Source: MIDH Suraksha portal (https://midh.gov.in) – Workflow and Key Facts on post-harvest management, processing, and marketing*

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the MIDH Suraksha portal at [https://midh.gov.in](https://midh.gov.in) and click on “Login” or “Register” if new user.  
2. Use Aadhaar-based OTP login for secure authentication.  
3. Select the relevant sub-scheme (NHM, HMNEH, NHB, CDB, etc.) based on beneficiary type and location.  
4. Fill in personal/farmer details exactly as per Aadhaar and bank records.  
5. Enter land details (survey number, area, ownership) matching land documents.  
6. Specify the horticulture activity (e.g., vegetable cultivation, mango orchard, cold storage) and crop details.  
7. Upload all required documents in PDF format, ensuring file names follow the prescribed format.  
8. Declare that all information is true and correct; tick the consent clause for Aadhaar-based payment (ABP).  
9. Preview the form before final submission to avoid errors.  
10. Submit and save the application reference number for tracking.  

*Source: MIDH Suraksha portal (https://midh.gov.in) – Login, Workflow, and Universalization of Aadhaar Based Payment (ABP) notice (10th March, 2026)*

- [ ] Land details are consistent in application and land documents  
- [ ] Bank account details are correct and active  
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents are attached and legible  
- [ ] Declarations and consent clauses are signed/ticked

- [ ] No blank mandatory fields in the form

- [ ] Application is submitted within the financial year for better fund allocation chances

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the MIDH Suraksha portal at [https://midh.gov.in](https://midh.gov.in)  
- Immediately after submission, take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing the **Application Reference Number** and timestamp  
- Store the screenshot in the case folder with name: `MIDH_SubmissionConfirmation_CaseID_Date.png`  
- Save the application reference number in the case tracker and set automated reminders for follow-up at 15, 30, and 45 days  
- Inform the client of successful submission and share the reference number

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from the District Horticulture Officer or State Horticulture Mission  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned amount and component-wise breakup (e.g., planting material, micro-irrigation, training)  
- [ ] Ensure funds are disbursed via Aadhaar Based Payment (ABP) to the beneficiary’s bank account  
- [ ] Guide client on utilization of funds as per approved project and MIDH guidelines  
- [ ] Assist in maintaining bills, vouchers, and utilization records for audit purposes  
- [ ] Remind client to submit Utilization Certificate (UC) within the stipulated time (usually within 12 months of fund release)  
- [ ] Verify that UC is submitted and approved by the concerned officer  
- [ ] Track project progress and ensure timely completion as per implementation schedule  
- [ ] Assist in claiming success fee only after satisfactory utilization and UC approval  
- [ ] Advise client on renewal or next-phase applications under MIDH for subsequent years  

*Source: MIDH Suraksha portal (https://midh.gov.in) – Workflow, Key Facts on financial support, and Universalization of Aadhaar Based Payment (ABP) notice (10th March, 2026)*
