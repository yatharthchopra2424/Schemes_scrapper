# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping farmers, farmer groups, and rural youth access government support for holistic horticulture development—covering fruits, vegetables, spices, flowers, coconut, cashew, cocoa, and bamboo through the Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH). Before we explore whether this fits your situation, I’d like to understand your current horticulture activities and goals to see if MIDH’s support for production enhancement, post-harvest management, or skill development could benefit you.

### Discovery Questions
1. What horticulture crops or activities are you currently engaged in (e.g., fruits, vegetables, flowers, spices, coconut, cashew, cocoa, bamboo)?
2. Are you an individual farmer, part of a farmer group (FIG/FPO/FPC), or involved in training rural youth in horticulture or post-harvest management?
3. In which state or district are you located? (MIDH is implemented via State Horticulture Missions in 18 states and 6 UTs for NHM, and in NE/Himalayan states for HMNEH.)
4. What is the scale of your operation—area under cultivation, number of beneficiaries in your group, or youth trained?
5. Are you looking to improve productivity through quality planting material, micro-irrigation, or protected cultivation?
6. Do you need support for post-harvest management, processing, marketing, or cold chain infrastructure?
7. Have you previously accessed any horticulture-related government schemes or subsidies?
8. What are your main goals for the next 12–24 months—expanding area, increasing income, adopting new technology, or creating employment?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Target beneficiary: Individual farmer, farmer group (FIG/FPO/FPC), or rural youth
- [ ] Located in a state/district covered under NHM (18 states + 6 UTs) or HMNEH (NE/Himalayan states)
- [ ] Engaged in eligible horticulture sectors: fruits, vegetables, root & tuber crops, mushrooms, spices, flowers, aromatic plants, coconut, cashew, cocoa, bamboo
- [ ] Seeking support for: production enhancement, post-harvest management, processing, marketing, micro-irrigation, quality germplasm, or skill development
- [ ] Willing to work with District Horticulture Officer and State-level agencies for approval

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re involved in [mention specific activity, e.g., vegetable farming or farmer group formation] and looking to improve [productivity/income/post-harvest capacity]. The Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH) offers subsidies and technical support exactly for these activities—implemented through your State Horticulture Mission. Let me explain how it works and what support you could access.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Farmers and horticulture entrepreneurs face challenges in accessing quality planting material, adopting water-efficient technologies like micro-irrigation, managing post-harvest losses, and accessing markets—especially when operating individually or without technical and financial support for aggregation, processing, or cold chain infrastructure.

**Scheme intro:**
The Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH), a Centrally Sponsored Scheme by the Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India, provides financial and technical support for holistic horticulture development. It covers fruits, vegetables, root & tuber crops, mushrooms, spices, flowers, aromatic plants, coconut, cashew, cocoa, and bamboo. While the Government of India contributes 85% of the outlay in most states (100% in NE/Himalayan states and for NHB, CDB, CIH, Nagaland, and NLAs), state governments contribute the remaining share. Support is delivered through State Horticulture Missions (SHMs), NHB, CDB, CIH, and National Level Agencies.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in eligible horticulture sectors such as [farmer’s crop/activity, e.g., vegetables, fruits, or bamboo]
- You are located in a state/district covered under NHM or HMNEH (as per MIDH’s implementation in 18 states + 6 UTs for NHM and NE/Himalayan states for HMNEH)
- You seek support for activities like [e.g., micro-irrigation, protected cultivation, post-harvest management, or farmer group formation] which are core components of MIDH
- You are willing to coordinate with the District Horticulture Officer for block-level verification and state-level forwarding

| "I'll try it myself first" | Common mistakes include submitting incomplete data at the village level without verifying eligibility for specific interventions (e.g., micro-irrigation or protected cultivation), and skipping district-level verification by the Horticulture Officer, which is mandatory for block project approval. We ensure your application meets all criteria at each stage. |
| "We're too small / too big" | MIDH has no strict revenue or employee thresholds—it serves individual farmers, farmer groups (FIGs/FPOs/FPCs), and rural youth. Eligibility is based on engagement in eligible horticulture activities and location in an implementing district, not scale. Even smallholder farmers in states like Mizoram (300 beneficiaries) or large FPCs in Tamil Nadu (23,393 beneficiaries) have benefited. |
| "I don't have the documents ready" | The three most commonly missing documents are: (1) land ownership or lease proof for the cultivation area, (2) detailed project report for post-harvest or processing units, and (3) bank account details linked to Aadhaar for DBT-enabled subsidy transfer. We help you prepare these in advance. |
