### Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH), Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://midh.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Farmers, farmer groups, FIGs/FPOs, FPCs, rural youth
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Horticulture (fruits, vegetables, root & tuber crops, mushrooms, spices, flowers, aromatic plants, coconut, cashew, cocoa, bamboo)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must contact the Horticulture Officer of the concerned district for availing benefits; implemented by State Horticulture Missions (SHM) in selected districts of 18 States and 6 Union Territories for NHM, and in North Eastern States and Himalayan States for HMNEH

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Government of India (GOI) contributes 85% of total outlay for developmental programmes in all states except North East and Himalayas (15% by State Governments); 100% GOI contribution for North Eastern States, Himalayan States, and for development of bamboo and programmes of NHB, CDB, CIH, Nagaland, and National Level Agencies (NLA)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (via online submission and approval workflow)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (project-based assistance)

#### Documents Required
1. Application form (submitted online via Hortnet Portal)
2. Farmer/group identification details
3. Land ownership/lease documents
4. Project proposal detailing intervention (e.g., micro irrigation, post-harvest management, processing, marketing)
5. Bank account details for direct benefit transfer
6. Any additional documents as specified by the District Horticulture Officer or State Level Agency

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Village Level: Farmers submit necessary data swiftly and easily via online forms instead of going to government offices.
2. Block Level: Farmer data is verified to ensure it fits the criteria for the programs to which it qualifies.
3. District Level: The District Horticulture Officer makes a decision regarding the approval of the block horticulture project.
4. State Level: The project is forwarded to the state-level agricultural or horticultural department.
5. Ministry Level: If the project meets the necessary criteria, it may receive approval at the ministry level.
