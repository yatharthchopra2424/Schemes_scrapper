## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation/Registration Certificate (for SHG, FPO, NGO, cooperative, etc.)  
- PAN Card of the entity or authorized signatory  
- Latest Income Tax Return (ITR) – if applicable  
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- Bank Statement (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs, if applicable)  
- Aadhaar Card of authorized signatory  
- Land Ownership/Lease Document (7/12, sale deed, lease agreement, etc.)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Project Proposal as per NHM component guidelines (e.g., nursery, polyhouse, pack house, tissue culture unit, etc.)  
- Quotations for inputs, equipment, and civil works from at least two authorized suppliers  
- Training Certificates (if required for the specific component, e.g., protected cultivation, organic farming)  
- Detailed Cost Estimate of the project  
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) from concerned department (if required, e.g., for water extraction, pollution)  
- Affidavit confirming no duplicate assistance for the same activity/component  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)  
- Name on Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account must match exactly  
- Land documents must be clear, registered, and in the name of the applicant or show valid lease  
- Quotations must be on supplier letterhead, valid for at least 30 days, and include GSTIN  
- Project proposal must be signed and stamped by the applicant and, if applicable, countersigned by a technical expert  
- Affidavits and declarations must be notarized on non-judicial stamp paper of appropriate value  
- All documents must be in English or Hindi; vernacular documents must be accompanied by a certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a **Project Proposal** is required (as per NHM Key Facts under "Required Documents" and "Application Process"). It must be prepared as per the guidelines of the specific NHM component.  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Background and Objectives  
- Project Description (activities, technology, scale)  
- Location and Land Details  
- Technical Feasibility  
- Financial Viability (cost breakdown, means of finance, subsidy calculation)  
- Implementation Timeline (Gantt chart)  
- Management Structure  
- Marketing and Post-Harvest Plan  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation  
- Expected Outcomes (yield, income, employment)  
- Annexures: Land documents, quotations, training certificates, affidavits  

> **Note**: Verify exact format and annexure requirements with the nodal State Horticulture Mission (SHM) or District Horticulture Office, as guidelines may vary by component and state.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the official application form from the State Horticulture Mission (SHM) website or collect it from the District Horticulture Office.  
2. Fill all fields in **block letters** using **black ink** (if physical) or ensure digital fields are fully completed (if online).  
3. Ensure beneficiary name, Aadhaar number, and PAN match exactly across all documents.  
4. Clearly specify the **NHM component** for which assistance is sought (e.g., "Protected Cultivation – Polyhouse", "Pack House", "Nursery").  
5. Provide accurate project cost based on quotations; do not inflate or understate costs.  
6. Declare any existing or pending subsidies from other government schemes for the same activity.  
7. Attach all required documents in the specified order and label them clearly (e.g., Annexure-I: Land Documents).  
8. Sign the declaration affirming correctness of information and compliance with scheme guidelines.  
9. Retain a photocopy/scanned copy of the fully filled application and attached documents.  
10. Submit to the designated SHM/District Office and obtain an acknowledgment receipt with Inward Number and date.  

> **Portal Note**: Applications are submitted physically or online via SHM portals – verify mode with respective SHM. Refer to [https://nhm.nic.in](https://nhm.nic.in) for state SHM links.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol

- Obtain a signed acknowledgment receipt with **Inward Number, Date, and Officer’s Name**  
- Take a **screenshot or scanned copy** of the acknowledgment for case records  
- Store the **application reference number** in the case file and set calendar reminders for follow-up  
- If submitted online, save the submission confirmation page and application ID  
- Notify the client of successful submission and expected timeline for scrutiny (typically 15–30 days)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the **Approval Letter** or **Administrative Sanction** from SHM/District Office  
- [ ] Confirm the **sanctioned subsidy amount** and **installment release schedule** as per field progress verification  
- [ ] Ensure beneficiary initiates project work only after administrative sanction  
- [ ] Monitor and assist in submitting **progress reports** (with photos, bills) at each stage for verification  
- [ ] Coordinate with nodal bank or implementing agency for **subsidy claim submission** after each verification  
- [ ] Verify that subsidy is released in **installments** (typically 2–3) based on milestone completion  
- [ ] Assist in obtaining **Utilization Certificate (UC)** after final installment and project completion  
- [ ] Ensure beneficiary maintains the asset for the **minimum period** as specified in the approval letter  
- [ ] Follow up for **success fee invoice** upon successful** invoicing as per engagement agreement with the client  
- [ ] Archive all approvals, UCs, and correspondence for audit and future reference
