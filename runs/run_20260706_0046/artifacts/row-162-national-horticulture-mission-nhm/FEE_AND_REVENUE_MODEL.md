## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are tied to the actual subsidy disbursed (40–50% of project cost), ensuring clients pay only for realized benefits.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the back-ended subsidy release and field verification requirements, success fees reflect the implementation risk and processing lag.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainers are scaled to client capacity, with micro and small farmers (Starter tier) typically excluded due to scheme focus on SHGs, FPOs, entrepreneurs, and cooperatives requiring structured proposals.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; no hidden charges. Clients receive itemized invoices linking effort to scheme milestones.

> *Scheme-Specific Note*: The NHM’s back-ended subsidy release (tied to field verification) and state-level implementation variability increase execution risk. Processing times average 4–6 months per installment, necessitating a 3-month lag in revenue recognition for success fees. Evidence shows successful disbursement when proposals align with SHM priorities and documentation is complete.
