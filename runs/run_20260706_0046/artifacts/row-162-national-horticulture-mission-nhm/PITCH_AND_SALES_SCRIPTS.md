## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping farmers, agri-entrepreneurs, and horticulture-focused organisations access government subsidies for setting up nurseries, protected cultivation units, post-harvest infrastructure, and market linkages. The National Horticulture Mission (NHM) offers back-ended subsidies of 40–50% for such projects—higher for SC/ST, small, marginal, and women farmers. I’d like to understand your current horticulture activities to see if this aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of horticulture activity are you currently engaged in or planning (e.g., nursery, tissue culture, floriculture, vegetable seed production, mushroom cultivation, beekeeping)?
- Are you involved in cultivation, processing, marketing, or post-harvest management of horticultural crops?
- What is your legal structure—individual farmer, self-help group, farmer producer organization, NGO, cooperative, or entrepreneur?
- Do you have land ownership or lease documents for the proposed project site?
- Are you seeking support for setting up infrastructure like polyhouses, shade nets, pack houses, cold storage, or ripening chambers?
- Have you explored government subsidies for horticulture before, or is this your first time looking into such support?
- Are you based in a state where the State Horticulture Mission (SHM) is actively implementing NHM components?
- Do you have access to nodal banks or implementing agencies for credit-linked subsidy routing?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Engaged in horticulture activities (cultivation, processing, marketing, or post-harvest management)
- [ ] Eligible beneficiary type: farmer, SHG, FPO, entrepreneur, NGO, cooperative, or similar entity
- [ ] Project falls under an NHM component (e.g., nursery, protected cultivation, organic farming, vermicompost, mushroom, beekeeping, post-harvest, market infrastructure)
- [ ] Has or can obtain land ownership/lease documents
- [ ] Has or can obtain Aadhaar card and PAN (if applicable)
- [ ] Can prepare a project proposal as per SHM guidelines
- [ ] Willing to submit quotations for inputs/equipment and progress reports
- [ ] Located in a state with active SHM implementation (pan-India, but state-level execution)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like your [mention specific activity, e.g., floriculture unit or cold storage plan] could align well with the National Horticulture Mission (NHM), which offers 40–50% back-ended subsidies for precisely this kind of horticulture infrastructure. Let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Problem framing:
Farmers and agri-entrepreneurs often struggle with high upfront costs for setting up modern horticulture infrastructure—such as polyhouses, tissue culture units, or cold storage—limiting productivity, post-harvest losses, and market access. Without financial support, scaling adoption of improved technologies remains a barrier.

### Scheme intro:
The National Horticulture Mission (NHM), implemented by the Department of Agriculture, Cooperation & Farmers Welfare under the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, provides back-ended subsidies ranging from 40% to 50% of project cost for horticulture-related activities. Assistance is higher (up to 50%) for SC/ST, small, marginal, and women farmers. The subsidy is credit-linked, released in installments after field-level verification, and routed through nodal banks or implementing agencies.

### Why they qualify (personalised):
- You are engaged in [specific activity from discovery, e.g., protected cultivation or mushroom cultivation], which is an eligible NHM component.
- Your entity type—[e.g., farmer producer organization or self-help group]—is explicitly listed as eligible under NHM.
- You have or can arrange land ownership/lease documents, a core requirement for application.
- You are based in [state from discovery], where the State Horticulture Mission (SHM) oversees implementation and accepts applications year-round.
