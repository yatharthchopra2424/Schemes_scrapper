### National Horticulture Mission (NHM)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Agriculture, Cooperation & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
- **Portal / Application URL:** [https://nhm.dac.gov.in](https://nhm.dac.gov.in)
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (applications accepted throughout the year as per State Horticulture Mission notifications)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Farmers, self-help groups, farmer producer organizations, entrepreneurs, NGOs, cooperatives, and other entities engaged in horticulture activities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Horticulture (cultivation, processing, marketing, or post-harvest management of horticultural crops)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Specific eligibility may vary by component and state-level implementation guidelines; beneficiaries must be involved in cultivation, processing, marketing, or post-harvest management of horticultural crops

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Back-ended subsidies ranging from 40% to 50% of the project cost, with higher assistance (up to 50%) for SC/ST, small, marginal, and women farmers
- Disbursement mode: Released in installments based on verification of field-level progress; credit-linked and routed through nodal banks or implementing agencies
- Duration of benefit: One-time (subsidy released in installments upon project completion and verification)

#### Documents Required
1. Application form
2. Project proposal
3. Land ownership/lease documents
4. Bank account details
5. Aadhaar card
6. PAN card (if applicable)
7. Quotations for inputs/equipment
8. Training certificates (if required)
9. Other documents as specified by State Horticulture Mission (SHM)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the relevant component under NHM for which assistance is sought.
2. Prepare a project proposal as per the guidelines of the component.
3. Submit the application to the State Horticulture Mission (SHM) or District Horticulture Office.
4. Application is scrutinized by the technical committee at the SHM/District Office.
5. Upon approval, administrative sanction is issued.
6. Beneficiary initiates the project and submits progress reports.
7. Subsidy is released in installments after verification of field progress.
