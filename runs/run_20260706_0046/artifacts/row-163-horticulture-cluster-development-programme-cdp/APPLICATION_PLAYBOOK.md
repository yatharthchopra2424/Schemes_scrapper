# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Implementing Agency (IA) is a legal entity excluding proprietorships or natural persons (eligible: Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs/FPCs), Cooperatives/Societies, Partnership Firms, Companies, or combination thereof)
- [ ] IA demonstrates net worth at least equivalent to the equity contribution proposed in the project
- [ ] IA contributes a minimum of 20% of the IA’s Cost Component as equity
- [ ] IA possesses substantial and relevant experience in agricultural or horticultural input supply, production, aggregation, trading, food processing, exporting, retailing, or logistics provision
- [ ] IA has an annual turnover at least equal to the total project cost
- [ ] IA secures a term loan from RBI Approved Scheduled Commercial Bank or Financial Institution constituting at least 20% of the IA’s Cost Component
- [ ] IA provides a detailed Appraisal Note from the Bank/FI sanctioning the term loan
- [ ] Farmer component is minimum 40% of the total project cost
- [ ] IA maintains a clean financial record with no defaults on debt obligations over the past three years
- [ ] IA is not classified as non-performing assets by any lender
- [ ] IA, including its shareholder(s), partner(s), director(s), and key management personnel, has no defaults on debt obligations over the past three years
- [ ] IA, including its shareholder(s), partner(s), director(s), and key management personnel, is not classified as ‘non-performing assets’ by any lender
- [ ] Joint Ventures are not allowed to fulfill eligibility criteria
- [ ] Application is submitted online only at https://cdp.nhb.gov.in (physical or other means not accepted)

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate
- PAN Card
- ITR (last 3 years)
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Concept note
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) / Business case
- Financial documentation substantiating equity contribution (minimum 20% of IA’s Cost Component)
- Documentation of relevant experience in agricultural/horticultural fields
- Audited balance sheet and Statutory Auditor Certificate for turnover
- Term loan sanction letter from RBI Approved Scheduled Commercial Bank or Financial Institution (minimum 20% of IA’s Cost Component)
- Detailed Appraisal Note from Bank/FI sanctioning the term loan (mandatory; mere stamping/endorsement not valid)
- Documents validating net worth (equivalent to equity contribution)
- Self-declaration of no blacklisting or debarring by Central/State Government, PSU, or autonomous body

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible scans (minimum 300 DPI)
- [ ] File names match document type (e.g., "IA_Name_Audited_Balance_Sheet_2023-24.pdf")
- [ ] File size does not exceed 10 MB per document
- [ ] All certificates are valid and not expired
- [ ] Audited financial statements are certified by a Statutory Auditor
- [ ] Term loan sanction letter and Appraisal Note are on bank letterhead and signed by authorized signatory
- [ ] Self-declaration is on company letterhead, signed, and stamped
- [ ] Documents are in English or Hindi; if in another language, certified translation is attached
- [ ] No overwriting or tampering visible on scanned documents

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**DPR is REQUIRED.**  
Standard sections to include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Objectives and Alignment with CDP Goals  
- Cluster Profile (geography, crops, farmer base)  
- Technology and GAP Adoption Plan (mandatory GAP component of MIDH)  
- Financials: Equity (min. 20%), Term Loan (min. 20%), Farmer Component (min. 40%), Total Project Cost  
- Implementation Timeline  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation  
- Sustainability and Exit Strategy  
- Annexures: Bank Appraisal Note, Equity Proof, Experience Documents, Net Worth Proof  

*Note: The DPR must be accompanied by a detailed Appraisal Note from the Bank/FI; a mere stamp/endorsement is invalid.*

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the portal exclusively at https://cdp.nhb.gov.in — physical submissions are not accepted.  
2. Begin with Expression of Interest (EoI) by submitting the concept note.  
3. Ensure all mandatory fields are filled; the system will not allow submission with incomplete data.  
4. Upload documents in the specified format (PDF preferred) and verify file sizes (<10 MB each).  
5. Double-check that the term loan sanction letter and Appraisal Note are uploaded as separate documents.  
6. Confirm the farmer component is explicitly stated as ≥40% of total project cost in the financials section.  
7. Verify equity contribution is ≥20% of IA’s Cost Component and net worth equivalence is documented.  
8. Use the self-declaration template provided in the portal for no blacklisting/debarring; do not alter the format.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All eligibility criteria verified and documented  
- [ ] Concept note and DPR are complete and aligned  
- [ ] Financials show: Equity ≥20%, Term Loan ≥20%, Farmer Component ≥40%  
- [ ] Term loan sanction letter and detailed Appraisal Note are uploaded  
- [ ] Audited balance sheet and Statutory Auditor Certificate for turnover are attached  
- [ ] Experience documents validate agri/horti sector expertise  
- [ ] Net worth documents match or exceed equity contribution  
- [ ] Self-declaration of no blacklisting/debarring is signed and stamped  
- [ ] No Joint Venture structure is present in ownership/management  
- [ ] All documents are legible, correctly named, and under 10 MB  
- [ ] Declarations are signed and dated  
- [ ] Application is submitted only via https://cdp.nhb.gov.in

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application exclusively via https://cdp.nhb.gov.in  
- Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing the Application Reference Number and timestamp  
- Store the reference number in the case file and set automated reminders for follow-up  
- Download and save the submitted application PDF for records  
- Notify the client of successful submission with reference number and next steps

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from NHB specifying sanctioned financial assistance  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned amount aligns with the approved project cost and funding pattern  
- [ ] Ensure utilization certificate format is understood and timelines are noted  
- [ ] Track disbursement tranches against submitted utilization certificates and invoices  
- [ ] Coordinate with client for timely submission of progress reports and utilization certificates  
- [ ] Verify that the farmer component remains ≥40% and GAP adoption is monitored during implementation  
- [ ] Prepare and submit invoice for success fee as per consultancy agreement  
- [ ] Maintain all records (approval, correspondence, UC, invoices) for audit purposes  
- [ ] Guide client on dovetailing with other schemes (e.g., SHM, MIDH) for holistic cluster development as per scheme objectives
