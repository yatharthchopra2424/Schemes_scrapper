## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial impact of the Horticulture Cluster Development Programme (CDP), which offers grant support for project execution with a total fund size of ₹10,000 Cr, ensuring consultant compensation aligns with the scale of benefits delivered.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees account for the scheme’s moderate risk profile, given the 6-week DPR submission window post-shortlisting and the requirement for IA equity (20%), term loan (20%), and farmer component (40%), which necessitates thorough due diligence but is supported by clear guidelines and NHB oversight.
3. **Transparency & Predictability**: Retainer fees cover advisory services for eligibility assessment, DPR preparation, and liaison with NHB, while success fees are contingent on fund disbursement, ensuring clients pay only for achieved outcomes.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The CDP’s structured application process — including concept note submission, technical evaluation, and a fixed six-week DPR preparation window — reduces uncertainty in timelines, allowing for predictable revenue lag modeling. Evidence of active NHB project management and PMU involvement (Grant Thornton Bharat LLP) further supports feasibility of successful outcomes.
