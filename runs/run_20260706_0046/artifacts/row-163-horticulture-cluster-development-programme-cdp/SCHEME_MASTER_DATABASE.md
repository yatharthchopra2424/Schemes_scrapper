### Horticulture Cluster Development Programme (CDP)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Horticulture Board (NHB), Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://cdp.nhb.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Last date for submission of application is 31st July 2026 (6:00 PM, Friday)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Implementing Agency (IA) must be a legal entity excluding proprietorships or natural persons, including Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs/FPCs), Cooperatives/Societies, Partnership Firms, Companies, or combination thereof
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Horticulture
- Revenue cap: Annual turnover at least equal to the total project cost
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: 
  - Net worth at least equivalent to the equity contribution of the IA proposed in the project
  - Minimum 20% of IA’s Cost Component as equity contribution
  - Substantial and relevant experience in agricultural or horticultural input supply, production, aggregation, trading, food processing, exporting, retailing, or logistics provision
  - Term loan from RBI Approved Scheduled Commercial Bank or Financial Institution constituting at least 20% of IA’s Cost Component
  - Detailed Appraisal Note from Bank/FI sanctioning the term loan
  - Farmer component minimum 40% of total project cost
  - Clean financial record with no defaults on debt obligations over past three years and no classification as non-performing assets by any lender
  - Joint Ventures not allowed to fulfill eligibility criteria

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance for project execution under a total investment of $1.3 Billion (INR 10000 Cr)
- Disbursement mode: Released in a transparent and judicious manner adhering to principles of accountability and fiscal responsibility (specific mode not detailed in evidence)
- Duration of benefit: Not specified (implied to be project-based)

#### Documents Required
1. Concept note
2. Detailed Project Report (DPR) / Business case
3. Financial documentation substantiating equity contribution
4. Documentation of relevant experience in agricultural/horticultural fields
5. Audited balance sheet and Statutory Auditor Certificate for turnover
6. Term loan sanction letter from RBI Approved Scheduled Commercial Bank or Financial Institution
7. Detailed Appraisal Note from Bank/FI sanctioning the term loan
8. Documents validating net worth
9. Self-declaration of no blacklisting or debarring by Central/State Government, PSU, or autonomous body

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Submit concept note in response to EoI (Expression of Interest)
2. Responsiveness check and technical evaluation of concept notes
3. Shortlisted applicants invited to submit detailed business plan/ DPR
4. Six weeks provided for preparation and submission of DPR/ Business case
5. Post evaluation of the business case/ DPR, projects awarded to IA and Contract signed between NHB and IA
