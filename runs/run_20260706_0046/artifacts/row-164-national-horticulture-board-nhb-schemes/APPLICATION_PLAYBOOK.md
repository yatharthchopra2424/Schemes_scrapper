# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Implementing Agency (IA) is a legal entity excluding proprietorships or natural persons (eligible types: Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs/FPCs), Cooperatives/Societies, Partnership Firms, Companies, or combination thereof)
- [ ] IA demonstrates net worth at least equivalent to the equity contribution proposed in the project
- [ ] IA contributes a minimum of 20% of the IA’s Cost Component as equity in the proposed project
- [ ] IA possesses substantial and relevant experience in agricultural or horticultural input supply, production, aggregation, trading, food processing, exporting, retailing, or logistics provision
- [ ] IA has an annual turnover at least equal to the total cost of the project
- [ ] IA secures a term loan from RBI Approved Scheduled Commercial Bank or Financial Institution constituting at least 20% of the IA’s Cost Component
- [ ] IA provides a detailed Appraisal Note from the Bank/FI
- [ ] Farmer component is minimum 40% of the total project cost
- [ ] IA maintains a clean financial record with no defaults on debt obligations or non-performing asset classification over the past three years
- [ ] IA, including its shareholder(s), partner(s), director(s), and key management personnel, has not been classified as ‘non-performing assets’ by any lender during the past three years
- [ ] IA has not been blacklisted or debarred by Central/State Government, PSU, or autonomous body

> **Sources**: Eligibility criteria from KEY FACTS block; Application Portal: https://cdp.nhb.gov.in

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate
- PAN Card
- ITR (last 3 years)
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable)
- Audited Balance Sheet and Statutory Auditor Certificate for turnover verification
- Self-declaration of no blacklisting or debarring by Central/State Government, PSU, or autonomous body

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Concept note
- Detailed Project Report (DPR)
- Business case
- Financial documentation substantiating equity contribution
- Proof of term loan sanction letter from RBI Approved Scheduled Commercial Bank or Financial Institution
- Bank/FI Appraisal Note
- Documents demonstrating net worth
- Evidence of relevant experience in agricultural or horticultural fields

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible scans (minimum 300 DPI)
- [ ] File names match the document type exactly (e.g., "Concept_Note.pdf", "DPR_Final.pdf")
- [ ] PDF file size does not exceed 10 MB per document
- [ ] All certificates are valid and not expired (where applicable)
- [ ] Documents are in English or accompanied by certified Hindi/English translation
- [ ] Bank sanction letter and appraisal note are on bank letterhead, signed, and stamped
- [ ] Audited financials are certified by a Statutory Auditor
- [ ] Self-declaration is notarized or signed with company seal

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Required**: Yes, Detailed Project Report (DPR) is mandatory for shortlisted applicants.  
**Standard Sections to Include**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Objectives and Justification  
- Cluster Profile (geography, crops, farmers involved)  
- Technical Details (package of practices, GAPs, infrastructure)  
- Financial Plan (equity:debt split, farmer contribution ≥40%, IA equity ≥20%, term loan ≥20% of IA’s Cost Component)  
- Implementation Timeline (Gantt chart)  
- Management Structure and Experience of IA  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
- Sustainability and Environmental Impact Assessment  
- Utilization Plan for NHB Financial Assistance  
- Annexures: Bank sanction letter, appraisal note, audited financials, experience certificates  

> **Note**: DPR must be submitted within six weeks of shortlisting. No extensions will be granted.  
> **Sources**: Application Process and Key Caveats from KEY FACTS block

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Access the portal only via https://cdp.nhb.gov.in** – physical or email submissions are not accepted.  
2. **Begin with Concept Note submission** in response to the Expression of Interest (EoI).  
3. **Ensure all fields are filled accurately** – no blank fields allowed; use “Not Applicable” only if explicitly permitted.  
4. **Upload documents in PDF format only**, with file names matching the document type (e.g., “Term_Loan_Sanction_Letter.pdf”).  
5. **Verify that the term loan sanction letter specifies the exact amount** constituting at least 20% of the IA’s Cost Component.  
6. **Confirm the Bank/FI Appraisal Note is detailed** – mere stamping or endorsement without appraisal is invalid.  
7. **Double-check equity and farmer contribution percentages** – IA equity ≥20% of IA’s Cost Component; farmer component ≥40% of total project cost.  
8. **Review the self-declaration** for no defaults/non-performing assets over the past three years – ensure it is signed and dated.  
9. **Save a screenshot of the final submission confirmation** with timestamp and reference number.  
10. **Do not submit after the deadline** – 31st July 2026, 6:00 PM for Concept Note; 17th April 2026, 6:00 PM for DPR (if shortlisted).  

> **Sources**: Application Process, Key Caveats, and Portal from KEY FACTS block

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the Concept Note exclusively via the online portal at **https://cdp.nhb.gov.in**  
- Upon successful submission, take a **screenshot of the confirmation page** showing:  
  - Application Reference Number  
  - Timestamp (date and time)  
  - “Submission Successful” message  
- Store the screenshot and reference number in the client’s case folder with label:  
  `NHB_ConceptNote_Submission_[ReferenceNumber]_[Date]`  
- Set a calendar reminder for **17th April 2026** to check for shortlisting notification (if applicable)  
- If shortlisted, begin DPR preparation immediately to meet the **17th April 2026, 6:00 PM** deadline  

> **Sources**: Submission protocol and deadlines from KEY FACTS block; Portal: https://cdp.nhb.gov.in

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the official approval letter from NHB specifying sanctioned financial assistance amount  
- [ ] Confirm that the sanctioned amount aligns with the financial plan submitted in the DPR/business case  
- [ ] Ensure the utilization plan for NHB funds is clearly defined and approved  
- [ ] Initiate disbursement requests as per NHB’s milestone-based release schedule  
- [ ] Maintain proper books of accounts and records for all project expenditures  
- [ ] Prepare and submit Utilization Certificate (UC) as per NHB guidelines and timelines  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with CVC guidelines, Ministry of Finance procurement manuals, and GAPs  
- [ ] Coordinate with State Horticulture Mission (SHM) for timely implementation  
- [ ] Dovetail with other ministries/departments for holistic cluster development  
- [ ] Provide technical guidance on package of practices and GAPs to producers in the cluster  
- [ ] Track and document progress toward enhancing farmers’ wallet share (cost reduction and revenue increase)  
- [ ] Prepare for success fee invoice upon verification of milestone achievement  

> **Sources**: Benefits and objectives from KEY FACTS block (financial assistance, utilization of funds, CVC guidelines, SHM coordination, technical guidance, farmer wallet share)
