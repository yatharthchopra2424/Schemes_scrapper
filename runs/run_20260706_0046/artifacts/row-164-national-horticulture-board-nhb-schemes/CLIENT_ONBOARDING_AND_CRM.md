- Must be a legal entity excluding proprietorships or natural persons (eligible: Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs/FPCs), Cooperatives/Societies, Partnership Firms, Companies, or combination thereof)
- Must have an annual turnover at least equal to the total cost of the project
- Must possess substantial and relevant experience in agricultural or horticultural input supply, production, aggregation, trading, food processing, exporting, retailing, or logistics provision
- Must secure a term loan from RBI Approved Scheduled Commercial Bank or Financial Institution constituting at least 20% of the IA’s Cost Component

- Must contribute a minimum of 20% of the IA’s Cost Component as equity in the proposed project
- Must ensure the farmer component is minimum 40% of the total project cost
- Must maintain a clean financial record with no defaults on debt obligations or non-performing asset classification over the past three years
