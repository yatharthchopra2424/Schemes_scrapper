## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified the National Horticulture Board (NHB) Schemes — a subsidy scheme administered by the National Horticulture Board, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is financial assistance under the Programme for project execution, support for achieving financial closure, facilitation in obtaining statutory approvals and clearances, guidance on utilization of funds in a transparent and judicious manner, support for maintaining proper books of accounts, dovetailing with other ministries and departments for holistic cluster development, coordination with State Horticulture Mission (SHM) for timely implementation, adherence to Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) guidelines and Ministry of Finance procurement manuals, and provision of technical guidance on package of practices and good agriculture practices (GAPs) to producers in the cluster.
