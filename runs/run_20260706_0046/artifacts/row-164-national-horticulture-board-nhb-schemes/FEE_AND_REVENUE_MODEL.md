## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial benefit delivered through NHB schemes, particularly the Cluster Development Programme offering financial assistance for project execution and facilitation of financial closure.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the moderate risk profile of NHB schemes — while financial support is significant, processing involves rigorous technical evaluation, DPR submission within strict timelines (6 weeks post-shortlisting), and mandatory bank appraisal notes, increasing complexity and timeline risk.
3. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover initial concept note preparation and eligibility assessment, while success fees are contingent on project award and contract signing between NHB and the IA.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Given the NHB scheme’s processing time (concept note submission by 31 July 2026, DPR by 17 April 2026 for shortlisted applicants) and evidence of multi-stage evaluation (concept note → technical evaluation → DPR → business case → award), revenue recognition assumes a 3-month lag from client engagement to approval, reflecting the sequential gatekeeping in the application process.
