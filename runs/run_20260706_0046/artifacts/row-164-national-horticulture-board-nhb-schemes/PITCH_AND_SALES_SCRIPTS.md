# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs), cooperatives, partnership firms, and companies in the horticulture sector access government subsidies for cluster development. The National Horticulture Board (NHB) Scheme provides financial assistance for integrated horticulture cluster projects covering pre-production to marketing. I’d like to understand your current operations and see if this scheme aligns with your growth plans.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of legal entity is your organisation (e.g., FPO/FPC, cooperative, partnership firm, company)?
2. When was your organisation incorporated or registered?
3. What is your annual turnover, and does it meet or exceed the total project cost you are planning?
4. What is your net worth, and is it at least equivalent to the equity you plan to contribute?
5. Do you have substantial experience in horticultural input supply, production, aggregation, trading, food processing, exporting, retailing, or logistics?
6. Have you secured or are you planning to secure a term loan from an RBI-approved scheduled commercial bank for at least 20% of your project’s IA cost component?
7. Are farmer members contributing at least 40% of the total project cost?
8. Do you have a clean financial record with no debt defaults or non-performing asset classification in the past three years?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Legal entity type: FPO/FPC, cooperative, partnership firm, or company (proprietorships/natural persons excluded)
- [ ] Net worth ≥ equity contribution of IA proposed in project
- [ ] Minimum 20% of IA’s Cost Component contributed as equity by applicant entity
- [ ] Substantial relevant experience in agricultural/horticultural input supply, production, aggregation, trading, food processing, exporting, retailing, or logistics
- [ ] Annual turnover ≥ total project cost
- [ ] Term loan secured from RBI-approved scheduled commercial bank ≥20% of IA’s Cost Component
- [ ] Bank/FI Appraisal Note obtained
- [ ] Farmer component ≥ 40% of total project cost
- [ ] Clean financial record: no defaults on debt or NPAs in past 3 years
- [ ] No blacklisting/debarring by Central/State Government, PSU, or autonomous body

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your organisation may meet the core eligibility criteria for the National Horticulture Board’s Cluster Development Programme. This scheme offers financial assistance for project execution, financial closure support, and technical guidance—specifically designed for entities like yours in the horticulture value chain. Let me outline how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Horticulture clusters face fragmented value chains, high post-harvest losses (20-44%), limited access to finance, and insufficient adoption of good agricultural practices (GAPs), which hinder competitiveness and farmer incomes.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Horticulture Board (NHB) Cluster Development Programme, implemented by the National Horticulture Board under the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India, provides financial assistance for project execution, support for financial closure, facilitation of statutory approvals, guidance on fund utilisation, technical guidance on GAPs, and coordination with State Horticulture Missions. The scheme requires the applicant to contribute a minimum of 20% of the IA’s Cost Component as equity, secure a term loan of at least 20% from an RBI-approved scheduled commercial bank, and ensure the farmer component is minimum 40% of the total project cost.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your organisation is a [FPO/cooperative/partnership firm/company], which is an eligible legal entity under the scheme.
- You have confirmed net worth sufficient to cover your planned equity contribution (≥20% of IA’s Cost Component).
- You are in the process of securing a term loan from an RBI-approved scheduled commercial bank for at least 20% of the IA’s Cost Component.
- Farmer members in your cluster are committed to contributing at least 40% of the total project cost.

| "I'll try it myself first" | Common mistakes include submitting physical applications (only online at https://cdp.nhb.gov.in is accepted) and submitting a DPR stamped by a bank without a detailed Appraisal Note—both lead to rejection. | "We're too small / too big" | The scheme evaluates based on project cost alignment: your annual turnover must be at least equal to the total project cost, and there is no upper limit on turnover. | "I don't have the documents ready" | The three most commonly missing documents are: (1) detailed Bank/FI Appraisal Note (not just a sanction letter), (2) audited balance sheet and Statutory Auditor Certificate for turnover verification, and (3) self-declaration of no blacklisting or debarring by government bodies. We help you prepare and validate these upfront. |
