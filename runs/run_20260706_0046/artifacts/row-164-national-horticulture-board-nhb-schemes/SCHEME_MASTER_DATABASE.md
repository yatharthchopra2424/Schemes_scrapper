### National Horticulture Board (NHB) Schemes
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Horticulture Board (NHB), Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://cdp.nhb.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Always Open (with specific cut-off dates: Application submission by 31st July 2026, 6:00 PM; DPR submission by shortlisted applicants by 17th April 2026, 06:00 PM)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Implementing Agency (IA) must be a legal entity excluding proprietorships or natural persons, including Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs/FPCs), Cooperatives/Societies, Partnership Firms, Companies, or combination thereof
- Age of business: Not specified in evidence
- Sector: Horticulture (including pre-production, production, post-harvest management, value addition, logistics, marketing, branding)
- Revenue cap: Annual turnover at least equal to the total cost of the project
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: 
  - Must demonstrate net worth at least equivalent to the equity contribution of the IA proposed in the project
  - Must contribute a minimum of 20% of the IA’s Cost Component as equity
  - Must possess substantial and relevant experience in agricultural or horticultural input supply, production, aggregation, trading, food processing, exporting, retailing, or logistics provision
  - Must secure a term loan from RBI Approved Scheduled Commercial Bank or Financial Institution constituting at least 20% of the IA’s Cost Component
  - Must provide a detailed Appraisal Note from the Bank/FI
  - Farmer component must be minimum 40% of the total project cost
  - Must maintain a clean financial record with no defaults on debt obligations or non-performing asset classification over the past three years
  - Must provide self-declaration of no blacklisting or debarring by Central/State Government, PSU, or autonomous body

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance under the Programme for project execution, support for achieving financial closure, facilitation in obtaining statutory approvals and clearances, guidance on utilization of funds, support for maintaining proper books of accounts, dovetailing with other ministries, coordination with State Horticulture Mission (SHM), adherence to CVC guidelines and Ministry of Finance procurement manuals, and provision of technical guidance on GAPs to producers in the cluster
- Disbursement mode: Not explicitly stated in evidence (inferred as direct credit based on subsidy nature and requirement for equity/debt split)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (linked to project execution and financial closure)

#### Documents Required
1. Concept note
2. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
3. Business case
4. Financial documentation substantiating equity contribution
5. Proof of term loan sanction letter from RBI Approved Scheduled Commercial Bank or Financial Institution
6. Bank/ FI Appraisal Note
7. Documents demonstrating net worth
8. Evidence of relevant experience in agricultural or horticultural fields
9. Audited balance sheet and Statutory Auditor Certificate for turnover verification
10. Self-declaration of no blacklisting or debarring by Central/State Government, PSU, or autonomous body

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Submit concept note in response to Expression of Interest (EoI) via online portal at https://cdp.nhb.gov.in
2. Await responsiveness check and technical evaluation of concept notes received on or before the cut-off date
3. If shortlisted, submit detailed business plan/ Detailed Project Report (DPR) within six weeks
4. Post evaluation of the business case/DPR, projects will be awarded to the IA and contract will be signed between NHB and IA
5. No deadline date extensions will be provided for DPR submission
