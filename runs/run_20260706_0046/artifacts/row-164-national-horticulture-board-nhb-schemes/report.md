# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **National Horticulture Board (NHB) Schemes** (Scheme ID: row-164) is a **subsidy**-type initiative implemented by the **National Horticulture Board (NHB)**, under the **Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India**. It operates with a **Pan-India** geographic scope and is designed to holistically develop horticulture clusters across the value chain. The scheme’s official application portal is **[https://cdp.nhb.gov.in](https://cdp.nhb.gov.in)**, and it remains active with a **last updated** status of **2026**.

### Objectives
The scheme aims to:
- Address the horticulture cluster value chain comprehensively, covering **pre-production, production, post-harvest management, value addition, logistics, marketing, and branding** in an integrated manner.
- Facilitate the introduction of **innovative technologies and practices** to enhance the **global competitiveness** of focus cluster crops and promote **exports**.
- Enhance **farmers’ wallet share** by reducing costs and increasing revenue.

### Eligibility Matrix
The following table outlines the exhaustive eligibility criteria for the Implementing Agency (IA), derived directly from the key facts and supporting evidence:

| **Eligibility Criteria** | **Requirement** | **Details / Evidence** |
|--------------------------|-----------------|------------------------|
| **Legal Entity Type** | Must be a legal entity excluding proprietorships or natural persons | Includes Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs/FPCs), Cooperatives/Societies, Partnership Firms, Companies, or combination thereof. |
| **Net Worth** | Must be at least equivalent to the equity contribution of the IA proposed in the project | Demonstrated via audited financial statements and statutory auditor certificate. |
| **Equity Contribution (IA)** | Minimum 20% of the IA’s Cost Component | Must be contributed as equity by the applicant entity. |
| **Farmer Component** | Minimum 40% of the total project cost | Must be contributed by farmers in the cluster. |
| **Annual Turnover** | At least equal to the total cost of the project | Verified via audited balance sheet and Statutory Auditor Certificate. |
| **Experience** | Substantial and relevant experience in agricultural/horticultural fields | Must be in input supply, production, aggregation, trading, food processing, exporting, retailing, or logistics provision. |
| **Term Loan** | Must secure from RBI Approved Scheduled Commercial Bank or Financial Institution | Must constitute at least 20% of the IA’s Cost Component; requires a detailed Appraisal Note from the bank/FI. |
| **Financial Record** | Clean financial record with no defaults or NPA classification | Over the past three years; applies Note from the bank/FI. |
| **Appraisal Note** | Detailed Appraisal Note from Bank/FI required | A mere stamp or endorsement without detailed appraisal is **not valid** (Key Caveat). |
| **Debt Obligations** | No defaults on debt obligations or NPA classification | Must be clean for the past three years; applies to entity, shareholders, partners, directors, and key management personnel. |
| **Blacklisting** | Self-declaration of no blacklisting or debarring | By Central/State Government, PSU, or autonomous body. |

> **Key Caveats on Eligibility**  
> - Submission of application **physically or through any other means** is **not accepted**; only **online submission at [https://cdp.nhb.gov.in](https://cdp.nhb.gov.in)** is allowed.  
> - Detailed Project Report/Techno-Economic Viability Report **merely stamped or endorsed** by the Scheduled Commercial Bank **without detailed Appraisal Note** shall **not be considered valid**.  
> - The applicant entity, including its shareholder(s), partner(s), director(s), and key management personnel, must have a **clean financial record with no defaults on debt obligations** over the past three years and must **not have been classified as ‘non-performing assets’** by any lender during this period.

#### Financial Structure Requirements (Mandatory for IA)
The following financial structure is **mandatory** for the Implementing Agency’s cost component and must be demonstrated in the financing plan:

| **Component** | **Minimum Requirement** | **Purpose / Evidence** |
|---------------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| **Farmer Equity** | Minimum 40% of total project cost | Ensures farmer participation and benefit sharing; Key Facts: "The farmer component shall be minimum 40% of the total project cost." |
| **IA Equity** | Minimum 20% of IA’s Cost Component | Applicant entity’s own equity contribution; Key Facts: "The applicant entity must contribute a minimum of 20% of the IA’s Cost Component as equity." |
| **Term Loan (Debt)** | At least 20% of IA’s Cost Component | Must be secured from RBI Approved Scheduled Commercial Bank or FI; Key Facts: "The IA must secure a term loan from RBI Approved Scheduled Commercial Bank or Financial Institution that constitutes at least 20% of the IA’s Cost Component." |
| **Financing Plan** | Must clearly identify sources of funds and equity:debt split | Required for IA component of project cost; Key Facts: "The applicant must provide a financing plan clearly identifying sources of funds and indicating equity: debt split for the IA component of project cost." |

> **Financial Closure Note**  
> The scheme does not provide a direct cash subsidy but enables financial closure by mandating structured funding (equity + debt) and providing facilitation support. The IA must arrange 100% of its cost component via:  
> - ≥20% IA Equity  
> - ≥20% Term Loan (from RBI-approved bank/FI)  
> - Remaining up to 60% can be additional equity/debt (subject to lender approval and IA’s net worth)  
> Meanwhile, the **farmer component (≥40% of total project cost)** is separate and must be sourced from farmer contributions (equity or in-kind).

- **DPR Submission Deadline**: Last date for submission of Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) by shortlisted applicants is **17th April 2026 (06:00 PM)**.

### Supporting Evidence Summary
The scheme is reinforced by broader government initiatives detailed in the downloaded PDFs:
- Aligns with the **Cluster Development Programme (CDP)** under the **Vision 2030** framework, targeting **total investment of $1.3 Billion (INR 10,000 Cr)**.  
- Supports formation of **10,000 new Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)** and development of **100+ horticulture clusters**.  
- Complements schemes like **Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)** and **Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH)**.  
- Emphasizes **post-harvest loss reduction** (currently 20-44% in India due to inadequate infrastructure) and **export competitiveness** (India’s horticulture export share is only 1.7% globally).  
- Promotes **innovation, sustainability, and technology adoption** (e.g., cold chain, processing, GAPs) to enhance global competitiveness.
