## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Cluster of micro and small enterprises (MSEs) formed or willing to form for mutual benefit
- [ ] Cluster engaged in viable economic activities with potential for growth and employment generation
- [ ] Cluster operates in manufacturing or service sector
- [ ] Cluster members are registered as MSME (Udyam registration preferred)
- [ ] Cluster has a defined Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) structure (if applicable)
- [ ] Cluster demonstrates common economic interests among members
- [ ] Cluster proposal aligns with scheme objectives: productivity enhancement, infrastructure development, CFC setup, market access, technology upgradation
- [ ] Cluster is not already receiving duplicate funding for the same components under other central/state schemes
- [ ] Cluster SPV has legal entity status (registered under Companies Act, Partnership Act, or Cooperative Societies Act)
- [ ] Cluster members contribute minimum required percentage of project cost (varies by category: general vs special category clusters)
- [ ] Project cost does not exceed available funds under the scheme (subject to availability and appraisal cycles)
- [ ] Cluster is located in India (Pan-India scope)

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Cluster of micro and small enterprises (MSEs) formed or willing to form for mutual benefit
- [ ] Cluster engaged in viable economic activities with potential for growth and employment generation
- [ ] Cluster operates in manufacturing or service sector
- [ ] Cluster members are registered as MSME (Udyam registration preferred)
- [ ] Cluster has a defined Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) structure (if applicable)
- [ ] Cluster demonstrates common economic interests among members
- [ ] Cluster proposal aligns with scheme objectives: productivity enhancement, infrastructure development, CFC setup, market access, technology upgradation
- [ ] Cluster is not already receiving duplicate funding for the same components under other central/state schemes
- [ ] Cluster SPV has legal entity status (registered under Companies Act, Partnership Act, or Cooperative Societies Act)
- [ ] Cluster members contribute minimum required percentage of project cost (varies by category: general vs special category clusters)
- [ ] Project cost does not exceed available funds under the scheme (subject to availability and appraisal cycles)
- [ ] Cluster is located in India (Pan-India scope)
- [ ] Cluster proposal includes both soft and hard interventions as per scheme guidelines
- [ ] Cluster has identified nodal agency (Office of Development Commissioner, MSME or State-level implementing agencies) for submission

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Udyam Registration Certificate (URC) for cluster SPV and members
- PAN card of SPV and authorized signatory
- GST registration certificate (if applicable)
- ITR of SPV for last 2-3 years
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Audited financial statements of SPV
- Certificate of incorporation/registration of SPV
- Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association of SPV
- Board resolution authorizing the project
- Land documents (ownership/lease agreement) for CFC site
- Quotations from vendors for plant and machinery
- Bank appraisal note (if applicable)
- Undertaking from SPV regarding contribution and maintenance
- Details of cluster members and their products/services
- Land use conversion certificate (if agricultural land)
- Detailed Project Report (DPR)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining cluster profile, proposed interventions, expected outcomes, and budget
- Certificate of incorporation/registration of the cluster SPV
- Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association of SPV
- Board resolution authorizing the project
- Land documents (ownership/lease agreement) for CFC site
- Quotations from vendors for plant and machinery
- Bank appraisal note (if applicable)
- Undertaking from SPV regarding contribution and maintenance
- Details of cluster members and their products/services
- Land use conversion certificate (if agricultural land)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format
- File size should not exceed 5 MB per document
- Document names must match the checklist (e.g., "DPR_MSE-CDP.pdf", "SPV_Incorporation_Certificate.pdf")
- All certificates must be valid and not expired
- Documents in regional languages must be accompanied by certified English/Hindi translation
- Undertakings and resolutions must be on company letterhead and duly signed/sealed
- Bank documents must be on bank letterhead with authorized signatory
- Land documents must be registered and duly stamped
- Photographs must be clear and relevant (if required)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required for this scheme. Standard sections to include:
- Executive Summary
- Cluster Profile (member details, products, geographic spread, economic viability)
- Problem Statement and Need Assessment
- Proposed Interventions (soft and hard components)
- Project Objectives and Expected Outcomes
- Detailed Budget Breakdown (cost estimates, beneficiary contribution, grant sought)
- Implementation Plan (timeline, milestones, responsible agencies)
- Monitoring and Evaluation Framework
- Sustainability and Maintenance Plan
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Measures
- Annexures (supporting documents: land papers, quotations, SPV documents, etc.)

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal via the Office of Development Commissioner, MSME website or State-level implementing agencies.
2. Ensure all fields in the application form are filled accurately; leave no mandatory field blank.
3. Upload all required documents in PDF format, each under 5 MB, with clear and descriptive file names.
4. Verify that the SPV details (name, registration number, address) match exactly across all documents and the form.
5. Confirm the project cost breakdown aligns with the DPR and includes beneficiary contribution as per scheme norms.
6. Select the correct intervention type (soft/hard/both) and cluster category (general/special) to determine applicable grant percentage.
7. Attach a signed undertaking from the SPV regarding fund utilization and asset maintenance.
8. Double-check that the bank appraisal note (if applicable) is uploaded and reflects financial viability.
9. Save a copy of the submitted application and note the application reference number for tracking.
10. Submit only during official working hours to avoid technical delays; keep screenshots of the submission confirmation.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All application form fields completed and validated
- [ ] All required documents attached and correctly named
- [ ] DPR includes all standard sections and aligns with budget
- [ ] SPV documents (incorporation, MoA/AoA, board resolution) are valid and signed
- [ ] Land documents are clear, registered, and match the proposed CFC site
- [ ] Vendor quotations are recent and include item-wise cost breakdown
- [ ] Bank appraisal note (if applicable) is present and signed by authorized bank official
- [ ] Undertaking from SPV regarding contribution and maintenance is signed and sealed
- [ ] Declarations and consent forms are signed by authorized signatory
- [ ] File sizes are within limits (<5 MB per document)
- [ ] Application reference number has been generated and recorded
