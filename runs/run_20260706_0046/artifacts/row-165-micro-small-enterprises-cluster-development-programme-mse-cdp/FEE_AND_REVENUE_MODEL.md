## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client (grant amount received).
2. Tiered retainer reflects the complexity and documentation intensity of the scheme application.
3. Success fee capped at a percentage of the grant amount received, ensuring alignment of interests.
4. Scheme-specific note: The MSE-CDP scheme has a medium confidence level in evidence and involves a detailed appraisal process with rolling submissions; processing time can extend over several months due to Steering Committee review and fund availability, increasing operational risk and justifying retainer fees for ongoing case management.
