# Pitch & Sales Scripts for Micro & Small Enterprises Cluster Development Programme (MSE-CDP)

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping micro and small enterprises access government support for cluster development. The Micro & Small Enterprises Cluster Development Programme (MSE-CDP) is designed specifically for groups of MSMEs looking to upgrade infrastructure, set up Common Facility Centers, and improve market access together. I’d like to understand if your business cluster might benefit from this grant-based support.

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you part of a group of micro and small enterprises in the same sector or geographic area facing common challenges?
2. Has your cluster been formally registered as a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) or are you in the process of forming one?
3. What is the primary economic activity of your cluster members (manufacturing or services)?
4. Do you have a Detailed Project Report (DPR) ready, or would you need support in preparing one?
5. What specific interventions are you considering—such as infrastructure development, skill training, market development, or setting up a Common Facility Center?
6. Are your cluster members willing to contribute a minimum percentage of the project cost as beneficiary share?
7. Do you have land identified or secured for the proposed Common Facility Center or infrastructure project?
8. Are you seeking financial support for technology upgradation, quality certification, or capacity building for your cluster?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Cluster of micro and small enterprises engaged in viable economic activities
- [ ] Willing to form or already formed a cluster for mutual benefit
- [ ] Focus on sectors with potential for growth and employment generation
- [ ] Cluster SPV incorporated/registered (Certificate of incorporation/registration)
- [ ] Memorandum and Articles of Association of SPV available
- [ ] Board resolution authorizing the project
- [ ] Land documents (ownership/lease) for CFC site
- [ ] Quotations from vendors for plant and machinery
- [ ] Bank appraisal note (if applicable)
- [ ] Undertaking from SPV regarding contribution and maintenance
- [ ] Details of cluster members and their products/services
- [ ] Land use conversion certificate (if agricultural land)
- [ ] Detailed Project Report (DPR) prepared or in preparation

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, your cluster appears to align well with the Micro & Small Enterprises Cluster Development Programme (MSE-CDP), which offers grant-in-aid ranging from 30% to 90% of project cost for interventions like Common Facility Centers and infrastructure development. Let me walk you through how this scheme works and how we can support your application.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Micro and small enterprises often struggle with high costs for infrastructure, technology upgrades, and market access when acting individually. This limits their competitiveness, productivity, and ability to scale, especially in manufacturing and service sectors where shared facilities could significantly reduce operational burdens.

**Scheme intro:**
The Micro & Small Enterprises Cluster Development Programme (MSE-CDP), administered by the Office of the Development Commissioner, Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, provides grant-in-aid for both soft interventions (diagnostic studies, capacity building, skill training, market development) and hard interventions (setting up Common Facility Centers, infrastructure development). Financial support ranges from 30% to 90% of project cost, with higher support for special category clusters (SC/ST, women, NER, hilly areas).

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your cluster consists of micro and small enterprises engaged in viable economic activities  
- You are willing to form or have already formed a cluster for mutual benefit  
- Your focus sector shows potential for growth and employment generation  
- You have or can prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining interventions, budget, and outcomes

## 3. Objection Handlers

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I've heard these schemes never get approved" | The MSE-CDP has a rolling application process with funds released throughout the year based on appraisal cycles. Many clusters across manufacturing and services sectors have successfully received grants for Common Facility Centers and infrastructure development. Approval depends on a viable DPR and cluster commitment—not luck. |
| "How much do you charge?" | [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT] |
| "I'll try it myself first" | Common mistakes include submitting an incomplete DPR without clear milestones and failing to secure beneficiary contribution commitments upfront—both lead to delays or rejection. We help avoid these pitfalls. |
| "We're too small / too big" | The scheme supports clusters of micro and small enterprises—there is no upper limit on cluster size, but each member must qualify as an MSE. There’s no minimum number of units, but the cluster must show economic viability and mutual benefit. |
| "I don't have the documents ready" | The three most commonly missing documents are: Detailed Project Report (DPR), land ownership/lease agreement for the CFC site, and vendor quotations for plant and machinery. We help prepare all required documents. |
| "We already work with a CA" | Great — we work alongside CAs. They handle your accounts; we handle the scheme strategy and applications. |
