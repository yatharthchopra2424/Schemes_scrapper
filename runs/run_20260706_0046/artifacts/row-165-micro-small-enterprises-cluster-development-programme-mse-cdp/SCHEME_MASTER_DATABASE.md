### Micro & Small Enterprises Cluster Development Programme (MSE-CDP)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Office of the Development Commissioner, Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://msme.gov.in/mse-cdp
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  | me.gov.in/mse-cdp
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Rolling basis — proposals can be submitted throughout the year subject to availability of funds and appraisal cycles.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) engaged in viable economic activities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Manufacturing and service sectors
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be forming clusters or willing to form clusters for mutual benefit, with focus on sectors having potential for growth and employment generation; supports both new and existing clusters of MSEs

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Grant-in-aid ranging from 30% to 90% of project cost, with higher support for special category clusters (SC/ST, women, NER, hilly areas)
- Disbursement mode: Grant-in-aid
- Duration of benefit: One-time (based on project milestones)

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
2. Certificate of incorporation/registration of the cluster SPV
3. Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association of SPV
4. Board resolution authorizing the project
5. Land documents (ownership/lease agreement) for CFC site
6. Quotations from vendors for plant and machinery
7. Bank appraisal note (if applicable)
8. Undertaking from SPV regarding contribution and maintenance
9. Details of cluster members and their products/services
10. Land use conversion certificate (if agricultural land)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify and form a cluster of micro and small enterprises with common economic interests.
2. Prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining cluster profile, proposed interventions, expected outcomes, and budget.
3. Submit the DPR along with required documents to the nodal agency (Office of Development Commissioner, MSME) or State-level implementing agencies.
4. Project appraisal and approval by the Steering Committee.
5. Release of funds in installments based on project milestones and utilization certificates.
6. Implementation of approved interventions by the cluster Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV).
7. Monitoring, evaluation, and submission of progress reports.
