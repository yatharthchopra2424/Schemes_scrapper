# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Co-operative society, Private or Public Limited Company owned by SC/ST Entrepreneur  
- [ ] Engaged in manufacturing or service activities (trading activities not eligible for SCLCSS)  
- [ ] Valid Udyam Registration (mandatory for SCLCSS subsidy)  
- [ ] For reimbursement schemes: SC/ST MSE must have availed the relevant service (e.g., bank loan processing fee, testing fee, etc.)  
- [ ] For capacity building training programs: Candidate belonging to SC/ST community and above 18 years of age on date of commencement of training  
- [ ] For SCLCSS: Shareholding of SC/ST entrepreneur >51% (for partnership/Pvt. Ltd/LLP)  
- [ ] Unit not availing any other subsidy for the same plant & machinery/equipment from Central/State Government scheme (and vice-versa)  
- [ ] Industry not covered under RED category as per Classification of industries for consent management  
- [ ] Plant & machinery must be new (fabricated and second-hand not eligible for SCLCSS)  
- [ ] For training fee reimbursement: Maximum 2 courses in total in a financial year for SC/ST entrepreneur and their ward  
- [ ] For capacity building training programs: Minimum 75% attendance required (financial assistance not admissible if <75%)  

**Sources**:  
- Eligibility criteria from KEY FACTS: Sole Proprietorships, Partnerships, Co-operative societies, Private and Public limited companies owned by SC/ST Entrepreneurs of MSE sector engaged in manufacturing and service activities; valid Udyam Registration mandatory for SCLCSS; SC/ST MSEs must have availed relevant service for reimbursement schemes; capacity building training eligibility: SC/ST community and above 18 years; shareholding >51% for partnership/Pvt. Ltd/LLP; SCLCSS exclusions: no other subsidy for same plant & machinery, RED category industries ineligible, fabricated/second-hand plant & machinery not eligible; training fee reimbursement: max 2 courses in FY; attendance: <75% not admissible.  
- Portal: https://scsthub.in

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Self-certified copy of Udyam Registration (UR) & GST (if applicable)  
- Self-certified copy of PAN card (for proprietorship, PAN card of SC/ST proprietor)  
- Copy of Caste Certificate of proprietor/all partners/directors  
- Details of shareholding (for partnership/Pvt. Ltd/LLP to ascertain SC/ST status >51%)  
- Bank attested debit statement with fee charges mentioning transaction details  
- Proof of payment (original receipt, GST invoice, etc.)  
- Bank attested copy of loan sanction letter  
- Cancelled cheque of the current account  
- Business loan availment/disbursement certificate or bank statement  
- Proof of transferred amount as reimbursement by NSSHO/NSIC through PFMS (if previously availed)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- **Special Credit Linked Capital Subsidy Scheme (SCLCSS)**:  
  - Copy of Partnership Deed for Partnership Firm / Memorandum and Article of Association (for LLP/Private Limited Company)  
  - Business loan availment/disbursement certificate or bank statement  
  - Bank attested copy of loan sanction letter  
- **Reimbursement Schemes (Bank Loan Processing, Bank Guarantee Charges, Testing Fee, Export Promotion Council Membership, E-commerce Portal Membership)**:  
  - Attested copy of valid Registration Cum Membership Certificate (RCMC) for Export Promotion Council (if applicable)  
  - Attested copy of membership/registration certificate from e-commerce portal (if applicable)  
  - Test report duly attested (for testing fee reimbursement)  
  - Enrollment number, certificate of successful completion, fee receipt (for training programs)  
  - Supply Order / Purchase Order/ Work Order from CPSE / Central Govt. organization / Department / State PSE, State Govt. organization / State Department against which PBG is taken (for Bank Guarantee Charges reimbursement)  
- **Capacity Building Management Fee Reimbursement Scheme**:  
  - Application form duly filled and signed (to be filled online on NSSH website followed by a print of the form)  
  - Copy of Applicant's Aadhaar Card or other ID proof (Other ID proof allowed only in case of states where Aadhaar is not available)  
  - Proof of age of applicant (Pan Card, Driving License)  
  - Social category Certificate  
  - Original Bank Statement where the course debit charges are visible along with the name of entrepreneur/enterprise as per the cancelled cheque  
  - Enterprise Details  
  - Copy of Udyam Registration  
  - PAN Card (In case of proprietorship firm- PAN of entrepreneur/ in case of other categories of firm- PAN of the enterprise)  
  - GST Number  
  - Additional documents in case of ward of SC ST Entrepreneur:  
    - Proof for establishing relationship between the nominator and nominee (any government issued document with name of nominee and nominator on it e.g. passport, driving license, birth certificate, election I card, Aadhar card, educational certificate etc.)  
    - Nomination form duly filled and signed by entrepreneur in favor of ward declaring that she/he is that the applicant is his/ her ward Son/ daughter)  
    - Details of the course: Enrollment Number, Certificate of successful completion of course, Fee receipt/ challan of fee submission (original), Feedback form  
- **Single Point Registration Scheme (SPRS)**:  
  - Attested copy of valid Registration Cum Membership Certificate (RCMC) issued from e-commerce portal along with the copy of receipt of amount paid for membership  

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] File size: PDFs under 5 MB, images under 2 MB  
- [ ] File naming: Clear and consistent (e.g., `Udyam_Registration.pdf`, `PAN_Card_SCST_Proprietor.pdf`)  
- [ ] All documents must be self-certified or attested as specified (signature with stamp where required)  
- [ ] Name on documents must match exactly with application form and Udyam Registration  
- [ ] Language: English or Hindi; if regional language, provide certified translation  
- [ ] Bank statements: Must show transaction details, loan processing fee/PBG charges/testing fee debited  
- [ ] Proof of payment: Original receipt or system-generated GST invoice with breakup  
- [ ] Caste certificate: Must be valid and issued by competent authority  
- [ ] Udyam Registration: Must be active and valid on date of application

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Project Report / DPR is **not required** for reimbursement schemes, capacity building training fee reimbursement, or Single Point Registration Scheme under National SC/ST Hub.  
For Special Credit Linked Capital Subsidy Scheme (SCLCSS), a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required. Standard sections include:  
1. Executive Summary  
2. Project Background and Objectives  
3. Technical Details (Plant & Machinery specifications, rates, quantities, total value)  
4. Financial Analysis (Cost of scheme, sources of funding, incremental benefits)  
5. Implementation Timeline  
6. Risk Assessment and Mitigation  
7. Conclusion and Recommendations  
*Note: Verify exact DPR requirements with the nodal officer or Prime Lending Institution (PLI) as per SCLCSS guidelines.*  

**Sources**:  
- KEY FACTS: SCLCSS requires submission of "List of eligible plant and machinery along with their detailed specifications, rates, quantities, and total value" (Application Form for SCLCSS)  
- nssh-guidelines.pdf: Chapter on Special Credit Linked Capital Subsidy mentions "List of eligible plant and machinery..." under application procedure  
- Application Form for SCLCSS (PDF): Item 16 explicitly requests this list  
- No DPR mentioned for reimbursement schemes in evidence  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Use the official portal**: Submit application exclusively via https://scsthub.in or through the concerned nodal agency (bank for SCLCSS, NSIC/NSSH office for reimbursements).  
2. **Accuracy of details**: Ensure all information (name, address, Udyam number, PAN, caste certificate details) matches exactly across documents and the form.  
3. **SC/ST verification**: For partnership/Pvt. Ltd/LLP, explicitly calculate and confirm SC/ST shareholding >51% in the shareholding details section.  
4. **Bank details**: Provide current account details and ensure cancelled cheque matches the account used for fee payment.  
5. **Proof of payment clarity**: Attach original receipt or system-generated GST invoice showing the exact fee amount paid (loan processing, PBG, testing, etc.).  
6. **Previous reimbursement disclosure**: If previously availed reimbursement under the same scheme in the current financial year, disclose details in the "Proof of transferred amount" section.  
7. **Declaration and undertaking**: Sign the self-attested declaration and undertaking (where applicable) confirming no double-dipping from other schemes and correctness of information.  
8. **Submission method**: For reimbursement schemes, submit claim with supporting documents to NSIC/NSSH through PFMS portal; for SCLCSS, submit to the concerned Prime Lending Institution (Bank) for upload on MIS Portal.  

**Sources**:  
- KEY FACTS: Application Process section (steps 1–6)  
- Bank Loan Processing Reimbursement Scheme page: Lists documents and notes on self-certified copies, PAN card for proprietorship, shareholding >51%, Partnership Deed/MoA, bank attested debit statement, loan sanction letter, etc.  
- nssh-guidelines-sub-scheme-0.pdf: Application Procedure section (eligible claim(s) with reference date to reach by end of next quarter)

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing successful submission and application/reference number.  
- Store the application/reference number securely in the case file.  
- Set follow-up reminders:  
  - Initial follow-up: 7 days after submission  
  - Second follow-up: 15 days after submission  
  - Escalation trigger: 30 days with no response  
- For SCLCSS: Confirm with the nodal bank that the application has been uploaded on the MIS Portal.  
- For reimbursement schemes: Verify receipt of acknowledgment from NSIC/NSSH via email or portal.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify approval letter from NSIC/NSSH or nodal bank (for SCLCSS)  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount matches eligibility and claim limits (e.g., Rs. 25 Lakh max for SCLCSS, 80% or up to Rs. 1,00,000 for reimbursement schemes)  
- [ ] Utilize funds strictly for the approved purpose (e.g., plant & machinery procurement for SCLCSS, fee payment for reimbursement schemes)  
- [ ] Maintain all utilization records: invoices, payment proofs, asset register entries (for SCLCSS)  
- [ ] Submit Utilization Certificate (if required) within stipulated time (typically 3 months from disbursement)  
- [ ] For capacity building training: Submit outcome report (self-employment/job placement, CPSE linkage, bank loan facilitation, GeM enrollment, GST registration, turnover increase) within 6 months of training completion  
- [ ] Prepare and submit invoice for success fee as per consultancy agreement  
- [ ] Ensure no double-dipping: Confirm no other scheme subsidy claimed for same purpose/asset  
- [ ] Monitor PFMS portal for reimbursement credit to bank account  
- [ ] Retain all documents for audit purposes (minimum 5 years)  

**Sources**:  
- KEY FACTS: Benefits section (Funds released through PFMS portal after submission of claims and required documentation)  
- nssh-guidelines.pdf: Chapter on Capacity Building and Training Programs (Outcome report to be submitted within 6 months; Utilization Certificate as per GFR format)  
- nssh-guidelines-sub-scheme-0.pdf: Release of funds (through PFMS portal in bank account of applicant units)  
- Standard post-approval compliance for government schemes  
- Max per entity: Rs. 25 Lakh (KEY FACTS)  
- Reimbursement limits: 80% or up to specified amounts (KEY FACTS)
