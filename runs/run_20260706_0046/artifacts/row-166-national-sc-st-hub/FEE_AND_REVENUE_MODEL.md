## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client (e.g., subsidy amount received under SCLCSS or reimbursement schemes).  
2. Transparent fee structure with clear milestones for retainer (application preparation) and success fee (fund disbursement).  
3. Risk-adjusted pricing reflecting the scheme’s moderate processing time (claims submitted monthly/bi-monthly, disbursement within 3 months post-submission) and high success rate evidenced by Rs. 3737.23 Crores disbursed to 11,613 SC/ST Owned MSEs in FY 2025-26.  
4. Success fee capped at a percentage of the actual subsidy received, ensuring client retains majority of benefit.
