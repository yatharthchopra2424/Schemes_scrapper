# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping SC/ST entrepreneurs access government support schemes like the National SC/ST Hub. This scheme is designed specifically for businesses owned by Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe entrepreneurs in the manufacturing and service sectors, offering financial assistance for technology upgradation, marketing, training, and more. I’d like to understand your business and see if this could be a good fit for you.

### Discovery Questions
1. Can you confirm your business is at least 51% owned by an SC/ST entrepreneur(s)?  
2. Do you have a valid Udyam Registration number?  
3. Are you engaged in manufacturing or service activities (not trading)?  
4. Have you recently availed or are you planning to avail a term loan for plant and machinery or equipment?  
5. What is the approximate age of your business (date of incorporation/commencement)?  
6. Are you looking to participate in government procurement or public tenders?  
7. Have you incurred any of the following expenses recently: bank loan processing fees, performance bank guarantee charges, testing fees, export promotion council membership, or training from top NIRF institutes?  
8. Are you interested in capacity building training or special marketing assistance for domestic/international exhibitions?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] SC/ST ownership (>51% shareholding by SC/ST entrepreneur(s))  
- [ ] Valid Udyam Registration  
- [ ] Engaged in manufacturing or service sector (not trading)  
- [ ] Not in RED category industries (as per pollution control board classification)  
- [ ] Not using fabricated or second-hand plant & machinery for SCLCSS claims  
- [ ] For training programs: candidate is SC/ST and above 18 years of age  
- [ ] For reimbursement schemes: relevant service availed and proof of payment available  
- [ ] Not availing similar subsidy from another Central/State scheme for same purpose  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it looks like you may be eligible for the National SC/ST Hub scheme, which offers up to Rs. 25 lakh in subsidy for plant and machinery, along with reimbursements for various business expenses. Would you like me to walk you through how it works and what benefits you could access?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
SC/ST entrepreneurs often face challenges in accessing finance for technology upgradation, managing high compliance costs (like bank fees and testing charges), and participating in government procurement due to limited resources and awareness. The National SC/ST Hub addresses these gaps by providing targeted financial support.

**Scheme intro:**
The National SC/ST Hub (NSSH), implemented by the National Small Industries Corporation (NSIC) under the Ministry of MSME, offers a 25% subsidy (up to Rs. 25 lakh) for procurement of new plant and machinery/equipment via institutional credit under the Special Credit Linked Capital Subsidy Scheme (SCLCSS). It also provides reimbursements for bank loan processing charges (80% or up to Rs. 1,00,000), bank guarantee charges (80% or up to Rs. 1,00,000), testing fees (80% or up to Rs. 1,00,000), export promotion council membership (80% or up to Rs. 20,000), training course fees (90% or up to Rs. 1,00,000), and e-commerce portal membership (80% or up to Rs. 25,000), among others. Applications are processed via the portal: https://scsthub.in.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You confirmed SC/ST ownership (>51%)  
- You have a valid Udyam Registration (mandatory for SCLCSS)  
- You are in manufacturing/services (eligible sectors under SCLCSS)  
- You are not in RED category or using second-hand machinery (key exclusions)

**Next step:**
We offer a free, no-obligation eligibility check. If you qualify, we assist with documentation and application on a success-fee-only basis — you pay only when the subsidy is approved and disbursed.
