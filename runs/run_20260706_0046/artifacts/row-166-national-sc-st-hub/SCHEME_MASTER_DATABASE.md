### National SC/ST Hub
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Small Industries Corporation (NSIC), a public-sector undertaking under the administrative control of the Ministry of MSME
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://scsthub.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Sole Proprietorships, Partnerships, Co-operative societies, Private and Public limited companies owned by SC/ST Entrepreneurs of MSE sector engaged in manufacturing and service activities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Manufacturing and service activities (trading activities not eligible for SCLCSS)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have valid Udyam Registration for SCLCSS; for capacity building training programs, candidates must belong to SC/ST community and be above 18 years of age; for reimbursement schemes, SC/ST MSEs must have availed the relevant service and submit required documents including Udyam Registration, PAN card, caste certificate, and proof of payment

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - SCLCSS: 25% subsidy for procurement of plant and machinery/equipment through institutional credit, with overall ceiling of Rs. 25 lakhs
  - Reimbursement schemes: 
    - Bank loan processing charges: 80% or up to Rs. 1,00,000
    - Bank guarantee charges: 80% or up to Rs. 1,00,000
    - Testing fees: 80% or up to Rs. 1,00,000
    - Export promotion council membership: 80% or up to Rs. 20,000
    - Training course fees from top 50 NIRF-rated institutions: 90% or up to Rs. 1,00,000
    - E-commerce portal membership: 80% or up to Rs. 25,000
    - Single Point Registration Scheme: 100% subsidy with nominal fee of Rs. 100
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (funds released through PFMS portal to beneficiary's bank account)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (per claim/submission)

#### Documents Required
1. Self-certified copy of Udyam Registration (UR) & GST (if applicable)
2. Self-certified copy of PAN card (for proprietorship, PAN card of SC/ST proprietor)
3. Copy of Caste Certificate of proprietor/all partners/directors
4. Details of shareholding (for partnership/Pvt. Ltd/LLP to ascertain SC/ST status >51%)
5. Copy of Partnership Deed for Partnership Firm / Memorandum and Article of Association (for LLP/Private Limited Company)
6. Bank attested debit statement with fee charges mentioning transaction details
7. Proof of payment (original receipt, GST invoice, etc.)
8. Business loan availment/disbursement certificate or bank statement
9. Bank attested copy of loan sanction letter
10. Cancelled cheque of the current account
11. Proof of transferred amount as reimbursement by NSSHO/NSIC through PFMS (if previously availed)
12. Attested copy of valid Registration Cum Membership Certificate (RCMC) for export promotion council
13. Attested copy of membership/registration certificate from e-commerce portal
14. Test report duly attested for testing fee reimbursement
15. Enrollment number, certificate of successful completion, fee receipt for training programs

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the relevant sub-scheme (e.g., SCLCSS, reimbursement schemes, capacity building)
2. Ensure eligibility (SC/ST ownership, Udyam Registration, etc.)
3. Gather required documents (Udyam Registration, PAN card, caste certificate, proof of payment, bank statements, etc.)
4. Submit application either online via the NSSH website or through the concerned nodal agency (e.g., bank for SCLCSS, NSIC/NSSH office for reimbursements)
5. For reimbursement schemes, submit claim with supporting documents to the implementing agency (NSIC/NSSH) through PFMS
6. Await verification and disbursement of funds to the beneficiary's bank account
