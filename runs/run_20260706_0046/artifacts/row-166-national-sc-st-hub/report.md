# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The National SC/ST Hub (NSSH) is a flagship scheme of the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India, launched in October 2016 by the Hon'ble Prime Minister. It is implemented by the National Small Industries Corporation (NSIC), a public-sector undertaking under the administrative control of the Ministry of MSME. The scheme aims to enhance the capacity of SC/ST entrepreneurs, promote an "entrepreneurship culture" among the SC/ST population, and empower them to participate in public procurement to fulfill the mandated target of 4% procurement from SC/ST enterprises under the Public Procurement Policy.

### Objectives
- Capacity enhancement of SC/ST entrepreneurs
- Promoting 'entrepreneurship culture' amongst the SC/ST population
- Empowering SC/ST population to participate in the public procurement process
- Fulfilling the mandated target of 4% procurement from SC/ST enterprises under Public Procurement Policy
- Facilitating credit linkages for SC/ST entrepreneurs
- Providing handholding and mentoring support
- Sharing of SC/ST MSEs data with CPSEs to enhance procurement from SC/ST entrepreneurs
- Undertaking outreach activities for the promotion of the scheme among SC/ST population

### Eligibility Matrix

### Benefits & Financial Support
The scheme provides financial support through various reimbursement schemes and subsidies:

#### Special Credit Linked Capital Subsidy Scheme (SCLCSS)
| Parameter | Details |
|----------|---------|
| Subsidy Rate | 25% |
| Eligible Expenditure | Procurement of new Plant & Machinery/Equipment through institutional credit (bank loan) |
| Overall Ceiling | Rs. 25 Lakh |
| Sector Specific Restrictions | None (applicable to both manufacturing and service sectors) |
| Key Conditions | - Units availing subsidy under SCLCSS shall not be allowed to avail any other subsidy for procurement of the same plant & machinery and equipment from any other scheme of Central/State Government and vice-versa<br>- Industries covered under the RED category as per Classification of industries for consent management are not eligible<br>- Fabricated and second-hand plant and machinery shall not be eligible for consideration |

### Application Process
The application process varies slightly by sub-scheme but follows a common framework:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Identify relevant sub-scheme] --> B[Ensure eligibility (SC/ST ownership, Udyam Registration, etc.)]
    B --> C[Gather required documents]
    C --> D{Sub-scheme type}
    D -->|SCLCSS| E[Submit application to concerned Prime Lending Institution (Bank)]
    D -->|Reimbursement Schemes| F[Submit claim with supporting documents to implementing agency (NSIC/NSSH) through PFMS]
    D -->|Capacity Building| G[Apply online via NSSH website or through nodal agency]
    D -->|SMAS| H[Apply online via NSSH website]
    D -->|SPRS| I[Apply online via www.nsicspronline.com or prescribed form to Zonal/Branch Office of NSIC or NSSH Office]
    E --> J[Await verification and disbursement of funds]
    F --> J
    G --> J
    H --> J
    I --> J
    J --> K[Funds released through PFMS portal to beneficiary's bank account]
```

**Key Steps**:
1. Identify the relevant sub-scheme (e.g., SCLCSS, reimbursement schemes, capacity building).
2. Ensure eligibility (SC/ST ownership, Udyam Registration, etc.).
3. Gather required documents (Udyam Registration, PAN card, caste certificate, proof of payment, bank statements, etc.).
4. Submit application either online via the NSSH website or through the concerned nodal agency (e.g., bank for SCLCSS, NSIC/NSSH office for reimbursements).
5. For reimbursement schemes, submit claim with supporting documents to the implementing agency (NSIC/NSSH) through PFMS.
6. Await verification and disbursement of funds to the beneficiary's bank account.

**Key Caveats**:
> - Units availing subsidy under SCLCSS shall not be allowed to avail any other subsidy for procurement of the same plant & machinery and equipment from any other scheme of Central/State Government and vice-versa
> - Financial assistance will not be admissible for candidates with less than 75% attendance in capacity building training programs
> - The SC/ST entrepreneur and their ward will be eligible for a maximum of 2 courses in total in a financial year under the training fee reimbursement scheme
> - Clubbing these components with any other scheme is not permitted; any SC-ST MSE availing reimbursement under all the above-mentioned components cannot avail reimbursement from any other government or private agency
> - Industries covered under the RED category as per Classification of industries for consent management are not eligible for subsidy under SCLCSS
> - Fabricated and second-hand plant and machinery shall not be eligible for consideration for subsidy under SCLCSS
