## 8D. Internal Data Handling Policy

All client data related to scheme consulting engagements shall be handled in accordance with the following standards:

- **Storage:** Encrypted cloud storage with access controls; no storage on personal devices or unencrypted media.
- **Access:** Limited to authorized consultants on a need-to-know basis; role-based permissions enforced.
- **Authentication:** Two-factor authentication (2FA) required for all system access.
- **Security:** Regular security audits, intrusion monitoring, and up-to-date anti-malware protection.
- **Breach Notification:** In the event of a data breach, affected clients and relevant authorities will be notified within 24 hours.
- **Retention:** Client files will be retained for a minimum of two (2) years post case closure, after which they will be securely deleted.
- **Disposal:** Secure deletion methods (e.g., cryptographic erasure) will be used for data disposal.
