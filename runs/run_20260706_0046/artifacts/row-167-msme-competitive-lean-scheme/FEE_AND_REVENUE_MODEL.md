## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are tied to the actual subsidy disbursed under the MSME Competitive (LEAN) Scheme, ensuring clients pay only for realized benefits.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s rolling application basis and milestone-based disbursement (verified by MoMSME), success fees reflect the moderate implementation risk and processing lag.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainer structures are scaled to client size, with micro units accessing foundational support and larger MSMEs receiving end-to-end project management.
4. **Transparency & Compliance**: All fees are disclosed upfront; engagements adhere to MoMSME guidelines and avoid any conflict of interest with assigned Lean consultants.

*Note: The scheme’s 80% subsidy (up to ₹40 lakhs) and milestone-based release create a moderate risk profile. Processing time averages 4–6 months from approval to first disbursement, based on implementation and verification cycles documented in the application process.*
