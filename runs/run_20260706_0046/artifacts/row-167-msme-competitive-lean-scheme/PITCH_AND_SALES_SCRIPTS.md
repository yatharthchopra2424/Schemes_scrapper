## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I’m calling from [Your Firm Name]. We specialise in helping manufacturing MSMEs access government support for operational improvements. The MSME Competitive (LEAN) Scheme from the Ministry of MSME offers up to ₹40 lakhs in subsidy to implement Lean Manufacturing practices—designed specifically to boost productivity, reduce waste, and enhance competitiveness for units like yours. I’d like to understand your current operations and see if this could be a fit.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your manufacturing unit incorporated, and do you have a valid Udyam Registration Certificate?
2. What products do you manufacture, and what is your current annual turnover or investment in plant and machinery?
3. Are you currently facing challenges with operational inefficiencies, high waste levels, or inconsistent delivery performance?
4. Have you explored or implemented any Lean Manufacturing tools (like 5S, Kaizen, Value Stream Mapping) in your processes?
5. What are your goals for improving productivity, quality, or cost efficiency over the next 12–18 months?
6. Do you have access to audited financial statements for the last two years and bank account details for subsidy disbursement?
7. Are you planning any upgrades to machinery or production processes that could be linked to a Lean implementation project?
8. Who authorises major operational or financial decisions in your unit—proprietor, partner, or director?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Unit is engaged in manufacturing activities (as per MSMED Act, 2006)
- [ ] Valid Udyam Registration Certificate is available
- [ ] Unit is operational and based in India (Pan-India eligibility)
- [ ] Audited financial statements of the last two years are accessible
- [ ] Bank account details of the unit are available for subsidy transfer
- [ ] Authorization letter from proprietor/partner/director can be provided
- [ ] Willingness to implement Lean Manufacturing practices and commit to handholding period
- [ ] Project proposal for Lean implementation can be developed (or already exists)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your unit is actively looking to improve operational efficiency—and the MSME Competitive (LEAN) Scheme is designed exactly for this. It offers up to ₹40 lakhs in subsidy to implement Lean Manufacturing, with the government covering 80% of the project cost. Given your focus on [mention specific goal, e.g., reducing waste or improving delivery], this could be a strong fit. May I walk you through how it works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many manufacturing MSMEs struggle with high operational costs, inconsistent quality, excess inventory, and inefficient processes—limiting their ability to compete domestically and internationally. These challenges often stem from unoptimised workflows and lack of structured improvement methodologies.

**Scheme intro:**
The MSME Competitive (LEAN) Scheme, administered by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME), provides financial support of up to 80% of the project cost for implementing Lean Manufacturing practices, with a maximum subsidy of ₹40 lakhs per MSME unit. The remaining 20% is contributed by the beneficiary. Support is released in installments upon milestone verification. Applications are processed year-round via the official portal: https://lean.msme.gov.in.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your unit is engaged in manufacturing and holds a valid Udyam Registration (as confirmed)
- You have audited financial statements for the last two years and bank details ready
- You are willing to implement Lean practices and commit to the required handholding period
