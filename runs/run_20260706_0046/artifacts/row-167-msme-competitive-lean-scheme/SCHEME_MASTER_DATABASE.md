### MSME Competitive (LEAN) Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://lean.msme.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Manufacturing MSMEs
- Age of business: Operational unit (no specific age mentioned)
- Sector: Manufacturing
- Revenue cap: Not specified in evidence
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: Must have valid Udyam Registration; priority given to units willing to implement Lean Manufacturing practices and commit to required handholding and implementation period

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to INR 40 lakhs per MSME unit (80% of project cost, max ceiling INR 40 lakhs)
- Disbursement mode: Installments based on milestone completion (Direct credit after verification)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (project-based, disbursed in installments upon milestone achievement)

#### Documents Required
1. Udyam Registration Certificate
2. Project proposal detailing Lean implementation plan
3. Bank account details of the unit
4. Audited financial statements of the last two years
5. Details of existing machinery and production process
6. Authorization letter from the proprietor/partner/director
7. Quotations from Lean consultants or service providers (if applicable)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register on the official LEAN scheme portal at lean.msme.gov.in
2. Submit online application with details of the MSME unit and Udyam Registration
3. Upload required documents including project proposal and bank details
4. Application is screened by the implementing agency for eligibility
5. Eligible units are selected for implementation
6. A Lean consultant is assigned to conduct initial assessment
7. Detailed project report (DPR) is prepared and submitted
8. Upon approval, implementation of Lean tools begins
9. Progress is monitored and verified
10. Subsidy is disbursed in installments based on milestone completion
