## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **MSME Competitive (LEAN) Scheme** is a subsidy-based initiative launched by the **Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME)** to enhance the competitiveness of manufacturing MSMEs across India through the implementation of Lean Manufacturing practices. The scheme operates on a **rolling basis** with applications accepted throughout the year, subject to fund availability.

### Objectives
- Enhance competitiveness of MSMEs through Lean Manufacturing implementation  
- Reduce waste and improve productivity in manufacturing processes  
- Increase operational efficiency and profitability of participating units  
- Promote sustainable manufacturing practices among MSMEs  
- Enable MSMEs to adopt world-class manufacturing standards  
- Create awareness and build capacity in Lean methodologies  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Criteria | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| **Enterprise Type** | Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) engaged in manufacturing activities | Key Facts |
| **Registration** | Valid Udyam Registration under MSMED Act, 2006 | Key Facts |
| **Operational Status** | Unit must be operational | Key Facts |
| **Geographic Scope** | Pan-India | Key Facts |
| **Commitment** | Willingness to implement Lean Manufacturing practices and commit to required handholding and implementation period | Key Facts |
| **Priority** | Given to units committed to Lean implementation and handholding period | Key Facts |

> **> Warning**: Only manufacturing MSMEs with valid Udyam Registration are eligible. Service-sector MSMEs are excluded.

### Financial Support & Benefits
| Support Type | Details | Maximum Limit | Beneficiary Contribution | Disbursement Mechanism |
|--------------|---------|---------------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| **Subsidy for Lean Implementation** | Financial assistance for implementation of Lean Manufacturing tools | Up to **INR 40 lakhs** per MSME unit | 20% of project cost | Released in installments based on milestone achievement verified by implementing agency |
| **Expert Access** | Access to Lean consultants and trainers | Included in subsidy | N/A | Handholding support during implementation |
| **Operational Benefits** | Improved productivity, reduced operational costs, enhanced quality & delivery, better inventory management, increased competitiveness | N/A | N/A | Outcome of successful implementation |

> **> Key Takeaway**: The scheme covers **80% of project cost** via subsidy, capped at **INR 40 lakhs**. The beneficiary must bear **20% of the project cost**. For example:
> - A project costing INR 50 lakhs → Max subsidy = INR 40 lakhs (80%), Beneficiary pays INR 10 lakhs  
> - A project costing INR 30 lakhs → Subsidy = INR 24 lakhs (80%), Beneficiary pays INR 6 lakhs  
> - A project costing INR 60 lakhs → Max subsidy capped at INR 40 lakhs, Beneficiary pays INR 20 lakhs

### Application Process Steps (Detailed)
1. **Register** on the official LEAN scheme portal at [https://lean.msme.gov.in](https://lean.msme.gov.in)  
2. **Submit online application** with MSME unit details and Udyam Registration  
3. **Upload required documents** (see list below)  
4. **Application screened** by implementing agency (MoMSME) for eligibility  
5. **Eligible units selected** for implementation  
6. **Lean consultant assigned** to conduct initial assessment  
7. **Detailed Project Report (DPR)** prepared and submitted  
8. **Upon approval**, implementation of Lean tools begins  
9. **Progress monitored and verified** by agency/consultant  
10. **Subsidy disbursed in installments** based on milestone completion  

### Required Documents
1. Udyam Registration Certificate  
2. Project proposal detailing Lean implementation plan  
3. Bank account details of the unit  
4. Audited financial statements of the last two years  
5. Details of existing machinery and production process  
6. Authorization letter from proprietor/partner/director  
7. Quotations from Lean consultants or service providers (if applicable)  

### Key Caveats
- Financial support limited to **80% of project cost**; beneficiary bears **20%**  
- Maximum financial assistance capped at **INR 40 lakhs** per unit  
- Only manufacturing MSMEs with valid Udyam Registration eligible  
- Subsidy disbursed **only after verification of milestone completion**  
- Scheme subject to **availability of funds**; may be revised or withdrawn by government  
- Applications accepted on **rolling basis** throughout the year  

### Implementation Agency & Portal
- **Implementing Agency**: Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME)  
- **Application Portal**: [https://lean.msme.gov.in](https://lean.msme.gov.in)  
- **Status**: Rolling basis — applications accepted year-round subject to fund availability
