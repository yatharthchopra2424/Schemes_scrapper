## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Enterprise is registered under the MSME Act, 2006  
- [ ] Possesses a valid Udyam Registration Certificate  
- [ ] Classified as Micro, Small, or Medium Enterprise (based on investment and turnover criteria as per MSME Act)  
- [ ] Engaged in manufacturing or service activities  
- [ ] No sector-specific restrictions (open to all sectors)  
- [ ] No location-based restrictions (Pan-India applicability)  
- [ ] No ownership profile restrictions (open to all ownership types)  
- [ ] Project involves adoption of digital technologies (e.g., cloud, AI, IoT, digital marketing)  
- [ ] Intends to use an empanelled vendor/service provider from the Digital MSME Scheme list  
- [ ] Willing to bear 50% of the project cost (subsidy covers max 50%, up to INR 10 Lakhs)  
- [ ] Has not exceeded the maximum subsidy limit of INR 10 Lakhs under this scheme  

> **Sources**: Scheme eligibility criteria from key facts; Application Portal: https://digital.msme.gov.in

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Udyam Registration Certificate  
- PAN Card of the enterprise  
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- Bank statement (last 6 months)  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the last 2 financial years  
- Authorization letter from proprietor/partner/director  
- Cancelled cheque or bank account details for subsidy transfer  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Project proposal detailing the digital technology to be adopted (e.g., cloud, AI, IoT, digital marketing)  
- Cost estimates and quotations from the selected empanelled vendor  
- Proof of implementation (completion report) – *to be submitted post-implementation*  
- Invoices and payment receipts for the digital solution – *to be submitted post-implementation*  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format  
- File size for each document must not exceed 5 MB  
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ClientID_Date.pdf` (e.g., `DigitalMSME_UdyamCert_Case001_20240515.pdf`)  
- Udyam Registration Certificate must be valid and downloadable from udyamregistration.gov.in  
- Project proposal must clearly specify the digital technology, objectives, expected outcomes, and implementation timeline  
- Vendor quotations must be on official letterhead, include GSTIN, and be from an empanelled vendor under the scheme  
- Bank account details must match the name on Udyam registration and include IFSC code  
- All documents must be in English or accompanied by a certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A formal Detailed Project Report (DPR) is **not explicitly mandated**, but a comprehensive **project proposal** is required as per scheme guidelines. This should include:  
- Objective of adopting the digital technology  
- Description of the technology (e.g., cloud ERP, AI-based inventory, IoT sensors, digital marketing platform)  
- Implementation plan and timeline  
- Expected benefits (efficiency gains, cost reduction, market reach, etc.)  
- Total project cost and cost-breakup  
- Details of the empanelled vendor  
- Undertaking on 50% beneficiary contribution  

> **Note**: If the nodal officer requests a DPR during verification, prepare one with the above sections. Otherwise, the project proposal suffices. Verify with nodal officer if uncertain.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal at https://digital.msme.gov.in and register using Udyam Registration Number.  
2. Ensure the Udyam Registration Certificate is valid and active on the Udyam portal before proceeding.  
3. Fill in enterprise details exactly as per Udyam registration (name, address, PAN, GSTIN, etc.).  
4. Select the correct MSME category (Micro/Small/Medium) based on latest investment/turnover.  
5. Clearly specify the digital technology being adopted (must align with empanelled vendor list).  
6. Upload project proposal and vendor quotations in PDF format (max 5 MB each).  
7. Declare that 50% of the project cost will be borne by the enterprise and that subsidy is sought only for the eligible 50% (capped at INR 10 Lakhs).  
8. Confirm that the vendor is empanelled under the Digital MSME Scheme (verify list on portal).  
9. Double-check bank account details for subsidy transfer (must match Udyam-registered name).  
10. Review all declarations and ensure the authorization letter is uploaded and signed.  
11. Take a screenshot of the completed form before final submission.  
12. Submit only after verifying all fields and attachments; no edits allowed post-submission.  

> **Portal-Specific Note**: The portal validates Udyam registration in real-time; ensure no discrepancies in name or number.

- Immediately after submission, take a screenshot showing:  
  - Application reference number  
  - Timestamp  
  - “Application Submitted Successfully” message  
- Save the screenshot in the case folder with name: `DigitalMSME_Submission_Case[ID]_YYYYMMDD.png`  
- Store the application reference number in the case tracker and set follow-up reminders for:  
  - 7 days post-submission  
  - 14 days post-submission  
  - 30 days post-submission  
- Do not proceed to implementation until formal approval is received from the designated authority

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify approval letter from designated authority (check for sanctioned subsidy amount, project reference, and validity period)  
- [ ] Confirm that sanctioned subsidy does not exceed 50% of project cost or INR 10 Lakhs, whichever is lower  
- [ ] Notify client to proceed with implementation using the empanelled vendor as per approved proposal  
- [ ] Ensure all implementation activities are completed within the validity period mentioned in the approval letter  
- [ ] Collect post-implementation documents:  
  - Completion report signed by client and vendor  
  - Original invoices and payment receipts  
  - Proof of payment (bank statement showing vendor payment)  
- [ ] Submit completion report and invoices via the Digital MSME Portal: https://digital.msme.gov.in for verification  
- [ ] Follow up for subsidy disbursement (expected post-verification)  
- [ ] Verify that subsidy amount is credited to the beneficiary’s bank account (as per Udyam registration)  
- [ ] Advise client to retain all records for 3 years for audit purposes  
- [ ] Calculate and confirm success fee as per consultancy agreement  
- [ ] Obtain client feedback on the digital solution’s impact on operations, efficiency, and market reach  

> **Post-Submission Note**: Subsidy is disbursed only after successful implementation and verification; ensure client does not claim subsidy prematurely.
