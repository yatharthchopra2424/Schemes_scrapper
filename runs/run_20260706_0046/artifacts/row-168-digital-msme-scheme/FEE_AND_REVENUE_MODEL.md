## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-based pricing**: Fees aligned with the ₹10 Lakhs maximum subsidy benefit per MSME under the Digital MSME Scheme.
2. **Risk-adjusted success fees**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s post-implementation disbursement model and verification requirements, which introduce moderate execution risk.
3. **Volume incentives**: Lower success fee percentages for higher-value engagements (though scheme caps limit individual benefits to ₹10L, multiple projects or phased implementations may apply).
4. **Transparency**: Clear communication that the subsidy covers up to 50% of project costs, with a maximum of ₹10 Lakhs, requiring client co-investment.

*Note on risk profile*: The scheme has a rolling application basis with funds subject to availability, and subsidy is released only after successful implementation and verification. Processing time typically spans 3–6 months from application to disbursement, introducing revenue lag and execution risk that justify success-fee-only or hybrid models.
