## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I'm calling from [Your Firm Name]. We specialise in helping MSMEs access government support for digital transformation. The Digital MSME Scheme from the Ministry of MSME offers up to INR 10 Lakhs in subsidy for adopting technologies like cloud, AI, IoT, or digital marketing — and I'd like to understand if this could support your current growth plans.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your business incorporated, and do you have a valid Udyam Registration Certificate?
2. Are you currently exploring or planning to adopt any digital technologies such as cloud computing, AI, IoT, or digital marketing tools?
3. What is the estimated cost of the digital solution you're considering, and have you already identified a vendor?
4. What specific challenges are you facing in your operations that digital adoption could help solve (e.g., inefficiencies, limited market reach, high operational costs)?
5. Are you prepared to bear 50% of the project cost, as the scheme requires beneficiary contribution?
6. Do you have access to quotations, project proposals, and bank details needed for the application?
7. Have you previously applied for any MSME or digitalisation-related government schemes?
8. What is your timeline for implementing a digital solution, and are you looking to start soon?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is a registered MSME under the MSME Act, 2006 (manufacturing or service)
- [ ] Valid Udyam Registration Certificate is available
- [ ] Enterprise is located in India (Pan-India scheme)
- [ ] Project involves adoption of eligible digital technologies (cloud, AI, IoT, digital marketing, etc.)
- [ ] Vendor is empanelled under the Digital MSME Scheme
- [ ] Beneficiary can bear 50% of project cost
- [ ] Subsidy request does not exceed INR 10 Lakhs (50% of project cost)
- [ ] Application is being made on a rolling basis (funds subject to availability)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like you're actively considering a digital upgrade and meet the eligibility criteria for the **Digital MSME Scheme**, which could cover up to **INR 10 Lakhs** in subsidy for your project. Let me walk you through how it works and how we’ve helped similar MSMEs secure this support.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many MSMEs struggle with outdated processes, limited online visibility, and high operational costs due to manual systems. Adopting digital tools like cloud platforms, AI, or e-commerce can transform efficiency and market reach — but the upfront investment often poses a barrier, especially for smaller enterprises.

**Scheme intro:**
The **Digital MSME Scheme**, administered by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME), offers a **subsidy of up to 50% of project cost**, with a **maximum ceiling of INR 10 Lakhs per MSME**, for adopting approved digital technologies. The subsidy is disbursed post-implementation and verification, and the remaining 50% is borne by the beneficiary.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You hold a valid **Udyam Registration Certificate**, confirming your MSME status  
- You are planning to adopt **eligible digital technologies** (e.g., cloud, AI, IoT, digital marketing) from an empanelled vendor  
- You are prepared to **contribute 50% of the project cost**, as required by the scheme  
- Your **subsidy request is within the INR 10 Lakhs limit** based on your project cost estimates
