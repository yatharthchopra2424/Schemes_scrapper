### Digital MSME Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://digital.msme.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) registered under the MSME Act, 2006
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: No restrictions (all sectors eligible)
- Revenue cap: Not specified (eligibility based on MSME classification under MSME Act, 2006)
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have a valid Udyam Registration Certificate; open to manufacturing and service enterprises; no restrictions based on location or ownership profile

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Subsidy of up to 50% of project cost, with a maximum ceiling of INR 10 Lakhs per MSME
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (subsidy disbursed directly into the bank account after validation)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (subsidy released post-implementation and verification)

#### Documents Required
1. Udyam Registration Certificate
2. Project proposal detailing the digital technology to be adopted
3. Cost estimates and quotations from the vendor
4. Proof of implementation (completion report)
5. Invoices and payment receipts
6. Bank account details for subsidy transfer
7. Authorization letter from the proprietor/partner/director

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register on the Udyam portal and obtain Udyam Registration Certificate.
2. Identify the digital technology solution to be adopted (e.g., cloud, AI, IoT, digital marketing).
3. Select a certified vendor or service provider from the empanelled list.
4. Submit the project proposal along with cost estimates through the Digital MSME portal.
5. Obtain approval from the designated authority.
6. Implement the digital solution with the vendor.
7. Submit completion report and invoices for verification.
8. Receive subsidy disbursement directly into the bank account after validation.
