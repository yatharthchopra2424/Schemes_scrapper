## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The client is an Informal Micro Enterprise (IME) not registered on the Udyam Registration Portal (URP)
- [ ] The client is outside the GST ecosystem (i.e., not registered under GST)
- [ ] The client is a micro enterprise (as per MSME definition)
- [ ] The client is not eligible for direct registration on the Udyam Assist Platform (UAP); must approach a Designated Agency (DA)
- [ ] The client has provided consent for sharing their data with a Designated Agency (DA) for Udyam registration
- [ ] The client is located in India (Pan-India applicability)
- [ ] The client’s business activity falls under the National Industrial Classification (NIC) codes applicable to MSMEs
- [ ] The client is not currently availing benefits under any other Udyam registration (to avoid duplication)

> **Note**: Eligibility is determined at the Designated Agency (DA) level. The DA must be registered on the Udyam Assist Platform to facilitate registration on behalf of the IME.

**Sources**:  
- Scheme Key Facts: Eligibility, Target Beneficiaries, Application Process  
- Supporting Evidence: FAQ (Q10, Q13), MSME Formalisation page, Disclaimer, Terms and Conditions  
- Application Portal: https://udyamassist.gov.in

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- [ ] PAN card of the business proprietor/partner/director  
- [ ] Aadhaar card of the authorized signatory  
- [ ] Bank account statement (last 6 months)  
- [ ] Udyam Registration Portal (URP) check (to confirm non-registration)  
- [ ] GST registration status check (to confirm outside GST ecosystem)  
- [ ] Business address proof (electricity bill, rent agreement, or property tax receipt)  
- [ ] Cancelled cheque or bank passbook copy  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- [ ] **Designated Agency (DA) Letter of Authorisation** designating its Nodal Officer (required for all DAs)  
- [ ] **RBI registration certificate** (mandatory for NBFCs and NBFC-MFIs acting as DAs)  
- [ ] **Letter of confirmation from Self-Regulatory Organisation (SRO)** (required for MFIs which are not NBFCs)  
- [ ] **Consent letter from the IME** authorizing the DA to share their data for Udyam registration (implied via DA process; explicit consent recommended)  
- [ ] **Data upload template** (Smart Excel or SFTP-ready format) containing IME customer details as per UAP protocol  

> **Note**: The IME does not submit documents directly. The DA collects and submits the above on behalf of the IME after obtaining consent.

**Sources**:  
- Scheme Key Facts: Required Documents, Application Process  
- Supporting Evidence: FAQ (Q05, Q06, Q07, Q13), Designated Agencies page, Udyam Assist FAQ (udyamassist-faq), Disclaimer  

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)  
- [ ] Document names follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ClientID_Date.pdf` (e.g., `UAP_DA_AuthorisationLetter_DA001_20240515.pdf`)  
- [ ] Aadhaar and PAN numbers are masked (showing only last 4 digits) where full display is not required  
- [ ] Bank statements show clear transaction history, account holder name, and IFSC/MICR code  
- [ ] Letters are on official letterhead, signed, stamped, and dated  
- [ ] RBI registration certificate and SRO confirmation letter are valid and not expired  
- [ ] Consent letter includes IME name, DA name, purpose of data sharing, and signature/thumb impression  
- [ ] All documents are in English or accompanied by a certified translation  
- [ ] No alterations, overwrites, or unauthenticated edits visible

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- **Not required**. The Udyam Assist Platform does not require a Detailed Project Report (DPR) or project report for registration.  
- If the DA is seeking funding or linkage under another scheme (e.g., PMEGP, CGTMSE), a DPR may be needed separately — **verify with nodal officer or lending institution**.

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts (Financial Support, Application Process), Supporting Evidence (FAQ Q07, MSME Formalisation page)

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Only Designated Agencies (DAs) can register on the Udyam Assist Platform** — IMEs cannot apply directly.  
2. The DA must log in at https://udyamassist.gov.in and navigate to the registration section.  
3. Fill in the DA’s official details: legal name, address, PAN, CIN (if applicable), and contact information.  
4. Upload the **Letter of Authorisation designating the Nodal Officer** (mandatory for all DAs).  
5. **NBFCs and NBFC-MFIs** must additionally upload their **RBI registration certificate**.  
6. **MFIs not classified as NBFCs** must upload a **letter of confirmation from their Self-Regulatory Organisation (SRO)**.  
7. Accept the **Terms and Conditions** and **Privacy Policy** of the Udyam Assist Platform before submission.  
8. Ensure the Nodal Officer’s details (name, designation, email, mobile) are accurate — they will receive login credentials.  
9. After submission, await approval from SIDBI (Implementing Agency) — typically 3–7 working days.  
10. Upon approval, the DA receives login credentials and can add up to 5 sub-users for platform access.  
11. The Nodal Officer then uses the interface to upload IME customer data via **SFTP, Smart Excel, or other approved protocols**.  
12. Data must conform to the UAP-prescribed format (available in “Useful Documents” section on the portal).  
13. After upload, the platform validates data and generates URN and UAC — downloadable by the DA and shareable with the IME.  
14. The DA must inform the IME via SMS/email and assist them in downloading the UAC from https://udyamassist.gov.in using validation details (e.g., mobile/Otp).

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts (Application Process), Supporting Evidence: FAQ (Q05, Q06, Q07, Q08), Udyam Assist FAQ (udyamassist-faq), Designated Agencies page, Login/Registration pages

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] DA’s Letter of Authorisation is signed, stamped, and on official letterhead  
- [ ] RBI registration certificate (if applicable) is valid and legible  
- [ ] SRO confirmation letter (if applicable) is current and issued by a recognised SRO (e.g., MFIN, Sa-Dhan)  
- [ ] All uploaded documents are in PDF format, clearly scanned, and under 5 MB  
- [ ] Nodal Officer details are complete and functional (active email and mobile number)  
- [ ] DA has accepted the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy (checkbox confirmed)  
- [ ] Data upload template for IMEs follows UAP format (Smart Excel/SFTP-ready)  
- [ ] Consent from IMEs for data sharing is documented (signed form or verbal consent recorded as per policy)  
- [ ] No duplicate DA registration attempted (check existing login credentials)  
- [ ] All information matches DA’s official records (PAN, address, registration number)

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- [ ] Submit the DA registration form via https://udyamassist.gov.in after completing all fields and uploading documents  
- [ ] Take a **screenshot of the submission confirmation page** showing timestamp and application ID (if generated)  
- [ ] Save the **application reference number** (if provided) or note the date and time of submission  
- [ ] Set a **follow-up reminder for Day 5 post-submission** to check application status  
- [ ] Monitor the DA’s registered email (Nodal Officer’s) for login credentials or queries from SIDBI  
- [ ] Upon approval, download and securely store the login credentials  
- [ ] After first IME data upload, take a screenshot of the upload confirmation and URN/UAC generation status  
- [ ] Store all URNs and UACs in a secure, access-controlled database linked to IME consent records

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and securely store the DA’s login credentials (username, password, portal URL) from SIDBI  
- [ ] Log in to https://udyamassist.gov.in and change password on first login  
- [ ] Add sub-users (up to 5) as needed, assigning roles and permissions  
- [ ] Download and verify the DA’s approval certificate or registration confirmation from the portal  
- [ ] For each IME onboarded:  
    - Confirm data upload was successful via SFTP/Smart Excel  
    - Verify that URN and UAC have been generated and are available for download  
    - Notify the IME via SMS/email with download instructions and validation details (e.g., mobile number, OTP)  
    - Assist the IME in downloading the UAC from https://udyamassist.gov.in/verification-urn or /download-uac  
    - Confirm the Udyam Assist Certificate (UAC)  
    - [ ] Maintain a register of all IMEs assisted, including: IME name, URN issued, date of UAC download, consent record  
    - [ ] Ensure IMEs are informed about benefits: PSL eligibility, merchant QR codes, digital marketplace access  
    - [ ] Utilization check: Confirm that the IME has used the URN for loan applications or digital onboarding (optional, based on follow-up)  
    - [ ] For fee-based services (if any): Track and invoice success fee as per internal policy (UAP itself is free; any service charge is DA’s discretion)  
- [ ] Monthly: Download MIS/reports from DA dashboard to monitor registration trends and completion rates  
- [ ] Quarterly: Review data quality and consent records for compliance with UAP guidelines  
- [ ] Annually: Renew consent from IMEs if required under evolving guidelines (check for updates on portal)
