## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified the Udyam Assist Platform (UAP) for Informal Sector — a recognition scheme administered by Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI) — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is access to Priority Sector Lending (PSL) classification for bank loans and issuance of Udyam Registration Number (URN) and Udyam Assist Certificate (UAC), enabling integration into digital and financial ecosystems.

## 2. Recommended Schemes

| Udyam Assist Platform (UAP) for Informal Sector | Access to Priority Sector Lending (PSL) and Udyam Registration Number (URN) | Rolling basis — no fixed timeline; processing upon DA validation | High |

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment for Informal Micro Enterprises (IMEs)  
2. Guidance on engaging with a Designated Agency (DA) such as a bank, NBFC, or MFI  
3. Assistance in preparing and submitting required documents via the DA  
4. Portal registration support on [https://udyamassist.gov.in](https://udyamassist.gov.in) for the DA  
5. Facilitation of data upload by the DA using approved protocols (SFTP, smart EXCELs)  
6. Monitoring of validation and generation of Udyam Registration Number (URN) and Udyam Assist Certificate (UAC)  
7. Support in downloading UAC and informing the IME via SMS/Email  
*Note: No DPR/project report is required, as UAP is a registration and facilitation platform without direct financial disbursement.*

## 4. What We Need From You
1. Documents as per the scheme checklist: DA’s Letter of Authorisation designating its Nodal Officer (and, if applicable, RBI registration certificate for NBFCs/NBFC-MFIs or SRO confirmation letter for MFIs)  
2. Appoint a single point of contact within your organisation for coordination  
3. Respond to queries within 2 business days to avoid delays
