## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the tangible benefit derived — enabling access to Priority Sector Lending (PSL) and digital financial inclusion — rather than direct financial outlay from the scheme.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s reliance on third-party Designated Agencies (DAs) for data submission and validation, and the absence of direct financial disbursement, success fees are calibrated to reflect moderate operational risk and dependency on client-DA coordination.
3. **Volume Incentive**: Lower success fees at higher tiers reflect economies of scale in processing bulk IME registrations through institutional DAs (banks, NBFCs, MFIs).
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Udyam Assist Platform (UAP) does not provide direct financial grants or subsidies. Its value lies in enabling access to credit and digital systems via Udyam Registration Number (URN). Therefore, revenue models must focus on facilitation, registration support, and compliance services — not on claiming scheme payouts. Processing time is minimal (real-time validation post-data upload), so revenue lag is primarily driven by client onboarding and DA coordination, not scheme approval delays.
