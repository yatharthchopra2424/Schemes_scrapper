# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping informal micro enterprises like yours access formal recognition and financial inclusion through government initiatives. The Udyam Assist Platform is designed specifically for businesses operating outside the GST ecosystem that need a pathway to priority sector lending and digital integration. I’d like to understand your current operations to see if this platform can support your growth goals.

### Discovery Questions
- Is your business currently registered under GST, or do you operate outside the GST ecosystem?
- Are you seeking formal recognition to access bank loans classified under Priority Sector Lending (PSL)?
- Do you currently have a relationship with a bank, NBFC, or MFI that could act as a Designated Agency for your registration?
- What is the approximate age of your business, and are you classified as a micro enterprise based on investment in plant and machinery or equipment?
- Have you faced challenges in accessing digital payment systems or obtaining merchant QR codes for your business?
- Are you looking for a government-recognized identity (like a Udyam Registration Number) to access governance or financial services?
- Do you consent to sharing your basic enterprise data with a trusted financial institution for the purpose of registration?
- Are you interested in accessing knowledge and skills development resources alongside formal registration?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is an Informal Micro Enterprise (IME) not registered on Udyam Registration Portal (URP)
- [ ] Business operates outside the GST ecosystem
- [ ] Business is willing to approach a Designated Agency (DA) such as a bank, NBFC, or MFI for assistance
- [ ] Business consents to data sharing with the DA for validation and registration
- [ ] Business seeks Udyam Registration Number (URN) for PSL classification or digital integration
- [ ] Business is located in India (pan-India eligibility)
- [ ] Business is not seeking direct financial grants or subsidies from UAP (understands it’s a facilitation platform)
- [ ] Business understands that IMEs cannot register directly and must go through a DA

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your business aligns well with the Udyam Assist Platform’s goal of bringing informal micro enterprises into the formal financial system. The platform facilitates Udyam Registration Number (URN) issuance through trusted Designated Agencies, enabling access to priority sector lending and digital payment integration — all without requiring direct application from you. Let me explain how this works and the specific benefits it can deliver for your enterprise.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Informal micro enterprises often struggle to access formal credit because they lack recognized registration, making it difficult for banks to classify their loans under Priority Sector Lending (PSL) as per RBI guidelines. This limits credit flow, hinders digital payment integration (e.g., merchant QR codes), and excludes them from governance and marketplace systems that rely on a formal digital identity.

**Scheme intro:**
The Udyam Assist Platform (UAP), implemented by the Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI) under the Ministry of MSME, is a registration and facilitation platform that enables Informal Micro Enterprises (IMEs) to obtain a Udyam Registration Number (URN) and Udyam Assist Certificate (UAC) through Designated Agencies such as banks, NBFCs, and MFIs. While it does not provide direct financial support, the URN enables classification of bank loans under Priority Sector Lending (PSL) and facilitates access to digital payment ecosystems, governance systems, and skills development resources.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your business is an Informal Micro Enterprise (IME) not currently registered on the Udyam Registration Portal (URP)
- You operate outside the GST ecosystem, making you eligible for the assist-based registration model
- You are willing to work with a Designated Agency (DA) such as your bank or MFI to share data with consent
- You seek formal recognition to access financial and digital services — a core objective of UAP
