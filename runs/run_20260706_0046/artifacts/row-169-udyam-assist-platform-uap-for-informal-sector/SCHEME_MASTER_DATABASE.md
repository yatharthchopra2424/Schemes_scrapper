### Udyam Assist Platform (UAP) for Informal Sector
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://udyamassist.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Informal Micro Enterprises (IMEs)
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Not specified
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must not be registered on the Udyam Registration Portal (URP) and must be outside the GST ecosystem; registration must be facilitated through a Designated Agency (DA) such as Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs), Micro Finance Institutions (MFIs), or Non Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs); IMEs cannot register directly

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Udyam Registration Number (URN) and Udyam Assist Certificate (UAC); enables classification of bank loans under Priority Sector Lending (PSL) per RBI guidelines; facilitates issuance of merchant QR codes for digital payments; acts as a passport for access to digital systems in governance, financial services, and marketplaces; supports policy interventions; provides access to knowledge and skills development
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (URN/UAC issued electronically via platform)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (registration-based, with ongoing access to linked benefits)

#### Documents Required
1. DA’s Letter of Authorisation designating its Nodal Officer (for all DAs)
2. RBI registration certificate (for NBFCs and NBFC-MFIs)
3. Letter of confirmation from Self-Regulatory Organisation (SRO) (for MFIs which are not NBFCs)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Designated Agencies (DAs) such as banks, NBFCs, MFIs must register on the Udyam Assist Platform (www.udyamassist.gov.in) by submitting a Letter of Authorisation designating a Nodal Officer.
2. NBFCs and NBFC-MFIs must additionally submit their RBI registration certificate.
3. MFIs not classified as NBFCs must submit a letter of confirmation from their Self-Regulatory Organisation (SRO).
4. DAs must accept the Terms and Conditions of the platform.
5. Upon approval by the Implementing Agency (SIDBI), DAs receive login credentials.
6. The DA’s Nodal Officer can add up to 5 sub-users for platform access.
7. Registered DAs upload data of their Informal Micro Enterprise (IME) customers via SFTP, smart EXCELs, or other approved protocols.
8. The Udyam Assist Platform processes the data, conducts validations, and generates Udyam Registration Number (URN) and Udyam Assist Certificate (UAC).
9. The URN and UAC are made available to the DA, which then informs the DA, which then informs the IME via SMS/Email.
10. IMEs can download the UAC from the portal (www.udyamassist.gov.in) after validation.
