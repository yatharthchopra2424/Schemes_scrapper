# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Existing SIDBI customer (mandatory)
- [ ] Micro or Small Enterprise (MSE) as per MSME definition
- [ ] GST Registration mandatory
- [ ] Udyam Registration mandatory
- [ ] Minimum 2 years of business operations
- [ ] Cash accruals in the last audited financial year
- [ ] PAN-India geographic scope (no regional restrictions)
- [ ] Business must be engaged in manufacturing or service sector
- [ ] No defaults to banks or financial institutions

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate / Partnership Deed / LLP Agreement
- PAN Card of the business entity
- ITR (Income Tax Returns) for last 2 years
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration Certificate
- KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN of proprietor/partners/directors)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Proof of 2 years of business operations (e.g., old GST returns, bank statements, Udyam certificate with date)
- Last audited financial statement showing cash accruals (Profit & Loss Statement, Balance Sheet)
- Purchase Invoice (for PIBF) or Sales Invoice (for SIBF) as applicable
- Bank account details for NACH mandate (cancelled cheque or bank statement)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format
- File size per document should not exceed 5 MB
- Name on documents must match exactly with PAN/Udyam/GST registration
- Financial statements must be audited and signed by a Chartered Accountant
- Invoices must be clear, legible, and show supplier/customer details, invoice number, date, and value
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in regional language, certified translation required
- Udyam and GST certificates must be valid and active on the date of application

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for this scheme. The Working Capital Loan Scheme (SIDBI) is invoice-based and does not require a Detailed Project Report (DPR). However, if the client is applying for a higher limit or has complex needs, **verify with the nodal officer** whether supplementary documentation is needed.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the portal at [https://sidbi.in/home-product](https://sidbi.in/home-product) and click on “Working Capital Apply Now”  
2. Register as an existing SIDBI customer using your customer ID or linked mobile/email  
3. Complete KYC verification using Aadhaar OTP or net banking  
4. Select the product: “Working Capital Loan Scheme” or “GST Sahay – Invoice Based Financing”  
5. Choose PIBF (Purchase Invoice Based Financing) or SIBF (Sales Invoice Based Financing) based on invoice type  
6. Enter invoice details: number, date, value, and supplier/customer GSTIN  
7. Upload all required documents in PDF format (max 5 MB per file)  
8. Provide bank account details for NACH mandate (account number, IFSC, bank name)  
9. Review and accept the Key Fact Statement (KFS) before final submission  
10. Submit and save the application reference number  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All eligibility criteria verified and documented  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents collected and quality-checked  
- [ ] Application form filled completely with accurate data  
- [ ] Documents uploaded correctly and legibly  
- [ ] NACH mandate details provided and verified  
- [ ] Declarations and consents signed (digitally or scanned)  
- [ ] Application reference number saved and shared with client

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the portal: [https://sidbi.in/home-product](https://sidbi.in/home-product)  
- Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing the application reference number and timestamp  
- Store the reference number securely in the case file  
- Set an internal reminder to follow up on Day 25 if no update is received  
- Inform the client of submission and share the reference number

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify approval letter from SIDBI (via email or portal)  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount matches requested limit (up to ₹50 lakh)  
- [ ] Verify disbursement process:  
    - For PIBF: 100% of invoice value paid directly to supplier’s bank account  
    - For SIBF: Up to 90% of invoice value paid directly to customer’s bank account  
- [ ] Ensure NACH mandate is active and repayment schedule is set  
- [ ] Track utilization of funds against the invoice  
- [ ] Obtain utilization certificate or disbursement confirmation from SIDBI  
- [ ] Follow up on success fee invoice (if applicable per firm policy)  
- [ ] Advise client on repayment due dates and interest servicing  
- [ ] Maintain records for audit and future renewal (limits valid for 1 year with half-yearly review)
