## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the scheme’s maximum benefit of ₹50 lakh, ensuring consultant compensation reflects the tangible working capital support delivered.
2. **Risk Mitigation**: Success fees are tiered to balance effort with outcome certainty; the scheme’s digital, straight-through process and high approval likelihood for eligible SIDBI customers reduce risk, and GST/Udyam-registered MSMEs with 2+ years operations and cash accruals justify moderate success fees.
3. **Accessibility**: Retainer fees are structured to be affordable for micro and small enterprises, aligning with the scheme’s focus on existing SIDBI customers who may have limited upfront liquidity but strong cash flow.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Working Capital Loan Scheme features quick disbursement (often within 48 hours) and a high success rate for eligible applicants, as evidenced by the streamlined GST Sahay platform and SIDBI’s digital lending infrastructure. This predictable, low-failure profile supports the proposed success fee ranges, particularly for Starter and Growth tiers where most applicants fall.
