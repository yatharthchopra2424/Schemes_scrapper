## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
MSMEs often face liquidity crunches due to delayed payments from large buyers or the need to pay suppliers upfront for raw materials. This disrupts operations, limits growth, and increases reliance on expensive informal credit. Traditional loans require collateral and lengthy processing, which many small businesses cannot afford or access.

**Scheme intro:**
The Working Capital Loan Scheme (SIDBI) is a collateral-free loan product offered by the Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI). It provides need-based working capital finance with a maximum credit limit of up to ₹50 lakh. For Purchase Invoice Based Financing (PIBF), 100% of the invoice value is processed and paid to the supplier’s bank account. For Sales Invoice Based Financing (SIBF), up to 90% of the invoice value is processed and paid directly to the customer’s bank account. Repayment is made by the customer along with applicable interest on the due date.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are an existing SIDBI customer — a mandatory requirement
- You have GST and Udyam Registration — both compulsory
- Your business has been operational for over 2 years — meeting the minimum threshold
- Your last audited financial statement showed cash accruals — a key eligibility criterion
