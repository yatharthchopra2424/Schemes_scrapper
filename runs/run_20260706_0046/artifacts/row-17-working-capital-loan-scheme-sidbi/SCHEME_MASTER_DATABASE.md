### Working Capital Loan Scheme (SIDBI)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://sidbi.in/home-product
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Existing SIDBI customers who are Micro & Small Enterprises
- Age of business: 2 years of business operations
- Sector: MSME; micro enterprises; small enterprises
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: GST and Udyam Registration mandatory; cash accruals in the last audited financial year

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Maximum credit limit of up to ₹50 lakh; for Purchase Invoice Based Financing (PIBF), 100% of the invoice value is processed and paid to the supplier’s bank account; for Sales Invoice Based Financing (SIBF), up to 90% of the invoice value is processed and paid directly to the customer’s bank account
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit
- Duration of benefit: One-time (per invoice/disbursement)

#### Documents Required
1. GST Registration
2. Udyam Registration
3. Proof of 2 years of business operations
4. Last audited financial statement showing cash accruals
5. Purchase or Sales Invoice (as applicable)
6. KYC documents
7. Bank account details for NACH mandate

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Registration & KYC
2. Sanction & Acceptance of limit/loan
3. Digital Document Execution & NACH mandate
4. Disbursement
