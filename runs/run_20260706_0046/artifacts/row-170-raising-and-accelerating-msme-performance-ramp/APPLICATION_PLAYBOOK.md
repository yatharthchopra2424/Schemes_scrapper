## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Business is registered under Udyam or EM-II (valid Udyam Registration Certificate)  
- [ ] Enterprise is operational and has a valid GSTIN  
- [ ] Business falls under manufacturing, services, or agro-processing sector (priority sectors)  
- [ ] Enterprise is an existing MSME (or prospective MSME seeking handholding/pre-investment support)  
- [ ] For women entrepreneurs, SC/ST owners, or enterprises in aspirational districts: verify eligibility for priority consideration  
- [ ] Business has not exceeded fund availability limits (subject to rolling basis and fund availability)  
- [ ] Project proposal aligns with eligible activities: technology upgradation, quality certification (ISO, patent), digital transformation, or market development  

> **Note**: Max financial support per entity is up to INR 10 lakhs for technology upgradation and quality certification grants. Interest subvention (2% p.a. for up to 2 years) and credit guarantee coverage (up to INR 2 crores under CGTMSE) are also available.  
> **Sources**: [RAMP Portal](https://ramp.gov.in), Scheme Key Facts

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
✅ **Required**. A detailed project report is mandatory for all RAMP applications.  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
1. Executive Summary  
2. Business Profile (Udyam details, GSTIN, PAN, years in operation)  
3. Project Description (objectives, scope, technology/process to be adopted)  
4. Implementation Plan (timeline, milestones, responsible personnel)  
5. Cost Breakdown (item-wise, vendor quotations, taxes)  
6. Funding Plan (loan amount, grant sought, promoter contribution)  
7. Expected Outcomes (productivity increase, cost savings, market access, employment generation)  
8. Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
9. Sustainability and Compliance Statement  
10. Annexures: Quotations, Udyam, PAN, GST, bank details, promoter KYC  

> **Note**: For grant components (ISO, patent, digital transformation), the project report must clearly specify the use of grant funds. Verify exact format with the State Implementation Agency or nodal officer if in doubt.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Register on Udyam portal first** if not already registered (https://udyamregistration.gov.in) — RAMP requires valid Udyam registration.  
2. **Approach a participating bank or financial institution** for loan application — RAMP benefits are routed through banks.  
3. **Submit project proposal to the bank** detailing purpose of fund utilization (technology upgradation, market development, etc.).  
4. **Ensure bank forwards proposal to State Level Bankers' Committee (SLBC) or designated nodal agency** for approval.  
5. **Fill all fields in the bank’s loan application form accurately** — mismatches in Udyam, PAN, or GSTIN will lead to rejection.  
6. **Attach all scheme-specific documents** (project report, quotations, etc.) as annexures to the bank submission.  
7. **Declare that the enterprise is operational and has valid GSTIN** — false declarations attract penalties.  
8. **Confirm that the loan is a term loan from a scheduled bank** — interest subvention (2% p.a. for up to 2 years) applies only to such loans.  
9. **For grant components, apply separately through the State Implementation Agency** with project report and required documents (as per Step 6 of application process).  
10. **Retain copies of all submissions and acknowledgments** for audit and follow-up.  

> **Portal Note**: While applications are processed via banks, the RAMP portal (https://ramp.gov.in) provides guidelines, FAQs, and status tracking. Use it for reference but do not submit directly unless specified by the nodal agency.

- [ ] Application timeline communicated to client (bank approval → SLBC → disbursement)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify approval letter from bank or nodal agency (specifying sanctioned amount, interest subvention rate, grant amount, and validity period)  
- [ ] Confirm that interest subvention is approved at 2% per annum for up to 2 years on the term loan  
- [ ] If grant component approved: verify sanctioned grant amount (up to INR 10 lakhs) and conditions for disbursement  
- [ ] Ensure credit guarantee coverage under CGTMSE is confirmed (if applicable, up to INR 2 crores)  
- [ ] Disburse loan and/or grant funds as per approved plan  
- [ ] Utilize funds strictly for the approved purpose (technology upgradation, quality certification, etc.)  
- [ ] Maintain all invoices, vouchers, and utilization records  
- [ ] Prepare and submit Utilization Certificate (UC) to the nodal agency/bank within stipulated time (usually within 3 months of expenditure)  
- [ ] Ensure UC is audited if required (grant components subject to audit)  
- [ ] Track and report on outcomes: productivity improvement, cost savings, market access, employment generation  
- [ ] Follow up on non-financial benefits: market development assistance, e-commerce access, exhibition participation, skill development programs  
- [ ] Confirm client maintains valid Udyam registration to continue availing benefits  
- [ ] Calculate and invoice success fee as per engagement agreement  
- [ ] Archive all documents for compliance and future reference  

> **Note**: Benefits may be withdrawn in case of misuse, non-compliance, or failure to submit utilization certificates. Advise client to retain records for at least 3 years for audit purposes.  
> **Sources**: [RAMP Portal](https://ramp.gov.in), Scheme Key Facts
