**Policy Statement:**  
All client data collected during the course of scheme consulting engagements shall be handled in
accordance with the highest standards of data security and privacy.

**Data Storage:**  
All client data shall be stored in encrypted cloud storage environments with access restricted to
authorized personnel only. Encryption shall be AES-256 or equivalent standard.

**Access Control:**  
Access to client data shall be role-based and limited to consultants directly engaged on the specific
case. Multi-factor authentication (2FA) shall be mandatory for all access points.

**Device Policy:**  
No personal devices shall be used for the storage, processing, or transmission of client data. All
work shall be conducted on firm-issued, secured devices.

**Retention:**  
Client data shall be retained for a period of two (2) years post case closure, after which it shall be
securely deleted unless longer retention is required by law or scheme-specific guidelines.

**Breach Notification:**  
In the event of a data breach, the firm shall notify affected clients and relevant authorities within
24 hours of discovery, in accordance with applicable data protection regulations.

**Scheme-Specific Retention Note:**  
All documents submitted to Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME) under Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance (RAMP)
must be retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit purposes.

**Compliance:**  
This policy aligns with the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and
Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 under the IT Act, 2000, and shall be reviewed
annually or as required by regulatory changes.
