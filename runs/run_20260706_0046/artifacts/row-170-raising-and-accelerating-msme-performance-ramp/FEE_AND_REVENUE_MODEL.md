## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are capped at a percentage of the actual financial benefit secured (grants, interest subvention value, or credit guarantee coverage), ensuring clients pay only for realized outcomes.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s rolling application basis and dependency on bank approvals and fund availability, success fees reflect moderate execution risk; retainers cover upfront effort in project report preparation and bank liaison.
3. **Transparency**: Clients receive a clear breakdown of potential benefits (e.g., up to ₹10 lakhs grant, 2% interest subvention for up to 2 years, credit guarantee up to ₹2 crores) and associated fees before engagement.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The RAMP scheme involves multi-stakeholder coordination (bank, SLBC, state agency) and has a processing time of 3–6 months for approvals, increasing operational risk. However, the high fund size (₹6,062 Cr) and rolling basis improve approval likelihood for well-prepared applications, justifying mid-range success fees.
