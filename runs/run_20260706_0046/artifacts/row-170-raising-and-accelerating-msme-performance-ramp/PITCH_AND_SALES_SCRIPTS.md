## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I'm calling from [Your Firm Name]. We specialise in helping MSMEs access government support for growth and technology upgradation. Given your work in [mention sector if known, e.g., manufacturing or services], I wanted to see if you might benefit from the Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance (RAMP) scheme, which offers up to INR 10 lakhs in grants for technology adoption and quality certification, along with interest subvention on bank loans.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your business incorporated or registered under Udyam?
2. Are you currently operational and do you have a valid GSTIN?
3. Are you looking to upgrade technology, obtain ISO certification, or adopt digital tools in the near future?
4. What is your primary sector — manufacturing, agro-processing, or services?
5. Are you a woman entrepreneur, or do you belong to an SC/ST community, or operate in an aspirational district?
6. Do you have a project in mind for which you might need financing, such as machinery purchase or certification?
7. Are you currently in touch with a bank for a term loan, or planning to approach one?
8. Have you previously applied for any MSME-related government schemes?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is registered under Udyam or EM-II
- [ ] Enterprise is operational and has a valid GSTIN
- [ ] Engaged in manufacturing, services, or agro-processing sector
- [ ] Seeking support for technology upgradation, quality certification, or digital transformation
- [ ] Priority consideration: woman entrepreneur, SC/ST owner, or located in an aspirational district
- [ ] Has a defined project requiring financial support (loan or grant)
- [ ] Willing to approach a participating bank or submit grant application via state agency

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you may be eligible for the Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance (RAMP) scheme, which offers up to INR 10 lakhs in grants for technology adoption and quality certification, plus interest subvention on bank loans. Let me share how this could work for your business.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many MSMEs struggle to afford technology upgrades, certification costs, or digital transformation due to high upfront expenses and limited access to affordable finance. This hinders their ability to compete in domestic and global markets, improve quality, and scale operations.

**Scheme intro:**
The Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance (RAMP) scheme, administered by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME), provides financial support through a combination of interest subvention on bank loans, credit guarantee coverage under CGTMSE, and grants of up to INR 10 lakhs for technology upgradation, quality certification (e.g., ISO), patent registration, and digital transformation. The scheme is accessible via the portal: https://ramp.gov.in

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are an existing MSME registered under Udyam or EM-II with a valid GSTIN
- Your business is operational and engaged in manufacturing, services, or agro-processing
- You are planning or considering technology upgradation, quality certification, or digital adoption
- [If applicable] You may receive priority support as a woman entrepreneur, SC/ST owner, or enterprise in an aspirational district
