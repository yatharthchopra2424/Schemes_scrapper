### Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance (RAMP)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://ramp.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (applications accepted throughout the year subject to fund availability)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Existing MSMEs registered under Udyam or EM-II, including manufacturing and services enterprises; prospective MSMEs through handholding and pre-investment support
- Age of business: Operational (must be currently functioning)
- Sector: Manufacturing, agro-processing, and services (priority sectors); all sectors eligible with focus on women entrepreneurs, SC/ST owners, and enterprises in aspirational districts
- Revenue cap: Not specified in evidence
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: Must have valid GSTIN; priority given to women entrepreneurs, SC/ST owners, and enterprises in aspirational districts

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - Interest subvention: 2% per annum for up to 2 years on term loans for technology upgradation
  - Grants: Up to INR 10 lakhs for ISO certification, patent registration, and digital transformation
  - Credit guarantee coverage: Up to INR 2 crores per borrower under CGTMSE
- Disbursement mode: Combination of interest subvention (via bank), direct credit (grants (through state implementation agency), and credit guarantee (via CGTMSE)
- Duration of benefit: 
  - Interest subvention: Up to 2 years
  - Grants: One-time
  - Credit guarantee: As per loan tenure under CGTMSE guidelines

#### Documents Required
1. Udyam Registration Certificate
2. PAN of the business
3. GSTIN certificate
4. Bank account details
5. Project report detailing the purpose of fund utilization
6. Quotations for machinery/equipment or technology adoption
7. Proof of address and identity of the proprietor/partners/directors
8. Financial statements (if applicable)
9. ISO certification application (if seeking grant for certification)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register on the Udyam portal if not already registered.
2. Approach a participating bank or financial institution for loan application.
3. Submit project proposal detailing the purpose of fund utilization (e.g., technology upgradation, market development).
4. Bank forwards the proposal to the State Level Bankers' Committee (SLBC) or designated nodal agency for approval.
5. Upon approval, loan is disbursed with applicable interest subvention.
6. For grant components, apply through the state implementation agency with required documents and project report.
7. Utilize funds as per approved plan and submit utilization certificates.
