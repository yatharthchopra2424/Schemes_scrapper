## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance (RAMP)** scheme is a pan-India initiative implemented by the **Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME)**. It is designed to enhance the competitiveness, productivity, and sustainability of MSMEs through a blend of financial and non-financial support mechanisms. The scheme operates on a **rolling basis** with applications accepted throughout the year, subject to fund availability. The total fund size is **INR 6,062 crore**, with a maximum per-entity benefit of **up to INR 10 lakhs** for technology upgradation and quality certification and technology adoption components.

### Objectives
The RAMP scheme aims to achieve the following strategic objectives:
- Improve access to finance and markets for MSMEs  
- Strengthen institutional capabilities at state and central levels  
- Promote technology upgradation and innovation among MSMEs  
- Enhance sustainability and resilience of MSMEs  
- Support formalization and growth of MSMEs  
- Facilitate market linkages and integration into value chains  
- Build capacity of MSMEs through skill development and training  
- Support women-led and SC/ST-owned MSMEs through targeted interventions

### Financial Support & Benefits
The scheme provides a **multi-layered support structure** combining interest subvention, credit guarantees, grants, and non-financial assistance.

#### Financial Support Components
| Support Type | Details | Maximum Benefit | Conditions |
|--------------|--------|------------------|------------|
| **Interest Subvention** | 2% per annum on term loans for technology upgradation | Up to 2 years | Only on term loans from scheduled banks; subject to fund availability |
| **Credit Guarantee** | Coverage under CGTMSE scheme | Up to INR 2 crores per borrower | Applicable on eligible loans; requires bank participation in CGTMSE |
| **Grants** | For technology adoption, quality certification (ISO), patent registration, digital transformation | Up to INR 10 lakhs per entity | Requires submission of utilization certificates; subject to audit |
| **Market Development Assistance** | Access to e-commerce platforms, participation in national/international exhibitions | Not quantified | Facilitated through scheme implementation |
| **Skill Development & Training** | Mentoring, handholding, capacity building programs | Not quantified | Includes formalization and growth support |
| **Incubation & Acceleration** | Access to incubation, acceleration, and business development services | Not quantified | Available through empanelled agencies |

> **Important**: Financial support is subject to availability of funds under the scheme. Benefits are available only for approved project proposals. Grant components require submission of utilization certificates and are subject to audit.

### Application Process Flow
The application process involves coordination between the MSME, participating banks, and nodal agencies. Below is a detailed flowchart:

#### Step-by-Step Breakdown
1. **Udyam Registration**: If not already registered, the MSME must register on the Udyam portal (https://udyamregistration.gov.in) to obtain a Udyam Registration Number (URN).
2. **Bank Engagement**: Approach a participating bank or financial institution that is part of the CGTMSE and RAMP network.
3. **Project Proposal Submission**: Submit a detailed project report outlining the purpose of fund utilization (e.g., machinery upgradation, ISO certification, patent filing, digital tools adoption).
4. **Nodal Agency Review**: The bank forwards the proposal to the State Level Bankers' Committee (SLBC) or designated state implementation agency for technical and financial appraisal.
5. **Loan Disbursement with Subvention**: Upon approval, the loan is disbursed. Interest subvention at **2% per annum** is credited for up to **2 years** on the term loan portion.
6. **Grant Application (if applicable)**: For grant components (technology adoption, quality certification, etc.), apply separately through the state implementation agency with:
   - Udyam Registration Certificate
   - PAN and GSTIN
   - Bank account details
   - Project report
   - Quotations for equipment/technology
   - Proof of address and identity
   - Financial statements (if applicable)
   - ISO certification application (if seeking grant for certification)
7. **Fund Utilization & Reporting**: Utilize funds strictly as per the approved plan. Submit **Utilization Certificates (UCs)** and audited expenditure statements upon completion.
8. **Monitoring & Audit**: The implementing agency may conduct audits or site visits to verify compliance. Non-submission of UCs or misuse can lead to benefit withdrawal and recovery.

> **Warning**: Do not assume automatic approval. Each step requires documentation and validation. Incomplete applications or failure to submit UCs will result in rejection or fund recovery.
