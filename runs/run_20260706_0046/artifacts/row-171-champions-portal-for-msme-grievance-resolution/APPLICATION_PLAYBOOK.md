# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is a Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise (MSME) as defined under the MSMED Act 2006.
- [ ] The applicant is an MSME or other related stakeholder (e.g., industry associations, experts, technocrats, Ministry functionaries) who can voice issues and grievances through the portal.
- [ ] The grievance relates to finance, raw materials, labor, regulatory permissions, or other issues faced by MSMEs.
- [ ] The applicant has access to the Champions Portal at https://champions.gov.in for registration and grievance submission.
- [ ] The applicant understands that grievances will not be accepted via telephone and must be submitted exclusively through the portal.
- [ ] The applicant acknowledges that the matter should not remain unattended for more than three days and should not remain inconclusive after seven days.

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Udyam Registration Certificate (URC)
- PAN Card of the business/entity
- Aadhaar of authorized signatory
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)
- Bank statement (last 6 months)
- ITR (last 1-3 years, if applicable)
- Business address proof (electricity bill, rent agreement, etc.)
- Incorporation/Partnership deed (if applicable)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- No scheme-specific documents are required beyond the universal set, as the Champions Portal is a grievance redressal platform and does not provide financial support. The primary requirement is submission of grievance details via the online form.

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible scans or clear photographs.
- File names should match the applicant’s Udyam-registered name.
- File size should not exceed 5 MB per document.
- Documents must be in PDF or JPEG/PNG format.
- Non-English documents must be accompanied by a certified Hindi or English translation.
- All declarations and undertakings must be signed and dated.

### Client Communication Template
**Subject:** Submission of Grievance via Champions Portal – MSME Grievance Redressal Scheme  

Dear [Client Name],  

This is to inform you that we will be assisting you in submitting your grievance through the **Champions Portal for MSME Grievance Resolution** (https://champions.gov.in), a rolling-basis initiative by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises.  

The portal allows MSMEs and stakeholders to register grievances related to finance, raw materials, labor, regulatory permissions, or other issues and receive timely resolution. Key points to note:  
- Grievances must be submitted **only through the portal** (not via telephone).  
- Each submission generates an auto-generated unique ID (starting with GR for general grievances).  
- The matter will not remain unattended for more than three days and will not remain inconclusive after seven days.  
- For extreme cases, you may be contacted telephonically or in person by the Champion Control Room (CCR), which operates from 8 AM to 10 PM daily.  

Please provide the details of your grievance (including category: GR, CP, CO, IN, or VI) and any supporting documents by [Date – 3 days from now] so we can proceed with registration.  

For assistance, contact the Champions Portal helpdesk at champions@gov.in.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required. The Champions Portal is a grievance redressal platform and does not require a Detailed Project Report (DPR).  
**Note:** Verify with nodal officer if any supporting documentation is needed for complex grievances.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Visit the Champions Portal at https://champions.gov.in and click on “Register” or “Log in”.
2. Complete registration using Udyam Registration Number, PAN, Aadhaar, and business details.
3. Navigate to the grievance submission section (e.g., “Register Grievance” or “Submit Complaint”).
4. Select the appropriate grievance type:  
   - GR: General Grievance  
   - CP: CPGRAMS-fetched  
   - CO: Collaborative Ideas  
   - IN: Administrative Interventions  
   - VI: VIP References  
5. Provide a clear, concise description of the grievance (finance, raw materials, labor, regulatory permissions, etc.).
6. Upload any supporting documents (if applicable) in PDF/JPEG format (<5 MB each).
7. Review the entered details before final submission.
8. Submit the form and note the auto-generated unique ID (e.g., GRXXXXXX) for tracking.
9. Save a screenshot of the submission confirmation page.
10. Do not submit grievances via telephone — portal submission is mandatory.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All mandatory fields in the grievance form are completed.
- [ ] Grievance type (GR/CP/CO/IN/VI) is correctly selected.
- [ ] Supporting documents (if any) are attached and meet quality standards.
- [ ] Screenshot of submission confirmation is saved.
- [ ] Unique ID (starting with GR/CP/CO/IN/VI) is recorded.
- [ ] Declaration of accuracy is acknowledged (implicit in portal submission).

---

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the grievance via the Champions Portal at https://champions.gov.in.
- Immediately after submission, take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing the auto-generated unique ID and timestamp.
- Store the screenshot and unique ID in the case file with label: “Champions Portal Submission – [Unique ID]”.
- Set a follow-up reminder for Day 3 to check if the grievance has been acknowledged.
- Set a second reminder for Day 7 to verify if resolution has been initiated (as matters should not remain inconclusive beyond seven days).

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Monitor the Champions Portal or registered email for updates on grievance status using the unique ID (GR/CP/CO/IN/VI).
- [ ] If resolved, obtain and save the resolution communication or closure notice from the portal.
- [ ] If escalated to Champion Control Room (CCR), note the intervention details and final outcome.
- [ ] If the matter remains unresolved after seven days, initiate follow-up as per the escalation path.
- [ ] For settled grievances, confirm that the client has received a way forward or resolution path.
- [ ] No utilization certificate or financial verification is required, as the scheme does not provide direct financial support.
- [ ] Invoice success fee: [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT] (if applicable per firm policy).
