## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the non-financial support delivered via the Champions Portal, focusing on grievance resolution and facilitation of access to government schemes.
2. Transparent fee structures with retainers covering advisory and handholding efforts, and success fees tied to measurable outcomes like grievance closure or scheme linkage.
3. Tiered pricing to reflect client scale and complexity, with MSMEs and startups forming the core beneficiary base per scheme eligibility.
4. Scheme-specific note: The Champions Portal offers no direct financial grants or subsidies; its value lies in timely grievance redressal (within 3–7 days) and handholding to access other schemes. Success fees are therefore lower-risk and process-driven, based on procedural completion rather than uncertain financial disbursement.
