# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping MSMEs navigate government support systems like the Champions Portal for MSME Grievance Resolution. This portal is designed specifically for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises to voice operational challenges—whether related to finance, raw materials, labor, or regulatory permissions—and get timely resolution through a technology-driven, single-window system. I’d like to understand your current pain points to see if this platform could help streamline issue resolution for your business.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your Udyam Registration number, and when was your enterprise registered?  
2. What types of operational challenges have you faced recently—such as delays in payments, raw material shortages, labor issues, or regulatory hurdles?  
3. Have you previously tried to resolve grievances through government channels, and if so, what was your experience in terms of response time and outcome?  
4. Are you currently facing any unresolved issues related to access to government schemes, credit, or market opportunities that you’d like escalated?  
5. Do you have access to digital tools and internet connectivity to submit and track grievances online via a portal?  
6. Are you open to using a centralized platform that auto-generates unique IDs and routes issues to concerned officials under the MSME Ministry for resolution within defined timelines?  
7. Would you benefit from handholding support to access other government schemes through guided facilitation?  
8. Are you interested in a system that integrates with CPGRAMS and other MSME Ministry portals for seamless grievance tracking?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is a registered MSME (Micro, Small, or Medium Enterprise) via Udyam Registration  
- [ ] Enterprise faces operational grievances in areas like finance, raw materials, labor, or regulatory permissions  
- [ ] Willing to submit grievances exclusively through the Champions Portal (not via telephone)  
- [ ] Has basic digital access to visit https://champions.gov.in and register/login  
- [ ] Seeks timely resolution (target: unattended ≤3 days, inconclusive ≤7 days)  
- [ ] Open to escalation to Champion Control Room (Central or State Level) if needed  
- [ ] Interested in handholding to access other government schemes  
- [ ] Located in India (Pan-India applicability)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like the Champions Portal for MSME Grievance Resolution could help you resolve ongoing operational challenges quickly and systematically—especially since it’s designed for MSMEs like yours, offers auto-tracking, and ensures timely follow-up. Let me explain how it works and how we’ve helped similar businesses get their issues addressed efficiently.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**  
MSMEs often struggle with unresolved grievances related to delayed payments, supply chain disruptions, labor compliance, or regulatory approvals. These issues, if not addressed promptly, can disrupt operations, increase costs, and hinder access to other government schemes. Traditional channels are often slow, opaque, and lack accountability, leaving businesses without recourse.

**Scheme intro:**  
The Champions Portal for MSME Grievance Resolution, launched by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME), is a technology-driven, single-window platform at https://champions.gov.in. It does not provide direct financial support but offers non-financial benefits such as grievance redressal, guidance, and facilitation of access to government schemes. The portal uses AI, machine learning, and data analytics to reduce delays and duplication, integrates with CPGRAMS and other MSME Ministry portals, and assigns auto-generated unique IDs (e.g., GR for general grievances) for tracking. Issues are routed to concerned officials and monitored by Champion Control Rooms (Central and State Level), with a commitment that no grievance remains unattended for more than three days or inconclusive after seven days.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**  
- Your business is a registered MSME (per Udyam), making you eligible to submit grievances via the portal  
- You face operational challenges in finance, raw materials, labor, or regulatory areas—exactly the domains the portal addresses  
- You have digital access to register and track grievances online at https://champions.gov.in  
- You seek timely, structured resolution—aligned with the portal’s service standards

"We've helped [N] similar businesses get their grievances registered and resolved through the Champions Portal. [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]"

Let’s conduct a free, no-obligation eligibility and readiness check to confirm your ability to use the portal effectively. We’ll guide you through registration, grievance submission, and tracking—charging only a success fee if we help you achieve resolution or access linked schemes. There’s no upfront cost.
