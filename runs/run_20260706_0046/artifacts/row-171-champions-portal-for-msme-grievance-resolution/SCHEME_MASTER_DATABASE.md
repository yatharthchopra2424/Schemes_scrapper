### Champions Portal for MSME Grievance Resolution
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://champions.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) and other related stakeholders
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Not specified
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must submit grievances through the portal only; grievances will not be invited on telephone

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: No direct financial support; provides grievance redressal, handholding, and facilitation of access to government schemes
- Disbursement mode: Not applicable (non-financial support)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing (timely resolution with matters not remaining unattended for more than three days and not inconclusive after seven days)

#### Documents Required
1. No specific documents required for grievance submission; registration or login to the portal is needed

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the Champions Portal at https://champions.gov.in
2. Register or log in to the portal
3. Submit grievances related to finance, raw materials, labor, regulatory permissions, or other issues
4. Receive an auto-generated unique ID (starting with GR for general grievances, CP for CPGRAMS-fetched, CO for collaborative ideas, IN for administrative interventions, VI for VIP references)
5. The issue is automatically segregated subject-wise and officer-wise and transferred to concerned officials under the MSME Ministry
6. Concerned officers deal with the issue with promptness, sensitivity, and seriousness
7. If unresolved, the matter can be escalated to the Champion Control Room (CCR) for further intervention
8. The Secretary MSME reviews the functioning and holds fortnightly sessions with applicants whose grievances are not settled
