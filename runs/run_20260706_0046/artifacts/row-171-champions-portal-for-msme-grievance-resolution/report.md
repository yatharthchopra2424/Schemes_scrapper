# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
The **Champions Portal for MSME Grievance Resolution** (Scheme ID: row-171) is a pan-India initiative implemented by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME). Launched on a rolling basis with no fixed deadline, it serves as a grievance redressal and handholding platform focused on non-financial support. The portal does not provide direct financial assistance such as grants, loans, or subsidies but facilitates access to government schemes and resolves MSME issues through technology-driven processes.

**Application Portal:** https://champions.gov.in  
**Status:** Active (Last Updated: 2020)  
**Contact Email:** champions@gov.in  

### Objectives
The Champions Portal aims to:
- Resolve MSME grievances including finance, raw materials, labor, and regulatory permissions, particularly in the Covid-19 context.
- Help MSMEs capture new opportunities, such as manufacturing medical equipment (PPEs, masks) for national and international markets.
- Identify and encourage "sparks"—potential MSMEs capable of withstanding economic challenges to become national and international champions.
- Provide handholding, guidance, empowerment, and encouragement to the MSME sector.
- Capture and encourage ideas and collaborative ecosystems of experts and technocrats to guide MSMEs on technical, managerial, and financial issues.
- Record self-proactive administrative interventions by MSME Ministry functionaries.
- Capture VIP references from Hon. Ministers and other dignitaries.

### Eligibility Matrix
| **Eligible Entities** | **Details** |
|----------------------|-------------|
| Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) | Defined under MSMED Act, 2006; must be registered on Udyam portal. |
| Other Related Stakeholders | Includes industry associations, experts, technocrats, government officials, and dignitaries who can submit grievances or collaborative ideas. |
| Geographic Scope | Pan-India (all states and union territories). |
| Grievance Types | Finance, raw materials, labor, regulatory permissions, administrative interventions, VIP references, collaborative ideas, and CPGRAMS-fetched grievances. |

> **Note:** Grievances will NOT be accepted via telephone; submission is ONLY through the Champions Portal at https://champions.gov.in.

### Benefits & Financial Support
The Champions Portal provides **non-financial support** only. No direct monetary benefits are offered.

| **Benefit Category** | **Details** |
|----------------------|-------------|
| Grievance Redressal | Facilities to voice issues and receive resolution and way forward. |
| AI/ML & Data Analytics | Extensive use to reduce human intervention, delay, and duplication. |
| Portal Integration | Seamless integration with CPGRAMS and other MSME Ministry portals. |
| Unique ID Generation | Auto-generated IDs: GR (general), CP (CPGRAMS-fetched), CO (collaborative ideas), IN (administrative interventions), VI (VIP references). |
| Timely Resolution | Matters not unattended >3 days; not inconclusive after 7 days. |
| Champion Control Rooms (CCR) | Access to Central and State-level CCRs for monitoring and issue resolution. |
| Scheme Facilitation | Support for MSMEs to avail benefits of government schemes and access inputs/requirements. |
| Handholding & Guidance | Empowerment and ease for MSME sector through technology-driven support. |

> **Critical Caveats:**
> - Grievances must be submitted ONLY via the portal (not telephone).
> - Issues must not remain unattended for >3 days or inconclusive after >7 days.
> - In extreme humanitarian/injustice cases where decision is against the unit, promoters must be heard telephonically or in person.
> - Champion Control Rooms (Central and State) operate daily from **8 AM to 10 PM**.
> - State-level CCRs require **4–6 officials** (preferably Group A/B) from nominated institutions.

### Application Process
The following Mermaid.js flowchart illustrates the step-by-step grievance submission and resolution process:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Start: Visit Champions Portal] --> B[https://champions.gov.in]
    B --> C{Register/Login}
    C -->|New User| D[Complete Registration]
    C -->|Existing User| E[Login]
    D --> F[Submit Grievance]
    E --> F
    F --> G[Select Grievance Type:<br/>Finance, Raw Materials, Labor,<br/>Regulatory Permissions, etc.]
    G --> H[Auto-generated Unique ID:<br/>GR/CP/CO/IN/VI + Number]
    H --> I[Automatic Segregation:<br/>Subject-wise & Officer-wise]
    I --> J[Transfer to Concerned MSME Ministry Officials]
    J --> K[Officer Addresses Issue:<br/>Promptness, Sensitivity, Seriousness]
    K --> L{Issue Resolved?}
    L -->|Yes| M[Close Case with Unique ID]
    L -->|No| N[Escalate to Champion Control Room<br/>(Central/State Level)]
    N --> O[CCR Intervention:<br/>8 AM–10 PM Daily]
    O --> P{Resolved at CCR?}
    P -->|Yes| M
    P -->|No| Q[Fortnightly Review by Secretary MSME<br/>with Unsettled Applicants]
    Q --> M
    M --> End[Grievance Resolution Complete]
```

**Key Process Notes:**
- Registration requires basic details; Udyam Registration is not mandatory for grievance submission but enhances credibility.
- Grievances are auto-segregated and routed to relevant officials without manual intervention.
- The Secretary of MSME reviews unresolved cases every fortnight via video conference through the Control Room.
- All interactions are tracked via the unique ID; applicants can check status at https://champions.gov.in/MyMsme/grievance/resolution_solution_status.htm.
