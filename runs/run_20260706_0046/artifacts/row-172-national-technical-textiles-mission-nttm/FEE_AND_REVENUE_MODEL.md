## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit delivered under NTTM, which provides grants-in-aid, subsidies, and equity participation of up to 50% of project cost for R&D, 25–50% for market development, and 100% government funding for infrastructure in certain cases.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the rolling application basis and dependency on fund availability and approval cycles (with processing time subject to technical and financial appraisal, milestone verification, and utilization certificate submission), success fees reflect moderate execution risk.
3. **Tiered Access**: The scheme’s eligibility — covering startups, MSMEs, research institutions, universities, and industry associations engaged in technical textiles — aligns with Growth, Scale, and Enterprise tiers; Starter tier (micro/proprietorship < ₹10L turnover) is typically ineligible due to scale and documentation requirements (e.g., audited financials of last 3 years).
4. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover proposal preparation, documentation, and portal submission via https://technicaltext.in, while success fees are contingent on fund sanction and disbursement.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Retainer fee for National Technical Textiles Mission (NTTM) application support, including project proposal preparation, documentation compilation (Certificate of Incorporation, PAN/GST, audited financials, technical details, land/building/MoU documents as applicable), and submission via the designated portal (https://technicaltext.in).

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for National Technical Textiles Mission (NTTM) grant acquisition, contingent on approval and disbursement of financial support (grants-in-aid, subsidies, or equity participation) by the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, implementing agency.
