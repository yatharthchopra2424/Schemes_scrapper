## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping technical textiles manufacturers, startups, MSMEs, and research institutions access government funding for innovation and growth. The National Technical Textiles Mission (NTTM) supports exactly these kinds of initiatives—whether you're developing new materials, scaling production, or expanding into export markets. I’d like to understand your current projects and see if NTTM could be a fit for your goals.

### Discovery Questions
- What stage is your technical textiles project or business currently at—idea, prototype, production, or scaling?
- Are you engaged in R&D, innovation, or skill development activities related to technical textiles manufacturing?
- Is your organisation registered in India as a company, startup, MSME, research institution, university, or industry association?
- Have you incurred or planned project costs for which you’re seeking financial support (e.g., R&D, market development, infrastructure)?
- Are you targeting domestic manufacturing, export promotion, or import substitution in technical textiles?
- Do you have a project proposal or concept note ready that outlines technical innovation or market development goals?
- Are you collaborating with academia, industry partners, or planning to set up a common facility center?
- Are you aware of any budget or timing constraints that might affect your ability to apply this fiscal year?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is an Indian company, startup, MSME, research institution, university, or industry association  
- [ ] Engaged in technical textiles manufacturing, research, innovation, or skill development  
- [ ] Project aligns with national technical textiles priorities (R&D, market development, export promotion, infrastructure, etc.)  
- [ ] Willing to comply with GFR, CVC guidelines, and submission of utilization certificates  
- [ ] Project falls within mission period (2021–2026, co-terminus with 15th Finance Commission cycle)  
- [ ] Applicant has or can prepare required documents (project proposal, incorporation certificate, PAN/GST, audited financials, etc.)  
- [ ] Funding request corresponds to eligible components (up to 50% for R&D, 25–50% for market development, 100% for certain infrastructure)  
- [ ] No duplicate funding for same activity from other government schemes (implied by compliance requirements)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your work in [mention specific area: e.g., R&D, export promotion, or infrastructure] could align well with the National Technical Textiles Mission. NTTM offers grants-in-aid and subsidies of up to 50% of project cost for R&D and similar support for market development—let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Technical textiles innovators often struggle with high R&D costs, limited access to testing facilities, and challenges in scaling market access or export promotion—especially when competing with established global players. Many promising projects stall due to lack of early-stage funding or infrastructure support.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Technical Textiles Mission (NTTM), implemented by the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, provides financial support through grants-in-aid, subsidies, and equity participation. Support includes up to 50% of project cost for R&D, 25–50% for market development, and 100% government funding for eligible infrastructure components such as common facility centers. Applications are accepted year-round via the portal: https://technicaltext.in.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in technical textiles manufacturing, R&D, or skill development activities in India
