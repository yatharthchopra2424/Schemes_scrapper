### National Technical Textiles Mission (NTTM)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Textiles, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://technicaltext.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (applications accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds and approval cycles)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Indian companies, startups, MSMEs, research institutions, universities, industry associations
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Technical textiles manufacturing, research, innovation, or skill development activities
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Specific eligibility varies by component (R&D, market development, export promotion, etc.) and is defined under each sub-mission; projects must align with national technical textiles priorities; beneficiaries must comply with GFR and CVC guidelines

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to 50% of project cost for R&D, 25-50% for market development, and 100% government funding for infrastructure in certain cases; total fund size of ₹1,480 Cr
- Disbursement mode: Funds disbursed through implementing agencies after project approval and milestone-based verification
- Duration of benefit: Milestone-based installments (duration tied to project timeline)

#### Documents Required
1. Project proposal document
2. Certificate of Incorporation/Registration
3. PAN and GST details of the applicant
4. Audited financial statements of last 3 years
5. Technical details and innovation aspect of the project
6. Land/building documents (if infrastructure component)
7. Partnership/MoU documents (if collaboration involved)
8. Undertaking and declaration forms

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the relevant component of NTTM (R&D, market development, export promotion, etc.)
2. Prepare project proposal as per guidelines
3. Submit application through the designated nodal agency or online portal
4. Application undergoes technical and financial appraisal
5. Approved projects are sanctioned and funds released in installments
6. Progress monitored through periodic reports and site visits
7. Final submission and utilization certificate required for closure
