## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Unit is engaged in textile activities: spinning, weaving, processing, garmenting, technical textiles, or jute  
- [ ] Unit is registered under the Companies Act, 1956/2013, or any other relevant statute  
- [ ] Unit has a valid Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum (UAM) or Udyam Registration  
- [ ] Project involves technology upgradation through indigenous, state-of-the-art, and energy-efficient machinery  
- [ ] Machinery is from the approved list under ATUFS (to be verified with PMA/lending institution)  
- [ ] Unit has not availed benefits under any other similar scheme for the same machinery  
- [ ] For garmenting and technical textiles segments: subsidy rate is 10%; for all other segments: 15%  
- [ ] Maximum subsidy claim does not exceed INR 30 crores per unit  
- [ ] Unit has a valid PAN and GST registration  
- [ ] Unit has a bank account for subsidy disbursement through lending institution  

> **Sources**: Eligibility criteria from Scheme Key Facts; Application Portal: https://tufs.nic.in

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a **detailed project report is required** as per scheme guidelines.  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
- Executive summary  
- Details of existing unit and proposed technology upgradation  
- List of approved machinery (with specifications, cost, and supplier)  
- Technical description of machinery (indigenous, state-of-the-art, energy-efficient features)  
- Project implementation timeline  
- Cost breakdown and means of finance (loan vs. equity)  
- Expected benefits: productivity gain, energy savings, quality improvement, employment impact  
- Compliance statement: machinery from ATUFS approved list, no double benefit claim  
- Undertaking on indigenous and energy-efficient nature of machinery  

> **Note**: Confirm exact DPR format with the Project Management Agency (PMA) or lending institution, as requirements may vary.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Start with lending institution**: The loan application and project proposal must first be submitted to a bank or financial institution for appraisal and sanction.  
2. **Use sanctioned loan details**: Only after loan sanction, submit the sanctioned loan amount and documents to the PMA for registration via https://tufs.nic.in.  
3. **Ensure machinery eligibility**: Verify that all machinery is from the approved list under ATUFS before proceeding.  
4. **Accurate project cost**: Match the project cost in the DPR, loan sanction letter, and machinery quotations exactly.  
5. **Correct beneficiary details**: Enter the unit’s legal name, Udyam number, PAN, and GSTIN consistently across all fields.  
6. **Segment-specific subsidy**: Clearly indicate if the unit falls under garmenting/technical textiles (10% subsidy) or other segments (15%).  
7. **Undertaking compliance**: duly sign and upload the undertaking on indigenous and energy-efficient machinery.  
8. **Portal readiness**: Keep scanned documents ready (PDF, <5 MB) for upload during PMA registration step.  

> **Portal Note**: The application portal https://tufs.nic.in is used for PMA registration and subsidy claim tracking, not for initial loan application.

> **Note**: Subsidy is released in installments after verification by PMA; ensure timely submission of installation and commissioning certificates and proof of payment for each tranche.
