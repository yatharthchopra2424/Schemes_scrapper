## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the substantial financial benefit delivered — up to ₹30 crores in subsidy per unit under ATUFS.
2. **Risk-Sharing Model**: Success fees are contingent on verified subsidy disbursement, reducing client risk and aligning consultant incentives with successful outcomes.
3. **Transparency**: Clear communication of retainer (for project preparation, documentation, and liaison) and success fee (only upon subsidy credit to beneficiary account via lending institution).
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: ATUFS involves a moderate risk profile due to its multi-stage approval process (lending institution sanction → PMA registration → installation verification → subsidy release). Processing time typically spans 6–12 months from loan sanction to subsidy disbursement, based on evidence of verification timelines and installment-based release. This necessitates a 3-month lag in revenue recognition for success fees in projections.
