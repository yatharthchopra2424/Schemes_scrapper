## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping textile units access government support for technology upgradation. The Amended Technology Upgradation Fund Scheme (ATUFS) from the Ministry of Textiles offers a credit-linked subsidy of up to INR 30 crores for eligible machinery investments. I’d like to understand your current operations and expansion plans to see if this could be a fit for your unit.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of textile activities is your unit engaged in (spinning, weaving, processing, garmenting, technical textiles, or jute)?
- When was your unit incorporated or registered under the Companies Act or Udyam Registration?
- Are you planning to invest in new machinery for technology upgradation, and if so, what is the approximate capital investment amount?
- Have you identified machinery from the approved list under ATUFS for your upgradation project?
- Do you have a lending institution (bank or financial institution) in place or identified for financing this machinery investment?
- Is your unit registered with Udyam Registration or holding a valid Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum (UAM)?
- Have you previously availed benefits under any other similar technology upgradation scheme for the same machinery?
- What are your primary goals for this technology upgradation (e.g., improving operational efficiency, reducing energy consumption, enhancing product quality)?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Textile units face high capital costs when upgrading to modern, energy-efficient machinery, which limits their ability to improve productivity, quality, and global competitiveness. Many units delay or scale back upgradation plans due to financial constraints, despite the long-term benefits of reduced energy consumption and enhanced operational efficiency.

**Scheme intro:**
The Amended Technology Upgradation Fund Scheme (ATUFS), administered by the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, provides a credit-linked subsidy where the government reimburses 15% (10% for garmenting and technical textiles) of the qualifying capital investment in approved machinery, up to a maximum of INR 30 crores per unit. The subsidy is released through lending institutions after verification of installation and commissioning by the Project Management Agency (PMA). Applications are accepted on a rolling basis throughout the year, subject to fund availability. More details are available at the official portal: https://tufs.nic.in

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your unit is engaged in [spinning/weaving/processing/garmenting/technical textiles/jute] — an eligible activity under ATUFS
- You have valid Udyam Registration or Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum (UAM), meeting the registration requirement
- Your planned machinery investment involves indigenous, state-of-the-art, and energy-efficient technology from the approved ATUFS list
- You have not previously claimed benefits under any similar scheme for this machinery, avoiding duplication of benefits
