### Amended Technology Upgradation Fund Scheme (ATUFS)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Textiles, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://tufs.nic.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Existing and new textile units
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Textiles (spinning, weaving, processing, garmenting, technical textiles, jute)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be registered under the Companies Act, 1956/2013, or any other relevant statute; must have a valid Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum (UAM) or Udyam Registration; project must involve technology upgradation through indigenous, state-of-the-art, and energy-efficient machinery as per the approved list under ATUFS

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Credit-linked subsidy at 15% (10% for garmenting and technical textiles) of qualifying capital investment in approved machinery, with a maximum subsidy of INR 30 crores per unit
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement (via lending institution after verification)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (released in installments based on verification)

#### Documents Required
1. Project report detailing the technology upgradation
2. Quotations and invoices of approved machinery
3. Loan sanction letter from the lending institution
4. Certificate of Incorporation / Udyam Registration
5. PAN and GST registration of the unit
6. Bank account details
7. Undertaking regarding indigenous and energy-efficient nature of machinery
8. Installation and commissioning certificate of machinery
9. Proof of payment to machinery suppliers

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify eligible machinery from the approved list under ATUFS
2. Submit a project proposal to a lending institution (bank or financial institution) along with required documents
3. The lending institution appraises the project and sanctions the loan
4. Submit the sanctioned loan details and documents to the Project Management Agency (PMA) for registration
5. After installation and commissioning of machinery, submit verification documents to the PMA
6. PMA verifies the claim and recommends subsidy release
7. The subsidy is released by the Ministry of Textiles to the lending institution, which credits the beneficiary’s account
