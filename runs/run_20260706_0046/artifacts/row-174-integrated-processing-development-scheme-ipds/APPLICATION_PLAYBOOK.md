## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Business is registered as a proprietorship, partnership, LLP, private limited company, or public limited company under Indian law  
- [ ] Entity operates in the textile sector (including spinning, weaving, processing, garmenting, made-ups, technical textiles, or related activities)  
- [ ] Unit is engaged in textile processing activities such as dyeing, printing, bleaching, finishing, or effluent treatment  
- [ ] Business has been in operation for at least 3 years as of the date of application  
- [ ] Annual turnover does not exceed ₹250 crore (as per audited financial statements of the preceding financial year)  
- [ ] Unit has obtained necessary statutory clearances (including pollution control board consent for effluent discharge, if applicable)  
- [ ] Unit is not defunct or under liquidation  
- [ ] Unit has not availed similar central government subsidy for the same processing machinery/equipment under any other scheme in the last 5 years  
- [ ] Unit possesses valid Udyam Registration (MSME) or is eligible for registration under the MSME Act, 2006  
- [ ] Unit has a valid GSTIN and PAN  
- [ ] Unit maintains a separate bank account for the business  

> **Sources**: Scheme eligibility inferred from Integrated Processing Development Scheme (IPDS) guidelines under Ministry of Textiles; standard criteria for textile processing subsidies including operational history, turnover cap, sector specificity, and environmental compliance. Portal: [https://textilescommittee.nic.in](https://textilescommittee.nic.in) (primary source for scheme details and application portal).

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Partnership Deed / Proprietorship Declaration  
- PAN Card of the business entity  
- GST Registration Certificate  
- Udyam Registration Certificate (MSME)  
- Audited Financial Statements (Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss Account) for the last 3 financial years  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the business for the last 3 financial years  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months) of the business current account  
- Cancelled cheque or bank passbook copy showing account details  
- Electricity Bill / Property Tax Receipt / Rent Agreement (as proof of business address)  
- Authorized Signatory List with specimen signatures and board resolution (if applicable)  
- Photograph of the unit’s premises (exterior and interior)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining the proposed processing machinery/equipment, technical specifications, cost estimates, and expected output  
- Quotations from at least two shortlisted suppliers for the proposed machinery/equipment (with validity period)  
- Consent to Establish / Operate from State Pollution Control Board (SPCB) or Pollution Control Committee (PCC) for effluent treatment and emissions  
- Layout plan of the processing unit showing existing and proposed machinery installation  
- Certificate of Chartered Accountant certifying the project cost and means of finance  
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the local municipal authority or concerned civic body (if required for construction/installation)  
- Undertaking on non-availing of similar central subsidy for the same purpose under any other scheme in the last 5 years  
- Affidavit confirming the authenticity of documents submitted and correctness of information provided  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (minimum 300 DPI)  
- File names must follow the format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ClientName_Date.pdf` (e.g., `IPDS_UdyamCertificate_ABC_Textiles_20240515.pdf`)  
- Scanned copies must be certified true copies by a Gazetted Officer or Notary Public (where applicable)  
- Documents in regional languages must be accompanied by a certified Hindi or English translation  
- Financial documents (ITR, audited statements) must be clearly labeled with assessment year and PAN  
- Bank statements must show the business name and account number clearly  
- Pollution control certificates must be valid and not expired at the time of submission  
- DPR and quotations must be dated within the last 6 months  
- Total upload size per document must not exceed 10 MB; total application package should ideally be under 50 MB

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required** for the Integrated Processing Development Scheme (IPDS).  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
1. Executive Summary  
2. Promoter(s) Profile and Experience in Textile Processing  
3. Existing Unit Profile (if applicable): Current machinery, production capacity, product mix  
4. Objectives of the Proposed Project  
5. Detailed Description of Proposed Machinery/Equipment:  
   - Technical specifications (capacity, power consumption, effluent generation, etc.)  
   - Make, model, and supplier details  
   - Energy efficiency and eco-friendly features (if any)  
6. Process Flow Diagram (before and after implementation)  
7. Layout Plan of the Unit showing installation area  
8. Project Implementation Schedule (timeline with milestones)  
9. Cost Breakdown:  
   - Machinery and equipment cost  
   - Installation and civil works  
   - Preliminary and preoperative expenses  
   - Contingencies  
   - Total project cost  
10. Means of Finance:  
    - Promoter’s equity  
    - Term loan (if any)  
    - Expected subsidy under IPDS  
    - Other sources  
11. Financial Viability:  
    - Projected income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow for 5 years  
    - Key ratios (IRR, NPV, DSCR, payback period)  
12. Environmental and Safety Compliance:  
    - Effluent treatment plan  
    - Emission control measures  
    - Worker safety provisions  
13. Employment Generation (direct and indirect)  
14. Annexures:  
    - Supplier quotations  
    - Pollution control consent copies  
    - CA certificate  
    - Land/building ownership documents  

> **Note**: If the nodal officer specifies a different format, adjust accordingly. Always verify DPR requirements with the Office of the Textile Commissioner during pre-submission consultation.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal at [https://textilescommittee.nic.in](https://textilescommittee.nic.in) and navigate to the IPDS section under “Schemes” or “Textile Commissioner’s Office.”  
2. Use only the latest version of the application form downloaded directly from the portal; do not use outdated or third-party forms.  
3. Fill all fields in **Block Letters** using **black ink** (if physical) or ensure digital entries are accurate and complete (if online).  
4. Enter the **Udyam Registration Number** and **GSTIN** exactly as they appear on the certificates; any mismatch will lead to rejection.  
5. In the “Project Cost” section, break down costs as per the DPR and ensure the total matches the CA-certified figure.  
6. Clearly specify the **type and quantity of processing machinery** being proposed (e.g., “2 nos. of jet dyeing machines, 150 kg capacity each”).  
7. Attach all documents in the prescribed order and format as listed in the Document Collection stage; label each attachment clearly.  
8. Sign the declaration section digitally or physically; if physical, ensure the signature matches the authorized signatory list and is dated.  
9. Before final submission, preview the entire form to confirm no fields are left blank or marked “NA” without justification.  
10. Save a draft periodically to avoid data loss due to session timeout.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Log in to the portal [https://textilescommittee.nic.in](https://textilescommittee.nic.in) using the credentials created during registration  
- Navigate to “Apply for IPDS” and upload the completed application form and all supporting documents  
- After upload, click “Final Submit” – do not proceed to payment unless explicitly prompted (IPDS is a subsidy scheme, typically no fee)  
- Upon successful submission, a system-generated **Application Reference Number (ARN)** will appear – **screenshot this immediately**  
- Save the screenshot in the case folder with the filename: `IPDS_SubmissionConfirmation_CaseID_Date.png`  
- Note down the ARN and expected timeline for processing (typically 60–90 days)  
- Set a follow-up reminder for **Day 25** to check status via portal or contact nodal office  
- Store all submission records (form, docs, ARN) in both digital and physical case files

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify the official approval letter from the Office of the Textile Commissioner  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned subsidy amount matches the approved project cost and eligibility limits (typically up to 50% of project cost, capped at ₹5 crore for general category, higher for special category states/SC/ST/women entrepreneurs)  
- [ ] Sign and submit the acceptance copy of the approval letter within the stipulated time  
- [ ] Ensure the subsidy amount is disbursed to the designated bank account as per the scheme’s installment schedule (if applicable)  
- [ ] Begin procurement and installation of machinery only after approval and as per the implementation timeline in the DPR  
- [ ] Maintain all vouchers, invoices, and payment records related to the subsidized machinery  
- [ ] Prepare and submit the Utilization Certificate (UC) along with audited expenditure statement within the time frame specified in the approval letter  
- [ ] Submit proof of installation and commissioning (including photographs, trial run reports, and supplier’s completion certificate)  
- [ ] Coordinate with the client to ensure successful invoice generation and payment collection for the success fee as per the engagement agreement  
- [ ] Archive all approval documents, UC, and completion reports in the client’s permanent file for audit and future reference
