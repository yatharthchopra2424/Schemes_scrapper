## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-aligned pricing**: Fees are structured as a percentage of the financial benefit secured, ensuring consultant incentives align with client outcomes.
2. **Risk-adjusted retainers**: Retainer amounts reflect the effort required to compile technical documentation and navigate multi-agency approvals under IPDS.
3. **Transparency**: All fee structures are disclosed upfront, with retainers covering initial assessment and application preparation, and success fees payable only upon fund disbursement.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: IPDS involves moderate risk due to its technical appraisal process and dependency on state-level implementing agencies; however, historical data shows consistent approvals for eligible textile units with complete documentation, supporting a mid-range success fee structure.
