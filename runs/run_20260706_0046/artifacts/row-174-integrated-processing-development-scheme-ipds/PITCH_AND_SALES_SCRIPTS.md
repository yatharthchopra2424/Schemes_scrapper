## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
We specialise in helping textile and garment manufacturers access government support for modernising their processing units. The Integrated Processing Development Scheme (IPDS) is designed specifically for businesses like yours that are looking to upgrade dyeing, printing, or finishing infrastructure to improve quality, reduce pollution, and stay competitive.

### Discovery Questions
- When was your textile processing unit incorporated or commenced commercial operations?
- Are you currently engaged in textile processing activities such as dyeing, printing, bleaching, or finishing?
- What is the current status of your factory’s effluent treatment and water recycling systems?
- Are you planning to invest in new or upgraded processing machinery in the next 12 months?
- What is your annual turnover from textile processing operations?
- Do you have clear title or lease rights to the premises where the processing unit is located?
- Are you aware of any existing pollution control violations or show-cause notices from state PCB?
- Have you previously applied for any central or state government subsidy for textile upgradation?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Unit is engaged in textile processing (dyeing, printing, bleaching, finishing, etc.)
- [ ] Unit has been in commercial operation for at least 3 years
- [ ] Unit is located in India and has valid factory licence
- [ ] Unit has clear ownership or leasehold rights to the premises
- [ ] Unit is not under liquidation or bankruptcy proceedings
- [ ] Unit has no unresolved pollution control violations from State PCB
- [ ] Unit proposes to install new or upgrade existing processing machinery with effluent treatment
- [ ] Unit’s project cost does not exceed ₹50 crores (as per scheme caps)

### Transition to Pitch

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many textile processing units struggle with outdated machinery that leads to high water and chemical consumption, poor dyeing consistency, and difficulty meeting state pollution control norms. Upgrading requires significant capital, which small and mid-sized units often cannot access without external support.

**Scheme intro:**
The Integrated Processing Development Scheme (IPDS), administered by the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, provides financial assistance in the form of a capital subsidy for the modernisation of textile processing units. The scheme supports the installation of new or upgraded machinery, including effluent treatment plants, to improve environmental compliance and product quality. Eligible units can receive a subsidy of up to 50% of the project cost, capped at ₹25 crores per unit.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your unit is engaged in core textile processing activities (dyeing/printing/finishing), which is a mandatory eligibility criterion
- You have been in commercial operation for over 3 years, meeting the minimum operational track record
- You possess clear ownership or leasehold rights to your factory premises, as required for subsidy disbursement
- You are planning to invest in new processing machinery with effluent treatment, aligning with the scheme’s focus on pollution control and quality upgradation
