- DPR must include layout plan, phasing, financials, and implementation schedule

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Required**: Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is mandatory for SITP.  
**Standard Sections**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Overview and Objectives  
- Land Details (location, area, title, land use)  
- Layout Plan and Phasing of Development  
- Infrastructure Facilities (power, water, effluent treatment, roads, security)  
- Textile Unit Allocation Plan (minimum 50% area to textile units, no unit >25%)  
- Project Cost Breakdown (equity, loans, grant sought)  
- Financing Plan and Loan Agreements  
- Implementation Schedule (Gantt chart)  
- Financial Projections (IRR, NPV, payback period)  
- Employment Generation Estimates  
- Environmental and Safety Compliance  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
- Annexures: Land documents, NOCs, clearances, undertakings, SPV documents  

> **Note**: DPR must be prepared by a qualified consultant and appraised by the Project Appraisal Committee (PAC).  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use only the official application format prescribed by the Office of the Textile Commissioner.  
2. Fill all fields in block letters; avoid overwriting or correction fluid.  
3. Ensure SPV name, PAN, and registration number match exactly across all documents.  
4. Declare equity contribution of SPV (minimum 25% of project cost) in the financing section.  
5. Specify land area (minimum 10 acres) and confirm clear title in possession of SPV.  
6. Clearly state the percentage of area allotted to textile units (must be ≥50%).  
7. Declare that no individual textile unit will occupy more than 25% of developed area.  
8. Attach all scheme-specific documents as per checklist; label annexures clearly.  
9. Sign the declaration page with authorized signatory; affix SPV seal if applicable.  
10. Verify that the application is submitted to the **Office of the Textile Commissioner** via the designated channel (physical or online as notified).  

> **Portal Note**: Applications are submitted to the Office of the Textile Commissioner. Confirm current submission mode (online/physical) at [https://texcom.nic.in](https://texcom.nic.in).

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed application along with all annexures to the Office of the Textile Commissioner.  
- Obtain a receipt or acknowledgment (physical or digital) upon submission.  
- Take a screenshot/scan of the acknowledgment showing:  
  - Date of submission  
  - Application reference number (if generated)  
  - Name of receiving officer/department  
- Store the acknowledgment and reference number in the case file.  
- Set calendar reminders for follow-up at 15, 30, and 45 days post-submission.  
- Monitor the scheme portal [https://texcom.nic.in](https://texcom.nic.in) for updates or requests for additional information.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from the Inter-Ministerial Steering Committee  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned grant amount (up to 40% of project cost, max INR 40 crore; or up to 90% for hilly/tribal/difficult areas, max INR 60 crore)  
- [ ] Sign the Memorandum of Agreement (MoA) between SPV and Government of India  
- [ ] Receive first installment of grant-in-aid upon verification of pre-defined milestones  
-estones  
- [ ] Submit utilization certificates and progress reports as per MoA schedule  
- [ ] Ensure implementation of park phases as per approved DPR  
- [ ] Monitor that ≥50% of developed area is allotted to textile units and no unit exceeds 25%  
- [ ] Verify continued SPV equity commitment (≥25% of project cost)  
- [ ] Prepare and submit final utilization certificate upon project completion  
- [ ] Coordinate with financial institutions for loan disbursement as per financing plan  
- [ ] Notify constituent textile units of plug-and-play facility availability  
- [ ] Track employment generation and export competitiveness outcomes for reporting  
- [ ] Invoice success fee as per consultancy agreement  

> **Note**: Grant is released in installments based on milestone verification. Adherence to timelines in approved DPR is mandatory.
