## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees aligned with the substantial financial benefit (up to ₹60 Cr grant) and long-term infrastructure value created.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Returns**: Success fees reflect moderate risk due to lengthy approval process (appraisal by PAC, Inter-Ministerial Steering Committee) and milestone-based disbursement.
3. **Transparency**: Clear retainer for advisory services (SPV formation, DPR preparation, application submission) and success fee only upon grant approval.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: This scheme involves a complex, multi-stage approval process with significant documentation and inter-agency coordination. Processing time typically spans 6–12 months from application to approval, based on evidence of PAC and Steering Committee reviews. Success is contingent on meeting equity (min 25%), land (10+ acres), and textile unit allotment (min 50%) criteria, which increases execution risk but is mitigated by strong government commitment and rolling application window.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Advisory services for Scheme for Integrated Textile Parks (SITP) – including SPV structuring, land acquisition support, Detailed Project Report (DPR) preparation, application submission to Office of the Textile Commissioner, and liaison with Project Appraisal Committee (PAC).

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for securing grant-in-aid under the Scheme for Integrated Textile Parks (SITP) administered by the Office of the Textile Commissioner, Ministry of Textiles, Government of India. Fee payable upon signing of Memorandum of Agreement (MoA) between SPV and Government of India.
