## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping textile entrepreneurs and industry associations access government infrastructure support like the Scheme for Integrated Textile Parks (SITP), which provides up to INR 40 crore in grant-in-aid for developing world-class textile parks with plug-and-play facilities. I’d like to understand your current expansion or cluster development plans to see if this could be a strategic fit — no obligation, just a diagnostic conversation.

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you part of an industry association, private entrepreneur group, or working with a state agency to develop a textile park or industrial cluster?
2. What is the current status of land acquisition for your proposed textile park — do you have at least 10 acres in possession with clear title?
3. Have you formed a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) for this project, or are you planning to?
4. What percentage of the developed area do you plan to allot to textile units, and are you ensuring no single unit exceeds 25% of the total area?
5. Are you prepared to contribute at least 25% of the project cost as equity from the SPV?
6. Does your project include common infrastructure like power, water, effluent treatment, roads, and security?
7. Is your textile park located in a hilly, tribal, or difficult area, which may qualify for higher grant support (up to INR 60 crore)?
8. What is your expected timeline for project completion, and have you prepared a Detailed Project Report (DPR) with layout, phasing, and financials?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) formed by industry associations, private entrepreneurs, or state government agencies
- [ ] Minimum 10 acres of land in possession with clear title
- [ ] Commitment to allot at least 50% of developed area to textile units
- [ ] No individual textile unit to occupy more than 25% of developed area
- [ ] SPV to contribute at least 25% of project cost as equity
- [ ] Project includes infrastructure: power, water, effluent treatment, roads, security
- [ ] Detailed Project Report (DPR) prepared with layout, phasing, and financials
- [ ] Required NOCs and clearances (e.g., pollution control, railways, highways) obtainable
- [ ] Undertakings regarding area allotment and unit size limits can be provided
- [ ] Project located in India (Pan-India eligibility)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like you're actively developing a textile park with the scale and structure that aligns well with the Scheme for Integrated Textile Parks (SITP). This scheme offers up to INR 40 crore in grant-in-aid (or INR 60 crore in difficult areas) for exactly the kind of infrastructure you're planning — let me walk you through how it works and whether you qualify.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Textile entrepreneurs and associations face high upfront costs in developing industrial infrastructure — power, water, effluent treatment, roads, and security — which delays park development, increases unit-level costs, and limits competitiveness, especially for MSMEs seeking plug-and-play facilities.

**Scheme intro:**
The Scheme for Integrated Textile Parks (SITP), administered by the Office of the Textile Commissioner, Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, provides grant-in-aid of up to 40% of project cost, with a ceiling of INR 40 crore per textile park) for infrastructure development. For projects in hilly, tribal, or difficult areas, support increases to up to 90% of project cost with a ceiling of INR 60 crore. The scheme supports land development, common facilities, and plug-and-play infrastructure to promote cluster-based textile manufacturing.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are forming or have formed an SPV eligible under the scheme (industry association, private entrepreneurs, or state agency)
- You have or are acquiring at least 10 acres of land with clear title — a mandatory requirement
- You plan to allot at least 50% of the developed area to textile units, with no single unit exceeding 25%, meeting core allocation rules
- You are prepared to contribute minimum 25% equity from the SPV, satisfying the promoter’s stake condition
