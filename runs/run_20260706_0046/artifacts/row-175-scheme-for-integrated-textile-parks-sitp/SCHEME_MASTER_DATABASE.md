### Scheme for Integrated Textile Parks (SITP)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Office of the Textile Commissioner, Ministry of Textiles, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** [https://texcom.nic.in](https://texcom.nic.in)
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (subject to availability of funds)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) formed by industry associations, private entrepreneurs, or state government agencies
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Textiles
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: 
  - Minimum 10 acres of land with clear title
  - Commitment to develop the park with at least 50% area allotted to textile units
  - SPV must contribute at least 25% of the project cost as equity
  - No individual unit occupies more than 25% of the developed area

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - Up to INR 40 crore per textile park (40% of project cost)
  of project cost) for normal areas
  - Up to INR 60 crore per textile park (90% of project cost) for hilly, tribal, or difficult areas
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (grant-in-aid released in installments based on milestone verification)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (released in installments upon milestone achievement)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation/Registration of SPV
2. Memorandum and Articles of Association
3. Board Resolution authorizing the application
4. Land documents (sale/lease deed, title report, encumbrance certificate)
5. Land use conversion certificate (if applicable)
6. Detailed Project Report (DPR) with layout plan, phasing, and financials
7. Project financing details including equity contribution and loan arrangements
8. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from State Pollution Control Board
9. Clearance from relevant authorities (e.g., airport, railways, highways if applicable)
10. Undertaking regarding minimum 50% area allotment to textile units
11. Undertaking that no single unit will occupy more than 25% of developed area

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Formation of Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) by eligible entities
2. Identification and acquisition of land (minimum 10 acres) with clear title
3. Preparation of Detailed Project Report (DPR) including layout plan, financials, and implementation schedule
4. Submission of application along with DPR to the Office of the Textile Commissioner
5. Appraisal by the Project Appraisal Committee (PAC)
6. Approval by the Inter-Ministerial Steering Committee
7. Signing of Memorandum of Agreement (MoA) between SPV and Government of India
8. Implementation of the park in phases as per approved DPR
9. Submission of utilization certificates and progress reports
10. Release of grant-in-aid in installments based on verification of milestones
