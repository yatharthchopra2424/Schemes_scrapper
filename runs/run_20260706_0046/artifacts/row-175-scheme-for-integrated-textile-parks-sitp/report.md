## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Scheme for Integrated Textile Parks (SITP)** is a centrally sponsored subsidy scheme implemented by the **Office of the Textile Commissioner, Ministry of Textiles, Government of India**. It aims to develop world-class infrastructure for textile units through the creation of integrated textile parks on a pan-India basis. Applications are accepted on a **rolling basis throughout the year**, subject to fund availability.

### Objectives
- Provide world-class infrastructure facilities for textile units  
- Enhance productivity and global competitiveness of the Indian textiles industry  
- Attract investment in the textiles sector through plug-and-play facilities  
- Promote backward and forward linkages by integrating various stages of textile production  
- Generate employment opportunities in the textiles sector  
- Encourage adoption of modern technology and sustainable practices in textile manufacturing

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Aspect | Details | Source |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **Grant-in-Aid (General Areas)** | Up to 40% of project cost, ceiling of **INR 40 crore** per textile park | Key Facts, Financial Support |
| **Grant-in-Aid (Hilly/Tribal/Difficult Areas)** | Up to 90% of project cost, ceiling of **INR 60 crore** per textile park | Key Facts, Financial Support |
| **Remaining Cost** | To be borne by SPV via equity (min 25%) and term loans from financial institutions | Financial Support |
| **Covered Infrastructure** | Power, water, effluent treatment plants, roads, security, and other common facilities | Benefits |

> **> Warning**: Grant-in-aid is released in installments based on verification of pre-defined milestones. No funds are disbursed upfront. The SPV must bear initial costs and seek reimbursement upon milestone completion.

### Key Caveats
- Grant is released in installments based on verification of pre-defined milestones  
- Land must be in possession of SPV with clear title before application  
- At least 50% of the developed area must be allotted to textile units  
- No individual textile unit can occupy more than 25% of the total developed area  
- SPV must contribute minimum 25% of project cost as equity  
- Projects must be completed within the stipulated timeline as per approved DPR  
- Grant-in-aid is subject to availability of funds under the scheme  

### Required Documents Checklist
1. Certificate of Incorporation/Registration of SPV  
2. Memorandum and Articles of Association  
3. Board Resolution authorizing the application  
4. Land documents (sale/lease deed, title report, encumbrance certificate)  
5. Land use conversion certificate (if applicable)  
6. Detailed Project Report (DPR) with layout plan, phasing, and financials  
7. Project financing details including equity contribution and loan arrangements  
8. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from State Pollution Control Board  
9. Clearance from relevant authorities (e.g., airport, railways, highways if applicable)  
10. Undertaking regarding minimum 50% area allotment to textile units  
11. Undertaking that no single unit will occupy more than 25% of developed area
