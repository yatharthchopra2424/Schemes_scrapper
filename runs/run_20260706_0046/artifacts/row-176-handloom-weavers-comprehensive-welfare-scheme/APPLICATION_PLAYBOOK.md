# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Implementing Agency (IA) is a handloom weaver or allied worker engaged in pre-loom (winding, warping, dyeing etc.) and post-loom activities (finishing, calendaring etc.)
- [ ] IA is a Self Help Group, producer company, or other handloom agency
- [ ] IA belongs to disadvantaged sections of society such as SC, ST, women, transgender, or differently-abled individuals
- [ ] IA is recommended by the State Government and SLPC)
- [ ] IA has submitted Minutes of SLPC duly signed by the Chairman and Members
- [ ] IA has completed Baseline Survey in prescribed proforma (Annexure A1)
- [ ] IA has prepared Diagnostic study and Intervention-wise Action Plan (Annexure A2)
- [ ] IA has submitted Profile of IA indicating name, address, contact, PAN/TAN, Bank account details (Annexure A3)
- [ ] IA has submitted Profit & Loss Account and Balance Sheet for last two years (if applicable; not required if IA is Central/State Govt. office like WSC/IIHT or State Directorate)
- [ ] If IA is an NGO, it has submitted Score pattern for grading and certificate of registration on NITI Aayog Darpan Portal
- [ ] IA has provided List of weavers for availing benefit of individual interventions (Annexure A7(I))
- [ ] IA has submitted Details of land for Common workshed with area, location, and title documents
- [ ] IA has submitted Cost break-up, lighting capacity, warranty, and installation details for Solar Lighting System
- [ ] At least 50% of weavers in the cluster are non-members of the Implementing Agency
- [ ] NGO IAs are registered on NITI Aayog Darpan Portal and have submitted PAN and Aadhar of trustees

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation certificate
- PAN card
- ITR (last 2 years)
- GST registration (if applicable)
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam registration
- Aadhar card of authorized signatory

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Minutes of SLPC duly signed by the Chairman and Members
- Baseline Survey in prescribed proforma (Annexure A1)
- Diagnostic study and Intervention-wise Action Plan (Annexure A2)
- Profile of IA indicating name, address, contact, PAN/TAN, Bank account details (Annexure A3)
- Profit & Loss Account and Balance Sheet of IA for last two years (if applicable)
- Score pattern for grading and certificate of registration on NITI Aayog Darpan Portal (if IA is NGO)
- List of weavers for availing benefit of individual interventions (Annexure A7(I))
- Details of land for Common workshed with area, location, and title documents
- Cost break-up, lighting capacity, warranty, and installation details for Solar Lighting System

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible scans or clear photographs
- File names must match the document type (e.g., "SLPC_Minutes.pdf", "Baseline_Survey_AnnexureA1.pdf")
- PDF file size should not exceed 10 MB per document
- All government forms must be duly signed and stamped where required
- Documents in regional languages must be accompanied by a certified Hindi/English translation
- Bank statements must show the IA's name and account number clearly
- Utilization Certificates (if applicable) must be in GFR-12-A format

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required for this scheme. It must include the following standard sections:
- Executive Summary
- Project Background and Objectives
- Baseline Survey Findings (Annexure A1)
- Diagnostic Study and Intervention-wise Action Plan (Annexure A2)
- Implementing Agency Profile (Annexure A3)
- Financials (Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet for last 2 years)
- Intervention Details (HSS, lighting units, worksheds, solar lighting)
- Beneficiary List (Annexure A7(I))
- Land details for Common workshed
- Solar Lighting System technical and financial specifications
- Project Management Plan
- Monitoring and Evaluation Framework
- Utilization Certificate plan
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use the official proposal format as outlined in Annexures A1–A4 and A7(I) of the NHDP guidelines.
2. Ensure all annexures are duly signed by the IA and countersigned by the Director of Handlooms.
3. For NGO IAs, attach NITI Aayog Darpan registration certificate and PAN/Aadhar of trustees.
4. In Annexure A1, confirm that at least 50% of weavers listed are non-members of the IA.
5. Provide clear land title documents for Common workshed in the IA’s name.
6. For Solar Lighting System, include cost break-up, lumen output, warranty period, and installation location.
7. Attach audited Profit & Loss Account and Balance Sheet for the last two years (exempt if IA is WSC/IIHT/State Directorate).
8. All financial figures must be in Indian Rupees (₹) and match across documents.
9. Submit the complete proposal to the State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles for SLPC recommendation.
10. Keep a copy of all submitted documents and obtain an acknowledgment receipt.
11. Do not leave any field in the annexures blank; write “Not Applicable” if relevant.
12. Verify that the IA’s bank account details are correct and active for fund transfer.
13. Label all annexures clearly (e.g., “Annexure A1 – Baseline Survey”) and paginate the entire proposal.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All annexures (A1, A2, A3, A4 if NGO, A7(I)) are complete and signed
- [ ] Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet attached (if applicable)
- [ ] NGO Darpan certificate and trustee PAN/Aadhar attached (if NGO)
- [ ] Land documents for Common workshed submitted
- [ ] Solar Lighting System specifications included
- [ ] At least 50% of weavers in Annexure A7(I) are non-IA members
- [ ] All documents are legible, properly named, and within file size limits
- [ ] Declarations and undertakings are signed where required
- [ ] Bank account details match across documents
- [ ] Proposal has been countersigned by the State Directorate of Handlooms (if submitted through them)

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the complete proposal to the State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles for SLPC scrutiny
- Obtain a signed acknowledgment receipt with date and inward number
- If the IA is WSC/IIHT/National Level Organization, submit directly to O/o DC (HL) under intimation to the State Directorate
- Store screenshots of the submission portal/email and save the reference number
- Set calendar reminders for follow-up at 15 days and 30 days post-submission
- Track submission in the case management system with Case ID and scheme name

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from O/o DC (HL) or State Directorate
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount matches the approved GoI share (typically 50% advance of total GoI commitment)
- [ ] Utilization Certificate (UC) preparation: ensure at least 70% utilization of first installment before claiming second installment
- [ ] Maintain records of all expenditure with bills and vouchers
- [ ] Submit Intervention-wise physical & financial progress report signed by IA and countersigned by State Director of Handlooms
- [ ] Submit list of weavers who availed benefit from first installment (Annexure A7(I))
- [ ] Ensure all assets (looms, worksheds, lighting systems) are registered in the beneficiary’s name
- [ ] Track project milestones against the 3-year implementation timeline
- [ ] Prepare for second installment claim upon 70% UC submission
- [ ] Coordinate with State Directorate for monitoring by SIMRC/NIMRC
- [ ] Submit final Utilization Certificate and project completion report
- [ ] Follow up on release of second installment
- [ ] Ensure beneficiary contributions (10% or 25%) are collected and utilized as per sharing pattern
- [ ] Conduct physical verification of worksheds and assets with local committee (WSC & State Govt. representative)
- [ ] Close case upon final settlement and document archiving
