## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the tangible welfare outcomes delivered—such as life/accidental insurance coverage, scholarships for wards, and financial support to indigent awardees—ensuring clients pay for measurable social impact.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate processing risk due to the requirement of Utilization Certificates showing ≥70% fund utilization before second installment release, which introduces a revenue lag and conditional payout.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The retainer-success fee structure ensures accessibility for handloom agencies (Implementing Agencies) across scales, from SHGs and Producer Companies to State Directorates, matching the scheme’s eligibility for Central/State Govt. organizations, NHDC, WSCs, IIHTs, and eligible NGOs.
4. **Transparency & Compliance**: All fees are structured to comply with NHDP guidelines, with clear documentation requirements (e.g., SLPC minutes, baseline survey, audited accounts) supporting fee justification and audit readiness.
