# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping handloom weavers, allied workers, and handloom agencies access government welfare schemes. The Handloom Weavers Comprehensive Welfare Scheme is designed specifically for individuals and groups like you—handloom weavers, pre-loom and post-loom workers, Self Help Groups, producer companies, and others from disadvantaged sections such as SC, ST, women, transgender, and differently-abled communities. I’d like to understand your current situation to see if this scheme could support your work or your weavers’ welfare.

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you or your organization engaged in handloom weaving or allied activities such as winding, warping, dyeing, finishing, or calendaring?
2. What is the legal structure of your entity—are you an individual weaver, Self Help Group, producer company, cooperative society, or another handloom agency?
3. Are you based in a rural or urban area, and which state do you operate from?
4. Do you or your members belong to any of the disadvantaged sections—SC, ST, women, transgender, or differently-abled individuals?
5. Are you currently providing or seeking life insurance, accidental insurance, scholarships for weavers’ children, or financial support for indigent awardees?
6. Do you have access to raw materials at subsidized prices, or do you face challenges in procuring yarn and dyes?
7. Are you looking for support in setting up work sheds, lighting units, or accessing easy credit at low interest rates?
8. Have you previously applied for any government handloom schemes, and if so, what was the outcome?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Engaged in handloom weaving or allied activities (pre-loom/post-loom)
- [ ] Individual weaver, SHG, producer company, cooperative, or other handloom agency
- [ ] Belongs to disadvantaged section (SC, ST, women, transgender, differently-abled) or serves such communities
- [ ] Seeking welfare benefits: insurance, scholarships, financial aid, raw material access, work sheds, or credit
- [ ] Operating in India (Pan-India scheme)
- [ ] Not excluded: Central/State Govt. offices like WSC/IIHT may have relaxed documentation

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like the Handloom Weavers Comprehensive Welfare Scheme could directly support your needs—particularly with life and accidental insurance coverage, scholarships for your weavers’ children, and financial aid for indigent awardees. Let me explain how it works and how we can help you access it.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Handloom weavers and allied workers often lack access to social security, face financial instability, struggle to educate their children, and find it difficult to afford raw materials or set up proper work infrastructure. Many are from disadvantaged backgrounds and need targeted welfare support to sustain their livelihoods.

**Scheme intro:**
The Handloom Weavers Comprehensive Welfare Scheme, administered by the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms under the Ministry of Textiles, provides life and accidental insurance cover (via PMJJBY and PMSBY), scholarships for school and higher education for weavers’ wards, direct financial assistance to indigent handloom awardees, easy access to raw materials at subsidized prices, work sheds, lighting units, and concessional credit at low interest rates. It is implemented through State Directorates, Weavers’ Service Centres, and eligible handloom agencies across India on a rolling basis.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You or your members are engaged in handloom weaving or allied activities (pre-loom/post-loom)  
- You belong to or serve disadvantaged sections (SC, ST, women, transgender, differently-abled)  
- You seek welfare benefits such as insurance, scholarships, or financial support for awardees  
- You operate in India and are eligible to apply via State Directorate or Weavers’ Service Centre
