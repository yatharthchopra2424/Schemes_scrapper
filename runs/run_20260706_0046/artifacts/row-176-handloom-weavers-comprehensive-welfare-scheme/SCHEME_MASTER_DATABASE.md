### Handloom Weavers Comprehensive Welfare Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms, Ministry of Textiles
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://handlooms.nic.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (proposals can be submitted throughout the year as per State Directorate timelines)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Handloom weavers and allied workers engaged in pre-loom (winding, warping, dyeing etc.) and post-loom activities (finishing, calendaring etc.), including Self Help Groups, producer companies, and other handloom agencies
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Handloom sector
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Particularly those belonging to disadvantaged sections of society such as SC, ST, women, transgender, and differently-abled individuals

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Life and accidental insurance cover under Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana and Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana, scholarships for school and higher education for wards of weavers, financial support to indigent handloom awardees, easy access to raw material at subsidized prices, enhanced capabilities including social security, better healthcare, life insurance, work sheds, and easy credit flow at low interest rate
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (funds released in installments based on utilization certificates and project progress)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (financial support released in two equal installments: 50% advance, second installment upon receipt of Utilization Certificate for at least 70% of first installment)

#### Documents Required
1. Minutes of SLPC duly signed by the Chairman and Members
2. Baseline Survey in prescribed proforma (Annexure A1)
3. Diagnostic study and Intervention-wise Action Plan (Annexure A2)
4. Profile of IA indicating name, address, contact, PAN/TAN, Bank account details (Annexure A3)
5. Profit & Loss Account and Balance Sheet of IA for last two years (if applicable)
6. Score pattern for grading and certificate of registration on NITI Aayog Darpan Portal (if IA is NGO)
7. List of weavers for availing benefit of individual interventions (Annexure A7(I))
8. Details of land for Common workshed with area, location, and title documents
9. Cost break-up, lighting capacity, warranty, and installation details for Solar Lighting System

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles submits project proposals to the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms (O/o DC HL)
2. Proposals are scrutinized and approved by O/o DC HL based on recommendations from State Level Project Committees (SLPC)
3. Funds are released in two equal installments: 50% of GoI share as advance, and the second installment upon receipt of Utilization Certificate (UC) for at least 70% of the first installment
4. Implementation is carried out by State Directorates, Weavers' Service Centres, National/State Level Handloom Organizations, Apex/Primary Handloom Weavers’ Co-operative Societies, Self Help Groups, and Producers’ Companies
5. Monitoring and evaluation are conducted by State Implementation Monitoring & Review Committee (SIMRC) and National Implementation Monitoring & Review Committee (NIMRC)
