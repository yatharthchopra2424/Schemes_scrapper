# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
The **Handloom Weavers Comprehensive Welfare Scheme** (Scheme ID: row-176) is a **subsidy-type** scheme under the **Ministry of Textiles**, implemented pan-India by the **Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms**. It operates under the **National Handloom Development Programme (NHDP)** and is delivered through **State Directorates of Handlooms and Textiles**, **Weavers' Service Centres**, and other eligible handloom agencies. The scheme has been active since its last update in **2024** and accepts proposals on a **rolling basis** throughout the year, subject to State Directorate timelines.

### Objectives
The scheme aims to:
- Ensure welfare of handloom workers through life & accidental insurance cover  
- Provide scholarships for school and higher education to wards of handloom workers  
- Offer financial support to indigent handloom awardees  
- Enhance capabilities including social security, better healthcare, life insurance, and work sheds  
- Ensure easy credit flow at low interest rates  
- Provide easy access to raw material at subsidized prices  
- Develop a strong, competitive, and vibrant handloom sector  
- Provide sustainable employment to handloom weavers, especially from disadvantaged sections  

### Eligibility Matrix
| **Eligible Beneficiaries** | **Details** |
|----------------------------|-------------|
| Handloom weavers | Engaged in pre-loom (winding, warping, dyeing) and post-loom (finishing, calendaring) activities |
| Allied workers | Involved in pre-loom and post-loom activities |
| Self Help Groups (SHGs) | Eligible as implementing agencies |
|  
| Other handloom agencies |  
| Producer Companies | Eligible as implementing agencies |
| Other handloom agencies | Includes NGOs, cooperatives, etc. |
| Disadvantaged sections | Priority given to SC, ST, women, transgender, and differently-abled individuals |

> **Note**: At least 50% of weavers in the cluster must be non-members of the Implementing Agency (IA).

### Benefits & Financial Support
| **Benefit Type** | **Details** | **Funding Pattern** |
|------------------|-------------|---------------------|
| Life Insurance Cover | Under Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) | GoI: Rs.198/-, State/Beneficiary: Rs.238/-, Total Premium: Rs.436/- |
| Accidental Insurance Cover | Under Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) | Entire annual premium of Rs.20 borne by GoI |
| Converged MGBBY | Life & accidental cover for ages 51-59 (closed group enrolled under MGBBY by 31.05.2017) | GoI: Rs.290/-, State/Beneficiary: Rs.180/-, Total Premium: Rs.470/- |
| Scholarships | For wards of weavers (up to 2 children) for 3/4-year Diploma/UG/PG courses in recognized textile institutions | Max. Rs.2.00 lakh/annum/child + Rs.5,000/month stipend |
| Financial Support to Indigent Awardees | For awardees (Padma/Sant Kabir/National/State) above 60 years, annual income < Rs.1.00 lakh | Rs.8,000/month per awardee |
| Raw Material Access | Subsidized prices via Raw Material Supply Scheme (RMSS) | 15% price subsidy on yarn (DBT), freight reimbursement |
| Work Sheds | Individual worksheds (25 sq. mts.) | 100% GoI for BPL/SC/ST/Women/Transgender/Differently-abled; 75:25 GoI:Beneficiary for others |
| Common Workshed | Max. Rs.25.00 lakh/cluster | 90:10 GoI:Beneficiary |
| Solar Lighting System | For common worksheds | Max. Rs.10.00 lakh/unit, 90:10 GoI:Beneficiary |
| Hathkargha Samvardhan Sahayata (HSS) | Loom/accessories upgradation | Max. Rs.150.00 lakh for HSS interventions, 90:10 GoI:Beneficiary |
| Lighting Units | Including solar | Max. Rs.15,000/unit, 90:10 GoI:Beneficiary |
| Skill Up-gradation | Wage loss compensation | Rs.300/day/trainee under SAMARTH or NHDP guidelines |
| Textile Designer Engagement | Contractual basis | Max. Rs.15.00 lakh for 3 years: Fixed Rs.30,000/month + Rs.500/month local travel + TA reimbursement |
| Project Management Cost | CDE engagement | Max. Rs.15.00 lakh for 3 years: Fixed Rs.30,000/month + Rs.500/month local travel + TA reimbursement + computer/printer (Rs.1.00 lakh) + admin costs |
| Other Interventions | Baseline survey, product development, exhibitions, etc. | Max. Rs.20.00 lakh |

> **Maximum GoI Financial Assistance**: Rs.2.00 crore per cluster  
> **Land Cost**: Excluded from GoI funding for certain interventions  
> **NGO Requirements**: Must be registered on NITI Aayog Darpan Portal; submit PAN and Aadhar of trustees  
> **Financial Statements**: P&L and Balance Sheet for last 2 years required unless IA is Central/State Govt. office (WSC/IIHT/State Directorate)

### Key Caveats
> - Funds released only upon receipt of Utilization Certificate showing ≥70% utilization of first installment  
> - Land cost excluded from GoI funding for certain interventions  
> - NGOs must be registered on NITI Aayog Darpan Portal and submit PAN and Aadhar of trustees  
> - Profit & Loss Account and Balance Sheet required for last two years unless IA is Central/State Govt. office (WSC/IIHT/State Directorate)  
> - At least 50% of weavers in the cluster must be non-members of the Implementing Agency
