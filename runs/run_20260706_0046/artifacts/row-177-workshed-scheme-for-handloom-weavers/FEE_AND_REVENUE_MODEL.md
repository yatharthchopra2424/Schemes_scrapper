## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered (up to ₹2.00 crore GoI share per cluster under the Workshed Scheme for Handloom Weavers).
2. Tiered success fees reflect decreasing marginal effort as scheme value increases, with lower percentages for larger clusters due to economies of scale in documentation and submission.
3. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, document preparation, and liaison with implementing agencies (IA), adjusted for scheme complexity.
4. Scheme-specific note: The Workshed Scheme involves moderate risk due to a 3-year project duration and two-installment funding (50% advance, balance on 70% UC), requiring careful milestone tracking; however, high success rates are evidenced by 797 clusters assisted under SCDP (NHDP) as of 2025-26, indicating reliable outcomes when documentation is complete.
