# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping handloom weaver groups, Self Help Groups, and producers’ companies access government support for infrastructure like worksheds. The Workshed Scheme for Handloom Weavers, run by the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms, provides need-based financial assistance for constructing individual and common worksheds—up to Rs. 2.00 crore per cluster—to improve productivity, working conditions, and earning potential. I’d like to understand your group’s current setup to see if this could be a fit.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of organization are you (e.g., Self Help Group, Producers’ Company, Handloom Weavers’ Co-operative Society)?
2. How many handloom workers are in your group or cluster?
3. Are you looking to construct individual worksheds for members or a common workshed for shared use?
4. What is the primary beneficiary profile of your group (e.g., BPL, SC/ST, Women, Differently-abled, or general)?
5. Do you have land available in the implementing agency’s name for the workshed construction?
6. Have you conducted a baseline survey or diagnostic study for your cluster?
7. Are you registered on the NITI Aayog Darpan Portal (if applicable as an NGO)?
8. What is your timeline for starting infrastructure development?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Implementing agency is eligible: Central/State Govt. Org, National/State Level Handloom Org, Apex/Primary Handloom Weavers’ Co-op Society, Self Help Group, or Producers’ Company  
- [ ] For NGO: Registered on NITI Aayog Darpan Portal  
- [ ] Group size: Up to 500 handloom workers per cluster  
- [ ] Land available in IA’s name for common workshed (or beneficiary/spouse name for individual workshed)  
- [ ] Baseline Survey (Annexure A1) completed or can be undertaken  
- [ ] Diagnostic Study and Action Plan (Annexure A2) prepared  
- [ ] IA Profile (Annexure A3) with PAN/TAN, bank details ready  
- [ ] P&L and Balance Sheet for last two years (if applicable)  
- [ ] For individual worksheds: List of weavers (Annexure A7(I)) prepared  
- [ ] For Solar Lighting System: Cost break-up, lighting capacity, warranty details available  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, your group appears eligible for the Workshed Scheme for Handloom Weavers, which offers up to Rs. 2.00 crore in GoI funding per cluster for workshed construction—let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Handloom weavers often work in makeshift or shared spaces with poor lighting, ventilation, and ergonomics, leading to drudgery, lower productivity, and inconsistent output. Lack of dedicated infrastructure limits skill upgradation, access to credit, and market readiness—especially for groups aiming to scale or integrate with tourism and craft villages.

**Scheme intro:**
The Workshed Scheme for Handloom Weavers, administered by the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms under the Ministry of Textiles, provides need-based financial assistance for constructing individual and common worksheds. Under the Small Cluster Development Programme (SCDP) of NHDP, clusters with up to 500 handloom workers can receive up to Rs. 2.00 crore (GoI share). Funding patterns:  
- Individual worksheds for BPL/SC/ST/Women/Differently-abled: 100% GoI  
- Others: 75% GoI : 25% beneficiary  
- Common workshed: 90% GoI : 10% beneficiary  
- Solar Lighting System for Common Workshed: 90% GoI : 10% beneficiary  
Funds are released in two installments: 50% advance, balance on 70% utilization certificate of first tranche.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your group has [insert number] handloom workers, within the 500-worker cluster limit  
- You are a [insert org type, e.g., Self Help Group/Producers’ Company], an eligible implementing agency  
- You have land identified in the IA’s name (or can secure it) for workshed construction  
- You serve beneficiaries from [insert: BPL/SC/ST/Women/etc.], qualifying for higher GoI share
