### Workshed Scheme for Handloom Weavers
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms, Ministry of Textiles
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://handlooms.nic.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Central/State Government Organizations, National/State Level Handloom Organizations, Apex/Primary Handloom Weavers’ Co-operative Societies, Self Help Groups, Producers’ Companies
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Handloom
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Implementing agencies must have net profit in last 2 years (except NGOs, Central/State Govt. organizations); NGOs must be registered on NITI Aayog Darpan Portal and submit PAN and Aadhar of each trustee/office bearer

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to Rs. 2.00 crore (GoI share) per cluster for groups having up to 500 handloom workers; for individual worksheds: BPL/SC/ST/Women/differently-abled – 100% GoI share; Others – 75% GoI: 25% beneficiary; for common worksheds: 90% GoI: 10% beneficiary; for Solar Lighting System for Common Workshed: 90% GoI: 10% beneficiary
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (released in two equal installments: 50% of GoI share as advance, and the second installment on receipt of Utilization Certificate for at least 70% of the first installment)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (project-based, with implementation duration of 3 years)

#### Documents Required
1. Minutes of SLPC duly signed by Chairman and Members
2. Baseline Survey of SCDP in prescribed proforma (Annexure A1)
3. Diagnostic study, Intervention-wise Action Plan/total financial outlay (Annexure A2)
4. Profile of IA indicating name, address, contact, PAN/TAN, Bank accounts (Annexure A3)
5. Profit & Loss Account and Balance Sheet of IA for last two years
6. For NGO: Score pattern for grading (Annexure A4) and certificate of registration on NITI Aayog Darpan Portal
7. List of weavers for availing benefit of individual interventions (Annexure A7(I))
8. For Common workshed: details of land with area, location, title in IA's name
9. For Solar Lighting System: cost break-up, lighting capacity, warranty, installation place, workshed size, number of looms

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. State Directorate submits proposals along with SLPC recommendations.
2. Proposals recommended by State Directorate are scrutinized and approved by O/o DC (HL).
3. If State Directorate does not forward cluster proposal within two months, WSC concerned submits directly to O/o DC (HL) with intimation to State Directorate.
4. If WSCs, IIHTs, or National Level Organizations are implementing agencies, they submit proposal directly to O/o DC (HL) under intimation to State Directorate.
5. Required documents include: Minutes of SLPC, Baseline Survey (Annexure A1), Diagnostic Study and Action Plan (Annexure A2), Profile of IA (Annexure A3), Profit & Loss Account and Balance Sheet for last two years (if applicable), NGO registration documents (if applicable), list of weavers for individual interventions (Annexure A7(I)), land details for common workshed, and solar lighting system details.
