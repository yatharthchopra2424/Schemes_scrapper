# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
The **Workshed Scheme for Handloom Weavers** (Scheme ID: row-177) is a **subsidy** scheme implemented pan-India by the **Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms, Ministry of Textiles**. Last updated in 2024, it falls under the **National Handloom Development Programme (NHDP)** and specifically the **Small Cluster Development Programme (SCDP)**. The scheme provides need-based financial assistance for the construction of individual and common worksheds, solar lighting systems, and related infrastructure to improve productivity, working conditions, and earning potential of handloom weavers.

### Objectives
- Provide need-based financial assistance for construction of individual worksheds  
- Support common worksheds for groups of handloom weavers  
- Improve productivity and working conditions of handloom weavers  
- Reduce drudgery and enhance earning potential of weavers  
- Provide infrastructure support for skill upgradation and capacity building  
- Ensure availability of concessional credit and other support mechanisms  
- Promote holistic development of handloom clusters  
- Empower weavers through entrepreneurial support and training  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Beneficiary Category | Workshed Type | GoI Share | Beneficiary Share | Notes |
|----------------------|---------------|-----------|-------------------|-------|
| BPL/SC/ST/Women/Differently-abled | Individual Workshed | 100% | 0% | Fully funded by GoI |
| Others (General) | Individual Workshed | 75% | 25% | Cost-sharing ratio |
| Any Eligible Group | Common Workshed | 90% | 10% | For groups of handloom weavers |
| Any Eligible Group | Solar Lighting System for Common Workshed | 90% | 10% | Specific to solar lighting implementation |

**Implementing Agencies (IAs) Eligible to Apply:**  
- Central/State Government Organizations  
- National/State Level Handloom Organizations  
- Apex/Primary Handloom Weavers’ Co-operative Societies  
- Self Help Groups (SHGs)  
- Producers’ Companies  

**Additional IA Requirements:**  
- For NGOs: Registration on **NITI Aayog Darpan Portal** is mandatory  
- IA (except NGOs, Central/State Govt. organizations) must have net profit in last 2 years  
- IA must submit audited Profit & Loss Account and Balance Sheet for last two years (if applicable)  

**Target Beneficiaries:**  
Handloom weavers; BPL; SC/ST; Women; Differently-abled; Self Help Groups; Producers' Companies

**Funds Release Mechanism:**  
- 50% of GoI share released as **advance (1st installment)**  
- 2nd installment released on receipt of **Utilization Certificate (UC) for at least 70% of the 1st installment**  
- Funds routed through **PFMS** with EAT/REAT modules for tracking  

**Key Caveats:**  
> - Land cost is **excluded** from GoI funding for individual and common worksheds  
> - Inter-component diversion within approved cost requires **SLPC approval** and intimation to O/o DC (HL)  
> - For NGO IAs, registration on **NITI Aayog Darpan Portal** is mandatory  
> - State Directorate must submit proposal copy to WSCs **at least two weeks** before SLPC meeting  
> - Funds released in **two equal installments**: 50% advance, balance on 70% UC of first installment
