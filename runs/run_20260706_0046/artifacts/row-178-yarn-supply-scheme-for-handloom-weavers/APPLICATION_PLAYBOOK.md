# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a handloom weaver, handloom agency, Self Help Group (SHG), Producer Company, or other eligible handloom entity
- [ ] Applicant is recommended by the State Government and approved by the Development Commissioner (Handlooms)
- [ ] Applicant possesses valid handloom weaver's identity card or EPehchan registration
- [ ] Applicant has specified details of yarn requirement
- [ ] Applicant is an individual weaver, or part of an agency where weavers are members (SHG, JLG, Cooperative Society), or a Handloom Producer Company, or a Weaver Entrepreneur involved in actual weaving activity with functional handlooms on premises
- [ ] Application is submitted through the nearest Weavers' Service Centre (WSC) or State Directorate of Handlooms

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Identity proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, etc.)
- Address proof
- PAN card
- Bank account details (passbook or cancelled cheque)
- GST registration (if applicable)
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of last 2 years (if applicable)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Application form (as per scheme)
- Identity proof of the weaver/agency
- Address proof
- Bank account details
- Handloom weaver's identity card or EPehchan registration
- Details of yarn requirement
- Any other document as specified by the implementing agency

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and clear scans or photocopies
- File size should not exceed 2 MB per document
- Document names must match the applicant's name as per identity proof
- Self-attested copies are acceptable; gazetted officer attestation preferred for identity and address proofs
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in regional language, provide certified translation
- Handloom weaver's identity card or EPehchan registration must be valid and not expired

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for this scheme. The application is document-based and processed at the WSC/State Directorate level. However, verify with the nodal officer if a brief project note is needed for agency-based applications.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use the official application form obtained from the nearest WSC or State Directorate of Handlooms.
2. Fill all fields in block letters; avoid overwriting or correction fluid.
3. Enter accurate personal details: name, father’s/spouse’s name, date of birth, gender, and contact information as per identity proof.
4. Clearly specify the type and quantity of yarn required (e.g., cotton hank, silk, woollen, linen, blended yarn).
5. Declare whether you are an individual weaver, part of an SHG/JLG/Cooperative Society, Producer Company, or Weaver Entrepreneur (with number of functional handlooms on premises).
6. Attach self-attested copies of all required documents; do not submit originals unless requested.
7. Sign the declaration form affirming the authenticity of information and documents submitted.
8. Submit the completed application in person at the WSC or State Directorate office; online submission is not currently available for this scheme.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Application form is fully completed with no blank fields
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents are attached and legible
- [ ] Identity and address proofs match the applicant’s details
- [ ] Bank account details are correct and active
- [ ] Handloom weaver's identity card or EPehchan registration is valid
- [ ] Yarn requirement details are specific and realistic
- [ ] Declaration form is signed and dated
- [ ] Photocopies are clear and within file size limits
- [ ] Application is ready for submission at WSC/State Directorate

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application package in person at the nearest Weavers' Service Centre (WSC) or State Directorate of Handlooms
- Obtain a receipt or acknowledgment slip with date, time, and inward number
- Store a scanned copy of the submitted application and acknowledgment for records
- Note down the WSC/Directorate name, official receiving the application, and contact details
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days after submission to check status

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Collect approval letter or sanction order from WSC/State Directorate
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned benefits: transport subsidy (freight reimbursement) and 15% price subsidy on yarn (via DBT to linked bank account)
- [ ] Confirm linkage of bank account for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) of price subsidy
- [ ] Upon yarn supply, retain proof of delivery and subsidy availed (e.g., depot invoice showing subsidized rate)
- [ ] Prepare and submit Utilization Certificate (UC) if required for subsequent tranches of support (as per GFR 2017 rules)
- [ ] Monitor yarn stock and fund availability for continued support; reapply as needed
- [ ] Ensure compliance with scheme caveats: subsidy subject to fund/yarn stock, valid identity/EPehchan, and verification by State Directorate/WSC
- [ ] Inform client about the supply mechanism: 511 yarn depots and 46 warehouses across the country for assured quality yarn supply
- [ ] Advise client to maintain records of yarn procured and utilized for audit and future applications
