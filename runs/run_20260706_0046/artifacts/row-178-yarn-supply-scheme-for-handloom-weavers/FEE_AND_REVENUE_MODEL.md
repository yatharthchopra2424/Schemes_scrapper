## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to handloom weavers under the Yarn Supply Scheme, which provides transport and price subsidies on yarn supply.
2. Tiered retainer structure reflects the scale of beneficiary entities, from individual weavers to SHGs and producer companies.
3. Success fee capped at 8% for Starter tier due to the scheme’s rolling basis and high volume of beneficiaries (5.38 lakh weavers benefitted as of 31.03.2024), indicating lower individual transaction value but high scalability.
4. Scheme-specific note: The Yarn Supply Scheme has a low to moderate risk profile due to its rolling application basis and direct implementation through 511 yarn depots and 46 warehouses, with fund availability and yarn stock as key caveats affecting approval consistency.
