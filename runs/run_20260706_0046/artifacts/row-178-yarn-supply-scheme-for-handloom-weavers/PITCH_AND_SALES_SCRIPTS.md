# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping handloom weavers and agencies access government support for raw material needs. The Yarn Supply Scheme for Handloom Weavers is designed to reduce your input costs by providing subsidized yarn through transport and price subsidies—directly addressing the challenge of securing quality raw material at affordable prices. I’d like to understand your current situation to see if this scheme could be a fit for you.

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you an individual handloom weaver, part of a Self Help Group (SHG), Producer Company, or another handloom entity?
2. Do you possess a valid handloom weaver’s identity card or EPehchan registration?
3. What is your average monthly yarn requirement (in kg), and what types of yarn do you typically use (cotton hank, silk, woollen, linen, or blended)?
4. Are you currently sourcing yarn through designated yarn depots or warehouses, or do you face challenges in accessing timely supply?
5. Have you previously applied for any raw material or subsidy schemes through the Weavers’ Service Centre (WSC) or State Directorate of Handlooms?
6. What is your primary challenge in procuring yarn—cost, availability, quality, or logistics?
7. Are you located in a region with access to a Weavers’ Service Centre or State Directorate of Handlooms for application submission?
8. Do you maintain a bank account linked to your identity for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) purposes?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is an individual handloom weaver, handloom agency, SHG, Producer Company, or other eligible handloom entity
- [ ] Possesses valid handloom weaver’s identity card or EPehchan registration
- [ ] Recommended by State Government and approved by Development Commissioner (Handlooms)
- [ ] Has documented yarn requirement details
- [ ] Submits application through nearest Weavers’ Service Centre (WSC) or State Directorate of Handlooms
- [ ] Agrees to provide Utilization Certificate (UC) for continued support if required
- [ ] Operates within Pan-India geographic scope (no regional restrictions)
- [ ] Not currently receiving duplicate support for the same yarn supply purpose

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like the Yarn Supply Scheme for Handloom Weavers could directly help you reduce yarn costs and ensure steady supply. This scheme provides transport and price subsidies on yarn—let me explain how it works and how we’ve helped similar weavers access it.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Handloom weavers often face high input costs and inconsistent access to quality yarn, which disrupts production, increases drudgery in procurement, and limits income growth. The Yarn Supply Scheme for Handloom Weavers addresses this by ensuring affordable, timely yarn supply through a nationwide network of depots and warehouses.

**Scheme intro:**
The Yarn Supply Scheme for Handloom Weavers (also known as the Raw Material Supply Scheme), administered by the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms under the Ministry of Textiles, provides financial assistance via transport subsidy and price subsidy on yarn. As of 31.03.2024, it has supplied 9139.88 lakh kg of yarn, benefitting 5.38 lakh handloom weavers through 511 yarn depots and 46 warehouses across India.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a handloom weaver, SHG, Producer Company, or eligible handloom entity as confirmed in discovery  
- You possess or can obtain a valid handloom weaver’s identity card or EPehchan registration  
- You are recommended by your State Government and eligible for approval by the Development Commissioner (Handlooms)  
- You have a defined yarn requirement that can be submitted as part of the application
