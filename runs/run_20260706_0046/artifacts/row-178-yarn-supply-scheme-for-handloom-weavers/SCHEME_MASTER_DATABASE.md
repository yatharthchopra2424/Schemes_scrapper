### Yarn Supply Scheme for Handloom Weavers
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms, Ministry of Textiles
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://handlooms.nic.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Handloom weavers, handloom agencies, Self Help Groups (SHGs), Producer Companies, and other eligible handloom entities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Handloom
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must possess valid handloom weaver's identity or EPehchan registration; recommended by State Government and approved by Development Commissioner (Handlooms)

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Transport subsidy and price subsidy on yarn (15% price subsidy on yarn through DBT; freight reimbursement rates vary by region and yarn type)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (via DBT to linked bank account) and reimbursement (freight)
- Duration of benefit: One-time per supply cycle (subject to fund and stock availability)

#### Documents Required
1. Application form
2. Identity proof of the weaver/agency
3. Address proof
4. Bank account details
5. Handloom weaver's identity card or EPehchan registration
6. Details of yarn requirement
7. Any other document as specified by the implementing agency

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Handloom weavers or agencies approach the nearest Weavers' Service Centre (WSC) or State Directorate of Handlooms.
2. Submit application along with required documents.
3. Application is forwarded to the concerned authority for verification and approval.
4. Upon approval, yarn is supplied through designated yarn depots at subsidized rates.
5. Utilization Certificate (UC) may be required for continued support.
