For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is an Indian Citizen  
- [ ] Business plan is for a non-farm income generating activity (manufacturing, processing, trading, service sector, or agri-allied activities)  
- [ ] Credit need is up to ₹20 lakh  
- [ ] Applicant can approach a Bank, MFI, or NBFC for loan under PMMY  
- [ ] Applicant follows the usual terms and conditions of the lending agency  
- [ ] Applicant is not a corporate entity (non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprise)  
- [ ] Applicant belongs to target beneficiary groups: weavers, micro enterprises, small businesses, SC/ST, OBC, women entrepreneurs (priority given)  

**Sources**:  
- Eligibility criteria from KEY FACTS: "Any Indian Citizen who has a business plan for a non-farm income generating activity such as manufacturing, processing, trading or service sector whose credit need is up to 20 lakh can approach either a Bank, MFI or NBFC for availing of MUDRA loans under PMMY."  
- Target beneficiaries: weavers; micro enterprises; small businesses; SC/ST; OBC; women entrepreneurs; non-corporate entities  
- Application Process: "Ensure the loan proposal is for a non-farm income generating activity such as manufacturing, processing, trading or service sector as well as agri-allied activities. Ensure credit need is up to 20 lakh."  
- Portal: www.udyamimitra.in

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Identity Proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Passport)  
- Address Proof (Utility bill, Rent agreement, Aadhaar)  
- Business Proof (Udyam Registration, Shop Act license, GST certificate)  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of last 2 years (if applicable)  
- Project Report / Business Plan  
- Quotations for machinery/equipment (if applicable)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- No scheme-specific documents beyond the universal set are mandated for MUDRA loans under PMMY.  
- However, the lending institution may require additional documents based on their internal policies (e.g., audited financial statements for existing businesses).  
- For online application via Udyamimitra portal, the system guides the applicant through required fields and document uploads.  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be clear, legible, and scanned in PDF format  
- File size per document should not exceed 5 MB  
- Name on documents must match exactly with the applicant’s identity proof  
- Self-attested copies are acceptable; notarization not required unless specified by the lending institution  
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; vernacular documents require certified translation  
- Bank statements must show active account and consistent transactions

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- A detailed **Project Report** is required for loan applications under PMMY, especially for loans above ₹50,000.  
- Standard sections to include:  
  1. Executive Summary  
  2. Promoter Details  
  3. Business Description and Objectives  
  4. Market Analysis  
  5. Technical Details (process, machinery, location)  
  6. Financial Projections (cost of project, means of finance, profitability, break-even)  
  7. Loan Utilization Plan  
  8. Risk Assessment and Mitigation  
  9. Conclusion and Prayer  
- If the loan is for working capital only, a simplified business plan may suffice.  
- **Note**: Verify exact DPR requirements with the nodal officer or lending institution, as requirements may vary based on loan product and lender.  

**Sources**:  
- Application Process (KEY FACTS): "Ensure the loan proposal is for a non-farm income generating activity..." implies need for a proposal/report  
- Offerings section (mudra.org.in): References to project profiles and business activities  
- No explicit DPR mandate in evidence, but standard practice for business loans  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
Follow these 8 critical rules when filling the MUDRA loan application form on the Udyamimitra portal:  
1. **Accuracy**: Enter all personal and business details exactly as per identity and address proofs  
2. **Activity Selection**: Clearly select "non-farm income generating activity" and specify the exact nature (e.g., handloom weaving, textile trading)  
3. **Loan Amount**: Request amount must align with credit need and not exceed ₹20 lakh; select appropriate product (Shishu/Kishor/Tarun/TarunPlus)  
4. **Financials**: Provide realistic and verifiable figures for project cost, means of finance, and projected income  
5. **Document Uploads**: Upload only the required documents in prescribed format (PDF/JPEG, <5 MB each)  
6. **Declaration**: Read and sign the self-declaration affirming correctness of information and eligibility  
7. **Review**: Double-check all entries before final submission; errors may lead to rejection  
8. **Acknowledgement**: Save and note the application reference number upon successful submission  

**Portal-Specific Note**:  
- The Udyamimitra portal (www.udyamimitra.in) enables online filing of MUDRA loan applications  
- The form is structured to capture borrower, business, and loan details as per PMMY guidelines

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed application via the Udyamimitra portal (www.udyamimitra.in)  
- Upon successful submission, take a **screenshot** of the confirmation page showing the **Application Reference Number**  
- Store the reference number securely in the case file  
- Set a follow-up reminder for **15 days** after submission to check application status  
- If no update is received, initiate follow-up via the portal helpdesk or nodal officer

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the **approval letter** from the lending institution (Bank/NBFC/MFI)  
- [ ] Confirm the **sanctioned amount** matches the approved loan product and request  
- [ ] Review and accept the **terms of sanction** (interest rate, repayment schedule, etc.)  
- [ ] Ensure loan disbursement is received in the designated bank account  
- [ ] For working capital portion, confirm issuance and activation of **MUDRA Card** (if applicable)  
- [ ] Maintain records of all loan-related documents for utilization and audit purposes  
- [ ] Submit **Utilization Certificate** (if required by the lending institution or scheme guidelines)  
- [ ] Track invoice and success fee as per consultancy agreement
