## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the actual loan amount sanctioned under PMMY (average ₹54,218 per account in FY 2019-20).
2. Success fee tied to disbursement confirmation via the Udyamimitra portal, reducing risk of non-payment.
3. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, documentation support, and application submission via www.udyamimitra.in.
4. Scheme-specific note: Processing time is typically immediate to 7 days for online applications via Udyamimitra portal, enabling faster revenue realization; success is evidenced by high sanction rates (104% of target in FY 2019-20) and widespread adoption across lending institutions.
