# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping weavers and micro enterprises in the textile and allied sectors access government-backed financing like the MUDRA Loan for Weavers scheme. Many artisans and small business owners in this space struggle to get affordable credit for working capital, equipment, or raw materials—especially when they’re non-corporate entities. I’d like to understand your current setup to see if this scheme could support your growth.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of weaving or textile-related activity are you currently engaged in (handloom, powerloom, khadi, etc.)?
2. When was your business registered or started? (Note: Scheme supports non-corporate entities; no age limit but must be operational)
3. Are you seeking finance for working capital, equipment purchase, or another specific purpose like raw materials or transport?
4. What is your estimated credit need—is it under ₹20 lakh?
5. Do you have a clear business plan for income generation through manufacturing, processing, trading, or service activities?
6. Are you operating as an individual proprietor, partnership, or non-corporate entity?
7. Do you belong to any of the prioritized groups: SC/ST, OBC, or are you a woman entrepreneur?
8. Have you previously approached banks or MFIs for loans, and what challenges did you face?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Indian citizen
- [ ] Engaged in non-farm income generating activity (manufacturing, processing, trading, service, or agri-allied)
- [ ] Credit requirement up to ₹20 lakh
- [ ] Business plan for income generation exists
- [ ] Not seeking direct lending from MUDRA (understands lending via banks/NBFCs/MFIs)
- [ ] No agents/middlemen involved (aware of MUDRA’s no-agent policy)
- [ ] Activity is non-farm (even if agri-allied like pisciculture, poultry, etc.)
- [ ] Willing to apply via bank, MFI, NBFC, or Udyamimitra portal

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re a weaver or textile artisan with a clear income-generating activity and a funding need under ₹20 lakh—precisely who the MUDRA Loan for Weavers (under Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana) is designed to support. Let me explain how this scheme can help you access affordable credit without collateral through partner lenders.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Weavers and micro textile entrepreneurs often struggle to get timely, affordable credit for working capital, loom upgrades, or raw material purchases. Traditional lenders may require collateral, have lengthy processes, or exclude non-corporate entities—leaving many reliant on high-interest informal loans.

**Scheme intro:**
The MUDRA Loan for Weavers is part of the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY), implemented by the Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency Ltd. (MUDRA). It offers refinance support to banks, NBFCs, and MFIs so they can lend up to ₹20 lakh to non-corporate, non-farm micro enterprises for income-generating activities. Loans are categorized as Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishor (₹50,000–₹5 lakh), Tarun (₹5–10 lakh), and TarunPlus (₹10–20 lakh). The scheme has sanctioned over ₹12.30 lakh crore to 24.48 crore accounts since inception, with 68% of loan accounts going to women beneficiaries.

- You operate as a non-corporate entity (proprietorship/partnership)  
- You may belong to a prioritized group (SC/ST/OBC/woman entrepreneur)—which the scheme actively supports
