### MUDRA Loan for Weavers (Weaver MUDRA Scheme)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency Ltd. (MUDRA)
- **Portal / Application URL:** www.udyamimitra.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Non-farm income generating activity such as manufacturing, processing, trading or service sector as well as agri-allied activities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Manufacturing, processing, trading, services, agri-allied sectors
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Any Indian Citizen with a business plan for a non-farm income generating activity whose credit need is up to ₹20 lakh

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Loans up to ₹20 lakh per unit
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (via banks, NBFCs, MFIs)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (term loan and working capital loan)

#### Documents Required
1. Business plan for non-farm income generating activity
2. Proof of Indian citizenship
3. Loan proposal details (purpose, amount, repayment capacity)
4. KYC documents (identity and address proof)
5. Project report (if applicable)
6. Bank statements (if required by lending institution)
7. Any other documents as per the lending institution's usual terms and conditions

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Approach any of the lending institutions mentioned above (Commercial Banks, RRBs, Small Finance Banks, MFIs and NBFCs) or apply online through this portal www.udyamimitra.in.
2. File online application for MUDRA loans on Udyamimitra portal (www.udyamimitra.in).
3. Ensure the loan proposal is for a non-farm income generating activity such as manufacturing, processing, trading or service sector as well as agri-allied activities.
4. Ensure credit need is up to 20 lakh.
5. Follow the usual terms and conditions of the lending agency.
6. Avail the loan from the selected lending institution.
