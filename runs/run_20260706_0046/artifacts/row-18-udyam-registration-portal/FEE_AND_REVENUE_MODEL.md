## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the scheme’s zero-cost registration and access to financial benefits.
2. Transparent fee structure with no hidden charges, reflecting the scheme’s free and digital nature.
3. Success fee tied to measurable outcomes such as access to credit, subsidies, or government procurement.
4. Scheme-specific note: Low risk due to instant processing, zero cost, and high success rate evidenced by mass adoption (over 50 lakh registrations by Sept 2021) and mandatory status for accessing MSME benefits.
