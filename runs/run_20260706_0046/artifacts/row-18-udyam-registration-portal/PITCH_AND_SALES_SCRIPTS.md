# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping MSMEs access government benefits through the Udyam Registration Portal. Since this registration is mandatory for availing benefits under most MSME Ministry schemes—including Priority Sector Lending from banks—I’d like to understand your enterprise’s current status to see if we can assist you in unlocking these opportunities.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your enterprise incorporated or started operations?
2. What is your current investment in plant and machinery or equipment?
3. What is your annual turnover (excluding exports)?
4. Are you currently registered under any MSME scheme (U scheme like Udyog Aadhaar or EM Part-II?
5. Do you have your Aadhaar number and PAN details readily available?
6. What is your primary business activity (manufacturing, services, or trading) and NIC code?
7. Are you planning to apply for bank loans, government subsidies, or participate in government procurement via GeM?
8. Have you faced challenges in accessing credit or government tenders due to lack of MSME recognition?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Investment in plant and machinery ≤ ₹50 crore  
- [ ] Turnover (excluding exports) ≤ ₹250 crore  
- [ ] Enterprise is proprietorship, partnership, company, LLP, etc.  
- [ ] Aadhaar number of proprietor/managing partner/karta is available  
- [ ] PAN is available (mandatory for all except proprietorships not registered under any Act)  
- [ ] Business activity has a valid NIC code  
- [ ] No existing Udyam Registration (only one allowed)  
- [ ] Enterprise is engaged in manufacturing, services, or trading  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your enterprise may qualify for Udyam Registration—a free, lifetime-valid recognition from the Ministry of MSME that enables access to collateral-free loans, interest subvention, and government procurement. Shall I walk you through how this works and the benefits it unlocks?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many MSMEs struggle to access bank loans, government subsidies, or participate in government tenders because they lack formal recognition as a micro, small, or medium enterprise. Without Udyam Registration, banks may not classify your loan under Priority Sector Lending, and government departments may not recognize your eligibility for MSME-specific benefits.

**Scheme intro:**
The Udyam Registration Portal, launched by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, provides a free, digital, lifetime-valid recognition certificate with a dynamic QR code. While it does not offer direct financial aid, it is mandatory for accessing benefits like Priority Sector Lending (PSL) from banks, credit guarantee schemes (e.g., CGTMSE), collateral-free loans, interest subvention, and participation in government procurement via GeM integration.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your investment in plant and machinery is within the ₹50 crore limit for medium enterprises  
- Your turnover (excluding exports) is under ₹250 crore  
- You have an Aadhaar number and PAN (or are eligible to use proprietor’s PAN if unregistered)  
- Your business activity has a valid NIC code for classification
