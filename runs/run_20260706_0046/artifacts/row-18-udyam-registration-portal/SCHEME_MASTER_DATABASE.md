### Udyam Registration Portal
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://udyamregistration.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Micro, Small, or Medium Enterprises
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: All sectors (manufacturing and services treated equally)
- Revenue cap: 
  - Micro: turnover ≤ ₹5 crore
  - Small: turnover ≤ ₹50 crore  
  - Medium: turnover ≤ ₹250 crore
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: 
  - Classification based on investment in plant and machinery or equipment and turnover
  - Micro: investment ≤ ₹1 crore and turnover ≤ ₹5 crore
  - Small: investment ≤ ₹10 crore and turnover ≤ ₹50 crore
  - Medium: investment ≤ ₹50 crore and turnover ≤ ₹250 crore
  - Exports excluded from turnover calculation for MSME classification
  - PAN mandatory for all enterprise types except proprietorships not registered under any Act
  - No enterprise can file more than one Udyam Registration, but multiple activities (manufacturing, services, trading) can be added under one registration
  - Classification based on frozen data from Income Tax and GSTN (previous financial year)
  - Udyam Registration Certificate must be updated if investment or turnover crosses category thresholds

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Udyam Registration Certificate with dynamic QR code for verification; mandatory for availing benefits under most MSME Ministry schemes; facilitates Priority Sector Lending from banks; enables participation in government procurement via GeM integration; lifetime validity; free of cost; paperless and digital process; access to government subsidies, incentives, and support programs; data sharing with CBDT and GSTN for accurate classification
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (certificate issued instantly upon successful registration)
- Duration of benefit: Lifetime validity (no renewal required)

#### Documents Required
1. Aadhaar number
2. PAN (for all enterprises except proprietorships not registered under any Act)
3. Bank account details
4. Details of investment in plant and machinery or equipment
5. Turnover details
6. Social category and gender of entrepreneur
7. NIC code for economic activity
8. Enterprise type (proprietorship, partnership, etc.)
9. Geographic location and contact details

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the Udyam Registration Portal at https://udyamregistration.gov.in
2. Enter Aadhaar number and validate via OTP
3. Provide PAN details (mandatory for all enterprises except proprietorships not registered under any Act, where PAN of proprietor may be used)
4. Fill in enterprise details including name, type of organization, address, bank details, and NIC code for activity
5. The system automatically fetches investment and turnover data from Income Tax and GST Networks
6. Submit the form to receive the Udyam Registration Certificate with a dynamic QR code
7. Certificate can be downloaded and printed; no further renewal is required
