# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Beneficiary is a handloom weaver, self-help group (SHG), producer company, primary handloom weavers’ cooperative society, or other handloom agency engaged in handloom production  
- [ ] Beneficiary is recommended by the State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles  
- [ ] Beneficiary is approved by the Development Commissioner (Handlooms) (DCHL)  
- [ ] Beneficiary is engaged in actual handloom production (proof via registration, weaver’s identity card, or similar)  
- [ ] Beneficiary has a bank account for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)  
- [ ] Beneficiary possesses or is willing to obtain a Yarn Passbook for tracking entitlements  
- [ ] Beneficiary falls under the target categories: handloom weavers, handloom agencies, self-help groups, producer companies, or cooperative societies  
- [ ] Beneficiary is located in India (Pan-India scheme)  
- [ ] Beneficiary is not applying for subsidies on non-yarn raw materials (e.g., dyes, chemicals) — scheme covers yarn only  

**Sources**:  
- Eligibility: Eligible beneficiaries include handloom weavers, self-help groups, producer companies, primary handloom weavers’ cooperative societies, and other handloom agencies engaged in handloom production and recommended by State Directorates of Handlooms and Textiles and approved by the Development Commissioner (Handlooms).  
- Target Beneficiaries: handloom weavers; handloom agencies; self-help groups; producer companies; co-operative societies  
- Application Process: Eligible handloom agencies or weavers apply through the State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles. The State Directorate forwards the proposal to the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms (DCHL) for approval.  
- Key Caveats: Only handloom agencies recommended by State Directorates and approved by DCHL are eligible. Subsidy is limited to yarn only and does not cover other raw materials like dyes or chemicals.  
- Portal: https://handlooms.nic.in (Development Commissioner Handlooms official website)

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN card of beneficiary/agency  
- Aadhaar card of authorized signatory  
- Bank account details (cancelled cheque or bank passbook) for DBT  
- GST registration (if applicable)  
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of last 2 years  
- Business registration proof (e.g., partnership deed, incorporation certificate, society registration)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Application form forwarded by State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles  
- Yarn Passbook (for tracking entitlements)  
- Bank account details for DBT transfer  
- Utilization Certificate (UC) for previous fund releases (if applicable)  
- Proof of engagement in handloom production (e.g., registration, weaver’s identity card)  
- Recommendation from State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)  
- Names on documents must match exactly as per bank records and PAN  
- Bank account must be active and enabled for DBT  
- Utilization Certificate (if required) must be countersigned by State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles  
- Recommendation letter must be on official letterhead of State Directorate, signed and stamped  
- Yarn Passbook must be issued by authorized depot or NHDC  
- All documents must be in English or Hindi; vernacular documents require certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for RMSS. The scheme operates via a Yarn Passbook system and does not require a Detailed Project Report (DPR). However, if the client is applying as an agency seeking infrastructure or cluster-level support under related schemes, verify with the nodal officer (State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles or DCHL) whether a DPR is needed for ancillary components.  

**Sources**:  
- Application Process: No mention of DPR; process focuses on enrollment in Yarn Passbook system and yarn supply through depots.  
- Scheme Type: Subsidy-based (price and transport subsidy on yarn), not project-based.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Ensure the application form is forwarded by the State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles — do not submit directly to DCHL without this endorsement.  
2. Fill in beneficiary details exactly as per PAN and bank records (name, address, contact).  
3. Specify the type of beneficiary (weaver, SHG, producer company, cooperative society, etc.).  
4. Provide bank account details for DBT (include IFSC, account number, branch name).  
5. Attach proof of handloom engagement (registration, identity card, or similar).  
6. Attach the Yarn Passbook details or application for issuance (if not already issued).  
7. Attach the recommendation letter from the State Directorate on official letterhead.  
8. If previously funded, attach Utilization Certificate (UC) countersigned by the State Directorate.  
9. Declaration: Sign and date the form declaring that subsidy will be used only for yarn procurement under RMSS.  
10. Submit the completed form and documents to the State Directorate for forwarding to DCHL.  

**Sources**:  
- Application Process: Eligible handloom agencies or weavers apply through the State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles. The State Directorate forwards the proposal to the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms (DCHL) for approval.  
- Required Documents: Application form forwarded by State Directorate, Yarn Passbook, bank account details, UC (if applicable), proof of engagement, recommendation from State Directorate.  
- Portal: https://handlooms.nic.in  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Application form is forwarded by State Directorate (not submitted directly)  
- [ ] Beneficiary eligibility verified (handloom weaver/agency, State recommendation, DCHL approval pathway)  
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents attached: Yarn Passbook, bank details, UC (if applicable), proof of engagement, State recommendation  
- [ ] Bank account validated for DBT (active, correct IFSC, name match)  
- [ ] Names consistent across PAN, Aadhaar, bank, and application form  
- [ ] Utilization Certificate (if applicable) is recent and countersigned by State Directorate  
- [ ] Declaration signed by authorized signatory  
- [ ] Documents are legible, PDF format, under 5 MB each  
- [ ] Application package ready for submission to State Directorate

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the complete application package to the **State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles** (not directly to DCHL portal).  
- Obtain a receipt or diary number from the State Directorate upon submission.  
- Screenshot or scan the receipt and store it in the case file.  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 7 days to check on forwarding status to DCHL.  
- Once forwarded by State Directorate, obtain the DCHL inward number or reference.  
- Store all reference numbers securely.  
- Monitor for updates (though primary communication).  

**Sources**:  
- Application Process: Apply for Yarn Passbook enrollment if not already done (through NHDC or authorized depot).  
- After approval, coordinate with authorized yarn depot for subsidized yarn supply.  
- Ensure DBT readiness: confirm bank account is linked and active for subsidy credit.  

**Sources**:  
- Application Process: Apply through State Directorate → forwarded to DCHL → approval → enrollment in Yarn Passbook → yarn supplied via authorized depots → subsidy via DBT.  
- Portal: https://handlooms.nic.in (for general information; submission is via State Directorate).

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain approval letter from DCHL (via State Directorate) confirming enrollment in RMSS  
- [ ] Verify sanctioned subsidy entitlements in the Yarn Passbook (price subsidy: 15% on yarn; transport subsidy as per rates)  
- [ ] Confirm bank account is active and ready for DBT receipt of subsidy  
- [ ] Coordinate with authorized yarn depot for supply of subsidized yarn (cotton hank, domestic silk, woollen, linen, blended natural fibre yarn)  
- [ ] Maintain records of yarn received and subsidy credited per Yarn Passbook entries  
- [ ] For subsequent fund releases, prepare and submit Utilization Certificate (UC) to State Directorate, countersigned by them, for forwarding to DCHL  
- [ ] Ensure UC is submitted within stipulated time to avoid disruption in yarn supply  
- [ ] Track utilization and submit success fee invoice to client upon verification of subsidy receipt  

**Sources**:  
- Financial Support: Subsidy disbursed via DBT to bank accounts; operated via Yarn Passbook system; funds released to implementing agencies based on verified utilization and submission of Utilization Certificates (UCs).  
- Benefits: Price subsidy and transport subsidy on yarn; assured availability through 511 yarn depots and 46 warehouses; streamlined supply via Yarn Passbook and DBT.  
- Application Process: Upon approval, agency enrolled in Yarn Passbook system; yarn supplied through authorized depots at subsidized rates; subsidy transferred via DBT; UC required for further fund releases.  
- Key Caveats: Yarn supply contingent on verification through Yarn Passbook system and submission of Utilization Certificates.  
- Portal: https://handlooms.nic.in
