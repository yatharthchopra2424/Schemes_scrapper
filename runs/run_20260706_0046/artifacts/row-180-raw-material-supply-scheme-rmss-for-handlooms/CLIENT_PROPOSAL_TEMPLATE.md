## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified Raw Material Supply Scheme (RMSS) for Handlooms — a subsidy scheme administered by the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms, Ministry of Textiles — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is approximately price subsidy and transport subsidy on yarn supplied under the scheme.

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment and scheme suitability analysis  
2. Documentation preparation and verification as per scheme requirements  
3. Application submission and liaison with State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles  
4. Coordination with Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms (DCHL) for approval  
5. Portal registration on https://handlooms.nic.in  
6. Utilization Certificate (UC) preparation and submission for fund releases  
7. Post-sanction monitoring and compliance support  

*Note: A DPR/project report is not required for this scheme.*

## 4. What We Need From You
1. Documents as per the scheme's document list: Application form forwarded by State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles, Yarn Passbook (for tracking entitlements), Bank account details for DBT transfer, Utilization Certificate (UC) for previous fund releases (if applicable), Proof of engagement in handloom production (e.g., registration, weaver’s identity card), Recommendation from State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles  
2. Designated point of contact within your organization  
3. Response to queries within 2 business days
