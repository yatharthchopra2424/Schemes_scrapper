## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, documentation support, and application submission facilitation.
2. Success fee is contingent upon verified subsidy disbursement via DBT under the Raw Material Supply Scheme (RMSS).
3. Fees are structured to align with client outcomes, ensuring payment only upon successful yarn subsidy realization.
4. Scheme-specific note: RMSS has a moderate risk profile due to its rolling application basis and dependency on State Directorate recommendations and DCHL approval cycles; however, the scheme demonstrates strong uptake (5.38 lakh weavers benefitted, 9139.88 lakh kg yarn supplied), indicating reliable processing when documentation is complete and Utilization Certificates are submitted timely.
