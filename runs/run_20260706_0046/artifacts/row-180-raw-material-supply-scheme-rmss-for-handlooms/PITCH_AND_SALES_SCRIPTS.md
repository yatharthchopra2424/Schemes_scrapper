# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping handloom weavers, self-help groups, producer companies, and cooperative societies access government schemes that reduce raw material costs. The Raw Material Supply Scheme (RMSS) for Handlooms is designed specifically to ensure yarn availability at subsidized prices through Direct Benefit Transfer, so I’d like to understand if this could support your handloom production activities.

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you currently engaged in handloom production as an individual weaver, self-help group, producer company, or cooperative society?  
2. Have you been recommended by your State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles for any government support in the past?  
3. Do you possess a Yarn Passbook or are you enrolled in the yarn depot system for tracking entitlements?  
4. Is your bank account linked for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to receive subsidies?  
5. What type of yarn (cotton, silk, woollen, linen, or blended natural fibre) do you primarily use in your production?  
6. Have you previously received subsidies under RMSS and need to submit a Utilization Certificate (UC) for further fund releases?  
7. Are you facing challenges in accessing yarn at competitive prices due to supply chain or cost issues?  
8. Are you registered as a handloom agency with valid proof of engagement in handloom production (e.g., registration certificate, weaver’s identity card)?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Beneficiary type: Individual handloom weaver, self-help group, producer company, primary handloom weavers’ cooperative society, or other handloom agency  
- [ ] Engaged in handloom production  
- [ ] Recommended by State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles  
- [ ] Approved by Development Commissioner (Handlooms)  
- [ ] Possesses or willing to obtain Yarn Passbook for entitlement tracking  
- [ ] Has bank account details for DBT transfer  
- [ ] Submits Utilization Certificate (UC) for previous fund releases (if applicable)  
- [ ] Uses eligible yarn types: cotton hank, domestic silk, woollen, linen, or blended natural fibre yarn  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you may be eligible for the Raw Material Supply Scheme (RMSS) for Handlooms, which provides price and transport subsidies on yarn via Direct Benefit Transfer. Let me explain how this scheme can reduce your raw material costs and streamline your yarn supply.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Handloom weavers and agencies often face inconsistent yarn supply, high procurement costs, and drudgery in sourcing raw materials, which increases production costs and reduces competitiveness in domestic and export markets.

**Scheme intro:**
The Raw Material Supply Scheme (RMSS) for Handlooms, administered by the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms under the Ministry of Textiles, provides a 15% price subsidy and transport subsidy on yarn (cotton, silk, woollen, linen, or blended natural fibre) supplied through authorized depots. Subsidy is disbursed via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to beneficiaries’ bank accounts, with entitlements tracked through the Yarn Passbook system.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in handloom production as an individual weaver, SHG, producer company, or cooperative society  
- You have or can obtain recommendation from your State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles  
- You possess or can enroll in the Yarn Passbook system for tracking yarn entitlements  
- You have a bank account ready for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) of subsidies
