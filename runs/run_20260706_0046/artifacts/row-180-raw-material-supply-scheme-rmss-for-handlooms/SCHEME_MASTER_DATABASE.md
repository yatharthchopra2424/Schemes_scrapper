### Raw Material Supply Scheme (RMSS) for Handlooms
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms, Ministry of Textiles
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://handlooms.nic.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Handloom weavers, self-help groups, producer companies, primary handloom weavers’ cooperative societies, and other handloom agencies
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Handloom production
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be recommended by State Directorates of Handlooms and Textiles and approved by the Development Commissioner (Handlooms)

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Price subsidy and transport subsidy on yarn supplied
- Disbursement mode: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to bank accounts
- Duration of benefit: One-time per supply cycle (ongoing eligibility)

#### Documents Required
1. Application form forwarded by State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles
2. Yarn Passbook (for tracking entitlements)
3. Bank account details for DBT transfer
4. Utilization Certificate (UC) for previous fund releases (if applicable)
5. Proof of engagement in handloom production (e.g., registration, weaver’s identity card)
6. Recommendation from State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Eligible handloom agencies or weavers apply through the State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles.
2. The State Directorate forwards the proposal to the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms (DCHL) for approval.
3. Upon approval, the agency is enrolled in the Yarn Passbook system.
4. Yarn is supplied through authorized yarn depots at subsidized rates.
5. Subsidy amount is transferred directly to the beneficiary’s bank account via DBT.
6. Utilization Certificate (UC) must be submitted for further fund releases.
