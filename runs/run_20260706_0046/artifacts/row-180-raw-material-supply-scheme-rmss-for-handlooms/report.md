# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The Raw Material Supply Scheme (RMSS) for Handlooms is a subsidy scheme implemented pan-India by the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms (DCHL), Ministry of Textiles. The scheme operates on a rolling basis, with proposals accepted throughout the year as per State Directorate recommendations and DCHL approval cycles. It was last updated in 2024 and has a medium confidence level in data extraction.

### Objectives
- To ensure availability of raw materials at subsidized prices to handloom weavers  
- To reduce the cost of production and improve the competitiveness of handloom products  
- To streamline yarn supply through a structured distribution network of yarn depots and warehouses  
- To provide financial assistance via price and transport subsidies on yarn procurement  
- To enhance productivity and earnings of handloom weavers through assured raw material availability  
- To support handloom agencies and weavers in both cooperative and non-cooperative sectors  
- To promote inclusive growth by benefiting disadvantaged sections including SC/ST and women weavers

> **Key Caveat**: Only handloom agencies recommended by State Directorates and approved by DCHL are eligible. Benefits are subject to availability of funds and approval by the Development Commissioner (Handlooms).

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Benefit Type | Details | Mechanism |
|--------------|---------|-----------|
| Price Subsidy | 15% subsidy on yarn (cotton hank yarn, domestic silk, woollen, linen yarn, and blended yarn of natural fibres) with quantitative restrictions | Disbursed through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to linked bank accounts |
| Transport Subsidy | Freight reimbursement for transportation of yarn (all types) | Varies by region and yarn type (see table below) |
| Non-Financial Benefits | Assured yarn availability via 511 yarn depots and 46 warehouses; streamlined supply via Yarn Passbook and DBT; reduced drudgery in procuring raw materials | Yarn Passbook system tracks entitlements; yarn supplied through authorized depots |

**Transport Subsidy Rates (% of value of yarn supplied):**
| Yarn Type / Region | General States | NER and Hilly Areas |
|--------------------|----------------|---------------------|
| Other than Silk/Jute/Coir yarn | 2.5% | 7.5% |
| Silk yarn | 1% | 2.25% |
| Jute/Coir yarn | 10% | 10% |
| Depot operating charges (Service Charge to NHDC) | 2.0% (limited to Rs 15,000/- per month) | 2.5% |

**Scheme Achievements (as per evidence):**
- Total yarn supplied: 9139.88 lakh kg  
- Handloom weavers benefitted: 5.38 lakh  
- Functioning infrastructure: 511 yarn depots and 46 warehouses  
- Funds released to implementing agency (NHDC): Rs. 1,707.25 crore (cumulative)  

> **Important Note**: Subsidy is limited to yarn only and does not cover other raw materials like dyes or chemicals. All yarn supplied under the price subsidy component also receives transport subsidy.

**Required Documents:**
1. Application form forwarded by State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles  
2. Yarn Passbook (for tracking entitlements)  
3. Bank account details for DBT transfer  
4. Utilization Certificate (UC) for previous fund releases (if applicable)  
5. Proof of engagement in handloom production (e.g., registration, weaver’s identity card)  
6. Recommendation from State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles  

**Process Source**: Evidence confirms the 6-step application process involving State Directorate forwarding, DCHL approval, Yarn Passbook enrollment, subsidized yarn supply via depots, DBT subsidy transfer, and UC submission for further releases.

**Portal Reference**: Application portal URL is not explicitly mentioned in evidence, but the scheme is administered by Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms (DCHL), Ministry of Textiles. Key informational source: https://handlooms.nic.in
