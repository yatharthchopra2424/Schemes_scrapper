## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a registered handicraft artisan or craftsperson engaged in traditional handicraft activities  
- [ ] Artisan is registered with the Development Commissioner for Handicrafts (DCH) or State Handicrafts Corporation  
- [ ] Artisan possesses a valid Artisan Identity Card issued by the DCH or State Government  
- [ ] Artisan is actively practicing a recognized handicraft craft (e.g., weaving, pottery, metalwork, woodcraft, embroidery)  
- [ ] Artisan’s annual income from handicraft activities does not exceed ₹2,00,000 (as per scheme targeting economically weaker sections)  
- [ ] Artisan is not in receipt of similar benefits from other central or state government welfare schemes for the same purpose  
- [ ] Artisan is an Indian citizen and resident of India  
- [ ] Artisan is above 18 years of age  
- [ ] Artisan is not a defaulter of any government loan or scheme  

> *Note: Specific thresholds for turnover, employee count, or business age are not explicitly defined in the evidence. Eligibility is primarily based on artisan registration, craft practice, and income level. Verify with nodal officer for any updates.*

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN Card of the artisan  
- Aadhaar Card for identity and address verification  
- Bank passbook or statement (last 6 months) showing active account in artisan’s name  
- Udyam Registration Certificate (if applicable, though handicraft artisans may register under MSME or DCH)  
- Income Tax Return (ITR) for the last financial year (if applicable; exemption may apply for low-income artisans)  
- Recent passport-sized photographs (white background)  
- Proof of address (Aadhaar, voter ID, or utility bill)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Valid Artisan Identity Card issued by the Development Commissioner for Handicrafts (DCH) or State Handicrafts Corporation  
- Certificate of craft practice issued by a recognized handicrafts board, cooperative, or NGO affiliated with DCH  
- Declaration of annual income from handicraft activities (self-attested, Format as per scheme guidelines)  
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) from previous welfare scheme beneficiary (if applicable, to confirm no duplicate benefits)  
- Detailed project proposal or activity plan (if applying for skill upgradation, toolkit, or workshop support)  
- Photographs of the artisan engaged in their craft (for verification)  
- Recommendation letter from District Handicrafts Officer or State Handicrafts Directorate  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (minimum 300 DPI)  
- File names must follow format: `CaseID_DocumentType_Name.pdf` (e.g., `CASE001_ArtisanID_RameshKumar.pdf`)  
- Photographs must be clear, recent (within last 3 months), and show the artisan’s face and craft activity  
- Self-attested documents must include signature, date, and place of attestation  
- All documents must be in English or Hindi; vernacular documents require certified translation  
- File size per document must not exceed 5 MB  
- Bank statement must show consistent transactions related to handicraft income

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is **not typically required** for individual artisan welfare benefits under this scheme. However, if the application includes requests for skill upgradation, workshop setup, or toolkit procurement, a brief project note (2–3 pages) outlining:  
- Current craft practice and income  
- Proposed intervention (e.g., training, equipment)  
- Expected outcome (skill enhancement, income increase)  
- Budget breakdown  
may be required.  
**Verify with nodal officer** whether a DPR or project note is needed for your specific request under the scheme.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official application portal via the Ministry of Textiles website (verify current URL: **https://handicrafts.nic.in** or **https://textiles.gov.in**)  
2. Select “Handicrafts Artisan Comprehensive Welfare Scheme” from the list of active schemes  
3. Fill artisan personal details exactly as per Aadhaar and Artisan ID (name, DOB, address, mobile)  
4. Enter craft type, years of practice, and average monthly income from handicrafts  
5. Upload all scheme-specific and universal documents in the prescribed order and format  
6. Declare that you are not availing similar benefits from any other central/state scheme  
7. Self-attest the income declaration and craft practice certificate  
8. Preview all entries before final submission; ensure no blank mandatory fields  
9. Keep a copy of the submitted application and note the Application Reference Number  
10. Do not use third-party agents or pay fees for submission — the scheme is free of cost  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All personal details match Aadhaar and Artisan ID

- [ ] All uploaded documents are in PDF, under 5 MB, and clearly legible  
- [ ] Declarations of no duplicate benefits and Indian citizenship are signed  
- [ ] Application preview shows no errors or missing fields  
- [ ] Reference number and screenshot of submission saved in case file
