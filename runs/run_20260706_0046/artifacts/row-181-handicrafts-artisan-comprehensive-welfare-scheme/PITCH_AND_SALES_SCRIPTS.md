## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good [morning/afternoon], I’m reaching out because I specialise in helping artisans and handicraft businesses access government welfare schemes designed specifically for their sector. The Handicrafts Artisan Comprehensive Welfare Scheme supports individual artisans and craftspersons with financial assistance for livelihood, health, and skill development — and I’d like to see if you might qualify.

### Discovery Questions
- Are you an individual artisan or craftsperson actively engaged in handicraft production?
- How long have you been practicing your craft, and are you currently earning income from it?
- Do you belong to any recognised handicraft cluster, cooperative, or association?
- Have you previously applied for any government welfare or artisan support schemes?
- Are you facing challenges related to healthcare access, skill upgradation, or raw material costs?
- Do you have an Aadhaar-linked bank account and valid identity proof?
- Are you based in a rural or semi-urban area where handicrafts are a traditional livelihood?
- Are you interested in support for marketing, design development, or participation in exhibitions?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Individual artisan or craftsperson (not a company or NGO)
- [ ] Actively engaged in handicraft production as primary livelihood
- [ ] Possesses Aadhaar card and bank account linked to it
- [ ] Income primarily derived from handicraft activities
- [ ] May belong to a recognised handicraft cluster, cooperative, or association (preferred but not always mandatory)
- [ ] Resident of India (scheme is for Indian artisans)
- [ ] Not currently receiving duplicate benefits for the same purpose from another central scheme
- [ ] Willing to participate in skill upgradation or welfare activities if required

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you may be eligible for the Handicrafts Artisan Comprehensive Welfare Scheme — which provides direct financial support for livelihood, health, and skill development to individual artisans like yourself. Let me tell you how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Problem framing:
Many artisans struggle with irregular income, lack of access to healthcare, limited opportunities for skill upgradation, and difficulty accessing government support due to complex application processes or lack of awareness. This leaves them vulnerable to economic shocks and prevents them from growing their craft sustainably.

### Scheme intro:
The Handicrafts Artisan Comprehensive Welfare Scheme, administered by the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handicrafts under the Ministry of Textiles, provides financial assistance to individual artisans for livelihood support, medical emergencies, skill training, and participation in marketing events. Benefits include direct cash assistance (amount varies by component, e.g., ₹5,000–₹10,000 for livelihood support, up to ₹25,000 for medical aid, and subsidies for training and exhibitions).

### Why they qualify (personalised):
- You are an individual artisan actively engaged in handicraft production (confirmed in discovery)
- You have an Aadhaar-linked bank account, enabling direct benefit transfer
- Your primary income comes from handicraft work, meeting the livelihood criteria
- You may belong to a traditional handicraft cluster or community, which is often prioritised
