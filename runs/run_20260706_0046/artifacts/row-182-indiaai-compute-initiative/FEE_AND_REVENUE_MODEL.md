## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client.
2. Transparent fee structure with clear milestones for retainer and success fees.
3. Fees calibrated to the scheme’s risk profile: moderate risk due to undefined financial support amounts and lack of explicit eligibility criteria in evidence, though strong government backing and clear implementation agency reduce uncertainty.
4. Success fees reflect the effort required to navigate application processes where procedural details are not fully specified in public sources.
