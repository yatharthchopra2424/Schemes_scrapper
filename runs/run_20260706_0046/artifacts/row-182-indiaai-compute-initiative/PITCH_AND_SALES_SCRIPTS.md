# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I’m [Your Name] from [Your Firm]. We specialize in helping AI-focused startups, researchers, enterprises, and academic institutions access government-backed compute infrastructure and resources. The IndiaAI Compute Initiative is designed to democratize AI innovation by providing scalable computing access—so I’d love to understand your current AI workloads and infrastructure challenges to see if this initiative aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of AI workloads are you currently running or planning (e.g., LLM training, computer vision, healthcare AI)?  
2. Are you facing limitations in accessing GPU compute for model development or experimentation?  
3. Is your organization based in India and engaged in AI research, development, or deployment?  
4. Are you looking for access to indigenous AI computing infrastructure to reduce reliance on imported cloud services?  
5. Do you collaborate with industry, academia, or government on AI projects that could benefit from shared compute resources?  
6. Are you developing AI models with a focus on social impact, ethical AI, or sector-specific applications (e.g., healthcare, agritech, fintech)?  
7. What is your timeline for scaling AI initiatives—are you in early experimentation or preparing for production deployment?  
8. Have you previously applied for government AI support programs, or is this your first exploration?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Target beneficiary: startup, researcher, enterprise, or academic institution  
- [ ] Engaged in AI innovation, research, or development  
- [ ] Based in India (Pan-India scope)  
- [ ] Seeking access to scalable AI computing infrastructure (GPUs, AI services)  
- [ ] Interested in indigenous AI model development or industry collaboration  
- [ ] Working on socially impactful or ethically aligned AI projects  
- [ ] Requires democratized access to high-performance computing for AI  
- [ ] Aligns with IndiaAI Mission objectives: building AI ecosystem, attracting talent, enabling collaboration  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re aiming to scale AI innovation but hit bottlenecks in compute access—exactly what the **IndiaAI Compute Initiative** was built to solve. Let me walk you through how it provides access to 10,000+ GPUs via public-private partnerships to support your AI development at no direct cost.
