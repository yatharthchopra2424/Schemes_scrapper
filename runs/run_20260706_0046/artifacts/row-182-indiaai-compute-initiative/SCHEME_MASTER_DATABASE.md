### IndiaAI Compute Initiative
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** IndiaAI Mission under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://indiaai.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: startups; researchers; enterprises; academic institutions
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: AI ecosystem (broad, including healthcare, fintech, edutech, agritech, cybersecurity, etc.)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be based in India; contributions must be India-specific, non-personal, and anonymized datasets or AI artefacts (models and use cases)

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Access to scalable AI computing infrastructure (10,000+ GPUs), AI services and resources, support for indigenous AI model development, facilitation of industry collaboration and innovation in the AI ecosystem
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (access to compute resources and services via platform)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing (as long as eligibility and compliance are maintained)

#### Documents Required
1. Valid and verifiable legal status (Registered Society, Corporate Body, Statutory Body, Registered Company, etc.)
2. Designation of at least one authorized representative (Administrative SPOC)
3. Complete and accurate metadata for submitted datasets or AI artefacts
4. Licensing terms for submitted data or models
5. Compliance with Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), National Data Sharing and Accessibility Policy (NDSAP), and other applicable Government of India data sharing, privacy, and security laws
6. Adherence to AIKosh Terms of Use and Privacy Policy
7. India-specific content or artifacts in submitted datasets
8. Non-personal, anonymized data (for dataset contributions)
9. Original, non-infringing AI models and documented use cases (for AI artefact contributions)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register on the AIKosh platform using the designated onboarding process for private entities (including startups, companies, MSMEs, academic institutions, research organizations, thinktanks, non-profits, etc.)
2. Designate an authorized representative (Administrative SPOC) responsible for managing submissions, metadata maintenance, and contributor management
3. Upload non-personal, India-specific datasets or AI artefacts (models and use cases) ensuring metadata completeness and compliance with platform guidelines
4. Submit datasets or artefacts for backend validation to ensure alignment with AIKosh’s data quality standards and compliance guidelines
5. Upon approval, datasets or artefacts are published on the AIKosh platform
6. For additional support or clarifications, contact support@indiaai.gov.in
