## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit delivered under the Cyber Security Centre of Excellence (CCoE) scheme, which provides grants, seed funding, or subsidies for prototype development, proof-of-concept, and scaling of cybersecurity innovations, with disbursement tied to project milestones.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s rolling application basis and milestone-dependent disbursement, introducing moderate execution risk due to dependency on expert committee review, presentation/interview stages, and fund availability.
3. **Market Benchmarking**: Tiered success fees (6–8% for Growth tier) align with typical consulting rates for MSME/startup-focused government innovation schemes in India, considering the scheme’s pan-India scope and MeitY implementation.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The CCoE scheme’s rolling basis and milestone-linked disbursement create a moderate revenue lag; success is contingent on technical proposal quality and alignment with cybersecurity innovation priorities, as evidenced by the requirement for detailed project proposals, technical specifications, and expert committee review.
