# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping cybersecurity startups, MSMEs, academic institutions, and research organizations access government support for innovation. The Cyber Security Centre of Excellence (CCoE) by MeitY is designed specifically to strengthen India’s cybersecurity ecosystem through funding, infrastructure, and collaboration—so I’d like to understand if this aligns with your current work in cybersecurity innovation.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your organization’s incorporation date and registration status in India?  
2. Are you currently working on cybersecurity innovation, research, or solution development?  
3. Do you fall under the category of a startup, MSME, academic institution, or research organization?  
4. What stage is your cybersecurity project at—idea, prototype, proof-of-concept, or scaling?  
5. Are you seeking support for prototype development, proof-of-concept, or scaling of your cybersecurity innovation?  
6. Do you have a project proposal outlining objectives, methodology, and expected outcomes?  
7. Are you open to collaboration with government and defense sectors or participation in cybersecurity drills and exercises?  
8. Do you have access to the required documents such as Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, GST details, and bank account information?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity is registered in India  
- [ ] Works in the cybersecurity domain (innovation, research, or solution development)  
- [ ] Is an Indian startup, MSME, academic institution, or research organization  
- [ ] Has a project proposal detailing objectives, methodology, and expected outcomes  
- [ ] Possesses Certificate of Incorporation or Registration  
- [ ] Has PAN and GST details  
- [ ] Has technical specifications or proof of concept (if applicable)  
- [ ] Has bank account details for financial transactions  
- [ ] Can provide an authorized signatory letter  
- [ ] Has details of existing funding or partnerships (if applicable)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your organization may be eligible for the Cyber Security Centre of Excellence (CCoE) scheme by MeitY, which offers financial support through grants, seed funding, or subsidies for prototype development, proof-of-concept, and scaling of cybersecurity innovations—let me walk you through how it works.
