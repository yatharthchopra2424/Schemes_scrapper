## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-based pricing**: Fees are aligned with the tangible outcomes delivered — access to incubation, mentorship, investor connects, and prototype support — which collectively enhance startup valuation and funding readiness.
2. **Risk-adjusted success fees**: Success fees reflect the moderate risk profile of this scheme; while rolling applications and structured support improve odds, final selection depends on committee evaluation and performance review, introducing variability in outcomes.
3. **Transparency**: All fees and payment terms are disclosed upfront; retainers cover advisory workload (eligibility assessment, documentation, portal navigation), while success fees are contingent on measurable outcomes like grant access or funding connections.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: The MeitY Startup Hub operates on a rolling basis with no fixed deadlines, reducing time-bound pressure. However, the multi-stage selection process (application review, committee evaluation, onboarding) and periodic performance assessments mean results typically materialize over 3–6 months. This medium processing lag justifies a 3-month approval lag in revenue projections and supports moderate success fee ranges (4–8%) rather than premium rates.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Advisory services for MeitY Startup Hub (MSH) application support — including eligibility assessment, document preparation (Certificate of Incorporation, business plan, technology focus details, PAN, authorization letter, funding details), online application submission via https://msh.meity.gov.in, and liaison with selection committee during review phase.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for successful onboarding into MeitY Startup Hub (MSH), implemented by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), resulting in access to incubation facilities, mentorship, investor connect, prototype development, testing/certification support, and regulatory compliance guidance.
