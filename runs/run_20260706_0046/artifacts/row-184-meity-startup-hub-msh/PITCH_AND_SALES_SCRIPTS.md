## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I’m reaching out because I specialise in helping technology startups in electronics, IT, AI, IoT, blockchain, and other emerging technologies access government-backed incubation support through the MeitY Startup Hub (MSH). Many founders I speak with are focused on product development but may not be aware of the infrastructure, mentorship, and investor connect available through this Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology initiative. I’d like to understand your startup’s current stage and goals to see if MSH could be a strategic fit for you.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated in India?  
2. What specific technology domain are you working in (e.g., electronics, IT, AI, IoT, blockchain, or other emerging technologies)?  
3. Are you currently seeking support?  
4. Do you have a clear business plan or project proposal outlining your innovation and scalability potential?  
5. Are you looking for mentorship from industry experts or guidance on regulatory compliance?  
6. Have you already received any funding or support (e.g., seed grants, angel investment), and if so, what kind?  
7. Are you based anywhere in India, and are you open to accessing pan-India incubation facilities or virtual support?  
8. What are your immediate goals over the next 6–12 months — prototype development, investor readiness, market linkages, or scaling?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup is incorporated in India  
- [ ] Working in technology domains: electronics, IT, AI, IoT, blockchain, or other emerging technologies  
- [ ] Focused on innovation and scalability  
- [ ] Has a business plan or project proposal  
- [ ] Possesses PAN of the entity  
- [ ] Can provide authorization letter from authorized signatory  
- [ ] Willing to disclose details of existing funding or support received  
- [ ] Operates within the pan-India geographic scope (no location restrictions)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your startup is working on [mention specific tech domain from response] and is incorporated in India — which aligns closely with the MeitY Startup Hub’s focus on early-stage technology startups. MSH provides access to incubation facilities, mentorship, investor connects, and prototype support — all at no direct cost — to help startups like yours de-risk innovation and accelerate growth. Would you like me to walk you through how it works and what’s involved?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Early-stage technology startups often struggle to access reliable incubation infrastructure, expert mentorship, and investor networks — especially in deep-tech domains like AI, IoT, or electronics. Without structured support, prototyping delays, regulatory hurdles, and limited industry connect can slow down innovation and market readiness, even for promising ideas.

**Scheme intro:**
The MeitY Startup Hub (MSH), administered by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), is a pan-India incubation scheme that supports technology-based startups in electronics, IT, AI, IoT, blockchain, and other emerging technologies. While it does not provide direct grants, it enables access to government funding pathways, seed funding connections, venture capital and angel investor networks, incubation facilities, mentorship, prototype development, testing and certification support, and regulatory guidance — all aimed at de-risking early-stage innovation.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your startup is incorporated in India and working in [insert tech domain from discovery, e.g., AI/IoT], which falls under MSH’s priority technology areas  
- You have a business plan or project proposal outlining your innovation — a key document required for application  
- You are seeking mentorship, investor connect, or prototype support — core benefits offered through MSH  
- Your focus on innovation and scalability matches MSH’s objective to promote high-potential, technology-driven startups

**Proof:**
"We've helped [N] similar businesses get approved for this. [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]"

**Next step:**
We offer a free, no-obligation eligibility check — you share your incorporation details and tech focus, and we’ll confirm if you meet MSH’s criteria. If eligible, we can support your application with a success-fee-only model: you only pay if we help you secure admission to the hub. There’s no upfront cost or retainer.
