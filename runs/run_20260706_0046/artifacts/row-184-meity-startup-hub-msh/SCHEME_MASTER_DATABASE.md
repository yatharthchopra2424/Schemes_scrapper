### MeitY Startup Hub (MSH)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Incubation
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://msh.meity.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Technology-based startups incorporated in India
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Electronics, IT, AI, IoT, blockchain, and other emerging technologies
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Focus on innovation and scalability

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Access to incubation facilities, mentorship from industry experts, networking opportunities, investor connect, prototype development support, testing and certification support, and guidance on regulatory compliance
- Disbursement mode: Non-monetary support (incubation, mentorship, networking)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing during incubation period (subject to performance review)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation
2. Business plan or project proposal
3. Details of technology or innovation focus
4. PAN of the entity
5. Authorization letter from authorized signatory
6. Details of any existing funding or support received

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the MeitY Startup Hub official website at https://msh.meity.gov.in
2. Review eligibility criteria and program details
3. Submit an online application through the portal
4. Provide required documents including business plan, incorporation details, and technology focus
5. Await review by the selection committee
6. If selected, complete onboarding formalities to access incubation support
