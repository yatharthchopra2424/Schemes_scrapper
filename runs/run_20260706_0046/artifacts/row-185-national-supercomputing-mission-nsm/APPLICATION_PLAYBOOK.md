# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is an academic institution (university, college, IIT, IISER, NIT, etc.)
- [ ] Applicant is an R&D institution (government or private research lab)
- [ ] Applicant is a key user department/ministry of the Government of India
- [ ] Applicant is affiliated with an academic or R&D institution participating in NSM (if individual researcher)
- [ ] Research problem is of national relevance (aligned with NSM objectives: AI, quantum phenomena, turbulence, catalyst design, flood prediction, urban modeling, etc.)
- [ ] Project requires high-performance computing (HPC) resources for simulations, modeling, or analysis
- [ ] Applicant agrees to acknowledge NSM support in all publications and outcomes
- [ ] Applicant understands that access is for academic and R&D use only, not commercial exploitation
- [ ] Applicant is prepared to submit outcomes and publications as part of mission deliverables
- [ ] Applicant has access to the National Knowledge Network (NKN) or can connect through affiliated institution

> **Note**: There are no turnover, employee count, or age-of-business thresholds. Eligibility is based on institutional type and research relevance.  
> **Sources**: [NSM Key Facts](https://nsmindia.in/about-nsm), [Applications](https://nsmindia.in/applications/), [About NSM](https://nsmindia.in/about-nsm)

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Institutional registration certificate
- PAN card of the institution
- Audited financial statements (last 2 years)
- Bank account details (cancelled cheque or bank statement)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable for R&D labs)
- Authorized signatory letter

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Project proposal detailing objectives, methodology, and national relevance
- Institutional affiliation proof (appointment letter, ID card, or letter from Head of Department)
- Scientific justification for HPC requirement (why regular computing is insufficient)
- Expected outcomes and deliverables (publications, software, models, decision support systems)
- Budget and resource plan (including estimated computing time required)
- Letter of support from the Head of Department or Registrar (for academic applicants)
- Undertaking to acknowledge NSM support in all outputs

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible scans (minimum 300 DPI)
- File names must match the document type (e.g., "Project_Proposal.pdf", "Institutional_Affiliation.pdf")
- PDF format preferred; file size < 10 MB per document
- All certificates must be valid and not expired
- Documents in regional languages must be accompanied by English translation (notarized)
- Undertakings and declarations must be signed and stamped by authorized signatory

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A formal Detailed Project Report (DPR) is **not required**. However, a comprehensive **project proposal** is mandatory and must include:  
- Title and abstract  
- Objectives and national relevance  
- Literature review and gap analysis  
- Methodology and HPC requirement justification  
- Work plan and timeline  
- Expected outcomes (publications, software, models, decision support systems)  
- Budget and resource plan (computing time, manpower, software)  
- References  

> **Note**: If applying through an institution, the proposal must be routed via institutional channels. Verify with the nodal officer or C-DAC support for any template requirements.  
> **Source**: [Application Process](https://nsmindia.in/applications/), [Key Facts](https://nsmindia.in/about-nsm)

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Access the portal**: Visit https://nsmindia.in and navigate to the "Applications" or "Access to PARAM Systems" section.  
2. **Institutional routing**: Applications must be submitted through the applicant’s academic or R&D institution; individual researchers cannot apply directly.  
3. **Complete all fields**: Fill in project title, PI details, institutional information, objectives, methodology, expected outcomes, and resource requirements.  
4. **Attach documents**: Upload all required documents (proposal, affiliation proof, justification, outcomes, budget, support letter, undertaking).  
5. **Declaration**: Tick the checkbox acknowledging that NSM support will be acknowledged in all publications and that the facility will be used for academic/R&D purposes only.  
6. **Review**: Double-check for spelling errors, mismatched names, and missing attachments before submission.  
7. **Submit and save**: Click submit, save the confirmation page, and note the application reference number.  
8. **Follow up**: Expect a review cycle of 4–8 weeks; monitor email for communication from NSM/C-DAC.  

> **Portal Note**: The NSM website (https://nsmindia.in) hosts application guidelines and links to the submission portal managed by C-DAC.  
> **Source**: [Applications](https://nsmindia.in/applications/), [Infrastructure](https://nsmindia.in/infrastructure/)

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Project proposal is complete with all sections: objectives, methodology, justification, outcomes, budget  
- [ ] National relevance of research is clearly articulated (aligned with NSM focus areas)  
- [ ] HPC requirement is justified (why local resources are insufficient)  
- [ ] Expected outcomes are specific and measurable (publications, software, decision support systems)  
- [ ] Budget includes realistic estimate of computing time (in core-hours or PF-days)  
- [ ] All documents are attached, legible, and correctly named  
- [ ] Undertaking to acknowledge NSM support is signed  
- [ ] Letter of support from HoD/Registrar is present (for academic applicants)  
- [ ] Authorized signatory has completed and signed the application form  
- [ ] No commercial use or profit motive is indicated in the proposal

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the institutional channel on the NSM portal (https://nsmindia.in)  
- Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing application reference number and timestamp  
- Save the reference number in the case file and set a calendar reminder for follow-up at 30 days  
- Store all submitted documents in a dedicated folder (digital and backup)  
- Inform the client of successful submission and share the reference number

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and save the approval letter (email or PDF) specifying allocated computing time and system (e.g., PARAM Shakti, 5000 core-hours)  
- [ ] Verify sanctioned amount/resources against the request in the proposal  
- [ ] Complete any required onboarding or training session (if notified by C-DAC)  
- [ ] Begin utilizing allocated HPC resources as per the approved work plan  
- [ ] Maintain usage logs and monitor consumption via the NSM user portal  
- [ ] Acknowledge NSM support in all preliminary reports, presentations, and internal reviews  
- [ ] Prepare for mid-term review (if applicable)  
- [ ] Upon completion, submit final outcomes: publications, software, models, decision support systems, and utilization certificate  
- [ ] Ensure all publications include the acknowledgment: "This work was supported by the National Supercomputing Mission (NSM), Government of India"  
- [ ] Submit utilization certificate and final report to NSM/C-DAC as per guidelines  
- [ ] Invoice client for success fee as per consultancy agreement  

> **Note**: There is no direct financial subsidy; the benefit is access to HPC resources. The "sanctioned amount" refers to allocated computing time.  
> **Source**: [Key Facts](https://nsmindia.in/about-nsm), [Applications](https://nsmindia.in/applications/), [Testimonials](https://nsmindia.in/testimonials/)
