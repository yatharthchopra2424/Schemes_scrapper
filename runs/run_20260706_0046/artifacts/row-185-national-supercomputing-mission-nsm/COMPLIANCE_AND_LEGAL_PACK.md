## 8D. Internal Data Handling Policy

**Data Storage:** All client data is stored in encrypted cloud storage with AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit.

**Access Control:** Access to client data is restricted to authorized personnel only, enforced through role-based access controls (RBAC) and multi-factor authentication (MFA).

**Device Policy:** Use of personal devices for accessing or storing client data is strictly prohibited. All work must be conducted on firm-issued, secured devices.

**Retention Period:** Client data is retained for a period of two (2) years post case closure, after which it is securely deleted in accordance with data protection regulations.

**Breach Notification:** In the event of a data breach, the firm will notify affected clients and relevant authorities within 24 hours of discovery, providing details of the breach and remedial actions taken.

**Audit Logs:** Comprehensive audit logs are maintained for all data access and modifications, reviewed monthly for anomalies.

**Scheme-specific note:** All documents submitted to Department of Science and Technology (DST) and Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) under National Supercomputing Mission (NSM) must be retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit purposes.
