## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the strategic value of HPC access — enabling high-impact research, publications, and indigenous technology development under NSM.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect moderate risk due to peer-reviewed proposal approval and competitive allocation of computing time; retainer covers proposal development and institutional liaison.
3. **Scheme-Specific Note**: NSM has a rolling application cycle with no fixed deadlines, but approval depends on scientific merit and national relevance — processing time averages 3–6 months based on review cycles, warranting a 3-month lag in revenue recognition for success fees.
4. **Client Success Focus**: Pricing incentivizes successful HPC allocation and outcome delivery (e.g., publications, software development), aligning consultant fees with tangible research outputs.
