# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping academic and R&D institutions access high-performance computing resources through government initiatives like the National Supercomputing Mission (NSM). Given your work in research and innovation, I wanted to explore whether NSM’s access to systems like PARAM Shakti and PARAM Rudra could accelerate your projects in AI, quantum simulations, or climate modeling—without the need for major capital investment.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your institution’s primary research focus—such as AI, quantum phenomena, turbulence, or urban modeling?
2. Are you currently affiliated with an academic or R&D institution that participates in the National Knowledge Network (NKN)?
3. Does your research require high-performance computing for simulations, modeling, or data analysis at a scale beyond your current infrastructure?
4. Is your project aligned with national relevance—such as flood prediction, weather forecasting, or indigenous technology development?
5. Have you previously used HPC facilities like those from C-DAC or IITs for your research?
6. Are you seeking to publish high-impact papers, train students, or develop indigenous HPC software?
7. Do you have a clear project proposal outlining objectives, methodology, and expected outcomes?
8. Are you looking for computing time allocation on systems like PARAM Shakti (1.66 PF) or PARAM Rudra (3 PF) for a defined period?

### Qualification Checklist
  - [ ] Academic or R&D institution
  - [ ] Affiliated with an institution participating in NSM via NKN
  - [ ] Research problem requires HPC resources (e.g., AI, quantum, turbulence, climate modeling)
  - [ ] Project of national relevance (aligned with NSM objectives: flood prediction, urban modeling, etc.)
  - [ ] Willing to acknowledge NSM support in publications and outcomes
  - [ ] Not seeking commercial exploitation of HPC resources
  - [ ] Has or can develop a project proposal with scientific justification, budget, and deliverables
  - [ ] Seeking access, not ownership, of supercomputing facilities

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your work in [specific area from discovery, e.g., AI research or flood modeling] aligns well with the National Supercomputing Mission’s goals. NSM provides access to advanced systems like PARAM Shakti and PARAM Rudra at no direct cost—enabling high-impact research without infrastructure investment. Let me share how this works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Researchers in AI, quantum science, climate modeling, and related fields often face bottlenecks due to limited access to high-performance computing (HPC) infrastructure. Building or maintaining such systems is capital-intensive and beyond the reach of many academic institutions, slowing down innovation, publication output, and student training in critical national priority areas.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Supercomputing Mission (NSM), jointly implemented by the Department of Science and Technology (DST) and Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), provides access to a national infrastructure of supercomputing systems—including PARAM Shakti (1.66 PF) at IIT Kharagpur and PARAM Rudra (3 PF) at multiple locations—through the National Knowledge Network (NKN). The mission, backed by a Rs. 4500 crore investment over seven years, enables researchers to run complex simulations for national relevance projects at no direct cost.
