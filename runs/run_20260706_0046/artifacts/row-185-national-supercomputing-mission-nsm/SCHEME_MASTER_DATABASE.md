### National Supercomputing Mission (NSM)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Science and Technology (DST) and Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nsmindia.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Academic and R&D institutions, key user departments/ministries
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Scientific and technological domains (AI, quantum phenomena, turbulence simulations, catalyst design, flood prediction, urban modeling, etc.)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be affiliated with an academic or R&D institution participating in NSM; project must be of national relevance; access limited to institutions and researchers with approved projects

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Access to high-performance computing systems (e.g., PARAM Shakti, PARAM Rudra) for research; financial support of Rs. 4500 crore over seven years
- Disbursement mode: Allocation of computing time on supercomputing systems
- Duration of benefit: Based on approved project duration and availability of computing resources

#### Documents Required
1. Project proposal detailing objectives and methodology
2. Institutional affiliation proof
3. Scientific justification for HPC requirement
4. Expected outcomes and deliverables
5. Budget and resource plan

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify research problem of national relevance requiring HPC resources.
2. Affiliate with an academic or R&D institution participating in NSM.
3. Submit proposal through institutional channels for access to NSM supercomputing facilities.
4. Proposal reviewed for scientific merit and alignment with NSM objectives.
5. Upon approval, allocate computing time on systems like PARAM Shakti or PARAM Rudra.
6. Utilize allocated resources for simulations, modeling, and analysis.
7. Submit outcomes and publications as part of mission deliverables.
