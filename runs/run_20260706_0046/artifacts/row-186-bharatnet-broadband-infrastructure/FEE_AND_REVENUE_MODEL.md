## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial project scoping, eligibility assessment, and documentation preparation.
2. Success fee is contingent on successful fund disbursement or milestone achievement under the scheme.
3. Fees are structured to align consultant incentives with client outcomes.
4. Scheme-specific note: BharatNet involves long implementation cycles (typically 12–24 months per phase) and multi-stakeholder coordination (state governments, BBNL, DoT), resulting in moderate execution risk but high certainty of fund release upon milestone verification.
