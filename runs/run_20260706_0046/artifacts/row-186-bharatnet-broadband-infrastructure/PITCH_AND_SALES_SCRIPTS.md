# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping government entities and implementing agencies access central infrastructure schemes like BharatNet Broadband Infrastructure. This scheme, led by Bharat Broadband Network Limited (BBNL) under the Department of Telecommunications, focuses on bringing high-speed broadband connectivity to Gram Panchayats across India to enable digital services like telemedicine, online education, and e-governance. Before we explore whether this is relevant to your work, I’d like to understand your current priorities and challenges around rural connectivity and digital inclusion.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your role in relation to Gram Panchayat development or digital infrastructure projects in your state?
2. Are you currently involved in identifying or proposing Gram Panchayats for broadband connectivity under central or state initiatives?
3. What stage is your proposal or project at — have you already submitted a technical feasibility report or financial plan to the Department of Telecommunications?
4. Do you have clarity on the right-of-way requirements and coordination needed with local authorities for laying optical fibre in your target areas?
5. Are you seeking financial support for setting up network infrastructure, or are you focused more on operational sustainability models post-deployment?
6. What is your timeline for implementation, and are you aligning with annual budget cycles for funding disbursement based on milestones?
7. Have you engaged with BBNL or the DoT for technical appraisal of your project proposal?
8. Are you preparing monitoring and maintenance plans to ensure service continuity after handover of network infrastructure?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Target beneficiary includes Gram Panchayats (primary unit for connectivity)
- [ ] Proposal is being submitted by state government or implementing agency
- [ ] Technical feasibility report is available or in preparation
- [ ] Financial plan and cost estimates are defined
- [ ] Right-of-way clearance documents are being arranged or obtained
- [ ] Implementation agency details (e.g., BBNL or SPV) are specified
- [ ] Monitoring and maintenance plan is part of the project design
- [ ] Project aligns with BharatNet objectives: e-governance, e-health, e-education, digital inclusion in rural areas
- [ ] Funding sought is for optical fibre laying, network infrastructure, or operational sustainability
- [ ] Client understands rolling basis approval tied to annual budget allocations

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re working on expanding broadband access in rural Gram Panchayats — exactly what the BharatNet Broadband Infrastructure scheme supports. If eligible, this scheme provides subsidy-based financial assistance for laying optical fibre and setting up infrastructure, disbursed upon milestone achievement. Shall I walk you through how it works and whether it aligns with your current initiative?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many Gram Panchayats and rural communities lack reliable high-speed broadband, limiting access to essential digital services such as telemedicine, online education, e-agriculture, and digital banking. This digital divide hinders socio-economic development and prevents effective delivery of government-to-citizen (G2C) and government-to-government (G2G) services in remote areas.

**Scheme intro:**
The BharatNet Broadband Infrastructure scheme, implemented by Bharat Broadband Network Limited (BBNL) under the Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications, provides financial subsidies for laying optical fibre, setting up network infrastructure, and ensuring operational sustainability. Funds are sourced from government budgetary support, the Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF), and state government contributions, disbursed to BBNL and implementing agencies based on milestone achievement.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are targeting Gram Panchayats as the primary unit for connectivity — the core eligibility criterion under BharatNet
- You are preparing or have a project proposal from the state government, which is required for application
- You are arranging technical feasibility reports and financial plans — both mandatory documents
- You are addressing right-of-way clearances and implementation agency details — key requirements for approval
