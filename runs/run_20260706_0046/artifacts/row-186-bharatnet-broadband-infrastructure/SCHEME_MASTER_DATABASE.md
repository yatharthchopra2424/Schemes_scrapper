### BharatNet Broadband Infrastructure
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Bharat Broadband Network Limited (BBNL), under the Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dot.gov.in/bharatnet
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Gram Panchayats (primary unit), government institutions
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Rural broadband infrastructure, digital services (telemedicine, online education, e-agriculture, digital banking)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Implementation carried out by BBNL; state governments and local bodies facilitate right-of-way and infrastructure sharing; central and state funding utilised based on defined cost-sharing model

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Subsidies for laying optical fibre, setting up network infrastructure, and operational sustainability; funded through government budgetary support, Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF), and state government contributions
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit to BBNL and implementing agencies based on milestone achievement
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing (operational sustainability support)

#### Documents Required
1. Project proposal from state government
2. Technical feasibility report
3. Financial plan and cost estimates
4. Right-of-way clearance documents
5. Implementation agency details
6. Monitoring and maintenance plan

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identification of Gram Panchayats for inclusion in the BharatNet project.
2. Submission of proposals by state governments or implementing agencies to the Department of Telecommunications.
3. Technical and financial appraisal of the project by BBNL and DoT.
4. Approval of the project and allocation of funds.
5. Execution of civil works, laying of optical fibre, and installation of equipment.
6. Testing, commissioning, and handover of network infrastructure.
7. Monitoring and maintenance for service continuity.
