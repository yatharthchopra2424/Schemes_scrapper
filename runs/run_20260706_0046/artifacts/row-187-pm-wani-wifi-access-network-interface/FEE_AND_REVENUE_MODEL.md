## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the revenue-generating potential of PM-WANI through user fees at Public Data Offices (PDOs).
2. Tiered success fees reflect the scheme’s lack of direct financial subsidy — revenue depends on user uptake and service usage, making outcomes variable and performance-linked.
3. Retainer covers advisory costs for navigating technical guidelines, registration, and compliance with Department of Telecommunications standards.
4. Scheme-specific note: PM-WANI has no guaranteed financial support; success is contingent on user adoption and infrastructure utilization, resulting in medium risk profile due to rolling approvals and dependency on market demand.
