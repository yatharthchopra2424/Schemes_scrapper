# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping startups, MSMEs, entrepreneurs, and small businesses expand their digital footprint through government-enabled Wi-Fi infrastructure under the PM-WANI scheme. Rather than pushing a solution, I’d like to understand your current connectivity challenges and growth goals to see if this initiative aligns with your plans for scaling internet access in underserved areas.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your business’s incorporation date, and are you classified as a startup, MSME, entrepreneur, or small business?  
2. Are you planning to set up or already operating Wi-Fi hotspots in public or semi-public spaces such as shops, cafes, or community centers?  
3. Do you currently have the technical capability to deploy and manage Wi-Fi access points, or would you need support in selecting compliant equipment?  
4. Are you registered on the PM-WANI portal (https://dot.gov.in/pm-wani) or familiar with the Department of Telecommunications’ guidelines for Public Data Offices (PDOs) or PDO aggregators?  
5. What is your expected user uptake or footfall at the location where you plan to deploy Wi-Fi services?  
6. Are you prepared to bear the cost of setting up and maintaining Wi-Fi infrastructure, as PM-WANI does not provide direct financial subsidies?  
7. Do you have the required documents ready, such as proof of identity, address, business registration, bank details, and technical specifications of your Wi-Fi equipment?  
8. Are you interested in generating revenue through user fees collected at your Public Data Office (PDO), with billing and settlement handled by a PDO aggregator?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you’re well-positioned to become a Public Data Office (PDO) or PDO aggregator under the **PM-WANI** scheme — a Department of Telecommunications initiative that enables revenue generation through user fees while expanding digital access across India. Let me walk you through how it works and why it could be a strong fit for your business.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many small businesses and entrepreneurs struggle to offer reliable Wi-Fi services due to complex licensing requirements, high setup costs, and lack of centralized billing systems. This limits their ability to monetize connectivity and contribute to digital inclusion in underserved areas.

**Scheme intro:**
The **PM-WANI (WiFi Access Network Interface)** scheme, administered by the **Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications**, enables entities to set up and operate Wi-Fi hotspots without individual licensing for each access point. It provides a centralized authentication and billing system through PDO aggregators, allowing revenue sharing for PDO owners. While it does **not** offer direct financial subsidies, it creates income opportunities through user fees collected at Public Data Offices (PDOs), with financial returns dependent on user adoption and service usage.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a startup, MSME, entrepreneur, or small business — explicitly eligible under PM-WANI  
- You plan to deploy Wi-Fi access points in public or semi-public spaces — aligned with the scheme’s objective to expand broadband access  
- You are willing to comply with technical guidelines and bear infrastructure costs — a core requirement for participation  
- You aim to generate revenue from user uptake — consistent with the scheme’s revenue-sharing model
