### PM-WANI (WiFi Access Network Interface)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dot.gov.in/pm-wani
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Any entity, including startups, MSMEs, entrepreneurs, or small businesses
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Not specified
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must comply with technical and operational guidelines issued by the Department of Telecommunications, including registration on the designated portal and adherence to security and service quality standards

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: No direct financial subsidy or grant; enables revenue generation through user fees collected at Public Data Offices (PDOs)
- Disbursement mode: Revenue sharing through PDO aggregators facilitating billing, settlement, and reimbursement processes
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing, dependent on user uptake and service usage

#### Documents Required
1. Proof of identity (Aadhaar, PAN, etc.)
2. Proof of address
3. Business registration certificate
4. Bank account details
5. Technical details of Wi-Fi equipment to be deployed

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the PM-WANI portal on the Department of Telecommunications website.
2. Register as a Public Data Office (PDO) or PDO aggregator by providing entity details.
3. Submit required documents including proof of identity, address, and business registration.
4. Await verification and approval from the designated authority.
5. Upon approval, deploy Wi-Fi access points and integrate with the PM-WANI ecosystem for authentication and service delivery.
