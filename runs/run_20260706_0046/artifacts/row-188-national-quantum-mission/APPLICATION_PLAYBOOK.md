# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Entity is engaged in developing R&D-intensive quantum technologies at Technology Readiness Levels (TRLs) 4 and above  
- [ ] Entity is a legal entity: company registered under Companies Act 2013, partnership under Indian Partnership Act 1932, or LLP under Limited Liability Partnership Act 2008  
- [ ] Entity is an industry player, startup (as defined by DPIIT Notification G.S.R. 127(E) dated 19 February 2019), research institution, or academia  
- [ ] Entity is not submitting proposals directly to DST (funding must be routed through Second-Level Fund Managers)  
- [ ] Entity has a project proposal detailing quantum technology innovation  
- [ ] Entity has a Technology Readiness Level (TRL) assessment  
- [ ] Entity has a business plan and financial projections  
- [ ] Entity has incorporation/registration certificate  
- [ ] Entity has PAN and bank account details  
- [ ] Entity has details of existing funding or partnerships  
- [ ] Entity has intellectual property information (if any)  
- [ ] Entity has team qualifications and expertise in quantum technologies  
- [ ] Entity has letters of support or collaboration (if applicable)  
- [ ] Entity has compliance documents as per DST and ANRF guidelines  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation/registration certificate  
- PAN card  
- Bank account details  
- Audited financial statements / ITR  
- GST registration certificate (if applicable)  
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs/startups)  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Project proposal detailing quantum technology innovation  
- Technology Readiness Level (TRL) assessment  
- Business plan and financial projections  
- Details of existing funding or partnerships  
- Intellectual property information (if any)  
- Team qualifications and expertise in quantum technologies  
- Letters of support or collaboration (if applicable)  
- Compliance documents as per DST and ANRF guidelines  

### Document Quality Check
- File size: Each document < 10 MB  
- File naming: Use format `SchemeName_EntityName_DocumentType_Date.pdf` (e.g., `NationalQuantumMission_ABCStartups_ProjectProposal_20250405.pdf`)  
- All documents must be legible, scanned in color if originally colored, and text-searchable where applicable  
- Certificates must be valid and not expired  
- Declarations and undertakings must be signed and stamped (if applicable)  
- Language: English or Hindi; if other language, provide certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is **not explicitly mandated** in the evidence, but a comprehensive project proposal is required. Standard sections should include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Problem Statement and Objectives  
- Technology Innovation and TRL Justification  
- Methodology and Work Plan  
- Budget Breakdown and Financial Projections  
- Implementation Timeline  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation  
- Expected Outcomes and Impact  
- Team Credentials  
- Collaboration Details (if any)  
- Sustainability and Commercialization Plan  

*Note: Verify with nodal officer (Dr. Jyoti Sharma) whether a formal DPR is required beyond the project proposal.*

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Do not apply directly to DST** – All applications must be channeled through designated Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs)  
2. Ensure the project targets **transformative R&D at TRL 4 and above** in quantum technologies  
3. Clearly state that financing requested does not exceed **50% of assessed project cost** (unless EGoS approval is sought)  
4. Explicitly mention that the entity understands **grants and short-term loans are not supported** under this scheme  
5. Highlight alignment with **strategic technology goals** (e.g., quantum computing, quantum-enabled digital agriculture, health, education)  
6. Provide accurate **TRL assessment** from a recognized body or self-assessment with justification  
7. Disclose all **existing funding sources and partnerships** to avoid duplication  
8. Ensure all documents are uploaded in the correct format via the SLFM portal (refer to https://rdifund.anrf.gov.in/)  
9. Retain a copy of the submitted application and reference number for follow-up  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All eligibility criteria verified and documented  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents collected and quality-checked  
- [ ] Project proposal is complete, coherent, and aligned with scheme objectives  
- [ ] TRL assessment is clearly presented and justified  
- [ ] Financial projections are realistic and match the requested funding (≤50% of project cost)  
- [ ] Declarations regarding non-acceptance of direct DST proposals and SLFM routing are included  
- [ ] Application form fields are fully filled with no blanks or placeholders  
- [ ] All documents are correctly attached and named  
- [ ] Applicant has confirmed understanding that grants/short-term loans are not available  
- [ ] Internal reviewer has signed off on completeness and accuracy

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application package exclusively through the designated **Second-Level Fund Manager (SLFM)** portal (access via https://rdifund.anrf.gov.in/)  
- Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing submission timestamp and reference number  
- Store the reference number securely in the case file  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days post-submission  
- Notify the client of successful submission with reference number and next steps

- Level 2: Nodal officer – Dr. Jyoti Sharma, Head, RDI Cell, DST at sharma[dot]jyoti[at]nic[dot]in

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and securely store the approval letter from the SLFM or ANRF  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount matches the approved request (≤50% of project cost, or EGoS-approved exception)  
- [ ] Confirm the mode of financing: long-term loan at low/nil interest rate, equity infusion, or contribution to Deep-Tech Fund of Funds  
- [ ] Prepare utilization certificate format as per DST/ANRF guidelines for future submission  
- [ ] Track milestone-based disbursement schedule as per agreement with SLFM  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with reporting requirements (technical and financial) as stipulated in the approval  
- [ ] Plan for technology transfer, patent filing, and commercialization support as per scheme benefits  
- [ ] Invoice success fee as per consultancy agreement (to be filled by consultant)  
- [ ] Maintain all records for audit and future reference
