## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-based pricing**: Fees aligned with the financial benefit delivered (up to 50% of project cost via long-term loans/equity infusion).
2. **Risk-adjusted success fee**: Success fee reflects the scheme’s moderate risk profile — funding is routed exclusively through Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs), with no direct industry proposals to DST, and disbursements subject to SLFM evaluation and EGoS approvals for exceptions.
3. **Retainer for engagement**: Covers initial eligibility assessment, SLFM identification, and proposal structuring — critical given the scheme’s requirement for SLFM intermediation and TRL 4+ threshold.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: The National Quantum Mission under the RDI Scheme has a rolling application basis with no fixed deadline, but processing time depends on SLFM due diligence and EGoS review, creating a revenue lag of ~3 months from application to approval.
