# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping quantum technology startups, research institutions, and industry players access strategic R&D funding through the National Quantum Mission. I’d like to understand your current quantum innovation work and see if this ₹1 lakh crore mission could support your goals—no obligation, just a diagnostic conversation.

### Discovery Questions
1. What specific quantum technology area are you developing (e.g., quantum computing, quantum sensing, quantum communication)?
2. What is your entity type—startup (DPIIT-recognized), research institution, academia, or industry player?
3. What is your current Technology Readiness Level (TRL) for the quantum project?
4. Are you seeking funding for transformative R&D at TRL 4 or above?
5. Do you have a detailed project proposal outlining your quantum innovation?
6. Have you identified potential Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs) you’d like to work with?
7. What percentage of your project cost are you looking to finance through external support?
8. Are you open to long-term loans at low or nil interest rates or equity infusion as financing modes?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity is a startup as per DPIIT Notification G.S.R. 127(E) dated 19 February 2019, or a research institution, academia, or industry player
- [ ] Engaged in developing R&D-intensive quantum technologies
- [ ] Technology Readiness Level (TRL) 4 or above
- [ ] Legal entity registered under Companies Act 2013, Indian Partnership Act 1932, or LLP Act 2008
- [ ] Project proposal, TRL assessment, business plan, incorporation certificate, PAN/bank details, existing funding/IP info, team qualifications, letters of support, and compliance documents are preparable
- [ ] Not submitting proposals directly to DST (understands routing via SLFMs)
- [ ] Aware that grants and short-term loans are not supported

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you’re working on a TRL 4+ quantum innovation and exploring strategic funding—exactly what the National Quantum Mission supports through long-term loans or equity infusion covering up to 50% of project cost. Let me share how this scheme works for beneficiaries like you.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Quantum technology startups and researchers face high capital costs and long development cycles for TRL 4+ projects, yet traditional financing avoids early-stage deep tech due to perceived risk, limiting India’s progress in strategic quantum domains like computing, sensing, and communication.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Quantum Mission, administered by the Department of Science and Technology (DST) under the Ministry of Science and Technology, is a ₹1 lakh crore transformative initiative under the Research, Development, and Innovation (RDI) Scheme. It provides financial support via long-term loans at low or nil interest rates and equity infusion (especially for startups), channeled through Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs) such as AIFs, DFIs, NBFCs, or FROs. Grants and short-term loans are not supported.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are developing quantum technology at TRL 4 or above (per your TRL assessment)
- Your entity is a qualified startup, research institution, academia, or industry player
- You are prepared to submit a project proposal with business plan, financials, and team expertise
- You understand funding flows exclusively through designated SLFMs, not direct to DST
