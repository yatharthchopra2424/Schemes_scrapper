### National Quantum Mission
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Science and Technology (DST), Ministry of Science & Technology), Ministry of Science and Technology
- **Portal /Science and Technology
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://rdifund.anrf.gov.inrf.gov.in/
-Open
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Industry players, startups, research institutions, academia
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Quantum technology development
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be engaged in developing R&D-intensive quantum technologies at Technology Readiness Levels (TRLs) 4 and above; startups as defined by DPIIT Notification G.S.R. 127(E) dated 19 February 2019; legal entities such as companies registered under the Companies Act 2013, partnerships under the Indian Partnership Act 1932, or limited liability partnerships under the Limited Liability Partnership Act 2008

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support through long-term loans at low or nil interest rates, equity infusion especially for startups, and contributions to the Deep-Tech Fund of Funds; financing can cover up to 50% of assessed project cost for transformative R&D projects at TRLs 4 and above, with exceptions possible via the Empowered Group of Secretaries (EGoS)
- Disbursement mode: Long-term loan at low or nil interest rates, equity infusion
- Duration of benefit: Not specified (implied project-based)

#### Documents Required
1. Project proposal detailing quantum technology innovation
2. Technology Readiness Level (TRL) assessment
3. Business plan and financial projections
4. Incorporation/registration certificate of the entity
5. PAN and bank account details
6. Details of existing funding or partnerships
7. Intellectual property information (if any)
8. Team qualifications and expertise in quantum technologies
9. Letters of support or collaboration (if applicable)
10. Compliance documents as per DST and ANRF guidelines

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Interested Second Level Fund Managers (SLFMs) express interest by contacting Dr Jyoti Sharma, Head, RDI Cell at sharma[dot]jyoti[at]nic[dot]in
2. Existing NBFCs planning to establish AIFs are encouraged to contact DST for potential participation
3. Industry stakeholders seeking funding must not submit proposals directly to DST
4. All disbursements are routed exclusively through designated SLFMs via separate advertisement
5. For more details, visit https://rdifund.anrf.gov.in/
6. Applications are evaluated based on project viability, innovation, and alignment with strategic technology goals
