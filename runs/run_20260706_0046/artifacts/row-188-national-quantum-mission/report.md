# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **National Quantum Mission** (Scheme ID: row-188) is a **grant-type** scheme under the **Electronics & IT** category, implemented pan-India by the **Department of Science and Technology (DST)**, Ministry of Science and Technology. It operates under the **Research, Development, and Innovation (RDI) Scheme** with a total outlay of **₹1 lakh crore** (₹100,000 crore). The scheme has **no fixed deadline** and accepts applications on a **rolling basis** throughout the year. The application portal is **https://rdifund.anrf.gov.in/**. The scheme was last updated in **2025**.

### Objectives
The mission aims to:
- Encourage private sector participation in high-impact R&D in quantum technologies
- Strengthen India's capabilities in strategic technologies including quantum computing
- Promote technological self-reliance and economic security in quantum domains
- Ensure flexibility in sectoral support for emerging quantum technologies
- Foster innovation in deep technologies such as quantum computing, robotics, and space
- Support development of quantum applications in agriculture, health, and education
- Advance digital economy initiatives including quantum-enabled digital agriculture
- Catalyze innovation in mission-critical areas vital for national development and global competitiveness

### Eligibility Matrix
| Eligibility Criteria | Details |
|----------------------|---------|
| **Eligible Entities** | Industry players, startups, research institutions, and academia engaged in quantum technology development |
| **Technology Readiness Level (TRL)** | Must be engaged in developing R&D-intensive quantum technologies at **TRLs 4 and above** |
| **Startup Definition** | Startups as defined by **DPIIT Notification G.S.R. 127(E) dated 19 February 2019** are eligible |
| **Legal Entity Status** | Entities must be legal entities such as:<br>- Companies registered under the Companies Act 2013<br>- Partnerships under the Indian Partnership Act 1932<br>- Limited liability partnerships under the Limited Liability Partnership Act 2008 |
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Startups, research institutions, academia, industry players |

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Support Type | Details |
|--------------|---------|
| **Modes of Financing** | Long-term loans at low or nil interest rates; Equity infusion (especially for startups); Contributions to the Deep-Tech Fund of Funds |
| **Exclusions** | **Grants and short-term loans are NOT supported** under the scheme |
| **Coverage** | Financing can cover **up to 50% of assessed project cost** for transformative R&D projects at TRLs 4 and above; exceptions possible via the **Empowered Group of Secretaries (EGoS)** |
| **Funding Channel** | Funds are channeled through **Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs)** such as Alternate Investment Funds (AIFs), Development Finance Institutions (DFIs), NBFCs, or Focused Research Organizations (FROs) |
| **Non-Financial Benefits** | Access to specialized infrastructure, mentorship, collaboration opportunities with research organizations, facilitation of technology transfer, support for patent filing, commercialization, and scaling of quantum innovations through SLFMs |

> **> Important Caveats**  
> > - Grants and short-term loans are not supported  
> > - Funding limited to up to 50% of assessed project cost unless approved by EGoS  
> > - Direct proposals from industry stakeholders to DST are **not accepted**  
> > - All disbursements must go through designated Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs)  
> > - The scheme is subject to the financial rules and governance structure of the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF)

### Application Process
The application process is **not direct** for industry stakeholders. All disbursements are routed exclusively through designated Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs). The process involves:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Industry Stakeholder/Startup/Research Institution] --> B{Seek Funding via SLFM}
    B --> C[Contact Designated SLFM<br/>(AIF, DFI, NBFC, FRO)]
    C --> D[SLFM Evaluates Project<br/>Based on Viability, Innovation, Alignment]
    D --> E[SLFM Submits Proposal to DST/ANRF]
    E --> F[DST/ANRF Approves Funding]
    F --> G[Disbursement via SLFM<br/>(Long-term Loan/Equity Infusion)]
    G --> H[Project Execution<br/>(TRL 4+ Quantum R&D)]
    H --> I[Monitoring & Reporting<br/>to SLFM & DST]

    style H fill:#bbf,stroke:#333
    style I fill:#bfb,stroke:#333
```

### Required Documents
1. Project proposal detailing quantum technology innovation  
2. Technology Readiness Level (TRL) assessment  
3. Business plan and financial projections  
4. Incorporation/registration certificate of the entity  
5. PAN and bank account details  
6. Details of existing funding or partnerships  
7. Intellectual property information (if any)  
8. Team qualifications and expertise in quantum technologies  
9. Letters of support or collaboration (if applicable)  
10. Compliance documents as per DST and ANRF guidelines  

### Financial Scale
- **Total Fund Size:** ₹1 lakh crore (₹100,000 crore)  
- **Per Project Funding:** Up to 50% of assessed project cost (with EGoS exceptions)  
- **Financial Support Mechanism:** Long-term loans at low/nil interest, equity infusion (startups), Deep-Tech Fund of Funds contributions  

### Implementation Structure
- **Special Purpose Fund (SPF):** Being set up under the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF) as first-level custodian  
- **Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs):** May include AIFs, DFIs, NBFCs, or FROs such as TDB, BIRAC, IIT Research Parks, or similar entities  
- **Governance:** Subject to financial rules and governance structure of ANRF
