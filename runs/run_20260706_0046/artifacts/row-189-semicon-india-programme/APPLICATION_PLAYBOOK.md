## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] **Registration Type**: Entity must be registered under Companies Act 2013 or earlier, or as a partnership firm or LLP  
- [ ] **Sector Alignment**: Proposed activity must fall under one of the following:  
  - Semiconductor wafer fabrication facility (semiconductor fab)  
  - Display panel fabrication facility (display fab)  
  - Compound semiconductor, silicon photonics, sensors, or ATMP/OSAT facility  
  - Semiconductor design (ICs, chipsets, SoCs, systems & IP cores) or design-linked deployment (IoT, automotive, aerospace, defense, communication, industrial automation, medical devices, etc.) – applicable only for Design Linked Incentive (DLI) Scheme  
- [ ] **Technical Capability**: Entity must demonstrate necessary technical capability to implement the proposed project  
- [ ] **Financial Capability**: Entity must demonstrate necessary financial capability to implement the proposed project  
- [ ] **Land Acquisition**: Land for the project must be acquired or possessed within specified timelines (as per scheme notification)  
- [ ] **Environmental & Statutory Clearances**: Environmental clearance documents (if applicable) and clearances from relevant authorities (pollution control, fire safety, etc.) must be obtained or in process  
- [ ] **FDI Compliance (if applicable)**: Foreign investment proposals must comply with extant FDI policy and obtain required security clearances  
- [ ] **Scheme-Specific Caps (for DLI)**: For Product Design Linked Incentive under DLI Scheme, benefits capped at ₹15 Crore per application  
- [ ] **Deployment Linked Incentive (DLI)**: Limited to 5 years from approval date (if applying under DLI Scheme)  
- [ ] **Milestone Commitment**: Entity must commit to achieving project milestones for continued disbursement of incentives  

> **Portal Reference**: [https://semicon.gov.in](https://semicon.gov.in)  
> **Source**: Semicon India Programme Key Facts (Eligibility, Target Beneficiaries, Key Caveats)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Memorandum and Articles of Association / Partnership Deed / LLP Agreement  
- Board Resolution authorizing the application  
- Detailed Project Report (DPR)  
- Land documents (title deed, lease agreement, or allotment letter)  
- Projected financial statements for 5–10 years  
- Details of promoters and directors/partners (including PAN, address, background)  
- Technical details of proposed technology/process  
- Environmental clearance documents (if applicable)  
- Clearances from relevant authorities (pollution control, fire safety, etc.)  
- Bank appraisal report or letter of sanction for debt portion  
- Undertaking regarding compliance with scheme guidelines  
- **For DLI Scheme applicants**: Details of semiconductor design IP, proposed products, and deployment plans

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- **Required**: Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is mandatory for all applications under the Semicon India Programme.  
- **Standard Sections to Include**:  
  1. Executive Summary  
  2. Project Overview and Objectives  
  3. Technical Feasibility (technology selection, process flow, capacity)  
  4. Site Details and Land Acquisition Plan  
  5. Water and Power Requirements  
  6. Environmental Impact Assessment and Clearance Status  
  7. Financial Viability (project cost, funding structure, profitability projections for 5–10 years)  
  8. Employment Generation Plan (direct and indirect jobs)  
  9. Implementation Timeline (milestone-based Gantt chart)  
  10. Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
  11. Annexures: Promoter profiles, technical specifications, letters of intent, clearances  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Access the portal only via the official URL**: https://semicon.gov.in – do not use third-party links.  
2. **Register entity first** using PAN and incorporation details; verify OTP sent to registered email/phone.  
3. **Select the correct scheme component** (e.g., Semiconductor Fab, Display Fab, Compound Semiconductor, ATMP/OSAT, or DLI Scheme) based on proposed activity.  
4. **Fill all fields in sequence** – do not skip sections; the portal may lock incomplete sections.  
5. **Upload documents in prescribed order and format** – follow the checklist in Stage 2; ensure file names match the template.  
6. **Enter financials accurately** – project cost, debt/equity ratio, and promoter contribution must align with bank appraisal and DPR.  
7. **Declare milestones clearly** – use quarterly or semi-annual milestones tied to disbursement tranches (e.g., land acquisition, civil work, plant installation, trial production).  
8. **Review declarations** – ensure the undertaking regarding compliance with scheme guidelines is checked and digitally signed.  
9. **Preview before final submit** – use the portal’s preview function to catch errors.  
10. **Save application reference number** immediately after submission – it is critical for tracking.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the **approval letter** from India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) – check for sanctioned amount, scheme component, and validity period  
- [ ] Confirm the **sanctioned incentive amount** matches the approval (e.g., up to 50% of project cost for fabs, up to ₹15 Crore for DLI Product Design)  
- [ ] Sign the **Memorandum of Understanding (MoU)** or agreement with ISM within the stipulated time  
- [ ] Initiate project implementation as per the approved timeline and milestones  
- [ ] Submit **utilization certificates** and expenditure proofs for each tranche as per disbursement schedule  
- [ ] Ensure **milestone achievement** is verified and reported to trigger next incentive tranche  
- [ ] Maintain records for **audit and compliance** – all invoices, bank statements, and reports must be retained for minimum 5 years  
- [ ] Track **invoice success fee** as per consultancy agreement (to be confirmed with client)  
- [ ] Monitor for **clawback conditions** – ensure project is implemented as per approved plan to avoid incentive recovery  
- [ ] Plan for **post-completion reporting** – final utilization certificate and project completion report as required
