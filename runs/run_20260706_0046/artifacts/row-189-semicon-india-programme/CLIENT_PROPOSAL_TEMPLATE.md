## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client Legal Name]'s business profile, we have identified the Semicon India Programme — a subsidy scheme administered by India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is up to 50% of project cost for semiconductor/display fabs or up to ₹15 Crore per application under the Product Design Linked Incentive (DLI) Scheme.

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment and scheme selection under Semicon India Programme  
2. Preparation of Detailed Project Report (DPR) including technical feasibility, financial viability, and employment generation details  
3. Compilation and verification of all required documents as per scheme guidelines  
4. Online application submission through the designated portal: https://semicon.gov.in  
5. Liaison with India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) for application tracking and query resolution  
6. Support during technical evaluation by expert committee and financial appraisal  
7. Assistance in signing MoU/agreement and post-approval compliance for milestone-based disbursement

1. Documents as per the scheme checklist: Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, Board Resolution, Detailed Project Report (DPR), land documents, projected financial statements, promoter details, technical details, environmental and statutory clearances, bank appraisal report, undertaking of compliance, and for DLI Scheme: design IP and deployment plan details  
2. A dedicated point of contact from your team for coordination and document provision  
3. Timely responses within a 2-day SLA to keep the application process on track
