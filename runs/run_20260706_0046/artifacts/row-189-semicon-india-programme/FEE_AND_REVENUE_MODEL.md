## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial incentives available under the Semicon India Programme, with success fees calibrated to the magnitude of support (up to 50% of project cost for fabs, capped at ₹15 Crore for DLI Scheme).
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees account for the scheme’s competitive nature and rigorous multi-stage evaluation (technical, financial, Cabinet approval), which increases engagement risk despite high potential rewards.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainers are scaled to client capacity, ensuring MSMEs and startups in semiconductor design can access support, while larger fab projects warrant custom enterprise engagements.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Given the lengthy approval process (involving expert committees, financial appraisal, and Union Cabinet clearance) and milestone-based disbursement, success fees are structured with a 3-month lag from approval to receipt, reflecting real-world payment timelines observed in similar PLI schemes.
