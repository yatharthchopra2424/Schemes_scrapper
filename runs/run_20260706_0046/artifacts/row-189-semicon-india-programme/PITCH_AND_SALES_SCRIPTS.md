## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, [Client Name]. I specialise in helping semiconductor and electronics design companies access government incentives for setting up fabrication facilities or developing semiconductor IP in India. The Semicon India Programme, administered by the India Semiconductor Mission under MeitY, offers substantial financial support—up to 50% of project cost for fabs and up to ₹15 Crore for design-linked incentives—to strengthen domestic capabilities in this critical sector. I’d like to understand your current plans and see if this aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is the nature of your semiconductor or electronics-related activity—are you planning to set up a fabrication facility (wafer fab, display fab, compound semiconductor, sensor, ATMP/OSAT) or engaged in semiconductor design (ICs, chipsets, SoCs, IP cores)?
2. Have you incorporated your entity under the Companies Act 2013, or are you registered as a partnership firm or LLP?
3. What is the proposed location for your facility, and have you acquired or do you plan to acquire land for the project?
4. Do you have a Detailed Project Report (DPR) prepared, including technical feasibility, financial projections, and power/water requirements?
5. Are you seeking support for setting up a new facility, expanding an existing one, or deploying semiconductor designs in end-products like IoT, automotive, or medical devices?
6. What is your estimated project cost, and are you looking for capital expenditure support or incentives tied to sales/deployment?
7. Have you obtained or initiated applications for necessary statutory clearances (environmental, pollution control, fire safety)?
8. Are you looking for reimbursement-based incentives or milestone-linked payouts for verified expenditure?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Setting up semiconductor fabrication facilities or developing advanced semiconductor designs requires significant capital investment, with high risks due to long gestation periods, technological complexity, and global competition. Many Indian companies struggle to access affordable financing or incentives to offset these costs, limiting domestic value addition and increasing reliance on imports.

**Scheme intro:**
The Semicon India Programme, administered by the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), is a Pan-India subsidy scheme with a fund size of ₹76,000 Crore. It provides fiscal incentives as capital expenditure support: up to 50% of project cost for semiconductor and display fabs, up to 30% for compound semiconductors, silicon photonics, sensors, and ATMP/OSAT facilities, and under the Design Linked Incentive (DLI) Scheme, up to 50% of eligible expenditure (capped at ₹15 Crore per application) for product design, plus deployment-linked (up to 6% of net sales over 5 years) and facility-linked (up to 30% of capex for design infrastructure) incentives.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a domestic company/startup/MSME engaged in semiconductor design or fabrication activities  
- You are registered under the Companies Act 2013 or as a partnership/LLP  
- You propose to set up a facility in India (fab or design infrastructure) or deploy semiconductor designs in high-priority sectors  
- You have or are preparing a Detailed Project Report (DPR) with technical and financial details
