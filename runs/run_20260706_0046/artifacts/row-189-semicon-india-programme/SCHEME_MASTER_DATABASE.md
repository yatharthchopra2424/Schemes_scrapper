### Semicon India Programme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://semicon.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Applications are accepted periodically as per notification windows announced by India Semiconductor Mission; specific opening and closing dates are notified for each scheme under Semicon India Programme

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Companies registered under Companies Act 2013 or earlier, partnership firms, or LLPs
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Semiconductor manufacturing, display manufacturing, compound semiconductors, silicon photonics, sensors, ATMP/OSAT, semiconductor design (ICs, chipsets, SoCs, systems & IP cores), and semiconductor design linked deployment (IoT, automotive, aerospace, defense, communication, industrial automation, medical devices, etc.)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have necessary technical and financial capability to implement the project; land must be acquired or possessed within specified timelines; environmental and statutory clearances must be obtained; foreign investment proposals subject to FDI policy and security clearance

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - Up to 50% of project cost for semiconductor fabs and display fabs
  - Up to 30% of capital expenditure for compound semiconductors, silicon photonics, sensors, and ATMP/OSAT facilities
  - Under Design Linked Incentive (DLI) Scheme:
    - Product Design Linked Incentive: up to 50% of eligible expenditure, capped at ₹15 Crore per application
    - Deployment Linked Incentive: up to 6% of net sales turnover over 5 years
    - Facility Linked Incentive: up to 30% of capital expenditure for design infrastructure
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement against verified expenditure or periodic payouts based on achievement of milestones
- Duration of benefit: 
  - One-time capital support for fabs
  - Up to 5 years for Deployment Linked Incentive under DLI Scheme
  - Milestone-based tranches for other incentives

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation/Registration
2. Memorandum and Articles of Association/Partnership Deed/LLP Agreement
3. Board Resolution authorizing the application
4. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
5. Land documents (title deed, lease agreement, or allotment letter)
6. Projected financial statements for 5-10 years
7. Details of promoters and directors/partners
8. Technical details of proposed technology/process
9. Environmental clearance documents (if applicable)
10. Clearances from relevant authorities (pollution control, fire safety, etc.)
11. Bank appraisal report or letter of sanction for debt portion
12. Undertaking regarding compliance with scheme guidelines
13. For DLI Scheme: details of semiconductor design IP, proposed products, and deployment plans

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the relevant scheme under Semicon India Programme based on the proposed activity (semiconductor fab, display fab, compound semiconductor/silicon photonics/sensor/ATMP/OSAT facility, or semiconductor design)
2. Prepare detailed project report including technical feasibility, financial viability, land acquisition plan, water and power requirements, environmental clearance status, and employment generation details
3. Submit application online through the designated portal of the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM)
4. Application undergoes preliminary scrutiny for completeness and eligibility
5. Technical evaluation by expert committee constituted by ISM
6. Financial appraisal and due diligence
7. Recommendation for approval by Inter-Ministerial Committee
8. Final approval by the Union Cabinet or designated authority
9. Signing of Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) or agreement with India Semiconductor Mission
10. Disbursement of incentives in tranches based on achievement of project milestones and verification of expenditure
