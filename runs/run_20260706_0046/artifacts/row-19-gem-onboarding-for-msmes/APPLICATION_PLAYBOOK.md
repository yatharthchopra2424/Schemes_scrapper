# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Business is registered as an MSME (Udyam Registration required)
- [ ] Business is a seller/service provider seeking to participate in government procurement
- [ ] No specific turnover threshold mentioned for registration (open to all MSMEs)
- [ ] No specific employee count requirement mentioned
- [ ] Business operates in any sector (GeM covers all product and service categories)
- [ ] No age of business restriction mentioned (new and existing businesses eligible)
- [ ] Must have valid PAN and bank account for registration and payments
- [ ] Must comply with GeM's terms and conditions, including no bid rigging or cartelization
- [ ] Must update TReDS Exchange onboarding status in GeM profile (Profile -> TReDS) if applicable
- [ ] Must adhere to PPP-MII Order, 2017, DPIIT OMs, and MeitY's directions as applicable

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN Card of the business
- ITR (Income Tax Return) for last 2-3 years (if applicable based on turnover)
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration Certificate
- Business Registration Proof (Partnership deed, Incorporation certificate, etc.)
- Address Proof of business (Utility bill, Rent agreement, etc.)
- Cancelled cheque or bank passbook for bank account verification
- Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for e-signing documents on portal

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Udyam Registration Certificate (mandatory for MSME benefits on GeM)
- TReDS onboarding consent/status update (if seeking early payment via TReDS integration)
- Vendor Assessment (VA) / Vendor Assessment Exemption (VAE) documents (if applicable for OEMs)
- Undertaking for List of shareholders OR Undertaking from Statutory Auditor(s)/Director for BOD (if required for bid participation)
- GeM Sahay App registration (optional but recommended for mobile access)
- Authorization letter for representative (if applying through agent/consultant)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible scanned copies (PDF/JPEG format)
- File size per document should not exceed 5 MB unless specified otherwise
- Document names must match the content (e.g., "Udyam_Registration.pdf")
- All certificates must be valid and not expired
- Documents in regional languages must be accompanied by Hindi/English translation
- Bank account details must match across PAN, GST, Udyam, and bank statements
- DSC must be valid and registered on the GeM portal for e-signing
- Udyam Registration must reflect current status (verify via udyamregistration.gov.in)
