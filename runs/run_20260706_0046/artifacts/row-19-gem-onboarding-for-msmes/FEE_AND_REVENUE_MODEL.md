## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit derived from GeM onboarding for MSMEs.
2. Transparent fee structure with no hidden charges; retainer covers advisory and documentation, success fee tied to realized GMV.
3. Scalable model accommodating clients from micro-enterprises to large corporations utilizing GeM.
4. Scheme-specific note: GeM onboarding carries low financial risk due to zero registration cost and proven success in enabling MSMEs to access government procurement; however, revenue realization depends on client’s ability to secure orders post-onboarding, which varies by sector and product readiness. Processing time for order acquisition is not fixed but typically begins within 3–6 months post-registration, affecting revenue lag.
