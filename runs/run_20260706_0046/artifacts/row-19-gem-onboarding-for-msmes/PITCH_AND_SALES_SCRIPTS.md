# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping MSMEs access government procurement opportunities through the Government e Marketplace (GeM). Many small businesses struggle to connect with government buyers despite having quality products and services. I’d like to understand your current sales channels and explore whether GeM could open new avenues for your business to sell to government departments, PSUs, and other public sector entities across India.

### Discovery Questions
1. What products or services does your business currently offer, and to which customers do you primarily sell?
2. Are you currently registered as an MSME with a valid Udyam Registration certificate?
3. Have you previously attempted to sell to government departments or public sector undertakings (PSUs)?
4. What challenges have you faced in accessing government procurement opportunities, if any?
5. Are you looking to expand your customer base beyond private clients to include government buyers?
6. Do you have the capacity to fulfill larger orders, including delivery timelines of up to 730 days or more with approval?
7. Are you interested in participating in bid-based procurement processes such as Direct Purchase, L1, Bid/RA, or Forward Auction?
8. Have you heard of GeM or tried registering on the Government e Marketplace portal before?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is classified as an MSME (Micro, Small, or Medium Enterprise) with valid Udyam Registration  
- [ ] Business offers goods or services that can be supplied to government buyers (Central/State Govt, PSUs, etc.)  
- [ ] Business is willing to comply with GeM’s registration and transaction processes  
- [ ] Business has or can develop capacity to meet government delivery and quality standards  
- [ ] Business seeks to expand market access to government procurement opportunities  
- [ ] Business is not currently debarred or blacklisted from government procurement  
- [ ] Business operates in India and can invoice in INR  
- [ ] Business is open to online, paperless registration and transaction processes  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like GeM could be a strong fit for your business. The Government e Marketplace (GeM) is the national public procurement portal that connects MSMEs like yours directly with government buyers across India—offering access to thousands of tenders and procurement opportunities without any registration fees.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many MSMEs struggle to access government procurement due to complex bidding processes, lack of transparency, and difficulty reaching the right buyers. This limits their growth potential despite having competitive products and services.

**Scheme intro:**
The Government e Marketplace (GeM) is the National Public Procurement Portal operated by the Government of India. It provides MSMEs with free, online registration and access to government procurement opportunities across Central and State Government Ministries, Departments, PSUs, and affiliated organizations. There are no charges or fees for registration, and sellers gain access to over 10,584 product categories and 350 service categories.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are an MSME with valid Udyam Registration (as confirmed in discovery)  
- You offer goods or services that can be supplied to government buyers  
- You are seeking to expand your market reach to include public sector clients  
- You are willing to participate in transparent, online procurement processes
