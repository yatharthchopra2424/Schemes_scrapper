### GeM Onboarding for MSMEs
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Commerce, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://gem.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: MSMEs (Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises)
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: All sectors (goods and services)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be registered as an MSME (Udyam Registration); must comply with Public Procurement (Preference to Make in India) Order, 2017; must not engage in bid rigging, cartelisation, or collusion

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Access to national public procurement market; no registration or participation fees; access to GeM Exclusive Business Opportunities (e.g., Startup Runway, Womaniya, MSME SC/ST initiatives); integration with TReDS for faster financing; access to over 10,584 product categories and 350 service categories
- Disbursement mode: Direct access to government procurement opportunities (not a monetary benefit)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing as long as the seller remains active and compliant on the GeM portal

#### Documents Required
1. Udyam Registration Certificate
2. PAN Card of the business/entity
3. GSTIN (if applicable)
4. Bank account details (for PFMS verification)
5. Address proof of the business
6. Authorized signatory details (Aadhaar/PAN)
7. Certificate of Incorporation (for companies)
8. Partnership deed (for partnerships)
9. Proprietorship declaration (for sole proprietors)
10. Any other document as specified during registration process on GeM portal

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the GeM portal at https://gem.gov.in
2. Click on "Sign Up" and select "Seller"
3. Choose the type of business entity (e.g., Private Limited, Proprietorship, etc.)
4. Fill in the registration form with business details (name, address, PAN, GSTIN, etc.)
5. Provide Udyam Registration number and validate it
6. Enter bank account details and complete PFMS verification
7. Upload required documents (Udyam certificate, PAN, address proof, etc.)
8. Submit the application for verification
9. Await approval from GeM admin (typically within 2-3 working days)
10. Upon approval, log in to the seller dashboard and complete profile setup
11. Update TReDS onboarding status in profile (Profile → TReDS) to enable invoice discounting
12. Begin listing products/services and participating in bids
