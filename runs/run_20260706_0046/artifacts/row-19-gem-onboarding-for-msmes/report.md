# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
**Scheme Name:** GeM Onboarding for MSMEs  
**Scheme ID:** row-19  
**Ministry / Category:** Compliance & Registration  
**Scheme Type:** other  
**Confidence:** unknown  

**Portal URL:** https://gem.gov.in  

GeM (Government e-Marketplace) is the National Public Procurement Portal of the Government of India, designed to facilitate online procurement of common use goods and services required by various Government Departments / Organizations / PSUs. It aims to enhance transparency, efficiency, and speed in public procurement while providing a level playing field for suppliers, especially MSMEs.

### Objectives
- To create a unified, transparent, and efficient public procurement platform for government buyers and sellers.
- To drive inclusiveness and ease of doing business (EoDB) with the government, particularly for MSMEs, startups, and women entrepreneurs.
- To standardize procurement processes backed by regulatory frameworks (GFRs, PPP-MII Order, 2017).
- To enable a fully automated, technology-driven system for end-to-end procurement (requisition to payment).
- To promote digital inclusion and support the Prime Minister’s vision of Digital India.
- To integrate with TReDS exchanges for faster financing of MSME receivables.
- To ensure compliance with Make in India, Public Procurement (Preference to Make in India) Order, 2017, and related directives.
- To provide MSMEs access to national public procurement markets without registration fees.
- To offer special provisions and dedicated sections for startups, MSMEs, and Emporium products on the platform.
- To enable real-time visibility of bids, prices, and vendor details, ensuring transparency and reducing corruption.
- To empower small vendors by providing access to government markets across India.

### Application Process
The GeM onboarding process for MSMEs is entirely online, free, and self-service. Below is the detailed step-by-step flow.

#### Detailed Steps & Requirements
1.  **Portal Access:** Go to [https://gem.gov.in](https://gem.gov.in) and click "Sign Up" → Choose "Seller/Service Provider".
2.  **Basic Registration:** Provide name, valid email ID, mobile number, and create a secure password.
3.  **OTP Verification:** Verify email and mobile via One-Time Password (OTP) sent to the respective channels.
4.  **Profile Creation:**
    *   Enter legal business name.
    *   Select type of business entity (Proprietorship, Partnership, LLP, Private Limited Company, Public Limited Company, OPC, Trust, NGO, etc.).
    *   Provide complete registered office address.
    *   Enter PAN number (mandatory for tax compliance).
    *   Add primary bank account details (account number, IFSC, bank name) – **must be PFMS-verified** for transactions and TReDS integration.
5.  **Document Upload (Mandatory):**
    *   **PAN Card** (scanned copy).
    *   **Bank Proof:** Cancelled cheque or bank statement (not older than 3 months) showing account name, number, IFSC.
    *   **Address Proof:** Any one – Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License, or Utility Bill (not older than 3 months).
    *   **Udyam Registration Certificate** (Highly Recommended): To avail MSME-specific benefits like purchase preference, exemption from Earnest Money Deposit (EMD), and eligibility for TReDS financing. Without this, you register as a general seller.
6.  **System Validation:** GeM system auto-validates the uploaded documents against the entered details.
7.  **Profile Activation:** Upon successful validation, the seller profile is activated, and a unique **GeM Seller ID** is generated.
8.  **Post-Registration Actions:**
    *   **Update TReDS Status:** Navigate to Profile → TReDS section to declare onboarding status with TReDS exchanges (mandatory for CPSE sellers seeking early payment via bill discounting).
    *   **List Products/Services:** Upload catalogue with accurate descriptions, HSN/SAC codes, prices, images, etc.
    *   **Access Seller Dashboard:** Monitor orders, payments, pending invoices, bid participation, and performance metrics.
    *   **Opt-in for TReDS:** For MSME sellers, enable the TReDS option while generating invoices to receive early payment via bill discounting by financiers.
    *   **Attend Workshops (if applicable):** Prospective OEMs should look out for emails regarding Vendor Assessment (VA) Workshops (tentative dates: 24th Apr 2026, 15th May 2026, 19th Jun 2026, 17th Jul 2026) to understand VA/VAE process and documentation requirements.
9.  **Ongoing Compliance:**
    *   Keep Udyam Registration, bank details, and TReDS status updated.
    *   Adhere to GeM policies, GFRs, PPP-MII Order, 2017, and related OMs/circulars (available under Support → Government OMs Circulars).
    *   Participate in training sessions (regularly updated; check Training section on GeM portal).
    *   Respond promptly to grievances and compliance notices via the GeM Helpdesk (Toll Free: 1800-419-3436 / 1800-102-3436).

> **> Important Notes:**
> *   **No Fees:** Registration and basic usage are free. No charges for getting registered or participating in bids.
> *   **Bank Verification:** PFMS verification of bank account is critical for receiving government payments and using TReDS.
> *   **Udyam is Key:** While not mandatory for registration, a valid Udyam Registration is essential to unlock MSME-specific benefits on GeM.
> *   **TReDS Linkage:** Explicitly update TReDS onboarding status in your GeM profile (Profile → TReDS) to enable the invoice discounting feature.
> *   **Workshop Attendance:** VA workshops are critical for OEMs; details are sent to registered email IDs 2 weeks prior to the tentative date.
