## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

- [ ] Entity is a company registered in India under the Companies Act, 2013 or earlier

- [ ] Entity has audited financial statements for the last 2 years

- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration  
- PAN of the entity  
- GST registration details  
- Audited financial statements for the last 2 years  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (if MSME)

- Technical details of the electrolyser or green hydrogen plant  
- Land ownership or lease documents  
- Clearances from relevant authorities (if applicable)  
- Details of proposed investment and funding sources  
- Undertaking and declaration as per format prescribed by SECI  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible scans or certified copies  
- File names must match document type (e.g., "Certificate_of_Incorporation.pdf")  
- PDF file size must not exceed 10 MB per document  
- PAN, GSTIN, and company name must match exactly across all documents  
- Financial statements must be audited and signed by a Chartered Accountant  
- Technical documents must include capacity, efficiency, and specifications  
- Land documents must show clear title or valid lease agreement (minimum 10 years)  
- Undertaking must be on company letterhead and signed by authorized signatory

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is **required** for this scheme. Standard sections include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Objectives and Alignment with National Green Hydrogen Mission  
- Technology Selection and Process Flow Diagram  
- Plant Capacity and Annual Production Targets  
- Site Details (land, location, connectivity)  
- Equipment Specifications (electrolyser, balance of plant)  
- Implementation Schedule (Gantt chart)  
- Financial Projections (CAPEX, OPEX, funding structure)  
- Revenue Model and Incentive Calculation (based on INR 4,440/kW or INR 50/kg)  
- Environmental and Safety Compliance  
- Employment Generation Details  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
- Monitoring and Verification Protocol  
- Annexures: Technical datasheets, land documents, clearances, financial statements  

> **Note**: Verify DPR template and specific requirements with the nodal officer (SECI) as guidelines may vary by notification.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Register on the National Green Hydrogen Mission portal: [https://greenhydrogen.mnre.gov.in](https://greenhydrogen.mnre.gov.in)  
2. Select the correct application window under the SIGHT programme (electrolyser manufacturing or green hydrogen production)  
3. Fill all fields accurately — **do not leave any mandatory field blank**  
4. Ensure entity name, PAN, and GSTIN match exactly across form and documents  
5. Enter technical specifications (capacity, efficiency, technology type) as per supporting documents  
6. Declare investment amount and funding sources clearly (debt/equity ratio)  
7. Upload documents in prescribed format (PDF, <10 MB) with correct naming  
8. Read and accept the undertaking and declaration before final submission  
9. **Do not use special characters in text fields** unless explicitly allowed  
10. Preview the entire application before submission to avoid errors

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify Letter of Award (LoA) from SECI  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned incentive rate (INR 4,440 per kW for electrolyser or INR 50 per kg for green hydrogen)  
- [ ] Validate approval period (5 years for electrolyser manufacturing, 3 years for green hydrogen production)  
- [ ] Sign agreement with SECI as per LoA terms  
- [ ] Commission facility within approved timeline  
- [ ] Submit commissioning report and performance test results to SECI  
- [ ] Initiate monitoring and verification protocol as per SECI guidelines  
- [ ] Claim first disbursement based on verified production/capacity  
- [ ] Maintain records for annual verification and compliance  
- [ ] Prepare utilization certificate and financial reports for SECI audit  
- [ ] Invoice success fee as per consultancy agreement  
- [ ] Set reminders for annual performance review and incentive renewal (if applicable)  

> **Note**: Incentives are subject to annual caps and availability of funds. Beneficiaries must comply with monitoring and verification protocols. Failure to achieve project milestones may result in withdrawal of incentives. Domestic value addition requirements may apply for electrolyser manufacturing.
