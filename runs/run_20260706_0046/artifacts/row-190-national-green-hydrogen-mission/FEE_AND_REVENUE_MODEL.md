## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the scheme’s substantial per-unit incentives (up to ₹4,440/kW for electrolysers or ₹50/kg for green hydrogen), ensuring consultant compensation reflects the scale of financial benefit secured.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s annual fund availability is subject to periodic notifications and annual caps, and the technical/financial rigor of SECI evaluation, success fees reflect moderate execution risk.
3. **Tiered Access**: Mid-market and large enterprises are best positioned to meet eligibility (incorporated Indian entities, audited financials, technical/land readiness) and scale requirements for meaningful incentive capture.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The National Green Hydrogen Mission involves multi-year incentive disbursement (5 years for electrolyser manufacturing, 3 years for production) post-commissioning, introducing revenue lag and monitoring risk; success fees are structured to align with milestone-based disbursement verification.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Retainer for consulting services related to the National Green Hydrogen Mission (SIGHT programme), including eligibility assessment, application preparation, document compilation, and submission support via the SECI portal (https://greenhydrogen.mnre.gov.in).

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for securing financial incentives under the National Green Hydrogen Mission, implemented by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) through the Solar Energy Corporation of India (SECI), for approved electrolyser manufacturing or green hydrogen production projects.
