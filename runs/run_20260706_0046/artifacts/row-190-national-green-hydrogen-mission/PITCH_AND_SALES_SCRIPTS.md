## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping manufacturers and energy producers access government incentives for green hydrogen and electrolyser projects under the National Green Hydrogen Mission. Many companies in this space are exploring decarbonisation but aren’t aware of the direct subsidies available for setting up production or manufacturing facilities. I’d like to understand your current plans and see if this scheme could support your goals.

### Discovery Questions
- Are you planning to set up a green hydrogen production facility or an electrolyser manufacturing unit in India?
- What is your proposed capacity for green hydrogen production (in tonnes per day) or electrolyser manufacturing (in kW/year)?
- Has your company been incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 or earlier, or are you a central/state PSU, joint venture, or consortium?
- Do you have land secured or identified for the project, along with any clearances from relevant authorities?
- Are you preparing technical specifications and funding details for your electrolyser or green hydrogen plant?
- Have you reviewed the SIGHT programme guidelines issued by MNRE and SECI for eligibility and incentive structure?
- What is your timeline for project commissioning, and are you seeking financial support for 3–5 years of production-linked incentives?
- Are you currently audited and able to provide financial statements for the last two years?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity is a company registered in India under Companies Act, 2013 or earlier, or a central/state PSU, joint venture, or consortium  
- [ ] Engaged in or planning electrolyser manufacturing or green hydrogen production  
- [ ] Meets technical and financial criteria as per SIGHT programme guidelines  
- [ ] Has or can secure land and necessary statutory clearances  
- [ ] Can provide audited financial statements for last 2 years  
- [ ] Able to submit technical details of electrolyser or green hydrogen plant  
- [ ] Prepared to comply with monitoring, verification, and domestic value addition (if applicable)  
- [ ] Applying during an open application window as notified by SECI  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like your green hydrogen or electrolyser project may align well with the National Green Hydrogen Mission’s SIGHT programme, which offers direct subsidies of up to INR 50 per kg for green hydrogen production or INR 4,440 per kW for electrolyser manufacturing. Let me share how this works and whether it’s a fit for your plans.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Manufacturers and energy producers face high upfront and operational costs when setting up green hydrogen or electrolyser facilities, which can delay or deter investment despite long-term decarbonisation goals and market opportunities.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Green Hydrogen Mission, implemented by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) through the Solar Energy Corporation of India (SECI), provides financial incentives under the SIGHT programme. Eligible entities receive direct subsidies: up to INR 50 per kg of green hydrogen produced for 3 years, or up to INR 4,440 per kW of electrolyser manufacturing capacity for 5 years, subject to annual caps and fund availability.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
