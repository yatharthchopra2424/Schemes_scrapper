### National Green Hydrogen Mission
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), implemented through Solar Energy Corporation of India (SECI)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://greenhydrogen.mnre.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Applications are invited periodically through notifications issued by SECI; specific windows are announced in scheme guidelines. Not rolling basis.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Companies registered in India under the Companies Act, 2013 or earlier, central/public sector undertakings, state government entities, joint ventures, and consortiums
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Electrolyser manufacturing, green hydrogen production, pilot projects in end-use sectors (mobility, shipping, steel), research and development
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: For the SIGHT programme, eligibility is open to manufacturers of electrolysers and producers of green hydrogen who meet technical and financial criteria as specified in the scheme guidelines issued by MNRE and SECI

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - For electrolyser manufacturing: up to INR 4,440 per kW for a period of 5 years
  - For green hydrogen production: up to INR 50 per kg for a period of 3 years
  - Subject to annual caps
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (funds disbursed through SECI as the implementing agency)
- Duration of benefit: 
  - Electrolyser manufacturing: 5 years
  - Green hydrogen production: 3 years

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. GST registration details
4. Audited financial statements for the last 2 years
5. Technical details of the electrolyser or green hydrogen plant
6. Land ownership or lease documents
7. Clearances from relevant authorities (if applicable)
8. Details of proposed investment and funding sources
9. Undertaking and declaration as per format prescribed by SECI

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Eligible entities must register on the National Green Hydrogen Mission portal managed by SECI.
2. Submit an online application under the SIGHT programme for either electrolyser manufacturing or green hydrogen production.
3. Provide required documents including company incorporation details, technical specifications, financial statements, and land/clearance proofs.
4. Applications are evaluated by SECI based on technical and financial criteria.
5. Selected applicants receive a Letter of Award (LoA) and must sign an agreement with SECI.
6. After commissioning of the facility, incentives are disbursed based on verified performance.
