## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **National Green Hydrogen Mission** (Scheme ID: row-190) is a **subsidy**-type initiative under the **Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE)**, implemented through the **Solar Energy Corporation of India (SECI)**. It operates with **Pan-India** geographic scope and aims to position India as a global leader in green hydrogen production and export. The mission is backed by a substantial fund size of **INR 19,744 crore** and was last updated in **2024**. Applications are **not rolling**; they are invited periodically through notifications issued by SECI, with specific windows announced in scheme guidelines.

### Objectives
The mission has clearly defined, time-bound objectives to be achieved by **2030**:
- Make India a leading producer and supplier of green hydrogen globally.
- Achieve annual green hydrogen production capacity of **at least 5 Million Metric Tonnes (MMT)**.
- Develop electrolyser manufacturing capacity of **at least 15 GW per year**.
- Attract over **INR 8 lakh crore** in cumulative investments.
- Create over **6 lakh (600,000)** jobs.
- Reduce cumulative CO₂ emissions by approximately **50 MMT per annum**.
- Develop enabling infrastructure (storage, transportation, refuelling networks).
- Support research, development, and demonstration (RD&D) projects for technology innovation.

### Eligibility Matrix
Eligibility is open to a broad range of entities, with specific criteria for the **Strategic Interventions for Green Hydrogen Transition (SIGHT)** programme.

| Eligible Entity Type | Details |
|----------------------|--------|
| Companies registered in India | Under the Companies Act, 2013 or earlier |
| Central/Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) | Eligible to apply |
| State Government Entities | Eligible to apply |
| Joint Ventures | Permitted structure |
| Consortiums | Allowed to participate |
| **SIGHT Programme Specific** | Manufacturers of electrolysers and producers of green hydrogen meeting technical & financial criteria per MNRE/SECI guidelines |

**Target Beneficiaries**: Manufacturers, industries, research institutions, startups, and MSMEs.

> **Key Caveats on Eligibility**  
> - Incentives are subject to **annual caps** and **availability of funds**.  
> - Beneficiaries must comply with **monitoring and verification protocols**.  
> - Failure to achieve project milestones may result in **withdrawal of incentives**.  
> - **Domestic value addition requirements** may apply for electrolyser manufacturing.

### Benefits & Financial Support
Financial incentives are delivered via the **SIGHT programme** as **direct subsidies** for two primary activities:

#### Electrolyser Manufacturing Incentive
| Parameter | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Incentive Rate | Up to **INR 4,440 per kW** |
| Duration | **5 years** |
| Disbursing Agency | Solar Energy Corporation of India (SECI) |
| Basis | Verified performance post-commissioning |
| Caps | Subject to annual fund availability |

#### Green Hydrogen Production Incentive
| Parameter | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Incentive Rate | Up to **INR 50 per kg** |
| Duration | **3 years** |
| Disbursing Agency | Solar Energy Corporation of India (SECI) |
| Basis | Verified production output |
| Caps | Subject to annual fund availability |

#### Additional Benefits
- Support for **pilot projects** in end-use sectors: mobility, shipping, steel.
- Funding for **research and development (R&D)**.
- Facilitation of **land acquisition** and **statutory clearances**.
- Creation of **green hydrogen hubs**.
- Support for **export infrastructure** and **international partnerships**.

> **Financial Support Note**  
> Funds are disbursed through SECI as the implementing agency. The total mission fund size is **INR 19,744 crore**, with incentives structured to leverage significant private investment (target: **INR 8 lakh crore** by 2030).

### Required Documents
Applicants must submit the following documents via the online portal:
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration  
2. PAN of the entity  
3. GST registration details  
4. Audited financial statements for the last **2 years**  
5. Technical details of the electrolyser or green hydrogen plant  
6. Land ownership or lease documents  
7. Clearances from relevant authorities (if applicable)  
8. Details of proposed investment and funding sources  
9. Undertaking and declaration as per format prescribed by SECI
