# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is a State Government or Union Territory (eligible to benefit under the scheme)
- [ ] The applicant has designated an implementing agency for developing and maintaining the solar park (as per State Government designation)
- [ ] The proposed solar park capacity is 500 MW and above (or smaller parks only in Himalayan Region, other hilly States, or States with acute shortage of non-agricultural land)
- [ ] The implementing agency has prepared or will prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR) for the solar park
- [ ] The implementing agency has land availability and title documents for the solar park site
- [ ] The implementing agency has transmission connectivity details for grid integration
- [ ] The implementing agency has obtained or will obtain clearances from relevant authorities (environmental, forest, etc.)
- [ ] The implementing agency has a board resolution from the State Government designating it as the implementing agency
- [ ] The implementing agency has provided an undertaking to follow scheme guidelines
- [ ] The implementing agency has a milestone-wise implementation plan
- [ ] The implementing agency has provided bank account details for grant disbursement

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation certificate of the implementing agency
- PAN card of the implementing agency
- ITR (Income Tax Returns) of the implementing agency for last 3 years
- GST registration certificate of the implementing agency
- Bank statements of the implementing agency for last 6 months
- Udyam registration (if applicable, for MSME status)
- Board resolution from State Government designating the implementing agency
- Undertaking from implementing agency to follow scheme guidelines

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) of the Solar Park
- Land availability and title documents
- Transmission connectivity details
- Clearances from relevant authorities (environmental, forest, etc.)
- Milestone-wise implementation plan
- Bank account details for grant disbursement

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and in PDF format (scanned copies acceptable if clear)
- File size for each document must not exceed 10 MB
- Document names must match the checklist exactly (e.g., "DPR_SolarPark_Name.pdf")
- All certificates and clearances must be valid and issued by competent authorities
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, a certified translation must be attached
- Land documents must show clear title and possession in the name of the implementing agency or State Government
- Bank account details must be in the name of the implementing agency and include IFSC, account number, and bank name
- DPR must be signed and stamped by the authorized signatory of the implementing agency

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required for this scheme. The implementing agency must prepare the DPR and apply for a grant of up to Rs. 25 Lakh from SECI for DPR preparation. Standard sections of the DPR should include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Overview and Objectives  
- Site Selection and Land Details  
- Solar Resource Assessment  
- Technology Selection  
- Grid Connectivity and Transmission Plan  
- Environmental and Social Impact Assessment  
- Clearances and Approvals Status  
- Detailed Cost Estimate  
- Financial Analysis and Viability  
- Implementation Schedule and Milestones  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
- Annexures (land documents, clearances, etc.)  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the SECI portal at https://seci.co.in for application submission and guidelines.  
2. Ensure all fields in the application form are filled accurately; leave no mandatory field blank.  
3. Upload documents in the specified format (PDF) and ensure file names match the document checklist.  
4. Double-check that the implementing agency name, PAN, and bank details match exactly across all documents and the form.  
5. Specify the exact solar park capacity (in MW) and location in the application.  
6. Clearly state the grant amount being sought for DPR preparation (up to Rs. 25 Lakh) and/or for project cost (up to Rs. 20 lakhs/MW or 30% of project cost, whichever is lower).  
7. Attach the milestone-wise implementation plan and ensure it aligns with SECI’s prescribed milestones for grant release.  
8. Have the authorized signatory of the implementing agency sign and stamp the application form and declarations.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All application form fields are complete and accurate  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached and properly named  
- [ ] Document quality checks have been passed (legibility, format, validity)  
- [ ] Declarations and undertakings are signed by the authorized signatory  
- [ ] Bank account details for grant disbursement are correct and verified  
- [ ] Milestone-wise implementation plan is included and realistic  
- [ ] DPR is complete and signed/stamped  
- [ ] Board resolution and undertaking from implementing agency are present  
- [ ] Application is ready for submission via SECI portal (https://seci.co.in)

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the SECI portal at https://seci.co.in  
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation page showing the reference number and timestamp  
- Store the reference number securely in the case file  
- Set follow-up reminders for 7 days, 15 days, and 30 days post-submission  
- Monitor the SECI portal for any updates or requests for additional information

### Escalation Path
- Level 1: SECI Portal Helpdesk (via https://seci.co.in or contact details available on the website)  
- Level 2: Nodal Officer at SECI (details to be obtained from SECI website or official communication; if not available, [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT])  
- Level 3: RTI filing if no response within statutory SLA (30 days for initial response, 60 days for final resolution as per Central Government guidelines)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and securely store the approval letter from SECI sanctioning the grant  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount against the applied amount (up to Rs. 25 Lakh for DPR and/or up to Rs. 20 lakhs/MW or 30% of project cost for project cost)  
- [ ] Monitor milestone achievement as per the approved implementation plan  
- [ ] Prepare and submit utilization certificates and progress reports to SECI as per milestone-linked release schedule  
- [ ] Ensure grant disbursement is received in the designated bank account  
- [ ] Maintain records of all communications and submissions to SECI for audit purposes  
- [ ] Coordinate with the implementing agency for timely milestone completion  
- [ ] Upon project completion, assist in submitting final completion report and utilization certificate  
- [ ] Track and ensure the success fee invoice is raised as per consultancy agreement (if applicable)
