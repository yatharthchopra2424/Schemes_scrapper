## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the scheme’s financial benefit structure, where grants can reach up to ₹20 lakhs/MW or 30% of project cost (including grid-connectivity), whichever is lower, with DPR preparation grants up to ₹25 lakh per park.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile, given SECI’s milestone-based grant release and the requirement for state-government-designated implementing agencies, which adds procedural complexity but is offset by high fund utilization (₹141.50 Cr released by Dec 2014).
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The retainer-success fee hybrid model ensures accessibility for state governments and large implementing agencies while aligning consultant incentives with successful grant approval and milestone completion.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: This scheme targets large-scale solar parks (500 MW and above), typically undertaken by state governments or their designated agencies, placing it firmly in the Enterprise tier due to high capital outlay and infrastructure scope.
