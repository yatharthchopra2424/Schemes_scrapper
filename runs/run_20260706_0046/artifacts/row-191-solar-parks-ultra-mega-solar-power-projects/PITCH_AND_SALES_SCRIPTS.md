# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping State Governments and designated implementing agencies access central financial assistance for solar park development under the Solar Parks & Ultra Mega Solar Power Projects scheme. Given your role in renewable energy infrastructure, I wanted to understand your current plans for solar park development and see if this scheme could support your objectives.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is the current status of your State’s solar park development plans under the National Solar Mission?
2. Has your State Government designated an implementing agency for developing and maintaining a solar park of 500 MW or above?
3. Are you facing challenges in land acquisition, transmission connectivity, or securing clearances for solar park development?
4. What is the proposed capacity of the solar park you are planning, and is it located in a Himalayan, hilly, or land-scarce region where smaller parks may be considered?
5. Have you begun preparing a Detailed Project Report (DPR) for the solar park, and do you require financial support for this stage?
6. Are you seeking Central Financial Assistance (CFA) for grid-connectivity and project cost components?
7. What milestones have you established for the solar park development, and how do you plan to track progress?
8. Do you have land availability and title documents, transmission connectivity details, and clearances from relevant authorities ready for submission?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] State Government or Union Territory is the beneficiary
- [ ] Implementing agency designated by the State Government
- [ ] Solar park capacity of 500 MW and above (or smaller park in Himalayan/hilly/land-scarce State)
- [ ] Detailed Project Report (DPR) prepared or in preparation
- [ ] Land availability and title documents accessible
- [ ] Transmission connectivity details available
- [ ] Clearances from relevant authorities (environmental, forest, etc.) obtained or in process
- [ ] Board resolution from State Government designating implementing agency
- [ ] Undertaking from implementing agency to follow scheme guidelines
- [ ] Milestone-wise implementation plan developed
- [ ] Bank account details for grant disbursement available

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your solar park initiative aligns well with the Solar Parks & Ultra Mega Solar Power Projects scheme, which offers grants of up to Rs. 25 Lakh for DPR preparation and up to Rs. 20 lakhs/MW or 30% of project cost (including grid-connectivity), whichever is lower, through SECI. Shall I walk you through how this works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
State Governments face significant delays and cost overruns in solar park development due to fragmented land acquisition, lack of transmission infrastructure, and lengthy clearance processes, which hinder timely achievement of solar renewable purchase obligations and national renewable energy targets.

**Scheme intro:**
The Solar Parks & Ultra Mega Solar Power Projects scheme, administered by the Solar Energy Corporation of India (SECI) under the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy, provides Central Financial Assistance (CFA) for solar park development. It offers a grant of up to Rs. 25 Lakh per park for Detailed Project Report (DPR) preparation and up to Rs. 20 lakhs per MW or 30% of the project cost (including grid-connectivity), whichever is lower, released in milestone-linked installments.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your State Government has designated an implementing agency for solar park development
- The proposed solar park meets the minimum capacity threshold of 500 MW (or qualifies for smaller park consideration in Himalayan/hilly/land-scarce regions)
- You are preparing or have prepared a Detailed Project Report (DPR) requiring financial support
- You require CFA for grid-connectivity and project cost components to accelerate development
