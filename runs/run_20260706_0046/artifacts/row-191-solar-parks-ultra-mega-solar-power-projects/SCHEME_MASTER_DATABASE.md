### Solar Parks & Ultra Mega Solar Power Projects
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://seci.co.in
- **Current Status:** Closed
- **Window:** As of 31st December 2014

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: State Governments; Union Territories; Implementing Agencies designated by State Governments
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Renewable Energy (Solar Power)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: 
  - All States and Union Territories are eligible to benefit under the scheme.
  - The implementing agency for developing and maintaining the solar park is chosen by the State Government.
  - The scheme allows for solar parks of 500 MW and above, with smaller parks considered in Himalayan and other hilly states where contiguous land is difficult to acquire or in states with acute shortage of non-agricultural land.

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - Grant of up to Rs. 25 Lakh per park for preparing the Detailed Project Report (DPR).
  - Grant of up to Rs. 20 lakhs per MW or 30% of the project cost (including grid-connectivity), whichever is lower.
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (released by SECI as per milestones prescribed in the scheme)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (linked to project milestones)

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR) of the Solar Park
2. Land availability and title documents
3. Transmission connectivity details
4. Clearances from relevant authorities (environmental, forest, etc.)
5. Board resolution from the State Government designating the implementing agency
6. Undertaking from the implementing agency to follow scheme guidelines
7. Milestone-wise implementation plan
8. Bank account details for grant disbursement

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. State Government designates an implementing agency for the solar park.
2. The implementing agency prepares the Detailed Project Report (DPR) and applies for a grant of up to Rs. 25 Lakh from SECI for DPR preparation.
3. Upon DPR approval, the implementing agency applies to SECI for the main project grant of up to Rs. 20 lakhs/MW or 30% of the project cost (including grid-connectivity), whichever is lower.
4. SECI appraises the application and sanctions the grant.
5. Grant is released by SECI in installments linked to project milestones.
6. SECI monitors the project progress and ensures compliance with scheme guidelines.
