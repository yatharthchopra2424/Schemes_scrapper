# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Solar Parks & Ultra Mega Solar Power Projects** scheme (Scheme ID: row-191) is a **subsidy**-type initiative under the **Renewable Energy** category, implemented pan-India by the **Solar Energy Corporation of India (SECI)**. Last updated in 2014, the scheme has a sanctioned fund size of **Rs. 141.50 Crore**, with grants released to SECI by the Ministry as of 31st December 2014. The scheme aims to accelerate solar power deployment by providing developed land with essential infrastructure and central financial assistance (CFA) for solar park development.

### Objectives
The scheme’s objectives are exhaustive and multi-faceted:
- Support States in setting up solar parks to create infrastructure for solar power projects.
- Facilitate and speed up installation of grid-connected solar power projects for large-scale electricity generation.
- Provide suitable developed land with clearances, transmission systems, water access, road connectivity, and communication networks.
- Achieve over **20,000 MW** of solar power installed capacity through solar parks and ultra mega solar power projects.
- Promote solar power development in difficult terrains and hilly states by allowing smaller parks where contiguous land is scarce.
- Enable central financial assistance for detailed project reports (DPR) and project cost components.
- Ensure timely release of grants linked to project milestones.
- Monitor and oversee solar park development through SECI as the central implementing agency.

Grant release is **strictly linked to achievement of prescribed milestones**. The scheme allows smaller parks **only** in Himalayan Region, other hilly States, and States with acute shortage of non-agricultural land.

### Application Process
The application process is sequential and milestone-driven:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[State Government designates an implementing agency] --> B[Implementing agency prepares DPR]
    B --> C[Apply to SECI for DPR grant: up to Rs. 25 Lakh per park]
    C --> D[SECI appraises and sanctions DPR grant]
    D --> E[Upon DPR approval, apply for main project grant]
    E --> F[Apply to SECI for main grant: up to Rs. 20 lakhs/MW or 30% of project cost (whichever is lower)]
    F --> G[SECI appraises and sanctions main grant]
    G --> H[SECI releases grant in installments linked to project milestones]
    H --> I[SECI monitors project progress and ensures compliance]
    I --> J[Project completion and operationalization]
```

**Key Steps**:
1. State Government designates an implementing agency for the solar park.
2. Implementing agency prepares the Detailed Project Report (DPR) and applies for a grant of up to Rs. 25 Lakh from SECI for DPR preparation.
3. Upon DPR approval, the implementing agency applies to SECI for the main project grant of up to Rs. 20 lakhs/MW or 30% of the project cost (including grid-connectivity), whichever is lower.
4. SECI appraises the application and sanctions the grant.
5. Grant is released by SECI in installments linked to project milestones.
6. SECI monitors the project progress and ensures compliance with scheme guidelines.

**Application Portal**: https://seci.co.in  
**Key Sources**: Key Facts block; Supporting Evidence (PDF: approved-corp-brochure-1.pdf)

### Key Caveats
- The implementing agency **must be designated by the State Government**.
- Smaller parks (<500 MW) are permitted **only** in Himalayan Region, other hilly States, and States with acute shortage of non-agricultural land.
- Grant release is **strictly milestone-linked**; no advance or lump-sum disbursement.
- As of 31st December 2014, a grant of **Rs. 141.50 Crore** has been released to SECI by the Ministry.
- The scheme supports India’s renewable energy targets under the National Solar Mission.
