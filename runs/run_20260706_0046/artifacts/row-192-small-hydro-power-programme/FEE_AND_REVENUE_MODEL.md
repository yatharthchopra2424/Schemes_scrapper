## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered (CFA up to ₹20 crore per project).
2. Risk-adjusted fees reflecting the scheme’s rolling basis, moderate processing time, and dependency on DPR quality and clearances.
3. Transparent fee structure with retainer covering initial advisory and success fee tied to fund disbursement milestones.
4. Scheme-specific note: Moderate risk profile due to rolling application basis and dependency on Project Appraisal Committee (PAC) approval; however, strong evidence of fund availability and clear disbursement milestones reduces uncertainty.
