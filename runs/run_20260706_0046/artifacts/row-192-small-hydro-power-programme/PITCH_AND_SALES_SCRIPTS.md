# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping renewable energy developers access government funding for small hydro power projects. The Small Hydro Power Programme from MNRE provides up to ₹20 crore per project for implementation and 90% CFA for survey and investigation—ideal for entities developing projects up to 25 MW in hilly or remote areas. I’d like to understand your project’s current stage to see if this scheme aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is the proposed capacity of your small hydro power project (in MW)?
2. Have you completed the Detailed Project Report (DPR) as per CEA guidelines?
3. What is the current status of land acquisition or lease agreements for the project site?
4. Have you obtained clearances from forest, environment, and irrigation departments?
5. Do you have a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) or consent from the State Utility?
6. Are you a State Government, State Power Utility, PSU, private developer, or cooperative society?
7. Is the project located in a hilly or remote area (especially North Eastern or Himalayan states)?
8. What is your timeline for project commissioning?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Project capacity ≤ 25 MW
- [ ] Detailed Project Report (DPR) prepared as per CEA guidelines
- [ ] Land ownership or lease documents available
- [ ] Clearances from forest, environment, and irrigation departments obtained or in process
- [ ] Geological and hydrological survey reports completed
- [ ] Single line diagram and layout plan ready
- [ ] Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) or State Utility consent secured
- [ ] Incorporation/registration documents of applicant available
- [ ] Financial viability report prepared
- [ ] Undertaking for timely project completion can be provided
- [ ] Applicant is State Government, State Power Utility, PSU, private developer, or cooperative society
- [ ] Project is technically feasible as per CEA guidelines
- [ ] Preference given if located in hilly/remote areas (North Eastern/Himalayan states get enhanced CFA)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, your project appears to align well with the Small Hydro Power Programme. This scheme offers up to ₹20 crore in Central Financial Assistance for implementation and 90% CFA for survey and investigation—let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Developers face high upfront costs for survey, investigation, and implementation of small hydro projects, especially in remote areas where clearances and infrastructure are challenging. Many viable projects stall due to lack of early-stage funding and complex approval processes.

**Scheme intro:**
The Small Hydro Power Programme, administered by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), provides Central Financial Assistance (CFA) of up to 90% of project cost for survey and investigation (capped at ₹10 lakh per project) and up to ₹5 crore per MW for implementation, with a ceiling of ₹20 crore per project. For special category states (North Eastern and Himalayan), CFA is enhanced to 100% for survey and investigation and up to ₹7 crore per MW for implementation. Funds are released in instalments based on project milestones.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Project capacity is up to 25 MW, meeting the scheme’s eligibility limit
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) is being prepared as per CEA guidelines
- Land documents and clearances (forest, environment, irrigation) are in progress or secured
- Applicant entity type (State Government/PSU/private developer/cooperative society) is eligible
- Preference for hilly/remote areas may apply, potentially qualifying for enhanced CFA
