### Small Hydro Power Programme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://mnre.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: State Governments, State Power Utilities, Public Sector Undertakings, private developers, cooperative societies
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Renewable Energy (Small Hydro Power)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Projects must have a capacity of up to 25 MW and be technically feasible as per CEA guidelines; preference given to projects in hilly and remote areas; implementing agency must submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR) and obtain necessary clearances

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Central Financial Assistance (CFA) at 90% of project cost for survey and investigation (up to ₹10 lakh per project) and up to ₹5 crore per MW for project implementation, subject to a ceiling of ₹20 crore per project; for special category states (North Eastern and Himalayan states), CFA enhanced to 100% for survey and investigation and up to ₹7 crore per MW for implementation
- Disbursement mode: Released in instalments based on project milestones
- Duration of benefit: One-time (based on project milestones)

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
2. Land ownership or lease documents
3. Clearances from forest, environment, and irrigation departments
4. Geological and hydrological survey reports
5. Single line diagram and layout plan
6. Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) or consent from State Utility
7. Incorporation/registration documents of the applicant
8. Financial viability report
9. Undertaking for completion of project within stipulated time

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify a potential small hydro site and prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR) as per CEA guidelines.
2. Obtain necessary clearances from concerned authorities (e.g., forest, environment, irrigation).
3. Submit the DPR along with application to the State Nodal Agency (SNA).
4. SNA forwards the proposal to MNRE for appraisal.
5. MNRE appraises the proposal through the Project Appraisal Committee (PAC).
6. Upon approval, administrative sanction is issued by MNRE.
7. Funds are released in instalments based on project progress and submission of utilization certificates.
