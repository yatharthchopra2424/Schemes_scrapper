## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the financial benefit derived by the client from accessing incentives under the National Wind-Solar Hybrid Policy, such as accelerated depreciation, concessional finance, and generation-based incentives (GBI).
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the moderate risk profile of this scheme due to its reliance on existing wind and solar policies for benefits (no direct subsidy), with processing timelines dependent on technical evaluation by SECI or state nodal agencies and subsequent incentive applications.
3. **Transparency**: Clients are informed that the policy enables access to benefits but does not guarantee them; success fees are contingent on successful availing of incentives under linked schemes.
4. **Scalability**: Retainer structures scale with client complexity, from micro-projects to large hybrid developments requiring grid connectivity approvals and compliance with CEA standards.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Retainer fee for consulting services related to the National Wind-Solar Hybrid Policy, including DPR preparation, technical compliance assessment, and liaison with SECI or state nodal agencies for project registration.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for securing access to financial benefits under the National Wind-Solar Hybrid Policy via linked wind and solar schemes (e.g., GBI, accelerated depreciation, concessional finance through IREDA), as facilitated by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) and implemented by agencies such as SECI and IREDA.
