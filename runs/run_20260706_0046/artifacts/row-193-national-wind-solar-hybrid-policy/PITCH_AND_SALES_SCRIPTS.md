# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because I specialize in helping renewable energy developers and power producers access government schemes that optimize project economics. The National Wind-Solar Hybrid Policy from the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) is designed specifically for grid-connected wind and solar projects—whether new or existing—to enhance grid stability, reduce power variability, and unlock fiscal benefits like accelerated depreciation and concessional finance. I’d like to understand your current projects to see if this policy could add value to your portfolio.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is the current status of your wind or solar power projects—are they operational, under construction, or in planning?
2. Are your projects grid-connected, and do you have approval from the Central Electricity Authority (CEA) or the concerned transmission utility?
3. What is the sanctioned capacity at your point of interconnection, and are you considering hybridization to optimize land and transmission use?
4. Do you have a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) or intent to enter into one for your project?
5. Are you seeking to reduce variability in power output to improve grid stability and extend generation hours?
6. Have you already secured land documents, technical specifications, and environmental clearances for your project?
7. Are you looking to avail benefits under existing wind and solar schemes, such as generation-based incentives (GBI) or concessional finance through IREDA?
8. Is your project located in India, and are you a power producer, IPP, state utility, or renewable energy developer?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Project is grid-connected and complies with CEA technical standards for grid connectivity  
- [ ] Hybridization ensures optimal utilization of infrastructure and does not exceed sanctioned capacity at point of interconnection  
- [ ] Applicant is a power producer, renewable energy developer, IPP, or state utility  
- [ ] Project involves wind and solar components connected at a single point to the grid  
- [ ] Detailed Project Report (DPR) is prepared or can be prepared  
- [ ] Land documents (ownership or lease) are available  
- [ ] Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) or intent to enter into PPA is in place  
- [ ] Single line diagram, layout plan, and technical specifications of wind turbines and solar PV modules are ready  
- [ ] Grid connectivity approval from transmission utility is obtained  
- [ ] Environmental and forest clearances (if applicable) are secured  
- [ ] No Objection Certificate (NOC) from Aviation Authority (if applicable) is obtained  
- [ ] Clearance from Ministry of Defence (if applicable) is obtained  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like your project aligns well with the National Wind-Solar Hybrid Policy—especially if you're looking to enhance grid stability and access financial benefits under existing wind and solar schemes. This policy enables hybrid projects to avail of incentives like accelerated depreciation and concessional finance, which could significantly improve your project’s viability. Shall I walk you through how it works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Renewable energy developers face challenges with power variability due to intermittent wind and solar generation, leading to grid instability and underutilized transmission infrastructure. Existing projects often operate below capacity during non-peak hours, and new projects struggle to secure financing without clear pathways to fiscal incentives.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Wind-Solar Hybrid Policy, administered by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), is a framework that enables grid-connected wind-solar hybrid systems to access financial and fiscal benefits under existing wind and solar schemes—such as generation-based incentives (GBI), accelerated depreciation, concessional customs duty exemption, and priority sector lending through institutions like IREDA. While it does not provide direct subsidies, it allows hybrid projects to optimize transmission and land use, reduce power variability, extend generation hours, and improve grid stability. Applications are accepted year-round via the MNRE portal: https://mnre.gov.in.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your project is grid-connected and complies with CEA technical standards for grid connectivity  
- Wind and solar components are (or will be) connected at a single point to the grid  
- Hybridization will not exceed the sanctioned capacity at the point of interconnection  
- You are a power producer, IPP, state utility, or renewable energy developer—the exact beneficiary group targeted by the policy
