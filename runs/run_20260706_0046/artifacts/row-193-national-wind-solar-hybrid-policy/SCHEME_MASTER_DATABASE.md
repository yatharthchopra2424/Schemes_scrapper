### National Wind-Solar Hybrid Policy
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://mnre.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Power producers; renewable energy developers; independent power producers (IPPs); state utilities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Wind and solar power projects
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Projects must be grid-connected and comply with technical standards and grid connectivity norms as specified by the Central Electricity Authority (CEA); hybridization must ensure optimal utilization of infrastructure and must not exceed the sanctioned capacity at the point of interconnection

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: No direct financial subsidies; enables access to benefits under existing wind and solar schemes such as generation-based incentives (GBI), accelerated depreciation, concessional finance through institutions like IREDA, concessional customs duty exemption, and priority sector lending
- Disbursement mode: Depends on the specific scheme under which benefits are availed (e.g., tax offset for accelerated depreciation, direct credit for GBI)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (for incentives like accelerated depreciation) or annual (for GBI), depending on the underlying scheme

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
2. Certificate of Incorporation/Registration
3. Memorandum and Articles of Association
4. Board resolution authorizing the project
5. Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) or intent to enter into PPA
6. Land documents (ownership or lease agreement)
7. Single line diagram and layout plan
8. Technical specifications of wind turbines and solar PV modules
9. Grid connectivity approval from the concerned transmission utility
10. Environmental and forest clearances (if applicable)
11. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the Aviation Authority (if applicable)
12. Clearance from the Ministry of Defence (if applicable)

#### Application Process
1. Project developers must submit a detailed project report (DPR) to the Solar Energy Corporation of India Limited (SECI) or the respective state nodal agency.
2. The DPR must include details of wind and solar components, hybridization methodology, single point of grid connection, and compliance with CEA technical standards.
3. SECI or the state nodal agency evaluates the proposal for technical feasibility and grid impact.
4. Upon approval, the project is registered and proceeds to implementation.
5. After commissioning, the project can apply for applicable incentives under wind and solar policies through the respective implementing agencies.
