# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Project developer or investor interested in setting up offshore wind energy projects in India
- [ ] Technical and financial capability demonstrated
- [ ] Compliance with regulatory norms as per scheme guidelines
- [ ] Adherence to environmental and safety standards as outlined in scheme guidelines
- [ ] Project located within India's offshore wind zones (Pan-India geographic scope)
- [ ] Project capacity aligned with 1,000 MW target under Viability Gap Funding (VGF) scheme
- [ ] Proposal submitted in response to official call for proposals under the policy
- [ ] Project designed to bridge gap between project costs and revenue for financial viability
- [ ] Commitment to achieve project milestones and timely completion
- [ ] Alignment with objectives: promote offshore wind development, harness offshore wind potential, reduce fossil fuel dependence, attract investment via VGF, establish offshore wind ecosystem

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation
- PAN Card
- GST Registration
- ITR for last 3 years
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable)
- Audited Financial Statements
- Director's KYC (Aadhar, PAN)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) covering technical, financial, and environmental aspects
- Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) report
- Safety compliance certificate
- Regulatory clearance documents (as per scheme guidelines)
- Proof of technical capability (past project experience, certifications)
- Financial capability documents (net worth, credit rating, funding plan)
- Site feasibility report (wind resource assessment, seabed study, grid connectivity plan)
- Undertaking to comply with environmental, safety, and regulatory standards
- Milestone-based project timeline and completion plan
- Declaration of no prior funding from other central government schemes for same project (if applicable)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be self-attested by authorized signatory
- File size: PDFs under 10 MB each; scanned copies legible and clear
- File naming convention: `SchemeName_DocumentType_CaseID_Version.pdf`
- Documents in English or accompanied by certified Hindi/English translation
- Supporting documents for authorisation required where self-attestation is used
- All pages numbered and indexed as first page of proposal
- Certificates of incorporation, registration, and approvals must be valid and current
- Bank statements must show sufficient funds or funding arrangements
- Undertakings and declarations must be signed and stamped where required

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required. Standard sections include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Overview (location, capacity, technology)  
- Wind Resource Assessment  
- Seabed and Geotechnical Studies  
- Grid Connectivity and Transmission Plan  
- Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)  
- Safety and Risk Management Plan  
- Financial Model (CAPEX, OPEX, revenue projections, VGF requirement)  
- Implementation Schedule and Milestones  
- Procurement and Construction Strategy  
- Operations and Maintenance Plan  
- Economic and Social Benefits Analysis  
- Compliance with Regulatory and Statutory Requirements  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Measures  
- Annexures: Certificates, approvals, technical details, financial statements  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Ensure all eligibility criteria are met before starting the form  
2. Fill in all fields accurately; leave no blank sections unless marked optional  
3. Attach all required documents as per checklist and reference them in the form  
4. Self-attest all documents and enclosures; provide certified translations if in regional language  
5. Clearly state the Technology Readiness Level (TRL) if applicable (though not explicitly required for this scheme, maintain readiness to specify)  
6. Declare any prior funding from central government schemes in the last five years for the same project  
7. Provide complete promoter/director details including Aadhar, PAN, contact information  
8. Specify the exact quantum of Viability Gap Funding (VGF) sought based on project cost-revenue gap  
9. Define clear, measurable project milestones for disbursement tracking  
10. Undertake to comply with all scheme guidelines, including environmental, safety, and regulatory standards  
11. Ensure the Authorized Signatory signs and stamps the application and all enclosures  
12. Submit only one proposal per entity; multiple submissions lead to disqualification  
13. Retain a complete copy of the submitted application for records  
14. Submit via the designated portal or email as specified in the call for proposals  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All application form fields completed accurately  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents attached and properly referenced  
- [ ] Documents self-attested and in correct format (PDF, legible, named correctly)  
- [ ] Declarations signed (prior funding, compliance, undertaking)  
- [ ] Financials and technical details consistent across documents  
- [ ] Environmental and safety clearances included or proof of application attached  
- [ ] Milestone-based implementation plan defined  
- [ ] VGF amount justified with detailed financial modeling  
- [ ] Authorized Signatory identified and contact details provided  
- [ ] No duplicate or multiple submissions from same entity  
- [ ] Application submitted before deadline as per call for proposals

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Take screenshot of submission confirmation (portal or email)  
- Store application reference number securely  
- Save copy of submitted application and all enclosures  
- Set follow-up reminders for 7, 15, and 30 days post-submission  
- Monitor portal for updates or requests for additional information

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify approval letter from MNRE/NISE  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount of Viability Gap Funding (VGF)  
- [ ] Review and accept Letter of Award (LoA) with terms and conditions  
- [ ] Submit Acceptance Letter as per prescribed format within 7 days of LoA receipt  
- [ ] Open dedicated bank accounts: (i) EA contribution, (ii) grants-in-aid from MNRE  
- [ ] Utilize funds exclusively for approved project components as per LoA  
- [ ] Submit Utilization Certificate (UC) signed by Authorised Signatory for each instalment  
- [ ] Provide progress reports and achieved timeline activities against approved scope  
- [ ] Obtain and submit proof of safety, environmental, and regulatory approvals  
- [ ] Submit geo-tagged photographs of project from various angles and locations  
- [ ] Provide detailed list of approved equipment procured and installed with approved vs actual cost  
- [ ] Submit details of Technical Civil Work and Furniture & Fixtures purchased  
- [ ] Provide bank statements showing payment to suppliers  
- [ ] Display signage prominently: "Project is funded under National Green Hydrogen Mission" (Note: While this scheme is Offshore Wind Energy Policy, the signage requirement may be adapted; verify with nodal officer)  
- [ ] Submit final Project Completion Report with self-declaration letter and proof of signage  
- [ ] Comply with all other terms and conditions in the Sanction Letter  
- [ ] Ensure no diversion of funds to other purposes  
- [ ] Prepare for potential audit or verification by MNRE/NISE or authorised entities  
- [ ] In case of delay beyond six months, be aware of penalty @1% of financial support released  
- [ ] If funds misused, be prepared to refund entire grant with interest as per GFR rules
