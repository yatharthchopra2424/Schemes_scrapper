## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the substantial financial benefit derived from the Offshore Wind Energy Policy’s Viability Gap Funding (VGF), which targets 1,000 MW of offshore wind projects with significant capital outlays, ensuring consultant compensation reflects the scale of impact.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Returns**: Success fees account for the scheme’s medium confidence level, dependency on fund availability, and milestone-based disbursement, which introduce execution risk despite strong policy intent.
3. **Market Competitiveness**: Tiered structure ensures accessibility for large offshore wind developers while maintaining profitability, benchmarked against similar renewable energy consulting engagements in India.
4. **Transparency & Predictability**: Clear retainer and success fee terms reduce client uncertainty, with payments tied to verifiable scheme outcomes like VGF sanction and project commissioning.
