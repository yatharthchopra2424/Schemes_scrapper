# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping renewable energy project developers and investors access government financial support. Given your interest in offshore wind energy, I wanted to explore whether the Offshore Wind Energy Policy’s Viability Gap Funding (VGF) scheme could support your project’s financial viability—especially since it’s designed to bridge the gap between project costs and revenue for offshore wind initiatives in India.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is the current stage of your offshore wind project (concept, planning, pre-construction, or under development)?
2. What is the planned capacity (in MW) of your offshore wind project, and is it located within India’s maritime boundaries?
3. Have you secured the necessary technical and financial capability documentation required for offshore wind project approval?
4. Are you compliant with environmental, safety, and regulatory standards as outlined by MNRE guidelines from the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE)?
5. What is your timeline for achieving key project milestones, and are you prepared to meet completion deadlines tied to funding disbursement?
6. Have you explored financial support mechanisms like Viability Gap Funding (VGF) to address the cost-revenue gap in your project?
7. Are you seeking regulatory facilitation, single-window clearances, or grid connectivity support as part of your project development?
8. Are you interested in contributing to domestic manufacturing and job creation in the renewable energy sector through your project?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Project developer or investor interested in setting up offshore wind energy projects in India
- [ ] Demonstrated technical and financial capability
- [ ] Compliance with regulatory norms
- [ ] Adherence to environmental and safety standards as per scheme guidelines
- [ ] Project located within India’s offshore wind zones
- [ ] Alignment with objectives: promoting offshore wind development, harnessing offshore wind potential, reducing fossil fuel dependence, attracting investment via VGF, establishing offshore wind ecosystem

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your offshore wind project aligns well with the Offshore Wind Energy Policy’s Viability Gap Funding (VGF) scheme, which provides financial support to bridge the gap between project costs and revenue for up to 1,000 MW of offshore wind energy projects across India. Shall I walk you through how this scheme works and how it could benefit your specific initiative?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Offshore wind projects in India face high upfront capital costs and uncertain revenue streams, making them financially challenging to develop without government support. Many developers struggle to achieve financial viability due to the gap between project costs and expected returns, which hinders investment in this clean energy sector.

**Scheme intro:**
The Offshore Wind Energy Policy, administered by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), offers financial support through the Viability Gap Funding (VGF) scheme for up to 1,000 MW of offshore wind energy projects. The exact quantum and disbursement mechanism are defined in the scheme guidelines, with funding aimed at bridging the gap between project costs and revenue to ensure financial viability.
