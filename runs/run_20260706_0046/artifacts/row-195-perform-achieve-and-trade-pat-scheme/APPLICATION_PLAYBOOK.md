# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Industrial unit belongs to notified energy intensive sectors: Aluminum, Cement, Chlor-Alkali, Fertilizer, Iron & Steel, Paper & Pulp, Railways, Thermal Power, Textile, Petrochemicals, Commercial Buildings (Hotels), Airports, Railway workshops and production units, Cement Grinding Units (as per notifications)
- [ ] Unit meets the threshold level of annual energy consumption as specified in the notifications issued under the Energy Conservation Act, 2001
- [ ] Unit is designated as a Designated Consumer (DC) by the Central Government
- [ ] Unit is located in Pan-India (scheme has nationwide coverage)
- [ ] Unit is part of the current PAT cycle (e.g., PAT Cycle VII notified in October 2021 for period 2022-23 to 2024-25; verify latest cycle notification)
- [ ] Unit has not been disqualified due to non-compliance in previous PAT cycles (penalties may apply under Energy Conservation Act, 2001)

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate
- PAN Card
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of last 3 years
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable)
- Authorization Letter for Consultant/Representative

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Baseline year energy consumption data
- Project reports on energy efficiency measures implemented
- Monitoring and verification reports by accredited energy auditors
- Documentation of actual energy consumption and production data
- Certificates of compliance with PAT cycle targets
- Normalization and M&V Guidelines specific to the sector (e.g., for Aluminum, Cement, etc., available on BEE PAT Downloads portal)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format
- File size per document should not exceed 10 MB
- File names must match the document description (e.g., "Baseline_Energy_Consumption_2022.pdf")
- All certificates and reports must be issued by authorized bodies (e.g., accredited energy auditors for M&V reports)
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, provide certified translation
- Declarations and undertakings must be signed and stamped by authorized signatory

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Project Report is required. Standard sections include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Introduction and Objectives  
- Baseline Energy Consumption Analysis  
- Description of Energy Efficiency Measures Implemented  
- Methodology for Measurement and Verification  
- Results and Energy Savings Achieved  
- Comparison with PAT Cycle Targets  
- Financial Analysis and Investment Details  
- Conclusion and Recommendations  
- Annexures (data logs, audit reports, certificates)  

*Note: Verify exact DPR requirements with the nodal officer or BEE PAT officials, as guidelines may vary by cycle and sector.*

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal via the BEE website (https://beeindia.gov.in/pat) or designated PAT cycle notification links.  
2. Ensure all fields are filled accurately; leave no mandatory field blank.  
3. Use data exactly as per supporting documents (e.g., baseline energy consumption must match audit reports).  
4. Upload documents in the prescribed format and size limits (PDF, <10 MB per file).  
5. Verify sector-specific thresholds and targets from the latest PAT cycle notification (e.g., PAT Cycle VII Notification PDF).  
6. Confirm that the unit is correctly classified under the notified energy intensive sectors.  
7. Double-check declarations and undertakings before final submission.  
8. Save a draft and review internally before final submission.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All eligibility criteria verified and documented  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents collected and quality-checked  
- [ ] Project Report/DPR completed and reviewed  
- [ ] Application form filled completely with accurate data  
- [ ] All uploaded documents are legible, correctly named, and within size limits  
- [ ] Declarations and undertakings are signed and dated  
- [ ] Application reference number noted post-submission (to be filled during submission)  
- [ ] Follow-up reminders set for Day 18 and Day 30

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the official BEE PAT portal (https://beeindia.gov.in/pat) or as directed in the latest cycle notification.  
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation page showing the timestamp and application/reference number.  
- Store the reference number securely in the case file.  
- Set calendar reminders for follow-up at Day 25 and Day 35.  
- Notify the client of successful submission with the reference number.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and store the approval letter from BEE  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount or number of Energy Savings Certificates (ESCerts) issued  
- [ ] Monitor the validity period of ESCerts (must be traded within the compliance period as per cycle notification)  
- [ ] Assist client in listing ESCerts for trade on power exchanges (e.g., Indian Energy Exchange)  
- [ ] Ensure utilization certificate is submitted if required for any associated incentives  
- [ ] Track and verify the client’s investment in energy efficiency measures (as reported in scheme documents, e.g., INR 43,721 Crores mobilized in PAT Cycle II)  
- [ ] Prepare and submit invoice for success fee as per consultancy agreement  
- [ ] Advise client on next PAT cycle opportunities and re-verification requirements
