## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified the Perform Achieve and Trade (PAT) Scheme — a market-based energy efficiency scheme administered by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), Ministry of Power, Government of India — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is financial gains through trading of Energy Savings Certificates (ESCerts) on power exchanges, with no direct subsidy but significant private investment mobilized (e.g., DCs invested approximately INR 43,721 Crores in PAT Cycle II).

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment based on sector notification and energy consumption thresholds.  
2. Guidance on implementation of energy efficiency measures to meet or exceed Specific Energy Consumption (SEC) reduction targets.  
3. Assistance in preparing baseline year energy consumption data and project reports.  
4. Coordination with accredited energy auditors for monitoring and verification (M&V) of energy savings.  
5. Support in documentation of actual energy consumption and production data for certification.  
6. Facilitation of issuance of Energy Savings Certificates (ESCerts) upon achieving excess savings beyond targets.  
7. Advisory on portal registration and trading of ESCerts on power exchanges (such as Indian Energy Exchange) via [https://beeindia.gov.in/pat](https://beeindia.gov.in/pat).  
*Note: A DPR/project report is required as per evidence (item 2 under Required Documents).*

## 4. What We Need From You
1. Documents as per the scheme's checklist: baseline year energy consumption data, project reports on energy efficiency measures implemented, monitoring and verification reports by accredited energy auditors, documentation of actual energy consumption and production data, and certificates of compliance with PAT cycle targets.  
2. Designated point of contact within your organization for coordination and timely updates.  
3. Response to queries within 2 working days to ensure adherence to the scheme’s application timelines.
