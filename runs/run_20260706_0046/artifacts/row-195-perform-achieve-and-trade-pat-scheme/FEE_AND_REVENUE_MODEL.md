## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit derived from ESCert trading.
2. Transparent fee structure with clear retainer and success fee components.
3. Risk-adjusted pricing reflecting the scheme’s 3-year cycle duration and verification requirements.
4. Custom pricing for Enterprise tier clients due to high scheme value and complex compliance needs.

*Note: The PAT scheme operates on a market-based mechanism with financial benefits derived from trading Energy Savings Certificates (ESCerts). Processing time spans 3 years per cycle, introducing revenue lag. Success is contingent on verified energy savings by accredited auditors, which introduces moderate risk but high reward potential for eligible Designated Consumers in energy-intensive sectors.*

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description:** Retainer fee for consulting services related to the Perform Achieve and Trade (PAT) Scheme, including eligibility assessment, target setting guidance, and preparation for energy efficiency measure implementation.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description:** Success fee for consulting services related to the Perform Achieve and Trade (PAT) Scheme, payable upon issuance and trading of Energy Savings Certificates (ESCerts) through the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), Ministry of Power, Government of India.
