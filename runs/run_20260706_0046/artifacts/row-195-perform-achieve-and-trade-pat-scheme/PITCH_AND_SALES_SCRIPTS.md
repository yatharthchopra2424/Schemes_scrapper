## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**  
Energy-intensive industries face rising operational costs due to high energy consumption, yet many lack structured pathways to measure, verify, and monetize energy efficiency gains. Without a recognized mechanism, investments in efficiency often go unrewarded, and compliance with energy norms remains challenging.

**Scheme intro:**  
The Perform Achieve and Trade (PAT) Scheme, administered by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) under the Ministry of Power, Government of India, is a market-based mechanism that sets Specific Energy Consumption (SEC) reduction targets for Designated Consumers (DCs) in notified sectors. Facilities that exceed their targets earn Energy Savings Certificates (ESCerts), which can be traded on power exchanges like the Indian Energy Exchange (IEX), providing a financial return on energy efficiency investments.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**  
- Your facility operates in a notified energy-intensive sector (e.g., [Client’s Sector])  
- Your annual energy consumption meets the threshold for Designated Consumer status under the Energy Conservation Act, 2001  
- You are (or can be) designated as a Designated Consumer (DC) by the Central Government  
- You have or can engage accredited energy auditors for monitoring and verification (M&V) of energy savings
