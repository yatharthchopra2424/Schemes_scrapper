### Perform Achieve and Trade (PAT) Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), Ministry of Power, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://beeindia.gov.in/pat
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Based on PAT cycle notifications; each cycle spans three years with specific target years and verification periods as per official notifications (e.g., PAT Cycle VII notified in October 2021 for period 2022-23 to 2024-25)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Industrial units from notified energy intensive sectors
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Aluminum, Cement, Chlor-Alkali, Fertilizer, Iron & Steel, Paper & Pulp, Railways, Thermal Power and Textile
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must meet threshold level of annual energy consumption as specified in notifications issued under the Energy Conservation Act, 2001, and be designated as Designated Consumers (DCs) by the Central Government

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial benefits through trading of Energy Savings Certificates (ESCerts) on power exchanges; no direct financial subsidies or grants provided
- Disbursement mode: Market-based mechanism via trading of ESCerts on power exchanges
- Duration of benefit: Cycle-specific (each PAT cycle spans three years)

#### Documents Required
1. Baseline year energy consumption data
2. Project reports on energy efficiency measures implemented
3. Monitoring and verification reports by accredited energy auditors
4. Documentation of actual energy consumption and production data
5. Certificates of compliance with PAT cycle targets

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identification of energy intensive sectors and notification of threshold levels for designation as Designated Consumers (DCs)
2. Notification of energy consumption norms and targets for each PAT cycle by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE)
3. Designated Consumers (DCs) implement energy efficiency measures to meet or exceed their specific energy consumption (SEC) reduction targets
4. Monitoring and verification of energy savings achieved by DCs through accredited energy auditors
5. Issuance of Energy Savings Certificates (ESCerts) to DCs achieving excess savings beyond targets
6. Trading of ESCerts on power exchanges (such as Indian Energy Exchange) by DCs that have surplus certificates, and purchase by DCs needing to meet their targets
