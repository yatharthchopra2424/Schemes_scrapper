## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the actual financial benefit delivered to clients under the UJALA Scheme, which provides subsidized LED bulbs to households at significantly reduced costs (typically ₹70 per bulb vs. market price of ₹150–200), resulting in measurable energy savings.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees account for implementation risk; however, the UJALA Scheme has a low technical risk profile due to its straightforward distribution model through designated outlets and online platforms, though beneficiary verification and awareness gaps may affect uptake.
3. **Volume Incentives**: Lower success fee percentages at higher tiers reflect economies of scale in managing large-volume client engagements, applicable when aggregating demand for residential LED distribution under UJALA.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The UJALA Scheme features a high success rate in distribution (over 360 million LEDs distributed as per portal archives) and minimal processing time (instant purchase at point of sale), resulting in low implementation lag and high predictability of benefit realization — supporting lower success fee brackets for volume-driven models.
