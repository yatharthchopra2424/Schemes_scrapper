## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because I specialise in helping businesses and households reduce energy costs through government-backed efficiency programmes. The UJALA Scheme – Unnat Jyoti by Affordable LEDs is designed specifically for residential consumers looking to lower electricity bills by switching to energy-efficient LED lighting. I’d like to understand your current lighting setup and energy usage to see if this scheme could deliver meaningful savings for you.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of lighting (bulbs, tubes, etc.) are you currently using in your home or property?
- Approximately how many light fixtures do you have that could be upgraded to LED?
- Are you responsible for paying the electricity bill for this property?
- Have you previously purchased or installed any energy-efficient lighting products?
- Are you aware of any government subsidies or schemes for LED lighting in your area?
- What is your average monthly electricity bill, and are you looking to reduce it?
- Do you own the property, or are you a tenant seeking approval for upgrades?
- Are you interested in upfront cost savings or long-term energy reduction as a priority?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is a residential consumer (household)
- [ ] Property is located in India (scheme is nationally implemented)
- [ ] Seeking to procure LED bulbs, tube lights, or energy-efficient fans
- [ ] Purchasing through authorised distribution channels (DISCOMs, EESL counters, or empaneled vendors)
- [ ] Not availing duplicate subsidy for the same product under another scheme
- [ ] Willing to provide valid ID proof and address verification at point of sale
- [ ] Purchase quantity within per-household limits (as per phase/state guidelines)
- [ ] Not a commercial, industrial, or institutional bulk buyer (unless under specific empanelment)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you’re a residential consumer aiming to cut electricity costs — exactly who the UJALA Scheme – Unnat Jyoti by Affordable LEDs is designed for. This programme offers subsidised LED bulbs and tube lights through government empanelled channels, helping households save up to 80–90% on lighting energy use. Let me walk you through how it works and what you could save.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Households across India face high electricity bills due to inefficient lighting, with traditional incandescent and CFL bulbs consuming significantly more power than LEDs. Many consumers want to switch but are deterred by upfront costs or lack awareness of available subsidies, leading to avoidable energy waste and higher monthly expenses.

**Scheme intro:**
The UJALA Scheme – Unnat Jyoti by Affordable LEDs is a national initiative led by the Ministry of Power and implemented by Energy Efficiency Services Limited (EESL). It provides subsidised LED bulbs, tube lights, and energy-efficient fans to residential consumers through authorised distribution channels, with LED bulbs available for as low as ₹70 (vs. market price of ₹150–200) and tube lights at ₹220 (vs. ₹400–500), enabling immediate cost savings.
