### UJALA Scheme – Unnat Jyoti by Affordable LEDs
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Energy Efficiency Services Limited (EESL)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://ujala.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Closed

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Domestic households, government institutions, urban local bodies, and private establishments
- Age of business: Not applicable (scheme targets end-users, not businesses)
- Sector: Residential, commercial, institutional, and municipal lighting
- Revenue cap: Not mentioned
- Employee count: Not mentioned
- Other: Open to all electricity consumers across India; no income or business restrictions for LED bulb purchases under the scheme

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: LED bulbs, tube lights, and energy-efficient fans distributed at subsidized prices (up to 40-50% below market rate)
- Disbursement mode: Direct distribution through designated counters and online portal (when functional)
- Duration of benefit: One-time per eligible product category per household/entity

#### Documents Required
1. Proof of identity (Aadhaar card, voter ID, passport, or driver’s license)
2. Proof of address (electricity bill, Aadhaar card, or ration card)
3. Electricity consumer number (for verification of connection)
4. For institutional buyers: authorization letter and GST registration (if applicable)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the UJALA portal (https://ujala.gov.in) or visit a designated distribution center
2. Submit required documents for verification
3. Receive a unique token or slip upon successful verification
4. Collect the subsidized LED products (bulbs, tube lights, fans) from the distribution center
5. Pay the subsidized amount (if applicable) at the counter or online
