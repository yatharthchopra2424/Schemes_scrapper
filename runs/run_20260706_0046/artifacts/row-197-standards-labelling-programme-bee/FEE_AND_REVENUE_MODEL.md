## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the financial benefit derived from the scheme, leveraging the actual grant/benefit amount from evidence where available.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s processing time and success likelihood; the Standards & Labelling Programme has a rolling application basis with no fixed deadline, indicating moderate processing risk.
3. **Transparency**: All fees are clearly communicated upfront, with retainers covering initial assessment and success fees contingent on approval.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Standards & Labelling Programme (BEE) offers recognition and market transformation benefits rather than direct financial grants. Financial support details are not explicitly mentioned in the evidence, so the scheme’s value is indirect (e.g., energy savings, market access). Success fees are calibrated to the estimated value of energy savings and market benefits derived from compliance, based on BEE’s reported impact (e.g., 89.80 Billion Units saved due to Standards & Labelling programme).
