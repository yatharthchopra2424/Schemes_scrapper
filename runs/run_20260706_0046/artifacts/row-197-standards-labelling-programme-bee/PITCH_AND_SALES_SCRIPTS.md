## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Manufacturers struggle to communicate the real energy-saving potential of their appliances to consumers, leading to purchase decisions based on price alone rather than long-term value. Counterfeit or misleading star labels further erode trust in the market, making it difficult for genuine energy-efficient products to stand out.

**Scheme intro:**
The Standards & Labelling Programme (S&L) by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), Ministry of Power, Government of India, is a national initiative that provides star labels on appliances to indicate energy efficiency. Manufacturers can file applications online to get their models star-labelled. The scheme now mandates Unique QR codes on every star-labelled appliance to ensure authenticity, enable consumer verification via the BEE Star Label mobile app, and track sales data across the supply chain. The programme covers 30 appliances — 11 under mandatory regime (e.g., Room Air Conditioners, Frost Free Refrigerators, LED lamps) and 19 under voluntary regime (e.g., Washing Machines, Computer Notebooks, Tyres).

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You manufacture appliances listed under the S&L Programme’s mandatory or voluntary regime (e.g., ACs, refrigerators, TVs, etc.)
- You are willing to comply with the QR code mandate for every star-labelled unit
- You can provide test reports from NABL-accredited labs to support your application
- You target the Indian market, where the scheme has pan-India applicability and consumer recognition
