### Standards & Labelling Programme (BEE)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), Ministry of Power, Govt. of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.beestarlabel.com/
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Manufacturers
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Appliance manufacturing (eligible appliances include Room Air Conditioners, Frost Free Refrigerator, Tubular Florescent Lamp, Distribution Transformer, etc.)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Manufacturers can file their applications online for star labelling of appliances under the S&L programme

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: No direct financial assistance; benefits include star labels enabling informed consumer choices, QR code authentication, real-time feedback via mobile app, and sales tracking
- Disbursement mode: Not applicable (non-financial recognition scheme)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing (as long as compliance is maintained)

#### Documents Required
1. Online application filed by manufacturers
2. Supporting documents for appliance models (as per BEE requirements)
3. Test reports from BEE-approved laboratories
4. Details of appliance specifications for QR code integration
5. Payment gateway integration details (if applicable for processing fees)
6. SMS gateway integration details (for smoother operations)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Manufacturers can file their applications online
2. Easy tracking of applications filed by manufacturers
3. Reports can be downloaded easily
4. Minimizes paperwork
5. The list of BEE approved models is available in the public domain through linkage of the S & L application with the website of BEE
6. More effective communication between manufacturers and BEE
7. Analysis of energy savings and generation of reports thereof
8. Integration of SMS gateways and Payment gateways for smoother operations
