# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] **Registration Type**: Entity must be a Designated Consumer (DC) under PAT scheme (energy intensive sectors: Aluminum, Cement, Chlor-Alkali, Fertilizer, Iron & Steel, Paper & Pulp, Railways, Thermal Power, Textile, Petrochemicals, Commercial Buildings, Airports) OR an MSME as per MSME Act for SME programmes OR a manufacturer of appliances/equipment for Standards & Labelling OR a State Designated Agency (SDA) for ECBC implementation.
- [ ] **Turnover Threshold**: For PAT scheme, must meet specific energy consumption norms notified by Central Government (varies by sector); for SME programmes, must qualify as MSME under MSME Act (investment in plant & machinery or turnover limits); for Standards & Labelling, no turnover threshold specified.
- [ ] **Employee Count**: For PAT scheme, based on energy consumption norms; for SME programmes, as per MSME classification (micro: <10, small: 10–50, medium: 50–250); for other sub-schemes, not specified.
- [ ] **Sector**: Must fall under notified sectors for PAT (energy intensive industries), or be an appliance manufacturer (Standards & Labelling), or be involved in building construction/ECBC implementation, or be an MSME/SDA as applicable.
- [ ] **Age of Business**: Not explicitly specified; compliance with notified norms and rules is required.
- [ ] **Other Conditions**: 
  - For PAT: Must comply with notified energy consumption norms, rules, and regulations; must undergo mandatory energy audit by Accredited Energy Auditor.
  - For Standards & Labelling: Manufacturer must file application online through BEE web portal for star labelling.
  - For SME programmes: Eligibility based on MSME classification; apply through designated channels per programme guidelines.
  - For ECBC: Implementation at state and local level through integration in municipal bye-laws; BEE provides technical support and capacity building.
  - General: Eligibility and benefits vary significantly across sub-schemes (PAT, SME, Standards & Labelling, ECBC, etc.); financial support subject to availability of funds and specific programme guidelines.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation/Registration Certificate
- PAN Card
- Income Tax Returns (last 3 years)
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- **For PAT Scheme**: 
  - Notification letter confirming status as Designated Consumer (DC)
  - Baseline year Specific Energy Consumption (SEC) data
  - Projected SEC for target year
  - Energy audit report from Accredited Energy Auditor
  - Details of energy saving measures implemented/planned
  - Form 1 (Notification of Form 1, 2007) or equivalent
  - Measurement & Verification (M&V) plan
- **For Standards & Labelling**: 
  - Product test reports from NABL-accredited labs
  - Brand registration details
  - Model details for star labelling application
  - Manufacturing unit details
- **For SME Programmes**: 
  - MSME/Udyam registration certificate
  - Project report detailing energy efficiency intervention
  - Quotations for energy efficient equipment
  - Bank sanction letter for financing (if seeking financial support)
- **For ECBC Implementation**: 
  - Building plan approval documents
  - ECBC compliance report from empaneled ECBC Expert
  - Details of building envelope, lighting, HVAC systems
  - Undertaking from State Designated Agency (SDA) or local authority

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size 5 MB per document unless specified otherwise)
- File names must match document description (e.g., "PAT_Baseline_SEC_Data.pdf")
- Certifications must be valid and issued by recognized authorities (e.g., NABL for test labs, BEE for empanelled auditors)
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, certified translation must be attached
- Declarations and undertakings must be signed and stamped by authorized signatory

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- **Required for**: SME Programmes, ECBC implementation, and financing-seeking applications under NMEEE.
- **Not explicitly required for**: PAT scheme (compliance-based) or Standards & Labelling (product certification-based).
- **If required, standard sections include**: Executive Summary, Project Background, Objectives, Methodology, Technical Specifications, Energy Savings Calculation, Financial Analysis, Implementation Schedule, Risk Assessment, Monitoring & Evaluation Plan, Sustainability Measures.
- **If not mentioned, flag**: "Verify with nodal officer whether DPR is required for your specific sub-scheme and activity."

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal at https://beeindia.gov.in/ and navigate to the relevant sub-scheme section (PAT, Standards & Labelling, etc.).
2. Ensure all mandatory fields marked with * are completed; incomplete forms will be rejected.
3. Enter accurate details matching supporting documents (e.g., DC registration number, Udyam ID, product model numbers).
4. For PAT scheme, provide baseline and target year Specific Energy Consumption (SEC) data as per notified norms.
5. For Standards & Labelling, select the correct appliance category and provide test report details from NABL-accredited lab.
6. Upload documents in prescribed format (PDF) and size (typically <5 MB per file).
7. Review the declaration section carefully; ensure it is signed digitally or physically as required.
8. Take a screenshot of the completed form before final submission for record-keeping.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All eligibility criteria verified and documented

- [ ] Application form fields complete and accurate

- [ ] Documents attached in correct format and size
- [ ] Reference numbers/IDs (e.g., Udyam, DC notification) correctly entered
- [ ] Portal-specific instructions followed (e.g., format for SEC data)
- [ ] Final review by senior consultant before submission

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the BEE portal at https://beeindia.gov.in/
- Immediately after submission, take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing the application reference number and timestamp
- Store the reference number securely in the case file
- Set follow-up reminders for Day 25 and Day 35 to check application status
- Save all submission-related communications (emails, portal messages) in the case folder

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from BEE specifying sanctioned benefits (e.g., ESCerts eligibility, financial assistance amount, star labelling certification)
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount or benefits against the approved project proposal
- [ ] For financial assistance: Utilize funds as per approved plan and maintain detailed records of expenditures
- [ ] For PAT scheme: Monitor energy savings, submit Measurement & Verification (M&V) reports to Accredited Energy Auditor, and claim/trade Energy Savings Certificates (ESCerts) on power exchanges
- [ ] For Standards & Labelling: Affix star labels on certified products as per BEE guidelines
- [ ] For ECBC: Ensure construction compliance with ECBC norms and obtain completion certificate from local authority
- [ ] Prepare and submit Utilization Certificate (UC) to BEE as per timelines specified in approval letter
- [ ] Coordinate with client for success fee invoicing as per engagement agreement
- [ ] Maintain all approvals, correspondence, and compliance documents for audit purposes for minimum 5 years
