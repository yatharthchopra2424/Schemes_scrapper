## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the financial benefit delivered, leveraging NMEEE’s mechanisms such as Energy Savings Certificates (ESCerts) trading and financing support under the BEE-WB-SIDBI-GEF project for MSMEs.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile, considering the rolling application basis, multi-year implementation cycles (e.g., PAT cycles of 3 years), and dependency on verification and issuance of tradable instruments.
3. **Transparency**: All fees are disclosed upfront, with retainers covering initial assessment and application preparation, and success fees contingent on verified outcomes like ESCert issuance or financing approval.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: NMEEE involves complex sub-schemes (PAT, SME, Standards & Labelling, ECBC) with varying processing times and success evidence; PAT cycle VII, for example, targets 6.627 MTOE energy savings, indicating high potential value but requiring technical and regulatory compliance, which justifies tiered success fees based on client scale and scheme applicability.
