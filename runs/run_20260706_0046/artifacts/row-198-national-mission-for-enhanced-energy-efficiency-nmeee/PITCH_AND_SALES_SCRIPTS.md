# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping energy-intensive industries and MSMEs access government-backed energy efficiency incentives like the National Mission for Enhanced Energy Efficiency (NMEEE), which offers financial support, technical guidance, and market-based incentives such as tradable Energy Savings Certificates. Before we explore whether this fits your operations, I’d like to understand your current energy use and efficiency goals to ensure we’re aligned.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your primary industry or sector (e.g., Aluminum, Cement, Textile, Thermal Power, etc.)?  
2. Are you classified as a Designated Consumer under the Perform, Achieve and Trade (PAT) scheme based on your energy consumption norms?  
3. What is your annual energy consumption in metric tonnes of oil equivalent (MTOE)?  
4. Have you undergone an energy audit by an Accredited Energy Auditor in the last 3 years?  
5. Are you an MSME as per the MSME Act, and if so, what is your investment in plant and machinery or annual turnover?  
6. Are you a manufacturer of appliances or equipment eligible for star labelling under the Standards & Labelling programme?  
7. Are you involved in commercial building construction or ownership where the Energy Conservation Building Code (ECBC) may apply?  
8. Do you currently work with an Energy Service Company (ESCO) or have plans to implement energy efficiency projects requiring financing or technical support?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Belongs to an energy-intensive sector notified under PAT (Aluminum, Cement, Chlor-Alkali, Fertilizer, Iron & Steel, Paper & Pulp, Railways, Thermal Power, Textile, Petrochemicals, Commercial Buildings, Airports, etc.)  
- [ ] Meets specific energy consumption thresholds to qualify as a Designated Consumer (DC)  
- [ ] Is an MSME as per MSME Act classification  
- [ ] Is a manufacturer of appliances/equipment eligible for star labelling  
- [ ] Is engaged in commercial building projects where ECBC compliance is required  
- [ ] Requires financing or technical support for energy efficiency initiatives  
- [ ] Is located in India (Pan-India scheme)  
- [ ] Has not already availed benefits under the same NMEEE sub-scheme in the current cycle  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your operations may align with the National Mission for Enhanced Energy Efficiency (NMEEE), particularly through its Perform, Achieve and Trade (PAT) scheme or SME-focused programmes. This scheme enables eligible industries to earn tradable Energy Savings Certificates (ESCerts) by exceeding energy efficiency targets—creating a direct financial incentive. Shall I walk you through how it works and what benefits you could access?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**  
Energy-intensive industries and MSMEs face rising energy costs, regulatory pressure to reduce emissions, and limited access to financing for efficiency upgrades. Many lack the technical expertise or awareness to identify savings opportunities or monetize efficiency gains through market mechanisms like certificate trading.

**Scheme intro:**  
The National Mission for Enhanced Energy Efficiency (NMEEE), implemented by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) under the Ministry of Power, Government of India, is a Pan-India initiative promoting energy efficiency through self-regulation and market principles. It includes the Perform, Achieve and Trade (PAT) scheme, which sets energy consumption norms for Designated Consumers and allows trading of Energy Savings Certificates (ESCerts) on power exchanges, creating a financial incentive for over-achievement. Financial support is also available for MSMEs, standards and labelling for appliances, and ECBC implementation in commercial buildings.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**  
- You operate in a sector notified under PAT (e.g., [Client’s Sector from Discovery])  
- Your energy consumption meets or exceeds the threshold for Designated Consumer status  
- As an MSME, you are eligible for dedicated SME programmes under NMEEE offering financing and technical support  
- If applicable, your appliances qualify for star labelling, enabling market differentiation and consumer trust
