### National Mission for Enhanced Energy Efficiency (NMEEE)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), Ministry of Power, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://beeindia.gov.in/
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: MSME; industries; designated consumers; state designated agencies; energy service companies
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Energy intensive sectors (Aluminum, Cement, Chlor-Alkali, Fertilizer, Iron & Steel, Paper & Pulp, Railways, Thermal Power, Textile, Petrochemicals, Commercial Buildings, etc.) for PAT; MSME for SME programmes; manufacturers of appliances for Standards & Labelling; state and local governments for ECBC
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Eligibility varies by sub-scheme; for PAT, notified as Designated Consumers based on specific energy consumption norms; for SME programmes, based on MSME classification; for Standards & Labelling, manufacturers can apply for star rating certification

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support includes innovative financing mechanisms, tradable Energy Savings Certificates (ESCerts), access to financing, technical support, capacity building, star labelling, and implementation support for ECBC; monetary savings from PAT cycle-II estimated at INR 31,445 Crores
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement / Direct credit / Tax offset (varies by sub-mechanism: ESCerts traded on power exchanges, financing via BEE-WB-SIDBI-GEF project)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (for project-based support) / Annual (for ongoing mechanisms like ESCert trading) / 3 years (PAT cycle duration)

#### Documents Required
1. Application form (varies by sub-scheme)
2. Energy audit reports (for PAT and SME programmes)
3. Technical specifications and test reports (for Standards & Labelling)
4. Building plans and compliance documents (for ECBC)
5. MSME registration certificate (for SME programmes)
6. Sector-specific normalization and M&V guidelines (for PAT)
7. Proof of energy consumption baseline (for PAT)
8. Project proposal and financing plan (for financing mechanisms)
9. KYC documents of the applicant
10. Bank details for disbursement

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the relevant sub-scheme (PAT, SME, Standards & Labelling, ECBC, etc.)
2. Check eligibility criteria for the specific sub-scheme
3. Gather required documents (energy audit, technical specs, MSME certificate, etc.)
4. Submit application through the designated channel (online portal for Standards & Labelling, notified process for PAT, state-level for ECBC)
5. Undergo technical scrutiny and verification by BEE or designated agencies
6. Receive approval and notification of acceptance
7. Implement the energy efficiency measures as per guidelines
8. Submit measurement and verification (M&V) reports post-implementation
9. Claim benefits (ESCerts, financial assistance, star rating, etc.) as per sub-scheme rules
