## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the scheme’s non-financial benefit structure (digital health infrastructure enablement, not direct monetary grants).
2. Risk-adjusted fees reflecting the scheme’s voluntary participation model and processing time variability (evidence shows rolling applications with no fixed deadline, leading to uncertain timelines).
3. Transparent fee disclosure tied to measurable outcomes (e.g., ABHA creation, registry integrations, sandbox validations).
4. Scheme-specific note: The National Digital Health Mission (NDHM) / Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) has no direct financial outlay to beneficiaries; revenue potential lies in enabling digital health adoption for health tech companies, facilities, and professionals — making success fees applicable only where tangible operational or market benefits (e.g., ABDM-compliant HMIS demand, credibility, ease of doing business) can be attributed to consulting intervention.
