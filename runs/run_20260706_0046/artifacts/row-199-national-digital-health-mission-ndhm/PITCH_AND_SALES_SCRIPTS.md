# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping healthcare stakeholders—whether you're a citizen, health facility, healthcare professional, health tech company, or state authority—navigate the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) to unlock seamless digital health integration. Today, I’d like to understand your current digital health challenges and see if ABDM’s infrastructure—like ABHA creation, registry integration, or sandbox validation—can support your goals. This is a consultative conversation; I’ll ask a few targeted questions to see if there’s a fit.

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you an individual looking to create and manage your digital health records via an Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA)?
2. If you represent a health facility (hospital, clinic, lab, pharmacy), are you currently registered on the Health Facility Registry (HFR) or planning to join?
3. Are you a healthcare professional (doctor, nurse, allied health worker) seeking to generate a Healthcare Professional ID (HP-ID) for ABDM participation?
4. If you’re a health tech company, are you aiming to integrate your software with ABDM APIs via the sandbox for certification and market credibility?
5. Are you a state health authority evaluating ABDM adoption to improve healthcare planning, data-driven policies, or service accessibility?
6. Do you have an Aadhaar-linked mobile number ready for OTP-based verification during registration?
7. What specific digital health pain point are you trying to solve—such as fragmented records, lack of interoperability, or difficulty accessing lab reports digitally?
8. Are you aware that participation in ABDM is voluntary and requires consent for data sharing under the Health Data Management Policy?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Individual seeking ABHA creation (Aadhaar + mobile + personal details)
- [ ] Health facility ready to register on HFR (facility name, location, services, ownership)
- [ ] Healthcare professional with credentials for HPR registration
- [ ] Health tech company prepared to integrate with ABDM sandbox APIs
- [ ] State authority reviewing ABDM adoption guidelines and dashboard
- [ ] User has Aadhaar ID and linked mobile number for OTP verification
- [ ] Willing to provide consent for data sharing and processing
- [ ] Located in India (Pan-India eligibility)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like ABDM could help you achieve [specific benefit mentioned, e.g., seamless health record sharing, registry integration, or sandbox validation]. The Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission, managed by the National Health Authority, enables exactly this—offering digital infrastructure like ABHA, HFR, HPR, and sandbox access—without direct financial grants but with strong operational and ecosystem benefits. Would you like me to walk you through how it works and what’s involved?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Healthcare stakeholders face fragmented systems: citizens struggle to access and share health records securely; facilities lack a unified registry; professionals can’t easily verify credentials; health tech firms struggle with interoperability; and states find it hard to formulate data-driven health policies. This leads to inefficiencies, duplicated tests, delayed care, and poor healthcare planning.

**Scheme intro:**
The Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM), led by the National Health Authority (NHA) under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, is a digital infrastructure initiative that creates registries, enables interoperability, and provides digital health services. It does not offer direct financial support but delivers value through digital inclusion: citizens get a unique ABHA number to access and share records; health facilities and professionals join national registries; health tech companies gain credibility via ABDM-compliant certification; and states improve healthcare planning through data integration. Access the portal at https://abha.abdm.gov.in/register.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You have an Aadhaar ID and a mobile number linked to it for OTP verification (required for ABHA, HPR, HFR, or sandbox access).
- You are willing to provide informed consent for data sharing and processing (a core ABDM principle).
- You fall under one of the target beneficiary groups: citizen, health facility, healthcare professional, health tech company, or state authority.
- You are located in India, as ABDM has pan-India geographic scope.
