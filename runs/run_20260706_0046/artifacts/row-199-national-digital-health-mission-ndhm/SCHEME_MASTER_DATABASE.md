### National Digital Health Mission (NDHM)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-ography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Health Authority (NHA), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
- **Administering Body:** National Health Authority (NHA), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://abha.abdm.gov.in/register
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Citizens or individuals; health facilities (public and private); healthcare professionals; health tech companies; state governments and health authorities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Healthcare
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Participation is purely voluntary; ABHA creation requires consent for data sharing and processing

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: No direct financial support; digital infrastructure initiative focused on creating registries, enabling interoperability, and providing digital health services
- Disbursement mode: Not applicable (no financial disbursement)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing access to digital health ecosystem

#### Documents Required
1. Aadhaar ID (for ABHA, HPR, HFR registration)
2. Mobile number linked to Aadhaar (for OTP verification)
3. Personal details: Name, Year of Birth, Gender, State, District, Email (for ABHA registration)
4. Facility name, location, operational status, services offered, ownership details (for HFR registration)
5. Healthcare professional details (for HPR registration)
6. Credentials to create an account on e-Sushrut Clinic

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. For citizens: Visit https://abha.abdm.gov.in/register to generate an ABHA number.
2. For health facilities: Visit https://facility.abdm.gov.in/ to register on the Health Facility Registry.
3. For healthcare professionals: Visit https://hpr.abdm.gov.in/en to register on the Healthcare Professionals Registry.
4. For health tech companies: Integrate and validate software systems with ABDM APIs via the ABDM Sandbox at https://sandbox.abdm.gov.in/.
5. For states: Refer to https://abdm.gov.in/resources/for-states for strategic overview and technical guidelines, and check adoption status at https://dashboard.abdm.gov.in/abdm/.
6. For e-Sushrut Clinic registration: Visit https://ehmis-lite.in/AHIMSG5/hissso/logoutLogin, generate or validate HPR ID, generate or validate HFR ID, and complete onboarding.
