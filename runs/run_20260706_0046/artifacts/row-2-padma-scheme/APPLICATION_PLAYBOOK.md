# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The unit is a micro or small enterprise (MSE) located inside or outside an approved PADMA cluster in Haryana.
- [ ] The unit has filed Udyam Registration Certificate (URC) and Haryana Udhyam Memorandum (HUM).
- [ ] The unit is in commercial production.
- [ ] The unit is in regular production at the time of application and will remain in regular production for 5 years from the date of commercial production.
- [ ] The unit is engaged in manufacturing/processing of the approved product category for the PADMA cluster it belongs to.
- [ ] The unit will use the PADMA logo on the product manufactured.
- [ ] The land for the project is arranged by the promoter either by purchase or on a registered lease deed of at least 10 years.
- [ ] The unit availing incentive under this scheme will not obtain incentives under any capital subsidy/similar scheme of other State Government policies.
- [ ] The CLU/NOC/Industrial License obtained by the Implementing Agency (IA) for the approved PADMA cluster is applicable to the unit.
- [ ] The applicant has not obtained/applied for or will not obtain any grant/subsidy from any Central/State Government Ministry/Department/organization/agency for the same purpose/activity/same components.
- [ ] The application is submitted within 03 months from the start of commercial production or from the date of notification/re-notification of the PADMA policy (whichever is later).
- [ ] The unit is located in Haryana (geographic scope of the scheme).

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Udyam Registration Certificate (URC)
- Haryana Udhyam Memorandum (HUM)
- PAN Card
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of last 3 years
- Certificate of Incorporation/Registration
- Memorandum and Articles of Association (or Partnership Deed, if applicable)
- Udyam Registration Portal: https://udyamregistration.gov.in/
- HUM Registration Portal: https://msme.haryana.gov.in/hum-registration/

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Application in the prescribed format (Annexure – I)
- Certificate of incorporation/registration, Memorandum and Articles of Association, Bye laws (if applicable), or partnership deed
- Annual reports and Audited Statement of Accounts of last three years (for expansion/diversification/modernization)
- Land title documents: sale deed or registered lease deed for at least 10 years
- CA certificate regarding the proposed project cost and means of finance
- Declaration that the organization has not obtained/applied for/will not obtain any grant/subsidy from any Central/State Government Ministry/Department/organization/agency for the same purpose/activity/same components
- Latest Udyam Registration Certificate (URC) and Haryana Udhyam Memorandum (HUM)
- Copy of first sale bill
- Board Resolution/Power of Attorney
- GST Certificate
- Copy of list of plant & machinery along with invoices
- Undertaking/Declaration on non-judicial stamp paper
- Land title documents: Allotment Letter/Occupation Certificate/Sale Deed/Transfer Deed/Conveyance Deed in favour of the Applicant Unit/Proprietor
- Copy of CLU/NOC/Industrial License obtained by Implementing Agency (IA) of the approved cluster
- CA Certificate regarding expenses incurred with original invoices related to expenses

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format, and clearly named (e.g., "URC_Certificate.pdf", "GST_Certificate.pdf").
- File size for each document should not exceed 5 MB.
- Names on documents must match exactly as per the application form.
- Certificates must be valid and not expired (where applicable).
- Undertakings/declarations must be on non-judicial stamp paper of appropriate value and duly notarized.
- Land documents must reflect a minimum lease period of 10 years or clear ownership.
- CA certificates must be signed and stamped by a practicing Chartered Accountant.
- All declarations must be signed and dated by the authorized signatory.
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, a certified translation must be attached.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is **not explicitly mandated** for individual unit applications under the PADMA Scheme's Capital Investment Subsidy, Interest Subsidy, or other sub-schemes based on the evidence. However, for certain components like Cluster Infrastructure Development (PCIDS), progress reports and utilization certificates are required at each tranche.  
**Action:** Verify with the nodal officer (Joint Director/Deputy Director, District MSME Centre) whether a DPR is required for your specific sub-scheme (e.g., Capital Investment, Interest Subsidy, etc.).

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the prescribed application form (Annexure – I) from the portal: https://msme.haryana.gov.in/padma-schemes/
2. Fill all fields in block letters; do not leave any mandatory field blank.
3. Attach all scheme-specific and universal documents as per the checklist in Stage 2.
4. Ensure the declaration on non-duplication of subsidy is signed and on non-judicial stamp paper.
5. Enter the correct URC and HUM numbers as issued by the respective portals.
6. Specify the exact sub-scheme you are applying for (e.g., PADMA Capital Investment Subsidy Scheme).
7. Confirm the unit’s location (inside/outside PADMA cluster) and beneficiary category (women/SC/SHG or other) to determine eligibility for subsidy limits.
8. Declare that the unit will use the PADMA logo on manufactured products.
9. Confirm land arrangement (purchase or registered lease ≥10 years).
10. Double-check all entries before submission; errors may lead to rejection or deficiency notice.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All sections of the application form are completely filled.
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached and clearly labeled.
- [ ] Declarations (non-duplication, PADMA logo use, land arrangement) are signed and dated.
- [ ] URC and HUM numbers are valid and match the certificates.
- [ ] Bank account details are correct for subsidy disbursement.
- [ ] The application is being submitted within the 3-month window from commercial production or scheme re-notification (whichever is later).
- [ ] The unit meets the eligibility criteria verified in Stage 1.
- [ ] The correct sub-scheme is selected and indicated in the form.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed application along with all documents via the portal: **https://msme.haryana.gov.in/**
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation page showing the application reference number and timestamp.
- Save the reference number securely for tracking.
- Set a follow-up reminder for 7 days after submission to check for deficiency notices.
- If a deficiency notice is received, rectify and resubmit within 10 days as per scheme guidelines.

- Level 2: Nodal officer – Joint Director/Deputy Director, District MSME Centre (contact details to be obtained from the district office; [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT])

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and securely store the approval letter from the Competent Authority (Head of Department, Directorate of MSME, Haryana).
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount against the eligibility limits (e.g., up to Rs. 30 lakh for women/SC/SHG units inside PADMA cluster under Capital Investment Subsidy).
- [ ] Initiate utilization of funds as per the approved purpose (e.g., capital investment, interest reimbursement, etc.).
- [ ] Prepare and submit Utilization Certificate (UC) and progress reports as required (especially for tranche-based disbursement in schemes like PCIDS).
- [ ] Ensure the unit remains in regular production for 5 years from commercial production date as a continuing eligibility condition.
- [ ] Maintain records of all invoices and expenditure related to the subsidized component for audit and verification.
- [ ] Track and claim subsequent benefits (e.g., annual interest subsidy for up to 5 years) as per scheme timelines.
- [ ] Coordinate with the client to ensure the PADMA logo is used on all manufactured products as per scheme mandate.
- [ ] Confirm that no duplicate subsidies from other State Government schemes are availed during the subsidy period.
