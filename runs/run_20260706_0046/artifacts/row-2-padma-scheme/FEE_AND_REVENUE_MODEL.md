## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, document preparation, and application submission.
2. Success fee is contingent on grant approval and disbursement, aligning consultant incentives with client outcomes.
3. Tiered pricing reflects scheme accessibility: PADMA supports micro and small enterprises (including startups, women/SC/SHG units) inside and outside clusters, with maximum benefits up to ₹30 lakh, making Starter and Growth tiers most relevant.
4. Scheme-specific note: Processing involves application scrutiny, deficiency resolution (within 7 days to notify, 10 days to rectify), and sanction by Competent Authority; typical approval timeline is 3–6 months, introducing revenue lag but low risk due to clear guidelines and high scheme confidence.
