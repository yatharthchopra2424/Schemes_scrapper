# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping micro and small enterprises in Haryana access government financial support schemes like the PADMA Scheme, which provides up to Rs. 30 lakh in capital investment subsidy for eligible units inside PADMA clusters. I’d like to understand your business to see if this scheme aligns with your growth plans.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your enterprise incorporated or registered under Udyam?
2. Are you currently in commercial production, and have you filed both Udyam Registration Certificate (URC) and Haryana Udhyam Memorandum (HUM)?
3. Is your unit located inside or outside an approved PADMA cluster in Haryana?
4. What is the nature of your manufacturing/processing activity—does it match the approved product category for your cluster?
5. Have you arranged land for your project via purchase or a registered lease deed of at least 10 years?
6. Are you availing or planning to avail any other capital subsidy from state government schemes?
7. What is your annual turnover and investment in plant and machinery to confirm MSME classification?
8. Are you willing to use the PADMA logo on your manufactured products as required by the scheme?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Unit is a micro or small enterprise (investment < Rs. 10 Crore, turnover < Rs. 50 Crore)
- [ ] Located inside or outside an approved PADMA cluster in Haryana
- [ ] Has filed Udyam Registration Certificate (URC) and Haryana Udhyam Memorandum (HUM)
- [ ] Currently in commercial production
- [ ] Engaged in manufacturing/processing of the approved product category for the cluster
- [ ] Land arranged via purchase or registered lease deed of at least 10 years
- [ ] Not availing incentives under any other capital subsidy/similar scheme of state government policies
- [ ] Willing to use PADMA logo on products
- [ ] Commitment to remain in regular production for 5 years from date of commercial production

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your business may meet the core eligibility criteria for the PADMA Scheme. Let me walk you through how this scheme can provide up to Rs. 30 lakh in financial support specifically for units like yours.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many micro and small enterprises in Haryana struggle to scale due to high capital costs for machinery, technology upgrades, and working capital—especially when trying to meet compliance requirements or expand within designated industrial clusters like PADMA.

**Scheme intro:**
The PADMA Scheme (Programme to Accelerate Development for MSME Advancement), administered by the Directorate of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Haryana (Department of Industries & Commerce, Haryana), offers financial support including:
- Up to 30% capital investment subsidy (max Rs. 30 lakh for women/SC/SHG units, Rs. 25 lakh for others) inside PADMA clusters
- Interest subsidy reimbursement @6% p.a. or actual rate paid (max Rs. 20 lakh/year inside, Rs. 10 lakh/year outside)
- Entrepreneurship acceleration grant of Rs. 20 lakh inside / Rs. 10 lakh outside per startup for proof of concept
- 50% reimbursement of designing, branding, marketing & exports expenses (max Rs. 10 lakh/year inside, Rs. 5 lakh/year outside)
Access to the scheme is via the portal: https://msme.haryana.gov.in/

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your unit is a registered micro or small enterprise with Udyam and Haryana Udyam Memorandum (HUM)
- You are in commercial production and engaged in an approved product category under PADMA
- You have secured land via purchase or registered lease deed of at least 10 years
- You are not currently availing benefits under any other state capital subsidy scheme
