### PADMA Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Haryana only
- **Administering Body:** Directorate of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Haryana (Department of Industries & Commerce, Haryana)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://msme.haryana.gov.in/
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** 17.08.2023 to 16.08.2028

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Micro and small enterprises (MSEs)
- Age of business: Must be in commercial production
- Sector: Manufacturing/processing of approved product category of the cluster
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have filed Udyam Registration Certificate (URC) and Haryana Udhyam Memorandum (HUM); must remain in regular production for 5 years from date of commercial production; must use PADMA logo on product; land arranged by promoter via purchase or registered lease deed of at least 10 years; not eligible for incentives under any other capital subsidy/similar scheme of State Government policies

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - Capital Investment Subsidy: Up to 30% subsidy on eligible capital investment (max Rs. 30 lakh for women/SC/SHG units, Rs. 25 lakh for others) inside PADMA cluster; half for units outside
  - Interest Subsidy: Reimbursement of interest @6% p.a. or actual rate paid, whichever is less, up to Rs. 20 lakh per financial year inside and Rs. 10 lakh outside
  - Entrepreneurship Acceleration: Financial assistance of Rs. 20 lakh for startups inside and Rs. 10 lakh outside per innovative idea for proof of concept
  - Designing, Branding, Marketing & Exports Promotion: 50% reimbursement of expenses, max Rs. 10 lakh/year inside and Rs. 5 lakh/year outside
  - Cluster Infrastructure Development: 50% to 85% financial assistance, max Rs. 35 Crore for up to 50 acres and Rs. 45 Crore for more than 50 acres, based on block category (B: 50%, C: 75%, D: 85%)
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement / Direct credit (varies by sub-scheme)
- Duration of benefit: 
  - Capital Investment Subsidy: One-time
  - Interest Subsidy: 5 consecutive financial years
  - Entrepreneurship Acceleration: One-time per idea
  - Designing, Branding, Marketing & Exports Promotion: Annual, up to 5 consecutive financial years
  - Cluster Infrastructure Development: Tranche-based disbursement

#### Documents Required
1. Application in the prescribed format (Annexure – I)
2. Certificate of incorporation/ registration of the organization, Memorandum and Articles of Association and Bye laws of the society (If applicable)/partnership deed etc.
3. Annual reports and Audited Statement of Accounts of last three years, in case of expansion/diversification/modernization
4. Land title documents i.e., sale deed or registered lease deed for at least 10 years
5. CA certificate regarding the proposed project cost and means of finance
6. Declaration that the organization has not obtained/applied or will not obtain any grant/subsidy from any Ministry/Department of Central Govt./State Govt. organization/agencies for the same purpose/activity/same components
7. Latest Udyam Registration Certificate (URC) and Haryana Udhyam Memorandum (HUM)
8. Copy of first sale bill
9. Board Resolution/Power of Attorney
10. GST Certificate
11. Copy of list of plant & machinery along with invoices
12. Undertaking/Declaration on non-judicial stamp paper
13. Land title documents i.e., Allotment Letter/Occupation Certificate/Sale Deed/Transfer Deed/Conveyance Deed in favour of the Applicant Unit/Proprietor
14. Copy of CLU/NOC/Industrial License obtained by Implementing Agency (IA) of approved cluster
15. CA Certificate regarding expenses incurred with original invoices related to expenses

#### Application Process
1. Apply on the prescribed form (Annexure-I) for the respective sub-scheme (e.g., Capital Investment Subsidy, Interest Subsidy, etc.) along with required documents.
2. Submit the application to the Director General, Directorate of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Haryana, via the web portal within the stipulated time (e.g., 03 months from start of commercial production or date of scheme notification, whichever is later).
3. The application is scrutinized by the Joint Director/Deputy Director, District MSME Centre.
4. Deficiencies, if any, are communicated in writing within 07 days, with 10 days to rectify.
5. If deficiencies are not rectified, the claim is recommended for rejection.
6. The Competent Authority (Head of Department, Directorate of MSME, Haryana) sanctions the assistance after compliance of formalities.
7. For certain schemes like Cluster Infrastructure Development, progress reports and utilization certificates are required at each tranche of disbursement.
