# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
The **PADMA Scheme** (Programme to Accelerate Development for MSME Advancement) is a state-level initiative implemented by the **Directorate of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Haryana** under the Department of Industries & Commerce, Haryana. It is designed to accelerate MSME development through cluster-based interventions across Haryana. The scheme commenced on **17.08.2023** and remains operational until **16.08.2028** (5 years) or until the validity of the PADMA policy, whichever is earlier. The total fund size is **Rs. 1000 Crore**.

### Objectives
The PADMA Scheme aims to:
- Set up a new MSME cluster in each block on approximately **4000 acres of land**
- Enable the establishment of **10,000+ new enterprises**
- Attract new investment of **INR 25,000–30,000 Crores**
- Create **3,00,000 new employment opportunities** (direct & indirect)
- Encourage holistic socio-economic development and a robust industrial ecosystem at the block level
- Empower Haryana’s youth to set up new enterprises by integrating them into the formal economy
- Promote locally relevant products and enable state farmers to transition from producers to processors
- Implement developmental interventions for micro and small enterprises at the block level using a cluster approach to foster sustainable employment and entrepreneurship

### Eligibility Matrix
Eligibility under the PADMA Scheme varies based on enterprise type, location (inside/outside PADMA cluster), and beneficiary category (women/SC/SHG vs. others). The following table summarizes key eligibility criteria:

| **Criteria** | **Details** |
|--------------|-------------|
| **Enterprise Type** | Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) inside or outside approved PADMA clusters |
| **Registration** | Must have filed Udyam Registration Certificate (URC) and Haryana Udhyam Memorandum (HUM) |
| **Production Status** | Must be in commercial production and regular production at the time of disbursement; subsidy not released to closed units |
| **Production Duration** | Must remain in regular production for **5 years** from the date of commercial production |
| **Product Category** | Must be engaged in manufacturing/processing of the approved product category for the specific cluster |
| **Branding Requirement** | Must use the PADMA logo on manufactured products |
| **Land Requirement** | Land must be arranged by promoter via purchase or registered lease deed of **at least 10 years** |
| **Exclusivity Clause** | Units availing incentives under PADMA are **not eligible** for incentives under any other capital subsidy/similar scheme of the State Government |
| **Cluster Approval** | CLU/NOC/Industrial License obtained by Implementing Agency (IA) for the PADMA cluster is applicable |
| **Target Beneficiaries** | MSMEs; micro enterprises; small enterprises; startups; women entrepreneurs; SC/ST; Self-Help Groups (SHGs) |

### Benefits & Financial Support
Financial support under PADMA is delivered through five distinct sub-schemes, with varying benefits based on location (inside/outside PADMA cluster) and beneficiary category.

#### Capital Investment Subsidy
| **Beneficiary Type** | **Inside PADMA Cluster** | **Outside PADMA Cluster** |
|----------------------|--------------------------|----------------------------|
| Women/SC/SHG-owned units | 30% subsidy on eligible capital investment, **max Rs. 30 lakh** | 15% subsidy, **max Rs. 15 lakh** |
| Other units | 30% subsidy, **max Rs. 25 lakh** | 15% subsidy, **max Rs. 12.50 lakh** |

#### Interest Subsidy
| **Location** | **Benefit** | **Maximum per Financial Year** |
|--------------|-------------|-------------------------------|
| Inside PADMA cluster | Reimbursement of interest @6% p.a. or actual rate paid, whichever is less | **Rs. 20 lakh** |
| Outside PADMA cluster | Reimbursement of interest @6% p.a. or actual rate paid, whichever is less | **Rs. 10 lakh** |
- Available for **5 consecutive financial years** from the date of re-notification of PADMA policy or loan sanction/disbursement, whichever is later

#### Entrepreneurship Acceleration Scheme
| **Location** | **Benefit** |
|--------------|-------------|
| Inside PADMA cluster | Financial assistance of **Rs. 20 lakh** per innovative idea for proof of concept |
| Outside PADMA cluster | Financial assistance of **Rs. 10 lakh** per innovative idea for proof of concept |
- Limited to **maximum 10 startup units per PADMA cluster** and **50 startups outside PADMA cluster** during the policy period (first come, first served)

#### Designing, Branding, Marketing & Exports Promotion Scheme
| **Location** | **Benefit** | **Annual Maximum** |
|--------------|-------------|---------------------|
| Inside PADMA cluster | 50% reimbursement of expenses | **Rs. 10 lakh/year** |
| Outside PADMA cluster | 50% reimbursement of expenses | **Rs. 5 lakh/year** |
- Available for **up to 5 consecutive financial years** from the date of re-notification of PADMA policy or as extended by the State Government

#### Cluster Infrastructure Development Scheme (PCIDS)
| **Block Category** | **Financial Assistance** | **Maximum Limit** |
|--------------------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| **B** | 50% | **Rs. 35 Crore** (for up to 50 acres) |
| **C** | 75% | **Rs. 45 Crore** (for more than 50 acres) |
| **D** | 85% | **Rs. 45 Crore** (for more than 50 acres) |
- For **up to 50 acres**: Max Rs. 35 Crore
- For **more than 50 acres**: Max Rs. 45 Crore
- **Grant shall not be used for procurement/purchase of land**

### Application Process
The application process for the PADMA Scheme is standardized across sub-schemes and involves the following steps:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Start: Identify Eligible Sub-scheme] --> B[Apply on Prescribed Form (Annexure-I) with Required Documents]
    B --> C[Submit Application via Web Portal: https://msme.haryana.gov.in/]
    C --> D[Application Scrutinized by Joint/Deputy Director, District MSME Centre]
    D --> E{Deficiencies Found?}
    E -->|Yes| F[Deficiencies Communicated in Writing within 07 Days]
    F --> G[10 Days to Rectify Deficiencies]
    G --> H{Deficiencies Rectified?}
    H -->|No| I[Claim Recommended for Rejection]
    H -->|Yes| J[Competent Authority Sanctions Assistance]
    E -->|No| J
    J --> K[For Certain Schemes (e.g., PCIDS): Submit Progress Reports & Utilization Certificates at Each Tranche]
    K --> L[End: Disbursement of Assistance]
```

#### Key Application Rules
- Applicants must apply within **03 months** from the **later** of:
  - Start of commercial production
  - Date of scheme notification (17.08.2023)
  - Date of re-notification of PADMA policy
- Application must be submitted via the **official portal**: https://msme.haryana.gov.in/
- Required documents include:
  1. Application in prescribed format (Annexure-I)
  2. Certificate of incorporation/registration, MoA, AoA, bye-laws, partnership deed
  3. Annual reports and audited statements of accounts (last 3 years) for expansion/diversification/modernization
  4. Land title documents (sale deed or registered lease deed for ≥10 years)
  5. CA certificate on proposed project cost and means of finance
  6. Declaration of no prior/current/future grant/subsidy from Central/State Govt. for same purpose
  7. Latest Udyam Registration Certificate (URC) and Haryana Udhyam Memorandum (HUM)
  8. Copy of first sale bill
  9. Board Resolution/Power of Attorney
  10. GST Certificate
  11. List of plant & machinery with invoices
  12. Undertaking/Declaration on non-judicial stamp paper
  13. Land title documents (Allotment Letter/Occupation Certificate/Sale Deed/Transfer Deed/Conveyance Deed)
  14. Copy of CLU/NOC/Industrial License obtained by IA for approved cluster
  15. CA certificate on expenses incurred with original invoices

### Key Caveats
> **Exclusivity**: Units availing incentives under PADMA cannot access incentives under any other capital subsidy/similar scheme of the State Government.  
> **Production Commitment**: Units must remain in regular production for **5 years** from commercial production date.  
> **Land Tenure**: Land must be held via purchase or registered lease deed of **minimum 10 years**.  
> **Cluster Caps**:  
> - Capital Investment Subsidy: Max 20 units in first 5 PADMA clusters, 10 units in clusters 6–15, 5 units in clusters 16+, and 100 units outside PADMA block (first come, first served)  
> - Entrepreneurship Acceleration: Max 10 startups per PADMA cluster, 50 outside (first come, first served)  
> - Interest Subsidy: Available for **5 consecutive financial years** from policy re-notification or loan sanction  
> - Designing/Branding/Marketing: Available for **up to 5 consecutive financial years** from policy re-notification  
> - PCIDS: Grant **cannot be used for land procurement**
