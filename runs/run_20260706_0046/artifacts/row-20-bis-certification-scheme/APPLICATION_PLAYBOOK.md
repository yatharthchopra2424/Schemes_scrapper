# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Manufacturer seeking product certification must comply with relevant Indian Standard for the product  
- [ ] Jeweller or assaying centre seeking hallmarking must meet BIS requirements for hallmarking  
- [ ] Laboratory seeking recognition must meet IS/ISO/IEC 17025 criteria  
- [ ] Manufacturer of notified electronics and IT goods must register under Compulsory Registration Scheme (MeitY order)  
- [ ] Applicant must identify the relevant Indian Standard for the product or service  
- [ ] Applicant must ensure compliance with the standard through appropriate manufacturing and quality control  
- [ ] Applicant must be prepared to undergo factory inspection and testing by BIS or recognized labs  
- [ ] Applicant must be willing to pay applicable fees for licensing, testing, and certification  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Application form  
- Proof of factory address  
- Product details and specifications  
- Quality control arrangements  
- Test reports from BIS or recognized labs  
- Fee payment proof  
- PAN card  
- ITR (last 2–3 years)  
- GST registration certificate  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- For product certification: Evidence of compliance with relevant Indian Standard (e.g., test reports, manufacturing process details)  
- For hallmarking: Details of jewellery items, purity standards, and hallmarking centre infrastructure  
- For laboratory recognition: ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation documents, scope of testing, equipment calibration records  
- For compulsory registration: Test reports from BIS-recognized labs for notified electronics and IT goods  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size 5 MB per document)  
- Document names must match the applicant’s legal name as per PAN and Udyam  
- Affidavits and declarations must be notarized where required  
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, certified translation must be attached  
- Fee payment proof must show transaction ID, amount, and date, and must be from the applicant’s bank account

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not explicitly required for BIS certification; however, applicants must document compliance with the relevant Indian Standard, including manufacturing process, quality control, and test results. Verify with nodal officer if a detailed project report is needed for your specific product category.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Identify the correct Indian Standard (IS) for your product or service via the BIS website  
2. Ensure all fields in the online form are filled accurately, matching details in uploaded documents  
3. Upload documents in the specified format (PDF, max 5 MB per file)  
4. Pay the applicable fee online through the portal (licensing, inspection, and marking fees as applicable)  
5. Select the correct scheme type (product certification, hallmarking, laboratory recognition, or compulsory registration)  
6. Provide accurate factory address and contact details for BIS inspection  
7. Declare that the product complies with the relevant Indian Standard and that quality control systems are in place  
8. Double-check all entries before final submission to avoid rejection  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All application form fields completed accurately  
- [ ] All required documents uploaded and legible  
- [ ] Fee payment proof attached and verified  
- [ ] Declarations signed and dated  
- [ ] Product standard correctly identified and compliance evidence provided  
- [ ] Contact details match those in factory address proof  
- [ ] Application submitted under the correct scheme category

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via www.manakonline.in  
- Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing the application reference number  
- Store the reference number securely for tracking  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days after submission to check status

### Escalation Path
- Level 1: Portal helpdesk via www.manakonline.in (support email: contact@bis.org.in or via portal contact form)  
- Level 2: Nodal Officer – Head, Laboratory Recognition & Management Department, 312, Manak Bhavan, 9 Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg, New Delhi – 110002 | Email: lrmd-bis[at]bis[dot]gov[dot]in | Phone: +91 11 23230860  
- Level 3: RTI filing if no response within statutory SLA (typically 30 days for initial response)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Retrieve and store the approval licence/registration certificate from the portal  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned licence type, validity period, and scope of products covered  
- [ ] Arrange for utilization of the BIS Standard Mark on products and packaging  
- [ ] Prepare for periodic surveillance and renewal as per scheme requirements (e.g., annual licence fee, factory inspection)  
- [ ] Maintain records of test reports and quality control for renewal purposes  
- [ ] Coordinate with the client to ensure successful use of the licence in market access and government tenders  
- [ ] Process success fee invoice upon confirmation of licence grant
