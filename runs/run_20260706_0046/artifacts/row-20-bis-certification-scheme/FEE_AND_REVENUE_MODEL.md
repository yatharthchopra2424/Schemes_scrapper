## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the tangible benefits of BIS certification, including market access, legal compliance, and eligibility for government tenders.
2. Tiered structure reflects client scale and certification scope, with lower success fees for larger clients due to higher contract values and longer engagement periods.
3. Success fees are contingent on successful grant of licence or registration, mitigating client risk while ensuring consultant accountability.
4. Scheme-specific note: BIS certification involves moderate risk due to factory inspections, testing requirements, and variable processing times (typically 3–6 months based on evidence of inspection and testing cycles), but success rates are high for compliant applicants, as evidenced by the large number of operative licences (29,516 as of 2014–15) and consistent year-round issuance.
