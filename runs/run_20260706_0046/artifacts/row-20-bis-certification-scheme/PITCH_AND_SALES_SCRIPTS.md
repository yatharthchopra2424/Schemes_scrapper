# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping manufacturers, laboratories, jewellers, and other businesses achieve compliance and market access through the BIS Certification Scheme. Whether you're looking to use the ISI mark, hallmark your jewellery, get your lab recognised, or register electronics for compulsory certification, I can guide you through the process. Let’s start by understanding your business and goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of product, service, or facility are you seeking certification for (e.g., manufactured goods, jewellery hallmarking, laboratory testing, electronics)?
2. Have you identified the relevant Indian Standard(s) applicable to your product or service?
3. Is your manufacturing or testing facility already compliant with BIS requirements, or do you need guidance on achieving compliance?
4. Are you pursuing voluntary certification, or is your product under a compulsory registration order (e.g., electronics and IT goods)?
5. Do you have a quality control system in place, and is it documented?
6. Have you previously applied for BIS certification, hallmarking, or lab recognition?
7. What is your timeline for obtaining certification or registration?
8. Are you targeting government tenders or export markets where BIS recognition is required or preferred?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Manufacturer seeking product certification (voluntary or compulsory)
- [ ] Jeweller or assaying centre seeking hallmarking licence
- [ ] Laboratory seeking BIS recognition under IS/ISO/IEC 17025
- [ ] Manufacturer of notified electronics/IT goods applying for compulsory registration
- [ ] Has identified relevant Indian Standard(s)
- [ ] Has or can implement quality control arrangements
- [ ] Can provide proof of factory address and product specifications
- [ ] Prepared to undergo factory inspection and testing
- [ ] Willing to pay applicable fees (licensing, testing, marking)
- [ ] Targets market access, consumer trust, or government tenders

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like the BIS Certification Scheme could help you achieve legal compliance, consumer trust, and market access—including eligibility for government tenders. Let me explain how it works and what benefits you can expect.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Manufacturers, jewellers, and laboratories often struggle to prove product quality and compliance, which limits market access, consumer trust, and eligibility for government contracts. Without BIS certification or recognition, businesses face barriers in both domestic and international markets.

**Scheme intro:**
The BIS Certification Scheme, administered by the Bureau of Indian Standards (under the Department of Consumer Affairs, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution), offers product certification, hallmarking for jewellers, laboratory recognition, and compulsory registration for electronics and IT goods. While BIS does not provide direct financial support, the scheme delivers significant non-financial benefits including legal compliance, consumer trust, market access, quality assurance, hallmarking purity guarantee, the right to use the BIS standard mark, access to government tenders, and recognition under national and international frameworks.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You manufacture products or provide services that fall under BIS’s scope of standardization and conformity assessment.
- You have or can establish quality control arrangements and product specifications as required.
- You are willing to comply with relevant Indian Standards and undergo inspection and testing.
- You seek benefits such as the ISI/BIS mark, hallmarking purity, or eligibility for government tenders.
