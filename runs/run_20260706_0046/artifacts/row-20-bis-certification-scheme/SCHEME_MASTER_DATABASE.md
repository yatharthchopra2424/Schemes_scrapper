### BIS Certification Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Bureau of Indian Standards (under Department of Consumer Affairs, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution)
- **Portal / Application URL:** www.manakonline.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: manufacturers; laboratories; jewellers; consumers; industry
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Varies by scheme (product certification, hallmarking, laboratory recognition, compulsory registration)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Eligibility varies by scheme: For product certification, manufacturers must comply with relevant Indian standards; for hallmarking, jewellers and assaying centres must meet BIS requirements; for laboratory recognition, outside labs must meet IS/ISO/IEC 17025 criteria; for compulsory registration, electronics and IT goods manufacturers must register under the order notified by MeitY

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Benefits include legal compliance, consumer trust, market access, quality assurance, hallmarking purity guarantee, ability to use BIS standard mark, access to government tenders, and recognition under various national and international frameworks
- Disbursement mode: Not applicable (no direct financial support)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing (licence/registration validity varies by scheme)

#### Documents Required
1. Application form
2. Proof of factory address
3. Product details and specifications
4. Quality control arrangements
5. Test reports from BIS or recognized labs
6. Fee payment proof

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the relevant Indian Standard for the product or service.
2. Ensure compliance with the standard through appropriate manufacturing and quality control.
3. Apply online via the BIS portal (www.manakonline.in) for licensing or registration.
4. Submit required documents and pay applicable fees.
5. Undergo factory inspection and testing by BIS or recognized labs.
6. Upon successful evaluation, grant of licence or registration is issued.
