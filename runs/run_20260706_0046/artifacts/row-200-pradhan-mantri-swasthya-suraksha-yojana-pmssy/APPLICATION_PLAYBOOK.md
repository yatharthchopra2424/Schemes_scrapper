## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The beneficiary is seeking treatment at an AIIMS or upgraded government medical college established under PMSSY  
- [ ] The beneficiary is a medical student seeking admission to a course at an AIIMS or upgraded government medical college under PMSSY  
- [ ] The beneficiary is a healthcare professional seeking training or employment at a PMSSY-supported institution  
- [ ] The institution in question is confirmed to be part of the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY) network (new AIIMS or upgraded government medical college)  
- [ ] The service sought (treatment, admission, training) is available at the institution under its standard procedures  

> **Note**: PMSSY does not have direct beneficiary eligibility criteria; access is mediated through institutional services. Verification focuses on confirming institutional participation under PMSSY.

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- [ ] For treatment: Medical referral letter or prescription from a registered medical practitioner  
- [ ] For admission: Academic transcripts, mark sheets, and qualifying exam scorecards (e.g., NEET-UG, NEET-PG, INI-CET) as required by the respective AIIMS or institution  
- [ ] For training/employment: Professional qualification certificates, registration with relevant medical council (MCI/NMC or state council), and experience certificates  
- [ ] Proof of reservation category (if applicable): Caste certificate, disability certificate, or other relevant documents as per institutional reservation policy

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not applicable. PMSSY does not require a Detailed Project Report (DPR) from individual beneficiaries, as the scheme focuses on infrastructure development and service delivery through institutions. Individual access is via standard institutional procedures.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
Not applicable. There is no application form for individual beneficiaries under PMSSY. Access to benefits is governed by the admission, treatment, or training procedures of the respective AIIMS or upgraded medical college.  

**Protocol Notes**:  
- Do not attempt to fill a central PMSSY application form — none exists for end beneficiaries.  
- Focus on completing institution-specific processes:  
  - For admission: Use portals like [INI-CET](https://aiimsexams.ac.in/) or state/UGC counselling portals.  
  - For treatment: Register at the hospital’s OPD or emergency desk.  
  - For training: Apply via institutional notifications or the Ministry’s training portals.  
- Verify all procedural requirements directly with the nodal institution (AIIMS or upgraded medical college).  
- Keep records of all communications and transaction slips for reference.  
- Ensure compliance with institutional deadlines and eligibility norms (e.g., age, qualifying exam scores).  
- Retain copies of submitted documents and acknowledgements.  
- If uncertain about institutional PMSSY status, confirm with the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Client’s purpose (treatment/admission/training) is clearly defined

- [ ] Client communication includes scheme name, implementing agency, and access pathway

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
Not applicable. There is no central submission under PMSSY for individual beneficiaries.  
- For admission: Submit via the institution’s admission portal (e.g., INI-CET, NEET counselling) and save confirmation slip.  
- For treatment: Register at the hospital counter and collect OPD/registration slip.  
- For training: Submit application as per institutional notification and retain acknowledgement.  
- Store all reference numbers, slips, and communication records in the case file.  
- Set follow-up reminders based on institutional timelines (e.g., admission result dates, OPD appointment schedules).
