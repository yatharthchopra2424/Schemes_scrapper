## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial value delivered through central government grants for healthcare infrastructure under PMSSY, ensuring consultant compensation scales with project magnitude.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Returns**: Success fees account for the long implementation timelines and execution risks inherent in large-scale public infrastructure projects, including civil works, medical equipment procurement, and institutional readiness.
3. **Transparency & Predictability**: Retainer fees cover initial advisory, eligibility mapping, and documentation preparation, providing clients with predictable upfront costs for engaging in complex central sector schemes.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: PMSSY involves infrastructure development with benefits realized through ongoing hospital services and academic admissions, resulting in extended processing and realization periods. Success is measured by institutional completion and operational readiness rather than individual disbursements, requiring sustained engagement and milestone-based tracking.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Advisory services for Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY) – including scheme eligibility assessment, project structuring support, documentation for central financial assistance application, and liaison with Ministry of Health and Family Welfare for capital cost funding of new AIIMS or upgradation of government medical colleges.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY) – payable upon confirmation of central government grant disbursement for capital works (civil, medical equipment, infrastructure) by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, implementing agency for new AIIMS establishment or upgradation of existing government medical colleges.
