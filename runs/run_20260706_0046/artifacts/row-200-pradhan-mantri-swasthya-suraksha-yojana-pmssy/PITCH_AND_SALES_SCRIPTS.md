## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping healthcare institutions and medical education providers access government infrastructure funding schemes. Given your work in the healthcare or medical education sector, I wanted to understand if you might benefit from central financial assistance for setting up new AIIMS or upgrading existing government medical colleges under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY).

### Discovery Questions
- Are you involved in the planning, development, or operation of a government medical college or healthcare institution seeking infrastructure upgrades?
- Is your institution looking to establish new tertiary healthcare facilities or expand medical education capacity in an underserved region?
- Do you require financial support for civil works, medical equipment, or other infrastructure development for a government medical college?
- Is your institution aiming to correct regional imbalances in access to affordable tertiary healthcare services?
- Are you seeking to augment facilities for quality medical education, such as increasing specialist doctor availability?
- Has your institution been identified for potential upgradation or is it involved in setting up a new AIIMS-like facility?
- Are you looking for central government grants to support capital expenditure on healthcare infrastructure?
- Does your institution serve a target beneficiary group that includes the general public, medical students, or healthcare professionals seeking improved healthcare delivery?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Institution is a government medical college or healthcare facility
- [ ] Seeking to establish new AIIMS or upgrade existing government medical college
- [ ] Requires central financial assistance for capital cost (civil works, medical equipment, infrastructure)
- [ ] Aims to improve access to affordable tertiary healthcare services
- [ ] Focused on augmenting medical education facilities or specialist doctor availability
- [ ] Located in or serving an underserved region with regional imbalances in healthcare
- [ ] Aligns with objectives of setting up new AIIMS-like institutions or upgrading government medical colleges
- [ ] Beneficiaries include general public, medical students, or healthcare professionals

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your institution may be eligible for central financial assistance under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY), which provides grants for setting up new AIIMS and upgrading government medical colleges to improve healthcare access and medical education. Let me explain how this scheme works and how we can support your application.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many government medical colleges and healthcare institutions face challenges in accessing sufficient funds for infrastructure development, leading to regional imbalances in affordable tertiary healthcare services and limitations in medical education capacity, particularly in underserved areas.

**Scheme intro:**
The Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY), administered by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, provides central financial assistance for the capital cost of setting up new AIIMS and upgrading existing government medical colleges. The funding supports civil works, medical equipment, and other infrastructure development through central government grants to implementing agencies.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your institution is a government medical college or healthcare facility seeking infrastructure upgrades
