### Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
- **Portal / Application URL:** [https://pmssy-mohfw.nic.in](https://pmssy-mohfw.nic.in)
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Not applicable (scheme targets individuals and institutions, not businesses)
- Age of business: Not applicable
- Sector: Healthcare (tertiary care and medical education)
- Revenue cap: Not applicable
- Employee count: Not applicable
- Other: Benefits are accessed by patients seeking treatment at upgraded/newly established AIIMS and government medical colleges, and by students seeking admission to medical courses at these institutions; specific eligibility for treatment or admission is determined by the respective institutions

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Central financial assistance for capital cost of setting up new AIIMS and upgradation of existing government medical colleges (civil works, medical equipment, infrastructure development)
- Disbursement mode: Central government grants to implementing agencies
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing (benefits realized through sustained hospital services and academic operations post-infrastructure completion)

#### Documents Required
1. Not applicable for direct beneficiary access (benefits availed through institutional services)
2. For treatment: Standard hospital admission/registration documents as per respective AIIMS/medical college
3. For admission: Academic eligibility documents as per institution-specific admission criteria (e.g., NEET scorecard, 10+2 marksheet)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the nearest AIIMS or upgraded government medical college under PMSSY
2. For treatment: Visit the hospital’s Visit the hospital’s outpatient department (in person
3. For admission: Check admission notifications released by the respective institution
4. Apply through the institution’s standard admission or treatment procedure
5. Avail benefits upon successful registration for treatment or enrollment in academic program
