# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a State Government or Union Territory (UT)  
- [ ] Proposal is submitted through a State Annual Action Plan (SAAP)  
- [ ] SAAP aligns with National AYUSH Mission (NAM) guidelines and national AYUSH priorities  
- [ ] Project proposal includes one or more of the following:  
  - Upgrading AYUSH hospitals and dispensaries  
  - Setting up integrated AYUSH hospitals  
  - Co-locating AYUSH facilities at PHCs/CHCs/DHs  
  - Establishing AYUSH wellness centres  
  - Supporting cultivation and conservation of medicinal plants  
  - Strengthening AYUSH educational institutions  
  - Promoting AYUSH research and development  
- [ ] Land for infrastructure projects is free from encumbrances and available for government use  
- [ ] Cost-sharing ratio understood: 60:40 (Centre:State) for most states, 90:10 for Northeastern and Himalayan states  
- [ ] SAAP submitted before the start of the financial year (annual cycle)  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation/Registration Certificate  
- PAN Card  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of last 3 years  
- GST Registration Certificate  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (if applicable)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- State Annual Action Plan (SAAP)  
- Utilization Certificates (UCs) for previous fund releases (if applicable)  
- Progress Reports (Physical and Financial)  
- Audit Reports  
- Details of land availability and ownership  
- Architectural and structural plans for infrastructure projects  
- List of equipment and medicines to be procured  
- Human resource deployment plan  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be self-attested by the applicant  
- File size: Each document < 10 MB; combined ZIP < 25 MB  
- File naming: Use format `SchemeName_DocumentType_StateName_Date.pdf` (e.g., `NAM_SAP_Maharashtra_2026.pdf`)  
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in regional language, provide certified translation  
- Scanned copies must be clear, legible, and in PDF format  
- Certificates (e.g., land ownership) must be issued by competent authority and valid

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required for infrastructure projects under NAM. Standard sections include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Background and Justification  
- Objectives and Outcomes  
- Site Details and Land Information  
- Architectural and Structural Plans  
- Equipment and Medicines Procurement Plan  
- Human Resource Plan  
- Implementation Schedule  
- Budget Estimate (Centre and State shares)  
- Sustainability and Maintenance Plan  
- Monitoring and Evaluation Framework  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use only the official application format prescribed by the Ministry of Ayush for SAAP submission.  
2. All fields must be filled; leave no blank entries. If not applicable, write “NA” or “Not Applicable”.  
3. Attach all supporting documents as per the document checklist and reference them in the form.  
4. Self-attest all documents; unauthenticated documents will lead to rejection.  
5. Clearly indicate the financial year for which SAAP is being submitted.  
6. Specify the cost-sharing ratio applicable to your state (60:40 or 90:10).  
7. Ensure the SAAP aligns with NAM guidelines and national AYUSH priorities (refer to scheme objectives).  
8. Declaration must be signed by the authorized signatory (e.g., Secretary, Health Department) with date and seal.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All eligibility criteria verified and documented  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents collected and self-attested  
- [ ] Application form filled completely with no blank fields  
- [ ] Documents attached and referenced correctly in the form  
- [ ] Declarations signed and dated  
- [ ] SAAP aligns with NAM objectives (e.g., upgrading AYUSH hospitals, medicinal plant cultivation, etc.)  
- [ ] Land documents confirm encumbrance-free status  
- [ ] Human resource and equipment lists are detailed and justified  
- [ ] Budget breakdown shows Centre and State shares as per cost-sharing norm

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed SAAP along with all documents to the Ministry of Ayush via the designated portal or nodal agency.  
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation showing timestamp and reference number.  
- Store the reference number securely in the case file.  
- Set follow-up reminders for:  
  - Day 25: Check application status  
  - Day 35: Follow up if no acknowledgment received  
  - Day 45: Escalate if no response

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and save the approval letter from the Ministry of Ayush  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount against the approved SAAP  
- [ ] Confirm the installment release schedule as per NAM guidelines  
- [ ] Prepare Utilization Certificate (UC) format for each installment  
- [ ] Ensure progress reporting mechanism is set up (quarterly physical and financial reports)  
- [ ] Confirm invoice submission process for success fee (if applicable under consultancy agreement)  
- [ ] Monitor field visits and third-party evaluations as part of scheme monitoring  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with cost-sharing ratio (60:40 or 90:10) in fund utilization  
- [ ] Maintain records of all communications, approvals, and reports for audit purposes
