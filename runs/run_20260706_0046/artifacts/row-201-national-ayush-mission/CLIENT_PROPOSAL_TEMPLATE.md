## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified National AYUSH Mission — a grant scheme administered by Ministry of Ayush, Government of India — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is financial assistance for upgrading AYUSH hospitals and dispensaries, setting up integrated AYUSH hospitals, co-locating AYUSH facilities at PHCs/CHCs/DHs, establishing AYUSH wellness centres, supporting cultivation and conservation of medicinal plants, strengthening AYUSH educational institutions, and promoting AYUSH research and development.

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment and scheme suitability analysis  
2. Preparation of State Annual Action Plan (SAAP) as per scheme guidelines  
3. Compilation and verification of required documents  
4. Submission of SAAP to Ministry of Ayush for appraisal and approval  
5. Liaison with Ministry of Ayush during review and approval process  
6. Support in fund utilization and installment tracking post-approval  
7. Portal registration on https://ayush.gov.in and ongoing compliance monitoring  

*Note: A DPR/project report is required for infrastructure projects under this scheme.*
