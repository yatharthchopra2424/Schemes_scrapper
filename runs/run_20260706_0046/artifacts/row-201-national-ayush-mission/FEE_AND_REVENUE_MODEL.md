## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees aligned with the substantial financial benefit delivered (grant-in-aid for State Annual Action Plans under NAM, often running into crores).
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect moderate risk due to annual cycle dependency, utilization certificate requirements, and cost-sharing ratios (60:40 or 90:10).
3. **Transparency**: Clear linkage between retainer (for SAAP preparation) and success fee (upon fund release).
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The National AYUSH Mission operates on an annual cycle with funds released in installments contingent on utilization certificates and progress reports. Processing time typically spans 6–12 months from SAAP submission to first installment, affecting revenue lag. Evidence of success is high for states with compliant SAAPs, but delays occur if documentation is incomplete.
