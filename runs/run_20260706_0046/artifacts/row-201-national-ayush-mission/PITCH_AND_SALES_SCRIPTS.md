# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping state governments, AYUSH institutions, farmers, and medicinal plant growers access government grants for healthcare and wellness initiatives. The National AYUSH Mission is designed to strengthen AYUSH services across India by supporting infrastructure, cultivation, education, and research. I’d like to understand your current initiatives and see if this scheme aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of AYUSH-related initiative are you currently planning or implementing (e.g., hospital upgradation, medicinal plant cultivation, wellness centre, educational institution support)?
2. Are you representing a State Government, Union Territory, AYUSH institution, farmer group, or organization involved in medicinal plant conservation?
3. Have you prepared or are you planning to submit a State Annual Action Plan (SAAP) for the upcoming financial year?
4. What specific components are you seeking funding for—infrastructure, equipment, medicines, human resources, or program management?
5. Do you have land available that is free from encumbrances and suitable for government use for infrastructure projects?
6. Are you able to provide utilization certificates and progress reports for fund releases, as required by the scheme?
7. What is your timeline for project implementation, and are you aligned with the annual SAAP submission cycle?
8. Have you previously accessed central sponsored schemes, and what challenges did you face in documentation or reporting?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is a State Government or Union Territory
- [ ] Initiative supports AYUSH institutions, farmers, medicinal plant growers, or conservation organizations
- [ ] Project proposal is submitted via State Annual Action Plan (SAAP)
- [ ] Land for infrastructure is free from encumbrances and available for government use
- [ ] Commitment to submit utilization certificates and progress reports
- [ ] Project aligns with NAM guidelines and national AYUSH priorities
- [ ] Understanding of cost-sharing ratio (60:40 for most states, 90:10 for Northeastern and Himalayan states)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your initiative aligns well with the National AYUSH Mission, which provides grant-in-aid for infrastructure, equipment, medicines, and human resources based on approved SAAPs. Shall I walk you through how this scheme can support your specific project?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
State governments and AYUSH stakeholders often struggle to secure funding for upgrading AYUSH hospitals, setting up wellness centres, cultivating medicinal plants, or strengthening educational institutions due to limited budgets and complex grant processes.

**Scheme intro:**
The National AYUSH Mission (NAM), administered by the Ministry of Ayush, Government of India, is a centrally sponsored scheme providing grant-in-aid to State Governments and Union Territories based on approved State Annual Action Plans (SAAPs). Financial support covers infrastructure development, procurement of equipment, supply of medicines, human resource engagement, and program management, released in installments subject to utilization certificates and progress reports.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a State Government/UT or represent an eligible AYUSH institution, farmer group, or conservation organization
- Your project is being formulated through a State Annual Action Plan (SAAP)
- You have or can secure land free from encumbrances for government use
- You commit to submitting utilization certificates and progress reports for fund releases
