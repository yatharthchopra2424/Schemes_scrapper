### National AYUSH Mission
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Ayush, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://ayush.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Annual cycle — SAAPs are typically submitted before the start of each financial year for approval.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: State Governments and Union Territories
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Healthcare (AYUSH systems)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Eligibility is based on state-specific project proposals submitted through State Annual Action Plans (SAAPs) approved by the Ministry of Ayush. The scheme supports AYUSH institutions, farmers engaged in medicinal plant cultivation, and organizations involved in the conservation and sustainable management of medicinal plants.

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Grant-in-aid to State Governments and Union Territories based on approved State Annual Action Plans (SAAPs). Funding covers infrastructure development, procurement of equipment, supply of medicines, human resource engagement, and program management.
- Disbursement mode: Released in installments subject to utilization certificates and progress reports.
- Duration of benefit: Annual cycle (funding released in installments per financial year)

#### Documents Required
1. State Annual Action Plan (SAAP)
2. Utilization Certificates (UCs)
3. Progress Reports (Physical and Financial)
4. Audit Reports
5. Details of land availability and ownership
6. Architectural and structural plans for infrastructure projects
7. List of equipment and medicines to be procured
8. Human resource deployment plan

#### Application Process
1. State Governments/UTs prepare State Annual Action Plans (SAAPs) based on local needs and scheme guidelines.
2. SAAPs are submitted to the Ministry of Ayush for appraisal and approval.
3. Upon approval, funds are released in installments to the State Nodal Agency.
4. States implement projects through designated implementing agencies.
5. Progress is monitored through quarterly reports, utilization certificates, and field visits.
6. Subsequent releases are contingent on satisfactory utilization of previous installments.
