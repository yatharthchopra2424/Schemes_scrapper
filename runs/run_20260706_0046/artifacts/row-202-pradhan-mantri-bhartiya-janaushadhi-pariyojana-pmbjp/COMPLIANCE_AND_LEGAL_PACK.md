**Data Classification:** All client data related to scheme applications shall be classified as "Confidential" or "Highly Confidential" based on sensitivity.

**Storage:** All data shall be stored in encrypted cloud storage with AES-256 encryption. Local storage on personal devices is strictly prohibited.

**Access Control:** Access to client data shall be role-based and limited to authorized personnel only. Multi-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for all access.

**Data Transmission:** All data transfers shall occur over encrypted channels (TLS 1.2 or higher). No data shall be transmitted via unsecured email or public networks.

**Retention:** Client data shall be retained for a period of two (2) years post case closure, after which it shall be securely deleted unless otherwise required by law or scheme-specific guidelines.

**Scheme-specific note:** All documents submitted to Pharmaceuticals & Medical Devices Bureau of India (PMBI) under Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP)
must be retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit purposes.

**Breach Notification:** In the event of a data breach, the firm shall notify affected clients and relevant authorities within 24 hours of discovery.

**Disposal:** Secure deletion methods (e.g., cryptographic erasure, physical destruction) shall be used for data disposal.

**Compliance:** This policy aligns with the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the SPDI Rules, 2011, and is subject to periodic review and audit.
