## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment with client outcome – fees correlate directly with the financial benefit secured (e.g., special incentive of ₹2.00 lakhs or ongoing 20% margin on MRP).
2. Risk-adjusted pricing – success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate processing time (typically 3–6 months for approval and incentive disbursement) and high success rate for eligible applicants who meet documentation and stocking mandate requirements.
3. Transparency – retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, document preparation, and application submission; success fee is contingent on verified disbursement of incentives.
4. Scheme-specific note – The Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) offers a one-time special incentive of ₹2.00 lakhs for eligible categories (women entrepreneurs, Divyang, SC/ST, ex-servicemen, aspirational districts, Himalayan, Island, North-Eastern states) plus a normal incentive of up to ₹20,000/month (20% of monthly purchases from PMBI, capped). The special incentive is disbursed within 90 days of Kendra opening against original bills, creating a predictable revenue lag for success fees.
