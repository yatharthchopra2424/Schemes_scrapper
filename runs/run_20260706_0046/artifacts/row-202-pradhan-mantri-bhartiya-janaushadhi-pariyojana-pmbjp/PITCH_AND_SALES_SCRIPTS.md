# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping healthcare entrepreneurs and pharmacists access government support for opening Jan Aushadhi Kendras under the Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP). This scheme is designed to make quality generic medicines affordable while creating sustainable self-employment opportunities. I’d like to understand your background and goals to see if this aligns with your plans.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your professional background—are you a qualified pharmacist (D.Pharm/B.Pharm) or do you employ one?
2. Have you secured or are you looking for a space of at least 120 sq. ft. for a Jan Aushadhi Kendra?
3. Are you located in an aspirational district (as notified by NITI Aayog), Himalayan region, Island territory, or North-Eastern state?
4. Do you belong to any of the eligible categories for fee exemption and special incentives—woman entrepreneur, Divyang (PWD), SC/ST, ex-serviceman?
5. What is your planned investment capacity for setting up the Kendra, including furniture, fixtures, and IT equipment?
6. Do you have access to financial documents like ITR for the last two years and bank statements for the last six months?
7. Are you prepared to obtain a drug license in the name of 'Pradhan Mantri Janaushadhi Kendra'?
8. Have you considered the distance policy requiring a minimum 1 km between Kendras (with exceptions near hospitals with 100+ beds)?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant holds D.Pharm/B.Pharm degree or employs degree holders  
- [ ] Minimum 120 sq. ft. owned or leased space with proper agreement  
- [ ] Proof of securing a registered pharmacist (name and State Council registration)  
- [ ] Financial capacity documents: ITR (last 2 years), bank statements (last 6 months), PAN/Aadhar  
- [ ] Drug license obtainable in the name of 'Pradhan Mantri Janaushadhi Kendra'  
- [ ] Adherence to distance policy: minimum 1 km between Kendras (no restriction within 500m of hospitals with 100+ beds or medical colleges)  
- [ ] For special incentive eligibility: woman entrepreneur, Divyang, SC/ST, ex-serviceman, or located in aspirational district (NITI Aayog), Himalayan, Island, or North-Eastern state  
- [ ] Willingness to submit original bills for reimbursement of special incentive within 90 days of opening  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you may be well-positioned to benefit from the Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana, which offers a 20% operating margin on MRP, monthly incentives up to Rs. 20,000, and a special one-time incentive of Rs. 2.00 lakhs for eligible categories. Shall I walk you through how this works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many aspiring pharmacists and entrepreneurs struggle with high setup costs and low margins when opening medicine stores, limiting access to affordable generic medicines in underserved areas. The PMBJP addresses this by providing financial incentives and supply chain support to reduce barriers to entry.

**Scheme intro:**
The Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP), implemented by the Pharmaceuticals & Medical Devices Bureau of India (PMBI), offers a 20% operating margin on MRP (excluding taxes) on each generic medicine sold, a normal incentive of up to Rs. 20,000 per month (based on purchases and stocking mandate), and a special one-time incentive of Rs. 2.00 lakhs (Rs. 1.50 lakhs for furniture/fixtures and Rs. 0.50 lakhs for computer/internet/printer/scanner) for eligible categories including women entrepreneurs, Divyang, SC/ST, ex-servicemen, and those in aspirational districts, Himalayan, Island, or North-Eastern states.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You hold or employ a D.Pharm/B.Pharm degree holder, meeting the core educational requirement  
- You have or are securing a minimum 120 sq. ft. space with proper lease/ownership documentation  
- You belong to an eligible category for special incentives and fee exemption (e.g., woman entrepreneur, SC/ST, etc.)  
- You are prepared to obtain a drug license in the Kendra’s name and comply with the distance policy
