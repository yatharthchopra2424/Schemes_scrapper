### Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Pharmaceuticals & Medical Devices Bureau of India (PMBI)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://janaushadhi.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Individual entrepreneurs, proprietors, partnerships, charitable institutes/hospitals, NGOs/trusts/societies, government/nominated agencies
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Healthcare (generic medicine retail)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: 
  - Individual applicants must hold a D. Pharma / B. Pharma degree or employ degree holders and provide proof at application or final approval
  - Entrepreneurs, pharmacists, trusts, societies, and charitable organizations must employ B.Pharma/D.Pharma degree holders and provide proof
  - In government hospital premises, reputed NGOs/charitable organizations are preferred, though individuals are also eligible
  - Applicants must own or hire space (minimum 120 sq. ft.) with proper lease agreement
  - Secure a registered pharmacist
  - Submit financial capacity documents (ITR, bank statements, PAN/Aadhar)
  - Obtain a drug license in the Kendra's name
  - Adhere to distance policy (minimum 1 km between Kendras, no restriction within 500m of hospitals with 100+ beds or medical college-associated hospitals)

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - Operating margin of 20% on MRP (excluding taxes) of each drug sold
  - Normal incentive of 20% of monthly purchases from PMBI, capped at Rs. 20,000 per month (disbursed 50:50 based on purchase and stocking mandate)
  - Special one-time incentive of Rs. 2.00 lakhs (Rs. 1.50 lakh for furniture/fixtures, Rs. 0.50 lakh for computer/internet/printer/scanner) for eligible categories (women entrepreneurs, Divyang, SC/ST, ex-servicemen, aspirational districts, Himalayan, Island, North-Eastern states)
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement (against original bills within 90 days of opening for special incentive; direct credit for normal incentive)
- Duration of benefit: 
  - Operating margin: Ongoing (per sale)
  - Normal incentive: Monthly (subject to renewal)
  - Special one-time incentive: One-time (reimbursed against actual expenditure within 90 days of opening)

#### Documents Required
1. Aadhaar Card
2. PAN Card
3. Certificate of SC/ST or Divyang (PWD) or General category Registration certificate
4. Pharmacist Registration Certification
5. ITR for last two years
6. Bank statement for last 6 months
7. Declaration for GST registration once threshold limit is achieved
8. Undertaking of distance policy as per guideline
9. Self-Undertaking of PMBJP Kendra for availing one-time special incentive (if applicable under woman entrepreneur/divyaang/SC/ST/ex-serviceman/aspirational district/Himalayan/Island Territories/North-Eastern States)
10. Three cheques from Indian Nationalized Banks in favor of PMBI
11. One cancelled cheque from Indian Nationalized Banks to PMBI
12. Proof of space ownership or lease agreement (minimum 120 sq. ft.)
13. Proof of securing a pharmacist (name, State Council registration)
14. Drug License in the name of 'Pradhan Mantri Janaushadhi Kendra'

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit https://janaushadhi.gov.in and click on 'APPLY FOR KENDRA' tab
2. Click 'REGISTER NOW' and fill applicant details including mobile number and email ID
3. Upon successful registration, receive email with unique USER ID and PASSWORD for portal login
4. Login using USER ID/PASSWORD or via registered mobile/email using 'Get OTP' option
5. Fill application form: enter basic and proposed Kendra details, adhere to distance policy, upload required documents in PDF (each <5 MB)
6. For location tagging, drag and drop location icon within blue circle to exact address/shop/building
7. Pay application fee of Rs. 5,000/- via online payment gateway (exempt for eligible categories with category proof)
8. After submission, confirmation sent to registered email; check status online via portal or call helpline 18001808080
