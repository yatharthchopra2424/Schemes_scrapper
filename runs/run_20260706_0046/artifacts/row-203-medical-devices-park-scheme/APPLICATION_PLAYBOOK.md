## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Entity is registered under the Companies Act, 2013 or any other relevant law  
- [ ] Entity is engaged in or planning to engage in the manufacturing of medical devices  
- [ ] Entity is a company, consortium, or special purpose vehicle (SPV)  
- [ ] Project involves setting up common infrastructure facilities in a medical device park (not individual factory sheds or plant machinery)  
- [ ] Land for the park is available (owned or leased) with clear title or lease agreement  
- [ ] Project cost does not exceed the limit where 70% grant-in-aid would surpass INR 100 crore (i.e., total project cost ≤ INR ~142.85 crore)  
- [ ] Implementing entity can bear 30% of the project cost from own resources  
- [ ] Project proposal demonstrates technical feasibility, financial viability, and potential for employment generation and export promotion  
- [ ] Undertaking can be provided for compliance with environmental and safety norms  
- [ ] Park will be used exclusively for medical device manufacturing activities

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
✅ **Required** – The scheme explicitly mandates a Detailed Project Report (DPR).  
**Standard Sections to Include**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Overview and Objectives  
- Land Details (location, area, ownership/lease, zoning compliance)  
- Layout and Site Plan (internal roads, utilities, common facilities)  
- Description of Common Infrastructure Facilities (to be funded under scheme)  
- Financial Plan (total project cost, means of financing, 30% promoter contribution)  
- Cost Estimates (item-wise breakup for infrastructure)  
- Revenue Model (if any, e.g., user charges for common facilities)  
- Employment Generation Projections (direct and indirect)  
- Export Promotion Potential  
- Environmental and Safety Compliance Undertaking  
- Implementation Timeline (milestone-based)  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
- Annexures: Land documents, board resolution, promoter details, financial statements  

> **Note**: The DPR must focus on **common infrastructure only** — individual factory sheds or plant machinery are **not eligible** for grant support (Key Caveat).  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal: https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/medical-device-park  
2. Register/create entity profile using official email and mobile number  
3. Select “Medical Devices Park Scheme” from the list of active schemes  
4. Fill in entity details exactly as per Certificate of Incorporation and PAN  
5. Enter project name, location, and proposed park capacity (in terms of number of units it can support)  
6. Clearly state total project cost and requested grant amount (70% of cost, max INR 100 crore)  
7. In the “Project Description” field, emphasize: common infrastructure facilities, plug-and-play readiness, and exclusivity for medical device manufacturing  
8. Upload all documents in the specified order and format as per portal instructions  
9. Undertaking must be uploaded separately and state: “The park shall be used exclusively for medical device manufacturing activities”  
10. Declaration: Confirm that individual factory sheds or plant machinery are not included in the grant request  
11. Preview all entries before final submission  
12. Do not refresh or navigate away during final submission
