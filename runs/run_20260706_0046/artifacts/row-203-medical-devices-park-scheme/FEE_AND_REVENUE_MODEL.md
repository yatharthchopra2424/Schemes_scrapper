## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial benefit available under the Medical Devices Park Scheme (up to ₹100 crore per entity), ensuring consultant compensation scales with the value delivered to clients pursuing large-scale infrastructure projects.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees account for the scheme’s competitive nature and multi-stage approval process (technical committee evaluation, site verification, milestone-based disbursement), which introduces moderate execution risk despite rolling applications and clear guidelines.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The retainer model accommodates varying client capacities — from MSMEs forming SPVs to large corporations — while success fees remain competitive given the high grant ceiling and long project timelines typical of infrastructure development.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Given the scheme’s focus on common infrastructure (not individual plant/machinery) and its target beneficiaries (manufacturers, MSMEs, startups, entrepreneurs via SPVs), pricing is most relevant for mid-market and enterprise clients capable of forming consortia or SPVs to meet eligibility and scale requirements. Processing timelines (typically 6–12 months for approval and milestone verification) support a success fee structure that aligns with fund disbursement stages.
