## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I'm calling from [Your Firm Name]. We specialize in helping medical device manufacturers and entrepreneurs access government support for infrastructure development. Given your work in the medical devices sector, I wanted to explore whether the Medical Devices Park Scheme could support your plans for scaling manufacturing operations through shared infrastructure facilities.

### Discovery Questions
1. What stage are you at in your medical device manufacturing plans — are you already operating, planning to set up a facility, or looking to expand existing operations?
2. Have you incorporated your entity under the Companies Act, 2013 or another relevant law, and if so, when was it registered?
3. Are you considering setting up your facility independently, or are you open to collaborating with other manufacturers through a consortium or special purpose vehicle (SPV)?
4. Do you have access to land for the project, either through ownership or a long-term lease agreement?
5. What common infrastructure facilities (e.g., roads, power, water, effluent treatment, testing labs) are you planning to include in your medical device park?
6. Have you prepared a Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining your project layout, financial plan, and employment generation projections?
7. What is your estimated total project cost for setting up the common infrastructure facilities in the medical device park?
8. Are you projecting significant employment generation and export potential from the medical devices manufactured in your proposed park?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity is a company, consortium, or SPV registered under the Companies Act, 2013 or relevant law
- [ ] Engaged in or planning to engage in medical device manufacturing
- [ ] Has access to land (owned or leased) for the medical device park
- [ ] Project focuses on common infrastructure facilities (not individual factory sheds or plant machinery)
- [ ] Detailed Project Report (DPR) can be prepared or is available
- [ ] Commitment to use the park exclusively for medical device manufacturing activities
- [ ] Willingness to provide undertaking on compliance with environmental and safety norms
- [ ] Board resolution authorizing the application can be obtained

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you're actively planning or expanding medical device manufacturing infrastructure and could benefit from government support. The Medical Devices Park Scheme offers grant-in-aid of up to 70% of project cost, capped at INR 100 crore, specifically for creating common infrastructure facilities. Let me walk you through how this could work for your project.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Medical device manufacturers face high upfront costs when setting up manufacturing facilities, particularly for essential common infrastructure like roads, power, water supply, effluent treatment, and testing labs. These costs can be prohibitive for MSMEs, startups, and entrepreneurs, delaying market entry and limiting scalability. Additionally, fragmented efforts lead to duplicated infrastructure and higher operational costs across the sector.

**Scheme intro:**
The Medical Devices Park Scheme, administered by the Department of Pharmaceuticals under the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers, provides grant-in-aid of up to 70% of the project cost for setting up common infrastructure facilities in medical device parks, with a maximum ceiling of INR 100 crore per park. The remaining 30% must be borne by the implementing entity. Funds are disbursed in installments based on milestone achievement. The application portal is https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/medical-device-park, and applications are accepted on a rolling basis subject to fund availability.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your entity is registered under the Companies Act, 2013 or a relevant law (as confirmed in discovery)
- You are engaged in or planning to engage in medical device manufacturing (core eligibility)
- You have or are securing land for the project (owned or leased)
- Your project focuses on common infrastructure facilities such as roads, water, power, effluent treatment, and testing labs — exactly what the scheme supports
