### Medical Devices Park Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/medical-device-park
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (applications accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Companies, consortiums, or special purpose vehicles (SPVs) registered under the Companies Act, 2013 or any other relevant law
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Medical device manufacturing (engaged in or planning to engage in)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Proposals evaluated based on technical feasibility, financial viability, and potential for employment generation and export promotion

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Grant-in-aid up to 70% of project cost, with a maximum ceiling of INR 100 crore per medical device park
- Disbursement mode: Installments based on achievement of predefined milestones
- Duration of benefit: One-time (disbursed in stages upon milestone completion)

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
2. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration of the entity
3. Land ownership or lease documents
4. Project layout and site plan
5. Financial viability report including means of financing
6. Employment generation projections
7. Undertaking regarding compliance with environmental and safety norms
8. Board resolution authorizing the application

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Submit a detailed project report (DPR) along with the application form to the implementing agency.
2. The DPR must include details on land availability, project layout, proposed facilities, financial plan, and employment generation.
3. Applications are evaluated by a technical committee appointed by the Department of Pharmaceuticals.
4. Shortlisted proposals undergo site verification and due diligence.
5. Upon approval, a formal sanction letter is issued.
6. Funds are released in stages based on milestone completion verified by the agency.
