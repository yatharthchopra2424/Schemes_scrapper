## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Medical Devices Park Scheme** (Scheme ID: row-203) is a **subsidy** initiative under the **Healthcare** category, implemented by the **Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers**. It operates with **Pan-India** geographic scope and accepts applications on a **rolling basis** throughout the year, subject to fund availability. The scheme aims to strengthen domestic medical device manufacturing by providing financial and infrastructural support for the creation of medical device parks.

### Objectives
The scheme pursues the following strategic objectives:
- To provide world-class infrastructure and common facilities for medical device manufacturing  
- To reduce import dependence on medical devices and promote domestic manufacturing  
- To create an ecosystem that supports innovation, research, and development in medical devices  
- To enhance export competitiveness of Indian medical devices  
- To attract investment in the medical devices sector through plug-and-play facilities  
- To generate employment and support the growth of MSMEs and startups in the healthcare domain

### Key Caveats & Warnings
> **> ⚠️ Critical Limitations**  
> - The grant is **strictly limited to common infrastructure facilities**; individual factory sheds, plant machinery, or core manufacturing equipment are **not covered**.  
> - The park **must be used exclusively for medical device manufacturing activities**; diversion to other uses violates scheme terms.  
> - Failure to utilize the grant for the approved purpose may trigger **recovery of funds with interest**.  
> - Scheme continuation is **subject to annual budgetary allocations**; the government may revise or discontinue it without notice.  
> - Applications are processed on a **rolling basis** but are **contingent on fund availability** — early submission is advised when budgets are fresh.  

> **> 💡 Key Takeaway**  
> This scheme is ideal for consortia, SPVs, or large developers aiming to create medical device parks with shared infrastructure. It is **not suitable** for individual manufacturers seeking funding for their own factory setup or machinery. Success hinges on a robust DPR demonstrating land readiness, financial viability, employment potential, and export orientation.
