## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Entity is a State Government agency, Government-owned corporation, joint venture with State Government equity participation, or private sector company selected via transparent bidding process  
- [ ] Implementing agency is a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) formed by the State Government or its nominee  
- [ ] Project involves setting up a Bulk Drug Park with common infrastructure facilities  
- [ ] Land is in possession of the State Government or SPV (required before fund release)  
- [ ] Environmental clearance obtained or application status available  
- [ ] Water and power availability certificates secured  
- [ ] Undertaking on cost sharing by State Government provided (30% contribution, or 10% for hill states)  
- [ ] Letter of consent from potential bulk drug manufacturers obtained  
- [ ] Project feasibility report prepared  
- [ ] Details of proposed SPV structure submitted  
- [ ] Proposal submitted by State Government to Department of Pharmaceuticals via official channel  

> **Note**: The scheme operates on a rolling basis with no fixed deadline; proposals are evaluated as per notifications issued by the Department of Pharmaceuticals.  
> **Sources**: Application Portal: https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/bulk-drug-parks | Key Facts: Eligibility, Application Process, Key Caveats

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate of the SPV or State Agency  
- PAN Card of the SPV or State Agency  
- Audited Financial Statements / ITR of the SPV or State Agency (last 3 years)  
- GST Registration Certificate  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (if applicable, for MSME participation)  
- Authorization Letter / Board Resolution authorizing application submission  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Project proposal from State Government (official letter/form)  
- Land details and ownership documents (7/12 extract, sale deed, possession certificate)  
- Environmental clearance certificate or status of application (from MoEFCC/SEIAA)  
- Water availability certificate (from State Water Board/CGWB)  
- Power availability certificate (from State Electricity Board/DISCOM)  
- Project feasibility report (including technical, financial, and market analysis)  
- Details of proposed SPV structure (shareholding pattern, MoA, AoA)  
- Undertaking on cost sharing by State Government (on stamp paper, duly notarized)  
- Letter of consent from potential bulk drug manufacturers (on company letterhead, signed)  

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)  
- [ ] Names on documents exactly match the SPV/State Agency name as per incorporation  
- [ ] Certificates (environmental, water, power) are valid and not expired  
- [ ] Undertakings and letters are on official letterhead, signed, and stamped where applicable  
- [ ] Land documents show clear title and possession by State Government or SPV  
- [ ] SPV documents (MoA, AoA) reflect objectives aligned with Bulk Drug Park development  
- [ ] All documents are in English or accompanied by certified Hindi/English translation  
- [ ] Financial documents are audited and certified by a Chartered Accountant

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required** for final approval after 'In-Principle' approval.  
Standard sections to include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Overview and Objectives  
- Land Details and Site Analysis  
- Infrastructure Planning (common facilities: CETP, solvent recovery, steam/power, water, logistics, safety)  
- SPV Structure and Governance Model  
- Financial Plan (project cost, funding mix, phasing)  
- Implementation Schedule (milestones tied to fund release)  
- Environmental Management Plan  
- Safety and Security Plan  
- Beneficiary Allotment Policy (transparent, non-discriminatory)  
- Economic Impact Analysis (employment, import substitution, investment attraction)  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
- Annexures: Land documents, clearances, SPV documents, manufacturer consent letters  

> **Note**: The DPR is submitted by the SPV after obtaining statutory clearances and forming the SPV. Verify exact template requirements with the nodal officer if needed.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official portal: **https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/bulk-drug-parks**  
2. Ensure the applicant is the **State Government or its nominated SPV** — private entities cannot apply directly  
3. Fill in all fields accurately; do not leave any mandatory section blank  
4. Attach documents in the specified order and format as per portal instructions  
5. Validate that the project cost does not exceed the limit for 70% (or 90%) grant calculation  
6. Confirm that the undertaking on State Government cost sharing (30%/10%) is attached and signed  
7. Double-check SPV details, land possession, and clearance status before final submission  
8. Take a screenshot of the confirmation page and save the application reference number immediately  

> **Portal Note**: The portal may host forms or guidelines; always refer to the latest notification from the Department of Pharmaceuticals.
