## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the substantial financial benefit (up to ₹1000 Cr grant) and long-term strategic value of establishing a Bulk Drug Park.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the moderate risk profile due to the scheme’s rolling application basis and high confidence in approvals for well-prepared proposals, though offset by lengthy statutory clearance processes and milestone-based fund disbursement.
3. **Transparency**: All fee structures are disclosed upfront; retainers cover initial proposal development and SPV structuring, while success fees are contingent on grant approval and fund release.
