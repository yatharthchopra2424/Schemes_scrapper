## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping pharmaceutical manufacturers and state agencies access government infrastructure support for bulk drug production. The Bulk Drug Parks Scheme is designed to reduce capital expenditure through shared infrastructure like effluent treatment, power, and logistics—critical for scaling manufacturing sustainably. I’d like to understand your current expansion or setup plans to see if this scheme aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
- Are you a state government agency, government-owned corporation, or part of a joint venture with state equity looking to develop industrial infrastructure for pharmaceutical manufacturing?
- Is the proposed Bulk Drug Park located in a state where land is either already in possession or can be acquired by the state government or its nominee?
- Have you initiated or plan to initiate environmental clearance processes for the proposed site?
- Do you have confirmation of water and power availability at the identified land parcel?
- Are you in discussions with bulk drug manufacturers (including MSMEs and large enterprises) who would commit to leasing or purchasing units in the park?
- Is the project envisioned to include common facilities such as solvent recovery systems, steam supply, logistics hubs, and safety services?
- Are you prepared to form a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) to implement and manage the park?
- Does your state government intend to contribute at least 30% of the project cost (or 10% for hill states like Himachal Pradesh) as required cost-sharing?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is a State Government agency, government-owned corporation, joint venture with state equity, or private entity selected via transparent bidding
- [ ] Implementing agency is or will be a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) formed by the State Government or its nominee
- [ ] Land is in possession of the State Government or SPV (or will be before fund release)
- [ ] Environmental clearance obtained or application status available
- [ ] Water and power availability certificates secured
- [ ] Project feasibility report prepared
- [ ] SPV structure details documented
- [ ] Undertaking on cost sharing by State Government (70/30 split; 90/10 for hill states)
- [ ] Letter of consent from potential bulk drug manufacturers obtained
- [ ] Project proposal submitted by State Government to Department of Pharmaceuticals

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you’re actively exploring or advancing a Bulk Drug Park initiative—which is exactly what the **Bulk Drug Parks Scheme** supports. This scheme offers up to **INR 1000 Crore in grant-in-aid** (70% of project cost; 90% for hill states) for common infrastructure, directly reducing your upfront capital burden. Let me walk you through how it works and whether you qualify.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
State governments and pharmaceutical clusters face high upfront costs when setting up bulk drug manufacturing hubs. Individual units bear duplicated expenses for effluent treatment, power, water, logistics, and security—making projects financially unviable, especially for MSMEs. Without shared infrastructure, India struggles to reduce import dependence on critical drugs and attract sustainable investment in the sector.

**Scheme intro:**
The **Bulk Drug Parks Scheme**, administered by the **Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers**, provides **grant-in-aid of up to INR 1000 Crore per Bulk Drug Park**—covering 70% of the project cost for common infrastructure facilities (90% for Himachal Pradesh and other hill states). The scheme supports the development of plug-and-play infrastructure including common effluent treatment plants, solvent recovery systems, steam and power supply, water supply, logistics, and safety services.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a **State Government agency or nominee** forming an **SPV** to implement the park  
- You have or are securing **land possession**, **environmental clearance status**, and **water/power availability**  
- You have obtained **letters of consent from bulk drug manufacturers** interested in allotment  
- Your state government commits to **cost sharing** (30% of project cost; 10% for hill states)
