### Bulk Drug Parks Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/bulk-drug-parks
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (proposals can be submitted throughout the year as per notifications issued by the Department of Pharmaceuticals)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: State Government agencies, Government-owned corporations, joint ventures with equity participation from State Governments, and private sector companies selected through a transparent bidding process
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Pharmaceutical (bulk drug manufacturing)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Implementing agency must be a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) formed by the State Government or its nominee; land must be in possession of the State Government or SPV before fund release; all statutory clearances (environmental, etc.) must be obtained by the SPV

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Grant-in-aid limited to 70% of the project cost for common infrastructure facilities, with a maximum of INR 1000 Crore per Bulk Drug Park; for Himachal Pradesh and other hill states, grant increased to 90% of project cost
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (funds released in installments based on progress milestones)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (grant released upon achievement of physical progress milestones)

#### Documents Required
1. Project proposal from State Government
2. Land details and ownership documents
3. Environmental clearance or status of application
4. Water and power availability certificates
5. Project feasibility report
6. Details of proposed SPV structure
7. Undertaking on cost sharing by State Government
8. Letter of consent from potential bulk drug manufacturers

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. State Governments submit proposals for setting up Bulk Drug Parks to the Department of Pharmaceuticals.
2. Proposals are evaluated based on predefined criteria including land availability, water and power connectivity, environmental clearance status, and industrial ecosystem.
3. Selected proposals are accorded 'In-Principle' approval.
4. After obtaining necessary statutory clearances, the State Governments form a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV).
5. The SPV submits a Detailed Project Report (DPR) for final approval.
6. Upon approval, funds are released in installments based on progress milestones.
7. The SPV undertakes implementation of common infrastructure facilities.
8. Facilities are made operational and allotted to bulk drug manufacturers on lease or ownership basis.
