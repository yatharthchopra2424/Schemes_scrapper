## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Bulk Drug Parks Scheme** is a central sector subsidy scheme launched by the **Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers**, Government of India. It aims to develop world-class infrastructure for bulk drug manufacturing through the establishment of Bulk Drug Parks across India. The scheme operates on a **rolling basis**, allowing State Governments to submit proposals throughout the year as per notifications issued by the Department of Pharmaceuticals. The last update was in 2024, and the scheme has a total fund size of **INR 3,000 Crore**.

### Objectives
The scheme seeks to achieve the following objectives:
- Create world-class infrastructure for bulk drug manufacturing  
- Reduce the cost of production through shared common infrastructure facilities  
- Ensure availability of critical drugs and reduce import dependence  
- Promote environmental sustainability via common effluent treatment plants and solvent recovery systems  
- Attract investment in the pharmaceutical sector  
- Generate employment in the manufacturing sector  

### Implementing Agency & Portal
- **Implementing Agency**: Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers  
- **Application Portal**: [https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/bulk-drug-parks](https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/bulk-drug-parks)  
- **Contact Details**:  
  - Email: bulkdrugparks@nic.in  
  - Phone: 011-2306-1756  

### Financial Support & Fund Size
| Parameter | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Total Fund Size** | INR 3,000 Crore |
| **Maximum Grant per Bulk Drug Park** | Up to INR 1,000 Crore |
| **Grant-in-Aid Percentage (General States)** | 70% of project cost for common infrastructure facilities |
| **Grant-in-Aid Percentage (Hill States: Himachal Pradesh & others)** | 90% of project cost |
| **State Government & SPV Contribution (General States)** | 30% of project cost |
| **State Government & SPV Contribution (Hill States)** | 10% of project cost |
| **Form of Support** | Grant-in-aid (non-repayable) for common infrastructure facilities only |

> **Important Caveat**: The grant is released **only after achievement of physical progress milestones**. Funds are disbursed in installments tied to verified progress, not as a lump sum.

### Benefits
#### Financial Benefits
- Grant-in-aid covering 70% (90% for hill states) of the cost of common infrastructure facilities  
- Maximum central financial assistance capped at **INR 1,000 Crore per Bulk Drug Park**

#### Non-Financial Benefits
- Plug-and-play infrastructure for immediate operational readiness  
- Common Effluent Treatment Plants (CETP)  
- Solvent Recovery Systems  
- Steam and Power Supply  
- Water Supply  
- Logistics Support  
- Safety and Security Services  
- Economies of scale leading to reduced capital expenditure for individual units  

### Eligibility Criteria
| Eligible Entity Type | Details |
|----------------------|--------|
| **State Government Agencies** | Can propose and sponsor the creation of a Bulk Drug Park |
| **Government-Owned Corporations** | Eligible to act as project promoters or SPV partners |
| **Joint Ventures** | With equity participation from State Governments |
| **Private Sector Companies** | Eligible if selected through a transparent bidding process by the State Government |
| **Implementing Entity** | Must be a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) formed by the State Government or its nominee |

> **Note**: Individual bulk drug manufacturers are **not direct applicants** but are the **target beneficiaries** who will lease or purchase plots within the developed park.

### Target Beneficiaries
- Bulk drug manufacturers  
- Pharmaceutical companies  
- MSMEs (Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises)  
- Large enterprises  

### Required Documents
The following documents must be submitted as part of the proposal:
1. Project proposal from State Government  
2. Land details and ownership documents  
3. Environmental clearance or status of application  
4. Water and power availability certificates  
5. Project feasibility report  
6. Details of proposed SPV structure  
7. Undertaking on cost sharing by State Government  
8. Letter of consent from potential bulk drug manufacturers  

### Application Process
The application process involves eight sequential steps, initiated by the State Government:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[State Government submits proposal to Department of Pharmaceuticals] --> B{Proposal Evaluation}
    B -->|Based on:<br/>- Land availability<br/>- Water & power connectivity<br/>- Environmental clearance status<br/>- Industrial ecosystem| C[In-Principle Approval]
    C --> D[Obtain Statutory Clearances<br/>(Environmental, etc.)]
    D --> E[Formation of Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) by State Government]
    E --> F[SPV submits Detailed Project Report (DPR)]
    F --> G[Final Approval by Department of Pharmaceuticals]
    G --> H[Funds Released in Installments<br/>Based on Progress Milestones]
    H --> I[SPV Implements Common Infrastructure Facilities]
    I --> J[Facilities Made Operational<br/>and Allotted to Manufacturers<br/>on Lease/Ownership Basis]
```

### Key Timelines & Deadlines
- **Application Basis**: Rolling — proposals accepted throughout the year  
- **Trigger**: Notifications issued by the Department of Pharmaceuticals  
- **Last Updated**: 2024  
- **Status**: Active  

### Key Caveats & Conditions
> - Grant release is **strictly tied to physical progress milestones** — no advance or lump-sum disbursement  
> - Land must be **in possession of the State Government or SPV** before any fund release  
> - All **statutory clearances** (environmental, industrial, etc.) **must be obtained by the SPV** prior to implementation  
> - The scheme is **subject to periodic review and modification** by the Government of India  
> - Allotment of facilities within the park to bulk drug manufacturers must follow **transparent and non-discriminatory procedures**  
> - Only **common infrastructure facilities** are eligible for funding — individual unit costs are not covered
