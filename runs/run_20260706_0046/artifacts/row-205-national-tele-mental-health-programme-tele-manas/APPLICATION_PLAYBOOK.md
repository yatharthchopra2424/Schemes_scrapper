# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Individual seeking mental health support (any individual with mental health issues)
- [ ] Family member of a person with mental health issues seeking support on their behalf
- [ ] Grass-root health care provider (e.g., Accredited Social Health Activist - ASHA) or community volunteer reaching out on behalf of an individual or community with mental health issues
- [ ] No registration type, turnover threshold, employee count, sector, or age of business requirements apply (service is available to general public without economic or business criteria)
- [ ] No financial eligibility criteria (service is free of cost to all users)
- [ ] Available to individuals across all Indian States and UTs (Pan-India geographic scope)
- [ ] Service accessible 24x7, throughout the year (no time-based restrictions)

> **Note**: This is a public health service, not a financial benefit scheme. Eligibility is based solely on need for mental health support, not economic or business parameters.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- [ ] Proof of identity (any government-issued ID: Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License) – *only if voluntarily provided during call; not mandatory*
- [ ] Address proof (if district/state details are shared voluntarily) – *not mandatory*
- [ ] Bank statement – *not required for service access*
- [ ] Udyam Registration – *not applicable*
- [ ] GST certificate – *not applicable*
- [ ] ITR documents – *not applicable*
- [ ] PAN card – *only if voluntarily shared for personalized care; not mandatory*

> **Note**: Universal documents like PAN, ITR, GST, bank statements, and Udyam are **not required** to access Tele MANAS services. The service is designed for barrier-free access, and personal data sharing is optional.

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- [ ] **No scheme-specific documents are required** to access the service.
- [ ] Personal details (name, age, gender, district, state) may be queried during the call but are **not mandatory**.
- [ ] For video consultations: Voluntary upload of a photo ID (e.g., Aadhaar, PAN) may be required to verify identity, but only if the caller opts for video service and consents to share.
- [ ] Electronic case details or health records: Voluntarily shared by the caller during counselling (if they choose to provide).

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] File size: Not applicable (no document upload required for basic service access)
- [ ] Name matching: Not applicable (data shared verbally during call; optional)
- [ ] Certification requirements: Not applicable
- [ ] Language: Services available in 20 Indian languages; caller can choose preferred language during IVRS interaction
- [ ] Format: Audio/video calls only; no document upload portal for general access
- [ ] For video calls: If photo ID is uploaded, ensure it is clear, valid, and matches the details provided (name, age, gender)

### Client Communication Template
**Subject**: Accessing Free 24x7 Mental Health Support via Tele MANAS – No Documents Required  

Dear [Client Name],  

This is to inform you that the **National Tele Mental Health Programme (Tele MANAS)** offers **free, round-the-clock mental health support** accessible to anyone in India experiencing mental health concerns, or supporting someone who is.  

**How to Access**:  
Simply dial the toll-free number **14416** or **1-800-891-4416** at any time, day or night. The service is available 24x7, throughout the year, in 20 Indian languages.  

**No Documents Needed**:  
You do **not** need to submit any documents, PAN, ITR, GST, bank statements, or Udyam registration to use this service. Personal details like name, age, gender, district, and state are **optional** and only asked if you wish to share them for personalized care. You can remain anonymous if preferred.  

**Services Available**:  
- Telephone-based counselling and psychotherapy  
- Psychiatric consultations and e-prescriptions (if medication is needed)  
- Video consultations with mental health specialists (with consent)  
- Referral to in-person services or eSanjeevani for audio-visual consultation  
- Support for parents/caretakers of children with developmental disorders  
- Emergency support and referral to nearest in-person facility in crisis situations  

**Important Notes**:  
- Audio/video call recordings are **not stored**; only anonymized data is retained for 3 years for research.  
- In emergencies (e.g., suicide risk), limited personal data may be shared with authorities to ensure safety, as per law.  
- The service is a public health initiative by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) and is completely free of cost.  

For more information, visit: https://telemanas.mohfw.gov.in  
For queries or support, email: mentalhealth-mohfw[at]gov[dot]in or telemanas[dot]nimhans[at]gmail.com  

You are not alone. Help is just a call away.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- [ ] **Not applicable**. Tele MANAS is a direct-access public helpline service. There is no application form, project report, or Detailed Project Report (DPR) required to access the service.  
- [ ] If seeking to **partner with or implement** Tele MANAS at an institutional level (e.g., as a State Cell, Mentoring Institute, or RCC), verify requirements with the nodal officer (NIMHANS or MoHFW Mental Health Division).  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
- [ ] **Not applicable**. There is no online or offline application form to fill for availing Tele MANAS services.  
- [ ] Access is initiated solely by calling the toll-free number: **14416** or **1-800-891-4416**.  
- [ ] The call begins with an IVRS (Interactive Voice Response) system that routes the call to the appropriate State Tele MANAS Cell based on location and language preference.  
- [ ] No forms, registrations, or pre-enrollment are needed.  
- [ ] If opting for video consultation, the mental health professional will send an SMS with a secure link to join the call — no form filling is involved.  
- [ ] **Critical Rule**: Do not attempt to fill any non-existent "application form." Misguiding clients to fill forms may delay or prevent access to urgent care.  
- [ ] **Critical Rule**: Advise clients to call the helpline directly — this is the only and official access method.  
- [ ] **Critical Rule**: Inform clients that sharing personal details (name, age, etc.) is optional and will not affect service access if withheld.  
- [ ] **Critical Rule**: For video calls, clarify that photo ID upload is only for identity verification (per telemedicine guidelines) and is optional if the caller declines.  
- [ ] **Critical Rule**: Remind clients that the service is confidential, and data is not shared without consent except in life-threatening emergencies as per law.  
- [ ] **Critical Rule**: Warn against fraudulent websites or numbers claiming to be Tele MANAS — only use the official numbers and website: https://telemanas.mohfw.gov.in  
- [ ] **Critical Rule**: Document the call date/time and reference (if provided by counsellor) for follow-up, but no formal application ID is generated for general counselling calls.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Confirm the client understands Tele MANAS is a **helpline service**, not a financial grant or loan scheme requiring documentation.  
- [ ] Verify the client has been informed of the correct toll-free numbers: **14416** or **1-800-891-4416**.  
- [ ] Ensure the client knows service is available **24x7**, including nights, weekends, and holidays.  
- [ ] Confirm the client understands **no documents are mandatory** to access basic counselling.  
- [ ] Verify the client is aware of language options (20 Indian languages) and can request preferred language during the call.  
- [ ] Ensure the client knows how to access video consultation and e-prescription services (via counsellor referral and SMS link).  
- [ ] Confirm the client understands the confidentiality policy and limits (emergency disclosure to authorities only).  
- [ ] Check that the client has been directed to the official website: https://telemanas.mohfw.gov.in for app download, FAQs, and contact details.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- [ ] **Not applicable**. There is no formal "submission" as the service is accessed via phone call.  
- [ ] After the call, advise the client to:  
    - Note down the date and time of the call.  
    - If provided, record any reference number or callback number given by the counsellor.  
    - Save the Tele MANAS contact details:  
        - Helpline: 14416 or 1-800-891-4416  
        - Email: mentalhealth-mohfw[at]gov[dot]in, telemanas[dot]nimhans[at]gmail.com  
        - Website: https://telemanas.mohfw.gov.in  
- [ ] Set a follow-up reminder (if agreed with client) to check on their well-being after 3–5 days, respecting their privacy and autonomy.  
- [ ] Do **not** take screenshots of a "submission portal" — none exists for end-user access.  
- [ ] If the client is an institution seeking to become a Tele MANAS Cell or partner, then submission protocols apply — but this requires direct engagement with NIMHANS/MoHFW (flag for consultant to verify).

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+  
### Approval Actions
- [ ] **Not applicable**. Tele MANAS does not applicable – service access is immediate)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] **Not applicable**. There is no "approval" process for accessing Tele MANAS services. Access is immediate upon calling the helpline.  
- [ ] If the client receives counselling:  
    - Encourage them to follow up on any advice, referrals, or e-prescriptions given by the counsellor.  
    - Suggest they save the helpline number for future use.  
    - Remind them the service is available 24x7 for repeat access as needed.  
- [ ] If referred to video consultation:  
    - Advise the client to check their SMS for the secure link from the mental health professional.  
    - Ensure they have a stable internet connection and a private, safe space for the video call.  
    - Confirm they understand they can decline video consultation and opt for audio-only if uncomfortable.  
- [ ] If an e-prescription is issued:  
    - Guide the client to present it at any licensed pharmacy for medicine dispensing (valid as per Indian telemedicine guidelines).  
    - Remind them to follow dosage instructions and consult the psychiatrist if side effects occur.  
- [ ] If referred to in-person care or eSanjeevani:  
    - Provide support in locating the nearest DMHP, Health and Wellness Centre (HWC), or medical college as advised.  
    - Assist with navigating eSanjeevani if needed (https://esanjeevani.ap.gov.in or state-specific portal).  
- [ ] **No utilization certificate, sanction letter, or success fee invoicing applies** — this is a free public health service.  
- [ ] Do **not** issue any approval letter or sanctioned amount — none exists for end-user access.  
- [ ] Focus on **well-being follow-up** (with client consent) rather than administrative closure.  
- [ ] Document case closure in your internal records as: “Service accessed via Tele MANAS helpline on [date]; client advised to re-access as needed.”
