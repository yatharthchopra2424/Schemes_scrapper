## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the strategic value delivered, ensuring clients pay for measurable outcomes such as improved access to mental health services and enhanced organizational well-being programs.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s nature as a fully government-funded public health service with no direct monetary benefit to beneficiaries, consulting engagements focus on awareness, implementation support, and utilization optimization rather than financial incentive processing, resulting in lower revenue volatility.
3. **Transparency & Predictability**: Retainer fees cover ongoing advisory, compliance, and program integration efforts, while success fees are tied to defined KPIs like increased helpline utilization, app downloads, or training completion rates — not direct financial transfers.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The National Tele Mental Health Programme (Tele-MANAS) provides free mental health services with no direct financial disbursement to end-users. Therefore, consulting revenue is derived from supporting implementation, outreach, training, and monitoring — not from processing grants or subsidies. The 24x7 availability and pan-India reach reduce delivery risk, but success depends on behavioral change and stakeholder engagement, which may involve longer sales cycles.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Ongoing consulting support for National Tele Mental Health Programme (Tele-MANAS) implementation, including stakeholder engagement, utilization strategy, training coordination, and performance monitoring for government or organizational clients seeking to integrate or promote Tele-MANAS services.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success-linked fee for National Tele Mental Health Programme (Tele-MANAS) engagement, payable upon achievement of agreed-upon milestones such as increased helpline call volume, mobile app adoption, training completion for ASHAs/community workers, or referral linkage improvements, as verified by Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) reporting or third-party audit.
