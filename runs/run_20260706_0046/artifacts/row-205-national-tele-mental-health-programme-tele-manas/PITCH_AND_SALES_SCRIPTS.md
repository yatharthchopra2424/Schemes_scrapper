# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialise in helping individuals and community workers access government mental health support services. I understand you may be involved in community health work or supporting others with mental wellbeing—our focus today is to see if the **National Tele Mental Health Programme (Tele-MANAS)** could be a useful resource for you or the people you serve. This is a free, 24x7 government helpline and digital service for mental health support across India.

### Discovery Questions
- Are you currently working as a community health provider, such as an ASHA worker, ANM, or volunteer supporting individuals with mental health concerns?
- Do you or members of your community often experience stress, anxiety, depression, or other emotional challenges that affect daily life?
- Have you or someone you know ever struggled to access mental health counselling due to location, cost, or stigma?
- Are you aware of any free, government-run mental health helplines available in your area or language?
- Would a service offering telephone-based counselling, video consultations with specialists, and e-prescriptions be helpful for your community?
- Do you work in a remote or underserved area where access to psychiatrists or psychologists is limited?
- Have you ever referred someone for mental health support but faced challenges with follow-up or specialist linkage?
- Are you interested in learning about a service that supports not just individuals, but also caregivers and family members of those with mental health issues?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Individual experiencing mental health issues (e.g., stress, anxiety, depression, psychosis, addiction)
- [ ] Family member or caregiver of someone with mental health issues
- [ ] Grass-root health care provider (ASHA, ANM, community health worker, volunteer)
- [ ] Resident of any Indian State or UT (pan-India coverage)
- [ ] Seeking support in one of the 20 supported Indian languages
- [ ] Needs access to counselling, psychiatric consultation, referral, or emergency mental health support
- [ ] Requires video consultation or e-prescription facility (if applicable)
- [ ] Located in a hard-to-reach or vulnerable population group

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like you or the people you support could benefit from immediate, free access to mental health care—especially given the challenges of access and stigma. The **National Tele Mental Health Programme (Tele-MANAS)** is designed exactly for this: a 24x7, toll-free helpline and digital platform offering counselling, specialist consultations, and support—all at no cost. Let me tell you how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many individuals in India face mental health challenges such as anxiety, depression, or stress but struggle to access help due to cost, stigma, lack of nearby specialists, or difficulty taking time off work. Community health workers often want to help but lack the tools to provide counselling or referrals effectively.

**Scheme intro:**
The **National Tele Mental Health Programme (Tele-MANAS)**, launched by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), is a free, 24x7 tele-mental health service available across all Indian States and UTs. It offers toll-free helpline access (14416 or 1-800-891-4416), mobile app support, telephone-based counselling, psychiatric consultations, video consultations with e-prescriptions, referral services, and emergency support—all in 20 Indian languages.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You or someone you support is experiencing mental health issues (e.g., anxiety, depression, sleep disturbances, relationship stress)
- You are a family member or caregiver seeking guidance on how to help a loved one
- You work as an ASHA, community health worker, or volunteer and can use this service to support others in your community
- You reside in any part of India—rural, urban, or remote—and need support in your local language

**Proof:**
"We've helped [N] similar community workers and individuals access Tele-MANAS for timely mental health support. [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]"

**Next step:**
We offer a free, no-obligation eligibility and service fit check—just a 15-minute review of your needs or those of your community. If Tele-MANAS is right for you, we’ll guide you on how to access it directly—no forms, no fees, and no commitment. Our support is purely advisory; we only succeed when you get connected to the help you need.
