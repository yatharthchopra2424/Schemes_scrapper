### National Tele Mental Health Programme (Tele-MANAS)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://telemanas.mohfw.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Not applicable (service for individuals)
- Age of business: Not applicable
- Sector: Healthcare / Mental Health
- Revenue cap: Not applicable
- Employee count: Not applicable
- Other: Any individual with mental health issues; family members of persons with mental health issues; grass-root health care providers/community health providers (e.g., ASHAs) and community volunteers can access services on behalf of individuals in the community

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Free of cost service (no financial support or direct monetary benefit)
- Disbursement mode: Service-based access (helpline, app, video consultation)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing (24x7 service available round the clock, throughout the year)

#### Documents Required
1. No documents required to access the service
2. Personal details (name, age, gender, district, state) are queried during calls but are not mandatory
3. Phone number is captured automatically (masked and stored) for follow-up and continuity of care
4. For video calls, a photo ID may be voluntarily uploaded to verify identity (name, age, gender must match ID)
5. Electronic case details or health records may be voluntarily provided as part of counselling activity

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Dial the toll-free number 14416 or 1-800 891 4416 to access the Tele MANAS helpline
2. The call will be an IVRS-based audio call with timely auto-call back
3. A trained counsellor will attend the call and assess the level of care required
4. If needed, the counsellor will provide care within their capabilities or refer the caller to a mental health specialist
5. For specialist care, a video consultation can be initiated with consent, and a link will be sent via SMS to join the call
6. If medication is needed, psychiatrists can issue e-prescriptions
7. For urgent in-person intervention, the caller will be referred to the nearest in-person service or an audio-visual consultation arranged via eSanjeevani
