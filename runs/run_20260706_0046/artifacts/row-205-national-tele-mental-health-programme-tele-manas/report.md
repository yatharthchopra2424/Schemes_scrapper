# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **National Tele Mental Health Programme (Tele-MANAS)** is a fully government-funded public health service launched by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) under the Union Budget 2022. It operates as a 24x7, round-the-clock, toll-free tele-mental health helpline service accessible across all Indian States and Union Territories (UTs). The service is designed to provide universal access to equitable, accessible, affordable, and quality mental health care as a digital component of the National Mental Health Programme (NMHP). There is **no financial support or direct monetary benefit** provided to beneficiaries, as the service is entirely free of cost and funded by the government.

### Objectives
The programme aims to:
- Provide universal access to equitable, accessible, affordable, and quality mental health care through 24X7 tele-mental health services as a digital component of the NMHP across all Indian States and UTs with assured linkages.
- Exponentially scale up the reach of mental health services to anybody who reaches out, across India, any time, by setting up a 24x7 tele-mental health facility in each of the States and UTs of the country.
- Implement a full-fledged mental health-service network that, in addition to counselling, provides integrated medical and psychosocial interventions including video consultations with mental health specialists, e-prescriptions, follow-up services, and linkages to in-person services.
- Extend services to vulnerable groups of the population and difficult-to-reach populations.

### Eligibility Matrix
Tele-MANAS is accessible to a broad range of individuals without financial, educational, or institutional barriers. Eligibility is based solely on the need for mental health support.

| Eligible Group | Description | Notes |
|----------------|-------------|-------|
| Individuals with mental health issues | Any person experiencing mental health concerns such as depression, anxiety, psychosis, dementia, addiction, or psychosocial distress | Self-referral via helpline or app |
| Family members of persons with mental health issues | Relatives seeking guidance, support, or counselling on behalf of a loved one | Can access services for caregiver support and guidance |
| Grass-root health care providers / Community health providers | Accredited Social Health Activists (ASHAs), community volunteers, and other frontline workers | Can reach out on behalf of individuals in their community requiring mental health assistance |
| Vulnerable and hard-to-reach populations | Includes marginalized groups, rural populations, and those with limited access to in-person care | Specifically targeted for outreach and inclusion |

> **Key Eligibility Insight**: There are **no exclusion criteria** based on income, age, gender, geography, or diagnosis. Personal data such as name, age, gender, district, and state are queried during calls but are **not mandatory**. Service is provided regardless of whether such details are shared.

#### Key Process Details:
- **Helpline Numbers**: 14416 (short code) or 1-800-891-4416 (toll-free) — accessible nationwide.
- **IVRS System**: Ensures timely auto-call back; call routed to state-specific Tele MANAS cell based on caller’s region and language preference.
- **Counsellor Role**: First point of contact; assesses needs and provides care within scope or escalates to specialist.
- **Specialist Care**: Includes clinical psychologists, psychiatric social workers, psychiatric nurses, or psychiatrists.
- **Video Consultation**: Requires caller consent; SMS link sent to join; supports e-prescriptions.
- **E-Prescriptions**: Issued by psychiatrists for medication management; digital and valid nationwide.
- **Emergency Protocol**: In cases of suicide risk or harm to others, personal data (phone number) may be shared with police or mental health specialists in compliance with law.
- **Data Handling**: Audio/video calls are **not recorded**; only anonymized data retained for 3 years for research and analysis. Personal data (name, age, gender, district, state) is queried but **not mandatory**.
- **Follow-Up**: Enabled via phone number history (masked and stored) to ensure continuity of care.
- **Linkages**: To in-person services via District Mental Health Programme (DMHP), Medical Colleges, or eSanjeevani for audio-visual consultation.
- **Service Availability**: **24x7, 365 days a year** — no downtime, no holidays.
- **Last Updated**: 2026 (per scheme key facts).
- **Total Calls Milestone**: Over 10 lakh (1 million) calls received since launch in October 2022 (as of May 29, 2024).
- **Portal URL**: https://telemanas.mohfw.gov.in
- **Contact Details**:  
  - Email: mentalhealth-mohfw[at]gov[dot]in, telemanas[dot]nimhans[at]gmail.com  
  - Helpline: 14416 OR 1-800-891-4416

### Organizational Structure
Tele-MANAS operates under a two-tier system:
- **Tier 1**: State Tele MANAS Cells — comprising trained counsellors and mental health specialists at the state/UT level.
- **Tier 2**: District Mental Health Programme (DMHP)/Medical College resources — for physical consultation and/or eSanjeevani-based audio-visual consultation.
- **Apex Nodal Institute**: National Institute of Mental Health and Neurosciences (NIMHANS), Bengaluru — responsible for mentoring and monitoring.
- **Regional Coordinating Centers (5)**: NIMHANS (Bengaluru), LGBRIMH (Tezpur), CIP (Ranchi), IHBAS (Delhi), PGIMER (Chandigarh).
- **Mentoring Institutes (23)**: Support technical issues, capacity building, and implementation.
- **National Technical Advisory Group (NTAG)**: Steers strategy, scope, and activities at the national level.

### Key Caveats
> - Personal data such as name, age, gender, district, and state are queried during calls but are **not mandatory**.  
> - Audio or video call recordings are **not stored**; only anonymized data is retained for **3 years** for research and analysis.  
> - In case of emergency or risk to life, personal data may be shared with police or mental health specialists **in compliance with law**.  
> - The service is **completely free** — no charges, no hidden fees, no premium tiers.  
> - Available in **20 Indian languages** to ensure linguistic inclusivity.  
> - Designed to bridge the **treatment gap** (70-92% for mental disorders in India) by reaching underserved and hard-to-access populations.
