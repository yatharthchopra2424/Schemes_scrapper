## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] School is managed by Central Government (e.g., Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan - KVS)
- [ ] School is managed by State/UT Government
- [ ] School is managed by local bodies (including Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti - NVS)
- [ ] School is not privately managed or aided (only government-managed schools are eligible)
- [ ] School is located within the geographic scope of India (Pan-India scheme)
- [ ] School has not already been selected/developed under PM SHRI Schools scheme (verify via portal or nodal officer)

> **Note**: The scheme does not specify turnover, employee count, or age of business criteria, as it applies to government schools. Eligibility is based solely on school management type.

**Source**: Eligibility criteria from KEY FACTS block: "Schools managed by Central Government, State/UT Government, local bodies including Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS) and Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) are eligible to be developed under the PM SHRI Schools scheme."

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- School registration/recognition certificate from appropriate government authority
- PAN card of the school (if applicable) or sponsoring government body
- Audited financial statements / Income Tax Returns (ITR) for last 3 years (if applicable)
- Bank account details and recent bank statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable, though typically not required for government schools)
- Authorization letter from competent authority (e.g., District Education Officer, State Project Director) permitting application

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- School development plan aligned with National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 objectives
- Self-assessment report on current infrastructure, learning environment, and quality parameters
- Commitment letter to implement experiential, holistic, and inquiry-driven learning methodologies
- Details of existing infrastructure (classrooms, labs, library, sports facilities, ICT facilities)
- Proposed plan for upgradation under PM SHRI framework (including timeline and budget outline)
- Resolution from School Management Committee (SMC) or equivalent governing body approving application
- Undertaking to scale up learnings to other schools in the district/state post-selection

> **Note**: While the evidence does not explicitly list document requirements, these are inferred from scheme objectives, workshop materials (e.g., focus on NEP, quality parameters, skill initiatives), and standard government grant application practices for school upgradation schemes.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is **not explicitly mentioned** in the evidence as a mandatory requirement. However, given the scheme’s focus on infrastructural upgradation, quality benchmarks, and school transformation framework (as referenced in workshop materials), a DPR or equivalent project proposal may be required.

**Action**: Verify with the nodal officer or scheme guidelines whether a DPR is required. If yes, it should include:
- Executive Summary
- School Profile (location, management, student strength, current infrastructure)
- Needs Assessment (gaps in infrastructure, learning environment, quality parameters)
- Objectives aligned with NEP 2020 and PM SHRI vision
- Detailed Intervention Plan (civil works, ICT upgradation, library, labs, sports, teacher training)
- Implementation Timeline (phased over 5-year scheme period)
- Budget Breakdown (cost estimates for each component)
- Monitoring and Evaluation Framework (quality benchmarks, SQAF, third-party assessment)
- Sustainability Plan (post-approval maintenance and scaling up of learnings)
- Annexures (drawings, certificates, resolutions, undertakings)

> **Note**: The workshop report references "SQAF, quality benchmarks as part of school transformation framework," which should be incorporated if DPR is required.

**Source**: PM SHRI Workshop Report (pdf) – mentions "modalities of PM SHRI scheme (SQAF, quality benchmarks as part of school transformation framework)".

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official portal: **https://pmshrischools.education.gov.in** (use National/State/District User or School User login as applicable).
2. Verify login credentials are active; if not, register using official school details and authorization.
3. Fill all mandatory fields marked with (*) – do not leave any blank.
4. Ensure school name, address, and UDISE code match exactly with official records.
5. Select correct management type (Central Govt/State Govt/Local Body/KVS/NVS) from dropdown.
6. Provide accurate student enrollment data (as per latest UDISE+).
7. Upload documents in prescribed format (PDF/JPG, under size limit) with correct naming convention.
8. In the "Vision and Plan" section, explicitly reference NEP 2020, experiential learning, and commitment to scale up learnings.
9. Double-check all entries before final submission – no edits allowed after submission.
10. Save draft periodically to avoid loss due to portal maintenance or timeout.
11. After submission, download and save the acknowledgment slip with application number.
12. Do not share login credentials or OTPs with unauthorized persons.

> **Note**: Portal-specific notes are derived from crawled login pages (user/login, school/login) and standard government portal practices.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify official approval letter from Ministry of Education or designated state nodal agency
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount, components approved (civil works, ICT, library, etc.), and release schedule
- [ ] Sign memorandum of understanding (MoU) or agreement as required by the scheme
- [ ] Utilize first installment only after approval of detailed work plan and procurement procedures
- [ ] Maintain separate bank account or ledger for PM SHRI funds (if required)
- [ ] Submit monthly/quarterly progress reports as per monitoring framework
- [ ] Prepare and submit Utilization Certificate (UC) after full utilization of funds, audited by chartered accountant
- [ ] Ensure compliance with quality benchmarks and SQAF (School Quality Assessment Framework) as referenced in workshop materials
- [ ] Document and report on scaling up of learnings to other schools in the cluster/district
- [ ] Track and claim success fee as per consultancy agreement (typically 5–10% of sanctioned amount, upon milestone achievement)
- [ ] Coordinate with nodal officer for third-party verification or site visits, if scheduled
