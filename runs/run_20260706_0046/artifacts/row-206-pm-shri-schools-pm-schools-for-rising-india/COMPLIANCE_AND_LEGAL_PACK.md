**Data Handling Standards:**
- All client data is stored in encrypted cloud storage with AES-256 encryption.
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for all system access.
- Access to client data is limited to authorized personnel only on a need-to-know basis.
- Use of personal devices for accessing or storing client data is strictly prohibited.
- Regular security audits and penetration testing are conducted quarterly.
- Data retention: Client files are retained for 2 years post case closure, except as noted below.
- Breach notification: Any suspected or confirmed data breach will be reported to affected clients
  within 24 hours of discovery.

**Scheme-Specific Retention Note:**  
All documents submitted to Ministry of Education, Department of School Education & Literacy under PM SHRI Schools (PM Schools for Rising India)
must be retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit purposes. This extends beyond the standard 2-year retention period to comply with audit requirements
associated with centrally sponsored schemes administered by the Ministry of Education.

**Data Disposal:**  
After the applicable retention period, all data is securely deleted using cryptographic erasure
methods, and backups are destroyed in accordance with NIST 800-88 guidelines.

**Compliance:**  
This policy aligns with the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the SPDI Rules, 2011, as well as
sector-specific guidelines for handling educational scheme data.
