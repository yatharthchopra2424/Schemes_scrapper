## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the strategic value delivered in securing government grants for educational infrastructure and quality improvement under PM SHRI Schools.
2. **Risk Mitigation**: Success fees are calibrated to the scheme’s moderate risk profile — while funding is confirmed and implementing agency (Ministry of Education) is established, processing times are lengthy due to multi-layered government approvals (central/state/district levels) and scheme implementation over 5 years (2022-23 to 2026-27), requiring sustained client engagement.
3. **Transparency & Predictability**: Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, documentation preparation, and liaison with nodal agencies; success fee is contingent only on formal approval of school inclusion in the PM SHRI list.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Given the scheme’s 5-year rollout period and reliance on state/UT coordination (evidenced by workshop participation across multiple states), revenue recognition may lag 6–9 months post-application due to intergovernmental approval cycles, despite clear eligibility criteria for government/state/UT/local body schools including KVS and NVS.
