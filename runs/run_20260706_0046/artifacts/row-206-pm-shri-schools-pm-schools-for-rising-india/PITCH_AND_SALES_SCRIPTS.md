## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping government schools access central government grants for infrastructure and quality improvements. The PM SHRI Schools scheme is designed specifically for schools managed by Central Government, State/UT Government, or local bodies (including KVS and NVS) to create safe, stimulating learning environments with modern infrastructure and resources. I’d like to understand your school’s current situation to see if this scheme aligns with your development goals.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of management does your school have — Central Government, State/UT Government, local body, KVS, or NVS?
- What is the current condition of your school’s physical infrastructure (classrooms, labs, toilets, playgrounds)?
- Are you planning any upgrades or renovations to improve the learning environment for students?
- Do you have access to modern learning resources such as digital tools, science kits, or library materials?
- Are you looking to implement aspects of the National Education Policy 2020 in your school?
- What are your school’s goals for student development — academic engagement, productivity, and civic contribution?
- Have you previously applied for any central government school improvement grants?
- What challenges do you face in providing a wide range of learning experiences to all students?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] School is managed by Central Government
- [ ] School is managed by State/UT Government
- [ ] School is managed by a local body (municipal corporation, panchayat, etc.)
- [ ] School is a Kendriya Vidyalaya (KVS)
- [ ] School is a Navodaya Vidyalaya (NVS)
- [ ] School aims to provide a safe and stimulating learning environment
- [ ] School seeks to offer a wide range of learning experiences
- [ ] School requires improved physical infrastructure and learning resources
- [ ] School is committed to nurturing engaged, productive, and contributing citizens
- [ ] School aligns with National Education Policy 2020 implementation goals

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your school meets the core eligibility criteria for the PM SHRI Schools scheme — a centrally sponsored grant initiative by the Ministry of Education to develop over 14,500 exemplar schools. This scheme provides financial support for upgradation and development, directly benefiting students through improved infrastructure and learning environments. Shall I outline how it works and what support is available?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many government schools struggle with outdated infrastructure, limited learning resources, and insufficient facilities to provide a holistic, stimulating learning environment. This hinders student engagement, safety, and the ability to implement modern pedagogical approaches envisioned under the National Education Policy 2020.

**Scheme intro:**
The PM SHRI Schools (PM Schools for Rising India) scheme is a centrally sponsored grant by the Government of India, administered by the Ministry of Education, Department of School Education & Literacy. It aims to develop more than 14,500 PM SHRI Schools across India, with financial support provided for the development and upgradation of eligible schools managed by Central Government, State/UT Government, local bodies, KVS, or NVS. While the exact quantum of financial support is not specified in the evidence, the scheme focuses on creating safe, stimulating learning environments with wide-ranging learning experiences, good physical infrastructure, and appropriate resources conducive to learning.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your school is managed by [Central Government/State/UT Government/local body/KVS/NVS] — an eligible managing entity under the scheme
- You seek to provide a safe and stimulating learning environment — a core objective of PM SHRI Schools
- You aim to offer a wide range of learning experiences and improve physical infrastructure — key benefits delivered by the scheme
- You are aligned with implementing the National Education Policy 2020 — a guiding framework for the scheme
