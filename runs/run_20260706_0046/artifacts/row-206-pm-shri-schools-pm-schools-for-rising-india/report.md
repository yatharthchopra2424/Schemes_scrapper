## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
PM SHRI Schools (PM Schools for Rising India) is a centrally sponsored scheme by the Government of India under the Ministry of Education, Department of School Education & Literacy. The scheme aims to develop more than 14,500 exemplar schools across India that showcase the implementation of the National Education Policy 2020 (NEP 2020). These schools will serve as models of quality, inclusivity, and innovation, with learnings scaled up to other schools nationwide.

### Objectives
The scheme has the following clearly defined objectives:
- Develop more than 14,500 PM SHRI Schools across India
- Ensure every student feels welcomed and cared for in a safe and stimulating learning environment
- Provide wide-ranging learning experiences and good physical infrastructure conducive to learning
- Nurture students to become engaged, productive, and contributing citizens for an equitable, inclusive, and plural society
- Promote understanding of various dimensions of quality in school education to inform policy, practice, and implementation
- Scale up learnings from PM SHRI Schools to other schools in the country
- Showcase implementation of the National Education Policy 2020 and emerge as exemplar schools

### Eligibility Matrix
| Eligible Entity Type | Details |
|----------------------|---------|
| Central Government Schools | Schools directly managed by the Central Government of India |
| State/UT Government Schools | Schools managed by any State Government or Union Territory Administration |
| Local Body Schools | Schools managed by municipal corporations, municipal councils, zilla parishads, or other local self-government institutions |
| Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS) | Schools under the Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan system |
| Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) | Schools under the Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti system |

> **Note**: Only schools falling under the above management types are eligible to apply. Private schools, aided schools, or schools managed by NGOs/trusts are **not eligible** under this scheme.

### Target Beneficiaries

- **Expected Direct Beneficiaries**: More than 20 lakh (2 million) students across India
- **Indirect Beneficiaries**: Students in non-PM SHRI Schools through scaling up of best practices, quality parameters, and innovative pedagogies
