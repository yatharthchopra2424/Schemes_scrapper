## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Institution is a higher education institution (public or private) seeking recognition as an Institute of Eminence  
- [ ] Institution demonstrates potential to achieve global ranking among top 500 institutions within 10 years  
- [ ] Institution has a clear vision and strategic plan for excellence in teaching, research, and innovation  
- [ ] Institution possesses adequate infrastructure, faculty, and financial resources to support IoE objectives  
- [ ] Institution is not already designated as an Institute of National Importance (INI) or similar central government-funded premier institution  
- [ ] Institution commits to autonomy in academic, administrative, and financial matters as per IoE guidelines  
- [ ] Institution agrees to adhere to performance benchmarks and monitoring mechanisms under the scheme  

> **Note**: Eligibility criteria are based on the Institutes of Eminence (IoE) Scheme guidelines as referenced from the Ministry of Education portal. Specific thresholds (e.g., funding, faculty-student ratio) are defined in the scheme notification and must be verified with the nodal officer.

**Sources**:  
- Scheme portal: https://education.gov.in/ioe  
- Ministry of Education: https://education.gov.in/

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration  
- PAN Card of the Institution  
- Audited Financial Statements (last 3 years)  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the Institution (last 3 years)  
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (if applicable as an MSME entity)  
- Authorization Letter from Governing Body / Board of Trustees  
- Resolution for Application Submission  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining vision, mission, strategic plan, and 10-year roadmap to achieve global excellence  
- Self-Assessment Report against IoE performance parameters (teaching, research, innovation, internationalization, governance)  
- Proof of Academic Autonomy (existing statutes, ordinances, or board resolutions granting autonomy)  
- Faculty Details: List, qualifications, publications, and research grants (last 3 years)  
- Student Data: Enrollment, pass-out rates, placement statistics, and demographic diversity  
- Infrastructure Details: Labs, libraries, ICT facilities, sports, and hostel capacity  
- Financial Plan: Breakdown of proposed investment, funding sources, and utilization schedule for IoE grant  
- Memorandum of Understanding (MoUs) with international institutions (if any)  
- Annual Reports (last 3 years)  
- Accreditation Certificates (NAAC, NBA, or equivalent)  
- Research Output: Patents, publications in SCOPUS/Web of Science, PhDs awarded  
- Innovation and Entrepreneurship Support: Incubation center details, startups funded, industry collaborations  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (minimum 300 DPI)  
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_InstitutionName_DocumentType_YYYYMMDD.pdf`  
- Certificates and reports must be issued by competent authorities and be valid on the date of submission  
- Financial documents must be audited by a Chartered Accountant (CA) firm  
- All documents must be in English or accompanied by a certified English translation  
- DPR and self-assessment report must be signed and stamped by the Head of Institution  
- File size for individual documents must not exceed 10 MB

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required.**  
Standard sections to include:  
1. Executive Summary  
2. Institutional Vision and Mission  
3. Strategic Plan (10-year roadmap) to achieve top 500 global ranking  
4. Academic Excellence Plan (teaching, learning, curriculum innovation)  
5. Research and Innovation Strategy (funding, publications, patents, PhDs)  
6. Internationalization Plan (collaborations, student/faculty exchange, global rankings)  
7. Governance and Autonomy Framework  
8. Financial Plan and Resource Mobilization  
9. Infrastructure Development Plan  
10. Monitoring and Evaluation Mechanism  
11. Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
12. Annexures: Supporting data, MoUs, accreditation proofs  

> **Note**: DPR format and weightage criteria must be verified with the nodal officer as per latest IoE guidelines.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal exclusively via **https://education.gov.in/ioe**  
2. Use only the latest version of the application form downloaded from the portal  
3. Fill all fields in English; do not leave any mandatory field blank  
4. Enter numerical data (e.g., faculty count, budget) in whole numbers unless decimals are specified  
5. Upload documents in PDF format only, ensuring file names match the checklist  
6. Verify that the Head of Institution’s digital signature is applied where required  
7. Cross-check all entered data against source documents before final submission  
8. Do not use special characters or symbols in text fields unless explicitly allowed  
9. Save draft periodically to avoid data loss due to session timeout  
10. Take a screenshot of the final confirmation page after submission
