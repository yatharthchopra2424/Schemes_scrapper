## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-based alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial and strategic value delivered to eligible institutions under the Institutes of Eminence (IoE) Scheme, which includes significant funding and regulatory autonomy.
2. **Risk-adjusted pricing**: Success fees account for the scheme’s competitive selection process and moderate processing time, as evidenced by the multi-stage evaluation framework involving expert committees and UGC oversight.
3. **Transparency & predictability**: Retainer fees cover initial eligibility assessment, documentation preparation, and liaison with the Ministry of Education, ensuring clients understand costs upfront.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: The IoE Scheme involves a rigorous, multi-phase selection process with no guaranteed outcome, leading to a conservative success fee range (2–4%) reflective of its competitive nature and reliance on institutional readiness rather than automatic entitlement.
