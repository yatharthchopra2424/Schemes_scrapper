## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping higher education institutions access government recognition and funding schemes like the Institutes of Eminence (IoE) Scheme, which supports universities aiming to achieve global excellence through enhanced autonomy and financial grants. I’d like to understand your institution’s goals and see if this scheme aligns with your vision for growth and international competitiveness.

### Discovery Questions
- What is the current incorporation or establishment year of your institution, and are you recognized under the UGC Act, 1956?
- Is your institution planning to pursue or currently engaged in initiatives to enhance global rankings, research output, or international collaborations?
- Are you seeking greater academic and administrative autonomy to innovate in curriculum, admissions, or faculty recruitment?
- Does your institution have a strategic plan to achieve top 500 global rankings within a defined timeline?
- Are you open to public-private partnerships or foreign collaborations to strengthen academic offerings?
- What is your current annual revenue or funding capacity, and are you looking for supplementary government support for infrastructure or research?
- Have you previously applied for any central government recognition or funding schemes in higher education?
- Are the key decision-makers (e.g., Board of Management, Vice-Chancellor) aligned on pursuing eminence status?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Institution is a higher education entity (university or deemed-to-be-university)
- [ ] Established and functioning for at least 3 years
- [ ] Recognized under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956 (for deemed universities) or established by Central/State Act
- [ ] Demonstrates commitment to achieving global excellence (top 500 ranking aspiration)
- [ ] Willing to adopt innovative practices in governance, curriculum, and research
- [ ] Open to public-private partnerships and international collaborations
- [ ] Not currently under any regulatory restriction or disqualification by UGC/MoE
- [ ] Capable of meeting financial and operational commitments under the IoE framework

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your institution is aiming for global recognition and greater autonomy—exactly what the Institutes of Eminence (IoE) Scheme is designed to support. Let me share how this scheme can provide the financial backing and regulatory flexibility needed to accelerate your journey toward eminence.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many Indian higher education institutions struggle to compete globally due to regulatory constraints, limited funding for innovation, and lack of autonomy to adopt international best practices in teaching, research, and governance. This hinders their ability to attract top faculty, global partnerships, and high-impact research opportunities.

**Scheme intro:**
The Institutes of Eminence (IoE) Scheme, launched by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, selects public and private higher education institutions and grants them the status of "Institute of Eminence." Selected institutions receive ₹1,000 crore in funding over five years (for public institutions) and greater autonomy, including freedom to admit up to 30% foreign students, fix fees for foreign students, offer online courses up to 20% of total programs, and collaborate with top 500 global institutions without prior approval.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Recognized under the UGC Act, 1956 (as deemed university or state/central university)
- Established and operational for a minimum of 3 years
- Demonstrates a clear vision and roadmap to achieve global top 500 ranking
- Committed to academic innovation, internationalization, and excellence in research
