## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a faculty member or researcher at a premier academic institution (IIT, IISc, NIT, IISER, or other centrally funded technical institution)  
- [ ] Proposal is submitted in collaboration with an identified user agency (e.g., DRDO, ISRO, Ministry of Railways, etc.) that defines the problem statement and commits to potential adoption of the developed technology  
- [ ] Project addresses a relevant challenge area under the IMPRINT India thematic domains  
- [ ] Project has clear user agency involvement from inception  
- [ ] Project is not basic or theoretical research without clear application potential  
- [ ] Intellectual property rights terms are pre-agreed between participating institutions and user agencies (to be verified during proposal development)  
- [ ] Project scope and duration allow for disbursement of grant in installments tied to milestones and deliverables  

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts (Eligibility, Application Process, Key Caveats); Application Portal: https://www.imprintindia.org

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- [ ] Incorporation/Registration Certificate of the academic institution  
- [ ] PAN Card of the academic institution  
- [ ] GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- [ ] Bank Statements (last 6 months) of the institutional account  
- [ ] Udyam Registration Certificate (if the institution is registered as an MSME, though typically not required for premier institutions)  
- [ ] Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the institution for the last 2 financial years  
- [ ] Audited Financial Statements of the institution for the last 2 years  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- [ ] Project proposal document (detailed, including objectives, methodology, budget, timeline, expected outcomes)  
- [ ] Budget breakdown (itemized, covering manpower, equipment, consumables, travel, contingency)  
- [ ] Timeline and milestones (clearly defined, tied to deliverables)  
- [ ] Letter of collaboration from user agency (defining problem statement and committing to potential adoption)  
- [ ] CVs of principal investigators and all team members  
- [ ] Institutional endorsement from participating academic institutions (letter of support from Head of Department/Institute)  
- [ ] Details of prior work and intellectual property status (including any existing IP, patents, or background IP)  

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible, scanned in PDF format (unless otherwise specified)  
- [ ] File names follow the naming convention: `SchemeName_InstitutionName_DocumentType_YYYYMMDD.pdf`  
- [ ] File size does not exceed 10 MB per document (standard portal limit; verify on portal)  
- [ ] All certificates are valid and not expired (check issue/expiry dates)  
- [ ] Letters of collaboration and endorsement are on official letterhead, signed, and stamped  
- [ ] CVs are updated and highlight relevant research experience  
- [ ] Budget breakdown is realistic and justifies each cost head  
- [ ] All documents are in English or accompanied by a certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a detailed **Project Proposal Document** is required (functionally equivalent to a DPR). It must include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Problem Statement (defined by user agency)  
- Objectives and Scope  
- Methodology and Work Plan  
- Budget Breakdown (manpower, equipment, consumables, travel, contingency)  
- Timeline and Milestones (with deliverables)  
- Expected Outcomes and Impact (societal, strategic, commercialization potential)  
- Details of Prior Work and IP Status  
- Team Composition and CVs of Principal Investigators  
- Institutional Endorsement  
- Letter of Collaboration from User Agency  

> **Note**: Verify specific formatting or length requirements with the nodal agency (currently IIT Madras) or via the portal https://www.imprintindia.org.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal at **https://www.imprintindia.org** and register/create an account if required.  
2. Download the official proposal template (if available) or follow the structure outlined in the scheme guidelines.  
3. Fill in all fields accurately; do not leave any mandatory section blank.  
4. Ensure the problem statement is clearly defined by and aligned with the user agency’s needs.  
5. Double-check that the budget is itemized, realistic, and justifies each cost head (no lump-sum entries).  
6. Attach all scheme-specific documents as per the checklist in Stage 2, using the correct file names and formats.  
7. Confirm that the letter of collaboration from the user agency is on official letterhead, signed, and stamped.  
8. Review the entire proposal for clarity, coherence, and alignment with IMPRINT India’s objectives (translation of research into usable technology).  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All sections of the project proposal are complete and well-structured  
- [ ] Budget breakdown is itemized, realistic, and sums correctly  
- [ ] Timeline and milestones are specific, measurable, and tied to deliverables  
- [ ] Letter of collaboration from user agency is present, signed, and on official letterhead  
- [ ] CVs of all team members are included and updated  
- [ ] Institutional endorsement letter is present and signed by competent authority  
- [ ] Details of prior work and IP status are disclosed  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached and meet quality checks  
- [ ] Declarations (if any) are signed by the Principal Investigator and authorized institutional representative  
- [ ] Application is submitted within the portal’s technical guidelines (file types, sizes)
