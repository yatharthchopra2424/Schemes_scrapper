## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual grant amount awarded under IMPRINT India, which varies by project scope and duration but typically supports substantial R&D investments in manpower, equipment, and consumables.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s reliance on user agency collaboration and milestone-based disbursements, success fees reflect the moderate risk of non-approval or delayed funding due to progress reviews and technical scrutiny by expert committees.
3. **Tiered Access**: The scheme targets premier academic institutions (IITs, IISc, NITs, IISERs) and their collaborations with user agencies (e.g., DRDO, ISRO, Railways), placing it firmly in the Scale and Enterprise tiers due to the institutional scale and project complexity involved.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover proposal development, consortium building, and technical writing, while success fees are contingent on fund sanction and disbursement.
