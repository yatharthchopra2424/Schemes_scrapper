## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping academic researchers and faculty from premier technical institutions like IITs, IISc, NITs, and IISERs access government-funded R&D grants that solve real-world national challenges. The IMPRINT India scheme is designed specifically for researchers who want their work to move beyond the lab and into strategic deployment — whether in defence, space, railways, or other user agencies. I’d like to understand your current research focus and see if this aligns with opportunities where your expertise could be funded and adopted.

### Discovery Questions
- What is your current academic affiliation, and are you based at a centrally funded technical institution such as an IIT, IISc, NIT, or IISER?
- Are you currently working on, or planning, a research project that addresses a specific challenge in defence, space, railways, healthcare, energy, or another national priority area?
- Do you already have, or are you in discussions with, a user agency (e.g., DRDO, ISRO, Ministry of Railways, etc.) that has defined a problem statement and expressed interest in adopting your research outcomes?
- Is your research focused on applied innovation with a clear pathway to technology transfer, product development, or strategic deployment — rather than purely theoretical or basic science?
- Have you previously collaborated with industry, government agencies, or user departments on research projects?
- Are you prepared to develop a detailed project proposal, including budget, timeline, milestones, and institutional endorsement, in collaboration with a user agency?
- Does your institution support and endorse inter-institutional and agency-collaborative research projects for external funding?
- Are you seeking grant-based funding that covers manpower, equipment, consumables, travel, and contingency costs for a defined R&D project?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is a faculty member or researcher at a premier academic institution (IIT, IISc, NIT, IISER, or other centrally funded technical institution)
- [ ] Research proposal is in collaboration with a identified user agency (e.g., DRDO, ISRO, Ministry of Railways, etc.)
- [ ] User agency has defined the problem statement and committed to potential adoption of the technology
- [ ] Project focuses on applied research with clear application potential (not basic/theoretical research)
- [ ] Project aligns with IMPRINT India thematic domains (defence, space, railways, healthcare, energy, etc.)
- [ ] Detailed project proposal, budget breakdown, timeline, and milestones can be developed
- [ ] Letter of collaboration from user agency is available or can be obtained
- [ ] Institutional endorsement from the participating academic institution can be secured
- [ ] CVs of principal investigators and team members are available
- [ ] Details of prior work and IP status can be provided

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like your research has strong alignment with IMPRINT India’s goal of translating academic innovation into nationally strategic solutions. If you’re working with a user agency on an applied R&D project, this scheme could provide grant-based funding to cover your project costs — while ensuring your work has a clear path to adoption. Let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many excellent research projects in India’s premier technical institutions struggle to move beyond the lab due to lack of funding for applied development, weak industry or user agency linkages, and unclear pathways to adoption. Researchers often face challenges in securing sustained support for prototyping, trials, and technology transfer — especially when working on national priority areas like defence, space, or infrastructure.

**Scheme intro:**
IMPRINT India (Impacting Research Innovation and Technology) is a joint initiative by the Ministry of Education, Ministry of Defence, and other user agencies. It provides grant-based financial support to collaborative research projects between premier academic institutions (IITs, IISc, NITs, IISERs, etc.) and user agencies that define real-world problem statements. Funding covers manpower, equipment, consumables, travel, and contingency costs, disbursed in milestone-linked installments. The quantum varies per project based on scope and duration, with no fixed upper limit — support is tailored to the agreed-upon project plan.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a faculty member or researcher at a premier technical institution (IIT, IISc, NIT, IISER, or similar)
- Your project involves collaboration with a user agency that has defined the problem statement
- Your research focuses on applied innovation with potential for technology transfer and strategic deployment
- Your work aligns with national priority domains such as defence, space, railways, healthcare, or energy
