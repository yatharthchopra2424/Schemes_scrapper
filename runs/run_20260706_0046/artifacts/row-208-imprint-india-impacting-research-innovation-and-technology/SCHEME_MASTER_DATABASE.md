### IMPRINT India (Impacting Research Innovation and Technology)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Education, Government of India (in collaboration with Ministry of Defence and other user agencies)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.imprintindia.org
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (proposals can be submitted throughout the year as per announcements from the coordinating agency)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Academic institutions and research organisations
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Higher education and research (engineering, technology, science)
- Revenue cap: Not applicable
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: 
  - Faculty members and researchers from premier academic institutions (IITs, IISc, NITs, IISERs, and other centrally funded technical institutions)
  - Proposals must be submitted in collaboration with identified user agencies (e.g., DRDO, ISRO, Ministry of Railways, etc.) that define the problem statement and commit to potential adoption of the developed technology
  - Projects must have clear user agency involvement from inception
  - The scheme does not support basic or theoretical research without clear application potential

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Grant-based financial support covering manpower, equipment, consumables, travel, and contingency costs; quantum varies per project based on scope and duration
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit in installments tied to project milestones and deliverables as agreed upon with the user agency
- Duration of benefit: Project-based (disbursed in installments over project duration)

#### Documents Required
1. Project proposal document
2. Budget breakdown
3. Timeline and milestones
4. Letter of collaboration from user agency
5. CVs of principal investigators and team members
6. Institutional endorsement from participating academic institutions
7. Details of prior work and intellectual property status

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify a relevant challenge area under the IMPRINT India thematic domains
2. Form a consortium with academic partners and a user agency that defines the problem statement
3. Develop a detailed project proposal including objectives, methodology, budget, timeline, and expected outcomes
4. Submit the proposal through the designated portal or nodal agency (currently IIT Madras acts as the coordinating hub)
5. Proposals undergo technical review by expert committees
6. Selected projects are approved for funding and implementation begins upon sanction
