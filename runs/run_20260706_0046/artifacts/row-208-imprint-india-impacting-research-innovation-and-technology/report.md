## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
IMPRINT India (Impacting Research Innovation and Technology) is a pan-India grant scheme implemented by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, in collaboration with the Ministry of Defence and other user agencies. The scheme operates on a rolling basis, allowing proposals to be submitted throughout the year as per announcements from the coordinating agency. It aims to bridge the gap between academic research and societal/strategic needs by fostering collaboration between premier academic institutions and user agencies.

### Objectives
- Identify and address major engineering and technology challenges in domains relevant to national development  
- Promote collaborative research between premier academic institutions (IITs, IISc, NITs, etc.) and user agencies (including defence, space, railways, etc.)  
- Ensure higher funding for research into usable and affordable technology solutions  
- Accelerate the translation of research into products, processes, and services for societal and strategic applications  
- Strengthen industry-academia partnership in research and innovation  
- Enhance the quality and relevance of research output from Indian higher educational institutions  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Criteria | Details |
|---------|--------|
| **Eligible Applicants** | Faculty members and researchers of premier academic institutions such as IITs, IISc, NITs, IISERs, and other centrally funded technical institutions |
| **Collaboration Requirement** | Proposals must be submitted in collaboration with identified user agencies (e.g., Defence Research and Development Organisation, Indian Space Research Organisation, Ministry of Railways, etc.) that define the problem statement and commit to potential adoption of the developed technology |
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Academic institutions; researchers; scientists; faculty members; user agencies |
| **Research Focus** | Must address major engineering and technology challenges in domains relevant to national development; basic or theoretical research without clear application potential is not supported |
| **Geographic Scope** | Pan-India |

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Support Type | Details |
|-------------|--------|
| **Financial Support** | Grant-based funding covering manpower, equipment, consumables, travel, and contingency costs; quantum varies per project based on scope and duration; disbursed in installments tied to project milestones and deliverables as agreed upon with the user agency |
| **Non-Financial Benefits** | Access to user agency problem statements and domain expertise; opportunity for technology transfer and product development; enhanced visibility and impact of academic research; potential for commercialization and strategic deployment of outcomes; facilitation of industry-academia-government collaboration |

### Application Process Flowchart
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Identify a relevant challenge area under IMPRINT India thematic domains] --> B[Form a consortium with academic partners and a user agency that defines the problem statement]
    B --> C[Develop a detailed project proposal including objectives, methodology, budget, timeline, and expected outcomes]
    C --> D[Submit the proposal through the designated portal or nodal agency (currently IIT Madras acts as the coordinating hub)]
    D --> E[Proposals undergo technical review by expert committees]
    E --> F[Selected projects are approved for funding]
    F --> G[Implementation begins upon sanction]
    G --> H[Funding disbursed in installments tied to project milestones and deliverables]
    H --> I[Progress reviews and achievement of milestones monitored]
    I --> J[Intellectual property rights governed by pre-agreed terms between participating institutions and user agencies]
```

### Key Caveats
> - Projects must have clear user agency involvement from inception  
> - Funding is contingent on progress reviews and achievement of milestones  
> - Intellectual property rights are governed by pre-agreed terms between participating institutions and user agencies  
> - The scheme does not support basic or theoretical research without clear application potential  

### Required Documents
1. Project proposal document  
2. Budget breakdown  
3. Timeline and milestones  
4. Letter of collaboration from user agency  
5. CVs of principal investigators and team members  
6. Institutional endorsement from participating academic institutions  
7. Details of prior work and intellectual property status  

### Application Portal
**Official Portal:** https://www.imprintindia.org  
**Coordinating Hub:** IIT Madras (currently acts as the nodal agency for proposal submission)  

### Status & Deadlines
- **Status:** Rolling basis  
- **Deadlines:** Proposals can be submitted throughout the year as per announcements from the coordinating agency
