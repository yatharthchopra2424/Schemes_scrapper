## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the scheme’s financial benefit (up to US$12,000 ≈ ₹9.96L for longer courses).
2. Tiered success fees reflect effort-to-reward ratio; lower % for higher-value schemes due to standardized deliverables.
3. Retainer covers eligibility assessment, documentation, and portal coordination — fixed effort regardless of scheme value.
4. Scheme-specific note: Low risk profile due to clear eligibility (top 200 NIRF, NAAC A+), high success rate evidenced by 2163 approved courses, and predictable 3-month review timeline.
