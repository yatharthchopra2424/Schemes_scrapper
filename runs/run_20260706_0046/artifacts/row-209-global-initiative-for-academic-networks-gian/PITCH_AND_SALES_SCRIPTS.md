# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping government higher education institutions access international academic collaboration funding. The Global Initiative for Academic Networks (GIAN) brings world-renowned foreign faculty to Indian campuses for short-term courses, covering their honorarium and travel—up to US$12,000 per course. I’d like to understand your institute’s goals for global engagement and see if GIAN aligns with your strategy.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your institution’s current NIRF overall ranking, and are you within the top 200?
2. Does your institute hold a valid NAAC ‘A’ grade certificate (CGPA 3.0 or above)?
3. Are you a Government (State or Central) higher education institution or university?
4. Have you previously hosted international faculty for short-term courses or workshops?
5. What are your strategic goals for increasing international collaboration and global exposure for students and faculty?
6. Which academic departments are most interested in hosting foreign experts in cutting-edge areas?
7. Do you have a designated local coordinator who could manage the GIAN application and course coordination?
8. Are you planning to conduct any faculty development programs or student exposure initiatives in the next 6–12 months?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Government (State or Central) higher education institution / university
- [ ] NIRF overall ranking within top 200
- [ ] Valid NAAC ‘A’ grade certificate (CGPA ≥ 3.0)
- [ ] Willingness to appoint a local coordinator for GIAN
- [ ] Capacity to arrange local hospitality for visiting foreign faculty
- [ ] Interest in international faculty engagement, knowledge exchange, or collaborative research

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your institute meets GIAN’s core eligibility criteria—being a government institution ranked in the top 200 NIRF with NAAC ‘A’ grade. The Global Initiative for Academic Networks (GIAN) can support you in bringing international faculty for short-term courses, with funding of up to US$12,000 to cover honorarium and travel. Let me explain how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many Indian higher education institutions struggle to access global expertise due to budget constraints, limiting opportunities for faculty development, student exposure to international perspectives, and collaborative research with renowned overseas experts.

**Scheme intro:**
The Global Initiative for Academic Networks (GIAN), launched by the Ministry of Education (formerly MHRD) and coordinated by IIT Kharagpur, provides funding of up to US$8,000 for short-duration courses (12–14 contact hours) and up to US$12,000 for longer-duration courses (20–28 contact hours) to cover honorarium and travel for foreign faculty. Local hospitality is arranged by the host institute.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your institute is a Government (State or Central) higher education institution  
- You are ranked within the top 200 in NIRF overall rankings  
- You hold a valid NAAC ‘A’ grade certificate (CGPA 3.0 or above)  
- You can appoint a local coordinator to manage the GIAN portal and course logistics
