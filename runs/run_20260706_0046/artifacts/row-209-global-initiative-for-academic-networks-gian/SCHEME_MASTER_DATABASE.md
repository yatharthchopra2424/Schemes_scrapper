### Global Initiative for Academic Networks (GIAN)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Education (formerly Ministry of Human Resource Development)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.gian.iitkgp.ac.in/GREGN/
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Proposals may be submitted throughout the year. Three months required for review to final approval. Preferably, six months lead time from date of holding the course.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Government (State or Central) higher education Institutions / University
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Higher Education
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be in top 200 in NIRF overall rankings and having at least NAAC ‘A’ grade (3.0 and above)

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to US$ 8000 for short duration courses (12-14 hours) and up to US$ 12000 for longer duration courses (20-28 hours) for foreign faculty honorarium and travel
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit via PFMS after brochure upload
- Duration of benefit: One-time per approved course

#### Documents Required
1. Formal request letter from the highest Authority (VC / Director) of the Institute
2. NAAC ‘A’ grade certificate
3. Proof of NIRF Ranking in top 200
4. Formal office order for appointment of local coordinator, GIAN of the Institute/University (including name, email address and mobile number)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Institute confirms eligibility (top 200 NIRF, NAAC A grade)
2. Competent authority appoints a local coordinator for GIAN
3. Institute sends formal request letter from VC/Director, NAAC ‘A’ grade certificate, proof of NIRF ranking in top 200, and formal office order for local coordinator appointment (with name, email, mobile) to National Coordinator at IIT Kharagpur via email/speed post
4. After document verification, Institute is added to GIAN program and login created for local coordinator
5. Local coordinator logs into GIAN portal, edits profile, circulates proposal template to faculty
6. Local coordinator invites course coordinators to submit proposals via portal
7. Course coordinator uploads proposal in PDF format and submits
8. Local coordinator scrutinizes proposal and foreign faculty consent form, then forwards to GIAN office by clicking ‘Accept’
9. Proposal sent for document verification; if in order, forwarded to Chairman of appropriate sectional committee for review
10. Status updates until approval by Apex body (GIAN Implementation Committee)
11. Upon approval, local coordinator receives mail to upload final brochure and course dates
12. Local coordinator coordinates with course coordinator to upload brochure and finalize dates
13. Official signed sanction letter with fund details sent to Institute
14. Fund allocated released via PFMS after brochure upload
15. During course, local coordinator monitors via portal, ensures participant feedback submission
16. After course, local coordinator submits course completion report and fund utilization certificate to IIT Kharagpur within 15 days
