# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The Global Initiative for Academic Networks (GIAN) is a Government of India initiative under the Ministry of Education (formerly Ministry of Human Resource Development) aimed at tapping the talent pool of international scientists and entrepreneurs to engage with Indian higher education institutions. Launched in 2015, GIAN seeks to augment India's academic resources, accelerate quality reform, and elevate scientific and technological capacity to global excellence by facilitating international faculty engagement through short-term courses.

### Objectives
- Increase footfalls of reputed international faculty in Indian academic institutes  
- Provide opportunity for faculty to learn and share knowledge in cutting-edge areas  
- Provide opportunity for students to seek knowledge from international faculty  
- Create avenue for collaborative research with international faculty  
- Increase participation of international students in academic institutes  
- Enable interaction between students of different institutes in niche areas  
- Provide opportunity for technical persons from Indian industry to update knowledge  
- Motivate international experts to work on India-related problems  
- Develop high-quality course material in niche areas (video and print) for broader use  
- Document and develop new pedagogic methods in emerging topics of national and international interest

### Application Process (Mermaid Flowchart)
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Institute confirms eligibility: Top 200 NIRF + NAAC A grade] --> B[Competent authority appoints local coordinator for GIAN]
    B --> C[Institute sends formal request letter from VC/Director, NAAC A grade certificate, proof of NIRF ranking in top 200, and formal office order for local coordinator appointment (with name, email, mobile) to National Coordinator at IIT Kharagpur via email/speed post]
    C --> D{Document verification by National Coordinator}
    D -->|Documents in order| E[Institute added to GIAN program; login created for local coordinator]
    D -->|Documents deficient| F[Proposal sent back for modification/resubmission]
    F --> C
    E --> G[Local coordinator logs into GIAN portal, edits profile, circulates proposal template to faculty]
    G --> H[Local coordinator invites course coordinators to submit proposals via portal]
    H --> I[Course coordinator uploads proposal in PDF format and submits]
    I --> J[Local coordinator scrutinizes proposal and foreign faculty consent form, then forwards to GIAN office by clicking ‘Accept’]
    J --> K{Proposal sent for document verification}
    K -->|In order| L[Forwarded to Chairman of appropriate sectional committee for review]
    K -->|Not in order| M[Sent back to Course Coordinator for modification]
    M --> I
    L --> N[Review by Sectional Committee; recommendations forwarded to GIAN Implementation Committee]
    N --> O[Final approval by Apex body: GIAN Implementation Committee]
    O --> P[Upon approval, local coordinator receives mail to upload final brochure and course dates]
    P --> Q[Local coordinator coordinates with course coordinator to upload brochure and finalize dates]
    Q --> R[Official signed sanction letter with fund details sent to Institute]
    R --> S[Fund allocated released via PFMS after brochure upload]
    S --> T[During course: local coordinator monitors via portal, ensures participant feedback submission]
    T --> U[After course: local coordinator submits course completion report and fund utilization certificate to IIT Kharagpur within 15 days]
    U --> V[Course completion; funds utilized per guidelines]
```

#### Key Process Notes
- Proposals may be submitted throughout the year  
- Three months required for review to final approval  
- Preferably, six months lead time from date of holding the course  
- All approved courses must be completed by 31st December 2020 (per Local Coordinator manual; note: may be outdated)  
- GIAN to MOOC conversion of completed courses discontinued for both in-person and virtual mode  
- Foreign faculty not entitled to travel allowance in virtual mode; only honorarium paid  
- Honorarium for foreign faculty in virtual mode limited to $3000 for one week course (≥12 hrs) and $5000 for 2 week course (≥20 hrs)  
- Contingencies and other expenses (excluding travel and honorarium of foreign faculty) should not exceed 35% of total fund provided for a course  
- A maximum of Rs. 5000/- per course may be earmarked to local coordinator for office expenses and honorarium from contingency expense
