## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Business is engaged in food business activities (manufacturing, processing, packaging, storage, distribution, sale, or import of food products)  
- [ ] Person supervising the production process possesses at least a degree in Science with Chemistry/Bio Chemistry/Food and Nutrition/Microbiology OR a degree/diploma in food technology/dairy technology/dairy microbiology/dairy chemistry/dairy engineering/oil technology/veterinary science/hotel management & catering technology OR any degree/diploma in a discipline related to the specific requirements of the business from a recognized university/institute or equivalent  
- [ ] Business type identified (proprietorship, partnership, company, etc.) for correct document selection  
- [ ] Annual turnover assessed to determine license type (Basic: ≤ ₹12 lakhs, State: > ₹12 lakhs to ≤ ₹20 crores, Central: > ₹20 crores)  
- [ ] Business premises address verified for NOC and address proof requirements  
- [ ] Water source identified for potability report requirement (if applicable)  
- [ ] Recall plan applicability assessed (for manufacturers/processors)  
- [ ] EOU status confirmed (if 100% Export Oriented Unit)  
- [ ] Import/Export activities identified (for IE code requirement)  
- [ ] Hotel status verified (if applicable for Ministry of Tourism certificate)  
- [ ] Business is operating within India (Pan-India scheme)  

> **Note**: License type (Basic, State, Central) depends on annual turnover and nature of food business. Applications can be submitted throughout the year on a rolling basis. License/registration must be renewed periodically (validity: 1 to 5 years).  
> **Source**: [FSSAI Licensing & Registration Scheme Key Facts](https://foscos.fssai.gov.in)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for FSSAI licensing and registration. This scheme is a regulatory compliance mechanism, not a subsidy or grant-based program. However, if the client intends to link the FSSAI license to other government schemes requiring a DPR, verify with the nodal officer of the respective scheme.  

> **Source**: FSSAI Licensing & Registration Scheme Key Facts – "FSSAI licensing and registration under FoSCoS does not provide direct financial support such as grants, loans, or subsidies."

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Access the correct portal**: Always use https://foscos.fssai.gov.in for application submission  
2. **Select accurate license type**: Choose Basic (turnover ≤ ₹12 lakhs), State (₹12 lakhs–₹20 crores), or Central (> ₹20 crores) based on verified turnover  
3. **Enter business details exactly**: Match legal name, address, and PAN as per official documents (no abbreviations or variations)  
4. **List all food products comprehensively**: Use FSSAI product categories; include sub-items and variants (e.g., "Milk – Full Cream, Toned, Skimmed")  
5. **Specify supervisor qualifications**: Clearly state the degree/diploma of the person supervising production (as per eligibility criteria)  
6. **Upload documents in correct format**: Use PDF only; ensure each file is under 2 MB and named clearly  
7. **Pay fees online**: Fee varies by license type and duration (1–5 years); keep transaction ID for reference  
8. **Review before submission**: Use portal preview function to verify all fields and attachments  
9. **Submit during operational hours**: Avoid 11:30 PM–3:00 AM daily maintenance window  
10. **Save application reference number**: Immediately after submission, screenshot and store the application ID
