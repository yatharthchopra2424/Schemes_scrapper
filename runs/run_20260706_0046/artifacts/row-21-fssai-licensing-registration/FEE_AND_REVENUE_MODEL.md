## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees reflect the critical nature of compliance**: Pricing is aligned with the risk mitigation and market access value delivered by securing FSSAI license/registration, which enables legal operation, consumer trust, and eligibility for government tenders.
2. **Tiered by Business Scale**: Retainer and success fee percentages decrease with client size, reflecting economies of scale in documentation and process familiarity for larger, repeat clients.
3. **Risk-Adjusted for Scheme Profile**: FSSAI licensing has a **medium risk profile** due to moderate processing times (typically 15–60 days depending on license type and document completeness) and high dependency on accurate documentation and supervisory qualifications. Success in the crawled evidence shows consistent approvals when documentation is complete and eligibility criteria are met, but delays occur during system maintenance windows (daily 11:30 PM–3:00 AM) or if supervisory qualifications are lacking.
4. **Success Fee Contingent on Approval**: Success fees are payable only upon successful issuance of the FSSAI license or registration certificate, ensuring alignment of consultant incentives with client outcomes.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Retainer for FSSAI Licensing & Registration Consulting Services  
This invoice covers professional advisory services for assessing eligibility, determining appropriate license type (Basic/State/Central), document preparation, application submission via FoSCoS portal, and liaison with FSSAI authorities until acknowledgment of receipt. Services are rendered on a monthly basis to ensure timely compliance and application readiness.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success Fee for FSSAI Licensing & Registration – Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare  
This invoice is payable upon successful issuance of the FSSAI license or registration certificate through the FoSCoS portal (https://foscos.fssai.gov.in). The fee is contingent on the client receiving formal approval and downloadable certificate from FSSAI, confirming compliance to operate as a licensed food business operator.
