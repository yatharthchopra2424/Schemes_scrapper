### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Food business operators in India face mandatory regulatory compliance under the FSS Act, 2006. Operating without the appropriate FSSAI license or registration risks penalties, fines, business closure, and loss of consumer trust. Many businesses struggle with identifying the correct license type (Basic, State, or Central), compiling the required documentation, and navigating the FoSCoS portal—especially during daily maintenance windows (11:30 PM to 3:00 AM).

**Scheme intro:**
The FSSAI Licensing & Registration scheme, administered by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, is a pan-India regulatory compliance mechanism. It does not provide direct financial support but requires applicants to pay prescribed fees for license issuance and renewal. Licenses are valid for 1 to 5 years and must be renewed periodically. The scheme ensures food safety and hygiene standards across the supply chain through the FoSCoS portal at https://foscos.fssai.gov.in.
