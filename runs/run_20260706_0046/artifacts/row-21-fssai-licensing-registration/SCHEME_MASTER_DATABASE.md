### FSSAI Licensing & Registration
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://foscos.fssai.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (applications can be submitted throughout the year)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Any person or entity engaged in food business activities including manufacturing, processing, packaging, storage, distribution, sale, or import of food products
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Food business (manufacturing, processing, packaging, storage, distribution, sale, import)
- Revenue cap: License type (Basic, State, Central) depends on annual turnover (specific slabs not detailed in evidence)
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: The person supervising the production process must possess at least a degree in Science with Chemistry/Bio Chemistry/Food and Nutrition/Microbiology or a degree/diploma in food technology/dairy technology/dairy microbiology/dairy chemistry/dairy engineering/oil technology/veterinary science/hotel management & catering technology or any related discipline from a recognized university/institute

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: No direct financial support; benefits include legal compliance, consumer trust, access to government tenders/markets, facilitation of food exports, eligibility for government schemes/subsidies, and right to display FSSAI logo
- Disbursement mode: Not applicable (regulatory compliance mechanism; fees paid by applicant)
- Duration of benefit: License/registration validity ranges from 1 to 5 years (renewable)

#### Documents Required
1. Photo ID proof of proprietor/partner/director  
2. Address proof of business premises  
3. List of food products to be manufactured/processed/sold  
4. Blueprint/layout plan of the processing unit (for manufacturing units)  
5. List of equipment and machinery installed  
6. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from municipality or local body  
7. Water potability report  
8. Recall plan (where applicable)  
9. Ministry of Commerce certificate for 100% EOU  
10. NOC/PA document issued by FSSAI  
11. IE code document issued by DGFT (for importers/exporters)  
12. Form IX: Nomination of persons by a company along with the board resolution  
13. Certificate from Ministry of Tourism (for hotels)  
14. Proof of possession of premises (sale deed, rent agreement, electricity bill, etc.)  
15. Partnership deed/affidavit of proprietorship/Memorandum & Articles of Association  

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the FoSCoS portal at https://foscos.fssai.gov.in  
2. Sign up or log in using existing credentials  
3. Select 'Apply for License/Registration'  
4. Choose the appropriate license type (Basic, State, or Central) based on turnover and business scale  
5. Fill in the online application form with business and product details  
6. Upload required documents  
7. Pay the applicable fee online  
8. Submit the application and track status via the portal  
9. Upon approval, download the license/registration certificate
