## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- **Not required**. SWAYAM is a self-enrollment platform for individual learners. No Detailed Project Report (DPR) or project proposal is needed for registration or course enrollment.  
- *Note: If a university or institution wishes to adopt SWAYAM courses for credit transfer, they must follow the UGC Credit Framework and appoint a Nodal Officer — but this is outside the scope of individual learner enrollment.*

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Access the correct portal**: Always use https://swayam.gov.in (verify URL to avoid phishing sites).
2. **Click “Register as Student”**: This is the only entry point for learner registration.
3. **Enter accurate Name and Email ID**: Use the name as it appears on your government ID (for future exam alignment) and a valid, accessible email ID.
4. **Optional social login**: You may use Google or Microsoft accounts to speed up registration, but manual entry is equally valid.
5. **Verify your account**: Check your inbox (and spam folder) for a verification email from **support@swayam.gov.in** and click the link to activate your account.
6. **Browse courses responsibly**: Use filters (category, language, duration, exam date) to find courses aligned with your goals.
7. **Check course-specific details**: Before enrolling, review the course page for enrollment end dates, exam dates, and any recommended prerequisites (though not mandatory).
8. **Keep records**: Save a screenshot of your enrollment confirmation and the course details page for future reference (especially for exam registration or credit transfer requests).

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- **Screenshot proof**: Capture and store:
  - The registration confirmation page showing Name and Email ID.
  - The course enrollment confirmation page(s).
  - The verification email from support@swayam.gov.in (or screenshot of verified account status).
- **Reference number**: SWAYAM does not issue a traditional application reference number. Instead, use:
  - Your registered Email ID as the primary identifier.
  - The SWAYAM dashboard shows enrolled courses and progress.
- **Follow-up reminders**: 
  - Set reminders for course enrollment end dates (if applicable).
  - Set reminders for exam registration end dates (typically 1–2 months before exam date).
  - For self-paced courses: No enrollment deadline, but aim for steady progress.
