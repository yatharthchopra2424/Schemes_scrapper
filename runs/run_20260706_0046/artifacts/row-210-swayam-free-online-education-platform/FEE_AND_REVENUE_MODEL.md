## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned to the financial benefit delivered to the client.
2. Transparent fee structure with clear milestones for retainer and success fee.
3. Competitive rates benchmarked against similar government scheme consulting services.
4. Scheme-specific note: This scheme (SWAYAM Free Online Education Platform) offers free course access with nominal exam fees only (₹750–₹1000 per course). The financial benefit per learner is low, resulting in a low-risk, high-volume engagement model. Processing time is immediate for enrollment, but certification depends on exam schedules (typically 3–6 months post-enrollment), creating a moderate revenue lag for success fees tied to certification outcomes.
