# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good day! I specialize in helping learners, faculty, and working professionals access free, high-quality education from India’s top institutions through the SWAYAM Free Online Education Platform. Whether you're looking to upskill, earn certifications, or transfer academic credits, I’d like to understand your goals to see if SWAYAM is the right fit for you.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your primary goal in pursuing online learning—skill development, certification, academic credit transfer, or personal enrichment?  
2. Are you currently a student, faculty member, working professional, or lifelong learner?  
3. Do you have any specific subject areas or industries you’re interested in (e.g., engineering, management, health sciences, languages)?  
4. Are you looking for self-paced learning flexibility, or do you prefer structured courses with fixed timelines?  
5. Would you be interested in earning a certification that requires an in-person proctored exam (with a nominal fee)?  
6. Are you seeking to transfer SWAYAM course credits into your current academic programme under UGC/AICTE frameworks?  
7. Do you prefer learning in any of the 11 Indian languages offered on SWAYAM (e.g., Hindi, Tamil, Bengali)?  
8. Are you aiming to enhance employability through industry-relevant or faculty development courses?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Target beneficiary: student, faculty, lifelong learner, out-of-school student, or working professional  
- [ ] No age or formal eligibility restrictions—open to anyone globally  
- [ ] Interest in free access to courses from top Indian institutions (IITs, IIMs, etc.)  
- [ ] Willingness to pay nominal exam fee (Rs. 750–1000) for certification if desired  
- [ ] Access to internet and ability to register with name and email ID  
- [ ] Interest in credit transfer, skill development, or lifelong learning opportunities  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your goals, SWAYAM could be an excellent fit—it offers free access to thousands of courses from premier institutions, with the option to earn recognized certifications for a small fee. Let me walk you through how it works and how it can benefit you specifically.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many learners struggle to access affordable, high-quality education from top Indian institutions due to cost, geographic barriers, or rigid schedules. Others want to upskill or earn certifications but lack flexible, recognized pathways that fit their work or study commitments.

**Scheme intro:**
The SWAYAM Free Online Education Platform, initiated by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, provides free access to over 20,000 courses from top institutions like IITs, IIMs, and central universities. While course enrollment and learning are completely free, learners seeking certification can opt for an in-person, proctored exam at a nominal fee—Rs. 1000 per course for NPTEL offerings and Rs. 750 per course for IIMB offerings.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Open to anyone in the world—no age, educational background, or eligibility restrictions  
- Ideal for students, faculty, working professionals, and lifelong learners seeking free, flexible learning  
- Supports credit transfer into academic programmes per UGC and AICTE frameworks  
- Offers courses in 11 Indian languages for inclusive access

Let’s start with a free eligibility and course fit check—no commitment, no upfront cost. We only charge a success fee if we help you enroll, complete a course, and achieve your certification or credit transfer goal.
