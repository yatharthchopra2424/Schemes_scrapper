### SWAYAM Free Online Education Platform
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Education, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://swayam.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (self-paced courses); specific enrollment and exam windows apply for scheduled courses (e.g., enrollment ends 31 Aug, 2026 for many courses; exam registration ends 14 Aug, 2026 for July 2026 semester courses)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Not applicable (open to individuals)
- Age of business: Not applicable
- Sector: Not applicable
- Revenue cap: Not applicable
- Employee count: Not applicable
- Other: No specific eligibility or age criterion; anyone in the world can register for a course; faculty may recommend basic knowledge for better comprehension, but no formal prerequisites are mandated

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Free access to courses; certification available for a nominal fee (Rs. 1000 per course for NPTEL offerings, Rs. 750 per course for IIMB offerings)
- Disbursement mode: Direct access to course content; certification upon passing in-person proctored exam
- Duration of benefit: One-time access per course; self-paced learning anytime; course durations vary (4 weeks to 24 weeks)

#### Documents Required
1. Name
2. Email ID

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the SWAYAM portal at https://swayam.gov.in
2. Click on 'Register as Student'
3. Provide essential information: Name and Email ID
4. Optionally sign up using Google or Microsoft login
5. Verify account via email notification from support@swayam.gov.in
6. Browse or search for courses by category, keyword, or university
7. Select a suitable course and click to enroll
8. Access course content immediately after enrollment
9. For certification, register for the proctored exam when announced
10. Pay the exam fee (if applicable) and appear at the designated centre on the specified date
