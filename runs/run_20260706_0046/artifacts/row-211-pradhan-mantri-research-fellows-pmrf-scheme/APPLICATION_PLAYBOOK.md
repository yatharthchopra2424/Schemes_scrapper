# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Candidate is pursuing or has completed Ph.D. at a PMRF granting institute (IISc, IITs, IISERs, NITs, Central Universities offering Science/Technology degrees)
- [ ] For **Direct Entry**: 
  - [ ] Completed or pursuing final year of 4/5-year UG, 5-year integrated M.Tech., 2-year M.Sc., or 5-year UG-PG dual degree from IISc/IITs/NITs/IISERs/IIEST/centrally funded IIITs with CGPA/CPI ≥ 8.0  
    OR  
  - [ ] Same from any other recognized Institute/University with CGPA ≥ 8 and GATE score ≥ 650 or UGC/CSIR JRF rank ≤ 100 or NBHM fellowship  
    OR  
  - [ ] Qualified GATE and pursuing/completed M.Tech./MS by research at a PMRF granting institute with CGPA/CPI ≥ 8.0 and minimum four courses at time of application  
  - [ ] Selected for Ph.D. at a PMRF granting institute  
  - [ ] Not upgraded from M.S./M.Tech. to Ph.D. (ineligible for Direct Entry)  
  - [ ] Not an Integrated PhD student at time of Masters completion (ineligible for Direct Entry)  
  - [ ] Has not deferred PhD admission (ineligible for Direct Entry at time of joining)
- [ ] For **Lateral Entry**: 
  - [ ] Currently pursuing Ph.D. at a PMRF granting institute  
  - [ ] Completed at most 12 months in PhD (if joined with Master’s) or 24 months (if joined with Bachelor’s)  
  - [ ] Completed at least four full-semester courses with aggregate CGPA ≥ 8.5  
  - [ ] Strong recommendation from host institute  
  - [ ] Has not applied for Lateral Entry more than twice  
- [ ] Candidate is applying for Ph.D. in Science and Engineering/Technology broad areas (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Computer Science, Biomedical, Chemical, Materials, Physics, Chemistry, Interdisciplinary)
- [ ] Candidate has not received retrospective payment eligibility bar (thesis submission on or after Nov 1, 2022 disqualifies retrospective payments)

> **Sources**: PMRF Key Facts, Guidelines (https://pmrf.in/guidelines.html), FAQ (https://pmrf.in/faq.html), Admission Guidelines PDFs (14-01-2025-guidelines-for-pmrf.pdf, 16-01-2026-guidelines-for-pmrf-1-0-1.pdf, overall-guidelines.pdf)

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- [ ] PAN Card
- [ ] Aadhaar Card
- [ ] Bank Statement (last 3 months)
- [ ] Udyam Registration (if applicable)
- [ ] GST Certificate (if applicable)
- [ ] Income Tax Returns (last 2 years)
- [ ] Educational Certificates (10th, 12th, UG, PG as applicable)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- [ ] Official grade sheet/transcript (to support CGPA/CPI requirement)
- [ ] GATE scorecard (if applicable under Direct Entry criteria)
- [ ] Academic background details (degree certificates, mark sheets)
- [ ] Transcripts (consolidated academic record)
- [ ] Details of research experience or internship
- [ ] Updated CV
- [ ] Research proposal / Statement of Purpose (SOP)
- [ ] List of publications (journal, conference, etc.)
- [ ] Details of recommenders (names, designations, contact info willing to write strong LORs)

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible scans (minimum 300 DPI)
- [ ] File size < 5 MB per document
- [ ] File names follow format: `CaseID_DocumentType_Name.pdf` (e.g., `CASE001_Transcript_JohnDoe.pdf`)
- [ ] All certificates are self-attested or notarized as required
- [ ] Documents are in English or accompanied by certified translation
- [ ] Research proposal is ≤ 2 pages, includes objectives, methodology, expected outcomes
- [ ] CV is ≤ 2 pages, highlights academic and research achievements
- [ ] Transcripts show CGPA/CPI clearly visible
- [ ] GATE scorecard is valid (within 3 years of application)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- **Not required**. The PMRF scheme does not require a Detailed Project Report (DPR). Instead, applicants must submit a **Research Proposal / Statement of Purpose** (max 2 pages) outlining intended research objectives, methodology, and expected outcomes.  
- **Verify with nodal officer** if any institute-specific variations exist, but national guidelines do not mandate a DPR.

> **Sources**: Application Process (Key Facts), Guidelines (https://pmrf.in/guidelines.html), FAQ (https://pmrf.in/faq.html)

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Wait for nomination**: Application can only be initiated after receiving nomination link from the PMRF granting institute via the PMRF portal.  
2. **Use official portal only**: Apply exclusively at https://www.pmrf.in/ — third-party sites are not valid.  
3. **Select correct Broad Area**: Choose from 10 designated areas (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Computer Science, Biomedical, Chemical, Materials, Physics, Chemistry, Interdisciplinary) based on research alignment.  
4. **Fill all fields meticulously**: Incomplete forms lead to disqualification; double-check CGPA, GATE scores, course counts, and dates.  
5. **Upload documents in prescribed format**: PDF only, <5 MB, named as per checklist (e.g., `ResearchProposal_JohnDoe.pdf`).  
6. **Provide accurate recommender details**: Ensure recommenders are aware they will receive an email to upload LORs directly to the portal.  
7. **Declare eligibility truthfully**: False declarations (e.g., on CGPA, course completion, PhD selection) lead to permanent debarment.  
8. **Save and review before final submit**: Use “Save as Draft” to review; once submitted, edits are not allowed until review cycle.

> **Sources**: Application Process (Key Facts), FAQ (https://pmrf.in/faq.html#Q14), Guidelines (https://pmrf.in/guidelines.html), Portal Login Instructions (past-announcements.html)

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All personal and academic details correctly entered  
- [ ] CGPA/CPI meets threshold (≥8.0 for Direct Entry, ≥8.5 aggregate for Lateral Entry after 4 courses)  
- [ ] GATE scorecard uploaded (if applicable) and score ≥650  
- [ ] Research proposal/SOP is clear, feasible, and aligned with selected Broad Area  
- [ ] CV is updated and highlights research/publications  
- [ ] List of publications is accurate and verifiable  
- [ ] Recommender details are complete and confirm willingness to submit LOR  
- [ ] All uploaded documents are legible, correctly named, and under 5 MB  
- [ ] Declarations regarding eligibility (no prior PMRF award, not upgraded from M.Tech. to Ph.D. for Direct Entry, etc.) are truthfully checked  
- [ ] Application is submitted within the official window (check https://pmrf.in/pmrf-calendar.html)  
- [ ] Screenshot of submission confirmation saved with reference number  

> **Sources**: Internal best practice, Portal Instructions (past-announcements.html), Guidelines (https://pmrf.in/guidelines.html)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify approval letter from PMRF NCC/Nodal Coordinator  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned fellowship amount (Year 1: ₹70,000/month; Year 2: ₹70,000/month; Year 3: ₹75,000/month; Year 4: ₹80,000/month; Year 5: ₹80,000/month)  
- [ ] Verify research grant eligibility: ₹2 lakhs/year (up to ₹10 lakhs over 5 years)  
- [ ] Ensure TAship compliance: ~1 hour/week teaching in non-PMRF institutes (ITI, Polytechnic, Engineering College, Science College, School, NPTEL)  
- [ ] Submit TAship Certificate during annual review (format: LETTERHEAD OF TA-SHIP INSTITUTE, with hours, dates, signatures)  
- [ ] Utilize research grant before thesis submission or PMRF tenure end (whichever earlier); refund any unspent amount  
- [ ] Prepare for annual review: submit latest CV, Research Progress Report, Teaching Report, webpage link, presentation file  
- [ ] Track invoice for success fee as per consultancy agreement  

> **Sources**: Financial Support (Key Facts), Fellowship (https://pmrf.in/fellowship.html), TAship Guidelines (https://pmrf.in/deliverables.html, taship-guidelines.pdf, overall-guidelines.pdf), Research Grant Utilization (Key Facts, guidelines PDFs), Annual Review Process (may25-review-website.pdf, dec-24-slides-published.pdf, review-process-instructions-to-fellows.pdf)
