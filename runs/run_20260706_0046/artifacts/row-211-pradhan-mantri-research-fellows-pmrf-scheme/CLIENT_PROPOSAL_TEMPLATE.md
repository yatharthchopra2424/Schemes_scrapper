## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment against PMRF Direct Entry and Lateral Entry criteria  
2. Documentation review and preparation (transcripts, GATE scorecard, research proposal, CV, publications, etc.)  
3. Guidance on institute nomination process via PMRF granting institute coordinator  
4. Portal registration and application submission support on [https://www.pmrf.in/](https://www.pmrf.in/)  
5. Coordination with recommenders for letter submission  
6. Annual review process support (TAship certificate, progress report, presentation)  
7. Compliance monitoring for TAship duty and research grant utilization  

*Note: A DPR/project report is not required; a research proposal/statement of purpose is mandatory.*

## 4. What We Need From You
1. Documents as per the PMRF checklist: official grade sheet/transcript, GATE scorecard (if applicable), academic background details, transcripts, research experience/internship details, CV, research proposal/statement of purpose, list of publications, and recommender details  
2. Designated point of contact for coordination with your institute and our team  
3. 2-day response SLA for queries and document submission
