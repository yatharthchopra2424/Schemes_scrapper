## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the scheme’s financial benefit (₹10 lakhs research grant over five years plus monthly fellowship).
2. Transparent fee structure with no hidden charges; all fees disclosed upfront.
3. Success fee contingent on successful fellowship award and grant utilization compliance.
4. Scheme-specific note: The PMRF scheme has a moderate risk profile due to its biennial application cycles (May and December) and a typical processing time of 6–8 months from application to final approval, requiring careful timeline management in revenue recognition.
