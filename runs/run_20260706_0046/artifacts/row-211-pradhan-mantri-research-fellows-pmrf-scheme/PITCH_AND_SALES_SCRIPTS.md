# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping PhD scholars in Science and Engineering secure government-funded fellowships like the Pradhan Mantri Research Fellows (PMRF) Scheme. I’ve been reviewing your academic background and research interests, and I believe you may be an excellent fit for this prestigious MoE-backed program that supports top doctoral talent with monthly fellowships up to ₹80,000 and annual research grants of ₹2 lakhs. Before we explore whether this is right for you, may I ask a few quick questions to better understand your current situation?

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you currently enrolled in a PhD programme in Science or Engineering at a PMRF granting institute (such as an IIT, IISc, IISER, NIT, or centrally funded IIIT)?
2. Have you completed at least four full-semester courses in your PhD programme with a CGPA of 8.5 or higher?
3. Are you within 12 months of joining your PhD if you entered with a Master’s degree, or within 24 months if you joined with a Bachelor’s degree?
4. Do you have a strong research proposal and at least one publication or significant research experience to support your application?
5. Are you willing to fulfill the mandatory TAship duty of approximately 1 hour per week (50 hours/year) at an approved external institution?
6. Have you secured admission to your PhD programme through a PMRF granting institute, and are you prepared to seek nomination from your institute coordinator?
7. Are you aware that the PMRF Scheme offers a monthly fellowship (₹70,000–₹80,000) and an annual research grant of ₹2 lakhs (up to ₹10 lakhs over five years)?
8. Do you have your academic transcripts, GATE scorecard (if applicable), CV, research proposal, and details of recommenders ready for submission?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Currently pursuing PhD in Science/Engineering at a PMRF granting institute  
- [ ] Completed at least four full-semester courses with CGPA ≥ 8.5  
- [ ] Within 12 months (if joined with Master’s) or 24 months (if joined with Bachelor’s) of PhD admission  
- [ ] Has strong research proposal and publications/research experience  
- [ ] Willing to complete mandatory TAship duty (~1 hour/week)  
- [ ] Has secured PhD admission and can obtain nomination from institute coordinator  
- [ ] Understands financial benefits: ₹70,000–₹80,000/month fellowship + ₹2 lakhs/year research grant  
- [ ] Has or can prepare: transcripts, GATE/UG/PG details, CV, research proposal, SOP, publication list, recommender details  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you meet several key eligibility criteria for the Pradhan Mantri Research Fellows (PMRF) Scheme — particularly your academic standing, stage of PhD, and research readiness. The PMRF Scheme offers an attractive monthly fellowship (up to ₹80,000 in Year 5) and a research grant of ₹2 lakhs per year (up to ₹10 lakhs over five years) to support scholars like you in conducting high-impact doctoral research. Would you like me to walk you through how the scheme works and how we can help you strengthen your application?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many talented PhD scholars in Science and Engineering struggle to secure stable funding for their research, often relying on limited institutional support or personal savings. This financial constraint can limit access to resources, delay experiments, and hinder progress on innovative, high-risk projects — especially in emerging fields where early-stage funding is scarce.

**Scheme intro:**
The Pradhan Mantri Research Fellows (PMRF) Scheme, administered by the Ministry of Education (MoE), is a prestigious grant that provides PhD scholars in Science and Engineering with a monthly fellowship (₹70,000 in Year 1, ₹70,000 in Year 2, ₹75,000 in Year 3, ₹80,000 in Year 4, and ₹80,000 in Year 5) and an annual research grant of ₹2 lakhs per year (totaling up to ₹10 lakhs over five years). The scheme is designed to support the very best talents in doctoral research and build outstanding research ecosystems across India.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are currently pursuing a PhD in Science or Engineering at a PMRF granting institute (e.g., IIT, IISc, IISER, NIT)  
- You have completed at least four full-semester courses with a CGPA of 8.5 or higher  
- You are within the eligible time window for Lateral Entry (12–24 months from PhD admission, depending on prior degree)  
- You have or can prepare a strong research proposal, CV, transcripts, and details of recommenders
