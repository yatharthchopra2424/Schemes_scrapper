### Pradhan Mantri Research Fellows (PMRF) Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Education (MoE)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.pmrf.in/
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Admissions occur twice a year (May and December cycles); specific cycles have defined timelines (e.g., for May 2025 review: submission 08-May-2025 to 14-May-2025 with extension to 20-May-2025 for 1 scholar; for December 2024 review: submission 01-Nov-2024 to 07-Nov-2024)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Individual PhD scholars (not applicable to businesses)
- Age of business: Not applicable
- Sector: Science and Engineering/Technology
- Revenue cap: Not applicable
- Employee count: Not applicable
- Other: 
  - Must be selected for Ph.D. at a PMRF granting institute
  - Direct Entry: Completed/pursuing final year of 4/5-year UG or 5-year integrated M.Tech./2-year M.Sc./5-year UG-PG dual degree from IISc/IITs/NITs/IISERs/IIEST/centrally funded IIITs with CGPA/CPI ≥ 8.0; OR same from any other recognized Institute/University with CGPA ≥ 8 and GATE ≥ 650 or UGC/CSIR JRF rank ≤ 100 or NBHM fellowship; OR qualified GATE and pursuing/completed M.Tech./MS by research at a PMRF granting institute with CGPA/CPI ≥ 8.0 and minimum four courses at time of application
  - Lateral Entry: Currently pursuing Ph.D. at a PMRF granting institute, completed at most 12 months (if joined with Master’s) or 24 months (if joined with Bachelor’s) in PhD programme, completed at least four full-semester courses with aggregate CGPA ≥ 8.5, and strong recommendation from host institute
  - Target beneficiaries: PhD scholars in Science and Engineering
  - Scholars who upgrade from M.S./M.Tech. to Ph.D. are not eligible for Direct Entry
  - Integrated PhD students are not eligible for Direct Entry at the time of completion of Masters requirement
  - A candidate who defers admission in a PhD programme cannot be considered for Direct Entry at the time of joining
  - Can apply for Lateral Entry at most twice

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - Monthly fellowship: ₹70,000 (Year 1), ₹70,000 (Year 2), ₹75,000 (Year 3), ₹80,000 (Year 4), ₹80,000 (Year 5)
  - Annual research grant: ₹2 lakhs per year (up to ₹10 lakhs over five years)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (monthly fellowship and research grant paid until date of Final Viva Voce or end of PMRF tenure, whichever is earlier)
- Duration of benefit: 5 years (or until thesis submission or PMRF tenure end, whichever is earlier)

#### Documents Required
1. Official grade sheet/transcript
2. GATE scorecard (if applicable)
3. Academic background details
4. Transcripts
5. Details of research experience or internship
6. CV
7. Research proposal / statement of purpose
8. List of publications
9. Details of recommenders willing to write strong letters of recommendation

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Get nominated by the PMRF granting institute after meeting eligibility criteria
2. Receive a link to the PMRF portal to submit application
3. Submit academic background, transcripts, research experience/internship, CV, research proposal/statement of purpose, list of publications, and details of recommenders
4. Await evaluation by a National Panel for each broad area
5. Final selection by the National Coordination Committee (NCC) based on ranks and seat allocation
