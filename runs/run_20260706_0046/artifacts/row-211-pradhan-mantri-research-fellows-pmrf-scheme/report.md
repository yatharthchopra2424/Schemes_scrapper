# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The Pradhan Mantri Research Fellows (PMRF) Scheme is a flagship initiative by the Ministry of Education (MoE), Government of India, designed to attract and nurture top-tier talent in doctoral research in Science and Engineering. Announced in the Budget 2018-19, the scheme offers attractive fellowships and research grants to PhD scholars across India, with a focus on building outstanding research ecosystems and solving challenging, innovative problems. The scheme is implemented through the National Coordinating Institute (NCI), currently IIT Madras (2022–2028), and overseen by the National Coordination Committee (NCC). Applications are processed twice yearly (May and December cycles) via the portal [https://www.pmrf.in/](https://www.pmrf.in/), with specific windows announced for each cycle.

### Objectives
- Support the very best talents in doctoral research in India with attractive fellowships  
- Build and nurture outstanding research ecosystems in the country  
- Focus on extremely challenging and innovative research problems  
- Attract the best talent into research to realize the vision of development through innovation  
- Improve the quality of research in various higher educational institutions  

### Eligibility Matrix
Candidates can apply through **Direct Entry** or **Lateral Entry** channels. All applicants must be selected for a Ph.D. programme at a PMRF granting institute.

| Entry Type | Eligibility Criteria | Additional Requirements |
|------------|----------------------|--------------------------|
| **Direct Entry** | (a) Completed/pursuing final year of 4/5-year UG or 5-year integrated M.Tech./2-year M.Sc./5-year UG-PG dual degree in Science & Technology from IISc/IITs/NITs/IISERs/IIEST and centrally funded IIITs with CGPA/CPI ≥ 8.0<br>OR (b) Same from any other recognized Institute/University with CGPA ≥ 8 and GATE score ≥ 650 **or** UGC/CSIR JRF rank ≤ 100 **or** NBHM fellowship<br>OR (c) Qualified GATE and pursuing/completed M.Tech./MS by research at a PMRF granting institute with CGPA/CPI ≥ 8.0 and minimum four courses at time of application | • Must be selected for Ph.D. at a PMRF granting institute<br>• Official grade sheet/transcript and GATE scorecard (if applicable) required<br>• **Not eligible**: Scholars who upgraded from M.S./M.Tech. to Ph.D., Integrated PhD students at Masters completion, candidates who deferred PhD admission |
| **Lateral Entry** | • Currently pursuing Ph.D. at a PMRF granting institute<br>• Completed at most 12 months (if joined with Master’s) or 24 months (if joined with Bachelor’s) in PhD programme<br>• Completed at least four full-semester courses with aggregate CGPA ≥ 8.5<br>• Strong recommendation from host institute | • Can apply at most twice<br>• In Integrated M.Tech/MSc and PhD programmes, eligible within 12 months of satisfying Master’s degree requirement<br>• General courses (Seminar, Communication Skill, Research Methodology) not counted as full-subject courses |

> **Key Caveats**:  
> - Research grant must be refunded if unspent  
> - Guideline ceases to apply after 31st Dec 2028  
> - No retrospective payments for thesis submissions on or after November 1, 2022  
> - TAship duty is mandatory; failure may lead to fellowship reduction/discontinuation  
> - Fellowship and research grant paid until Final Viva Voce or end of PMRF tenure, whichever is earlier  

### Benefits & Financial Support
The PMRF provides a monthly fellowship and an annual research grant, disbursed until thesis submission (if before Nov 1, 2022) or PMRF tenure end, whichever is earlier.

#### Research Grant
- ₹2 lakhs per year, up to ₹10 lakhs over five years

### Application Process
The process involves nomination by the institute, portal submission, national panel evaluation, and final selection by NCC.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Candidate meets eligibility criteria] --> B[Get nominated by PMRF granting institute]
    B --> C[Receive link to PMRF portal from institute]
    C --> D[Submit application via portal: academic background, transcripts, research experience/internship, CV, research proposal/SOP, list of publications, recommender details]
    D --> E[Await evaluation by National Panel for each broad area]
    E --> F[Final selection by National Coordination Committee (NCC) based on ranks and seat allocation]
    F --> G[Selected candidates receive fellowship and research grant]
```

#### Required Documents
1. Official grade sheet/transcript  
2. GATE scorecard (if applicable)  
3. Academic background details  
4. Transcripts  
5. Details of research experience or internship  
6. CV  
7. Research proposal / statement of purpose  
8. List of publications  
9. Details of recommenders willing to write strong letters of recommendation

## Consultant's Field Guide to Generated Files

### 1. SCHEME_MASTER_DATABASE.md
**Real-time Usage**: Keep this open in a background tab during all client calls. When a client asks "What is the turnover limit?" or "Who administers this?", CTRL+F in this document to give an immediate, authoritative answer without checking the portal.  
*Example*: If a client asks about the research grant limit, search "₹10 lakhs" to confirm the total grant over five years. For eligibility queries, search "CGPA ≥ 8.0" or "GATE score ≥ 650" to provide instant clarification.

### 2. PITCH_AND_SALES_SCRIPTS.md
**Real-time Usage**: Open this file 5 minutes before your first Discovery Call with a lead. Read the "Problem Framing" out loud to hook them, then use the Qualification Checklist to interrogate their eligibility live on the phone. Keep the Objection Handlers table visible so you can immediately counter when they say "We're too small for this."  
*Example*: Use the script to frame the problem: "Many talented PhD scholars in India struggle to access funding for innovative research despite having excellent academic credentials." Then run through the checklist: "Are you currently enrolled in a PhD program at an IIT, IISER, IISc, or other PMRF granting institute?" If they say they're in their first year, use the lateral entry objection handler: "Even if you're early in your PhD, you may qualify through lateral entry if you've completed four courses with CGPA ≥ 8.5."

### 3. APPLICATION_PLAYBOOK.md
**Real-time Usage**: Print this out or pin it to your desktop once the client signs the retainer. Check off each box in "Stage 1" before moving to "Stage 2". Use the "Client Communication Template" to copy-paste directly into your email when chasing them for pending documents.  
*Example*: After signing the retainer, verify Stage 1 tasks: "Confirm client’s PhD admission status at a PMRF granting institute" and "Collect official transcripts and GATE scorecard."
