## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with client outcomes  
2. Transparent fee structures with no hidden costs  
3. Risk-sharing model where success fees are contingent on measurable results  
4. Customizable tiers to match client scale and complexity  

**Scheme-specific note:** The SWAYAM PRABHA scheme provides free 24x7 educational DTH channels with no direct financial disbursement to beneficiaries. As such, there is no financial grant, subsidy, or monetary benefit to quantify for success fee calculation. The scheme’s value lies in educational access, not fiscal transfer. Consulting engagements would focus on awareness, installation support, or utilization optimization rather than benefit acquisition. Processing time is not applicable as the service is continuously available with no application required. Success fees would be inappropriate here; alternative models (e.g., fixed-fee for implementation support) are recommended.
