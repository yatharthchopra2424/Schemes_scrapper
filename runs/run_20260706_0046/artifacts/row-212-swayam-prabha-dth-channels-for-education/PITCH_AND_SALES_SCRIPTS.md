## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

This scheme delivers 24x7 curriculum-based content from premier institutions directly to your TV or mobile device—completely free of cost. Before we explore whether this fits your needs, I’d like to understand your current learning setup and goals.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of educational content are you or your learners currently accessing (e.g., school curriculum, competitive exam prep, higher education subjects)?
- Are you primarily using online platforms, or do you face challenges with internet connectivity in your area?
- Do you or your learners have access to a television set at home or in a community center?
- Are you located in a rural, tribal, or remote area where consistent internet access is unreliable?
- Are you preparing for competitive exams (e.g., engineering, medical, banking, SSC) or supporting students who are?
- What languages do you prefer for educational content—Hindi, English, or regional languages?
- Are you currently using any DTH service like DD Free Dish or Dish TV, or would you need guidance on setting it up?
- Are you seeking free, certificate-free learning resources that can be accessed anytime, without registration or fees?
