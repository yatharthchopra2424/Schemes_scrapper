### SWAYAM PRABHA DTH Channels for Education
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Education, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://swayamprabha.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Not applicable (service is for individuals, not businesses)
- Age of business: Not applicable
- Sector: Education
- Revenue cap: Not applicable
- Employee count: Not applicable
- Other: There are no eligibility criteria for viewing SWAYAM PRABHA channels; the service is free and open to all students, learners, and the general public across India.

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Free access to 24x7 educational DTH channels; no fees for viewing
- Disbursement mode: Direct broadcast via satellite (DD Free Dish and Dish TV) and mobile (JIO TV application)
- Duration of benefit: Continuous, available 24x7

#### Documents Required
1. No documents required to access SWAYAM PRABHA channels
2. For DD Free Dish installation: Set-top box (MPEG4 compatible after June 7, 2023) and satellite dish
3. For mobile access: JIO TV application installed on smartphone

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. No application is required to access SWAYAM PRABHA channels
2. To view via DD Free Dish: Install DD Free Dish as per directions at https://prasarbharati.gov.in/free-dish/
3. Configure set-top box with the following parameters for SWAYAM PRABHA channels:
   - Satellite/Orbital Position: GSAT-15 / 93.5° East
   - Modulation: 8 PSK / DVB-S2
   - Rx Frequency: 11630 MHz
   - Polarization: Horizontal
   - Symbol Rate: 29500 KSPS
   - FEC: 3/4
4. To view via mobile: Install JIO TV application and search for 'Swayam Prabha'
5. To access archival videos and programme schedules: Visit https://swayamprabha.gov.in and use the Channel & Allocation section
