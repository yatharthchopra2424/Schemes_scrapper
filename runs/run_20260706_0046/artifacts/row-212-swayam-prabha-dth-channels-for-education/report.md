# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
SWAYAM PRABHA is a group of **40 DTH channels** devoted to telecasting high-quality educational programmes on a **24X7 basis** using the **GSAT-15 satellite**. The scheme is implemented by the **Ministry of Education, Government of India**, with the **INFLIBNET Centre** maintaining the web portal. It is a **free educational broadcasting service** funded by the Government of India and does **not provide direct financial support** to beneficiaries. The service is available **continuously on a 24x7 basis** with no application deadlines.

### Objectives
- To reach the unreached segment of India with quality education  
- To provide free 24x7 educational DTH channels  
- To telecast high-quality educational programmes using GSAT-15 satellite  
- To offer content from IITs, UGC, CEC, IGNOU, and other reputed institutions  
- To allow students to choose viewing time as per convenience through repeated content  
- To support education in rural, tribal, and remote areas via DTH presence under PM e-Vidya  
- To provide curriculum-based content for school education and competitive exam preparation  
- To facilitate multi-mode access to digital/online teaching-learning contents

### Application Process
**No application is required** to access SWAYAM PRABHA channels. Users access the service via:

1. **DD Free Dish Installation**  
   - Install DD Free Dish per directions at [https://prasarbharati.gov.in/free-dish/](https://prasarbharati.gov.in/free-dish/)  
   - One-time STB cost: **< Rs. 2,000** (available with TV dealers)  
2. **Set-Top Box Configuration**  
   - Set parameters for SWAYAM PRABHA channels:  
     - Satellite/Orbital Position: **GSAT-15 / 93.5° East**  
     - Modulation: **8 PSK / DVB-S2**  
     - Receive Frequency: **11630 MHz**  
     - Polarization: **Horizontal**  
     - Symbol Rate: **29500 KSPS**  
     - FEC: **3/4**  
   - For SATHEE channels: Frequency shifts to **11670 MHz** (other parameters identical)  
3. **Alternative Access**  
   - **JIO TV Application**: Search for 'Swayam Prabha' under 'All Services' tab  
   - **UMANG Mobile App**: Install UMANG, select 'Swayam Prabha' under 'All Services'  

> **Critical Note**: No certification, credit transfer, or formal enrollment is provided for viewing channels. The service is purely educational broadcasting.

### Key Caveats & Limitations
- **No Certification**: Viewing does **not** yield certificates, diplomas, or credit transfer (unlike SWAYAM online courses)  
- **Platform Restriction**: Available **only** via DD Free Dish and Dish TV; **excluded** from TataSky, Airtel, Videocon  
- **Hardware Dependency**: Requires MPEG4-compatible STB post-June 7, 2023; separate STB per TV set  
- **Content Limitation**: No distance learning courses, interactive doubt-solving, or personalized learning paths  
- **Geographic Signal**: Dependent on GSAT-15 satellite coverage; potential obstructions in extreme terrain  
- **Language Coverage**: While multilingual, not all regional languages are equally represented across all 40 channels
