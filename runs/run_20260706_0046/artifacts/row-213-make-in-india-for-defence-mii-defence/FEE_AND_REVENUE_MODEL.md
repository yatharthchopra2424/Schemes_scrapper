## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit delivered under the Make in India for Defence (MII Defence) scheme, ensuring client interests are prioritized.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s low confidence rating and lack of verifiable application portal or detailed benefit structure in public sources, retainer fees reflect higher upfront effort for eligibility assessment and documentation.
3. **Transparency**: All fees are disclosed upfront; no hidden charges. Clients receive a clear breakdown of retainer vs. success fee components.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The MII Defence scheme exhibits uncertain processing times and limited public data on approval rates or average benefit amounts, increasing execution risk. Fees for Scale and Enterprise tiers are set higher to compensate for extended engagement cycles and regulatory complexity.
