## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping defence and aerospace manufacturers access government incentives designed to strengthen India’s self-reliance in defence production. The Make in India for Defence (MII Defence) initiative is specifically created to support companies like yours that are contributing to indigenous defence manufacturing — whether you're producing components, systems, or complete platforms under the 'Make in India' framework. I’d like to understand your current operations to see if this scheme could support your growth and compliance goals.

### Discovery Questions
- When was your company incorporated, and are you primarily engaged in the manufacturing of defence-related goods or services?  
- What percentage of your current turnover comes from defence production or defence-linked supply chains?  
- Are you currently registered with the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) or recognised under any defence-specific industrial policy?  
- Do you have ongoing or planned contracts with the Ministry of Defence, Defence Public Sector Undertakings (DPSUs), or armed forces?  
- Are you investing in new manufacturing facilities, technology upgradation, or R&D for defence products?  
- Are you exporting defence goods, or planning to enter international defence markets?  
- Have you previously applied for any defence production-linked incentives, subsidies, or tax benefits from the government?  
- What are your annual turnover and employee strength — particularly in your defence manufacturing division?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Company is engaged in manufacturing of defence equipment, components, or related services  
- [ ] Entity is registered in India (incorporation date verifiable)  
- [ ] Involved in defence production — either as a supplier to MoD/DPSUs or with defence export activities  
- [ ] Possesses valid industrial licence (if required) under Industries (Development and Regulation) Act, 1951  
- [ ] Complies with offset guidelines or defence procurement procedures (where applicable)  
- [ ] Not blacklisted by any government agency or defence organisation  
- [ ] Financials and manufacturing capabilities can be substantiated with audited records  
- [ ] Aligns with the strategic defence manufacturing goals outlined in the Defence Acquisition Procedure (DAP) 2020

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your defence manufacturing activities align closely with the goals of the **Make in India for Defence (MII Defence)** scheme — which offers targeted support to boost indigenous defence production. Let me walk you through how this scheme works and what it could mean for your business.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Defence manufacturers in India face high capital costs, long development cycles, and intense global competition. Despite policy push for self-reliance (Aatmanirbhar Bharat), many domestic producers struggle to access structured incentives that offset R&D, manufacturing, and compliance expenses — limiting their ability to scale and compete with established global defence players.

**Scheme intro:**
The **Make in India for Defence (MII Defence)** initiative, administered by the **Ministry of Defence**, is a policy framework designed to promote indigenous design, development, and manufacturing of defence equipment and components. While it does not provide direct cash subsidies, it offers preferential treatment in government procurement, including reservation of certain items for local purchase, price preference for domestically manufactured goods, and streamlined approvals under the 'Make in India' framework — effectively enhancing market access and competitiveness for eligible defence manufacturers.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your company is engaged in the **manufacturing of defence equipment or components**, meeting the core beneficiary criteria  
- You are **registered and operating in India**, with verifiable incorporation and industrial compliance  
- You have **existing or aspirations for contracts with the Ministry of Defence, DPSUs, or armed forces**, aligning with procurement preference benefits  
- Your operations support **indigenous defence production**, a key strategic objective under the Defence Acquisition Procedure (DAP) 2020
