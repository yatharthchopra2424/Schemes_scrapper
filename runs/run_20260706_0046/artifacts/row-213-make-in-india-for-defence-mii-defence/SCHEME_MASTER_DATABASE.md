### Make in India for Defence (MII Defence)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Defence
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://mod.gov.in/make-in-india
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Defence manufacturing entities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Defence and aerospace manufacturing
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be engaged in defence production under the Make in India initiative; preference given to Indian-owned and controlled entities

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Preferential treatment in government procurement; financial incentives under associated defence production schemes (specific amounts not detailed in evidence)
- Disbursement mode: Procurement preference / indirect subsidy via contract awards
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing as long as eligibility is maintained

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN and GST registration details
3. Audited financial statements (last 3 years)
4. Industrial licence (if applicable under IDR Act)
5. Details of defence products manufactured or intended for manufacture
6. Export licence (if applicable)
7. Ownership and control declaration (to prove Indian ownership)
8. Self-certification of compliance with Make in India conditions
9. Project report detailing investment, capacity, and employment generation
10. Any other document as specified by the Defence Acquisition Procedure (DAP) 2020

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Verify eligibility under the Make in India for Defence initiative
2. Prepare required documentation as per Defence Acquisition Procedure (DAP) 2020
3. Submit application through the Defence Procurement Portal or designated nodal agency
4. Application reviewed by the Ministry of Defence or designated authority
5. Clarifications or additional information may be requested during review
6. Upon approval, entity is registered as an eligible Make in India defence supplier
7. Registered entities become eligible for preference in government defence procurement tenders
