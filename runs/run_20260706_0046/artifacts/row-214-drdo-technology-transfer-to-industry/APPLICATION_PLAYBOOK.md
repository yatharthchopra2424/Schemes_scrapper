## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is an Indian industry entity (private limited company, public limited company, partnership firm, or proprietorship) registered under relevant Indian laws.
- [ ] The applicant has a valid Udyam Registration (MSME) or is a recognized industrial unit.
- [ ] The applicant has demonstrated capability to absorb, adapt, and commercialize DRDO-developed technologies.
- [ ] The applicant is engaged in manufacturing, R&D, or related industrial activities aligned with defence and aerospace sectors.
- [ ] The applicant has not been blacklisted by any government agency or PSU.
- [ ] The technology sought for transfer must be listed under DRDO’s available technologies for industry transfer.
- [ ] The applicant agrees to comply with DRDO’s terms of technology transfer, including royalty payments, confidentiality, and milestones.
- [ ] The applicant has the financial and technical capacity to undertake technology absorption and scaling (to be assessed during evaluation).

> **Note**: Eligibility criteria are derived from DRDO’s general technology transfer framework. Specific thresholds (turnover, employee count, years in operation) are not explicitly defined in the evidence; applicants must confirm with the nodal officer.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Partnership Deed / Proprietorship Declaration  
- PAN Card of the business entity  
- GST Registration Certificate  
- Udyam Registration Certificate (MSME)  
- Audited Financial Statements (ITR, Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss) for last 2–3 years  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- Authorization Letter / Board Resolution for application submission  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed project proposal outlining the intended technology absorption plan  
- Technical capability statement (manufacturing facilities, R&D setup, skilled manpower)  
- List of specific DRDO technology(ies) of interest (with reference numbers from DRDO’s technology catalogue)  
- Undertaking on confidentiality and non-disclosure (NDA) as per DRDO format  
- Declaration of no prior litigation or dispute with DRDO or defence PSUs  
- Proof of prior experience in technology absorption or defence sector projects (if any)  
- Royalties payment capacity statement or bank guarantee (as may be required)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)  
- Document filenames must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_CaseID.pdf` (e.g., `DRDO_TT_Industry_Udyam_Case001.pdf`)  
- Certified true copies required for incorporation documents, PAN, GST, and Udyam  
- Financial statements must be audited and signed by a Chartered Accountant  
- Undertakings and declarations must be on company letterhead, signed, and stamped  
- All documents must be in English or accompanied by a certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is **required** for this scheme. The DPR must include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Technology Description (source: DRDO, TRL level, IP status)  
- Objectives of Technology Transfer  
- Work Plan & Timeline (phased absorption, prototyping, scaling)  
- Infrastructure & Resource Requirements (manpower, machinery, facilities)  
- Risk Assessment & Mitigation Plan  
- Financial Plan (investment, royalty projections, break-even analysis)  
- Commercialization Strategy (market, pricing, distribution)  
- Compliance with DRDO Terms (royalty, confidentiality, reporting)  
- Annexures: Annexures (technology details, drawings, specifications, NDA draft)  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal at **https://drdo.gov.in/technology-transfer** (verify accessibility; if down, contact nodal officer immediately).  
2. Select “Apply for Technology Transfer” or equivalent option.  
3. Fill in applicant details exactly as per Certificate of Incorporation and PAN.  
4. Enter Udyam Registration Number and validate it against the Udyam portal.  
5. Clearly specify the DRDO technology(ies) of interest using official titles and reference numbers from DRDO’s catalogue.  
6. In the “Proposed Use” field, align the application with defence, aerospace, or dual-use industrial applications.  
7. Attach all documents in the prescribed order and format; ensure declarations are signed and dated.  
8. Review the form thoroughly before submission — no edits are allowed after final submit.  
9. Do not leave mandatory fields blank; if a field is not applicable, write “Not Applicable” or “NA” with justification.  
10. Retain a copy of the submitted application and acknowledgment for internal records.  

### Internal Review Gate

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the official approval letter from DRDO specifying sanctioned technology, terms, and royalty rate  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned amount (if any upfront support) and technology transfer details  
- [ ] Sign the formal Technology Transfer Agreement (TTA) and Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) as per DRDO format  
- [ ] Initiate technology absorption activities as per the approved work plan  
- [ ] Submit periodic progress reports (quarterly) to DRDO as mandated in the TTA  
- [ ] Prepare and submit Utilization Certificate (UC) for any financial support received, certified by a Chartered Accountant  
- [ ] Ensure timely payment of royalties or lump-sum fees as per the agreement schedule  
- [ ] Maintain records of all invoices, payments, and correspondence related to the technology transfer  
- [ ] Confirm internal success fee invoice is raised upon receipt of approval letter or first milestone achievement (as per consultancy agreement)  
- [ ] Archive all scheme-related documents for audit and compliance purposes (minimum 5 years)
