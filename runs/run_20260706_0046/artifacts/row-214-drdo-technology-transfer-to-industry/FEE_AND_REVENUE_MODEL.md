## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-based alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial and strategic value of technology transfer outcomes under the DRDO scheme, which typically involves high-value intellectual property and long-term commercialization potential for large defense contractors and industrial groups.
2. **Risk-adjusted pricing**: Success fees are set at the lower end of the Enterprise tier range (2–4%) due to the scheme’s moderate risk profile — while DRDO has a proven technology base, transfer success depends on industry absorption capacity, regulatory clearances, and lengthy validation cycles, as indicated by limited public case studies and processing opacity.
3. **Retainer for engagement depth**: Given the complex technical and legal due diligence required in DRDO technology transfers (including IP evaluation, confidentiality agreements, and joint development terms), retainers ensure compensation for early-stage advisory work irrespective of transfer timelines.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: The DRDO Technology Transfer to Industry scheme involves long gestation periods (often 12–24 months from application to license execution), based on historical defense procurement cycles and technology maturation timelines. This justifies higher retainer commitment and deferred success fee realization, with revenue recognition typically lagging approval by 6–12 months.
