## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping defence and aerospace technology companies access government support for transferring DRDO-developed technologies into commercial applications. The DRDO Technology Transfer to Industry scheme is designed specifically for businesses looking to leverage defence R&D for civilian market opportunities. I’d like to understand if this could be relevant for your current technology commercialisation goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your company’s primary focus in defence or aerospace technology development?
2. Have you previously engaged with any DRDO laboratories or defence research establishments?
3. Are you currently developing or seeking to commercialise any technologies that originated from DRDO research?
4. What is your company’s incorporation date, and how long have you been operating in the defence-tech space?
5. Are you looking for support in licensing, prototyping, or scaling a DRDO-derived technology?
6. Do you have a clear end-use application or civilian market for the technology you wish to transfer?
7. Are you a private limited company, LLP, or registered partnership firm operating in India?
8. Have you identified a specific DRDO laboratory or technology domain you wish to collaborate with?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Company is incorporated in India (private limited, LLP, or partnership)
- [ ] Engaged in defence, aerospace, or dual-use technology development
- [ ] Seeking to commercialise technology originating from DRDO laboratories
- [ ] Has identified a specific DRDO lab or technology for transfer
- [ ] Intends to apply the technology for civilian or industrial use
- [ ] Not a government entity or public sector undertaking (unless specified under special provisions)
- [ ] Capable of bearing complementary costs (prototyping, testing, scaling)
- [ ] Willing to enter into a formal technology transfer agreement with DRDO

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like you’re actively looking to bring DRDO-developed technology into the commercial domain — which is exactly what the **DRDO Technology Transfer to Industry** scheme supports. This scheme facilitates access to defence technologies for industrial application, including licensing support and technical collaboration. Let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many Indian tech companies struggle to access cutting-edge defence R&D due to lack of clear pathways, limited awareness of DRDO’s available technologies, and complex negotiation processes for technology transfer. This delays innovation and increases R&D costs for civilian applications.

**Scheme intro:**
The **DRDO Technology Transfer to Industry** scheme, administered by the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO), enables Indian industries to access and commercialise technologies developed in DRDO laboratories. The scheme supports technology transfer through licensing, joint development, and technical know-how sharing, with DRDO providing access to patented technologies, prototypes, and scientific expertise. While the scheme does not offer direct financial grants, it reduces R&D risk and cost by providing access to matured defence technologies at negotiated terms.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your company is incorporated in India and operates in the defence/aerospace technology sector  
- You have identified a specific DRDO laboratory or technology domain of interest  
- You intend to apply the transferred technology for civilian or industrial use  
- You are seeking a structured pathway to engage with DRDO for technology acquisition
