## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, documentation preparation, and application submission.
2. Success fee is contingent on successful linkage to financial incentives under allied schemes like DPEPP.
3. Fees are scaled to client size and scheme value, reflecting effort and risk.
4. Scheme-specific note: This policy facilitates access to defence procurement and linked financial incentives (e.g., DPEPP), but does not guarantee direct payouts; success depends on qualifying under allied schemes, introducing moderate risk due to dependency on external scheme approvals and variable processing times tied to defence procurement cycles.
