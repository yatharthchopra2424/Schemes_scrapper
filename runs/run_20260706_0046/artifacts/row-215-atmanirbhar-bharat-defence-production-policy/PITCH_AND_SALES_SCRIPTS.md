# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping defence manufacturers, startups, and MSMEs access government procurement opportunities and financial incentives through schemes like the Atmanirbhar Bharat Defence Production Policy. I’d like to understand your current work in defence manufacturing and see if this policy can support your growth.

### Discovery Questions
1. What defence equipment, systems, or services does your company currently design, develop, or supply?
2. When was your company incorporated or registered?
3. Are you registered as an MSME with UDYAM, or do you plan to apply?
4. Have you previously supplied to defence PSUs, ordnance factories, or participated in government tenders?
5. Are you seeking to indigenise technology, absorb foreign know-how, or contribute to Make in India in defence?
6. Do you have product specifications, technical brochures, or past performance certificates ready?
7. Are you interested in accessing defence testing facilities, joint ventures, or export facilitation?
8. Are you currently registered as a vendor on GeM or defence-specific procurement portals?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity is an Indian company, startup, MSME, or private sector organisation
- [ ] Engaged in defence manufacturing, design, development, or supply of defence equipment, systems, or services
- [ ] Contributes to indigenisation, technology absorption, or Make in India initiatives
- [ ] Has Certificate of Incorporation/Registration
- [ ] Has PAN and GST Registration
- [ ] Has UDYAM Registration (if MSME)
- [ ] Has product specifications and technical brochures
- [ ] Has manufacturing/industrial licence (if applicable)
- [ ] Has past performance certificates or supply orders
- [ ] Has bank details and cancelled cheque
- [ ] Has authorised signatory letter
- [ ] Has declaration of compliance with defence standards

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you align well with the Atmanirbhar Bharat Defence Production Policy, which enables access to defence procurement opportunities and financial incentives through linked schemes like DPEPP. Shall I outline how this policy can specifically benefit your business?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Defence manufacturers, especially startups and MSMEs, face challenges in accessing defence contracts due to complex vendor registration, limited access to testing facilities, and difficulty in partnering with defence PSUs or ordnance factories, hindering indigenisation and growth under Make in India.

**Scheme intro:**
The Atmanirbhar Bharat Defence Production Policy, administered by the Ministry of Defence, provides preferential treatment in defence procurement, simplified vendor registration, access to defence testing and evaluation facilities, support for technology transfer and joint ventures, facilitation of exports, and participation in defence exhibitions. Financial incentives are available through linked schemes such as the Defence Production and Export Promotion Policy (DPEPP).

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in defence manufacturing, design, development, or supply of defence equipment, systems, or services
- You contribute to indigenisation, technology absorption, and Make in India initiatives
- You possess or can obtain required documents such as Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, GST, UDYAM (if applicable), product specifications, and past performance certificates

"We've helped [N] similar businesses get approved for this. [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]"

**Next step:**
We offer a free eligibility check with no commitment. If eligible, we support you end-to-end—from vendor registration on GeM and defence portals to tender responses and accessing benefits under DPEPP—on a success-fee-only model.
