### Atmanirbhar Bharat Defence Production Policy
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Defence
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.mod.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Indian companies, startups, MSMEs, and private sector organisations
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Defence manufacturing, design, development, or supply of defence equipment, systems, or services
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Preference given to entities contributing to indigenisation, technology absorption, and Make in India initiatives under the Atmanirbhar Bharat framework

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support through linked schemes such as the Defence Production and Export Promotion Policy (DPEPP); access to government procurement opportunities; partnerships with defence PSUs and ordnance factories
- Disbursement mode: Through linked schemes (e.g., DPEPP offers financial incentives)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (linked to specific contracts or schemes)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. GST Registration Certificate
4. UDYAM Registration (for MSMEs)
5. Product specifications and technical brochures
6. Manufacturing licence or industrial licence (if applicable)
7. Past performance certificates or supply orders
8. Bank details and cancelled cheque
9. Authorised signatory letter
10. Declaration of compliance with defence standards

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify defence production opportunities through the Ministry of Defence or defence PSUs.
2. Register as a vendor on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) or defence-specific procurement portals.
3. Submit Expression of Interest (EOI) or tender response for defence contracts.
4. Undergo technical evaluation and quality assurance processes.
5. Upon selection, enter into contract for supply, development, or production of defence equipment.
6. Avail benefits under linked schemes like DPEPP for financial incentives.
