# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is a company registered under the Companies Act, 1956 or 2013.
- [ ] The company has completed at least three financial years after formation.
- [ ] The applicant has a regular source of income during the last two years to sustain the business.
- [ ] The company is engaged in manufacture, production, or rendering technical services.
- [ ] If fully engaged in contract research, independent infrastructure for research activities is available.
- [ ] If engaged in research only with plans to start manufacture later, there is potential for future manufacturing.
- [ ] The R&D unit(s) is not located in a residential area but operates in premises authorized by relevant Central/State Government.
- [ ] Independent infrastructure for research activities is available with adequate technically qualified manpower.
- [ ] Minimum area for R&D activities is at least 1000 Sq. ft.
- [ ] At the time of application, the R&D unit(s) is functional and has well-defined, time-bound R&D programmes leading to development of innovative products and/or technology.
- [ ] The company has a long-term R&D policy displayed prominently in the in-house R&D unit(s).
- [ ] R&D activities are separate from routine activities such as production and quality control, with separate and identifiable infrastructure.
- [ ] The R&D unit maintains proper records of R&D activities in the form of documentation.
- [ ] The R&D unit is headed by a full-time qualified and experienced R&D person with direct access to the Chief Executive or Board of Directors.
- [ ] The number of R&D manpower is commensurate with the S&T manpower size of the company.
- [ ] Separate books of accounts are maintained for all R&D expenditures.
- [ ] R&D expenditure is booked when incurred and reflected in the Annual Report and Statement of Accounts in separate schedules.
- [ ] R&D expenditure is commensurate with the financial size of the company.

**Sources:**  
- Eligibility criteria from KEY FACTS and PDF: 05d514e07138bda3a342a1837dfde57d.pdf (Sections 5, 6, 7, 8, 9)  
- Portal: https://dsir.gov.in/offerings/schemes-and-services/details/recognition-of-in-house-rd-units-rdi-AjM0ETMtQWa

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate (Companies Act, 1956 or 2013)
- PAN Card of the company
- Latest Income Tax Return (ITR)
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable)
- Latest Annual Report of the company

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Covering letter
- Filled-in application form (signed by MD/Director)
- Annexures (in PDF format, single file <20 MB)
- Note on R&D activities highlighting ongoing & proposed research, past achievements/completed projects
- Brief write-up on major past achievements, ongoing and future projects
- Details of scientific personnel working in the R&D unit(s) with qualifications & designation
- Details of infrastructure available for research with date of installation & value of major facilities
- Layout drawing of the R&D unit vis-à-vis the plant/production unit
- Copy of Memorandum & Articles of Association (for newly formed companies)
- All attachments in a single PDF file <20 MB

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be in PDF format.
- Single PDF file size must not exceed 20 MB.
- File names must match the document description (e.g., "Annual_Report_2023-24.pdf").
- Scanned documents must be legible and certified where required.
- All text must be in English or accompanied by a certified translation.
- Undertakings and declarations must be ink-signed and scanned.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is **not explicitly required** for DSIR RDI recognition. However, the application requires:  
- A note on R&D activities highlighting ongoing & proposed research, past achievements/completed projects  
- A brief write-up on major past achievements, ongoing and future projects  
- Details of R&D projects in progress and proposed for the next three years  

If the nodal officer requests a detailed project report, verify requirements directly with them.  

**Sources:**  
- Required Documents from KEY FACTS  
- Application Form sections from PDF: 05d514e07138bda3a342a1837dfde57d.pdf (Pages 3–8)  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use the latest application format downloaded from: https://dsir.gov.in/offerings/schemes-and-services/details/recognition-of-in-house-rd-units-rdi-AjM0ETMtQWa  
2. Fill all fields in the form; leave no field blank. Use "0" or "NIL" where applicable and attach an undertaking for nil information.  
3. Ensure the form is signed by the Managing Director or any Director on the Board.  
4. All annexures must be compiled into a **single PDF file not exceeding 20 MB**.  
5. Clearly indicate in the email subject:  
   - For Fresh Recognition: “Fresh Recognition of in-house R&D Unit(s) submitted by [Company Name]”  
   - For Renewal: “Renewal of Recognition of in-house R&D Unit(s) beyond 31.03.YYYY submitted by [Name and Recognition number]”  
6. Do not submit hard copies; only soft copy (PDF) via email is processed.  
7. Submit well in advance for renewal (3 months prior to expiry of valid recognition).

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from DSIR.  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned benefits (recognition enables access to fiscal incentives and R&D funds from DST, DBT, DeitY, MoEF, MNRE, MoFPI, CSIR, ICMR, ICAR, TDB, etc.).  
- [ ] Prepare and submit Utilization Certificate (if required for subsequent funding applications).  
- [ ] Track invoice for success fee as per consultancy agreement.  
- [ ] Advise client on leveraging recognition to apply for R&D funding from eligible agencies.  
- [ ] Monitor recognition validity (typically 3 years; renewal to be initiated 3 months prior to expiry).  

**Sources:**  
- Benefits from KEY FACTS: "Recognized in-house R&D units are eligible for fiscal incentives... and also become eligible to receive funds for R&D from other government departments and agencies such as DST, DBT, DeitY, MoEF, MNRE, MoFPI, CSIR, ICMR, ICAR, and TDB, where DSIR recognition is a requirement."  
- Application Process: Recognition is subject to evaluation; no hard copy needed; soft copy processed via email.  
- Renewal: Submit well in advance (3 months prior to expiry) to rdi-renewal@gov.in
