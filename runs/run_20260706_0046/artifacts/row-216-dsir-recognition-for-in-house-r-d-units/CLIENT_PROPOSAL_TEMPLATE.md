## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified DSIR Recognition for In-house R&D Units — a recognition scheme administered by the Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (DSIR), Ministry of Science and Technology, Government of India — for which your business appears eligible. The scheme enables access to financial support for R&D from other government departments and agencies, though it does not provide direct financial support itself.
