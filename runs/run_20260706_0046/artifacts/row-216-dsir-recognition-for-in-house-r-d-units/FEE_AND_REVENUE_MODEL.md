## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, document preparation, and application submission support.
2. Success fee is contingent on successful DSIR recognition, which enables access to fiscal incentives and R&D funding from other government departments.
3. Fees are scaled to client size and complexity of R&D unit infrastructure and documentation requirements.
4. Scheme-specific note: DSIR recognition has no direct financial benefit but unlocks access to significant R&D funding; success is contingent on technical evaluation and typically takes 3–6 months for processing, introducing revenue lag and moderate risk due to dependency on DSIR’s review timelines and potential for clarification requests.
