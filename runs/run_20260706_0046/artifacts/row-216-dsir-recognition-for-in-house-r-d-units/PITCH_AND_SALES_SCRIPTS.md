# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping corporate industry R&D units gain formal recognition from DSIR, which unlocks access to government R&D funding and fiscal incentives. I’d like to understand your current R&D setup to see if this scheme aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your company incorporated under the Companies Act, 1956 or 2013?
2. Has your company completed at least three financial years since formation?
3. Does your company have a regular source of income for the last two years to sustain operations?
4. Is your R&D unit engaged in manufacture, production, or rendering technical services (not pure contract research)?
5. Is your R&D unit located in premises authorized by Central/State Government (not residential)?
6. Do you have at least 1000 sq. ft. of independent infrastructure dedicated to R&D activities?
7. Is your R&D unit currently functional with well-defined, time-bound R&D programmes aimed at innovative products or technology?
8. Do you have adequate technically qualified manpower exclusively engaged in R&D?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Company registered under Companies Act, 1956 or 2013  
- [ ] Completed three financial years after formation  
- [ ] Regular source of income for at least last two years  
- [ ] Engaged in manufacture, production, or technical services  
- [ ] R&D unit not in residential area; operates in authorized premises  
- [ ] Minimum 1000 sq. ft. independent infrastructure for R&D  
- [ ] Adequate technically qualified manpower available  
- [ ] Functional R&D unit with defined, time-bound programmes leading to innovation  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, your R&D unit appears eligible for DSIR Recognition for In-house R&D Units. This recognition is the gateway to accessing R&D funds from DST, DBT, DeitY, and other agencies, plus fiscal incentives where applicable. Shall I outline how it works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Corporate R&D units often struggle to access government R&D funding because they lack formal recognition, which is a mandatory prerequisite for grants and incentives from departments like DST, DBT, and CSIR.

**Scheme intro:**
The DSIR Recognition for In-house R&D Units (RDI) scheme, administered by the Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (Ministry of Science and Technology), grants formal recognition to eligible corporate R&D units. While it does not provide direct financial support, it enables access to R&D funds from DST, DBT, DeitY, MoEF, MNRE, MoFPI, CSIR, ICMR, ICAR, TDB, and fiscal incentives where applicable.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your company is registered under the Companies Act, 1956 or 2013  
- You have completed three financial years since formation  
- You maintain a regular source of income for the last two years  
- Your R&D unit has independent infrastructure (≥1000 sq. ft.) and qualified manpower
