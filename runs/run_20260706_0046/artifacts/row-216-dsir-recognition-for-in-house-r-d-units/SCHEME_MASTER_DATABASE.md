### DSIR Recognition for In-house R&D Units
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (DSIR), Ministry of Science and Technology, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dsir.gov.in/offerings/schemes-and-services/details/recognition-of-in-house-rd-units-rdi-AjM0ETMtQWa
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Applications can be submitted throughout the year. No fixed deadline.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Company registered under the Companies Act, 1956 or 2013
- Age of business: Eligible only after completion of three financial years after formation
- Sector: Engaged in manufacture or production or rendering technical services; companies fully engaged in contract research are eligible if independent infrastructure is available for research activities; those engaged in research only at present but with plans to start manufacture later may be considered if there is potential
- Revenue cap: Regular source of income at least during the last two years to sustain the business
- Employee count: Not explicitly mentioned
- Other: 
  - R&D unit(s) should not be located in residential areas but should operate in premises authorized by relevant Central/State Government
  - Independent infrastructure for research activities and adequate technically qualified manpower should be available (minimum area for R&D activities should be at least 1000 Sq. ft.)
  - At the time of application, the R&D unit(s) should be functional and have well-defined, time-bound R&D programmes leading to development of innovative products and/or technology
  - The company must spell out a long term R&D policy which should be displayed prominently in the in-house R&D unit(s)
  - R&D activities should be separate from routine activities of the firm, such as production and quality control
  - The units should have qualified staff exclusively engaged in R&D and should be headed by a full-time qualified & experienced R&D person who has direct access to the Chief Executive or to the Board of Directors
  - The number of R&D manpower should be commensurate with the S&T manpower size of the company
  - The R&D units should maintain separate books of accounts for all the R&D expenditures
  - Expenditure should be booked when incurred and not allocated; the company should reflect the R&D expenditure (both capital & revenue) in the Annual Report and Statement of Accounts of the company in separate schedules
  - The R&D expenditure incurred should be commensurate with the financial size of the company

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: The scheme itself does not provide direct financial support but enables access to financial support for R&D from other government departments and agencies
- Disbursement mode: Not applicable (no direct financial support)
- Duration of benefit: Recognition is generally granted for a period of 3 years terminating on the 31st March of the third financial year; however, in certain cases this recognition may be granted for a shorter period

#### Documents Required
1. Covering letter
2. Filled-in application form (signed by MD/Director)
3. Annexures (in PDF format, single file <20 MB)
4. Latest Annual Report of the company
5. Note on R&D activities highlighting ongoing & proposed research, past achievements/completed projects
6. Brief write-up on major past achievements, ongoing and future projects
7. Details of scientific personnel working in the R&D unit(s) with qualifications & designation
8. Details of infrastructure available for research with date of installation & value of major facilities
9. Layout drawing of the R&D unit vis-à-vis the plant/production unit
10. Copy of Memorandum & Articles of Association (for newly formed companies)
11. All attachments in a single PDF file <20 MB

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Companies interested in applying for DSIR in-house R&D recognition need not submit hard copy.
2. Submit a covering letter, completely filled-in application form (signed by Managing Director or any Director on Board) along with requisite Annexures in PDF format (single attachment less than 20 MB).
3. For Fresh Recognition, submit the signed PDF application to the email: rdi-fresh@gov.in.
4. For Renewal of Recognition, submit the signed PDF application to the email: rdi-renewal@gov.in.
5. Applications can be submitted throughout the year.
6. No hard copy of the application needs to be submitted; only soft copy is processed.
