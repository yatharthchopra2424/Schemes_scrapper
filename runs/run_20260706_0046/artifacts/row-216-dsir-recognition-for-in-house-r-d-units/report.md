# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **DSIR Recognition for In-house R&D Units** (Scheme ID: row-216) is a recognition-type scheme administered by the Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (DSIR), Ministry of Science and Technology, Government of India. It operates pan-India and is currently active with no fixed deadline—applications can be submitted throughout the year. The scheme does not provide direct financial support but serves as a prerequisite for accessing fiscal incentives and R&D funding from other government departments and agencies.

### Objectives
- Grant recognition & registration to in-house R&D units established by corporate industry  
- Benchmark industrial R&D across the government setup  
- Enable eligibility for fiscal incentives for R&D implemented through DSIR  
- Facilitate access to R&D funds from other government departments such as DST, DBT, DeitY, MoEF, MNRE, MoFPI, CSIR, ICMR, ICAR, TDB  
- Promote innovative research & development activities related to the line of business of the firm  
- Encourage development of new technologies, design & engineering, process/product improvements, and new methods of analysis & testing  
- Support research for increased efficiency in use of resources such as capital equipment, materials & energy  
- Promote pollution control, effluent treatment & recycling of waste products

#### Detailed Steps
1. **No hard copy required** – Only soft copy (PDF) is processed  
2. **Prepare application**:  
   - Covering letter  
   - Filled-in application form (signed by MD/Director)  
   - Annexures in PDF format (single file <20 MB)  
3. **Submit via email**:  
   - Fresh Recognition: `rdi-fresh@gov.in`  
   - Renewal of Recognition: `rdi-renewal@gov.in`  
4. **Application can be submitted throughout the year** – No fixed deadline  
5. **DSIR evaluates**:  
   - Based on submitted documents  
   - May involve discussions with chief of R&D unit and firm executives  
   - Chairman of Screening Committee can relax any condition  
6. **Outcome**:  
   - Recognition granted → Registration certificate issued  
   - Enables access to fiscal incentives and R&D funds from DST, DBT, DeitY, MoEF, MNRE, MoFPI, CSIR, ICMR, ICAR, TDB  

#### Required Documents (Annexures)
1. Covering letter  
2. Filled-in application form (signed by MD/Director)  
3. Annexures (PDF, single file <20 MB)  
4. Latest Annual Report of the company  
5. Note on R&D activities (ongoing & proposed research, past achievements/completed projects)  
6. Brief write-up on major past achievements, ongoing and future projects  
7. Details of scientific personnel (qualifications & designation)  
8. Details of infrastructure (date of installation & value of major facilities)  
9. Layout drawing of R&D unit vis-à-vis plant/production unit  
10. Copy of Memorandum & Articles of Association (for newly formed companies)  
11. All attachments in a single PDF file <20 MB
