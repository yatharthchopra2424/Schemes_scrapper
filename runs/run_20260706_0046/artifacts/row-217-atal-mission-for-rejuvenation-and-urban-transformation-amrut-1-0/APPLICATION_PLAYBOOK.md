## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is an Urban Local Body (ULB) of a city selected under AMRUT 1.0 (500 selected cities based on: population >1 lakh with notified Municipality/Cantonment Board, State/UT capital, Heritage City under HRIDAY, or located on stem of major rivers between 5-50 km)
- [ ] The applicant is a Municipal Corporation, Municipality, Cantonment Board, or equivalent ULB of an eligible city
- [ ] The project falls under one of the eligible sectors: water supply, sewerage and septage management, storm water drainage, green spaces and parks, or non-motorized urban transport
- [ ] The State Annual Action Plan (SAAP) has been prepared and approved by the State Level Sanctioning Committee (SLSC)
- [ ] The project conforms to approved Service Level Benchmarks (SLBs) and urban reform milestones
- [ ] The State/UT has committed its matching share as per funding pattern (90:10 for NE/Himalayan States, 80:20 for other States, 50:50 for UTs)
- [ ] Land availability and ownership documents are in place for the project site
- [ ] Environmental and social clearances have been obtained (if applicable)
- [ ] Detailed Project Report (DPR) has been prepared for the project

> **Note**: AMRUT 1.0 was implemented from 2015-16 to 2019-20 and has been subsumed into AMRUT 2.0. No new applications are being accepted under AMRUT 1.0. This playbook is for reference or for cases involving utilization, reporting, or closure of ongoing AMRUT 1.0 projects.  
> **Source**: [AMRUT Official Portal](https://amrut.gov.in)

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Establishment (for ULB)
- PAN Card of the ULB
- Audited Financial Statements and Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the ULB for last 3 years
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)
- Bank Statements of the ULB (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable for any ULB-owned enterprises)
- Authorization Letter from Commissioner / Municipal Secretary
- Board/Council Resolution authorizing the project and application

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- State Annual Action Plan (SAAP) approved by State Level Sanctioning Committee (SLSC)
- Utilization Certificates (UCs) for previous fund releases (if applicable)
- Progress Reports (Physical and Financial) for ongoing projects
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) for the proposed project
- Land availability and ownership documents (title deed, lease agreement, etc.)
- Environmental and Social Clearance certificates (if applicable)
- Approval from State Level Sanctioning Committee (SLSC)
- Service Level Benchmark (SLB) compliance report
- Urban Reform Milestone achievement documentation

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 10 MB per document)
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ULBName_Date.pdf` (e.g., `AMRUT_SAAP_MunicipalCorp_XYZ_20240515.pdf`)
- All certificates and approvals must be valid and not expired
- Documents in regional languages must be accompanied by certified Hindi/English translations
- Utilization Certificates must be in GFR 12-A format and signed by authorized finance officer
- DPR must include technical specifications, cost estimates, implementation schedule, and sustainability plan
- Land documents must show clear title and possession in favor of the ULB or concerned agency

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required** for AMRUT 1.0 projects.  
Standard sections to include:
- Executive Summary
- Project Background and Justification
- Technical Description (design, capacity, technology)
- Site Survey and Land Details
- Environmental and Social Impact Assessment
- Cost Estimates (itemized, with base year and contingencies)
- Implementation Schedule (bar chart/Gantt)
- Financing Plan (central, state, ULB share)
- Operations and Maintenance (O&M) Plan
- Sustainability and Revenue Model
- Monitoring and Evaluation Framework
- Compliance with Service Level Benchmarks (SLBs)
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan

> **Note**: Verify DPR requirements with the nodal officer or State Mission Director, as formats may vary slightly by state.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Confirm project is under an existing AMRUT 1.0 SAAP** – new SAAPs are not being accepted.
2. **Ensure all fields in the utilization/progress reporting module are filled** – no blank fields allowed.
3. **Match financial figures exactly with audited statements and UCs** – any discrepancy will cause rejection.
4. **Upload documents in the prescribed order and naming convention** as per portal instructions.
5. **Select correct project sector** (water supply, sewerage, drainage, green spaces, non-motorized transport).
6. **Enter correct central fund release installment number** for which UC is being submitted.
7. **Verify that State/UT matching share has been released** and reflected in financials.
8. **Cross-check SLSC approval number and date** in the form matches the uploaded document.
9. **Save draft before final submission** and take screenshot of each step.
10. **Do not submit after 5:00 PM on working days** to avoid portal timeout issues (based on common portal behavior).

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached and legible
- [ ] DPR is complete, signed, and includes all required sections
- [ ] Utilization Certificate is in GFR 12-A format and arithmetically correct
- [ ] Financial figures in form match UCs, audited statements, and bank statements
- [ ] Land documents show clear title in favor of implementing agency
- [ ] Environmental/social clearances are present if project triggers applicability
- [ ] SLSC approval is visible and matches project details
- [ ] Sector selected matches project type (water, sewerage, etc.)
- [ ] All declarations are signed by authorized signatory (Commissioner/Municipal Secretary)
- [ ] Application draft has been reviewed by senior consultant before submission

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify approval letter / sanction order from MoHUA via State Finance Department
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount matches claimed eligible cost as per DPR and SLSC approval
- [ ] Verify central fund release installment has been credited to State/UT account
- [ ] Ensure Utilization Certificate (UC) for the current installment is prepared and submitted within stipulated time (usually within 6 months of fund release)
- [ ] Collect and submit next set of documents (Progress Report, UC) for subsequent installment as per disbursement schedule
- [ ] Monitor project implementation against timelines in DPR
- [ ] Ensure compliance with urban reform milestones and Service Level Benchmarks (SLBs)
- [ ] Prepare project completion report and final UC upon closure
- [ ] Coordinate with ULB for successful fee invoicing (if applicable under consultancy agreement)

> **Note**: Central assistance under AMRUT 1.0 is released in installments based on submission of Utilization Certificates and progress reports. States/UTs must submit UCs within the stipulated time to avoid interest or penalty clauses.
