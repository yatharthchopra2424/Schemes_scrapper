## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the scale of infrastructure grant benefits secured for Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) under AMRUT 1.0.
2. Retainer covers initial assessment, SAAP preparation, and liaison with MoHUA and State Level Sanctioning Committee (SLSC).
3. Success fee is contingent upon approval of State Annual Action Plans (SAAPs) and subsequent fund release based on Utilization Certificates (UCs) and progress reports.
4. Scheme-specific note: AMRUT 1.0 has a moderate risk profile due to its completed status (subsumed into AMRUT 2.0 in 2021) and reliance on multi-stage approvals (SAAP → Apex Committee → UC-based tranche releases). Processing timelines are lengthy (typically 6–12 months per tranche), increasing working capital risk for consultants. However, historical evidence shows consistent central fund releases upon compliance, supporting predictable success fee realization for eligible ULBs that meet reform milestones and SLBs.
