## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping Urban Local Bodies (ULBs), municipal corporations, and state governments access central government grants for urban infrastructure transformation. The Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation (AMRUT 1.0) was designed to ensure every household has access to tap water and sewerage, while improving urban amenities through green spaces and sustainable transport. I’d like to understand your city’s current infrastructure priorities to see if there’s alignment with this mission’s objectives.

### Discovery Questions
- What is the current status of water supply and sewerage coverage in your ULB’s jurisdiction?
- Are you planning or implementing projects in storm water drainage, green space development, or non-motorized transport (e.g., walking/cycling infrastructure)?
- Has your city been selected under the AMRUT mission (based on population (>1 lakh), capital city status, heritage designation, or location on a major river stem?
- What is the timeline for your State Annual Action Plan (SAAP) preparation and submission to MoHUA?
- Do you have Utilization Certificates (UCs) and progress reports ready from previous central-funded projects?
- Are you facing challenges in securing matching state/UT shares as per the 80:20 or 50:50 funding pattern?
- Have you obtained environmental/social clearances or land ownership documents for proposed infrastructure projects?
- Is your ULB looking to strengthen institutional capacity or pursue PPP models for service delivery?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] ULB/municipal corporation/state government of a selected AMRUT 1.0 city (population >1 lakh, capital city, HRIDAY heritage city, or located on stem of major river between 5-50 km)
- [ ] Project proposal falls under water supply, sewerage, septage management, storm water drainage, green spaces/parks, or non-motorized urban transport
- [ ] Ready to submit State Annual Action Plan (SAAP) for appraisal by MoHUA’s Apex Committee
- [ ] Able to provide Utilization Certificates (UCs), progress reports, and Detailed Project Reports (DPRs)
- [ ] Land availability and ownership documents prepared for project sites
- [ ] Environmental and social clearances obtained (if applicable)
- [ ] Approval from State Level Sanctioning Committee (SLSC) secured or in process
- [ ] Willing to meet matching share requirements (80:20 for most states, 90:10 for NE/Himalayan, 50:50 for UTs)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your ULB is actively working on infrastructure upgrades in core AMRUT sectors and meets the city eligibility criteria. The Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation (AMRUT 1.0) provides central grant-in-aid for exactly these types of projects — let me share how it works and how we can support your application.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many Urban Local Bodies struggle to fund critical infrastructure projects in water supply, sewerage, drainage, and urban green spaces due to limited budgets and complex funding requirements. Without central support, delays in implementation affect service delivery to urban populations, particularly the urban poor, and hinder progress on national urban renewal goals.

**Scheme intro:**
The Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation (AMRUT 1.0) is a central government grant scheme administered by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), Government of India. It provided a total central outlay of INR 50,000 crore from 2015-16 to 2019-20, with funding shared between Centre and States/UTs (80:20 for most states, 90:10 for NE/Himalayan, 50:50 for UTs). Financial assistance is released as grant-in-aid to States/UTs based on approved State Annual Action Plans (SAAPs) for projects in water supply, sewerage, septage management, storm water drainage, green spaces and parks, and non-motorized urban transport.

- Your city is among the 500 selected under AMRUT 1.0 based on population (>1 lakh), capital city status, HRIDAY heritage city designation, or location on the stem of a major river (5-50 km)
- You are proposing projects in eligible sectors: water supply, sewerage, drainage, green spaces, or non-motorized transport
- You are prepared to submit a State Annual Action Plan (SAAP) and provide Utilization Certificates (UCs) and progress reports for fund release
- You can meet the matching share requirement as per the prescribed funding pattern (e.g., 80:20 Centre:State)
