### Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation (AMRUT 1.0)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://amrut.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Closed
- **Window:** 2015-16 to 2019-20

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Urban Local Bodies (ULBs), municipal corporations, state governments
- Age of business: Not applicable (government entities)
- Sector: Urban infrastructure (water supply, sewerage, storm water drainage, green spaces and parks, non-motorized urban transport)
- Revenue cap: Not mentioned
- Employee count: Not mentioned
- Other: Must be one of 500 selected cities based on: population over one lakh with notified Municipalities (including Cantonment Boards), capital cities of States/UTs, Heritage Cities under HRIDAY scheme, or cities located on the stem of major rivers (between 5-50 km)

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Central outlay of INR 50,000 crore (shared Centre-State: 90:10 for NE/Himalayan States, 80:20 for other States, 50:50 for UTs)
- Disbursement mode: Grant-in-aid to States/UTs based on approved State Annual Action Plans (SAAPs)
- Duration of benefit: Project-based (2015-16 to 2019-20)

#### Documents Required
1. State Annual Action Plan (SAAP)
2. Utilization Certificates (UCs)
3. Progress Reports (Physical and Financial)
4. Project Detailed Project Reports (DPRs)
5. Land availability and ownership documents
6. Environmental and social clearances (if applicable)
7. Approval from State Level Sanctioning Committee (SLSC)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. States/UTs prepare State Annual Action Plans (SAAPs) detailing project proposals for water supply, sewerage, drainage, green spaces, and non-motorized transport.
2. SAAPs are submitted to the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) for appraisal.
3. The Apex Committee under MoHUA reviews and approves the SAAPs.
4. Upon approval, central funds are released to States/UTs in installments based on utilization certificates and progress reports.
5. States/UTs further disburse funds to Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) for project implementation.
6. ULBs execute projects through tendering and project management, with monitoring by State and Central authorities.
