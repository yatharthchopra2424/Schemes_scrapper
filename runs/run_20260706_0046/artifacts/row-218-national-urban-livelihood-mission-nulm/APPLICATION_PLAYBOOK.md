# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- ✅ Applicant is from urban poor household (identified as below poverty line by State/UT)
- ✅ Applicant belongs to target beneficiary group: urban poor, urban homeless, street vendors, SCs, STs, women, minorities, differently-abled persons
- ✅ For Self-Employment Programme (SEP) individual enterprise: No minimum educational qualification required; training provided if special skills needed
- ✅ For SEP group enterprise: Minimum 3 members in group; at least 70% members from urban poor families; no more than one person from same family in group
- ✅ For Employment through Skills Training & Placement (EST&P): 
  - Fresh entrants: Minimum 200 hours training
  - Reskilling: Minimum 16 hours training  
  - Formal recognition: Bridge courses if needed
- ✅ Applicant is willing to undergo skill training if required for enterprise
- ✅ Applicant resides in urban area covered by DAY-NULM (Pan-India scope)
- ✅ Applicant has not undergone skill training under SJSRY/NULM in same trade in last 3 years (can receive advanced training on same skills)
- ✅ For group enterprises under SEP: Group strength verified and urban poor membership confirmed (≥70% from urban poor families)

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Identity proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, etc.)
- Address proof (utility bill, ration card, etc.)
- Bank account details (bank passbook copy)
- PAN card
- ITR (if applicable)
- GST registration certificate (if applicable)
- Udyam registration (if applicable)
- Recent bank statements (last 3 months)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Loan Application Form (LAF)
- Photograph of enterprise
- FSSAI license (if available, for food processing)
- Declaration signed by SHG office bearers (for SHG applications)
- Completed application (Form I & II)
- Enterprise bank account copy (for group/activity group)
- Passbook of proprietor (for individual enterprise)
- Aadhaar details (if available)

### Document Quality Check
- ✅ All documents are legible and clear scans/photocopies
- ✅ File size does not exceed 5 MB per document
- ✅ Document names match applicant's identity proof exactly
- ✅ Self-attested documents include applicant's signature and date
- ✅ Photograph of enterprise is recent (within last 3 months) and shows business activity
- ✅ Bank passbook copy shows active account with transactions
- ✅ All declarations are signed and dated
- ✅ Documents are in English or Hindi; if regional language, provide certified translation
- ✅ No overwriting or tampering visible on documents

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not explicitly required for standard DAY-NULM applications under SEP or EST&P. However, for innovative projects or City Livelihood Centres (CLCs), a detailed project proposal is needed. **Verify with nodal officer** whether a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required for your specific activity under DAY-NULM.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use only the official application forms available at https://nulm.gov.in or through ULB/CMMU
2. Fill all fields in Block Letters to avoid ambiguity
3. Ensure Aadhaar number is correctly entered if available (mandatory for bank linkage)
4. Enterprise type must be clearly specified (individual, group, SHG, street vendor)
5. Loan amount requested must align with eligibility: individual enterprise ≤ Rs. 2 lakhs, group enterprise ≤ Rs. 10 lakhs
6. For group enterprises, list all members with their Aadhaar, urban poor status, and relationship declaration (no more than one from same family)
7. Attach all scheme-specific documents as per Stage 2 checklist
8. Sign and date the declaration form; ensure SHG office bearers sign if applicable
9. Submit application to ULB first for acknowledgement and registration number
10. Keep copies of all submitted documents and the acknowledgement receipt

### Internal Review Gate
- ✅ All application form fields completed accurately
- ✅ Universal documents attached and verified
- ✅ Scheme-specific documents attached as per eligibility type (individual/group/SHG)
- ✅ Photograph of enterprise is clear and recent
- ✅ Declarations are signed and dated by authorized signatories
- ✅ Bank account details match submitted passbook copy
- ✅ For group enterprises: minimum 3 members, ≥70% urban poor, no intra-family duplication
- ✅ Application reference number obtained from ULB acknowledgement
- ✅ All documents within file size limits and properly named
- ✅ Client has been informed of next steps and follow-up timeline

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit completed application package to Urban Local Body (ULB) or designated Community Organiser (CO)
- Obtain acknowledgement with unique registration number from ULB/MIS portal
- Take screenshot of submission confirmation (if online) or scan of physical acknowledgement
- Store application reference number securely in case file
- Set follow-up reminders for Day 25 (ULB screening) and Day 30 (Task Force review)
- Monitor application status via ULB or DAY-NULM MIS portal if accessible

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- ✅ Obtain and verify approval letter from ULB or sanctioning authority
- ✅ Confirm sanctioned amount matches approved project cost (individual: ≤ Rs. 2 lakhs, group: ≤ Rs. 10 lakhs)
- ✅ Verify interest subsidy terms: difference between 7% per annum and prevailing interest rate
- ✅ For SHGs: Confirm revolving fund support disbursement process
- ✅ For food processing enterprises: Validate 35% credit-linked subsidy (up to Rs. 10 lakh)
- ✅ Under PMFME convergence: Confirm seed capital of Rs. 40,000 per SHG member as repayable loan at ≤6% interest
- ✅ Ensure utilization certificate format is obtained from implementing agency
- ✅ Track loan disbursement from bank and subsidy claim submission by bank to ULB
- ✅ Monitor quarterly interest subsidy claims (banks submit to ULB after disbursement)
- ✅ Verify timely repayment condition for continued subsidy release
- ✅ Document invoice for success fee as per consultancy agreement
- ✅ Guide client on submission of utilization certificate and post-loan monitoring requirements
- ✅ For EST&P beneficiaries: Confirm placement tracking and post-placement support if applicable
- ✅ Ensure all benefits are accessed as per eligibility: skill training, financial inclusion, shelter access (if applicable), identity cards for street vendors, etc.
