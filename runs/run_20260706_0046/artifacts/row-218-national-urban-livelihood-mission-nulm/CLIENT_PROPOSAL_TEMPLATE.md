## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified National Urban Livelihood Mission (NULM) — a livelihood promotion scheme administered by Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Government of India — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit includes interest subsidy on bank loans (difference between 7% and prevailing rate), seed capital of ₹40,000 per SHG member under PMFME convergence, and credit-linked subsidy of 35% of project cost up to ₹10 lakh for food processing enterprises.

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment against NULM criteria (urban poor, SHG, micro-enterprise requirements)
2. Documentation review and preparation as per NULM checklist (Loan Application Form, identity/address proof, bank details, Aadhaar, enterprise photo, FSSAI/GST if applicable, SHG declarations, Forms I & II)
3. Portal registration and application submission via https://nulm.gov.in
4. Liaison with ULB/TLF for application forwarding and verification
5. Bank coordination for loan processing and subsidy claims
6. Interest subsidy claim tracking (quarterly after disbursement)
7. Compliance monitoring for timely repayment (subsidy release condition)
