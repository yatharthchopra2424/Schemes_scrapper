## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit delivered under NULM, which includes interest subsidies (difference between 7% and prevailing rate), seed capital of ₹40,000 per SHG member, and credit-linked subsidies up to ₹10 lakh for food processing enterprises.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s rolling application basis and multi-stage approval process (involving ULB screening, Task Force review, and bank processing within 15 days), success fees reflect moderate execution risk. Subsidy disbursement is contingent on timely loan repayment, introducing performance risk.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The Starter and Growth tiers align with NULM’s target beneficiaries—urban poor, SHGs, and micro-enterprises with project costs up to ₹2 lakhs (individual) and ₹10 lakhs (group). These tiers reflect the scheme’s focus on grassroots-level institutions.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: Fees are structured to ensure clients pay only for measurable outcomes, with success fees tied to verified subsidy disbursement or seed capital release, as documented in ULB and bank reports.
