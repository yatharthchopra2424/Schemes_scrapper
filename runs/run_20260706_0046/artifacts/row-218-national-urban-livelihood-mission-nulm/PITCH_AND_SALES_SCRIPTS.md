# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good day! I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping urban poor entrepreneurs and self-help groups access government livelihood schemes like the **National Urban Livelihood Mission (NULM)**. This scheme supports skill training, interest subsidies on loans, and revolving fund support for micro-enterprises—especially for street vendors, SHGs, and vulnerable urban communities. Before we explore whether this fits your situation, may I ask a few quick questions to understand your background and needs?

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you currently operating or planning to start a micro-enterprise as an individual or as part of a Self-Help Group (SHG) in an urban area?
2. What is the approximate project cost you’re seeking funding for—individual enterprise (up to ₹2 lakhs) or group enterprise (up to ₹10 lakhs)?
3. For group enterprises: Does your group have at least 3 members, with at least 70% from urban poor families, and no more than one member from the same family?
4. Are you involved in any of these activities: street vending, skill training for employment, or setting up a food processing unit (which may qualify for 35% credit-linked subsidy up to ₹10 lakhs)?
5. Do you have identity proof, address proof, bank account details, and Aadhaar (if available) ready for application?
6. Are you willing to ensure timely loan repayment, as interest subsidy under NULM is released only on timely repayment?
7. Have you previously applied for any government livelihood or skill training scheme, and if so, what was the outcome?
8. Are you part of a disadvantaged group (SC, ST, woman, minority, differently-abled) that NULM prioritizes for inclusion?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is urban poor, urban homeless, street vendor, SC, ST, woman, minority, or differently-abled  
- [ ] For individual enterprise: project cost ≤ ₹2 lakhs  
- [ ] For group enterprise: project cost ≤ ₹10 lakhs, minimum 3 members, ≥70% from urban poor families, no more than one member from same family  
- [ ] For SHG seed capital: eligibility for ₹40,000 per member under PMFME convergence (repayable loan at ≤6% interest)  
- [ ] For food processing enterprises: eligible for 35% credit-linked subsidy up to ₹10 lakhs  
- [ ] Applicant has or can obtain: identity proof, address proof, bank details, Aadhaar, enterprise photo, bank passbook, FSSAI/GST (if applicable), SHG declaration  
- [ ] Willing to comply with timely repayment for interest subsidy disbursement  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you may be eligible for financial support under the **National Urban Livelihood Mission (NULM)**—particularly the interest subsidy on bank loans or seed capital for SHGs. Let me share how this scheme can directly support your enterprise goals.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Urban poor entrepreneurs often struggle to access affordable credit due to high interest rates and lack of collateral, limiting their ability to start or grow micro-enterprises. Skill gaps and informal sector barriers further reduce income potential, trapping households in poverty cycles.

**Scheme intro:**
The **National Urban Livelihood Mission (NULM)**, administered by the **Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Government of India**, provides financial support including:  
- Interest subsidy on bank loans (difference between 7% per annum and prevailing rate) for individual micro-enterprises (project cost up to ₹2 lakhs) and group enterprises (up to ₹10 lakhs)  
- Revolving fund support for Self-Help Groups (SHGs)  
- Seed capital of ₹40,000 per SHG member under convergence with PMFME (repayable as loan at ≤6% interest)  
- Credit-linked subsidy of 35% of project cost, up to ₹10 lakhs, for food processing enterprises  

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You operate in an urban area and belong to a priority beneficiary group (e.g., urban poor, street vendor, SHG member)  
- Your enterprise project cost falls within the eligible limit (individual: ≤₹2 lakhs; group: ≤₹10 lakhs)  
- Your SHG meets the group criteria: minimum 3 members, ≥70% urban poor representation, no intra-family duplication  
- You possess or can arrange the required documents: identity, address, bank details, Aadhaar, enterprise photo, and SHG declarations
