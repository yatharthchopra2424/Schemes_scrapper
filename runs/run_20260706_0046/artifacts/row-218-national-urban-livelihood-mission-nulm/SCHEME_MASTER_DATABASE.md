### National Urban Livelihood Mission (NULM)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nulm.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Urban poor households, including urban homeless, street vendors, Self-Help Groups (SHGs), individual and group enterprises
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: All sectors eligible for self-employment and skilled wage employment (including but not limited to food processing, skill training, micro-enterprises)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: For group enterprises under SEP: minimum 3 members; for individual enterprises: 1 member
- Other: 
  - For group enterprises under SEP: minimum 3 members with at least 70% from urban poor families, no more than one person from the same family
  - For Self-Employment Programme (SEP): no minimum educational qualification required, but training must be provided if special skills are needed
  - For Employment through Skills Training & Placement (EST&P): eligibility varies by training type: fresh entrants require minimum 200 hours, reskilling requires 16 hours, formal recognition requires bridge courses if needed
  - Target beneficiaries include urban poor, urban homeless, street vendors, SCs, STs, women, minorities, differently-abled persons

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - Interest subsidy on bank loans: difference between 7% per annum and prevailing interest rate (for individual micro-enterprises: project cost up to Rs. 2 lakhs; for group enterprises: project cost up to Rs. 10 lakhs)
  - Revolving fund support to Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and their federations
  - Seed capital of Rs. 40,000 per SHG member under convergence with PMFME (repayable as loan from SHG federations at interest not exceeding 6% per annum)
  - Credit-linked subsidy of 35% of project cost, up to Rs. 10 lakh, for food processing enterprises
  - Financial support for City Livelihood Centres (CLCs) and shelters
  - Overall expenditure on Capacity Building and Training (CB&T) capped at 12% of total allocation
- Disbursement mode: 
  - Interest subsidy: Released to banks after loan disbursement (banks submit quarterly claims to ULB; ULB verifies and releases subsidy)
  - Seed capital and credit-linked subsidy: Disbursed as loans through SHG federations or banks
  - Revolving fund: Provided to SHGs
- Duration of benefit: 
  - Interest subsidy: Ongoing, subject to timely repayment
  - Seed capital: Repayable loan terms: One-time for specific components)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify prospective beneficiaries through Community Organisers (COs) or ULB officials from urban poor households.
2. Submit application of intent with basic details (name, age, contact, address, Aadhaar, loan required, bank account, enterprise type) to ULB.
3. ULB enters details into a register or MIS and issues acknowledgement with unique registration number.
4. ULB screens applications and forwards recommended cases to Task Force at ULB level.
5. Task Force scrutinises applications, conducts interviews, and recommends or rejects based on viability.
6. Recommended cases are sent to concerned banks for processing within 15 days.
7. Banks process loans and submit periodic reports to ULB on application status.
8. For interest subsidy, banks submit quarterly claims to ULB after loan disbursement; ULB verifies and releases subsidy (difference between 7% and prevailing rate) to banks.
9. For SEP-G (group enterprises), similar process applies with group entry form and verification of group strength and urban poor membership.
10. For SHG bank linkage, SHGs apply through ULB, and subsidy is released on timely repayment.
11. For innovative projects, proposals are submitted to State/UT Government or SULM, screened by National Mission Directorate, and approved by Project Approval Committee (PAC).
