# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- ✅ The user must have a bank account at one of the covered Indian banks listed on the HRI Day India website (e.g., SBI, IOB, Canara Bank, PNB, etc.)
- ✅ The user needs assistance with online banking services such as balance checks, debit card activation, statement downloads, password recovery, or online banking registration
- ✅ The user must be an individual/retail user (not a business or corporate entity)
- ✅ The user must have access to a registered mobile number linked to their bank account for OTP verification where required
- ✅ The user must possess their own bank credentials (user ID, password, etc.) to perform guided actions
- ✅ The user must be seeking informational guidance only — no financial support, grants, loans, or subsidies are offered under this scheme
- ✅ The user must be willing to follow step-by-step guides available on the portal and may need to visit official bank websites or use bank-specific apps as directed

> **Note**: There are no turnover thresholds, employee count requirements, sector restrictions, or age-of-business conditions. Eligibility is open to any individual with a bank account needing digital banking guidance.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- ✅ PAN Card (for identity verification if required by bank during guided processes)
- ✅ Aadhaar Card (for KYC and mobile number linkage confirmation)
- ✅ Bank Passbook or Account Statement (to verify account details and registered mobile number)
- ✅ Registered Mobile Number (active and linked to the bank account for OTP reception)
- ✅ Email ID (for receiving statements and communications where applicable)
- ✅ Udyam Registration (only if the user is a sole proprietor seeking guidance for business account — otherwise not required)
- ✅ GST Certificate (only if applicable to the user’s business activities — not required for pure retail banking guidance)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- No scheme-specific documents are required beyond the universal set, as **HRIDAY – Heritage City Development and Augmentation Yojana** (as misnamed in the evidence; actual scheme name: HRI Day India) is an informational platform offering guides and tutorials.  
  > **Clarification**: Despite the scheme name in the KEY FACTS resembling a government urban development program, the evidence confirms this is **HRI Day India**, a banking guidance website with no financial disbursement. Therefore, no application, approval, or documentation submission is involved.

### Document Quality Check
- ✅ File size: Under 5 MB for uploads (if any verification is needed externally)
- ✅ File naming: Clear and consistent (e.g., `PAN_[FullName].pdf`, `Aadhaar_[FullName].pdf`)
- ✅ All documents must be legible, scanned copies or clear photographs
- ✅ Names on documents must match exactly as per bank records
- ✅ Documents must be valid and not expired (especially Aadhaar, PAN)
- ✅ No certification or notarization is required for accessing the guides

  - Activating debit cards for online transactions  
  - Recovering internet banking passwords

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- ❌ **Not required**. HRI Day India is an informational portal with no financial disbursement, project funding, or implementation component. No Detailed Project Report (DPR) or business plan is needed.  
- **Note**: If a client mistakenly believes this is a grant scheme, verify with the nodal officer (if any) that no funding is disbursed. However, evidence confirms **no financial support** is offered.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
- ❌ **No application form exists** for this scheme, as it does not offer financial assistance.  
- **Protocol for Guidance Access (Informational Equivalent)**:  
  1. Always verify the client’s bank name and specific service need before directing them to a guide  
  2. Use the search/filter function on https://hridayindia.in to locate accurate, bank-specific tutorials  
  3. Confirm the guide is updated (check “Last Updated” date — e.g., April 2026 for SBI balance check)  
  4. Advise clients to have their bank credentials, registered mobile number, and internet access ready before following steps  
  5. Warn against sharing sensitive details (password, OTP) with anyone — including consultants  
  6. Remind users that guides are for assistance only and do not guarantee success if bank systems change  
  7. Encourage users to contact official bank support if they encounter issues beyond the guide’s scope  
  8. Document the guide URL and date accessed for client reference and audit trail  

### Internal Review Gate
- ✅ Client’s need matched to an available guide on HRI Day India  
- ✅ Correct bank and service specified (e.g., “IOB statement download” not generic “banking help”)  
- ✅ Client informed that no documents are submitted or approved — this is self-service guidance  
- ✅ Client warned about OTP security and credential safety  
- ✅ Alternative support paths (bank helpline, branch visit) communicated if self-guided steps fail  
- ✅ Case notes include: bank name, service needed, guide URL accessed, date of guidance provided
