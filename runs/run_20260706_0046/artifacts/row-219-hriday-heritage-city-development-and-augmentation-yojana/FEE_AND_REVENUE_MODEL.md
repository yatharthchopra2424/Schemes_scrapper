## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with client outcomes  
2. Transparent fee structures with no hidden charges  
3. Risk-sharing model where success fees are contingent on measurable benefit delivery  
4. Volume discounts for long-term engagements and multi-scheme advisory retainers  

**Scheme-specific note:** HRIDAY (HRI Day India) provides only informational guides for banking services with **no financial support, grants, loans, or subsidies**. The scheme offers zero monetary benefit to users, resulting in **no financial upside for success-based fees**. Processing time is immediate (guides accessible year-round), but since there is no tangible financial outcome, the risk profile for success fees is **non-applicable** — revenue must derive solely from retainer-based advisory for process optimization or digital literacy training, not benefit realization.
