# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping bank customers across India get clear, step-by-step guidance for managing their accounts online—whether it's activating debit cards, downloading statements, or resetting passwords—through trusted informational platforms like HRI Day India. Many people struggle with digital banking because official bank guides can be scattered or hard to follow. I’d like to understand your current banking challenges to see if our resources could save you time and confusion.

### Discovery Questions
1. Which bank do you hold your primary account with (e.g., SBI, IOB, Canara, PNB, etc.)?
2. Have you ever tried to download your bank statement online or activate net banking but found the process confusing?
3. Do you currently use mobile banking, internet banking, or rely mostly on branch visits for routine tasks?
4. Are you comfortable retrieving OTPs via SMS for banking logins, or does that pose a challenge?
5. Have you needed help with specific tasks like debit card activation, password reset, or viewing mini statements via missed call?
6. Would you prefer having all banking guides in one searchable place rather than searching across multiple bank websites?
7. Is there a particular banking service you’ve struggled with recently (e.g., downloading IOB statements, checking SBI balance via SMS)?
8. Do you assist family members (like parents) with their banking, and do they find digital services difficult to navigate?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] User has a bank account with an Indian bank covered by HRI Day India (SBI, IOB, PNB, Canara, etc.)
- [ ] User needs assistance with online banking tasks (balance check, statement download, debit card activation, etc.)
- [ ] User has registered mobile number linked to their bank account (required for OTP)
- [ ] User seeks informational guidance—not direct financial aid or transaction processing
- [ ] User is located in India (pan-India availability)
- [ ] User is not seeking loans, grants, or subsidies (scheme provides no financial support)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re looking for reliable, easy-to-follow guidance to manage your [specific bank, e.g., IOB] account online without visiting branches or getting stuck in confusing processes. That’s exactly what HRIDAY – Heritage City Development and Augmentation Yojana (operated as HRI Day India) provides—free, step-by-step guides for banking services like downloading IOB statements online, activating SBI debit cards, and checking balances via missed call. Let me show you how it works.
