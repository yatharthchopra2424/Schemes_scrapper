# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
HRIDAY – Heritage City Development and Augmentation Yojana (Scheme ID: row-219) is an **informational platform** operated by **HRI Day India (Editorial Team)** under the **Infrastructure** ministry/category. It provides **pan-India** access to step-by-step banking guides with **no financial support, grants, loans, or subsidies**. The scheme functions as a **year-round rolling basis** resource with **no fixed deadlines** for access, last updated in **2026**. The application portal is https://hridayindia.in.

### Objectives
- Provide easy, accurate instructions for banking tasks  
- Help users activate debit cards and enable online transactions  
- Assist in resetting passwords and managing bank accounts without confusion  
- Offer guidance on downloading bank statements online  
- Support users in accessing net banking and mobile banking services

### Application Process
The scheme operates as a **self-service informational guide**. There is no formal application, approval, or fund disbursement process. Users access benefits by following step-by-step instructions on the portal.

> **Important Caveats**  
> - The website provides **only informational guides** and does **not offer direct banking services**  
> - Users **must have their own bank credentials and registered mobile numbers** to perform certain actions (e.g., OTP verification)  
> - Some procedures may require visiting **official bank websites** or using **bank-specific apps** (e.g., IOB mPassbook, Nanban, SBI Yono Lite)  
> - Guides are for **assistance only** and do **not replace official bank support**  
> - For app-based methods, users must **grant required permissions** (GPS, SMS, phone) during installation  
> - An **account password or MPIN** is required to open downloaded PDF statements
