# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The startup is incorporated as a Private Limited Company, registered as a Partnership Firm, Limited Liability Partnership, or Cooperative Society.
- [ ] The startup’s turnover is less than INR 200 Crores (INR 300 Crores for DeepTech startups) in any of the previous financial years.
- [ ] The startup is within 10 years from the date of incorporation (20 years for DeepTech startups).
- [ ] The startup is working towards innovation or improvement of existing products, services, and processes.
- [ ] The startup has the potential to generate employment or create wealth.
- [ ] The entity is not formed by splitting up or reconstruction of an existing business.

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate
- PAN Card
- Income Tax Returns (last three financial years)
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank Statements (last six months)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Memorandum of Association for Pvt. Ltd. / LLP Deed
- Board Resolution (if any)
- Annual Accounts of the startup for the last three financial years
- Income Tax returns for the last three financial years

### Document Quality Check
- File size: Each document must be under 5 MB.
- File name: Must match the document type (e.g., "Annual_Accounts_FY2023.pdf").
- All documents must be legible, scanned in color or black & white, and certified where required.
- Language: Documents must be in English or accompanied by a certified translation.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for DPIIT Recognition. However, a write-up about the nature of business highlighting innovation, development, or improvement of products/services/processes, or scalability in terms of employment/wealth creation is mandatory as part of the application.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Register on the Startup India portal (https://www.startupindia.gov.in/) before applying for recognition.
2. Post-login, navigate to the “Get Recognised” section to access the DPIIT recognition application.
3. Fill in all details accurately, including incorporation date, PAN, turnover, and business description.
4. Upload the required scheme-specific documents: Memorandum of Association/LLP Deed, Board Resolution (if any), Annual Accounts (last 3 years), and Income Tax Returns (last 3 years).
5. Ensure the business description clearly states innovation, scalability, and potential for employment/wealth generation.
6. Confirm that the entity is not formed by splitting or reconstructing an existing business.
7. Double-check that turnover does not exceed INR 200 Cr (or INR 300 Cr for DeepTech) in any financial year.
8. Submit the application and note the application reference number for tracking.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All application fields are complete and accurate.
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents are uploaded and legible.
- [ ] Universal documents (PAN, GST, bank statements, Udyam) are attached.
- [ ] Business description emphasizes innovation, scalability, and employment/wealth potential.
- [ ] Declarations are signed and dated.
- [ ] Application reference number is saved post-submission.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Take a screenshot of the confirmation page after submission.
- Save the application reference number displayed on the portal.
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days post-submission to check status.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Download and verify the DPIIT Recognition certificate from the Startup India portal dashboard.
- [ ] Confirm the sanction of recognition (no financial amount, but enables access to benefits).
- [ ] Inform the client about subsequent benefit applications (e.g., Section 80-IAC tax exemption, Startup India Seed Fund Scheme, Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups).
- [ ] Advise the client to utilize the recognition for self-certification compliance, IPR fast-tracking, GeM listing, and tender exemptions.
- [ ] Monitor renewal requirements: recognition is valid for 10 years (20 years for DeepTech) or until turnover exceeds INR 200 Cr (INR 300 Cr for DeepTech), whichever comes first.
