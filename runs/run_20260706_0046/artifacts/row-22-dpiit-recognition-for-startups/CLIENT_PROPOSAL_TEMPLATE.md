## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified DPIIT Recognition for Startups — a recognition scheme administered by Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is access to various funding schemes such as the Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS) offering up to INR 20 lakhs grant, Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS) with a corpus of INR 10,000 crore managed by SIDBI investing in SEBI-registered AIFs that fund startups, and Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups (CGSS) providing guarantee cover up to ₹20 crore per borrower for loans from Member Institutions.
