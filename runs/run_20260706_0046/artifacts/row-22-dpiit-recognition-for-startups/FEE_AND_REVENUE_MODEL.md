## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, document preparation, and portal registration support.
2. Success fee is contingent on securing DPIIT recognition certificate, which enables access to downstream benefits (tax exemptions, funding schemes, procurement advantages).
3. Fees are structured to align with client outcomes — payment only upon successful recognition.
4. Scheme-specific note: DPIIT recognition has high success rate based on 152,846+ recognised startups (evidence) and rolling-year-round processing; low risk due to clear eligibility criteria and government-backed process, though tax benefits (80-IAC) require separate IMB approval adding slight complexity.
