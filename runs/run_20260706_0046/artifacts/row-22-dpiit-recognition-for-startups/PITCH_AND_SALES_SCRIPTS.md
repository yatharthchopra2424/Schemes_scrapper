# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
I specialize in helping early-stage startups access government recognition and benefits under the Startup India initiative. Many founders I speak with are focused on product development but may not realize they could qualify for DPIIT recognition, which unlocks tax exemptions, faster compliance, and IPR support—without giving up equity. I’d like to understand your startup’s stage and goals to see if this is a fit for you.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your company incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, Partnership Firm, or Cooperative Society?
2. What has been your annual turnover in any of the previous financial years?
3. Are you working towards innovation, improvement, or scalability in your product, service, or process with potential for wealth or employment generation?
4. Has your entity been formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business?
5. Are you considering applying for tax benefits under Section 80-IAC or seeking faster patent examination?
6. Do you plan to participate in government tenders or list products on Government e-Marketplace (GeM)?
7. Are you looking to wind up operations easily under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code if needed?
8. Have you already applied for DPIIT recognition, or is this your first time exploring it?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Incorporated as Private Limited Company, LLP, Partnership Firm, or Cooperative Society  
- [ ] Annual turnover < INR 200 Cr (or < INR 300 Cr for DeepTech) in any previous financial year  
- [ ] Entity age ≤ 10 years from incorporation (≤ 20 years for DeepTech)  
- [ ] Not formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business  
- [ ] Working towards innovation, improvement, or scalability with potential for wealth/employment generation  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your answers, it sounds like you may meet the core criteria for DPIIT recognition under the Startup India initiative. Let me walk you through how this recognition works—and how it can unlock real benefits like 3 years of tax exemption and faster IP processing—without any upfront cost to you.
