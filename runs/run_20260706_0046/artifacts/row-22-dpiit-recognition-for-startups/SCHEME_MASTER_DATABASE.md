### DPIIT Recognition for Startups
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.startupindia.gov.in/
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Private Limited Company, Registered Partnership Firm, Limited Liability Partnership, or Cooperative Society
- Age of business: Up to 10 years from incorporation (20 years for DeepTech startups)
- Sector: Innovation-driven sectors with potential for employment or wealth generation
- Revenue cap: Turnover less than INR 200 Crores (INR 300 Crores for DeepTech startups) in any previous financial year
- Employee count: [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]
- Other: Entity must not be formed by splitting up or reconstruction of an existing business; must work towards innovation or improvement of existing products, services, and processes

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: No direct financial support; enables access to funding schemes (e.g., SISFS up to INR 20 lakhs grant, FFS with INR 10,000 crore corpus, CGSS guarantee up to ₹20 crore)
- Disbursement mode: Recognition certificate issued; benefits accessed via separate applications
- Duration of benefit: Recognition valid for 10 years (20 years for DeepTech) or until turnover exceeds threshold

#### Documents Required
1. Memorandum of Association for Pvt. Ltd. / LLP Deed
2. Board Resolution (If Any)
3. Annual Accounts of the startup for the last three financial years
4. Income Tax returns for the last three financial years

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register on the Startup India portal.
2. Post registration, apply for DPIIT (Department for Industrial Policy and Promotion) recognition.
3. Access the Section 80 IAC exemption application form here.
4. Fill in all details with the below mentioned documents uploaded and submit the application form.
5. Refer to your Dashboard on the Startup India Portal for the status of your application.
