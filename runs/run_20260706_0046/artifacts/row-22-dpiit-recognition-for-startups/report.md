# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
DPIIT Recognition for Startups is a flagship initiative under the Startup India program, launched by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry. It provides formal recognition to eligible startups, enabling them to access a wide range of benefits under the Startup India Initiative, including tax exemptions, compliance relaxations, IPR support, and public procurement advantages. The scheme operates on a rolling basis with no fixed deadline, accepting applications year-round via the Startup India portal: https://www.startupindia.gov.in/. As of the latest update in 2026, the turnover threshold for recognition was revised from ₹100 crore to ₹200 crore (₹300 crore for DeepTech startups) per DPIIT Gazette Notification 108(E) dated 4 February 2026.

### Objectives
The scheme aims to:
- Reduce the regulatory burden on startups, allowing them to focus on core business and lower compliance costs.
- Create a single point of contact for the startup ecosystem to enable knowledge exchange and access to funding.
- Promote awareness and adoption of Intellectual Property Rights (IPRs) by providing fast-track patent examination, facilitator support, and 80% rebate on patent filing fees.
- Provide an equal platform for manufacturing startups in public procurement by exempting them from prior experience/turnover requirements and Earnest Money Deposit (EMD).
- Simplify winding up of operations under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (within 90 days for simple debt structures).
- Catalyze entrepreneurship by facilitating access to credit and funding schemes.
- Promote investments by mobilizing capital gains from sale of capital assets.

### Eligibility Matrix
| Criteria | Normal Startups | DeepTech Startups |
|--------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Entity Type** | Private Limited Company, Partnership Firm, Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), or Cooperative Society | Same as normal |
| **Incorporation Age** | Up to 10 years from date of incorporation | Up to 20 years from date of incorporation |
| **Annual Turnover** | Must not exceed INR 200 Crores in any financial year since incorporation | Must not exceed INR 300 Crores in any financial year since incorporation |
| **Original Entity** | Must not be formed by splitting up or reconstruction of an existing business | Same as normal |
| **Innovation & Scalability** | Must work towards innovation/improvement of products, services, or processes; must have scalable business model with high potential for wealth and employment generation | Same as normal |
| **Tax Exemption (80-IAC) Eligibility** | Must be DPIIT-recognized; only Private Limited Company or LLP eligible; incorporated after 1st April 2016 | Same as normal |
| **Angel Tax Exemption (Sec 56)** | Must be DPIIT-recognized; aggregate paid-up share capital + share premium ≤ INR 25 Crores (excluding shares issued to non-residents or VCs); must not invest in specified assets for 7 years post-premium share issue | Same as normal |

> **Key Caveats**:
> - Startup status expires when turnover exceeds INVALID if turnover exceeds INR 200 Cr (₹300 Cr for DeepTech) OR after 10 years (20 years for DeepTech) from incorporation.
> - Tax benefits under Section 80-IAC require separate Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB) approval.
> - Recognition only makes a startup ELIGIBLE to apply for benefits; each incentive (e.g., tax exemption, credit guarantee, funding) has its own application process and qualifying criteria.
> - DPIIT does NOT charge any fee for recognition or eligibility certificates.

### Benefits & Financial Support
While DPIIT recognition itself does not provide direct financial support, it unlocks access to multiple central government schemes:

| Benefit Category | Specific Benefit | Details |
|------------------|------------------|---------|
| **Compliance & Regulatory** | Self-certification | Self-certify compliance with 6 labour laws and 3 environmental laws via online procedure; no inspections for 5 years for labour laws (only on credible complaint); random checks for environment laws under 'white category'. |
| | Faster Exit | Wind up operations within 90 days under Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 for simple debt structures. |
| **Intellectual Property (IPR)** | Fast-track Patent Examination | Startup patent applications fast-tracked for early realization of IP value. |
| | Facilitator Panel | 
| | Facilitator Support | Government bears facilitation cost; startup pays only statutory fees. |
| | Patent Filing Rebate | 80% rebate on patent filing fees compared to other companies. |
| **Tax Incentives** | Income Tax Exemption (80-IAC) | 100% tax exemption on profits for 3 consecutive financial years out of first 10 years since incorporation (requires IMB approval). |
| | Angel Tax Exemption (Sec 56) | Exemption from tax on issue of shares above fair market value; aggregate paid-up capital + share premium ≤ INR 25 Crores (excludes non-resident/VC investments). |
| **Public Procurement** | GeM Listing | Opportunity to list products on Government e-Marketplace (GeM) as sellers. |
| | Prior Experience/Turnover Exemption | Manufacturing startups exempt from prior experience/turnover criteria in government tenders (must demonstrate capability and have Indian manufacturing facility). |
| | EMD Exemption | Exempt from submitting Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) or bid security in government tenders. |
| **Funding Access** | Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS) | Up to INR 20 lakhs grant for proof of concept, prototype development, product trials, market entry, commercialization. |
| | Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS) | Corpus of INR 10,000 crore managed by SIDBI; invests in SEBI-registered Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs) that fund startups. |
| | Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups (CGSS) | Guarantee cover up to ₹20 crore per borrower for loans from Member Institutions (Scheduled Commercial Banks, NBFCs with BBB+ rating, SEBI-registered AIFs). |

> **Important Notes on Funding**:
> - SISFS: Application via Startup India portal; disbursement in tranches based on milestones.
> - FFS: Indirect investment; startups must approach SEBI-registered AIFs funded by SIDBI.
> - CGSS: Guarantee issued by National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company (NCGTC) via Member Institutions; startups apply through banks like Axis Bank, HDFC Bank, Bank of Baroda.

### Application Process
The application process for DPIIT recognition is entirely online and consists of the following stages:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Start: Visit Startup India Portal] --> B[Register on Startup India Portal]
    B --> C[Create BHASKAR Account & Complete Profile]
    C --> D[Log In and Navigate to 'Get Recognised' Section]
    D --> E[Select Entity Type: Pvt Ltd / Partnership / LLP / Cooperative Society]
    E --> F[Fill Online Application Form]
    F --> G[Upload Required Documents]
    G --> H[Submit Application for DPIIT Recognition]
    H --> I[Application Sent to National Single Window System (NSWS)]
    I --> J[DPIIT Reviews Application & Documents]
    J --> K{Approved?}
    K -->|Yes| L[Receive DPIIT Recognition Certificate via Email/DigiLocker]
    K -->|No| M[Receive Rejection Reason; Rectify & Resubmit]
    M --> F
    L --> N[Access Benefits: Apply for 80-IAC, CGSS, SISFS, etc. via respective portals]
    N --> O[Monitor Status on Startup India Dashboard]
    O --> P[Renewal Not Required; Status Valid Until Expiry Criteria Met]
```

#### Required Documents
1. Memorandum of Association (for Pvt. Ltd.) / LLP Deed  
2. Board Resolution (if any)  
3. Annual Accounts of the startup for the last three financial years  
4. Income Tax Returns for the last three financial years  

#### Process Steps (as per portal guidance)
1. Register on the Startup India portal (https://www.startupindia.gov.in/).  
2. Post registration, apply for DPIIT recognition via the "Get Recognised" option.  
3. Fill in all details and upload the required documents.  
4. Submit the application form.  
5. Refer to your Dashboard on the Startup India Portal for application status.  

#### Contact Details
- **Email**: startupindia@dipp.gov.in  
- **Helpline**: 1800-11-5565
