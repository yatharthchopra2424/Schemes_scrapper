# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Sponsoring Agency is a State Government, Central Public Sector Enterprise (CPSE), Private Developer, or joint venture thereof
- [ ] Land area requirement met: minimum 100 acres contiguous land in non-hilly states, or 25 acres in hilly states, northeast region, union territories, and states with population less than 1 crore
- [ ] Non-contiguous parcels (not exceeding 2) within 2 km radius with minimum 100 acres each may be considered
- [ ] 90% encumbrance-free land is in possession at the time of application submission
- [ ] Application is submitted by the sponsoring agency/authority in prescribed format through the designated portal
- [ ] Separate application submitted for development of each industrial park
- [ ] If State Government is sponsoring authority, application submitted after due consideration and with clear recommendation of the State Level Committee (SLC)
- [ ] If CPSE is sponsoring agency, application submitted with approval of the Board of CPSE and in compliance with applicable instructions of the Government of India
- [ ] Detailed Project Report (DPR) prepared in accordance with indicative template provided at Annexure II of the BHAVYA Scheme Guidelines
- [ ] Application submitted during the specified period following due public notice on the online portal developed and managed by Project Management Agency (PMA)

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
- PAN Card
- GST Registration Certificate
- Audited Financial Statements (last 3 years)
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) (last 3 years)
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable)
- Board Resolution authorizing application
- Land ownership documents / Lease agreement
- Environmental Clearance (if applicable)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) prepared in accordance with indicative template provided at Annexure II
- Recommendation of State Level Committee (SLC) (if State Government is sponsoring authority)
- Approval of Board of CPSE (if CPSE is sponsoring agency)
- Documents and information as may be specified in the guidelines (refer to Annexure I–VI of BHAVYA Scheme Guidelines)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible scans or certified copies
- File names must match document type and include Case ID (e.g., "CASE001_DPR.pdf")
- File size must not exceed 10 MB per document
- Documents in regional languages must be accompanied by certified English/Hindi translation
- All certificates must be valid and not expired
- DPR must follow the structure outlined in Annexure II of the BHAVYA Scheme Guidelines
- Land documents must show 90% encumbrance-free possession
- Board resolutions and SLC recommendations must be dated and signed

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Required.** The DPR must be prepared in accordance with the indicative template provided at Annexure II of the BHAVYA Scheme Guidelines. Standard sections include:
- Executive Summary
- Introduction (Project Background, Project Proponent, Project Rationale, Structure of DPR)
- Industrial Park at XXX location (Proposed site, District/Region Profile, Industrial Scenario, Promoters’ Details, Development Mode)
- Location Details and Connectivity (Location, External Connectivity-Rail/Road/Air/Port, Site Analysis, Environmental Clearance, Site features, External Infrastructure and Utilities)
- Proposed Park Master Plan (Proposed Master Plan, Planning Concept, Zoning, Access & Greens, Infrastructure Components, Land Use Distribution, Site Development & Compound Wall, Road Network, Storm Water Drainage System, Water Supply System, Sewage Collection and Treatment System, Integrated Solid Waste Management, Power Transmission & Distribution, Telecom & IT Infrastructure, Landscaping, Support Services/Common Amenities, Warehousing, Worker Housing, Administrative Setup, Engineering and Construction Management, Sustainability measures)
- Project Costing (Estimated Project cost, Cost of Land, Means of Finance, Eligible Grant)
- Financial Analysis (Appraisal Framework, Financial Assessment, Key Assumptions, Revenue Assumptions, Expenditure Assumptions, Depreciation, Operations and Maintenance Plan, Key Financial Indicators)
- Project Implementation Strategy (Project Conceptualization, Formulation, Stakeholders and Responsibilities, Execution, Allotment of Plots, Land/Shed Allotment, Implementation and Monitoring)
- Demand Assessment (Industry sectors identified, Demand validation, Manpower/human resource assessment)
- Project Impact (Private Investment Grounded, Number of Expected Units, Estimated Employment Generation)

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use only the official online portal developed and managed by Project Management Agency (PMA) – access via NICDC website
2. Submit a separate application for each industrial park (no bundling allowed)
3. Attach the DPR as a single PDF file following Annexure II template structure
4. Ensure all mandatory fields are completed – incomplete applications will not be evaluated
5. Upload land documents proving 90% encumbrance-free possession at time of submission
6. If State Government is sponsoring authority, upload clear recommendation of State Level Committee (SLC)
7. If CPSE is sponsoring agency, upload Board of CPSE approval and compliance declaration
8. Select correct sponsor type (State Government / CPSE / Private Developer / Joint Venture) in the form
9. Save draft periodically and take screenshots before final submission
10. Refer to the BHAVYA Scheme Guidelines (available at https://nicdc.in) for any clarification on eligibility or documentation
11. Do not submit until all documents are verified and complete – no amendments allowed after submission
12. Note the application reference number immediately upon successful submission

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify approval letter from NLSC (National Level Steering Committee)
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount and tranche release schedule (40:40:20 over three years)
- [ ] Verify land transfer to SPV within 3 months of approval (or request extension if bonafide reasons)
- [ ] Ensure delegation of planning and development powers to SPV by State/UT Government
- [ ] Confirm allocation of power & water by State/UT government for the industrial park
- [ ] Verify environmental clearance obtained
- [ ] Monitor commencement of work and utilization of funds as per tranche-wise milestones
- [ ] Track utilization of 75% of 1st tranche for Tranche II release
- [ ] Monitor land allotment to at least two manufacturing units (with investment commitment: ₹50 crore in non-hilly states, ₹10 crore in others)
- [ ] Verify completion of external infrastructure components for Tranche III release
- [ ] Ensure commencement of construction of at least two independent manufacturing facilities
- [ ] Obtain certification for final completion
- [ ] Submit Utilization Certificates (UCs), audit reports, and progress updates to DPIIT via PMA
- [ ] Verify O&M Corpus Fund setup (up to 5% of gross allotment premium) and its utilization rules
- [ ] Confirm that NICDIT’s equity participation does not exceed 50% of paid-up equity capital
- [ ] For private developer-led projects, note lock-in period of 5 years before equity transfer/sale permitted
- [ ] Prepare and submit invoice for success fee as per engagement letter
