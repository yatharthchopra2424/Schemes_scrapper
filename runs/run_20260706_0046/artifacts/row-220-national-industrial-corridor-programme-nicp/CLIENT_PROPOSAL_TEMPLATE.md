## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified National Industrial Corridor Programme (NICP) — a Central Sector Scheme administered by Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce & Industry — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is financial assistance under the BHAVYA Scheme provided by the Central Government through NICDIT in the form of equity, in tranches linked to achievement of predefined milestones.
