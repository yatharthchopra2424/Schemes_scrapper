## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the scale of industrial park development under NICP, where financial assistance is linked to land equity and infrastructure milestones.
2. Tiered success fees reflect decreasing percentage as project size increases, consistent with NICP’s funding model (up to ₹1 crore per acre equity support).
3. Retainer fees cover DPR preparation, eligibility assessment, and portal application support—critical for NICP’s 90% encumbrance-free land requirement.
4. Scheme-specific note: NICP involves long gestation periods (24–36 months for development, funds released in tranches), resulting in moderate risk due to milestone-dependent disbursement and land transfer timelines; success fees are contingent on achieving predefined milestones verified by NLSC.
