# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping state governments, CPSEs, private developers, and joint ventures access central government funding for industrial park development under the National Industrial Corridor Programme. I understand you’re exploring opportunities to develop plug-and-play industrial infrastructure with 24/7 power, water, and multi-modal connectivity—exactly what the National Industrial Corridor Programme (NICP) delivers. I’d like to ask a few quick questions to see if this aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of entity are you representing—State Government, CPSE, private developer, or a joint venture?
2. Do you currently have possession of at least 90% encumbrance-free land for the proposed industrial park?
3. What is the contiguous land area available for development—in non-hilly states, is it at least 100 acres? In hilly states, NE region, UTs, or states with population <1 crore, is it at least 25 acres?
4. Are you considering a greenfield or brownfield site, and if brownfield, is there unencumbered, litigation-free contiguous land of at least 100 acres?
5. Is the site within 2 km of up to two non-contiguous parcels, each minimum 100 acres, for potential aggregation?
6. Are you targeting development of an industrial park between 500–1,000 acres (with funding capped at 1,000 acres for larger parks)?
7. Have you initiated discussions with the State Level Committee (SLC) or CPSE Board for application endorsement?
8. Do you have a Detailed Project Report (DPR) prepared or planned per the indicative template in Annexure II of the BHAVYA Scheme guidelines?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Sponsoring agency is State Government, CPSE, Private Developer, or joint venture thereof  
- [ ] Minimum 90% encumbrance-free land in possession at time of application  
- [ ] Land area: ≥100 acres contiguous (non-hilly states) OR ≥25 acres contiguous (hilly states, NE, UTs, population <1 crore)  
- [ ] Non-contiguous parcels: ≤2 parcels within 2 km radius, each ≥100 acres  
- [ ] For larger parks: development area between 500–1,000 acres (funding capped at 1,000 acres)  
- [ ] Up to 20 industrial parks may be approved in 500–1,000 acre range  
- [ ] Application to be submitted via designated online portal with DPR per Annexure II  
- [ ] State Government applications require SLC recommendation; CPSE applications require Board approval  
- [ ] Project targets plug-and-play infrastructure: 24-hr power, potable/recycled water, ETP, multi-modal connectivity, gas pipelines, ICT, single-window clearance  
- [ ] Only green/clean industries permitted—no red/pollutive activities  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your project aligns well with the National Industrial Corridor Programme (NICP) under the BHAVYA Scheme, which provides central government equity funding of up to ₹1 crore per acre (or ₹50 lakh per acre in private developer-led projects) for eligible industrial parks. Shall I walk you through how this works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
State governments, CPSEs, and private developers face high upfront capital costs and lengthy timelines to develop industrial parks with plug-and-play infrastructure—especially for 24/7 power, treated water, effluent treatment, multi-modal connectivity, and single-window clearance. Without central support, many projects stall due to funding gaps, deterring investment and job creation in manufacturing hubs.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Industrial Corridor Programme (NICP), implemented by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) through the National Industrial Corridor Development and Investment Trust (NICDIT), provides financial assistance under the BHAVYA Scheme as central government equity in tranches linked to milestone achievement. The equity participation of NICDIT in any SPV shall not exceed 50% of paid-up equity capital. Funding is up to ₹1 crore per acre for government-led projects and ₹50 lakh per acre for private developer-led projects, capped at 1,000 acres per park. The scheme targets development of 100 industrial parks across India with a total outlay of ₹33,660 crore.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You represent a State Government, CPSE, private developer, or joint venture—eligible sponsoring agency under BHAVYA  
- You have or can secure ≥90% encumbrance-free land possession at application time  
- Your land parcel meets the minimum area criteria: ≥100 acres contiguous (non-hilly) or ≥25 acres (hilly/NE/UT/population <1 crore)  
- You are prepared to submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR) per Annexure II via the designated portal
