### National Industrial Corridor Programme (NICP)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Infrastructure
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce & Industry
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nicdc.in
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Rolling

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: State Governments, Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs), Private Developers, or joint ventures thereof
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Industries, investors, manufacturing units, MSMEs, state governments
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Sponsoring agency must ensure 90% encumbrance-free land is in possession at time of application; land area requirements: minimum 100 acres contiguous land in non-hilly states, 25 acres in hilly states, northeast region, union territories, and states with population less than 1 crore; non-contiguous parcels (not exceeding 2) within 2 km radius with minimum 100 acres each may be considered; up to 20 industrial parks may have development area between 500 to 1000 acres, with funding capped at 1000 acres for larger parks developed in phases

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance under the BHAVYA Scheme is provided by the Central Government through NICDIT in the form of equity, in tranches linked to achievement of predefined milestones; the equity participation of NICDIT in any SPV shall not exceed 50% of the paid-up equity capital; for SPVs formed between NICDIT and the State Government/State Nodal Agency (without private participation), the shareholding pattern shall not be altered; in cases where private developer participates as an anchor investor, they may be permitted to allot to themselves up to 25% of the developed land within the industrial park, with the remaining 75% allotted to other industries/units transparently and non-discriminatorily; the SPV shall be permitted to set aside up to 5% of the gross allotment premium into a dedicated O&M Corpus Fund for operational deficit during the first five years post-completion
- Disbursement mode: Equity
- Duration of benefit: Tranches linked to achievement of predefined milestones (typically over 3 years)

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR) prepared in accordance with indicative template provided at Annexure II
2. Documents and information as may be specified in the guidelines

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Application shall be submitted by the sponsoring agency/authority in prescribed format through the designated portal.
2. A separate application shall be submitted for development of each industrial park.
3. Each application shall be accompanied by a Detailed Project Report (DPR) prepared in accordance with the indicative template provided at Annexure II.
4. In case the State Government is the sponsoring authority, the application shall be submitted after due consideration and with clear recommendation of the State Level Committee (SLC).
5. In case a Central Public Sector Enterprise (CPSE) is the sponsoring agency, the application shall be submitted with the approval of the Board of CPSE and in compliance with applicable instructions of the Government of India.
6. Applications under this scheme will be received and processed through an online portal developed and managed by Project Management Agency (PMA).
7. The portal shall be opened for submission of applications for a specified period following due public notice.
8. Applications meeting the mandatory eligibility criteria will be taken up for evaluation and scoring in accordance with the prescribed evaluation criteria.
9. The highest-ranking applications scoring above the benchmark threshold shall be shortlisted and their DPR shall be examined and evaluated for further consideration and approval.
10. An appraisal report in respect of shortlisted applications will be prepared by PMA and presented before the NLSC for approval.
