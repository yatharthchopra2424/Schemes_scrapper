# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
**Scheme Name:** National Industrial Corridor Programme (NICP)  
**Scheme ID:** row-220  
**Ministry / Category:** Infrastructure  
**Scheme Type:** other  
**Geographic Scope:** Pan-India  
**Implementing Agency:** Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce & Industry  
**Last Updated:** 2025  
**Fund Size (Cr):** 33660  
**Application Portal:** https://nicdc.in (designated portal managed by Project Management Agency)  
**Key Sources:** NICDC website, BHAVYA Scheme Guidelines (DPIIT Notification dated 10th April, 2026), Delivery Monitoring Unit Report (31.05.2026)

### Objectives
- Develop futuristic industrial cities in India to compete with global manufacturing and investment destinations
- Create employment opportunities and economic growth leading to socio-economic development
- Develop industrial corridors with modern infrastructure and plug-and-play facilities
- Attract domestic and global investments in manufacturing and services
- Boost logistics and connectivity to make trade faster and cost-efficient
- Support employment generation and economic growth
- Develop state-of-the-art world-class infrastructure to promote local commerce and enhance investment
- Integrate next-generation technologies across infrastructure sectors to create smart cities

**Application Process Steps (from Evidence):**
1. Application submitted by sponsoring agency/authority in prescribed format through designated portal
2. Separate application for each industrial park
3. Application accompanied by Detailed Project Report (DPR) per Annexure II template
4. If State Government is sponsoring authority: application submitted after due consideration and clear recommendation of State Level Committee (SLC)
5. If CPSE is sponsoring agency: application submitted with Board approval and compliance with GoI instructions
6. Applications received and processed via online portal developed/managed by Project Management Agency (PMA)
7. Portal opened for submission following due public notice for specified period
8. Applications meeting mandatory eligibility criteria taken up for evaluation/scoring per prescribed criteria
9. Highest-ranking applications scoring above benchmark threshold shortlisted; DPR examined/evaluated for further consideration
10. Appraisal report prepared by PMA presented before NLSC for approval
11. Funds released in three tranches (40:40:20 ratio) over three years subject to milestone achievement
    - Tranche I (30%): NLSC approval, 90% encumbrance-free land transfer to SPV, delegation of powers, power/water allocation, environmental clearance, commencement of work (10%)
    - Tranche II (40%): Utilization of 75% of 1st tranche, proportionate physical progress, land allotment to ≥2 manufacturing units (₹50 crore investment in non-hilly states, ₹10 crore in others)
    - Tranche III (20%): Utilization of 90% of 1st & 2nd tranche, proportionate physical progress, completion of external infrastructure, commencement of construction of ≥2 independent manufacturing facilities (10%), certification for final completion (10%)
12. SPV responsible for operation, maintenance, management of assets; Central Government fund assets not to be disposed/encumbered/utilized for other purposes
13. In case of SPV liquidation, DPIIT has first right to recover equity contribution released for project

### Key Caveats & Conditions
> **Equity Limitation:** NICDIT equity participation in any SPV shall not exceed 50% of paid-up equity capital  
> **Private Developer Exit:** Transfer/sale/disinvestment of equity share permissible only after 5 years from project completion and operationalization  
> **O&M Corpus:** Drawdown requires SPV Board approval (including NICDIT and State Nodal Agency nominee directors); surplus after 5 years transferred to general reserves  
> **Land Treatment:** Land transferred to SPV treated as equity contribution in all cases  
> **Land Transfer Timeline:** Ownership must transfer to SPV within 3 months of project approval; delay beyond this period deemed annulled (with possible 3-month extension for bonafide reasons by NLSC)  
> **Funding Cap:** Maximum ₹1 crore per acre for most projects; ₹50 lakh per acre or 50% of infrastructure cost (whichever lower) for private developer collaborations  
> **Ineligible Components:** Land acquisition/development cost, commissioning fees, royalty, preliminary/pre-operative expenses, interest capitalized, transportation equipment/vehicles, working capital  
> **External Infrastructure:** Up to 25% of total funding may be allocated; limited to 25% of external infrastructure cost (State/UT Government funds balance 75%)  
> **Convergence:** SPV must leverage benefits from other Central/State Government schemes for non-funded/partially funded components; no double financing permitted  
> **Dispute Resolution:** For Private Developer-led SPV deadlocks (>30 days), escalation to State Level Committee (SLC) for conciliation within 30 days  
> **Final Approval:** NLSC decision on grant/rejection of project proposals is final and binding

- **NICDC Website:** Confirms implementing agency, portal details, project pipeline (4 developed, 4 nearing completion, 12 new projects), and BHAVYA guideline releases
- **BHAVYA Scheme Guidelines (DPIIT):** Provides exhaustive details on eligibility, funding structure, tranche release conditions, SPV governance, land valuation, and O&M corpus
- **Delivery Monitoring Unit Report (31.05.2026):** Tracks progress of industrial corridor projects including land transfers, equity releases, and infrastructure completion status across DMIC, AKIC, CBIC, and other corridors
