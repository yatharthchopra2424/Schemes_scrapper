## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Business entity must be registered under the Companies Act, 2013 or as a partnership firm/LLP under relevant Indian statutes  
- [ ] Entity must be engaged in manufacturing, logistics, warehousing, or other industrial activities aligned with DMIC’s industrial node development  
- [ ] Project must be located within the designated influence zone of the Delhi Mumbai Industrial Corridor (spanning Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, and Maharashtra)  
- [ ] Minimum project investment threshold: INR 10 crore (as per DMICDC guidelines for industrial plots and infrastructure support)  
- [ ] Entity must have a clear land title or lease agreement for the proposed project site within DMIC influence zone  
- [ ] Business must be operational or have a detailed project report (DPR) for greenfield projects  
- [ ] No prior default on loans from financial institutions or government bodies related to industrial projects  
- [ ] Compliance with environmental and safety norms as per MoEFCC and factory act requirements  

> *Note: Eligibility criteria are inferred from DMICDC’s industrial promotion framework due to limited scheme-specific details in evidence. Verify exact thresholds with nodal officer.*

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Partnership Deed / LLP Agreement  
- PAN Card of the business entity  
- GST Registration Certificate  
- Udyam Registration (MSME) certificate, if applicable  
- Audited Financial Statements (Balance Sheet, P&L) for last 2 years  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the business for last 2 years  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- KYC documents of authorized signatory (Aadhar, PAN, photograph)  
- Board Resolution authorizing application (for companies/LLPs)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining project scope, investment, employment generation, and timeline  
- Land ownership documents or lease agreement (minimum 30 years) for project site within DMIC influence zone  
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) from State Industrial Development Corporation or concerned state agency  
- Environmental Clearance (if applicable) or application receipt from MoEFCC/SEIAA  
- Approved building plan/layout plan from local planning authority  
- Undertaking on job creation (direct and indirect employment)  
- Certificate of no default from bank/financial institution  
- Sector-specific licenses (e.g., factory license under Factories Act, 1948, if manufacturing)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (minimum 300 DPI)  
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ClientName_Date.pdf` (e.g., `DMIC_DPR_ABC Ltd_20240515.pdf`)  
- Scanned copies must be certified true copy by a gazetted officer or CA/CS (where required)  
- Documents in regional languages must be accompanied by notarized English/Hindi translation  
- Financial documents must be audited and signed by a Chartered Accountant  
- No password protection or encryption on submitted files  
- Total upload size must not exceed portal limit (verify on portal; typically 25 MB per file)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
✅ **Required** – A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is mandatory for DMIC scheme applications.  
**Standard Sections to Include:**  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Promoters’ Background and Experience  
- Project Location (with map and coordinates within DMIC influence zone)  
- Land Details (area, ownership, lease terms, zoning compliance)  
- Project Description (industrial activity, technology, scale)  
- Investment Breakdown (land, building, plant & machinery, working capital)  
- Implementation Schedule (Gantt chart)  
- Employment Generation Plan (direct/indirect, skilled/unskilled)  
- Environmental Impact Assessment Summary  
- Financial Projections (5-year P&L, cash flow, IRR, NPV)  
- Risk Analysis and Mitigation Plan  
- Annexures: Land documents, approvals, promoter KYC, financial statements  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal via https://dmicdc.com (if SSL issues persist, contact nodal officer for alternate link or offline submission process)  
2. Select “Industrial Plot Allocation” or “Industrial Support Scheme” under incentives/schemes section  
3. Fill all fields in block letters; avoid abbreviations unless officially recognized  
4. Ensure project cost matches the figures in the audited DPR and bank loan sanction letter  
5. Enter correct DMIC node or influence zone district (e.g., Dadri, Greater Noida, Vadodara, etc.)  
6. Upload documents in prescribed format and size; verify each upload opens correctly  
7. Declaration must be signed digitally or physically scanned (if e-sign not supported)  
8. Preview the entire form before final submission; no edits allowed after submission
