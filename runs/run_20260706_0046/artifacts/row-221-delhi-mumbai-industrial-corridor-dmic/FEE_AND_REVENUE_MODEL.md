## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the scale of infrastructure benefits delivered under the Delhi Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC) scheme.
2. Transparent fee structures with clear milestones tied to project approval and fund disbursement stages.
3. Risk-adjusted success fees reflecting the long gestation period and multi-agency coordination typical of large infrastructure projects.
4. Custom retainer models for Enterprise-tier clients to accommodate phased engagement over extended project lifecycles.

*Note: The DMIC scheme involves large-scale infrastructure development with approval timelines often exceeding 12–18 months due to land acquisition, environmental clearances, and inter-state coordination. Success is evidenced by project sanction and fund allocation, though actual disbursement may lag, requiring careful revenue recognition planning.*
