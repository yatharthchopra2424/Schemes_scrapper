## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Discovery Questions
- Is your business engaged in manufacturing, logistics, warehousing, or industrial real estate development?
- Are you planning or currently executing a project within the Delhi Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC) region, spanning states like Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, or Maharashtra?

- Does your project align with DMIC’s focus sectors such as automotive, textiles, electronics, food processing, or pharmaceuticals?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Businesses looking to set up or expand industrial projects in India often face delays due to fragmented approvals, inadequate infrastructure (power, water, rail), and lack of coordination between central and state agencies—especially in high-potential but underdeveloped corridors. This increases project timelines, costs, and risks, particularly for manufacturing and logistics ventures aiming to serve domestic and export markets.

**Scheme intro:**
The Delhi Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC) is a flagship infrastructure initiative led by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India. It aims to develop smart industrial cities and integrate infrastructure across a 1,500 km stretch connecting Delhi and Mumbai. While not a direct financial grant scheme, DMIC provides facilitation services, infrastructure development (including dedicated freight corridors, power, water, and logistics parks), and single-window clearances to eligible industrial projects within its influence zone.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your project is engaged in manufacturing/logistics/industrial development — a core focus sector of DMIC  
- Your planned investment is located within the DMIC influence zone (spanning UP, Haryana, Rajasthan, MP, Gujarat, Maharashtra)  
- You require infrastructure support (land, power, water, rail connectivity) — which DMIC develops through integrated planning  
- You seek regulatory coordination and approvals — addressed via DMIC’s state-level SPVs and single-window mechanisms
