### Delhi Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Delhi Mumbai Industrial Corridor Development Corporation (DMICDC)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dmicdc.com
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Industrial units, manufacturing enterprises, logistics and warehousing facilities
- Age of business: Not specified in evidence
- Sector: Manufacturing, logistics, warehousing, and allied industries aligned with DMIC objectives
- Revenue cap: Not specified in evidence
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: Projects must be located within the Delhi Mumbai Industrial Corridor influence zone; must align with national industrial corridor development goals

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Land allocation, infrastructure support, fiscal incentives (specific amounts not detailed in evidence)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit / Infrastructure provision
- Duration of benefit: Long-term (project-linked)

#### Documents Required
1. Project proposal detailing scope, investment, and employment potential
2. Land acquisition or lease documents for the proposed site within DMIC zone
3. Environmental clearance certificates (if applicable)
4. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from relevant state authorities
5. Financial statements and bank appraisal reports
6. Proof of compliance with industrial safety and labor norms
7. Implementation timeline and milestones chart
8. Details of technology to be deployed and sustainability measures

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Submit preliminary project concept note to DMICDC via the official portal
2. Undergo initial screening for alignment with DMIC vision and sectoral priorities
3. Submit detailed project report (DPR) including technical, financial, and economic analysis
4. Present project before the Empowered Committee for evaluation
5. Receive in-principle approval upon satisfactory review
6. Complete land allotment process through DMICDC or state nodal agency
7. Fulfill statutory clearances and begin project implementation
8. Submit periodic progress reports for monitoring and continued support
