# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Project is a PPP structure sponsored by Central Government Ministries, Central Public Sector Undertakings (CPSUs), statutory authorities, or other entities under their administrative control.
- [ ] Project is in an infrastructure sector including social sectors (health, education, water supply, wastewater treatment, solid waste management) or economic sectors.
- [ ] For social sector projects under Sub Scheme-1: at least 100% Operational Cost recovery required.
- [ ] For social sector projects under Sub Scheme-2 (demonstration/pilot): at least 50% Operational Cost recovery required.
- [ ] Project must undergo appraisal by the Public Private Partnership Appraisal Committee (PPPAC).
- [ ] Project must be financially unviable but socially/economically desirable.
- [ ] Project is not under the purview of Ministry of Defence, Department of Atomic Energy, or Department of Space.
- [ ] Project cost documentation and financing arrangements are available for verification.

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation certificate
- PAN card
- ITR (last 3 years)
- GST registration certificate
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam registration (if applicable)
- Audited financial statements

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Pre-feasibility/feasibility report
- Term-sheet containing salient features of proposed project agreements
- Six copies (hard and soft) of the proposal
- All draft project agreements
- Project Report
- Concession agreement and supporting agreements/documents
- PPPAC Memo
- Proof of Operational Cost recovery (for social sector projects: at least 100% for Sub Scheme-1, 50% for Sub Scheme-2)
- Documentation of project cost and Capex
- Details of financing arrangements (equity, debt, etc.)
- Revenue streams and underlying assumptions
- Net Present Value (NPV) of revenue streams with 12% discounting
- Details of tariff/user charge fixation

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible and in PDF format (scanned copies acceptable if clear)
- [ ] File names match document descriptions (e.g., "Feasibility_Report.pdf")
- [ ] File size per document < 10 MB; total package < 50 MB
- [ ] All certificates (PAN, GST, Udyam) show valid and current status
- [ ] Bank statements show consistent transaction history
- [ ] Declarations and undertakings are signed and dated
- [ ] Documents are in English or Hindi; certified translation provided if in another language
- [ ] Six copies (hard and soft) of the proposal are prepared as required

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a detailed Project Report is required as part of the scheme-specific documents. Standard sections should include: project description, justification, alternatives, capital cost breakdown, phasing, implementation schedule, financing arrangements, revenue streams, assumptions, NPV at 12% discount rate, tariff fixation methodology, and risk mitigation measures.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use only the official format specified in Annex-II (for 'in principle' approval) or Annex-III (for final approval) from the PPPAC guidelines.
2. Fill all fields in S.No. 1 to 9 exactly as per the memorandum structure; leave no blanks.
3. Ensure consistency between the term-sheet (Appendix-A) and the main application form.
4. Attach six copies (hard and soft) of the proposal as mandated.
5. Include the pre-feasibility/feasibility report and term-sheet with the 'in principle' submission.
6. For final approval, submit concession agreement, supporting documents, PPPAC Memo, and Project Report.
7. Verify that all financial calculations (NPV, IRR, Capex) use a 12% discount rate as per guidelines.
8. Confirm that the sponsoring authority is a Central Government Ministry, CPSU, statutory authority, or entity under their administrative control.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All fields in the application form (Annex-II or Annex-III) are completed.
- [ ] Six copies (hard and soft) of the proposal are ready.
- [ ] Pre-feasibility/feasibility report and term-sheet are attached.
- [ ] All draft project agreements are included.
- [ ] Project Report is complete with NPV at 12% discount rate.
- [ ] Proof of Operational Cost recovery is attached (if social sector).
- [ ] Declarations and undertakings are signed by authorized signatory.
- [ ] File names and formats meet quality check standards.
- [ ] Reference to portal: https://dea.gov.in/schemes-services/viability-gap-funding-scheme is noted.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit six copies (hard and soft) of the proposal to the PPPAC Secretariat as per guidelines.
- Obtain and screenshot the submission acknowledgment/receipt.
- Store the reference number securely in the case file.
- Set follow-up reminders for Day 21 (to check on circulation) and Day 30 (for PPPAC meeting outcome).

### Follow-Up Script (to nodal officer or portal helpdesk)

### Escalation Path
- Level 1: Portal helpdesk – Contact via the DEA website contact section or email: secy-dea@nic.in
- Level 2: Nodal officer – Secretary, Department of Economic Affairs (DEA) (Chairman of PPPAC) – contact via secy-dea@nic.in or phone: 011-23092254 (as per induction material)
- Level 3: RTI filing if no response within statutory SLA (typically 30 days for government responses)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from the competent authority (same as for PIB approval).
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned VGF amount (up to 40% of Capex for economic; up to 80% Capex + 50% Opex for 5 years for social sector).
- [ ] Utilization certificate to be submitted post-award based on project milestones and verification.
- [ ] Track disbursement claims against milestones as per the post-award process.
- [ ] Ensure compliance with scheme guidelines for VGF fund usage.
- [ ] Maintain records for audit and potential RTI queries.
- [ ] Confirm that post-revamp (post-2020) provisions are applied if applicable.
- [ ] Verify that State Government/Sponsoring Central Ministry/Statutory Entity support (if claimed) is committed separately.
