## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified Viability Gap Funding (VGF) for PPP Projects — a grant scheme administered by the Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance, Government of India — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is up to 40% of Capex for economic sector projects and up to 80% of Capex plus 50% of Opex for 5 years after CoD for social sector projects.

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment and scheme suitability analysis  
2. Documentation review and gap analysis against scheme requirements  
3. Preparation and compilation of all required documents as per scheme checklist  
4. Application submission via the Public Private Partnership Appraisal Committee (PPPAC) Secretariat  
5. Coordination with PPPAC for 'in principle' and final approval stages  
6. Liaison with Department of Economic Affairs for query resolution and status updates  
7. Portal registration and application tracking on [https://dea.gov.in/schemes-services/viability-gap-funding-scheme](https://dea.gov.in/schemes-services/viability-gap-funding-scheme)
