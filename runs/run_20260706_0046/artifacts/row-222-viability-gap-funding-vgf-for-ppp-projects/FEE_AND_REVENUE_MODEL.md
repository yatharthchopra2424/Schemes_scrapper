## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the financial benefit delivered — VGF grants can cover up to 80% of Capex for social sector projects and up to 40% for economic sector projects, with total approved VGF reaching ₹7,682 crores across 67 projects.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile — while the VGF scheme has a high success rate (67 projects approved with ₹4,847 Cr disbursed), the multi-stage appraisal process (PPPAC review, Planning Commission appraisal, competent authority approval) and milestone-based disbursement introduce a 3–6 month revenue lag.
3. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover initial project structuring and documentation (feasibility reports, term-sheets, draft agreements), while success fees are contingent on VGF approval and disbursement.
4. **Scalability & Access**: The Enterprise tier is tailored for VGF’s typical clients — central ministries, CPSUs, statutory authorities, and large private sponsors — given the scheme’s focus on high-value infrastructure projects (average project cost ~₹683 Cr based on ₹45,802 Cr total cost for 67 projects).
