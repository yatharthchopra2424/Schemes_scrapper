#### Risks & Blockers
- VGF grant is only available for PPP projects that are financially unviable but socially/economically desirable
- Social sector projects under Sub Scheme-1 require at least 100% Operational Cost recovery; Sub Scheme-2 requires at least 50% Operational Cost recovery
- The Central Government's VGF share is subject to the total outlay of Rs. 8,100 crores till 2024-25
