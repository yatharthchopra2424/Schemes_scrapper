# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping infrastructure project sponsors access government funding for PPP projects that are socially or economically desirable but face viability challenges. The Viability Gap Funding (VGF) scheme is designed to bridge the financial gap in such projects, particularly in sectors like health, education, water supply, wastewater treatment, and solid waste management. I’d like to understand your project’s current stage and funding structure to see if VGF could support your initiative.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is the name and location of your PPP project, and which ministry or entity is sponsoring it?
2. Is your project in an economic sector (e.g., transport, ports) or a social sector (e.g., health, education, water, wastewater, solid waste)?
3. What is the estimated total project cost and capital expenditure (Capex)?
4. What is the expected Operational Cost recovery ratio (revenue vs. operating expenses) post-Commercial Operation Date (CoD)?
5. Has the project undergone appraisal by the Public Private Partnership Appraisal Committee (PPPAC) or received 'in principle' approval?
6. Are you seeking central government financial support, and are you open to additional support from state governments or sponsoring central ministries?
7. What stage is the project at — feasibility, documentation, bidding, or post-award?
8. Do you have the core documents ready, such as the feasibility report, term-sheet, draft concession agreement, and project report?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Project sponsored by Central Government Ministries, CPSUs, statutory authorities, or entities under their administrative control  
- [ ] Project is in an infrastructure sector (including social sectors: health, education, water supply, wastewater treatment, solid waste management)  
- [ ] For social sector projects under Sub Scheme-1: at least 100% Operational Cost recovery  
- [ ] For social sector projects under Sub Scheme-2: at least 50% Operational Cost recovery  
- [ ] Project follows a PPP structure and is undergoing or has undergone PPPAC appraisal  
- [ ] Project is financially unviable but socially/economically desirable  
- [ ] Not under Ministry of Defence, Department of Atomic Energy, or Department of Space  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, your project appears to align with the Viability Gap Funding (VGF) scheme, which offers up to 80% of Capex for social sector projects and up to 40% for economic sector projects. Let me walk you through how this works and what it could mean for your project’s financial structure.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Infrastructure projects, especially in social sectors, often face long gestation periods and limited financial returns, making them commercially unviable despite their high social or economic value. This deters private investment and stalls critical development in areas like healthcare, education, water, and sanitation.

**Scheme intro:**
The Viability Gap Funding (VGF) Scheme, administered by the Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance, Government of India, provides financial assistance in the form of grants to financially unviable but socially/economically desirable PPP projects. Launched in its revamped form in 2020 with a total outlay of Rs. 8,100 crores till 2024-25, the scheme offers:
- Up to 40% of Capex as VGF grant for economic sector projects  
- Up to 80% of Capex and up to 50% of Opex for 5 years after CoD for social sector projects  
- Sub Scheme-1 (social sectors): Central Government provides up to 30% of Capex, with potential additional 30% from State Government/Sponsoring Central Ministry/Statutory Entity  
- Sub Scheme-2 (demonstration/pilot social sectors): Central Government provides up to 40% of Capex and up to 25% of Opex for first five years, with potential additional 40% Capex and 25% Opex from state or central entities  

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your project is sponsored by a Central Government entity or CPSU, meeting the sponsoring authority requirement  
- It falls within an eligible infrastructure sector (e.g., health, water, etc.)  
- It targets at least [50%/100%] Operational Cost recovery, aligning with Sub Scheme-1 or Sub Scheme-2 criteria  
- It follows a PPP structure and is set for or has undergone PPPAC appraisal
