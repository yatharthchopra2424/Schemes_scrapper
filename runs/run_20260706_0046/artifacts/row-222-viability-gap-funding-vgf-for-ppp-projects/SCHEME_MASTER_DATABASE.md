### Viability Gap Funding (VGF) for PPP Projects
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dea.gov.in/schemes-services/viability-gap-funding-scheme
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: PPP projects sponsored by Central Government Ministries, Central Public Sector Undertakings (CPSUs), statutory authorities, or other entities under their administrative control
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Infrastructure sectors including social sectors like health, education, water supply, wastewater treatment, and solid waste management
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Project must be a PPP structure and undergo appraisal by the Public Private Partnership Appraisal Committee (PPPAC); Social sector projects under Sub Scheme-1 must have at least 100% Operational Cost recovery; Sub Scheme-2 projects (demonstration/pilot) must have at least 50% Operational Cost recovery

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: For economic sector projects: up to 40% of Capex as VGF grant; For social sector projects: up to 80% of Capex and up to 50% of Opex for 5 years after Commercial Operation Date (CoD); Sub Scheme-1 (social sectors): Central Government provides up to 30% of Capex, with State Government/Sponsoring Central Ministry/Statutory Entity providing additional support up to 30% of Capex; Sub Scheme-2 (demonstration/pilot social sectors): Central Government provides up to 40% of Capex and up to 25% of Opex for first five years of commercial operations, with State Government/Sponsoring Central Ministry/Statutory Entity providing additional support up to 40% of Capex and up to 25% of Opex
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (based on project milestones and verification)
- Duration of benefit: One-time grant for Capex; Opex support for 5 years after CoD for social sector projects

#### Documents Required
1. Pre-feasibility/feasibility report
2. Term-sheet containing salient features of proposed project agreements
3. Six copies (hard and soft) of the proposal
4. All draft project agreements
5. Project Report
6. Concession agreement and supporting agreements/documents
7. PPPAC Memo
8. Proof of Operational Cost recovery (for social sector projects: at least 100% for Sub Scheme-1, 50% for Sub Scheme-2)
9. Documentation of project cost and Capex
10. Details of financing arrangements (equity, debt, etc.)
11. Revenue streams and underlying assumptions
12. Net Present Value (NPV) of revenue streams with 12% discounting
13. Details of tariff/user charge fixation

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Project Sponsoring Authority (PSA) identifies the PPP project and prepares feasibility studies, project agreements, etc. with expert assistance.
2. PSA submits proposal to the Public Private Partnership Appraisal Committee (PPPAC) Secretariat in the specified format (six copies, hard and soft) along with pre-feasibility/feasibility report and term-sheet of proposed project agreements.
3. PPPAC Secretariat circulates documents to all members and convenes a meeting within three weeks to consider the proposal for 'in principle' approval.
4. Planning Commission appraises the project and forwards its Appraisal Note to PPPAC Secretariat; other involved ministries/departments forward written comments within stipulated time.
5. PPPAC Secretariat forwards all comments to PSA for written response.
6. PSA submits concession agreement, supporting agreements/documents, PPPAC Memo, and project report for consideration by PPPAC.
7. PPPAC evaluates the Appraisal Note, comments, and PSA's response, and either recommends approval (with or without modifications) or requests necessary changes for further consideration.
8. Once cleared by PPPAC, the project is put up to the competent authority (same as for PIB approval) for final approval.
9. After final approval, financial bids are invited.
10. Post-award, claims for VGF disbursement are submitted based on project milestones and verification.
