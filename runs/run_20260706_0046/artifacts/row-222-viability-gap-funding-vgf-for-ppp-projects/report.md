# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The Viability Gap Funding (VGF) Scheme is a flagship initiative of the Government of India, administered by the Department of Economic Affairs (DEA), Ministry of Finance. It provides financial assistance to financially unviable but socially/economically desirable Public Private Partnership (PPP) projects to attract private investment in infrastructure, particularly in social sectors like health, education, water supply, wastewater treatment, and solid waste management. The scheme was revamped in 2020 with a total outlay of Rs. 8,100 crores till 2024-25.

### Objectives
- Promote PPPs to bring private efficiencies and attract private capital in infrastructure development
- Provide financial assistance to financially unviable but socially/economically desirable PPP projects
- Attract more PPP projects and facilitate private investment in social sectors (Health, Education, Wastewater, Solid Waste Management, Water Supply etc.)
- Support infrastructure development in sectors with long gestation periods and limited financial returns
- Increase the quality and quantity of bankable PPP projects through financial viability enhancement

### Eligibility Matrix
| Eligibility Criteria | Details |
|----------------------|---------|
| **Project Sponsor** | PPP projects must be sponsored by Central Government Ministries, Central Public Sector Undertakings (CPSUs), statutory authorities, or other entities under their administrative control |
| **Project Sectors** | Infrastructure sectors including social sectors: health, education, water supply, wastewater treatment, solid waste management |
| **PPP Structure** | Project must be a PPP structure and undergo appraisal by the Public Private Partnership Appraisal Committee (PPPAC) |
| **Operational Cost Recovery (Social Sector)** | Sub Scheme-1: At least 100% Operational Cost recovery<br>Sub Scheme-2 (demonstration/pilot): At least 50% Operational Cost recovery |
| **Geographic Scope** | Pan-India |
| **Exclusions** | Projects under Ministry of Defence, Department of Atomic Energy, and Department of Space (per general PPP guidelines) |

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Project Type | Central Government VGF Share | Additional Support Possible | Opex Support | Notes |
|--------------|------------------------------|-----------------------------|--------------|-------|
| **Economic Sector Projects** | Up to 40% of Capex | State/Central entities may provide additional support | Not covered under VGF | Standard provision under revamped scheme |
| **Social Sector Projects (Sub Scheme-1)** | Up to 30% of Capex | State Government/Sponsoring Central Ministry/Statutory Entity may provide up to 30% of Capex | Not covered under VGF | Requires ≥100% Operational Cost recovery |
| **Social Sector Projects (Sub Scheme-2)** | Up to 40% of Capex and up to 25% of Opex for first five years | State Government/Sponsoring Central Ministry/Statutory Entity may provide up to 40% of Capex and up to 25% of Opex | Up to 25% of Opex for first five years | Requires ≥50% Operational Cost recovery; for demonstration/pilot social sectors |

**Key Financial Figures:**
- Total Scheme Outlay: **Rs. 8,100 crores** (till 2024-25)
- Post-revamp Disbursement: **Rs. 470.46 Cr** have been disbursed under VGF Scheme
- Cumulative Approvals: **67 projects** with total project cost of **Rs. 45,802 crores** have received final approval
- VGF Approved: **Rs. 7,682 crores** approved
- VGF Disbursed: **Rs. 4,847 Crore** completed

### Application Process
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Project Sponsoring Authority identifies PPP project] --> B[Prepares feasibility studies, project agreements with expert assistance]
    B --> C[Submits proposal to PPPAC Secretariat in specified format<br/>(6 copies hard/soft + pre-feasibility/feasibility report + term-sheet)]
    C --> D[PPPAC Secretariat circulates documents & convenes meeting within 3 weeks for 'in principle' approval]
    D --> E[Planning Commission appraises project & forwards Appraisal Note<br/>Other ministries forward written comments within stipulated time]
    E --> F[PPPAC Secretariat forwards comments to PSA for written response]
    F --> G[PSA submits concession agreement, supporting agreements, PPPAC Memo, project report for PPPAC consideration]
    G --> H[PPPAC evaluates Appraisal Note, comments, PSA response<br/>Recommends approval (with/without mods) OR requests changes]
    H --> I{Approved by PPPAC?}
    I -->|Yes| J[Project put up to competent authority (same as PIB approval) for final approval]
    I -->|No| K[PSA makes necessary changes for further consideration]
    J --> L[After final approval, financial bids invited]
    L --> M[Post-award: Claims for VGF disbursement submitted based on project milestones & verification]
```

**Application Portal:** https://dea.gov.in/schemes-services/viability-gap-funding-scheme  
**Status:** Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications accepted year-round per PPPAC appraisal and approval procedure.

### Key Caveats
> - VGF grant is only available for PPP projects that are financially unviable but socially/economically desirable  
> - Social sector projects under Sub Scheme-1 require ≥100% Operational Cost recovery; Sub Scheme-2 requires ≥50% Operational Cost recovery  
> - Central Government's VGF share is subject to the total outlay of Rs. 8,100 crores till 2024-25  
> - Post-revamp, only projects approved after 2020 are eligible under enhanced social sector provisions  
> - VGF disbursement is contingent upon project progress, verification, and compliance with scheme guidelines  
> - State Government/Sponsoring Central Ministry/Statutory Entity support is additional and not guaranteed; must be committed separately  
> - Scheme does not cover projects under Ministry of Defence, Department of Atomic Energy, and Department of Space  

### Required Documents
1. Pre-feasibility/feasibility report  
2. Term-sheet containing salient features of proposed project agreements  
3. Six copies (hard and soft) of the proposal  
4. All draft project agreements  
5. Project Report  
6. Concession agreement and supporting agreements/documents  
7. PPPAC Memo  
8. Proof of Operational Cost recovery (for social sector projects: ≥100% for Sub Scheme-1, ≥50% for Sub Scheme-2)  
9. Documentation of project cost and Capex  
10. Details of financing arrangements (equity, debt, etc.)  
11. Revenue streams and underlying assumptions  
12. Net Present Value (NPV) of revenue streams with 12% discounting  
13. Details of tariff/user charge fixation
