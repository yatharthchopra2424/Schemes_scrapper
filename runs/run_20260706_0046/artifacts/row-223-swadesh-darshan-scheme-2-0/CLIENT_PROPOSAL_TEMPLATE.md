## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified the Swadesh Darshan Scheme 2.0 — a grant scheme administered by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is central financial assistance (CFA) for tourism infrastructure development, with funding patterns of 60:40 for general category states, 90:10 for North Eastern and Himalayan states, and 100% for Union Territories, released in installments based on project progress.
