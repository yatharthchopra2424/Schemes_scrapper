## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial outlay and strategic importance of tourism infrastructure projects under Swadesh Darshan 2.0, where central financial assistance can reach hundreds of crores for large circuits.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile — while funding is assured upon approval, disbursement is utilization-linked and subject to strict timelines, progress reporting, and compliance with environmental/land clearance requirements, introducing execution risk.
3. **Transparency & Trust**: Clear retainer for feasibility, DPR preparation, and agency coordination; success fee tied strictly to sanctioned fund release, ensuring client pays only for measurable outcomes.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Given the 6–18 month typical processing time from DPR submission to first installment (based on utilization-linked tranche release and PAC appraisal cycles), success fees are structured with a 3-month lag in revenue projections to reflect realistic approval-to-disbursement timelines.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for securing central financial assistance (CFA) under the Swadesh Darshan Scheme 2.0, administered by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India, upon receipt of sanction letter and first installment release.
