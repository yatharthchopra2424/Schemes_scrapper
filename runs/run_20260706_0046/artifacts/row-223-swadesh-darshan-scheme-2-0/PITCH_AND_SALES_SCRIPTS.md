## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping state governments and union territory administrations access central funding for tourism infrastructure development under initiatives like the Swadesh Darshan Scheme 2.0. I’d like to understand your current tourism development priorities and see if there’s alignment with this scheme’s goals of developing theme-based tourist circuits and enhancing visitor facilities.

### Discovery Questions
- Which thematic circuit(s) is your state/UT currently focusing on for tourism development (e.g., Buddhist Circuit, Coastal Circuit, Desert Circuit, etc.)?
- What stage is your Detailed Project Report (DPR) at for the proposed tourism infrastructure project?
- Do you have land availability certificates and clear title for the proposed project sites?
- Have you obtained or initiated the process for environmental clearances, if applicable to your project?
- Is there a convergence plan in place with other central or state schemes (e.g., PRASAD, UDAN, MGNREGA)?
- Has your State Level Empowered Committee (SLEC) cleared the project proposal?
- What is your planned timeline for project completion, and are you prepared to meet the stipulated deadlines?
- Are you aware of the funding pattern applicable to your state/UT (60:40, 90:10, or 100% central funding)?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is a State Government or Union Territory Administration
- [ ] Project is aligned with one of the identified thematic circuits under Swadesh Darshan Scheme 2.0
- [ ] Detailed Project Report (DPR) prepared as per scheme guidelines
- [ ] Land availability certificate obtained with clear title, free from encumbrances
- [ ] Environmental clearance secured (if applicable)
- [ ] Undertaking for operation and maintenance provided
- [ ] Convergence plan with other schemes submitted
- [ ] Circuit map and thematic justification included
- [ ] State Level Empowered Committee (SLEC) clearance obtained
- [ ] Commitment to bear the prescribed state/UT share of project cost (as per funding pattern)
- [ ] Timeline for completion agreed upon and realistic
- [ ] No request for recurring expenses or manpower costs

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your project aligns well with the Swadesh Darshan Scheme 2.0’s focus on developing theme-based tourist circuits. This scheme offers central financial assistance for tourism infrastructure like wayside amenities, accommodation, and eco-lodges, with funding patterns tailored to your state/UT’s category. Shall I walk you through how it works and what support is available?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
State governments and UT administrations often face challenges in securing adequate funding for tourism infrastructure development, particularly for theme-based circuits requiring coordinated development of wayside amenities, tourist centers, accommodation, and signage. Limited budgets and fragmented planning can delay projects that could boost domestic and international tourist footfalls, generate employment, and preserve cultural heritage.

**Scheme intro:**
The Swadesh Darshan Scheme 2.0, administered by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India, provides central financial assistance (CFA) for the development of tourism infrastructure in identified thematic circuits across India. The funding pattern is 60:40 for general category states (Centre:State), 90:10 for North Eastern and Himalayan states, and 100% central funding for Union Territories. Assistance covers projects such as wayside amenities, tourist centers, accommodation, eco-lodges, sound & light shows, pathways, landscaping, illumination, and signage, released in installments based on physical and financial progress.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your project is aligned with an identified thematic circuit under the scheme guidelines  
- You have or are preparing a Detailed Project Report (DPR) as required  
- Land availability and clear title are confirmed or in process  
- Environmental clearance (if applicable) and SLEC clearance are addressed or underway  
- You are committed to bearing the prescribed state/UT share of project cost per the funding pattern
