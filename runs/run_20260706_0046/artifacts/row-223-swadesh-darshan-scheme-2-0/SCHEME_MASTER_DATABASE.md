### Swadesh Darshan Scheme 2.0
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Tourism, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://tourism.gov.in/swadesh-darshan
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: State Governments and Union Territory Administrations
- Age of business: Not applicable
- Sector: Tourism infrastructure development
- Revenue cap: Not applicable
- Employee count: Not applicable
- Other: Projects must be aligned with identified thematic circuits and adhere to scheme guidelines, including norms for sustainable tourism, environmental clearances, and land availability

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Central financial assistance (CFA) with funding pattern of 60:40 for general category states (Centre:State), 90:10 for North Eastern and Himalayan states, and 100% central funding for Union Territories
- Disbursement mode: Installments based on physical and financial progress of the project
- Duration of benefit: Released in installments subject to utilization-linked manner (project-based)

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
2. Land availability certificate
3. Environmental clearance (if applicable)
4. Undertaking for operation and maintenance
5. Convergence plan with other schemes
6. Circuit map and thematic justification
7. State Level Empowered Committee (SLEC) clearance

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. State Governments/UT Administrations identify potential tourist circuits based on thematic themes
2. Prepare Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) as per scheme guidelines
3. Submit proposals to the Ministry of Tourism along with required documents
4. Proposals are appraised by the Project Appraisal Committee (PAC)
5. Upon approval, sanction letters are issued
6. Funds are released in installments subject to utilization-linked manner
7. Projects are monitored through site visits and progress reports
