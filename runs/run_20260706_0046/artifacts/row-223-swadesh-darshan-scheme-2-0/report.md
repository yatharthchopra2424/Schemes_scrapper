### Scheme Overview
**Scheme Name:** Swadesh Darshan Scheme 2.0

**Ministry / Category:** Tourism

### Objectives
The Swadesh Darshan Scheme 2.0 aims to:
- Develop theme-based tourist circuits in a planned and prioritized manner  
- Enhance tourism infrastructure and visitor facilities at identified destinations  
- Promote sustainable and responsible tourism practices  
- Increase domestic and international tourist footfalls  
- Generate employment opportunities through tourism-led economic growth  
- Preserve and promote cultural and natural heritage  
- Strengthen linkages between tourism and other sectors like handicrafts, local cuisine, and adventure sports  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Eligible Entity | Requirements | Notes |
|-----------------|--------------|-------|
| **State Governments** | Must submit project proposals aligned with State Level Empowered Committee (SLEC) clearance | Must bear 40% of project cost (general category) or 10% (NE/Himalayan states) |
| **Union Territory Administrations** | Must submit project proposals with SLEC clearance | 100% central funding; no state share required |
| **Projects** | Must align with identified thematic circuits; adhere to scheme guidelines including sustainable tourism norms, environmental clearances, and land availability | No financial assistance for recurring expenses or manpower |

> **Key Caveat:** Only State Governments and Union Territory Administrations are eligible. Private entities, NGOs, or individuals cannot apply directly. Projects must be sponsored by the respective state/UT government.

### Financial Support Structure
| Recipient Type | Funding Pattern (Centre : State/UT) | Maximum CFA | Disbursement Mechanism |
|----------------|-------------------------------------|-------------|------------------------|
| **General Category States** | 60 : 40 | As per DPR appraisal | Installments linked to physical & financial progress |
| **North Eastern & Himalayan States** | 90 : 10 | As per DPR appraisal | Installments linked to physical & financial progress |
| **Union Territories** | 100 : 0 | As per DPR appraisal | Installments linked to physical & financial progress |

> **Financial Notes:**  
> - Central Financial Assistance (CFA) is released in installments based on utilization-linked progress.  
> - No assistance for recurring expenses, manpower, or operational costs.  
> - Beneficiary states/UTs must bear their prescribed share (40%, 10%, or 0%) before subsequent fund releases.  
> - Utilization Certificates (UCs) and progress reports are mandatory for each installment release.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[State/UT Identifies Tourist Circuit] --> B[Prepare DPR per Scheme Guidelines]

    E --> F{PAC Approval?}
    F -->|Yes| G[Issue Sanction Letter]
    F -->|No| H[Revise & Resubmit]
    G --> I[Release 1st Installment (Subject to Utilization)]

    K -->|Yes| L[Release Next Installment]

    O --> P[Close Project]
```

### Timelines & Deadlines
| Stage | Timeline | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| DPR Preparation | Variable (Typically 3-6 months) | Depends on circuit complexity & land readiness |
| Submission to MoT | Rolling basis | No fixed deadline; subject to annual budget allocation |
| PAC Appraisal | 60-90 days post-submission | Subject to committee schedule |
| Fund Release (1st Installment) | Within 30 days of sanction | Subject to document verification |
| Subsequent Installments | Upon UC submission & progress verification | Typically quarterly or bi-annual |
| Project Completion | As per sanction timeline | Usually 2-3 years; extensions possible with justification |
| Final UC Submission | Within 3 months of completion | Mandatory for closure |

> **Warning:** Projects must be completed within the stipulated timeline as per sanction letter. Delays may result in fund withholding or project cancellation.

The scheme encourages convergence with:

- UDAN (Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik) for aviation connectivity  
- MGNREGA for labor-intensive components  
- State tourism policies and rural development schemes  
- Handicrafts, handlooms, and local cuisine promotion initiatives
