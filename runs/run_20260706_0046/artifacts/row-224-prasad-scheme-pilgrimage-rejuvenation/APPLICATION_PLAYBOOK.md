## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is a registered entity under the Companies Act, 2013, or a Society registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, or a Trust registered under the Indian Trusts Act, 1882, or a Statutory Body established under an Act of Parliament or State Legislature.
- [ ] The applicant has a minimum average annual turnover of ₹50 crore over the last three financial years.
- [ ] The applicant has been in operation for a minimum of 5 years as of the date of application.
- [ ] The applicant is engaged in the development, operation, or maintenance of pilgrimage and heritage tourism infrastructure.
- [ ] The project is located at a notified pilgrimage destination as identified by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India.
- [ ] The applicant has not defaulted on any loan or financial obligation to any bank or financial institution in the last 3 years.
- [ ] The applicant possesses clear title or lease rights to the land where the project is to be implemented, with a minimum lease period of 30 years remaining.
- [ ] The project proposal includes a detailed plan for sustainable tourism practices, including waste management, water conservation, and energy efficiency.
- [ ] The applicant has obtained all necessary statutory clearances (environmental, forest, heritage, etc.) required for the project, or has applied for them and expects to obtain them within 6 months of in-principle approval.
- [ ] The project does not involve any commercial activity unrelated to pilgrimage and heritage tourism (e.g., hotels, restaurants, retail) exceeding 20% of the total project cost.

> **Sources**: Scheme eligibility criteria inferred from PRASAD Scheme guidelines (Ministry of Tourism, Government of India). Portal: [https://tourism.gov.in/prasad](https://tourism.gov.in/prasad)

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate (Companies Act, Societies Act, or Trust Act)
- PAN Card of the applicant entity
- Audited Financial Statements (ITR copies) for the last 3 financial years
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration Certificate (if applicable)
- Board Resolution authorizing the application and designating signatory
- Cancelled cheque or bank letter for NEFT/RTGS details

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) including technical feasibility, cost estimates, and implementation timeline
- Land ownership documents or lease agreement (minimum 30 years residual lease)
- Statutory clearances applied for or obtained (environmental, forest, heritage, pollution control board, etc.)
- Certificate from State Tourism Department endorsing the project as a priority pilgrimage destination
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) from local municipal authority or panchayat
- Photographs and maps of the project site showing current status and proposed interventions
- Undertaking that the project will not be used for any commercial activity inconsistent with pilgrimage tourism
- Undertaking on utilization of funds only for approved components and submission of Utilization Certificate (UC) within stipulated time
- Affidavit confirming no default on loans or financial obligations in the last 3 years

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (minimum 300 DPI)
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ApplicantName_Date.pdf` (e.g., `PRASAD_LandLease_ABCTrust_20240515.pdf`)
- Scanned copies must be certified true copy by a Gazetted Officer or Notary Public (where applicable)
- Financial documents must be audited and signed by a Chartered Accountant
- All documents must be in English or Hindi; if in regional language, a certified translation must be attached
- No password protection or encryption on submitted files
- Maximum file size per document: 10 MB

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
✅ **Required**  
The Detailed Project Report (DPR) is mandatory for the PRASAD Scheme. It must include the following sections:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Background and Justification (link to pilgrimage tourism development)  
- Site Analysis (current infrastructure, footfall data, heritage significance)  
- Project Components (detailed civil works, landscaping, signage, sanitation, lighting, etc.)  
- Technical Feasibility and Engineering Details  
- Cost Estimate (item-wise, with quotes and justifications)  
- Implementation Schedule (Gantt chart or timeline)  
- Financing Plan (equity vs. loan vs. grant components)  
- Revenue Model (if any, limited to permissible pilgrimage-related activities)  
- Environmental and Social Impact Assessment (mitigation measures)  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
- Operations and Maintenance Plan (post-completion)  
- Compliance with Scheme Guidelines (PRASAD)  
- Annexures: Maps, photographs, clearances, certificates, undertakings  

> **Note**: DPR must be prepared by a certified consultant or engineering firm and signed by the applicant’s authorized signatory.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal exclusively via the official Ministry of Tourism website: [https://tourism.gov.in/prasad](https://tourism.gov.in/prasad)  
2. Use only the latest version of the application form downloaded directly from the portal; do not use outdated or third-party forms.  
3. Fill all fields in **English** using **block letters** or typed text; handwritten entries are not accepted.  
4. Ensure the applicant name, PAN, and registration number match exactly across all documents and the form.  
5. In the "Project Cost" section, break down costs as per DPR and ensure the total matches the sanctioned amount sought (typically up to 80% of project cost, capped at ₹50 crore).  
6. Under "Project Location", provide the exact latitude/longitude and revenue village/district/state as per land documents.  
7. Attach all documents in the specified order and format as listed in the Document Collection stage; misplaced attachments may lead to rejection.  
8. Before final submission, preview the entire form and verify that all declarations are signed (digitally or scanned wet signature) and dates are current.  
9. Do not leave any mandatory field blank; if not applicable, write "NA" with a brief justification.  
10. Save a draft periodically to avoid data loss due to portal timeout.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify the official approval letter from the Ministry of Tourism (check ARN, project name, sanctioned amount, and validity period)  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned amount matches the approved DPR cost and scheme limits (typically 80% of project cost, max ₹50 crore)  
- [ ] Sign the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) or Agreement with the Ministry of Tourism within the stipulated time  
- [ ] Utilize the first installment only after release and receipt in the designated bank account  
- [ ] Maintain separate books of account for PRASAD funds and ensure audit readiness  
- [ ] Submit Utilization Certificate (UC) and progress reports as per schedule (usually quarterly)  
- [ ] Ensure funds are used **only** for approved components as per DPR; any deviation requires prior approval  
- [ ] Display signage at the project site acknowledging PRASAD Scheme support (as per guidelines)  
- [ ] Cooperate with any mid-term or final inspection by Ministry-appointed officials  
- [ ] Submit final UC, completion certificate, and audit report within 3 months of project completion  
- [ ] Invoice client for success fee only after receipt of funds and verification of approval  

> **Sources**: PRASAD Scheme Guidelines, Ministry of Tourism, Government of India. Portal: [https://tourism.gov.in/prasad](https://tourism.gov.in/prasad)  
> **Note**: Always verify current guidelines and financial limits on the portal, as they may be updated periodically.
