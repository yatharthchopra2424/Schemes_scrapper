## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the scale and strategic impact of the benefit secured, ensuring client returns significantly exceed consulting costs.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees account for scheme-specific risks; for PRASAD Scheme – Pilgrimage Rejuvenation, the moderate processing time and dependency on government approvals necessitate a balanced risk-reward share.
3. **Transparency & Predictability**: Clear retainer and success fee terms prevent billing surprises, with milestones tied to scheme application stages.
4. **Scalability & Firm Sustainability**: Tiered pricing enables serving diverse clients while maintaining firm profitability, with Enterprise tier tailored for large-scale infrastructure schemes like PRASAD.

*Note: The PRASAD Scheme involves medium to high implementation risk due to multi-agency coordination, land acquisition dependencies, and variable processing times (typically 6–12 months based on similar tourism infrastructure schemes), justifying the lower success fee range for Enterprise clients.*
