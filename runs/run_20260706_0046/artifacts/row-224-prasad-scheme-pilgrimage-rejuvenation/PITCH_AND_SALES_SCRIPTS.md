## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping tourism and pilgrimage-related businesses access government support for infrastructure and service upgrades. The PRASAD Scheme – Pilgrimage Rejuvenation is designed specifically to enhance the visitor experience at religious and cultural sites across India. I’d like to understand if your organisation might benefit from this initiative.

### Discovery Questions
- Is your organisation involved in the development, maintenance, or management of facilities at a recognised pilgrimage or heritage tourism site in India?
- Has your organisation been operational for at least three years, as required for eligibility under the PRASAD Scheme?
- Are you seeking financial support for projects such as basic amenities, signage, landscaping, or infrastructure improvements around a pilgrimage destination?
- Is your project located at a site identified under the PRASAD Scheme’s list of eligible pilgrimage centres?
- Do you have clear land rights or necessary permissions for the proposed development at the site?
- Are you looking for central financial assistance covering up to a significant portion of eligible project costs?
- Is your organisation a government body, public sector undertaking, or a registered trust/society engaged in pilgrimage site management?
- Have you previously applied for or received funding under any tourism or heritage development scheme?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Problem framing:
Pilgrimage sites in India often face challenges related to inadequate basic amenities, poor signage, congestion, and insufficient visitor infrastructure, which affect the overall experience and safety of millions of devotees and tourists each year.

### Scheme intro:
The PRASAD Scheme – Pilgrimage Rejuvenation (full name: Pilgrimage Rejuvenation and Spiritual, Heritage Augmentation Drive) is a central sector scheme administered by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India. It provides financial assistance for the development and augmentation of infrastructure at identified pilgrimage and heritage tourism sites. Support includes grants for basic amenities such as drinking water, sanitation, seating, environmental improvement, signage, and landscaping, with funding typically covering up to a significant portion of eligible project costs (exact percentage varies by project and site classification).

### Why they qualify (personalised):
- Your organisation manages facilities at a recognised pilgrimage site eligible under PRASAD  
- You have been operational for over three years, meeting the minimum track record requirement  
- Your proposed project focuses on core eligible components like sanitation, water supply, or visitor facilitation  
- You possess the necessary land rights or approvals for development at the site
