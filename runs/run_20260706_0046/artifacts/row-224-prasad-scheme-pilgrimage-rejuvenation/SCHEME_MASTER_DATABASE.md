### PRASAD Scheme – Pilgrimage Rejuvenation
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Tourism
- **Portal / Application URL:** [https://tourism.gov.in/prasad](https://tourism.gov.in/prasad)
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: State Governments / Union Territory Administrations / Central Public Sector Undertakings
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Tourism infrastructure development
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Projects must focus on pilgrimage and heritage tourism destinations; proposals must include detailed project reports, land availability, and clear implementation timelines

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Central financial assistance up to 100% of project cost for public-funded components; varies by project
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement against verified milestones
- Duration of benefit: Project-based (typically 2–3 years per project)

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
2. Land ownership/lease documents
3. Clearances from relevant authorities (environment, heritage, etc.)
4. Financial appraisal from lending institution (if applicable)
5. Implementation schedule and milestones
6. State Level Empowered Committee (SLEC) clearance
7. Utilization Certificates (for ongoing projects)
8. Audited financial statements of implementing agency
9. MoU between Central and State Government (if applicable)
10. Photographs of project site (before and during implementation)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. State Government/UT Administration/Central PSU identifies eligible pilgrimage/tourism site
2. Prepares Detailed Project Report (DPR) with cost estimates and timelines
3. Obtains necessary statutory clearances (environment, heritage, local bodies)
4. Submits proposal to Ministry of Tourism through State Level Empowered Committee (SLEC)
5. Ministry appraises proposal via Project Appraisal Committee (PAC)
6. Upon approval, sanction letter issued with funding terms
7. Implementing agency executes project as per approved DPR and milestones
8. Submits progress reports and utilization certificates for reimbursement claims
9. Ministry releases funds in installments based on verified progress
10. Project completion reported with final utilization certificate and impact assessment
