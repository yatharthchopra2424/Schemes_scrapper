# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Target beneficiary is a person with not much means and in need of skills facilitative to employment  
- [ ] For fresh entrants to the job market: training duration must be minimum 200 hours (including practical and/or on-the-job training)  
- [ ] For re-skilling or skill up-gradation of persons already engaged in an occupation: training programmes must have a minimum duration of 16 hours or more of trainings including practical and/or on-the-job training  
- [ ] Persons who have acquired skill through informal, non-formal or experiential training in any vocational trade or craft are eligible if formal recognition and certification of such skill is obtained after imparting bridge courses  
- [ ] Minimum age of trainees: 18 years and above  
- [ ] Training provider must be a government or private institute/organization with required infrastructure for at least two HSRT courses  
- [ ] Private training providers must undergo inspection and meet infrastructure requirements  
- [ ] Security deposit of Rs.20,000/- required for new applicants seeking empanelment as training providers  
- [ ] Entity must not be blacklisted from MSDE, NSDA, or MoT  
- [ ] Re-inspection fee of Rs.10,000/- applies if rejected due to lack of infrastructure

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN card of applicant/organization  
- ITR (Income Tax Returns) of last 2 years  
- GST registration certificate  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam registration (for MSMEs)  
- Aadhar card of authorized signatory  
- Certificate of incorporation/registration  
- Audited financial statements  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Application form with trainee details  
- Proof of Aadhar card for trainees  
- Educational qualification certificates of trainees  
- Bank account details linked to Aadhar for trainees  
- Course-specific infrastructure proof (for training providers)  
- Attendance register  
- Assessment and certification records  
- Placement and employment proof  
- Utilization certificate  
- Monthly progress reports  
- Video clippings of training sessions (to be uploaded on 10th and concluding day of training to pmuhsrt@gmail.com)  

### Document Quality Check
- File size: PDFs under 10 MB, images under 5 MB  
- File naming: Use format `SchemeName_DocumentType_CaseID.pdf` (e.g., `HSRT_AttendanceRegister_CASE001.pdf`)  
- All documents must be legible and in English or Hindi  
- Certificates must be self-attested or gazetted officer attested  
- Bank details must show Aadhar linkage  
- Video clips must be in MP4 format, max 100 MB, showing trainees in uniform with tool kits

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for HSRT scheme application. The scheme uses a standard application form and infrastructure verification process. However, training providers must maintain internal records of trainees, attendance, assessment, and placement for monitoring and fund release.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use only the prescribed proforma in Annexures A & B of the HSRT Empanelment Guidelines  
2. Fill all fields in block letters; leave no blanks  
3. Attach self-attested copies of all supporting documents  
4. Clearly specify the HSRT courses applied for (Multi Cuisine Cook, Food & Beverage Service Steward, Room Attendant, Front Office Associate, etc.)  
5. Declare infrastructure availability as per Annexure-B (classrooms, labs, equipment) with photographic proof  
6. Submit security deposit DD of Rs.20,000/- drawn in favour of "Pay & Accounts Officer, Ministry of Tourism" (valid for minimum 3 months)  
7. Provide bank account details for PFMS linkage (account must be in the organization’s name)  
8. Declare that the organization is not blacklisted by MSDE, NSDA, or MoT  
9. Sign and stamp the application form by authorized signatory  
10. Submit application quarterly (up to 31st March, 30th June, 30th September, 31st December) to Project Manager, PMUSD, Ministry of Tourism, Room No.30, C-1 Hutments, Dara Shikoh Road, New Delhi -110011  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Application form fully completed and signed

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify approval letter from Ministry of Tourism  
- [ ] Confirm annual target allocation for HSRT courses  
- [ ] Open dedicated savings/current bank account for HSRT programme  
- [ ] Submit Mandate Form (Annexure-D) to Ministry for PFMS linkage  
- [ ] Execute affidavit (Annexure-C) and submit to Ministry  
- [ ] Begin trainee mobilization and maintain personal files (application form, Aadhar copy, educational qualification)  
- [ ] Conduct training with minimum 6 hours daily for five/six days a week  
- [ ] Maintain attendance register (minimum 80% attendance required for stipend and certification)  
- [ ] Upload video clippings on 10th and concluding day of training to pmuhsrt@gmail.com  
- [ ] Conduct internal and external assessment (one internal, one external examiner from relevant industry)  
- [ ] Award certification to successful trainees  
- [ ] Track trainees for one year post-certification for career progression and retention  
- [ ] Submit utilization reports and progress reports to Ministry of Tourism  
- [ ] Ensure Aadhaar and bank account linkage for stipend transfer via PFMS  
- [ ] Claim fund release in instalments: 30% on commencement against validated candidates, 50% on successful certification, 20% on outcomes (placements)  
- [ ] Maintain records of third-party assessment and certification costs for reimbursement  
- [ ] Submit quarterly progress report on THIMS portal (Tourism & Hospitality Institute Management System)  
- [ ] Verify stipend disbursement to trainees’ Aadhar-linked bank accounts via PFMS  
- [ ] Prepare for utilization certificate submission after fund utilization  
- [ ] Invoice success fee as per consultancy agreement
