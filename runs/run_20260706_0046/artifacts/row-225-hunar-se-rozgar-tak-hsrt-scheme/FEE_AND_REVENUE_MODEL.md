## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, documentation preparation, and application submission.
2. Success fee is contingent upon successful empanelment as a training provider and release of first tranche of funds.
3. Fees are aligned with the scheme’s outcome-based funding model, where disbursement is linked to validated candidates and certification outcomes.
4. Scheme-specific note: The Hunar Se Rozgar Tak (HSRT) scheme has a moderate risk profile due to quarterly application cycles and outcome-linked fund releases; however, clear guidelines and standardized processes reduce uncertainty for experienced consultants.
