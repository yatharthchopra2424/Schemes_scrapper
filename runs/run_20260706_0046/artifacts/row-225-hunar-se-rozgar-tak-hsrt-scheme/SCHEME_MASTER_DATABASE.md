### Hunar Se Rozgar Tak (HSRT) Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Tourism, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://tourism.gov.in/hsrt
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Applications accepted quarterly (up to 31st March, 30th June, 30th September, and 31st December every year) for empanelment of training providers; training batches conducted as per institutional capacity and prior permission from the Ministry.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Training providers (Govt. Institutes of Hotel Management/Food Craft Institutes, State/UT Governments, State/UT Tourism Development Corporations, industrial units/associations with proven credentials, hospitality institutes approved by AICTE/NSDA/State & UT Govts., classified hotels)
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Hospitality trades (Multi Cuisine Cook, Food & Beverage Service Steward, Room Attendant, Front Office Associate, Laundry Machine Operator, Kitchen Steward, Home Delivery Boy, Traditional Snack & Savoury Maker)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: 
  - Training providers must have required infrastructure for at least two HSRT courses
  - Private training providers must undergo inspection and meet infrastructure requirements
  - Security deposit of Rs.20,000/- required for new applicants seeking empanelment
  - Blacklisted entities from MSDE, NSDA, or MoT are ineligible
  - Re-inspection fee of Rs.10,000/- applies if rejected due to lack of infrastructure
  - Target beneficiaries: persons with not much means, fresh entrants to job market (min 200 hours training), re-skilling/up-skilling persons (min 16 hours training), persons with informal/non-formal/experiential training if formal recognition/certification obtained after bridge courses

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - For Entrepreneurship Programme (EP): Total cost per trainee Rs.11,340/- (training fee Rs.5,000, course material Rs.500, certification fee Rs.1,000, apron Rs.300, lunch Rs.3,000, stipend Rs.1,000, administrative expenses 5% of total cost Rs.540)
  - Stipend: Rs.1,000/- for certain courses under EP, or Rs.300/- per day under Tourism Awareness Programme
  - Certification upon successful completion with minimum 80% attendance
  - Placement tracking for one year
  - Support for self-employment via loans/licenses
  - Third-party assessment and certification
  - Training of trainers, curriculum, equipment, raw material, and trainer salaries as part of funding norms
- Disbursement mode: Funds released to implementing agencies in instalments based on outcomes achieved (10% released upon tracking 90% of candidates in a batch or Rs.5,000 per candidate, whichever is less)
- Duration of benefit: One-time per trainee batch (training duration varies by course: 240-740 hours including OJT)

#### Documents Required
1. Application form with trainee details
2. Proof of Aadhar card
3. Educational qualification certificates
4. Bank account details linked to Aadhar
5. Course-specific infrastructure proof (for training providers)
6. Attendance register
7. Assessment and certification records
8. Placement and employment proof
9. Utilization certificate
10. Monthly progress reports
11. Video clippings of training sessions

#### Application Process
1. Identify eligible trainees meeting attendance and eligibility criteria
2. Enroll trainees in NSQF-approved hospitality courses under HSRT
3. Conduct training with minimum 6 hours daily for five/six days a week
4. Maintain attendance records and ensure minimum 80% attendance for eligibility to appear in final assessment
5. Conduct internal and external assessment by independent examiners
6. Award certification to successful trainees
7. Track trainees for one year post-certification for career progression and retention
8. Submit utilization reports and progress reports to the Ministry of Tourism
9. Ensure Aadhaar and bank account linkage for stipend transfer via PFMS
10. Upload video clippings on 10th and concluding day of training to the Programme Monitoring Unit (Skill Development) at pmuhsrt@gmail.com
