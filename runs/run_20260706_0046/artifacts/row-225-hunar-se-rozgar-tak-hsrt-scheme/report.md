# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Hunar Se Rozgar Tak (HSRT) Scheme** is a centrally sponsored subsidy scheme implemented by the **Ministry of Tourism, Government of India** with pan-India geographic scope. It targets skill development in hospitality trades to enhance employability among persons with limited means, focusing on fresh entrants to the job market and re-skilling/up-skilling of existing hospitality workers. The scheme operates on a rolling basis with quarterly application deadlines (31st March, 30th June, 30th September, and 31st December annually) for empanelment of training providers, while training batches are conducted as per institutional capacity and prior Ministry permission. Last updated in 2019, the scheme aligns with the Common Norms of the Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship.

### Objectives
The scheme aims to:
- Provide skill development training in hospitality trades to enhance employability
- Target persons with limited means who need skills facilitative to employment
- Ensure at least 70% of successfully certified trainees are employed (wage or self) within three months of training completion
- Promote wage employment with remuneration at least equal to minimum wages
- Support self-employment through livelihood enhancement and enterprise development
- Up-skill persons already engaged in hospitality occupations with at least 3% remuneration increase within 14 months
- Integrate soft skills like computer literacy, language, and workplace interpersonal skills into training
- Standardize training implementation across providers using Common Norms of the Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship

### Eligibility Matrix
| Criteria | Fresh Entrants to Job Market | Re-skilling/Up-skilling (Already Employed) | Informal/Non-formal/Experiential Training |
|----------|------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| **Minimum Training Duration** | 200 hours (including practical and/or on-the-job training) | 16 hours or more of training including practical and/or on-the-job training | Eligible if formal recognition and certification obtained after imparting bridge courses |
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Youth; economically weaker sections; unemployed persons; persons seeking skill development in hospitality | Persons already engaged in hospitality occupations | Persons who acquired skill through informal, non-formal or experiential training in any vocational trade or craft |
| **Key Requirement** | Must meet attendance and eligibility criteria | Training programmes must have minimum 16 hours duration | Formal recognition and certification of skill required after bridge courses |

#### Stipend Details
- **Entrepreneurship Programme (EP)**: Rs. 1,000/- per trainee (lump sum, payable in two instalments)
- **Tourism Awareness Programme**: Rs. 300/- per day per person (not admissible if trainee is already employed and receiving salary)
- **Multi Cuisine Cook & Traditional Snack & Savoury Maker**: Rs. 2,000/- lump-sum stipend/incentive (over and above Base Cost)
- **Food & Beverage Service Steward, Room Attendant, Front Office Associate, Laundry Machine Operator, Kitchen Steward, Home Delivery Boy**: Rs. 1,500/- lump-sum stipend/incentive (over and above Base Cost)
- **Skill Testing & Certification courses**: Rs. 1,800/- lump-sum incentive/stipend towards wage loss (over and above Base Cost)

#### Additional Support
- **Special Areas** (North Eastern States, Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Andaman & Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep, LWE-affected districts): Additional 10% of Base Cost permitted
- **Special Groups** (BPL, persons with disability, women): Conveyance reimbursement (Rs. 1,000/- within district, Rs. 1,500/- outside district per month); Post-placement support (Rs. 1,500/- per month for 1-3 months depending on placement location)
- **Persons with Disability (PwD)**: Additional 10% of Base Cost (total 120% if in Special Areas)
- **Uniforms**: Rs. 1,900/- per trainee (over and above Base Cost)

### Application Process
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Identify eligible trainees meeting attendance and eligibility criteria] --> B[Enroll trainees in NSQF-approved hospitality courses under HSRT]
    B --> C[Conduct training with minimum 6 hours daily for five/six days a week]
    C --> D[Maintain attendance records and ensure minimum 80% attendance for eligibility to appear in final assessment]
    D --> E[Conduct internal and external assessment by independent examiners]
    E --> F[Award certification to successful trainees]
    F --> G[Track trainees for one year post-certification for career progression and retention]
    G --> H[Submit utilization reports and progress reports to the Ministry of Tourism]
    H --> I[Ensure Aadhaar and bank account linkage for stipend transfer via PFMS]
    I --> J[Upload video clippings on 10th and concluding day of training to Programme Monitoring Unit (Skill Development) at pmuhsrt@gmail.com]
```

#### Key Process Details
1. **Training Delivery**: Minimum 6 hours daily for five/six days a week
2. **Attendance Requirement**: Minimum 80% attendance required to appear in final assessment and receive stipend
3. **Assessment**: One internal and one external examiner from relevant industry; training provider must not use same entity as both trainer and assessor
4. **Certification**: Awarded to successful trainees with minimum 80% attendance
5. **Post-Training Tracking**: One year tracking for career progression and retention
6. **Reporting**: Utilization reports, progress reports, monthly progress reports submitted to Ministry of Tourism
7. **Fund Transfer**: Stipend transferred only to Aadhar-linked bank account via PFMS
8. **Video Evidence**: Upload video clippings on 10th and concluding day of training to pmuhsrt@gmail.com

### Key Caveats
> **Stipend is payable only to trainees with minimum 80% attendance**  
> **Assessment and certification require one internal and one external examiner from relevant industry**  
> **Training providers must not use the same entity as both trainer and assessor to avoid conflict of interest**  
> **Funding is released based on outcomes achieved, not mere enrollment**  
> **Private training providers must undergo inspection and meet infrastructure requirements for at least two HSRT courses**  
> **Security deposit of Rs.20,000/- is required for new applicants seeking empanelment as training providers**  
> **Blacklisted entities from MSDE, NSDA, or MoT are ineligible to apply**  
> **Re-inspection fee of Rs.10,000/- applies if rejected due to lack of infrastructure**  
> **Applications for empanelment accepted quarterly (upto 31st March, 30th June, 30th September, and 31st December every year)**  
> **Funds released to implementing agencies in instalments based on outcomes achieved, with 10% released upon tracking 90% of candidates in a batch (or Rs.5,000 per candidate, whichever is less)**
