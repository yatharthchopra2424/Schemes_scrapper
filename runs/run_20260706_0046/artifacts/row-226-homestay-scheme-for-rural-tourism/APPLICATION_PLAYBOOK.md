# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is a State Government or UT Administration (as the scheme is designed for State/UT level submission)
- [ ] The applicant has identified suitable tribal village(s) with tourism potential in tribal areas across 30 States/UTs
- [ ] The applicant has consulted with the local community in identifying tribal villages and beneficiaries
- [ ] The applicant has prioritized Dharti Aaba villages for homestay establishment (if applicable)
- [ ] The applicant has identified required interventions for the village community to enhance tourism potential
- [ ] The applicant will prepare a project proposal as per the Model Template (Annexure-I) including:
  - Preference to female homestay owners
  - Model homestay design according to local vernacular architecture
  - Marketing plan for identified tribal villages or cluster
  - Convergence with central and state schemes
  - Development of community space in each identified tribal village
  - Capacity building plan for homestay owners
- [ ] The applicant will submit the project proposal on the portal (sd2.tourism.gov.in) with a covering letter of the State/UT Tourism Secretary
- [ ] The applicant understands that funding under the scheme cannot be utilized for land acquisition, resettlement and rehabilitation, relocation, dredging/development of bunds of a water body (man-made & natural both)
- [ ] The applicant ensures that the proposed land parcel for homestay development is in possession of the beneficiary with clear ownership title and available for development
- [ ] The applicant ensures that the identified homestay owner belongs to the tribal community
- [ ] The applicant will provide requisite capacity building support (upskilling/reskilling) to beneficiaries during development phase and will be responsible for marketing and promotion of developed homestays under the scheme
- [ ] The applicant may partner with experienced NGO/Rural Tourism Societies etc. to support in identification of tribal villages, tribal households, stakeholder consultations, sensitization of homestay owners, community development, and overall project implementation
- [ ] The applicant may assist for upgrading the hospitality experience from state/UT funds where required and encourage stakeholders to adopt sustainable tourism practices in tribal villages
- [ ] Technical upskilling and training of homestay owners will be done by States/UTs in collaboration with IHMs, SIHMs and Ministry of Tribal Affairs etc.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Covering letter of State/UT Tourism Secretary
- PAN card of the Implementing Agency
- ITR of the Implementing Agency (last 3 years)
- GST registration certificate
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam registration (if applicable)
- Aadhaar-linked bank account details for fund transfer via PFMS

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Model Template (Annexure-I) for project proposal
- Preference to female homestay owners (documentation or declaration)
- Model homestay design / prototype according to local vernacular architecture (drawings, sketches, or design documents)
- Marketing Plan for identified tribal villages or cluster
- Documentation showing convergence with central and state schemes
- Plan for development of community space in each identified tribal village
- Capacity Building Plan for homestay owners
- Details of identified tribal villages (including number of villages, names, district location, coordinates)
- Details of proposed homestays (number per village, type: new construction/renovation)
- Details of beneficiary tribal families (number, ownership proof, tribal status)
- Stakeholder consultation records (minutes, attendance sheets, photographs)
- Community participation plan
- Land ownership documents (sale/lease deed, revenue papers) for homestay sites
- Clearance/NOC documents (if any required for land use, construction, etc.)
- Undertaking (Annexure-III) signed by the Implementing Agency

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible scans or certified copies
- File names must match the document description (e.g., "Covering_Letter_State_Tourism_Secretary.pdf")
- File size should not exceed 10 MB per document; compress if necessary
- All documents must be in English or Hindi; if in regional language, provide certified translation
- Undertaking and covering letter must be signed and stamped by authorized signatory
- Land ownership documents must show clear title in beneficiary's name
- Photographs of villages and tourist assets must be geotagged and recent
- All financial documents must be audited and certified

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required as per the Model Template (Annexure-I). The DPR must include:
- Basic Details of the Project (cluster names, number of villages, project name, State/UT, Implementing Agency, total cost, funding support, number of homestays, beneficiary families, duration)
- Project Description (brief about the tribal cluster, village details, tourism potential, proposed interventions)
- Cluster-wise details (village names, district, coordinates, photographs, tourism potential, tourist assets)
- Proposed Interventions:
  - Homestay Development (new construction/renovation units)
  - Village Community Development (facilities like streetlights, wayfinding, sanitation, OAT, camping grounds, community centre)
  - Local sensitization and marketing initiatives
  - Proposed tourism activities (organic farming, tribal dance, local cuisine, handicrafts experience)
  - Sustainability Measures
- Stakeholder Consultation and details (key stakeholders, community participation, meeting minutes with photographs)
- State Contribution / Support mechanism (peripheral infrastructure, water/power supply, civic infrastructure, security, hygiene, etc.)
- Prerequisites compliances (clearances/NOCs required)
- Alignment with the scheme objective
- Expected outcome and impact (tourism benefits, socio-cultural benefits, economic benefits)
- Annexures: Design and Drawings, Relevant Clearance and NOCs, Undertaking

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal at [sd2.tourism.gov.in](https://sd2.tourism.gov.in)
2. Ensure you have valid login credentials (provided by the Ministry or obtained via registration)
3. Download the Model Template (Annexure-I) from the portal or guidelines section
4. Fill all sections of the template completely; leave no field blank
5. Attach all required documents as per the checklist in Stage 2
6. Ensure the covering letter is on official State/UT Tourism Department letterhead, signed and stamped by the Tourism Secretary
7. Validate that all financial figures are consistent across sections (e.g., total project cost matches sum of components)
8. Confirm that the proposal includes preference to female homestay owners (explicitly stated)
9. Verify that the model homestay design adheres to local vernacular architecture (supported by drawings/sketches)
10. Check that the marketing plan outlines strategies for promoting the homestays (online/offline, target audience, channels)
11. Confirm convergence with central and state schemes is detailed (named schemes, how they integrate)
12. Ensure community space development plan is village-specific and includes essential facilities
13. Validate that the capacity building plan includes training in hospitality, guiding, cultural shows, and is linked to IHMs/SIHMs/Ministry of Tribal Affairs
14. Undertaking (Annexure-III) must be signed by the authorized signatory of the Implementing Agency with seal
15. Double-check that all annexures are correctly referenced and attached
16. Submit the proposal online; if portal issues arise, email to swadesh.darshan@gov.in and inform the Ministry immediately
17. Save the submission confirmation/screenshot and note the application reference number

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed project proposal via the portal [sd2.tourism.gov.in](https://sd2.tourism.gov.in)
- If the portal is inaccessible, submit via email to swadesh.darshan@gov.in and immediately inform the Ministry
- Capture a screenshot of the submission confirmation page or save the email sent receipt
- Record and store the application reference number (if provided by portal) or email timestamp
- Set follow-up reminders for:
  - 3 days after submission: Check for acknowledgment
  - 7 days after submission: Follow up if no acknowledgment received
  - 15 days after submission: Check status with Mission Directorate if no update

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from the Ministry of Tourism
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned amount matches the approved project proposal
- [ ] Ensure funds are released to the Implementing Agency via PFMS as per the disbursement schedule (50% on approval, 30% on 75% utilization of first instalment, 20% on completion)
- [ ] Monitor utilization of funds against the approved DPR
- [ ] Ensure the Implementing Agency disburses funds to beneficiaries in a transparent and well-defined manner
- [ ] Verify that capacity building training is conducted as per plan (in collaboration with IHMs, SIHMs, Ministry of Tribal Affairs)
- [ ] Confirm that marketing and promotion of developed homestays is undertaken by the State/UT
- [ ] Ensure integration of feedback mechanism on promotional websites/platforms
- [ ] Verify periodic data collection on revenue generation, occupancy, and feedback received
- [ ] Confirm that land acquisition, resettlement, rehabilitation, relocation, or water body bund development was not funded
- [ ] Ensure convergence with central and state schemes is maintained during implementation
- [ ] Obtain Utilization Certificate (UC) as per GFR Form 12-C, signed by Finance Head and Administrative Head of Implementing Agency, and countersigned by Administrative Secretary-in-charge of Department of Tourism
- [ ] Submit UC and consolidated accounts to the Ministry of Tourism for final tranche release
- [ ] Collect success fee/invoice from client as per agreed terms (e.g., % of sanctioned amount or fixed fee) upon UC acceptance
