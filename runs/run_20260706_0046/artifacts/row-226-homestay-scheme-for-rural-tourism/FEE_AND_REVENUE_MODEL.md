## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the scheme’s financial benefit (up to ₹5 Crore per State/UT, with exceptional cases up to ₹2 Crore additional allocation).
2. Transparent fee structure with clear retainer for advisory work and success fee tied to fund disbursement.
3. Risk-adjusted pricing: Moderate risk due to 1-year project timeline and dependency on State/UT implementation capacity; success likelihood high based on scheme’s structured approval process via Mission Directorate and CSMC.
4. Scalable model: Fees adjusted per client tier reflecting scheme’s focus on State/UT-level projects (not individual homestays), making Scale and Enterprise tiers most relevant.
