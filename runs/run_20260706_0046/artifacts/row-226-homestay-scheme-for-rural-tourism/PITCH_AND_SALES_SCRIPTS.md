# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping State Governments and UT Administrations access central funding for tribal homestay development under the Homestay Scheme for Rural Tourism. This scheme supports the creation of community-based responsible tourism in tribal villages through cultural exchange and authentic rural experiences. I’d like to understand your current tribal tourism initiatives to see if this 100% central funding opportunity aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. Which tribal villages in your State/UT have been identified as having tourism potential for homestay development?
2. Have you prioritized Dharti Aaba villages for homestay establishment as per the scheme guidelines?
3. What is the current status of tribal household identification for homestay beneficiaries in your target areas?
4. Do you have a model homestay design/prototype ready according to local vernacular architecture for the proposed villages?
5. Have you prepared a marketing plan for the identified tribal villages or cluster to promote the homestays?
6. Is there a capacity building plan in place for homestay owners in hospitality, guiding, and cultural shows?
7. Have you identified required interventions for village community development (e.g., community space, infrastructure) to enhance tourism potential?
8. Are you ensuring convergence with other central and state schemes while planning the homestay project?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] State Government/UT Administration is the applicant
- [ ] Suitable tribal village(s) identified with tourism potential
- [ ] Tribal beneficiary(ies) identified for homestay construction/renovation
- [ ] Consultation with local community conducted
- [ ] Priority given to Dharti Aaba villages for homestay establishment
- [ ] Required interventions for village community enhancement identified
- [ ] Project proposal prepared as per Model Template (Annexure-I)
- [ ] Preference given to female homestay owners
- [ ] Model homestay design according to local vernacular architecture included
- [ ] Marketing plan for tribal villages/cluster developed
- [ ] Convergence with central and state schemes planned
- [ ] Development of community space in each village planned
- [ ] Capacity building plan for homestay owners prepared
- [ ] Covering letter of State/UT Tourism Secretary ready
- [ ] Land parcel for homestays in possession of beneficiary with clear ownership title
- [ ] Identified homestay owner belongs to tribal community
- [ ] Technical upskilling/training plan for homestay owners in place (via IHMs, SIHMs, Ministry of Tribal Affairs)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your State/UT is well-positioned to access the Homestay Scheme for Rural Tourism, which provides up to Rs. 5 crores in 100% central funding for tribal homestay development. Let me walk you through how this scheme works and why it’s an ideal fit for your tribal tourism objectives.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Tribal communities in rural areas often lack sustainable livelihood opportunities despite rich cultural heritage. Existing tourism infrastructure in tribal villages is underdeveloped, limiting income generation from homestays and cultural experiences. States/UTs face challenges in accessing dedicated funding for tribal homestay development that includes capacity building, community infrastructure, and marketing support—all essential for creating authentic, responsible tourism that benefits both visitors and indigenous communities.

**Scheme intro:**
The Homestay Scheme for Rural Tourism, administered by the Ministry of Tourism, provides 100% central grant funding for the development and renovation of tribal homestays and village community requirements. The scheme offers up to Rs. 5 crores per State/UT, with potential for higher allocation (up to Rs. 2 crores) for exceptional projects that have exhausted the initial ceiling. Funds are routed through a State/UT-designated Implementing Agency and disbursed to beneficiaries in a transparent manner.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your State/UT has identified tribal villages with tourism potential and engaged local communities in the selection process  
- You are prioritizing Dharti Aaba villages for homestay establishment as per scheme guidelines  
- You have or can develop a model homestay design based on local vernacular architecture  
- You are planning convergence with central and state schemes to maximize impact  
- You intend to provide capacity building training for homestay owners in hospitality, guiding, and cultural shows
