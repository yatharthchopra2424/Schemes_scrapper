### Homestay Scheme for Rural Tourism
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Tourism
- **Portal / Application URL:** sd2.tourism.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** As per timeline communicated by the Ministry of Tourism from time to time

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: State Government / UT Administrations
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Tourism (Tribal Homestays)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: States/UTs must identify suitable tribal village(s) and tribal beneficiary(ies) in tribal areas for construction/renovation of homestays in consultation with local community; priority to Dharti Aaba villages; preference to female homestay owners; proposed land parcel must be in possession of beneficiary with clear ownership title; identified homestay owner must belong to tribal community

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Maximum of Rs. 5 crores per State/UT; for exceptional projects, higher allocation up to Rs. 2 crore may be proposed by MoT for best performing States/UTs
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (funds released to Implementing Agency of concerned States/UTs after in-principle approval by Ministry of Tourism; Implementing Agency disburses to individual beneficiaries/village communities)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (project-based funding)

#### Documents Required
1. Covering letter of State/UT Tourism Secretary
2. Model Template (Annexure-I) for project proposal
3. Preference to female homestay owners
4. Model homestay design / prototype according to local vernacular architecture
5. Marketing Plan for identified tribal villages or cluster
6. Convergence with central and state schemes
7. Development of community space in each identified tribal village
8. Capacity Building Plan for homestay owners

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. States/UTs shall identify suitable tribal villages with tourism potential and tribal beneficiaries for homestay development in consultation with local community.
2. States/UTs shall prioritize Dharti Aaba villages for homestay establishment.
3. States/UTs shall identify required interventions for village community to enhance tourism potential.
4. States/UTs shall prepare project proposal as per the Model Template (Annexure-I) including preference to female homestay owners, model homestay design according to local vernacular architecture, marketing plan, convergence with central and state schemes, development of community space, and capacity building plan.
5. States/UTs shall submit the project proposal on the portal (sd2.tourism.gov.in) with covering letter of State/UT Tourism Secretary as per timeline communicated by Ministry of Tourism.
6. In case of any issues related to proposal submission on the portal, States/UTs may submit the proposal on email id swadesh.darshan@gov.in and inform the same to the Ministry.
7. Proposals meeting guidelines shall be placed before Mission Directorate (MD) for review and recommendation to Central Sanctioning and Monitoring Committee (CSMC).
8. CSMC after due examination will grant approval to the project and further, the project will be sanctioned by the Ministry of Tourism.
9. State Government has to implement the project as per approved project proposal and scheme guidelines.
10. In case of any revisions in the project, approval of CSMC will be obtained.
