# Application Playbook

## How to Use this case. Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Tourism Service Provider must be approved by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India, or by the concerned State / Union Territory Tourism Department  
- [ ] Tourism Service Provider showing minimum FEE of Rs. 1.00 crore – Rs. 2.00 crore in any one of the preceding two (2) years (2018-19 or 2019-20) – maximum of two (02) tours allowed in one financial year  
- [ ] Tourism Service Provider showing minimum FEE of Rs. 2.00 crore and above in any one of the preceding two (2) years (2018-19 or 2019-20) – maximum of three (03) tours allowed in one financial year  
- [ ] Tourism Departments of State Governments / UT Administrations are eligible (eligibility conditions above do not apply to them)  
- [ ] Target beneficiaries: tourism service providers; state governments; union territory administrations  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation certificate  
- PAN card  
- ITR (last 3 years)  
- GST registration  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam registration (if applicable)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Self-certified copy of proof of approval of the company by Ministry of Tourism or by the State Government/UT Administration  
- Declaration / Undertaking with official seal, date and on letter head of the company to the effect that the claimant is not under any investigation and/or has not been charged/prosecuted/debarred/ blacklisted by the Ministry of Tourism, Govt. of India/ Dept. of Tourism State Govt./UT Administration or any other Government agency  
- Certificate of Foreign Exchange Earnings duly certified by Chartered Accountant as per details given at para 7 (ii) of the guidelines. The Certificate must also indicate the membership number of the Chartered Accountant  
- Details of financial assistance availed during the last three years under the MDA Scheme, from the Government including Ministry of Commerce/FIEO & Ministry of Tourism  
- Air ticket(s) for journeys performed along with Boarding Pass for each sector OR first page of the passport along with pages containing immigration stamps (entry/exit)  
- Tour Report in max. 250 words indicating the details of the activity participated / undertaken, outcome of the activity participated/undertaken (format at Annexure IV)  
- Original receipts/bank advice, etc., as proof of payments made for air tickets / booth / participation fee/ hotel accommodation, as the case may be, for which reimbursement is being claimed  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be in PDF format only  
- File size should be reasonable for email submission (typically <10 MB per file)  
- File names should clearly indicate document type and applicant name (e.g., "Approval_Certificate_ABC_Tours.pdf")  
- All certificates must be self-certified or duly certified by authorized signatory (CA, company official)  
- Declarations must be on company letterhead with official seal, date, and signature  
- Passport/immigration documents must be legible and show clear entry/exit stamps  
- Receipts must be original or certified true copies showing payment details

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required. However, a Tour Report (max. 250 words) is mandatory as part of the claim submission (Annexure IV format).  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the correct annexure form (Annexure I for Prior Approval, Annexure III for reimbursement claim) from the MDA guidelines  
2. Fill all fields in block letters or as specified; leave no blank fields  
3. Attach self-certified proof of approval from Ministry of Tourism or State Tourism Department  
4. Include CA-certified Foreign Exchange Earnings certificate with membership number  
5. Declare no pending investigations or blacklisting by any government agency on company letterhead  
6. Provide details of prior MDA assistance availed in last three years  
7. Submit air tickets with boarding passes OR passport with immigration stamps (entry/exit)  
8. Ensure Tour Report is within 250 words and describes activity, outcome, and recommendations  
9. All documents must be in PDF format only  
10. Submit via email to the designated address: mda-tourism@gov.in (Prior Approval) or mda-tourism@nic.in (reimbursement)  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All application form fields completed  
- [ ] Prior Approval obtained at least 21 days before tour (if applicable)  
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents attached in PDF format  
- [ ] Declaration on letterhead with seal and date signed  
- [ ] Foreign Exchange Earnings certificate includes CA membership number  
- [ ] Air tickets and boarding passes or passport with stamps provided  
- [ ] Tour report within 250 words  
- [ ] Proof of payments (receipts/bank advice) for claimed expenses  
- [ ] No claims for same activity submitted previously under MDA  
- [ ] Application submitted to correct email ID based on stage (Prior Approval vs. reimbursement)

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Take screenshot of sent email showing timestamp, recipient (mda-tourism@gov.in or mda-tourism@nic.in), and subject line  
- Save sent email and all attached PDFs in a dedicated case folder  
- Note the date of submission and set follow-up reminders:  
  - For Prior Approval: follow up after 7 days if no acknowledgment  
  - For reimbursement claim: follow up after 15 days if no update; final escalation at 40 days (to ensure submission within 45-day window)

### Escalation Path
- Level 1: Portal helpdesk – Email: mda-tourism@gov.in / mda-tourism@nic.in  
- Level 2: Nodal officer – Assistant Director General (Overseas Marketing), Ministry of Tourism, Govt. of India, Transport Bhawan, 1, Parliament Street, New Delhi 110 001  
- Level 3: RTI filing if no response within statutory SLA (45 days for claim submission, 21 days for Prior Approval)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain approval letter from Ministry of Tourism (for Prior Approval or reimbursement sanction)  
- [ ] Verify sanctioned amount against approved expenses (90% of eligible costs, capped at Rs. 3.50 Lakh for tours; 50% up to Rs. 1.00 lakh for Online Promotion)  
- [ ] Utilize funds strictly for the approved purpose (airfare, stall costs, lodging, or online promotion)  
- [ ] Maintain all original bills, receipts, and bank statements for audit purposes  
- [ ] Submit Utilization Certificate if required by the Ministry (though not explicitly stated in evidence, standard practice)  
- [ ] Track invoice and success fee for consulting services as per engagement letter  
- [ ] Ensure no further claims are submitted for the same activity under MDA Scheme  
- [ ] Monitor budget availability disclaimer – financial support subject to annual budget under MDA Scheme

- Timelines: Prior Approval at least 21 days in advance; claim within 45 days of activity completion

- Eligibility: Approved Tourism Service Providers with FEE ≥ Rs. 1.00 crore; State/UT Tourism Departments exempt from FEE/tour limits  
- Caveats: Incomplete applications rejected; priority to first-time applicants; travel by Air India where connected; claims subject to budget availability
