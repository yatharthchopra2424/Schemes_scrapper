## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the scheme’s financial benefit ceiling of ₹3.50 lakh per case/tour for study tours and travel show participation, and ₹1.00 lakh for online promotion.
2. Success fees capped at 8% for Starter tier reflect the scheme’s moderate processing complexity and documentation requirements.
3. Retainer fees cover pre-approval support, document preparation, and liaison with the Ministry of Tourism’s Overseas Marketing Division.
4. Scheme-specific note: The scheme has a moderate risk profile due to its 45-day claim submission deadline and budget-dependent approvals, requiring proactive client management to avoid revenue lag.
