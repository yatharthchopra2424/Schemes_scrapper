## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
I understand you're in the medical and wellness tourism sector, looking to expand your reach internationally. The Ministry of Tourism's Medical & Wellness Tourism Policy supports approved tourism service providers like you with financial assistance for overseas promotion and participation in travel shows—helping you showcase India's wellness offerings to global audiences.

### Discovery Questions
1. Is your tourism service provider approved by the Ministry of Tourism or your State/UT Tourism Department?
2. What were your foreign exchange earnings (FEE) in either FY 2018-19 or 2019-20?
3. How many international study tours or travel show participations have you undertaken in the last financial year?
4. Are you planning overseas travel for tourism promotion in the next 6–12 months?
5. Do you have documentation ready for foreign exchange earnings certified by a Chartered Accountant?
6. Have you previously claimed financial assistance under the MDA Scheme?
7. Are you targeting specific overseas markets for medical and wellness tourism promotion?
8. What is your estimated budget for overseas promotional activities like stall setup, airfare, or digital content creation?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Approved by Ministry of Tourism or State/UT Tourism Department
- [ ] Minimum FEE of Rs. 1.00 crore in either 2018-19 or 2019-20
- [ ] Planning overseas tourism promotional activity (study tour, travel show, online promotion)
- [ ] Not under investigation or blacklisted by Ministry of Tourism or any government agency
- [ ] Will submit claim within 45 days of return to India
- [ ] Will travel by Air India for sectors directly connected, or private airlines only if Air India not available (booked directly)
- [ ] Has not claimed MDA assistance for the same activity previously

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, if you meet the approval and FEE criteria, the Medical & Wellness Tourism Policy could reimburse up to Rs. 3.50 lakh per overseas tour for airfare, stall costs, and lodging—or Rs. 1.00 lakh for online promotion. Let me show you how this works for businesses like yours.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Medical and wellness tourism providers struggle with high costs of overseas marketing—airfare, exhibition stalls, lodging, and digital content—limiting their ability to attract international patients despite India’s strong offerings in Ayurveda, Yoga, and wellness.

**Scheme intro:**
The Medical & Wellness Tourism Policy (Marketing Development Assistance - MDA), administered by the Ministry of Tourism, provides financial support for overseas promotion: up to 90% of economy airfare, stall costs, and lodging (max Rs. 3.50 lakh per tour), and 50% of online promotion costs (capped at Rs. 1.00 lakh per year).

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your business is approved by the Ministry of Tourism or State/UT Tourism Department  
- You reported minimum FEE of Rs. 1.00–2.00 crore in 2018-19 or 2019-20 (making you eligible for up to 2 tours/year)  
- You’re planning overseas travel for tourism promotion (study show, travel fair, or online campaign)  
- You’re not under investigation or blacklisted by the Ministry of Tourism

| "We're too small / too big" | The scheme requires minimum FEE of Rs. 1.00 crore in one of the last two years (2018-19 or 2019-20). There’s no upper limit—providers with FEE above Rs. 2.00 crore can claim up to 3 tours/year. |
| "I don't have the documents ready" | Top 3 missing documents: (1) Self-certified approval proof from Ministry/State Tourism, (2) CA-certified Foreign Exchange Earnings certificate with membership number, (3) Original receipts for airfare, stall, or hotel payments. |
