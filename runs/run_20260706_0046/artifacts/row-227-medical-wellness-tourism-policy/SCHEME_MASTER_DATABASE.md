### Medical & Wellness Tourism Policy
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Tourism
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://tourism.gov.in/offerings/schemes-and-services/details/marketing-promotion-international-cooperation-UTN0ATMtQWa
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Tourism Service Providers approved by Ministry of Tourism or State/UT Tourism Department; Tourism Departments of State Governments/UT Administrations
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Medical and wellness tourism
- Revenue cap: Minimum FEE of Rs. 1.00 crore – Rs. 2.00 crore (for 2 tours/year) or Rs. 2.00 crore and above (for 3 tours/year) in any one of preceding two years (2018-19 or 2019-20)
- Employee count: [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]
- Other: Tourism Service Providers must not be under investigation/charged/prosecuted/debarred/blacklisted by Ministry of Tourism or any Government Agency; claim must be submitted within 45 days of return to India; travel by Air India only for sectors directly connected by Air India; tickets must be booked directly from airline portal/office, not through agencies

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - For Study Tours/Travel Shows/Exhibitions/Road Shows: 
    - Economy class air fare: 90% 
    - Stall/booth costs (built-up/furnished stall, electricity, water, participation fee): 90% 
    - Lodging: Max. 5 nights, ceiling of Rs. 10,000/- per night 
    - Overall ceiling: Rs. 3.50 Lakh per case/tour
  - For Tourism Departments of State Governments/UT Administrations (Travel Shows/Exhibitions): 
    - Stall/booth costs: 90% 
    - Overall ceiling: Rs. 3.50 Lakh
  - For Online Promotion of tourism destinations/products: 
    - 50% of total cost 
    - Upper ceiling: Rs. 1.00 lakh once per financial year
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement
- Duration of benefit: One-time per approved activity (subject to annual caps)

#### Documents Required
1. Self-certified copy of proof of approval of the company by Ministry of Tourism or by the State Government/UT Administration
2. Declaration / Undertaking with official seal, date and on letter head of the company to the effect that the claimant is not under any investigation and/or has not been charged/prosecuted/debarred/blacklisted by the Ministry of Tourism, Govt. of India/ Dept. of Tourism State Govt./UT Administration or any other Government agency
3. Certificate of Foreign Exchange Earnings duly certified by Chartered Accountant as per details given at para 7 (ii) of the guidelines. The Certificate must also indicate the membership number of the Chartered Accountant
4. Details of financial assistance availed during the last three years under the MDA Scheme, from the Government including Ministry of Commerce/FIEO & Ministry of Tourism
5. Air ticket(s) for journeys performed along with Boarding Pass for each sector OR first page of the passport along with pages containing immigration stamps (entry/exit)
6. Tour Report in max. 250 words indicating the details of the activity participated / undertaken, outcome of the activity participated/undertaken (format at Annexure IV)
7. Original receipts/bank advice, etc., as proof of payments made for air tickets / booth / participation fee/ hotel accommodation, as the case may be, for which reimbursement is being claimed

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. The eligible TSP/SG/UT shall obtain Prior Approval of the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India, before undertaking tour overseas
2. The application as at Annexure -I for Prior Approval, shall be submitted to the Assistant Director General (Overseas Marketing), Government of India, Ministry of Tourism, on e-mail: mda-tourism@gov.in at least 21 days in advance prior to undertaking tour
3. Prior Approval shall be granted subject to receipt of application complete in all respect
4. After undertaking the tourism promotional activity/tour abroad for which Prior Approval had been accorded by the Ministry of Tourism, the Tourism Service Provider / State Govt./ UT Administration will submit the application for re -imbursement/claim, in the prescribed / applicable formats placed at Annexures – II, III & IV to the Assistant Director General (Overseas Marketing), Ministry of Tourism, Govt. of India, Transport Bhawan, 1, Parliament Street, New Delhi 110 001 on email mda-tourism@nic.in positively within a period of 45 days of completion of the activity
5. Submission of claims in physical form will not be accepted. All documents submitted should be in pdf format only
