Based on our initial review of [Client Legal Name]'s business profile, we have identified the National Mineral Exploration Trust (NMET) Grants — a grant scheme administered by the National Mineral Exploration Trust (NMET), under the Ministry of Mines, Government of India — for which your business appears eligible. ## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment and scheme suitability confirmation  
2. Preparation of Detailed Project Report (DPR) as per NMET guidelines  
3. Compilation and verification of all required documents per scheme checklist  
4. Online application submission via the NMET portal: https://nmet.nic.in  
5. Liaison with NMET Secretariat and Technical Committee during review process  
6. Assistance with utilization certificate preparation and progress reporting for installment claims  
7. Final project closure support including audit report submission  

## 4. What We Need From You
1. Documents as per the scheme checklist: Detailed project report, budget estimate, exploration license or permission letter from concerned state government, undertaking for 50% cost sharing, and templates for utilization certificates and progress reports  
2. Designated point of contact within your organization for coordination and queries  
3. Timely responses to our queries within a 2-day SLA to avoid delays in application processing
