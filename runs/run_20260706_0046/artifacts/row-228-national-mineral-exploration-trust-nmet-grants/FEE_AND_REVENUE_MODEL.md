## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are tied to the actual grant disbursement (up to 50% of project cost), ensuring consultant compensation reflects client outcomes.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Retainers**: Retainer amounts reflect the technical complexity of mineral exploration proposals, including detailed project reports, budget estimates, and compliance with NMET guidelines.
3. **Transparency in Cost Sharing**: Clients are advised upfront that NMET grants cover only 50% of project costs; the remaining 50% must be borne by the applicant, which affects affordability and project scoping.
4. **Long-Term Engagement Model**: Given the milestone-based disbursement and requirement for utilization certificates and progress reports, consulting engagements often span 12–24 months, justifying phased billing and retainer structures.

> **Scheme-Specific Risk Note**: The NMET grant scheme has a medium confidence rating due to dependency on technical committee approval and fund availability. Processing times are not explicitly stated in the evidence, but the requirement for staged disbursements upon submission of utilization certificates and progress reports implies a revenue lag of 3–6 months between approval and fee realization. Success is contingent on technical feasibility and adherence to timelines, with extensions requiring prior approval.
