## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping entities engaged in mineral exploration access government funding to de-risk their projects. The National Mineral Exploration Trust (NMET) Grants, administered by the Ministry of Mines, provides up to 50% grant funding for exploration activities across India. I’d like to understand your current exploration initiatives to see if this could support your work.

### Discovery Questions
- What minerals are you currently exploring or planning to explore, and are they listed in the First Schedule of the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 2015?
- What stage is your exploration project at (reconnaissance, prospecting, or general exploration), and what is the estimated total project cost?
- Do you hold a valid exploration license or have permission from the concerned state government for the mineral block in question?
- Are you structured as a State Government entity, Public Sector Undertaking (PSU), Joint Venture Company, or another entity engaged in mineral exploration?
- What percentage of the project cost are you currently able to self-fund, given that NMET requires a 50% cost-sharing commitment from the applicant?
- What is your expected timeline for completing the exploration project, and do you have systems in place to submit quarterly progress reports and utilization certificates?
- Have you previously applied for NMET grants or similar central government exploration funding?
- What are your primary objectives for this exploration (e.g., resource augmentation, critical minerals, import substitution)?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is a State Government, PSU, Joint Venture Company, or other entity engaged in mineral exploration
- [ ] Exploration is for minerals specified in the First Schedule of the MMDR Act, 2015
- [ ] Applicant holds or can obtain an exploration license/permission from the concerned state government
- [ ] Applicant commits to bearing 50% of the total project cost (cost-sharing requirement)
- [ ] Project includes reconnaissance, prospecting, or general exploration activities
- [ ] Applicant can submit a detailed project report, budget estimate, and exploration license
- [ ] Applicant agrees to provide utilization certificates and progress reports for installment claims
- [ ] Project timeline is realistic and extensions (if needed) will seek prior NMET approval
- [ ] Final report and final audit report will be submitted upon project completion

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your exploration project aligns well with the National Mineral Exploration Trust (NMET) Grants. This scheme offers up to 50% grant funding for eligible exploration activities, which could significantly reduce your upfront financial burden. Let me walk you through how it works and whether you qualify.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Mineral exploration is capital-intensive and high-risk, with significant upfront costs for geophysical surveys, drilling, sampling, and reporting. Many entities—especially state agencies and junior explorers—struggle to fund early-stage exploration fully, leading to delayed or abandoned projects despite promising mineral potential.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Mineral Exploration Trust (NMET) Grants, administered by the Ministry of Mines, Government of India, provides grants covering up to 50% of the total project cost for mineral exploration activities. The grant supports reconnaissance, prospecting, and general exploration, including geophysical surveys, geochemical sampling, drilling, and related activities. Funds are disbursed in installments based on milestones, subject to submission of utilization certificates and progress reports. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis via the NMET portal: https://nmet.nic.in.

- You have or can obtain an exploration license or permission from the concerned state government — a mandatory requirement
- You are prepared to bear 50% of the project cost, meeting the cost-sharing obligation for grant disbursement
