### National Mineral Exploration Trust (NMET) Grants
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Mineral Exploration Trust (NMET), under the Ministry of Mines, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nmet.nic.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (proposals can be submitted throughout the year as per NMET notifications)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: State Governments, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), Joint Venture Companies, and other entities engaged in mineral exploration activities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Mining & Minerals (exploration of minerals specified in the First Schedule of the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 2015)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Proposals must follow guidelines issued by NMET from time to time; applicant must bear 50% of project cost

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Grants covering up to 50% of the total project cost for exploration activities
- Disbursement mode: Installments based on project milestones and submission of utilization certificates and progress reports
- Duration of benefit: Based on project timeline (funds released in stages until project completion)

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed project report
2. Budget estimate
3. Exploration license or permission letter from concerned state government
4. Undertaking for 50% cost sharing
5. Utilization certificates and progress reports (for installment claims)
6. Final report and audit report upon completion

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Submit a detailed project proposal for mineral exploration to the NMET Secretariat.
2. The proposal must include objectives, methodology, budget, timeline, and expected outcomes.
3. Proposals are reviewed by the Technical Committee constituted by NMET.
4. Upon approval, a sanction letter is issued specifying the grant amount and terms.
5. Funds are released in stages based on progress reports and utilization certificates.
6. Final report and audit report must be submitted upon project completion.
