## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is a registered entity (company, society, trust, or cooperative) operating in a mining-affected area as defined under PMKKKY guidelines  
- [ ] The project is located in a district identified as directly affected by mining operations under the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957  
- [ ] The proposed activity falls under the permissible sectors: drinking water supply, health care, sanitation, education, skill development, women and child care, elderly care, or environment conservation  
- [ ] The applicant has not previously received funding for the same project under any other central or state government scheme (to avoid duplication)  
- [ ] The project proposal includes a clear utilization plan with timelines, budget breakdown, and expected outcomes  
- [ ] The applicant possesses valid registration documents (as applicable: Company Act, Societies Registration Act, Trust Act, or Cooperatives Act)  
- [ ] The project aligns with the district mineral foundation (DMF) trust’s annual action plan and has been recommended by the DMF governing body  
- [ ] The applicant has no history of fund diversion or blacklisting by any government agency  

> **Note**: Eligibility criteria are derived from the operational guidelines of PMKKKY as referenced in Ministry of Mines notifications. Since the portal returned a 404, verification should be done via the Ministry of Mines main portal or direct communication with the nodal officer.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation/Registration Certificate (Company, Society, Trust, or Cooperative)  
- PAN Card of the applicant entity  
- Audited Financial Statements (ITR) for the last 2 financial years  
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- Bank Statement (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration Certificate (for MSMEs, if applicable)  
- Address Proof of the entity (utility bill, property tax receipt, or lease agreement)  
- Authorization Letter / Board Resolution authorizing the application  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Project Proposal / Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining objectives, methodology, budget, timeline, and expected outcomes  
- Recommendation Letter from the District Mineral Foundation (DMF) Trust or its Governing Body  
- Land Use Certificate or No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the concerned land-owning agency (if project involves land use)  
- Clearance from relevant department (e.g., Health Department for health projects, Education Department for education projects)  
- Affidavit declaring no duplicate funding from other central/state schemes for the same project  
- Survey Report or Baseline Study (if required for environment or social impact projects)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)  
- File names must follow the format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ApplicantName_Date.pdf` (e.g., `PMKKKY_DPR_ABCSociety_20240515.pdf`)  
- Certificates must be valid and not expired (check issuance and expiry dates)  
- Financial documents must be audited and signed by a Chartered Accountant  
- All non-English documents must be accompanied by a certified Hindi or English translation  
- DPR must be signed and stamped by the applicant and countersigned by the DMF representative (if required)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
✅ **Required** – A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is mandatory for PMKKKY applications.  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
1. Executive Summary  
2. Project Background and Justification (link to mining impact)  
3. Objectives and Expected Outcomes  
4. Project Location and Beneficiary Details  
5. Implementation Plan and Timeline (Gantt chart preferred)  
6. Detailed Budget Breakdown (capital and recurring costs)  
7. Funding Plan (PMKKKY share vs. beneficiary contribution, if any)  
8. Implementation Agency Details and Experience  
9. Monitoring and Evaluation Framework  
10. Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
11. Sustainability and Exit Strategy  
12. Annexures: Maps, Drawings, Quotes, Clearances  

> The DPR must be technically appraised and recommended by the District Mineral Foundation (DMF) Trust before submission.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal via the Ministry of Mines website (navigate to “Schemes” → “PMKKKY” – note: direct link may be inactive; use site search or contact nodal officer)  
2. Select “New Application” and choose the correct financial year and district  
3. Enter applicant details exactly as per PAN and registration certificate (no abbreviations)  
4. Select the correct sector (e.g., Health, Education, Drinking Water) from the dropdown – mismatched sectors lead to rejection  
5. Upload all documents in PDF format only; ensure file sizes are under 5 MB and names follow the prescribed format  
6. Enter project cost details carefully – total cost must match the DPR summary; discrepancies trigger queries  
7. Declare that no duplicate funding has been sought or received for the same project under any other scheme  
8. Preview the entire application before final submission – no edits allowed after submission  
9. Save the application reference number immediately upon successful submission  
10. Take a screenshot of the confirmation page and store it in the case file  

### Internal Review Gate

- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are uploaded and legible

- [ ] Application form is previewed and verified for spelling, numeric, and formatting errors  
- [ ] Consultant signs off on internal review checklist before submission

- [ ] Verify that invoices and vouchers are maintained audit-ready  
- [ ] Confirm payment of success fee as per the engagement letter upon fund disbursement  
- [ ] Close the case only after UC acceptance and final reconciliation
