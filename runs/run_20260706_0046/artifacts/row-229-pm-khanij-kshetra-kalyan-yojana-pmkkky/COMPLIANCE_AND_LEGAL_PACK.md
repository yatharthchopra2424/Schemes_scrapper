## 8D. Internal Data Handling Policy

All client data related to scheme applications shall be handled in accordance with the following standards:

- **Storage:** Encrypted cloud storage (AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit).
- **Access Control:** Role-based access with multi-factor authentication (2FA) required for all sensitive data access.
- **Device Policy:** No personal devices permitted for accessing or storing client data; all work must be conducted on firm-issued, secured devices.
- **Retention Period:** Client data shall be retained for a minimum of two (2) years post case closure, after which it shall be securely deleted unless longer retention is required by law or scheme-specific guidelines.
- **Breach Notification:** In the event of a data breach, the firm shall notify affected clients and relevant authorities within 24 hours of discovery.
- **Audit Logs:** All access to client data shall be logged and monitored for unauthorized access attempts.
- **Third-Party Sharing:** Data shall not be shared with third parties except as strictly necessary for submitting applications to the Ministry of Mines, Government of India, and only under binding confidentiality agreements.

**Scheme-Specific Note:** All documents submitted to Ministry of Mines, Government of India under PM Khanij Kshetra Kalyan Yojana (PMKKKY) must be retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit purposes, as per scheme guidelines from evidence. Therefore, the firm shall extend retention of all PMKKKY-related application documents to three (3) years post case closure to comply with audit requirements, overriding the standard two-year retention period for this scheme only.
