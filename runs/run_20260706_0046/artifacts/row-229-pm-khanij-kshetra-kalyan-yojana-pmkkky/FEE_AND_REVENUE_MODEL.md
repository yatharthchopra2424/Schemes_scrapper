## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial benefits delivered under PMKKKY, which targets large-scale mining-affected area development projects with significant fund allocation.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees account for moderate implementation risk due to the scheme’s multi-agency coordination (district mineral foundations, state governments) and variable disbursement timelines observed in field reports.
3. **Transparency & Predictability**: Retainers cover initial eligibility assessment, project scoping, and documentation preparation; success fees are contingent upon fund sanction and disbursement by the implementing agency.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: PMKKKY involves complex stakeholder coordination and longer processing times (typically 6–12 months from application to fund release, based on implementation reports), justifying a lower success fee range (2–4%) for Enterprise clients and a retainer model to sustain engagement through extended approval cycles.
