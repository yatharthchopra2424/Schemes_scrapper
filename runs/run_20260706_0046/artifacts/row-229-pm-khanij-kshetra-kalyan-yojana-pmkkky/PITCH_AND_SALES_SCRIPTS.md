## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because I specialise in helping mining-affected communities and related enterprises access government welfare schemes designed specifically for their upliftment. The PM Khanij Kshetra Kalyan Yojana (PMKKKY) focuses on inclusive development in mining districts — supporting livelihoods, infrastructure, and skill-building for those directly or indirectly impacted by mining operations. I’d like to understand if your organisation or initiative aligns with this mission and could benefit from targeted funding under this scheme.

### Discovery Questions
1. Is your organisation or project based in, or directly serving, a district identified as affected by mining activities (as per the District Mineral Foundation trust)?
2. Are you working in sectors such as drinking water supply, healthcare, education, women and child welfare, skill development, or environmental conservation in mining areas?
3. Is your initiative aimed at benefiting tribal communities, displaced persons, or other vulnerable groups residing in mining-impacted regions?
4. Do you have a clear project proposal with defined outcomes related to social or economic welfare in mining-affected areas?
5. Are you seeking financial support for either new initiatives or the scaling up of existing welfare activities in these districts?
6. Is your organisation a registered entity (such as a trust, society, Section 8 company, or panchayat institution) capable of receiving and utilising government funds?
7. Have you previously applied for or received funding under any central or state government scheme related to mining area development?
8. Are you prepared to submit utilisation reports and monitor project progress as required under the scheme’s guidelines?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Organisation/project located in or serving a mining-affected district (as per DMF identification)
- [ ] Activity falls under permitted sectors: drinking water, health, education, women & child welfare, skill development, environment, etc.
- [ ] Target beneficiaries include local residents, tribals, displaced persons, or other vulnerable groups in mining areas
- [ ] Project has clear welfare or developmental objectives aligned with inclusive growth
- [ ] Applicant is a legally registered entity capable of receiving government grants
- [ ] Intention to utilise funds for new or expanded welfare initiatives (not routine operations)
- [ ] Willingness to comply with reporting, monitoring, and utilisation certificate requirements
- [ ] No duplicate funding for the same activity from other government sources (to be verified)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your work in [mention specific area, e.g., skill development or clean water access] in mining-affected communities could align well with the PM Khanij Kshetra Kalyan Yojana. This scheme provides direct financial support for exactly the kind of welfare and developmental projects you’re describing — let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Communities living in mining-affected districts often face inadequate access to basic services like clean water, healthcare, education, and livelihood opportunities. Despite the mineral wealth extracted from their regions, inclusive development lags due to limited funding and implementation capacity at the grassroots level.

**Scheme intro:**
The PM Khanij Kshetra Kalyan Yojana (PMKKKY), administered by the Ministry of Mines, Government of India, is a central sector scheme that provides financial assistance for welfare and developmental projects in mining-affected areas. Funds are sourced from the District Mineral Foundation (DMF) Trusts and support initiatives in sectors such as drinking water supply, healthcare, education, women and child welfare, skill development, sanitation, and environmental conservation. There is no fixed upper limit per project; funding is sanctioned based on project appraisal and availability of DMF funds.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your project is located in or serves a district identified as affected by mining operations  
- Your work falls under an eligible sector such as [e.g., skill development / healthcare / drinking water]  
- You aim to benefit local residents, tribal communities, or other vulnerable groups in the mining area  
- You are a registered entity capable of managing and accounting for scheme funds
