## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified MEIS (Merchandise Exports from India Scheme) — a subsidy scheme administered by Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is duty credit scrips calculated as a percentage of the FOB value of exports of notified products.

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment based on product description and ITC(HS) code  
2. Document collection and verification as per MEIS requirements  
3. IEC validation and linkage on DGFT portal  
4. Application preparation and submission via DGFT portal (https://dgft.gov.in)  
5. Coordination with DGFT for application processing  
6. Follow-up on application status and query resolution  
7. Assistance with duty credit scrip issuance and utilization guidance  

*Note: No DPR/project report is required for MEIS based on evidence.*
