## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit delivered under MEIS, which provides duty credit scrips as a percentage of FOB value of exports for notified products, ensuring consultant compensation reflects the tangible incentive received by clients.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile — while MEIS offers substantial benefits, its discontinuation for exports on or after 1 January 2021 (replaced by RoSCTL for textiles/garmets) and product-specific incentive rates notified periodically introduce policy and eligibility uncertainty.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The retainer-success fee structure ensures accessibility across client types, with MSMEs and startups (primary MEIS beneficiaries per evidence) falling into the Growth tier, where lower retainers and moderate success fees align with their export scale and benefit realization.
4. **Transparency & Predictability**: All fees are clearly defined upfront, with success fees contingent on measurable outcomes (duty credit scrip issuance), and processing timelines (typically within the financial year of export, per DGFT notifications) informing revenue recognition lag.
