# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping exporters access government incentives like the Merchandise Exports from India Scheme (MEIS), which provides duty credit scrips based on your FOB export value—directly reducing your customs and tax liabilities. Since you're involved in exporting notified goods, I’d like to understand your current export profile to see if MEIS could benefit your business.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your Importer Exporter Code (IEC) status, and is it currently active with DGFT?
2. Which products are you exporting, and do you know their ITC(HS) codes?
3. Are your exported products listed under the notified goods in the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP) for MEIS eligibility?
4. What has been your average annual FOB value of exports over the past 1–2 years?
5. Do you maintain shipping bills, bank realization certificates, and proof of export proceeds realization?
6. Have you previously applied for any export promotion schemes like MEIS, SEIS, or RoSCTL?
7. Are you an MSME, manufacturer, or trader engaged in export of notified products?
8. Are your exports made under the financial year for which you’re seeking incentives?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Exporter holds a valid IEC issued by DGFT  
- [ ] Exports consist of notified products as per Foreign Trade Policy (FTP)  
- [ ] Exports made under IEC and within the eligible financial year  
- [ ] Proof of realization of export proceeds available (bank realization certificate)  
- [ ] Shipping bills, invoices, and packing lists maintained  
- [ ] Beneficiary type: exporter, manufacturer, or MSME  
- [ ] Product-specific incentive rate notified by DGFT applicable  
- [ ] Exports made on or before 31 December 2020 (MEIS discontinued thereafter)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you export notified products under a valid IEC and maintain the required documentation—making you a strong candidate for the Merchandise Exports from India Scheme (MEIS), which offers duty credit scrips as a percentage of your FOB export value. Let me explain how this works and how much you could potentially recover.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Exporters face significant cost burdens from customs duties, excise, and service taxes on inputs, which reduce competitiveness in global markets. Many MSMEs and manufacturers struggle to offset these infrastructural inefficiencies without direct financial support.

**Scheme intro:**
The Merchandise Exports from India Scheme (MEIS), administered by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, provides duty credit scrips as a percentage of the FOB value of exports of notified products. These scrips can be used to pay basic customs duty, excise duty, service tax, and other dues, and are transferable or tradable in the market.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You hold a valid IEC issued by DGFT, which is mandatory for MEIS eligibility  
- You export notified products as per the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP), a core eligibility criterion  
- You maintain shipping bills and proof of realization of export proceeds—key required documents  
- Your exports were made within the financial year and before the scheme’s discontinuation date (on or before 31 Dec 2020)
