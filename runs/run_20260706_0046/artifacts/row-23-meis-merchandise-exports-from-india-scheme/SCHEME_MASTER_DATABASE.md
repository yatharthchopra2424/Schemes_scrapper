### MEIS (Merchandise Exports from India Scheme)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dgft.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Closed (discontinued for exports made on or after 1 January 2021)
- **Window:** Applications are typically submitted within the financial year of export; specific timelines are notified by DGFT from time to time.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Exporters, manufacturers, MSME
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Exports of notified goods/products as per the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: 
  - Must have Importer Exporter Code (IEC) granted by DGFT
  - Eligibility determined based on product description and ITC(HS) code as notified under the scheme
  - Benefits available only for notified products as per the Foreign Trade Policy

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Duty credit scrips calculated as a percentage of the FOB value of exports of notified products; incentive rate is product-specific and notified periodically by DGFT
- Disbursement mode: Duty credit scrips issued electronically
- Duration of benefit: One-time per export realization

#### Documents Required
1. Shipping Bill
2. Proof of realization of export proceeds
3. Importer Exporter Code (IEC) certificate
4. Bank realization certificate
5. Packing list
6. Invoice

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register on the DGFT website (https://dgft.gov.in) with a valid email and mobile number.
2. Login to the DGFT portal.
3. Navigate to the MEIS section under Export Promotion Schemes.
4. Submit an application for MEIS benefits along with required documents such as shipping bills, realization proof, and IEC details.
5. The application is processed by DGFT, and upon approval, duty credit scrips are issued electronically.
