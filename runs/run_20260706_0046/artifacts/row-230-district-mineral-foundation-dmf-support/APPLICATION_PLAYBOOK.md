## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Entity is a registered mining lease holder or mineral concessionaire under the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957  
- [ ] Entity operates within a district where District Mineral Foundation (DMF) Trust has been constituted  
- [ ] Entity is engaged in mining or mineral processing activities within the notified mining lease area  
- [ ] Entity has no pending violations or penalties under MMDR Act, 1957 or Mineral Concession Rules  
- [ ] Entity is compliant with environmental and safety norms as per Mines Act, 1952 and allied regulations  
- [ ] Entity submits annual returns and pays DMF contributions as per applicable rates (if applicable)  
- [ ] Entity is not blacklisted or debarred by any central or state government agency  
- [ ] Entity has a valid GST registration and PAN linked to the mining lease  
- [ ] Entity’s mining lease is valid and not under suspension or cancellation  

> **Note**: Eligibility criteria for DMF Support are derived from the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 and respective State DMF Rules. Since the central portal (mines.gov.in/dmf) returned a 404, eligibility must be verified with the respective State Mines & Geology Department or DMF Trust office.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Partnership Deed / Proprietorship Declaration  
- PAN Card of the entity  
- GST Registration Certificate  
- Udyam Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- Audited Financial Statements (Balance Sheet, P&L) for last 2 years  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) for last 3 years  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- Mining Lease / Mineral Concession Certificate issued by State Government  
- Environmental Clearance (EC) from MoEFCC or SEIAA  
- Consent to Operate (CTO) from State Pollution Control Board  
- Approved Mining Plan  
- Annual Return submitted to IBM (Indian Bureau of Mines)  
- Royalty payment challans for last 12 months  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Application form for DMF Support (as prescribed by respective State DMF Trust)  
- Detailed project proposal outlining intended use of DMF funds (e.g., welfare, infrastructure, skill development in mining-affected areas)  
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) from District Collector or DMF Trust Chairperson  
- Affidavit confirming no duplicate funding from other government schemes for the same purpose  
- Utilization Certificate (UC) format for prior DMF funds (if applicable)  
- List of beneficiaries (if support is for community welfare activities)  
- Photographs and GPS coordinates of project site (if infrastructure-related)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)  
- Names on documents must exactly match the entity name as per PAN and Mining Lease  
- Certificates (EC, CTO, Mining Lease) must be valid and not expired  
- Financial documents must be audited and signed by a Chartered Accountant  
- Affidavits must be notarized on non-judicial stamp paper of appropriate value  
- Hindi or English language only; vernacular documents must be accompanied by certified translation  
- All copies must be self-attested by authorized signatory

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required for DMF Support applications. The DPR must include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Background and Justification (link to mining-affected area welfare)  
- Objectives and Expected Outcomes  
- Detailed Description of Activities  
- Implementation Timeline (Gantt chart)  
- Budget Breakdown (capital, recurring, contingency)  
- Monitoring and Evaluation Framework  
- Sustainability Plan  
- Annexures: Maps, NOCs, Beneficiary List, Cost Estimates  

> **Note**: Format may vary by State DMF Trust. Verify exact DPR structure with the nodal officer.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the official application form from the State DMF Trust website or collect from District Collector’s office.  
2. Fill all fields in block letters; do not leave any mandatory field blank.  
3. Use the exact entity name as per PAN and Mining Lease Certificate across all fields.  
4. Enter correct GSTIN, PAN, and Udyam (if applicable) in designated sections.  
5. Specify the exact amount of support sought and break it down by activity head.  
6. Attach all documents as per the checklist; label annexures clearly (Annexure-A, Annexure-B, etc.).  
7. Declarations must be signed by the authorized signatory (Director/Partner/Proprietor) with date and seal.  
8. If applying online, take a screenshot of the submitted form before final submission and save the application reference number.  
9. Do not use correction fluid; if editing is needed, strike out, rewrite, and initial the change.  
10. Verify that the project location falls within the district where DMF Trust is active.  

> **Source**: [https://mines.gov.in/dmf](https://mines.gov.in/dmf) (404 implies state-level form; protocol based on standard government application norms)

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- For online submission: Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing application number, date, and time. Save as PDF.  
- For offline submission: Obtain a signed and stamped acknowledgment receipt from the DMF Trust office or District Collector’s office.  
- Store the application reference number or receipt number in the case file and share with the client.  
- Set calendar reminders for follow-up at 15 days, 30 days, and 45 days post-submission.  
- Do not submit incomplete applications; verify internal review gate clearance before submission.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the official approval letter from DMF Trust specifying sanctioned amount and validity period  
- [ ] Confirm that the sanctioned amount matches the approved project budget  
- [ ] Open a separate bank account for DMF funds (if required by State DMF Trust)  
- [ ] Utilize funds strictly as per the approved project proposal and DMF guidelines  
- [ ] Maintain detailed records of all expenditures, including vouchers, bills, and bank statements  
- [ ] Submit periodic progress reports (quarterly or as specified) to the DMF Trust  
- [ ] Prepare and submit Utilization Certificate (UC) along with audited statement of expenditure within stipulated time  
- [ ] Ensure third-party audit of UC if required by the State DMF Trust  
- [ ] Coordinate with client for success fee invoicing upon satisfactory utilization and UC acceptance  
- [ ] Archive all approval documents, UC, and correspondence in the case file for audit purposes  

> **Note**: Post-approval compliance is critical; non-utilization or diversion may lead to recovery and blacklisting.
