## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial benefits delivered to clients through successful scheme utilization, ensuring consultant compensation scales with client outcomes.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Returns**: Success fees account for scheme-specific risks, including the District Mineral Foundation (DMF) Support scheme’s low confidence rating and limited publicly available operational details, which may increase application complexity and uncertainty.
3. **Market Competitiveness**: Retainer and success fee ranges are benchmarked against standard consulting rates for government scheme advisory services in India, adjusted for the scheme’s niche focus on mining-affected communities and infrastructure.
4. **Transparency & Predictability**: Clear fee structures with defined milestones (retainer for engagement, success fee upon approval) build trust and allow clients to forecast consulting costs accurately.

*Note on Scheme Risk Profile*: The DMF Support scheme exhibits a **moderate to high risk profile** due to its low confidence rating in the evidence base, lack of accessible application guidelines (evidenced by the 404 error on the official portal), and absence of detailed eligibility, benefit amounts, or processing timeframes in the crawled data. This increases the likelihood of application delays or rejections, warranting careful client scoping and potentially higher success fees within the Enterprise tier range to reflect advisory effort and outcome uncertainty.
