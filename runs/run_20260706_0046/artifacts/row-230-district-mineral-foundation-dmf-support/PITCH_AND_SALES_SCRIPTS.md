## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because I specialise in helping mining-affected communities and related enterprises access government support designed to offset the socio-economic impacts of mineral extraction. The District Mineral Foundation (DMF) Support scheme is specifically created to fund welfare and development projects in districts impacted by mining — whether you're a local enterprise, cooperative, or institution working in health, education, livelihoods, or infrastructure in these areas, I’d like to understand if this could support your work.

### Discovery Questions
- Is your organisation based in or primarily serving a district that has active or recent mining operations (coal, metallic, or non-metallic minerals)?
- Are you incorporated as a trust, society, Section 8 company, cooperative, or panchayat-level institution working in the social or economic development space?
- Does your project focus on one or more of the following: drinking water, health, education, women and child welfare, aged care, skill development, or sanitation?
- Have you already identified a specific project or intervention that addresses a gap caused by mining activity in your district?
- Are you seeking funding for capital expenditure (e.g., building infrastructure) or recurring costs (e.g., running a health clinic or school)?
- Do you have audited financial statements or a track record of project implementation from the past 2–3 years?
- Are you able to provide a detailed project report, including budget, timeline, and expected outcomes?
- Is your organisation willing to comply with monitoring and reporting requirements set by the District Mineral Foundation Trust?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Organisation is located in a district with notified mining activity (coal, metallic, or non-metallic)
- [ ] Legal entity type: Trust, Society, Section 8 Company, Cooperative, or Panchayat-level institution
- [ ] Project falls under permissible DMF sectors: drinking water, health, education, women & child welfare, aged care, skill development, sanitation
- [ ] Project addresses socio-economic impacts of mining in the district
- [ ] Applicant has implementation capacity (track record, audited accounts)
- [ ] Detailed project proposal available (budget, timeline, outcomes)
- [ ] Willingness to comply with DMF Trust monitoring and reporting norms
- [ ] No duplicate funding sought for same activity from other government schemes (if applicable)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your project aligns closely with what the District Mineral Foundation (DMF) Support scheme is designed to fund — particularly in addressing mining-related gaps in health, education, or livelihoods. The DMF scheme provides direct financial support for such initiatives, and I’d be happy to walk you through how it works and whether you’re a strong fit.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Mining-affected districts often face underinvestment in critical social sectors like clean water, healthcare, and education, despite generating significant mineral revenue. Local organisations striving to fill these gaps frequently lack access to dedicated funding streams that recognise the unique socio-economic burden of mining activity.

**Scheme intro:**
The District Mineral Foundation (DMF) Support scheme, established under the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 2015, is administered by the respective District Mineral Foundation Trusts in mining-affected districts across India. It provides grant-based financial support for projects in high-priority sectors such as drinking water, health, education, women and child welfare, aged care, skill development, and sanitation — directly mitigating the adverse effects of mining.
