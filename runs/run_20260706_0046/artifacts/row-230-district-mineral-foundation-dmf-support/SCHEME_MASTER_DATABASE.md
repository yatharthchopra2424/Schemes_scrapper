### District Mineral Foundation (DMF) Support
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** India (Pan-India, implemented at district level)
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Mines, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://mines.gov.in/dmf
- **Current Status:** Closed (based on 404 error indicating inactive or removed portal)
- **Window:** Not specified (portal inaccessible; historical windows were project-based and tied to mining lease approvals)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Entities undertaking projects in mining-affected areas (including NGOs, local bodies, and private firms under public-private partnerships)
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Mining and mineral development, watershed management, drinking water supply, health, education, sanitation, skill development, women and child care, aged and disabled welfare, environment conservation, and other priority sectors as per DMF Trust rules
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Projects must be located in districts affected by mining operations; must align with the Pradhan Mantri Khanij Kshetra Kalyan Yojana (PMKKKY) guidelines; funding is routed through District Mineral Foundation Trusts established under Section 9B of the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support varies by project and district; typically covers up to 100% of project cost for eligible interventions in mining-affected areas (exact caps not standardized nationally; subject to DMF Trust availability and state-specific rules)
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement (after submission of utilization certificates and progress reports) or direct credit to implementing agency (as per trust deed and state guidelines)
- Duration of benefit: Project-based (duration defined in project proposal; typically 1–3 years per intervention)

#### Documents Required
1. Project proposal detailing objectives, budget, timeline, and expected outcomes
2. Registration certificate of the applicant entity (NGO, society, company, etc.)
3. Audited financial statements of the applicant for the last 2–3 years
4. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the concerned District Mineral Foundation Trust
5. Land use certificate or proof of project location within mining-affected area
6. Environmental and social impact assessment (if applicable)
7. Utilization certificates and progress reports (for ongoing or phased projects)
8. Bank account details of the applicant for fund transfer
9. Copy of PAN and TAN of the applicant
10. Declaration of non-receipt of duplicate funding for the same project from other government sources

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify a mining-affected district and confirm eligibility under PMKKKY guidelines
2. Prepare a detailed project proposal aligned with permissible sectors under DMF
3. Submit the proposal to the respective District Mineral Foundation Trust for preliminary review
4. Obtain No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the District Collector or DMF Trust Chairperson
5. Submit final application with all required documents to the DMF Trust
6. Await scrutiny and approval by the DMF Trust Governing Body
7. Sign Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) or agreement with the DMF Trust upon approval
8. Implement the project as per approved timeline and budget
9. Submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates
10. Undergo audit and final evaluation; receive final disbursement upon satisfactory completion
