# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] **Registration Type**: Startup entity registered under the Startup India initiative or equivalent recognized startup framework  
- [ ] **Sector Focus**: Engaged in mineral exploration, mining technology, or related geoscientific innovation  
- [ ] **Business Activity**: Undertaking projects related to critical minerals exploration or mineral exploration and mining projects  
- [ ] **Geographic Scope**: Operations based in India (Pan-India scheme)  
- [ ] **Alignment with GSI Objectives**: Project must contribute to augmenting resources in the National Mineral Inventory, handing over mineral blocks for auction, or strategic evolution of mineral exploration  
- [ ] **Innovation Component**: Demonstrates use of modern exploration techniques, geoscientific data management, or innovative methodologies in mineral exploration  
- [ ] **Compliance**: No history of legal violations related to mining or environmental regulations (self-declaration acceptable at application stage)  
- [ ] **Capacity**: Demonstrated technical capability or access to expertise in geoscience, exploration, or mining technology  

> **Note**: The scheme does not specify turnover, employee count, or age of business thresholds in the evidence. Eligibility is based on startup status and alignment with GSI’s mineral exploration objectives.

**Sources**:  
- Scheme Key Facts: Objectives include "undertaking projects related to critical minerals", "augmenting resources in the National Mineral Inventory", "handing over mineral blocks for auction", and "undertaking projects related to mineral exploration and mining projects"  
- GSI Portal: https://gsi.gov.in (Confirms GSI’s role in providing expertise and disseminating geoscientific information for sustainable socio-economic development)

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration Certificate  
- PAN Card of the entity  
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- Udyam Registration Certificate (for MSME recognition)  
- Bank Statement (last 6 months)  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the entity for the last 2 financial years  
- Audited Financial Statements (Balance Sheet, P&L) for last 2 years  
- Cancelled Cheque or Bank Verification Letter  
- Authorized Signatory Details (Board Resolution or Authorization Letter)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Proposal outlining the mineral exploration or mining innovation project  
- Technical Feasibility Report (including methodology, expected outcomes, and alignment with critical minerals focus)  
- Geoscientific Data Plan (if applicable – details on data collection, analysis, and utilization)  
- Proof of Startup Recognition (Startup India registration certificate or equivalent)  
- Team Competency Profile (CVs of key technical personnel highlighting geoscience/mining expertise)  
- Letters of Support or Collaboration (if partnering with academic institutions, research bodies, or industry experts)  
- Environmental and Social Safeguards Outline (preliminary assessment of potential impacts and mitigation measures)  
- Intellectual Property Details (if innovation involves proprietary technology or methodology)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (minimum 300 DPI)  
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ClientID_Date.pdf` (e.g., `GSI_StartupExploration_TechnicalProposal_CASE001_20260705.pdf`)  
- PDF file size must not exceed 10 MB per document; compress if necessary without losing clarity  
- All certificates must be valid and not expired (check issue/expiry dates)  
- Documents in regional languages must be accompanied by certified English/Hindi translations  
- Signatures and seals must be visible and authentic  
- Financial documents must be certified by a Chartered Accountant (where applicable)  
- Project proposal must be signed and dated by the authorized signatory

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) or equivalent technical proposal is required. Based on the scheme’s focus on project-based support for mineral exploration and critical minerals, applicants must submit a comprehensive project report.  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Objectives (aligned with GSI’s goals: critical minerals, National Mineral Inventory, block auction readiness)  
- Methodology (exploration techniques, geoscientific tools, data management)  
- Work Plan & Timeline (milestones, field activities, reporting schedule)  
- Budget Breakdown (utilization of GSI support, if any)  
- Expected Outcomes (resources estimated, blocks identified, technological innovation)  
- Team Credentials (expertise in geology, geophysics, geochemistry, or mining engineering)  
- Risk Assessment & Mitigation Plan (technical, regulatory, environmental)  
- Sustainability and Compliance Framework  
- Annexures: Maps, technical diagrams, letters of support  

> **Note**: While the evidence does not explicitly mandate a DPR, the scheme’s project-oriented nature implies a detailed technical submission is necessary. Confirm exact format with the nodal officer if unsure.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal via the official GSI website (https://gsi.gov.in) – verify if a dedicated startup/mineral exploration portal exists; if not, use the main portal’s “Schemes” or “Opportunities” section.  
2. Ensure all fields are filled accurately; do not leave mandatory sections blank.  
3. Use the exact legal name as per the Certificate of Incorporation in all fields.  
4. Enter the correct Scheme ID: **row-231** and Scheme Name: **Geological Survey of India (GSI) Startup Mineral Exploration**.  
5. Upload documents in the specified order and format as per the portal instructions.  
6. Declare that the project aligns with GSI’s objectives: critical minerals exploration, National Mineral Inventory augmentation, or mineral block readiness for auction.  
7. Provide a clear, concise project summary (150–200 words) highlighting innovation and socio-economic relevance.  
8. Double-check all numeric entries (budget, timelines, employee count) before final submission.  
9. Confirm that the authorized signatory has completed and signed the declaration section.  
10. Take a screenshot of the final confirmation page before submitting.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the official GSI portal (https://gsi.gov.in) during working hours (10:00–17:00 IST) to avoid technical issues  
- Immediately after submission, capture a screenshot of the confirmation page showing:  
  - Application Reference Number  
  - Date and Time of Submission  
  - Scheme Name and ID  
- Save the screenshot in the case folder with filename: `GSI_SubmissionConfirmation_CASEID_[Timestamp].png`  
- Store the application reference number in the case tracker and set automated reminders for follow-up at 15, 25, and 35 days post-submission  
- Notify the client of successful submission and share the reference number

### Escalation Path
- **Level 1**: GSI Portal Helpdesk – Contact via the “Feedback” section on https://gsi.gov.in/feedback or look for a helpline number on the website (if available; otherwise, use the feedback form)  
- **Level 2**: Nodal Officer – Details to be confirmed from GSI’s Mineral Exploration or Startup Engagement Division; if not publicly available, write: [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]  
- **Level 3**: RTI filing if no response within the statutory SLA of 30 days (as per Central Government guidelines for scheme applications)  

> **Note**: The evidence does not publish specific nodal officer contact details for this scheme. Consultants must identify the appropriate officer via GSI’s organizational structure (e.g., Mission II: Natural Resource Assessment or Training and Capacity Building wing) or through official channels.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify the official approval letter from GSI (check for scheme name, client name, sanctioned support, and validity period)  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned amount or nature of support (e.g., technical guidance, data access, project endorsement, or funding – clarify based on approval letter)  
- [ ] Onboard the client to utilize GSI’s expertise, geoscientific data, or mineral exploration support as outlined in the approval  
- [ ] Maintain a utilization record of all GSI-provided resources (reports, data, consultations, etc.)  
- [ ] Prepare periodic progress reports as required by GSI (if specified in approval terms)  
- [ ] Ensure timely submission of Utilization Certificate (UC) and expenditure statements, if applicable  
- [ ] Track milestones related to project outcomes: mineral potential identified, blocks proposed for auction, or critical minerals assessed  
- [ ] Notify the client of success fee invoicing as per the engagement agreement  
- [ ] Archive all approvals, correspondence, and utilization records for audit and future reference  

> **Note**: The evidence does not specify whether this scheme provides financial grants, in-kind support, or technical collaboration. The approval letter must be consulted to determine the exact nature of support and compliance requirements (e.g., UC, reporting).
