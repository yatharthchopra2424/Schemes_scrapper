**Data Classification:** All client data is classified as Confidential and handled according to the
highest security standards.

**Storage:** All data is stored in encrypted cloud storage with AES-256 encryption at rest and in
transit. Access is restricted to authorized personnel only.

**Access Control:** Multi-factor authentication (2FA) is required for all system access. Role-based

must be performed on company-issued, encrypted devices with updated security software.

**Retention:** Client data is retained for 2 years post case closure, after which it is securely
deleted unless longer retention is required by law or scheme-specific requirements.

**Scheme-specific note:** All documents submitted to Geological Survey of India (GSI) under
Geological Survey of India (GSI) Startup Mineral Exploration must be retained for a minimum of 3
years for audit purposes.

**Breach Notification:** In the event of a data breach, affected clients and relevant authorities will
be notified within 24 hours of discovery, in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

**Training:** All personnel undergo mandatory data protection and security training upon hiring and
annually thereafter.

**Audit:** Regular security audits and penetration testing are conducted to ensure compliance with
this policy and identify potential vulnerabilities.

**Disposal:** Secure deletion methods are used for data disposal, ensuring that data cannot be
recovered after deletion.
