## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the tangible benefit delivered — in this scheme, access to GSI’s geoscientific expertise, mineral block facilitation, and National Mineral Inventory augmentation for startups engaged in critical mineral exploration.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect moderate risk due to the scheme’s low confidence rating and reliance on GSI’s internal project prioritization; processing timelines are not publicly standardized, introducing variability in approval lag.
3. **Accessibility for Early-Stage Ventures**: The Growth tier pricing is calibrated for startups and MSMEs in the scheme’s focus on mineral exploration projects aligns with early-to-mid stage ventures seeking technical validation and resource access.
4. **Transparency and Predictability**: Retainer fees cover advisory work (eligibility assessment, proposal drafting, GSI liaison), while success fees are contingent on tangible outcomes such as project acceptance or mineral block allocation.
