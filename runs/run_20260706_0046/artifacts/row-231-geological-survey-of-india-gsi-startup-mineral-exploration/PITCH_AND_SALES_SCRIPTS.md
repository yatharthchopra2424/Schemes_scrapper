# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping mining and mineral exploration startups access government support for critical minerals projects. The Geological Survey of India (GSI) Startup Mineral Exploration scheme provides expertise and geoscientific information to augment resources in the National Mineral Inventory and hand over mineral blocks for auction—directly supporting sustainable socio-economic development in the mining sector. I’d like to understand if your startup’s work in mineral exploration aligns with this opportunity.

### Discovery Questions
1. What year was your startup incorporated, and are you focused on mineral exploration or mining projects?
2. Are you currently undertaking projects related to critical minerals or seeking to augment resources in the National Mineral Inventory?
3. Do you aim to have mineral blocks handed over for auction through government collaboration?
4. Is your work aligned with the strategic evolution of mineral exploration in India, particularly for sustainable socio-economic development?
5. Are you looking for expert geoscientific support or data generation for your exploration projects?
6. Have you previously engaged with government agencies like GSI for technical expertise or project collaboration?
7. What stage is your mineral exploration project at—early reconnaissance, feasibility, or resource estimation?
8. Are you based in India and operating across any geographic scope for your exploration activities?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup incorporated in India (implied by Pan-India scope and GSI as implementing agency)
- [ ] Engaged in mineral exploration and mining projects
- [ ] Focus on critical minerals or augmenting National Mineral Inventory
- [ ] Objective to hand over mineral blocks for auction
- [ ] Alignment with strategic evolution of mineral exploration in GSI
- [ ] Undertaking projects related to mineral exploration and mining
- [ ] Seeking expertise and dissemination of geoscientific information
- [ ] Contributing to sustainable socio-economic development through mineral projects

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like your startup’s focus on critical mineral exploration and goal of augmenting resources aligns well with the Geological Survey of India (GSI) Startup Mineral Exploration scheme. This scheme provides expertise, geoscientific information, and support for handing over mineral blocks for auction—exactly the kind of support that could accelerate your project’s progress.
