### Geological Survey of India (GSI) Startup Mineral Exploration
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Geological Survey of India (GSI)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://gsi.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Startups
- Age of business: Not specified in evidence
- Sector: Mineral exploration and mining
- Revenue cap: Not specified in evidence
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: Must be engaged in mineral exploration and mining projects; focus on critical minerals

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Expertise and dissemination of geoscientific information; augmentation of resources in National Mineral Inventory; handing over mineral blocks for auction
- Disbursement mode: Technical support and knowledge transfer (non-monetary benefit)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing (project-based)

#### Documents Required
1. Business registration certificate
2. Project proposal detailing mineral exploration objectives
3. Proof of focus on critical minerals (if applicable)
4. Team credentials and expertise in geosciences or mining
5. Financial statements or funding proof (for startup validation)
6. Clearance or NOC from relevant state mining department (if required)
7. Environmental and land use clearances (if fieldwork involved)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the GSI portal at https://gsi.gov.in
2. Navigate to the "Training and Capacity Building" or "Mission" sections to identify relevant programs
3. Prepare a project proposal aligned with GSI’s objectives (e.g., critical minerals, National Mineral Inventory augmentation)
4. Submit the proposal through the appropriate channel (likely via GSITI or regional GSI units)
5. Await review by GSI technical committees
6. If selected, enter into a collaboration or MoU with GSI for technical support
7. Receive geoscientific expertise, data, and field support as per project scope
8. Submit progress reports and final outcomes as agreed
9. Upon successful completion, benefit from mineral blocks being handed over for auction (indirect benefit)
