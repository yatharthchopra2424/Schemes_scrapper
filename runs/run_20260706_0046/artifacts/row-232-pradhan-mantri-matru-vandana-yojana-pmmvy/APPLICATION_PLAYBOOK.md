# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Beneficiary is a pregnant woman or lactating mother  
- [ ] Beneficiary belongs to one of the following eligible categories:  
    - Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST)  
    - Partially (40%) or fully disabled (Divyang Jan)  
    - Holder of BPL ration card  
    - Beneficiary under Pradhan Mantri Jan Aarogya Yojana (PMJAY) under Ayushman Bharat  
    - Holder of E-shram card  
    - Beneficiary under Kisan Samman Nidhi (women farmers)  
    - Holder of MGNREGA job card  
    - Net family income less than ₹8 lakh per annum  
    - Pregnant or lactating Anganwadi Worker (AWW), Anganwadi Helper (AWH), or ASHA  
    - Holder of ration card under NFSA Act 2013  
    - Any other category as may be prescribed by the Central Government  
- [ ] Beneficiary’s age is between 18 years 7 months and 55 years at the time of childbirth  
- [ ] Application is being made within 270 days from childbirth (for registration)  
- [ ] Beneficiary is not in regular employment with Central Government, State Government, or Public Sector Undertaking, nor in receipt of similar benefits under any law  
- [ ] For second child benefit: second child must be a girl (effective from 01.04.2022)  
- [ ] Beneficiary possesses Aadhaar number (mandatory for availing the scheme)  
- [ ] Beneficiary is not residing in Telangana or Odisha (scheme not implemented there due to state-specific maternity benefit schemes)  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Aadhaar card  
- PAN card  
- Income Tax Return (ITR) – if applicable for income proof  
- GST certificate – if applicable  
- Bank statements (last 3 months)  
- Udyam registration (if applicable)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Aadhaar-mapped bank/post office account details  
- Mobile number (registered and active)  
- Eligibility proof (any one of the following):  
    - SC/ST certificate  
    - Disability certificate (40% or more)  
    - BPL ration card  
    - PMJAY card (Ayushman Bharat)  
    - E-shram card  
    - Kisan Samman Nidhi benefit proof  
    - MGNREGA job card  
    - Income proof (net family income < ₹8 lakh/annum)  
    - NFSA ration card  
- Mother and Child Protection (MCP) / RCHI card  
- Last Menstrual Period (LMP) date  
- Ante-Natal Check-up (ANC) date and proof (at least one ANC within 6 months from LMP)  
- Child birth certificate  
- Child immunization details (proof of first cycle of immunization within 14 weeks)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be clear, legible scans or photographs  
- File size: each document < 2 MB  
- File naming convention: `CaseID_DocumentType_Name.pdf` (e.g., `CASE001_Aadhaar_BeneficiaryName.pdf`)  
- Aadhaar number must match across Aadhaar card, bank account, and application form  
- Mobile number must be active and accessible for OTP verification  
- Bank/post office account must be Aadhaar-linked and active  
- All certificates (caste, disability, income, etc.) must be issued by competent authority and valid  
- MCP/RCHI card, LMP, ANC, birth certificate, and immunization records must be legible and dated correctly  
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in regional language, provide certified translation  

### Client Communication Template
**Subject:** Action Required: Document Collection for Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY) Application  

Dear [Beneficiary Name],  

This is to inform you that we have initiated your application for the **Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)** under the Ministry of Women and Child Development.  

To proceed, please gather and submit the following documents within the next 7 days:  
- Your Aadhaar card  
- Aadhaar-linked bank/post office account details (passbook or cancelled cheque)  
- Active mobile number (for OTP verification)  
- Proof of eligibility (any one: SC/ST certificate, disability certificate, BPL ration card, PMJAY card, E-shram card, Kisan Samman Nidhi, MGNREGA job card, income proof < ₹8 lakh, or NFSA ration card)  
- Mother and Child Protection (MCP) / RCHI card  
- LMP date (from MCP card or medical record)  
- Proof of at least one Ante-Natal Check-up (ANC) within 6 months from LMP  
- Child’s birth certificate  
- Proof of child’s immunization (first cycle completed within 14 weeks of birth)  

Please ensure all documents are clear, legible, and in PDF or JPEG format (each < 2 MB). Name each file as: `CASE[ID]_[DocumentType]_[YourName].pdf`

- Portal for self-registration: https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in  
- For assistance, contact your nearest Anganwadi Worker (AWW) or ASHA worker, or call the PMMVY helpline at **1515** (9:00 AM to 6:00 PM)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for PMMVY. The scheme involves direct benefit transfer based on eligibility and document verification; no detailed project report is needed.  
**Note:** Verify with the block-level officer or AWW/ASHA if any local documentation is required beyond the standard list.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official PMMVY portal: https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in  
2. Click on “New Registration” or “Self-Registration” (citizen login)  
3. Enter beneficiary’s Aadhaar number and verify via OTP sent to registered mobile number  
4. Fill in personal details: name, date of birth, address, etc. (must match Aadhaar)  
5. Enter LMP date (from MCP/RCHI card)  
6. Select eligibility category and upload corresponding proof document  
7. Enter ANC details (date and facility) and upload ANC proof  
8. Enter bank/post office details (ensure Aadhaar-linking) and upload proof (passbook/cancelled cheque)  
9. Enter mobile number (for OTP and status updates)  
10. For pregnant women: upload MCP/RCHI card and LMP/ANC details  
11. For lactating women/post-delivery: upload child’s birth certificate and immunization details (first cycle within 14 weeks)  
12. Review all entries for accuracy before submission  
13. Submit the application and save the acknowledgment slip with Application ID  
14. Note: Field functionaries (AWW/ASHA) can also fill the form on behalf of the beneficiary using their login  
15. Ensure all mandatory fields (marked *) are completed; incomplete forms will be rejected  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All personal details match Aadhaar card  
- [ ] LMP date is entered and within valid range (LMP to childbirth: 140–300 days)  
- [ ] ANC proof uploaded (at least one check-up within 6 months from LMP)  
- [ ] Eligibility proof uploaded and valid  
- [ ] Bank/post office details entered and Aadhaar-linked proof uploaded  
- [ ] Mobile number entered and active  
- [ ] For pregnant applicants: MCP/RCHI card uploaded  
- [ ] For postnatal applicants: child’s birth certificate and immunization proof uploaded  
- [ ] Second child benefit: girl child confirmed and registration done during pregnancy  
- [ ] Application submitted within 270 days from childbirth  
- [ ] Declarations and consent boxes checked  
- [ ] Screenshot of submission saved with Application ID and timestamp

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing Application ID, date, and time of submission  
- Store the Application ID securely in the case file  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 7 days after submission to check status  
- Advise beneficiary to track status via portal using Application ID or registered mobile number  
- Note: Approval is done at block level; funds disbursed via DBT after approval

### Escalation Path
- Level 1: Portal helpdesk – Available via the PMMVY portal (https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in) or helpline **1515** (9:00 AM to 6:00 PM)  
- Level 2: Nodal officer – Block Level Officer (Child Development Project Officer / CDPO) or District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO) – [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]  
- Level 3: RTI filing if no response within statutory SLA (30 days for grievance redressal as per portal grievance dashboard showing avg. resolution time of 5 days)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify approval notification via SMS/email or portal status update  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount: ₹5,000 (first child) or ₹6,000 (second girl child)  
- [ ] Verify instalment schedule:  
    - First child: ₹3,000 after ANC, ₹2,000 after childbirth + 14 weeks immunization  
    - Second girl child: ₹6,000 single instalment after childbirth + 14 weeks immunization  
- [ ] Confirm disbursement via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to Aadhaar-linked bank/post office account  
- [ ] Advise beneficiary to check account for credit and retain transaction proof  
- [ ] Guide beneficiary on utilization of funds for nutrition, rest, and wage loss compensation  
- [ ] Inform beneficiary about eligibility for Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY) after institutional delivery (if applicable)  
- [ ] Obtain beneficiary feedback on application disbursement process  
- [ ] If applicable, assist in submitting Utilization Certificate (UC) – though not typically required for individual beneficiaries under PMMVY (UC is for implementing agencies)  
- [ ] Close case after successful disbursement and beneficiary confirmation
