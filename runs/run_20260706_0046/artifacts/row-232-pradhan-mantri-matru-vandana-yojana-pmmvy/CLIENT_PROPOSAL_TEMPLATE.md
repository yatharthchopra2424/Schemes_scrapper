## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment against PMMVY criteria (SC/ST, disabled, BPL, PMJAY, E-shram, Kisan Samman Nidhi, MGNREGA, income < ₹8 Lakh, NFSA ration card, etc.)
2. Document collection and verification as per scheme requirements
3. Online registration on the PMMVY portal at https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in
4. Application submission and tracking
5. Coordination with block level officer for approval
6. Liaison for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to Aadhaar-linked bank/post office account
7. Post-sanction follow-up and grievance redressal support

*Note: A DPR/project report is not required for this scheme.*
