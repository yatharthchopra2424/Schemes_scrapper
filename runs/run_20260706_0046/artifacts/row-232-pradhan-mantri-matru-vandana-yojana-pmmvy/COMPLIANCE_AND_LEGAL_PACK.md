**Data Handling Policy:**

1. **Storage:** All client data shall be stored in encrypted cloud storage with AES-256 encryption.
2. **Access Control:** Access to client data is restricted to authorized personnel only, using role-based
   access controls (RBAC).
3. **Authentication:** Multi-factor authentication (2FA) is required for all access to sensitive data.
4. **Device Policy:** No personal devices may be used to access or store client data; all work must be
   performed on company-issued, secured devices.
5. **Retention:** Client data shall be retained for a period of 2 years post case closure, after which it
   shall be securely deleted unless longer retention is required by law or scheme guidelines.
6. **Breach Notification:** In the event of a data breach, affected clients and relevant authorities shall
   be notified within 24 hours of discovery.
7. **Scheme-specific note:** All documents submitted to Ministry of Women and Child Development under
   Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY) must be retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit
   purposes.
8. **Disposal:** Secure deletion methods (e.g., cryptographic erasure) shall be used for data disposal
   to ensure data cannot be recovered.
