## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the beneficiary under PMMVY (₹5,000 for first child, ₹6,000 for second girl child).
2. Success fees are contingent on successful application approval and fund disbursement via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT), minimizing client risk.
3. Retainer fees cover documentation, eligibility verification, and application submission support, reflecting the administrative burden of compiling required documents (Aadhaar, bank details, eligibility proof, MCP/RCHI card, LMP/ANC dates, birth certificate, immunization details).
4. Scheme-specific note: PMMVY has a low to moderate risk profile due to its high success rate (4.32 Cr beneficiaries paid out of 5.04 Cr enrolled as of June 2026) and standardized DBT disbursement, though processing delays may occur due to block-level officer approval and bank KYC issues, as evidenced in field reports from Assam and Meghalaya.
