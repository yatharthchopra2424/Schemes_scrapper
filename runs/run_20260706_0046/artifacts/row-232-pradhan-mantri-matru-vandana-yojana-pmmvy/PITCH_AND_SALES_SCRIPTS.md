# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping eligible pregnant and lactating women from socially and economically disadvantaged backgrounds access government maternity benefit schemes like the Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY). This scheme provides direct financial support to improve health and nutrition for mother and child, and compensate for wage loss during pregnancy and after delivery. I’d like to ask a few quick questions to see if you or someone you know might qualify for this support — it only takes a couple of minutes.

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you currently pregnant, or have you given birth in the last 270 days?  
2. Do you belong to any of the following categories: Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), or are you a person with 40% or more disability?  
3. Do you hold a BPL ration card, or are you covered under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Aarogya Yojana (PMJAY/Ayushman Bharat)?  
4. Do you possess an E-shram card, MGNREGA job card, or are you a beneficiary of the Kisan Samman Nidhi scheme?  
5. Is your annual family income less than ₹8 lakh per annum?  
6. Are you a pregnant or lactating Anganwadi Worker (AWW), Anganwadi Helper (AWH), or ASHA worker?  
7. Do you have a ration card under the National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013?  
8. Do you have your Aadhaar card and an Aadhaar-linked bank or post office account ready for Direct Benefit Transfer?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Pregnant or lactating woman (within 270 days of childbirth)  
- [ ] Belongs to SC/ST community  
- [ ] Has 40% or more disability (Divyang Jan)  
- [ ] Holds a BPL ration card  
- [ ] Is a PMJAY (Ayushman Bharat) beneficiary  
- [ ] Possesses an E-shram card  
- [ ] Is a Kisan Samman Nidhi beneficiary (woman farmer)  
- [ ] Holds an MGNREGA job card  
- [ ] Annual family income < ₹8 lakh  
- [ ] Is a pregnant/lactating AWW/AWH/ASHA  
- [ ] Holds NFSA ration card  
- [ ] Has Aadhaar and Aadhaar-linked bank/post office account  
- [ ] Not residing in Telangana or Odisha (scheme not implemented there)  
- [ ] Second child applicant only eligible if the child is a girl (born on or after 01.04.2022)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you may be eligible for the Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY), which provides ₹5,000 for the first child in two instalments or ₹6,000 for a second girl child in a single instalment — paid directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account. Would you like me to explain how the scheme works and how we can help you apply?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**  
Pregnant and lactating women from disadvantaged backgrounds often face wage loss, inadequate nutrition, and limited access to healthcare during pregnancy and after childbirth. Many struggle to afford nutritious food, medical care, or take sufficient rest, which impacts both maternal and child health. The lack of financial support during this critical period can lead to poor health outcomes and increased vulnerability.

**Scheme intro:**  
The Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY), administered by the Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India, is a maternity benefit scheme that provides financial assistance of ₹5,000 for the first child in two instalments (₹3,000 after Ante-Natal Check-up and ₹2,000 after childbirth and 14 weeks of immunization) or ₹6,000 for the second child if it is a girl, paid in a single instalment after childbirth and completion of immunization. Benefits are transferred via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the beneficiary’s Aadhaar-linked bank or post office account.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**  
- You are pregnant or lactating and within 270 days of childbirth  
- You belong to an eligible category (e.g., SC/ST, BPL card holder, PMJAY beneficiary, or have family income < ₹8 lakh)  
- You have Aadhaar and an Aadhaar-linked bank/post office account for DBT  
- You are not residing in Telangana or Odisha (where the scheme is not implemented)

We offer a free, no-obligation eligibility check. If you qualify, we’ll help you gather documents, fill the online application, and track it until approval — all on a success-fee-only basis. You pay nothing unless the benefit is approved and disbursed.
