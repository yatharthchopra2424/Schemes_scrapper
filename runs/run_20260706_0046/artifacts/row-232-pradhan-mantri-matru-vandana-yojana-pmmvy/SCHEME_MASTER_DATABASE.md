### Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India (except Telangana and Odisha)
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Women and Child Development
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** A beneficiary is eligible to register in the PMMVY portal till 270 days from childbirth.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Not applicable (individual beneficiary scheme)
- Age of business: Not applicable
- Sector: Not applicable
- Revenue cap: Women whose net family income is less than ₹8 Lakh per annum
- Employee count: Not applicable
- Other: Women belonging to scheduled castes and scheduled tribes; Women who are partially (40%) or fully disabled (Divyang Jan); Women holder of BPL ration card; Women beneficiaries under Pradhan Mantri Jan Aarogya Yojana (PMJAY) under Ayushman Bharat; Women holding E-shram card; Women farmers who are beneficiaries under Kisan Samman Nidhi; Women holding MGNREGA job card; Pregnant and lactating AWWs/AWHs/ASHAs; Women holding ration card under NFSA Act 2013; Any other category as may be prescribed by the Central Government

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Rs 5000/- for the first child in two instalments (₹3000/- after Ante-Natal Check-up and ₹2000/- after childbirth and 14 weeks of universal immunization); Rs. 6000/- for the second child (if girl child) in a single instalment after childbirth and completion of universal immunization within 14 weeks
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (Direct Benefit Transfer - DBT)
- Duration of benefit: One-time per eligible child

#### Documents Required
1. Aadhaar card
2. Aadhaar mapped bank/post office account details
3. Mobile number
4. Eligibility proof (e.g., SC/ST certificate, BPL ration card, PMJAY card, E-shram card, Kisan Samman Nidhi, MGNREGA job card, income proof, NFSA ration card, disability certificate)
5. MCP/RCHI card (Mother and Child Protection Card)
6. LMP date (Last Menstruation Period)
7. ANC date (Ante-Natal Check-up)
8. Child birth certificate
9. Child immunization details

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the nearest Anganwadi worker/ASHA worker or access the PMMVY portal at https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in.
2. Fill the online application form (self-registration or through AWW/ASHA).
3. Upload required documents including Aadhaar card, Aadhaar-mapped bank/post office account details, mobile number, eligibility proof, MCP/RCHI card, LMP date, ANC date, child birth certificate, and child immunization details.
4. Submit the application.
5. Await approval from block level officer.
6. Upon approval, funds are disbursed via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the Aadhaar-linked bank/post office account.
