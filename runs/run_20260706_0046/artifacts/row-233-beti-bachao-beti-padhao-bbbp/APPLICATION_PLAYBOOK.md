## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The family resides in a district identified with low Child Sex Ratio (CSR) as per Census data (BBBP targets such districts for interventions)
- [ ] The family has a girl child (target beneficiary: girl child)
- [ ] The girl child’s birth is registered (required for linked schemes like Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana)
- [ ] The parent/guardian possesses valid identity proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, etc.)
- [ ] The parent/guardian possesses valid address proof
- [ ] The family is willing to participate in awareness and mobilization activities under BBBP
- [ ] For financial benefits via linked schemes (e.g., Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana), the guardian is prepared to open an account and make a minimum deposit of INR 250
- [ ] The girl child is within the eligible age group for linked schemes (e.g., under 10 years for Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana at time of account opening)

> **Note**: BBBP itself does not provide direct financial transfers; eligibility for financial benefits is determined by linked convergent schemes (e.g., Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana). Verification should confirm linkage to such schemes where applicable.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required. BBBP is an awareness and advocacy-driven scheme; no Detailed Project Report (DPR) is needed for individual beneficiaries. Financial benefits are accessed via linked schemes (e.g., Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana), which require only standard account opening forms, not a DPR.  
**Action**: Verify with nodal officer if applying for district-level BBBP grants (e.g., for community mobilization), but for individual family benefits, DPR is not applicable.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official BBBP portal: https://wcd.nic.in/bbbp-schemes for scheme details and district contact information.  
2. For Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) linkage: Visit an authorized bank or post office with the girl child’s birth certificate and guardian’s ID/address proof.  
3. Fill the SSY account opening form (Form SSY-1) accurately—ensure name, date of birth, and Aadhaar match across documents.  
4. Specify the girl child as the account holder and guardian as the operator until she turns 18.  
5. Deposit the initial amount (minimum INR 250) in cash, cheque, or online transfer as per bank/post office rules.  
6. Nominate a guardian or trusted family member for the account in case of unforeseen circumstances.  
7. Ensure the form is signed by the guardian and attested by the bank/post office official.  
8. Retain the acknowledgment slip and account number for future transactions and tracking.  

> **Portal Note**: The BBBP portal (https://wcd.nic.in/bbbp-schemes) does not host an application form; it provides information and links to district units. Actual SSY account opening occurs at bank/post office branches.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Girl child’s name and date of birth match exactly on birth certificate, Aadhaar, and SSY form  
- [ ] Guardian’s identity and address proofs are valid and not expired and match application details  
- [ ] Photographs are recent and clearly show faces  
- [ ] SSY form (SSY-1) is fully completed, signed, and dated  
- [ ] Initial deposit slip or transaction proof is attached (if submitted)  
- [ ] All declarations in the form are signed by the guardian  
- [ ] Copies of all documents are retained in the case file  
- [ ] Reference to BBBP district unit or helpline is noted for follow-up

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed SSY account opening form and documents at the authorized bank or post office branch.  
- Obtain and screenshot the acknowledgment receipt (including application/reference number, date, and branch stamp).  
- Store the reference number in the case file with label: `SSY_AccountRef_[CaseID]`.  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 7 working days after submission to check account activation status.  
- If applying for non-financial BBBP benefits (e.g., training, awareness), submit a request letter to the District Women and Child Development Officer (DWCDO) or District Magistrate’s office.
