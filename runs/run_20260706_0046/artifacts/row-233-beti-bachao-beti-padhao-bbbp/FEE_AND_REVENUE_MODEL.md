## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with client outcomes and scheme impact  
2. Transparent fee structures with no hidden charges  
3. Risk-sharing model where success fees are contingent on verified benefit realization  
4. Volume discounts for long-term engagements and multi-scheme applications  

**Scheme-specific note:** Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP) does not provide direct financial transfers; benefits are accessed through convergent schemes like Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY). The scheme focuses on awareness, advocacy, and behavioral change, with financial benefits delivered indirectly via linked instruments. Processing is ongoing with no fixed deadlines, reducing time-bound risk but requiring sustained engagement for impact measurement. Success is contingent on district-level implementation and convergence with SSY, which offers a maximum annual deposit of ₹1.5 lakh per girl child. The lack of direct payouts under BBBP increases advisory complexity but lowers financial risk for clients, as benefits are accessed through established financial instruments.
