## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Problem framing:
Families with girl children often face barriers to securing their future due to lack of awareness about government savings schemes, social biases affecting girl child welfare, and difficulty accessing financial instruments that offer long-term, tax-advantaged growth. Many miss out on benefits simply because they don’t know how to link into convergent schemes like Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana through BBBP’s district-level implementation.

### Scheme intro:
The Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP) scheme is a Pan-India initiative by the Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India, designed to prevent gender-biased sex-selective elimination, ensure the survival and protection of the girl child, and promote her education and participation. While BBBP itself focuses on awareness and behavioral change, it facilitates access to financial benefits through convergent schemes like Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY), where guardians can open accounts for girl children with a minimum deposit of ₹250 and a maximum of ₹1.5 lakh per financial year, earning a government-fixed interest rate of 8.2% per annum (compounded annually), with tax-free returns and eligibility for deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act.

### Why they qualify (personalised):
- You have a girl child or work with families that include girl children — core target beneficiary of BBBP  
- You are located in or serve a district with low Child Sex Ratio (CSR), which BBBP prioritizes for intervention  
- You possess or can obtain the girl child’s birth certificate and parent/guardian identity and address proof — key documents required  
- You are interested in long-term, tax-advantaged savings for the girl child’s education and future — aligned with SSY benefits accessible via BBBP
