### Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://wcd.nic.in/bbbp-schemes
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Not applicable (scheme targets families and communities)
- Age of business: Not applicable
- Sector: Not applicable
- Revenue cap: Not applicable
- Employee count: Not applicable
- Other: Families with girl child; communities in districts identified with low Child Sex Ratio (CSR) as per Census data

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: No direct financial transfers under BBBP; financial benefits accessed via linked schemes (e.g., Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana: minimum deposit INR 250, maximum INR 1.5 lakh per financial year, interest rate 8.2% per annum, compounded annually; tax-free interest and tax deduction under Section 80C)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (via linked schemes like Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana)
- Duration of benefit: Annual (for linked schemes like Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana, deposits and benefits recur yearly)

#### Documents Required
1. Birth certificate of the girl child
2. Identity proof of parent/guardian (Aadhaar, Voter ID, etc.)
3. Address proof
4. Photographs of parent/guardian and girl child

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the district-level BBBP implementation unit (usually under District Magistrate or Women and Child Development Officer)
2. Awareness and mobilization activities are conducted at the community level through Anganwadi workers, ASHA, and school teachers
3. For financial benefits under linked schemes (e.g., Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana), visit an authorized bank or post office with the girl child’s birth certificate and parent/guardian ID proof
4. Open SSY account and deposit funds as per rules
5. Monitor progress through district-level BBBP task force meetings and periodic reviews
