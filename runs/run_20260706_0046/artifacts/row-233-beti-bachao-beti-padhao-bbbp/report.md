## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
**Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP)** is a pan-India social welfare scheme implemented by the **Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India**. It operates as a subsidy-type scheme focused on advocacy, behavioral change, and convergence with other government programs to address gender imbalance and promote the welfare of the girl child. The scheme does not provide direct financial transfers but facilitates access to financial benefits through linked schemes such as the Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY). Activities are ongoing throughout the year on a rolling basis, with no fixed deadline for enrollment in linked components.

### Objectives
The scheme has three core objectives:
- Prevent gender-biased sex-selective elimination  
- Ensure survival and protection of the girl child  
- Ensure education and participation of the girl child  

These objectives are pursued through awareness campaigns, community mobilization, training of functionaries, and monitoring of Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives.

### Eligibility Matrix
BBBP does not have individual beneficiary registration. Instead, it targets families and communities in districts identified with low Child Sex Ratio (CSR) as per Census data. Eligibility for financial benefits is accessed through convergent schemes like Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY), which have their own eligibility criteria.

| Criteria | Details |
|--------|--------|
| **Geographic Scope** | Pan-India, focused on districts with low Child Sex Ratio (CSR) as per Census |
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Girl child; families with girl child; communities in low CSR districts |
| **Individual Registration** | Not required for BBBP; benefits delivered via district-level interventions |
| **Linked Scheme Eligibility** | Governed by respective schemes (e.g., SSY requires girl child below 10 years of age) |
| **Implementing Agency** | Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India |
| **Application Portal** | [https://wcd.nic.in/bbbp-schemes](https://wcd.nic.in/bbbp-schemes) |

> **Key Caveat**: Direct financial benefits are not provided under BBBP; they are accessed through convergent schemes like Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana. The scheme focuses on advocacy and behavioral change, with fund utilization monitored for awareness and training components. Impact is assessed through improvements in Child Sex Ratio at birth and girl child enrollment rates.

### Benefits & Financial Support
BBBP itself does not offer direct financial transfers. Financial support is channeled through linked schemes, primarily Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY), and includes non-financial benefits from awareness and capacity-building activities.

#### Financial Benefits (via Linked Schemes)
| Benefit | Details |
|-------|--------|
| **Scheme** | Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) |
| **Minimum Deposit** | INR 250 per financial year |
| **Maximum Deposit** | INR 1.5 lakh per financial year |
| **Interest Rate** | 8.2% per annum (compounded annually, fixed by government) |
| **Tax Benefits** | Tax-free interest; eligible for deduction under Section 80C of Income Tax Act |
| **Account Tenure** | 21 years from date of opening or until marriage after age 18 |
| **Partial Withdrawal** | Allowed for higher education of girl child after she turns 18 (up to 50% of balance) |

#### Non-Financial Benefits
- Awareness campaigns at national, state, and district levels  
- Community mobilization through Anganwadi workers, ASHA, and school teachers  
- Training of government functionaries on gender sensitivity  
- Monitoring of CSR initiatives for girl child welfare  
- Facilitation of convergence with other schemes for:  
  - Scholarships  
  - Distribution of bicycles  
  - Construction of toilets in schools  
  - Financial incentives for girl child education  

### Application Process
The application process varies depending on whether the beneficiary seeks awareness/non-financial components or financial benefits via linked schemes like SSY. For financial benefits, the process involves opening an SSY account at an authorized bank or post office.

#### Required Documents for SSY (Linked Financial Benefit)
1. Birth certificate of the girl child  
2. Identity proof of parent/guardian (Aadhaar, Voter ID, etc.)  
3. Address proof  
4. Photographs of parent/guardian and girl child  

### Implementation & Monitoring
- **Implementing Agency**: Ministry of Women and Child Development (MWCD)  
- **District-Level Unit**: Usually under District Magistrate or Women and Child Development Officer  
- **Awareness Mobilization**: Conducted via Anganwadi workers, ASHA, and school teachers  
- **Monitoring Mechanism**: Progress reviewed through district-level BBBP task force meetings and periodic reviews  
- **Status**: Rolling basis — no fixed deadline for account opening under linked schemes like SSY. BBBP activities are ongoing throughout the year.
