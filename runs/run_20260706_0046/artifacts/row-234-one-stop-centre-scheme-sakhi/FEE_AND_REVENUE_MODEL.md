## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured around the tangible outcomes delivered, such as successful scheme application, approval, or benefit realization for the client.
2. **Risk Sharing**: Success fees are contingent on achieving the desired outcome, aligning consultant incentives with client success.
3. **Transparency**: All fees, including retainer and success fee components, are clearly communicated and agreed upon in writing prior to engagement.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The One Stop Centre Scheme (Sakhi) provides direct, free-of-cost services to women affected by violence and does not offer financial grants or subsidies to intermediaries, NGOs, or implementing entities for application consulting. As such, traditional success-fee consulting models based on financial benefit realization do not apply. Any consulting engagement would likely focus on operational support, training, or system setup for implementing agencies (e.g., State Governments/UT Administrations), which would require a different fee structure (e.g., fixed-price project-based or retainer for advisory services).
