## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because I specialise in helping organisations and individuals access government support schemes designed for vulnerable populations. The One Stop Centre Scheme (Sakhi), run by the Ministry of Women and Child Development, provides critical 24x7 support services—medical, legal, shelter, and counselling—for women affected by violence across India. I’d like to understand if your work intersects with supporting survivors of gender-based violence, so I can see whether this scheme could be a relevant resource for the communities you serve.

### Discovery Questions
- Are you currently working with or supporting women who have experienced domestic violence, sexual assault, trafficking, or other forms of gender-based violence?
- Do you operate in a district where access to integrated emergency services (medical, legal, psychological) for survivors is a challenge?
- Are you looking for ways to refer survivors to immediate, free-of-cost support such as temporary shelter, FIR assistance, or counselling?
- Does your organisation collaborate with local authorities, hospitals, or legal aid providers when assisting survivors?
- Are you aware of any One Stop Centres (OSCs) in your area, and have you referred clients to them before?
- What barriers have you encountered in ensuring timely access to medical, legal, or psychological care for women in distress?
- Would having a reliable, walk-in, 24x7 centre for immediate support improve your ability to assist survivors effectively?
- Are you involved in skill training, employment linkage, or long-term rehabilitation efforts for survivors of violence?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Works with or supports women affected by violence (domestic, sexual, trafficking, gender-based)
- [ ] Operates in India (Pan-India scheme coverage)
- [ ] Seeks to refer survivors to immediate emergency support (medical, legal, shelter, counselling)
- [ ] Interested in accessing free, no-documentation-required government support services
- [ ] Collaborates or wishes to collaborate with police, hospitals, legal aid, or shelter homes
- [ ] Focuses on or has capacity to support girls under 18 in sexual assault cases (if applicable)
- [ ] Aims to strengthen referral linkages to skill training, employment, or long-term rehabilitation
- [ ] Operates in a district where an OSC is functional or could be leveraged for referrals

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your work involves supporting women in crisis—and that’s exactly who the One Stop Centre Scheme (Sakhi) is designed to help. This Ministry of Women and Child Development initiative provides free, 24x7 integrated support—including medical aid, legal assistance, temporary shelter, and counselling—without requiring any documents upfront. Let me explain how it works and how it could support your efforts.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Women affected by violence often face fragmented, delayed, or inaccessible support—struggling to access medical care, file police reports, get legal advice, or find safe shelter, especially in moments of crisis. This lack of coordinated response can delay justice, worsen trauma, and leave survivors without critical immediate help.

**Scheme intro:**
The One Stop Centre Scheme (Sakhi), administered by the Ministry of Women and Child Development, is a centrally sponsored subsidy scheme that provides integrated emergency support and assistance to women affected by violence under one roof. Services include emergency medical aid, police assistance (FIR filing, NCR recording), legal aid and counselling, psycho-social counselling, temporary shelter for up to 5 days, video conferencing for court proceedings, and referrals to skill training, employment, and long-term rehabilitation. All services are free of cost, and the Government of India bears 100% of the cost for both non-recurring and recurring components.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You support women affected by domestic violence, sexual assault, trafficking, or gender-based violence — the core eligibility group.
- You seek immediate, accessible emergency response — which the OSC provides via walk-in, 24x7 access.
- You aim to coordinate with police, hospitals, or legal services — a key function of the OSC model.
- You work with or wish to support girls under 18 in sexual assault cases — explicitly covered under the scheme.
