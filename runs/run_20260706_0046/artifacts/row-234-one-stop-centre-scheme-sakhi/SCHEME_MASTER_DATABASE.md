### One Stop Centre Scheme (Sakhi)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Women and Child Development
- **Portal / Application URL:** [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Not applicable (individual beneficiary scheme)
- Age of business: Not applicable
- Sector: Not applicable
- Revenue cap: Not applicable
- Employee count: Not applicable
- Other: Any woman affected by violence, including domestic violence, sexual assault, trafficking, or any other form of gender-based violence, irrespective of age, class, caste, education, marital status, race, or culture, is eligible to access services at the One Stop Centre. The scheme also extends support to girls below 18 years of age in cases of sexual assault.

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 100% financial support for setting up and functioning of One Stop Centres (non-recurring and recurring components) borne by Government of India; all services provided free of cost to beneficiaries
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit to State Governments/UT Administrations for construction, renovation, equipment, manpower, and operational expenses as per approved norms
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing (services available 24x7, 365 days a year)

#### Documents Required
1. No documents are mandatory for availing services at the One Stop Centre
2. Services are accessible without any precondition of documentation

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Women affected by violence can directly walk into the nearest One Stop Centre or be referred by any individual or organization.
2. Upon arrival, the centre provides immediate medical, legal, and psychological first aid.
3. A case is registered and a needs assessment is conducted.
4. Based on assessment, coordinated with police, hospitals, legal services, and shelter homes as required.
5. Follow-up and tracking of the case is ensured for timely resolution and rehabilitation.
