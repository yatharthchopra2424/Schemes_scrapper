## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **One Stop Centre Scheme (Sakhi)** is a centrally sponsored sub-scheme under the Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India. It is designed to provide integrated support and assistance to women affected by violence under one roof. The scheme operates on a **pan-India** geographic scope and ensures services are available **24x7, 365 days a year** on a walk-in basis. The implementing agency is the Ministry of Women and Child Development, and the scheme follows a **subsidy** model where the Government of India bears **100% of the cost** for both non-recurring and recurring components.

### Objectives
The primary objectives of the One Stop Centre Scheme (Sakhi) are:
- To provide integrated support and assistance to women affected by violence under one roof.
- To facilitate immediate emergency response and access to medical, legal, psychological, and counselling services.
- To ensure coordination with existing mechanisms such as police, hospitals, and legal aid services.
- To create a safe and secure environment for women to seek help without fear or stigma.
- To strengthen the response system for addressing gender-based violence through convergence of services.

> **> Important Caveats**  
> - Temporary shelter is limited to a maximum of **5 days**; no long-term residential care is provided.  
> - Service availability is **contingent on the operational status** of the One Stop Centre in the respective district.  
> - The scheme focuses on **emergency and immediate response**; it does not provide long-term rehabilitation beyond initial support.  
> - Despite 24x7 availability, access depends on physical proximity to an operational OSC.

### Application Process Flow
The One Stop Centre Scheme (Sakhi) does not require a formal application process for beneficiaries. Access is immediate and walk-in based. However, internally, the centre follows a structured case management process.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Women affected by violence] --> B{Access Point}
    B -->|Direct Walk-in| C[Nearest One Stop Centre]
    B -->|Referral| C
    C --> D[Immediate First Aid: Medical, Legal, Psychological]
    D --> E[Case Registration & Needs Assessment]
    E --> F{Assessment Outcome}
    F -->|Medical Need| G[Coordination with Hospitals/Health Services]
    F -->|Legal Need| H[Police Assistance: FIR/NCR, Legal Aid]
    F -->|Shelter Need| I[Temporary Shelter (up to 5 days)]
    F -->|Psycho-social Need| J[Counselling Services]
    F -->|Referral Need| K[Linkage to Skill Training, Employment, Long-term Rehab]
    G --> L[Follow-up & Case Tracking]
    H --> L
    I --> L
    J --> L
    K --> L
    L --> M[Timely Resolution & Rehabilitation]
```

> **> Key Takeaway**  
> There is **no application form, documentation, or eligibility screening** required for beneficiaries to access services. The process is designed for **immediate, barrier-free access** during moments of crisis. The internal workflow focuses on rapid triage, coordinated response, and follow-up to ensure safety and rehabilitation.

### Contact & Access Information
- **National Helpline**: 181 (Women Helpline, integrated with OSC in many states)  
- **Access Mode**: Walk-in or referral-based entry to nearest operational One Stop Centre  
- **Availability**: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year  
- **Implementing Authority**: Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India  
- **Scheme Status**: Active and operational across Pan-India (subject to state-level implementation)
