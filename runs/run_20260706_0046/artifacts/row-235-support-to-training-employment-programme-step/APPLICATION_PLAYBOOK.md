## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is an Institution/Organisation set up as an Autonomous Body under Ministries/Departments of Government of India  
- [ ] Applicant is a State Government/UT Administration entity  
- [ ] Applicant is a Voluntary Organisation  
- [ ] Applicant is a Non-Governmental Organisation (NGO)  
- [ ] Applicant is a Co-operative  
- [ ] Applicant is another similar entity engaged in training and employment generation for women  
- [ ] Project proposal is aligned with STEP objectives in traditional sectors (agriculture, horticulture, food processing, handlooms, handicrafts, textile and embroidery, zari work, sericulture, fisheries, and allied sectors)  
- [ ] Training program targets women aged 16 years and above  
- [ ] Project includes skill training to enhance employability and entrepreneurship among women  
- [ ] Project aims to enable women to become self-employed or start their own enterprises  
- [ ] Project includes capacity building and income generation through training and market linkages  

> **Note**: The scheme does not specify turnover, employee count, or age of business as eligibility criteria for implementing agencies. Focus is on organizational type and project alignment.

- [ ] Detailed Project Report (DPR) including objectives, beneficiary details, training modules, budget, and sustainability plan  
- [ ] Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) draft with the Ministry of Women and Child Development (to be signed post-approval)  
- [ ] List of identified traditional sector(s) for training (as per scheme guidelines)  
- [ ] Beneficiary selection criteria and targeting plan for women aged 16+  
- [ ] Trainer qualifications and honorarium structure  
- [ ] Raw materials and equipment procurement plan  
- [ ] Monitoring and evaluation framework  

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document unless specified otherwise)  
- [ ] Organisation name matches exactly across all documents (especially PAN, incorporation certificate, and bank statements)  
- [ ] Financial documents are certified by a Chartered Accountant  
- [ ] DPR is signed and stamped by the authorised signatory of the organisation  
- [ ] All documents are in English or Hindi; if in regional language, provide certified translation  
- [ ] MOU draft is on organisation’s letterhead and includes clauses for compliance, reporting, and fund utilization

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Required**: Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is mandatory for STEP scheme application as per evidence.  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Organisation Background and Credentials  
- Project Objectives aligned with STEP (enhancing employability/entrepreneurship of women)  
- Target Beneficiaries (number, age 16+, demographic details)  
- Training Modules (specify traditional sector: e.g., handlooms, handicrafts, sericulture, etc.)  
- Training Duration, Frequency, and Location  
- Trainer Details (qualifications, honorarium)  
- Curriculum and Pedagogy  
- Raw Materials and Equipment List with Cost Estimates  
- Budget Breakdown (non-recurring and recurring expenses)  
- Sustainability and Exit Plan  
- Market Linkages and Post-Training Support  
- Monitoring and Evaluation Framework  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
- Annexures: Beneficiary list, trainer MOUs, quotations for equipment  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal via the Ministry of Women and Child Development website: [https://wcd.nic.in](https://wcd.nic.in)  
2. Navigate to the STEP scheme section and download the official application form or DPR submission guidelines  
3. Fill all fields in block letters; avoid overwriting or use of correction fluid  
4. Ensure the organisation’s name, PAN, and address match exactly with incorporation certificate and bank records  
5. In the ‘Project Details’ section, clearly specify the traditional sector(s) of training as per STEP guidelines  
6. State the number of women beneficiaries (aged 16+) to be trained, with breakup by age group if possible  
7. Provide a realistic and itemised budget; avoid lump-sum figures  
8. Declare that the organisation has no pending legal or financial disputes and is not blacklisted by any government agency  
9. Attach all documents in the prescribed order and format as per the checklist  
10. Authorised signatory must sign and stamp the final application and DPR  
11. Keep a copy of the submitted application for internal records  
12. Verify portal-specific requirements (e.g., file format, size limits) before final upload  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All sections of the application form and DPR are complete  
- [ ] Financial projections are realistic and based on quotations  
- [ ] Beneficiary count and age criteria (16+) are clearly stated  
- [ ] Training modules are in identified traditional sectors (agriculture, handlooms, handicrafts, etc.)  
- [ ] Budget distinguishes between non-recurring (equipment, infrastructure) and recurring (honorarium, raw materials) costs  
- [ ] Sustainability plan outlines post-training income generation or market linkages  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached and properly labelled  
- [ ] Declarations regarding legal compliance, non-blacklisting, and accuracy of information are signed  
- [ ] Authorised signatory has signed and stamped the DPR and application form  
- [ ] File names follow convention: `SchemeName_OrgName_DocumentType_Date.pdf` (e.g., `STEP_ABCNGO_DPR_20240515.pdf`)  
- [ ] PDF files are optimized (text-selectable, not scanned images unless necessary)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and securely store the official approval letter from the Ministry of Women and Child Development  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount and duration of the project as stated in the approval  
- [ ] Sign the Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the Ministry within the stipulated timeframe  
- [ ] Set up a dedicated bank account for STEP funds if required by the MOU  
- [ ] Communicate the approval to beneficiaries and trainers; commence training as per the approved DPR  
- [ ] Prepare and submit periodic progress reports (quarterly or as specified) to the Ministry  
- [ ] Utilize funds strictly as per the approved budget; maintain separate books of account  
- [ ] Prepare Utilization Certificates (UCs) along with audited accounts for each tranche  
- [ ] Submit UCs and progress reports to the Ministry for release of subsequent installments  
- [ ] Cooperate with monitoring and evaluation visits by the Ministry or its designated agencies  
- [ ] Upon project completion, submit final report, final UC, and asset verification statement  
- [ ] Ensure successful utilization of funds to process success fee invoice as per consultancy agreement
