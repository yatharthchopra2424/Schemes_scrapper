| Stage | Description | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 01 - Lead | Initial contact made | Schedule discovery call |
| 02 - Qualified | Eligibility confirmed for Support to Training & Employment Programme (STEP) | Send proposal |
| 03 - Engaged | Retainer signed | Start onboarding |
| 04 - Docs collection | Gathering documents | Chase missing items |
| 05 - In preparation | Drafting application on [https://www.wcd.nic.in/schemes/support-training-employment-programme-step](https://www.wcd.nic.in/schemes/support-training-employment-programme-step) | Internal review – Ensure DPR aligns with traditional sectors (agriculture, horticulture, food processing, handlooms, handicrafts, textile and embroidery, zari work, sericulture, fisheries, allied sectors) and includes beneficiary details (women aged 16+), training modules, budget, and sustainability plan. |
| 06 - Submitted | Application filed on [https://www.wcd.nic.in/schemes/support-training-employment-programme-step](https://www.wcd.nic.in/schemes/support-training-employment-programme-step) | Begin follow-up – Submission is typically via hard copy to the Ministry of Women and Child Development; confirm current mode via official notification. |
| 07 - Under review | With [Ministry of Women and Child Development] | Monitor + respond to queries – Be prepared for appraisal committee questions on project feasibility, beneficiary selection, and cost-effectiveness. |
| 08 - Approved | Sanction letter received | Invoice success fee – Follow up for MOU execution post-approval. |
| 09 - Disbursed | Money received by client | Close case, request testimonial – Ensure timely submission of progress reports and utilization certificates to avoid fund recall. |
| 10 - Rejected | Application declined | Analyze, re-apply or alternate scheme – Common reasons: weak DPR, misalignment with traditional sectors, insufficient beneficiary focus, or inadequate sustainability plan. |
