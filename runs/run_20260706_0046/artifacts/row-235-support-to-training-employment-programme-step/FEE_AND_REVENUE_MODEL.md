## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-aligned pricing**: Fees are calibrated to the project-based financial assistance under STEP, which varies by scope and is sanctioned by the Ministry of Women and Child Development based on detailed project reports.
2. **Risk-adjusted success fees**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s medium confidence level and project-based nature, where approval depends on DPR quality, MOU execution, and compliance with reporting requirements.
3. **Retainer for complexity**: Retainers cover the intensive effort required to prepare Detailed Project Reports (DPRs), MOUs, and sustainability plans — critical for STEP eligibility.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: STEP has a moderate risk profile due to its project-based sanctioning process and reliance on Ministry appraisal. Processing timelines are not explicitly defined in evidence, but the multi-step application (DPR submission → approval → MOU → implementation → reporting) suggests a lag of 3–6 months from submission to fund disbursement, affecting revenue recognition timing.
