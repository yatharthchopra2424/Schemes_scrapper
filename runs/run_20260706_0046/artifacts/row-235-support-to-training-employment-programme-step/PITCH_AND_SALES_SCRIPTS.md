## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping women-focused organisations and training providers access government support for skill development initiatives. The Support to Training & Employment Programme (STEP) from the Ministry of Women and Child Development is designed specifically to strengthen employability and entrepreneurship among women through training in traditional sectors. I’d like to understand your current work in this space to see if STEP could support your next phase of growth.

### Discovery Questions
- What traditional sectors are you currently providing training in (e.g., agriculture, handlooms, handicrafts, food processing, textiles, sericulture, fisheries)?
- What is the age profile of the women you train — are they 16 years and above?
- Are you an autonomous body, voluntary organisation, NGO, cooperative, or similar entity engaged in women’s training and employment generation?
- Do you have a project proposal ready for setting up or expanding training infrastructure in a traditional sector?
- What is your current capacity to manage recurring and non-recurring expenses such as trainer honorariums, equipment, and raw materials?
- Are you prepared to submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR) and enter into an MOU with the Ministry of Women and Child Development?
- What outcomes are you aiming for in terms of self-employment or enterprise creation among your trainees?
- Have you previously accessed government funding for skill development projects?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Organisation is an Autonomous Body under Govt. of India/State Govt., Voluntary Organisation, NGO, Cooperative, or similar entity
- [ ] Engaged in training and employment generation for women
- [ ] Target beneficiaries are women aged 16 years and above
- [ ] Project proposal aligns with STEP objectives in identified traditional sectors (agriculture, horticulture, food processing, handlooms, handicrafts, textile and embroidery, zari work, sericulture, fisheries, allied sectors)
- [ ] Capable of preparing a Detailed Project Report (DPR)
- [ ] Ready to enter into an MOU with the Ministry of Women and Child Development
- [ ] Able to submit progress reports, utilization certificates, and audit reports
- [ ] Project focuses on skill training to enhance employability and entrepreneurship

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your organisation’s work in [mention specific traditional sector from discovery] with women aged 16+ aligns closely with the Support to Training & Employment Programme (STEP). This scheme provides project-based financial assistance from the Ministry of Women and Child Development to cover training infrastructure, equipment, raw materials, and trainer honorariums — exactly the kind of support that could help scale your impact.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Women in traditional sectors often lack access to structured skill training that leads to sustainable income or entrepreneurship. Training providers face significant upfront and recurring costs — for equipment, raw materials, trainer honorariums — which limit their ability to scale quality programmes, especially in underserved areas.

**Scheme intro:**
The Support to Training & Employment Programme (STEP), administered by the Ministry of Women and Child Development, is a pan-India subsidy scheme that provides project-based financial assistance to eligible implementing agencies. Support covers training infrastructure, procurement of equipment and raw materials, honorarium to trainers, and other recurring and non-recurring expenses, sanctioned based on detailed project reports.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your organisation is a [insert from discovery: e.g., voluntary organisation/NGO/cooperative] engaged in training and employment generation for women  
- You train women aged 16 years and above in identified traditional sectors such as [insert sector from discovery: e.g., handlooms, food processing]  
- You are prepared to submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR) and enter into an MOU with the Ministry  
- Your project aims to enhance employability and enable self-employment or entrepreneurship among women

| "I don't have the documents ready" | The three most commonly missing documents are: 1) a comprehensive Detailed Project Report (DPR) with clear sustainability and market linkage plans, 2) audited accounts and audit reports from the previous financial year, and 3) a draft Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) ready for Ministry coordination.
