### Support to Training & Employment Programme (STEP)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Women and Child Development
- **Portal / Application URL:** [https://wcd.nic.in/schemes/support-training-employment-programme-step](https://wcd.nic.in/schemes/support-training-employment-programme-step)
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Implementing agencies (Institutions/Organisations set up as Autonomous Bodies under Ministries/Departments of Government of India, State Governments/UT Administrations, Voluntary Organisations, Non-Governmental Organisations, Co-operatives, and other similar entities engaged in training and employment generation for women)
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Traditional sectors (agriculture, horticulture, food processing, handlooms, handicrafts, textile and embroidery, zari work, sericulture, fisheries, and allied sectors)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Women aged 16 years and above across India are eligible to benefit from the scheme

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance for setting up training infrastructure, procurement of equipment, raw materials, honorarium to trainers, and other recurring and non-recurring expenses related to training programs (project-based, sanctioned by Ministry)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit to implementing agencies
- Duration of benefit: Project-based (duration as per approved Detailed Project Report)

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
2. Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the Ministry
3. Progress reports
4. Utilization certificates
5. Accounts and audit reports

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify a project proposal aligned with STEP objectives in traditional sectors.
2. Prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR) including objectives, beneficiary details, training modules, budget, and sustainability plan.
3. Submit the DPR to the Ministry of Women and Child Development for appraisal and approval.
4. Upon approval, enter into a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the Ministry.
5. Implement the training program as per the approved DPR.
6. Submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates of accounts to the Ministry.
7. Undergo monitoring and evaluation by the Ministry or its designated agencies.
