## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The Support to Training & Employment Programme (STEP) is a centrally sponsored subsidy scheme implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development (MWCD) with pan-India geographic scope. The scheme focuses on providing skill training in traditional sectors to enhance employability and entrepreneurship among women aged 16 years and above, enabling them to become self-employed or start their own enterprises.

### Objectives
- To provide skills that enhance employability and entrepreneurship among women
- To provide competencies and skills that enable women to become self-employed/entrepreneurs
- To cover traditional sectors such as agriculture, horticulture, food processing, handlooms, handicrafts, textile and embroidery, zari work, sericulture, fisheries, and allied sectors

**Application Portal**: Applications are submitted to the Ministry of Women and Child Development. Specific portal details should be verified on the MWCD official website.

**Key Documents Required**:
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
2. Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the Ministry
3. Progress reports
4. Utilization certificates
5. Accounts and audit reports

**Process Steps**:
1. Identify a project proposal aligned with STEP objectives in traditional sectors
2. Prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR) including objectives, beneficiary details, training modules, budget, and sustainability plan
3. Submit the DPR to the Ministry of Women and Child Development for appraisal and approval
4. Upon approval, enter into a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the Ministry
5. Implement the training program as per the approved DPR
6. Submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates of accounts to the Ministry
7. Undergo monitoring and evaluation by the Ministry or its designated agencies

> **Important Caveats**:
> - The scheme is implemented through project-based sanctions to eligible agencies
> - Benefits are extended only to women aged 16 years and above
> - Training must be conducted in identified traditional sectors as per scheme guidelines
> - Financial support is quantum-based and sanctioned by MWCD after DPR appraisal
> - Implementing agencies must comply with reporting requirements (progress reports, utilization certificates, audit reports)
