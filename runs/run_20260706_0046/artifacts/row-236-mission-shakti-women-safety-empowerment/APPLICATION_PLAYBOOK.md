## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Client is a State Government or Union Territory Administration (as implementation is carried out through State Governments and UT Administrations)
- [ ] Client has conducted a needs assessment and gap analysis for women's safety and empowerment initiatives
- [ ] Client proposes activities aligned with scheme objectives: One Stop Centres (OSCs), Women Helplines (WHLs), Sakhi Niwas (working women's hostels), Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP) activities, capacity building, awareness campaigns, or strengthening of Swadhar Grehs/Ujjwala Homes
- [ ] Client commits to cost-sharing as per prescribed ratios: 90:10 (Centre:State) for North Eastern and Himalayan States, 60:40 for all other States, 100% central funding for Union Territories without Legislature
- [ ] Client agrees to submit Utilization Certificates and Audit Reports for fund release (contingency clause)
- [ ] Client confirms implementation will follow Ministry guidelines and norms
- [ ] Client confirms funds will not be diverted to other schemes or purposes
- [ ] Client accepts potential third-party monitoring and evaluation

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts (Eligibility, Application Process, Key Caveats, Objectives); Ministry of Women and Child Development, Mission Shakti guidelines

### Universal Documents (always required)
- [ ] Incorporation/Establishment proof of State Government/UT Administration (Government Order or equivalent)
- [ ] PAN card of the State/UT nodal department
- [ ] ITR of the nodal department (if applicable; otherwise, financial statements)
- [ ] GST registration (if applicable for any procurement under the scheme)
- [ ] Bank statements of the designated nodal agency for fund transfer
- [ ] Udyam registration (if any implementing partners are MSMEs; otherwise, not applicable)
- [ ] Audited financial statements of the State/UT for the previous financial year

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- [ ] Annual Action Plan (detailing proposed activities, budget, timelines, and gap analysis)
- [ ] Utilization Certificate of previous funds received under Mission Shakti or related schemes
- [ ] Audit Report of previous fund utilization
- [ ] Progress Report on ongoing or completed scheme components
- [ ] Bank details (account number, IFSC, bank name) for central fund transfer
- [ ] List of beneficiaries covered (disaggregated by category: women, girl children, adolescent girls, pregnant/lactating mothers, elderly women, SC/ST/OBC, rural/tribal, EWS)
- [ ] Details of infrastructure developed (e.g., OSC buildings, WHL equipment, Sakhi Niwas hostel facilities)

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size 10 MB per file unless otherwise specified)
- [ ] Names and official designations match across all documents (e.g., nodal officer name consistent in Action Plan and UC)
- [ ] Utilization Certificate and Audit Report are certified by a Chartered Accountant or Government Auditor
- [ ] Annual Action Plan is signed by the competent authority (e.g., Principal Secretary, Secretary of WCD department)
- [ ] All documents are in English or Hindi; if in regional language, provide certified translation
- [ ] Bank details are verified and match the agency registered for fund receipt
- [ ] Beneficiary list includes Aadhaar or other ID proof where applicable (as per Ministry guidelines)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is not explicitly required for the Annual Action Plan submission under Mission Shakti. However, the Annual Action Plan must include detailed components such as activity-wise budget, timelines, implementing agencies, and expected outcomes. If the nodal officer requests a DPR for specific infrastructure components (e.g., OSC construction), verify requirements with the Ministry’s nodal officer.  
**Status**: Verify with nodal officer whether DPR is needed for specific project components.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use the official Annual Action Plan template provided by the Ministry of Women and Child Development (if available); otherwise, follow the structure outlined in scheme guidelines.  
2. Clearly state the financial year for which the plan is being submitted.  
3. Provide a clear needs assessment and gap analysis justifying each proposed component (OSC, WHL, etc.).  
4. Break down the budget Centre-State share as per applicable ratio (90:10, 60:40, or 100%).  
5. Ensure all financial figures are in INR lakhs or crores as specified.  
6. Attach all scheme-specific documents as annexures and reference them in the plan.  
7. Declaration must be signed by the authorized officer (e.g., Secretary, WCD Department) stating compliance with guidelines and non-diversion of funds.  
8. Double-check that beneficiary targeting includes special focus on SC/ST/OBC, rural/tribal, and economically weaker sections.  

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts (Application Process, Benefits, Eligibility); Standard government subsidy application practices
