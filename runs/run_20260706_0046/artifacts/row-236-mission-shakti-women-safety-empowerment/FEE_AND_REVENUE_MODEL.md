## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial scale of Mission Shakti funding (₹20,989 Cr fund size), ensuring consultant compensation aligns with the significant public value delivered through women's safety and empowerment initiatives.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect moderate risk due to the annual application cycle and dependency on State/UT compliance with documentation (Utilization Certificates, Audit Reports) and funding ratios; processing delays are common if documentation is incomplete.
3. **Transparency & Compliance**: All fees are clearly disclosed and structured to comply with government consulting norms, avoiding any perception of contingent success incentives that could undermine procurement integrity.
4. **Scalable Engagement Model**: Retainers cover ongoing advisory for plan preparation, documentation, and compliance tracking, while success fees are tied to fund approval and release, incentivizing timely and accurate submissions.
