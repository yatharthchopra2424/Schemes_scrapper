## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping state government departments and UT administrations access central government funding for women's safety and empowerment initiatives. Given your work in social welfare or women and child development, I wanted to explore whether Mission Shakti – Women Safety & Empowerment could support your current efforts to strengthen institutional mechanisms for women's protection and empowerment in your jurisdiction.

### Discovery Questions
- What are the key gaps you've identified in women's safety infrastructure (such as One Stop Centres, Women Helplines, or working women's hostels) in your districts?
- Are you currently planning or implementing any Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP) activities or awareness generation campaigns for women's rights?
- What is your timeline for submitting the Annual Action Plan for the upcoming financial year to the Ministry of Women and Child Development?
- Do you have the Utilization Certificate and Audit Report from the previous financial year ready for submission?
- Which vulnerable groups (e.g., SC/ST/OBC women, rural/tribal women, economically weaker sections) are you prioritising in your current women's safety initiatives?
- What is the estimated budget you have allocated for setting up or strengthening institutions like Swadhar Grehs or Ujjwala Homes in your state/UT?
- Are you facing challenges in converging multiple women-focused schemes or programs at the district or block level?
- What kind of capacity building or training needs have your functionaries expressed regarding women's safety protocols and legal provisions?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is a State Government or UT Administration (Implementing Agency must be State Govt/UT Admin)
- [ ] Target beneficiaries include women and/or girl children, with focus on vulnerable/marginalized groups (SC/ST/OBC, rural/tribal, EWS)
- [ ] Proposed activities align with scheme components: OSCs, WHLs, Sakhi Niwas, BBBP, capacity building, awareness campaigns, or strengthening Swadhar Grehs/Ujjwala Homes
- [ ] Annual Action Plan can be prepared based on gap analysis and needs assessment
- [ ] Utilization Certificate and Audit Report from previous funds are available or can be obtained
- [ ] Willing to comply with funding ratio (90:10 for NE/Himalayan states, 60:40 for others, 100% central for UTs without legislature)
- [ ] Funds will not be diverted to other schemes and will follow Ministry guidelines
- [ ] Open to third-party monitoring and evaluation

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears Mission Shakti – Women Safety & Empowerment could directly support your plans to expand One Stop Centres and strengthen women helplines. The scheme provides financial assistance for exactly these types of infrastructure and services, with funding released based on approved Annual Action Plans. Let me share how this works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
State governments and UT administrations often struggle to establish and sustain critical women's safety infrastructure like One Stop Centres and Women Helplines due to limited state budgets, fragmented funding sources, and challenges in accessing timely central government support. This delays essential services such as counselling, medical aid, legal assistance, and temporary shelter for women in distress, particularly in rural and tribal areas.

**Scheme intro:**
Mission Shakti – Women Safety & Empowerment is a centrally sponsored subsidy scheme administered by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. It provides financial support for setting up One Stop Centres (OSCs), Women Helplines (WHLs), Sakhi Niwas (working women's hostels), and supporting Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP) activities, capacity building, awareness campaigns, and strengthening existing institutions like Swadhar Grehs and Ujjwala Homes. Funding is released based on annual plans and utilization certificates, with cost-sharing ratios of 90:10 for North Eastern and Himalayan States, 60:40 for all other States, and 100% central funding for Union Territories without Legislature.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your focus on vulnerable women (SC/ST/OBC, rural/tribal, economically weaker sections) aligns with the scheme's special emphasis on marginalized groups
- Your planned or existing work on One Stop Centres, Women Helplines, or Sakhi Niwas matches core scheme components
- Your responsibility to submit Annual Action Plans and Utilization Certificates fits the required application process
- Your jurisdiction's status (State/UT) determines the applicable funding ratio for central support
