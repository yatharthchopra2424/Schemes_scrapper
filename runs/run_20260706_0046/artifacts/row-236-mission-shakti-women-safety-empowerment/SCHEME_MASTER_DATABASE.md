### Mission Shakti – Women Safety & Empowerment
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Women and Child Development
- **Portal / Application URL:** [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Annual cycle — Action Plans to be submitted by States/UTs each financial year, typically before the start of the fiscal year.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: State Governments and UT Administrations
- Age of business: Not applicable
- Sector: Social Welfare / Women and Child Development
- Revenue cap: Not applicable
- Employee count: Not applicable
- Other: Must submit Annual Action Plan based on gap analysis and needs assessment; must contribute State share as per prescribed funding ratio (90:10 for NE/Himalayan States, 60:40 for other States, 100% central for UTs without Legislature); implementation must follow Ministry guidelines; funds cannot be diverted to other schemes.

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support under a centrally sponsored scheme with cost-sharing; Fund Size: ₹20,989 Cr; benefits include financial assistance for One Stop Centres (OSCs), Women Helplines (WHLs), Sakhi Niwas (working women's hostels), Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP) activities, capacity building, training, awareness campaigns, and strengthening of institutions like Swadhar Grehs and Ujjwala Homes.
- Disbursement mode: Funds released in instalments based on utilization of previous grants and submission of required documents.
- Duration of benefit: Annual (renewable each financial year based on approved Action Plan).

#### Documents Required
1. Annual Action Plan
2. Utilization Certificate of previous funds
3. Audit Report
4. Progress Report
5. Bank details for fund transfer
6. List of beneficiaries covered
7. Details of infrastructure developed

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. State Governments/UT Administrations prepare Annual Action Plans based on gap analysis and needs assessment.
2. Plans are submitted to the Ministry of Women and Child Development for appraisal.
3. Ministry appraises and approves the plans.
4. Funds are released in instalments based on utilization of previous grants and submission of required documents.
5. States/UTs implement the scheme components through designated nodal agencies.
6. Monitoring and evaluation are conducted through periodic reviews and third-party audits.
