# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a woman who has attained 18 years of age  
- [ ] No other LPG connection from any Oil Marketing Company (OMC) exists within the same household  
- [ ] Applicant belongs to a poor household based on submission of a deprivation declaration (as per prescribed format)  
- [ ] Applicant belongs to one of the eligible categories:  
  - SC Households  
  - ST Households  
  - Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin)  
  - Most Backward Classes  
  - Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)  
  - Tea and Ex-Tea Garden tribes  
  - Forest Dwellers  
  - People residing in Islands and River Islands  
  - SECC Households (AHL TIN)  
  - Poor Household as per 14-point declaration  
- [ ] Household does not have a Piped Natural Gas (PNG) connection  
- [ ] Applicant will not position any other LPG gas installations in the same kitchen  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Proof of Identity (Aadhaar copy of Applicant)  
- Proof of Address (only if Address in Aadhaar differs from current residence; Self Declaration as per Annexure I for migrant applicants)  
- Bank Account details (Passbook copy/Cancelled cheque)  
- Udyam Registration (if applicable for business verification)  
- PAN Card  
- GST Certificate (if applicable)  
- Latest ITR (Income Tax Return)  
- Bank statements (last 3 months)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Know Your Customer (KYC) application form  
- Ration Card issued by the State from which application is being made / other State Govt. document certifying family composition (Self Declaration as per Annexure I for migrant applicants)  
- Aadhaar copy of Applicant and adult family members appearing in the family composition document  
- Deprivation Declaration  
- Self Declaration for Migrants (Annexure I)  
- Pre-Installation Check (Annexure II)  
- Unified PAHAL (DBTL) Joining Form  
- Declaration for new connection  
- Mandate For Giving up LPG Subsidy  
- Grievance Redressal Form for PAHAL (DBTL) Scheme  
- Unified Form for transfer/Regularization of LPG connection  
- Declaration for the loss of Subscription/Termination Voucher  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF or clear image format  
- File size should not exceed 5 MB per document  
- Names on documents must exactly match the applicant’s name as per Aadhaar  
- Aadhaar numbers must be valid and seeded with bank account and NPCI mapper  
- Self-declarations must be signed and dated by the applicant  
- For migrant applicants, Annexure I must be used for both Proof of Address and family composition  
- Deprivation Declaration must be dclaration must be signed and declare non-possession of exclusion criteria  
- All forms must be in English or Hindi; regional language versions acceptable if accompanied by certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for PMUY. The scheme does not mandate a Detailed Project Report (DPR). However, if the nodal officer or distributor requests additional verification, be prepared to submit a brief undertaking covering household composition and eligibility. **Verify with nodal officer if unsure.**

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Fill the KYC form in **BLOCK LETTERS** using **black ink only**  
2. Ensure all fields are mandatory and complete — leave no blanks  
3. Affix a recent passport-sized photograph of the applicant on the form  
4. Sign or provide thumb impression in the designated field  
5. Select applicant’s caste correctly (SC/ST/Others) as per eligibility  
6. Enter accurate Aadhaar number of applicant and all adult family members  
7. Provide correct bank account details (name, bank, branch, IFSC, account number) for DBTL subsidy transfer  
8. Declare migrant status accurately — if yes, Annexure I must be submitted for both PoA and family composition  
9. Confirm understanding of terms: no other LPG/PNG connection in household, connection for domestic use only, consent to biometric e-KYC and pre-installation check  
10. Submit the form along with all supporting documents to the LPG distributor or via https://pmuy.gov.in

- [ ] Caste/category selected correctly (SC/ST/Others)  
- [ ] Migrant status declared accurately; Annexure I attached if applicable  
- [ ] Deprivation Declaration signed and completed  
- [ ] Pre-Installation Check (Annexure II) filled out  
- [ ] Unified PAHAL (DBTL) Joining Form attached

- [ ] All documents legible, named correctly, and within size limits  
- [ ] No duplicate or existing LPG connection declared in household  
- [ ] Applicant confirmed as woman aged 18+

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed application either:  
  - Online at https://pmuy.gov.in (click “Apply for PMUY Connection”)  
  - Or offline at the nearest LPG distributorship of OMCs (Indane, Bharat Gas, HP Gas)  
  - Or via CSC Centre (₹20 fee applies)  
- Take a **screenshot of the submission confirmation** (if online) or **photograph of the submitted documents** (if offline)  
- Store the **application reference number** or **acknowledgement slip number** provided by the distributor  
- Set a follow-up reminder for **7 days after submission** to check status

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Collect and verify the **approval letter** from the LPG distributor  
- [ ] Confirm **sanctioned amount** received: ₹1,600 (14.2 kg cylinder) or ₹1,150 (5 kg cylinder) transferred to bank account via DBTL  
- [ ] Verify receipt of **first LPG refill** and **stove (hotplate)** free of cost from the distributor  
- [ ] Ensure **Utilization Certificate** is not required for PMUY (subsidy is upfront cash assistance; no utilization tracking mandated)  
- [ ] Confirm **invoice for success fee** as per your firm’s agreement with the client  
- [ ] Advise beneficiary to:  
  - Use the connection strictly for domestic cooking  
  - Not position any other LPG/PNG installations in the same kitchen  
  - Complete biometric Aadhaar authentication (e-KYC) when prompted by the distributor  
  - Retain all documents for future reference (e.g., for portability or grievance redressal)  
- [ ] Schedule a reminder for **first refill booking** (beneficiary is entitled to one free refill; subsequent refills are subsidized under PAHAL/DBTL)  
- [ ] Inform beneficiary about **grievance redressal process** via Helpline 1800 266 6696 or OMC consumer portals
