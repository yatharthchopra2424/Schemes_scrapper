## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client (PMUY provides ₹1,600 per 14.2kg cylinder connection or ₹1,150 per 5kg cylinder connection in direct cash assistance).
2. Success fees capped at a reasonable percentage of the scheme value to ensure client affordability and consultant motivation.
3. Retainer fees cover initial eligibility assessment, document preparation, and application submission effort.
4. Scheme-specific note: PMUY has a low financial risk profile due to its high approval rate (evidenced by over 10.56 crore connections released as of July 2026) and standardized processing through OMCs; however, revenue realization may lag due to verification timelines and Aadhaar seeding requirements.
