# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping women from poor households access clean cooking fuel through government schemes like the Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY). This scheme provides deposit-free LPG connections with financial support up to Rs. 1600, along with a free stove and first refill, specifically targeting women from SC/ST, PMAY(Gramin), Most Backward Classes, AAY, and other eligible categories. Before we explore if this fits your situation, may I ask a few quick questions to understand your current cooking setup and household details?

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you an adult woman aged 18 years or above seeking an LPG connection in your name?
2. Does your household currently have any existing LPG connection from any Oil Marketing Company (OMC) like Indane, Bharat Gas, or HP Gas?
3. Do you belong to any of these categories: SC Households, ST Households, Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin), Most Backward Classes, Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY), Tea and Ex-Tea Garden tribes, Forest Dwellers, people residing in Islands or River Islands, or SECC Households (AHL TIN)?
4. If not covered above, can you confirm your household is classified as poor based on a 14-point deprivation declaration (e.g., no member earns over ₹10,000/month, no income tax/professional tax paid, no government employee, etc.)?
5. Are you a migrant applicant (currently residing in a different state from your native place) who may need to use self-declaration for address and family composition?
6. Do you have your Aadhaar card and bank account details (passbook/cancelled cheque) ready for verification?
7. Do you possess a ration card or any state government document certifying your family composition?
8. Is your kitchen free of piped natural gas (PNG) connection, as PNG-connected households are not eligible for subsidized LPG under PMUY?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is a woman aged 18 years or above  
- [ ] No existing LPG connection in the household from any OMC  
- [ ] Belongs to eligible category: SC/ST/PMAY(Gramin)/Most Backward Classes/AAY/Tea and Ex-Tea Garden tribes/Forest Dwellers/Island or River Island dwellers/SECC Households (AHL TIN)/Poor household as per 14-point declaration  
- [ ] Aadhaar of applicant and all adult family members available  
- [ ] Bank account details (passbook/cancelled cheque) submitted  
- [ ] Ration card or state government family composition document available (or self-declaration for migrants)  
- [ ] Deprivation declaration signed (for poor household validation)  
- [ ] No piped natural gas (PNG) connection in the household  
- [ ] Applicant commits to using LPG solely for domestic cooking and not installing other LPG gas installations in the same kitchen  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, you appear to meet the core eligibility criteria for the Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY). This scheme can provide you with a deposit-free LPG connection, including financial assistance of up to Rs. 1600 for a 14.2kg cylinder (or Rs. 1150 for a 5kg cylinder), plus a free stove and first refill—all at zero cost to you. Would you like me to explain how this works and what documents you’d need to proceed?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many women in poor households still rely on traditional cooking fuels like firewood, coal, or cow-dung, which cause indoor air pollution, lead to respiratory illnesses, and consume significant time in fuel collection. The lack of access to clean LPG connections due to upfront costs (security deposit, regulator, hose, etc.) keeps them trapped in unhealthy and inefficient cooking practices, especially in rural and deprived areas.

**Scheme intro:**
The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY), launched by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas (MOPNG), provides deposit-free LPG connections to women from poor households. It offers financial assistance of Rs. 1600 for a 14.2kg cylinder connection (or Rs. 1150 for a 5kg cylinder), covering security deposit (Rs. 1250/800), pressure regulator (Rs. 150), LPG hose (Rs. 100), Domestic Gas Consumer Card (Rs. 25), and installation charges (Rs. 75). Additionally, beneficiaries receive a free LPG stove (hotplate) and first refill from Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) like Indane, Bharat Gas, or HP Gas.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are an adult woman aged 18 years or above  
- Your household has no existing LPG connection from any OMC  
- You belong to an eligible category (e.g., SC/ST/PMAY(Gramin)/Most Backward Classes/AAY or qualify as a poor household via 14-point deprivation declaration)  
- You have or can obtain Aadhaar, bank details, and family composition documents  

**Proof:**

**Next step:**
We offer a free, no-obligation eligibility check. If you qualify, we’ll guide you through document preparation and submission—either online at https://pmuy.gov.in or via your nearest LPG distributor—with success-based fees only (no upfront cost). Would you like to proceed with a 30-minute document review session to confirm your eligibility and start the application?
