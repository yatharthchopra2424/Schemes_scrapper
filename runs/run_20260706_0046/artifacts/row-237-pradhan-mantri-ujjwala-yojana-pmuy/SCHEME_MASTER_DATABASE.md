### Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas (MOPNG)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://pmuy.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Not applicable (individual/household scheme)
- Age of business: Not applicable
- Sector: Not applicable
- Revenue cap: Not applicable (based on poverty/deprivation criteria)
- Employee count: Not applicable
- Other: 
  - Applicant must be a woman who has attained 18 years of age
  - No other LPG connection from any Oil Marketing Company (OMC) within the same household
  - Adult woman must belong to a poor household based on submission of a deprivation declaration (as per prescribed format)
  - Eligible categories include: SC Households, ST Households, Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin), Most Backward Classes, Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY), Tea and Ex-Tea Garden tribes, Forest Dwellers, People residing in Islands and River Islands, SECC Households (AHL TIN), and Poor Household as per 14-point declaration

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - Rs. 1600 for a 14.2kg cylinder connection
  - Rs. 1150 for a 5 kg cylinder connection
  - Cash assistance covers: Security Deposit of Cylinder (Rs. 1250 for 14.2kg / Rs. 800 for 5kg), Pressure Regulator (Rs. 150), LPG Hose (Rs. 100), Domestic Gas Consumer Card (Rs. 25), Inspection/Installation/Demonstration charges (Rs. 75)
  - Additionally, all PMUY beneficiaries are provided with first LPG refill and stove (hotplate) both free of cost along with their deposit-free connection by the Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (cash assistance provided by Government of India)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (per connection)

#### Documents Required
1. Know Your Customer (KYC) application form
2. Proof of Identity (Aadhaar copy of Applicant)
3. Proof of Address (only in case Address in Aadhaar is different from currently residing address) (Self Declaration as per Annexure I for migrant applicants)
4. Ration Card issued by the State from which application is being made / other State Govt. document certifying family composition ((Self Declaration as per Annexure I for migrant applicants)
5. Aadhaar copy of Applicant and adult family members appearing in document at Sl. 4
6. Bank Account details (Passbook copy/Cancelled cheque)
7. Deprivation Declaration
8. Self Declaration for Migrants (Annexure I)
9. Pre-Installation Check (Annexure II)
10. Unified PAHAL (DBTL) Joining Form
11. Declaration for new connection
12. Mandate For Giving up LPG Subsidy
13. Grievance Redressal Form for PAHAL (DBTL) Scheme
14. Unified Form for transfer/Regularization of LPG connection
15. Declaration for the loss of Subscription/Termination Voucher

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Applicants can apply online through the application form available at https://pmuy.gov.in or visit the nearest CSC Centre for assistance in submitting the application.
2. There is no fee for self-submission. However, a charge of ₹20 will be applicable if the application is submitted through CSC Centres.
3. Offline applicants can directly submit the application at preferably nearest LPG distributorship of OMCs.
4. Required documents include KYC form, Aadhaar copy, proof of address, ration card or state government document certifying family composition, bank account details, and deprivation declaration.
5. For migrant applicants, self-declaration as per Annexure I for PoA and family composition is sufficient.
6. After submission, the application is verified by the LPG distributor and connection is released upon successful verification.

#### Internal Notes
- Key officer contact: Helpline: 1800 266 6696
- Common rejection reasons: 
  - Households having Piped Natural Gas connection are not entitled for subsidized LPG
  - LPG connection cannot be provided to a person under 18 years of age
  - Beneficiary must not position any other LPG gas installations in the same kitchen
  - Biometric Aadhaar authentication (eKYC) is mandatory for connections enrolled under the PMUY scheme
  - Pre-Installation inspection is mandatory prior to release of a PMUY connection
  - If Aadhaar is not seeded or mapped correctly in NPCI mapper, subsidy may not be received
  - PMUY cylinder and stove are subsidized assets and cannot be sold or transferred to anyone else
