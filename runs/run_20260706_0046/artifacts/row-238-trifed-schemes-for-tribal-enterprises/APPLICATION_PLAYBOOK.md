# Application Playbook

## How to TRIFED Schemes for Tribal Enterprises

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a tribal individual, Self Help Group (SHG), Van Dhan Vikas Kendra (VDVK), or tribal producer group engaged in Minor Forest Produce (MFP) collection, value addition, and marketing under Van Dhan Yojana or Pradhan Mantri Van Dhan Vikas Yojana (PMVDY)
- [ ] If applying as a Van Dhan Producer Enterprise (VDPE), the entity must be a federation of four or more geographically proximate VDVKs (relaxation possible after TRIFED review based on feasibility and justification)
- [ ] At least 70% of the Board of Directors must be from the tribal community, and at least one-third must be women (with 50% of women from tribal community)
- [ ] Applicant must have a bank account as per scheme guidelines (VDVKs and SHGs must open bank accounts)
- [ ] Applicant must be located in one of the 27 States and 308 Tribal predominant districts covered under the Van Dhan program and MSP for MFP Scheme
- [ ] Applicant must be engaged in sustainable livelihood systems through marketing development and remunerative pricing of tribal products
- [ ] Applicant must demonstrate intent to strengthen tribal value chains by transforming VDVKs into legally registered VDPEs
- [ ] Applicant must show commitment to capacity building and skill upgradation via collaborations with IITs, TISS, or other reputed institutions
- [ ] Applicant must aim to enhance market access through institutional partnerships, exhibitions, and e-commerce platforms
- [ ] Applicant must seek to facilitate access to institutional finance and scale operations for tribal enterprises

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation/Registration Certificate
- PAN Card
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) for last 2–3 years
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)
- Aadhaar Card of authorized signatory
- Address Proof (utility bill, rent agreement, etc.)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR)
- Utilization Certificate (UC)
- Monthly Progress Reports (MPRs)
- Annual Reports
- Geographical and resource mapping
- Activities plan for awareness generation and handholding support
- Revival plan of defunct VDVKs
- Business plan for Level 2 and Level 3 VDPEs
- Supporting documentation including progress report and field verification
- Audited statements of accounts

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible, scanned in PDF format, and clearly named (e.g., "CaseID_DocumentName_Date.pdf")
- [ ] File size does not exceed 10 MB per document
- [ ] Names on documents match exactly with the applicant’s official records
- [ ] All certificates are valid and not expired
- [ ] Documents are self-attested or notarized as required
- [ ] Hindi or English language only; if regional language, provide certified translation
- [ ] Bank account details match the name of the applicant entity
- [ ] Utilization Certificate is signed and stamped by a Chartered Accountant
- [ ] DPR includes financial projections, implementation timeline, and risk assessment

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required for this scheme.  
Standard sections to include:
- Executive Summary
- Organizational Background and Legal Status
- Project Objectives aligned with TRIFED Schemes
- Beneficiary Details (tribal individuals, SHGs, VDVKs, VDPEs)
- Implementation Plan with timelines and milestones
- Financial Plan (budget, fund utilization, tranche-wise release)
- Value Addition and Marketing Strategy
- Capacity Building and Training Plan
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Measures
- Monitoring and Evaluation Framework
- Sustainability and Exit Strategy
- Annexures (maps, letters of support, audited financials)

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal at https://trifed.tribal.gov.in and navigate to the relevant scheme section.
2. Ensure all mandatory fields are filled; leave no blank unless explicitly marked optional.
3. Use exact names and registration numbers as per official documents (PAN, Udyam, Bank Account).
4. Enter financial figures in INR lakhs or crores as specified in the form.
5. Upload documents in PDF format only, with correct naming convention.
6. Declarations and undertakings must be signed digitally or scanned with wet signature.
7. Double-check bank account details for fund transfer compliance (all direct financial assistance must be via bank transfer).
8. Save draft periodically and review before final submission.
9. Take a screenshot of the completed form before submission for records.
10. Note down the application reference number immediately after submission.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the TRIFED portal at https://trifed.tribal.gov.in
- Immediately after submission, take a screenshot showing the application reference number and timestamp
- Store the reference number in the case file and set automated reminders for follow-up at 7, 15, and 30 days post-submission
- Notify the client of successful submission with reference number and expected timelines

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from TRIFED specifying sanctioned amount and tranche details
- [ ] Confirm that the first tranche (10% for SIA, 10–20% for ISA) received for initiation activities like staff deployment and awareness generation
- [ ] Utilization Certificate (UC) and Detailed Project Report (DPR) prepared and submitted for second tranche (40% for SIA, 30–40% for ISA)
- [ ] Full utilization of second tranche achieved and verified
- [ ] Supporting documents submitted for final tranche (50% for SIA, remaining balance for ISA)
- [ ] TRIFED-conducted physical verification, audit, and performance evaluation completed
- [ ] Fund utilization monitored as per three-tranche release model
- [ ] Success fee invoice issued to client as per consultancy agreement
- [ ] Client advised on compliance with post-approval requirements: at least 70% tribal Board, women representation, bank account usage for all financial transactions
- [ ] Market linkage, exhibitions, and e-commerce platform integration supported as per scheme benefits
- [ ] Periodic progress reports (MPRs, Annual Reports) prepared and submitted to TRIFED
- [ ] All direct financial assistance ensured to be carried out only through bank account transfers
- [ ] Van Dhan Vikas Kendras and SHGs confirmed to have operational bank accounts as per scheme guidelines
