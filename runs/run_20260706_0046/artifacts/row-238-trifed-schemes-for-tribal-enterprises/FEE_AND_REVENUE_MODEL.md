## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to tribal enterprises under TRIFED schemes.
2. Transparent fee structure with clear milestones tied to fund tranche releases (10%, 40%, 50%).
3. Success fee calibrated to risk profile: moderate risk due to rolling basis approvals and verified milestone-based disbursements, but mitigated by TRIFED’s high-confidence implementation framework and documented fund size of ₹143.52 Cr.
4. Incentivizes timely delivery by linking success fees to verified utilization of funds and submission of Utilization Certificates and Detailed Project Reports.
