# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping tribal enterprises and self-help groups access government schemes that support sustainable livelihoods through value addition, market access, and institutional support. Given your work in Minor Forest Produce (MFP) collection, processing, or marketing, I wanted to explore whether the TRIFED Schemes for Tribal Enterprises might align with your goals—particularly around capacity building, financial support for infrastructure, and market linkages under the Van Dhan Yojana or Pradhan Mantri Van Dhan Vikas Yojana (PMVDY).

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you currently engaged in Minor Forest Produce (MFP) collection, value addition, or marketing as an individual, Self Help Group (SHG), Van Dhan Vikas Kendra (VDVK), or tribal producer group?  
2. What is the approximate number of members in your SHG or VDVK, and are they primarily from tribal communities?  
3. Have you already initiated any steps toward forming a Van Dhan Producer Enterprise (VDPE) by federating with nearby VDVKs?  
4. Are you seeking financial support for equipment, infrastructure, or training under a structured fund-release model?  
5. Do you have a Detailed Project Report (DPR) or business plan ready for your enterprise’s capacity building or market linkage activities?  
6. Are you operating in one of the 27 states or 308 tribal-predominant districts covered under the Van Dhan program?  
7. Does your group include at least one-third women in leadership, with at least 50% of those women from tribal communities?  
8. Are you looking to access institutional finance, e-commerce platforms, or participate in national/international fairs to scale your tribal enterprise?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Tribal individual, SHG, VDVK, or tribal producer group engaged in MFP collection, value addition, or marketing  
- [ ] Located in one of the 27 states or 308 tribal-predominant districts under Van Dhan program  
- [ ] For VDPE: Federation of four or more geographically proximate VDVKs (or feasible justification submitted to TRIFED)  
- [ ] At least 70% of Board of Directors from tribal community  
- [ ] At least one-third of Board are women, with 50% of those women from tribal community  
- [ ] Willing to open and use bank accounts for all financial transactions as per scheme guidelines  
- [ ] Prepared to submit Utilization Certificates (UCs), Detailed Project Reports (DPRs), and Monthly Progress Reports (MPRs)  
- [ ] Not seeking direct working capital assistance outside bank-transfer mechanism  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your enterprise aligns well with the TRIFED Schemes for Tribal Enterprises—particularly around financial support through the three-tranche fund release model and market access via TRIBES India outlets and e-commerce platforms. Let me share how this scheme can specifically support your goals.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Tribal gatherers and producers often remain at the raw material collection stage, missing out on higher income from value addition, branding, and market access. They face challenges in accessing finance, infrastructure, training, and institutional markets—keeping livelihoods at subsistence levels despite the vast potential of Minor Forest Produce (MFP).

**Scheme intro:**
The TRIFED Schemes for Tribal Enterprises, implemented by the Tribal Cooperative Marketing Development Federation of India Limited (TRIFED) under the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, provide financial support through a three-tranche fund release model: 10% upon proposal approval for initiation, 40% upon submission of Utilization Certificate and Detailed Project Report for capacity building and infrastructure, and 50% upon full utilization and verification. Benefits include capacity building (e.g., Tech for Tribal with 120 sessions), market access through TRIBES India retail outlets and exhibitions, financial support for equipment and infrastructure, brand development under 'Tribes India', e-commerce access, participation in national/international fairs, skill upgradation, and institutional support for legal registration of Van Dhan Producer Enterprises (VDPEs). Van Dhan Kendras are sanctioned Rs. 15 lakhs each, with Rs. 1 lakh per SHG of 20 members for training, equipment, and activities. Under MSP for MFP, Rs. 5 lakhs is advised for VDVKs to become primary procurement agencies.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in MFP collection, value addition, or marketing as a tribal individual, SHG, VDVK, or producer group  
- You operate in one of the 27 states or 308 tribal-predominant districts covered under the Van Dhan program  
- You are open to forming or are already part of a VDPE through federation of four or more geographically proximate VDVKs (or can submit feasibility justification to TRIFED)  
- Your leadership includes at least one-third women, with half of them from tribal communities, meeting governance requirements
