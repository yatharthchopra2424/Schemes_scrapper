### TRIFED Schemes for Tribal Enterprises
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Tribal Cooperative Marketing Development Federation of India Limited (TRIFED)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://trifed.tribal.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Tribal individuals, Self Help Groups (SHGs), Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (VDVKs), tribal producer groups
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Minor Forest Produce (MFP) collection, value addition, and marketing
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: VDPEs require federation of four or more geographically proximate VDVKs; at least 70% of Board of Directors must be from the tribal community, and at least one-third must be women (with 50% of women from tribal community); all direct financial assistance for working capital must be carried out only through bank account transfers; Van Dhan Vikas Kendras and SHGs must open bank accounts as per scheme guidelines

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Van Dhan Kendras have been sanctioned Rs. 15 lakhs each, with Rs. 1 lakh per SHG of 20 members for training, equipment, and activities; under MSP for MFP, Rs. 5 lakhs is advised to be released to VDVKs to make them primary procurement agencies
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (three-tranche fund release model: 10% upon proposal approval, 40% upon submission of Utilization Certificate and Detailed Project Report, 50% upon full utilization of the second tranche and verified implementation)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (per tranche, based on milestones)

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
2. Utilization Certificate (UC)
3. Monthly Progress Reports (MPRs)
4. Annual Reports
5. Geographical and resource mapping
6. Activities plan for awareness generation and handholding support
7. Revival plan of defunct VDVKs
8. Business plan for Level 2 and Level 3 VDPEs
9. Supporting documentation including progress report and field verification
10. Audited statements of accounts

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. State Implementing Agency (SIA) or Implementation Support Agency (ISA) prepares project proposal in coordination with State Nodal Department (SND) and TRIFED Regional Office (RO).
2. Submit proposal to TRIFED for approval.
3. Upon approval, receive first tranche (10% for SIA, 10–20% for ISA) to initiate activities like staff deployment and awareness generation.
4. Submit Utilization Certificate (UC) and Detailed Project Report (DPR) for second tranche (40% for SIA, 30–40% for ISA) to fund capacity building, infrastructure setup, and revival of defunct VDVKs.
5. Achieve full utilization of second tranche, undergo verification, and submit supporting documents for final tranche (50% for SIA, remaining balance for ISA) to support market linkage, operationalization, and monitoring & evaluation.
6. TRIFED conducts periodic physical verifications, audits, and performance evaluations throughout the lifecycle.
