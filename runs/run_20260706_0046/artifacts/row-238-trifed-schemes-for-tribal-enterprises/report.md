# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
**Scheme Name:** TRIFED Schemes for Tribal Enterprises  
**Scheme ID:** row-238  
**Ministry / Category:** Tribal Welfare  
**Scheme Type:** other  
**Geographic Scope:** Pan-India  
**Implementing Agency:** Tribal Cooperative Marketing Development Federation of India Limited (TRIFED)  
**Status / Deadlines:** Rolling basis  
**Last Updated:** 2024  
**Fund Size (Cr):** 143.52  
**Application Portal URL:** https://trifed.tribal.gov.in  

### Objectives
- Promote sustainable livelihood systems for tribal people through marketing development and remunerative pricing  
- Empower tribal producers to embrace entrepreneurship as a way of life  
- Strengthen tribal value chains by transforming VDVKs into legally registered Van Dhan Producer Enterprises (VDPEs)  
- Enhance market access through institutional partnerships, exhibitions, and e-commerce platforms  
- Provide capacity building and skill upgradation via collaborations with IITs, TISS, and other reputed institutions  
- Ensure socio-economic empowerment through collective processing, value addition, and marketing of forest and farm products  
- Facilitate access to institutional finance and scale operations for tribal enterprises  
- Promote tribal products in domestic and international markets through brand development and strategic alliances  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Eligible Entity | Description |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Tribal individuals | Engaged in Minor Forest Produce (MFP) collection, value addition, and marketing |
| Self Help Groups (SHGs) | Tribal SHGs involved in MFP activities |
| Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (VDVKs) | Units engaged in MFP collection, value addition, and marketing under Van Dhan Yojana or PMVDY |
| Tribal producer groups | Groups engaged in MFP collection, value addition, and marketing |
| Van Dhan Producer Enterprises (VDPEs) | Requires federation of four or more geographically proximate VDVKs; relaxation possible based on feasibility and justification reviewed by TRIFED |

**Target Beneficiaries:** tribal entrepreneurs; tribal gatherers; self-help groups; MSME  

### Benefits & Financial Support
#### Financial Support Structure
| Tranche | Percentage | Conditions for Release | Applicable To |
|---------|------------|------------------------|---------------|
| First Tranche | 10% | Upon proposal approval for initiation activities | SIA: 10%; ISA: 10–20% |
| Second Tranche | 40% | Upon submission of Utilization Certificate (UC) and Detailed Project Report (DPR) for capacity building and infrastructure setup | SIA: 40%; ISA: 30–40% |
| Third Tranche | 50% | Upon full utilization of second tranche and verified implementation | SIA: 50%; ISA: Remaining balance |

#### Specific Financial Allocations
- Van Dhan Kendras: Sanctioned Rs. 15 lakhs each  
- Per SHG of 20 members: Rs. 1 lakh for training, equipment, and activities  
- Under MSP for MFP: Rs. 5 lakhs advised to be released to VDVKs to make them primary procurement agencies  

#### Benefits
- Capacity building and training programs (e.g., Tech for Tribal with 120 sessions)  
- Market access through TRIBES India retail outlets and exhibitions  
- Financial support for equipment and infrastructure  
- Brand development under 'Tribes India'  
- Access to e-commerce platforms  
- Participation in national and international fairs  
- Skill upgradation  
- Institutional support for legal registration and governance of Van Dhan Producer Enterprises (VDPEs)  

### Required Documents
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)  
2. Utilization Certificate (UC)  
3. Monthly Progress Reports (MPRs)  
4. Annual Reports  
5. Geographical and resource mapping  
6. Activities plan for awareness generation and handholding support  
7. Revival plan of defunct VDVKs  
8. Business plan for Level 2 and Level 3 VDPEs  
9. Supporting documentation including progress report and field verification  
10. Audited statements of accounts  

### Application Process
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[State Implementing Agency (SIA) or Implementation Support Agency (ISA) prepares project proposal] --> B[Submit proposal to TRIFED for approval]
    B --> C{Approval?}
    C -->|Yes| D[Receive first tranche (10% for SIA, 10–20% for ISA)]
    D --> E[Initiate activities: staff deployment, awareness generation]
    E --> F[Submit UC and DPR for second tranche]
    F --> G[Receive second tranche (40% for SIA, 30–40% for ISA)]
    G --> H[Fund capacity building, infrastructure setup, revival of defunct VDVKs]
    H --> I[Achieve full utilization of second tranche]
    I --> J[Undergo verification]
    J --> K[Submit supporting documents for final tranche]
    K --> L[Receive final tranche (50% for SIA, remaining balance for ISA)]
    L --> M[Support market linkage, operationalization, monitoring & evaluation]
    M --> N[TRIFED conducts periodic physical verifications, audits, and performance evaluations]
```

**Key Process Notes:**  
- Fund release is contingent upon achievement of key implementation milestones and verified completion of deliverables  
- At least 70% of Board of Directors must be from the tribal community, and at least one-third must be women (with 50% of women from tribal community)  
- All direct financial assistance for working capital must be carried out only through bank account transfers  
- Van Dhan Vikas Kendras and SHGs must open bank accounts as per scheme guidelines  
- The scheme is an ongoing intervention covering 27 States and 308 Tribal pre-dominant districts under Van Dhan program and MSP for MFP Scheme  

### Key Caveats
