## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a registered tribal community-based organization (e.g., Van Dhan Vikas Kendras, Self-Help Groups, Cooperatives, or Producer Companies) under relevant Indian laws (Societies Act, Trust Act, Companies Act, or State Cooperatives Act)
- [ ] Organization is primarily engaged in collection, value addition, processing, packaging, branding, and marketing of Minor Forest Produce (MFP)
- [ ] Organization operates in tribal-dominated areas as identified by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs
- [ ] Organization has been operational for at least 1 year prior to application
- [ ] Organization has a minimum of 100 tribal members or beneficiaries directly involved in MFP-related activities
- [ ] Organization possesses valid registration documents (Registration Certificate, PAN, GSTIN if applicable, and bank account in the organization's name)
- [ ] Organization demonstrates capacity to manage funds and implement value addition projects for MFP
- [ ] Organization is not blacklisted by any central or state government agency
- [ ] Organization agrees to utilize funds strictly for the establishment or upgradation of Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (VDVKs) for MFP value addition

> **Note**: Eligibility criteria are derived from the operational guidelines of Pradhan Mantri Van Dhan Yojana (PMVDY) as implemented by TRIFED under the Ministry of Tribal Affairs. Since the direct portal (https://trifed.tribal.gov.in/vdvk) returned a 403 Forbidden error, criteria are based on publicly available scheme documentation and TRIFED's VDVK framework.

- [ ] Commitment letter from at least 100 tribal members affirming participation in MFP collection and value addition

- [ ] DPR includes clear budget utilization, timelines, and sustainability plan
- [ ] MFP list is specific and regionally relevant (e.g., tendu leaf, mahua, honey, lac, sal seed, etc.)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required** for Pradhan Mantri Van Dhan Yojana (PMVDY) applications under the Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (VDVK) initiative.  

**Standard DPR Sections to Include**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Organization Background and Tribal Engagement  
- Objectives of the VDVK  
- Details of Minor Forest Produce (MFP) to be processed (species, volume, seasonality)  
- Process Flow Diagram (collection → cleaning → processing → packaging → storage)  
- Machinery and Equipment Layout Plan  
- Location Analysis and Site Map  
- Manpower Plan (roles, responsibilities, training needs)  
- Marketing and Linkage Strategy (TRIFED tie-ups, e-commerce, local markets)  
- Financial Plan (project cost, funding request, utilization schedule)  
- Sustainability and Exit Plan  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Measures  
- Annexures: Quotations, NOCs, Member Commitments, Maps  

> **Note**: While the exact DPR template is not publicly accessible due to portal restrictions, the above structure aligns with TRIFED’s VDVK guidelines. Confirm specific format with the nodal TRIFED State Office.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal via TRIFED’s official website or designated State TRIFED Office link (verify current access route).  
2. Use only the organization’s official name as per Registration Certificate and PAN.  
3. Fill all fields in English; avoid abbreviations unless officially recognized.  
4. Ensure turnover, employee count, and tribal member numbers are consistent across form and documents.  
5. Upload documents in the specified order and format (PDF, max 5 MB/file).  
6. Double-check bank account details for fund transfer accuracy.  
7. Confirm that the authorized signatory has digital signature capability (if e-signature required).  
8. Review the entire form before final submission — no edits allowed after submission in most cases.  

> **Portal Note**: Due to 403 error on https://trifed.tribal.gov.in/vdvk, applicants must contact TRIFED State Office or Ministry of Tribal Affairs for the active application link or offline submission protocol.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the official approval letter from TRIFED/Ministry of Tribal Affairs  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount and disbursement schedule (usually in installments)  
- [ ] Ensure utilization of funds strictly as per approved DPR for VDVK setup (machinery, civil works, training, etc.)  
- [ ] Maintain separate books of account and vouchers for all expenditures  
- [ ] Submit Utilization Certificate (UC) and progress reports as per timelines in approval letter  
- [ ] Assist client in successful invoice submission and verification for success fee processing  
- [ ] Monitor project completion and coordinate final inspection if required  
- [ ] Retain all records (approvals, UC, audit reports) for minimum 5 years for audit compliance  

> **Note**: Post-approval compliance is critical. Failure to submit UCs or misuse of funds may lead to recovery and blacklisting. Advise clients to engage a CA for audit and UC preparation.
