## Pricing Principles
1. **Value Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the financial benefit delivered to the client, ensuring affordability while maintaining sustainable margins for advisory services.
2. **Risk-Based Pricing**: Success fees are adjusted according to scheme-specific risks, including application complexity, documentation burden, and historical approval rates.
3. **Transparency**: All fee components (retainer and success fee) are clearly communicated upfront, with no hidden charges.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Pradhan Mantri Van Dhan Yojana targets tribal gatherers and forest-dependent communities for value addition of non-timber forest products (NTFPs). Based on eligibility criteria focusing on individual tribal beneficiaries, self-help groups (SHGs), and van dhan vikas kendras (VDVKs), this scheme does not apply to conventional business tiers (Starter to Enterprise) as defined in our standard pricing model. Engagement would require a customized approach tailored to community-based enterprises or SHGs, outside the scope of standard corporate client tiers.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Retainer fee for advisory services under Pradhan Mantri Van Dhan Yojana (PMVDY) – including eligibility assessment, documentation preparation, application submission, and liaison with TRIFED/State Nodal Agencies for value addition of non-timber forest products (NTFPs).

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for successful sanction of benefits under Pradhan Mantri Van Dhan Yojana (PMVDY), implemented by Tribal Cooperative Marketing Development Federation of India Limited (TRIFED), upon approval of project proposal for establishment or upgradation of Van Dhan Vikas Kendra (VDVK) or NTFP processing units.
