## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping tribal communities and forest-dependent artisans access government support for sustainable livelihoods through value addition of non-timber forest products. The Pradhan Mantri Van Dhan Yojana is designed specifically to empower tribal gatherers by strengthening Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (VDVKs) and enhancing income through collective enterprise. I’d like to understand if this could support your community’s goals.

### Discovery Questions
- Are you part of a tribal community or forest-dependent group engaged in the collection of non-timber forest products (NTFPs)?
- Has your group been formally recognised or registered as a Van Dhan Vikas Kendra (VDVK) under the Pradhan Mantri Van Dhan Yojana?
- Are you currently involved in primary collection, or are you looking to move into value addition, processing, or packaging of NTFPs?
- Does your group consist of primarily tribal gatherers, with a focus on sustainable harvesting practices?
- Are you seeking support for infrastructure, training, or market linkages to improve the value of your forest produce?
- Is your group based in a scheduled tribal area or region covered under the Van Dhan Yojana implementation?
- Have you previously accessed any government support for NTFP-based livelihoods, or is this your first application?
- Are you operating as a self-help group, cooperative, or producer collective with at least 150 tribal members?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is a tribal community or forest-dependent group
- [ ] Group is engaged in collection of non-timber forest products (NTFPs)
- [ ] Located in a scheduled tribal area or implemented VDVK zone
- [ ] Preferably organised as a self-help group, cooperative, or producer collective
- [ ] Minimum of 150 tribal members involved in NTFP gathering
- [ ] Focus on value addition, processing, packaging, or marketing of NTFPs
- [ ] Not currently receiving duplicative support for the same activity under another central scheme
- [ ] Commitment to sustainable harvesting and forest conservation principles

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your group aligns well with the Pradhan Mantri Van Dhan Yojana’s goal of boosting tribal livelihoods through NTFP value addition. This scheme provides grants for setting up Van Dhan Vikas Kendras, including infrastructure, training, and market support — let me explain how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Problem framing:
Tribal gatherers often collect non-timber forest products but receive low returns due to lack of processing, value addition, and direct market access. Middlemen capture most of the profit, leaving artisans with minimal income despite their skilled labour and sustainable practices.

### Scheme intro:
The **Pradhan Mantri Van Dhan Yojana**, administered by **TRIFED** under the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, provides financial and technical support to establish **Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (VDVKs)**. It offers **up to ₹15 lakhs per Kendra** for infrastructure, equipment, training, and market linkage development to enhance the value of non-timber forest products collected by tribal communities.

### Why they qualify (personalised):
- Your group is engaged in the collection of non-timber forest products (NTFPs), a core eligibility criterion  
- You are based in a scheduled tribal area or region where VDVKs are operational  
- You operate as a collective (self-help group, cooperative, or producer group) with sufficient tribal membership  
- You seek support for value addition, processing, or market access — exactly what the scheme funds
