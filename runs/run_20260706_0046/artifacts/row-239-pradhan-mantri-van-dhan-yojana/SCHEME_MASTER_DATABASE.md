### Pradhan Mantri Van Dhan Yojana
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** TRIFED (Tribal Cooperative Marketing Development Federation of India) under Ministry of Tribal Affairs
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://trifed.tribal.gov.in/vdvk
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Tribal gatherers and artisans engaged in collection, processing, and value addition of Minor Forest Produce (MFP)
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Forest-based livelihoods, tribal handicrafts, and non-timber forest produce (NTFP) processing
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be part of Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (VDVKs) or Self Help Groups (SHGs) of tribal gatherers; priority to Scheduled Tribe (ST) communities residing in forest fringe villages

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance for setting up Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (VDVKs), including infrastructure, equipment, and training; subsidy support for value addition of MFPs
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit to implementing agencies (TRIFED and State Nodal Agencies) for establishment of VDVKs
- Duration of benefit: One-time support for setup; ongoing operational support through market linkages and training

#### Documents Required
1. Identity proof of applicants (Aadhaar/Voter ID)
2. Address proof of the proposed VDVK location
3. Certificate of registration of Self Help Group (SHG) or cooperative
4. Bank account details of the SHG/cooperative
5. Project proposal detailing planned MFP collection, processing, and value addition activities
6. List of members of the SHG with their tribal identity proof
7. Land use permission or no-objection certificate for the VDVK site (if required)
8. Quotations for equipment and infrastructure to be procured

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify and form a Self Help Group (SHG) of tribal gatherers engaged in MFP collection
2. Register the SHG under relevant State Act (Societies Act, Cooperatives Act, etc.)
3. Prepare a detailed project proposal for setting up a Van Dhan Vikas Kendra (VDVK)
4. Submit the application along with required documents to the State Nodal Agency or TRIFED
5. Application undergoes scrutiny and field verification by the concerned authorities
6. Upon approval, sanction letter is issued and funds are released for establishment of VDVK
7. Procurement of equipment and setup of infrastructure as per approved plan
8. Commence operations and begin MFP collection, processing, and value addition activities
9. Avail training and market linkage support provided under the scheme
