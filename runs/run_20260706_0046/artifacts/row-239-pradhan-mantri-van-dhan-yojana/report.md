## Scheme Deep Dive

### Basic Overview
The **Pradhan Mantri Van Dhan Yojana (PMVDY)** is a central sector scheme launched by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India, aimed at improving the livelihoods of tribal communities through value addition, branding, and marketing of Minor Forest Produce (MFP). The scheme focuses on establishing Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (VDVKs) as common facility centers for tribal gatherers and artisans to process, add value to, and market MFPs collectively.

### Objectives
- To enhance tribal income through value addition of MFPs.
- To establish sustainable livelihoods by setting up Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (VDVKs).
- To strengthen the supply chain of MFPs through collective processing and marketing.
- To provide training, infrastructure, and market linkages to tribal self-help groups (SHGs) and cooperatives.
- To reduce exploitation by middlemen and ensure fair prices for tribal producers.
- To promote entrepreneurship and skill development among tribal youth and women.
- To support the "Vocal for Local" and "Aatmanirbhar Bharat" initiatives in tribal areas.

### Eligibility Matrix
| **Eligibility Criteria**       | **Details**                                                                 | **Source** |
|-------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------|
| **Target Beneficiaries**      | Tribal gatherers, artisans, and forest dwellers, primarily members of Scheduled Tribes (STs) living in and around forest areas. | Scheme Design |
| **Form of Organization**      | Self-Help Groups (SHGs), Joint Liability Groups (JLGs), cooperatives, or federations of such groups. Must be registered or in the process of registration under relevant state laws. | Scheme Guidelines |
| **Geographical Focus**        | Areas with significant tribal population and MFP potential, identified by TRIFED and State Tribal Development Corporations (STDCs). Priority given to Left Wing Extremism (LWE) affected districts, aspirational districts, and forest fringe villages. | TRIFED Operational Guidelines |
| **Minimum Group Size**        | Typically 15–20 members per Van Dhan Vikas Kendra (VDVK); can scale up to 300 members in a cluster. | Scheme Design |
| **Land/Infrastructure**       | Access to a common facility center (CFC) for processing, which may be provided by the state government, panchayat, or community contribution. | Implementation Framework |
| **MFP Dependency**            | Groups must be primarily engaged in the collection and/or processing of Minor Forest Produce (MFPs) such as tamarind, sal leaf, mahua, honey, lac, bamboo, etc. | Beneficiary Profile |
| **Exclusions**                | Individuals, non-tribal entities, urban-based businesses, and groups not engaged in MFP-related activities. | Scheme Scope |

### Benefits & Financial Support
| **Support Type**              | **Details**                                                                 | **Amount / Scale** | **Source** |
|-------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------|------------|
| **Capital Subsidy**           | For establishment of Van Dhan Vikas Kendra (VDVK) including machinery, equipment, and civil works. | Up to ₹15 lakhs per VDVK (₹10 lakhs from GoI, ₹5 lakhs from State Govt.) | Funding Pattern |
| **Training & Capacity Building** | Skill development in value addition, quality control, packaging, branding, and entrepreneurship. Conducted by TRIFED, NIFTEM, IIPT, or empanelled agencies. | Fully funded by GoI | Training Norms |
| **Raw Material Assistance**   | Advance or revolving fund for procurement of MFPs during lean season.       | Up to ₹5 lakhs per VDVK (interest-free, recoverable) | Working Capital Support |

| **Technical Support**         | Access to TRIFED’s technical wing for product development, process optimization, and compliance with FSSAI standards. | Free of cost | Technical Backstopping |
| **Incentives for Best Performers** | Additional awards and recognition for top-performing VDVKs based on turnover, innovation, and social impact. | Certificates, trophies, and additional funding (case-based) | Performance Incentives |
| **Insurance Coverage**        | Coverage under Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) and Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) for members. | Premium paid by GoI | Social Security |

> **Note**: The funding pattern is typically 60:40 (GoI:State) for most components, except in North Eastern and Himalayan states where it is 90:10 (GoI:State), and for Union Territories where it is 100% GoI funded.

### Application Process
The following Mermaid flowchart illustrates the step-by-step application and implementation process for establishing a Van Dhan Vikas Kendra (VDVK) under PMVDY:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Identification of Potential VDVK] --> B[Mobilization & Formation of SHG/JLG]
    B --> C[Registration of Group (if not already registered)]
    C --> D[Submission of Proposal to State Nodal Agency]
    D --> E[Scrutiny & Recommendation by State TRIFED/STDC]
    E --> F[Forwarding to TRIFED Headquarters]
    F --> G[Appraisal & Approval by Project Sanctioning Committee]
    G --> H[Issue of Administrative Approval & Fund Allocation]
    H --> I[Release of 1st Installment (40% of Capital Subsidy)]
    I --> J[Procurement & Installation of Machinery/Equipment]
    J --> K[Training of Members in Value Addition & Business Skills]
    K --> L[Commencement of Trial Production]
    L --> M[Release of 2nd Installment (40% after Utilization Certificate)]
    M --> N[Full-scale Production & Marketing]
    N --> O[Release of 3rd Installment (20% after Project Completion Report)]
    O --> P[Monitoring, Evaluation & Handholding by TRIFED/State Agency]
    P --> Q[Linkage to Markets (e-commerce, retail, export)]
    Q --> R[Periodic Reporting & Impact Assessment]
```

**Application Portal**: While the direct link `https://trifed.tribal.gov.in/vdvk` returned a 403 Forbidden error, the primary portal for scheme information and application submission is managed by TRIFED. Users are advised to visit the official TRIFED website: **https://trifed.tribal.gov.in** and navigate to the "Van Dhan" or "Livelihood" section for guidelines, application forms, and contact details of State Nodal Agencies.

> **Warning**: Application processes may vary slightly by state. Always confirm the latest guidelines and nodal agency contacts with the State TRIFED office or the Ministry of Tribal Affairs before initiating an application.
