## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Business is registered as a service exporter under the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP) of India  
- [ ] Service exports are made in freely convertible foreign exchange (FCFE)  
- [ ] Service falls under the notified list of services eligible under SEIS (e.g., legal, accounting, auditing, bookkeeping, tax consulting, business and management consulting, architectural, engineering, IT, software, R&D, education, training, hospital, tourism, hotel, etc.)  
- [ ] Service export realization is received through normal banking channels  
- [ ] Exporter holds a valid Importer Exporter Code (IEC)  
- [ ] Business is not availing benefits under any other export promotion scheme for the same service export (e.g., not claiming SEIS and RoSCTL/MEIS for same service)  
- [ ] Service export is made during the applicable fiscal year (FY) for which SEIS scrip is being claimed  
- [ ] Net foreign exchange earned (NFE) from eligible service exports is positive  

> **Sources**: Scheme eligibility inferred from SEIS policy framework under Foreign Trade Policy 2015-2020 (extended), DGFT notifications. Portal currently inaccessible (https://dgft.gov.in/seis returns 404), but scheme details are well-established in public domain and archived DGFT circulars.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- [ ] PAN Card of the business/entity  
- [ ] GST Registration Certificate  
- [ ] Import Export Code (IEC) certificate  
- [ ] Bank Realization Certificate (BRC) or e-BRC for service export receipts  
- [ ] Audited financial statements (Balance Sheet, P&L) for the relevant FY  
- [ ] Income Tax Returns (ITR) for the last 2–3 years  
- [ ] Udyam Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- [ ] Cancelled cheque or bank statement for refund/accounting purposes  
- [ ] Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for authorized signatory  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- [ ] Service export invoices (clearly showing service description, FCFE amount, client details overseas)  
- [ ] Foreign Inward Remittance Certificates (FIRC) or e-FIRC for each eligible service export transaction  
- [ ] Service contract or agreement with overseas client  
- [ ] Declaration of Net Foreign Exchange Earned (NFE) from eligible services  
- [ ] Self-certified list of eligible service exports with IEC, invoice no., date, amount in FCFE and INR, and service code (as per SEIS notified list)  
- [ ] Annexure ANF 3B (Application form for SEIS scrip) – to be filled and submitted online via DGFT portal  
- [ ] Copy of RBI approval (if service export requires prior approval under FEMA)  

> **Note**: Although the direct URL https://dgft.gov.in/seis returns a 404, SEIS is administered by DGFT under FTP 2015-2020. Application is made via DGFT’s online portal (https://www.dgft.gov.in) under incentives → SEIS. Refer to DGFT Public Notice No. 03/2015-20 and subsequent amendments.

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per file unless otherwise specified)  
- [ ] Names on PAN, GST, IEC, bank account, and invoices match exactly  
- [ ] Bank statements and BRC/e-FIRC clearly show inward remittance in FCFE  
- [ ] Service description on invoices matches the notified SEIS service list  
- [ ] Declarations are signed and stamped by authorized signatory  
- [ ] Documents are in English or accompanied by certified translation  
- [ ] e-BRC/e-FIRC are downloaded directly from ICEGATE or bank portal with valid digital signature

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required. SEIS is a claim-based incentive scheme; no Detailed Project Report (DPR) is needed. However, applicants must submit a self-declaration of eligible service exports (Annexure ANF 3B) along with supporting documents.  
> **Verify with nodal officer**: Confirm latest format of ANF 3B and any additional declarations required due to policy updates.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the DGFT portal (https://www.dgft.gov.in) and log in using IEC and DSC  
2. Navigate to: Incentives → SEIS → Apply for SEIS Scrip  
3. Select the correct fiscal year for which the claim is being made  
4. Enter IEC, PAN, GSTIN, and business details accurately (must match master data)  
5. Upload Annexure ANF 3B with item-wise details of eligible service exports (service description, invoice no., date, FCFE amount, INR equivalent, service code)  
6. Attach all scheme-specific and universal documents as per Stage 2 checklist  
7. Declare that the service exports are not claimed under any other export promotion scheme  
8. Verify net foreign exchange earned (NFE) is correctly calculated (gross FCFE inflow minus eligible outflows)  
9. Submit the application and save the acknowledgment with application number  
10. Take a screenshot of the final submission confirmation page  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All fields in the application form are filled accurately and match supporting documents  
- [ ] Annexure ANF 3B is complete, arithmetically correct, and duly signed  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached and clearly labeled  
- [ ] Declarations regarding non-duplication of benefits and FCFE realization are included  
- [ ] Application is submitted within the stipulated time frame (usually within 12 months from end of FY)  
- [ ] Application reference number is generated and recorded  
- [ ] Copy of submitted application is saved for internal records

- [ ] Safeguard the duty credit scrip (electronic scrip issued via DGFT portal)

- [ ] Maintain records of scrip utilization for audit and compliance purposes  
- [ ] Prepare and submit Utilization Certificate (if required by DGFT for validation)  
- [ ] Invoice client for success fee as per engagement agreement  
- [ ] Close case file and retain documents for statutory period (minimum 5 years)
