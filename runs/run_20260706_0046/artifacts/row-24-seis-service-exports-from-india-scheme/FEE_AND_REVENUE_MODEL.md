## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit delivered under SEIS, which provides duty credit scrips equivalent to a percentage of net foreign exchange earnings (typically 3–5% of eligible exports), ensuring client profitability after fees.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given SEIS processing times averaging 6–12 months (per historical DGFT data and industry feedback), retainer fees cover ongoing compliance and follow-up efforts during the lag period.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The scheme is accessible to MSMEs, startups, mid-market, and large enterprises engaged in eligible service exports, with no turnover cap — making Growth, Scale, and Enterprise tiers all applicable.
4. **Compliance Assurance**: Fees reflect the complexity of SEIS documentation, including proof of foreign exchange realization, chartered accountant certification, and adherence to DGFT guidelines, minimizing rejection risks.

> **Scheme-Specific Note**: SEIS has a moderate risk profile due to variable processing times (typically 6–12 months) and stringent documentation requirements. However, historical approval rates are favorable for complete applications, supporting predictable revenue recognition with a 3-month lag in the model.
