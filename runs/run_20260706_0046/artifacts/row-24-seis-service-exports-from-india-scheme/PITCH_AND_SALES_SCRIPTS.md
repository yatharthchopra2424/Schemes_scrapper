## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping Indian service exporters access government incentives that directly improve their bottom line. The Service Exports from India Scheme (SEIS) is designed specifically for businesses like yours that earn foreign exchange through eligible services exports — putting money back into your operations without diluting equity or taking on debt.

### Discovery Questions
- What year was your business incorporated or registered under the Companies Act/LLP Act?
- Which specific services do you export (e.g., IT, consulting, engineering, R&D, or other notified services)?
- In which financial year did you first earn foreign exchange from service exports?
- What percentage of your total revenue comes from service exports versus domestic sales or goods exports?
- Are you currently claiming any other export incentives like RoDTEP or MEIS (now replaced by RoDTEP)?
- Do you maintain separate records of foreign exchange earnings realized through normal banking channels?
- Has your business undergone any statutory audit for the years you intend to claim SEIS benefits?
- Are you aware that SEIS benefits are issued as freely transferable duty credit scrips usable for customs duty payment?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is a legal entity registered in India (Company/LLP/Partnership/Proprietorship)
- [ ] Entity is engaged in export of 'eligible services' as notified under SEIS (e.g., IT, consulting, engineering, R&D, healthcare, education, etc.)
- [ ] Foreign exchange earnings realized through normal banking channels in the relevant financial year
- [ ] Minimum net foreign exchange earnings of ₹10 lakhs in a financial year (as per scheme thresholds)
- [ ] Service exports must be under the list of notified services under SEIS (as per DGFT notifications)
- [ ] Claim must be made within the stipulated time frame (usually within 12 months from end of FY)
- [ ] Not claiming benefits under any other scheme for the same export transaction (e.g., not double-dipping with RoDTEP)
- [ ] Valid IEC (Importer Exporter Code) and GST registration (where applicable)

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Problem framing:
Indian service exporters face intense global competition and pressure on margins. Unlike goods exporters who benefit from schemes like RoDTEP, service exporters often overlook available incentives, leaving money on the table. SEIS addresses this gap by providing tangible financial support specifically for service export earnings, yet many eligible businesses remain unaware or find the application process complex.

### Scheme intro:
The Service Exports from India Scheme (SEIS), administered by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, provides duty credit scrips to eligible service exporters. The benefit is calculated as a percentage (typically 3-7%, depending on the service category and year) of net foreign exchange earned, issued as freely transferable scrips usable for payment of customs duty, excise duty, or service tax — effectively functioning as a cash equivalent.

### Why they qualify (personalised):
- Your business exports notified services under SEIS (e.g., IT, consulting, or similar) as confirmed in discovery
- You have realized foreign exchange earnings through normal banking channels in the relevant financial year
- You meet the minimum threshold of net foreign exchange earnings (₹10 lakhs or more annually)
- You hold a valid IEC and are not claiming duplicate benefits for the same exports under other schemes
