### SEIS (Service Exports from India Scheme)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Export schemes
- **Geography:** India
- **Administering Body:** Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dgft.gov.in/seis
- **Current Status:** Closed
- **Window:** Always Open (historically; scheme has been discontinued)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Service exporters
- Age of business: Not specified in evidence
- Sector: Services (eligible for export under SEIS)
- Revenue cap: Not specified in evidence
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: Must be registered with DGFT and have valid IEC (Importer Exporter Code); must have earned foreign exchange through eligible service exports

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Duty credit scrips @ 3-5% of net foreign exchange earned (historical rate; exact % varied by service category)
- Disbursement mode: Duty credit scrips (transferable instruments usable for customs duty payment)
- Duration of benefit: One-time per export realization (claimed annually based on FY performance)

#### Documents Required
1. IEC (Importer Exporter Code) certificate
2. Bank realization certificate (BRC) or e-BRC for foreign exchange earnings
3. Chartered Accountant (CA) certificate verifying net foreign exchange earned
4. Statement of service exports (category-wise)
5. Copy of shipping bills or invoices (for deemed exports, if applicable)
6. GST returns (GSTR-1/GSTR-3B) for verification of export turnover
7. PAN card of the business entity
8. Cancelled cheque or bank account details
9. Digital signature certificate (DSC) for online filing
10. Annexure A1 (SEIS application form) as per DGFT format

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register on the DGFT website and obtain login credentials
2. Download and fill the SEIS application form (Annexure A1)
3. Prepare all required documents as per the checklist
4. Get the CA certificate and bank realization certificate (e-BRC) verified
5. Upload documents and submit the application online via DGFT portal
6. Pay the applicable application fee (if any)
7. Track application status through the DGFT portal
8. Receive duty credit scrips in electronic form upon approval
9. Utilize scrips for payment of customs duty or transfer/sell them
