## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the tangible economic upliftment delivered to tribal MFP gatherers through MSP and value-chain development.
2. Tiered success fees reflect the scheme’s focus on mid-market and enterprise-level tribal producer groups (SHGs, VDVKs) requiring scalable aggregation).
3. Risk-adjusted retainers account for the scheme’s moderate implementation complexity, multi-agency coordination, and 3–6 month fund disbursement lag from MoTA to TRIFED to district units.
4. Scheme-specific note: The Mechanism for Marketing of MFP through MSP & DVC has a medium-risk profile due to its reliance on state-level nodal departments and district implementing units for fund flow, with processing delays of 3–6 months observed in TRIFED’s operational guidelines; however, strong evidence of fund disbursement (e.g., ₹587 crores funded across 381 districts) and MSP implementation supports predictable revenue realization post-approval.
