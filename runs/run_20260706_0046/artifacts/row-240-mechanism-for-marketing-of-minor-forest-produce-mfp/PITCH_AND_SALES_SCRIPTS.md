## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Tribal MFP gatherers often sell raw forest produce at low prices due to lack of bargaining power, limited access to processing, and weak market linkages. Despite being the legal owners of MFPs under the Forest Rights Act, 2006, many tribals do not realize the full economic value of their produce because it moves out of the tribal economy in unprocessed form, missing out on income from value addition and marketing.

**Scheme intro:**
The Mechanism for Marketing of Minor Forest Produce (MFP) through Minimum Support Price (MSP) & Development of Value Chain for MFP is a central sector scheme launched by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India, with TRIFED as the technical support unit. It provides Minimum Support Price (MSP) for 50 non-nationalized MFPs, supports training and capacity building, develops procurement and marketing infrastructure through Haat Bazaars and Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (VDVKs), and promotes value addition and market access for tribal producers.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You collect one or more of the 50 covered MFPs (e.g., tamarind, mahua, sal seeds, guggul, wild honey) as per Annexure 1 of the scheme guidelines
- You are engaged in MFP collection, which is the core activity targeted by the scheme for MSP and value addition support
- You operate in any state or union territory of India, as the scheme has pan-India geographic coverage
- You are interested in or already part of an SHG, VDVK, or tribal producer group, which aligns with the scheme’s focus on collectivization for bargaining power
