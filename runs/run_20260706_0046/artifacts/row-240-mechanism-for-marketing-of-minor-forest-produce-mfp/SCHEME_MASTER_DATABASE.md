### Mechanism for Marketing of Minor Forest Produce (MFP)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Tribal Cooperative Marketing Development Federation of India Limited (TRIFED)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://trifed.tribal.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Tribal MFP gatherers; Self Help Groups (SHGs); Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (VDVKs); tribal artisans; forest dwellers
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Minor Forest Produce (MFP) collection, value addition, processing, marketing
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Covers 50 non-nationalized MFPs (Annexure 1); implemented through State and District level functionaries; Ministry of Tribal Affairs as Nodal Ministry; TRIFED as technical support unit; Minimum Support Price recommended by Pricing Cell in TRIFED; State Government identifies State Nodal Department and District Implementing Units

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Minimum Support Price (MSP) for 50 MFPs (e.g., Bael pulp dried – Rs. 27/kg, Baheda – Rs. 30/kg, Chironji pods with seeds – Rs. 109/kg, Dry Shikakai Pods – Rs. 43/kg, Guggul – Rs. 700/kg, Gum Karaya – Rs. 98/kg, Hill Broom Grass – Rs. 30/kg, Kalmegh – Rs. 33/kg, Karanj seeds – Rs. 19/kg, Kusum seeds – Rs. 20/kg, Kusumi lac – Rs. 203/kg, Mahua flowers dried – Rs. 17/kg, Mahua seeds – Rs. 25/kg, Myrobalan – Rs. 15/kg, Nagarmotha – Rs. 27/kg, Neem seeds – Rs. 23/kg, Puwad seeds – Rs. 14/kg, Sal leaves – Rs. 30/kg, Sal seeds – Rs. 20/kg, Shatavari roots dried – Rs. 92/kg, Tamarind with seeds – Rs. 31/kg, Tamarind without seeds – Rs. 54/kg, Wild Honey – Rs. 195/kg); training on sustainable harvesting, primary level value addition, skill upgradation, processing; infrastructure development (Haat bazars, storage, transportation); marketing support; value addition and marketing through tribal groups and clusters under Van Dhan Vikas Karyakram
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (via bank account transfers to SHGs and VDVKs)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (per procurement cycle under MSP); ongoing support for value addition and marketing

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Ministry of Tribal Affairs as Nodal Ministry obtains budgetary resources and provides policy oversight
2. TRIFED functions as technical support unit and coordinates implementation and flow of funds through State and District Implementing Units via Executive Committee
3. Minimum Support Price recommended by Pricing Cell in TRIFED
4. Announcement of MSP done by Ministry of Tribal Affairs
5. State Government identifies State Nodal Department and District Implementing Units
6. State Nodal Department coordinates flow of funds and related matters
7. District Implementation Unit headed by District Collector chairs District Level Coordination and Monitoring Committee
8. District Collector responsible for overall preparation of haat bazaar wise consolidated District Plan (procurement, infrastructure, value addition, marketing)
9. District Project Management Unit setup to support district unit
10. Primary level procurement at Haat Bazaars through existing setup or engagement of Primary Procurement Agencies (Cooperative Societies/LAMPS/Mahila Samities/VDCs/JFMCs/SHGs)
11. SHGs paid commission not exceeding 7.5% of value of MFP procured
12. Haat Bazaars developed as composite vibrant grid to ensure last mile connectivity
13. Buyers including Government led SHGs operate in Haat Bazaars regulated by Gram Sabha
14. Haat Bazaars serve as basis for setting up of Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (value addition and marketing centers)
15. Primary, Secondary and Tertiary level value addition units setup in villages, tehsils/talukas, district headquarters based on MFP availability and tribal population
16. Primary Level Gatherers as rightful owners of forest produces under FRA Act 2006
17. SHGs for procurement and/or SHGs for value addition & marketing developed as producer groups through hierarchy within federation of SHGs at National, State, District & below levels
18. TRIFED signs MoU with State Government to ensure implementation of Scheme
19. TRIFED undertakes assessment of procurement & infrastructure plans for each state with State Nodal Department and recommends proposals to Central Apex Committee
20. TRIFED coordinates with State Nodal Department and other agencies to prepare & implement Advocacy Plan for promotion of Scheme
21. TRIFED organizes setting up of MSP procurement units and Value Addition and Marketing units (Van Dhan) in Haat Bazaar
22. TRIFED facilitates training of MFP gatherers, Self Help Groups on proper harvesting, conservation, regeneration, value addition and marketing (Van Dhan)
23. TRIFED sets up robust oversight and monitoring system for scrutinizing accounts
24. TRIFED sets up IT enabled Management Information System (MIS) platform for monitoring implementation and information flow
25. TRIFED raises additional funds under CSR and other sources
26. TRIFED coordinates and facilitates tie up with Financial Institutions / State Government for providing working capital to tribal gatherers/SHGs
27. TRIFED engages Programme Management Unit (PMU) to oversee functions: MSP operational unit, Market Intelligence & knowledge base Expansion unit, Monitoring, Inspection & Accounts Unit, Minimum Support Price Fixation Cell, MFP gatherers’ Training & Capacity Building Unit (Van Dhan), R&D Unit, Field Implementation Units
28. State Government designates nodal department and other agencies for implementation
29. State Government constitutes state level and district level coordination and monitoring committees headed by Chief Secretary and district collector
30. State Government develops procurement plan assessing number of purchase centres, estimated quantity of each MFP, arrangement of funds, storage & transportation, man-power & involvement of agencies/experts
31. State Government submits detailed action plan of procurement agencies for undertaking procurement operations of MFP in States by end of December
32. State Government releases funds to state nodal department and other agencies
33. State Government ensures funds reach field level implementing units at district level with 75:25 contribution from Central & State Government
34. State Government ensures sufficient infrastructure and manpower support
35. State Government ensures Gram Sabhas are involved in implementation
36. State Government ensures Gram Sabha/PRIs consulted by Forest Department in formulation of working plan of regeneration, conservation & management of MFP
37. State Government ensures setting up of adequate no. of procurement centres with necessary manpower and storage facility
38. State Government examines proposal of state agencies for reimbursement of loss and forwards to TRIFED for onward transmission to MoTA
39. State Government constitutes State Level Coordination & Monitoring Committee under chairmanship of Chief Secretaries
40. State Government constitutes District Implementation Unit and District Level Coordination & Monitoring Committee headed by district collector
41. State Government appoints Audit agency for audit of statement of account of State Nodal Department and other Agencies for the Scheme
42. State Government duly budgets pro-rata State’s share of funds required and facilitates timely release to Agencies
43. State Nodal Department obtains district-wise proposals and detailed action plans from District Implementation Units
44. State Nodal Department collates district plans and submits to TRIFED for onward transmission to Ministry of Tribal Affairs
45. State Nodal Department ensures timely submission/approval of procurement plan submitted by District Implementing Units
46. State Nodal Department, in consultation with District Implementing Units, prepares format for monitoring and audit of Scheme on yearly basis
47. State designated agencies maintain separate account of working capital and utilize funds only for procurement at fixed MSP
48. State Nodal Department finalizes account related to each district/commodity yearly and submits commodity wise statement of account in respect of transaction of procurement & disposal, duly audited by Accountant General of State, to MoTA
49. State Nodal Department liaises with State Level and District Level Coordination Monitoring Committee under chairmanship of Chief Secretaries & district collectors
50. State Nodal Department responsible for timely release of state share (25%) to District Implementing Unit
51. State Nodal Department submits consolidated proposal to TRIFED no later than 31st December for operations of next financial year
52. State Nodal Department and designated District Implementing Units maintain proper records of procurement, efficient utilization of funds, submission of utilization statement to TRIFED, ascertaining profit/loss in transaction and disposal of MFP stock within stipulated time
53. State Nodal Department facilitates audit of Scheme operations through Accountant General of State
54. State Nodal Department submits completion and outcome reports to TRIFED through State Nodal Department
55. State Nodal Department addresses district specific requirements by altering parameters within overall spirit of Scheme, informs State Nodal Department and TRIFED with justification
56. Panchayati Raj Institutions park funds pertaining to Scheme in Zila Panchayat operated by District Implementation Unit
57. Panchayati Raj Institutions implement Advocacy Plan for District in coordination and support of Panchayati Raj Institutions and other elected representatives
58. Panchayati Raj Institutions enforce: promote less cash transactions, planning IEC for advocacy and enforcement of Minimum Support Price, registration of Primary Procurement Agencies with provision for advance & commission payments, allotment of space to Primary Procurement Agencies, create and strengthen fair weighment practices through electronic weighing machines, provision of hired dry and cold storage, create and strengthen logistics network for transportation, establish linkages for value addition activities, transaction audit and agency based Third Party/Gram Sabha Monitoring, IT enabled communication/information/monitoring network linking haat bazaar with National network, time-bound dispute resolution mechanism at Gram Panchayat/Gram Sabha
59. Panchayati Raj Institutions act as per provisions of PESA Act, 1996 and FRA Act, 2006 for implementation of various activities under the Scheme
