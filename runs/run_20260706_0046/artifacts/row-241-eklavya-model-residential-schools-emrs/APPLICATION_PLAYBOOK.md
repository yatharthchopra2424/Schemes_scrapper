## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a student belonging to a Scheduled Tribe (ST) community  
- [ ] Student is seeking admission in classes VI to XII (inclusive)  
- [ ] Student belongs to a Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group (PVTG), displaced family, or is affected by left-wing extremism (priority category)  
- [ ] Student resides in a block with 50% or more ST population and at least 20,000 tribal persons (as per EMRS establishment norms)  
- [ ] Student meets age criteria corresponding to class VI–XII (typically 11–18 years, verified via birth certificate)  
- [ ] Student has satisfactory academic record from previous school (for continuation, subject to performance and conduct)  
- [ ] Student is not currently enrolled in another EMRS or equivalent residential school under the scheme (unless transferring with due process)  
- [ ] Student possesses valid domicile certificate (if required by state-specific admission notification)  
- [ ] Student possesses income certificate (if applicable, though not mandatory for eligibility as scheme provides 100% assistance)  

> **Note**: Admission is conducted annually before the academic session; exact dates vary by state and are notified by respective EMRS or state tribal welfare department. Eligibility is subject to seat availability and fulfillment of criteria. Continuation depends on satisfactory academic performance and conduct.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not applicable. The EMRS scheme does not require a Detailed Project Report (DPR) from applicants (students/parents). Proposals for new EMRS are submitted by state governments to the Ministry of Tribal Affairs through NESTS. For admission applications, only personal and academic documents are required.  
**Action**: Verify with nodal officer if any state-specific undertaking or affidavit is required.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Obtain the official application form from the respective EMRS or state tribal welfare department portal (admissions are state-level).  
2. Fill all fields in block letters; avoid overwriting or use of correction fluid.  
3. Ensure student name matches exactly as per birth certificate and school records.  
4. Enter correct date of birth and age as per academic eligibility (VI–XII).  
5. Specify Scheduled Tribe category and sub-group (if PVTG, mention explicitly).  
6. Attach self-attested copies of all required documents; originals to be produced for verification.  
7. Declaration by parent/guardian must be signed and dated; include contact details and address.  
8. Double-check that all scheme-specific documents (ST certificate, domicile, academic records) are attached before submission.  

> **Note**: Application process is managed by state governments/EMRS; no central online portal exists for student admissions. Follow state-specific instructions.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Application form is completely filled with no blank mandatory fields  
- [ ] Student’s name, date of birth, and parent/guardian details match across all documents  
- [ ] Proof of ST status is valid and issued by competent authority  
- [ ] Academic records are complete and show eligibility for class VI–XII  
- [ ] Domicile certificate is valid and issued by appropriate authority  
- [ ] Medical fitness certificate is recent and signed by registered practitioner  
- [ ] All documents are legible, properly named, and within size limits  
- [ ] Declaration form is signed and dated by parent/guardian  
- [ ] Photographs meet specifications (recent, white background, correct size)  
- [ ] Copy of complete application set is retained for records

- Take a clear screenshot or scan of the acknowledgment for record-keeping.  
- Store the application reference number securely and set follow-up reminders for 15 and 30 days post-submission.  
- Monitor official EMRS or state tribal welfare department website for merit list or selection announcement.
