## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the scale of infrastructure and operational support facilitated  
2. Transparent fee structures with clear milestones tied to proposal approval and fund sanction stages  
3. Risk-adjusted success fees reflecting the multi-stakeholder approval process (state → Ministry of Tribal Affairs → NESTS)  
4. Long-term engagement potential due to recurring annual admissions and school operational cycles  

**Scheme-specific note:** The EMRS scheme involves a lengthy approval cycle (proposal submission → ministry appraisal → fund sanction → construction → admissions), with admissions conducted annually. Success is contingent on state-level proposal quality and ministry appraisal timelines, introducing moderate execution risk. However, once approved, funding is nearly guaranteed as the central government bears 100% of costs, reducing financial risk for consultants who successfully navigate the application process.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description:** Retainer fee for advisory services related to Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS) scheme — including proposal preparation, tribal block eligibility assessment, and liaison with state tribal welfare departments for submission to the Ministry of Tribal Affairs.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description:** Success fee for securing approval and sanction of funds under the Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS) scheme, administered by the National Education Society for Tribal Students (NESTS) under the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India.
