## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping tribal communities access government education initiatives like the Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS), which provides completely free, quality education from Class VI to XII for Scheduled Tribe students—including lodging, boarding, uniforms, textbooks, and medical care. I’d like to understand if this could support a child or community you’re connected with, and if so, how we might assist.

### Discovery Questions
- Is the student a member of a Scheduled Tribe (ST) community, and do you have access to a valid ST certificate?
- What class is the student currently in, or planning to enter? (EMRS admits students from Class VI to XII only)
- Is the family residing in a tribal block with 50% or more ST population and at least 20,000 tribal persons?
- Has the student been identified as belonging to a Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group (PVTG), or are they from a displaced family or an area affected by left-wing extremism?
- Does the student have access to their birth certificate, previous academic records, and domicile certificate?
- Are you seeking a residential school option that provides free lodging, boarding, and holistic development alongside academics?
- Is the student medically fit for residential schooling, or able to obtain a medical fitness certificate?
- Are you looking for a school that follows the CBSE curriculum and emphasizes tribal culture, life skills, and sports?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Student belongs to a Scheduled Tribe (ST) community

Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like the Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS) scheme could be an excellent fit—offering 100% free education, lodging, boarding, and support for ST students from Class VI to XII. Let me explain how it works and how we can help.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many Scheduled Tribe families in remote or underserved areas struggle to access quality education due to distance, cost, and lack of infrastructure. Students often drop out after primary school because residential, affordable, and culturally sensitive schooling options are scarce—especially for those from PVTGs or conflict-affected regions.

**Scheme intro:**
The Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS) scheme, administered by the National Education Society for Tribal Students (NESTS) under the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India, provides 100% financial assistance for free education from Class VI to XII. This includes tuition, lodging, boarding, school uniforms, textbooks, stationery, and medical care. Schools follow the CBSE curriculum and emphasize holistic development through sports, life skills, and tribal cultural preservation.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- The student belongs to a Scheduled Tribe (ST) community, meeting the core beneficiary criterion  
- Seeking admission for Classes VI to XII, which aligns with EMRS’s target grade levels  
- Family resides in a tribal block with 50% or more ST population and at least 20,000 tribal persons (as per scheme norms)  
- Priority access may apply if the student is from a PVTG, displaced family, or left-wing extremism-affected area
