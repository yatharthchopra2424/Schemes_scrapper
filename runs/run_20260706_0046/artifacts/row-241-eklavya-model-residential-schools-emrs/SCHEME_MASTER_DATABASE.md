### Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Education Society for Tribal Students (NESTS), under the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** [https://tribal.nic.in/emrs](https://tribal.nic.in/emrs)
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Admissions are conducted annually, typically before the start of the academic session. Exact dates vary by state and are notified by the respective EMRS or state tribal welfare department.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Not applicable (scheme targets students, not businesses)
- Age of business: Not applicable
- Sector: Education (targeting Scheduled Tribe students in classes VI to XII)
- Revenue cap: Not applicable
- Employee count: Not applicable
- Other: 
  - Must be a Scheduled Tribe (ST) student
  - Studying in or seeking admission to classes VI to XII
  - Priority given to students from Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs), displaced families, and those affected by left-wing extremism
  - Schools are established only in blocks with 50% or more ST population and at least 20,000 tribal persons
  - Admission based on merit and availability of seats

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 100% financial assistance from the Government of India for both recurring and non-recurring expenses; covers infrastructure development, staff salaries, student maintenance, educational resources, free education, lodging, boarding, school uniforms, textbooks, stationery, and medical care
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (funds released directly to the implementing agency, National Education Society for Tribal Students (NESTS))
- Duration of benefit: Annual (renewable each academic year subject to satisfactory performance and conduct)

#### Documents Required
1. Proof of Scheduled Tribe status
2. Birth certificate or age proof
3. Previous academic records
4. Domicile certificate
5. Income certificate (if applicable)
6. Passport-sized photographs
7. Medical fitness certificate

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. State governments or UT administrations identify suitable locations for EMRS based on tribal population density.
2. Proposals for new schools are submitted to the Ministry of Tribal Affairs through the state government.
3. The Ministry appraises the proposal based on norms and availability of funds.
4. Upon approval, funds are sanctioned to NESTS for construction and operation.
5. Admissions are conducted annually through a selection process based on merit and eligibility criteria.
6. Selected students are enrolled in the respective EMRS for classes VI to XII.
