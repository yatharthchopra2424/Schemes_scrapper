## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, documentation preparation, and application submission support for the MedTech Regulatory Sandbox.
2. Success fee is contingent on formal acceptance into the sandbox program, reflecting value derived from regulatory facilitation and accelerated pathways.
3. Pricing aligns with the scheme’s non-financial benefit structure; no direct grant is provided, so fees are for advisory and process support services.
4. Scheme-specific note: The MedTech Regulatory Sandbox has a rolling application basis with evaluation cycles; success depends on innovation quality and readiness for testing, leading to moderate risk profile — success fees reflect uncertainty in selection despite strong eligibility fit for MSME/startups.
