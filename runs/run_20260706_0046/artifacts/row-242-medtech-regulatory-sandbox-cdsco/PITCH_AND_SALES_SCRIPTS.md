# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping MedTech innovators—startups, MSMEs, and individual developers—navigate regulatory pathways to bring their medical devices, diagnostics, or digital health solutions to market faster. The MedTech Regulatory Sandbox by CDSCO is designed exactly for this: to reduce approval timelines through hands-on regulatory guidance. I’d like to understand where you are in your development journey to see if this sandbox could be a strategic fit for you.

### Discovery Questions
1. What stage is your MedTech innovation currently at—concept, prototype, or ready for clinical validation?
2. Is your entity a legally registered Indian startup, MSME, or individual innovator?
3. What specific medical device, in-vitro diagnostic, or digital health solution are you developing?
4. Have you begun preclinical testing or do you have data ready for submission?
5. Are you targeting an unmet medical need or aligning with national health programs like Ayushman Bharat?
6. Do you anticipate needing help with clinical trial design or regulatory documentation?
7. Are you aware of the sandbox’s non-financial support model—regulatory mentorship, accelerated review, and infrastructure access?
8. What is your biggest concern about navigating CDSCO’s approval process today?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is an Indian startup, MSME, or innovator
- [ ] Developing medical devices, IVDs, digital health, or other MedTech products
- [ ] Legally registered entity in India
- [ ] Possesses a clear innovation with potential healthcare impact
- [ ] Demonstrates readiness for prototype testing or clinical validation
- [ ] Addresses unmet medical needs or aligns with national health programs (preferred)
- [ ] Willing to bear all costs related to development, testing, and trials
- [ ] Understands sandbox participation does not guarantee market approval

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, your [prototype/clinical readiness stage] and focus on [specific innovation area] align well with the MedTech Regulatory Sandbox by CDSCO. This sandbox offers regulatory hand-holding, accelerated review pathways, and access to testing infrastructure—all critical for de-risking your path to market authorization. Let me explain how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
MedTech innovators in India face prolonged regulatory timelines, complex documentation requirements, and limited access to expert guidance during early-stage validation—especially when navigating CDSCO’s pathways for new medical devices, diagnostics, or digital health tools. This delays market entry, increases development costs, and discourages innovation in critical healthcare areas.

**Scheme intro:**
The MedTech Regulatory Sandbox (CDSCO), administered by the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation under the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India, provides a structured, non-financial support framework for eligible innovators. It offers regulatory guidance, accelerated review pathways, procedural relaxations during the sandbox phase, access to testing labs and hospitals, and mentorship for clinical trial design and market authorization preparation—without providing direct grants or funding.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a legally registered Indian entity developing a medical device, IVD, or digital health solution  
- Your innovation demonstrates readiness for prototype testing or clinical validation  
- You are seeking regulatory de-risking and faster pathways to market authorization

## 3. Objection Handlers

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I've heard these schemes never get approved" | The sandbox doesn’t guarantee approval, but it significantly improves readiness. Past participants have successfully used sandbox feedback to strengthen their submissions and accelerate CDSCO review cycles—especially for novel diagnostics and Class C/D devices where guidance is scarce. |
| "How much do you charge?" | [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT] |
| "I'll try it myself first" | Common mistakes include submitting incomplete Form-40 applications without proper agent authorization or overlooking the need for a valid wholesale license for the Indian agent—both mandatory for sandbox entry. We help avoid these pitfalls upfront. |
| "We're too small / too big" | The sandbox explicitly welcomes Indian startups and MSMEs—there’s no upper revenue or employee limit. Eligibility hinges on innovation readiness and legal registration, not scale. Even solo innovators with a prototype have been accepted. |
| "I don't have the documents ready" | The three most commonly missing documents are: (1) Original Power of Attorney authenticated by Indian Embassy, (2) Notarized copy of Plant Master File (PMF), and (3) Copy of valid wholesale or manufacturing license of the Indian agent. We provide a checklist and support to prepare these. |
| "We already work with a CA" | Great — we work alongside CAs. They handle your accounts and compliance; we handle sandbox strategy, application drafting, and regulatory liaison with CDSCO. |
