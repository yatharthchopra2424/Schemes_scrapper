### MedTech Regulatory Sandbox (CDSCO)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Incubation
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO), Directorate General of Health Services, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://cdsco.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Indian startups, MSMEs, and innovators
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Medical devices, in-vitro diagnostics, digital health solutions, or other MedTech products
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be a legally registered entity in India; possess a clear innovation or technological solution with potential for healthcare impact; demonstrate readiness for prototype testing or clinical validation; preference may be given to solutions addressing unmet medical needs or aligned with national health programs

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Non-financial support only (no direct financial grants or funding)
- Disbursement mode: Non-financial (regulatory facilitation, technical guidance, access to infrastructure and expertise)
- Duration of benefit: As per agreed plan in MoU (sandbox phase duration)

#### Documents Required
1. Covering letter indicating type of application
2. Application in Form-40 with date, signature, and seal/stamp of Indian agent or manufacturer
3. Name and address of authorized agent in India (with valid wholesale license)
4. Names and address of manufacturer and factory premises
5. Name of the drug(s) or medical device to be registered
6. Number of sites involved in manufacturing
7. Original Power of Attorney
8. TR-6 Challan of fees paid
9. Copy of import permission for new drug(s) in Form-45 or Form-45A
10. Copy of valid wholesale or manufacturing license of the Indian agent
11. Company's authorization letter for bearer
12. Schedule D(I) and Schedule D(II) with undertaking
13. Notarized copy of Plant Master File (PMF)
14. Notarized copy of Drug Master File (DMF)
15. Original notarized copy of manufacturing license, FSC, GMP, COPP (for finished products)

#### Application Process
1. Submit an online application through the designated CDSCO portal detailing the innovation, intended use, and development stage.
2. Provide supporting documents including product description, technical specifications, preclinical data (if any), and team credentials.
3. Undergo initial screening by CDSCO to assess eligibility and sandbox suitability.
4. If shortlisted, present the proposal to the sandbox evaluation committee for review.
5. Upon acceptance, sign a memorandum of understanding outlining the scope, duration, and responsibilities.
6. Begin sandbox activities including product testing, data generation, and regulatory interactions as per the agreed plan.
7. Submit periodic progress reports and final outcomes for evaluation.
8. Receive feedback and recommendations for next steps toward regulatory approval or commercialization.
