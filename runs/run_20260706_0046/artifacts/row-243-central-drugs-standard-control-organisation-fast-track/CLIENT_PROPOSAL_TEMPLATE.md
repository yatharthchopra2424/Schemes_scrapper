## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation Fast Track — a recognition scheme administered by Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO), Directorate General of Health Services, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India — for which your business appears eligible. The scheme does not provide direct financial support but offers procedural and regulatory benefits including faster processing of registration certificates, import licenses, and market authorizations via the SUGAM portal.

## 2. Recommended Schemes

| Scheme | Potential Benefit | Est. Timeline | Confidence Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation Fast Track | Procedural and regulatory benefits (faster processing, reduced timelines, enhanced transparency) | Rolling basis — applications accepted year-round via SUGAM portal | High |

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment against scheme requirements  
2. Document preparation and review as per the scheme's checklist  
3. Portal registration and application submission on [https://sugam.cdsco.in]  
4. Liaison with CDSCO officials for query resolution  
5. Monitoring application status and providing updates  
6. Assistance with post-approval compliance requirements  
7. Final certification delivery and closure  

*Note: A DPR/project report is not required for this scheme.*

## 4. What We Need From You
1. Documents as per the scheme's document list: Covering Letter, Application in Form-40 or Form 8/8A, Original Power of Attorney, TR-6 Challan of fees paid, Copy of Import permission for new drug (Form-45 or Form-45A), Copy of Valid Wholesale License (20B/21C) or Manufacturing License of Indian agent, Company’s authorization letter for bearer, Schedule D (I) and (II) Undertaking, Notarised copy of Plant Master File (PMF), Notarised copy of Drug Master File (DMF), Original Notarised Copy of Manufacturing Licence, FSC, GMP, COPP (for finished products), Attested/Appostilled copy of Product Registration Certificate (e.g., CFDA, EDQM), Original label/specimen label complying with Rule 96  
2. Designated point of contact for all communications  
3. Response to queries within 2 business days
