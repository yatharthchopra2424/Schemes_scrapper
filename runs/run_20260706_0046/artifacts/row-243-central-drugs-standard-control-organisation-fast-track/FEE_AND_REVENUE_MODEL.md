## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the regulatory and procedural benefits of faster market access via the SUGAM portal.
2. Tiered retainer reflects client complexity and documentation burden (e.g., MSMEs/startups benefit from relaxed experience/turnover criteria).
3. Success fee scaled to scheme value, recognizing that the Fast Track mechanism provides no direct financial grant but reduces time-to-market and compliance risk.
4. Risk profile: Moderate — processing time is accelerated but approval is contingent on strict regulatory compliance; success depends on complete documentation (e.g., TR-6 challan, GMP certificates, stability data) and responsiveness to queries during review.
