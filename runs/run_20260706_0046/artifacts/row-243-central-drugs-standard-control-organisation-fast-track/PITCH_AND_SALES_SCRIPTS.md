## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping pharmaceutical, biologics, and medical device companies navigate regulatory pathways with CDSCO. The Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation Fast Track scheme is designed to accelerate approvals for registration certificates, import licenses, and market authorizations—especially for innovators and exporters. I’d like to understand your current regulatory challenges and see if this scheme aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of product are you seeking approval for—drug, biologic, medical device, or cosmetic—and is it for manufacture, import, or both in India?  
2. Are you a manufacturer, importer, exporter, or authorized agent applying via the SUGAM portal?  
3. Have you already obtained a valid wholesale or manufacturing license (Form 20B/21C) for your operations in India?  
4. Do you have stability data, product specifications, and labeling compliant with Rule 96 of the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules ready for submission?  
5. Are you submitting a new drug application requiring Import Permission (Form-45/45A), or is this for a registered product?  
6. Have you prepared a notarized Plant Master File (PMF) and Drug Master File (DMF) for your product?  
7. Are you claiming any MSME or DPIIT-recognized startup benefits for relaxed experience or turnover criteria under government policy?  
8. What is your target timeline for obtaining registration or import license, and are you facing delays in the current review process?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is a manufacturer, importer, exporter, or authorized agent of drugs, biologics, medical devices, or cosmetics  
- [ ] Seeking registration, import license, or market authorization in India  
- [ ] Complies with Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and Rules 1945  
- [ ] Has submitted required documentation via SUGAM portal (https://sugam.cdsco.in)  
- [ ] Adheres to GMP, GDP, and holds valid wholesale/manufacturing licenses  
- [ ] Provides product details, stability data, and undertakings as per CDSCO guidelines  
- [ ] (If applicable) Qualifies as an MSME or DPIIT-recognized startup for relaxations in experience/turnover  
- [ ] Not relying on the scheme for direct financial support (it provides procedural/regulatory benefits only)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you’re aiming to accelerate approval for your [product type] via the SUGAM portal—exactly what the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation Fast Track scheme supports. This scheme reduces scrutiny timelines and enhances transparency through digital tracking, helping clients like you get to market faster without compromising safety or quality.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**  
Pharmaceutical, biologic, and medical device applicants face lengthy, opaque approval processes for registration certificates and import licenses, delaying market access and increasing compliance costs—especially when managing complex documentation across multiple regulatory stages.

**Scheme intro:**  
The Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation Fast Track scheme, administered by CDSCO under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, offers procedural and regulatory benefits via the SUGAM portal (https://sugam.cdsco.in). It does not provide direct financial support but accelerates processing of registration certificates (Form-40), import licenses (Form-8/8A), and market authorizations through digital submission, real-time tracking, and coordinated review by Drugs Inspectors up to the Drug Controller General of India.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**  
- You are a manufacturer/importer/exporter of drugs, biologics, medical devices, or cosmetics seeking registration or import licensing in India  
- You comply with the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and Rules 1945, including GMP/GDP adherence  
- You are preparing documentation for submission via the SUGAM portal, including TR-6 challan for fees (1500 USD per site or 1000 USD per drug)  
- You may qualify for MSME/startup relaxations on experience and turnover criteria if applicable
