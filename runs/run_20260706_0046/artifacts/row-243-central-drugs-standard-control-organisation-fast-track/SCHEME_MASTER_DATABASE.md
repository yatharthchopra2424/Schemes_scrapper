### Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation Fast Track
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO), Directorate General of Health Services, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://sugam.cdsco.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Manufacturers, importers, exporters, and authorized agents of drugs, biologics, medical devices, and cosmetics
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Pharmaceuticals, biologics, medical devices, cosmetics
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must comply with Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940, and Rules 1945; submission of required documentation via SUGAM portal; adherence to GMP, GDP; valid wholesale/manufacturing licenses; product details; stability data; undertakings as per CDSCO guidelines; startups and MSMEs may avail relaxations in experience and turnover criteria under relevant government policies

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: No direct financial support; offers procedural and regulatory benefits including faster processing of registration certificates, import licenses, and market authorizations; reduced timelines for scrutiny and approval; enhanced transparency through online tracking; facilitation of export-oriented permissions and no-objection certificates; support for innovation via accelerated pathways for biologics and medical devices; alignment with international regulatory standards; access to guidance documents, public notices, stakeholder consultation mechanisms; integration with national pharmacovigilance and quality control systems
- Disbursement mode: Not applicable (no financial disbursement)
- Duration of benefit: One-time per application (benefit realized upon approval)

#### Documents Required
1. Covering Letter indicating type of application
2. Application in Form-40 (for Registration Certificate) or Form 8/8A (for Import License)
3. Original Power of Attorney
4. TR-6 Challan of fees paid (1500 USD per site or equivalent in INR; 1000 USD per drug or equivalent)
5. Copy of Import permission for new drug (Form-45 or Form-45A)
6. Copy of Valid Wholesale License (20B/21C) or Manufacturing License of Indian agent
7. Company’s authorization letter for bearer
8. Schedule D (I) and (II) Undertaking
9. Notarised copy of Plant Master File (PMF)
10. Notarised copy of Drug Master File (DMF)
11. Original Notarised Copy of Manufacturing Licence, FSC, GMP, COPP (for finished products)
12. Attested/Appostilled copy of Product Registration Certificate (e.g., CFDA, EDQM)
13. Original label/specimen label complying with Rule 96, indicating subject drug with pharmacopoeial specification, importer name & address, and Import License number

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Access the SUGAM portal at https://sugam.cdsco.in for online submission.
2. Determine the appropriate application form (e.g., Form 40 for Registration Certificate, Form 8/8A for Import License) based on the product type.
3. Prepare and upload required documents including covering letter, application form, power of attorney, TR-6 challan of fees paid, import permissions, wholesale/manufacturing licenses, authorization letters, product specifications, stability data, and labels.
4. Submit the application through the SUGAM portal.
5. Undergo technical review by Drugs Inspector, Assistant Drugs Inspector, Assistant Drugs Controller, Deputy Drugs Controller, and Joint Drugs Controller.
6. Respond to any queries raised during review.
7. Receive final approval or rejection from the Drug Controller General of India (for Registration Certificate) or Joint Drugs Controller (for Import License).
8. Upon approval, download the certificate or license from the portal.
