# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Entity is involved in logistics, transport, warehousing, or industrial operations  
- [ ] Seeking to establish or upgrade a multimodal logistics park in India  
- [ ] Project location aligns with national logistics plans and Bharatmala Pariyojana objectives  
- [ ] Priority consideration given if project is in North Eastern Region, border areas, or strategic corridors  
- [ ] Private sector participation through PPP models is encouraged (not mandatory)  
- [ ] Environmental and social safeguards compliance is required during implementation  
- [ ] Land acquisition and statutory clearances are the responsibility of the project developer  
- [ ] Entity must be capable of preparing a Detailed Project Report (DPR)  
- [ ] Entity must have financial viability to support project execution  

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts (Eligibility, Objectives, Key Caveats, Application Process)

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation documents of the applicant entity  
- PAN card  
- GST registration certificate  
- Audited financial statements / ITR (last 3 years)  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (if MSME)  
- Board resolution authorizing application  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) including technical, financial, and environmental aspects  
- Land ownership or lease documents for the proposed MMLP site  
- Environmental clearance certificates  
- No Objection Certificates (NOCs) from relevant departments (e.g., Pollution Control Board, Forest, Railways, Customs)  
- Financial viability report  
- Project implementation plan  
- Bank solvency certificate  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 10 MB per document)  
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ClientID_Date.pdf` (e.g., `MMLP_DPR_ClientX_20250405.pdf`)  
- Certified true copies required for incorporation documents, land deeds, and financial statements  
- Non-English documents must be accompanied by certified Hindi/English translations  
- DPR must be signed and stamped by a registered consultant or chartered engineer  
- Bank solvency certificate must be issued by a scheduled commercial bank and not older than 3 months

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Required**: Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is mandatory for the MMLP Scheme.  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Location and Site Analysis (alignment with Bharatmala, Act East Policy)  
- Technical Design (modal integration: road, rail, IWT, air cargo linkages)  
- Land Acquisition Plan and Status  
- Environmental Impact Assessment and Mitigation Measures  
- Financial Model (CAPEX, OPEX, funding sources: equity, debt, grants, PPP viability)  
- Economic Analysis (logistics cost reduction, employment generation, regional impact)  
- Implementation Schedule (Gantt chart, milestones)  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
- Concession Agreement Structure (if PPP)  
- Annexures: Drawings, clearances, NOCs, land documents  

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts (Required Documents, Application Process, Benefits)

### Application Form Filling Protocol
As no standardized online application form is specified in the evidence, proposals are submitted via email or hard copy to NHIDCL. Follow these protocols:  

1. **Address Submission Correctly**: Send to `info[at]nhidcl[dot]com` with subject: “Proposal for Multimodal Logistics Park under MMLP Scheme – [Client Name]”  
2. **Include Cover Letter**: Summarize project location, estimated cost, expected benefits, and alignment with national logistics plans  
3. **Attach All Documents**: Ensure all scheme-specific and universal documents are attached as per checklist  
4. **Declare PPP Intent**: Clearly state if proposing under PPP model and preferred structure (BOT, DBFOT, etc.)  
5. **Highlight Priority Areas**: Emphasize if project is in North Eastern Region, border area, or strategic corridor  
6. **Confirm Compliance**: Explicitly state adherence to environmental and social safeguards  
7. **Provide Contact Details**: Include authorized signatory’s name, designation, email, and phone  
8. **Retain Proof**: Save sent email with timestamp and request read receipt; if hard copy, obtain acknowledgment  

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts (Application Process, Contact Details), Supporting Evidence (NHIDCL contact info)

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All eligibility criteria verified and documented  
- [ ] DPR completed and reviewed by technical expert  
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents attached and certified  
- [ ] Universal documents (PAN, GST, incorporation, bank solvency) are valid and current  
- [ ] Cover letter includes project alignment with Bharatmala and Act East Policy  
- [ ] Financial viability report shows sustainable debt-service coverage ratio (DSCR > 1.2)  
- [ ] Environmental clearances and NOCs are either attached or applied for (with proof)  
- [ ] Declarations signed by authorized signatory  
- [ ] Submission protocol followed (email sent to correct address with proper subject)  
- [ ] Reference number or acknowledgment requested and logged

  NHIDCL Corporate Office, 1st & 2nd Floor, Tower A, World Trade Centre, Nauroji Nagar, New Delhi – 110029

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and review approval letter from NHIDCL specifying sanctioned support (in-principle approval, concession agreement terms, or MoU)  
- [ ] Verify sanctioned amount and form of support (e.g., viability gap funding, land support, facilitation role)  
- [ ] Sign concession agreement or MoU with NHIDCL as per approved terms  
- [ ] Initiate land acquisition (if not already completed)  
- [ ] Obtain pending statutory clearances (environmental, forest, etc.)  
- [ ] Begin EPC tendering or developer selection (if PPP)  
- [ ] Track utilization of funds against approved milestones  
- [ ] Prepare utilization certificates and progress reports for submission to NHIDCL  
- [ ] Confirm invoicing protocol for success fee (if applicable under consultancy agreement)  
- [ ] Monitor commissioning and operational readiness as per agreed framework  
- [ ] Ensure integration with digital platforms (INAMPRO, INFRACON, ePACE) as mentioned in scheme benefits  

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts (Benefits, Application Process, Financial Support)  
> **Note**: Exact post-approval steps depend on the nature of approval (in-principle, concession, etc.) — verify with nodal officer if unclear.
