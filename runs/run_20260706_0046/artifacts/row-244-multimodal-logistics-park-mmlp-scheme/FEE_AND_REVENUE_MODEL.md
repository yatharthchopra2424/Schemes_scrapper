## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the scale and strategic impact of the Multimodal Logistics Park (MMLP) Scheme benefits, which include reduced logistics costs, enhanced regional connectivity, and access to global funding via PPP models.
2. Risk-adjusted fees reflecting the scheme’s rolling basis, long implementation timelines, and dependency on land acquisition, statutory clearances, and alignment with Bharatmala Pariyojana objectives.
3. Transparent fee structures tied to milestones such as DPR approval, concession agreement signing, and project commissioning under NHIDCL.
4. Custom pricing for Enterprise-tier clients reflects the scheme’s focus on large-scale infrastructure projects requiring private sector participation through PPP models and funding from global agencies like ADB and JICA.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description:** Retainer fee for consulting services related to the Multimodal Logistics Park (MMLP) Scheme, including project structuring, DPR preparation, and alignment with NHIDCL requirements under the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description:** Success fee for securing approvals, concession agreements, or funding under the Multimodal Logistics Park (MMLP) Scheme implemented by the National Highways & Infrastructure Development Corporation Limited (NHIDCL), under the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways, Government of India.
