# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping logistics operators, transport agencies, industrial units, and MSMEs engaged in logistics access government infrastructure schemes like the Multimodal Logistics Park (MMLP) Scheme. Given your work in logistics, transport, warehousing, or industrial operations, I wanted to explore whether this scheme could support your plans to establish or upgrade a multimodal logistics park in India — particularly in the North Eastern Region, border areas, or strategic corridors under Bharatmala Pariyojana.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is the current status of your logistics or warehousing operations — are you planning to establish a new multimodal logistics park or upgrade an existing facility?  
2. Where is your proposed or existing site located? Is it in the North Eastern Region, a border area, or along a strategic corridor under Bharatmala Pariyojana?  
3. What mode(s) of transport are you looking to integrate — road, rail, inland waterways, or others?  
4. Have you already prepared a Detailed Project Report (DPR) for the project?  
5. Do you have clear land ownership or lease documents for the proposed site?  
6. What is the estimated project cost and funding structure you are considering — are you exploring PPP models or seeking funding from institutions like ADB or JICA?  
7. Are you looking to integrate digital platforms like INAMPRO, INFRACON, or ePACE for transparent operations?  
8. What is your timeline for land acquisition, clearances, and project commissioning?

- [ ] Entity is involved in logistics, transport, warehousing, or industrial operations  
- [ ] Seeking to establish or upgrade a multimodal logistics park in India

Based on your responses, it sounds like your project aligns well with the Multimodal Logistics Park (MMLP) Scheme — which supports entities like yours in developing integrated logistics infrastructure through PPP models, with facilitation from NHIDCL and access to funding from global agencies like ADB and JICA. ## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Logistics costs in India remain high due to fragmented infrastructure, poor connectivity between road, rail, and inland waterways, and limited access to integrated logistics hubs — especially in remote and strategic regions like the North East, border areas, and economic corridors. This increases supply chain inefficiencies and hinders market access for businesses.

**Scheme intro:**
The Multimodal Logistics Park (MMLP) Scheme, administered by the National Highways & Infrastructure Development Corporation Limited (NHIDCL) under the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways, Government of India, supports the development of integrated multimodal logistics infrastructure through public-private partnership (PPP) models. It facilitates access to funding from government budgetary allocations, financial institutions, and global agencies such as the Asian Development Bank (ADB) and Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA). While the scheme does not provide direct subsidies, it enables project structuring, clearances, and implementation support to reduce logistics costs and enhance regional connectivity.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are involved in logistics, transport, warehousing, or industrial operations seeking to establish or upgrade a multimodal logistics park  
- Your proposed site is in the North Eastern Region, border area, or strategic corridor under Bharatmala Pariyojana (a key eligibility priority)  
- You are open to PPP models and private sector participation (encouraged under the scheme)  
- You aim to integrate road, rail, and/or inland waterways for seamless freight movement (aligned with scheme objectives)
