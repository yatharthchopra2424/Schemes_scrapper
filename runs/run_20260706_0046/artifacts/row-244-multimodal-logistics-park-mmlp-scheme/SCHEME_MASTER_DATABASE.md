### Multimodal Logistics Park (MMLP) Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Highways & Infrastructure Development Corporation Limited (NHIDCL), under the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nhidcl.com
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Entities involved in logistics, transport, warehousing, or industrial operations
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Logistics, transport, warehousing, industrial operations
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Entities seeking to establish or upgrade multimodal logistics parks in India, particularly in the North Eastern Region, border areas, and strategic corridors under Bharatmala Pariyojana; must align with national logistics plans and Bharatmala Pariyojana objectives; private sector participation encouraged through PPP models; environmental and social safeguards must be complied with; land acquisition and statutory clearances are the responsibility of the project developer

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support structured through public-private partnership (PPP) models, with funding sourced from government budgetary allocations, loans from financial institutions, and investments from global agencies such as the Asian Development Bank (ADB) and Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA); benefits include reduced logistics costs, improved supply chain efficiency, enhanced regional connectivity, better access to national and international markets, support for industrial development, job creation in logistics and allied sectors, integration with digital platforms like INAMPRO, INFRACON, and ePACE
- Disbursement mode: Not specified (PPP model implies phased disbursement tied to project milestones)
- Duration of benefit: Not specified (project-based, tied to implementation and operations phase)

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
2. Land ownership or lease documents
3. Environmental clearance certificates
4. No Objection Certificates (NOCs) from relevant departments
5. Financial viability report
6. Project implementation plan
7. Incorporation documents of the applicant entity
8. PAN and GST registration
9. Bank solvency certificate

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify a suitable location for the Multimodal Logistics Park (MMLP) in alignment with national logistics plans and regional connectivity goals.
2. Prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR) including technical, financial, and environmental aspects.
3. Submit the proposal to NHIDCL or the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways for appraisal.
4. Obtain necessary clearances and approvals from relevant authorities.
5. Enter into a concession agreement or MoU with NHIDCL for project execution.
6. Proceed with land acquisition, design, and construction as per approved plans.
7. Commission the MMLP and initiate operations under the agreed operational framework.
