# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
The **Multimodal Logistics Park (MMLP) Scheme** is a government initiative under the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways, implemented by the **National Highways & Infrastructure Development Corporation Limited (NHIDCL)**. The scheme operates on a **rolling basis** with no fixed deadline, accepting proposals periodically based on fund availability and project readiness. It is a **Pan-India** scheme focused on developing integrated multimodal logistics infrastructure to reduce logistics costs and enhance regional connectivity, particularly in the **North Eastern Region, border areas, and strategic corridors under Bharatmala Pariyojana**.

### Objectives
The MMLP Scheme aims to:
- Develop integrated multimodal logistics infrastructure to reduce logistics costs  
- Enhance connectivity between road, rail, and inland waterways for seamless freight movement  
- Support the Act East Policy by improving regional connectivity in the North Eastern Region  
- Promote economic growth in remote and strategic regions through improved infrastructure  
- Facilitate international trade by strengthening links with neighbouring countries  
- Develop automated parking, multi-modal logistics parks, and bus ports as part of IT-enabled infrastructure  
- Act as a nodal agency for infrastructure development in border and international connectivity roads  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Eligibility Criteria | Details |
|----------------------|---------|
| **Entity Type** | Entities involved in logistics, transport, warehousing, or industrial operations seeking to establish or upgrade multimodal logistics parks in India |
| **Geographic Focus** | Particularly in the North Eastern Region, border areas, and strategic corridors under Bharatmala Pariyojana |
| **Participation Mode** | Through collaboration with NHIDCL; private sector participation encouraged via PPP models |
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Logistics operators; transport agencies; industrial units; freight forwarders; MSMEs engaged in logistics |
| **Key Caveats** | Projects must align with national logistics plans and Bharatmala Pariyojana objectives; priority given to projects in North Eastern Region, border areas, and strategic corridors; environmental and social safeguards must be complied with; land acquisition and statutory clearances are the responsibility of the project developer |

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Benefit Category | Details |
|------------------|---------|
| **Financial Support Structure** | Structured through public-private partnership (PPP) models; funding sourced from government budgetary allocations, loans from financial institutions, and investments from global agencies such as the Asian Development Bank (ADB) and Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) |
| **NHIDCL Role** | Facilitates project structuring and implementation, leveraging expertise in infrastructure development in challenging terrains |
| **Operational Benefits** | Reduced logistics costs; improved supply chain efficiency; enhanced regional connectivity; better access to national and international markets; support for industrial development; job creation in logistics and allied sectors |
| **Digital Integration** | Integration with digital platforms like INAMPRO, INFRACON, and ePACE for transparent operations |
| **Funding Access** | Enables private sector participation through PPP models and facilitates access to funding from global institutions like ADB and JICA |

### Required Documents
Applicants must submit the following documents:
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)  
2. Land ownership or lease documents  
3. Environmental clearance certificates  
4. No Objection Certificates (NOCs) from relevant departments  
5. Financial viability report  
6. Project implementation plan  
7. Incorporation documents of the applicant entity  
8. PAN and GST registration  
9. Bank solvency certificate  

### Application Process
The application process involves the following steps:
1. Identify a suitable location for the Multimodal Logistics Park (MMLP) in alignment with national logistics plans and regional connectivity goals  
2. Prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR) including technical, financial, and environmental aspects  
3. Submit the proposal to NHIDCL or the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways for appraisal  
4. Obtain necessary clearances and approvals from relevant authorities  
5. Enter into a concession agreement or MoU with NHIDCL for project execution  
6. Proceed with land acquisition, design, and construction as per approved plans  
7. Commission the MMLP and initiate operations under the agreed operational framework
