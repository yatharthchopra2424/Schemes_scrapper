## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Entity is an industry, manufacturing unit, or logistics provider  
- [ ] Entity is located near or planning to locate along the Dedicated Freight Corridor (DFC) network  
- [ ] Entity has valid industrial registration (e.g., Udyam, MSME, or equivalent)  
- [ ] Entity complies with DFCCIL's technical and safety standards for siding connections  
- [ ] Entity has identified a feasible siding alignment along the DFC network  
- [ ] Entity can provide traffic projections and commodity details for the proposed linkage  
- [ ] Entity is prepared to commit to minimum traffic volume thresholds (as determined by DFCCIL)  
- [ ] Entity has land ownership or lease rights for the proposed siding alignment  

> **Note**: There is no explicit turnover threshold, employee count, or age of business specified in the evidence. Eligibility is based on operational feasibility, location, and technical compliance.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration  
- PAN Card of the entity  
- GST Registration Certificate  
- Audited Financial Statements (last 2 years)  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the entity (last 2 years)  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration Certificate (if MSME)  
- Authorization Letter from Authorized Signatory  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Land ownership or lease documents for siding alignment agreement  
- Project report with traffic projections and commodity details  
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) from relevant authorities (e.g., land, environment, local bodies)  
- Detailed engineering drawings of the proposed siding  
- Environmental and safety compliance documents  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)  
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_CaseID.pdf` (e.g., `DFC_Industry_Linkage_LandDocs_CASE001.pdf`)  
- Names on documents must match exactly with the Certificate of Incorporation and PAN  
- Certificates (e.g., NOC, environmental compliance) must be issued by competent authorities and be valid (not expired)  
- Engineering drawings must be signed and stamped by a licensed engineer  
- All documents must be in English or accompanied by a certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
✅ **Required**  
The project report (DPR) is explicitly listed as a scheme-specific document. It must include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Location and Alignment with DFC Network  
- Traffic Projections (volume, commodity type, origin-destination)  
- Commodity Details (type, handling requirements, volume forecasts)  
- Technical Feasibility Study  
- Cost Estimate for Siding Construction  
- Implementation Timeline (milestones)  
- Environmental and Safety Impact Assessment  
- Financial Plan and Funding Structure  
- Commitment Letter on Minimum Traffic Volume (to be coordinated with DFCCIL)  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the DFCCIL portal at https://dfccil.com and navigate to the "Industry Linkage" or "Private Siding" section.  
2. Download and fill the official application form (if available) or prepare a cover letter addressing the application as per the process described.  
3. Enter all entity details exactly as per Certificate of Incorporation and PAN (no abbreviations).  
4. Clearly state the proposed siding location, nearest DFC section, and alignment details.  
5. Attach all scheme-specific and universal documents in the specified order and format.  
6. Declare compliance with DFCCIL’s technical and safety standards for siding connections.  
7. Specify the expected traffic volume and commodity details as per the project report.  
8. Ensure the authorized signatory signs the form/application letter and attaches their authorization letter.  
> **Portal Note**: Since the portal is https://dfccil.com (DFCCIL’s corporate site), applications may be submitted via email or online form—verify the exact submission method with the nodal officer if not clearly available on the portal.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and store the official approval letter from DFCCIL  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount and disbursement milestones (linked to construction progress)  
- [ ] Sign the Licensing Agreement for siding construction and maintenance as per DFCCIL specifications  
- [ ] Ensure construction is supervised and compliant with DFCCIL and Indian Railways technical standards  
- [ ] Complete safety inspection and commissioning as per DFCCIL protocols  
- [ ] Obtain Utilization Certificate (if required for tranche release)  
- [ ] Begin operations and avail benefits: priority path allocation, reduced freight charges, assured wagon availability  
- [ ] Monitor and maintain minimum traffic volume commitments to avoid review of financial support  
- [ ] Track milestone-based disbursements and submit progress reports as required  
- [ ] Prepare for success fee invoicing upon confirmation of fund disbursement  

> **Note**: Financial support is disbursed in milestones tied to construction progress and operational readiness. Continued eligibility depends on meeting traffic volume commitments and maintaining siding to DFCCIL safety standards.
