## Pricing Principles

1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial benefit derived from DFC linkage, which includes capital cost support for private siding construction, reduced freight charges, and improved logistics efficiency.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees account for the technical and procedural complexity of the application, including feasibility studies, licensing agreements, and compliance with DFCCIL’s safety and technical standards.
3. **Transparency & Milestone Linking**: Retainers cover initial advisory and documentation preparation; success fees are triggered upon formal approval of the linkage agreement and disbursement of financial support.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Dedicated Freight Corridor (DFC) Industry Linkage scheme involves a medium-risk profile due to its rolling application basis, technical feasibility assessments, and dependency on traffic volume commitments. Processing timelines can extend over several months due to engineering evaluations, licensing agreements, and construction supervision, which affects revenue realization lag. However, the scheme’s clear eligibility criteria and structured disbursement milestones (linked to construction progress) support predictable success fee realization upon project milestones.
