## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**  
Industries and logistics providers along the DFC corridor face high logistics costs, unpredictable transit times, and unreliable wagon availability due to inadequate last-mile rail connectivity. This increases supply chain costs and reduces competitiveness, especially for EXIM and bulk cargo movement.

**Scheme intro:**  
The **Dedicated Freight Corridor (DFC) Industry Linkage** scheme, administered by the **Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Limited (DFCCIL)**, provides **financial support for the construction of private sidings** connecting industries to the DFC network. Support is disbursed in milestones based on construction progress and operational readiness, typically covering a portion of the capital cost based on traffic volume and commitment. Benefits include priority path allocation, reduced freight charges, assured wagon availability, and end-to-end logistics solutions.
