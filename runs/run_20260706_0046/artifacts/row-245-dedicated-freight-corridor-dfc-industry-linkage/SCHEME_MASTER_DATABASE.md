### Dedicated Freight Corridor (DFC) Industry Linkage
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Limited (DFCCIL)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dfccil.com
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Industries, manufacturing units, and logistics providers
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Logistics & Transport, Manufacturing, Industrial
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have valid industrial registration and comply with DFCCIL's technical and safety standards for siding connections; located near or planning to locate along the Dedicated Freight Corridor network

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support for construction of private sidings connecting industries to the DFC network, typically covering a portion of the capital cost based on traffic volume and commitment
- Disbursement mode: Disbursed in milestones linked to construction progress and operational readiness
- Duration of benefit: One-time (for construction support); ongoing benefits include priority path allocation, reduced freight charges, assured wagon availability

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. Land ownership or lease documents for siding alignment agreement
3. Project report with traffic projections and commodity details
4. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from relevant authorities
5. Detailed engineering drawings of the proposed siding
6. Environmental and safety compliance documents
7. Authorization letter from authorized signatory

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the nearest DFC alignment and conduct feasibility study for siding connection.
2. Submit a formal application to DFCCIL with project details, land documents, and traffic projections.
3. Obtain in-principle approval from DFCCIL after technical evaluation.
4. Sign a Licensing Agreement for siding construction and maintenance.
5. Construct the siding as per DFCCIL specifications under supervision.
6. Complete safety inspection and commissioning.
7. Begin operations and avail linkage benefits.
