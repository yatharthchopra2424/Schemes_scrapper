## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Entity is a state government department or agency involved in logistics and supply chain operations  
- [ ] Entity is a logistics service provider (e.g., transporter, warehousing, freight forwarder) operating across Indian states  
- [ ] Entity is an industry association representing stakeholders in logistics, transport, or supply chain sectors  
- [ ] Entity is engaged in inter-state or international trade logistics activities  
- [ ] Entity is willing to participate in data sharing, stakeholder consultations, or performance assessments under LEEP  
- [ ] Entity operates within the geographic scope of Pan-India (no regional restrictions)  
- [ ] Entity is not seeking direct financial subsidy (LEEP does not provide financial support to beneficiaries)  

> **Note**: LEEP is a policy coordination and performance assessment framework. Eligibility is based on stakeholder role in logistics ecosystem, not financial or turnover thresholds. No MSME registration, Udyam, or GST turnover criteria apply for participation.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- **Not required**. LEEP does not involve project funding or financial disbursement.  
- A brief operational profile (1–2 pages) describing logistics activities, geographic reach, and stakeholder role is sufficient.  
- If a detailed proposal is sought by the nodal officer for specific interventions, verify requirements directly with DPIIT Logistics Division.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official LEEP portal ([https://leepportal.dpiit.gov.in](https://leepportal.dpiit.gov.in)) and register using entity PAN and authorized email.  
2. Select “Stakeholder Registration” or “Participate in LEEP” as the applicant type (not “beneficiary” or “subsidy applicant”).  
3. Fill in entity details exactly as per PAN and registration documents (name, address, contact person).  
4. Clearly state your role: e.g., “Logistics Service Provider – Inter-state Freight,” “State Logistics Department,” or “Industry Association – Warehousing Sector.”  
5. In the “Operations Profile” section, specify corridors served (e.g., Delhi-Mumbai, Kolkata-Haldia) and modes (road, rail, multimodal).  
6. Declare willingness to participate in data sharing, surveys, and consultations under LEEP framework.  
7. Upload all documents in PDF format as per naming convention.  
8. Review all entries before final submission — no edits allowed after submission; contact helpdesk for corrections.
