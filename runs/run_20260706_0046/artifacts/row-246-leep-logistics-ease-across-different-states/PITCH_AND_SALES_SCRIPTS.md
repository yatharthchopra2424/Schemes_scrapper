## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping logistics providers, MSMEs, and industry stakeholders navigate government initiatives that improve supply chain efficiency and reduce inter-state trade friction. The LEEP – Logistics Ease Across Different States framework, led by DPIIT, is designed to enhance logistics performance through policy coordination and infrastructure planning—critical for businesses moving goods across India. I’d like to understand your current logistics challenges and see if aligning with LEEP’s objectives could support your operational goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What are your biggest pain points in inter-state logistics—such as transit delays, regulatory hurdles, or infrastructure bottlenecks?  
2. Are you currently engaged with any state-level logistics initiatives or DPIIT-led programs like the LEEP index or Ease of Logistics?  
3. What is your primary role in the supply chain—are you a logistics service provider, manufacturer, trader, or industry association?  
4. How do you currently track or measure logistics performance, transit times, or cost efficiency across states?  
5. Are you involved in any multimodal logistics planning or infrastructure utilisation initiatives?  
6. Does your business engage in international trade or cross-border supply chains that could benefit from streamlined Indian logistics corridors?  
7. Are you collaborating with state governments or industry bodies on logistics policy or infrastructure development?  
8. What improvements would most impact your competitiveness—reduced transit times, lower logistics costs, or better supply chain reliability?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Engaged in logistics and supply chain operations across India  
- [ ] Operates as a logistics service provider, MSME, industry association, or state government entity  
- [ ] Involved in inter-state or international trade logistics  
- [ ] Interested in improving logistics efficiency, reducing transit times, or enhancing supply chain reliability  
- [ ] Open to participating in policy coordination, data sharing, or stakeholder consultations  
- [ ] Seeking to benchmark performance or contribute to logistics performance monitoring (e.g., LEEP index)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like LEEP’s focus on reducing logistics costs, improving inter-state coordination, and enhancing supply chain reliability aligns closely with your goals. While LEEP doesn’t offer direct financial support, it provides a strategic framework for influencing policy, accessing performance insights, and benefiting from systemic improvements—let me explain how.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**  
Businesses engaged in inter-state trade face fragmented regulations, infrastructure inefficiencies, and coordination gaps between central and state agencies, leading to higher logistics costs, transit delays, and unreliable supply chains—especially for MSMEs and logistics providers without direct influence on policy.

**Scheme intro:**  
LEEP – Logistics Ease Across Different States, administered by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry, is a pan-India policy and coordination framework. It does not provide direct financial support but drives systemic improvements through governance, infrastructure planning, regulatory harmonization, and performance monitoring via the LEEP index. Benefits include improved logistics efficiency, reduced transit times and costs, enhanced supply chain reliability, better infrastructure utilization, and data-driven policy insights.
