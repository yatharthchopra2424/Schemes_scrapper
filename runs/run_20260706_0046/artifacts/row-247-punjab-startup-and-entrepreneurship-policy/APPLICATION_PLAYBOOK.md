## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Startup is incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm under relevant Indian laws

- [ ] Working on innovative products, services, or processes

- Business plan or project report

- Proof of office address in Punjab (rent agreement, utility bill, property tax receipt)

### Document Quality Check

- Certificates must be valid and not expired (especially GST, Udyam, address proof)  
- PAN and incorporation details must match across all documents  
- Bank account details must be in the name of the entity  
- Declarations and undertakings must be signed and stamped (where applicable  
- Documents in regional language must be accompanied by certified English/Hindi translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a **business plan or project report** is explicitly required as per the scheme evidence.  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Founder(s) Profile  
- Problem Statement & Innovation  
- Product/Service Description  
- Technology or Process Involved  
- Market Analysis (TAM, SAM, SOM)  
- Business Model & Revenue Strategy  
- Go-to-Market Plan  
- Financial Projections (3–5 years)  
- Funding Requirements & Utilization Plan  
- Milestones & Timeline  
- Team & Advisory Board  
- Risks & Mitigation  
- Annexures: Prototypes, test results, IP details  

> **Note**: While not explicitly termed a DPR, the project report must substantiate innovation, scalability, and fund utilization. Verify depth requirements with nodal officer if unsure.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Register first** on the portal [https://invest.punjab.gov.in](https://invest.punjab.gov.in) as a startup before applying for recognition or funding.  
2. Ensure **all mandatory fields** are filled; the portal may not allow submission with blanks.  
3. Use **exact legal name** as per Certificate of Incorporation in all fields.  
4. Enter **PAN and bank details accurately** – mismatches lead to rejection.  
5. Describe **innovation clearly** in the designated field – highlight novelty, scalability, and Punjab relevance.  
6. Upload documents in **PDF format only**, under 2 MB each, with correct naming.  
7. Select **correct entity type** (Private Limited / LLP / Partnership) – incorrect selection invalidates application.  
8. Declare **commitment to retain operations in Punjab for 3 years** if availing support – this is a conditional clause.  
9. Save draft periodically and **take screenshot before final submission**.  
10. Do **not submit without internal review** – incomplete applications are not reconsidered.  

> **Portal Note**: The application involves two stages – (1) startup recognition, (2) financial support application. Complete both sequentially.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All personal and entity details match incorporation documents  
- [ ] PAN, bank account, and address proof are consistent and valid  
- [ ] Business plan clearly outlines innovation, market, and financials  
- [ ] Details of innovation are specific and technically sound

- [ ] Funding details (if any) are accurately disclosed  
- [ ] Patent/IPR details (if claimed) are correctly entered with application numbers  
- [ ] Recognition certificate from DI Punjab or approved incubator is attached  
- [ ] Declarations regarding 3-year Punjab operations and no false information are signed  
- [ ] All uploaded files are PDF, <2 MB, and correctly named  
- [ ] Application form is fully filled with no blank mandatory fields  
- [ ] Final screenshot taken and reference number generation confirmed
