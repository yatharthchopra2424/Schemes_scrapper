**Purpose:** To ensure the secure handling, storage, and disposal of client data in compliance with
data protection principles and scheme-specific requirements.

**Scope:** Applies to all consultants, staff, and contractors handling client data for government
scheme consulting engagements.

**Policy Statements:**

1. **Data Storage:** All client data shall be stored in encrypted cloud storage with access controls.
2. **Access Control:** Access to client data is limited to authorized personnel only, based on role
   and necessity.
3. **Authentication:** Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for all access to client data
   systems.
4. **Device Usage:** No personal devices may be used to store or process client data unless
   explicitly authorized and secured by the firm.
5. **Retention Period:** Client data shall be retained for 2 years post case closure, unless a
   longer period is required by scheme guidelines or legal obligation.
6. **Scheme-specific Retention:** All documents submitted to Department of Industries, Government of Punjab under Punjab Startup and Entrepreneurship Policy
   must be retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit purposes.
7. **Breach Notification:** In the event of a data breach, the firm shall notify affected clients
   and relevant authorities within 24 hours of discovery.
8. **Data Disposal:** After the retention period, data shall be securely deleted or destroyed using
   approved methods.
9. **Training:** All staff shall receive annual training on data handling and security protocols.
10. **Compliance:** This policy aligns with the Information Technology Act, 2000, and relevant
    data protection guidelines.

**Review:** This policy shall be reviewed annually or as required by changes in regulations or
scheme guidelines.
