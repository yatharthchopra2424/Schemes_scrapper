## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the tangible financial benefit delivered to the client, with success fees calibrated to the scheme’s maximum grant of ₹25 Lakhs per startup.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees account for the scheme’s rolling application basis and dependency on fund availability under the Punjab Startup Fund, which introduces moderate uncertainty in approval timing and outcome.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The retainer-success fee model ensures accessibility for early-stage startups (Growth tier) while scaling effort and reward with client complexity and potential benefit.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Given the scheme’s rolling deadlines and typical 3–6 month processing timeline (inferred from multi-stage disbursement and progress reporting requirements), success fee realization may lag, warranting a slightly higher success fee band (6–8%) for Growth-tier clients to compensate for working capital and follow-up effort.
