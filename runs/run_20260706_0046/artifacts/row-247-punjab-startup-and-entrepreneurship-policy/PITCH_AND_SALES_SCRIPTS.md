## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I’m calling from [Your Firm Name]. We specialise in helping Punjab-based startups access government funding and support schemes like the Punjab Startup and Entrepreneurship Policy. I’d like to understand your startup’s current stage and goals to see if this scheme could be a good fit for you — no obligation, just a quick diagnostic chat.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated, and under what legal structure (Private Limited, LLP, or Partnership Firm)?
2. Is your headquarters or primary place of operations located in Punjab?
3. Are you working on an innovative product, service, or process that solves a specific problem?
4. Have you received recognition from the Department of Industries, Government of Punjab, or an approved incubator?
5. Are you currently seeking seed funding or financial support for product development or market entry?
6. Have you incurred any costs related to patent or intellectual property rights (IPR) filing?
7. Are you looking for access to mentorship, incubators, or investor networks?
8. Do you plan to maintain operations in Punjab for at least the next three years?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Incorporated as Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm under Indian laws  
- [ ] Headquarters or significant operations in Punjab  
- [ ] Working on innovative products, services, or processes  
- [ ] Recognised by Department of Industries, Government of Punjab, or an approved incubator  
- [ ] Target beneficiary: startup, entrepreneur, or MSME  
- [ ] Willing to maintain operations in Punjab for minimum 3 years post-support  
- [ ] Not currently receiving conflicting state funding that would disqualify eligibility  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like your startup may meet the core eligibility criteria for the Punjab Startup and Entrepreneurship Policy. This scheme offers seed funding of up to INR 25 Lakhs and additional support for IPR filing — let me walk you through how it works and whether it aligns with your needs.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Early-stage startups in Punjab often struggle to access seed funding, mentorship, and infrastructure needed to validate and scale innovative ideas. High costs of product development, patent filing, and limited access to incubators and investor networks can slow growth or force founders to rely on personal savings or high-interest debt.

**Scheme intro:**
The Punjab Startup and Entrepreneurship Policy, administered by the Department of Industries, Government of Punjab, provides seed funding of up to INR 25 Lakhs per startup in the form of grants or convertible equity. It also offers reimbursement of up to 50% of patent filing costs, capped at INR 2 Lakhs per startup, along with access to state-funded incubators, mentorship, fast-track approvals, and investor linkages.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm  
- Headquartered or maintaining significant operations in Punjab  
- Working on an innovative product, service, or process  
- Recognised by the Department of Industries or an approved incubator (or willing to pursue recognition)
