### Punjab Startup and Entrepreneurship Policy
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Punjab
- **Administering Body:** Department of Industries, Government of Punjab
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://invest.punjab.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Innovative products, services, or processes (focus on IT, agro-processing, biotech, manufacturing)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have headquarters or significant operations in Punjab; must be recognized by the Department of Industries, Government of Punjab, or an approved incubator; must be working on innovative products, services, or processes

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to INR 25 Lakhs seed funding (grants or convertible equity); reimbursement of up to 50% of patent filing costs, max INR 2 Lakhs per startup
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (in stages based on milestones); reimbursement for IPR costs
- Duration of benefit: One-time seed funding; IPR reimbursement per application

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. Business plan or project report
4. Details of innovation or technology involved
5. Bank account details
6. Incorporation documents (MoA, AoA, Partnership deed)
7. Proof of office address in Punjab
8. Details of funding received (if any)
9. Patent or IPR application details (if applicable)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register the startup with the Department of Industries, Government of Punjab, or an approved incubator.
2. Submit the incorporation documents, business plan, and details of innovation.
3. Apply for recognition as an eligible startup under the policy.
4. Submit application for financial support along with required documents such as project report, cost estimates, and bank details.
5. Application is reviewed by the designated committee.
6. Upon approval, financial support is disbursed in stages based on milestones.
7. Submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates.
