# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Investor is looking to set up a business in Punjab (domestic or international)
- [ ] Business type: startup, MSME, entrepreneur, investor, or industry seeking state-level regulatory clearances
- [ ] Seeking fiscal incentives or regulatory approvals through the Invest Punjab portal
- [ ] No minimum turnover threshold specified (eligibility based on intent to establish/expand in Punjab)
- [ ] No minimum employee count specified
- [ ] No sector restrictions (open to all sectors seeking Punjab state-level clearances)
- [ ] No age of business restriction (new setup or expansion eligible)
- [ ] Must intend to avail benefits from central/state schemes facilitated by Invest Punjab (e.g., via SIDBI partnership for MSMEs)
- [ ] Must be willing to use the Business First Portal for unified regulatory clearances

> **Note**: Invest Punjab does not provide direct financial support but facilitates access to fiscal incentives and regulatory approvals. Eligibility is based on the investor’s intent to establish or expand a business in Punjab and seek state-level clearances/incentives.

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
- PAN of the entity
- GST registration certificate (if applicable)
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)
- ITR of the entity (last 2 years, if applicable)
- Aadhaar and PAN of authorized signatory
- Passport and visa (for international investors/directors)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Business description or project report
- Details of investment and employment generation
- Land documents or site plan (if applicable)
- Authorization letter from authorized signatory
- Any sector-specific clearances as applicable (e.g., pollution, fire, building plan)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)
- Document names must match the entity name as per Certificate of Incorporation
- Authorization letter must be on company letterhead and notarized if required
- Land documents must show clear title or lease agreement
- Project report must include investment amount, employment details, and timeline
- All documents must be in English or accompanied by certified translation
- Self-declarations must be signed and dated

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- A project report is **required** as per the scheme’s document checklist.  
- Standard sections to include:  
  - Executive summary  
  - Promoter details  
  - Business model and objectives  
  - Investment plan (capital expenditure, sources of funding)  
  - Location details (land/premises in Punjab)  
  - Employment generation plan (direct and indirect)  
  - Timeline for implementation  
  - Expected benefits (fiscal incentives sought)  
  - Environmental and social considerations (if applicable)  
- If the client is only seeking regulatory clearances (not fiscal incentives), a detailed project report may still be required for the Common Application Form (CAF).  
- **Verify with nodal officer** if a full DPR is needed for incentive-linked applications.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Visit the official portal: **https://investpunjab.gov.in**  
2. Complete **investor registration** and create login credentials  
3. Log in and access the **Online Common Application Form (CAF)** for regulatory clearances  
4. Fill in all fields accurately — do not leave mandatory fields blank  
5. Upload documents in the specified format (PDF, max 5 MB per file)  
6. Select the relevant departments for which clearances are sought (based on business activity)  
7. Apply online for **fiscal incentives** via the portal’s incentive module (if applicable)  
8. Review all entries before final submission — no edits allowed after submission  
9. Keep a copy of the submitted application and acknowledgment  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All mandatory fields in the CAF are filled  
- [ ] Universal and scheme-specific documents are attached and legible  
- [ ] Authorization letter is signed and matches the signatory in the form  
- [ ] Investment and employment details are realistic and consistent  
- [ ] Project report aligns with the information in the CAF  
- [ ] Declarations and self-certifications are signed  
- [ ] Application is submitted through the official portal only  
- [ ] Reference number is captured and stored securely

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the **Invest Punjab Business First Portal** at https://investpunjab.gov.in  
- Take a **screenshot of the submission confirmation page** showing the application reference number and timestamp  
- Save the reference number in the case file and set up automated reminders for follow-up  
- Download and store the submitted application PDF for records  
- Inform the client of successful submission and share the reference number

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the digital approval letter from the Unified Regulator system  
- [ ] Confirm the list of approved clearances (e.g., fire, pollution, building plan, land use)  
- [ ] If fiscal incentives were applied for, track sanction letters from relevant departments (via portal facilitation)  
- [ ] Guide client on utilization of approved incentives (if any) and compliance requirements  
- [ ] Assist in obtaining the **Certificate of In-Principle Approval** (valid for 3.5 years, self-declaration based)  
- [ ] Advise on post-approval compliance: timely renewal of clearances, annual returns, etc.  
- [ ] Coordinate with the client’s team for successful implementation and aftercare engagement  
- [ ] Prepare and submit utilization certificate (if required for incentive claims)  
- [ ] Follow up on aftercare services: relationship manager check-ins, grievance redressal, and continuous industry engagement support  
- [ ] Invoice success fee as per engagement agreement  

> **Note**: Invest Punjab does not disburse funds directly but enables access to incentives. Post-approval support includes ensuring the client can avail facilitated benefits and comply with approval conditions.
