## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the tangible benefits delivered—specifically, the facilitation of regulatory clearances and access to fiscal incentives via the Invest Punjab Business First Portal, which reduces compliance burden and accelerates project timelines.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile—while Invest Punjab does not directly disburse financial incentives, it enables access to central and state schemes with high success rates for compliant applicants, as evidenced by testimonials from major investors (ITC, Sun Pharma, Trident Group) and processed approvals within 45 working days post re-engineering.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The retainer-success fee structure ensures accessibility for MSMEs and startups (primary beneficiaries per eligibility) while capturing higher value from mid-market and enterprise clients undertaking large-scale investments in Punjab.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; success fees are contingent on successful facilitation of approvals or incentive access, aligning consultant incentives with client outcomes in a scheme known for time-bound digital approvals and dedicated relationship management.
