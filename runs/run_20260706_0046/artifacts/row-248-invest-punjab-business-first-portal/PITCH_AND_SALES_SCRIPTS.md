# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping businesses like yours navigate Punjab’s regulatory landscape to set up or expand operations smoothly. Since Invest Punjab – Business First Portal acts as a single point of contact for state-level clearances and fiscal incentive facilitation, I’d like to understand your current plans for establishing or growing a business in Punjab to see how we can support you.

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you planning to set up a new business or expand an existing operation in Punjab?
2. What is your incorporation date or planned date of establishment in Punjab?
3. Are you a domestic or international investor, startup, MSME, or industry player?
4. Have you begun the process of applying for state-level regulatory approvals (e.g., factory licenses, land use change, pollution clearances)?
5. Do you require assistance with fiscal incentives from central or state schemes, such as those facilitated through SIDBI partnerships?
6. What is your expected investment amount and projected employment generation in Punjab?
7. Do you have land identified or a site plan ready for your project?
8. Are you seeking a dedicated relationship manager to guide you through the investment lifecycle?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Investor is looking to set up or expand a business in Punjab  
- [ ] Applicant is a domestic or international investor, startup, MSME, or industry  
- [ ] Seeking state-level regulatory clearances across departments (e.g., industry, labour, environment, power)  
- [ ] Requires facilitation for fiscal incentives (not direct financial grants)  
- [ ] Has or plans to have: Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, business description, investment/employment details, land documents, authorization letter  
- [ ] Project involves new setup or expansion in Punjab (services limited to these)  
- [ ] Willing to use the online Common Application Form (CAF) via https://investpunjab.gov.in  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you’re planning to establish or expand a business in Punjab and need support with regulatory clearances and incentive facilitation. Invest Punjab – Business First Portal is designed exactly for this—providing a unified regulator for 40+ state-level approvals, a dedicated relationship manager, and digital, time-bound processing. Let me walk you through how it works.
