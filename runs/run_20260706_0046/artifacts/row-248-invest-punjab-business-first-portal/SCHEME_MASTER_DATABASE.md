### Invest Punjab – Business First Portal
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Punjab
- **Administering Body:** Punjab Bureau of Investment Promotion (Invest Punjab), Government of Punjab
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://investpunjab.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Domestic and international investors, startups, MSMEs, industries
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: All sectors seeking state-level regulatory clearances and fiscal incentives
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Investors looking to set up a business in Punjab or expand existing operations

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Facilitates access to fiscal incentives and regulatory approvals; does not provide direct financial support
- Disbursement mode: Facilitation service (not direct disbursement)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing facilitation throughout investment lifecycle

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. Business description or project report
4. Details of investment and employment generation
5. Land documents or site plan
6. Authorization letter from authorized signatory
7. Any sector-specific clearances as applicable

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the Invest Punjab Business First Portal at https://investpunjab.gov.in
2. Complete investor registration and login
3. Submit the Online Common Application Form (CAF) for regulatory clearances
4. Apply online for fiscal incentives
5. Receive digital regulatory approvals and make e-payments through the Unified Regulator system
6. Access dedicated helpdesk for assistance
7. Utilize aftercare services for continuous industry engagement
