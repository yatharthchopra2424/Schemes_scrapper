# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
Invest Punjab – Business First Portal is a state-level initiative by the Punjab Bureau of Investment Promotion (Invest Punjab), Government of Punjab, designed to serve as a single point of contact for investors seeking to establish or expand businesses in Punjab. It functions as a unified regulator offering time-bound digital approvals, fiscal incentive facilitation, and end-to-end investment lifecycle support. The scheme operates on a rolling basis with no fixed deadline, accepting applications year-round via its official portal: https://investpunjab.gov.in.

### Objectives
- To act as a single point of contact for facilitation of investors looking to set up a business in Punjab  
- To provide state-level regulatory clearances and approvals for fiscal incentives  
- To offer a unified and transparent interface for all regulatory and fiscal services throughout the investment lifecycle  
- To guide investors through the entire investment lifecycle – from initiating the proposal to setting up the business  
- To maintain integrity while serving investors promptly and transparently  
- To build long-term relationships with the state's industries  
- To support the MSME sector and facilitate its growth as the backbone of Punjab’s economy  
- To position Punjab as a global investment destination and boost high-quality jobs in the State

### Benefits & Financial Support
Invest Punjab does not directly provide financial support such as grants, loans, or subsidies. Instead, it facilitates access to fiscal incentives and regulatory approvals through the state government and supports investors in availing benefits from central and state schemes, including partnerships with SIDBI for MSME development.
