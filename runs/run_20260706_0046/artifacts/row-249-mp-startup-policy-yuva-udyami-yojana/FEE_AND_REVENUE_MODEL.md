## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the financial benefit delivered—up to ₹10 lakhs in seed funding per eligible startup under the MP Startup Policy & Yuva Udyami Yojana.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile, considering rolling applications, fund availability constraints, and milestone-based disbursement.
3. **Transparency**: All fees (retainer and success) are disclosed upfront; no hidden charges.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The scheme has a moderate risk profile due to its rolling application basis and dependency on fund availability. Processing involves milestone verification, which may extend timelines, but the high success rate for eligible applicants (as evidenced by structured disbursement tranches) supports mid-tier success fees (6–8% for Growth tier clients).
