# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping youth entrepreneurs and first-time founders in Madhya Pradesh access government startup support. The MP Startup Policy & Yuva Udyami Yojana is designed specifically for residents aged 18–45 with viable early-stage ideas who need seed funding and mentorship to launch. I’d like to understand your venture to see if this scheme aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your age, and are you a resident of Madhya Pradesh?  
2. Have you previously received benefits from any other state or central startup scheme?  
3. Is your business idea or startup registered or in the process of registration (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, LLP, Private Limited)?  
4. What stage is your venture at—idea, prototype, or early revenue?  
5. Do you have a detailed business plan or project report ready?  
6. Are you seeking funding up to INR 10 lakhs for seed support, interest subsidy, or credit guarantee?  
7. Are you open to mentorship, incubation support, and assistance with regulatory approvals and market linkage?  
8. Can you commit to keeping the business operational for at least 2 years post-funding to avoid clawback?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is aged 18–45 years  
- [ ] Applicant is a resident of Madhya Pradesh  
- [ ] Applicant is a first-time entrepreneur (no prior benefit from similar state startup scheme)  
- [ ] Business idea is viable or startup is early-stage  
- [ ] Business is registered or in process of registration under Indian laws (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLP, Private Limited)  
- [ ] Applicant has not availed benefits under similar state startup schemes before  
- [ ] Willing to remain operational for minimum 2 years post-funding  
- [ ] Prepared to submit required documents (ID, address, business plan, bank details, etc.)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you may be a strong fit for the MP Startup Policy & Yuva Udyami Yojana, which offers up to INR 10 lakhs in seed funding along with interest subsidies and mentorship. Let me walk you through how it works and how we’ve helped similar founders in MP secure approval.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many youth entrepreneurs in Madhya Pradesh struggle to access early-stage capital, mentorship, and regulatory support—especially first-time founders who lack credit history or collateral. Traditional lenders often reject early-stage ideas, and navigating government schemes alone can be overwhelming due to documentation and process complexity.

**Scheme intro:**
The MP Startup Policy & Yuva Udyami Yojana, administered by the Madhya Pradesh State Agency for Public Services (MPSAPS), provides financial support of up to INR 10 lakhs in seed funding, interest subsidy of up to 5% on term loans, and credit guarantee cover for loans up to INR 20 lakhs. It also includes non-financial benefits like access to incubation centers, mentorship, patent filing assistance, and market linkage support. Applications are accepted year-round via the MP e-District portal at https://mpedistrict.gov.in.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are between 18 and 45 years old and a resident of Madhya Pradesh  
- You are a first-time entrepreneur with no prior benefits from similar state startup schemes  
- Your business is registered or in the process of registration (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, LLP, etc.)  
- You have a viable business idea or early-stage startup requiring seed support
