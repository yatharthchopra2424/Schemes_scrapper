### MP Startup Policy & Yuva Udyami Yojana
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Madhya Pradesh
- **Administering Body:** Madhya Pradesh State Agency for Public Services (MPSAPS)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://mpedistrict.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (applications accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Startup or early-stage venture (Private Limited, LLP, Partnership, Sole Proprietorship)
- Age of business: Not specified (open to individuals with viable business idea or in process of registration)
- Sector: Not specified (open to all sectors)
- Revenue cap: Not mentioned
- Employee count: Not mentioned
- Other: 
  - Individuals aged 18 to 45 years who are residents of Madhya Pradesh
  - First-time entrepreneurs or those who have not previously availed benefits under similar state startup schemes
  - Business must be registered or in the process of registration under relevant Indian laws

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to INR 10 lakhs seed funding per eligible startup; interest subsidy of up to 5% on term loans; credit guarantee cover for loans up to INR 20 lakhs
- Disbursement mode: Tranche-based disbursement routed through state-designated nodal agency after due diligence
- Duration of benefit: One-time (seed funding), with interest subsidy and credit guarantee tied to loan tenure

#### Documents Required
1. Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN)
2. Address Proof (Electricity Bill, Rent Agreement, Aadhaar)
3. Educational Qualification Certificates
4. Business Plan / Project Report
5. Bank Account Details (Cancelled Cheque or Passbook Copy)
6. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration (if applicable)
7. Partnership Deed or LLP Agreement (if applicable)
8. Passport-sized Photographs
9. Declaration of No Prior Benefit from Similar State Scheme
10. Proof of Residency in Madhya Pradesh

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the official MP e-District portal at https://mpedistrict.gov.in
2. Register as a new user by providing personal and contact details
3. Log in to the portal using credentials
4. Select 'MP Startup Policy & Yuva Udyami Yojana' from the list of available services
5. Fill in the online application form with business details, project report, and financial projections
6. Upload required documents including identity proof, address proof, business plan, and bank details
7. Pay the applicable processing fee, if any
8. Submit the application and note the application number for tracking
9. Await verification and approval by the designated authority
10. Upon approval, funds are disbursed in stages as per milestones
