# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **MP Startup Policy & Yuva Udyami Yojana** (Scheme ID: row-249) is a state-level initiative launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh to promote entrepreneurship among youth. Administered by the **Madhya Pradesh State Agency for Public Services (MPSAPS)**, the scheme operates through the **MP e-District portal** (https://mpedistrict.gov.in) and provides financial and non-financial support to early-stage startups and first-time entrepreneurs. The scheme has a total fund size of **INR 200 crore**, with a maximum assistance of **INR 10 lakhs per eligible entity**. Applications are accepted on a **rolling basis throughout the year**, subject to fund availability.

### Objectives
The scheme aims to:
- Promote entrepreneurship among youth in Madhya Pradesh  
- Provide financial and non-financial support to startups  
- Facilitate ease of doing business through single-window clearance  
- Encourage innovation and technology-driven ventures  
- Create employment opportunities through startup ecosystem development  
- Support women and marginalized communities in entrepreneurship  
- Strengthen incubation and mentorship infrastructure  
- Link startups with funding sources and market access  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Criteria | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| **Age** | 18 to 45 years | Key Facts |
| **Residency** | Must be a resident of Madhya Pradesh | Key Facts |
| **Entrepreneur Status** | First-time entrepreneur or those who have not previously availed benefits under similar state startup schemes | Key Facts |
| **Business Status** | Viable business idea or early-stage startup; must be registered or in process of registration under Indian laws (Private Limited, LLP, Partnership, Sole Proprietorship) | Key Facts |
| **Prior Benefit** | Declaration of no prior benefit from similar state startup schemes required | Key Facts |
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Startups, youth entrepreneurs, first-time entrepreneurs | Key Facts |

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Benefit Type | Details | Source |
|-------------|---------|--------|
| **Seed Funding** | Up to INR 10 lakhs per eligible startup | Key Facts |
| **Interest Subsidy** | Up to 5% on term loans from recognized financial institutions | Key Facts |
| **Credit Guarantee Cover** | For loans up to INR 20 lakhs | Key Facts |
| **Disbursement** | Made in tranches based on milestone achievement | Key Facts |
| **Non-Financial Support** | Access to incubation centers, mentorship from industry experts, facilitation in regulatory approvals, assistance in patent filing, support for market linkage and product commercialization, training programs, networking opportunities, access to shared infrastructure | Key Facts |

> **Important Caveats**  
> - Financial assistance is subject to availability of funds under the state budget  
> - Benefits cannot be availed concurrently with other similar central or state startup schemes  
> - Misrepresentation of facts or submission of false documents leads to rejection and blacklisting  
> - The startup must remain operational for a minimum of 2 years post-funding to avoid clawback  
> - Funding is disbursed in stages and contingent upon achieving predefined milestones  

### Required Documents
1. Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN)  
2. Address Proof (Electricity Bill, Rent Agreement, Aadhaar)  
3. Educational Qualification Certificates  
4. Business Plan / Project Report  
5. Bank Account Details (Cancelled Cheque or Passbook Copy)  
6. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration (if applicable)  
7. Partnership Deed or LLP Agreement (if applicable)  
8. Passport-sized Photographs  
9. Declaration of No Prior Benefit from Similar State Scheme  
10. Proof of Residency in Madhya Pradesh
