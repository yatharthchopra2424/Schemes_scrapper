# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Exporter is a resident of India  
- [ ] Exporter is availing pre- or post-shipment rupee export credit from a bank in India  
- [ ] Export is in freely convertible currency  
- [ ] Export is covered under the Foreign Trade Policy of India  
- [ ] For MSME exporters seeking additional benefit: Valid MSME registration certificate is possessed  
- [ ] Export credit is not on foreign currency credit  
- [ ] Export credit is not on deferred credit or usance bills beyond the usance period  
- [ ] Benefit is claimed only on the outstanding amount of export credit, not on overdue amounts  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN card of the exporter  
- ITR (Income Tax Return) of the exporter  
- GST registration certificate  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)  
- IEC (Import Export Code) certificate  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Export order or contract  
- Shipping bill or bill of export  
- Bank certificate of export credit availed  
- Bank statement showing interest charged on export credit  
- Proof of realization of export proceeds  
- MSME certificate (if applicable for additional 2% benefit)  
- Bank reconciliation statement  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and in English or Hindi  
- File size for each document must not exceed 5 MB  
- File names must follow the format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ClientName_Date.pdf`  
- Bank certificates and statements must be signed and stamped by the issuing bank  
- Export documents must match the details in the export order/contract  
- MSME certificate must be valid and issued by the competent authority

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for this scheme. The benefit is claimed by banks based on export credit documentation; no separate project report or DPR is needed from the exporter.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. The exporter does not fill a direct application form; the bank claims reimbursement from the RBI on behalf of the exporter.  
2. Ensure all export documents are submitted to the bank with a clear request to avail the Interest Equalization Scheme benefit.  
3. Verify that the bank has credited the benefit by reducing the effective interest rate on the export credit.  
4. Confirm that the bank has claimed reimbursement from the RBI at 3% p.a. (or 5% p.a. for MSMEs) on the outstanding export credit.  
5. Maintain copies of all documents submitted to the bank for audit and verification.  
6. Track the quarterly claim submission timeline by the bank to the RBI (within stipulated time after quarter-end).  
7. Ensure the export is under the Foreign Trade Policy of India and in freely convertible currency.  
8. For MSME exporters, confirm that the MSME certificate is valid and submitted to the bank to avail the additional 2% benefit.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents collected and verified  
- [ ] Exporter’s eligibility confirmed (residency, rupee export credit, FTP coverage)  
- [ ] MSME status verified (if applicable for additional benefit)  
- [ ] Documents match across export order, shipping bill, and bank records  
- [ ] Bank certificate and statement obtained and stamped  
- [ ] Proof of export proceeds realization available  
- [ ] Client communication sent and acknowledged  
- [ ] File naming and storage compliant with quality check

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Obtain screenshot or acknowledgment from the bank confirming submission of documents for Interest Equalization Scheme claim  
- Store the bank’s reference number or acknowledgment ID for the claim  
- Set follow-up reminders to check with the bank after one month for status of RBI reimbursement  
- Verify that the benefit (interest equalization) has been passed on to the exporter’s account by the bank  

### Follow-Up Script (to nodal officer or portal helpdesk)
**Subject:** Follow-up on Interest Equalization Scheme Claim for [Client Name] – Bank Reference: [Reference Number]  

Dear RBI Helpdesk Team,  

We are following up on the Interest Equalization Scheme claim submitted by [Bank Name] on behalf of [Client Name] (IEC: [IEC Number]) for export credit availed in [Quarter/Year]. The bank’s reference number for the claim is [Reference Number].  

As per the scheme, banks are to submit claims to the RBI on a quarterly basis within the stipulated time frame after the end of each quarter. We kindly request an update on the status of reimbursement and confirmation that the benefit has been passed on to the exporter.  

For reference, the scheme provides 3% per annum interest equalization for all exporters and an additional 2% per annum for MSME exporters on outstanding rupee export credit under the Foreign Trade Policy of India.  

Please advise if any additional information is required.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Confirm receipt of approval/intimation from the bank regarding RBI reimbursement  
- [ ] Verify that the sanctioned amount (3% or 5% p.a. on outstanding export credit) has been credited to the exporter’s account  
- [ ] Ensure the benefit is reflected as a reduction in the effective interest rate on the export credit  
- [ ] Obtain utilization certificate or statement from the bank showing the interest equalization benefit passed on  
- [ ] Invoice success fee as per consultancy agreement  
- [ ] Archive all documents and correspondence for audit and future reference
