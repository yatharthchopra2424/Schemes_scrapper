## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the tangible financial benefit delivered—specifically, the interest equalization support reducing effective credit costs by 3% for all exporters and an additional 2% for MSME exporters (total 5%) on outstanding rupee export credit.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees are tiered to reflect lower risk and higher predictability for larger, established exporters with consistent export credit flows, versus higher variability in MSME segments due to documentation and eligibility nuances.
3. **Transparency & Predictability**: Retainer covers advisory, documentation, and bank liaison efforts; success fee is contingent on verified benefit realization via bank reimbursement from RBI, ensuring no upfront risk for clients.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Interest Equalization Scheme has a moderate risk profile due to its quarterly claim cycle by banks to RBI and dependency on bank initiative—though benefits are automatic upon credit disbursement, delays in bank claims can extend realization lag. Evidence shows consistent RBI administration and reimbursement mechanics, supporting reliable fee collection post-approval.
