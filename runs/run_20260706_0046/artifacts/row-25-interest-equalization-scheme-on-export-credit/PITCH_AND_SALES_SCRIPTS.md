# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping Indian exporters—particularly in manufacturing, agriculture, and services—reduce their financing costs through government-backed schemes. The Interest Equalization Scheme on Export Credit, administered by the Reserve Bank of India, directly lowers the effective interest rate on rupee export credit. I’d like to understand your current export financing setup to see if this scheme could benefit your business.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your business’s incorporation date, and are you classified as an MSME under the MSMED Act?  
2. Are you currently availing pre- or post-shipment rupee export credit from an Indian bank?  
3. Is your export invoiced and realized in freely convertible currency (e.g., USD, EUR, GBP)?  
4. What is the approximate outstanding amount of your rupee export credit on which banks charge interest?  
5. Do you have a valid MSME registration certificate (if seeking the additional 2% benefit)?  
6. Are your exports covered under India’s Foreign Trade Policy?  
7. Have you previously claimed interest equalization benefits through your bank?  
8. What is your typical export realization cycle (time between shipment and payment receipt)?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Exporter of merchandise or services  
- [ ] Availing pre- or post-shipment rupee export credit from an Indian bank  
- [ ] Resident of India  
- [ ] Export realized in freely convertible currency  
- [ ] Exports covered under Foreign Trade Policy of India  
- [ ] MSME exporter (for additional 2% benefit — requires valid MSME certificate)  
- [ ] Benefit claimed on outstanding export credit (not overdue amounts)  
- [ ] Bank submits quarterly claims to RBI within stipulated timeframe  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you may qualify for the Interest Equalization Scheme on Export Credit, which can reduce your effective interest cost by 3% per annum (or 5% if you’re an MSME exporter) on your rupee export credit. Let me explain how this works and what it could mean for your financing costs.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**  
Indian exporters face high financing costs due to interest charges on pre- and post-shipment rupee export credit, which reduces competitiveness in global markets—especially for MSMEs with limited access to affordable credit.

**Scheme intro:**  
The Interest Equalization Scheme on Export Credit, administered by the Reserve Bank of India, provides a subsidy of 3% per annum for all exporters and an additional 2% per annum for MSME exporters on the outstanding amount of rupee export credit. The benefit is reimbursed to banks, which pass it on to exporters by lowering the effective interest rate on export credit.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**  
- You are availing rupee export credit from an Indian bank (pre- or post-shipment)  
- Your exports are realized in freely convertible currency and covered under the Foreign Trade Policy  
- [If MSME] You hold a valid MSME registration, making you eligible for the additional 2% benefit (total 5%)  
- The benefit applies to the outstanding export credit amount (not overdue dues)

## 3. Objection Handlers

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|

Approval depends on documentation completeness, not discretion. We’ve helped exporters secure this benefit by ensuring correct submission of export orders, shipping bills, bank certificates, and realization proof. |
| "How much do you charge?" | [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT] |
| "I'll try it myself first" | Common mistakes include claiming benefit on overdue amounts (not allowed) or submitting claims without a valid MSME certificate (for the extra 2%). Banks also reject claims if export proceeds aren’t realized in freely convertible currency or if the export isn’t under the Foreign Trade Policy. |
| "We're too small / too big" | There is no turnover or size cap—eligibility is based on availing rupee export credit. Whether you’re a small handicraft exporter or a large manufacturing firm, if you meet the credit and export criteria, you qualify. MSME status only affects the rate (3% vs 5%), not eligibility. |
| "I don't have the documents ready" | The three most commonly missing documents are: (1) bank certificate of export credit availed, (2) proof of realization of export proceeds, and (3) MSME certificate (for additional benefit). We help you gather and format these correctly for bank submission. |
| "We already work with a CA" | Great — we work alongside CAs. They handle your accounts and tax compliance; we handle scheme strategy, documentation for export credit benefits, and coordination with your bank to ensure timely reimbursement from the RBI. |
