### Interest Equalization Scheme on Export Credit
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Reserve Bank of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://rbi.org.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Exporters of merchandise and services
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Manufacturing, services, agriculture (as per target beneficiaries)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be a resident of India; exports must be in freely convertible currency; must avail pre- or post-shipment rupee export credit from banks in India; exports must be under the Foreign Trade Policy of India; MSME exporters must possess a valid MSME registration for additional benefit

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 3% per annum interest equalization for all exporters; additional 2% per annum for MSME exporters (total 5% for MSMEs) on outstanding rupee export credit
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement to banks, which pass on the benefit by reducing the effective interest rate on export credit
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing as long as export credit is outstanding and claims are submitted quarterly

#### Documents Required
1. Export order or contract
2. Shipping bill or bill of export
3. Bank certificate of export credit availed
4. Bank statement showing interest charged
5. Proof of realization of export proceeds
6. MSME certificate (if applicable for additional benefit)
7. Bank reconciliation statement

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Exporter avails pre- or post-shipment rupee export credit from a bank in India.
2. Bank charges interest on the export credit as per prevailing rates.
3. Bank claims reimbursement of interest equalization from the Reserve Bank of India at the rate of 3% per annum (or 5% for MSME exporters) on the outstanding export credit.
4. RBI reimburses the claimed amount to the bank.
5. Bank passes on the benefit to the exporter by reducing the effective interest rate on the export credit.
