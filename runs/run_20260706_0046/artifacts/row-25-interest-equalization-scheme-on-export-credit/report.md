# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Interest Equalization Scheme on Export Credit** is a subsidy scheme administered by the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)** under the Ministry of Export Promotion. It provides interest equalization support to reduce the effective cost of rupee export credit for exporters, with enhanced benefits for MSME exporters. The scheme is funded by the Government of India and operates on a reimbursement model where banks claim support from the RBI and pass the benefit to exporters by lowering their effective interest rate.

### Objectives
- To enhance the competitiveness of Indian exports in the global market  
- To reduce the effective cost of rupee export credit for exporters  
- To support MSME exporters in accessing affordable credit  
- To promote exports in sectors such as manufacturing, agriculture, and services  
- To align with the Foreign Trade Policy of India for export promotion  
- To provide interest equalization support on pre- and post-shipment rupee export credit  

### Eligibility Matrix
| **Criteria** | **Details** |
|--------------|-------------|
| **Beneficiary Type** | All exporters of merchandise and services availing pre- and post-shipment rupee export credit from banks in India |
| **Residency Requirement** | Exporter must be a resident of India |
| **Export Currency** | Export must be in freely convertible currency |
| **Policy Alignment** | Exports must be covered under the Foreign Trade Policy of India |
| **MSME Additional Benefit** | MSME exporters must possess a valid MSME registration to avail the additional 2% benefit |
| **Credit Type** | Benefit available only on rupee export credit (not foreign currency credit) |
| **Outstanding Amount** | Benefit applies only to the outstanding amount of export credit, not overdue amounts |
| **Deferred Credit** | Not available on deferred credit or usance bills beyond the usance period |
| **Claim Timing** | Banks must submit claims to RBI within the stipulated quarterly timeframe after the end of each quarter |

### Benefits & Financial Support
| **Beneficiary Category** | **Interest Equalization Rate** | **Description** |
|--------------------------|-------------------------------|-----------------|
| All Exporters | 3% per annum | Reimbursement to banks on outstanding rupee export credit |
| MSME Exporters | Additional 2% per annum (total 5%) | Higher rate for MSMEs with valid MSME registration |
| **Mechanism** | Reimbursement to banks | Banks claim from RBI and pass benefit to exporters by reducing effective interest rate |
| **Funding Source** | Government of India | Administered by Reserve Bank of India |
| **Benefit Disbursement** | Credited to exporter's account | After bank receives reimbursement from RBI |

> **Key Takeaway**: The scheme reduces the effective interest rate on export credit by reimbursing banks, which then transfer the benefit to exporters. MSME exporters receive a total of 5% per annum support.

### Application Process Flow
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Exporter avails pre- or post-shipment rupee export credit from bank] --> B[Bank charges interest on export credit as per prevailing rates]
    B --> C[Bank claims reimbursement from RBI at 3% p.a. (5% for MSMEs) on outstanding export credit]
    C --> D[RBI reimburses claimed amount to bank]
    D --> E[Bank passes benefit to exporter by reducing effective interest rate on export credit]
    E --> F[Exporter receives lower effective cost of credit]
```

### Timelines & Deadlines
| **Activity** | **Timeline** | **Details** |
|--------------|--------------|-------------|
| Bank Claims Submission | Quarterly | Within stipulated time frame after end of each quarter |
| Exporter Benefit Availability | Ongoing | No fixed deadline; benefit passed on by banks upon disbursement of export credit |
| Scheme Status | Active | Last updated in 2026; no expiration date specified |

> **Note**: There is no fixed deadline for exporters to avail the benefit, as it is passed on by banks upon disbursement of export credit. However, banks must submit claims to RBI quarterly within stipulated timelines.

### Required Documents
1. Export order or contract  
2. Shipping bill or bill of export  
3. Bank certificate of export credit availed  
4. Bank statement showing interest charged  
5. Proof of realization of export proceeds  
6. MSME certificate (if applicable for additional benefit)  
7. Bank reconciliation statement  

### Key Caveats
- Benefit available only on rupee export credit, not foreign currency credit  
- Benefit available only for exports under the Foreign Trade Policy of India  
- MSME exporters must possess valid MSME registration for additional 2% benefit  
- Benefit available only on outstanding export credit, not overdue amounts  
- Banks must submit claims to RBI within stipulated quarterly timeframe  
- Benefit not available on deferred credit or usance bills beyond usance period
