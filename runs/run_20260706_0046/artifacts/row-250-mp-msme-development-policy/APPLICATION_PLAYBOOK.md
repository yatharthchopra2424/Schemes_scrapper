# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The enterprise is registered as an MSME under the MSMED Act, 2006 (Udyam Registration Certificate required)
- [ ] The enterprise operates in Madhya Pradesh (geographic scope)
- [ ] The enterprise is engaged in manufacturing, services, or agro-based activities
- [ ] The enterprise is either new or existing
- [ ] For specific sub-schemes under the policy: additional criteria may apply such as investment limits, employment generation, or location-based preferences for rural/backward areas
- [ ] For women entrepreneurs: Gender certificate required
- [ ] For SC/ST beneficiaries: Caste certificate required
- [ ] The enterprise must have a valid Udyam Registration as per the MSMED Act, 2006
- [ ] The enterprise must comply with Udyam Registration and MSMED Act, 2006 to be eligible for benefits

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Udyam Registration Certificate
- PAN of the entity
- ITR (Income Tax Returns)
- GST registration certificate
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam (already listed above, but reiterated as mandatory)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Aadhaar of the authorized signatory
- Bank account details (cancelled cheque or passbook)
- Project report or business plan
- Quotations for machinery/equipment
- Land allotment order or lease agreement (if applicable)
- Caste certificate (for SC/ST beneficiaries)
- Gender certificate (for women entrepreneurs)
- Affidavit as per prescribed format

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and in PDF or clear image format
- File size should not exceed 5 MB per document
- Names on documents must match exactly with the Udyam Registration and PAN
- Affidavit must be notarized as per prescribed format
- Certificates (caste, gender) must be issued by competent authority
- Bank details must be validated (cancelled cheque or passbook with IFSC/MICR)
- All documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, certified translation required

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a project report or business plan is required as per the scheme-specific documents list.  
Standard sections to include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Business Profile (name, address, Udyam details, constitution)  
- Project Description (products/services, technology used)  
- Market Analysis (demand, competition, target market)  
- Organizational Structure  
- Implementation Timeline  
- Financial Projections (cost of project, means of financing, profitability)  
- Employment Generation Details  
- Social Impact (especially for women, SC/ST, rural beneficiaries)  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Visit the official MP MSME website: https://mpmsme.gov.in  
2. Navigate to the relevant scheme section (e.g., MP MSME Promotion Scheme 2025 or Incentive Scheme)  
3. Download the application proforma and guidelines  
4. Fill in all required details accurately — do not leave any mandatory field blank  
5. Attach all supporting documents as per the checklist  
6. Ensure the authorized signatory signs the application form and affidavit  
7. Validate that all entered data matches the supporting documents (e.g., investment amount, employment numbers)  
8. Submit the application online through the designated portal or offline at the District Trade and Industries Centre  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All fields in the application form are complete and accurate  
- [ ] All required documents are attached and clearly labeled  
- [ ] Udyam Registration number is correctly entered and valid  
- [ ] PAN and Aadhaar details match across documents  
- [ ] Bank account details are correct and match the cancelled cheque/passbook  
- [ ] Project report includes all standard sections and is signed/stamped  
- [ ] Quotations are recent and on vendor letterhead  
- [ ] Land documents (if applicable) are valid and show ownership/lease  
- [ ] Caste/gender certificates are issued by competent authority and valid  
- [ ] Affidavit is notarized and as per prescribed format  
- [ ] Declarations are signed by the authorized signatory  
- [ ] No false or misleading information is present (as per scheme caveats)

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Take a screenshot of the successful submission confirmation page showing the application reference number  
- Store the reference number securely in the case file  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days after submission to check status  
- If submitted offline, obtain and preserve the acknowledgment receipt from the District Trade and Industries Centre

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from the Department of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned amount of financial assistance matches the approved proposal  
- [ ] Ensure funds are disbursed via direct bank transfer to the beneficiary’s account (as per single-click disbursement mechanism)  
- [ ] Monitor utilization of funds for the approved purpose (e.g., machinery, land, quality certification)  
- [ ] Prepare and submit Utilization Certificate (UC) as per scheme guidelines  
- [ ] Maintain records of all invoices and expenditure related to the sanctioned amount  
- [ ] Follow up for success fee invoice (if applicable per consultancy agreement)  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with post-approval conditions (e.g., no withdrawal of benefits due to false information)  
- [ ] Advise client on timelines for any pending benefits (e.g., land allotment, interest subvention) as per scheme notifications
