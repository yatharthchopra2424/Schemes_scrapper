## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client, leveraging the scheme’s direct bank transfer mechanism and single-click disbursement for timely realization of benefits.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate processing risk—while disbursement is streamlined via single-click approval, fund release depends on verification and budget availability, introducing a moderate lag and approval uncertainty.
3. **Accessibility for MSMEs**: Retainer structures are designed to be affordable for micro and small enterprises, matching the scheme’s focus on Udyam-registered MSMEs in Madhya Pradesh, including women entrepreneurs, SC/ST beneficiaries, and rural units.
4. **Transparency and Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront, with success fees contingent only on verified fund disbursement, building confidence through the scheme’s documented direct transfer mechanism and audit-proof documentation requirements.
