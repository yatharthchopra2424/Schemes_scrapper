# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping MSMEs in Madhya Pradesh access government support schemes like the MP MSME Development Policy. I’d like to understand your business goals and see if this scheme could support your growth—whether you’re looking to upgrade technology, access land, or get financial incentives for quality certification or export promotion.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your business incorporated or registered under Udyam?
2. Is your enterprise currently operating in Madhya Pradesh?
3. Are you engaged in manufacturing, services, or agro-based activities?
4. Are you planning any new investment in machinery, technology upgradation, or infrastructure?
5. Do you fall under any priority category—such as women entrepreneur, SC/ST, or located in a backward/rural area?
6. Are you seeking support for land allotment, interest subvention, or reimbursement for patent/quality certification?
7. Have you previously applied for any MSME incentives or subsidies in Madhya Pradesh?
8. What is your current annual turnover or investment in plant and machinery (to assess MSME classification)?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is registered as MSME under Udyam (as per MSMED Act, 2006)
- [ ] Enterprise operates in Madhya Pradesh
- [ ] Engaged in manufacturing, services, or agro-based activities
- [ ] New or existing unit (scheme applies to both)
- [ ] Meets investment/employment criteria for specific sub-schemes (if applicable)
- [ ] Located in backward/rural area OR belongs to women/SC/ST entrepreneur (for additional incentives)
- [ ] Has project report, quotations, and Udyam/PAN/Aadhaar/documents ready or in process

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like the MP MSME Development Policy could be a strong fit—especially with its financial assistance for land, technology upgradation, and quality certification. Let me walk you through how it works and what support you could access.

**Problem framing:**

**Scheme intro:**

Benefits include subsidies for land allotment, interest subvention, capital investment support, and reimbursement for quality certification and patent filing—disbursed via a single-click mechanism directly to beneficiaries’ bank accounts.
