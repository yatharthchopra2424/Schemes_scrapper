### MP MSME Development Policy
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Madhya Pradesh
- **Administering Body:** Department of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises, Government of Madhya Pradesh
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://mpmsme.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) as defined under the MSMED Act, 2006
- Age of business: New and existing MSMEs
- Sector: Manufacturing, services, and agro-based activities
- Revenue cap: Not explicitly mentioned
- Employee count: Not explicitly mentioned
- Other: Must be operating in Madhya Pradesh; specific schemes may have additional criteria such as investment limits, employment generation, and location-based preferences for rural or backward areas

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance for industrial land allotment, interest subvention, capital investment support, reimbursement for quality certification and patent filing, support for cluster development, access to infrastructure and testing facilities, assistance under self-employment schemes, technology upgradation, marketing assistance, credit linkage facilitation, incentives for women entrepreneurs, SC/ST beneficiaries, units in backward areas, export promotion, and participation in national/international events
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (funds transferred directly to eligible beneficiaries’ bank accounts after verification)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (varies by specific sub-scheme)

#### Documents Required
1. Udyam Registration Certificate
2. PAN of the entity
3. Aadhaar of the authorized signatory
4. Bank account details (cancelled cheque or passbook)
5. Project report or business plan
6. Quotations for machinery/equipment
7. Land allotment order or lease agreement (if applicable)
8. Caste certificate (for SC/ST beneficiaries)
9. Gender certificate (for women entrepreneurs)
10. Affidavit as per prescribed format

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the official MP MSME website (https://mpmsme.gov.in)
2. Navigate to the relevant scheme section (e.g., MP MSME Promotion Scheme 2025 or Incentive Scheme)
3. Download the application proforma and guidelines
4. Fill in the required details and attach supporting documents
5. Submit the application online through the designated portal or offline at the District Trade and Industries Centre
6. Applications are scrutinized by the concerned authorities
7. Upon approval, financial assistance is disbursed through direct bank transfer
