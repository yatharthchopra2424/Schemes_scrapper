# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Incorporation certificate of the entity (verified from MCA-21 portal)
- [ ] Self-certification for Startup definition:
  - Date of incorporation/registration is not prior to 7 years (10 years for Biotechnology Startups)
  - Entity’s annual turnover does not exceed Rs.25 Crore for any preceding financial year
  - If not registered in Odisha, the entity employs at least 50% of its total qualified workforce in Odisha
  - The entity is not an extension of the existing family business; or formed by splitting up or reconstruction of a business already in existence
- [ ] Self-certification for Proof of innovation (one of at least one of the following:
  - DIPP Startup India recognition certificate
  - Startup has a patent filed in the entity name and published in the Journal by the India Patent Office in areas affiliated with the nature of business being promoted
  - Sanction Letter of funding/grant to the entity by Government of India or any State Government as part of any specified scheme to promote innovation
  - Letter of funding of not less than 20 percent in equity by any Incubation Fund/Angel Fund/Private Equity Fund/Accelerator/Angel Network duly registered with SEBI that endorses innovative nature of the business
  - If none of the above, submit a one page write-up/note explaining the innovative nature of the product
- [ ] Business plan along with note on innovation (encouraged but not mandatory)

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation certificate
- PAN card
- Income Tax Returns (ITR)
- GST registration certificate
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam registration (if applicable)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Self-certification for Startup definition
- Self-certification for Proof of innovation (choose one from the eligibility list)
- Business plan along with note on innovation (encouraged)

### Document Quality Check
- File size: Each document < 5 MB
- File naming: Use format `SchemeName_DocumentType_ClientName.pdf` (e.g., `OdishaStartupPolicy_IncorporationCertificate_ABCStartups.pdf`)
- All documents must be legible, scanned in color if originally colored, and certified true copies where applicable
- Documents in languages other than English/Odia must be accompanied by a certified translation
- Self-certifications must be on company letterhead, signed, and stamped
- Incorporation certificate must match MCA-21 records exactly

### Client Communication Template

**Key Scheme Details:**  
- **Application Portal:** https://startupodisha.gov.in  
- **Status:** Rolling basis — applications accepted year-round with no fixed deadline  
- **Processing Time:** Approximately 2 weeks for Nodal Agency certification, followed by up to 6 weeks for final approval by Task Force/Startup Council

Please prepare the following for submission:

3. Self-certification for Proof of innovation (select one):

   - Patent filed in entity name and published by India Patent Office  
   - Government sanction letter (Central/State) for innovation promotion  
   - SEBI-registered fund investment letter (≥20% equity)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for initial recognition. However, if applying for monetary benefits (e.g., monthly allowance, product development grant), a detailed project plan/justification is required. Verify with nodal officer if a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is needed for specific benefit applications.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Register on https://startupodisha.gov.in using valid email and mobile number  
2. Complete the Startup Registration Form with accurate entity details  
3. Upload incorporation certificate (ensure it matches MCA-21 records)  
4. Provide self-certification for Startup definition (tick all applicable conditions)  
5. Provide self-certification for Proof of innovation (select one option and upload supporting doc)  
6. Attach business plan with innovation note (if available)  
7. Review all entries before final submission — no edits allowed after submit  
8. Save application reference number immediately upon submission  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All form fields completed accurately  
- [ ] Incorporation certificate uploaded and matches MCA-21  
- [ ] Self-certification for Startup definition signed and stamped  
- [ ] Proof of innovation document uploaded and valid  
- [ ] Business plan attached (if prepared)  
- [ ] Declarations signed and dated  
- [ ] Application reference number saved and shared with client

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Take screenshot of confirmation page after submission  
- Store application reference number securely  
- Set calendar reminder for follow-up at 15 days (Nodal Agency review) and 30 days (final approval)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and save approval letter/email from Startup Odisha  
- [ ] Verify sanctioned benefit amount and type (e.g., monthly allowance, product development grant)  
- [ ] Ensure funds are received in the entity’s registered bank account  
- [ ] For monetary benefits: Prepare utilization plan and timeline  
- [ ] For grants requiring utilization certificates: Begin tracking expenses and bills from day 1  
- [ ] Consult consultant for success fee invoicing (if applicable)
