## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment against scheme criteria  
2. Document preparation and verification as per checklist  
3. Online registration on [https://startupodisha.gov.in](https://startupodisha.gov.in)  
4. Submission of startup recognition application with self-certification  
5. Coordination with Nodal Agency for recognition approval (within 2 weeks)  
6. Preparation and submission of monetary benefit application post-recognition  
7. Liaison with Startup Secretariat and Task Force for final approval (within 2–4 weeks post-submission)
