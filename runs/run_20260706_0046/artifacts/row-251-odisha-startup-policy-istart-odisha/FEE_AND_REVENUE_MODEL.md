## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, documentation preparation, and application submission support.
2. Success fee is contingent on approval of monetary benefits under the Odisha Startup Policy & iStart Odisha scheme.
3. Tiered pricing aligns with client scale and scheme benefit range, ensuring accessibility for early-stage startups.
4. Scheme-specific note: The Odisha Startup Policy offers monthly allowance (₹20,000–₹22,000/month for 12 months) and product development assistance (up to ₹15–16 lakhs), with a typical combined benefit of ₹2.4–3.1 lakhs per approved startup. Processing time is approximately 2–3 months for recognition and an additional 4–6 weeks for monetary benefit approval, creating a moderate revenue lag. Success is evidenced by high application volumes (2,940+ applications) and consistent grant disbursement, indicating low risk for consultants with proper documentation.
