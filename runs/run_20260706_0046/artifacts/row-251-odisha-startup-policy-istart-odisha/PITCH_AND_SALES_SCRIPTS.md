# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping startups in Odisha access government support through the Odisha Startup Policy & iStart Odisha initiative. This scheme is designed specifically for early-stage startups in sectors like Agritech, IT, AI, Healthcare, Fintech, and Biotechnology, providing financial and infrastructural support to help them scale. I’d like to understand your startup’s current stage and goals to see if this could be a good fit.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated or registered? (Eligibility requires incorporation within the last 7 years, or 10 years for biotech startups)
2. What is your current annual turnover? (Must not exceed Rs. 25 Crore in any preceding financial year)
3. Are you registered in Odisha, or if not, do you employ at least 50% of your qualified workforce in Odisha?
4. Is your startup an independent entity, or is it an extension or reconstruction of an existing family business?
5. Do you have any proof of innovation, such as a DIPP Startup India recognition certificate, a published patent, a government sanction letter, or equity funding from a SEBI-registered fund?
6. Have you received any equity investment of at least 20% from a SEBI-registered incubation fund, angel fund, or accelerator?
7. What is your monthly revenue run rate over the last three months? (Eligibility for monthly allowance requires Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 10 Lakh per month)
8. Are the founder(s) or co-founder(s) women, transgender, or from SC/ST/SEBC/PH communities, holding at least 50% equity? (This affects benefit amounts)

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Incorporated within last 7 years (10 years for biotech)
- [ ] Annual turnover ≤ Rs. 25 Crore
- [ ] If not registered in Odisha, ≥50% of qualified workforce employed in Odisha
- [ ] Not an extension or reconstruction of an existing family business
- [ ] Possesses at least one proof of innovation (DIPP certificate, patent, govt. sanction letter, SEBI-funded equity ≥20%, or innovation note)
- [ ] Meets at least one financial/traction criterion for benefits (equity funding ₹5L–1Cr, patent, govt. grant, or revenue run rate ₹50K–10L/month)
- [ ] For enhanced benefits: Founder(s) woman/transgender/SC/ST/SEBC/PH with ≥50% equity

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your startup may qualify for the Odisha Startup Policy & iStart Odisha initiative, which offers a monthly allowance of up to Rs. 22,000 and product development assistance of up to Rs. 16 lakhs for eligible founders. Let me walk you through how this works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Early-stage startups in Odisha often struggle with cash flow during product development, lack access to subsidized incubation, and face challenges in securing early funding or government recognition—especially women-led and socially disadvantaged founders who need tailored support to scale.

**Scheme intro:**
The Odisha Startup Policy & iStart Odisha, administered by the MSME Department, Government of Odisha, is a rolling-basis incubation scheme offering financial support including a monthly allowance of Rs. 20,000 (Rs. 22,000 for women, transgender, or SC/ST/SEBC/PH founders with ≥50% equity) for up to one year, and product development and marketing assistance up to Rs. 15 lakh (Rs. 16 lakh for eligible founders), along with subsidized incubation, event participation support, and need-based raw material assistance.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Incorporated within the last 7 years (or 10 years for biotech)  
- Annual turnover does not exceed Rs. 25 Crore  
- Possesses proof of innovation (e.g., DIPP recognition, patent, government grant, or SEBI-funded equity)  
- Meets financial/traction criteria such as revenue run rate of Rs. 50,000–10 Lakh/month or equity funding of ₹5 Lakh–1 Crore
