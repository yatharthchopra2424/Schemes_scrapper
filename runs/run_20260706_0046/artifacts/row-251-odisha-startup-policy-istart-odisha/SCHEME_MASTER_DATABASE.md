### Odisha Startup Policy & iStart Odisha
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Incubation
- **Geography:** Odisha only
- **Administering Body:** MSME Department, Government of Odisha
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://startupodisha.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Startup entity
- Age of business: Not prior to 7 years (10 years for Biotechnology Startups)
- Sector: All sectors (Agritech, IT, AI, Healthcare, Fintech, Bio-Technology, etc.)
- Revenue cap: Annual turnover does not exceed Rs.25 Crore for any preceding financial year
- Employee count: [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]
- Other: 
  - If not registered in Odisha, the entity employs at least 50% of its total qualified workforce in Odisha
  - The entity is not an extension of the existing family business; or formed by splitting up or reconstruction of a business already in existence
  - Self-certification for Proof of innovation: DIPP Startup India recognition certificate; OR Startup has a patent filed in the entity name and published in the Journal by the India Patent Office in areas affiliated with the nature of business being promoted; OR Sanction Letter of funding/grant to the entity by Government of India or any State Government as part of any specified scheme to promote innovation; OR Letter of funding of not less than 20 percent in equity by any Incubation Fund/Angel Fund/Private Equity Fund/Accelerator/Angel Network duly registered with SEBI that endorses innovative nature of the business; OR If an entity does not possess any of the above mentioned four documents, it may submit a one page write-up/note explaining the innovative nature of its product

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - Monthly allowance of Rs.20,000 (Rs.22,000/- if the startup Founder(s)/Co-Founder(s) are Women or transgender or from Social Categories such as SC/ST/SEBC/PH, with at least 50% equity)
  - Product Development and Marketing/Publicity assistance up to Rs. 15 lakh (Upto Rs.16 lakhs if the startup Founder(s)/Co-Founder(s) is Woman or transgender or from Social Categories such as SC/ST/SEBC/PH, with at least 50% equity)
  - Need based assistance towards cost of raw material/components and other related equipments required for the innovative process for new product development/existing product improvement
  - Financial Support for event Participation: financial support to state recognized Startups for participating in reputed national and international events, where they are representing the state (support may include economy class air fare or 3 tier AC train fare for travel & free space in the stall, if set up by State Government)
  - Subsidised Incubation: Recognised startups may avail subsidy benefit to the extent of 50% of monthly rental of the incubation space (subject to a maximum of Rs. 5000) for a period of maximum one year, if getting incubated at any of the incubators recognised by the Startup Odisha Initiative
  - Additional benefits to Women led Startups: Monthly allowance shall be Rs.22,000/-, if the startup Founder(s)/Co-Founder(s) are Women or transgender or from Social Categories such as SC/ST/SEBC/PH (with at least 50% equity). Up-to Rs.16 lakhs towards product development and marketing/publicity assistance, if the startup Founder(s)/Co-Founder(s) is Woman or transgender or from Social Categories such as SC/ST/SEBC/PH (with at least 50% equity)
  - Benefits to Incubators: One-Time Capital Grant of 50% of the capital cost (excluding the cost of building) up to a maximum of Rs.1 crore, which may be given to an approved incubator to set up or scale up incubation facility. In case, a university recognised under the Central/State Act is able to raise funding for development of its incubation infrastructure, the Startup Council may consider providing a matching grant subject to an upper limit of Rs.5 crore on case-to-case basis. Such grant from the government shall only be used for development of incubation infrastructure including purchase of equipment for the incubation facility. Reimbursement of Utility Expense of Rs.1 lakh per year for a period of 3 years, which may be provided to an approved incubator for providing free internet, free electricity and free mentoring services to the incubatees. This benefit will not be available to virtual incubators. Performance capital grant of Rs.5 lakhs, which may be given to an approved incubator for its each successful Startup incubated at least for a minimum period of 3 months
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit
- Duration of benefit: 
  - Monthly allowance: One-time (for a period of one year only)
  - Product Development and Marketing/Publicity assistance: One-time
  - Need based assistance: One-time
  - Financial Support for event Participation: One-time per event
  - Subsidised Incubation: One-time (for a period of maximum one year)
  - Benefits to Incubators: One-time grants

#### Documents Required
1. Incorporation certificate of the entity (verified from MCA-21 portal)
2. Self-certification for Startup definition
3. Self-certification for Proof of innovation (DIPP Startup India recognition certificate; OR patent filed in entity name and published in Journal by India Patent Office; OR Sanction Letter of funding/grant by Govt. of India or any State Govt.; OR Letter of funding of not less than 20 percent in equity by SEBI registered Incubation Fund/Angel Fund/Private Equity Fund/Accelerator/Angel Network; OR one page write-up/note explaining innovative nature of product)
4. Business plan along with note on innovation (encouraged)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register on startupodisha.gov.in
2. Fill Startup Registration Form with self-certification for incorporation, startup definition, and proof of innovation
3. Upload Incorporation certificate (verified from MCA-21 portal)
4. Submit and await approval by Nodal Agency within 2 weeks
5. Nodal Agency forwards to Startup Secretariat collates certified applications and presents to Task Force for consideration and final approval
6. Result published online and emailed to the concerned Startup entity
7. A startup applicant can reapply for startup recognition/financial assistance one more time after a gap of 3 months from the date of communication of rejection
8. Once recognized, apply online for monetary benefit by filling a standard form
9. Nodal Agency reviews monetary application and shares review with Startup Secretariat within next 2 weeks
10. Startup Secretariat collates applications, undertakes due diligence, presents to Task Force/Startup Council for consideration and final approval within next 2 weeks
11. Result published online and emailed to the concerned Startup entity
12. Once approved by the Task Force, Startup receives the monetary benefit(s) directly in the entity’s bank account
