# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Odisha Startup Policy & iStart Odisha** is a state-level incubation scheme implemented by the MSME Department, Government of Odisha. It aims to foster entrepreneurship and innovation by providing financial and non-financial support to startups and incubators. The scheme operates on a rolling basis with no fixed deadline, accepting applications year-round via the portal: **https://startupodisha.gov.in**. The implementing agency is the MSME Department, with the Startup Secretariat handling day-to-day operations. The scheme has a fund size of **Rs 100 Crore**, to be disbursed over 5 years to support **5,000 startups** in the state. Last updated in 2026, the scheme targets startups, entrepreneurs, women entrepreneurs, SC/ST communities, MSMEs, and agri-startups.

### Objectives
- To emerge amongst the top 3 'Startup Hubs' in India by 2020  
- To unleash the potential of women entrepreneurship in Odisha by encouraging at least 33% of startups with Founders/Co-Founders as women  
- To build a holistic Startup ecosystem in Odisha to enable the State to become one of the top three Startup destinations in the country  
- To implement a supportive regulatory framework and robust governance structure that enables hassle-free, time-bound statutory clearances and monitoring  
- To develop a world class 'Startup Hub' in Odisha by 2020  
- To encourage incubators including sector-specific ones aligning with the State’s strengths and requirements  
- To create enabling environment and supporting eco-system that facilitates at least 1,000 Startups in next five years in the state  
- To create a strong institutional framework for effective implementation, monitoring and evaluation of this initiative  

### Eligibility Matrix
Eligibility for startup recognition under the scheme requires self-certification and submission of specific documents. The criteria are detailed below:

| **Criteria** | **Requirement** | **Details** |
|--------------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Incorporation** | Incorporation certificate of the entity | Verified from MCA-21 portal |
| **Startup Definition** | Self-certification | - Date of incorporation/registration not prior to 7 years (10 years for Biotechnology Startups)<br>- Annual turnover does not exceed Rs.25 Crore for any preceding financial year<br>- If not registered in Odisha, entity employs at least 50% of its total qualified workforce in Odisha<br>- Entity is not an extension of existing family business or formed by splitting/reconstruction of an existing business |
| **Proof of Innovation** | Self-certification (one of the following) | - DIPP Startup India recognition certificate<br>- Patent filed in entity name and published in Journal by India Patent Office in areas affiliated with nature of business<br>- Sanction Letter of funding/grant by Govt. of India or any State Govt. as part of innovation-promoting scheme<br>- Letter of funding of not less than 20% in equity by SEBI-registered Incubation Fund/Angel Fund/Private Equity Fund/Accelerator/Angel Network<br>- One page write-up/note explaining innovative nature of product (if none of above documents available) |
| **Additional** | Business plan along with note on innovation | Encouraged but not mandatory |

### Benefits & Financial Support
The scheme provides multiple financial and non-financial benefits to recognized startups and incubators. Benefits vary based on founder demographics (women, transgender, SC/ST/SEBC/PH) with at least 50% equity holding.

#### Startup Benefits
| **Benefit Type** | **Amount** | **Conditions for Eligibility** | **Special Provisions for Women/Transgender/SC/ST/SEBC/PH Founders (≥50% equity)** |
|------------------|------------|--------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Monthly Allowance** | Rs. 20,000/month (Rs. 22,000/month for eligible categories) | - Total equity financing by SEBI-registered AIF Category 1 & 2 fund or Angel Networks: Rs. 5 Lakh to Rs. 1.00 Crore<br>- OR Patent filed in entity name and published in Journal by India Patent Office in relevant area<br>- OR Sanction letter of funding/grant by Govt. of India or any State Govt.<br>- OR Revenue run rate of Rs. 50 thousand to Rs. 10 Lakh per month over last 3 months | Allowance increases to Rs. 22,000/month if founder(s) are women, transgender, or from SC/ST/SEBC/PH with ≥50% equity |
| **Product Development & Marketing Assistance** | Up to Rs. 15 lakh (Up to Rs. 16 lakh for eligible categories) | - Total equity financing by SEBI-registered AIF Category 1 & 2 fund or Angel Networks: ≥ Rs. 25 Lakh<br>- OR Sanction Letter of funding/grant by Govt. of India or any State Govt. of ≥ Rs. 5 Lakh<br>- OR Revenue run rate of Rs. 2.5 Lakh per month over last 6 months | Assistance increases to up to Rs. 16 lakh if founder(s) are women, transgender, or from SC/ST/SEBC/PH with ≥50% equity |
| **Need-Based Assistance** | Cost of raw material/components/equipment for innovative process | - Only if innovation is dependent on specific raw material/equipment<br>- Subject to approval by Startup Council | No special enhancement mentioned; based on need and justification |
| **Financial Support for Event Participation** | Economy class air fare or 3-tier AC train fare + free stall space (if set up by State Govt.) | - For state-recognized startups participating in reputed national/international events representing the state | No special enhancement mentioned |
| **Subsidised Incubation** | 50% of monthly rental of incubation space (max Rs. 5,000/month) | - For max 1 year<br>- Only if incubated at incubators recognised by Startup Odisha Initiative | No special enhancement mentioned |

#### Incubator Benefits
| **Benefit Type** | **Amount** | **Eligibility Conditions** | **Special Notes** |
|------------------|------------|----------------------------|-------------------|
| **One-Time Capital Grant** | 50% of capital cost (excl. building), max Rs. 1 crore | - For approved incubator to set up/scale up facility<br>- Must dedicate min 5,000 sq. ft carpet area to incubatees | Disbursed in phases per approved budget/utilisation plan; proportional to funds brought in by incubator |

| **Performance Capital Grant** | Rs. 5 lakhs per successful startup | - For approved incubator<br>- For each startup incubated ≥ 3 months | One-time payment; incubator must submit list of successful incubatees |

### Application Process
The application process involves two stages: **Startup Recognition** and **Monetary Benefit Application**. The process is illustrated below using a Mermaid flowchart.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Start: Register on startupodisha.gov.in] --> B[Fill Startup Registration Form with self-certification for incorporation, startup definition, and proof of innovation]
    B --> C[Upload Incorporation certificate (verified from MCA-21 portal)]
    C --> D[Submit and await approval by Nodal Agency within 2 weeks]
    D --> E[Nodal Agency forwards to Startup Secretariat]
    E --> F[Startup Secretariat collates certified applications and presents to Task Force for consideration and final approval]
    F --> G[Result published online and emailed to concerned Startup entity]
    G --> H{Application Status}
    H -->|Approved| I[Once recognized, apply online for monetary benefit by filling standard form]
    H -->|Rejected| J[Can reapply after gap of 3 months from date of rejection communication]
    J --> B
    I --> K[Nodal Agency reviews monetary application and shares review with Startup Secretariat within next 2 weeks]
    K --> L[Startup Secretariat collates applications, undertakes due diligence, presents to Task Force/Startup Council for consideration and final approval within next 2 weeks]
    L --> M[Result published online and emailed to concerned Startup entity]
    M --> N{Monetary Application Status}
    N -->|Approved| O[Startup receives monetary benefit(s) received directly in entity’s bank account
    N -->|Rejected| P[Can reapply after gap of 3 months from date of rejection communication]
    P --> I
    O --> End[End]
```

**Key Process Notes:**
- Nodal Agency certification for recognition: within 2 weeks
- Startup Secretariat to Task Force for recognition: within next 2 weeks after Nodal Agency certification
- Nodal Agency review for monetary benefit: within 2 weeks of receipt
- Startup Secretariat to Task Force for monetary benefit: within next 2 weeks after Nodal Agency review
- Total estimated timeline: ~8 weeks for recognition, ~4 weeks for monetary benefit post-recognition
- Applicants can reapply for recognition/financial assistance one more time after a 3-month gap from rejection communication
- Once recognized, startups apply online for monetary benefits via standard form
- Funds disbursed directly to the startup’s registered bank account upon Task Force approval

### Fund Release Guidelines
#### Monthly Allowance
- First 3 months: Direct monthly payment to registered bank account
- After 3 months: Payment released based on utilization report submitted online to Nodal Agency within 15 days of completion of first 3 months
- Startup must submit utilization report with supporting documents/bills for first 3 months
- Payment made for max 1 year only; benefit available only once
- Final utilization certificate with supporting documents/bills required within 1 month of completion of 1 year

#### Product Development & Marketing Assistance
- Subject to approval: 50% payment in advance, remaining 50% upon utilization of advance
- Startup must submit utilization certificate with bills for advance payment within 3 months of receiving grant
- Final utilization certificate with bills for entire amount required within 6 months of grant release

#### Need-Based Assistance
- Subject to approval: Payment made directly to registered bank account
- Startup must submit utilization certificate with bills for all expenses within 3 months of receiving grant

#### Incubator Benefits
- **One-Time Capital Grant**: Disbursed in phases per approved budget/utilisation plan; proportional to funds brought in by incubator
- **Utility Expense Reimbursement**: One-time payment up to Rs. 1 lakh/year for max 3 years; incubator must submit bills and utilization certificate within 3 months of release
- **Performance Capital Grant**: One-time payment; incubator must submit list of successful incubatees incubated ≥ 3 months

### Key Caveats
> **Payment of monthly allowance is limited to one year only and can be availed only once.**  
> **Utilization certificates and supporting documents/bills are mandatory for all grants within specified timelines (3 months for advance/utilization, 6 months for final product grant, 1 year + 1 month for monthly allowance final report).**  
> **Need-based assistance requires submission of a self-contained plan with justification online for approval.**  
> **Incubator utility expense reimbursement is not available to virtual incubators.**  
> **Performance capital grant for incubators requires incubation of startup for minimum 3 months.**  
> **Returns from the Fund of Funds are to be reinvested in strengthening the ecosystem.**  
> **The Fund of Funds scheme money is invested in selected funds with a minimum 1:1 availability ratio to state-registered startups within 5 years.**
