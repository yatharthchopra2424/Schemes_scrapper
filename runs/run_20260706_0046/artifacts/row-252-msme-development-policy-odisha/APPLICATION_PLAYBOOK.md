# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Enterprise is registered under the MSME Act and has obtained Udyam Registration Certificate  
- [ ] Enterprise is a new Micro & Small Enterprise (fixed capital investment commenced on or after the policy's effective date)  
- [ ] For Capital Investment Subsidy:  
  - [ ] General enterprise: @25% of capital investment in Plant & Machinery, upper limit Rs.1 crore  
  - [ ] SC/ST-owned enterprise: @33% of capital investment in Plant & Machinery, upper limit Rs.1.5 crore  
  - [ ] Women-owned enterprise: @30% of capital investment in Plant & Machinery, upper limit Rs.1.25 crore  
- [ ] For Seed Capital Assistance: Assistance up to Rs.10 lakh per unit for project report preparation and pre-operative expenses  
- [ ] Enterprise has commenced fixed capital investment on or after the policy's effective date  
- [ ] Enterprise will commence production within three years for MSMEs and five years for large industries from the date of starting fixed capital investment  
- [ ] Enterprise is located in Odisha (geographic scope: Odisha only)  
- [ ] Enterprise has not availed similar incentives under any other State or Central Government policies  
- [ ] Claim will be filed complete in all respects within 2 years of commencement of production (as amended in 2023)  
- [ ] For SC/ST beneficiaries: Caste Certificate available  
- [ ] For women beneficiaries: Gender Certificate available

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Udyam Registration Certificate  
- PAN Card of the Enterprise  
- ITR (Income Tax Returns)  
- GST Registration Certificate  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- Aadhaar of Proprietor/Partner/Director  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Project Report  
- Quotations for Plant & Machinery  
- Bank Sanction Letter for Loan (if applicable)  
- Proof of Payment for Capital Investment  
- Certificate of Commencement of Production  
- Partnership Deed / Certificate of Incorporation  
- Factory License / Trade License  
- Site Plan / Layout Plan  
- Pollution Clearance Certificate (if applicable)  
- Caste Certificate (for SC/ST beneficiaries)  
- Gender Certificate (for women beneficiaries)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and in PDF or JPEG format  
- File size must not exceed 5 MB per document  
- Document names must match the enterprise name as per Udyam Registration  
- Scanned copies must be certified true copies where applicable  
- Documents must be in English or Odia; if in another language, certified translation must be attached  
- Certificates (Caste, Gender, Pollution) must be issued by competent authorities and valid

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Project Report is required for this scheme. It must include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Description (product/service, technology, location)  
- Market Analysis (demand, competition, target market)  
- Technical Details (process flow, machinery specifications, utilities)  
- Financial Projections (cost of project, means of financing, profitability, break-even analysis)  
- Implementation Schedule (timeline for setup and commencement of production)  
- Management Profile (details of promoters and key personnel)  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Measures  
- Environmental and Social Impact Assessment (if applicable)  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the portal at **https://msme.odisha.gov.in** and log in/register  
2. Select the specific incentive scheme (e.g., Capital Investment Subsidy, Seed Capital Assistance) under Odisha MSME Development Policy, 2016  
3. Fill all mandatory fields marked with *  
4. Ensure Udyam Registration Number is entered correctly and matches the certificate  
5. Enter enterprise details exactly as per Udyam Registration (name, address, constitution)  
6. Provide accurate bank account details for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)  
7. Upload documents in the prescribed format (PDF/JPEG, <5 MB each)  
8. Select the correct beneficiary category (General, SC/ST, Women) to avail applicable subsidy rates  
9. Declare that no similar incentives have been availed under other State/Central policies  
10. Confirm that the claim is being filed within 2 years of commencement of production  
11. Review all entries before final submission  
12. Keep a copy of the submitted application and acknowledgment  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All application fields are complete and accurate  
- [ ] Udyam Registration Number matches the certificate  
- [ ] Bank account details are correct for DBT  
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents are attached and legible  
- [ ] Declarations (no duplicate incentives, claim within 2 years) are signed  
- [ ] Correct incentive category selected (General/SC/ST/Women) for applicable subsidy  
- [ ] Proof of payment for capital investment matches quotations and bank statements  
- [ ] Certificate of Commencement of Production is issued by GM, RIC/DIC/DI, Odisha  
- [ ] Application is saved as draft for final review before submission

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the portal at **https://msme.odisha.gov.in**  
- Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing the Application Reference Number  
- Store the reference number securely in the case file  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days after submission to check status  
- Save a copy of the submitted application and all uploaded documents
