## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, document preparation, and application submission.
2. Success fee is contingent on sanction and release of incentive, aligned with client outcomes.
3. Tiered pricing reflects scheme accessibility: Capital Investment Subsidy (up to ₹1 crore) and Seed Capital Assistance (up to ₹10 lakh) primarily serve micro and small enterprises, making Starter and Growth tiers most relevant.
4. Scheme-specific note: Processing time for incentives under the Odisha MSME Development Policy typically involves scrutiny by State/District Level Committees and release via DBT, with claims required within 2 years of commencement of production (as amended in 2023), indicating moderate administrative lag but high success potential for complete applications.
