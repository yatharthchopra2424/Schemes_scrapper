# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping MSMEs in Odisha access government incentives designed to boost manufacturing capacity and support local entrepreneurial talent. The Odisha MSME Development Policy offers direct financial support like capital investment subsidies and seed capital assistance to help new enterprises establish and grow. I’d like to understand your business to see if this scheme aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your enterprise incorporated or when did you commence fixed capital investment?
2. Are you registered under the MSME Act and do you have an Udyam Registration Certificate?
3. What is the nature of your business—manufacturing, services, or agro-processing—and where is your unit located in Odisha?
4. Have you made any capital investment in plant and machinery since your commencement of production?
5. Are you a new enterprise that commenced production within the last three years (for MSMEs) or five years (for large industries)?
6. Do you belong to any of the priority beneficiary categories—SC, ST, women entrepreneur, or located in an industrially backward district (like KBK)?
7. Are you planning to apply for incentives such as Capital Investment Subsidy, Seed Capital Assistance, or Trade Mark Assistance?
8. Have you previously claimed similar incentives under any other State or Central Government policy?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Enterprise is registered under MSME Act with Udyam Registration
- [ ] Fixed capital investment commenced on or after the policy's effective date (2016)
- [ ] Commenced production within 3 years for MSMEs / 5 years for large industries from date of fixed capital investment
- [ ] Located in Odisha only
- [ ] Not availing similar incentives under any other State or Central Government policy
- [ ] Falls under target beneficiary: MSME, startup, women entrepreneur, SC/ST
- [ ] For Capital Investment Subsidy: new Micro & Small Enterprise making investment in plant & machinery
- [ ] For Seed Capital Assistance: engaged in project report preparation and pre-operative expenses
- [ ] Claims to be filed within 2 years of commencement of production (with one-time relaxation till 30.06.2023 for delayed cases)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you may qualify for the Odisha MSME Development Policy’s financial support, particularly the Capital Investment Subsidy of up to Rs.1 crore or Seed Capital Assistance of up to Rs.10 lakh. Let me walk you through how this scheme works and how we can assist.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many new MSMEs in Odisha struggle with high upfront costs for plant and machinery, lack of funds for project reports, and limited access to capital—hindering their ability to commence production and scale operations and generate employment, especially in industrially backward districts.

**Scheme intro:**
The Odisha MSME Development Policy, 2016, administered by the Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises Department, Government of Odisha, provides financial support through multiple incentives. Key benefits include Capital Investment Subsidy (@25% of plant & machinery investment, up to Rs.1 crore for general enterprises, higher for SC/ST/women), Seed Capital Assistance (up to Rs.10 lakh for project report and pre-operative expenses), Project Report Subsidy (50% of cost, max Rs.25,000), and Trade Mark Assistance (50% of fees, max Rs.10,000 domestic / Rs.50,000 international). Applications are accepted year-round via the portal: https://msme.odisha.gov.in.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a registered MSME with Udyam Registration (as confirmed)
- Your fixed capital investment commenced on or after the policy’s effective date
- You have commenced production within the eligible timeframe (3 years for MSMEs)
- You are located in Odisha and not availing similar incentives elsewhere
