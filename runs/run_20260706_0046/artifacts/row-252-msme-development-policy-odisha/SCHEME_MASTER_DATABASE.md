### MSME Development Policy Odisha
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Odisha only
- **Administering Body:** Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises Department, Government of Odisha
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://msme.odisha.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (applications accepted throughout the year as per Operational Guidelines and notifications issued from time to time)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: New Micro & Small Enterprises (registered under MSME Act)
- Age of business: Must commence fixed capital investment on or after policy's effective date and commence production within three years for MSMEs and five years for large industries from the date of starting fixed capital investment
- Sector: All sectors (general, with specific provisions for SC/ST/women enterprises, industrially backward districts including KBK, and enterprises engaged in recycling of E-waste, Hospital waste, Construction & demolition waste)
- Revenue cap: Not explicitly mentioned in evidence
- Employee count: Not mentioned in evidence
- Other: 
  - Enterprises must file claims complete in all respects within 2 years of commencement of production (as amended in 2023)
  - One-time relaxation allows MSMEs that commenced production but missed the deadline to file claims by 30.06.2023 to become eligible
  - Government may consider condonation of delay beyond 2 years due to force majeure on case-to-case basis via Empowered Committee recommendation
  - Incentives are not available if similar incentives are availed under any other State or Central Government policies
  - Assistance is subject to availability of funds and approval by the respective Level Committee (DLC/SDLC/SLC)

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - Capital Investment Subsidy: @25% of capital investment in Plant & Machinery for new Micro & Small Enterprises, subject to an upper limit of Rs.1 crore (higher limits for SC/ST/women enterprises: Rs.1.5 crore for SC/ST, Rs.1.25 crore for women)
  - Seed Capital Assistance: Up to Rs.10 lakh per unit for project report preparation and pre-operative expenses
  - Project Report Subsidy: 50% of cost, max Rs.25,000
  - Reimbursement of Audit Cost for Water Conservation: 50% of cost, max Rs.10,000
  - Assistance for raising capital through SME Exchange: 50% of listing expenses, max Rs.2 lakh
  - Trade Mark Assistance: 50% of fees for domestic registration (max Rs.10,000) and international (max Rs.50,000)
  - Reimbursement of Training Expenditure: 50% of cost, max Rs.5,000 per person
  - Awards to MSME Enterprises/Entrepreneurs: Cash prizes up to Rs.1 lakh
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit / Reimbursement (via online mechanism for time-bound sanction and release of incentives
- Duration of benefit: One-time (for most subsidies); Annual (for some benefits like Awards)

#### Documents Required
1. Udyam Registration Certificate
2. Project Report
3. Quotations for Plant & Machinery
4. Bank Sanction Letter for Loan (if applicable)
5. Proof of Payment for Capital Investment
6. Certificate of Commencement of Production
7. Partnership Deed / Certificate of Incorporation
8. PAN Card of the Enterprise
9. Aadhaar of Proprietor/Partner/Director
10. Factory License / Trade License
11. Site Plan / Layout Plan
12. Pollution Clearance Certificate (if applicable)
13. Caste Certificate (for SC/ST beneficiaries)
14. Gender Certificate (for women beneficiaries)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Enterprises must first register under the MSME Act and obtain Udyam Registration.
2. Identify the specific incentive scheme under the Odisha MSME Development Policy, 2016 (e.g., Capital Investment Subsidy, Seed Capital Assistance).
3. Prepare the required documents including project reports, quotations, bank sanction letters, and proof of investment.
4. Submit the application online through the MSME Department's designated portal or offline at the District Industries Centre (DIC) or Regional Industries Centre (RIC).
5. The application is scrutinized by the State Directorate Level Committee (SDLC) or relevant committee based on investment size.
6. Upon approval, the incentive is sanctioned and released through direct benefit transfer or reimbursement after verification of expenditure and installation.
