## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Entity is registered as a Private Limited Company, Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), or Partnership Firm under relevant Indian laws  
- [ ] Startup is incorporated in West Bengal **or** has at least 50% of its operations based in West Bengal  
- [ ] Entity is working on innovative products, services, or processes with potential for scalability and employment generation  
- [ ] Preference may be given to startups led by women, SC/ST, or differently-abled entrepreneurs *(note: not mandatory but advantageous)*  
- [ ] Entity falls under the definition of a startup as per the policy (typically: incorporated ≤10 years ago, turnover < INR 100 crore in any preceding financial year)  
- [ ] Business plan or project report demonstrates innovation and commercial viability  
- [ ] Proof of innovation is available (prototype details, IPR documents, or equivalent evidence)  
- [ ] Bank account is in the name of the entity and operational  
- [ ] Principal place of business is verifiable and located in West Bengal (if claiming operational presence)  

> **Note**: There is no explicit turnover or employee threshold stated in the evidence. However, standard startup definitions (DPIIT) apply by implication: incorporated ≤10 years, turnover < INR 100 crore. Verify with nodal officer if stricter limits apply.

- Declaration of compliance with West Bengal Startup Policy eligibility  

> **Note**: If applying for IPR reimbursement, include patent application details, filing receipts, and authorization letters. For incubation support, attach incubator agreement.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Access the portal**: Visit https://msmewb.gov.in and navigate to the Startup Policy section  
2. **Register as a startup**: Provide basic entity and promoter details (name, contact, incorporation details)  
3. **Select the scheme**: Choose “West Bengal Startup Policy” from the list of available schemes  
4. **Fill all mandatory fields**: Do not leave any asterisk-marked (*) fields blank  
5. **Entity details**: Ensure registration number, date of incorporation, and PAN match the Certificate of Incorporation  
6. **Promoter details**: Enter Aadhaar and PAN of all directors/promoters exactly as per documents  
7. **Business description**: Clearly state the innovative nature of the product/service and its scalability potential  
8. **Innovation proof**: Upload prototype details, IPR documents, or technical validation as per scheme-specific requirements  
9. **Location confirmation**: Confirm that the entity is incorporated in West Bengal or has ≥50% operations in the state  
10. **Declaration**: Tick the compliance declaration box and upload the signed declaration document  
11. **Review before submission**: Preview the entire form to avoid errors  
12. **Submit only once**: Avoid multiple submissions; note the application reference number  

> **Portal-specific note**: The application is processed by a designated committee; ensure all documents are uploaded in the correct sections as per portal instructions.
