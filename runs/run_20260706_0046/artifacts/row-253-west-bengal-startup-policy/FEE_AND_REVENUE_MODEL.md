## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit received by the client (e.g., seed funding up to ₹25 lakhs or patent reimbursement up to ₹10 lakhs), ensuring clients pay only for realized outcomes.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the rolling application basis and dependency on state-level committee approval (with medium confidence in evidence), success fees reflect moderate execution risk due to fund availability and compliance requirements.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The Growth tier pricing aligns with the scheme’s eligibility for startups and MSMEs incorporated in or operating 50%+ in West Bengal, making it accessible to early-stage innovators.
4. **Transparency & Compliance**: All fees are disclosed upfront; engagements adhere to West Bengal MSME & Textiles Department guidelines, avoiding any conflict with public funding ethics.
