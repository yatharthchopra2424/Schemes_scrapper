## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping startups in West Bengal access government support for innovation and growth. The West Bengal Startup Policy offers seed funding, patent reimbursement, and incubation subsidies specifically for early-stage ventures like yours. I’d like to understand your current stage and goals to see if this could be a fit.

### Discovery Questions
- When was your entity incorporated, and under which structure (Private Limited, LLP, or Partnership)?
- Is your principal place of business located in West Bengal, or do you have at least 50% of your operations based in the state?
- What stage is your startup at — are you working on prototype development, product commercialization, or IPR protection?
- Are you developing an innovative product, service, or process with potential for scalability and employment generation?
- Have you filed or are you planning to file for domestic or international patents?
- Are you currently operating from a government-recognised incubator, or seeking incubation support?
- Are you looking for support in accessing investor networks, government procurement, or international markets?
- Are you a woman-led, SC/ST, or differently-abled led startup? (This may influence preference in selection.)

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity is registered as Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm under Indian laws
- [ ] Incorporated in West Bengal OR at least 50% of operations based in West Bengal
- [ ] Working on innovative products, services, or processes with scalability and employment generation potential
- [ ] Seeking support for seed funding (up to INR 25 lakhs), prototype development, or IPR protection
- [ ] Not blacklisted or previously rejected for misrepresentation
- [ ] Prepared to maintain operational presence in West Bengal to continue availing benefits
- [ ] Willing to provide required documents: Incorporation certificate, PAN, Aadhaar of promoters, business plan, proof of innovation, bank details, address proof, and compliance declaration

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re building an innovative venture in West Bengal with clear growth potential — exactly the kind of startup the West Bengal Startup Policy is designed to support. Let me walk you through how this scheme can provide up to INR 25 lakhs in seed funding, along with patent reimbursement and incubation support, to help you scale.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many startups in West Bengal struggle to access early-stage funding for prototype development, face high costs in patent filing, and lack access to incubation infrastructure and investor networks — slowing their path to market and scalability.

**Scheme intro:**
The West Bengal Startup Policy, administered by the Department of MSME & Textiles, Government of West Bengal, provides financial and non-financial support to registered startups. Benefits include seed funding of up to INR 25 lakhs for prototype development and product commercialization, reimbursement of up to INR 2 lakhs for domestic patent filing and INR 10 lakhs for international patent filing, and subsidies on seat rent in government-recognised incubators.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Registered as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm under Indian laws  
- Incorporated in West Bengal or has at least 50% of operations based in the state  
- Working on innovative products, services, or processes with potential for scalability and employment generation  
- Seeking support for seed funding, patent reimbursement, or incubation — core benefits under the policy
