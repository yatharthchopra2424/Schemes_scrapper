### West Bengal Startup Policy
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** West Bengal
- **Administering Body:** Department of MSME & Textiles, Government of West Bengal
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://msmewb.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Private Limited Company, Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), or Partnership Firm
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Innovative products, services, or processes with potential for scalability and employment generation (preference for IT, biotech, agri)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be incorporated in West Bengal or have at least 50% of operations based in the state; preference may be given to startups led by women, SC/ST, or differently-abled entrepreneurs

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to INR 25 lakhs for seed funding; up to INR 2 lakhs for domestic patent reimbursement; up to INR 10 lakhs for international patent reimbursement; subsidy on seat rent in government-recognized incubators
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (for seed funding and prototype development); Reimbursement (for patent filing fees)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (for seed funding and patent reimbursement); Ongoing (for incubation subsidy, subject to continued eligibility)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. Aadhaar of promoters/directors
4. Business plan or project report
5. Proof of innovation (prototype details, IPR documents, etc.)
6. Bank account details of the entity
7. Address proof of the startup's principal place of business
8. Declaration of compliance with eligibility criteria

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the official MSME West Bengal website or designated startup portal.
2. Register as a startup by providing basic entity and promoter details.
3. Fill out the online application form under the West Bengal Startup Policy.
4. Upload required documents including Certificate of Incorporation, business plan, and proof of innovation.
5. Submit the application for review by the designated committee.
6. Upon approval, receive acknowledgment and further instructions for benefit disbursement.
