## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Startup is registered in West Bengal (proof of registration required)  
- [ ] Startup has an innovative idea or technology-based solution (project proposal/business plan must demonstrate this)  
- [ ] Startup is a registered entity (Certificate of Incorporation/Registration available)  
- [ ] Startup has a defined team (details of founders and team available)  
- [ ] Startup possesses a valid PAN (PAN of the entity available)  
- [ ] Startup is not restricted to any specific turnover threshold, employee count, or age of business (no such criteria specified in evidence)  
- [ ] Startup operates within the geographic scope of West Bengal only  

> **Note**: The scheme does not specify turnover, employee count, or minimum age of business. Eligibility is based on registration in West Bengal and innovation/technology focus.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration  
- PAN of the entity  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (if applicable for MSME classification)  
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the entity for last 2 financial years (if filed)  
- Address proof of the registered office (utility bill, rental agreement, or property tax receipt)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Project proposal or business plan (detailing innovative idea or technology-based solution)  
- Proof of registration in West Bengal (e.g., Certificate of Incorporation showing West Bengal address, or state-level registration certificate)  
- Details of founders and team (including names, roles, qualifications, and contact information)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (minimum 300 DPI)  
- File size for each document must not exceed 5 MB  
- File names must follow the format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ClientName_Date.pdf` (e.g., `BengalSiliconValley_ProjectProposal_ABCStartups_20240515.pdf`)  
- Certificates (Incorporation, PAN, GST, Udyam) must be valid and not expired  
- Project proposal must be in English or Bengali; if in another language, a certified translation must be attached  
- Bank statements must show the entity’s name and be stamped/signed by the bank  
- All documents must match the legal name of the entity as per Certificate of Incorporation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A detailed project proposal or business plan is **required** as per the scheme evidence. While not explicitly termed a DPR, it must include:  
- Executive summary  
- Problem statement and innovative solution  
- Technology or methodology used  
- Market analysis and target audience  
- Business model and revenue projections  
- Team details and qualifications  
- Implementation roadmap and milestones  
- Resource requirements (infrastructure, mentorship needs)  
- Expected outcomes and impact on West Bengal’s startup ecosystem  

> **Note**: If uncertain about depth or format, verify with the nodal officer at WBSIDCO during application drafting.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official portal: https://wbsidco.gov.in and navigate to the **Bengal Silicon Valley Startup Hub** section  
2. Create an account or log in using valid credentials (email and mobile number verified via OTP)  
3. Fill in the startup’s legal name exactly as per Certificate of Incorporation  
4. Enter the registered office address in West Bengal (must match address proof)  
5. Provide accurate PAN and TAN details (if applicable)  
6. Describe the innovative idea or technology-based solution clearly in the project proposal field (upload as PDF)  
7. Upload all scheme-specific and universal documents in the prescribed format and size  
8. Declare that the information provided is true and correct; ensure the authorized signatory signs the declaration  
9. Review all entries before final submission — no edits allowed after submission  
10. Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing the application reference number  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All mandatory fields in the application form are filled  
- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation / Registration uploaded and valid

- [ ] All documents are PDF, under 5 MB, and correctly named  
- [ ] Declarations are signed and dated
