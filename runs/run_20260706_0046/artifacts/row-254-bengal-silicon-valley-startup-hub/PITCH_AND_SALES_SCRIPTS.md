## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping West Bengal-based startups access government incubation support like the Bengal Silicon Valley Startup Hub, which provides infrastructure, mentorship, and networking to help technology-driven ventures grow. I’d like to understand your startup’s current stage and goals to see if this scheme could be a fit — no obligation, just a diagnostic conversation.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated, and is it registered in West Bengal?
2. Are you working on an innovative idea or technology-based solution (e.g., software, hardware, biotech, agritech)?
3. Do you currently have access to dedicated office or workspace infrastructure, or are you operating remotely/from home?
4. Are you seeking mentorship or guidance from industry experts to refine your product or business model?
5. Are you looking to connect with other entrepreneurs, investors, or industry partners in West Bengal’s startup ecosystem?
6. Have you previously applied for any government incubation or startup support schemes?
7. What stage is your startup at — idea, prototype, early revenue, or scaling?
8. Are the founders and core team based in West Bengal, and can you provide details of their involvement?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup is registered in West Bengal  
- [ ] Startup has an innovative idea or technology-based solution  
- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation/Registration is available  
- [ ] Project proposal or business plan can be provided  
- [ ] PAN of the entity is available  
- [ ] Proof of registration in West Bengal is available  
- [ ] Details of founders and team can be submitted  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like your West Bengal-registered tech startup could benefit from the incubation infrastructure and expert mentorship offered through the **Bengal Silicon Valley Startup Hub**. Let me share how this scheme helps startups like yours access shared facilities and guidance to accelerate growth.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many early-stage startups in West Bengal struggle with high operational costs due to lack of affordable workspace, limited access to experienced mentors, and fragmented networks — making it difficult to validate ideas, build teams, and scale efficiently.

**Scheme intro:**
The **Bengal Silicon Valley Startup Hub**, administered by the West Bengal Small Industries Development Corporation Limited (WBSIDCO), is an incubation scheme offering access to shared office infrastructure, mentorship from industry experts, networking opportunities, and support services — all designed to help technology-driven startups grow and scale. While it does not provide direct financial grants, it delivers significant non-financial support through facilitated access to resources.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your startup is registered in West Bengal (confirmed in discovery)  
- You are developing an innovative or technology-based solution  
- You can provide Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, and proof of West Bengal registration  
- You have a project proposal or business plan ready for submission
