## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the actual financial benefit delivered to the client, ensuring fairness and transparency.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees account for scheme-specific uncertainties, including fund availability and policy continuity risks.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainer amounts are scaled to client size and capacity to pay, enabling broad access across startup stages.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Jharkhand Startup Policy offers non-monetary benefits (incubation, mentorship, regulatory facilitation) with no specified quantum for direct financial grants. Evidence indicates no fixed grant amount is defined, and disbursement is subject to fund availability. This introduces moderate revenue lag risk due to dependency on evaluation cycles and fund allocation timelines, though non-financial support delivery is more immediate.
