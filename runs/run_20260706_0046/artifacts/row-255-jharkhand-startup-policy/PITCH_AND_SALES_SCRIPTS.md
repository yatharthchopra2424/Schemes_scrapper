# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping Jharkhand-based startups access government support for innovation and growth. The Jharkhand Startup Policy-2021 is designed specifically for early-stage ventures like yours that are working on scalable ideas with employment potential. I’d like to understand your current stage and goals to see if this policy aligns with your needs.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated or registered?
2. Is your primary place of business currently located in Jharkhand, or are you planning to establish operations there?
3. What stage is your startup at—idea, prototype, early revenue, or scaling?
4. Are you working on an innovative product, service, or technology with potential for scalability?
5. Do you have a clear plan for how your venture will generate employment in Jharkhand?
6. Have you accessed any state-level startup support or incubation programs before?
7. Are you looking for support in areas like mentorship, prototype development, regulatory facilitation, or market access?
8. Do you have your incorporation certificate, PAN, business plan, and proof of innovation ready or in progress?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup is registered in Jharkhand OR willing to establish operations in Jharkhand  
- [ ] Working on an innovative idea, product, or service  
- [ ] Demonstrates potential for scalability  
- [ ] Has potential for employment generation  
- [ ] Has Certificate of Incorporation/Registration  
- [ ] Has PAN of the entity  
- [ ] Has business plan or pitch deck  
- [ ] Has proof of innovation or prototype  
- [ ] Has bank account details  
- [ ] Has authorized signatory details  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your startup aligns well with the Jharkhand Startup Policy-2021’s focus on innovation, scalability, and local impact. This policy offers access to incubation, mentorship, prototype support, and regulatory facilitation—exactly the kind of enablement early-stage ventures need. Let me walk you through how it works.

**Problem framing:**
Early-stage startups in Jharkhand often struggle to access structured support—whether it’s mentorship, prototype development funding, or help navigating regulatory clearances. Without this, even promising ideas can stall due to isolation, lack of guidance, or operational hurdles.

**Scheme intro:**

While it does not specify fixed cash grants, it enables access to incubation centers, mentorship, networking events, prototype development assistance, market validation support, and facilitation in regulatory approvals—key enablers for early growth.
