## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
- PAN of the entity
- GST registration certificate (if applicable)
- Udyam Registration (MSME) certificate
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the entity (last 2 years, if applicable)
- Aadhaar and PAN of directors/partners
- Passport-sized photographs of directors/partners
- Board resolution authorizing application (for companies/LLPs)
- Registered office address proof (utility bill, rental agreement)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Business plan or pitch deck (detailing innovation, product development, and fund utilization)
- Details of any existing funding or revenue (investment letters, bank statements showing revenue, etc.)
- Declaration of compliance with eligibility criteria (signed by authorized signatory)
- Startup profile completion confirmation from Assam Startup portal (screenshot or certificate)

> **Sources**: Assam Startup – Nidhi Program Key Facts (Required Documents, Application Process)

- [ ] Bank account details include account number, IFSC, bank name, and branch (matching submitted documents)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required. The scheme requires a **business plan or pitch deck** instead of a detailed project report (DPR). However, if the selection committee requests additional technical or financial details during evaluation, be prepared to provide a DPR. **Verify with nodal officer if DPR is needed post-shortlisting.**

> **Sources**: Assam Startup – Nidhi Program Key Facts (Required Documents: Business plan or pitch deck; Application Process)

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Register first**: Complete startup registration on https://assamstartup.com before applying to the Nidhi Program  
2. **Profile completeness**: Ensure all sections of the startup profile are filled (team, product, traction, financials) before application  
3. **Accuracy**: Enter exact legal name as per Certificate of Incorporation; match PAN, Aadhaar, and bank details exactly  
4. **Business plan upload**: Upload pitch deck/business plan as a single PDF (max 10 MB); ensure it highlights innovation, Assam linkage, and fund utilization  
5. **Declaration**: Tick the eligibility declaration box and upload signed declaration as separate document  
6. **Existing funding**: Disclose all existing grants, investments, or revenue transparently in the designated field  
7. **Save draft**: Use the “Save as Draft” option to review before final submission; no edits allowed after submission  
8. **Confirmation**: After submission, download and save the application receipt with reference number  

> **Sources**: Assam Startup – Nidhi Program Key Facts (Application Process: Steps 1–4)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify approval letter specifying sanctioned amount, tranche details, and milestones  
- [ ] Confirm disbursement schedule and linkage to milestones (e.g., product prototype, market testing, team expansion)  
- [ ] Utilize funds strictly for purposes outlined in approved business plan (operational and product development costs)  
- [ ] Prepare and submit periodic progress reports as required by the selection committee  
- [ ] Generate utilization certificates (UC) for each tranche duly certified by a Chartered Accountant  
- [ ] Maintain auditable records of all expenditures (vouchers, invoices, bank statements)  
- [ ] Coordinate with Assam Startup for mentorship, networking, and incubation support access  
- [ ] Track milestone achievement and prepare for next tranche release  
- [ ] Ensure compliance to avoid withdrawal of grant due to misuse or non-performance  
- [ ] Upon project completion, submit final report and utilization certificate for closure  
- [ ] Invoice success fee as per consultancy agreement (only after fund receipt confirmation by client)  

> **Sources**: Assam Startup – Nidhi Program Key Facts (Financial Support: tranches linked to milestones; Key Caveats: utilization, progress reports, withdrawal conditions; Benefits: mentorship, networking, incubation support)
