## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the financial benefit delivered — this scheme provides seed funding grants, with amounts determined case-by-case based on startup stage and needs, so success fees are calibrated to the actual grant value realized.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the moderate risk profile of this scheme — while the Assam Startup – Nidhi Program has a rolling application basis and clear eligibility, funding is subject to budget availability and selection committee approval, introducing variability in outcomes.
3. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover initial eligibility assessment, documentation, and application submission, while success fees are contingent on actual grant approval and disbursement.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The processing time for this scheme is not explicitly stated in the evidence, but given the multi-stage evaluation (portal registration, profile completion, application, selection committee review), a 3-month lag between application and approval is a reasonable assumption for revenue modeling, affecting when success fee revenue is realized.
