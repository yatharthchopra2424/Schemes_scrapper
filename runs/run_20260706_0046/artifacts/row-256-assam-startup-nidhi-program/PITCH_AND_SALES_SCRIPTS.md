## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I'm calling from [Your Firm Name]. We specialise in helping early-stage startups in Assam access government grant funding to accelerate their growth. I noticed your work in [mention sector if known, otherwise keep general] and wanted to explore if the Assam Startup – Nidhi Program could support your next phase of development. This scheme provides seed funding grants specifically for Assam-registered startups in their early stages, and I’d like to ask a few quick questions to see if it might be a fit for you.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated or registered, and under which legal structure (e.g., Private Limited, LLP, Partnership)?
2. Is your startup currently registered and active on the Assam Startup portal (assamstartup.com)?
3. What stage of development would you describe your startup as being in—idea, prototype, early revenue, or pre-revenue?
4. Are you working on an innovative product, service, or solution that addresses a specific problem or market need?
5. Do you have a clear business plan or pitch deck outlining your product development and operational goals for the next 6–12 months?
6. Have you already received any external funding or generated revenue so far?
7. Are you based in Assam, and are your core operations and team located within the state?
8. Are you prepared to utilise grant funds strictly for operational and product development costs as outlined in an approved plan?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup is registered in Assam (Certificate of Incorporation/Registration)
- [ ] Recognised by the Assam Startup portal (profile complete on assamstartup.com)
- [ ] Legal entity under Companies Act, LLP Act, or partnership firm
- [ ] Early stage of development (idea, prototype, or pre-revenue)
- [ ] Demonstrates innovative potential
- [ ] Has a business plan or pitch deck
- [ ] PAN of entity and Aadhaar of directors/partners available
- [ ] Bank account details ready for disbursement
- [ ] No current misuse of funds or eligibility conflicts

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like your startup aligns well with the Assam Startup – Nidhi Program’s focus on early-stage, innovative ventures in Assam. This scheme offers seed funding grants to help cover operational and product development costs, with disbursement tied to milestones. Let me share how it works and how we’ve helped similar startups secure approval.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many early-stage startups in Assam struggle to access the initial capital needed to develop their product, cover operational costs, or scale their team—especially when they lack revenue or investor traction. Traditional funding sources are often out of reach at this stage, leaving promising ideas under-resourced.

**Scheme intro:**
The Assam Startup – Nidhi Program is a grant scheme implemented by Assam Startup under the Department of Industries and Commerce, Government of Assam. It provides seed funding as a grant to eligible startups registered in Assam, with the exact amount determined by the startup’s stage, needs, and evaluation by the selection committee. Funds are disbursed in tranches linked to agreed-upon milestones.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your startup is registered in Assam and recognised on the Assam Startup portal (assamstartup.com)
- You are in the early stage of development and have demonstrated innovative potential
- You are a legal entity (e.g., Private Limited, LLP, or partnership) with a clear business plan or pitch deck
- You intend to use funds strictly for operational and product development purposes as per your approved plan
