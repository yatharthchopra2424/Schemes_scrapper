### Assam Startup – Nidhi Program
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Assam
- **Administering Body:** Assam Startup, under the Department of Industries and Commerce, Government of Assam
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://assamstartup.com
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Legal entity registered under the Companies Act, LLP Act, or as a partnership firm
- Age of business: Early stage of development
- Sector: Not specified (open to all sectors demonstrating innovative potential)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be registered in Assam and recognized by the Assam Startup portal; must demonstrate innovative potential

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Seed funding (exact quantum determined by startup's stage, needs, and evaluation by selection committee)
- Disbursement mode: Tranches linked to milestones
- Duration of benefit: Milestone-based (not fixed duration)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. Aadhaar of directors/partners
4. Bank account details
5. Business plan or pitch deck
6. Details of any existing funding or revenue
7. Declaration of compliance with eligibility criteria

#### Application Process
1. Register the startup on the Assam Startup portal (assamstartup.com)
2. Ensure the startup profile is complete and up to date
3. Apply for the Nidhi Program through the designated section on the portal
4. Submit the required documents along with the application
5. Await evaluation by the selection committee
6. If approved, proceed with fund disbursement as per the agreed terms
