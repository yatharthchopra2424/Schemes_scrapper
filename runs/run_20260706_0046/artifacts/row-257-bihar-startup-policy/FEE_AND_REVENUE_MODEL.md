## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-based pricing**: Fees aligned with the ₹10 lakh interest-free loan benefit per startup under the Bihar Startup Policy.
2. **Risk-adjusted success fee**: Success fee reflects moderate risk due to 3–6 month processing lag (evident from evaluation test cycles) and fund availability dependencies.
3. **Tiered accessibility**: Starter and Growth tiers target eligible startups (<₹10L benefit), matching the scheme’s focus on early-stage ventures.
4. **Transparency**: Clear disclosure of retainer and success fee structure, with scheme-specific note on processing time affecting revenue recognition.
