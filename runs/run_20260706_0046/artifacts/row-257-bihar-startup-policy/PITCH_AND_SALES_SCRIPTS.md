# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping startups in Bihar access government support like the Bihar Startup Policy, which offers interest-free loans up to ₹10 lakh along with incubation, mentorship, and statutory clearance support. I’d love to learn about your venture and see if this could be a fit for your growth journey in Bihar.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated or registered?
2. Are you currently operating primarily within the state of Bihar?
3. What stage is your startup at — idea, prototype, early revenue, or scaling?
4. Are you seeking funding support without interest burden to scale operations or product development?
5. Do you have a founding team in place, and are any of the founders women entrepreneurs?
6. Are you looking for support beyond funding — such as mentorship, legal/IP help, or access to co-working spaces?
7. Have you previously applied for any startup grants or loans in Bihar or elsewhere?
8. Are you preparing documents like incorporation certificate, PAN, Aadhaar of founders, and a pitch deck for potential funding applications?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup is registered and operating in Bihar (geographic scope)
- [ ] Applicant is a startup (implied target beneficiary)
- [ ] Has certificate of incorporation/registration
- [ ] Has PAN of the entity
- [ ] Founders have Aadhaar cards
- [ ] Has bank account details ready
- [ ] Has pitch deck or business description prepared
- [ ] Can provide authorization letter from signatory
- [ ] Details of any prior funding available (if applicable)
- [ ] Application submitted before 31 July 2026, 11:59 PM deadline
- [ ] Not re-applying with same mobile/email if Aadhaar was previously used (per re-application rule)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re building something meaningful in Bihar and could benefit from structured support. The Bihar Startup Policy offers exactly what many early-stage founders need — interest-free loans up to ₹10 lakh, mentorship, and help with clearances — all at no cost. Let me walk you through how it works and whether you qualify.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many startups in Bihar struggle to access early-stage funding without taking on debt burden or giving away equity. They also face challenges in navigating statutory clearances, finding mentorship, and accessing affordable workspaces — slowing down growth despite strong ideas.

**Scheme intro:**
The Bihar Startup Policy 2022, administered by the Department of Industries, Government of Bihar, provides interest-free loans of up to ₹10 lakh to eligible startups. The scheme is backed by the Bihar Start-up Fund Trust (BSFT) with a ₹500 crore corpus, and over ₹75+ crore has already been disbursed to support 1,500+ startups across sectors like food, edutech, healthtech, and agritech.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your startup is registered and operating in Bihar — meeting the geographic focus of the scheme  
- You have your certificate of incorporation, PAN, and founders’ Aadhaar ready — core documents required  
- You’re seeking non-dilutive funding to scale — aligned with the scheme’s interest-free loan objective  
- [If applicable] You have a woman founder — eligible for dedicated support under the policy
