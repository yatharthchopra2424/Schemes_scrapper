### Bihar Startup Policy
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Bihar only
- **Administering Body:** Department of Industries, Government of Bihar
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://startup.bihar.gov.in/startupregistration
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Application window is open till 31 July 2026, 11:59 PM

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Startups
- Age of business: Not specified in evidence
- Sector: Not specified in evidence
- Revenue cap: Not specified in evidence
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: General access implied for startups registering via the portal; re-applicants must use a new mobile number and new email ID if Aadhaar has been released for re-application

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Interest-free loans of up to ₹10 lakh
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (through Bihar Start-up Fund Trust)
- Duration of benefit: One-time

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. Aadhaar of founders
4. Bank account details
5. Pitch deck or business description
6. Authorization letter from authorized signatory
7. Details of funding received (if any)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the Startup Bihar portal at https://startup.bihar.gov.in
2. Click on 'Register New Startup' or navigate to the registration page
3. Fill in basic details including founder and startup information
4. Provide entity details, co-founder details, and business idea
5. Complete the work checklist
6. Preview and acknowledge the application
7. Submit the application before the deadline
8. Await evaluation test results and interview calls if qualified
