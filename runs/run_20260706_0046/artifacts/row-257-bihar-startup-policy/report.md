# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Bihar Startup Policy** is a state-level loan scheme administered by the **Department of Industries, Government of Bihar**, designed to empower local entrepreneurs by providing interest-free loans and comprehensive support to startups operating within Bihar. The scheme is implemented through the **Bihar Start-up Fund Trust (BSFT)**, which has an initial corpus of **₹500 crores**. As of the latest data, over **₹75+ crores** have been disbursed to more than **1,500 startups**. The application window is open until **31 July 2026, 11:59 PM**, and the portal is accessible at [https://startup.bihar.gov.in/startupregistration](https://startup.bihar.gov.in/startupregistration).

### Objectives
The policy aims to:
- Empower local entrepreneurs by providing resources, mentorship, and financial support.
- Build scalable businesses that contribute to the state’s economic development.
- Provide interest-free loans of up to **₹10 lakh** to eligible startups.
- Offer dedicated support for women entrepreneurs.
- Foster innovation and entrepreneurship across diverse sectors in Bihar.
- Enable hassle-free and time-bound statutory clearances for startups.
- Provide institutional support including mentoring, hand-holding, and training/orientation visits.
- Facilitate Entrepreneurship Development Centers and Entrepreneurship Facilitation Centers.

### Required Documents
Applicants must submit the following documents during registration:

1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration  
2. PAN of the entity  
3. Aadhaar of founders  
4. Bank account details  
5. Pitch deck or business description  
6. Authorization letter from authorized signatory  
7. Details of funding received (if any)  

> **Source**: Scheme Key Facts

### Application Process
The application process is entirely online via the Startup Bihar portal. Below is a detailed Mermaid flowchart illustrating the step-by-step journey:

```mermaid

    A[Start: Visit Startup Bihar Portal] --> B[Navigate to https://startup.bihar.gov.in/startupregistration]
    B --> C[Click 'Register New Startup' or Login if Returning Applicant]

    D --> E[Step 2: Provide Entity Details]
    E --> F[Step 3: Enter Co-Founder Details]

    G --> H[Step 5: Complete Work Checklist]

    M -->|Yes| N[Attend Interview (Date Released Later)]
    M -->|No| O[Application Not Selected; May Reapply with New Mobile/Email if Aadhaar Released]

```

**Key Process Notes**:
- Application ID is generated upon registration.
- Returning applicants can log in using registration number, email, or mobile number.
- If Aadhaar has been released for re-application, applicants **must use a new mobile number and new email ID**.
- The portal provides real-time status updates (visible in the "Status" tab after login).
- Evaluation tests are conducted periodically (e.g., June 2026, April 2026, etc.), with results published on the portal.
- Qualified applicants are called for interviews; final disbursement follows successful interview.
- The process is managed by the BSFT, PSC (Preliminary Scrutiny Committee), and SMIC (Start-up Monitoring and Implementation Committee).

### Key Caveats & Warnings
- **Fund Availability**: Financial support is subject to the availability of funds under the BSFT.
- **Document Accuracy**: Incomplete, false, or misleading information may result in application cancellation.
- **Re-application Rule**: If Aadhaar is released, re-applicants must use a new mobile number and email ID.
- **No Guarantee**: Qualification in evaluation tests only leads to an interview call; final selection is not guaranteed.
- **Sector Agnostic**: While no sector restrictions are stated, funded startups span food, edu-tech, health, agri-tech, e-commerce, manufacturing, etc.
- **Geographic Restriction**: Only startups based in or operating within Bihar are eligible.
