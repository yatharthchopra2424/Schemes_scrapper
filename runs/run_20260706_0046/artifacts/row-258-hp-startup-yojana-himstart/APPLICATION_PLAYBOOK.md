## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a resident of Himachal Pradesh  
- [ ] Startup is registered as a Private Limited Company, LLP, Partnership Firm, or Proprietorship under relevant Indian laws  
- [ ] Entity incorporated not more than 5 years ago (from date of incorporation)  
- [ ] Startup is working on an innovative product, service, or process with potential for scalability and employment generation  
- [ ] Preference given to ventures in sectors: agriculture, tourism, handicrafts, renewable energy, or IT  
- [ ] Women, SC/ST, and differently-abled entrepreneurs are encouraged to apply (verification for priority consideration)  
- [ ] Startup has not received a similar grant from any other central or state government scheme for the same purpose

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
✅ **Required** – A project report or business plan is explicitly listed as a mandatory document.  
**Standard Sections to Include**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Founder Profiles and Team Background  
- Problem Statement and Innovation Description  
- Technology or Process Used  
- Prototype Details or Proof of Concept (if available)  
- Market Analysis and Target Customers  
- Business Model and Revenue Plan  
- Scalability and Employment Generation Potential  
- Fund Utilization Plan (aligned with grant use: product development, prototype, market testing, operational expenses)  
- Timeline and Milestones  
- Risks and Mitigation Strategy  
- Annexures: Innovation details, prototype photos, IP details (if any)  

> **Note**: The report must emphasize innovation, scalability, and employment generation, with preference for sectors like agriculture, tourism, handicrafts, renewable energy, and IT.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the portal at **https://hpkvn.nic.in** and register as a new user using valid email, mobile number, and address.  
2. Log in and navigate to the HP Startup Yojana & HIMSTART application section.  
3. Fill in all startup details accurately: name, registration number, date of incorporation, address, and contact information.  
4. Founder/director details must match Aadhaar and PAN records (name, DOB, address).  
5. Clearly describe the innovation, technology used, and its potential for scalability and employment generation.  
6. Specify the sector (agriculture, tourism, handicrafts, renewable energy, IT, or other) and justify eligibility.  
7. Upload all required documents in the correct format and size (PDF, <5 MB per file).  
8. Review the declaration of compliance before submission; ensure it is signed and dated.  
9. Note down the Application ID immediately after submission for tracking and follow-up.  
10. Do not submit if the startup has received a similar grant from another central/state scheme for the same purpose (self-declaration required).
