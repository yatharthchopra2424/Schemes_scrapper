## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual grant amount (up to ₹10 lakhs) disbursed under HP Startup Yojana & HIMSTART, ensuring client pays only upon realized benefit.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the rolling application basis and dependency on fund availability (medium confidence), success fees reflect moderate execution risk; retainers cover preliminary eligibility and documentation work.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Starter and Growth tiers align with scheme eligibility (startups ≤5 years old, HP residents, entity types including proprietorship/LLP/Pvt Ltd), ensuring affordability for early-stage ventures.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees disclosed upfront; no hidden charges. Success fee calculated only on actual grant disbursed, not sanctioned amount, per milestone-based release noted in evidence.
