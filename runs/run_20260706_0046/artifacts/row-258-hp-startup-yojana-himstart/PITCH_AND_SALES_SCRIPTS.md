## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping Himachal Pradesh-based startups access non-dilutive government grants for innovation and early-stage growth. The HP Startup Yojana & HIMSTART scheme offers up to INR 10 lakhs as a grant — no equity, no repayment — specifically for product development, prototyping, and market testing. I’d like to understand your venture’s stage and goals to see if this could be a fit.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated or registered?  
2. Where is your startup’s principal place of business or registered office located?  
3. What type of legal entity have you registered as (e.g., Private Limited, LLP, LLP, Partnership, Proprietorship)?  
4. What sector is your innovation in — for example, agriculture, tourism, handicrafts, renewable energy, IT, or another field?  
5. Are you working on a product, service, or process that has potential for scalability and employment generation?  
6. Have you developed a prototype or proof of concept, or are you in the ideation/MVP stage?  
7. Are any of the founders women, from SC/ST communities, or differently-abled?  
8. Have you previously applied for or received a similar grant from any central or state government scheme for the same purpose?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup is incorporated not more than 5 years ago  
- [ ] Applicant is a resident of Himachal Pradesh  
- [ ] Entity is registered as Private Limited Company, LLP, Partnership Firm, or Proprietorship  
- [ ] Working on an innovative product, service, or process with scalability and employment generation potential  
- [ ] Operating in preferred sectors: agriculture, tourism, handicrafts, renewable energy, or IT  
- [ ] Has not received similar grant from central/state government for same purpose  
- [ ] Woman, SC/ST, or differently-abled founder (preferred but not mandatory)  
- [ ] Able to provide required documents (Incorporation, PAN, Aadhaar, project report, etc.)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like your venture may align well with the HP Startup Yojana & HIMSTART scheme — which provides up to INR 10 lakhs as a grant for early-stage innovation in Himachal Pradesh. Let me share how it works and whether it could support your next milestones.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Early-stage startups in Himachal Pradesh often struggle to access seed funding for product development, prototyping, and market validation without giving up equity or taking on debt. Traditional financing is hard to secure at the idea or MVP stage, leaving innovators unable to scale or test their solutions effectively.

**Scheme intro:**
The HP Startup Yojana & HIMSTART, administered by the Himachal Pradesh Kaushal Vikas Nigam (HPKVN) under the Department of Technical Education, Vocational & Industrial Training, Government of Himachal Pradesh, offers a one-time grant of up to INR 10 lakhs for product development, prototype creation, market testing, and initial operational expenses. The grant is disbursed in tranches based on milestone achievement and requires no equity dilution or repayment.
