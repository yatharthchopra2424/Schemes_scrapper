### HP Startup Yojana & HIMSTART
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Himachal Pradesh
- **Administering Body:** Himachal Pradesh Kaushal Vikas Nigam (HPKVN), Department of Technical Education, Vocational & Industrial Training, Government of Himachal Pradesh
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://hpkvn.nic.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (subject to availability of funds)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Private Limited Company, LLP, Partnership Firm, or Proprietorship
- Age of business: Incorporated not more than 5 years ago
- Sector: Agriculture, tourism, handicrafts, renewable energy, IT (preference given)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Applicant must be a resident of Himachal Pradesh; working on an innovative product, service, or process with potential for scalability and employment generation; women, SC/ST, and differently-abled entrepreneurs encouraged to apply

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to INR 10 lakhs (one-time grant)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (released in tranches based on milestone achievement)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (disbursed in installments upon milestone completion)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN Card of the startup
3. Aadhaar Card of all founders/directors
4. Project report or business plan
5. Details of innovation and technology used
6. Prototype details or proof of concept (if available)
7. Bank account details of the startup
8. Address proof of the startup or founders
9. Declaration of compliance with eligibility criteria

#### Application Process
1. Visit the official HPKVN website or designated portal for HP Startup Yojana & HIMSTART.
2. Register as a new user by providing basic details such as name, email, mobile number, and address.
3. Log in and fill out the online application form with information about the startup, founders, innovation details, and business model.
4. Upload required documents including Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, Aadhaar of founders, project report, and any prototype or IP details.
5. Submit the application and note the application ID for future reference.
6. Await screening by the evaluation committee; shortlisted applicants may be called for a pitch or interview.
7. Upon selection, sign the grant agreement and fulfill any pre-disbursement formalities.
8. Receive the grant amount in installments as per milestone completion.
