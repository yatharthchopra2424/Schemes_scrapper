- Pitch deck or business description  
- Details of promoters/directors (names, roles, qualifications, contact)  
- Proof of innovation or prototype (if applicable) — e.g., prototype photos, video demo, lab test reports

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per file)

- Certificates must be valid and not expired

- Authorization letter must be signed by an authorized director/partner and stamped (if applicable)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not explicitly required as per evidence. However, a detailed pitch deck or business description is mandatory. If the screening committee requests a detailed project report during evaluation, prepare one with the following sections:  
- Executive Summary  
- Problem Statement  
- Solution & Innovation  
- Market Analysis  
- Business Model & Revenue Plan  
- Technology or Social Impact Details  
- Go-to-Market Strategy  
- Financial Projections (3 years)  
- Funding Requirement & Utilization Plan  
- Team Details  
- Milestones & Timeline  
- Annexures (prototype proof, certificates, etc.)  

> **Verify with nodal officer** whether a formal DPR is needed beyond the pitch deck.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the portal at **https://cgstartup.in** and register as a startup using entity and promoter details.  
2. Fill all fields in the online application form accurately; do not leave any mandatory field blank.  
3. Ensure the business model description clearly highlights **innovation, scalability, and technology/social impact focus**.  
4. In the funding requirement section, specify the amount sought and proposed utilization (aligned with milestones).  
5. Upload documents in the correct sections as per portal prompts; verify file types and sizes before final upload.  
6. Double-check that the entity name, PAN, and bank details match exactly across all fields and documents.  
7. Confirm that the authorization letter is uploaded and signed by an authorized signatory.  
8. Review the entire form for spelling, consistency, and completeness before submission.
