## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Pricing**: Fees are structured to reflect the tangible benefit delivered (e.g., seed grant access, incubation, mentorship), ensuring clients pay only for realized outcomes.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Terms**: Success fees correlate with scheme risk; this policy involves milestone-based disbursement and committee approval, introducing moderate execution risk due to funding availability and evaluation variability.
3. **Transparency & Trust**: All fee structures are disclosed upfront via retainer and success fee invoices, with clear linkage to scheme-specific deliverables.
4. **Scalable Engagement Model**: Tiered pricing allows firms to serve startups at different maturity levels, though this scheme primarily suits the Growth tier due to its focus on early-stage, technology-driven ventures with turnover/age limits.

*Note on Scheme Risk Profile*: The CG Startup Policy & Nava Raipur Innovation City has a **moderate risk profile** due to its rolling application basis and dependence on state-level screening committee approval. While the portal (https://cgstartup.in) enables accessible applications, disbursement is tranche-based and contingent on milestone achievement, which may extend timelines. Evidence indicates benefits are subject to fund availability, requiring careful client expectation management. However, the structured process and defined eligibility reduce ambiguity, supporting reliable fee forecasting for consultants.
