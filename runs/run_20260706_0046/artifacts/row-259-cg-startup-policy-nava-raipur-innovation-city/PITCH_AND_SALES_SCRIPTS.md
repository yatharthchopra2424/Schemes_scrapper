## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping startups in Chhattisgarh access government-backed innovation support, particularly through the CG Startup Policy & Nava Raipur Innovation City initiative. This scheme is designed to nurture early-stage, technology-driven ventures with seed funding, incubation, and mentorship—especially for founders looking to build scalable solutions in sectors like agri-tech, health-tech, or manufacturing. I’d like to understand your startup’s current stage and goals to see if this could be a relevant fit.

### Discovery Questions
- When and where was your startup incorporated? (To assess eligibility: must be incorporated in Chhattisgarh or willing to relocate)
- What stage is your startup currently at—idea, prototype, early revenue, or scaling? (Helps determine fit for seed grants or soft loans)
- Are you working on a technology-driven or socially impactful solution? (Core eligibility: innovation focus)
- What is your current annual turnover? (To check against unspecified turnover limits under the policy)
- How old is your startup? (Preference for early-stage ventures; age limit applies per policy)
- Are you registered as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or partnership firm? (Preference for entities under Companies Act, 2013 or LLPs/partnerships)
- Do you plan to maintain operational presence in Chhattisgarh to access ongoing benefits like incubation at Nava Raipur Innovation City?
- Are you seeking support for prototype development, IPR filing, or investor linkages? (To align with scheme benefits)

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)

- [ ] Focus on innovative, scalable business model  
- [ ] Working on technology-driven or socially impactful solution

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many early-stage startups in Chhattisgarh struggle to access patient capital, mentorship, and infrastructure needed to validate ideas and scale. Founders often face challenges in securing seed funding, finding suitable workspace, or connecting with investors and experts—especially outside major metro hubs.

**Scheme intro:**
The **CG Startup Policy & Nava Raipur Innovation City**, administered by the Department of Commerce and Industries, Government of Chhattisgarh, offers eligible startups access to seed grants, equity-based funding, soft loans, and incubation support at Nava Raipur Innovation City.
