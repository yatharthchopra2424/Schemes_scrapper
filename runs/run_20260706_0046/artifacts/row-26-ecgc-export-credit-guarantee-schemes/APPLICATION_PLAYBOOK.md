# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Indian exporter engaged in export of goods or services  
- [ ] Has obtained an export order or contract  
- [ ] Requires credit protection against buyer default (commercial or political risks)  
- [ ] MSME exporter (if applying under RELIEF Component III)  
- [ ] Valid IEC (Import Export Code) certificate  
- [ ] Valid RCMC (Registration Cum Membership Certificate)  
- [ ] GST registration certificate  
- [ ] Bank sanction letter (if seeking finance)  
- [ ] Export order or contract value within permissible limits (as per ECGC underwriting norms)  
- [ ] Buyer details and creditworthiness information available  
- [ ] Policy must be obtained before shipment or extension of credit  
- [ ] Not engaged in fraudulent activities or misrepresentation  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN card of the business/entity  
- ITR (Income Tax Return) for last 2-3 years  
- GST registration certificate  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)  
- IEC (Import Export Code) certificate  
- RCMC (Registration Cum Membership Certificate)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Export order or contract  
- Commercial invoice  
- Bill of lading or airway bill  
- Buyer details and creditworthiness information  
- Bank sanction letter (if seeking finance)  
- Export credit guarantee application form  
- For RELIEF Component III:  
  - IEC certificate  
  - MSME certificate  
  - Export contract/order  
  - Invoice and packing list  
  - Shipping bill  
  - On-board bill of lading or airway bill  
  - Correspondence with buyer regarding extra-ordinary charges  
  - Proof of payment of extra-ordinary charges  
  - Bank realisation certificate  
  - Bank account verification and cancelled cheque  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and in PDF or clear scanned format  
- File size should not exceed 5 MB per document  
- Name on documents must match the applicant’s IEC and GST details  
- Documents must be self-attested or certified where required  
- Hindi or English language accepted; translations required for other languages  
- Export documents must clearly show shipment details and buyer information  

### Client Communication Template
**Subject:** Application Invitation for ECGC Export Credit Guarantee Scheme (Including RELIEF Support)  

Dear [Client Name],  

We are initiating your application for the **ECGC Export Credit Guarantee Schemes**, implemented by the Export Credit Guarantee Corporation of India Limited (ECGC), under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.  

This scheme provides credit guarantee covers up to 90% of export value, protecting against commercial and political risks, and facilitating access to bank finance. Additionally, under the **RELIEF (Resilience & Logistics Intervention for Export Facilitation)** scheme, time-bound support is available for exports to West Asian countries (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Israel, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Iraq, Iran, Yemen) affected by geopolitical disruptions.  

**Key Details:**  
- **Application Portal:** https://www.ecgc.in  
- **Status:** Rolling basis — applications accepted throughout the year based on export transactions  
- **RELIEF Timelines:**  
  - Component I & II: Retrospective and prospective coverage for shipments  
  - Component III: Reimbursement up to 50% of extra-ordinary logistics and insurance charges (capped at ₹50 lakhs per exporter) for non-ECGC-insured MSME exporters

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for standard ECGC guarantee applications. For RELIEF Component III claims, a detailed statement of extra-ordinary charges incurred is necessary. **Verify with nodal officer if a formal DPR is needed for complex cases.**

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use the official ECGC client portal: https://www.ecgcltd.in  
2. Ensure all fields in the application form are filled accurately — no blank fields  
3. Match export value in invoice with declared value in shipping bill and bank realisation  
4. Select correct policy type: pre-shipment, post-shipment, or specific RELIEF component  
5. Upload documents in the prescribed order and format as per ECGC checklist  
6. Declare buyer country and verify it is not in the excluded high-risk list (if applicable)  
7. Confirm that the policy is applied for before shipment or credit extension  
8. Double-check declarations regarding no fraud or misrepresentation  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All application form fields completed  
- [ ] All scheme-specific and universal documents attached  
- [ ] Export order, invoice, and shipping documents are consistent  
- [ ] Buyer details and creditworthiness information included  
- [ ] Bank sanction letter attached (if finance is sought)  
- [ ] Declarations signed and dated  
- [ ] File sizes and naming conventions checked  
- [ ] Reference number noted for follow-up

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit application via ECGC client portal: https://www.ecgcltd.in  
- Take screenshot of confirmation page with timestamp and application/reference number  
- Store reference number securely and set calendar reminders for follow-up at 15 and 30 days  
- Save all submitted documents in a dedicated case folder

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify approval letter or policy document from ECGC  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned guarantee coverage amount and premium details  
- [ ] Ensure policy is active and covers the intended export transaction  
- [ ] Utilize the guarantee to secure pre-shipment or post-shipment finance from bank  
- [ ] In case of buyer default, file claim with ECGC using required documents:  
  - Policy copy  
  - Proof of default (legal notices, correspondence)  
  - Export documents (invoice, shipping bill, etc.)  
  - Bank realisation certificate  
  - Proof of exhaustion of legal remedies  
- [ ] Maintain records for utilization certificate and audit purposes  
- [ ] Coordinate with client for success fee invoicing upon successful claim settlement or policy utilization
