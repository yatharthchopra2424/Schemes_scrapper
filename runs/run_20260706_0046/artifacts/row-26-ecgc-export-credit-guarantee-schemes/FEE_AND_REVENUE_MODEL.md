## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the financial benefit delivered—ECGC schemes provide credit guarantees up to 90% of export value, enabling access to finance and risk mitigation.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile; claims require proof of default and exhaustion of legal remedies, with processing times varying (typically 3–6 months for settlement based on evidence of claim disbursements).
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Lower retainers for MSMEs/exporters (Growth tier) align with scheme eligibility targeting Indian exporters, including MSMEs, engaged in export of goods or services.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: Clear fee structures build confidence, mirroring ECGC’s objective to enhance creditworthiness and promote exports by mitigating commercial and political risks.
