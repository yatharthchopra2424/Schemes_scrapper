# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping Indian exporters and banks manage payment risks in international trade—especially in volatile regions like West Asia. Given the recent disruptions in Gulf shipping routes and rising insurance costs, many exporters are seeking reliable ways to protect their overseas receivables. I’d like to understand your current export operations and see if the ECGC Export Credit Guarantee Schemes could support your business.

### Discovery Questions
1. What goods or services do you export, and to which countries (particularly in West Asia or GCC regions)?
2. Do you currently have export orders or contracts requiring shipment or credit extension?
3. Are you seeking pre-shipment or post-shipment finance from banks for these exports?
4. Have you faced payment delays or defaults from overseas buyers in the past 12 months?
5. Are you an MSME exporter registered with Udyam or holding an IEC/RCMC certificate?
6. Do you have an existing ECGC policy, or are you looking to obtain credit protection for new export transactions?
7. Are you a bank lending to exporters and seeking to secure your export credit exposure?
8. What documents do you typically maintain for export transactions (e.g., export order, invoice, bill of lading)?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Indian exporter (including MSME) engaged in export of goods or services  
- [ ] Has obtained an export order or contract  
- [ ] Requires credit protection against buyer default (commercial or political risk)  
- [ ] Possesses IEC or RCMC certificate  
- [ ] Has GST registration  
- [ ] Exports to countries covered under ECGC’s country risk classifications (including West Asia)  
- [ ] Seeking coverage for pre-shipment and/or post-shipment risks  
- [ ] Bank lending to such exporters (if applicable)  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you’re actively exporting and managing buyer payment risks—especially relevant given current West Asia disruptions. The ECGC Export Credit Guarantee Schemes can provide credit cover up to 90% of export value, helping you secure better bank finance and protect against defaults. Let me explain how this works for exporters like you.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Indian exporters face growing risks of non-payment from overseas buyers due to insolvency, protracted default, or political events—especially in volatile regions like West Asia. This disrupts cash flow, increases financing costs, and makes banks hesitant to lend against export receivables.

**Scheme intro:**
The ECGC Export Credit Guarantee Schemes, administered by the Export Credit Guarantee Corporation of India Limited (ECGC) under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, provide credit guarantee covers up to 90% of the export value. This protects exporters against commercial and political risks, improves access to bank finance at better terms, and supports both pre-shipment and post-shipment finance.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are an Indian exporter with an active export order or contract  
- You require protection against buyer default due to insolvency or political events  
- You hold IEC/RCMC and GST registration (mandatory for eligibility)  
- You are seeking pre-shipment and/or post-shipment finance from banks
