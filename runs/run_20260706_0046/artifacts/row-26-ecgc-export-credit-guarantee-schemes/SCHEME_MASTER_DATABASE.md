### ECGC Export Credit Guarantee Schemes
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Export schemes
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Export Credit Guarantee Corporation of India Limited (ECGC), Ministry of Commerce and Industry
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.ecgc.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Indian exporters, including MSMEs, engaged in export of goods or services
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Export of goods or services
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have obtained an export order and require credit protection against buyer default; banks lending to such exporters can also avail guarantees

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Credit guarantee covers typically up to 90% of the export value
- Disbursement mode: Claims settled upon buyer default due to insolvency, protracted default, or political events
- Duration of benefit: One-time per export transaction

#### Documents Required
1. Export order or contract
2. Commercial invoice
3. Bill of lading or airway bill
4. Buyer details and creditworthiness information
5. Bank sanction letter (if seeking finance)
6. Export credit guarantee application form
7. RCMC or IEC certificate
8. GST registration certificate

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Exporter or bank approaches ECGC with export contract details and buyer information.
2. Submit required documents including export order, invoice, and buyer creditworthiness details.
3. ECGC evaluates the risk and issues a credit guarantee policy.
4. Premium is paid based on risk assessment and coverage amount.
5. Policy is issued and can be used to secure bank finance.
6. In case of default, claim is filed with supporting documents for settlement.
