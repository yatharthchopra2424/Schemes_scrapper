# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Entity incorporated/registered in the State of Goa and/or operating in the State of Goa  
- [ ] Up to ten years from the date of incorporation/registration  
- [ ] Annual turnover not exceeding INR 100 crore in any of the preceding financial years  
- [ ] Incorporated as a Private Limited Company, a Registered Partnership Firm, or a Limited Liability Partnership  
- [ ] Working towards innovation, development, or improvement of a product, process, or service and/or having a scalable business model  
- [ ] Entity not formed by splitting up or reconstructing an already existing business  
- [ ] For specific schemes (e.g., Seed Capital Scheme): Valid startup certificate from Startup and IT Promotion Cell (SITPC)  
- [ ] For Salary Reimbursement Scheme: Employees hired for at least 12 months; freshers defined as graduates/postgraduates within 2 years of completion  
- [ ] For Lease Rental Subsidy Scheme: At least 50% equity/share held by Goans continuously since inception (local startup definition)  
- [ ] For IPR/Trademark Reimbursement Schemes: Successful IP/trademark registration; expenditure incurred after notification of Goa Start-up Policy 2021 and paid digitally  
- [ ] For R&D Reimbursement Scheme: Working prototype of technology/product; preference for startups that have applied for/obtained a patent  
- [ ] For Grant for Incubation Centers: Educational institute affiliated to a recognized public university in Goa

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN card  
- ITR (Income Tax Returns)  
- GST registration certificate  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam registration (if applicable)  
- Certificate of Incorporation/Registration  
- Address proof of operational office in Goa (utility bill not older than 2 months)  
- Cancelled cheque of the startup (bank account in Goa)  
- Identity proof (Aadhaar) of Director/CEO  
- Identity proof (Aadhaar) of employees (for salary-linked schemes)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- **Co-Working Spaces/Incubators/Accelerators Subsidy Scheme**:  
  Agreement entered into by the startup and the co-working space/incubator/accelerator  
- **Salary Reimbursement Scheme**:  
  Bank certification/bank account statement showing debit of salary  
  Degree certificates of employees  
  Employee Identity Proof (Aadhaar card of employee)  
  Employment Contract Proof (Contract of Employment of employees)  
  Goan Proof (Birth Certificate)  
  HR letter certifying that employment contracts fulfill conditions  
  Salary increment letter of each employee  
- **Reimbursement of Expenses for Startups Operating from Leased/Privately Owned Premises Scheme**:  
  Goan Domicile/Residence Certificate of 10 years  
  Bills/Invoices from company certified vendors  
  Proof of payment towards lease  
  Proof of Payment for Cloud charges  
  Proof of Payment for Internet charges  
  Proof of Payment for Software cost  
  Property Proof - Valid registered Lease Deed  
  Software License - License details of software purchased  
- **Seed Capital Scheme**:  
  CV of founder in World Bank CV format and other document(s) (if any)  
  Presentation to Panel of Experts  
  Detailed plan of action to create a Minimum Viable Product  
- **IPR Reimbursement Scheme**:  
  Copy of successful IP registration  
  Original Proof of Payment - Detailed statement of expenses incurred towards IP Registration along with copies of invoices & receipts from competent authority and legal counsel  
  Detailed report submitted  
- **Trademark Reimbursement Scheme**:  
  Company Logo Design  
  Trademark Certificate  
  Copy of trademark application form and document attached  
  Bills/Invoices from company  
  Proof of Payment  
- **Grant for Incubation Centers within Educational Institutes Scheme**:  
  Copy of plan expenditure of proposed expenses  
  Expenses towards capital expenses (Accounting Standard 101 for Fixed Assets by Institute of Cost Accountants of India)  
- **R&D Reimbursement Scheme**:  
  Proof of payment for R&D expenses including PhD/Master salaries  
  Employee Identity Proof (Aadhaar card) of employees whose salary is claimed  
  Degree certificates of PhD/Master holders  
  Employment Contract Proof for employees  
  HR letter certifying employment period of at least 12 months  

### Document Quality Check
- File size: Each document < 5 MB  
- File name: Must match document type (e.g., "Address_Proof_Goa_Office.pdf")  
- All documents must be legible scans or clear photographs  
- Aadhaar numbers must be visible and match PAN/bank details  
- Bank statements must show the startup’s name and account number  
- Cancelled cheque must be clearly visible with account details  
- All documents must be in English or accompanied by certified translation  
- Digital payment proof required for all reimbursement claims (unless exception granted by SITPC)  
- Lease deed/agreement must be registered and valid  
- IP/trademark certificates must be issued by competent authority

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
DPR is required for the Seed Capital Scheme (detailed plan of action to create a Minimum Viable Product). For other schemes, verify with nodal officer if a detailed project report is needed for evaluation.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Register on the Startup Goa portal (startup.goa.gov.in) to obtain a startup certificate from SITPC before applying for any incentive.  
2. Ensure all fields in the application form are filled accurately; leave no mandatory field blank.  
3. Upload documents in the specified format (PDF/JPEG, <5 MB each) with correct file names as per checklist.  
4. Declare that expenditure was incurred after notification of Goa Start-up Policy 2021 and paid digitally (unless exception applies).  
5. Link Aadhaar of directors and employees to bank accounts as required.  
6. For salary-linked schemes, confirm employment period of at least 12 months and fresher status (within 2 years of graduation).  
7. For IPR/trademark schemes, declare that no duplicate reimbursement has been claimed under any other central/state scheme for the same IP.  
8. Save a screenshot of the completed form before final submission.  
9. Select the correct incentive scheme from the dropdown (e.g., Seed Capital Scheme, Salary Reimbursement Scheme).  
10. Double-check the declared amount against scheme caps (e.g., INR 10 lakh for Seed Capital, INR 25,000 for Trademark).  
11. Provide honest and verifiable information; false declarations may lead to rejection and recovery with interest.  
12. Use the exact legal name of the startup as per incorporation certificate.  
13. Ensure the bank account details match the cancelled cheque and statements submitted.  
14. Apply well before any annual scheme caps are reached (e.g., 100 startups for Seed Capital, 200 for IPR/trademark).  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All eligibility criteria verified and documented  
- [ ] All universal documents collected and quality-checked  
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents collected and quality-checked  
- [ ] Application form filled completely with accurate data  
- [ ] Documents uploaded correctly to the portal  
- [ ] Declarations signed (digital signature acceptable)  
- [ ] Screenshot of submitted application saved  
- [ ] Application reference number noted  
- [ ] No duplicate claims for same expenditure/IP/trademark under other schemes  
- [ ] Digital payment proof attached for all reimbursement claims  
- [ ] Bank accounts linked to Aadhaar for directors and employees  
- [ ] For Salary Reimbursement: HR letter and employment contracts validate 12+ month period  
- [ ] For Seed Capital: MVP plan and presentation to panel ready  
- [ ] For Incubation Grant: Plan expenditure and operational budget aligned with Accounting Standards

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Take a screenshot of the final submission confirmation page showing the application reference number and timestamp.  
- Store the reference number securely in the case file.  
- Set calendar reminders for follow-up at 15 days, 30 days, and 45 days post-submission.  
- Monitor the portal for any "Memo of Deficiency" notifications and respond within 7 days.  
- Keep all original documents available for potential audit by SITPC or Empowered Committee.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and save the approval letter from SITPC/Empowered Committee  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount against the applied amount and scheme caps  
- [ ] Request payment via the incentive application process on the portal (if not auto-disbursed)  
- [ ] Track disbursement from ITG (Disbursing Body) and confirm receipt in bank account  
- [ ] Maintain utilization records: invoices, bills, payment proofs for all sanctioned expenses  
- [ ] Prepare Utilization Certificate (if required) – certified by Chartered Accountant confirming funds used for intended purpose  
- [ ] Submit progress reports as per scheme requirements (quarterly/bi-annual/annual)  
- [ ] For ongoing schemes (e.g., Salary Reimbursement, Incubation Grant): submit monthly/quarterly salary/attendance proof and operational reports  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with scheme-specific conditions (e.g., 2-year limit for co-working subsidy, 3-year cap for salary reimbursement)  
- [ ] Retain all records for audit purposes for minimum 5 years  
- [ ] Calculate and invoice success fee as per consultancy agreement
