(Standard data handling policy — encrypted cloud storage, 2FA, limited access, no personal devices,

**Purpose:** To ensure the secure handling, storage, and disposal of client data in compliance with data protection principles and scheme-specific requirements.

**Scope:** Applies to all consultants, employees, and contractors handling client data for Goa Startup Policy applications.

**Policy Statements:**

1. **Data Storage:** All client data shall be stored in encrypted cloud storage with access controls.
2. **Access Control:** Access to client data is limited to authorized personnel only, using role-based permissions.
3. **Authentication:** Multi-factor authentication (2FA) is required for all access to systems containing client data.
4. **Device Usage:** No personal devices may be used to store or process client data.
5. **Retention Period:** Client data shall be retained for a minimum of 2 years post case closure.
6. **Scheme-specific note:** All documents submitted to Department of Information Technology, Electronics and Communications (DITE&C), Government of Goa under Goa Startup Policy must be retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit purposes.
7. **Breach Notification:** In the event of a data breach, affected parties and relevant authorities shall be notified within 24 hours.
8. **Data Disposal:** After the retention period, data shall be securely deleted or destroyed using approved methods.
9. **Compliance:** This policy aligns with applicable data protection regulations and scheme guidelines from the Department of Information Technology, Electronics and Communications (DITE&C), Government of Goa.
10. **Review:** This policy shall be reviewed annually or as required by changes in scheme guidelines or legal requirements.
