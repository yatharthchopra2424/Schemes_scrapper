## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the actual financial benefit received by the client (e.g., up to ₹10 lakh for Seed Capital Scheme, ₹5 lakh per annum for R&D, etc.).
2. Transparent fee structure with no hidden charges; all fees disclosed upfront.
3. Success fee only payable upon successful disbursement of funds from the Goa Startup Policy portal (https://startup.goa.gov.in).
4. Scheme-specific note: The Goa Startup Policy has a high success rate for well-prepared applications (evidenced by 241 certified startups out of 299 applications), but processing involves multiple stages (portal registration, document submission, SITPC review, Empowered Committee approval) leading to a typical 3–6 month lag from application to disbursement, which affects revenue recognition timing.
