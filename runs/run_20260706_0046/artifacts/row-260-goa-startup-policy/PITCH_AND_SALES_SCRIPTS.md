# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping Goan startups access government incentives like the Goa Startup Policy. I noticed your work in [mention sector if known, e.g., IT services, agritech, or healthcare] and wanted to explore if you’re leveraging the full range of support available for startups incorporated and operating in Goa. Our goal is to understand your current stage and see if we can help you access non-dilutive funding — up to INR 10 lakh in seed capital, salary reimbursement, or R&D grants — without any upfront cost to you.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your company incorporated or registered in Goa?  
2. What is your current annual turnover, and has it exceeded INR 100 crore in any of the last three financial years?  
3. What is your legal structure — Private Limited Company, LLP, or Registered Partnership Firm?  
4. Are you working on innovation, development, or improvement of a product, process, or service with a scalable business model?  
5. Are at least 50% of your equity shares held by Goan individuals continuously since incorporation?  
6. Are you hiring freshers (graduates/postgraduates within 2 years of completion) or planning to?  
7. Do you incur R&D expenses, including salaries of PhD/Master’s degree holders?  
8. Are you currently using co-working spaces, incubators, or accelerators in Goa, or paying for leased premises, internet, cloud, or software?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Incorporated/registered in Goa and operating there  
- [ ] Up to 10 years from date of incorporation/registration  
- [ ] Annual turnover not exceeding INR 100 crore in any preceding financial year  
- [ ] Legal structure: Private Limited Company, LLP, or Registered Partnership Firm  
- [ ] Working towards innovation, development, or improvement of product/process/service OR has scalable business model  
- [ ] Not formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business  
- [ ] At least 50% equity held by Goans continuously since inception (for local startup benefits)  
- [ ] Employees hired for at least 12 months (for salary/R&D reimbursement)  
- [ ] Bank accounts of directors/employees linked to Aadhaar  
- [ ] Expenditure incurred digitally and after notification of Goa Startup Policy 2021  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your answers, it looks like you may qualify for several incentives under the Goa Startup Policy — including seed capital up to INR 10 lakh, 50% salary reimbursement for freshers, or R&D grants. Would you like me to walk you through how these work and what the next steps would be?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many Goan startups struggle with early-stage cash flow — whether it’s hiring talent, developing prototypes, paying for office space, or covering R&D costs. These expenses can slow growth, especially when founders are bootstrapping or avoiding equity dilution.

**Scheme intro:**
The Goa Startup Policy, administered by the Department of Information Technology, Electronics and Communications (DITE&C), Government of Goa, offers multiple non-dilutive incentives. Key benefits include:  
- **Seed Capital Scheme**: One-time grant of up to INR 10 lakh  
- **Salary Reimbursement Scheme**: 50% of fresher’s salary, capped at INR 15,000/month per recruit (max 25 people over 3 years)  
- **R&D Reimbursement Scheme**: 50% of R&D expenses (including PhD/Master salaries), capped at INR 5 lakh per annum (salary cap: INR 2 lakh)  
- **Co-working/Incubator/Accelerator Subsidy**: 50% of seat cost, up to INR 6,000/seat (accelerator), INR 5,000 (incubator), INR 3,000 (co-working), max 8 seats  
- **IPR Reimbursement**: Up to 100% of IP registration fees (INR 2 lakh national, INR 5 lakh international)  
- **Trademark Reimbursement**: 50% of cost, up to INR 25,000  
- **Lease Rental Subsidy**: Up to INR 20/sq ft/month for local startups in rented premises (cap: INR 3 lakh/annum)  
- **Internet/Cloud/Software Reimbursement**: Up to INR 1 lakh/quarter for one year  

All benefits are accessed via the single-window portal: [https://startup.goa.gov.in](https://startup.goa.gov.in)

- Within 10 years of incorporation/registration ✅

- Private Limited Company/LLP/Partnership structure ✅
