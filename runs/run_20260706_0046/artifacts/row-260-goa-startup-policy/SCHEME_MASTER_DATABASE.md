### Goa Startup Policy
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Goa only
- **Administering Body:** Department of Information Technology, Electronics and Communications (DITE&C), Government of Goa
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://startup.goa.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Private Limited Company, Registered Partnership Firm, or Limited Liability Partnership
- Age of business: Up to ten years from the date of incorporation/registration in the State of Goa and/or operating in the State of Goa
- Sector: Working towards innovation, development or improvement of a product, process or service and/or have a scalable business model
- Revenue cap: Annual turnover not exceeding INR 100 crore in any of the preceding financial years
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Entity should not have been formed by splitting up or reconstructing an already existing business; must be certified by the Startup and IT Promotion Cell (SITPC); local start-up definition requires at least 50% equity held by Goans continuously since inception; fresher definition limited to graduates/postgraduates within 2 years of completion; salary reimbursement requires employment period of at least 12 months

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support varies by scheme: Seed Capital Scheme offers a one-time grant of up to INR 10 lakh; Salary Reimbursement Scheme provides 50% of fresher salary capped at INR 15,000/month per recruit for up to 25 people over 3 years; Co-Working Spaces/Incubators/Accelerators Subsidy Scheme reimburses 50% of seat cost with caps of INR 3000 (co-working), INR 5000 (incubator), INR 6000 (accelerator) per seat, max 8 seats; IPR Reimbursement Scheme covers up to 100% of IP registration fees (INR 2 lakh national, INR 5 lakh international); Trademark Reimbursement Scheme reimburses 50% of trademark registration cost up to INR 25,000; R&D Reimbursement Scheme covers 50% of R&D expenses including PhD/Master salaries, capped at INR 5 lakh per annum with salary component not exceeding INR 2 lakh; Grant for Incubation Centers provides up to INR 10 lakh for capital expenses and INR 3 lakh per year for operational expenses over 3 years
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement / Direct credit (varies by scheme; e.g., reimbursement for salary, IPR, trademark, R&D; direct credit for seed capital)
- Duration of benefit: One-time / Annual / 3 years (varies by scheme; e.g., seed capital is one-time, salary reimbursement is up to 3 years, incubation grant is 3 years operational)

#### Documents Required
1. Address proof of operational office in the State of Goa
2. Cancelled Cheque of the start-up/incubator/accelerator
3. Agreement entered into by the start-up and the co-working space/incubator/accelerator
4. Bank certification/Bank account statement showing debit of salary
5. Degree certificates of employees
6. Employee Identity Proof - Aadhaar card of employee
7. Employment Contract Proof - Contract of Employment of employees
8. Goan Proof - Birth Certificate
9. HR letter certifying that employment contracts fulfill conditions
10. Identity Proof - Aadhaar card of Director/CEO
11. Salary increment letter of each employee
12. Bills/Invoices from company certified vendors
13. Proof of payment towards lease
14. Proof of Payment for Cloud charges
15. Proof of Payment for Internet charges

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register on the Startup Goa portal (startup.goa.gov.in) to obtain a startup certificate from the Startup and IT Promotion Cell (SITPC).
2. Ensure eligibility as per the specific scheme (e.g., turnover < INR 100 crore, incorporated < 10 years, Private Limited Company/LLP/Partnership Firm).
3. Gather required documents (address proof, cancelled cheque, Aadhaar-linked bank accounts, etc., varying by scheme).
4. Apply for the specific incentive scheme via the Startup Goa portal or designated application link.
5. Submit application with documents for SPC due diligence and panel of experts review.
6. Await approval/rejection; if approved, request payment via the incentive application process.
7. Receive disbursement from the ITG (Disbursing Body) after approval.
