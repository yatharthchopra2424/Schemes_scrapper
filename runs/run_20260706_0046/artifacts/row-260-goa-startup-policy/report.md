# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The Goa Startup Policy is a state-level initiative by the Government of Goa, implemented by the Department of Information Technology, Electronics and Communications (DITE&C) through the Startup and IT Promotion Cell (SITPC). The policy aims to foster a robust startup ecosystem in Goa by providing financial, infrastructural, and regulatory support to eligible startups. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis year-round via the portal: **https://startup.goa.gov.in**. The policy was last updated in 2025 and remains active with no fixed deadline.

### Objectives
The policy’s objectives are exhaustive and multi-faceted:
- To make Goa an aspirational geographical and human resources base for high value start-ups.
- To invite the best entrepreneurial minds to make Goa their professional base, thereby building a robust start-up ecosystem.
- To provide assistance to Goan entrepreneurs and local start-ups.
- To endeavour to create at least 100 successful start-ups in Goa in the next 5 years, targeting employment generation for at least 5000 Goans.
- To ensure continuous evolution of policy measures and creation of a responsive policy framework for accelerating growth.
- To infuse technology-enabled real-world problem-solving skills into the Goan education system through collaboration between industry, academia, DIY modules in school curriculum, and Massive Online Open Courses (MOOCs) in university curriculum.
- To develop minimum 2,00,000 lakh sq. ft. area for technology innovation and incubation centers in the next 5 years (from 2025 policy).
- To foster 1,000 startups, employ 10,000 locals, facilitate venture funding for 100 startups, and enable government collaboration for at least 50 startups (from 2025 policy).
- To promote diversity and inclusion, support social impact ventures, facilitate critical skill development, enhance competitiveness, streamline administrative procedures, establish a startup fund, and forge stakeholder collaboration (from 2025 policy).

### Application Process
The application process is standardized across schemes with minor variations in document requirements. The universal steps are:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Register on Startup Goa Portal] --> B[Obtain Startup Certificate from SITPC]
    B --> C[Ensure Eligibility for Specific Scheme]
    C --> D[Gather Required Documents (Scheme-Specific)]
    D --> E[Apply for Specific Incentive via Startup Goa Portal]
    E --> F[Submit Application with Documents for SPC Due Diligence]
    F --> G[Await Approval/Rejection by SPC and Panel of Experts]
    G --> H{Approved?}
    H -->|Yes| I[Request Payment via Incentive Application Process]
    H -->|No| J[Address Deficiency or Reapply]
    I --> K[Receive Disbursement from ITG (Disbursing Body)]
    K --> L[Utilize Funds per Scheme Guidelines]
```

**Key Process Details**:
- **Registration**: Mandatory first step via https://startup.goa.gov.in to obtain startup certificate from SITPC.
- **Eligibility Check**: Must verify against specific scheme criteria (e.g., turnover < INR 100 crore, incorporated < 10 years, Private Limited Company/LLP/Partnership Firm).
- **Document Gathering**: Varies by scheme; common items include address proof, cancelled cheque, Aadhaar-linked bank statements, identity proofs, employment contracts, degree certificates, bills/invoices, Goan proof (birth certificate/domicile), HR letters, salary increment letters.
- **Submission**: Apply via Startup Goa portal or designated link; submit documents for SPC due diligence and panel of experts review.
- **Approval Timeline**: Based on similar schemes (e.g., IPR Reimbursement), evaluation and approval/rejection notified within 45 days; disbursement within 60 days of approval.
- **Disbursement**: Handled by ITG (Disbursing Body) after approval; funds released per scheme guidelines.
- **Post-Approval**: Some schemes require quarterly progress reports (financial, milestone, utilization certificates) for continued disbursement.
- **Critical Caveats**:
  - Benefits are not entitlements; SITPC reserves sole right to accept/reject applications.
  - Only expenditure incurred after notification of Goa Start-up Policy 2021 and paid digitally is considered for reimbursement.
  - Bank accounts of directors and/or employees must be linked to Aadhaar.
  - Digital payment is mandatory unless SITPC makes an exception.
  - Certain schemes have annual caps (e.g., 100 startups for Seed Capital, 200 for IPR/trademark, 20 for co-working space subsidy).
  - Local startup definition requires ≥50% equity held by Goans continuously since inception.
  - Fresher definition limited to graduates/postgraduates within 2 years of completion.
  - Salary reimbursement requires employment period of at least 12 months.
