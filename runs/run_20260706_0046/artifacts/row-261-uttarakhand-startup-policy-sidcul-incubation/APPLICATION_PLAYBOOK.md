## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Business Registration  
- PAN of the entity or promoters  
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)  
- ITR of the entity or promoters (last 2 years, if available)  
- Passport-sized photographs of promoters  
- Address proof of business premises  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Business proposal or pitch deck (detailing innovation, scalability, and sector alignment)  
- Proof of concept or prototype details (if applicable)  
- Team profiles and CVs of founders  
- Bank account details (for fund disbursement)  
- Declaration of compliance with scheme guidelines  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per file)  
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ClientName_Date.pdf` (e.g., `Uttarakhand_Startup_Policy_BusinessProposal_ABC_20240515.pdf`)  
- Certificates must be valid and not expired (especially GST, Udyam, incorporation)  
- Bank statements must be stamped and signed by the bank  
- Declaration must be on company letterhead, signed, and stamped  
- Pitch deck must be in PDF or PPT format, max 20 slides  
- All documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, certified translation required

3. Fill in all mandatory fields marked with * — especially startup registration details, promoter information, and sector selection.
