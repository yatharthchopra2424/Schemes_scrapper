## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the actual financial benefit delivered to the client, ensuring fairness and transparency.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees account for the discretionary nature of financial support under this scheme, which is subject to evaluation and fund availability.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainer models are scaled to match the client’s stage and capacity to pay, enabling broad access across startup lifecycle stages.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Given the rolling application basis and discretionary grant disbursement (dependent on evaluation and fund availability), there is moderate risk in revenue realization. Processing timelines are not fixed, and approvals depend on incubation seat availability and committee review, which may extend the revenue lag beyond standard assumptions.
