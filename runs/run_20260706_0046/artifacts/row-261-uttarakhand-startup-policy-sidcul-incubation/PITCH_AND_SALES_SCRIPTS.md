## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping startups and entrepreneurs in Uttarakhand access government-backed incubation and funding support through the Uttarakhand Startup Policy & SIDCUL Incubation scheme. Many founders in tourism, agriculture, handicrafts, and IT tell me they’re building innovative solutions but struggle with infrastructure, mentorship, and early-stage funding. I’d like to understand your venture’s current stage and goals to see if this scheme could be a fit — no obligation, just a diagnostic conversation.

### Discovery Questions
- When was your startup incorporated or registered, and is it currently based in Uttarakhand or planning to establish operations there?
- Which sector does your venture operate in — tourism, agriculture, handicrafts, IT, or another innovative field?
- Do you have a working prototype, proof of concept, or a detailed business plan outlining your innovation and scalability?
- Are you currently seeking infrastructure support, mentorship, or assistance with regulatory approvals and funding access?
- What milestones are you aiming to achieve in the next 6–12 months (e.g., product launch, market testing, team expansion)?
- Have you previously applied for any state or central government startup schemes or incubation programmes?
- Are you open to locating your operations at a SIDCUL incubation center if selected?
- What kind of support do you feel is most critical right now — space, guidance, funding, or networking?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup is registered in Uttarakhand or willing to establish operations in the state  
- [ ] Focus on innovative idea or scalable business model  
- [ ] Operates in priority sectors: tourism, agriculture, handicrafts, or IT  
- [ ] Has a business proposal, pitch deck, or proof of concept  
- [ ] Can provide Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, and founder profiles  
- [ ] Agrees to comply with scheme guidelines and milestone-based reviews  
- [ ] Not currently receiving conflicting incubation support that would disqualify eligibility  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, your venture aligns well with the Uttarakhand Startup Policy & SIDCUL Incubation scheme — particularly if you're in tourism, agriculture, handicrafts, or IT and seeking structured support. This scheme offers incubation at SIDCUL centres, mentorship, and potential financial support for prototype development and scaling. Let me walk you through how it works and what it could mean for your startup.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Early-stage startups in Uttarakhand often face challenges in accessing affordable infrastructure, expert mentorship, and early-stage funding — especially in sectors like tourism, agriculture, handicrafts, and IT. Without structured support, many struggle to validate ideas, scale operations, or navigate regulatory and market entry hurdles.

**Scheme intro:**
The Uttarakhand Startup Policy & SIDCUL Incubation scheme, implemented by the State Infrastructure and Industrial Development Corporation of Uttarakhand Limited (SIDCUL) in coordination with the Department of Industries, Government of Uttarakhand, provides registered startups with access to SIDCUL incubation centers, infrastructure support, mentorship from industry experts, networking opportunities, and assistance in securing funding. Financial support includes seed funding, grants, or subsidies for prototype development, market testing, and scaling, determined case-by-case by the incubation and evaluation committee. Applications are accepted year-round via the portal: https://investuttarakhand.com.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your startup is either registered in Uttarakhand or willing to establish operations there  
- You are focused on an innovative, scalable model in a priority sector (e.g., tourism, agriculture, handicrafts, or IT)  
- You have a business proposal or proof of concept ready for submission  
- You are seeking infrastructure, mentorship, or funding support aligned with the scheme’s offerings
