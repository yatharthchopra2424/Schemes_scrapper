### Uttarakhand Startup Policy & SIDCUL Incubation
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Uttarakhand
- **Administering Body:** State Infrastructure and Industrial Development Corporation of Uttarakhand Limited (SIDCUL), in coordination with the Department of Industries, Government of Uttarakhand
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://investuttarakhand.com
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Startups, entrepreneurs, MSME
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Priority sectors such as tourism, agriculture, handicrafts, and IT
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Startups registered in Uttarakhand or willing to establish operations in the state; preference given to ventures utilizing SIDCUL infrastructure or demonstrating potential for employment generation and regional development

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Seed funding, grants, or subsidies for prototype development, market testing, and scaling (exact quantum determined case-by-case); access to SIDCUL incubation centers with infrastructure support, mentorship, networking, funding assistance, policy facilitation, and subsidies on utilities and infrastructure usage
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit / Reimbursement (based on evaluation and availability of funds)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing (subject to milestone tracking and periodic review)

#### Documents Required
1. Business proposal or pitch deck
2. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
3. PAN of the entity or promoters
4. Proof of concept or prototype details (if applicable)
5. Team profiles and CVs of founders
6. Bank account details
7. Declaration of compliance with scheme guidelines

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Apply through the official Uttarakhand investment portal or designated incubation center
2. Submit a detailed business proposal including innovation, scalability, and sector alignment
3. Undergo screening by the selection committee based on viability and impact
4. Shortlisted applicants are invited for pitch presentation or interview
5. Selected startups are offered incubation space and support services at SIDCUL centers
6. Post-selection, startups undergo mentorship, milestone tracking, and periodic review for continued support
