## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Unit is a **new industrial unit** setting up operations in Jammu and Kashmir **OR** an **existing unit undertaking substantial expansion**
- [ ] Unit is registered under the **Factories Act, 1948** **OR** as a **company/LLP**
- [ ] Unit is engaged in **manufacturing, agro-processing, mining, or services**
- [ ] Unit will commence **commercial production within the stipulated time frame** (within 2 years of approval)
- [ ] Unit adheres to **environmental and labor norms**
- [ ] Unit employs or plans to employ **local domiciled workforce** (preference given; minimum percentage may apply)
- [ ] Unit is located in **Jammu and Kashmir only** (geographic scope)
- [ ] For capital investment subsidy: Investment in plant and machinery must be **new investment** (not second-hand or relocated)
- [ ] Unit has not exceeded the **maximum subsidy ceiling of INR 50 lakhs** per entity under this scheme
- [ ] Unit is an **MSME or large industry** (target beneficiaries include both)
- [ ] Unit has access to **term loan from a bank** (required for bank appraisal note and interest subsidy)
- [ ] Unit has secured **land** (allotment/lease/sale deed required)
- [ ] Unit has obtained **No Objection Certificate from Pollution Control Board**
- [ ] Unit has obtained **clearance from Fire Department**

> **Sources**: Eligibility, Target Beneficiaries, Key Caveats sections from scheme evidence.

- [ ] Details of **Plant and Machinery** (invoice, specification, supplier details)

- [ ] All documents are **legible scans** (minimum 300 DPI if scanned)
- [ ] File names match document type (e.g., "Project_Report.pdf", "Land_Deed.pdf")

- [ ] Affidavit is **on stamp paper of requisite value** and **notarized**
- [ ] Bank Appraisal Note is on **bank’s letterhead**, signed and stamped by authorized officer
- [ ] All documents are in **English or Hindi**; if in another language, certified translation attached
- [ ] Total upload size per document does not exceed portal limit (verify on https://jkindustrialdev.nic.in)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
✅ **Required** – The scheme explicitly lists "Project Report" as a mandatory document.  
**Standard Sections to Include**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Promoter Details and Background  
- Project Location (with map and land details)  
- Description of Manufacturing Process / Services  
- List of Plant and Machinery (with cost, supplier, invoice details)  
- Investment Breakdown (land, building, plant & machinery, miscellaneous fixed assets)  
- Means of Finance (promoter’s contribution, term loan details, unsecured loans if any)  
- Production Capacity (installed and utilization)  
- Employment Plan (total employees, number of local domiciled workers, roles, wages)  
- Marketing and Sales Strategy  
- Project Implementation Timeline (including expected date of commencement of commercial production)  
- Environmental and Safety Compliance Measures  
- Financial Projections (Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow for 5 years)  
- Break-even Analysis  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  

> **Note**: Verify with District Industries Centre (DIC) or nodal officer if any additional project-specific annexures are required.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Access the portal** at https://jkindustrialdev.nic.in and register/create login using unit’s official email and mobile number.  
2. **Select “J&K New Industrial Development Scheme (J&K NIDS)”** from the list of available schemes.  
3. **Fill all fields accurately** – do not leave any mandatory field blank; if not applicable, write “NA” or “Not Applicable” after verifying with DIC.  
4. **Ensure promoter name, unit name, PAN, and GSTIN match exactly** across all documents and the application form.  
5. **Investment in plant and machinery must be clearly stated** – this determines eligibility for capital investment subsidy (up to 30%, capped at INR 50 lakhs).  
6. **Employment plan must specify number of local domiciled workers** – this is critical for employment generation subsidy (INR 15,000 per annum per employee for 3 years).  
7. **Upload documents in the correct format** (PDF/JPEG as specified) and ensure file sizes are within portal limits.  
8. **Review the entire application** before final submission – no edits are allowed after submission; save a draft first.  
9. **Declare compliance with environmental, labor, and scheme norms** in the affidavit section – misrepresentation leads to rejection and recovery.  
10. **Take a screenshot of the confirmation page** after submission and note the application/reference number.
