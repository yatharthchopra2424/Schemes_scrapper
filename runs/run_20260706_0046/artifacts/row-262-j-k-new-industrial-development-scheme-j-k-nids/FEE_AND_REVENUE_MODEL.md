## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial benefit available under J&K NIDS (up to ₹50 lakhs capital investment subsidy plus additional incentives), ensuring consultant compensation aligns with client outcomes.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees account for scheme-specific risks including the 2-year commercial production commencement requirement, fund availability constraints, and multi-stage approval process (DIC scrutiny → Empowered Committee evaluation).
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainer models are scaled to client capacity, with MSMEs (primary beneficiaries) accessing Growth tier pricing that matches their typical investment scale and subsidy eligibility.
4. **Outcome-Focused**: Success fees are contingent on verified subsidy disbursement post-commencement of commercial production, reducing client risk and aligning incentives with successful scheme utilization.
