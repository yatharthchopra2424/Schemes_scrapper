## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping industrial units in Jammu and Kashmir access government incentives designed to reduce capital costs and support sustainable growth. The J&K New Industrial Development Scheme (J&K NIDS) offers substantial subsidies—up to INR 50 lakhs—for new and expanding units in manufacturing, agro-processing, mining, and services. I’d like to understand your current plans to see if this could support your investment in plant and machinery, employment generation, or operational efficiency.

### Discovery Questions
- When was your unit incorporated or registered under the Factories Act, 1948, or as a company/LLP?
- Are you setting up a new industrial unit in Jammu and Kashmir, or undertaking a substantial expansion of an existing unit?
- What is the nature of your industrial activity—manufacturing, agro-processing, mining, or services?
- Have you commenced or do you plan to commence commercial production within the next two years?
- Are you investing in new plant and machinery as part of your project?
- Do you employ, or plan to employ, local domiciled workers from Jammu and Kashmir?
- Is your unit located in an identified industrially backward block of Jammu and Kashmir?
- Are you compliant with environmental and labor norms, and able to obtain clearances from the Pollution Control Board and Fire Department?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Industrial units in Jammu and Kashmir face high upfront capital costs, financing burdens, and operational expenses—particularly in power, GST, and employment—which can delay or deter investment in plant and machinery and expansion plans.

**Scheme intro:**
The J&K New Industrial Development Scheme (J&K NIDS), administered by the Industries and Commerce Department, Government of Jammu and Kashmir, provides a capital investment subsidy of up to 30% of investment in plant and machinery, capped at INR 50 lakhs per entity. Additional benefits include a 6% per annum interest subsidy on term loans for 5 years, GST reimbursement for 5 years, 50% power tariff concession for 5 years, and INR 15,000 per annum per employee for local domiciled workers over 3 years.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your unit is registered under the Factories Act, 1948 or as a company/LLP and engaged in an eligible sector (manufacturing/agro-processing/mining/services)  
- You are setting up a new unit or undertaking substantial expansion in Jammu and Kashmir  
- You plan to invest in new plant and machinery and commence commercial production within two years  
- You employ or intend to employ local domiciled workers from Jammu and Kashmir
