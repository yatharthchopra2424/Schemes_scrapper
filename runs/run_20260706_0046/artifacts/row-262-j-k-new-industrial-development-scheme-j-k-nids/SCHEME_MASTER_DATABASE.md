### J&K New Industrial Development Scheme (J&K NIDS)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Jammu and Kashmir only
- **Administering Body:** Industries and Commerce Department, Government of Jammu and Kashmir
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://jkindustrialdev.nic.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: New industrial units setting up operations in Jammu and Kashmir or existing units undertaking substantial expansion
- Age of business: Must be registered under the Factories Act, 1948 or as a company/LLP
- Sector: Manufacturing, agro-processing, mining, or services
- Revenue cap: Not specified in evidence
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: Must commence commercial production within the stipulated time frame; adhere to environmental and labor norms; preference given to units in identified industrially backward blocks and those employing local workforce

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - Capital investment subsidy: Up to 30% of investment in plant and machinery, subject to a ceiling of INR 50 lakhs
  - Interest subsidy: 6% per annum on term loans for a period of 5 years
  - GST reimbursement: Available for a period of 5 years from the date of commencement of commercial production
  - Employment generation subsidy: INR 15,000000 per annum per employee for a period of 3 years for local domiciled workers
  - Power tariff concession: 50% for a period of 5 years
  - Additional benefits: Stamp duty exemption, land cost subsidy, subsidy on quality certification, support for skill development, infrastructure development, and single-window clearance
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement (based on submission of proof of investment and production after commencement of commercial production)
- Duration of benefit: Varies by component (e.g., interest subsidy and power tariff concession for 5 years; GST reimbursement for 5 years; employment generation subsidy for 3 years; capital investment subsidy is one-time upon verification)

#### Documents Required
1. Project Report
2. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
3. Memorandum and Articles of Association / Partnership Deed
4. Land Allotment / Lease / Sale Deed
5. Bank Appraisal Note on Term Loan
6. No Objection Certificate from Pollution Control Board
7. Clearance from Fire Department
8. Details of Plant and Machinery
9. Employment Plan with local workforce details
10. Affidavit regarding compliance with scheme norms

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Submit application online through the single-window portal of the Jammu and Kashmir Industries and Commerce Department.
2. Upload required documents including project report, incorporation certificates, land documents, and bank appraisal note.
3. Application is scrutinized by the District Industries Centre (DIC) for preliminary verification.
4. Forwarded to the Empowered Committee for evaluation and approval.
5. Upon approval, letter of intent is issued.
6. After commencement of commercial production, final disbursement of subsidies is made upon submission of proof of investment and production.
