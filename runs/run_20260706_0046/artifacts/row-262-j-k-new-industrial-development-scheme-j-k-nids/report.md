## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **J&K New Industrial Development Scheme (J&K NIDS)** is a state-level subsidy scheme administered by the **Industries and Commerce Department, Government of Jammu and Kashmir**. It is designed to promote industrial investment, generate employment, and support industrial development exclusively within the Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir. The scheme operates on a rolling basis with applications accepted throughout the year, subject to fund availability, and is accessible via the single-window portal: [https://jkindustrialdev.nic.in](https://jkindustrialdev.nic.in).

### Objectives
The scheme aims to:
- Promote industrial investment in Jammu and Kashmir  
- Generate employment opportunities for local youth  
- Develop infrastructure and support services for industries  
- Encourage setting up of new industrial units in the UT  
- Support expansion and modernization of existing industrial units  
- Boost export-oriented and import-substituting industries  
- Develop thrust sectors including manufacturing, agro-processing, and tourism
