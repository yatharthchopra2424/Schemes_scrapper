## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a resident of Jammu and Kashmir (proof of residence required for all founders/directors)  
- [ ] Startup is registered as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm under relevant Indian laws  
- [ ] Venture is innovative, scalable, and has a clear business model (to be validated via pitch deck/business plan)  
- [ ] Preference given to ventures led by women, SC/ST, or differently abled individuals (verify founder demographics)  
- [ ] Startup has not received more than INR 25 lakhs in funding from other government sources prior to application  
- [ ] Startup is engaged in prototype development, product testing, or initial market traction (aligned with grant usage)  
- [ ] Business operates within Jammu and Kashmir (geographic scope compliance)  
- [ ] Startup is in early stage (implicit from focus on prototype development and initial traction)

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration  
- PAN card of the startup  
- ITR of the startup (for last 2 financial years, if applicable)  
- GST registration certificate (if applicable)  
- Bank statements of the startup (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration Certificate (MSME)  
- Aadhaar cards of all founders/directors  
- Authorized signatory’s PAN and Aadhaar  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed pitch deck or business plan (highlighting innovation, scalability, and business model)  
- Proof of residence of founders in Jammu and Kashmir (e.g., domicile certificate, voter ID, Aadhaar with J&K address, or utility bill)
- Declaration of no prior government funding exceeding INR 25 lakhs (self-declaration on company letterhead)
- Authorization letter from authorized signatory (for application submission and grant acceptance)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be clear, legible scans (PDF format preferred)
- File size per document: maximum 5 MB
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ApplicantName_Date.pdf` (e.g., `StartupJK_PitchDeck_ABC_PvtLtd_20240515.pdf`)
- Aadhaar and PAN details must match across documents
- Declaration and authorization letter must be on company letterhead, signed, and stamped
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, provide certified translation
- Pitch deck must include problem statement, solution, market size, business model, financial projections, and team details

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not explicitly required. The scheme mandates a **detailed pitch deck or business plan** as part of the application. A formal DPR is not mentioned in the evidence.  
→ **Verify with nodal officer** if a detailed project report is expected alongside the pitch deck, especially for fund utilization planning.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the portal at https://jkstartupsummit.in and register as a new user using valid email and mobile number.  
2. Log in and navigate to the “Apply Now” section for the StartUp J&K Initiative.  
3. Fill in all startup details accurately: legal name, registration number, date of incorporation, registered address, and contact information.  
4. Provide complete founder/director details including names, Aadhaar numbers, PAN, and residential addresses in Jammu and Kashmir.  
5. Describe the innovation, scalability, and business model clearly in the designated fields — align with pitch deck content.  
6. Upload financial projections (revenue, expenditure) for the next 12–18 months as part of the business plan section.  
7. Declare that the startup has not received more than INR 25 lakhs from other government sources — attach the signed declaration.  
8. Upload all scheme-specific and universal documents in the prescribed format and size limits.  
9. Review all entries before final submission — no edits allowed after submission.  
10. Submit the application and save/download the acknowledgment receipt with application reference number.  

> **Portal-Specific Note**: The application process involves a preliminary screening followed by a pitch presentation for shortlisted applicants (per Application Process step 5–6). Ensure the pitch deck is presentation-ready.
