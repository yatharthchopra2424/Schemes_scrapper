## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual grant amount (up to ₹10 lakhs) to ensure client profitability after consulting costs.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s biannual application cycles and milestone-based disbursement, success fees reflect the moderate risk of delayed or partial grant release.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Starter and Growth tiers are designed for early-stage startups in J&K, aligning with the scheme’s focus on innovative, scalable ventures with limited prior funding.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover application preparation, while success fees are contingent on net offtaken only upon verified grant sanction.
