## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping startups in Jammu and Kashmir access government grants for early-stage development. The StartUp J&K Initiative offers up to INR 10 lakhs in non-dilutive funding for prototype development and market traction — specifically designed for innovative ventures from the region. I’d like to understand your startup’s current stage and see if this could be a fit.

### Discovery Questions
- When was your startup incorporated or registered as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm?
- Are all founders/directors residents of Jammu and Kashmir, and do you have proof of residence (e.g., Aadhaar with J&K address)?
- What stage is your venture at — are you working on prototype development, product testing, or seeking initial market traction?
- Has your startup received more than INR 25 lakhs in funding from other government sources to date?
- Is your venture innovative, scalable, and based on a clear business model — particularly in sectors like tourism, handicrafts, agriculture, or IT?
- Are you open to participating in mentorship sessions and submitting progress reports if granted funding?
- Do you have a detailed pitch deck or business plan ready for submission?
- Are you interested in non-equity grant funding that does not require giving up ownership?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup is incorporated as Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm under Indian laws  
- [ ] All founders/directors are residents of Jammu and Kashmir  
- [ ] Startup is innovative, scalable, and has a clear business model  
- [ ] Has not received more than INR 25 lakhs in prior government funding  
- [ ] Focused on prototype development, product testing, or initial market traction  
- [ ] Willing to comply with milestone-based disbursement and reporting requirements  
- [ ] Able to provide Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, Aadhaar of founders, pitch deck, bank details, and residence proof  
- [ ] Preference given if led by women, SC/ST, or differently abled individuals (not mandatory but noted)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like your startup aligns well with the StartUp J&K Initiative’s criteria — particularly being a resident-led, early-stage venture seeking support for prototype or market entry. This scheme provides up to INR 10 lakhs as a grant, with no equity taken, and includes mentorship and incubator access. May I share how it works and how we’ve helped similar startups secure approval?

**Scheme intro:**
