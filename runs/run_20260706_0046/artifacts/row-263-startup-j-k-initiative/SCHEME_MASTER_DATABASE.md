### StartUp J&K Initiative
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Jammu and Kashmir only
- **Administering Body:** Department of Information Technology, Government of Jammu and Kashmir
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://jkstartupsummit.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Applications are accepted twice a year — typically in April and October. Exact dates are announced on the portal each cycle.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Innovative, scalable ventures with a clear business model (preference for tourism, handicrafts, agriculture, IT)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: 
  - Applicants must be residents of Jammu and Kashmir
  - Preference given to ventures led by women, SC/ST, or differently abled individuals
  - Startup must not have received more than INR 25 lakhs in funding from other government sources prior to application

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to INR 10 lakhs per eligible startup
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit in tranches based on milestone achievement
- Duration of benefit: One-time grant

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN card of the startup
3. Aadhaar cards of all founders/directors
4. Detailed pitch deck or business plan
5. Bank account details of the startup
6. Proof of residence of founders in Jammu and Kashmir
7. Declaration of no prior government funding exceeding INR 25 lakhs
8. Authorization letter from authorized signatory

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the official StartUp J&K portal at https://jkstartupsummit.in
2. Register as a new user by providing basic details and creating a login
3. Log in and fill out the online application form with startup details, business model, and financial projections
4. Upload required documents including Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, Aadhaar of founders, and a detailed pitch deck
5. Submit the application and await preliminary screening
6. Shortlisted applicants will be invited for a pitch presentation before the selection committee
7. Final selection will be based on innovation, feasibility, scalability, and team strength
8. Approved startups will receive a sanction letter and grant disbursement will begin post-agreement signing
