# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is a Second Level Fund Manager (SLFM) interested in partnering with the Research, Development, and Innovation (RDI) Fund.
- [ ] The applicant is an Alternate Investment Fund (AIF), Development Finance Institution (DFI), Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC), or Focused Research Organization (FRO) such as Technology Development Board (TDB), Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council (BIRAC), IIT Research Parks, or similar entities.
- [ ] Existing NBFCs planning to establish AIFs are eligible to apply.
- [ ] The applicant must express interest by writing to Dr Jyoti Sharma, Head, RDI Cell at sharma[dot]jyoti[at]nic[dot]in.
- [ ] Industry stakeholders seeking funding for projects under the RDI scheme are NOT eligible to apply directly to DST; all disbursements are routed exclusively through designated Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs).

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate
- PAN Card
- Audited Financial Statements (ITR for last 3 years)
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Expression of Interest letter addressed to Dr Jyoti Sharma, Head, RDI Cell (sharma[dot]jyoti[at]nic[dot]in)
- Details of the applicant’s fund structure and investment strategy
- Track record of managing alternative investment funds or similar financial instruments
- Proposed plan for contributing to the Deep-Tech Fund of Funds
- Any existing partnerships or MOUs with potential project sponsors or investees

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format, and clearly named (e.g., "Expression_of_Interest_TIH.pdf")
- File size for each document should not exceed 5 MB
- Names on documents must match exactly with the applicant’s legal name
- Documents must be self-attested or certified by a authorized signatory
- All documents must be in English or accompanied by a certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for Expression of Interest (EOI) stage. A detailed project report will be required only after selection as an SLFM and when submitting project proposals through the SLFM route. Verify with the nodal officer if a DPR is needed at a later stage.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
Since this scheme uses an Expression of Interest (EOI) process via email (not a portal-based form), follow these protocols:  
1. Address the EOI letter formally to Dr Jyoti Sharma, Head, RDI Cell, DST.  
2. Clearly state the applicant’s interest in becoming a Second-Level Fund Manager (SLFM) for the RDI Fund.  
3. Include legal name, registration details, and contact information of the applicant entity.  
4. Specify the type of entity (AIF, DFI, NBFC, FRO, etc.) and regulatory registrations (e.g., SEBI, RBI).  
5. Outline the fund’s investment focus, especially alignment with deep-tech and strategic technologies (quantum computing, AI, biotech, etc.).  
6. Mention any prior experience in managing funds for innovation, R&D, or technology development.  
7. Confirm willingness to contribute to the Deep-Tech Fund of Funds as per scheme guidelines.  
8. Attach all supporting documents as per the Document Collection section.  
9. Self-declare that the applicant understands that industry stakeholders must route proposals through SLFMs and will not approach DST directly.  
10. Sign and date the letter with an authorized signatory’s name, designation, and seal (if applicable).

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] EOI letter is addressed to the correct official (Dr Jyoti Sharma, Head, RDI Cell)  
- [ ] All applicant details (name, registration, contact) are accurate and complete  
- [ ] Entity type and regulatory status are clearly specified  
- [ ] Investment strategy aligns with RDI Scheme objectives (deep-tech, strategic technologies, Atmanirbhar Bharat)  
- [ ] Track record and fund management experience are documented  
- [ ] Willingness to participate in Deep-Tech Fund of Funds is explicitly stated  
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents are attached and properly labeled  
- [ ] Letter is signed, dated, and stamped (if applicable)  
- [ ] No direct project proposals or funding requests are included in the EOI  
- [ ] File names, sizes, and formats meet quality check standards

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Save a copy of the sent email as PDF with timestamp (e.g., "EOI_Submission_TIH_[Date].pdf")  
- Store the sent email in the case folder with BCC to internal team  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days after submission if no acknowledgment is received  
- Note: No application reference number is generated at EOI stage; tracking is via email correspondence

---

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive formal acknowledgment or approval email from DST RDI Cell confirming acceptance as an SLFM  
- [ ] Archive the approval communication with case number/reference (if provided)  
- [ ] Prepare for next steps: await separate advertisement for project proposals from SLFMs  
- [ ] Inform client that approved SLFMs will be invited to submit project proposals via a future notification  
- [ ] Utilization of funds will be monitored at the SLFM level; DST does not disburse directly to end beneficiaries  
- [ ] Success fee invoicing to be discussed and agreed upon with the client post-approval, based on milestones achieved
