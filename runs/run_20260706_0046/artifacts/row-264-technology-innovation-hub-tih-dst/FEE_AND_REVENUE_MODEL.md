## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-based pricing**: Fees are aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client, leveraging the scheme’s high-value support (up to 50% of project cost for transformative RDI projects at TRL 4+).
2. **Risk-adjusted pricing**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile — long approval timelines (evidence indicates multi-stage review via Empowered Group of Secretaries and Second-Level Fund Managers) but high success potential due to clear eligibility and structured disbursement via SLFMs.
3. **Market-competitive rates**: Tiered retainer and success fee bands are benchmarked against standard consulting practices for government innovation schemes in India.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: The RDI Scheme’s financing model (long-term loans at low/nil interest, equity infusion) reduces repayment risk for clients, increasing project viability and consultant success likelihood — justifying mid-to-high success fee ranges for Scale and Enterprise tiers.
