#### Risks & Blockers
- Projects must be at Technology Readiness Level (TRL) 4 or above to qualify for financing; lower TRL projects may be rejected.
- Financing is routed exclusively through Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs); direct applications to DST are not entertained, causing delays if applicants approach DST directly.
- The scheme excludes grants and short-term loans; only long-term loans at low/nil interest or equity infusion are available, which may not suit all applicants' financial structures.
