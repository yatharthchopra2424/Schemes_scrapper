# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping deep-tech innovators and R&D-focused businesses access government funding for strategic technology development. The Technology Innovation Hub (TIH) – DST scheme is designed to catalyze private sector participation in high-impact R&D, particularly in mission-critical areas like quantum computing, AI, biotechnology, and clean energy. I’d like to understand your current R&D initiatives to see if this ₹1 lakh crore initiative aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is the primary focus of your current R&D projects—are they in strategic technologies such as quantum computing, robotics, AI applications in agriculture/health, or biomanufacturing?
2. What is the Technology Readiness Level (TRL) of your most advanced project—are you at TRL 4 or above, where the scheme provides up to 50% financing?
3. Are you seeking long-term financing at low or nil interest rates, or equity infusion options for scaling your deep-tech innovation?
4. Is your organization a private sector entity looking to strengthen India’s technological self-reliance in areas like energy transition or digital economy?
5. Do you plan to collaborate with Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs) such as TDB, BIRAC, or IIT Research Parks for fund disbursement?
6. Are you open to contributing to a Deep-Tech Fund of Funds as part of the financing structure?
7. What is the assessed cost of your transformative RDI project, and are you seeking coverage for up to 50% of it under this scheme?
8. Have you reviewed the exclusion criteria—grants and short-term loans are not supported under this scheme?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Private sector entity seeking R&D funding  
- [ ] Project focuses on strategic technologies (energy security, quantum computing, AI, biotech, digital economy, etc.)  
- [ ] Technology Readiness Level (TRL) 4 or above  
- [ ] Seeking long-term loan at low/nil interest rates or equity infusion  
- [ ] Willing to work through Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs)  
- [ ] Project cost assessment available for financing up to 50%  
- [ ] Not seeking grants or short-term loans (excluded)  
- [ ] Aligns with Atmanirbhar Bharat and national self-reliance vision  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, your work in [specific technology area from discovery] at TRL [level] appears to align closely with the Technology Innovation Hub (TIH) – DST scheme, which offers up to 50% financing for transformative RDI projects through long-term loans or equity support. Let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Deep-tech innovators in India often struggle to access patient, long-term capital for high-risk R&D projects, especially when grants are unavailable and traditional financing is too costly or short-term. This limits progress in strategic sectors critical to national self-reliance and global competitiveness.

**Scheme intro:**
The Technology Innovation Hub (TIH) – DST scheme, launched by the Department of Science & Technology (DST) and approved by the Union Cabinet on July 1, 2025, is a transformative initiative with a total outlay of ₹1 lakh crore. It provides long-term loans at low or nil interest rates and equity infusion (especially for startups) to finance up to 50% of assessed project costs for transformative RDI projects at Technology Readiness Levels (TRLs) 4 and above. Grants and short-term loans are explicitly excluded.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your project focuses on [specific strategic tech from discovery, e.g., quantum computing/AI in health], which is a mission-critical area under the scheme  
- Your technology is at TRL [level from discovery], meeting the minimum TRL 4 requirement for financing  
- You are seeking long-term, low-cost capital—precisely what the scheme offers via loans or equity  
- You intend to work through authorized SLFMs like TDB or BIRAC, as required for disbursement
