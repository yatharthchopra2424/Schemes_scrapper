### Research, Development, and Innovation (RDI) Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Science & Technology (DST)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://rdifund.anrf.gov.in/
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Private sector entities, startups, industry players
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Strategic technologies including energy security and transition, climate action, deep technologies (quantum computing, robotics, space), AI applications to Indian problems (agriculture, health, education), biotechnology, biomanufacturing, synthetic biology, pharma, medical devices, digital economy including digital agriculture
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Projects must be transformative RDI at Technology Readiness Levels (TRLs) 4 and above; exceptions may be approved by the Empowered Group of Secretaries (EGoS)

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financing can cover up to 50% of assessed project cost for transformative RDI projects
- Disbursement mode: Long-term loan at low or nil interest rates; Equity infusion, especially in case of startups; Contributions to Deep-Tech Fund of Funds
- Duration of benefit: Not specified (implied project-based)

#### Documents Required
1. Expression of interest letter from Second Level Fund Managers (SLFMs)
2. Project proposal detailing RDI objectives and alignment with strategic technologies
3. Financial plan showing utilization of funds
4. Technology Readiness Level (TRL) assessment
5. Details of private sector participation
6. Compliance with Atmanirbhar Bharat vision
7. Sectoral justification for public interest
8. Any additional documentation as required by Empowered Group of Secretaries (EGoS)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Interested Second Level Fund Managers (SLFMs) express interest in partnering with the fund by writing to Dr Jyoti Sharma, Head, RDI Cell at sharma[dot]jyoti[at]nic[dot]in
2. Existing NBFCs planning to establish AIFs contact DST regarding potential participation
3. DST evaluates expressions of interest and selects SLFMs
4. Selected SLFMs receive project proposals from industry stakeholders
5. SLFMs conduct due diligence on projects
6. SLFMs approve and disburse funds to eligible projects
7. Disbursements are routed exclusively through designated SLFMs
8. Industry stakeholders do not send proposals directly to DST
9. Separate advertisements are issued for project calls
10. Funds are disbursed as per approved project milestones
