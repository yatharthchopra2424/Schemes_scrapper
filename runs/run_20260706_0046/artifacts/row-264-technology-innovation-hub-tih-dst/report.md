# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
The **Technology Innovation Hub (TIH) – DST** (Scheme ID: row-264) is a scheme under the **Deep Tech & Innovation** category, administered by the **Department of Science & Technology (DST)**. The scheme is classified as "other" type. The last update date is 1970-01-01, and the confidence level in the extracted data is low.

### Scheme Objectives
Based on the supporting evidence, particularly from the RDI Cell page (https://dst.gov.in/rdi-scheme/research-development-and-innovation-rdi-cell), the objectives of the scheme (which aligns with the broader RDI Scheme under which TIH may operate) are:

- **Encouraging Private Sector Involvement**: Incentivize active participation from industry players in cutting-edge research and innovation.
- **Promoting Strategic Technologies**: Focus on mission-critical areas vital for national development and global competitiveness, including:
  - Energy security and transition, and climate action
  - 'Deep technologies' including quantum computing, robotics and space
  - Artificial intelligence and its applications to Indian problems including in agriculture, health, and education
  - Biotechnology, biomanufacturing, synthetic biology, pharma, and medical devices
  - Digital economy including digital agriculture
- **Fostering Self-Reliance and Economic Security**: Support technologies where indigenization is essential for strategic or economic reasons, in line with the vision of Atmanirbharta.
- **Ensure Flexibility in Sectoral Support**: Allow support for any additional sectors or technologies deemed essential for public interest.

### Eligibility Matrix
The eligibility criteria are derived from the RDI Scheme documentation. The scheme targets **private sector entities** for high-impact R&D projects. Specific eligibility details include:

| Eligibility Criteria | Details | Source |
|----------------------|---------|--------|
| **Applicant Type** | Industry players, private sector entities | RDI Cell page: "Encouraging Private Sector Involvement: Incentivize active participation from industry players in cutting-edge research and innovation." |
| **Project Focus** | Transformative R&D projects in strategic technologies | RDI Cell page: Focus areas listed under "Promoting Strategic Technologies" |
| **Technology Readiness Level (TRL)** | TRL 4 and above for standard support; exceptions possible for lower TRL via Empowered Group of Secretaries (EGoS) | RDI Cell page: "Financing can cover up to 50% of assessed project cost for transformative RDI projects at Technology Readiness Levels (TRLs) 4 and above; exceptions may be approved by the Empowered Group of Secretaries (EGoS)" |
| **Geographic Scope** | Projects must be based in India | Implicit from DST's mandate and national focus (Atmanirbhar Bharat vision) |
| **Exclusions** | Grants and short-term loans are not supported | RDI Cell page: "Exclusions: Grants and short-term loans are not supported" |
| **Funding Routing** | Industry stakeholders must not send proposals directly to DST; funding routed through Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs) | RDI Cell page: "Note: Industry stakeholders seeking funding for projects under the RDI scheme are requested not to send proposals or emails directly to DST. All disbursements will be routed exclusively through the designated Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs) in due course through separate advertisement." |

> **Note**: The scheme does not appear to have a public-facing application portal for direct applicant submission. All applications are channeled through SLFMs. The portal for SLFM participation is https://rdifund.anrf.gov.in/.

Financial support details are explicitly stated in the RDI Scheme documentation:

| Benefit Type | Details | Source |

| **Modes of Financing** | Long-term loan at low or nil interest rates; Equity infusion (especially for startups); Contributions to Deep-Tech Fund of Funds | RDI Cell page: "Modes of Financing: Long-term loan at low or nil interest rates. ### Application Process
The application process is indirect: applicants do not apply directly to DST. Instead, they must engage with Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs). The process for SLFMs to join is outlined, and project disbursements flow through them.

```mermaid

    A[Industry Entity with R&D Project] --> B{Project Meets Criteria?<br/>- Strategic Tech Focus<br/>- TRL ≥ 4<br/>- Private Sector Led}
    B -->|Yes| C[Engage with SLFM<br/>(AIF, DFI, NBFC, FRO)]
    B -->|No| D[Project Not Eligible]
    C --> E[SLFM Appraises Project<br/>- Technical Due Diligence<br/>- Financial Viability]
    E --> F{SLFM Approves?}
    F -->|Yes| G[SLFM Structures Financing<br/>- Long-term Loan (Low/Nil Interest)<br/>- Equity Infusion (Startups)<br/>- Contribution to Deep-Tech FoF]
    F -->|No| H[Project Rejected by SLFM]
    G --> I[Disbursement via SLFM<br/>Up to 50% of Project Cost]
    I --> J[Project Execution & Monitoring]
    J --> K[Repayment/Returns per SLFM Terms]
```

### Key Takeaways and Warnings
> **Blockquote**: The scheme does not accept direct applications from industry. All proposals must be routed through empaneled Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs). Attempting to submit directly to DST will result in rejection.
>
> **Blockquote**: The total outlay is ₹1 lakh crore, but disbursements are contingent on SLFM onboarding and project appraisal. Funding is not guaranteed upon eligibility; it depends on SLFM capacity and deal terms.
>
> **Blockquote**: While the scheme covers up to 50% of project cost, the actual percentage may vary based on SLFM assessment and EGoS approval for exceptions. Startups may receive equity infusion instead of or alongside loans.
