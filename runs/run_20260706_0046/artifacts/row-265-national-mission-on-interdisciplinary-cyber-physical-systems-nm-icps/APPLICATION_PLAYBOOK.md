# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Entity is engaged in research, development, and innovation (RDI) in strategic technology areas such as energy security, climate action, deep technologies (quantum computing, robotics, space), AI applications in agriculture, health, education, biotechnology, biomanufacturing, synthetic biology, pharma, medical devices, and digital economy including digital agriculture.
- [ ] Entity is developing RDI-intensive technology at Technology Readiness Level (TRL) 4 and above.
- [ ] Entity falls under one of the target beneficiary categories: startups, MSMEs, research institutions, academia, or industry players.
- [ ] Entity is based in India (Pan-India geographic scope).
- [ ] Entity is not applying directly to DST; proposals must be routed through designated Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs).
- [ ] Project cost assessment allows financing up to 50% of assessed project cost for transformative RDI projects at TRL 4 and above (exceptions possible via Empowered Group of Secretaries approval).
- [ ] Entity understands that grants and short-term loans are not supported; financing is via long-term loans at low or nil interest rates, equity infusion (especially for startups), or contributions to the Deep-Tech Fund of Funds.

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation certificate
- PAN card
- Audited financial statements (ITR for last 3 years)
- GST registration certificate
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)
- MoA and AoA (for companies)
- Board resolution authorizing application
- KYC documents of authorized signatory

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining R&D objectives, methodology, budget, timeline, and expected outcomes
- Technology Readiness Level (TRL) assessment report (certified by recognized agency or self-assessment with justification)
- Intellectual Property (IP) details (if any): patents filed/granted, copyrights, trade secrets
- Letters of collaboration or MoUs with academic/research institutions (if applicable)
- Utilization plan for funds (aligned with SLFM disbursement mechanism)
- Declaration that entity will not send proposals directly to DST (as per scheme guidelines)
- For startups: pitch deck, product prototype/demo video, customer validation evidence
- For research institutions/academia: faculty credentials, lab infrastructure details, past project records

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size 10 MB per document)
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_EntityName_DocumentType_Date.pdf` (e.g., `NM-ICPS_ABCStartup_DPR_20250405.pdf`)
- All certificates must be valid and not expired
- Documents in regional languages must be accompanied by certified English/Hindi translation
- Financial documents must be audited by a Chartered Accountant (CA) or statutory auditor
- Declarations must be signed and stamped (where applicable)
- Bank statements must show consistent transactions and be from a scheduled commercial bank

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Required.** The DPR must include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Problem Statement and Innovation Proposition  
- Technology Readiness Level (TRL) justification (current and target TRL)  
- Detailed R&D methodology and work plan  
- Project timeline with milestones (Gantt chart preferred)  
- Budget breakdown (capital, operational, manpower, contingency)  
- Expected outcomes: technical, commercial, strategic (e.g., import substitution, job creation, IP generation)  
- Risk assessment and mitigation plan  
- Sustainability and scalability plan  
- Team credentials and institutional capabilities  
- Alignment with NM-ICPS objectives: promoting strategic technologies, fostering self-reliance, encouraging private sector participation in high-impact R&D  
- Utilization plan showing how funds will be used via SLFM  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Do not apply directly to DST** – all applications must go through an SLFM.  
2. Confirm SLFM eligibility and interest via email to Dr. Jyoti Sharma (sharma[dot]jyoti[at]nic[dot]in) before proceeding.  
3. Fill the application form **only via the SLFM’s portal or process** – DST portal (https://rdifund.anrf.gov.in/) is for information only.  
4. Ensure all fields are completed; leave no blank sections unless marked optional.  
5. Attach all scheme-specific and universal documents as per checklist.  
6. Declarations must be signed by the authorized signatory (Director/CEO/PI) and dated.  
7. Double-check financial numbers for consistency across documents (DPR, bank statements, ITR).  
8. Submit a copy of the SLFM acknowledgment/receipt as proof of engagement.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All eligibility criteria verified and documented  
- [ ] DPR completed and reviewed for technical and financial soundness  
- [ ] All universal documents collected and validated

- [ ] Declarations signed and stamped  
- [ ] File naming convention followed  
- [ ] Copy of SLFM engagement proof attached  
- [ ] Internal compliance sign-off by senior consultant

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Obtain submission acknowledgment from the SLFM (email or portal receipt)  
- Screenshot and store the SLFM reference number/application ID  
- Record submission date and expected response timeline (per SLFM SLA)  
- Set follow-up reminders:  
  - Initial follow-up: 7 days post-submission  
  - Second follow-up: 14 days post-submission  
  - Escalation trigger: 21 days with no response

- **Level 2:** Nodal officer – Dr. Jyoti Sharma, Head, RDI Cell, DST – sharma[dot]jyoti[at]nic[dot]in

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify approval letter from SLFM (not DST directly)  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount and disbursement schedule (tranche-based, Just-In-Time via TSA)  
- [ ] Ensure utilization of funds begins only after SLFM disbursement  
- [ ] Maintain records of all expenditures as per SLFM and DST audit requirements  
- [ ] Prepare Utilization Certificate (UC) as per SLFM format and timeline  
- [ ] Submit progress reports as required by SLFM (quarterly or milestone-based)  
- [ ] Coordinate with SLFM for any audit or site visit  
- [ ] Upon project completion, submit final UC and impact report  
- [ ] Follow up on success fee invoice (if applicable per consultancy agreement)  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with 50-year fund return clause to Consolidated Fund of India (CFI) – note for long-term tracking
