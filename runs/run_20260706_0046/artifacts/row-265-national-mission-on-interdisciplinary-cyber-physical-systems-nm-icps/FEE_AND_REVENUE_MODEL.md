## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the scheme’s financing structure (long-term loans at low/nil interest, equity infusion, up to 50% of project cost coverage).
2. Tiered success fees reflect decreasing marginal effort as client size and project scale increase.
3. Retainers cover initial eligibility assessment, TRL validation, and SLFM coordination — critical given the scheme’s exclusion of direct DST proposals.
4. Scheme-specific note: Moderate risk profile due to 50-year fund return requirement and EGoS approval dependency; however, strong pipeline evidence from DST’s RDI Cell and SLFM engagement reduces execution risk.
