# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping deep-tech innovators in sectors like quantum computing, robotics, AI in agriculture/health, and biotechnology access transformative R&D funding through government initiatives. Many startups and research institutions I work with are developing strategic technologies but face hurdles in scaling due to high upfront R&D costs. I’d like to understand your current innovation stage and funding needs to see if the National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems (NM-ICPS) could support your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your organization’s primary focus area—are you working in strategic technologies such as quantum computing, robotics, space, AI in agriculture/health/education, biotechnology, biomanufacturing, synthetic biology, pharma, medical devices, or digital economy including digital agriculture?
2. What is the current Technology Readiness Level (TRL) of your R&D project? (NM-ICPS supports TRL 4 and above)
3. Are you an industry player, startup, MSME, research institution, or academia?
4. What is the estimated total cost of your transformative R&D project?
5. Are you seeking long-term financing at low or nil interest rates, or equity infusion support—particularly relevant for startups?
6. Have you considered applying through a Second-Level Fund Manager (SLFM) such as an AIF, DFI, NBFC, or FRO like TDB, BIRAC, or IIT Research Parks?
7. Are you prepared to repay the entire funded amount to the Consolidated Fund of India (CFI) after 50 years without interest, as required by the scheme?
8. Is your project aligned with national priorities like energy security, climate action, or Atmanirbhar Bharat through indigenization of critical technologies?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Engaged in RDI-intensive technology at TRL 4 or above  
- [ ] Operates in strategic technology areas: energy security, climate action, deep technologies (quantum, robotics, space), AI in agri/health/edu, biotech/biomanufacturing/synthetic biology/pharma/medical devices, digital economy/digital agriculture  
- [ ] Entity type: startup, MSME, research institution, academia, or industry player  
- [ ] Seeking long-term loans at low/nil interest or equity infusion (not grants or short-term loans)  
- [ ] Willing to route funding through a designated Second-Level Fund Manager (SLFM)  
- [ ] Accepts obligation to return entire fund to CFI after 50 years without interest  
- [ ] Project cost allows for up to 50% financing (or higher with EGoS approval)  
- [ ] Not submitting proposals directly to DST (understands SLFM-mediated process)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your project aligns well with the National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems (NM-ICPS), which offers long-term financing at low or nil interest-free interest rates and equity infusion—covering up to 50% of project costs for TRL 4+ transformative R&D. Let me walk you through how this scheme works and whether it’s a fit for your innovation journey.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Deep-tech startups and research entities in India often struggle to scale transformative R&D due to high capital intensity, long gestation periods, and limited access to patient capital. Traditional financing is unsuitable for early-stage, high-risk innovation in strategic sectors like quantum computing, AI in health/agri, and biotechnology, leaving many promising projects stuck at pilot stage without pathways to commercialization or scale.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems (NM-ICPS), also known as the Research, Development, and Innovation (RDI) Scheme, is a ₹1 lakh crore initiative launched by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Ministry of Science and Technology, approved by the Union Cabinet on July 1, 2025. It provides long-term loans at low or nil interest rates and equity infusion—especially for startups—along with contributions to the Deep-Tech Fund of Funds. Grants and short-term loans are not supported. Financing can cover up to 50% of assessed project cost for transformative R&D projects at Technology Readiness Levels (TRLs) 4 and above, with exceptions possible via Empowered Group of Secretaries (EGoS) approval. Disbursements are routed exclusively through designated Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs) such as AIFs, DFIs, NBFCs, or FROs like TDB, BIRAC, or IIT Research Parks.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your project is in a strategic technology area such as [client’s sector from discovery], which is explicitly prioritized under NM-ICPS  
- Your innovation is at TRL 4 or above, meeting the minimum readiness threshold for funding  
- You are a [startup/MSME/research institution/academia/industry player], all of which are eligible beneficiary types  
- You seek long-term, non-grant financing—aligning with the scheme’s exclusion of grants and short-term loans
