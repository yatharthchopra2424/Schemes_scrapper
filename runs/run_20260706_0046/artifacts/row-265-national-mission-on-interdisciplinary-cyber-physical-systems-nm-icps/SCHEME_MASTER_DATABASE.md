### National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems (NM-ICPS)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Science and Technology (DST), Ministry of Science and Technology
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://rdifund.anrf.gov.in/
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Industry players, startups, MSMEs, research institutions, academia, and other stakeholders
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Strategic technology areas such as energy security, climate action, deep technologies (quantum computing, robotics, space), AI applications in agriculture, health, education, biotechnology, biomanufacturing, synthetic biology, pharma, medical devices, and digital economy including digital agriculture
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Entities must be engaged in developing RDI-intensive technology at Technology Readiness Level (TRL) 4 and above

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financing can cover up to 50% of the assessed project cost for transformative R&D projects at TRL 4 and above; total outlay of ₹1 lakh crore
- Disbursement mode: Long-term loans at low or nil interest rates, equity infusion (especially for startups), contributions to the Deep-Tech Fund of Funds
- Duration of benefit: Long-term (fund must be returned to CFI after 50 years)

#### Documents Required
1. Proposal submission through designated Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs)
2. Expression of interest from SLFMs to Dr Jyoti Sharma, Head, RDI Cell at sharma[dot]jyoti[at]nic[dot]in
3. Project details for assessment by SLFMs
4. Documentation for approval by Empowered Group of Secretaries (EGoS) for exceptions beyond standard coverage

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Interested Second Level Fund Managers (SLFMs) express interest by writing to Dr Jyoti Sharma, Head, RDI Cell at sharma[dot]jyoti[at]nic[dot]in
2. Existing NBFCs planning to establish AIFs are encouraged to contact DST regarding potential participation
3. Industry stakeholders seeking funding are advised not to send proposals directly to DST
4. All disbursements will be routed exclusively through designated SLFMs via separate advertisement
5. For more details, visit https://rdifund.anrf.gov.in/
6. The fund flow from CFI to RDIF is based on half-yearly net requirements
7. RDIF releases funds to SLFMs in a 'Just-In-Time' manner via the Treasury Single Account (TSA) system
8. SLFMs prepare fund requirements based on repayment or exit proceeds and forward them to RDIF
9. Upon receipt of demand, RDIF releases funds to the designated bank account of SLFM within a specified time frame
