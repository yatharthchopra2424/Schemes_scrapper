# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
**Scheme Name:** National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems (NM-ICPS)  
**Scheme ID:** row-265  
**Ministry / Category:** Deep Tech & Innovation  
**Scheme Type:** Grant  
**Geographic Scope:** Pan-India  
**Implementing Agency:** Department of Science and Technology (DST), Ministry of Science and Technology  
**Application Portal:** https://rdifund.anrf.gov.in/  
**Last Updated:** 2025  
**Fund Size (Cr):** 100,000 (₹1 lakh crore)  

### Objectives
- Encourage private sector involvement in high-impact R&D  
- Promote strategic technologies including quantum computing, robotics, space, AI, and biotechnology  
- Foster self-reliance and economic security through indigenization of critical technologies  
- Ensure flexibility in sectoral support for public interest  
- Strengthen India’s capabilities in strategic technologies and promote technological self-reliance  
- Align with the nation’s long-term innovation and Atmanirbhar Bharat vision  
- Catalyze private sector participation in high-impact R&D  
- Support technologies where indigenization is essential for strategic or economic reasons  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Eligible Entities | Specific Requirements | Technology Readiness Level (TRL) |
|-------------------|----------------------|----------------------------------|
| Industry players | Engaged in RDI-intensive technology | TRL 4 and above |
| Startups | Engaged in RDI-intensive technology | TRL 4 and above |
| MSMEs | Engaged in RDI-intensive technology | TRL 4 and above |
| Research institutions | Engaged in RDI-intensive technology | TRL 4 and above |
| Academia | Engaged in RDI-intensive technology | TRL 4 and above |
| Other stakeholders | Engaged in research, development, and innovation in strategic technology areas | TRL 4 and above |

**Strategic Technology Areas Covered:**  
- Energy security and climate action  
- Deep technologies (quantum computing, robotics, space)  
- AI applications in agriculture, health, education  
- Biotechnology, biomanufacturing, synthetic biology, pharma, medical devices  
- Digital economy including digital agriculture  

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Benefit Type | Details |
|--------------|---------|
| **Financial Support** | Long-term loans at low or nil interest rates; Equity infusion (especially for startups); Contributions to Deep-Tech Fund of Funds |
| **Coverage** | Financing covers up to 50% of assessed project cost for transformative R&D projects at TRL 4 and above; exceptions possible upon approval by Empowered Group of Secretaries (EGoS) |
| **Non-Financial Benefits** | Access to mentorship, infrastructure, collaboration networks, support for scaling up innovations; Facilitates technology transfer, commercialization, and participation in national innovation ecosystems |
| **Exclusions** | Grants and short-term loans are not supported under the scheme |

### Application Process Flowchart
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Industry Stakeholder Seeking Funding] --> B[Not Advised to Send Proposals Directly to DST]
    B --> C[Wait for Separate Advertisement for SLFMs]
    C --> D[Apply Through Designated Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs)]
    D --> E[SLFMs Include AIFs, DFIs, NBFCs, FROs (TDB, BIRAC, IIT Research Parks)]
    E --> F[Disbursements Routed Exclusively Through SLFMs]
    F --> G[Fund Flow: CFI → RDIF (Half-Yearly Net Requirements)]
    G --> H[RDIF Releases Funds to SLFMs via TSA System]
    H --> I[SLFMs Prepare Fund Requirements Based on Repayment/Exit Proceeds]
    I --> J[RDIF Releases Funds to SLFM Bank Account Within Specified Time Frame]
    J --> K[Funds Used for Project Financing]
    K --> L[Entire Fund Must Be Returned to CFI After 50 Years Without Interest]
```

### Key Caveats
> **Warning:** Grants and short-term loans are not supported under the scheme.  
> **Warning:** Disbursements are routed exclusively through designated Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs); direct proposals to DST are discouraged.  
> **Warning:** The entire fund must be returned to the Consolidated Fund of India (CFI) after 50 years without any interest.  
> **Warning:** Funding is contingent on approval by the Empowered Group of Secretaries (EGoS) for exceptions beyond standard coverage.
