## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Entity is a registered company, society, trust, or Section 8 company under Indian law  
- [ ] Entity is engaged in biopharmaceutical research, development, or manufacturing  
- [ ] Entity has a minimum of 2 years of operational history in the biopharma sector  
- [ ] Entity demonstrates capability in vaccine, biosimilar, medical device, or diagnostic development  
- [ ] Entity is not blacklisted by any government agency or PSU  
- [ ] Entity has not received duplicate funding for the same project from another central government scheme  
- [ ] Entity agrees to co-fund at least 30% of the project cost from internal or non-government sources  
- [ ] Entity commits to sharing intellectual property (IP) generated under the mission as per NBM guidelines  
- [ ] Entity is willing to collaborate with academic institutions, hospitals, or other industry partners as required  
- [ ] Entity has a registered office and operational presence in India  

> **Note**: Eligibility criteria are inferred from the National Biopharma Mission (NBM) objectives and typical BIRAC scheme requirements, as the primary portal (https://birac.nic.in/nbm.php) returned a "File not found" error. Verify current eligibility with BIRAC nodal officer before proceeding.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration  
- PAN Card of the entity  
- Audited Financial Statements (last 2 years)  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the entity (last 2 years)  
- GST Registration Certificate  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- Authorized Signatory Letter and Board Resolution  
- Cancelled Cheque / Bank Details for fund transfer  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining objectives, methodology, timeline, budget, and expected outcomes  
- Technical feasibility report and proof of concept (if applicable)  
- Collaborative agreements (if project involves academia/hospitals/industry partners)  
- IP management plan detailing ownership, sharing, and commercialization strategy  
- Utilization Certificate format (to be submitted post-approval, but draft required)  
- CVs of key project personnel (Principal Investigator and team leads)  
- Quotations for major equipment (>1 lakh INR) or subcontracts  
- Environmental and safety clearance certificates (if lab/manufacturing work involved)  
- Undertaking on non-duplication of funding and 30% co-funding commitment  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)  
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_EntityName_DocumentType_YYYYMMDD.pdf`  
- Certificates (e.g., Udyam, GST) must be valid and not expired  
- Financial statements must be audited and signed by a Chartered Accountant  
- DPR must be signed and stamped by the authorized signatory  
- All declarations must be on company letterhead and notarized where required  
- Documents must be in English or accompanied by a certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required.**  
Standard sections to include:  
1. Executive Summary  
2. Project Objectives and Alignment with NBM Goals  
3. Technical Approach and Methodology  
4. Work Plan and Timeline (Gantt chart)  
5. Budget Breakdown (capital, recurring, manpower, consumables, travel, overheads)  
6. Expected Outcomes and Deliverables (milestones, prototypes, clinical trials, regulatory filings)  
7. Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
8. IP Management and Commercialization Strategy  
9. Team Credentials and Roles  
10. Sustainability and Post-Project Plan  
11. Annexures: Quotations, Collaborative Agreements, CVs  

> **Note**: Confirm DPR template and page limits with BIRAC nodal officer, as the NBM-specific portal is currently inaccessible.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal via **https://birac.nic.in** (navigate to “Programs” → “National Biopharma Mission”)  
2. Register or log in using the entity’s official email and mobile number  
3. Fill in entity details exactly as per Certificate of Incorporation and PAN  
4. Select “National Biopharma Mission” as the scheme and choose the appropriate thematic area (vaccine, biosimilar, etc.)  
5. Enter project title, summary, and objectives in the prescribed character limits  
6. Upload all documents in PDF format, ensuring file names match the checklist  
7. Declare co-funding commitment (minimum 30%) and confirm no duplication of central government funding  
8. Review all entries before final submission; no edits allowed after submission  
9. Save the application reference number immediately upon submission  
10. Take a screenshot of the confirmation page and store it securely
