## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual grant amount awarded under the National Biopharma Mission, ensuring client interests are aligned with consultant performance.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Retainers**: Retainer amounts reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile, considering the National Biopharma Mission’s competitive selection process and variable processing timelines observed in implementation.
3. **Transparency**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover initial eligibility assessment and proposal preparation, while success fees are contingent on fund disbursement.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The National Biopharma Mission involves technical and scientific evaluation with a typical processing time of 6–9 months from application to approval, based on implementation evidence. This longer cycle increases revenue lag risk, justifying higher retainer engagement to secure client commitment during the extended review period.
