## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I hope you're doing well. I specialise in helping biopharma and deep-tech innovators access government funding to accelerate R&D and commercialisation. The National Biopharma Mission is designed specifically for organisations like yours working on breakthrough solutions in vaccines, biosimilars, medical devices, or agro-biotech — so I wanted to see if this might be a fit for your current stage and goals.

### Discovery Questions
- What year was your organisation incorporated or registered as a legal entity?
- Are you currently working on a biopharma-related R&D project — such as vaccine development, biosimilars, medical diagnostics, or agro-biotech innovation?
- Is your project focused on addressing a public health challenge or advancing India’s self-reliance in biopharmaceutical manufacturing?
- Do you have a clear project plan with milestones, budget, and timelines for the next 12–24 months?
- Are you seeking non-dilutive funding to support prototype development, preclinical/clinical trials, or technology transfer?
- Is your organisation based in India and primarily conducting R&D activities within the country?
- Do you have collaborations or partnerships with academic institutions, research labs, or industry players for your project?
- Are you looking to scale your innovation from lab-scale to pilot or pre-commercial stages?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Organisation incorporated in India as a legal entity (company, society, trust, or Section 8 company)
- [ ] Engaged in biopharma R&D: vaccines, biosimilars, medical devices, diagnostics, agro-biotech, or related fields
- [ ] Project addresses national public health priorities or import substitution goals
- [ ] Has a defined project plan with budget, timeline, and technical milestones
- [ ] Seeking support for prototype development, preclinical/clinical trials, or scale-up
- [ ] R&D activities primarily conducted in India
- [ ] Preference given to organisations with academic/industry collaborations
- [ ] Not currently receiving duplicate funding for the same work from another central government scheme

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your project aligns well with the National Biopharma Mission’s focus on accelerating indigenous biopharma innovation. This scheme offers grant-based support of up to ₹50 crore for qualifying R&D projects — let me walk you through how it works and whether you’d be eligible.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Biopharma innovators in India often face a funding valley of death — especially during the transition from lab proof-of-concept to preclinical or clinical stages. High R&D costs, long timelines, and limited access to patient capital make it difficult to de-risk innovations without diluting equity or taking on unsustainable debt.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Biopharma Mission, implemented by the Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council (BIRAC) under the Department of Biotechnology, Ministry of Science and Technology, provides grant-based funding of up to ₹50 crore for eligible biopharma R&D projects. It supports innovation across vaccines, biosimilars, medical devices, diagnostics, and agro-biotech, with a focus on public health impact and self-reliance.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your organisation is incorporated in India and conducting R&D domestically  
- Your project focuses on [vaccines/biosimilars/medical devices/etc.] — a priority area under the Mission
