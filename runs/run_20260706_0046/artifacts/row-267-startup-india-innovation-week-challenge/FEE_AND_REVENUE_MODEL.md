## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-based pricing**: Fees aligned with the financial benefit delivered (e.g., cash prizes up to ₹10 lakhs, seed funding up to ₹20 lakhs via SISFS, investment facilitation).
2. **Risk-adjusted pricing**: Success fees reflect moderate risk due to non-guaranteed financial support and dependency on challenge performance; processing time varies by challenge, introducing revenue lag.
3. **Tiered accessibility**: Lower retainers for early-stage startups to encourage participation in DPIIT recognition and Innovation Week Challenge.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: This scheme has a medium risk profile — financial support is not guaranteed and depends on challenge performance, though non-financial benefits (mentorship, networking, visibility) are high-value and more consistently delivered.
