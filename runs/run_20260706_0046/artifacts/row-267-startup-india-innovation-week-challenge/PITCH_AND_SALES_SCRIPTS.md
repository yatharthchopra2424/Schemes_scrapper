# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping deep tech and innovative startups gain recognition and access to government support through the Startup India initiative. Many founders I speak with are building scalable solutions but aren’t aware of how DPIIT recognition can unlock mentorship, investor connections, and eligibility for schemes like the Seed Fund Scheme. I’d love to learn about your innovation and see if we can help you access these benefits.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your company incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or partnership firm?
2. What is your current annual turnover, and has it exceeded INR 200 crore in any financial year since incorporation?
3. Are you working towards innovation, development, or improvement of products/services with potential for wealth and employment generation?
4. Has your entity been formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business?
5. Are you operating in a DeepTech sector (e.g., AI, biotech, aerospace, quantum computing)?
6. Do you have a pitch deck or business description ready to showcase your innovation?
7. Have you already obtained DPIIT recognition through the Startup India portal?
8. Are you seeking visibility, mentorship, or access to funding avenues like SISFS or Fund of Funds?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or partnership firm  
- [ ] Within 10 years of incorporation (20 years for DeepTech)  
- [ ] Turnover not exceeding INR 200 crore (INR 300 crore for DeepTech)  
- [ ] Working towards innovation, development, or improvement of products/services  
- [ ] Potential for wealth and employment generation  
- [ ] Not formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business  
- [ ] Targeting sectors like DeepTech, AgriTech, SpaceTech, etc.  
- [ ] Willing to participate in innovation challenges and events  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you may be eligible for DPIIT recognition under Startup India, which is the gateway to the Startup India Innovation Week Challenge. This recognition opens doors to mentorship, investor networking, and potential cash prizes—let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many innovative startups struggle to gain visibility, access mentorship, or connect with investors despite having scalable solutions. Without formal recognition, they miss out on government-backed opportunities that could accelerate growth.

**Scheme intro:**
The Startup India Innovation Week Challenge, administered by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry, is a pan-India recognition-based program that connects startups with mentors, investors, and industry leaders. While direct financial support varies by challenge, participants gain access to cash prizes (e.g., up to ₹10 lakhs in linked programs like National Startup Awards), seed funding via SISFS (up to ₹20 lakhs), and investment facilitation through Startup India Investor Connect.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are incorporated as a [Private Limited Company/LLP/partnership] — meeting the company type requirement  
- Your incorporation date is within the last [X] years — satisfying the 10-year (or 20-year for DeepTech) age limit  
- Your turnover is under INR 200 crore — within the eligibility threshold  
- You are innovating in [specific sector from discovery] — aligning with the innovation and scalability criteria

Let’s run a free eligibility check—no commitment, success-fee only model. We’ll review your documents and guide you through DPIIT recognition and Innovation Week Challenge application via the official portal: https://www.startupindia.gov.in/
