### Startup India Innovation Week Challenge
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.startupindia.gov.in/
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Private Limited Company, LLP, or partnership firm
- Age of business: Within 10 years of incorporation (20 years for DeepTech)
- Sector: Innovation, development, or improvement of products/services with potential for wealth and employment generation
- Revenue cap: Turnover not exceeding INR 200 crore (INR 300 crore for DeepTech)
- Employee count: 
- Other: Must be DPIIT recognised to participate

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support varies by challenge; may include cash prizes (e.g., up to ₹10 lakhs in some programs), seed funding via linked schemes (up to ₹20 lakhs), or investment facilitation
- Disbursement mode: Varies by challenge (cash prizes, seed funding, investment facilitation)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (per challenge)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. Pitch deck or business description
4. Authorization letter from authorized signatory
5. Details of funding received (if any)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register on the Startup India portal (startupindia.gov.in)
2. Obtain DPIIT recognition via the 'Get Recognised' section
3. Navigate to the Innovation Week Challenge or specific program page
4. Fill out the application form with startup details and innovation description
5. Upload required documents such as incorporation certificate, PAN, and pitch deck
6. Submit the application and await evaluation
7. If selected, participate in the challenge events and receive benefits
