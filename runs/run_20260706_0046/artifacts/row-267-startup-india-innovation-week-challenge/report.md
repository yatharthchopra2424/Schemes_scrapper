# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Startup India Innovation Week Challenge** is a recognition-type scheme under the **Startup India** initiative, implemented by the **Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT)**, Ministry of Commerce and Industry. It operates on a **pan-India** geographic scope with **rolling basis** applications accepted year-round for various challenges under Startup India Programs. The scheme was last updated in **2026**.

### Objectives
- To provide a platform for startups to showcase innovative solutions  
- To connect startups with mentors, investors, and industry experts  
- To promote sector-specific innovation in areas like DeepTech, AgriTech, and SpaceTech  
- To offer recognition and support through cash prizes and handholding  
- To strengthen the startup ecosystem via structured challenges and events  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Criteria | Requirement | Notes |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| **DPIIT Recognition** | Mandatory | Startups must be DPIIT recognised to participate in the Innovation Week Challenge |
| **Company Type** | Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm | Also includes Cooperative Society for DeepTech |
| **Age Limit** | ≤10 years from incorporation (≤20 years for DeepTech) | Entity ceases to be a Startup after this period |
| **Annual Turnover** | ≤₹200 crore (≤₹300 crore for DeepTech) | As defined under Companies Act, 2013; revised from ₹100 crore per DPIIT Gazette Notification 108(E) dated 4 February 2026 |
| **Original Entity** | Not formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business | Such entities are ineligible for recognition |
| **Innovative & Scalable** | Working towards development/improvement of products/services with potential for wealth and employment generation | Must have scalable business model |
| **Tax Exemption Eligibility (80IAC)** | Incorporated after 1st April 2016; Private Limited or LLP only | Additional requirement for income tax benefits |

> **Key Caveats**  
> - Participation requires prior DPIIT recognition  
> - Benefits such as tax exemptions and funding are subject to separate approvals  
> - Recognition and eligibility are subject to periodic review and may be revoked for non-compliance  
> - Financial support is not guaranteed and depends on performance in the challenge  
> - Disclaimer: Pursuant to the DPIIT Gazette Notification 108(E) dated 4 February 2026, the turnover threshold for Startup recognition has been revised from ₹100 crore to ₹200 crore. DPIIT-recognized startups are advised to log in to the Startup India portal using their registered credentials and download their updated Startup Recognition certificate.

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Benefit Type | Details | Source |
|--------------|---------|--------|
| **Financial Support** | Varies by challenge; may include:<br>- Cash prizes (e.g., up to ₹10 lakhs in National Startup Awards)<br>- Seed funding via SISFS (up to ₹20 lakhs)<br>- Investment facilitation via Startup India Investor Connect | Key Facts |
| **Non-Financial Benefits** | - Mentorship and networking with investors/industry leaders<br>- Handholding support<br>- Visibility through national platforms<br>- Eligibility for further government schemes (Seed Fund Scheme, Fund of Funds, Credit Guarantee Scheme)<br>- Support for scaling and market access (for winners) | Key Facts |
| **DPIIT Recognition Benefits** (linked prerequisite) | - Tax exemptions<br>- Easier compliance<br>- IPR fast-tracking<br>- Self-certification with 6 labour and 3 environment laws (no inspections for 5 years on credible complaint)<br>- Access to Government e-Marketplace (GeM)<br>- Exemption from prior experience/turnover in public procurement<br>- EMD exemption in government tenders<br>- Easy winding up of company<br>- Patent filing rebate (80%)<br>- Trademark filing rebate (50%) | Supporting Evidence (Startup India Scheme page) |

> **Important Note**: The Innovation Week Challenge itself may not provide direct grants but links startups to funding avenues. Financial support is not guaranteed and depends on performance in the challenge.

### Required Documents
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration  
2. PAN of the entity  
3. Pitch deck or business description  
4. Authorization letter from authorized signatory  
5. Details of funding received (if any)

### Supporting Evidence Highlights
- **DPIIT Recognised Startups**: 170,035 (as per portal homepage crawl)  
- **BHASKAR Users**: 533,122 (as per portal homepage crawl)  
- **Turnover Threshold Revision**: DPIIT Gazette Notification 108(E) dated 4 February 2026 increased limit from ₹100 crore to ₹200 crore (₹300 crore for DeepTech)  
- **Tax Holiday**: Eligible startups can avail income tax exemption for 3 consecutive financial years out of first ten years since incorporation (Section 80IAC)  
- **Public Procurement Benefits**: Exemption from prior experience/turnover criteria; EMD exemption; GeM registration access  
- **Winding Up**: Startups can be wound up within 90 days under Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 if meeting income criteria  
- **Patent Support**: 80% rebate on filing fees; government bears facilitator fees; statutory fees only payable by startup  
- **Trademark Support**: 50% rebate on filing fees
