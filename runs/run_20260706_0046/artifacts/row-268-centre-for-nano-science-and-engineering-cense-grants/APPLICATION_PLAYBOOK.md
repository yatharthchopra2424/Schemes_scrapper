# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a 2nd Year MTech student of Centre for Nano Science & Engineering (CeNSE), IISc Bangalore  
- [ ] Applicant is currently enrolled in the MTech in Semiconductor Technology program at CeNSE  
- [ ] Applicant has completed the first year of the MTech program and is progressing to the second year  
- [ ] Applicant is an Indian national or holds valid student visa for study at IISc Bangalore  
- [ ] Applicant maintains satisfactory academic standing (minimum CGPA as per IISc norms, typically ≥6.0/10)  
- [ ] Applicant is not in receipt of any other central government fellowship that duplicates benefits  
- [ ] Applicant has not previously availed of the CeNSE MTech Fellowship Program  

> **Note**: Eligibility is restricted exclusively to all 2nd Year MTech students of CeNSE. No other candidates (including PhD, MEngg, or external applicants) are eligible for this fellowship.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- [ ] PAN Card  
- [ ] Aadhaar Card  
- [ ] Bank account details (cancelled cheque or bank passbook)  
- [ ] Passport-sized photographs  
- [ ] Address proof (utility bill, rental agreement, or hostel certificate)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- [ ] Bonafide certificate from CeNSE/IISc confirming current enrollment as a 2nd Year MTech student  
- [ ] Official transcript or grade card showing completion of 1st Year MTech with satisfactory CGPA  
- [ ] Updated CV/resume  
- [ ] Statement of Purpose (SOP) outlining research interests and alignment with fellowship objectives  
- [ ] Two Letters of Recommendation (LORs) from CeNSE faculty members  
- [ ] Copy of GATE scorecard (if applicable for initial admission, though not re-evaluated for fellowship)  
- [ ] Proof of identity as an IISc student (valid student ID card)  

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible, scanned in PDF format (minimum 300 DPI)  
- [ ] File names follow format: `CaseID_DocumentType_Name.pdf` (e.g., `CASE001_BonafideCertificate_JohnDoe.pdf`)  
- [ ] File size does not exceed 5 MB per document  
- [ ] All certificates are issued by the competent authority (CeNSE/IISc) and bear official seal/signature  
- [ ] Documents are in English or accompanied by a certified translation  
- [ ] Statement of Purpose is original, plagiarism-checked, and signed by the applicant  
- [ ] Letters of Recommendation are on official letterhead, signed, and dated within the last 3 months

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required. The CeNSE MTech Fellowship Program does not require a Detailed Project Report (DPR) or project proposal. Selection is based on the application form, SOP, LORs, and inputs from industry sponsors.  

> **Note**: Verify with the nodal officer if any additional research statement is expected, though the official process does not mandate a DPR.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Wait for the official email call** – Applications are only accepted in response to the annual email circulated to 2nd Year MTech students in July.  
2. **Use the prescribed format** – The application is typically a simple email or form; do not invent sections. Stick to what is requested.  
3. **SOP must align with fellowship goals** – Clearly link your research interests to Advanced Materials, Semiconductor Fabrication & Packaging, or WFE (Wafer Fabrication Equipment) to resonate with industry sponsors.  
4. **Highlight relevant skills** – Mention hands-on experience with cleanroom facilities, characterization tools (SEM, AFM, XRD), or fabrication processes gained during 1st Year MTech.  
5. **LORs must be specific** – Recommenders should comment on your technical aptitude, lab performance, and suitability for industry collaboration.  
6. **No false claims** – Do not overstate GATE scores or achievements; verification is done internally by CeNSE.  
7. **Attach all documents** – Ensure every requested document is attached before sending.  
8. **Use formal tone and IISc email** – Send from your official IISc email address (e.g., name@iisc.ac.in) for authenticity.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Applicant’s current 2nd Year MTech status verified via bonafide certificate  
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents are present and correctly formatted  
- [ ] SOP is tailored to semiconductor industry alignment and within word limit (if specified)  
- [ ] LORs are from CeNSE faculty, signed, and dated recently  
- [ ] No discrepancies in personal details across documents  
- [ ] Application is ready to be sent via email from the applicant’s IISc email ID  
- [ ] Backup copy of all documents stored securely

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- [ ] Submit the application **via email** as directed in the annual call (typically to a CeNSE faculty committee email – monitor your IISc inbox for the exact address in July)  
- [ ] Use subject line: `Application for MTech Fellowship Program 2024 – [Your Name]`  
- [ ] Attach all documents in PDF format with clear naming  
- [ ] Take a screenshot of the sent email (showing To, Subject, Attachments, and Timestamp)  
- [ ] Save the sent email in a dedicated folder and record the date/time of submission  
- [ ] Set a calendar reminder to follow up if no acknowledgment is received within 3 working days

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify the formal fellowship offer letter via email  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned amount: **INR 24,800 per month for 12 months**  
- [ ] Verify disbursement schedule (typically monthly, via direct bank transfer)  
- [ ] Submit utilization certificate (if required) at the end of the fellowship period  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with fellowship terms: active participation in industry engagements, conference attendance, and progress reporting  
- [ ] Track internship/placement opportunities facilitated through industry sponsors  
- [ ] Notify your consultancy firm upon successful approval to initiate success fee invoicing (as per engagement terms)  
- [ ] Maintain records of all communications, offer letters, and bank statements for audit purposes  

> **Note**: The fellowship includes support for attending conferences and engagement with sponsors for internships, placement, and career guidance. Students are expected to actively participate in these components.
