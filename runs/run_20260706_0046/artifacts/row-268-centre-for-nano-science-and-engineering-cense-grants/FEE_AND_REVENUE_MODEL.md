## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit secured for the client.
2. Transparent fee structures with clear milestones for retainer and success fees.
3. Competitive rates benchmarked against deep-tech and innovation consulting market.
4. Scheme-specific note: The Centre for Nano Science and Engineering (CeNSE) Grants offer a fixed financial benefit of INR 24,800 per month for 12 months (total ₹2,97,600) under the MTech Fellowship Program. The scheme has a high success rate for eligible 2nd Year MTech students of CeNSE, with selection completed before the first week of August following a July application call. Processing time is approximately 3–4 weeks post-interview, resulting in low to moderate risk for consultants due to predictable timelines and high eligibility specificity.
