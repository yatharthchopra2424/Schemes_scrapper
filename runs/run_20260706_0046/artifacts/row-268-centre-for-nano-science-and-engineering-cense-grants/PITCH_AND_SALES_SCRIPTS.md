# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping students and early-career researchers access deep-tech funding and fellowship opportunities at premier institutions like the Indian Institute of Science. The Centre for Nano Science and Engineering (CeNSE) at IISc Bangalore offers targeted support for those pursuing M.Tech and PhD in nanoscience and semiconductor technology — including enhanced fellowships, access to world-class nanofabrication labs, and industry engagement. I’d like to understand your academic background and goals to see if this aligns with your path.

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you currently pursuing or planning to pursue an M.Tech or PhD in nanoscience, semiconductor technology, or related fields such as electronics, physics, or materials engineering?  
2. Have you qualified for GATE in a relevant paper (EC, EE, ME, IN, XE-C, MT for M.Tech; PH, IN, EC for M.Sc holders)?  
3. Are you a second-year M.Tech student at CeNSE, IISc Bangalore, or planning to enroll in their M.Tech in Semiconductor Technology programme?  
4. What is your academic background — BE/BTech in any discipline or MSc in Electronics/Physics?  
5. Are you interested in accessing enhanced fellowships, conference support, or industry-sponsored internship opportunities during your studies?  
6. Do you have access to documents such as your degree certificate, GATE scorecard, transcripts, and statement of purpose?  
7. Are you looking for opportunities to engage with industry sponsors like Lam Research, Applied Materials, or Tokyo Electron for research or placements?  
8. What is your timeline for applying — are you targeting the July admission session or the limited January PhD session?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Candidate is pursuing or eligible for M.Tech or PhD in nanoscience/engineering at IISc  
- [ ] For M.Tech: BE/BTech in any discipline with GATE in EC, EE, ME, IN, XE-C, MT OR MSc in Electronics/Physics with GATE in PH, IN, EC  
- [ ] For PhD: ME/MTech or equivalent OR BE/BTech with valid GATE OR MSc with valid GATE OR qualification in INSPIRE/CSIR UGC NET/DBT JRF/UGC NET/JEST  
- [ ] For MTech Fellowship: Must be a 2nd Year M.Tech student of CeNSE, IISc  
- [ ] Candidate has or can obtain required documents: degree certificate, GATE/CAM scorecard, transcripts, CV, SOP, LORs  
- [ ] Candidate is targeting July session (M.Tech/PhD) or limited January session (PhD only)  
- [ ] Candidate seeks access to nanofabrication facilities, industry mentorship, or enhanced fellowship (INR 24,800/month)  
- [ ] Candidate is interested in deep-tech domains: semiconductors, quantum tech, MEMS, photonics, nanomaterials  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you’re a strong fit for the Centre for Nano Science and Engineering (CeNSE) M.Tech Fellowship Program at IISc Bangalore — which offers an enhanced fellowship of INR 24,800 per month for 12 months, along with conference support and industry engagement. Let me walk you through how this works and why it’s ideal for your profile.

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**  
Many talented M.Tech students in nanoscience and semiconductor technology face financial constraints that limit their ability to focus on research, attend conferences, or engage with industry — despite having the academic merit and access to premier facilities like CeNSE’s 14,000 sq. ft. nanofabrication lab.

**Scheme intro:**  
The Centre for Nano Science and Engineering (CeNSE) MTech Fellowship Program, administered by the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore, provides an enhanced fellowship of INR 24,800 per month for 12 months to eligible second-year M.Tech students. It includes support for attending conferences and structured engagement with industry sponsors such as Lam Research, Applied Materials, and Tokyo Electron for internships, placements, and career guidance.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**  
- You are (or will be) a second-year M.Tech student at CeNSE, IISc — the core eligibility requirement for the fellowship  
- You hold a BE/BTech in a relevant discipline or MSc in Electronics/Physics with a valid GATE score in the specified papers (EC, EE, ME, IN, XE-C, MT or PH, IN, EC)  
- You are targeting the July admission session, when the fellowship call is issued via email and selection is completed before the first week of August  
- You seek access to CeNSE’s state-of-the-art facilities: NNFC (nanofabrication), MNCF (characterization), S&PF (packaging), and INCeNSE incubator
