### Centre for Nano Science and Engineering (CeNSE) Grants
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Incubation
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://cense.iisc.ac.in/for-students/
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Admissions to M.Tech and PhD programs are typically offered for the July session, with a limited number of topic/field-specific admissions possible in the January session for PhD. The MTech Fellowship Program call is sent via email in July, with selection completed before the first week of August.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Students, researchers, startups, industry partners
- Age of business: Not applicable (targets individuals and entities at various stages)
- Sector: Nanoscience and engineering, semiconductor technology, deep tech
- Revenue cap: Not mentioned
- Employee count: Not mentioned
- Other: 
  - For M.Tech in Semiconductor Technology: BE/BTech or equivalent in any discipline with GATE paper in EC, EE, ME, IN, XE-C, MT OR MSc or equivalent in Electronics or Physics with GATE paper in PH, IN, EC.
  - For PhD: ME/MTech or equivalent in any discipline OR BE/BTech in Engineering with valid GATE score OR MSc in Science with valid GATE score OR qualification in national exams like INSPIRE, CSIR UGC NET for JRF, DBT JRF, UGC NET for JRF, or JEST.
  - MTech Fellowship Program: Exclusively for 2nd Year MTech students of CeNSE.
  - Admission to M.Tech program is offered only for the July session.
  - PhD admissions are typically for July session, with limited January session for topic/field-specific admissions.

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - MTech Fellowship Program: INR 24,800 per month for 12 months, support for attending conferences, engagement with sponsors including opportunities for internships, placement, and career guidance.
  - Access to 14,000 sq. ft. state-of-the-art nanofabrication and clean room facility (NNFC), comprehensive micro nano characterization facility (MNCF), Systems and Packaging Facility (S&PF), and in-house technology business incubator INCeNSE which incubates 6 start-ups with infrastructure, labs, and mentoring support.
  - Industrial interactions, cutting-edge R&D facilities, industry fellowships, internships, and placements.
  - Faculty and researchers gain access to advanced equipment, collaborative projects, and national facility networks.
  - Industry partners receive facility usage, consultancy, research projects, training, talent access, networking, and distinguished lectures via the Industry Affiliate Program.
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (fellowship stipend), reimbursement (conference support), in-kind (facility access, mentoring)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (fellowship for 12 months), ongoing (facility access, incubation support during program tenure)

#### Documents Required
1. BE/BTech or equivalent degree certificate
2. MSc or equivalent degree certificate (if applicable)
3. GATE scorecard (with valid paper as per eligibility)
4. CAM scorecard (for CFTI mode of entry)
5. Proof of qualification in national exams (INSPIRE, CSIR UGC NET, DBT JRF, UGC NET, JEST) if applicable
6. Updated CV/resume
7. Statement of purpose
8. Letters of recommendation
9. Academic transcripts/mark sheets

#### Application Process
1. Visit the CeNSE website and navigate to the 'For Students' or 'How to apply' section.
2. Check eligibility for the desired program (M.Tech, PhD, M.Engg, Joint M.Tech, or Fellowship).
3. For M.Tech/PhD: Apply through the IISc admissions website during the July session (limited January session for PhD).
4. Candidates are shortlisted based on national exam scores (GATE/CAM) or qualification.
5. Shortlisted candidates must appear for an interview at CeNSE.
6. Final selection is based on interview performance and exam results, announced 2-3 weeks post-interview.
7. For Fellowships: Wait for the annual call (typically July), submit application via email, and undergo review by CeNSE faculty committee with industry sponsor inputs.
8. Selected candidates receive formal admission or fellowship offer.
