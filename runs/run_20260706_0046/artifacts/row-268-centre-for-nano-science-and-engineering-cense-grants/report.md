# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The Centre for Nano Science and Engineering (CeNSE) Grants scheme, administered by the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore, is a deep-tech incubation initiative focused on nanoscience and engineering. Established in 2010, CeNSE pursues interdisciplinary research across nanoscale systems and offers M.Tech and PhD programs with strong industry integration. The scheme supports students, researchers, startups, and industry partners through access to state-of-the-art facilities, fellowships, internships, placements, and technology transfer. The application portal is https://cense.iisc.ac.in/for-students/, and the scheme was last updated in 2024.

### Objectives
- Pursue interdisciplinary research across nanoscale systems  
- Offer M.Tech and PhD programs in nanoscience and engineering  
- Provide access to state-of-the-art nanofabrication and characterization facilities  
- Support industry collaboration and technology transfer  
- Incubate deep-tech startups through INCeNSE with infrastructure and mentoring  
- Align with national missions such as the India Semiconductor Mission  
- Deliver training programs in semiconductor technology for students and tribal communities  
- Foster innovation in deep-tech domains including quantum technologies, neuromorphic computing, and nanorobotics  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Program | Eligibility Criteria | Notes |
|---------|----------------------|-------|
| **M.Tech in Semiconductor Technology** | BE/BTech or equivalent in any discipline with GATE paper in EC, EE, ME, IN, XE-C, MT OR MSc or equivalent in Electronics or Physics with GATE paper in PH, IN, EC | Selection based on 100% GATE score (no interview) for regular program; 100% CAM score for CFTI mode of entry |
| **PhD in Nanoscience and Engineering** | ME/MTech or equivalent in any discipline OR BE/BTech in Engineering with valid GATE score OR MSc in Science with valid GATE score OR qualification in national exams (INSPIRE, CSIR UGC NET for JRF, DBT JRF, UGC NET for JRF, JEST) | Selection based on 100% GATE/CAM score for regular programs; interview performance post-shortlisting |
| **MTech Fellowship Program (2nd Year)** | All 2nd Year MTech students of CeNSE | Exclusively for current 2nd-year MTech students; selection via application review and industry sponsor inputs |
| **Summer Internship Program** | Participants must complete the online CeNSE summer/winter school program; top 25 performers invited | Must join before 31st May and complete at least 8 weeks; stipend of ₹5,000/month; no accommodation provided |
| **Industry Affiliate Program (IAP)** | Open to industry partners seeking facility usage, consultancy, research projects, training, talent access, networking, and distinguished lectures | No specific academic eligibility; engagement via formal application |

### Important Caveats & Warnings
> **Admission Restrictions**:  
> - M.Tech program admission is offered **only for the July session**  
> - PhD admissions are **typically for July session**, with limited January session for topic/field-specific admissions  
> - MTech Fellowship is **exclusively for 2nd Year MTech students of CeNSE**  
>   
> **Selection Process**:  
> - Fellowship selection based on application review **and inputs from industry sponsors**  
> - Regular M.Tech/PhD selection based on **100% GATE/CAM score** (no interview for regular M.Tech)  
> - Shortlisted candidates **must appear for an interview** at CeNSE  
>   
> **Internship Specifics**:  
> - Internship stipend is **₹5,000 per month** (as per Summer Internship Program)  
> - Interns **must join before 31st May** and complete **at least 8 weeks**  
> - **No accommodation provided** for interns  
>   
> **Academic Notes**:  
> - Getting a Minor in M.Tech program is **optional**; degree will not mention any Minor if not pursued  
> - M.Engg in Semiconductor Technology is a **one-year program** launched in Academic Year 2024-25 (Aug'24)  
> - Joint M.Tech in Semiconductor Technology with Taiwanese universities is a **two-year program** (1 year in India, 1 year in Taiwan)  
>   
> **Documentation Requirements**:  
> - Required documents include degree certificates, GATE/CAM scorecards, proof of national exam qualification, CV, SOP, LORs, and academic transcripts  
> - For CFTI mode of entry, CAM scorecard is mandatory  
> - For national exam routes (INSPIRE, CSIR UGC NET, etc.), proof of qualification is required
