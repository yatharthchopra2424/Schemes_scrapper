## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with client outcomes and scheme benefit realization  
2. Transparent fee structures with clear milestones for retainer and success fees  
3. Risk-adjusted pricing reflecting scheme competitiveness and processing timelines  
4. Customizable tiers to match client scale, readiness, and engagement depth  

**Scheme-specific note:** The Space Technology Incubation Centre (S-TIC) – ISRO program offers non-financial support (technical mentorship, infrastructure access, guidance) with no direct financial grants or funding. As such, traditional success fees based on grant amounts are not applicable. Revenue models should focus on value-added consulting services (e.g., application strengthening, IP strategy, investor readiness) rather than grant acquisition. Processing is rolling with competitive selection; typical incubation duration is fixed but subject to review, requiring sustained client engagement over 6–12 months.
