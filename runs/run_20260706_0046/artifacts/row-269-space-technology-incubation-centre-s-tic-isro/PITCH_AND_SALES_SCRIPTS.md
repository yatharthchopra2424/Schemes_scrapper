## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping space technology startups access strategic support from ISRO through the Space Technology Incubation Centre (S-TIC) programme. Given your work in satellite technology, remote sensing, or allied space domains, I wanted to explore whether ISRO’s incubation support—offering technical mentorship, infrastructure access, and ecosystem connections—could accelerate your development without dilution of equity.

### Discovery Questions
- What specific space technology domain are you working in (e.g., satellite systems, remote sensing, navigation, space applications)?
- When was your startup incorporated or registered, and what stage of development are you currently at (idea, prototype, early product)?
- Do you have a detailed project proposal or business plan outlining your technology’s innovation and feasibility?
- Have you developed a proof of concept or prototype that demonstrates your solution’s technical viability?
- What are the key qualifications and relevant experience of your founding team in space or deep tech domains?
- Have you considered or applied for any incubation or mentorship programmes from government or space agencies before?
- Are you seeking guidance on technology validation, testing, or access to specialised infrastructure for your space tech solution?
- Are you open to complying with ISRO’s safety, security, and confidentiality guidelines as part of the incubation process?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup or entrepreneur working on innovative ideas in space technology (satellite tech, remote sensing, navigation, space applications, allied fields)
- [ ] Has a certificate of incorporation or registration
- [ ] Has a detailed project proposal or business plan
- [ ] Has proof of concept or prototype details (if available)
- [ ] Has team profile and qualifications documented
- [ ] Has declared originality and compliance with guidelines
- [ ] Working on technology aligned with ISRO’s priorities (inferred from domain relevance)
- [ ] Willing to comply with ISRO’s safety, security, and confidentiality guidelines

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your work in [specific domain mentioned] aligns well with ISRO’s S-TIC programme. The Space Technology Incubation Centre offers selected startups access to ISRO’s technical expertise, mentorship from scientists, infrastructure support, and guidance on technology development—without requiring equity dilution. Let me share how this could specifically benefit your venture.
