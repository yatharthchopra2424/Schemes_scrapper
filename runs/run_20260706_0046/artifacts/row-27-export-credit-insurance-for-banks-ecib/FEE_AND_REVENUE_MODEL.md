## Pricing Principles

1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the financial benefit delivered — ECIB provides insurance cover up to 90% of loan amount, reducing bank risk and enabling increased lending to exporters.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect scheme risk profile; ECIB has moderate processing time (claim settlement after exhaustive recovery efforts) and proven payouts (e.g., ₹2.37 crore, ₹10.72 crore claims), supporting mid-tier success fees.
3. **Transparency & Predictability**: Clear retainer for advisory work (policy application, documentation, liaison with ECGC); success fee contingent on successful claim settlement or policy issuance.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: ECIB involves moderate operational risk due to requirement for banks to exhaust recovery efforts before claim submission, but high success likelihood given ECGC’s established claim settlement track record (evidenced by multiple large payouts in crawled data). Processing time averages 3–6 months from default to claim settlement, justifying a 3-month lag in revenue projections.
