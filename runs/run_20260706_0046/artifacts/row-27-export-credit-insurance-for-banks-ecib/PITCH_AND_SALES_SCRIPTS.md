## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping banks and financial institutions mitigate risks in export lending through government-backed schemes like the Export Credit Insurance for Banks (ECIB) offered by ECGC. This scheme is designed specifically for institutions extending pre-shipment and post-shipment credit to Indian exporters. I’d like to understand your current export credit portfolio and risk exposure to see if ECIB could add value to your lending operations.

### Discovery Questions
1. What types of export credit facilities (pre-shipment/post-shipment) do you currently extend to exporters?
2. Are you lending to Indian exporters engaged in goods or services exports with overseas buyers?
3. What is the typical tenure and value of your export credit facilities?
4. Do you face challenges related to buyer default due to insolvency, protracted default, or political events in regions like West Asia?
5. Are you looking to increase lending to MSME exporters but constrained by perceived credit risk?
6. What is your current process for assessing buyer creditworthiness and country risk?
7. Have you encountered non-performing assets (NPAs) in your export loan portfolio due to overseas buyer default?
8. Are you seeking ways to reduce risk premiums or improve confidence in cross-border trade financing?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] The client is a bank or financial institution in India
- [ ] They extend pre-shipment and/or post-shipment credit to exporters
- [ ] The exporter is an Indian entity engaged in export of goods or services
- [ ] The transaction involves an overseas buyer
- [ ] The credit facility is for a bonafide export transaction as per RBI guidelines
- [ ] The client seeks risk mitigation against commercial and political risks of non-payment
- [ ] The client aims to support MSME exporters or reduce NPAs in export lending
- [ ] The client is open to insurance solutions where the bank pays the premium

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it seems you’re actively managing export credit risk and looking for ways to lend more confidently to exporters—especially MSMEs—while protecting your portfolio. The Export Credit Insurance for Banks (ECIB) scheme by ECGC is designed exactly for this: it provides insurance cover up to 90% of the loan amount, letting you lend more freely against export receivables. Would you like me to walk you through how it works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Banks often hesitate to extend sufficient credit to exporters—particularly MSMEs—due to risks of non-payment by overseas buyers arising from insolvency, protracted default, or political events. This leads to conservative lending, higher NPAs in export loan portfolios, and constrained working capital access for exporters, ultimately affecting India’s export realization and growth.

**Scheme intro:**
The Export Credit Insurance for Banks (ECIB), administered by the Export Credit Guarantee Corporation of India Limited (ECGC), provides insurance cover up to 90% of the loan amount extended by banks to exporters for pre-shipment and post-shipment finance. The premium is paid by the bank, and claims are settled in case of buyer default due to insolvency, protracted default, or political events. The scheme operates on a rolling basis with applications via the portal: https://www.ecgcltd.in.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You extend pre-shipment and/or post-shipment credit to Indian exporters with overseas buyers  
- Your credit facilities are for bonafide export transactions as per RBI guidelines  
- You seek protection against commercial and political risks of non-payment  
- You aim to support MSME exporters or strengthen your export loan portfolio
