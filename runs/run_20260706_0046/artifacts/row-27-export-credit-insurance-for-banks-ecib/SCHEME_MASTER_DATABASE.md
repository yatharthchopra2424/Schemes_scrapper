### Export Credit Insurance for Banks (ECIB)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Insurance
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Export Credit Guarantee Corporation of India Limited (ECGC)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.ecgcltd.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Banks and financial institutions in India
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Banking/Financial Services
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must extend pre-shipment and post-shipment credit to exporters; exporter must be an Indian entity engaged in export of goods or services; transaction must involve an overseas buyer; credit facility must be for a bonafide export transaction as per RBI guidelines

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Insurance cover up to 90% of the loan amount extended by banks to exporters for pre-shipment and post-shipment finance
- Disbursement mode: Claim settlement in case of buyer default due to insolvency, protracted default, or political events
- Duration of benefit: One-time per covered facility (policy-based)

#### Documents Required
1. Loan sanction letter
2. Export order or contract
3. Invoice and shipping documents
4. Buyer’s creditworthiness details
5. Bank’s due diligence report
6. Policy application form
7. Details of security offered
8. RBI approval if applicable

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Banks approach ECGC with a request for ECIB cover for specific export credit facilities.
2. Submit details of the exporter, overseas buyer, nature of goods/services, value, and tenure of credit.
3. ECGC evaluates the risk based on buyer country, creditworthiness, and transaction type.
4. Upon approval, issue of insurance policy with terms and conditions.
5. Bank disburses loan to exporter under the covered facility.
6. In case of default, bank files claim with ECGC after due diligence and recovery attempts.
7. ECGC processes claim and settles payable amount as per policy terms.
