## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are calibrated to the tangible outcomes delivered — specifically, assistance in accessing seed funding, grants, and investment opportunities through the TBI’s network, as evidenced in the scheme’s financial support description.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the moderate risk profile of this scheme; while the TBI facilitates access to funding, direct quantum is not specified, and continued support depends on performance and milestones, introducing variability in outcomes.
3. **Transparency & Predictability**: Retainer fees cover ongoing advisory work (application support, documentation, networking), while success fees are contingent on measurable outcomes like grant approvals or investment closures.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Given the rolling basis application status and typical incubation duration of 1–3 years (per evidence), revenue realization may lag; success fees are structured to align with milestone-based disbursements observed in similar incubation programs.
