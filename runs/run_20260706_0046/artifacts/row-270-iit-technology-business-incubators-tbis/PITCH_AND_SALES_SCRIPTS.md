# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping technology-based startups and entrepreneurs access incubation support through premier institutions like IIT Bombay's Technology Business Incubator (TBI). Many founders I speak with are looking for mentorship, infrastructure, and investor connections to scale their innovations—does that align with what you're exploring for your venture?

### Discovery Questions
1. What stage is your technology-based startup currently at (idea, prototype, MVP, or early revenue)?
2. When was your company incorporated, and are you based in India?
3. What specific technology domain or innovation are you working on (e.g., AI, biotech, cleantech, hardware)?
4. Do you have a prototype or proof of concept developed, or are you still in the ideation phase?
5. Are you seeking access to lab facilities, mentorship from IIT faculty, or investor networks?
6. Have you incorporated your startup, or are you planning to do so soon?
7. What are your primary goals for the next 6–12 months (e.g., product development, fundraising, market validation)?
8. Are you open to a structured incubation program with milestones and performance-based support?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Technology-based startup or entrepreneur
- [ ] Innovation quotient and scalability demonstrated
- [ ] Alignment with IIT Bombay's focus areas (as assessed during selection)
- [ ] Incorporation documents available (if applicable)
- [ ] Business plan or project proposal prepared
- [ ] Team member profiles and CVs ready
- [ ] Proof of concept or prototype details available
- [ ] IP details or patent filings (if any) documented
- [ ] Funding details or financial projections prepared
- [ ] Willing to undergo screening, evaluation, and interview/pitch session
- [ ] Accepts that support is subject to space/resource availability and performance milestones
- [ ] Understands potential equity or revenue-sharing arrangements
- [ ] Comfortable with program duration typically limited to 1–3 years

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you've shared, it sounds like you're building a technology-driven venture with clear innovation potential—exactly the kind of founder IIT Bombay's Technology Business Incubator (TBI) is designed to support. The TBI offers access to IIT Bombay’s research infrastructure, mentorship, investor networks, and seed funding assistance to help startups like yours scale effectively.
