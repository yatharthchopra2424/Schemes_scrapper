# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The IIT Technology Business Incubators (TBIs) scheme, administered by the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IIT Bombay), is a pan-India incubation program designed to foster technology-based entrepreneurship and support the growth of startups through infrastructure, mentorship, and access to funding networks. The scheme operates on a rolling basis with no fixed deadlines, allowing continuous applications throughout the year.

### Objectives
- Foster technology-based entrepreneurship  
- Provide infrastructure and mentorship to startups  
- Convert research activity into entrepreneurial ventures  
- Support innovation and startup growth  
- Facilitate industry-academia collaboration

### Required Documents
1. Business plan or project proposal  
2. Team member profiles and CVs  
3. Proof of concept or prototype details  
4. Incorporation documents (if applicable)  
5. IP details or patent filings (if any)  
6. Funding details or financial projections  

### Application Process Flowchart
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Visit IIT Bombay TBI website/portal] --> B[Review eligibility and program details]
    B --> C[Submit online application with business plan, team details, innovation description]
    C --> D[Screening and evaluation by TBI selection committee]
    D --> E{Shortlisted?}
    E -->|Yes| F[Attend interview/pitch session]
    E -->|No| G[Application rejected; feedback may be provided]
    F --> H{Successful pitch/interview?}
    H -->|Yes| I[Receive incubation offer]
    H -->|No| J[Application rejected]
    I --> K[Complete onboarding formalities]
    K --> L[Begin incubation period]
    L --> M[Ongoing support: mentorship, infrastructure, funding access]
    M --> N{Performance & milestones met?}
    N -->|Yes| O[Continued support]
    N -->|No| P[Support may be withdrawn; equity/revenue-sharing may apply]
    O --> Q[Program completion (typically 1-3 years)]
    P --> Q
```

### Key Caveats & Conditions
- Incubation support is subject to availability of space and resources  
- Continued support depends on performance and milestones achieved  
- Equity or revenue-sharing may be applicable in some cases  
- Program duration is typically limited to a fixed period (e.g., 1-3 years)  
- Rolling basis application with no fixed deadlines
