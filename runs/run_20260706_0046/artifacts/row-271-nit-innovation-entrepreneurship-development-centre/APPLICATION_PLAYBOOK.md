# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a student, faculty, or alumni of NITK Surathkal  
- [ ] Applicant is engaged in an innovative project or startup idea  
- [ ] Applicant has identified a specific program or grant under the NIT Innovation & Entrepreneurship Development Centre  
- [ ] Applicant meets the specific eligibility criteria for the chosen program/grant (varies by opportunity)  
- [ ] Applicant has a project proposal or business plan ready  
- [ ] Applicant can provide team member details and qualifications  
- [ ] Applicant has proof of concept or prototype details  
- [ ] Applicant can provide identity and address proof  
- [ ] Applicant has any existing patent or research documentation (if applicable)  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, etc.)  
- Address proof  
- PAN card  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (if applicable for startups)  
- GST registration (if applicable)  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of last 2 years (if applicable)  
- Incorporation/Registration certificate (for startups/companies)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Project proposal or business plan  
- Team member details and qualifications  
- Proof of concept or prototype details  
- Identity and address proof of applicants  
- Any existing patent or research documentation  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be self-attested or certified as required  
- File size: Each document should be under 5 MB for upload  
- File naming: Use format `ApplicantName_DocumentType_Date.pdf` (e.g., `RajeshKumar_ProjectProposal_20240515.pdf`)  
- Language: Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, provide a certified translation  
- Photographs: Recent passport-size photo with white background  
- Certificates: Must be legible and issued by recognized authorities

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A detailed project report or business plan is required as part of the scheme-specific documents. While no fixed template is mandated, it should include:  
- Executive summary  
- Problem statement and solution  
- Market analysis  
- Technology or innovation description  
- Business model  
- Financial projections  
- Implementation roadmap  
- Team background  
- Risk assessment  
If unsure about depth or format, **verify with the nodal officer** (dean.ri@nitk.edu.in).  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal at https://www.nitk.ac.in and navigate to the relevant program/grant page.  
2. Fill in all personal and academic/professional details accurately.  
3. Upload documents in the specified format (PDF preferred, under 5 MB each).  
4. Ensure the project proposal clearly highlights innovation, feasibility, and alignment with the centre’s objectives.  
5. Double-check that all mandatory fields are completed before submission.  
6. Save a draft before final submission to avoid data loss.  
7. Use the exact name as per identity documents throughout the form.  
8. Provide a valid and frequently monitored email ID and mobile number for communication.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All personal details filled correctly and match identity documents  
- [ ] Project proposal/business plan is complete and well-structured  
- [ ] Team details include qualifications and roles  
- [ ] Proof of concept/prototype details are adequate (photos, videos, descriptions)  
- [ ] Identity and address proof are valid and legible  
- [ ] All documents are self-attested and properly named  
- [ ] Application form is reviewed for spelling and consistency  
- [ ] Declarations and consent boxes are checked  
- [ ] Copy of submitted application and documents saved for records

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the designated portal or email to dean.ri@nitk.edu.in as specified for the chosen program.  
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation or save the sent email with timestamp.  
- Store the application reference number (if provided) in the case file.  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days after submission if no acknowledgment is received.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and save the approval letter or email from NITK Surathkal  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned support (mentorship, infrastructure access, or financial grant amount if applicable)  
- [ ] If financial support is granted (e.g., DST NIDHI Prayas Grant example: Rs. 9.3 lakh), note the disbursement schedule and utilization terms  
- [ ] Prepare utilization certificate as per the funding agency’s requirements (if applicable)  
- [ ] Track milestone progress and maintain records for reporting  
- [ ] Coordinate with the centre for access to incubation infrastructure, mentorship, and networking events  
- [ ] Assist in applying for further government grants or funding schemes as supported by the centre  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with IP policies and periodic review requirements  
- [ ] Calculate and process success fee as per consultancy agreement
