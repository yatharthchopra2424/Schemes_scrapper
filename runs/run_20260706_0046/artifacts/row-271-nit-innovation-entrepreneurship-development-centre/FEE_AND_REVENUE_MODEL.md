## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, documentation preparation, and application submission.
2. Success fee is contingent on securing financial support or grant approval under the scheme.
3. Fees are aligned with the effort required, which varies by client complexity and funding competitiveness.
4. Scheme-specific note: The NIT Innovation & Entrepreneurship Development Centre facilitates access to funding (e.g., DST NIDHI Prayas Grant up to ₹9.3 lakh, CSR grants, sponsored research) but does not guarantee financial support. Success depends on external funding availability and project merit, resulting in a moderate risk profile. Processing times vary by program (some rolling, others with fixed deadlines), which may affect revenue realization timing.
