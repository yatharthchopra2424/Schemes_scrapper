# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping students, faculty, and alumni of NITK Surathkal access innovation and entrepreneurship support through the NIT Innovation & Entrepreneurship Development Centre. I understand you’re engaged in innovative projects or startup ideas, and I’d like to explore whether this centre’s incubation infrastructure, mentorship, and funding pathways could accelerate your work—without any obligation on your part.

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you currently a student, faculty member, or alumnus of NITK Surathkal?
2. What stage is your innovative project or startup idea at—concept, prototype, or early-stage venture?
3. Are you seeking mentorship from faculty or industry experts to refine your technology or business model?
4. Do you need access to incubation infrastructure (labs, workspace, or prototyping facilities) to develop your idea?
5. Are you looking for support in applying for external grants like DST NIDHI Prayas or CSR funding?
6. Have you already filed or are you planning to file for patent protection on your innovation?
7. Are you interested in participating in entrepreneurship development programs or networking events?
8. Do you have a clear project proposal or business plan ready for submission?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is a student, faculty, or alumnus of NITK Surathkal
- [ ] Engaged in an innovative project or startup idea
- [ ] Has a project proposal or business plan
- [ ] Can provide team member details and qualifications
- [ ] Has proof of concept or prototype details
- [ ] Can submit identity and address proof
- [ ] May have existing patent or research documentation (if applicable)
- [ ] Target beneficiary aligns with scheme: students, faculty, alumni, or startups

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like the NIT Innovation & Entrepreneurship Development Centre could be an excellent fit—offering incubation support, mentorship, and pathways to grants like the DST NIDHI Prayas (which has provided up to Rs. 9.3 lakh to startups like Oxyheal Pvt. Ltd). Let me share how it works and how we’ve helped similar innovators at NITK secure support.

**Problem framing:**
Innovators at NITK often struggle to access structured mentorship, incubation infrastructure, and funding pathways to turn their ideas into viable startups or commercialized technologies—despite having strong technical concepts and prototypes.

**Scheme intro:**
The NIT Innovation & Entrepreneurship Development Centre, administered by the National Institute of Technology Karnataka (NITK), Surathkal, provides incubation support, mentorship from faculty and industry experts, networking opportunities, patent filing assistance, and help applying for government grants—including access to funding like the DST NIDHI Prayas Grant (e.g., Rs. 9.3 lakh awarded to Oxyheal Pvt. - You are engaged in an innovative project or startup idea  
- You have or can prepare a project proposal or business plan
