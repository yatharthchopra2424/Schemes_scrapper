## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the indirect financial benefits facilitated by the NASSCOM 10000 Startups Programme, such as investor connections and funding access, rather than direct grant amounts.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the competitive selection process and variable outcomes in investor engagement, with no guaranteed funding.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The Growth tier pricing aligns with early-stage startups (idea, prototype, or early revenue) that are the primary beneficiaries of the programme.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Given the rolling application basis and competitive selection cycles, revenue realization may lag 3–6 months post-engagement, depending on pitch event schedules and investor response times.
