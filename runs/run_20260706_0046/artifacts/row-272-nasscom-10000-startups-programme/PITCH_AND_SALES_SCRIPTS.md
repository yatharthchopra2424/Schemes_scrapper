</think># Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I'm [Your Name] from [Your Firm]. We specialise in helping early-stage technology startups access government-backed incubation and acceleration programmes like the NASSCOM 10000 Startups Programme. I’d love to understand where you are in your journey and see if this programme could be a strategic fit for your growth.

### Discovery Questions
- When was your startup incorporated, and what stage are you currently at — idea, prototype, early revenue, or scaling?
- What technology domain are you working in (e.g., software, hardware, AI, IoT, fintech, healthtech, or other emerging tech)?
- Do you have a scalable business model, and are you seeking mentorship, investor connections, or incubation support?
- Are the founders actively committed and available to participate in mentorship sessions, workshops, and pitch events?
- Have you previously applied to any incubation or acceleration programmes, and what was your experience?
- Are you looking for support in accessing angel investors, venture capital, or corporate partnerships?
- Do you currently have a product description, pitch deck, or prototype ready to share?
- Are you based in India and incorporated as a legal entity there?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup is incorporated in India  
- [ ] Working on an innovative product or service in technology domains (software, hardware, AI, IoT, fintech, healthtech, etc.)  
- [ ] Has a scalable business model  
- [ ] Led by committed founders  
- [ ] At idea, prototype, or early revenue stage (preferred, though no strict incorporation year limit)  
- [ ] Seeking mentorship, investor access, incubation, or market linkages  
- [ ] Able to provide required documents (Incorporation certificate, PAN, founder CVs, pitch deck, tech description, business plan, funding details)  
- [ ] Willing to comply with reporting and participation requirements if selected  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your startup aligns well with the NASSCOM 10000 Startups Programme — especially given your focus on [mention specific tech domain from response] and stage of development. This programme doesn’t offer direct grants but provides powerful indirect support through mentorship, investor connects, and incubation. Let me walk you through how it works and how we’ve helped similar startups benefit from it.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Early-stage technology startups often struggle to access experienced mentors, investor networks, and structured incubation support — especially outside major hubs. Without these, validating product-market fit, refining business models, and securing follow-on funding becomes significantly harder and slower.

**Scheme intro:**
The NASSCOM 10000 Startups Programme, implemented by NASSCOM (National Association of Software and Service Companies), is a pan-India incubation and acceleration initiative that supports early-stage technology startups through mentorship, access to investors and corporates, incubation infrastructure, technical and business workshops, pitch events, demo days, and global market access — all facilitated via a competitive, rolling application process at https://10000startups.com.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Incorporated in India as a technology-driven startup  
- Working on an innovative product/service in a high-potential domain (e.g., AI, IoT, fintech, healthtech)  
- Led by committed founders with a scalable business model
