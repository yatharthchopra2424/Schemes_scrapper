### NASSCOM 10000 Startups Programme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Incubation
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** NASSCOM (National Association of Software and Service Companies)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://10000startups.com
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (applications accepted throughout the year with periodic selection cycles)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Early-stage technology startups incorporated in India
- Age of business: No restriction on year of incorporation; preference given to idea, prototype, or early revenue stage
- Sector: Software, hardware, AI, IoT, fintech, healthtech, and other emerging technologies
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have a scalable business model and be led by committed founders

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Indirect financial support through access to investor network, demo days, and pitch events; mentorship from industry leaders; incubation support; office infrastructure; technical and business workshops; pitch events; demo days; global market access opportunities; connections with potential customers, partners, and venture capital firms
- Disbursement mode: Non-monetary benefits (mentorship, infrastructure, network access); financial support facilitated indirectly via investor connections
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing during programme participation (typically tied to selection cycle and onboarding period)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN card of the startup
3. Founder profiles and CVs
4. Product description or pitch deck
5. Details of technology or innovation
6. Business model and revenue plan (if applicable)
7. Any existing funding or investment details

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the official NASSCOM 10000 Startups website at https://10000startups.com
2. Click on the 'Apply' or 'Register' section
3. Fill out the online application form with details about the startup, founders, product/service, and traction
4. Submit the application along with required documents
5. Applications are reviewed by the NASSCOM selection committee
6. Shortlisted startups are contacted for interviews or pitch sessions
7. Final selection is based on innovation, scalability, team strength, and market potential
8. Selected startups are onboarded into the programme with access to mentorship, incubation, and investor connect opportunities
