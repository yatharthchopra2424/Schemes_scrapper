# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Startup operates in an emerging technology domain (IoT, Blockchain, FinTech, AI, AR/VR, ESDM, Data Science & Analytics, Medical Electronics & Healthcare, Gaming & Animation, Machine Learning, Cyber Security, etc.)
- [ ] Startup demonstrates innovativeness in its technology or solution
- [ ] Startup has a strong founding team with relevant technical and business expertise
- [ ] Startup presents a sound business proposal with clear value proposition and market potential
- [ ] Startup is registered as a legal entity in India (Private Limited, LLP, OPC, or Partnership)
- [ ] Startup is not older than 7 years from date of incorporation (as per standard startup definition)
- [ ] Startup has not exceeded INR 100 crore turnover in any previous financial year
- [ ] Startup is engaged in software product development or embedded electronics (aligned with NGIS linkage)
- [ ] Startup is willing to undergo evaluation of Proof of Concept (POC), product development, and market connect
- [ ] Startup commits to active participation in mentoring, networking, and investor connect activities

> **Note**: Eligibility is competitive and based on predefined criteria evaluated by STPI CoE selection committee. Final selection is not guaranteed even if basic criteria are met.

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation / Partnership Deed / LLP Agreement
- [ ] PAN Card of the company
- [ ] GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)
- [ ] Udyam Registration Certificate (MSME)
- [ ] Audited Financial Statements (ITR) for last 2 years (or CA-certified projections for new startups)
- [ ] Bank Statement (last 6 months)
- [ ] Founder(s) PAN and Aadhaar cards
- [ ] Passport-sized photographs of all directors/partners
- [ ] Registered office address proof (utility bill or rental agreement)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- [ ] Detailed Project Proposal (5–10 pages) covering:
  - Problem statement and innovative solution
  - Technology stack and domain (must align with emerging tech: IoT, AI, Blockchain, etc.)
  - Proof of Concept (POC) details or prototype status
  - Market analysis and go-to-market strategy
  - Revenue model and financial projections (3–5 years)
  - Team background and roles
  - Funding requirements and utilization plan
- [ ] Pitch Deck (10–15 slides) summarizing the proposal for presentation to selection committee
- [ ] Video Demo (2–3 minutes) of prototype or MVP (if available)
- [ ] Letters of Support or Intent from potential customers, partners, or academic institutions (optional but recommended)
- [ ] IP Details (if any): Patent filings, copyrights, or software registration details
- [ ] Declaration of originality and ownership of technology/IP by founders

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)
- [ ] File names follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_Version_Date.pdf` (e.g., `STPI_CoE_Proposal_v1_20250615.pdf`)
- [ ] All certificates are valid and not expired (check Udyam, GST, Incorporation dates)
- [ ] Financial documents are certified by a Chartered Accountant (if audited statements unavailable)
- [ ] Declarations are signed and dated by authorized signatory (director/partner)
- [ ] Documents are in English or accompanied by certified English translation
- [ ] No watermarks, draft labels, or visible edits on official certificates

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- A formal Detailed Project Report (DPR) is **not explicitly mandated**, but a **comprehensive Project Proposal (5–10 pages)** is required as part of the application.
- If a DPR is requested during evaluation, it should include:
  - Executive Summary
  - Technology Innovation and Novelty
  - Market Need and Target Audience
  - Product Development Roadmap
  - Manufacturing and Operations Plan (if applicable)
  - Financial Plan and Funding Requirements
  - Risk Assessment and Mitigation
  - Team Composition and Advisory Board
  - Sustainability and Scalability Plan
  - Annexures: Prototypes, Test Results, IP Details, Letters of Support
- **Verify with nodal officer** whether a full DPR is needed based on the specific STPI CoE centre’s requirements.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal via the STPI CoE webpage: https://stpi.in/en/centre-of-entrepreneurship
2. Look for “Apply for Incubation” or “Submit Proposal” section (note: direct link may vary; navigate through STPI Labs Network or CoE pages if needed)
3. Fill in all fields accurately — **no blank fields allowed**; use “Not Applicable” if relevant
4. Ensure startup name, PAN, and incorporation number match exactly across all documents
5. Clearly state the emerging technology domain (e.g., “AI-based predictive maintenance for manufacturing”) in the focus area field
6. Attach all documents in the specified order and format as per upload guidelines
7. Double-check file sizes and names before final upload
8. Review the declaration section carefully — confirm originality of IP and accuracy of information
9. Take a screenshot of the final submission confirmation page before exiting

> **Portal Note**: The STPI website does not host a standalone application form; proposals are submitted via email to the respective STPI CoE centre or through the STPI Startup Ecosystem portal. Confirm submission mode with the nodal officer.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached and correctly named
- [ ] Project proposal clearly defines the emerging technology domain and innovation
- [ ] Financial projections are realistic and aligned with stage of startup
- [ ] Founder details match incorporation and PAN records
- [ ] Declarations are signed and dated
- [ ] No contradictions between proposal, pitch deck, and video demo
- [ ] File sizes are within limits and formats are correct (PDF only)
- [ ] Reference number or application ID is generated and recorded upon submission
- [ ] All team members have consented to data sharing and participation in STPI activities

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed application via the designated mode (email or portal) as advised by the STPI CoE centre
- Upon submission:
  - Save screenshot of confirmation page or sent email with attachments
  - Store application reference number (if provided) in case file
  - Note down date and time of submission
  - Set calendar reminders for follow-up at 15, 30, and 45 days
- Store all submission evidence in the case folder:

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify approval letter from STPI CoE centre (check for validity period and terms)
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned support: infrastructure access, mentoring hours, networking events, investor connect facilitation
- [ ] Note: Direct seed funding is **not provided** under STPI CoE; funding opportunities are facilitated via linked schemes like NGIS (up to INR 25 Lakhs) or external investors
- [ ] If applying for NGIS seed funding separately, initiate utilization certificate process upon disbursement
- [ ] Maintain monthly progress reports as required by the CoE centre (if applicable)
- [ ] Participate in mandatory mentoring, training, and demo day events
- [ ] Acknowledge STPI CoE support in all public communications, pitch decks, and investor meetings
- [ ] Submit final outcome report upon completion of incubation term (typically 6–12 months)
- [ ] Coordinate with finance team to track any success fee or revenue-sharing agreement (if applicable under linked schemes)
- [ ] Preserve all approvals, certificates, and correspondence for audit and future funding applications

> **Note**: STPI CoE does not provide direct grants; value is in-kind support (labs, mentorship, network). Financial support access is indirect and success-dependent.  
> Source: STPI CoE Key Facts – “Financial Support: STPI CoEs facilitate funding and investment opportunities... managed under linked schemes like NGIS”
