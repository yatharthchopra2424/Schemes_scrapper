## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit delivered — for STPI CoEs, this is primarily facilitated access to seed funding of up to ₹25 Lakhs per startup via linked schemes like NGIS, not direct grants.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s competitive selection process and non-guaranteed funding outcomes; retainer covers effort for proposal development, eligibility assessment, and investor readiness.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Pricing tiers match the startup lifecycle — Starter for idea-stage (not applicable here), Growth for early-stage startups seeking seed fund access (primary applicability), Scale/Enterprise for later-stage companies beyond CoE scope.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The STPI CoE scheme has a medium confidence rating and rolling application basis, with processing time dependent on proposal evaluation and POC validation stages. Revenue realization typically lags 3–6 months due to multi-step selection (proposal → POC → investor connect), necessitating phased billing aligned with milestones.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Retainer for STPI Centres of Entrepreneurship (CoE) incubation support services under the Software Technology Parks of India (STPI), Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).  
**Scope**: Includes eligibility assessment, proposal refinement, documentation support, and coordination with STPI CoE selection panels for startups in emerging tech domains (IoT, AI, Blockchain, etc.).

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for facilitated access to funding and investment opportunities through STPI Centres of Entrepreneurship (CoE), implemented by Software Technology Parks of India (STPI), Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).  
**Trigger**: Upon successful introduction to angel investors/VCs or securing seed fund commitment via linked schemes (e.g., NGIS offering up to ₹25 Lakhs per startup).
