# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good [morning/afternoon], I'm calling from [Your Firm Name]. We specialise in helping technology startups access government incubation support, particularly through initiatives like the STPI Centres of Entrepreneurship (CoE) programme. I understand you're working in an emerging technology space — could you share a bit about your current focus and stage? This helps me see if STPI CoE support might be relevant for you.

### Discovery Questions
1. What emerging technology domain is your startup focused on (e.g., IoT, AI/ML, Blockchain, FinTech, AR/VR, ESDM, Data Science, Medical Electronics, Gaming, Animation, Cyber Security, etc.)?
2. When was your company incorporated, and how long have you been operating?
3. Are you currently developing a Proof of Concept (POC) or working on product development in your technology domain?
4. Do you have a founding team in place, and what are their key strengths or domain expertise?
5. Are you seeking mentorship, infrastructure support (like labs or software tools), or connections to investors and industry experts?
6. Have you previously applied for any government incubation or startup support schemes?
7. What are your immediate goals for the next 6–12 months (e.g., MVP development, market validation, fundraising)?
8. Are you based in or willing to operate from one of the STPI CoE locations across India (e.g., Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Lucknow, Bhubaneswar, etc.)?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup operates in an eligible emerging technology domain (IoT, Blockchain, FinTech, AI, AR/VR, ESDM, Data Science & Analytics, Medical Electronics & Healthcare, Gaming & Animation, Machine Learning, Cyber Security, etc.)
- [ ] Startup is incorporated and operational (no minimum age specified, but early-stage focus)
- [ ] Demonstrates innovativeness in idea or solution
- [ ] Has a capable and committed founding team
- [ ] Presents a sound business proposal or Proof of Concept (POC)
- [ ] Seeking support in infrastructure, mentorship, funding connections, or market access
- [ ] Willing to engage with STPI CoE selection and evaluation process
- [ ] Located in or able to access one of the 26+ STPI CoE centres pan-India

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you've shared, it sounds like your work in [specific domain from answer] aligns well with what the STPI Centres of Entrepreneurship (CoE) programme supports. They offer 360-degree support — including infrastructure, mentorship, investor connects, and help accessing seed funding up to Rs. 25 Lakhs through linked schemes like NGIS — specifically for startups in emerging tech. Would you like me to walk you through how it works and whether you might qualify?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Early-stage startups in emerging technologies often struggle to access specialised infrastructure (like IoT, AI, or blockchain labs), expert mentorship, and credible pathways to funding and market validation — especially outside major metro hubs. This can slow product development, limit investor confidence, and increase early failure rates.

**Scheme intro:**
The STPI Centres of Entrepreneurship (CoE) programme, implemented by the Software Technology Parks of India (STPI) under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), is a pan-India initiative that provides domain-specific incubation support to startups in emerging technologies. It offers access to specialised labs, mentorship from academia and industry, networking opportunities, and facilitation of funding and investment opportunities — including seed funding of up to Rs. 25 Lakhs per startup through linked schemes like the Next Generation Incubation Scheme (NGIS).

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your startup operates in [specific domain from discovery], which is an explicitly eligible emerging technology area for STPI CoE support.
- You have a founding team with relevant expertise — a key criterion in STPI’s selection based on team strength.
- You are developing a Proof of Concept or early product — showing innovativeness and proposal soundness, which are central to the evaluation criteria.
- You are seeking mentorship, infrastructure, or investor connects — all core benefits delivered through the CoE ecosystem.
