# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **STPI Centres of Entrepreneurship (CoE)** scheme is a pan-India initiative implemented by the **Software Technology Parks of India (STPI)** under the **Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)**. It is designed to foster innovation and entrepreneurship in emerging technology domains by providing a 360-degree support ecosystem to startups. The scheme operates on a **rolling basis** with no fixed deadlines, allowing continuous application and selection.

### Objectives
The scheme aims to:
- Deliver a vibrant platform & 360 degree support to start-ups
- Foster Entrepreneurship, Innovation, and knowledge exchange
- Build Leadership in Emerging Technologies
- Help start-ups create world-class IT / EDSM products indigenously
- Generate direct & indirect employment
- Spread awareness about the CoE through various media & channels
- Accept proposals on a rolling basis
- Select startups based on pre-defined criteria

### Eligibility Matrix
| Criteria | Details |
|---------|--------|
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Startups; Entrepreneurs |
| **Eligible Domains** | Internet of Things (IoT), Blockchain, FinTech, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Augmented & Virtual Reality (AR/VR), ESDM, Data Science & Analytics, Medical Electronics & Healthcare, Gaming & Animation, Machine Learning, Cyber Security, and other emerging technologies |
| **Selection Criteria** | Innovativeness, team strength, and soundness of business proposal |
| **Geographic Scope** | Pan-India |
| **Implementing Agency** | Software Technology Parks of India (STPI), Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) |
| **Scheme Status** | Active (Last Updated: 2026) |
| **Application Basis** | Rolling basis |

> **Key Caveats**  
> - Support is domain-specific and limited to emerging technology areas  
> - Selection is competitive and based on predefined criteria  
> - Funding and investment opportunities are facilitated but not guaranteed

> **Financial Support Note**  
> While STPI CoEs facilitate funding and investment, direct financial grants are primarily managed under linked schemes. The **Next Generation Incubation Scheme (NGIS)** provides **seed funding of up to Rs. 25 Lakhs per startup** based on innovativeness, novelty of solutions, team strength, and business proposal soundness. NGIS has a budgetary outlay of **Rs. 95.03 Crores over 3 years**, targeting **300 select start-ups**.

### Application Process
The application process for STPI Centres of Entrepreneurship (CoE) is structured as follows:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Submit Proposal for Incubation at STPI CoE] --> B{Proposal Review}
    B -->|Meets Pre-defined Criteria| C[Selection for Incubation]
    B -->|Does Not Meet Criteria| D[Rejection / Feedback for Resubmission]
    C --> E[Mentorship from Academia & Industry Eminent Personalities]
    E --> F[Evaluation of Proof of Concept (POC)]
    F --> G[Product Development Phase]
    G --> H[Market Connect Activities]
    H --> I[Interaction with Angel Investors/VCs for Funding Opportunities]
    I --> J[Benefit from Collaborative Ecosystem<br/>(Government, Industry, Academia, Investors)]
    J --> K[Graduation / Exit from Incubation]
    D --> A
```

### Supporting Evidence Summary
- STPI has launched **24 Centres of Entrepreneurship (CoEs)** across India, including Electropreneur Park (Delhi), MOTION (Pune), NEURON (Mohali), FinBlue (Chennai), IoT OpenLab (Bengaluru), APIARY (Gurugram), MedTech CoE (Lucknow), and others.
- STPI has expanded to **73 centres pan-India**, with **65 in Tier-II/III cities**, supporting IT/ITeS/ESDM exports and startup ecosystem development.
- STPI’s efforts have contributed to software exports growing from **Rs. 52 crores (1992-93) to Rs. 10,69,270.59 crores (2024-25)**, approx. **50% of national software exports** and **3.2% of India’s GDP**.
- The **Next Generation Incubation Scheme (NGIS)** is a key linked scheme offering seed funding, pre-incubation stipends, cloud credits, and soft support.
- STPI provides **state-of-the-art infrastructure** including **Tier-III Data Centres** (Bengaluru, Bhubaneswar, Chennai, Mohali, Vijayawada) and **specialized labs** for emerging technologies.
- The scheme aligns with national initiatives like **Startup India**, **Digital India**, and **National Policy on Software Products (NPSP) 2019**.
