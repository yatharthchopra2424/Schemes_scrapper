## 8D. Internal Data Handling Policy

(Standard data handling policy — encrypted cloud storage, 2FA, limited access, no personal devices,

All client data shall be handled in accordance with the following internal data handling policy:

- **Storage:** All client data is stored in encrypted cloud storage with access restricted to authorized personnel only.

- **Device Policy:** No personal devices may be used to access or store client data without explicit authorization and encryption.

- **Audit Logs:** All access to client data is logged and monitored for unauthorized access attempts.

under IIM Ahmedabad CIIE.CO – Bharat Inclusion Initiative must be retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit purposes.
