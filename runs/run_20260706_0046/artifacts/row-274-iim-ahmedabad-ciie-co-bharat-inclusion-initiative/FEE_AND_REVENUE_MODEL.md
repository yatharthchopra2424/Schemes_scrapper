## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the non-financial, high-touch nature of incubation support, where success is measured by investment readiness and follow-on funding rather than direct grants.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the absence of direct financial grants and the competitive, impact-driven selection process, success fees are set at the lower end of industry ranges to reflect outcome uncertainty.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The Growth tier pricing aligns with early-stage startups targeting underserved markets, ensuring affordability while compensating for intensive mentorship and network facilitation.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Bharat Inclusion Initiative has a rolling application process with no fixed timelines, but selection is competitive and based on innovation, scalability, and social impact. Processing time varies (typically 2–4 months for screening and interviews), which introduces revenue lag; therefore, retainer fees are critical to cover ongoing advisory work during the incubation period.
