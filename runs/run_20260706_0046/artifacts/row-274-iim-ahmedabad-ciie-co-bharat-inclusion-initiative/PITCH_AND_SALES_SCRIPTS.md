</think># Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, [Client Name]. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], where we help early-stage ventures focused on social impact access the right support programs to scale their mission. The IIM Ahmedabad CIIE.CO – Bharat Inclusion Initiative is specifically designed for startups and social enterprises working to solve pressing challenges faced by underserved and low-income communities in India — whether in financial inclusion, agriculture, health, or livelihoods. I’d love to learn about your venture and see if this initiative could be a strategic fit for your growth journey.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your venture incorporated or registered? (To assess if you're an early-stage venture)
2. What specific problem are you solving, and which underserved or low-income population in India are you serving?
3. Which sector does your solution primarily operate in — financial inclusion, agriculture, health, or livelihoods?
4. Can you share your current stage of development? Do you have a prototype, pilot, early customers, or traction?
5. Who are the founders, and what relevant experience do they bring to solving this problem?
6. Have you already developed a pitch deck or business plan that outlines your model, impact, and roadmap?
7. Are you looking for mentorship, strategic guidance, and access to investors or industry experts to help scale your impact?
8. Beyond funding, what kind of non-financial support do you feel would most accelerate your venture’s readiness for investment or market expansion?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Venture is incorporated or registered (Certificate of Incorporation/Registration available)
- [ ] Focused on innovative product, service, or business model
- [ ] Addresses needs of underserved and low-income populations in India
- [ ] Operates in one or more of: financial inclusion, agriculture, health, or livelihoods
- [ ] Early-stage venture (no strict age limit, but preference for startups/social enterprises)
- [ ] Has documentation of problem and target underserved population
- [ ] Can provide pitch deck or business plan
- [ ] Can share details of founding team and their backgrounds
- [ ] Willing to participate in screening, interviews, or pitch sessions if shortlisted

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your venture is tackling a meaningful challenge for underserved communities in [mention sector, e.g., agriculture/health] — exactly the kind of impact-driven innovation the IIM Ahmedabad CIIE.CO – Bharat Inclusion Initiative was created to support. While this program doesn’t offer direct grants, it provides high-value incubation, mentorship from IIM faculty and industry experts, and access to a powerful investor network — all designed to get ventures investment-ready. Let me walk you through how it works and whether it aligns with your goals.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many early-stage ventures with strong social missions struggle to gain traction because they lack access to structured mentorship, strategic guidance, and networks that can help them refine their model, achieve product-market fit, and become investment-ready — especially when serving complex, low-income markets where traditional accelerators may not understand the nuances.

**Scheme intro:**
The IIM Ahmedabad CIIE.CO – Bharat Inclusion Initiative is a pan-India incubation program administered by CIIE.CO (formerly Centre for Innovation Incubation and Entrepreneurship) at IIM Ahmedabad. It does not provide direct grants or seed funding but offers comprehensive non-financial support including incubation, mentorship from industry experts and IIM Ahmedabad faculty, access to CIIE.CO’s investor and partner network, guidance on product-market fit and scaling, and opportunities for follow-on funding and pilot collaborations — all aimed at helping early-stage ventures become investment-ready.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your venture is working on an innovative solution for underserved and low-income populations in India  
- You are focused on one or more of the priority sectors: financial inclusion, agriculture, health, or livelihoods  
- You have a clear problem statement and can document the target community you serve  
- You are at an early stage and seeking strategic guidance to scale impact and attract follow-on funding
