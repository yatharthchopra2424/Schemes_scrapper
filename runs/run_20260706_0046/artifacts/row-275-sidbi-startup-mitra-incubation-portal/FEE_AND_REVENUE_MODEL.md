## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, documentation preparation, and application submission support for DPIIT recognition and scheme navigation.
2. Success fee is contingent on successful connection to funding schemes (e.g., SISFS, FFS, CGSS) via the portal, reflecting value delivered.
3. Tiered pricing aligns with client scale and complexity of incubation support needed.
4. Scheme-specific note: The SIDBI Startup Mitra portal does not provide direct financial support but facilitates access to schemes like SISFS (grants up to ₹20 lakh), FFS, and CGSS. Success is measured by enabling access to these schemes, with moderate risk due to dependency on incubator selection and partner terms.
