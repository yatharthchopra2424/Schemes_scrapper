# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping technology-enabled startups access incubation and acceleration support through government-backed platforms like the SIDBI Startup Mitra – Incubation Portal. This portal connects DPIIT-recognised startups with mentors, investors, and funding schemes such as the Startup India Seed Fund Scheme and Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups. I’d love to learn about your startup’s journey and see if we can help you unlock these resources.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated as a Private Limited Company, Registered Partnership Firm, LLP, or Cooperative Society?  
2. Do you hold a valid DPIIT recognition certificate under the Startup India initiative?  
3. Is your business model technology-enabled and focused on innovation, development, or improvement of products, processes, or services?  
4. What is your current annual turnover, and has it exceeded INR 200 crore in any financial year since incorporation?  
5. Are you working on a scalable business model with high potential for wealth and employment generation?  
6. Was your startup formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business?  
7. Have you already applied to any incubation programs or government schemes like SISFS, FFS, or CGSS?  
8. What stage is your startup at — idea, prototype, market entry, or scaling — and what support do you need most right now?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Incorporated as Private Limited Company, Registered Partnership Firm, LLP, or Cooperative Society  
- [ ] DPIIT-recognised startup  
- [ ] Technology-enabled business idea with innovation focus  
- [ ] Not formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business  
- [ ] Scalable business model with high potential for wealth and employment generation  
- [ ] Annual turnover does not exceed INR 200 crore in any financial year since incorporation  
- [ ] Existence does not exceed 10 years from date of incorporation (20 years for DeepTech)  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like your startup aligns well with the eligibility criteria for the SIDBI Startup Mitra – Incubation Portal. This portal doesn’t provide direct funding but connects startups like yours to high-value schemes such as the Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (up to INR 20 lakh grants) and the Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups (collateral-free debt up to INR 20 crore). Let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many early-stage startups struggle to access mentorship, investor networks, and government funding schemes due to lack of guidance, visibility, and application support — even when they are eligible for schemes like SISFS, FFS, or CGSS.

**Scheme intro:**
The SIDBI Startup Mitra – Incubation Portal, managed by the Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI) under the Startup India initiative, is a pan-India platform that connects DPIIT-recognised, technology-enabled startups with incubation and acceleration programs, expert mentorship, investor networking, and end-to-end support for applying to key government schemes such as the Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS), Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS), and Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups (CGSS).

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a DPIIT-recognised startup — a mandatory requirement for access  
- Your entity is incorporated as a Private Limited Company/LLP/Partnership/Cooperative Society  
- You are working on an innovative, scalable business model with potential for wealth and employment generation  
- You have not been formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business
