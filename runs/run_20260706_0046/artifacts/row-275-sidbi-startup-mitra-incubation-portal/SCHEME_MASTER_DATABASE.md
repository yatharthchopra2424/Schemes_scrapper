### SIDBI Startup Mitra – Incubation Portal
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Incubation
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/startupgov/startup-mitra.html
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Private Limited Company, Registered Partnership Firm, Limited Liability Partnership, or Cooperative Society
- Age of business: Not specified (DPIIT-recognised startups)
- Sector: Technology-enabled business ideas
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be DPIIT-recognised startup; working towards innovation, development, or improvement of products, processes, or services; scalable business model with high potential for wealth and employment generation; not formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Facilitates access to financial schemes (e.g., SISFS grants up to INR 20 lakh, FFS, CGSS); no direct financial support
- Disbursement mode: Not applicable (facilitation only)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing (as long as eligibility is maintained)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. Pitch deck or business description
4. Details of funding received (if any)
5. Authorized signatory authorization letter

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the Startup India portal at https://www.startupindia.gov.in/
2. Navigate to the Startup Mitra – Incubation Portal section
3. Register or log in to your Startup India profile
4. Complete the application form with details about your startup, including innovation, business model, and team
5. Upload required documents such as Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, and pitch deck
6. Submit the application for review
7. Await confirmation and further instructions from the incubation partner
