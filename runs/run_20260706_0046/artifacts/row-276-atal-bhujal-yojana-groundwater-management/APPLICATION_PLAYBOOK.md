## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Client is a gram panchayat, water user association, or local community in an identified water-stressed area  
- [ ] Location is within one of the seven participating states: Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, or Uttar Pradesh  
- [ ] Area has been officially identified as over-exploited or water-stressed by central and state governments  
- [ ] Client is prepared to form or has an existing Water User Association (WUA) or Gram Panchayat-level committee  
- [ ] Client is willing to participate in preparation and implementation of a community-led Water Security Plan (WSP)  
- [ ] Client agrees to provide 50% beneficiary contribution (in kind or cash) for applicable activities  
- [ ] Client commits to convergence with other government schemes for integrated water resource management (if applicable)  
- [ ] Client has access to technical support for preparation of technical feasibility report from concerned department  

> **Sources**: Eligibility criteria from scheme evidence; Application Portal: https://atalbhujal.gov.in

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration (for WUA or equivalent)  
- PAN Card of the applicant entity  
- Aadhaar-based authorization letter (if applicable)  
- Bank account details (cancelled cheque or bank statement)  
- Udyam Registration (if applicable, though not primary for PRIs)  
- Last 3 years’ Income Tax Returns (if applicable; for WUAs with tax liability)  
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- Audited Financial Statements (last 2 years)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Water Security Plan (WSP) document prepared through participatory process  
- Resolution from Gram Panchayat or Water User Association endorsing the WSP and application  
- Details of beneficiary contribution (in kind or cash) and community participation plan  
- Technical feasibility report from the concerned department (e.g., State Ground Water Department)  
- Convergence plan with other schemes (if applicable, for integrated water resource management)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size < 5 MB per document)  
- File names must follow format: `SchemeAbbr_ClientName_DocType_YYYYMMDD.pdf` (e.g., `ABY_GramPanchayatX_WSP_20240515.pdf`)  
- Official documents (resolutions, reports) must be signed and stamped by authorized signatory  
- WSP must reflect community participation and be prepared via participatory rural appraisal (PRA) methods  
- Technical feasibility report must be issued by a government department (e.g., SGWD, PWD, or equivalent)  
- Beneficiary contribution details must specify quantum, valuation method, and timeline  
- All documents must be in Hindi or English; vernacular documents require certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
The Water Security Plan (WSP) serves as the primary planning document and is **required** in lieu of a formal DPR. It must include:  
- Executive summary of groundwater challenges in the area  
- Participatory rural appraisal (PRA) findings and community consultation records  
- Water budgeting and demand-side management strategies  
- Aquifer recharge and water conservation interventions  
- IEC (Information, Education, Communication) and capacity building plan  
- Monitoring and evaluation framework based on Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs)  
- Convergence plan with other schemes (e.g., PMKSY, MGNREGA, Jal Jeevan Mission)  
- Implementation timeline and responsibility matrix  
- Beneficiary contribution details and sustainability plan  

> **Note**: Verify WSP format requirements with the State Level Scheme Sanctioning Committee (SLSSC) or nodal officer, as templates may vary by state.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal at https://atalbhujal.gov.in and navigate to the "Proposal Submission" section  
2. Select the correct state and district from the dropdown of identified water-stressed areas  
3. Enter official name of Gram Panchayat / Water User Association as per registration documents  
4. Upload all scheme-specific and universal documents in the specified format and size  
5. Clearly state the total project cost and break down the 50% central assistance and 50% beneficiary contribution  
6. Select relevant Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs) that the WSP aims to achieve (refer to SLSSC guidelines)  
7. Provide accurate contact details of the authorized signatory (name, designation, mobile, email)  
8. Declaration: Confirm that the WSP is community-led, technically feasible, and converges with other schemes  
9. **Portal-specific note**: The system may require offline submission to SLSSC; verify if portal is for registration only and actual submission is to state committee

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify approval letter from SLSSC specifying sanctioned amount and DLIs  
- [ ] Confirm that 50% central grant and 50% beneficiary contribution ratio is reflected in the sanction  
- [ ] Prepare utilization certificate format as per scheme guidelines for tranche release  
- [ ] Align implementation timeline with achievement of Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs)  
- [ ] Ensure IEC and capacity building activities are scheduled as per WSP  
- [ ] Set up monitoring system for groundwater data collection and reporting  
- [ ] Plan convergence activities with other schemes (e.g., MGNREGA for recharge structures, Jal Jeevan Mission for source sustainability)  
- [ ] Prepare for success fee invoicing upon verification of milestone achievement  
- [ ] Maintain all records for audit and third-party verification as per World Bank guidelines  

> **Note**: Funds are released based on achievement of Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs); ensure WSP includes clear, measurable DLIs for tracking.
