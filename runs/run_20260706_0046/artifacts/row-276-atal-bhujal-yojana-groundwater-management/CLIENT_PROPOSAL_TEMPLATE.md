## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified Atal Bhujal Yojana (Groundwater Management) — a subsidy scheme administered by the Department of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation, Ministry of Jal Shakti — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is financial and technical support for preparation and implementation of community-led Water Security Plans (WSPs), capacity building, and convergence with other schemes for integrated water resource management.

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment for Atal Bhujal Yojana based on geographic and beneficiary criteria  
2. Guidance on formation of Water User Associations (WUAs) or Gram Panchayat-level committees  
3. Support in preparation of Community-led Water Security Plan (WSP) document  
4. Assistance in obtaining Resolution from Gram Panchayat or Water User Association  
5. Compilation of details of beneficiary contribution and community participation  
6. Coordination for technical feasibility report from concerned department  
7. Portal registration and application submission via https://atalbhujal.gov.in (DPR/project report required as part of Water Security Plan)

## 4. What We Need From You
1. Documents as per attached checklist, including Water Security Plan (WSP) document, resolution from Gram Panchayat or Water User Association, details of beneficiary contribution, technical feasibility report, and convergence plan (if applicable)  
2. Designated point of contact for coordination and queries  
3. Response to queries within 2 working days to maintain application timelines
