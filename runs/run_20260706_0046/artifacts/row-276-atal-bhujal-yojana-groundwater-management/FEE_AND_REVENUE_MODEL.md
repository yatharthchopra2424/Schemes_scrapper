## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the scale and complexity of groundwater management projects under Atal Bhujal Yojana, which typically involve large institutional clients such as state agencies, water user associations, and gram panchayat federations managing multi-village water security plans.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees are set at the lower end (2–4%) due to the scheme’s reliance on Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs) for fund release, which introduces performance risk and potential delays despite high scheme value.
3. **Retainer for Technical Work**: Retainers cover intensive upfront work including Water Security Plan (WSP) preparation, stakeholder mobilization, technical feasibility reporting, and convergence planning — activities that are mandatory and resource-intensive before any funding is released.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Given the rolling application cycle tied to annual action plans and the requirement for 50% beneficiary contribution (in kind or cash), the scheme presents moderate execution risk. However, the large fund size (₹6,000 Cr) and central-government backing support high-value engagements, justifying Enterprise-tier pricing.
