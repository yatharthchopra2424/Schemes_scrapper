## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping gram panchayats, water user associations, and local communities in water-stressed areas of Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, and Uttar Pradesh access government support for sustainable groundwater management. Many local bodies struggle with declining groundwater levels and lack the resources to implement community-led water security plans. I’d like to understand your current groundwater challenges and see if the Atal Bhujal Yojana could support your efforts.

### Discovery Questions
- Is your gram panchayat, water user association, or community located in one of the seven participating states: Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, or Uttar Pradesh?
- Has your area been identified as over-exploited or water-stressed by central or state groundwater authorities?
- Are you currently engaged in or planning community-led groundwater management activities, such as forming a Water User Association or developing a Water Security Plan?
- What is the primary source of water for irrigation and drinking in your community, and have you noticed declining groundwater levels over the past 5 years?
- Does your gram panchayat or water user association have a formal resolution to participate in groundwater management initiatives?
- Are you prepared to contribute 50% of the project cost in kind or cash for eligible activities under the scheme?
- Do you have access to technical support for preparing a feasibility report or Water Security Plan?
- Are you open to converging this initiative with other government schemes for integrated water resource management?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many gram panchayats and water user associations in water-stressed areas face declining groundwater levels, lack of technical expertise, and insufficient funds to implement sustainable water management practices. Without structured support, communities struggle to recharge aquifers, conserve water, or monitor groundwater effectively, leading to long-term scarcity and agricultural distress.

**Scheme intro:**
The Atal Bhujal Yojana, implemented by the Department of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation under the Ministry of Jal Shakti, is a central government subsidy scheme providing financial and technical assistance for the preparation and implementation of community-led Water Security Plans (WSPs). The scheme offers grants covering 50% of project costs (with the remaining 50% contributed by beneficiaries), funded jointly by the World Bank and central government, with disbursement linked to achievement of predefined indicators.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your community is located in one of the seven participating states: Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, or Uttar Pradesh  
- Your area has been identified as over-exploited or water-stressed by government authorities  
- You are a gram panchayat, water user association, or local community eligible to participate  
- You are willing to provide the required 50% beneficiary contribution in kind or cash
