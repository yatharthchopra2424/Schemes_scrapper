### Atal Bhujal Yojana (Groundwater Management)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Seven states: Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh
- **Administering Body:** Department of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation, Ministry of Jal Shakti
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://atalbhujal.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (proposals invited periodically based on annual action plans)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Gram panchayats, water user associations, local communities, farmers
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Water & Sanitation / Groundwater Management
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Limited to identified over-exploited and water-stressed areas in the seven participating states; requires 50% beneficiary contribution in kind or cash for certain activities; implementation contingent on state government participation and fund allocation

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance as grants; Fund size of ₹6000 Cr; 50% from World Bank loan and 50% from central government assistance
- Disbursement mode: Released based on achievement of Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs)
- Duration of benefit: Based on annual action plans and achievement of DLIs (ongoing/project-based)

#### Documents Required
1. Water Security Plan (WSP) document
2. Resolution from Gram Panchayat or Water User Association
3. Details of beneficiary contribution and community participation
4. Technical feasibility report from concerned department
5. Convergence plan with other schemes (if applicable)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identification of water-stressed areas by the central and state governments
2. Formation of Water User Associations (WUAs) and Gram Panchayat-level committees
3. Preparation of Community-led Water Security Plans (WSPs) through participatory processes
4. Submission of WSPs to the State Level Scheme Sanctioning Committee (SLSSC for approval
5. Implementation of approved WSPs with financial and technical support from the scheme
6. Monitoring and evaluation based on Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs)
