## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Atal Bhujal Yojana (Groundwater Management)** is a centrally sponsored subsidy scheme implemented by the **Department of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation, Ministry of Jal Shakti**. It operates across seven Indian states: **Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, and Uttar Pradesh**. The scheme focuses on sustainable groundwater management through community-led initiatives in identified water-stressed and over-exploited areas.

**Application Portal:** [https://atalbhujal.gov.in](https://atalbhujal.gov.in)  
**Status:** Rolling basis — proposals invited periodically based on annual action plans  
**Last Updated:** 2024  
**Total Fund Size:** ₹6,000 Crore  
**Funding Pattern:** 50% World Bank loan + 50% Central Government Assistance  
**Fund Release Mechanism:** Based on achievement of Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs)  
**Implementing Agency:** Department of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation, Ministry of Jal Shakti  
**Contact:** Email: abhy@nic.in | Phone: 011-2436-1846  

---

### Objectives
The scheme aims to achieve the following objectives:
- Strengthen the institutional framework for participatory groundwater management  
- Promote behavioral changes at the community level for sustainable groundwater use  
- Implement demand-side management practices in identified water-stressed areas  
- Undertake aquifer recharge and water conservation activities  
- Improve the availability and reliability of groundwater data and monitoring systems  
- Enhance the capacity of stakeholders for effective groundwater governance  
- Ensure convergence with other government schemes for integrated water resource management

### Application Process (Mermaid Flowchart)
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Identification of Water-Stressed Areas<br/>(Central & State Govt.)] --> B[Formation of Water User Associations (WUAs)<br/>and Gram Panchayat-level Committees]
    B --> C[Preparation of Community-led Water Security Plans (WSPs)<br/>Through Participatory Processes]
    C --> D[Submission of WSPs to State Level Scheme Sanctioning Committee (SLSSC)]
    D --> E{SLSSC Approval?}
    E -->|Yes| F[Implementation of Approved WSPs<br/>With Financial & Technical Support]
    E -->|No| C
    F --> G[Monitoring & Evaluation<br/>Based on Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs)]
    G --> H[Fund Release Upon DLI Achievement]
    G --> I[Corrective Actions if DLIs Not Met]
    I --> F
```

**Process Notes:**
- The process is **cyclical** — if WSPs are rejected, they must be revised and resubmitted.
- **DLIs (Disbursement-Linked Indicators)** are pre-agreed performance metrics (e.g., % of WSPs approved, recharge structures built, community training sessions held) that trigger fund tranches.
- Submission is made to the **State Level Scheme Sanctioning Committee (SLSSC)**, not directly to the central portal.
- The portal ([https://atalbhujal.gov.in](https://atalbhujal.gov.in)) hosts guidelines, templates, status updates, and contact information — but **full proposal submission may involve state-level channels**.

### Key Caveats & Challenges
> - **Geographic Restriction:** Only applicable in notified over-exploited/critical blocks in the seven states. Blanket state-level eligibility does not exist.  
> - **DLI-Dependent Funding:** No upfront funds; tranches released only after verified achievement of DLIs. Requires robust monitoring systems.  
> - **50% Beneficiary Contribution:** Can be a barrier in poor communities; must be carefully planned (labor, materials, cash).  
> - **State Dependency:** Implementation hinges on state government active participation, fund matching, and nomination of SLSSC.  
> - **Rolling Basis with Periodic Calls:** No fixed deadline; proposals invited when state annual action plans are approved. Requires continuous portal monitoring.  
> - **Technical Complexity:** WSP preparation requires hydrological expertise; communities often need external technical support (consultants, NGOs, KVKs).  
> - **Convergence Requirement:** Siloed implementation is discouraged; must link with other water/agriculture schemes for sustainability.
