# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is an Urban Local Body (ULB) as defined under statutory towns (per Census 2011 and subsequent additions)
- [ ] The ULB is located in a statutory town in India (pan-India coverage)
- [ ] The ULB was formed on or before 31st December 2024 (to be assessed in Swachh Survekshan 2025-26); if formed after, a formal request to MoHUA from the respective State/UT is required
- [ ] The ULB maintains complete and accurate data on the Swachhatam Portal
- [ ] The ULB has updated contact details of representatives on the Swachhatam Portal
- [ ] The ULB regularly submits monthly Management Information System (MIS) data
- [ ] For star rating certification: ULB fills Common City Profile on SBM Urban Portal
- [ ] ULB fills MIS on SBM Urban Portal
- [ ] ULB checks self-assessment scores on SBM Urban Portal
- [ ] ULB applies for Star Rating as per eligibility on SBM Urban Portal
- [ ] ULB uploads required documents and declarations
- [ ] ULB undergoes Third Party Verification (Desktop and Field Assessment)
- [ ] For Swachh Shehar Jodi: Mentor and mentee cities finalize pairs by 5th September 2025, conduct As-Is Joint Assessment & Action Plan by end September 2025, sign MOUs by October 2025, implement Action Plan for 100 days, and assess progress during Swachh Survekshan 2025-26

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation certificate
- PAN card
- Income Tax Returns (ITR)
- GST registration
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam registration (if applicable)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Monthly Management Information System (MIS)
- Common City Profile
- Self-assessment scores
- Declaration formats for ODF/ODF+/ODF++/Water+
- Details of ward-wise collection and transportation (C&T) vehicles
- Details of ward-wise manpower deployed
- Undertaking on quantum of source segregated waste collected
- List of functional wet/dry waste processing facilities with TPD and capacity
- Photos and videos of functional plants
- Notification from Swachhatam Portal for Plastic Waste Management
- Swachhata App data for Grievance Redressal
- Bylaws notified for User Charges
- Undertaking for Scientific Landfill existence
- Details of functional C&D waste processing facility
- Details of functional Sanitary and Domestic Hazardous waste processing facility

### Document Quality Check
- File size: PDFs under 10 MB, images under 5 MB
- File naming: ULB_Name_DocumentType_YYYYMMDD.pdf (e.g., Delhi_MC_MIS_20250630.pdf)
- All documents must be legible, certified, and in English or Hindi
- Scanned copies must be clear; no blurry or distorted files
- Declarations must be signed and stamped by authorized ULB representative
- Photos/videos must be timestamped and geotagged where applicable

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not explicitly required for star rating or Swachh Shehar Jodi applications under SBM-U 2.0. However, for PPP-based waste-to-energy, composting, or MRF projects seeking financial support, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is advised. **Verify with nodal officer** if DPR is needed for your specific intervention.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the portal at **https://sbmurban.org** and log in using ULB credentials on the Swachhatam Portal.  
2. Ensure all profile fields (contact details, representative info) are updated before proceeding.  
3. Fill the **Common City Profile** completely and accurately—this is mandatory for star rating eligibility.  
4. Submit monthly **MIS data** without gaps; mismatches with third-party assessment attract penalties.  
5. Self-assessment scores must be checked and validated on the portal before applying for star rating.  
6. For star rating: Select the appropriate certification (ODF/ODF+/ODF++/Water+/Garbage Free City) based on eligibility and upload all required declarations.  
7. For Swachh Shehar Jodi: Confirm mentor/mentee pair status, upload joint Action Plan, and MOU copy.  
8. Declarations (e.g., ODF++, Scientific Landfill) must be signed by the Municipal Commissioner or authorized officer and stamped.  
9. Upload photos/videos of functional facilities—ensure they are recent, clear, and show operational status.  
10. Save draft frequently; final submission triggers third-party verification—no edits allowed after submission.  
11. Keep a copy of the submitted application and acknowledgment receipt.  
12. Monitor portal for verification status and respond promptly to any queries from the Third Party Agency (TPA).  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All application form fields completed  
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents uploaded and verified  
- [ ] Declarations signed and stamped by authorized ULB representative  
- [ ] Photos/videos are clear, timestamped, and relevant  
- [ ] MIS data is complete and up to date  
- [ ] Common City Profile is filled accurately  
- [ ] Self-assessment scores reviewed and validated  
- [ ] Application saved as draft before final submission  
- [ ] Submission reference number noted and stored securely

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via **https://sbmurban.org** after internal review  
- Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing the Application Reference Number and timestamp  
- Store the reference number in a secure, accessible location (e.g., case file and digital folder)  
- Set calendar reminders for follow-up at 7, 15, and 30 days post-submission  
- Monitor the portal regularly for status updates (e.g., "Under Review", "Third Party Verification Scheduled")

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter/certificate from MoHUA (valid for 1 year for star rating)  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount or benefit granted (e.g., star rating certification, access to CB Funds under Swachh Shehar Jodi)  
- [ ] Prepare Utilization Certificate (if funds are disbursed) as per GFR 2017 guidelines  
- [ ] Ensure timely submission of post-implementation reports (e.g., 100-day Action Plan completion report for Swachh Shehar Jodi)  
- [ ] Track invoice and success fee as per consultancy agreement  
- [ ] For star rating: Plan for renewal before expiry (certificate valid for 1 year only)  
- [ ] For Swachh Shehar Jodi: Monitor progress and prepare for assessment during Swachh Survekshan 2025-26  
- [ ] Maintain records of all correspondence, approvals, and reports for audit purposes
