## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the scale of financial benefit delivered; for Swachh Bharat Mission – Urban 2.0, the scheme targets ULBs (Urban Local Bodies) with significant infrastructure funding needs, justifying enterprise-tier engagement for cities accessing central grants, State/UT shares, and viability gap funding under PPP models.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect moderate risk due to rolling application basis, third-party verification requirements, and fund utilization restrictions under Swachh Shehar Jodi (soft and process-based interventions only), though processing timelines are predictable (e.g., 100-day implementation + assessment during Swachh Survekshan).
3. **Transparency & Compliance**: Pricing adheres to scheme guidelines; retainers cover documentation, portal navigation (Swachhatam Portal), MIS submission, and star rating preparation, while success fees are contingent on verified outcomes like Garbage Free City Star ratings or ODF++ certification.
4. **Scalable Effort**: Effort scales with ULB size and complexity (e.g., legacy dumpsite remediation, waste-to-energy projects), supporting higher retainers for Enterprise clients managing multi-crore PPP projects.
