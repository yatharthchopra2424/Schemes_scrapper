# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because the Swachh Bharat Mission – Urban 2.0 is helping urban local bodies across India achieve garbage-free status through central grants, technical support, and recognition via star ratings. Since your ULB is focused on improving sanitation and waste management, I wanted to explore whether this scheme could support your current goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your urban local body (ULB) incorporated or notified? (Eligibility requires formation on or before 31st Dec’24 for automatic inclusion in SS 2025-26 assessment)
2. What is the current population of your ULB? (Mechanized cleaning parameters M4 and M8 in ODF++ scoring apply only to ULBs with ≥30,000 population)
3. Are you maintaining complete and accurate data on the Swachhatam Portal, including monthly MIS and updated representative contact details?
4. Have you already submitted your Common City Profile for star rating certification on the SBM Urban Portal?
5. What is your current status regarding ODF/ODF+/ODF++/Water+ certification? (Minimum qualifying marks per parameter are required for ODF++)
6. Are you facing challenges with legacy dumpsite remediation, waste processing capacity, or source segregation at the ward level?
7. Are you interested in participating in Swachh Shehar Jodi as a mentor or mentee city to access Capacity Building Funds for soft interventions?
8. Do you have functional wet/dry waste processing facilities, and are you tracking their TPD capacity and utilization?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] ULB formed on or before 31st Dec’24 (or formal request submitted to MoHUA if formed after)
- [ ] Maintains complete and accurate data on Swachhatam Portal
- [ ] Regularly submits monthly MIS
- [ ] Has updated contact details of representatives on Swachhatam Portal
- [ ] Located in a statutory town (as per Census 2011 or added subsequently)
- [ ] Committed to achieving 'Garbage Free' status under SBM-U 2.0
- [ ] Willing to undertake third-party verification (desktop and field assessment)
- [ ] Prepared to submit declarations for ODF/ODF+/ODF++/Water+ and other required documents

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your ULB aligns well with the Swachh Bharat Mission – Urban 2.0’s goals of achieving garbage-free status and sustaining ODF++ status. The scheme provides central grants, State/UT shares, and access to Capacity Building Funds—particularly valuable for initiatives like Swachh Shehar Jodi—to support waste processing, legacy dumpsite remediation, and behavioral change. Shall I outline how we can help you access these benefits?

Many ULBs struggle to achieve and sustain ODF++ status, process waste scientifically, remediate legacy dumpsites, and maintain accurate sanitation data—hindering their progress toward garbage-free status and access to central funding under Swachh Bharat Mission – Urban 2.0.

**Scheme intro:**
Swachh Bharat Mission – Urban 2.0, administered by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), is a pan-India grant-based scheme supporting statutory towns in achieving garbage-free status through central grants, State/UT shares, ULB contributions, and beneficiary participation. It enables ULBs to set up waste processing facilities, mechanized sweeping, bioremediation projects, and access Capacity Building Funds for mentorship initiatives like Swachh Shehar Jodi.
