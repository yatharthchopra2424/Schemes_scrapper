### Swachh Bharat Mission – Urban 2.0
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://sbmurban.org
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Urban local bodies (ULBs), states, union territories
- Age of business: ULBs formed on or before 31st Dec’24 will be assessed in SS 2025-26; those formed after may be included on formal request to MoHUA from the respective State/UT
- Sector: Water & Sanitation
- Revenue cap: Not mentioned
- Employee count: Not mentioned
- Other: ULBs must maintain complete and accurate data on the Swachhatam Portal, update contact details of representatives, and regularly submit monthly MIS

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support via central grants, State/UT shares, and beneficiary contributions; outlay of SBM-U 1.0 was ₹62,009 crores (GoI share ₹14,623 crores, minimum State share ₹4,874 crores); SBM-U 2.0 follows similar pattern with central grants, State/UT shares, and ULB contributions
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (funds channeled through Capacity Building Funds allocated to States/UTs)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (for specific projects like Swachh Shehar Jodi) and ongoing (for star rating certification valid for 1 year)

#### Documents Required
1. Monthly Management Information System (MIS)
2. Common City Profile
3. Self-assessment scores
4. Declaration formats for ODF/ODF+/ODF++/Water+
5. Details of ward-wise C&T vehicles
6. Details of ward-wise manpower deployed
7. Undertaking on quantum of source segregated waste collected
8. List of functional wet/dry waste processing facilities with TPD and capacity
9. Photos and videos of functional plants
10. Notification from Swachhatam Portal for Plastic Waste Management
11. Swachhata App data for Grievance Redressal
12. Bylaws notified for User Charges
13. Undertaking for Scientific Landfill existence
14. Details of functional C&D waste processing facility
15. Details of functional Sanitary and Domestic Hazardous waste processing facility

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. ULBs must maintain complete and accurate data on the Swachhatam Portal.
2. ULBs must update contact details of their representatives on the Swachhatam Portal.
3. ULBs must regularly submit monthly Management Information System (MIS) data.
4. For star rating certification, ULBs fill Common City Profile on SBM Urban Portal.
5. Fill MIS on SBM Urban Portal.
6. Check self-assessment scores on SBM Urban Portal.
7. Apply for Star Rating as per eligibility on SBM Urban Portal.
8. Upload documents & declarations.
9. Undergo Third Party Verification through Desktop Assessment and Field Assessment.
10. Receive Final Star Rating Certification (Valid for 1 Year).
11. For Swachh Shehar Jodi, mentor and mentee cities finalize pairs by 5th September 2025, conduct As-Is Joint Assessment & Action Plan by end of September 2025, sign MOUs by October 2025, implement Action Plan for 100 days, and assess progress during Swachh Survekshan 2025-26.
