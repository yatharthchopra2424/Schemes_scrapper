# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Swachh Bharat Mission – Urban 2.0 (SBM-U 2.0)** is a centrally sponsored grant scheme launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) on 1st October 2021, with a vision to achieve “Garbage Free” status for all cities by 2026 and institutionalize ‘swachh’ behavior among citizens. It is implemented through States/UTs and Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) across all statutory towns in India (as per Census 2011 and subsequent additions). The scheme operates on a rolling basis with year-round applications for star rating and project proposals, and specific timelines for initiatives like Swachh Shehar Jodi.

**Application Portal:** https://sbmurban.org  
**Status:** Active (Last Updated: 2026)  
**Implementing Agency:** Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA)  
**Contact:** Email: js.sbm-mohua@gov.in | Tel.: 011-23061558, 23061300  

### Objectives
SBM-U 2.0 aims to:
- Achieve 'Garbage Free' status for all cities by 2026  
- Ensure 100% scientific solid waste management  
- Sustain Open Defecation Free (ODF) status and prevent slippage  
- Institutionalize 'swachh' behavior through Jan Andolan  
- Promote waste segregation, collection, transportation, and processing  
- Remediate legacy dumpsites and manage construction & demolition waste  
- Advance used water management and faecal sludge treatment  
- Strengthen capacity building, skill development, and knowledge management

### Required Documents
ULBs must submit the following for assessment and certification:
1. Monthly Management Information System (MIS)  
2. Common City Profile  
3. Self-assessment scores  
4. Declaration formats for ODF/ODF+/ODF++/Water+  
5. Details of ward-wise C&T vehicles  
6. Details of ward-wise manpower deployed  
7. Undertaking on quantum of source segregated waste collected  
8. List of functional wet/dry waste processing facilities with TPD and capacity  
9. Photos and videos of functional plants  
10. Notification from Swachhatam Portal for Plastic Waste Management  
11. Swachhata App data for Grievance Redressal  
12. Bylaws notified for User Charges  
13. Undertaking for Scientific Landfill existence  
14. Details of functional C&D waste processing facility  
15. Details of functional Sanitary and Domestic Hazardous waste processing facility  

> **> Warning:** Mismatch between MIS claims and third-party field assessment attracts additional penalties.

## Consultant's Field Guide to Generated Files

### 1. SCHEME_MASTER_DATABASE.md
**Real-time Usage:** Keep this open in a background tab during all client calls. When a client asks "What is the turnover limit?" or "Who administers this?", CTRL+F in this document to give an immediate, authoritative answer without checking the portal.  
*Example:* During a call with a municipal commissioner asking about eligibility for newly formed ULBs, quickly reference the cutoff date (31st Dec’24) and formal request process to provide accurate guidance.

### 2. PITCH_AND_SALES_SCRIPTS.md
**Real-time Usage:** Open this file 5 minutes before your first Discovery Call with a lead. Read the "Problem Framing" out loud to hook them, then use the Qualification Checklist to interrogate their eligibility live on the phone. Keep the Objection Handlers table visible so you can immediately counter when they say "We're too small for this."  
*Example:* When a small-town ULB officer states they lack capacity for star rating, use the objection handler: "Size isn't a barrier—ULBs <30,000 population are exempt from M4/M8 parameters in ODF++ scoring, and we’ll help you meet minimum qualifying marks per parameter."

### 3. APPLICATION_PLAYBOOK.md
**Real-time Usage:** Print this out or pin it to your desktop once the client signs the retainer. Check off each box in "Stage 1" before moving to "Stage 2". Use the "Client Communication Template" to copy-paste directly into your email when chasing them for pending documents.  
*Example:* After retaining a city for Swachh Shehar Jodi support, use the playbook to track completion of As-Is Joint Assessment by end September, then trigger MOU drafting in October using the embedded template.

### 4. CLIENT_ONBOARDING_AND_CRM.md
**Real-time Usage:** Fill this out during or immediately after the onboarding call. Use the Needs Assessment to record their exact pain points. Update the "Compliance Status" table as they email you documents to maintain a single source of truth for what's missing.  
*Example:* During onboarding, note that the ULB lacks ward-wise C&T vehicle details (Doc #5) and manpower deployment (Doc #6); update the CRM to prioritize collecting these before MIS submission.

### 5. LIVE_CASE_TRACKER.md
**Real-time Usage:** Review this document every morning during your standup. Update the "Stage" column daily. If a case hits "Stage 07 - Under review", use the Escalation Path notes here to know exactly who to call at the government department today.  
*Example:* When a ULB’s star rating application enters third-party verification, check the tracker for the assigned TPA contact and escalation protocol if desktop assessment flags discrepancies in wet waste processing claims.

### 6. FEE_AND_REVENUE_MODEL.md
**Real-time Usage:** Use this file when drafting the proposal. Look at the client's turnover, map them to the pricing tier in the table, and quote that exact Retainer and Success Fee. Use the monthly projection table to update your personal sales pipeline forecast for the quarter.  
*Example:* For an ULB with ₹500 Cr turnover, apply Tier 2 pricing (Retainer: ₹2.5 Lakh/month, Success Fee: 8% of sanctioned grant) and project quarterly revenue based on expected case closure rate.

### 7. CLIENT_PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE.md
**Real-time Usage:** Copy this entire file, paste it into an email or PDF generator, replace the [PLACEHOLDER] tags with the client's actual details gathered from the CRM, and send it immediately after a successful discovery call.  
*Example:* After discovering a city’s interest in legacy dumpsite remediation, populate the template with their specific waste volume (e.g., 50 Lacs MT), target completion date (aligned with SS 2025-26), and proposed bioremediation approach using CB funds.

### 8. COMPLIANCE_AND_LEGAL_PACK.md
**Real-time Usage:** Attach sections 8A and 8B as PDFs to the proposal email. Refuse to start Step 1 of the Application Playbook until the client signs these. Use the Disclaimers to protect yourself legally if the client is rejected by the government agency.  
*Example:* Before initiating MIS data collection, ensure the ULB signs the data accuracy undertaking (Section 8A) and Swachhatam Portal compliance declaration (Section 8B); retain signed copies to mitigate liability if third-party validation finds MIS overreporting.
