## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered (up to ₹50 Lakh per district for GOBARdhan model projects under SBM-G Phase II).
2. Transparent fee structure with no hidden charges; all fees disclosed upfront.
3. Retainer covers initial project scoping, documentation, and submission support.
4. Success fee contingent on fund disbursement, reflecting the scheme’s moderate risk profile due to defined timelines (GOBARdhan financial assistance available only until 2024–25) and dependency on district-level approvals and fund availability.
