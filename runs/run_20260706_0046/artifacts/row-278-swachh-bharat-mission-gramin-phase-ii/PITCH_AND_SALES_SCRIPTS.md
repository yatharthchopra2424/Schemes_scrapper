# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping rural entities like Gram Panchayats, self-help groups, NGOs, and CSR partners access government funding for sanitation and waste-to-wealth projects under the Swachh Bharat Mission – Gramin Phase II. Many of our clients are working to sustain ODF status and build solid/liquid waste management systems but aren’t aware of the financial support available—up to Rs. 50 Lakh per district for GOBARdhan model projects. I’d like to understand your current initiatives to see if this scheme aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of rural entity are you representing (e.g., Gram Panchayat, SHG, NGO, CSR arm of a CPSE)?
2. Are you currently working on or planning any sanitation projects such as individual household latrines, community sanitary complexes, solid or liquid waste management, or GOBARdhan (waste-to-wealth) initiatives?
3. Is your project located in a rural area, as required under SBM-G Phase II guidelines?
4. Do you have Gram Panchayat endorsement or resolution for the proposed project location and activities?
5. Have you prepared a project proposal with scope, location, beneficiaries, cost estimates, and implementation timeline?
6. Do you have proof of land availability or land use permission from the Gram Panchayat?
7. Are you able to provide bank account details of the implementing agency and registration certificates (if NGO/CSR entity)?
8. Are you aware that financial assistance for GOBARdhan projects is available only until 2024–25?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity type: Rural household, Gram Panchayat, village, self-help group, NGO, or CSR entity (including CPSE)
- [ ] Project type: IHHL, CSC, SWM, LWM, or GOBARdhan model project
- [ ] Location: Rural India only
- [ ] Gram Panchayat resolution endorsing the project
- [ ] Project proposal with scope, location, beneficiaries
- [ ] Land availability proof or GP resolution for land use
- [ ] Cost estimate and bill of quantities
- [ ] Implementation plan and timeline
- [ ] Monitoring and O&M plan
- [ ] Bank account details of implementing agency
- [ ] Registration certificate (if NGO/CSR entity)
- [ ] Environmental and social safeguards compliance (if required)
- [ ] Application for GOBARdhan financial assistance submitted before 2024–25 deadline

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your [project type, e.g., GOBARdhan or solid waste management] initiative in a rural Gram Panchayat setting could be eligible for financial support under the Swachh Bharat Mission – Gramin Phase II. This scheme offers up to Rs. 50 Lakh per district for model projects like yours, funded by the Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation. Shall I walk you through how it works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many rural entities struggle to sustain Open Defecation Free (ODF) status and manage solid and liquid waste effectively due to limited funding for infrastructure like compost pits, biogas plants, or waste segregation systems. Without financial support, projects stall, and communities miss out on health, economic, and environmental benefits such as reduced groundwater contamination, savings of INR 50,000 annually per household on health costs, and enhanced safety for women.

**Scheme intro:**
The Swachh Bharat Mission – Gramin Phase II, administered by the Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation, Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India, provides financial assistance for rural sanitation and waste-to-wealth projects. This includes up to Rs. 50 Lakh per district for setting up GOBARdhan model projects at community and cluster levels, available until 2024–25. The mission is backed by a total investment of Rs. 1.40 lakh crores to transform villages from ODF to ODF Plus Model status.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your entity is a [Gram Panchayat/SHG/NGO/CSR] operating in a rural area, meeting the core beneficiary criteria.
- You are planning or implementing a [solid waste management/liquid waste management/GOBARdhan] project, which falls under eligible SBM-G Phase II activities.
- You have or can obtain a Gram Panchayat resolution endorsing the project, a key requirement for fund release.
- Your project aligns with the goal of achieving ODF Plus status through sustainable waste management and visual cleanliness.
