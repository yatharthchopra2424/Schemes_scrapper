### Swachh Bharat Mission – Gramin Phase II
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Rural India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation, Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://swachhbharatmission.gov.in/
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Available until 2024–25 for GOBARdhan financial assistance; overall Phase II implementation ongoing until 2025

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Rural households, Gram Panchayats, villages, self-help groups, NGOs, CSR entities including CPSEs
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Sanitation, solid and liquid waste management, GOBARdhan projects
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Subject to guidelines issued by the Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation under Ministry of Jal Shakti; projects must be implemented in rural areas only; CSR funding from CPSEs must comply with Section 135 of the Companies Act and CSR Guidelines

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to Rs. 50 Lakh per district for GOBARdhan model projects; financial assistance for projects such as GOBARdhan, IHHL, CSC, SWM, LWM
- Disbursement mode: Funds released in installments linked to physical progress
- Duration of benefit: One-time financial assistance per approved project

#### Documents Required
1. Project proposal detailing scope, location, and beneficiaries
2. Land availability proof or Gram Panchayat resolution for land use
3. Cost estimate and bill of quantities
4. Implementation plan and timeline
5. Monitoring and operation & maintenance (O&M) plan
6. Bank account details of implementing agency
7. Registration certificate of NGO/CSR entity (if applicable)
8. Gram Panchayat resolution endorsing the project
9. Environmental and social safeguards compliance (if required)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify eligible sanitation project (e.g., IHHL, CSC, SWM, LWM, GOBARdhan) as per SBM-G Phase II guidelines.
2. Gram Panchayat or implementing agency submits proposal through the Swachh Bharat Mission Gramin portal or to the District Water and Sanitation Committee.
3. Project is appraised by the Block/District level officials for technical and financial feasibility.
4. Upon approval, funds are released in installments linked to physical progress.
5. Implementation is carried out by Gram Panchayat, SHG, NGO, or CSR partner.
6. Operation and maintenance plan is established, often with CPSEs/NGOs for 10 years.
7. Project is monitored via Swachh Survekshan Grameen and reported through MIS.
8. Upon completion, village is assessed for ODF Plus status (Aspiring, Rising, Model).
