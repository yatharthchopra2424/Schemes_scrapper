# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
The **Swachh Bharat Mission – Gramin Phase II (SBM-G Phase II)** is a centrally sponsored subsidy scheme implemented by the **Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation, Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India**. It aims to sustain Open Defecation Free (ODF) status achieved under Phase I and advance rural India toward 'Sampoorn Swachhata' (total cleanliness) through comprehensive solid and liquid waste management, waste-to-wealth initiatives, and behavioral change campaigns. The scheme operates across **Rural India** with a total fund size of **Rs. 1.40 lakh crores** (INR 1,40,000 crore) and is available until **2024–25 for GOBARdhan financial assistance**, while overall Phase II implementation continues until **2025**.

### Objectives
The scheme pursues the following objectives:
- Sustain Open Defecation Free (ODF) status achieved under Phase I  
- Achieve 'Sampoorn Swachhata' through comprehensive solid and liquid waste management  
- Promote waste-to-wealth initiatives like GOBARdhan to convert organic waste into resources  
- Innovate and scale cleanliness campaigns such as Swachhata Green Leaf Rating and Swachhata Hi Sewa  
- Establish educational and developmental infrastructure like Dr. Syama Prasad Mookerjee National Institute of Water and Sanitation (SPM-NIWAS)  
- Mobilize community participation through shramdaan and advocacy campaigns  
- Strengthen monitoring and evaluation via Swachh Survekshan Grameen and Swachhata Samachar  
- Enable ODF Plus status across villages through progressive stages: Aspiring, Rising, and Model  

### Eligibility Matrix
The following entities are eligible to participate in or implement SBM-G Phase II activities:

| **Eligible Entity**       | **Eligible Activities**                                                                 | **Notes**                                                                 |
|---------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Rural households          | Construction of Individual Household Latrines (IHHLs), soak pits, kitchen gardens       | Primary beneficiaries for sanitation infrastructure                       |
| Gram Panchayats           | Implementation of community sanitary complexes, solid/liquid waste management, GOBARdhan | Key local implementing bodies                                             |
| Villages                  | Area-based sanitation and waste management projects                                     | Eligible for ODF Plus status assessment                                   |
| Self-Help Groups (SHGs)   | Implementation and O&M of sanitation projects, waste management units                   | Often engaged in decentralized operations                                 |
| NGOs                      | Design, implementation, monitoring, and O&M of sanitation projects                      | Must submit registration certificate                                      |
| CSR Entities (CPSEs)      | Funding and implementation of sanitation projects under CSR guidelines                  | Must comply with Section 135 of Companies Act and CSR Guidelines          |

> **Note**: All projects must be implemented in **rural areas only**. Financial assistance for GOBARdhan is available **only until 2024–25**. Funds are subject to availability and adherence to guidelines issued by the Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation.

### Application Process
The application process for SBM-G Phase II involves the following steps:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Identify eligible sanitation project] --> B{Gram Panchayat or implementing agency submits proposal}
    B -->|Via SBM-G portal or to District Water and Sanitation Committee| C[Project appraisal by Block/District officials]
    C --> D{Technical and financial feasibility approved?}
    D -->|Yes| E[Funds released in installments linked to physical progress]
    D -->|No| F[Revise and resubmit proposal]
    E --> G[Implementation by Gram Panchayat, SHG, NGO, or CSR partner]
    G --> H[Establish O&M plan (often 10 years with CPSEs/NGOs)]
    H --> I[Monitoring via Swachh Survekshan Grameen and MIS]
    I --> J[Assessment for ODF Plus status: Aspiring, Rising, Model]
    J --> K[Project completion and closure]
```

**Application Portal**: https://swachhbharatmission.gov.in/

**Required Documents**:
1. Project proposal detailing scope, location, and beneficiaries  
2. Land availability proof or Gram Panchayat resolution for land use  
3. Cost estimate and bill of quantities  
4. Implementation plan and timeline  
5. Monitoring and operation & maintenance (O&M) plan  
6. Bank account details of implementing agency  
7. Registration certificate of NGO/CSR entity (if applicable)  
8. Gram Panchayat resolution endorsing the project  
9. Environmental and social safeguards compliance (if required)  

**Key Timelines**:
- GOBARdhan financial assistance: Available until **2024–25**  
- Overall SBM-G Phase II implementation: Ongoing until **2025**  
- Fund disbursement: In installments tied to physical progress milestones  
- O&M plan: Typically established for **10 years** post-implementation  

**Important Caveats**:
> - Financial assistance for GOBARdhan is available **only until 2024–25**  
> - Funds are subject to availability and adherence to guidelines issued by the Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation  
> - CSR funding from CPSEs must comply with **Section 135 of the Companies Act** and CSR Guidelines  
> - Projects must be implemented in **rural areas only**  
> - ODF Plus status requires **sustained ODF status** plus solid/liquid waste management and **visual cleanliness** (minimal litter, no stagnant wastewater, no plastic waste dump in public places, displayed IEC messages)  

**Progress Tracking**:
Villages are assessed for ODF Plus status across three progressive stages:
- **ODF Plus Aspiring**: Sustains ODF status and follows Solid Waste Management (SWM) **or** Liquid Waste Management (LWM)  
- **ODF Plus Rising**: Sustains ODF status and follows **both** SWM and LWM  
- **ODF Plus Model**: Sustains ODF status, follows both SWM and LWM, **and observes visual cleanliness**  

As of September 2024:
- **2.92 lakh** villages declared ODF Plus Model  
- **2.34 lakh** villages declared ODF Plus Aspiring  
- **26,066** villages declared ODF Plus Rising  
- **More than 94% of states** have declared themselves ODF Plus  
- **More than 49% of states** have declared themselves ODF Plus Model
