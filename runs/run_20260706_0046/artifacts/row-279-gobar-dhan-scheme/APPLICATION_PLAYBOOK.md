## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a registered entity under one of the following: Gram Panchayat, Farmer Producer Organization (FPO), Self-Help Group (SHG), Cooperative Society, Non-Governmental Organization (NGO), or Urban Local Body (ULB)
- [ ] The entity is engaged in or planning to set up a Biogas/CBG plant using cattle dung, agricultural residue, or other organic waste as feedstock
- [ ] The project site is located in a rural or peri-urban area with sufficient availability of feedstock (cattle dung, paddy straw, etc.)
- [ ] The applicant has clear land title or lease agreement for the proposed plant site (minimum 10 years lease preferred)
- [ ] The proposed plant capacity is between 1 TPD to 100 TPD of feedstock input (as per GOBAR-DHAN guidelines)
- [ ] The applicant has not availed subsidy for the same purpose under any other central or state government scheme for identical components
- [ ] The applicant agrees to operate and maintain the plant for a minimum of 10 years post-commissioning
- [ ] The applicant has basic technical or operational capacity (or plans to partner with a technology provider) to manage anaerobic digestion and biogas upgradation processes
- [ ] The project aligns with Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin) objectives and contributes to village cleanliness, waste management, and renewable energy generation

> - GOBAR-DHAN Scheme Portal: https://sbm.gov.in/gobardhan

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation/Registration Certificate (of applicant entity)
- PAN Card of the entity
- Audited Financial Statements (ITR) for last 2 years
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration Certificate (if MSME)
- Cancelled Cheque / Bank Account Details
- Authorization Letter (if applying through representative)
- Board Resolution / Governing Body Resolution authorizing the application

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) including:
  - Feedstock availability assessment (cattle dung, agricultural waste, etc.)
  - Technology selection justification (anaerobic digester type, biogas upgradation system)
  - Site layout plan and land ownership/lease documents
  - Process flow diagram (PFD) and P&ID
  - Mass and energy balance calculations
  - Financial projections (CAPEX, OPEX, revenue from CBG, fertilizer, tipping fees)
  - Environmental and social impact assessment (if applicable)
  - Implementation timeline (Gantt chart)
  - Operations and maintenance (O&M) plan for 10 years
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) from:
  - State Pollution Control Board (SPCB) or Pollution Control Committee (PCC)
  - Local Gram Panchayat or Urban Local Body (ULB)
  - Fire Department (if applicable)
- Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with:
  - Feedstock suppliers (farmers, dairy cooperatives, gaushalas)
  - Off-takers for Compressed Biogas (CBG) (e.g., OMCs, industries, CNG stations)
  - Off-takers for Fermented Organic Manure (FOM) or liquid fertilizer
- Technology Provider Quotation and Performance Guarantee
- Undertaking for 10-year operations and maintenance commitment
- Affidavit regarding non-duplication of subsidy from other central/state schemes

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (minimum 300 DPI)
- File size per document: ≤ 10 MB
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ApplicantName_Date.pdf` (e.g., `GOBAR-DHAN_DPR_ABC_FPO_20240515.pdf`)
- Certificates (PAN, GST, Udyam, etc.) must be valid and not expired
- Financial statements must be audited and signed by a Chartered Accountant
- DPR must be prepared by a qualified engineer or consultant and signed/stamped
- All declarations and undertakings must be on non-judicial stamp paper (value as per state norm) and notarized
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in regional language, attach certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
✅ **Required**  
The Detailed Project Report (DPR) is mandatory for GOBAR-DHAN Scheme applications. It must include:
- Executive Summary
- Project Background and Objectives
- Feedstock Assessment Survey (quantity, seasonality, collection mechanism)
- Technology Description (Anaerobic Digestion + Biogas Upgradation to CBG)
- Site Plan and Land Details
- Process Flow Diagram (PFD) and Piping & Instrumentation Diagram (P&ID)
- Mass and Energy Balance
- CAPEX Breakdown (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Instrumentation)
- OPEX Estimate (Manpower, Maintenance, Utilities)
- Revenue Projections (Sale of CBG, FOM, tipping fees, carbon credits if applicable)
- Financial Analysis (IRR, NPV, Payback Period, DSCR)
- Sensitivity Analysis
- Environmental and Social Impact Assessment (if applicable)
- Implementation Schedule (Gantt Chart)
- Operations and Maintenance (O&M) Plan for 10 years
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan
- Conclusions and Recommendations

> **Note**: DPR must be prepared by a qualified engineer or consultant. Verify specific formatting requirements with the State Nodal Agency (SNA) or District SBM-G cell.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official portal: https://sbm.gov.in/gobardhan (note: form may be hosted on State SBM-G website or require offline submission)
2. Download the latest application form from the "Downloads" or "Apply" section; if unavailable, contact State Nodal Agency
3. Fill all fields in **Block Letters** using black ink (if physical form) or ensure digital fields are fully populated
4. Enter correct **Scheme Name**: "GOBAR-DHAN (Galvanizing Organic Bio-Agro Resources Dhan)"
5. Provide accurate **Applicant Details**: Name, registration number, address, contact person, email, mobile
6. Specify **Project Location**: Village, Block, District, State, PIN code (must match land documents)
7. Describe **Feedstock Source**: Type (cattle dung, paddy straw, etc.), estimated daily quantity (TPD), sourcing radius (km)
8. Detail **Plant Capacity**: Input feedstock (TPD), expected biogas production (m³/day), CBG output (kg/day or tonnes/year)
9. Declare **Technology Provider**: Name, address, past experience, and confirm performance guarantee
10. Attach all required documents as per checklist; verify file names and sizes before upload
11. Sign the declaration: “I hereby declare that the information provided is true and correct to the best of my knowledge…”
12. Retain a copy of the submitted application and acknowledgment receipt

> **Portal-Specific Note**: The central portal https://sbm.gov.in/gobardhan primarily provides information. Actual application submission is often handled by State Nodal Agencies (SNAs) under SBM-G. Confirm the submission mode (online/offline) with the respective State Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin) office.
