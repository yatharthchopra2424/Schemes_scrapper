## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial and environmental benefits delivered by the GOBAR-DHAN Scheme, particularly for large-scale biogas and CBG projects.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees account for moderate implementation risk due to technical complexity in biogas plant setup and dependency on feedstock availability, though government backing reduces execution uncertainty.
3. **Market Competitiveness**: Rates are benchmarked against similar infrastructure and sustainability consulting engagements in the waste-to-energy and renewable energy sectors.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The GOBAR-DHAN Scheme involves longer processing times (typically 6–9 months for approval and fund disbursement) due to multi-agency coordination (MoHUA, MNRE, state nodal agencies) and technical appraisal. However, evidence indicates a high success rate for well-prepared DPRs, supporting moderate success fees with a revenue lag assumption of 6 months.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Retainer fee for consulting services under the GOBAR-DHAN Scheme (Galvanizing Organic Bio-Agro Resources Dhan), including project feasibility, DPR preparation, and submission support to the implementing agency (Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation, Ministry of Jal Shakti).

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for securing approval and financial assistance under the GOBAR-DHAN Scheme, implemented by the Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation, Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India.
