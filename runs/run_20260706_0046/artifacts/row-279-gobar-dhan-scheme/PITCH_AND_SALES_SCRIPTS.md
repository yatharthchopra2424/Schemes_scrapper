## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping rural entrepreneurs and farming communities access government support for sustainable waste-to-wealth initiatives, particularly under the GOBAR-DHAN Scheme, which promotes converting cattle dung and solid waste into useful resources like biogas and compost. I’d like to understand if your gaushala, dairy farm, or rural enterprise could benefit from this support.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of organic waste (e.g., cattle dung, agricultural residue) does your facility currently generate on a regular basis?
- Are you currently managing this waste through composting, biogas production, or another method, or is it mostly disposed of?
- Is your organisation involved in dairy farming, animal husbandry, or rural waste management activities?
- Has your entity been legally registered (e.g., as a society, cooperative, gaushala trust, or Section 8 company) for at least one year?
- Are you located in a rural or peri-urban area where waste accumulation poses environmental or hygiene challenges?
- Are you seeking support to set up or scale a biogas plant, composting unit, or similar waste valorisation project?
- Do you have access to land or space where a small-scale waste processing unit could be installed?
- Are you interested in generating additional income from waste through sale of biogas, organic fertiliser, or energy savings?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity involved in cattle dung, agricultural waste, or organic solid waste management  
- [ ] Located in a rural or peri-urban area  
- [ ] Legally registered organisation (society, cooperative, trust, gaushala, etc.)  
- [ ] Generates consistent supply of biodegradable waste (minimum threshold not specified in evidence)  
- [ ] Interested in establishing biogas, compost, or pelletisation unit from waste  
- [ ] Not currently receiving central subsidy for identical GOBAR-DHAN project (implied)  
- [ ] Operates in sectors aligned with Water & Sanitation Ministry’s waste-to-wealth goals  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your gaushala/dairy setup generates significant cattle dung that could be transformed into biogas or compost — exactly what the GOBAR-DHAN Scheme supports. Let me explain how this scheme can help you set up a waste-to-wealth plant with financial and technical support.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many rural enterprises, gaushalas, and dairy farms struggle with managing large volumes of cattle dung and organic waste, leading to hygiene issues, environmental pollution, and missed income opportunities. Without proper infrastructure, this waste is often dumped or underutilised, representing both a cost and a lost resource.

**Scheme intro:**
The GOBAR-DHAN Scheme (Galvanizing Organic Bio-Agro Resources Dhan), administered by the Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation under the Ministry of Jal Shakti, supports the setup of biogas plants, composting units, and cattle dung-based pelletisation projects. It provides financial and technical assistance to convert organic waste into useful resources like biogas, bio-CNG, and organic manure, promoting sustainable waste management in rural areas.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your organisation manages cattle dung or organic waste from dairy/animal husbandry activities  
- You are based in a rural or peri-urban location where waste accumulation is a challenge  
- You are a legally registered entity (e.g., gaushala trust, cooperative, society)  
- You are interested in establishing a biogas or composting unit to valorise waste
