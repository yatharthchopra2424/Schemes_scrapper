### GOBAR-DHAN Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Jal Shakti (Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://sbm.gov.in/gobardhan
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), Self Help Groups (SHGs), Cooperatives, NGOs, Entrepreneurs, and Local Bodies
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Agriculture, Animal Husbandry, Waste Management, Renewable Energy (Biogas/CBG production)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be engaged in or planning to set up Biogas/CBG plants using cattle dung, agricultural residue, and other organic waste; preference given to women-led and SC/ST enterprises

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance up to ₹50 lakh per project for setting up Biogas/CBG plants; additional support for slurry management and market linkage
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement (after verification of plant installation and commissioning)
- Duration of benefit: One-time capital subsidy

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR) including technology, capacity, and financials
2. Land ownership/lease documents for plant site
3. Proof of entity registration (FPO, SHG, Cooperative, NGO, etc.)
4. PAN and GST registration of the applicant
5. Bank account details and cancelled cheque
6. Consent to Establish and Consent to Operate from State Pollution Control Board (if applicable)
7. MoU with feedstock suppliers (dung, agricultural waste)
8. Off-take agreement for biogas/CBG and/or slurry (if available)
9. Affidavit confirming no duplicate subsidy for same project
10. Photographs of proposed site and waste source

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the official portal: https://sbm.gov.in/gobardhan
2. Register on the GOBAR-DHAN portal and create a login
3. Fill in the online application form with entity and project details
4. Upload all required documents as per the checklist
5. Submit the application and note the application reference number
6. Application undergoes preliminary scrutiny by the State Nodal Agency
7. Site verification and technical appraisal by designated agency
8. Approval by State Level Sanctioning Committee (SLSC)
9. Issuance of sanction letter and release of first installment (if applicable)
10. Installation and commissioning of the Biogas/CBG plant
11. Submission of completion report and utilization certificate
12. Verification and release of final subsidy amount
