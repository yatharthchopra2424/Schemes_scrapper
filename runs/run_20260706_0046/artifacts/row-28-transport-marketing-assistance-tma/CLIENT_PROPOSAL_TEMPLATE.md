## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified Transport & Marketing Assistance (TMA) — a subsidy scheme administered by Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA), Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is reimbursement of a specified percentage of international freight and marketing costs incurred on eligible exports, as per rates notified by DGFT under the Foreign Trade Policy.

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment against TMA scheme requirements  
2. Document collection and verification as per APEDA's checklist  
3. Application preparation and submission via APEDA portal

5. Status tracking and regular updates to client
