## 8D. Internal Data Handling Policy

All client data shall be handled in accordance with the following security protocols:

- **Encrypted Cloud Storage:** All files stored in encrypted cloud storage with AES-256 encryption.
- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** Mandatory for all system access.
- **Limited Access:** Role-based access controls; only authorized personnel may access client data.
- **No Personal Devices:** Prohibition on storing or processing client data on personal devices.
- **Retention Period:** Client data retained for 2 years post case closure.
- **Breach Notification:** 24-hour breach notification protocol to affected parties and relevant authorities.

**Scheme-specific note:** All documents submitted to Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA), Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry under Transport & Marketing Assistance (TMA) must be retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit purposes.
