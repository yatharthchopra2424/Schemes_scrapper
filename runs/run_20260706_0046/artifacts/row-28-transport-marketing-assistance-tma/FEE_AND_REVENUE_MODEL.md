## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the reimbursement-based nature of the Transport & Marketing Assistance (TMA) scheme, where benefits are disbursed post-shipment upon verification of freight and marketing costs, ensuring consultant compensation correlates with realized client gains.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s moderate risk profile—dependent on timely submission within notification-specified timelines, complete documentation (IEC, RCMC, shipping bills, payment proofs), and eligibility under periodic DGFT notifications—supported by evidence of structured claim processing via APEDA.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The retainer-success fee structure accommodates varying exporter scales, from MSMEs leveraging TMA for initial market entry to large enterprises optimizing logistics costs across notified destinations.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The TMA scheme exhibits a medium processing risk due to its post-shipment reimbursement model and dependency on APEDA verification timelines; however, the scheme’s consistent inclusion in the Foreign Trade Policy and documented disbursement mechanism support predictable success fee realization upon claim approval.
