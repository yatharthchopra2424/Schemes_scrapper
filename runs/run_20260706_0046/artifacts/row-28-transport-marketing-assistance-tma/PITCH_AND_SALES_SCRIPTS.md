# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping agri-exporters and processed food businesses reduce their international freight and marketing costs through government schemes like the Transport & Marketing Assistance (TMA) offered by APEDA. Many exporters in your sector leave money on the table simply because they don’t know how to access these reimbursements. I’d like to understand your current export activities to see if TMA could support your growth.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your Import Export Code (IEC) number, and when was it issued by DGFT?
2. Do you hold a valid Registration-cum-Membership Certificate (RCMC) from APEDA for your scheduled products?
3. Which APEDA-scheduled products do you export (e.g., processed foods, fruits, vegetables, cashew, etc.)?
4. To which countries have you exported in the last 12 months, and are they covered under current TMA notifications?
5. What percentage of your total export cost is typically attributed to international freight and marketing expenses?
6. Do you maintain documentation such as shipping bills, proof of freight payment, and marketing expense invoices for your shipments?
7. Have you previously applied for any APEDA financial assistance schemes or export promotion benefits?
8. What is your annual export turnover, and are you classified as an MSME under government criteria?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Exporter of APEDA-scheduled products  
- [ ] Valid Registration-cum-Membership Certificate (RCMC) from APEDA  
- [ ] Valid Import Export Code (IEC) from DGFT  
- [ ] Exports made to notified countries under current TMA notification  
- [ ] Claims to be submitted within stipulated time frame post-shipment  
- [ ] Complete documentation available (shipping bills, freight/marketing proof, bank details, self-declaration)  
- [ ] Exporter is either an MSME, agri-exporter, or processed food exporter  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you may be eligible for the Transport & Marketing Assistance (TMA) scheme, which reimburses a portion of international freight and marketing costs for eligible exports. Let me explain how it works and how much support you could receive.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Indian exporters of agricultural and processed food products often face high international freight and marketing costs, which reduce price competitiveness in global markets, especially when shipping to distant or notified countries under TMA guidelines.

**Scheme intro:**
The Transport & Marketing Assistance (TMA) scheme, administered by the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA) under the Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, provides reimbursement of a specified percentage of international freight and marketing costs incurred on eligible exports. The assistance is disbursed post-shipment upon submission of required documents and verification by APEDA, as per rates and conditions notified by DGFT under the Foreign Trade Policy.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You hold a valid RCMC from APEDA for your scheduled products  
- You possess an IEC issued by DGFT  
- Your exports are destined for countries covered under the current TMA notification  
- You incur international freight and marketing expenses that are reimbursable under the scheme
