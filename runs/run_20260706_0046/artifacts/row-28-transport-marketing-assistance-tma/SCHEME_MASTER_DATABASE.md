### Transport & Marketing Assistance (TMA)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA), Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://apeda.gov.in/tma
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** As per notifications issued under Foreign Trade Policy; claims must be submitted within the stipulated time frame post-shipment as specified in the respective TMA notification.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Exporters of APEDA-scheduled products
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Agricultural and processed food products
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have a valid Registration-cum-Membership Certificate (RCMC) from APEDA and an Import Export Code (IEC) from DGFT; exports must be made to notified countries under the TMA guidelines; specific product and destination eligibility as per periodic notifications issued by DGFT under the Foreign Trade Policy.

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Reimbursement of a specified percentage of international freight and marketing costs incurred on eligible exports, as per rates and conditions notified by DGFT from time to time under the Foreign Trade Policy.
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement
- Duration of benefit: One-time per eligible shipment

#### Documents Required
1. Import Export Code (IEC)
2. Registration-cum-Membership Certificate (RCMC) from APEDA
3. Shipping bills
4. Proof of payment of international freight
5. Proof of payment of marketing expenses
6. Bank details for reimbursement
7. Self-declaration form as per TMA guidelines

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Obtain IEC from DGFT and RCMC from APEDA.
2. Ensure the exported product and destination are covered under the current TMA notification.
3. Submit the application for TMA claim along with shipping bills, proof of payment of freight and marketing expenses, and other required documents.
4. APEDA verifies the claim and processes the reimbursement.
5. Funds are transferred to the exporter’s bank account upon approval.
