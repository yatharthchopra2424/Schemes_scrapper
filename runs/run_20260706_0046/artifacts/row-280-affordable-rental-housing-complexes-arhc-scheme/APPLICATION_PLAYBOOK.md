# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Entity is a Single Business Entity (Private/Public Agency) or a Consortium of up to 3 members (each with minimum 25% share)
- [ ] Entity is incorporated under the Indian Companies Act 1956/2013 or equivalent laws
- [ ] Entity has submitted Registration/Incorporation Certificate and GST Registration
- [ ] Entity has not been barred/blacklisted by Jaipur Development Authority or any State/UT/Central Government department/agency
- [ ] Entity has not failed to perform on any agreement/contract in the last three years (no penalty, judicial pronouncement, arbitration award, expulsion, or termination for breach)
- [ ] Entity submits only one proposal for the project
- [ ] Entity is a profit-making organization (audited balance sheet for last five years attached with technical bid)
- [ ] For Consortium: Power of Attorney for Lead Member submitted; MoU signed by all members; members nominate one Lead Member; members execute Power of Attorney for Lead Member
- [ ] Entity has experience of having successfully completed similar works during the last 5 years ending previous day of last date of submission of bids:
  - One similar work costing not less than 80% of the estimated project cost OR
  - Two similar works each costing not less than 60% of the estimated project cost OR
  - Three similar works each costing not less than 40% of the estimated project cost
  - "Similar works" means Construction/Repair/Retrofit/Development & Maintenance and Management of housing complexes/social housing/residential societies/hostels/hotels by Agency/Consortium themselves in last five years
- [ ] Average annual financial turnover of the Agency of last 5 consecutive financial years is at least 50% of the estimated project cost (certified by Chartered Accountant)
- [ ] Net Worth of the Agency as on 31st March of previous Financial Year is positive (if audited balance sheet for 2020-21 not available due to COVID-19, balance sheet of 2019-20 considered)
- [ ] Agency has not incurred any loss in more than two years during available last five consecutive balance sheets
- [ ] Self-certified copy of Bank Solvency Certificate issued from Nationalized or any Scheduled Bank is at least 40% of estimated project cost in this bid (issued within 6 months from original last date of submission of bid)
- [ ] For Model-2: Entity has available vacant land with clear title and free from encumbrance, sufficient for constructing at least 40 Dwelling Units or equivalent number of dormitory beds
- [ ] For Model-2: Land is serviced by infrastructure such as road, electricity, water, sewerage and convenient access to major road corridors and expressways
- [ ] Project must include at least 40 dwelling units or equivalent dormitory beds (1 single bedroom unit of up to 30 sqm carpet area = 3 dormitory beds)
- [ ] Maximum of 33% dwelling units in double bedroom form permissible in any ARHC project
- [ ] Beneficiaries are urban migrants/poor from EWS/LIG categories
- [ ] Project will be exclusively used for rental housing for a minimum period of 25 years

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are readable, printable, and legible
- [ ] File size within portal limits (if specified)
- [ ] Document names match the checklist (e.g., "Bank Solvency Certificate.pdf", "GST Registration.pdf")
- [ ] All certificates are signed and stamped where required
- [ ] Affidavits are notarized on Non-Judicial Stamp Paper of Rs. 100
- [ ] Documents are in English or accompanied by certified English translation
- [ ] Digital signatures used for e-submission (as per IT Act 2000)
- [ ] Scanned copies are clear and not blurred
- [ ] Latest financials (not older than 1 year for statements, 6 months for bank statements)
- [ ] EMD is in the form of BG or DD drawn in favour of “Secretary Jaipur Development Authority, Jaipur” payable at Jaipur
- [ ] EMD amount is 2% of estimated project cost (Rs. 20.52 lacs for the Jaipur RFP example)
- [ ] Bid Security options: Bank Guarantee (BG) against EMD or Electronic Fund Transfer (EFT/NEFT/RTGS) or Payment Gateway
- [ ] Bidder has Digital Signature Certificate (Type III) as per IT Act 2000
- [ ] Bidder registered on JDA website and e-Procurement Portal of Government of Rajasthan

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- **DPR is required for Model-2 only.**  
- For Model-2, shortlisted entities must submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR) to the respective ULB for approval and upload a copy on the ARHC website.  
- Suggested content of DPR (Model-2) includes:  
  - A. Description: Name of State/UT, City, Location of Project (with map)  
  - B. Entity Details: Type (Public/Private), Name, Address, Registration number  
  - C. Consortium Details (if applicable): Number of members, Lead Member details, Members details, Consortium agreement  
  - D. Land Details: Land area (hect.), Type and Status, Land documents (Title deed), Ownership, Distance from City Centre/GPO, Evidence of freedom from encumbrances, Evidence of fitness for construction, Status of possession, ‘Use permission’ requirement  
  - E. Project Details: Project Name, Basic FAR/FSI permissible, Enhanced FAR/FSI, FAR/FSI utilized, Number of Dwelling Units (single/double bedroom with carpet/built-up area), Number of Dormitories with carpet/built-up area, Type of Structure (No. of Floors, Blocks, DUs per Floor), Number of Beneficiaries, Duration of project, Environmental clearance certificate (if applicable), Building plans (Key, Site, Subdivision/layout, Building, Service plans), Specifications, General and detailed, Title of ownership of land/building, Certificate for structural design sufficiency, Certificate for engagement of builder/constructor(s), Certificates for supervision  
  - F. Project Planning: Overall planning of site, Access from major road corridors and expressways (preferably within 2-5 kms), Connection to required infrastructure (road, electricity, water, sewerage), Buffer separation from environmentally protected area (minimum 1 km), Construction technology used (Conventional or innovative technology), Approval/certification/listing of innovative technology by CPWD/MoHUA (GHTC-India)/BMTPC or any other organization  
  - G. Drawings: Approved Structural Drawings, Dimensions follow norms of Low-Income housing given in NBC-2016, Construction Technology Used, Details of Construction Technology  
  - H. Financial details: Summary of Total project cost, Technology innovation grant required, Construction Cost/Unit EWS  
  - I. Rent Details: Number of houses for rent, Proposed tie up details with third party for beneficiary, Affordable fixed rent  
  - J. Operation & Maintenance details  
  - K. List of Documents to be submitted with DPR (Land documents, Key plans, Site plans, Subdivision/layout plans, Building plans, Service plans, Specifications, General and detailed, Title of ownership of land/building, Certificate for structural design sufficiency, Certificate for engagement of builder/constructor(s), Certificates for supervision, Structural design and details, Undertaking for Operation & Maintenance, Costing and Estimation of Project, Declaration from Entity that dwelling units are exclusively for urban migrants/poor)  
- For Model-1, DPR is not explicitly required; however, entities must provide project details as per the RFP and Annex-2 (Information of ARHCs under Model-1) for listing after selection.  
- **Verify with nodal officer if DPR is required for Model-1 in specific State/UT contexts.**

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. All documents must be digitally signed using a Digital Signature Certificate (Type III) as per the Indian IT Act 2000 before upload.  
2. The bid must be submitted electronically only through the e-tendering system (JDA website or e-Procurement Portal of Government of Rajasthan for Model-1; ARHC website for Model-2 EoI).  
3. All information in forms must be furnished against the relevant columns; if information is on a separate sheet, this must be mentioned in the column.  
4. If any particulars/query is not applicable, state it as ‘Not applicable’.  
5. Do not give incomplete information or suppress information; this may result in disqualification.  
6. Technical bid must be written in English.  
7. The participating Agency must sign on each page of application, forms, and documents before scanning and uploading to ensure proper numbering and indexing.  
8. Corrections must be made by neatly crossing out, initialing, dating, and rewriting.  
9. References, information, and certificates from clients certifying suitability, technical knowledge, or capability must be signed by an authorized officer.  
10. All guidelines and amendments issued by GOI/GOR are applicable; the Tenancy Act as applicable from time to time is applicable on this bid.  
11. For Model-2 EoI submission on ARHC website: Entities must register on the website, log in, and click “Add Application” button against the published EOI in the Tender Menu.  
12. For Model-2, after shortlisting, entities must submit DPR to ULB for approval and upload a copy on ARHC website.  
13. For Model-1, entities must submit bids via the JDA portal or e-Procurement Portal of Government of Rajasthan as specified in the RFP.  
14. The bid security (EMD) must be submitted as per the prescribed format (Form-G) and must be verbatim; otherwise, the bid is liable for rejection.  
15. The bid validity is 120 days from the bid submission deadline.  
16. The Agency must furnish audited balance sheet for the last five years; if not available for 2020-21 due to COVID-19, the balance sheet of 2019-20 will be considered.  
17. The Agency must give an undertaking if annual accounts for the latest financial year are not audited, and the statutory auditor must certify the same.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application electronically via the designated portal:  
  - For Model-1: Through the JDA website (www.jda.urban.rajasthan.gov.in) or e-Procurement Portal of Government of Rajasthan (www.eproc.rajasthan.gov.in) as specified in the RFP.  
  - For Model-2: Via the ARHC website (https://arhc.mohua.gov.in) under the Tender Menu → EOI Page section.  
- Before upload, ensure the bid comprising all attached documents is digitally signed using an acceptable Certifying Authority (CA).  
- After submission, take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing the application ID and timestamp.  
- Store the application reference number securely.  
- Set follow-up reminders for:  
  - Technical Bid Opening Date  
  - Financial Bid Opening Date  
  - Expected date for shortlisting notification (for Model-2)  
  - Date for DPR submission (if shortlisted for Model-2)

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter/sanction order from the concerned authority (ULB/SLSMC/CSMC)  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount, incentives, and benefits (e.g., Tax exemptions, Concessional loan eligibility, FAR/FSI benefits, TIG if applicable)  
- [ ] Sign the Concession Agreement (for Model-1) or commence operations (for Model-2) within the stipulated time (15 days after LoI issuance for Model-1)  
- [ ] Ensure the project is listed on the ARHC website (https://arhc.mohua.gov.in) with a unique project code  
- [ ] Initiate construction/retrofitting as per the approved DPR and project timeline  
- [ ] Set up the Escrow account for financial transactions (as required for Model-2)  
- [ ] Begin rent fixation process based on local survey (as per scheme guidelines)  
- [ ] Establish tie-ups with local industries/institutions/aggregators for sustained occupancy and revenue  
- [ ] Apply for concessional project finance under Affordable Housing Fund (AHF) and Priority Sector Lending (PSL)  
- [ ] Apply for Technology Innovation Grant (TIG) if using identified innovative technologies (submit DPR to BMTPC via State/UT for CSMC approval)  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with 25-year exclusive rental housing requirement for urban migrants/poor from EWS/LIG categories  
- [ ] Prepare and submit quarterly Project Compliance Reports to the concerned ULB  
- [ ] Utilize single window system for design/drawings and statutory approvals (within 30 days)  
- [ ] Ensure trunk infrastructure (road, water, sanitation, electricity) is provided at no additional cost  
- [ ] Verify municipal services are levied at par with residential projects  
- [ ] Maintain records and documentation for accreditation and monitoring purposes  
- [ ] Plan for handover of ARHCs to ULB in livable condition after 25 years  
- [ ] Track and verify utilization of Viability Gap Funding (VGF) if applicable (released in two installments: 80% after development, 20% after operation and utilization certificates)  
- [ ] Ensure VGF is returned within 3 years from date of operation along with applicable interest rates  
- [ ] Monitor and report on project progress via ARHC website dashboard  
- [ ] Prepare for final audit and project closure after concession period
