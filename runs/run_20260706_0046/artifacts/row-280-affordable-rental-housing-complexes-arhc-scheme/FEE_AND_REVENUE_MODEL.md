## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial project scoping, eligibility assessment, documentation preparation, and submission support.  
2. Success fee is contingent on successful concession award or project approval under the ARHC scheme.  
3. Fees are structured to align consultant incentives with client outcomes—higher value schemes warrant lower success fees due to scaled effort.  
4. For the ARHC scheme, the risk profile is moderate: processing time is defined (25-year concession period with 30-day statutory approvals), and evidence shows successful bid awards via transparent RFP processes (e.g., Jaipur RFP for 588 flats), reducing uncertainty in approval timelines.
