# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping public and private entities leverage government schemes for affordable rental housing development. The Affordable Rental Housing Complexes (ARHC) Scheme, launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), supports entities like yours in constructing, operating, and maintaining rental housing for urban migrants and poor from EWS/LIG categories on your own vacant land. I’d like to understand your land availability and development goals to see if this scheme aligns with your objectives.

### Discovery Questions
1. Do you own vacant land that is free from encumbrances and suitable for residential construction?  
2. Is the land located within a statutory town, notified planning area, or area under a Special Area/Development Authority?  
3. Are you interested in developing rental housing for urban migrants, industrial workers, or service providers near your workplace or project site?  
4. Can you commit to operating and maintaining the ARHC for a minimum period of 25 years as per scheme guidelines?  
5. Are you open to adopting innovative construction technologies to qualify for the Technology Innovation Grant (TIG) of up to Rs. 1,00,000 per unit?  
6. Do you have experience in residential or institutional construction projects in the last seven years?  
7. Is your organization financially sound with positive net worth and audited balance sheets for the last five years?  
8. Are you willing to submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR) and undergo technical evaluation by the Urban Local Body (ULB)?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity owns vacant land with clear title and free from encumbrance  
- [ ] Land is sufficient for at least 40 dwelling units or equivalent dormitory beds  
- [ ] Land is serviced by road, electricity, water, and sewerage infrastructure  
- [ ] Entity is a public/private body, consortium, or joint venture (max 3 members)  
- [ ] Entity has positive net worth and audited financials for last 5 years  
- [ ] Entity has experience in residential construction in last 7 years  
- [ ] Entity agrees to use ARHCs exclusively for rental housing for min. 25 years  
- [ ] Entity willing to fix affordable rent based on local survey and increase biennially by max 8%  
- [ ] Entity open to statutory approvals via single window system (30 days)  
- [ ] Entity agrees to provide trunk infrastructure (road, water, sanitation, electricity) at no additional cost  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your vacant land and development intent align well with the Affordable Rental Housing Complexes (ARHC) Scheme under Model-2. This scheme enables private/public entities like yours to construct, operate, and maintain ARHCs on your own land for 25 years, with access to concessional finance, tax exemptions, and up to Rs. 1,00,000 per unit in Technology Innovation Grant. Shall I walk you through how this works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Urban migrants and poor workers often live in slums or informal settlements far from their workplaces, leading to long commutes, reduced productivity, and poor living conditions. They need dignified, affordable rental housing close to their workplaces but lack access to serviced land and financing for development.

**Scheme intro:**
The Affordable Rental Housing Complexes (ARHC) Scheme, administered by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), is a subsidy-based initiative under PMAY-U. Under Model-2, public/private entities can construct, operate, and maintain ARHCs on their own vacant land for 25 years. Benefits include concessional project finance via Affordable Housing Fund (AHF) and Priority Sector Lending (PSL), exemption of income tax and GST on profits, 50% additional FAR/FSI free of cost, single window approval within 30 days, provision of trunk infrastructure at no additional cost, municipal services at par with residential projects, and a Technology Innovation Grant (TIG) of Rs. 1,00,000 per double bedroom unit, Rs. 60,000 per single bedroom unit, and Rs. 20,000 per dormitory bed for using identified innovative technologies.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You own vacant land with clear title, free from encumbrance, and sufficient for at least 40 dwelling units  
- Your land is located in a statutory town or notified planning area, eligible for ARHC development  
- You have experience in residential construction and positive net worth, meeting financial strength criteria  
- You are willing to operate and maintain the complex exclusively for rental housing for 25 years
