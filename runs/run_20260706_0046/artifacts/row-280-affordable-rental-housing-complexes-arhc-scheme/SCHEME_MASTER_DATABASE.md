### Affordable Rental Housing Complexes (ARHC) Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://arhc.mohua.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Single Business Entity (Private/Public Agency) or Consortium of Business Entities (max 3 members)
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Entities with own vacant land (Model-2) or bidding for government-funded vacant houses (Model-1)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: 
  - For Model-1: Selected through transparent bidding by States/UTs/ULBs for repairing, retrofitting, developing, operating, and transferring ARHCs
  - For Model-2: Public/private entities with own vacant land can apply via Expression of Interest (EoI)
  - Entities must comply with NBC/State/Local Authority norms for dwelling units and dormitories
  - Projects must include at least 40 dwelling units or equivalent dormitory beds
  - Beneficiaries restricted to urban migrants/poor from EWS/LIG categories
  - Agency should be profit-making organization
  - Audited balance sheet for last five years required
  - Agency should not have incurred loss in more than two years during last five consecutive balance sheets

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 
  - Financial support includes concessional loans from Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) under Affordable Housing Fund (AHF) and Priority Sector Lending (PSL) by commercial banks at lower interest rates
  - Viability Gap Funding (VGF) may be provided from central assistance released to States/UTs for JnNURM/RAY projects if needed
  - Technology Innovation Grant (TIG) of Rs. 1,00,000 per double bedroom unit, Rs. 60,000 per single bedroom unit, and Rs. 20,000 per dormitory bed for projects using identified innovative technologies
  - Exemption of income tax and GST on profits from ARHC operations
  - Concessional project finance at lower interest rates through AHF and PSL
  - 50% additional FAR/FSI free of cost
  - Single window approval system for design/drawings and statutory approvals within 30 days
  - Provision of trunk infrastructure (road, water, sanitation, electricity) at no additional cost
  - Municipal services levied at par with residential projects
  - Use permission changes if needed
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (for loans), Reimbursement (for VGF/TIG), Tax offset (for tax exemptions)
- Duration of benefit: 25 years (concession period)

#### Documents Required
1. Letter of Invitation
2. Technical Proposal
3. Financial Proposal
4. Earnest Money Deposit (EMD)
5. Affidavit
6. GST Registration Details
7. Form of Bankers’ Solvency Certificate
8. Details of eligible similar nature of works completed in last seven years
9. Performance Report of Works referred in Form-C
10. Structure & Organization details
11. EMD receipt format
12. Memorandum of Understanding
13. Performa for Joint Venture Agreement (JVA)
14. Site laboratory and testing of materials setup
15. Suggestive list of plant and equipment at site

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
**For Model-1:**
1. States/UTs/ULBs prepare inventory of government-funded vacant houses
2. Customize and issue RFP based on model RFP from MoHUA
3. Conduct pre-bid meeting
4. Receive and evaluate technical and financial bids
5. Select concessionaire
6. Sign concession agreement

**For Model-2:**
1. MoHUA issues Expression of Interest (EoI)
2. Entities submit application with documents on ARHC website
3. Concerned ULBs shortlist entities based on eligibility
4. Shortlisted entities submit Detailed Project Report (DPR) to ULB for approval
5. Upon approval, entities construct, operate, and maintain ARHCs on their vacant land for 25 years
