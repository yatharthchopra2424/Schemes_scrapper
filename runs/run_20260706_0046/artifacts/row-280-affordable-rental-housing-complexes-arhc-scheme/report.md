# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
The Affordable Rental Housing Complexes (ARHC) Scheme is a sub-scheme under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Urban (PMAY-U) launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) to provide dignified affordable rental housing to urban migrants and poor from EWS/LIG categories close to their workplace. The scheme was approved by the Union Cabinet on 8th July 2020 and aims to address the vision of 'AatmaNirbhar Bharat' and the larger goal of 'Housing for All' under PMAY-U.

**Key Identifiers:**
- **Scheme Name:** Affordable Rental Housing Complexes (ARHC) Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** row-280
- **Ministry / Category:** Housing & Urban
- **Scheme Type:** subsidy
- **Geographic Scope:** Pan-India
- **Implementing Agency:** Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA)
- **Application Portal:** https://arhc.mohua.gov.in
- **Status / Deadlines:** Rolling basis — applications are accepted year-round through EoI for Model-2 and RFP notices for Model-1 as issued by States/UTs/ULBs.
- **Last Updated:** 2020
- **Confidence:** high

### Objectives
The scheme aims to:
- Provide ease of living to urban migrants/poor by ensuring access to dignified affordable rental housing close to their workplace
- Unlock existing vacant housing stock and make it available in urban space
- Propel new investment opportunities and promote entrepreneurship in the rental housing sector
- Create a conducive ecosystem for public/private entities to leverage investment in rental housing
- Prevent growth of slums by providing affordable rental accommodation
- Improve productivity of urban migrants/poor by reducing commute time and improving living conditions
- Support the vision of 'AatmaNirbhar Bharat' and the larger goal of 'Housing for All' under PMAY-U

### Eligibility Matrix
| Criteria | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Urban migrants/poor from EWS/LIG categories including labour, urban poor (street vendors, rickshaw pullers, other service providers etc.), industrial workers, migrants working with market/trade associations, educational/health institutions, hospitality sector, long-term tourists/visitors, students or any other persons of such category. Preference under the Scheme shall be given to persons belonging to Scheduled Castes/Scheduled Tribes/Other Backward Classes, Widows and working Women, Divyang, Minorities, subject to beneficiaries being from EWS/LIG segments as provisioned by the Government. |
| **Model-1 Eligibility (Concessionaire)** | Selected through transparent bidding process by States/UTs/ULBs for repairing, retrofitting, developing, operating, and transferring ARHCs. Entities must comply with NBC/State/Local Authority norms for dwelling units and dormitories, and projects must include at least 40 dwelling units or equivalent dormitory beds. |
| **Model-2 Eligibility (Entities)** | Public/private entities with their own vacant land can apply via Expression of Interest (EoI) to construct, operate, and maintain ARHCs. Entities must comply with NBC/State/Local Authority norms for dwelling units and dormitories, and projects must include at least 40 dwelling units or equivalent dormitory beds. |
| **Project Requirements** | ARHCs will be a mix of single/double bedroom Dwelling Units and Dormitory of 4/6 beds including all common facilities which will be exclusively used for rental housing for a minimum period of 25 years. A maximum of 33% dwelling units in double bedroom form is permissible in any ARHC project. |
| **Land Requirements (Model-2)** | Proposed land for ARHCs should have clear title & free from encumbrance. It should be sufficient enough for constructing at least 40 DUs or equivalent number of dormitory beds as prescribed. It should also be serviced by infrastructure such as road, electricity, water and sewerage and convenient access to major road corridors and expressways. |

### Benefits & Financial Support
#### Financial Support Mechanisms
| Support Type | Details |
|--------------|---------|
| **Concessional Loans** | From Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) under Affordable Housing Fund (AHF) and Priority Sector Lending (PSL) by commercial banks at lower interest rates |
| **Viability Gap Funding (VGF)** | May be provided from central assistance released to States/UTs for JnNURM/RAY projects if needed |
| **Technology Innovation Grant (TIG)** | Rs. 1,00,000 per double bedroom unit, Rs. 60,000 per single bedroom unit, and Rs. 20,000 per dormitory bed is available for projects using identified innovative technologies |
| **Model-1 Investment** | Concessionaires invest their own funds to make houses livable |
| **Model-2 Investment** | Entities bear the investment for construction on their vacant land |

### Application Process
The ARHC scheme operates through two distinct models with different application processes:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Start] --> B{Select Model}
    B -->|Model-1| C[States/UTs/ULBs prepare inventory of government-funded vacant houses]
    C --> D[Customize and issue RFP based on model RFP from MoHUA]
    D --> E[Conduct pre-bid meeting]
    E --> F[Receive and evaluate technical and financial bids]
    F --> G[Select concessionaire]
    G --> H[Sign concession agreement]
    H --> I[End]
    B -->|Model-2| J[MoHUA issues Expression of Interest (EoI)]
    J --> K[Entities submit application with documents on ARHC website]
    K --> L[Concerned ULBs shortlist entities based on eligibility]
    L --> M[Shortlisted entities submit Detailed Project Report (DPR) to ULB for approval]
    M --> N[Upon approval, entities construct, operate, and maintain ARHCs on their vacant land for 25 years]
    N --> O[End]
```

#### Detailed Process Flow

**Model-1 Process:**
1. States/UTs/ULBs prepare inventory of government-funded vacant houses
2. Customize and issue RFP based on model RFP from MoHUA
3. Conduct pre-bid meeting
4. Receive and evaluate technical and financial bids
5. Select concessionaire
6. Sign concession agreement

**Model-2 Process:**
1. MoHUA issues Expression of Interest (EoI)
2. Entities submit application with documents on ARHC website
3. Concerned ULBs shortlist entities based on eligibility
4. Shortlisted entities submit Detailed Project Report (DPR) to ULB for approval
5. Upon approval, entities construct, operate, and maintain ARHCs on their vacant land for 25 years

### Key Caveats & Important Notes
> **Warning:** Projects under ARHCs will be applicable for consideration and funding till PMAY (U) Mission period i.e. March 2022, though approved projects will be funded for another 18 months to enable completion
> 
> **Warning:** ARHCs must be exclusively used for rental housing for a minimum period of 25 years
> 
> **Warning:** A maximum of 33% dwelling units in double bedroom form is permissible in any ARHC project
> 
> **Warning:** Technology Innovation Grant (TIG) is released only for projects using identified innovative technologies
> 
> **Warning:** Beneficiaries are restricted to urban migrants/poor from EWS/LIG categories
