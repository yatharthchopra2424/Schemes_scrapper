## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Beneficiary belongs to Economically Weaker Section (EWS) as defined under PMAY-U (annual household income ≤ ₹3,00,000)
- [ ] Beneficiary belongs to Low Income Group (LIG) as defined under PMAY-U (annual household income > ₹3,00,000 and ≤ ₹6,00,000)
- [ ] Applicant is a resident of an urban area in one of the six LHP locations: Indore (Madhya Pradesh), Rajkot (Gujarat), Chennai (Tamil Nadu), Ranchi (Jharkhand), Agartala (Tripura), or Lucknow (Uttar Pradesh)
- [ ] Applicant does not own a pucca house anywhere in India (as per PMAY-U beneficiary criteria)
- [ ] Applicant is identified and recommended by the respective State/Union Territory Level Sanctioning and Monitoring Committee (SMC) under PMAY-U
- [ ] Applicant has valid identity and residence proof as required by state-level PMAY-U procedures
- [ ] Applicant is not a defaulter of any government housing scheme
- [ ] Applicant agrees to occupy the house as their primary residence and not sublet or transfer ownership without permission

> **Note**: There is no direct application portal for individual beneficiaries. Eligibility is determined through state-level PMAY-U procedures. Consultants must verify eligibility via the respective State Nodal Agency for PMAY-U.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Aadhaar Card of all adult family members
- PAN Card of the applicant
- Income Certificate (issued by competent authority, not older than 6 months)
- Address Proof (electricity bill, water bill, or rental agreement)
- Bank Passbook (first page showing account details)
- Passport-sized photographs (2–4 recent photos)
- Affidavit of self-declaration (stating no pucca house ownership, family details, income)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable for home-based work)
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Recommendation letter from the State/Union Territory Level Sanctioning and Monitoring Committee (SMC) under PMAY-U
- Allotment order or waitlist confirmation from the State Housing Board or Urban Local Body (ULB)
- Domicile certificate of the respective state (MP, GJ, TN, JH, TR, or UP) as per state PMAY-U rules
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the concerned Urban Local Body (ULB) or Development Authority
- Encumbrance Certificate (if applying for house allotment on leased land)
- Marriage certificate (if applying jointly with spouse)
- Birth certificates of dependent children (for family size verification)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (minimum 300 DPI)
- File size per document: ≤ 2 MB
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_ApplicantName_DocumentType_Date.pdf` (e.g., `LHP_RajeshKumar_IncomeCertificate_20240515.pdf`)
- Photographs must be on white background, with face clearly visible (no sunglasses or hats)
- Affidavits and income certificates must be notarized or issued by gazetted officer
- State-specific documents must be issued by competent authority of the respective state (MP, GJ, TN, JH, TR, or UP)
- All documents must be in English or Hindi; if in regional language, attach certified translation
- Aadhaar and PAN must be linked and validated via NSDL/UTIITSL portal

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not applicable. The Light House Projects are pre-approved model housing projects implemented by MoHUA through state agencies. Individual beneficiaries do not submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR). The DPR is prepared by the implementing agency (e.g., State Housing Board, ULB) for project sanction under PMAY-U Technology Sub-Mission.  
**Action**: Verify with the State Nodal Officer for PMAY-U whether any beneficiary-level undertaking or consent form is required.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
Since there is no direct beneficiary application portal for LHPs, the following protocol applies for coordination with state agencies:  
1. Confirm beneficiary eligibility against PMAY-U EWS/LIG criteria (income ≤ ₹6,00,000/annum)  
2. Verify ownership of no pucca house anywhere in India  
3. Ensure all documents are attested and notarized as per state requirements  
4. Cross-check names, addresses, and Aadhaar/PAN details across all documents for consistency  
5. Obtain duly signed and stamped recommendation from the State SMC  
6. Validate that the applicant’s name appears in the state’s PMAY-U beneficiary list (if digitized)  
7. Prepare a covering letter addressed to the State Nodal Agency, referencing the LHP scheme and PMAY-U guidelines  
8. Retain copies of all submitted documents and obtain acknowledgment receipt from the state office  

> **Portal Note**: No central portal exists for LHP beneficiary applications. All coordination occurs through state-level PMAY-U offices. Refer to [PMAY-U Official Website](https://pmay-urban.gov.in) for state contact details.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit documents to the respective State Nodal Agency for PMAY-U or Urban Local Body (ULB) in hard copy or as directed by the state

- Scan and store the acknowledgment receipt in the case folder  
- Record the submission date, office name, and officer contacted in the case log  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days post-submission  
- Do NOT submit via any central portal — LHPs have no direct beneficiary application interface
