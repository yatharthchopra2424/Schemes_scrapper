## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial benefit delivered through central and state government financial assistance under PMAY-U norms for EWS/LIG housing units, which represents significant value for implementing agencies and developers.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the scheme's medium confidence level and dependency on state-level Sanctioning and Monitoring Committees (SMCs) for beneficiary allotment, introducing execution risk despite central funding certainty.
3. **Tiered Engagement Model**: The Enterprise tier applies as LHPs are large-scale model projects requiring coordination with MoHUA, state governments, and implementing agencies — typically involving developers, contractors, or housing boards with project values exceeding ₹10Cr.
4. **Processing Time Consideration**: While central financial assistance is fixed per unit under PMAY-U, the overall project timeline and state-level allotment processes affect revenue recognition, warranting a lag in success fee realization.
