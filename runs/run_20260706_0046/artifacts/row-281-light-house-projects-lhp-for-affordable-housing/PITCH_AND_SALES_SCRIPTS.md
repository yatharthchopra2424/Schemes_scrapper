## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping urban poor, economically weaker sections (EWS), and low-income groups (LIG) access government housing schemes like the Light House Projects (LHP) for Affordable Housing, which delivers ready-to-live-in homes using innovative, sustainable technologies under PMAY-U. I’d like to understand your current housing situation and see if this scheme could support your needs—no obligation, just a conversation.

### Discovery Questions
- Are you currently seeking affordable housing in an urban area, and are you classified as Economically Weaker Section (EWS) or Low Income Group (LIG) under PMAY-U guidelines?
- Have you been identified or recommended by your State/Union Territory Level Sanctioning and Monitoring Committee (SMC) for housing assistance?
- Are you looking for a ready-to-live-in housing unit constructed with disaster-resilient and energy-efficient technology?
- Is your preferred location among the six LHP sites: Indore (MP), Rajkot (GJ), Chennai (TN), Ranchi (JH), Agartala (Tripura), or Lucknow (UP)?
- Are you open to housing allotment through state-level procedures under PMAY-U, rather than a direct central application?
- Do you have documentation ready to verify your income, identity, and residency as required by state SMCs?
- Are you interested in a housing solution that promotes sustainable construction and serves as a demonstration site for new technologies?
- Have you previously applied for or received benefits under any PMAY-U vertical (like AHP, BLC, or ISSR)?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many EWS and LIG households in urban areas face significant challenges in accessing safe, affordable, and quality housing. Many live in informal settlements or substandard housing lacking disaster resilience, energy efficiency, and basic amenities. The high cost of formal urban housing often puts homeownership out of reach, despite government efforts under PMAY-U.

**Scheme intro:**
The Light House Projects (LHP) for Affordable Housing is a flagship initiative of the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) under the Technology Sub-Mission of Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Urban (PMAY-U). It provides ready-to-live-in housing units constructed using innovative, sustainable, and disaster-resilient technologies. Financial support comes from central assistance under PMAY-U, supplemented by state government and beneficiary contributions, with per-unit costs varying by location and technology.

- You are identified as EWS or LIG under PMAY-U norms

- You are willing to undergo state-level SMC verification and allotment procedures under PMAY-U  
- You seek a housing solution that is not only affordable but also built with advanced, resilient, and energy-efficient construction methods
