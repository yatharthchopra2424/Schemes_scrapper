### Light House Projects (LHP) for Affordable Housing
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA)
- **Portal / Application URL:** [https://pmay-urban.gov.in](https://pmay-urban.gov.in)
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Not applicable (individual beneficiaries)
- Age of business: Not applicable
- Sector: Housing (Affordable Housing)
- Revenue cap: Not applicable (based on income criteria under PMAY-U for EWS/LIG)
- Employee count: Not applicable
- Other: Beneficiaries must be from Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) and Low Income Groups (LIG) as defined under PMAY-U; selected through State/Union Territory Level Sanctioning and Monitoring Committees (SMCs)

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Affordable, ready-to-live-in housing units constructed using innovative, sustainable, and disaster-resilient technologies; financial assistance per EWS/LIG house as per PMAY-U norms (central assistance) supplemented by state and beneficiary shares
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (to implementing agencies for construction; beneficiaries receive housing units)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (allotment of completed housing unit)

#### Documents Required
1. Identity proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, etc.)
2. Address proof
3. Income certificate (to establish EWS/LIG status)
4. Affidavit/declaration as per state-specific PMAY-U requirements
5. Any other documents mandated by the State/UT Level Sanctioning and Monitoring Committee (SMC)

#### Application Process
1. Beneficiaries are identified and selected by the respective State/Union Territory Level Sanctioning and Monitoring Committees (SMCs) under PMAY-U
2. Allotment is done through state-level procedures based on PMAY-U beneficiary criteria
3. No direct application portal exists for individual beneficiaries to apply for LHPs
4. Selection follows state-specific PMAY-U eligibility and verification processes
