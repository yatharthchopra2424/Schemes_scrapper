## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the 100% production support provided under the NFDC Production Grants scheme.
2. Tiered retainer structure reflecting client scale and project complexity, with higher tiers for mid-market and enterprise clients undertaking feature films, documentaries, or children’s films.
3. Success fee capped within industry-standard ranges, adjusted for the scheme’s non-repayable grant nature and high impact on filmmaker careers.
4. Scheme-specific note: The NFDC Production Grants scheme carries moderate risk due to competitive selection via government-supported film festivals (e.g., IFFI, Film Bazaar) and dependency on alignment with DCDFC guidelines; however, approved projects receive 100% production cost coverage, ensuring high client value upon success.
