# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good day! I’m reaching out because the NFDC Production Grants are designed specifically for debutant filmmakers, content creators, and filmmakers looking to produce their first feature film, children’s film, or documentary with full production support. I’d love to understand your project and see if this aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of project are you developing—feature film, children’s film, or documentary?
2. Is this your debut project in this format (first feature, first children’s film, or first documentary)?
3. Have you submitted or plan to submit through a government-supported film festival like IFFI, MIFF, or Film Bazaar?
4. What is the current stage of your project—script development, pre-production, or production?
5. Do you have a co-production partner in mind, either from India or abroad?
6. Does your project align with NFDC’s production guidelines and objectives, such as promoting Indian cultural diversity or supporting debutant talent?
7. Are you seeking 100% production cost support or a co-production arrangement?
8. What is your timeline for completion and submission?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Project is a debut feature film, children’s film, or documentary
- [ ] Applicant is a debutant filmmaker or content creator
- [ ] Project submitted or to be submitted via government-supported film festival (IFFI, MIFF, Film Bazaar, etc.)
- [ ] Project aligns with NFDC’s production guidelines and DCDFC scheme objectives
- [ ] Seeking 100% production support or co-production opportunity
- [ ] Project reflects diversity of Indian cinema and culture
- [ ] Applicant is open to partnership with Indian or international filmmakers
- [ ] Project is viable for completion within NFDC’s execution framework

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like your project could be a strong fit for the NFDC Production Grants under the DCDFC scheme, which offers 100% production support for debut feature films, children’s films, and documentaries. Let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Debutant filmmakers often struggle to secure full production funding for their first feature, children’s film, or documentary, especially when aiming to reflect India’s cultural diversity or pursue co-production opportunities. Many face barriers due to lack of access to grants, production guidelines, or platforms for global exposure.

**Scheme intro:**
The NFDC Production Grants, administered by the National Film Development Corporation Limited (NFDC) under the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting’s Development, Communication and Dissemination of Filmic Content (DCDFC) scheme, provide 100% production cost support for debut feature films, children’s films, and documentary films. The scheme also facilitates co-production opportunities with Indian and international filmmakers, access to NFDC’s production expertise, and participation in initiatives like Film Bazaar for global exposure.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are developing a debut feature film, children’s film, or documentary — core eligibility under DCDFC
- You plan to submit through a government-supported film festival (e.g., IFFI, MIFF, Film Bazaar) — a mandatory pathway
- Your project aims to promote Indian cultural diversity or support emerging talent — aligned with NFDC’s objectives
- You are open to co-production with Indian or international partners — encouraged under the scheme
