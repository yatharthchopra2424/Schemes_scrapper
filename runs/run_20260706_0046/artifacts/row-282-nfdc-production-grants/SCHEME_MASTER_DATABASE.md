### NFDC Production Grants
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Film Development Corporation Limited (NFDC)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nfdcindia.com/film-production/en
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Debutant filmmakers, filmmakers, content creators
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Media & Entertainment (film production)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must apply through government-supported film festivals such as IFFI (Goa), Mumbai International Film Festival, Film Bazaar, etc.; projects must align with NFDC's production guidelines and objectives; funding subject to DCDFC scheme guidelines

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: 100% production cost for debut feature films, children's films, and documentary films
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (NFDC undertakes 100% production or co-produces)
- Duration of benefit: One-time per project

#### Documents Required
1. Feature, documentary, animation project submissions
2. Application through government-supported film festivals (IFFI, MIFF, Film Bazaar, etc.)
3. Project proposal aligned with NFDC's production guidelines
4. Any additional documents as per DCDFC scheme guidelines

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Apply through government-supported film festivals such as IFFI (Goa), Mumbai International Film Festival, Film Bazaar, etc.
2. Submit feature, documentary, animation projects, etc. for consideration.
3. NFDC evaluates submissions under the DCDFC scheme.
4. Selected projects receive 100% production support or co-production opportunities.
5. Follow NFDC's extant production guidelines for execution.
