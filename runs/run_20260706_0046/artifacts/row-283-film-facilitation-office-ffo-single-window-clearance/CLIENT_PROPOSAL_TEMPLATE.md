## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment and scheme suitability analysis  
2. Preparation of application as per FFO document requirements  
3. Portal registration and user setup on https://ffo.gov.in  
4. Compilation and uploading of required documents (script/synopsis, production schedule, location details, drone/UAV info if applicable, identification proof, incorporation certificate, equipment and crew details)  
5. Application submission and coordination with FFO for forwarding to relevant agencies (MoD, MHA, MoCA, ASI, state governments, etc.)  
6. Status tracking and follow-up with concerned authorities for timely NOCs/clearances  
7. Final delivery of all obtained clearances and closure report
