## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are tied to the tangible benefit of time and cost savings from streamlined clearances, not direct financial grants.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s facilitative nature (no direct monetary disbursement) and dependency on external agency response times, success fees reflect execution risk in coordination and follow-up.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainers scale with client capacity to engage in ongoing compliance and project-based clearance needs, ensuring accessibility for production houses of varying sizes.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Since the Film Facilitation Office (FFO) provides no direct financial subsidy or grant, the “scheme value” is derived from operational efficiencies — reduced delays, avoided penalties, and accelerated project timelines. Processing times vary based on agency responsiveness (per evidence), introducing variability in fee realization; thus, success fees are structured to reflect effort expended rather than outcome guarantees.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Retainer for ongoing Film Facilitation Office (FFO) Single Window Clearance coordination services, including application preparation, document submission, inter-agency tracking, and status reporting for film shooting permissions across ministries (MoD, MHA, ASI, etc.) and state governments.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for successful procurement of No Objection Certificates (NOCs) and clearances via the Film Facilitation Office (FFO) portal (https://ffo.gov.in), implemented by the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, Government of India, covering coordination with central and state agencies for location, drone, road closure, and other regulatory permissions.
