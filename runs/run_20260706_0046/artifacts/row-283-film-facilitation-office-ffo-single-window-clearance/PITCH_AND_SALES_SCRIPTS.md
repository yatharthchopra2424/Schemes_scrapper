## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping filmmakers and production houses navigate government clearances for film shoots across India. Many of our clients in the media and entertainment sector tell us they spend weeks chasing permissions from multiple ministries — defence, home affairs, civil aviation, archaeology — just to get a single location approved. The Film Facilitation Office (FFO) was created exactly to solve this: a single window for all central and state clearances. I’d like to understand your upcoming shoot plans and see if we can help you avoid delays and administrative bottlenecks.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of film production are you planning (feature, documentary, ad, web series), and where do you intend to shoot?
- Have you already identified specific locations that require permissions from central government agencies (e.g., defence sites, heritage monuments, airports, border areas)?
- Are you planning to use drones or UAVs for aerial shots during the shoot?
- Do you need road closures, traffic management, or temporary structures on public land?
- What is your planned shoot schedule, and how much lead time do you have before filming begins?
- Have you previously applied for government clearances directly, and if so, what challenges did you face (delays, multiple touchpoints, unclear requirements)?
- Are you an Indian production house or a foreign entity planning to shoot in India?
- Do you have a certificate of incorporation or equivalent legal registration for your production entity?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Intends to shoot films in India (feature, documentary, ad, web series, etc.)
- [ ] Requires permissions from central or state government authorities
- [ ] Needs clearances for locations, drone usage, road closures, or other regulatory approvals
- [ ] Is a filmmaker, production house, or media and entertainment professional
- [ ] Has or can obtain: script/synopsis, production schedule, location details, ID proof, incorporation certificate (if applicable), equipment/crew details
- [ ] Seeks coordination with ministries such as MoD, MHA, MoCA, ASI, or state governments
- [ ] Wants time-bound processing and reduced procedural complexity
- [ ] Is open to using a facilitation service (not a financial grant) to streamline approvals

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re dealing with exactly the kind of multi-agency clearance complexity that the Film Facilitation Office (FFO) was designed to simplify. The FFO doesn’t provide funding, but it acts as a single-handedly coordinates with all the relevant ministries — defence, home affairs, civil aviation, archaeology, and state bodies — to get your NOCs faster and with less back-and-forth. Let me show you how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Filmmakers and production houses often waste valuable pre-production time navigating a fragmented clearance system. Shooting at defence sites, heritage monuments, airports, or using drones requires separate NOCs from ministries like MoD, MHA, MoCA, ASI, and state governments — each with its own process, timelines, and documentation needs. This leads to delays, miscommunication, and last-minute shoot cancellations, especially when timelines are tight.

**Scheme intro:**
The Film Facilitation Office (FFO), operated by the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, Government of India, is a single window clearance system that coordinates permissions for film shoots across India. It does not provide financial grants or subsidies but offers end-to-end facilitation: submitting your application, forwarding it to the relevant agencies (MoD, MHA, MoCA, ASI, state governments, etc.), tracking status, and returning consolidated NOCs/clearances via its portal at https://ffo.gov.in. The service is available year-round on a rolling basis for both Indian and foreign productions.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are planning a film shoot in India requiring permissions from central or state authorities  
- You need clearances for locations, drone usage, road closures, or similar regulatory approvals  
- You are a filmmaker, production house, or media and entertainment professional  
- You can provide core documents: script/synopsis, production schedule, location details, ID proof, and incorporation certificate (if applicable)
