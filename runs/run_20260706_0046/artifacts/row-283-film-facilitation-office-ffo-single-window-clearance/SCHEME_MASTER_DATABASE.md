### Film Facilitation Office (FFO) – Single Window Clearance
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://ffo.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Filmmakers, production houses, or any entity intending to shoot films in India
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Media and Entertainment
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Requires permissions from central or state government authorities for locations, drone usage, road closures, or other regulatory clearances

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: No direct financial support; facilitation of clearances and coordination services
- Disbursement mode: Not applicable (non-financial facilitation)
- Duration of benefit: One-time per project

#### Documents Required
1. Script or synopsis of the film
2. Production details including dates and schedule
3. Location details and maps
4. Drone/UAV details (if applicable)
5. Identification proof of applicant
6. Certificate of incorporation (for production companies)
7. Details of equipment and crew
8. Any other documents as required by specific agencies

#### Application Process
1. Visit the FFO portal at https://ffo.gov.in
2. Register as a user on the portal
3. Fill out the online application form for the required clearances
4. Upload necessary documents such as script, production details, location information, and identification proofs
5. Submit the application
6. FFO forwards the application to relevant concerned agencies
7. Track application status through the portal
8. Receive NOCs/clearances from respective agencies via FFO coordination
