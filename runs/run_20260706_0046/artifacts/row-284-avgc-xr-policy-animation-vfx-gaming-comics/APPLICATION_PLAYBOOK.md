# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- ✅ Entity type: Indian entity (startup, MSME, educational institution, or creative industry professional) engaged in animation, VFX, gaming, comics, or extended reality (XR) activities  
- ✅ Registration: Valid registration under Indian Companies Act 2013, LLP Act 2008, or recognized as a startup by DPIIT  
- ✅ Sector: Animation, VFX, Gaming, Comics, or XR (AVGC-XR)  
- ✅ Turnover: No specific threshold mentioned in evidence; verify with nodal officer for sub-scheme requirements  
- ✅ Employee count: No specific threshold mentioned; verify with nodal officer for sub-scheme requirements  
- ✅ Age of business: No specific threshold mentioned; verify with nodal officer for sub-scheme requirements  
- ✅ Geographic scope: Pan-India eligibility  
- ✅ Compliance: Must comply with terms and conditions of respective sub-schemes under AVGC-XR Policy  
- ✅ IP rights: Intellectual property rights must be clearly defined and owned by the applicant or appropriately licensed  

> **Note**: Specific eligibility criteria (grants, incubation, infrastructure support) are defined in individual scheme guidelines issued by MeitY or its authorized agencies (e.g., Digital Entertainment and Gaming Hub). Verify exact requirements for the target sub-scheme.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration  
- PAN Card  
- GST Registration Certificate  
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)  
- Audited Financial Statements (last 3 years)  
- Income Tax Returns (last 3 years)  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- Authorized Signatory Letter  
- Board Resolution authorizing application  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Project proposal detailing AVGC-XR activity (animation, VFX, gaming, comics, XR)  
- Proof of engagement in AVGC-XR sector (e.g., portfolio, past projects, client list)  
- Intellectual Property (IP) ownership or licensing documents  
- Detailed project report (DPR) including objectives, timeline, budget, milestones  
- Team credentials and expertise in AVGC-XR domain  
- Infrastructure/technology adoption plan (if applying for infrastructure support)  
- Skill development or training plan (if applicable)  
- Market access or international collaboration plan (if applicable)  
- Regulatory compliance documents (if applicable)  

### Document Quality Check
- ✅ All documents legible and in English or Hindi (certified translation if in another language)  
- ✅ File size < 10 MB per document (unless otherwise specified on portal)  
- ✅ Document names match checklist (e.g., "PAN_Card.pdf", "GST_Certificate.pdf")  
- ✅ Certificates are valid and not expired  
- ✅ Financial documents audited and signed by CA  
- ✅ Project report includes clear milestones and budget utilization plan  
- ✅ IP documents show clear ownership or licensing terms  
- ✅ All documents uploaded in PDF format (unless portal specifies otherwise)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- **Required**: Yes, a detailed project report (DPR) is required for AVGC-XR Policy applications, as specified in the evidence: "Applications are typically submitted online through designated government portals after registration and document upload" and "detailed calls for proposals, eligibility criteria, and submission procedures are published."  
- **Standard sections to include**:  
  1. Executive Summary  
  2. Project Objectives (aligned with AVGC-XR Policy goals: global destination, skill development, infrastructure, innovation, industry-academia collaboration, regulatory support, investment attraction, IP strengthening)  
  3. Technical Approach (animation, VFX, gaming, comics, XR methodology)  
  4. Work Plan & Timeline (with milestones)  
  5. Budget Breakdown (utilization of financial support: grants, seed funding, VC linkages, infrastructure subsidies)  
  6. Team Expertise  
  7. Risk Assessment & Mitigation  
  8. Sustainability & Scalability Plan  
  9. Expected Outcomes (market access, IP creation, employment, export potential)  
  10. Annexures (supporting documents)  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Register first** on the MeitY AVGC-XR portal (https://www.meity.gov.in/avgc) before filling the form.  
2. **Use official guidelines**: Refer to the latest call for proposals and eligibility criteria published on the portal for the specific sub-scheme (grants, incubation, infrastructure, etc.).  
3. **Fill all mandatory fields**: Do not leave any required field blank; if not applicable, write "NA" with justification.  
4. **Match document names**: Ensure uploaded files exactly match the names referenced in the form.  
5. **Budget accuracy**: Financial figures must match the audited statements and project report.  
6. **IP declaration**: Clearly state IP ownership/licensing in the designated section.  
7. **Declaration & signature**: Complete the self-declaration section and upload a signed copy (digital signature acceptable if permitted).  
8. **Preview before submit**: Use the portal’s preview function to verify all entries and attachments.  

### Internal Review Gate
- ✅ All eligibility criteria verified and documented  
- ✅ Application form 100% complete with no blank mandatory fields  
- ✅ All universal and scheme-specific documents attached and correctly named  
- ✅ Document quality check passed (legibility, size, format, validity)  
- ✅ Project report includes all standard sections and aligns with AVGC-XR Policy objectives  
- ✅ Financials consistent across documents (ITR, audited statements, project budget)  
- ✅ IP ownership/licensing clearly declared and supported  
- ✅ Authorized signatory declaration completed and uploaded  
- ✅ Application preview reviewed on portal before final submission

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the MeitY AVGC-XR portal: **https://www.meity.gov.in/avgc**  
- Take a **screenshot of the confirmation page** showing:  
  - Application Reference Number  
  - Date and time of submission  
  - Applicant name  
- Save the screenshot as: `AVGC_XR_Submission_[Case ID]_[ReferenceNumber].png`  
- Store the reference number in the case file and set automated reminders for:  
  - Follow-up at 15 days  
  - Escalation at 30 days if no acknowledgment  
  - Status check every 15 days thereafter until approval or rejection

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- ✅ Obtain and verify the approval letter from MeitY (check for sanctioned amount, validity period, and conditions)  
- ✅ Confirm the sanctioned amount matches the approved project budget and financial support mechanism (grant, seed funding, VC linkage, infrastructure subsidy)  
- ✅ Utilization Certificate (UC) preparation:  
  - Format as per MeitY guidelines (verify with nodal officer)  
  - Link expenditure to project milestones  
  - Get UC audited by a Chartered Accountant  
  - Submit UC within stipulated timeline (usually within 30 days of fund utilization or project completion)  
- ✅ Invoice success fee as per consultancy agreement  
- ✅ Monitor compliance with reporting requirements and milestones (to avoid withdrawal of support)  
- ✅ Ensure IP rights remain clearly defined and owned/licensed as per approval conditions  
- ✅ Maintain records for potential audit or site visit by MeitY or its agencies (e.g., DEGH)
