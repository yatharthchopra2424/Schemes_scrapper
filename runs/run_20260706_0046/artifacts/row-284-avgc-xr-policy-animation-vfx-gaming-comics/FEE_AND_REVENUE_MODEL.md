## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client under the AVGC-XR Policy.
2. Success fees are contingent on successful grant approval and disbursement, reducing client risk.
3. Retainers cover initial eligibility assessment, application preparation, and submission support.
4. Scheme-specific note: The AVGC-XR Policy has a medium confidence level in benefit quantification due to variable support mechanisms (grants, seed funding, VC linkages, subsidies). Processing time is not standardized and depends on the specific sub-scheme and project appraisal, introducing revenue lag uncertainty.
