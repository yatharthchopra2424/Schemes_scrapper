# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping animation, VFX, gaming, comics, and extended reality (XR) businesses access government support for project development, infrastructure, and skill-building. Many studios and creators in this space don’t realize they qualify for MeitY’s AVGC-XR Policy—which offers grants, seed funding, venture capital linkages, and infrastructure subsidies. I’d like to understand your current projects and growth goals to see if this policy could support your next phase.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of AVGC-XR work are you primarily engaged in—animation, VFX, gaming, comics, or extended reality (AR/VR/MR)?
2. When was your company or creative practice incorporated or established?
3. Are you currently registered as a startup, MSME, educational institution, or working as an independent creative professional?
4. Do you have a specific project in development that requires funding for production, technology adoption, or infrastructure?
5. Are you seeking support for skill development, mentorship, or access to shared facilities like studios or technology parks?
6. Have you explored international collaboration, co-production opportunities, or participation in global festivals as part of your growth strategy?
7. Are you looking to strengthen your intellectual property (IP) rights management or seeking regulatory facilitation for your projects?
8. What stage is your project at—concept, prototype, production, or scaling—and what kind of support would move it forward?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Engaged in animation, VFX, gaming, comics, or XR activities  
- [ ] Indian entity (startup, MSME, educational institution, or creative professional)  
- [ ] Has a defined project or activity requiring support (development, infrastructure, skill-building, etc.)  
- [ ] Seeking financial support via grants, seed funding, VC linkages, or infrastructure subsidies  
- [ ] Interested in market access, international collaboration, IP management, or regulatory facilitation  
- [ ] Willing to comply with sub-scheme guidelines, reporting requirements, and milestone adherence  
- [ ] IP rights clearly defined or licensable (if applicable to the sub-scheme)  
- [ ] Not currently under restriction for non-compliance with prior government scheme guidelines  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re working on [specific project type, e.g., an XR training module or animated short] and need support with [funding/infrastructure/skill development]. The **AVGC-XR Policy** by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) is designed exactly for creators like you—offering financial support, shared technology access, and mentorship to help Indian AVGC-XR ventures scale. Let me walk you through how it works.
