## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit delivered under the Ambedkar Hastshilp Vikas Yojana, ensuring clients pay only for realized outcomes.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s low confidence rating and limited evidence of disbursement or processing timelines, retainer fees should reflect the higher effort required for eligibility assessment and documentation.
3. **Transparency**: All fee structures are disclosed upfront, with clear linkage to scheme-specific milestones such as application submission and approval.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Ambedkar Hastshilp Vikas Yojana targets individual artisans and self-help groups in the handicrafts sector, typically involving grant amounts below ₹10 lakhs. Due to the scheme’s low confidence rating and lack of verifiable success metrics in the crawled data, the risk profile is elevated. Consultants should anticipate longer engagement cycles and allocate additional resources for beneficiary verification and application support.
