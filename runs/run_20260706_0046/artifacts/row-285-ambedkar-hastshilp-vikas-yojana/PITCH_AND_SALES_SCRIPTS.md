## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good [morning/afternoon], I'm reaching out because I specialise in helping artisans and handicraft businesses access government support designed specifically for the handicrafts sector. The Ambedkar Hastshilp Vikas Yojana is a Ministry of Textiles initiative focused on uplifting handicraft artisans through skill development, marketing support, and infrastructure assistance. I’d like to understand your current work and see if this scheme could support your growth — no obligation, just a conversation.

### Discovery Questions
- How long have you been practicing your handicraft craft, and are you registered as an artisan or part of a handicrafts cooperative/SHG/NGO?
- Are you currently engaged in any skill upgradation, design development, or marketing activities for your handicraft products?
- Do you have access to raw materials, tools, or workspace, or are you looking for support in these areas?
- Are you based in a recognised handicraft cluster or region targeted by government handicraft schemes?
- Have you previously applied for any government handicraft or artisan welfare schemes?
- Are you interested in participating in exhibitions, craft fairs, or e-commerce platforms to sell your products?
- Do you have identity proof, proof of artisan status, and details of your craft practice readily available?
- Are you working individually or as part of a group (e.g., self-help group, cooperative, NGO) that could benefit from collective training or infrastructure support?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Individual artisan, member of a Self-Help Group (SHG), cooperative, or NGO engaged in handicrafts
- [ ] Practising a recognised handicraft craft as defined by the Office of the Development Commissioner (Handicrafts)
- [ ] Based in India, preferably in a recognised handicraft cluster or region
- [ ] Possesses valid identity proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, etc.) and proof of artisan status
- [ ] Not currently receiving duplicate benefits for the same activity under another central government scheme
- [ ] Willing to participate in training, design, marketing, or infrastructure development activities
- [ ] Priority may be given to artisans from SC/ST, women, and weaker sections (as per scheme guidelines)
- [ ] Engaged in or planning activities such as skill upgradation, product development, market promotion, or infrastructure enhancement

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like the Ambedkar Hastshilp Vikas Yojana could be a strong fit — this scheme is designed exactly for artisans like you to access skill development, marketing, and infrastructure support through the Ministry of Textiles. Let me explain how it works and what it could mean for your craft.
