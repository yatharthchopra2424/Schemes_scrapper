- [ ] Udyam Registration Certificate (if MSME-registered)
- [ ] Aadhaar and PAN of authorized signatory
- [ ] Cancelled cheque or bank passbook copy

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- [ ] Detailed project report outlining proposed design and technical upgradation activities
- [ ] Quotations from vendors for machinery, software, or technical services to be upgraded
- [ ] Proof of existing design capabilities (photographs of products, design catalogs, or portfolio)
- [ ] Letter of intent or agreement with designer/technical expert (if outsourcing upgradation work)
- [ ] Certificate from District Industries Centre (DIC) or Handicrafts Marketing and Service Extension Centre (HMSEC) verifying the unit’s engagement in handicrafts
- [ ] Declaration that the unit has not received similar assistance under any other GoI scheme in the past 3 years

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)
- [ ] Names on documents match exactly with the business registration and authorized signatory
- [ ] GST, Udyam, and ITR documents are valid and not expired
- [ ] Bank statements are stamped and signed by the bank authority
- [ ] All certificates are issued by recognized government or statutory bodies
- [ ] Documents are in English or Hindi; if in regional language, attach certified translation
- [ ] Project report is signed and stamped by the applicant and/or chartered engineer (if applicable)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required for this scheme.  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Business Profile (existing product range, market presence, turnover)  
- Need for Design & Technical Upgradation (current limitations, market trends)  
- Objectives of the Project  
- Proposed Activities (specific design interventions, technology adoption, skill upgradation)  
- Implementation Timeline (Gantt chart)  
- Budget Breakdown (cost of machinery, software, training, consultant fees)  
- Expected Outcomes (increased productivity, new product lines, marketability, export potential)  
- Sustainability Plan (post-project maintenance, skill retention)  
- Annexures: Quotations, product photos, designer CVs, Udyam/GST certificates  

> **Note**: Confirm exact DPR format and length requirements with the nodal officer, as guidelines may vary by implementing agency.
