## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-aligned pricing**: Fees are structured as a percentage of the grant amount secured, ensuring our success is tied to client outcomes.
2. **Risk-adjusted retainers**: Retainer fees reflect the effort required for application preparation, documentation, and compliance specific to the Design & Technical Upgradation Scheme for Handicrafts.
3. **Transparency**: All fees are disclosed upfront; no hidden charges. Clients receive detailed invoices for both retainer and success fee components.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: This scheme has a **low confidence** rating in the evidence, indicating limited publicly available data on approval rates and processing times. This increases the perceived risk for applicants, justifying a moderate retainer to cover advisory effort in navigating uncertain eligibility and submission requirements.
