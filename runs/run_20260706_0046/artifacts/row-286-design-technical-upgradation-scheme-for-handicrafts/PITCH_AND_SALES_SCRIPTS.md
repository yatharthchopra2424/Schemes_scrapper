## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping handicraft artisans and small enterprises access government support for design and technical upgradation. Many makers in this sector struggle to modernise their tools or adopt new designs due to cost barriers—this scheme like you who are looking to enhance product quality and competitiveness through better technology and design input.

### Discovery Questions
- When was your handicraft unit established or registered?
- Are you currently engaged in the production of handicraft goods as defined under the Handicrafts sector?
- Have you recently invested in or are you planning to upgrade your production tools, machinery, or design capabilities?
- Do you have access to technical expertise or design inputs for product development, or are you seeking support in this area?
- What is your annual turnover from handicraft sales, and are you looking to scale or diversify your product range?
- Are you operating as an individual artisan, a self-help group, cooperative, or a small enterprise?
- Have you previously applied for any government scheme related to handicrafts or technical upgradation?
- Are you based in a recognised handicraft cluster or region supported by developmental initiatives?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Unit is engaged in handicraft production (as per scheme definition)
- [ ] Unit is registered or operational (individual artisan, SHG, cooperative, or small enterprise)
- [ ] Seeking support for design and/or technical upgradation (tools, machinery, design input)
- [ ] Not exceeding eligibility thresholds for turnover or investment (where specified)
- [ ] Located in India and operating within the handicrafts sector
- [ ] Preference may be given to units in recognised clusters or with potential for scalability
- [ ] No prior benefit availed under this specific scheme (if applicable)
- [ ] Willing to contribute beneficiary share as per scheme norms (if cost-sharing applies)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re looking to modernise your handicraft production—whether through better tools, new designs, or improved techniques. The **Design & Technical Upgradation Scheme for Handicrafts** is designed exactly for this: it offers financial support to artisans and enterprises like yours to upgrade design capabilities and technical infrastructure. ## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Handicraft artisans often struggle to compete in modern markets due to outdated tools, limited access to contemporary design, and high costs of technological upgradation. This leads to lower product value, reduced market reach, and difficulty in scaling—despite strong craftsmanship and cultural heritage.

**Scheme intro:**
The **Design & Technical Upgradation Scheme for Handicrafts**, administered by the Office of the Development Commissioner (Handicrafts) under the Ministry of Textiles, provides financial assistance for the procurement of improved tools, equipment, and design inputs to enhance product quality and market competitiveness. The scheme offers a grant-in-aid covering up to 75% of the project cost, subject to a maximum ceiling as per project appraisal.
