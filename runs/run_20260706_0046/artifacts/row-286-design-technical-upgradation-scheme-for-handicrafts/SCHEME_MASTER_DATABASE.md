#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance up to 50% of project cost for technical upgradation, design development, and infrastructure support (subject to scheme-specific caps)
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement (after verification of expenditure and project completion)
- Duration of benefit: One-time grant per approved project

#### Documents Required
1. Application form (duly filled and signed)
2. Project proposal detailing design upgradation, technical intervention, and expected outcomes
3. Cost estimates and quotations from vendors for equipment, software, or consultancy
4. Registration certificate of the unit/NGO/SHG/cooperative
5. Audited financial statements of the last 2 years (if applicable)
6. Bank account details (cancelled cheque or bank passbook copy)
7. Proof of engagement in handicrafts (e.g., product samples, photographs, past exhibition participation)
8. Artisan details (if applicable: list of artisans, their Aadhaar, bank details)
9. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from State Directorate of Handicrafts (if required)
10. Undertaking regarding utilization of funds and non-submission of duplicate application elsewhere

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify need for design/technical upgradation and prepare project proposal
2. Contact State Directorate of Handicrafts or designated implementing agency for guidance
3. Fill out application form and attach all required documents
4. Submit application to the concerned District Industry Centre (DIC) or State Handicrafts Corporation
5. Application undergoes preliminary scrutiny at state level
6. Shortlisted proposals forwarded to Office of the Development Commissioner (Handicrafts) for evaluation
7. Technical committee assesses feasibility, design innovation, and cost-effectiveness
8. Approved projects receive sanction letter with terms and conditions
9. Implement the project as per approved plan and timelines
10. Submit expenditure statements, utilization certificates, and completion report
11. Upon verification, reimbursement of eligible amount is processed to the beneficiary’s bank account
