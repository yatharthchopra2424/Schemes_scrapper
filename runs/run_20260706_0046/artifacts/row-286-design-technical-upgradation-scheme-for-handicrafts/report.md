### Overview
The **Design & Technical Upgradation Scheme for Handicrafts** (Scheme ID: row-286) is a government initiative administered under the Ministry of Textiles, specifically targeting the handicrafts sector. It falls under the broader category of Handicrafts & Culture and is classified as an "other" type scheme. The scheme aims to enhance the competitiveness of handicraft artisans and enterprises by supporting design innovation and technical upgradation of production processes. Despite a low confidence rating in the source extraction, the scheme is recognized as a key intervention for preserving traditional crafts while integrating modern design and technology.

### Objectives
The primary objectives of the scheme are:
- To provide financial assistance for design development and product diversification in handicrafts.
- To support the adoption of improved tools, equipment, and technology for better quality and efficiency.
- To enhance market linkages and value addition for handicraft products.
- To preserve traditional skills while encouraging innovation through contemporary design inputs.
- To increase the income and sustainability of handicraft artisans, particularly those in clusters and cooperatives.
