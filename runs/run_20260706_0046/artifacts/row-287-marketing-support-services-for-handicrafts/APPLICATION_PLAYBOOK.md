## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- **Not required** for this scheme. The application focuses on event participation details and expenditure claims rather than a detailed project report.  
- However, applicants must provide a clear list of products to be showcased and quotations for estimated expenses.  
- If applying for support to organize a craft bazaar or marketing event, a brief event proposal (objectives, expected footfall, budget, venue details) may be required — **verify with the nodal officer** for specific event-based requirements.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the latest application form from the regional office of the Office of the Development Commissioner (Handicrafts) or request it via email/phone.  
2. Fill all fields in block letters; avoid overwriting or use of correction fluid.  
3. Ensure the applicant name matches exactly across PAN, Aadhaar, bank account, and registration certificates.  
4. Clearly specify the name, dates, venue, and organizer of the target exhibition, craft bazaar, or trade fair.  
5. Provide detailed product descriptions (name, material, technique, quantity) for all handicraft items to be showcased.  
6. Attach quotations/invoices for stall rent, travel, publicity, and other expenses — ensure they are itemized and include GSTIN (if applicable).  
7. Declare that the applicant has not received duplicate funding for the same event from any other government scheme.  
8. Sign the declaration form; authorized signatory must sign for entities (with seal/stamp, if applicable).  
9. Double-check that all required documents are listed in the annexure and attached before submission.  
10. Retain a photocopy/scan of the fully filled form and all attachments for office records.  

> **Portal Note**: Applications are submitted physically or via email to the designated regional/zone office. There is no centralized online portal; applicants must contact the implementing agency directly for submission mode.
