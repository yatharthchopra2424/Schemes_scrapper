Based on our initial review of [Client Legal Name]'s business profile, we have identified Marketing Support & Services for Handicrafts — a subsidy scheme administered by the Office of the Development Commissioner (Handicrafts), Ministry of Textiles, Government of India — for which your business appears eligible.
