## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are tied to the actual reimbursement received by the client for eligible expenses (stall rent, travel, publicity) under the scheme, ensuring our compensation scales with the financial benefit delivered.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s dependency on event-specific schedules and fund availability, success fees reflect the uncertainty in approval timing and quantum of support, which varies by event nature, location, and participant category.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The retainer structure accommodates the diverse beneficiary base—from individual artisans to cooperatives—allowing micro and small entities to access support without prohibitive upfront costs.
4. **Transparency in Reimbursement**: Fees are calculated only on successfully claimed and reimbursed amounts, aligning with the scheme’s requirement that beneficiaries submit valid expenditure documents post-event for fund release.
