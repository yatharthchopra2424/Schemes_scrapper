## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because I specialise in helping handicraft artisans, self-help groups, NGOs, cooperatives, and private enterprises access government support for marketing their products. The Marketing Support & Services for Handicrafts scheme, run by the Office of the Development Commissioner (Handicrafts) under the Ministry of Textiles, provides financial assistance for participation in exhibitions, craft bazaars, and trade fairs—covering stall rent, travel, and publicity costs. I’d like to understand your current marketing activities and see if this scheme could help reduce your costs and expand your reach.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of handicraft products do you produce or promote, and how long have you been engaged in this activity?  
2. Are you registered or recognised as a handicraft producer/artisan with any government body or agency?  
3. Have you participated in any exhibitions, craft bazaars, or trade fairs (domestic or international) in the past 12–24 months?  
4. What are your main challenges in accessing markets or promoting your handicraft products?  
5. Do you currently incur expenses for stall rent, travel, or publicity when participating in such events?  
6. Are you part of a self-help group, NGO, cooperative, or private enterprise involved in handicraft production?  
7. Are you planning to participate in any upcoming exhibitions, fairs, or marketing events where you could benefit from financial support?  
8. What is your annual turnover or scale of operations in handicraft production and sales?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Engaged in the production of handicrafts  
- [ ] Individual artisan, self-help group, NGO, cooperative, or private enterprise  
- [ ] Possesses valid registration or recognition as a handicraft producer (where applicable)  
- [ ] Involved in or planning participation in approved exhibitions, craft bazaars, or trade fairs  
- [ ] Based in India (Pan-India scheme)  
- [ ] Seeking financial support for stall rent, travel, publicity, or marketing event organisation  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you’re actively involved in handicraft production and either participate in or plan to attend marketing events—exactly the kind of beneficiary the Marketing Support & Services for Handicrafts scheme is designed to help. This scheme offers subsidies for stall rent, travel, and publicity costs when you take part in approved exhibitions and fairs, which could directly reduce your out-of-pocket expenses and improve your market access.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**  
Handicraft producers often struggle with high costs associated with participating in exhibitions, craft bazaars, and trade fairs—such as stall rent, travel, and publicity—which limits their ability to access new markets, showcase products, and grow their sales, especially for small artisans and groups with limited budgets.

**Scheme intro:**  
The Marketing Support & Services for Handicrafts scheme is a subsidy programme administered by the Office of the Development Commissioner (Handicrafts), Ministry of Textiles, Government of India. It provides financial assistance for participation in domestic and international exhibitions, craft bazaars, and trade fairs, including reimbursement of stall rent, travel expenses, and publicity costs up to certain limits. It also supports organising craft bazaars, branding, product promotion, buyer-seller meets, and e-commerce initiatives. The exact amount of support varies based on the event, location, and participant category, but it is designed to significantly reduce marketing costs for eligible handicraft producers.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**  
- You are engaged in the production of handicrafts (artisan/SHG/NGO/cooperative/private enterprise)  
- You possess or can obtain valid registration or recognition as a handicraft producer  
- You participate in or plan to participate in approved marketing events (exhibitions, fairs, bazaars)  
- You incur eligible expenses such as stall rent, travel, or publicity for these events

We offer a free, no-obligation eligibility check to see if you qualify for support under this scheme. If you’re eligible, we’ll help you prepare and submit your application—handling the documentation, liaising with the implementing agency, and tracking approval—on a success-fee-only basis, so you only pay if we secure the subsidy for you.
