### Marketing Support & Services for Handicrafts
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Office of the Development Commissioner (Handicrafts), Ministry of Textiles, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** [https://handicrafts.nic.in](https://handicrafts.nic.in)
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Based on event-specific schedules; applications are typically invited well in advance of exhibitions, craft bazaars, and trade fairs.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Individual artisans, self-help groups, NGOs, cooperatives, and private enterprises engaged in the production of handicrafts
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Handicrafts
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Applicants must be involved in handicraft activities and possess valid registration or recognition as handicraft producers where applicable

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support in the form of subsidies for participation in exhibitions and fairs, including reimbursement of stall rent, travel expenses, and publicity costs up to certain limits; assistance for organizing craft bazaars and marketing events with funding shared between implementing agency and beneficiary; exact quantum varies based on event nature, location, and participant category
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement
- Duration of benefit: One-time per approved event

#### Documents Required
1. Application form
2. Proof of identity and address of the applicant
3. Registration certificate (if applicable) as handicraft producer/artisan
4. Details of the handicraft products to be showcased
5. Quotation or invoice for stall rent and other expenses
6. Bank account details for reimbursement
7. Any other document as specified by the implementing agency

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the relevant marketing event or exhibition under the scheme.
2. Contact the designated implementing agency or regional office for application details.
3. Submit the application form along with required documents.
4. Await scrutiny and approval from the sanctioning committee.
5. Upon approval, participate in the event and submit expenditure claims.
6. Receive reimbursement of eligible expenses as per scheme norms.
