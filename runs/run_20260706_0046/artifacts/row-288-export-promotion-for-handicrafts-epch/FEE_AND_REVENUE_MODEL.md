## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the tangible export promotion benefits delivered through EPCH membership (access to international trade fairs, marketing support, infrastructure facilities).
2. Success fees calibrated to the scheme’s low financial risk—membership fees are fixed and predictable, with no performance uncertainty in benefit delivery.
3. Tiered retainer structure reflects client scale: Starter/Growth tiers for handicraft exporters (MSME/artisans), Scale for established exporters seeking deeper market access.
4. Scheme-specific note: EPCH membership offers immediate, low-risk value (trade fair participation, marketing assistance) with processing time of <1 month for enrollment, enabling rapid revenue recognition and minimal lag in success fee realization.
