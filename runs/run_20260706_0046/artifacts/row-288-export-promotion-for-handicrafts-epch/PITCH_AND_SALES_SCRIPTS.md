# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping handicraft exporters access government-backed support to grow their international sales. The Export Promotion Council for Handicrafts (EPCH) supports artisans and MSMEs like yours by providing access to trade fairs, marketing infrastructure, and export readiness resources—all critical for scaling handicraft exports globally.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of handicraft products do you manufacture or export (e.g., woodware, textiles, metalware, agarbatti)?  
2. Are you currently exporting handicrafts, or are you planning to start exports?  
3. Do you have a valid Import Export Code (IEC) and GSTIN number?  
4. What is your business structure—proprietorship, partnership, private limited, or another type?  
5. How long have you been in the handicraft export business?  
6. Are you interested in participating in international trade fairs organized by EPCH (e.g., IHGF Delhi Fair, Maison&Objet Paris)?  
7. Do you need support with export documentation, market access, or compliance for handicrafts?  
8. Are you looking to renew your EPCH membership or apply for the first time?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Willing to venture into exports of handicrafts  
- [ ] Has or can obtain Import Export Code (IEC)  
- [ ] Has GSTIN number  
- [ ] Business type: individual, partnership firm, private/limited company, Export House, or manufacturer exporter  
- [ ] If partnership firm: can submit Partnership Deed  
- [ ] If private/limited company: can submit certificate of incorporation and resolution deed for signing authority  
- [ ] If Export House: can furnish Export House Certificate  
- [ ] If manufacturer exporter: can provide MSME UAM/Udyam and self-declaration on handicrafts product type  
- [ ] Name is not similar/duplicate to existing EPCH members (verifiable via EPCH member directory)  
- [ ] Prepared to pay entrance fee (Rs. 2500) + annual fee (Rs. 5000) + 18% GST (total Rs. 8850 in year of enrolment)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like EPCH membership could be a strong fit for your handicraft export goals. Let me share how this scheme helps exporters like you gain access to global markets, reduce marketing costs, and build credibility—all for an annual investment of just Rs. 5000 plus GST.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**  
Handicraft exporters struggle to reach international buyers due to limited marketing reach, lack of access to global trade fairs, and difficulty proving credibility overseas. Without institutional support, scaling exports remains costly and fragmented.

**Scheme intro:**  
The Export Promotion Council for Handicrafts (EPCH), established in 1986-87 under the Companies Act and sponsored by the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, is a self-sustaining apex body that promotes handicraft exports. Membership provides access to international trade fairs (e.g., IHGF Delhi Fair, Maison&Objet Paris), marketing infrastructure, seminars, and export readiness resources. The fee structure is: Entrance fee (Rs. 2500) + Annual Membership Fee (Rs. 5000) + 18% GST = Rs. 8850 in the year of enrolment. Renewal is Rs. 5000 + 18% GST = Rs. 5900 annually, due by 30th June each year.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**  
- You are willing to venture into or are already exporting handicrafts — a core eligibility criterion  
- You possess or can obtain a self-certified Import Export Code (IEC), which is mandatory  
- You have a GSTIN number, required for all applicants  
- Depending on your structure, you can submit the relevant documents (e.g., partnership deed, incorporation certificate, MSME declaration)
