### Export Promotion for Handicrafts (EPCH)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Export Promotion Council for Handicrafts (EPCH)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://epch.in/apply-new-membership
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Every year Membership Fee is due on 1st April and payment is to be made by 30th June.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Any person willing to venture into exports of handicrafts
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Handicrafts
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: A self certified copy of Import Export Code (IEC) is mandatory and needs to be enclosed with the application form. If the Company is a partnership firm it has to submit a Partnership Deed along with the Application Form. If the company is a private limited or a limited firm a photocopy of certificate of incorporation (Memorandum of Article of Association) and resolution deed regarding the signing authority has to be submitted. If an Export House applies for member ship it must furnish a copy of Export House Certificate. If a company is a manufacturer exporter must furnish MSME UAM or Udhyam & self declaration certifying what type of Handicrafts product manufacturing. A copy of GSTIN number is required.

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Entrance fee in the year of enrolment - Rs. 2500/-, Annual Membership Fee - Rs. 5000/-, Total (during the year of Enrollment) - Rs 7500/- + GST 18% (Rs. 8850/-). Membership renewal fee is Rs. 5900/- (Rs 5000/- + GST 18%).
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (payment of fees)
- Duration of benefit: Annual (renewable every year)

#### Documents Required
1. Self certified copy of Import Export Code (IEC)
2. Partnership Deed (if partnership firm)
3. Photocopy of certificate of incorporation (Memorandum of Article of Association) and resolution deed regarding signing authority (if private limited or limited firm)
4. Export House Certificate (if Export House applies for membership)
5. MSME UAM or Udhyam & self declaration certifying type of Handicrafts product manufacturing (if manufacturer exporter)
6. Copy of GSTIN number

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Apply for New Membership via EPCH website.
2. Pay entrance fee of Rs. 2500/- and annual membership fee of Rs. 5000/- (total Rs. 7500/- + 18% GST = Rs. 8850/-) during the year of enrollment.
3. Submit self certified copy of Import Export Code (IEC).
4. If partnership firm, submit Partnership Deed.
5. If private/limited company, submit photocopy of certificate of incorporation and resolution deed regarding signing authority.
6. If Export House, submit Export House Certificate.
7. If manufacturer exporter, furnish MSME UAM or Udhyam & self declaration certifying type of Handicrafts product manufacturing.
8. Submit copy of GSTIN number.
9. Avoid similar or duplicate names by checking EPCH website member directory.
10. Membership renewal fee of Rs. 5900/- (Rs 5000/- + GST 18%) is due every year by 30th June.
