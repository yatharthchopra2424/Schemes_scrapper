# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
Export Promotion for Handicrafts (EPCH) is a scheme administered by the Export Promotion Council for Handicrafts (EPCH), established under the Companies Act in 1986-87. It is a non-profit, self-sustaining apex body for handicrafts exporters, with the primary objective to promote, support, protect, maintain, and increase the export of handicrafts from India. The scheme operates pan-India and is funded entirely by membership fees, with no government grants. The application portal is https://epch.in/apply-new-membership.

### Objectives
- Promote, support, protect, maintain, and increase the export of handicrafts.
- Project India's image abroad as a reliable supplier of high-quality handicrafts goods and services.
- Ensure observance of international standards and specifications.
- Create necessary infrastructure, marketing, and information facilities for member exporters and importers.

| Name check | Must avoid similar or duplicate names by checking EPCH website member directory. | KEY FACTS, membership-registration page |

| Benefit Type | Details | Source |

| Membership fees (Year of Enrollment) | Entrance fee: Rs. 2500/-; Annual Membership Fee: Rs. 5000/-; Total: Rs. 7500/- + GST 18% (Rs. 8850/-) | KEY FACTS, membership-registration page, apply-new-membership page |
| Membership renewal fee (Annual) | Rs. 5900/- (Rs 5000/- + GST 18%) | KEY FACTS, membership-registration page |
| Fee due date | Every year Membership Fee is due on 1st April and payment is to be made by 30th June. | KEY FACTS, membership-registration page |
| Validity | Registration cum Membership Certificate issued is valid for 5 years subject to renewal of Membership by paying Annual fees every year. | KEY FACTS, membership-registration page |

> **Key Takeaway**: The scheme is self-financed through membership fees. The total cost for new members in the first year is Rs. 8850/- (inclusive of GST), and the annual renewal cost is Rs. 5900/-. Payment must be made by 30th June each year to maintain membership and RCMC validity.

### Application Process (Mermaid Flowchart)
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Start: Visit EPCH website] --> B[Apply for New Membership via EPCH website]
    B --> C[Pay entrance fee Rs. 2500/- and annual membership fee Rs. 5000/- (total Rs. 7500/- + 18% GST = Rs. 8850/-) during year of enrollment]
    C --> D[Submit self-certified copy of Import Export Code (IEC)]
    D --> E{Is applicant a partnership firm?}
    E -->|Yes| F[Submit Partnership Deed]
    E -->|No| G{Is applicant a private/limited company?}
    G -->|Yes| H[Submit photocopy of certificate of incorporation and resolution deed regarding signing authority]
    G -->|No| I{Is applicant an Export House?}
    I -->|Yes| J[Submit Export House Certificate]
    I -->|No| K{Is applicant a manufacturer exporter?}
    K -->|Yes| L[Furnish MSME UAM or Udhyam & self-declaration certifying type of Handicrafts product manufacturing]
    K -->|No| M[End of document-specific requirements]
    M --> N[Submit copy of GSTIN number]
    N --> O[Avoid similar or duplicate names by checking EPCH website member directory]
    O --> P[Receive Registration cum Membership Certificate (RCMC)]
    P --> Q[RCMC valid for 5 years subject to annual renewal]
    Q --> R[Pay annual renewal fee Rs. 5900/- by 30th June each year]
    R --> S[End: Maintain membership and export benefits]
```

**Application Portal**: https://epch.in/apply-new-membership

> **Warning**: Membership renewal fee of Rs. 5900/- (Rs 5000/- + GST 18%) is due every year by 30th June. Failure to pay results in loss of membership and RCMC validity.
