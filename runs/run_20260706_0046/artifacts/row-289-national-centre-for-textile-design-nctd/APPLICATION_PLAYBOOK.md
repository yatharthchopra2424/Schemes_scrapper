## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required. NCTD does not mandate a Detailed Project Report (DPR) for its design support, training, or market linkage services. Applicants submit a profile, product details, photographs, and a design brief instead.  
**Action**: Verify with NCTD nodal officer if a project report is needed for specific high-value or cluster-based programmes.  
**Source**: [NCTD Application Process](https://nctd.gov.in)

### Application Form Filling Protocol
There is no centralized online application form. The process is document-based and initiated via contact. Follow these rules:  

1. **Initiate contact first**: Email or call NCTD at info@nctd.gov.in or +91-11-2306 3281/2306 1752 to express interest in a specific programme before submitting documents.  
2. **Match documents to programme**: Tailor the profile, product details, and design brief to the specific NCTD programme (e.g., design development, training, trend forecasting) you are applying for.  
3. **Ensure clarity in design brief**: Clearly state objectives, target market, timeline, and expected deliverables in the design brief.  
4. **Use consistent applicant details**: Ensure name, address, and contact information match across all documents (PAN, Aadhaar, bank statement, profile).  
5. **Label photographs properly**: Tag each photo with craft type, material, and product name (e.g., “Banarasi Saree – Zari Work – Silk”).  
6. **Highlight traditional or innovative aspects**: Emphasize uniqueness of craft, heritage techniques, or innovation goals in the profile and product details.  
7. **Confirm financial linkage intent**: If seeking grant support via allied schemes (NHDP, HSS, CDP), explicitly state this in the cover note and ensure bank details are provided.  
8. **Keep documents updated**: Use latest bank statements (within 3 months) and recent product photographs (within 6 months).

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain formal approval letter or email from NCTD confirming enrolment in programme or acceptance of design request  
- [ ] Verify sanctioned services: type of support (design, training, market linkage), duration, deliverables, and timelines  
- [ ] If financial linkage is involved (via NHDP, HSS, CDP), obtain sanction letter from the respective scheme and confirm fund disbursement schedule  
- [ ] Schedule participation in training, design workshops, or prototype development sessions as per NCTD intimation  
- [ ] Maintain record of all services availed, including attendance certificates, design prototypes, and market linkage event participation  
- [ ] Prepare utilization certificate (if funds are received via allied schemes) detailing expenditure against sanctioned amount  
- [ ] Track invoice for success fee as per consultancy agreement with client  
- [ ] Collect feedback from client on design outcomes, skill upgradation, and market linkage benefits  
- [ ] Document case study or testimonial for future use (with client consent)  

> **Note**: NCTD does not directly disburse financial grants; any financial support is facilitated through allied Ministry of Textiles schemes. Verify linkage and compliance requirements with the relevant scheme (e.g., NHDP, HSS, CDP) if funds are involved.  
> **Source**: [NCTD Key Caveats](https://nctd.gov.in)
