## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the tangible design and technical support value delivered by NCTD, which enhances product competitiveness and market access rather than direct cash grants.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the non-financial nature of NCTD’s support (design services, training, market linkage), where outcomes depend on client implementation capacity and market response, introducing moderate execution risk.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainer structures ensure accessibility across beneficiary tiers — from individual artisans to large exporters — while success fees scale with the complexity and potential impact of design interventions.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Given that NCTD does not directly disburse financial grants but facilitates design and technical support (with financial linkage possible via allied schemes like NHDP, HSS, or CDP), success is measured by service delivery and client uptake rather than fund disbursement. Processing is rolling and dependent on expert availability and programme schedules, introducing variable timelines that support a retainer-led model to cover advisory effort irrespective of outcome timing.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Retainer for advisory services related to National Centre for Textile Design (NCTD) – including programme selection, documentation support, application submission, and liaison with Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms for design development, training, or market linkage services under NCTD.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for successful enrolment or service delivery under the National Centre for Textile Design (NCTD), implemented by the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms, Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, following client approval and commencement of design support, training, or prototype development services.
