## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping textile and handicraft businesses access government design and market linkage support. The National Centre for Textile Design (NCTD) offers free design development, trend forecasting, and prototype services specifically for artisans, weavers, and textile units looking to innovate and expand their market reach. I’d like to understand your current design and product development needs to see if NCTD’s programmes could support your goals.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of textile or handicraft products do you currently produce, and how long have you been in operation?
- Are you an individual artisan, part of a self-help group, cooperative, NGO, or a registered textile/handicraft unit?
- Do you currently face challenges in updating designs, accessing trend forecasts, or developing new product prototypes?
- Have you previously accessed any design or training support from government schemes or institutions?
- Are you looking to participate in design exhibitions, buyer-seller meets, or expand into export markets?
- What specific design software or traditional techniques would you or your team like to be trained in?
- Do you have a clear design brief or product development goal in mind for the next 6–12 months?
- Are you based in India and able to engage with NCTD’s programmes, which are available pan-India on a rolling basis?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is an individual designer, weaver, artisan, self-help group, cooperative, NGO, exporter, or textile/handicraft unit
- [ ] Seeking design support, training, trend forecasting, prototype development, or market linkage services
- [ ] Based in India (pan-India eligibility)
- [ ] Has a definable craft, product line, or design requirement
- [ ] Able to provide basic documentation: profile, product details, photographs, identity/address proof
- [ ] Not excluded by NCTD’s priority for clusters, cooperatives, or export-oriented units (though individuals may still apply)
- [ ] Understands that NCTD provides design and technical support; direct financial grants are not provided but may be linked to other Ministry schemes

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like NCTD’s design development and market linkage services could directly support your goal of innovating your product range and accessing new buyers. Let me explain how the National Centre for Textile Design works and Textile Design (NCTD) provides free design and technical support — including trend forecasting, prototype development, and exhibition participation — to help textile businesses like yours grow without upfront costs.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many textile and handicraft businesses struggle to keep designs fresh, access professional design expertise, or connect with buyers and exporters. Without design innovation and market linkage, artisans risk stagnation, low value realisation, and missed export opportunities — especially when competing in fast-changing domestic and international markets.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Centre for Textile Design (NCTD), implemented by the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms under the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, provides free design and technical support services. While NCTD does not directly disburse financial grants, it facilitates access to design development, trend forecasting, prototype creation, training in design software and traditional techniques, product development, market linkage assistance, and participation in design exhibitions and buyer-seller meets — all aimed at strengthening the competitiveness of handloom and handicraft sectors.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in textile or handicraft production as an individual artisan, weaver, self-help group, cooperative, NGO, or registered unit — all eligible beneficiary types
- You seek design support, training, or market linkage services, which align with NCTD’s core offerings
- You are based in India, making you eligible for pan-India access to NCTD programmes
- You have a clear product or design need, enabling submission of a specific design brief — a key requirement for design service requests
