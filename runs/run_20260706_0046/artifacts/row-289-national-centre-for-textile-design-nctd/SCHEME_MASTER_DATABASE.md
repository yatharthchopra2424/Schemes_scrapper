### National Centre for Textile Design (NCTD)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms, Ministry of Textiles, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nctd.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Individual designers, weavers, artisans, self-help groups, cooperatives, NGOs, exporters, textile/handicraft units
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Textile and handicrafts sector
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Eligibility varies by programme; priority may be given to clusters, cooperatives, and export-oriented units

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Access to professional design services, trend forecasting, prototype development, training in design software and traditional techniques, product development support, market linkage assistance, participation in design exhibitions and buyer-seller meets, documentation of traditional designs
- Disbursement mode: Service-based support (non-financial); financial assistance, if any, routed through allied Ministry of Textiles schemes
- Duration of benefit: Varies by programme (training, design development, etc.)

#### Documents Required
1. Profile of the applicant (individual/unit/organization)
2. Details of existing products or craft practiced
3. Photographs of samples or work
4. Identity and address proof
5. Bank details (if applicable for any financial linkage)
6. Registration certificate (for NGOs, cooperatives, companies)
7. Specific design brief or requirement (for design service requests)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the NCTD website at https://nctd.gov.in
2. Explore available programmes such as design development, training, or trend services
3. Contact NCTD via email or phone to express interest
4. Submit required documents based on the specific programme
5. Await evaluation and approval from NCTD officials
6. Upon acceptance, avail the selected service or enrol in the training programme.
