## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **National Centre for Textile Design (NCTD)** is a pan-India initiative implemented by the **Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms, Ministry of Textiles, Government of India**. It functions as a design and technical support hub for the textile and handicrafts sector, focusing on innovation, skill development, and market linkage rather than direct financial grants. Applications are accepted on a **rolling basis throughout the year**, subject to programme availability.

### Objectives
- To provide design development and forecasting services to the textile and handicrafts sector  
- To upgrade skills of designers, weavers, and artisans through training programmes  
- To promote traditional Indian textiles and contemporary design innovation  
- To strengthen market linkages for handloom and handicraft products  
- To conduct research and documentation on textile heritage and emerging trends  
- To support export promotion through design intervention and product development  

### Eligibility Matrix
Eligibility varies by specific programme, but the following entities are generally eligible to apply for design support, training, or market linkage services:

| **Beneficiary Category**       | **Eligibility Details**                                                                 |
|-------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Individual Designers          | Open to practicing designers seeking design development or skill upgradation support     |
| Weavers                       | Handloom weavers seeking design inputs for product development                         |
| Artisans                      | Handicraft artisans requiring design intervention for traditional or contemporary products |
| Self-Help Groups (SHGs)       | Registered SHGs engaged in textile or handicraft production                            |
| Cooperatives                  | Handloom/handicraft cooperatives seeking collective design and market support          |
| NGOs                          | Non-governmental organizations working in textile/handicraft sector with valid registration |
| Exporters                     | Export-oriented textile/handicraft units seeking design support for international markets |
| Textile Industries            | Manufacturing units in textiles seeking design forecasting and prototype development   |
| Handloom Clusters             | Geographic clusters of handloom producers seeking collective design services           |
| Handicraft Units              | Small-scale handicraft manufacturing units requiring design and technical assistance   |

> **Note**: Priority may be given to clusters, cooperatives, and export-oriented units based on programme objectives and resource availability.

### Benefits & Financial Support
NCTD does not directly provide grants or subsidies. Instead, it offers **in-kind design and technical support services**, which may be linked to financial assistance through allied Ministry of Textiles schemes.

| **Benefit Category**               | **Specific Services Provided**                                                                 |
|------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Design Services                    | Professional design development, trend forecasting, prototype development                      |
| Training & Skill Upgradation       | Training in design software (e.g., CAD), traditional techniques, and contemporary design methods |
| Product Development Support        | End-to-end support from concept to prototype for new product lines                             |
| Market Linkage Assistance          | Facilitation of buyer-seller meets, participation in design exhibitions, and market access     |
| Documentation & Research           | Documentation of traditional designs and research on textile heritage and emerging trends      |
| Financial Linkage (Indirect)       | Referral to Ministry of Textiles schemes such as NHDP (National Handloom Development Programme), HSS (Handloom Sector Scheme), or CDP (Cluster Development Programme) for potential grants/subsidies |

> **Financial Caveat**: Any financial support is **not directly disbursed by NCTD** but may be facilitated through linkage with other Ministry of Textiles schemes. The specific quantum or mechanism is not detailed in the evidence and depends on the allied scheme under which linkage is sought.

### Key Caveats
- Services are subject to availability of experts and programme schedules  
- Financial assistance, if any, is routed through allied Ministry of Textiles schemes and **not directly disbursed by NCTD**  
- Design intellectual property rights may be governed by specific agreements between NCTD and the beneficiary  
- Priority may be given to clusters, cooperatives, and export-oriented units  
- Applications are accepted on a **rolling basis**, but enrolment depends on programme availability and capacity
