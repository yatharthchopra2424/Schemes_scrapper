# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] **Registration Type**: Entity must be a registered legal entity (e.g., proprietorship, partnership, company, FPO, SHG, cooperative) as per the scheme guidelines for the specific component (e.g., for R&D: Universities, IITs, Government Institutions, CSIR-recognized R&D units; for infrastructure: entrepreneurs, MSMEs, FPOs, SHGs, producer cooperatives).
- [ ] **Turnover Threshold**: No universal turnover threshold specified; eligibility varies by component scheme (refer to individual scheme guidelines for MSME or other thresholds).
- [ ] **Employee Count**: No universal employee count specified; varies by component (e.g., MSME definitions apply for relevant sub-schemes).
- [ ] **Sector**: Must be engaged in food processing sector activities (e.g., setting up infrastructure, R&D in processed food, agro-processing, cold chain, food testing labs, etc.).
- [ ] **Age of Business**: No universal age requirement; varies by component (e.g., new or existing units eligible depending on sub-scheme).
- [ ] **Land Requirement**: Land must be owned or on registered leasehold with a minimum duration of 15 years (applicable to infrastructure components like Mega Food Parks, Cold Chain, etc.).
- [ ] **Other Conditions**: 
  - For R&D in Processed Food Sector: Applicant must be a University, IIT, Central/State Government Institution, Government-funded Organization, R&D laboratory, or CSIR-recognized R&D unit in private sector.
  - Surety Bond (Annexure-IX) required on non-judicial stamp paper of not less than Rs. 100/- for private PIAs (not required for Central/State Government Departments).
  - Must check for active Expressions of Interest (EoI) or notifications on the ministry website as deadlines are time-bound and component-specific.

**Sources**:  
- Eligibility criteria from KEY FACTS: Eligibility varies by component scheme. For R&D: Universities, IITs, Government Institutions, CSIR-recognized R&D units. For infrastructure: entrepreneurs, MSMEs, FPOs, SHGs, producer cooperatives.  
- Land requirement: "Land must be owned or on registered leasehold with duration not less than 15 years."  
- Surety Bond: "Surety Bond is required for private PIAs but not for Central/State Government Departments."  
- Application Process: "Check for active Expressions of Interest (EoI) or notifications inviting proposals."  
- Portal: https://mofpi.gov.in/en/Schemes/about-pmksy-scheme

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Partnership Deed / Proprietorship Registration
- PAN Card of the entity
- GST Registration Certificate
- ITR (Income Tax Return) for last 2–3 years
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)
- Cancelled Cheque / Bank Account Details
- Aadhaar and PAN of authorized signatory

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Project Report / Detailed Project Report (DPR)
- Eligibility documents (e.g., Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, GST registration)
- Bank account details
- Land documents (ownership/registered leasehold with minimum 15 years duration)
- Surety Bond (Annexure-IX) on non-judicial stamp paper of not less than Rs. 100/-
- Building plan/Blue Print/Lay-out
- Technical Civil Work and Furniture and Fixture details
- Details of approved equipment procured and installed
- Bank statements showing payment to suppliers
- Utilization certificate of earlier grants (if any)
- Self-attested English/Hindi version of land document
- Geo-tagged photographs of the project
- Term loan sanction letter from bank
- Confirmation of term loan disbursement from bank
- Self-declaration of project completion and commercialization

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document unless otherwise specified)
- File names must match the document description (e.g., "Land_Ownership_Deed.pdf")
- Certificates must be valid and not expired (e.g., GST, Udyam)
- Land documents must be self-attested in English or Hindi
- Photographs must be geo-tagged and clearly show project site/progress
- Surety Bond must be notarized and on stamp paper of Rs. 100 or higher
- All declarations must be signed and dated by authorized signatory

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- **Required**: Yes, a Project Report or Detailed Project Report (DPR) is mandatory for all components under SAMPADA.  
- **Standard Sections** (based on evidence from DPRs):  
  1. Project At a Glance  
  2. Introduction  
  3. The Proposal  
  4. Business Model  
  5. Project Details (Assumptions, Implementation Schedule, Statutory Approvals)  
  6. Manufacturing Process  
  7. Proposed Floor Plan  
  8. Process Flow Diagram  
  9. Financial Statements (Project Cost, Means of Finance, Working Capital, Business Projections, Cash Flow)  
  10. Feasibility Assessment (Technical, Socio-Economic, Market, Financial)  
  11. SWOT Analysis  
  12. Risk Assessment & Management  
  13. Monitoring & Control  
  14. Conclusion  
  15. Annexure  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the correct component portal (e.g., PMKSY, Mega Food Park, Cold Chain) via https://mofpi.gov.in/en/Schemes/about-pmksy-scheme based on your area of interest.  
2. Download the latest application form and guidelines from the 'Download Guidelines' or 'Download Circulars' section.  
3. Fill all fields accurately; do not leave any mandatory section blank.  
4. Ensure consistency between data in the application form and attached documents (e.g., project cost, capacity).  
5. Upload documents in the specified format (PDF) and size limits as per portal instructions.  
6. Self-attest all documents where required (e.g., land documents in English/Hindi).  
7. Confirm eligibility declarations (e.g., land lease duration, surety bond for private PIAs).  
8. Save a draft before final submission and verify all attachments.  

**Sources**:  
- Application Process: "Download guidelines and application forms from the 'Download Guidelines' or 'Download Circulars' section. Prepare the proposal as per the scheme guidelines..."  
- Portal structure observed across crawled pages (e.g., download sections for each scheme)

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application online via the designated component portal or via email to the concerned division (as specified in the EoI notification).  
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation page showing timestamp and application/reference number.  
- Store the application reference number securely in the case file.  
- Set calendar reminders for follow-up at 15, 30, and 45 days post-submission.  
- Monitor the portal for updates or requests for additional information.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from MoFPI or designated agency  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount matches the approved project cost and component-specific subsidy pattern  
- [ ] Fulfill first instalment conditions: Submit acceptance letter and compliance documents to receive 50% grant via PFMS (EAT module)  
- [ ] Utilize funds strictly as per approved project plan and component guidelines  
- [ ] Maintain records of all expenditures, invoices, and bank statements  
- [ ] Submit Utilization Certificate and proof of expenditure to claim second instalment (remaining 50%)  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with terms in approval letter (e.g., land use, surety bond validity)  
- [ ] Track project milestones for timely completion  
- [ ] Prepare for post-approval monitoring or audit by MoFPI/PMA  
- [ ] Invoice success fee as per consultancy agreement  

**Sources**:  
- Financial Support: "Financial assistance is provided as grants-in-aid... The pattern of release of funds involves two instalments: 50% upon submission of acceptance letter and compliance documents, and the remaining 50% upon submission of utilization certificate and proof of expenditure. Funds are released through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS) via the EAT module."  
- Benefits: "Access to subsidized credit through special funds; and facilitation of project approval via Program Management Agencies (PMAs) and Project Approval Committees."  
- Key Caveats: "Benefits are subject to compliance with terms in the approval letter and submission of utilization certificates."
