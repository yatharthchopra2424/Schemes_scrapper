## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the scale of infrastructure grants under SAMPADA (PMKSY), which range from lakhs to crores.
2. Tiered success fees reflect decreasing marginal effort as project size increases, with larger projects benefiting from economies of scale in consultancy delivery.
3. Retainer fees cover initial proposal preparation, documentation, and liaison with Program Management Agencies (PMAs), which are mandatory under the scheme.
4. Scheme-specific note: The SAMPADA scheme involves a two-installment fund release (50% on acceptance, 50% on utilization certificate), creating moderate payment risk. However, the high fund size (₹10,600 Cr) and strong implementation track record via PFMS reduce overall risk, supporting predictable revenue recognition.
