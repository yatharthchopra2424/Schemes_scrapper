# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping food processing entrepreneurs, MSMEs, agri-startups, and farmers access government grants for infrastructure and capacity building. The SAMPADA Scheme (Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana) by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries offers grant support for setting up food processing units, cold chains, agro-processing clusters, and more—directly aligned with your goal of scaling operations in the food processing sector. I’d like to understand your current setup to see if this scheme can support your growth.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of food processing activity are you currently engaged in or planning to start (e.g., bakery, dairy, fruits & vegetables, spices, meat processing)?
2. When was your business incorporated or registered? (Important for MSME eligibility under various SAMPADA sub-schemes)
3. Do you have ownership of land or a registered leasehold agreement with a minimum duration of 15 years? (Critical for infrastructure-linked grants)
4. Are you seeking support for setting up a new unit, expanding an existing one, or establishing cold chain/storage facilities?
5. What is your projected annual turnover or investment capacity for the food processing project?
6. Are you part of an FPO, SHG, producer cooperative, or working with farmers in the supply chain? (Priority beneficiary groups under SAMPADA)
7. Do you have access to term loan sanction from a bank? (Required for grant disbursement in two instalments)
8. Are you located in the North-East Region (NER) or belong to SC/ST category? (Special provisions exist under certain EOIs)

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business operates in food processing sector (entrepreneur, MSME, agri-startup, farmer, FPO, SHG, cooperative)
- [ ] Land owned or on registered leasehold ≥15 years (for infrastructure projects)
- [ ] Has or can secure term loan from bank (for matched funding)
- [ ] Project involves setting up/expanding food processing unit, cold chain, agro-processing cluster, or similar infrastructure
- [ ] Not applying for discontinued sub-schemes (e.g., Mega Food Park, HR Institution-Promotional Activities—only committed liability considered)
- [ ] Located in India (Pan-India scheme)
- [ ] Capable of submitting: project report, eligibility documents, bank details, land documents, surety bond (if private), building plans, equipment details, utilization certificate (if prior grant received), geo-tagged photos, term loan sanction/disbursement proof

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your [mention specific activity, e.g., bakery unit] with [land ownership/lease status] and plans for [expansion/new setup] aligns well with the SAMPADA Scheme’s support for food processing infrastructure. The scheme offers grants-in-aid—typically up to 35% of project cost, capped at ₹10 lakhs for units like yours—released in two instalments upon milestones. Would you like me to walk you through how it works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Food processing entrepreneurs often struggle with high upfront costs for machinery, civil works, and working capital, limiting their ability to scale, adopt technology, or meet quality standards. Access to collateral-free funding is difficult, especially for MSMEs and first-time entrepreneurs in the sector.

**Scheme intro:**
The **SAMPADA Scheme (Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana)**, administered by the **Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI)**, is a **grant-based** initiative providing **financial assistance as grants-in-aid** for infrastructure projects in the food processing sector. Support includes setting up food processing units, cold chains, agro-processing clusters, food testing laboratories, and backward/forward linkages. The grant is released in **two instalments**: 50% upon submission of acceptance letter and compliance documents, and the remaining 50% upon utilization certificate and proof of expenditure, via PFMS through the EAT module.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in [specific activity, e.g., milk bread/cake/cookies production], which falls under eligible food processing activities.
- You have [land owned/registered leasehold ≥15 years] or are planning to acquire it—a key requirement for infrastructure grants.
- You can secure a term loan from a bank, enabling matched funding under the scheme’s co-financing model.
- [If applicable] You belong to SC/ST category or are located in the NER, which may qualify you for special consideration in active EOIs.
