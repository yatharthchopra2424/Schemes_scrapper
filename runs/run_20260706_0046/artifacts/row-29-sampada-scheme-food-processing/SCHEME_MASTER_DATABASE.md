### SAMPADA Scheme (Food Processing)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://mofpi.gov.in/en/Schemes/about-pmksy-scheme
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (subject to component-specific EoI notifications)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Entrepreneurs, MSMEs, FPOs, SHGs, producer cooperatives, entities involved in food processing, Universities, IITs, Central/State Government Institutions, Government funded Organizations, R&D laboratories, CSIR recognized R&D units in private sector
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Food processing sector (including agro-processing, cold chain, food testing laboratories, backward and forward linkages, skill development, R&D, promotional activities)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Eligibility varies by component scheme; land must be owned or on registered leasehold with duration not less than 15 years; Surety Bond required for private PIAs but not for Central/State Government Departments

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance as grants-in-aid; leverages investment of Rs. 11,095.93 crore with total government allocation of Rs. 10,600 crore (initial Rs. 6000 crore + extension Rs. 4600 crore)
- Disbursement mode: Two instalments: 50% upon submission of acceptance letter and compliance documents, remaining 50% upon submission of utilization certificate and proof of expenditure; released through PFMS via EAT module
- Duration of benefit: One-time grant per approved project

#### Documents Required
1. Project report
2. Eligibility documents (e.g., Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, GST registration)
3. Bank account details
4. Land documents (ownership/registered leasehold with minimum 15 years duration)
5. Surety Bond (Annexure-IX) on non-judicial stamp paper of not less than Rs. 100/-
6. Building plan/Blue Print/Lay-out
7. Technical Civil Work and Furniture and Fixture details
8. Details of approved equipment procured and installed
9. Bank statements showing payment to suppliers
10. Utilization certificate of earlier grants (if any)
11. Self-attested English/Hindi version of land document
12. Geo-tagged photographs of the project
13. Term loan sanction letter from bank
14. Confirmation of term loan disbursement from bank
15. Self-declaration of project completion and commercialization

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the respective scheme portal (e.g., PMKSY, Mega Food Park, Cold Chain, etc.) based on the component of interest.
2. Check for active Expressions of Interest (EoI) or notifications inviting proposals.
3. Download guidelines and application forms from the 'Download Guidelines' or 'Download Circulars' section.
4. Prepare the proposal as per the scheme guidelines, including project report, eligibility documents, and financial details.
5. Submit the application online through the designated portal or via email to the concerned division.
6. Await scrutiny by the Technical Committee and Project Approval Committee.
7. Upon approval, receive approval letter and proceed with fund release in instalments as per the Pattern of Release of Funds.
