# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **SAMPADA Scheme (Food Processing)**, officially known as **Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana (PMKSY)**, is a central sector scheme implemented by the **Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI)**. It is a **grant-based** initiative with **Pan-India** geographic scope, designed to create modern infrastructure for efficient supply chain management from farm gate to retail outlet. The scheme aims to boost the food processing sector, increase farmers' income, reduce agricultural wastage, enhance processing levels, and promote exports of processed foods.

### Objectives
- Creation of modern infrastructure with efficient supply chain management from farm gate to retail outlet  
- Provide a big boost to the growth of food processing sector in the country  
- Help in providing better returns to farmers  
- Double farmers' income  
- Create huge employment opportunities, especially in rural areas  
- Reduce wastage of agricultural produce  
- Increase the processing level of agricultural produce  
- Enhance the export of processed foods  

### Financial Support & Fund Size
- **Total Government Allocation**: ₹10,600 crore (initial ₹6,000 crore + extension ₹4,600 crore)  
- **Leveraged Investment**: ₹11,095.93 crore  
- **Fund Size**: ₹10,600 crore  
- **Financial Assistance Type**: Grants-in-aid for infrastructure projects  
- **Pattern of Release of Funds**: Two instalments:  
  - 50% upon submission of acceptance letter and compliance documents  
  - Remaining 50% upon submission of utilization certificate and proof of expenditure  
- **Fund Disbursement Mechanism**: Through Public Financial Management System (PFMS) via the EAT module  

### Benefits
Financial assistance is provided as grants-in-aid for infrastructure projects in the food processing sector under various component schemes. Benefits include:
- Support for setting up infrastructure projects (Mega Food Parks, Cold Chain, Agro-Processing Clusters, etc.)  
- Technical and financial support for agro-processing clusters, cold chain, food testing laboratories, backward and forward linkages, and food processing unit expansion  
- Support for skill development, research and development, promotional activities, and operational guidelines  
- Access to subsidized credit through special funds  
- Facilitation of project approval via Program Management Agencies (PMAs) and Project Approval Committees  

### Eligibility Criteria
Eligibility varies by component scheme. Key details:

| Component Scheme | Eligible Entities |
|------------------|-------------------|
| **Research & Development in Processed Food Sector** | All Universities, IITs, Central/State Government Institutions, Government funded Organizations, R&D laboratories, and CSIR recognized R&D units in private sector |
| **Mega Food Parks, Cold Chain, Agro-Processing Clusters, etc.** | Entrepreneurs, MSMEs, FPOs, SHGs, producer cooperatives, and entities involved in food processing |
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Entrepreneurs, MSMEs, agri-startups, farmers, food processing units, SC/ST Category, NER Region |

> **Note**: Specific eligibility criteria are detailed in individual scheme guidelines. Applicants must refer to the respective component scheme portal for exact requirements.

### Required Documents
Applicants must submit the following documents:
1. Project report  
2. Eligibility documents (Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, GST registration)  
3. Bank account details  
4. Land documents (ownership/registered leasehold with minimum 15 years duration)  
5. Surety Bond (Annexure-IX) on non-judicial stamp paper of not less than ₹100/-  
6. Building plan/Blue Print/Lay-out  
7. Technical Civil Work and Furniture and Fixture details  
8. Details of approved equipment procured and installed  
9. Bank statements showing payment to suppliers  
10. Utilization certificate of earlier grants (if any)  
11. Self-attested English/Hindi version of land document  
12. Geo-tagged photographs of the project  
13. Term loan sanction letter from bank  
14. Confirmation of term loan disbursement from bank  
15. Self-declaration of project completion and commercialization  

### Key Caveats
- Certain sub-schemes (Mega Food Park, Creation of Backward & Forward Linkages, Human Resource & Institution - Promotional Activities, Skill Development & HACCP) have been discontinued in the 15th Finance Commission cycle with provision of committed liability only  
- Land must be owned or on registered leasehold with duration not less than 15 years  
- Surety Bond is required for private PIAs but not for Central/State Government Departments  
- Benefits are subject to compliance with terms in the approval letter and submission of utilization certificates  
- Funds are released in two instalments linked to specific milestones and documentation  

### Application Process
1. Visit the respective scheme portal (e.g., PMKSY, Mega Food Park, Cold Chain, etc.) based on the component of interest  
2. Check for active Expressions of Interest (EoI) or notifications inviting proposals  
3. Download guidelines and application forms from the 'Download Guidelines' or 'Download Circulars' section  
4. Prepare the proposal as per the scheme guidelines, including project report, eligibility documents, and financial details  
5. Submit the application online through the designated portal or via email to the concerned division  
6. Await scrutiny by the Technical Committee and Project Approval Committee  
7. Upon approval, receive approval letter and proceed with fund release in instalments as per the Pattern of Release of Funds  

### Application Portal
**Official Portal**: https://mofpi.gov.in/en/Schemes/about-pmksy-scheme  
**Note**: The direct SAMPADA page (https://mofpi.gov.in/sampada) returns a 404 error; users must navigate via the PMKSY portal.

### Status & Deadlines
- **Status**: Varies by component scheme and EoI notifications  
- **Deadlines**: Time-bound with extensions (e.g., EOI dated 04.10.2023 extended upto 15.12.2023 for several schemes)  
- **No universal rolling basis**: Applicants must check current EoIs on the ministry website  
- **Last Updated**: 2025
