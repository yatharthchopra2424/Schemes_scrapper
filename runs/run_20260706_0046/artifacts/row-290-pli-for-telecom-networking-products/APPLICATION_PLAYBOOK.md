# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Company is engaged in manufacturing of telecom and networking products in India  
- [ ] Company meets minimum investment thresholds (as per scheme guidelines)  
- [ ] Company achieves incremental sales of covered products over a base year  
- [ ] Company complies with Make in India guidelines  
- [ ] Company meets domestic value addition requirements  
- [ ] Company is registered as MSME, startup, or large enterprise  
- [ ] Company has valid GST registration and PAN  
- [ ] Company has Udyam registration (if applicable)  
- [ ] Company has BIS certification for products (if applicable)  
- [ ] Company has submitted undertaking on compliance with Make in India guidelines  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation  
- PAN Card  
- GST Registration Certificate  
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- Income Tax Returns (last 3 years)  
- Audited Financial Statements  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Details of manufacturing facility  
- Investment plan and capital expenditure details  
- Product specifications and BIS certification (if applicable)  
- Undertaking on compliance with Make in India guidelines  
- Base year sales and projected incremental sales details  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and in PDF format  
- File size must not exceed 5 MB per document  
- Document names must match the checklist (e.g., "Certificate_of_Incorporation.pdf")  
- All certificates must be valid and not expired  
- English language required; Hindi documents must be accompanied by certified translation  
- Undertakings and declarations must be signed and stamped

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not explicitly mentioned in evidence. **Verify with nodal officer** whether a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required for this scheme.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Register on the designated PLI portal: https://dot.gov.in/pli-telecom  
2. Fill in applicant details accurately as per Certificate of Incorporation and PAN  
3. Upload all documents in the specified format and size limits  
4. Provide truthful information on manufacturing capacity and investment plan  
5. Declare compliance with Make in India guidelines and domestic value addition  
6. Specify base year sales and projected incremental sales with supporting evidence  
7. Ensure undertaking is signed by authorized signatory  
8. Double-check all entries before final submission  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All application form fields are complete  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached  
- [ ] Declarations and undertakings are signed and dated  
- [ ] File sizes and naming conventions are compliant  
- [ ] Portal registration confirmation is saved  
- [ ] Base year and incremental sales data are consistent with financials  

---

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation page showing the application reference number  
- Store the reference number securely in the case file  
- Set calendar reminders for follow-up at 15, 30, and 45 days post-submission  
- Monitor the portal for any communication or requests for additional information

### Escalation Path
- Level 1: Portal helpdesk – Available via the PLI portal at https://dot.gov.in/pli-telecom  
- Level 2: Nodal officer – ceo-dibd@digitalindia.gov.in  
- Level 3: RTI filing if no response within statutory SLA (typically 30 days for initial acknowledgment, 60 days for evaluation)  

---

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and save the approval letter from the empowered committee  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned incentive amount and duration as per approval  
- [ ] Sign the agreement with the Department of Telecommunications upon approval  
- [ ] Commence production as per the investment plan  
- [ ] Submit periodic reports (quarterly/annually) as required by the scheme  
- [ ] Claim incentives based on verified incremental sales post-production  
- [ ] Maintain records for audit and verification purposes  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with domestic value addition and Make in India guidelines to avoid clawback  
- [ ] Track utilization of funds for technology upgradation and export promotion  
- [ ] Prepare for annual renewal and performance review as per scheme guidelines
