## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer fees cover initial eligibility assessment, documentation preparation, and application submission.
2. Success fees are contingent on verified incremental sales claims and disbursement of incentives.
3. Tiered pricing aligns with client scale and scheme benefit range (₹10L–₹10Cr+).
4. Scheme-specific note: The PLI for Telecom & Networking Products has a medium processing time risk due to annual application windows and verification cycles; success is evidenced by approved claims and disbursements, supporting a moderate risk profile for consulting engagements.
