# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping telecom and networking product manufacturers in India access government incentives that directly boost their bottom line. The PLI for Telecom & Networking Products scheme is designed specifically for companies like yours that are manufacturing in India and looking to scale through incremental sales incentives. I’d like to understand your current operations to see if this is a fit.

### Discovery Questions
1. What year was your company incorporated, and where is your manufacturing facility located in India?
2. Are you currently manufacturing telecom or networking products (e.g., routers, switches, optical equipment, antennas) in India?
3. What was your base year sales (FY 2023-24 or earlier) of eligible telecom and networking products manufactured in India?
4. Do you have a clear investment plan or capital expenditure details for expanding or upgrading your telecom manufacturing capacity?
5. Are you GST registered and do you have a valid PAN for your business?
6. Have you undertaken any compliance with Make in India guidelines or obtained BIS certification for your products?
7. Are you projecting incremental sales over your base year from new or expanded production?
8. Are you an MSME, startup, or large enterprise engaged in telecom manufacturing?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Engaged in manufacturing of telecom and networking products in India  
- [ ] Meets minimum investment thresholds (as per scheme guidelines)  
- [ ] Capable of achieving incremental sales of covered products over a base year  
- [ ] Complies with Make in India guidelines  
- [ ] Meets domestic value addition requirements  
- [ ] Has Certificate of Incorporation, GST registration, PAN  
- [ ] Has details of manufacturing facility and investment plan  
- [ ] Can provide product specifications and BIS certification (if applicable)  
- [ ] Can submit base year sales and projected incremental sales details  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like you meet the core eligibility for the **PLI for Telecom & Networking Products** scheme — particularly your manufacturing focus, incremental sales potential, and compliance readiness. This scheme offers financial incentives as a percentage of your incremental sales, which could significantly improve your project economics. Let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Telecom manufacturers in India face high capital costs, intense global competition, and pressure to scale domestic production while meeting Make in India and value addition norms. Many struggle to offset these costs without clear, performance-linked government support that scales with growth.

**Scheme intro:**
The **Production Linked Incentive (PLI) for Telecom & Networking Products**, administered by the Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications, provides financial incentives as a percentage of incremental sales of eligible telecom and networking products manufactured in India. Incentives are disbursed annually based on verified claims, with no upper cap — the more you sell, the more you earn. Access the portal: https://dot.gov.in/pli-telecom
