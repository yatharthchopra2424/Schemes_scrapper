### PLI for Telecom & Networking Products
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dot.gov.in/pli-telecom
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Application window typically opens annually with specific deadlines notified via official communications; exact dates vary by year.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Engaged in manufacturing of telecom and networking products in India
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Telecom and networking products manufacturing
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must meet minimum investment thresholds, achieve incremental sales of covered products over a base year, comply with Make in India guidelines, and meet domestic value addition requirements

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial incentives as a percentage of incremental sales of eligible telecom and networking products manufactured in India
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (disbursed annually based on verified claims)
- Duration of benefit: Annual (based on verified claims submitted by beneficiaries)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation
2.ation
3. Investment plan and capital expenditure details
2. Details of manufacturing facility
3. Product specifications and BIS certification (if applicable)
4. GST registration and PAN
5. Undertaking on compliance with Make in India guidelines
6. Base year sales and projected incremental sales details

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register on the designated PLI portal
2. Submit application with details of manufacturing capacity and investment plan
3. Provide proof of compliance with eligibility criteria
4. Await evaluation and approval by the empowered committee
5. Sign agreement upon approval
6. Commence production and submit periodic reports
7. Claim incentives based on verified incremental sales
