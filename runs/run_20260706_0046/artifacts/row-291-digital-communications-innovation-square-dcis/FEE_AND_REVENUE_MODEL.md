## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the non-financial incubation benefits of DCIS (access to testing infrastructure, mentorship, networking, visibility).
2. Success fees tied to measurable outcomes such as prototype validation, investor connections, or scaling support.
3. Retainer covers ongoing advisory, proposal preparation, and liaison with DoT experts.
4. Scheme-specific note: DCIS has a medium confidence level and rolling application basis; success depends on technical evaluation and progress reporting, introducing moderate risk in outcome prediction.
