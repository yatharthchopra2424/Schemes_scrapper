## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good [morning/afternoon], I'm reaching out from [Your Firm Name]. We specialise in helping telecom innovators, startups, and MSMEs access government incubation platforms like the Digital Communications Innovation Square (DCIS) to validate and scale their solutions without upfront cost. I'd love to understand your current innovation journey and see if DCIS could be a strategic fit for your telecom or digital communication solution.

### Discovery Questions
1. What stage is your telecom or digital communication innovation at—idea, prototype, or ready for testing?
2. Are you working on 5G/6G technologies, indigenous solutions, or aligned with national telecom priorities?
3. What type of entity are you—startup, MSME, or independent innovator?
4. Have you incorporated or registered your entity, and do you have your PAN and registration certificate ready?
5. Do you have a detailed proposal document outlining your innovation’s objectives and expected outcomes?
6. Have you developed a proof of concept or prototype that you’d like to test in a real-world telecom environment?
7. Are you seeking mentorship, investor connections, or access to telecom testing infrastructure?
8. Are you open to collaborating with government platforms that could enhance your solution’s scalability and market readiness?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity is a startup, MSME, or innovator engaged in telecom innovation, standardisation, or digital communication solutions
- [ ] Working on 5G/6G, indigenous solutions, or aligned with national telecom priorities (preference given)
- [ ] Has a proposal document detailing the innovation
- [ ] Possesses Certificate of Incorporation or Registration
- [ ] Has PAN of the entity
- [ ] Has technical specifications and diagrams
- [ ] Has proof of concept or prototype details
- [ ] Can provide team details and qualifications
- [ ] Willing to undergo technical evaluation by DoT experts
- [ ] Prepared to submit progress reports and outcomes for review

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your innovation in [specific area, e.g., 5G/6G or indigenous telecom tech] could be a strong fit for the Digital Communications Innovation Square (DCIS) by the Department of Telecommunications. DCIS offers access to testing infrastructure, expert mentorship, and visibility through government platforms—exactly the kind of validation and scaling support that can de-risk your path to market.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Telecom innovators often struggle to access reliable testing environments, expert guidance, and investor networks needed to validate and scale their solutions—especially when working on cutting-edge or indigenous technologies like 5G/6G. Without this support, even promising innovations face delays, high costs, and limited market traction.

**Scheme intro:**
The Digital Communications Innovation Square (DCIS), administered by the Department of Telecommunications under the Ministry of Communications, is a pan-India incubation scheme that provides access to testing infrastructure, mentorship from industry experts, networking with investors and telecom operators, and visibility through government platforms. While DCIS does not offer direct financial grants, it connects innovators to allied schemes like the Technology Development & Investment Promotion (TDIP) or Incentive (PLI) schemes that provide financial incentives based on investment and sales performance.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a startup, MSME, or innovator engaged in telecom innovation or digital communication solutions  
- Your work focuses on 5G/6G technologies, indigenous solutions, or aligns with national telecom priorities (as per DCIS preference)  
- You have a proposal document, incorporation certificate, PAN, technical specs, and prototype details ready  
- You are prepared to undergo technical evaluation and submit progress reports

| "How much do you charge?" | [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT] |

| "I don't have the documents ready" | The three most commonly missing documents are: (1) detailed proposal document with innovation objectives, (2) technical specifications and diagrams, and (3) proof of concept or prototype details. We help clients prepare these to DoT’s technical evaluation standards. |
