### Digital Communications Innovation Square (DCIS)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Incubation
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dot.gov.in/dcis
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Startups, MSMEs, innovators, and entities engaged in telecom innovation, standardisation, or development of digital communication solutions
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Telecom innovation, standardisation, digital communication solutions
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Preference may be given to entities working on 5G/6G technologies, indigenous solutions, and those aligned with national telecom priorities

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: No direct financial grants or loans; financial support may be available through allied schemes like TDIP or PLI
- Disbursement mode: Not applicable (non-financial support)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing participation based on progress and outcomes

#### Documents Required
1. Proposal document detailing the innovation
2. Certificate of Incorporation or Registration
3. PAN of the entity
4. Technical specifications and diagrams
5. Proof of concept or prototype details
6. Team details and qualifications

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the DCIS portal at https://dot.gov.in/dcis
2. Register as an innovator or startup
3. Submit a proposal detailing the telecom innovation, its objectives, and expected outcomes
4. Undergo technical evaluation by DoT experts
5. If selected, gain access to DCIS infrastructure for testing and validation
6. Receive mentorship and guidance for scaling the solution
7. Submit progress reports and outcomes for review
