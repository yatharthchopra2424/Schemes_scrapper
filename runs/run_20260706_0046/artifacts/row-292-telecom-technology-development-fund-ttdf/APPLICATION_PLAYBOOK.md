# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is an Indian company, startup, MSME, academic institution, or research organization
- [ ] Applicant is registered in India
- [ ] Applicant demonstrates capability to develop and deploy telecom solutions
- [ ] Project aligns with national telecom priorities (5G/6G, rural connectivity, secure communications)
- [ ] Project has clear commercialization potential
- [ ] Applicant is engaged in telecom technology development

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
- PAN Card
- Audited financial statements (ITR)
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed project report with technical specifications
- Budget breakdown and utilization plan
- Letters of collaboration or MoUs (if applicable)
- CVs of key technical personnel
- Proof of prior work or prototypes (if any)
- Declaration of fund utilization and no-duplication of funding

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and in PDF format
- File size must not exceed 10 MB per document
- Document names must match the applicant's legal name
- Scanned copies must be certified true copies where applicable
- All documents must be in English or accompanied by a certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required. Standard sections include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Project Objectives and Methodology  
- Technical Specifications  
- Work Plan and Timeline  
- Budget Breakdown and Utilization Plan  
- Expected Outcomes and Technology Transfer Plan  
- Commercialization Strategy  
- Team Qualifications and Past Experience  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
- Alignment with National Telecom Priorities (5G/6G, rural connectivity, secure communications)

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Ensure all applicant details match the Certificate of Incorporation exactly  
2. Clearly state the project’s alignment with national telecom priorities (5G/6G, rural connectivity, secure communications)  
3. Provide a detailed budget with itemized costs for research, prototyping, testing, and field trials  
4. Attach all scheme-specific documents as per the checklist  
5. Declare no duplication of funding from other government schemes for the same work  
6. Specify the proposed duration for which spectrum assignment or grant is sought  
7. Include details of any collaboration or MoUs with telecom service providers or academic institutions  
8. Validate that the technical team has relevant experience in telecom technology development  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All sections of the application form are complete  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached and legible  
- [ ] Project report includes technical specifications, budget, and commercialization plan  
- [ ] Declaration of fund utilization and no-duplication of funding is signed  
- [ ] CVs of key personnel are relevant to telecom R&D  
- [ ] Letters of collaboration (if any) are on official letterhead and signed  
- [ ] Application is ready for submission via the designated portal

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation page showing the application reference number  
- Store the reference number in the case file with date and time of submission  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days post-submission to check application status  
- Monitor the portal for any requests for additional information

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify the approval letter from the Department of Telecommunications  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned grant amount matches the approved budget  
- [ ] Sign the grant agreement as per scheme guidelines  
- [ ] Initiate project execution as per the approved timeline  
- [ ] Submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates as required  
- [ ] Prepare for final submission of completion report and audit  
- [ ] Coordinate with consultants for success fee invoicing upon fund utilization verification
