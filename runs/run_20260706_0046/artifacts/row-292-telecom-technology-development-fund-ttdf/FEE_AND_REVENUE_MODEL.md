## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the grant amount awarded under the Telecom Technology Development Fund (TTDF).
2. Success fees capped within standard industry ranges, reflecting the non-dilutive nature of the grant.
3. Retainer fees cover proposal development, technical documentation, and compliance with DoT guidelines.
4. Scheme-specific note: The TTDF scheme involves moderate risk due to a competitive evaluation process and dependency on fund availability, but offers high success potential for technically strong projects aligned with national telecom priorities (5G/6G, rural connectivity).
