# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good [morning/afternoon], I'm calling from [Your Firm Name]. We specialize in helping telecom innovators—startups, MSMEs, academic institutions, and research organizations—access government grants for R&D projects. The Telecom Technology Development Fund (TTDF) supports indigenous development in 5G, 6G, and secure communications, and I’d like to see if your work aligns with its priorities.

### Discovery Questions
1. What stage is your telecom technology project at—idea, prototype, or ready for field trials?
2. Is your organization registered in India as a company, startup, MSME, academic institution, or research organization?
3. Does your project focus on national telecom priorities like 5G/6G, rural connectivity, or secure communications?
4. Do you have a detailed project report with technical specifications and a clear commercialization plan?
5. What is your estimated budget for research, prototyping, testing, and field trials?
6. Do you have letters of collaboration or MoUs with telecom service providers or academic partners?
7. Are you seeking financial support for IPR filing or participation in international telecom standards forums?
8. Have you previously applied for government grants, and if so, what was the outcome?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is an Indian company, startup, MSME, academic institution, or research organization
- [ ] Registered in India
- [ ] Engaged in telecom technology development
- [ ] Demonstrates capability to develop and deploy telecom solutions
- [ ] Project shows clear commercialization potential
- [ ] Aligns with national telecom priorities (5G/6G, rural connectivity, secure communications)
- [ ] Has detailed project report with technical specifications
- [ ] Has budget breakdown and utilization plan
- [ ] Has CVs of key technical personnel
- [ ] Has declaration of fund utilization and no-duplication of funding

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, your project in [specific area, e.g., 5G rural connectivity] appears to align well with the Telecom Technology Development Fund (TTDF), which offers grant-based support for R&D projects including testing, certification, and field trials—let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Telecom innovators in India often struggle to fund critical R&D phases like prototyping, testing, and field trials—especially when aligning with national priorities such as 5G/6G or rural connectivity. Without financial support, promising projects stall before commercialization.

**Scheme intro:**
The Telecom Technology Development Fund (TTDF), administered by the Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications, Government of India, provides grant-based financial support for telecom technology development projects. Funding covers research, prototyping, testing, and field trials, with the quantum determined by project scope, technical merit, and commercialization potential.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your organization is registered in India as a [startup/MSME/academic institution/research organization]  
- Your project focuses on [5G/6G/rural connectivity/secure communications], a national telecom priority  
- You have a detailed project report and budget plan, meeting TTDF’s documentation requirements  
- You demonstrate capability to develop and deploy telecom solutions, as evidenced by [mention prototype/prior work if shared]

**Next step:**
We offer a free, no-obligation eligibility check. If you qualify, we assist with the end-to-end application—proposal preparation, document compilation, and submission—on a success-fee-only model. You pay only if the grant is approved.
