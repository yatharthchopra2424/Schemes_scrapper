### Telecom Technology Development Fund (TTDF)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dot.gov.in/ttdf
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Indian companies, startups, MSMEs, academic institutions, research organizations
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Telecom technology development
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be registered in India; demonstrate capability to develop and deploy telecom solutions; preference for projects with clear commercialization potential and alignment with national telecom priorities (5G/6G, rural connectivity, secure communications)

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Grant-based financial support; quantum determined by project scope, technical merit, commercialization potential, and fund availability
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (grant)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (per approved project)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. Audited financial statements of the applicant
3. Detailed project report with technical specifications
4. Budget breakdown and utilization plan
5. Letters of collaboration or MoUs (if applicable)
6. CVs of key technical personnel
7. Proof of prior work or prototypes (if any)
8. Declaration of fund utilization and no-duplication of funding

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Submit a detailed project proposal outlining objectives, methodology, timeline, and budget.
2. Include technical specifications, expected outcomes, and plan for technology transfer or commercialization.
3. Provide details of the applicant organization, including registration, technical team, and past experience.
4. Attach supporting documents such as certificates of incorporation, audited financial statements, and letters of collaboration (if applicable).
5. Submit the application through the designated portal or as specified in the official notification.
6. Await evaluation by the expert committee constituted by the Department of Telecommunications.
7. If approved, sign the grant agreement and commence project execution.
8. Submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates as per scheme guidelines.
