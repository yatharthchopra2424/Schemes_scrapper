## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the financial benefit derived from the scheme, which includes grants for prototype development, testing, and validation activities, with exact quantum determined case-by-case based on proposal and available budget.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Returns**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s rolling application basis and case-by-case funding approval, introducing moderate uncertainty in approval timing and amount.
3. **Market Competitiveness**: Rates are benchmark**: Pricing considers the scheme’s pan-India scope and access to 5G testbed infrastructure, technical mentorship, and validation facilities as non-financial benefits that enhance client value.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The 5G Use Case Labs & Testbeds Initiative has a medium confidence level in funding availability and involves technical evaluation by an expert committee, with processing time affecting revenue lag; success is contingent on proposal quality and budget allocation, making the scheme moderately risky for consultants.
