# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping startups, MSMEs, and enterprises working on 5G use cases access government support for innovation. The 5G Use Case Labs & Testbeds Initiative by the Department of Telecommunications is designed exactly for organisations like yours that are building scalable 5G solutions in sectors such as healthcare, manufacturing, or agriculture. Before we explore fit, may I understand your current focus?

### Discovery Questions
1. What specific 5G use case are you developing or planning to test (e.g., IoT in agriculture, AR/VR in manufacturing, remote healthcare)?
2. When was your entity incorporated or registered in India?
3. Are you a startup, MSME, or enterprise, and do you have a clear proposal outlining objectives and expected outcomes?
4. Do you have technical specifications ready for your 5G use case, including frequency band and performance requirements?
5. What is the size and expertise of your team working on this 5G project?
6. Have you prepared a budget utilisation plan for prototype development and testing activities?
7. Are you seeking access to 5G testbed infrastructure, technical mentorship, or validation facilities?
8. Are you looking for potential industry connections or funding opportunities beyond the test phase?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity is registered in India (Certificate of Incorporation/Registration available)
- [ ] Applicant is a startup, MSME, enterprise, or academic institution
- [ ] Working on a 5G use case with potential for scalability and impact
- [ ] Has a detailed project proposal outlining objectives and expected outcomes
- [ ] Possesses technical specifications of the 5G use case
- [ ] Has team credentials and expertise relevant to 5G development
- [ ] Has prepared a budget utilization plan
- [ ] Seeking access to 5G testbeds, technical mentorship, or validation facilities
- [ ] Willing to sign an MoU and submit final report if selected

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, your work on [specific use case] aligns closely with the 5G Use Case Labs & Testbeds Initiative, which provides access to 5G testbed infrastructure, technical mentorship, and case-by-case financial support for prototype development and testing. Let me explain how this scheme can help you de-risk your innovation journey.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Startups and MSMEs developing 5G applications often struggle to access reliable test environments, technical expertise, and validation facilities — critical for de-risking innovation before commercial deployment. Without these, prototyping costs rise, timelines slip, and investor confidence wanes.

**Scheme intro:**
The 5G Use Case Labs & Testbeds Initiative, administered by the Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications, provides access to 5G testbed infrastructure, technical mentorship, connectivity support, and validation facilities. Financial support for prototype development, testing, and validation is determined on a case-by-case basis depending on the proposal and available budget.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
