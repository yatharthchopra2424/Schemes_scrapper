### 5G Use Case Labs & Testbeds Initiative
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Incubation
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dot.gov.in/5g
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Startups, MSMEs, enterprises, and academic institutions
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: 5G use cases
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Entities must be registered in India and demonstrate a clear 5G innovation proposal with potential for scalability and impact

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Grants for prototype development, testing, and validation activities (quantum determined case-by-case); access to 5G testbed infrastructure, technical mentorship, connectivity support, and validation facilities
- Disbursement mode: Grant (financial support provided on case-by-case basis)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (based on proposal and available budget)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. Detailed project proposal
4. Technical specifications of the 5G use case
5. Team credentials and expertise
6. Budget utilization plan
7. Any relevant certifications or approvals

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the official 5G initiative portal at https://dot.gov.in/5g
2. Review eligibility criteria and guidelines
3. Prepare a detailed proposal outlining the 5G use case, objectives, and expected outcomes
4. Submit the application through the designated portal
5. Await evaluation by the expert committee
6. If selected, sign an MoU and proceed with lab/testbed access
7. Develop and test the 5G solution
8. Submit final report and outcomes for review
