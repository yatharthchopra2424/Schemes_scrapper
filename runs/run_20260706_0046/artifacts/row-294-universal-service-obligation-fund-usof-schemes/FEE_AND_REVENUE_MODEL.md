## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the scale of subsidy disbursement under USOF/DBN schemes, which range from lakhs to thousands of crores.
2. Success fees tied to measurable outcomes such as fund disbursement, project approval, or implementation milestones.
3. Retainers cover advisory, documentation, and liaison efforts with implementing agencies (e.g., BSNL, Reliance Jio, Bharti Airtel).
4. Scheme-specific note: Given the high value of individual projects (e.g., ₹26,316 crore for 4G saturation, ₹7,330 crore for LWE Phase II) and long processing times (18–36 months), this scheme carries moderate to high revenue potential but requires sustained engagement due to multi-year validation and compliance checks.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description:** Retainer for advisory services related to Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF) / Digital Bharat Nidhi (DBN) scheme consulting, including eligibility assessment, project proposal preparation, and liaison with Department of Telecommunications.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description:** Success fee for securing subsidy approval under the Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF), administered by the Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications, for telecom infrastructure projects in commercially non-viable areas.
