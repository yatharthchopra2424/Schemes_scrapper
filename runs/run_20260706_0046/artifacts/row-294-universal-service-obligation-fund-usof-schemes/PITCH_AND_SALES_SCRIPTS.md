# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping telecom service providers access government subsidies for projects in commercially non-viable areas. The Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF), now Digital Bharat Nidhi (DBN), supports providers like you to expand into rural and remote regions where market forces alone don’t justify investment. I’d like to understand your current expansion plans and see if this scheme aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your company’s current focus in terms of geographic expansion—are you targeting rural, remote, or underserved urban areas?  
2. Are you planning projects in areas where ICT services are not available due to sparse population, remoteness, lack of infrastructure, low income, or insurgency?  
3. What type of telecom infrastructure are you considering—mobile towers, broadband (FTTH/OFC), satellite connectivity, or international bandwidth?  
4. Have you identified specific projects such as 4G saturation in uncovered villages, BharatNet expansion, or CTDP for the North-Eastern Region?  
5. What is your expected timeline for project implementation, and are you seeking financial support to meet milestones?  
6. Are you currently registered as a telecom service provider under the Department of Telecommunications (DoT)?  
7. Do you have experience with subsidy-based projects or agreements involving milestone-linked disbursements?  
8. What is your annual revenue or scale of operations—are you a large TSP like BSNL, Reliance Jio, Bharti Airtel, or a regional player?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Telecom service provider (TSP) licensed by DoT  
- [ ] Project targets areas with no ICT services due to commercial non-viability (sparse population, remoteness, no infrastructure, low income, insurgency)  
- [ ] Project type aligns with DBN objectives: mobile coverage, broadband (BharatNet), international bandwidth, DSPTs, pilot projects, R&D, or new service adoption  
- [ ] Project located in rural, remote, or underserved urban areas across India (Pan-India scope)  
- [ ] Willing to adhere to Telecommunications (Administration of Digital Bharat Nidhi) Rules, 2024 and project-specific terms  
- [ ] Prepared for verification of progress and compliance with milestones for subsidy disbursement  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your [mobile tower/BharatNet/CTDP] project in [specific area] fits the Universal Service Obligation Fund’s criteria for subsidy support. The scheme provides financial assistance to telecom providers like you to deploy services in commercially non-viable areas—let me explain how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Telecom service providers face significant financial barriers when expanding into rural, remote, or insurgency-affected areas due to low population density, difficult terrain, lack of infrastructure, and limited purchasing power. These areas remain uncovered despite national digital inclusion goals, creating a persistent access gap.

**Scheme intro:**
The Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF), now known as Digital Bharat Nidhi (DBN), is a Government of India subsidy administered by the Bharat Nidhi (DBN)**, administered by the Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications, provides **subsidy support** to telecom service providers for projects in commercially non-viable areas. As of 31.03.2026, the fund has a total accretion of **₹201,690.52 crore**, with **₹95,138 crore disbursed** and a **potentially available balance of ₹106,552.50 crore**. Support includes funding for BharatNet, 4G saturation, mobile towers in LWE-affected areas, CTDP for NER, international bandwidth hiring, and Digital Satellite Phone Terminals.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a licensed telecom service provider undertaking projects in areas where ICT services are not available due to commercial non-viability  
- Your project targets rural, remote, or underserved urban areas across India (Pan-India eligibility)  
- You are willing to comply with the Telecommunications (Administration of Digital Bharat Nidhi) Rules, 2024 and milestone-based verification  
- Your project type (e.g., 4G mobile services, broadband connectivity, international bandwidth) aligns with DBN’s funded objectives
