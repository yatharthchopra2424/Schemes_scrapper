### Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF) Schemes
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://usof.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Telecom service providers
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Telecom
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Undertaking projects in areas where ICT services are not available due to commercial non-viability, on account of sparse population, remoteness of areas, absence of supporting infrastructure, low income of inhabitants and insurgency, as determined by the Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF) / Digital Bharat Nidhi (DBN)

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Subsidy support for projects including ₹7,330 crore for LWE Phase II (excluding taxes), ₹2,426 crore for LWE Phase I 4G upgradation, ₹17.15 crore for international bandwidth hiring to BSNL, ₹2028.80 crore for CTDP in Arunachal Pradesh and two districts of Assam, ₹726.65 crore for uncovered villages and national highways of Meghalaya, ₹686.71 crore for 502 uncovered villages in aspirational districts, ₹3,685 crore for 7,287 aspirational villages, and ₹26,316 crore for saturation of 4G mobile services in uncovered villages across the country
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit to implementing agencies
- Duration of benefit: Project-based (varies by scheme; e.g., Rural Wireline Broadband Scheme had a validity of nine years, extended by one year)

#### Documents Required
1. Project proposal detailing scope, location, and objectives
2. Technical feasibility report
3. Financial plan and cost estimates
4. Implementation timeline and milestones
5. Details of implementing agency (e.g., BSNL, Reliance Jio, Bharti Airtel)
6. Compliance certificates with Telecommunications (Administration of Digital Bharat Nidhi) Rules, 2024
7. Progress reports for fund disbursement tranches
8. Utilization certificates for previous funds received
9. Land allotment or right-of-way clearances (if applicable)
10. Environmental and security clearances (if applicable)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify eligible project in commercially non-viable area
2. Prepare project proposal with technical and financial details
3. Submit proposal to Department of Telecommunications / USOF
4. Proposal evaluated for eligibility and feasibility
5. Upon approval, enter into agreement with USOF
6. Implement project as per agreed milestones
7. Submit progress reports and utilization certificates
8. Receive subsidy disbursement based on verification of progress
9. Complete project and submit final completion report
10. Undergo audit and verification for final settlement
