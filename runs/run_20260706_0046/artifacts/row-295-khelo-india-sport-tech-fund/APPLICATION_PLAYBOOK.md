# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Athlete identified through talent search and development programs by the High-Powered Committee  
- [ ] Athlete participates in priority sports disciplines as determined by the High-Powered Committee  
- [ ] Athlete receives official induction notification (e.g., Induction Notification of Khelo India Athletes - [discipline])  
- [ ] Athlete meets age criteria specific to the sport discipline and vertical (e.g., Under-17, Under-21 categories)  
- [ ] Athlete provides proof of age and identity (birth certificate, Aadhaar, passport, etc.)  
- [ ] Athlete submits medical fitness certificate  
- [ ] Athlete provides performance records or trial results from recognized competitions/trials  
- [ ] Athlete possesses NSRS ID (National Sports Registry System) for their sport discipline  
- [ ] Athlete agrees to adhere to retention protocols and performance benchmarks  
- [ ] Athlete commits to participation in Khelo India programmes (State Level Centres, Annual Sports Competitions, Talent Search and Development, etc.)  
- [ ] Athlete provides bank account details for financial assistance transfer  

> **Note**: Eligibility varies by vertical (Talent Search and Development, State Level Khelo India Centres, Annual Sports Competitions, etc.). Specific criteria (age, performance, discipline) are determined by the High-Powered Committee. Refer to induction notifications for discipline-specific benchmarks.

**Sources**:  
- Scheme Key Facts: Eligibility, Target Beneficiaries, Application Process  
- Supporting Evidence: Induction Notifications (latestnews.html), About Page (about.html), State Level Centres (statelevel.html)

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation / Registration (for academies/institutions applying as entities)  
- [ ] PAN of the entity or individual  
- [ ] ITR (Income Tax Return) of the entity or individual (last 2 years, if applicable)  
- [ ] GST registration certificate (if applicable)  
- [ ] Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- [ ] Udyam Registration (for MSMEs, if applicable)  
- [ ] Aadhaar card of the athlete  
- [ ] Passport or other government-issued photo ID  
- [ ] Domicile certificate (if claiming state-specific benefits)  
- [ ] Educational certificates (for school/college-going athletes)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- [ ] Authorization letter from authorized signatory (for institutional applications)  
- [ ] Pitch deck or business description (for partnerships or academy applications)  
- [ ] Details of funding received (if any)  
- [ ] Athlete's NSRS ID and sport discipline  
- [ ] Proof of age and identity (birth certificate, school certificate, Aadhaar)  
- [ ] Medical fitness certificate (from authorized medical practitioner)  
- [ ] Performance records or trial results (from district/state/national level competitions)  
- [ ] Bank account details for financial assistance transfer (cancelled cheque or bank passbook copy)  

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible and in PDF/JPEG format  
- [ ] File size does not exceed 5 MB per document  
- [ ] Document names follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ApplicantName_Date.pdf`  
- [ ] Names on documents match exactly with application form and ID proof  
- [ ] Certificates are issued by recognized authorities (e.g., medical certificate from government hospital or registered practitioner)  
- [ ] Documents are in English or Hindi; if in regional language, attach certified translation  
- [ ] Bank account details include account holder name, account number, IFSC, and bank name  
- [ ] Authorization letter is on official letterhead with signatory details and seal

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for individual athlete applications under the Talent Search and Development vertical.  
**Note**: Verify with the nodal officer if applying for institutional support (e.g., State Level Khelo India Centres, KISCE) as DPR may be required for infrastructure or centre-based grants.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official portal: **https://kheloindia.gov.in**  
2. Look for "Induction Notification" or "Apply for Khelo India Athlete" sections under Latest News or About pages  
3. Fill in athlete’s personal details exactly as per ID proof (name, date of birth, gender)  
4. Enter NSRS ID and sport discipline accurately (cross-check with performance records)  
5. Upload all scheme-specific documents in the prescribed order and format  
6. Provide bank account details carefully — incorrect details will delay financial assistance  
7. Declare that the athlete agrees to adhere to retention protocols and performance benchmarks  
8. Double-check contact information (email, phone) for communication from SAI/Khelo India  
9. Submit only after verifying all fields are complete and documents are attached  
10. Save a copy of the filled application before final submission  

> **Portal-Specific Note**: The portal does not host a standalone application form; applications are submitted in response to official induction notifications. Follow the instructions in the notification document for submission method (email or portal upload).

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All personal details match ID proof and NSRS records  
- [ ] Sport discipline matches the priority list for the current induction cycle  
- [ ] Age falls within the eligible range for the discipline and vertical  
- [ ] Medical fitness certificate is valid (issued within last 30 days)  
- [ ] Performance records are recent (within last 6–12 months) and from recognized events  
- [ ] Bank account details are correct and active  
- [ ] Authorization letter (if applicable) is on letterhead and signed  
- [ ] All uploaded documents are legible and under 5 MB  
- [ ] Declarations regarding retention protocols are acknowledged  
- [ ] Application reference number or submission ID is noted

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation page or email acknowledgment  
- Store the application reference number or induction notification reference in the case file  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days after submission to check status  
- Save all submitted documents in a dedicated folder named: `KheloIndia_Application_[CaseID]_[Date]`

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify approval letter or induction notification from Khelo India  
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned amount: INR 5 lakh per annum for 8 years (as per scheme)  
- [ ] Ensure bank account details are correct for direct benefit transfer (DBT)  
- [ ] Guide athlete on utilization of funds for lodging, boarding, education, competition exposure, sports training, and equipment  
- [ ] Monitor compliance with retention protocols and performance benchmarks  
- [ ] Prepare for annual performance review to continue support  
- [ ] Assist in obtaining utilization certificate if required for institutional partners  
- [ ] Track invoice for success fee as per consultancy agreement  

> **Note**: Continued support is subject to annual performance review and adherence to retention protocols. Athletes may be weeded out based on performance, injury, or failure to meet benchmarks.
