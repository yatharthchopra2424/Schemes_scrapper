## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the financial benefit delivered—here, the Khelo India – Sport Tech Fund provides INR 5 lakh per annum for 8 years (total INR 40 lakh per athlete), justifying success fees in the 4–6% range for mid-market clients (e.g., sports academies, institutions).
2. **Risk Mitigation**: Success fees are contingent on approval and fund disbursement; given the scheme’s rolling basis and performance-linked retention (with weed-out protocols), fees reflect moderate risk—processing time varies by talent identification cycles, but approvals are regular and well-documented.
3. **Market Competitiveness**: Retainer fees cover initial eligibility assessment, documentation, and application submission; success fees are benchmarked against similar government grant schemes in sports and youth development.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Khelo India scheme has a **medium confidence** rating due to performance-contingent disbursement and annual renewal subject to benchmarks. However, the rolling application cycle and clear eligibility criteria (talented athletes in priority disciplines) reduce uncertainty, supporting a mid-tier success fee (4–6%) for institutions applying on behalf of athletes.
