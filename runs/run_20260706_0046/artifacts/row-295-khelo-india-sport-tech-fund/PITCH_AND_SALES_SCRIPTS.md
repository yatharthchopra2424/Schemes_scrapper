# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping athletes, sportspersons, and sports organisations access government support for talent development. The Khelo India – Sport Tech Fund is designed to nurture young talent across priority sports disciplines by providing financial assistance and access to top-tier training infrastructure. I’d like to understand your background and goals to see if this scheme aligns with your needs.

### Discovery Questions
1. What sport discipline do you or your athlete specialise in, and is it recognised as a priority discipline under Khelo India?  
2. What is the athlete’s date of birth and current age category (Under 17, Under 21, etc.)?  
3. Has the athlete been identified through any talent search program or participated in Khelo India Games (Youth, University, Para, etc.)?  
4. Are you affiliated with a State Level Khelo India Centre, Sports Authority of India centre, or a recognised sports academy?  
5. What performance records, trial results, or competition achievements does the athlete have?  
6. Does the athlete possess a valid NSRS ID and proof of age/identity?  
7. Is the athlete medically fit for intensive training, and do you have a recent medical fitness certificate?  
8. Are you seeking financial assistance for lodging, boarding, education, competition exposure, sports training, or equipment?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Athlete is identified in a priority sports discipline by the High-Powered Committee  
- [ ] Athlete is under the eligible age bracket (typically Under 17 or Under 21 for entry)  
- [ ] Athlete has participated in Khelo India Games or talent search programs  
- [ ] Athlete possesses NSRS ID, proof of age, and identity documents  
- [ ] Athlete has medical fitness certificate  
- [ ] Athlete submits performance records or trial results  
- [ ] Application is for financial assistance, centre support, or academy partnership  
- [ ] Beneficiary falls under target group: athlete, youth, woman, person with disability, rural/tribal community, or school-going child  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears the athlete may qualify for the Khelo India – Sport Tech Fund, which provides INR 5 lakh per annum for up to 8 years for talented athletes. Let me outline how this scheme works and how we can support your application.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Young athletes in India often lack consistent financial support for training, equipment, competition exposure, and access to high-performance centres, which hinders their development and ability to compete at national and international levels.

**Scheme intro:**
The Khelo India – Sport Tech Fund is a central sector grant scheme implemented by the Sports Authority of India, Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports, and Lakshmi Bai National Institute of Physical Education (LNIPE). It provides annual financial assistance of INR 5 lakh per annum for 8 years to talented athletes identified in priority sports disciplines, along with access to State Level Khelo India Centres, Annual Sports Competitions, and comprehensive support for lodging, boarding, education, training, and equipment.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Athlete identified in a priority sports discipline by the High-Powered Committee  
- Possesses valid NSRS ID, proof of age, identity, and medical fitness certificate  
- Has submitted performance records or trial results from recognised competitions  
- Falls under target beneficiary group: youth athlete, sportsperson, or school-going child
