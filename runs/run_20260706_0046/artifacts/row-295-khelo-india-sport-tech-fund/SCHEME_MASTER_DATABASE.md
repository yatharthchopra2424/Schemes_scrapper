### Khelo India – Sport Tech Fund
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Sports Authority of India, Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports, Lakshmi Bai National Institute of Physical Education (LNIPE)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://kheloindia.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Rolling basis — inductions and notifications are issued periodically throughout the year based on talent identification cycles.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Athletes, sportspersons, youth, women, persons with disabilities, rural and tribal communities, school-going children
- Age of business: Not applicable (individual athlete focus)
- Sector: Sports (priority disciplines as determined by High-Powered Committee)
- Revenue cap: Not mentioned
- Employee count: Not mentioned
- Other: Talented players identified in priority sports disciplines at various levels by the High-Powered Committee; specific eligibility varies by vertical including age, performance, and discipline-based criteria

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: INR 5 lakh per annum for 8 years (max per entity); additional grants include ₹2 lakhs per discipline as annual recurring grant for SAI Extension centres converted to Khelo India Centres, viability gap funding for Khelo India State Centres of Excellence (KISCE)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (financial assistance transfer via bank account)
- Duration of benefit: 8 years (annual financial assistance)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration (for academies/institutions)
2. PAN of the entity
3. Pitch deck or business description (for partnerships)
4. Authorization letter from authorized signatory
5. Details of funding received (if any)
6. Athlete's NSRS ID and sport discipline
7. Proof of age and identity
8. Medical fitness certificate
9. Performance records or trial results
10. Bank account details for financial assistance transfer

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Athletes are identified through talent search and development programs conducted by the High-Powered Committee.
2. Selected athletes are notified via official induction notifications (e.g., Induction Notification of Khelo India Athletes - [discipline]).
3. Athletes must submit required documents for verification and inclusion in the Khelo India athlete database.
4. Upon approval, athletes receive annual financial assistance and access to training facilities.
5. Continued support is subject to performance review and retention protocols.
