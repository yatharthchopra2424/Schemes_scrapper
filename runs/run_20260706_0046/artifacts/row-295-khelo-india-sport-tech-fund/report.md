# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Khelo India – Sport Tech Fund** is a pan-India grant scheme implemented by the Sports Authority of India (SAI), Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports, and Lakshmi Bai National Institute of Physical Education (LNIPE). It aims to revive sports culture at the grassroots level, build a strong framework for all sports, and establish India as a great sporting nation by inspiring young talent and providing top-notch infrastructure and training. The scheme operates on a rolling basis with inductions and notifications issued periodically throughout the year based on talent identification cycles. Last updated in 2022, it remains active with ongoing talent search and development programs.

### Objectives
- Revive sports culture in India at the grass-root level  
- Build a strong framework for all sports played in the country  
- Establish India as a great sporting nation  
- Inspire young talent and provide top-notch infrastructure and training  
- Inculcate a strong spirit of participation in sports  
- Enable players to demonstrate their true potential  
- Support National/Regional/State Sports Academies  
- Promote sports among persons with disabilities  

### Eligibility Matrix
| **Beneficiary Type** | **Eligibility Criteria** | **Key Requirements** |
|----------------------|--------------------------|----------------------|
| Talented Athletes | Identified in priority sports disciplines by the High-Powered Committee at various levels | Must meet age, performance, and discipline-based criteria; possess NSRS ID; submit proof of age/identity, medical fitness, performance records |
| Sport Academies/Institutions | Seeking partnership or funding under the scheme | Must submit Certificate of Incorporation/Registration, PAN, pitch deck/business description, authorization letter, details of existing funding |
| Athletes in State Level Khelo India Centres (SLKIC) | Enrolled in SAI Training Centres converted to Khelo India Centres | Must be part of the 3865 athletes currently covered (Boys-2465, Girls-1400) across 67 SAI Training Centres |
| Participants in Annual Sports Competitions | Eligible to compete in Khelo India Youth Games, University Games, Winter Games, Beach Games, Para Games | Must meet age and sport-specific qualification criteria (e.g., Under-17/Under-21 categories) |
| Beneficiaries of Sports for Women & Disabilities | Women athletes and persons with disabilities | Must participate in designated verticals; eligibility varies by sport and competition level |
| School-going Children | For Physical Fitness vertical | Targeted for fitness initiatives; no specific performance criteria mentioned |
| Rural & Tribal Communities | For promotion of rural/indigenous/tribal games | Must engage in identified traditional sports; eligibility based on community participation |

> **Key Caveats**:  
> - Financial assistance is contingent on continued performance and adherence to retention protocols.  
> - Athletes may be weeded out based on performance, injury, or failure to meet benchmarks.  
> - Support is limited to priority sports disciplines as determined by the High-Powered Committee.  
> - The programme does not guarantee permanent employment or long-term sponsorship beyond the 8-year assistance period.  
> - Funding for centres and infrastructure is subject to budgetary allocations and utilization certificates.  

### Benefits & Financial Support
| **Benefit Type** | **Details** | **Financial Value / Scope** |
|------------------|-----------|----------------------------|
| Annual Financial Assistance | For talented players in priority sports disciplines | **INR 5 lakh per annum for 8 years** (max INR 40 lakh per athlete) |
| State Level Khelo India Centres (SLKIC) | Lodging, boarding, education, competition exposure, sports training, equipment | Government-incurred expenditure; ₹2 lakhs per discipline as annual recurring grant for SAI Extension centres converted to Khelo India Centres |
| Khelo India State Centres of Excellence (KISCE) | Viability gap funding for high-performance training | Released for 23 centres in 22 States/UTs; assessment underway for 4 centres in 4 States/UTs |
| Annual Sports Competitions | Participation in Khelo India Youth Games, University Games, Winter Games, Beach Games, Para Games | Travel, lodging, boarding, competition exposure covered by government |
| Talent Search & Development | Identification and nurturing of talent | Conducted by High-Powered Committee; includes trials, assessments, and induction notifications |
| Sports for Women & Disabilities | Promotion and support | Access to competitions, training facilities, and financial assistance where applicable |
| Rural & Indigenous Games | Promotion and development | Support for traditional sports; no direct financial aid to individuals mentioned |
| Physical Fitness for School Children | Initiatives in schools | Infrastructure and program support; no individual stipend |
| Infrastructure Development | Play field creation/upgradation | Funding subject to budgetary allocations; utilization certificates required |
| Community Coaching Development | Training via LNIPE | Conducted by Lakshmi Bai National Institute of Physical Education |

> **Financial Support Notes**:  
> - Under Talent Search and Development: INR 5 lakh per annum for 8 years for talented athletes.  
> - Additional funding includes grants for State Level Khelo India Centres (₹2 lakhs per discipline annually).  
> - Viability gap funding for Khelo India State Centres of Excellence (KISCE).  
> - Expenditure on lodging, boarding, education, competition exposure, sports training, and equipment incurred by Government for athletes in supported centres.  

### Required Documents
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration (for academies/institutions)  
2. PAN of the entity  
3. Pitch deck or business description (for partnerships)  
4. Authorization letter from authorized signatory  
5. Details of funding received (if any)  
6. Athlete's NSRS ID and sport discipline  
7. Proof of age and identity  
8. Medical fitness certificate  
9. Performance records or trial results  
10. Bank account details for financial assistance transfer  

### Application Process
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Talent Search & Development Programs] --> B[Identification by High-Powered Committee]
    B --> C[Official Induction Notification (e.g., by discipline)]
    C --> D[Athlete submits required documents for verification]
    D --> E[Approval & inclusion in Khelo India athlete database]
    E --> F[Disbursement of annual financial assistance (INR 5 lakh/year)]
    F --> G[Access to training facilities, competitions, and support services]
    G --> H[Annual performance review]
    H --> I{Retention Protocols Met?}
    I -->|Yes| J[Continued support for next year]
    I -->|No| K[Weedout Notification & potential discontinuation]
    J --> H
    K --> L[Exit from scheme]
```

**Application Portal**: https://kheloindia.gov.in  
**Contact Details**: Tel No. : 01124364243 | Email id : kheloindia[dot]div[at]gov.in  

> **Process Notes**:  
> - Athletes are identified through talent search and development programs.  
> - Selected athletes receive induction notifications (e.g., "Induction Notification of Khelo India Athletes - [discipline]").  
> - Upon document verification and approval, athletes receive annual financial assistance and access to facilities.  
> - Continued support is subject to annual performance review and retention protocols; failure to meet benchmarks results in weedout.  
> - Rolling basis: inductions and notifications issued periodically throughout the year.
