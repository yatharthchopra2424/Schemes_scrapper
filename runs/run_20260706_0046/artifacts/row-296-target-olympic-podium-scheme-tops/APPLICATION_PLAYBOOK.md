## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Athlete has achieved a top-8 finish in World Championships or Olympic Games in an individual event  
- [ ] Athlete has achieved a top-3 finish in Asian Games or Commonwealth Games in an individual event  
- [ ] Athlete has medal-winning performance in team events at World Championships, Olympics, Asian Games, or Commonwealth Games  
- [ ] Athlete is identified as a developmental athlete showing consistent improvement and medal-winning potential (for TOPS Development Group)  
- [ ] Athlete is nominated by a recognized National Sports Federation (NSF) or the Sports Authority of India (SAI)  
- [ ] Athlete is an Indian citizen residing in India (Pan-India scheme)  
- [ ] Athlete is not currently serving a sanction for anti-doping violations  
- [ ] Athlete agrees to adhere to the Annual Training Plan (ATP) and submit progress reports as required  

> **Note**: Eligibility is assessed by the TOPS Mission Directorate based on performance benchmarks and potential. Inclusion is on a rolling basis throughout the year.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- **DPR Required?** No formal Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required for TOPS.  
- **Alternative Requirement**: An **Annual Training Plan (ATP)** must be formulated for each athlete and approved by the TOPS Mission Directorate.  
- **Standard ATP Sections to follow the ATP structure as advised by the Mission Directorate. If template is not provided, verify with nodal officer.  
  - Athlete profile and performance history  
  - Short-term and long-term goals (Olympic/Paralympic targets)  
  - Periodized training macrocycle (annual plan broken into phases)  
  - Competition calendar (national and international events)  
  - Coaching and support staff plan  
  - Equipment and infrastructure needs  
  - Sports science and medical support plan  
  - Budget breakdown (foreign training, competition, equipment, stipend, staff)  
  - Monitoring and evaluation framework  

> **Note**: The ATP must be approved by the TOPS Mission Directorate before fund disbursement. Confirm latest format with SAI or Mission Directorate.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Initiation**: Applications are not submitted directly by athletes; they are **nominated by NSFs or SAI**. Confirm nomination status with the athlete’s federation.  
2. **Nomination Letter**: Obtain a formal nomination letter from the NSF or SAI recommending inclusion in TOPS Core or Development Group.  
3. **ATP Submission**: Submit the Annual Training Plan (ATP) along with nomination and supporting documents to the TOPS Mission Directorate via SAI.  
4. **Accuracy**: Ensure all performance claims (e.g., rankings, medals) are verifiable and match certificate details.  
5. **Consistency**: Use the same name, date of birth, and ID numbers across all forms and documents.  
6. **Clarity**: Clearly specify the type of support sought (foreign training, equipment, stipend, etc.) with itemized justification.  
7. **Declaration**: Include a signed declaration by the athlete agreeing to comply with ATP, anti-doping rules, and reporting requirements.  
8. **Submission Channel**: All documents must be routed through the **Sports Authority of India (SAI)**; there is no public online portal for direct athlete application.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Nomination letter from NSF/SAI is present and signed  
- [ ] Annual Training Plan (ATP) is complete, signed, and aligned with athlete’s goals  
- [ ] All performance certificates are valid and issued by recognized federations  
- [ ] Utilization certificates (if applicable) are in GFR 12-A format and CA-certified  
- [ ] Medical/fitness certificates are recent and from authorized sources  
- [ ] Foreign training/competition proposal includes budget, dates, objectives, and approval from NSF  
- [ ] Declarations signed by athlete and countersigned by NSF/SAI representative  
- [ ] All documents are PDF, clearly named, and under 5 MB  
- [ ] Athlete’s bank details are verified for fund disbursement readiness

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and archive the **official approval letter** from the TOPS Mission Directorate confirming inclusion in Core or Development Group  
- [ ] Verify the **sanctioned amount and components** (foreign training, equipment, stipend, staff, etc.) as per approved ATP  
- [ ] Ensure the athlete signs any **bond or undertaking** if required for fund utilization  
- [ ] Confirm **bank details** are validated for direct benefit transfer (DBT) of stipend and reimbursements  
- [ ] Guide the athlete to initiate foreign training/competition only after **ATP approval and fund allocation**  
- [ ] Monitor timelines for **utilization certificate submission** (typically quarterly or post-activity)  
- [ ] Ensure all expenditures are supported by **valid invoices, bills, and payment proofs**  
- [ ] Prepare and submit **utilization certificates (GFR 12-A)** duly certified by a Chartered Accountant  
- [ ] Submit **progress reports** and **medical/fitness updates** as per ATP schedule  
- [ ] Track performance metrics and competition results for renewal consideration  
- [ ] Confirm **success fee invoice** is raised only after fund disbursement is verified in athlete’s account  
- [ ] Maintain a **dedicated folder** for all TOPS-related correspondence, approvals, and financial records  

> **Note**: Continued support is contingent on satisfactory performance, ATP adherence, anti-doping compliance, and timely reporting. Non-compliance may lead to withdrawal of funding.
