## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the transformative impact of securing elite athlete funding under TOPS.
2. Transparent fee structures with clear milestones tied to application stages and fund disbursement.
3. Customized retainers reflecting the intensive, long-term support required for athlete profiling, documentation, and liaison with NSFs/SAI.
4. Success fees calibrated to the scheme’s high value and competitive nature; moderate risk profile due to rolling applications and performance-dependent continuation, though initial inclusion has strong precedent for eligible athletes meeting podium-level benchmarks.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description:** Retainer for Target Olympic Podium Scheme (TOPS) consulting services — includes athlete eligibility assessment, documentation preparation, coordination with National Sports Federations (NSFs) and Sports Authority of India (SAI), and liaison with the TOPS Mission Directorate for inclusion evaluation.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description:** Success fee for securing financial support under the Target Olympic Podium Scheme (TOPS), implemented by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India, upon approval of athlete inclusion in the TOPS core or development group and disbursement of funds as per the Annual Training Plan (ATP).
