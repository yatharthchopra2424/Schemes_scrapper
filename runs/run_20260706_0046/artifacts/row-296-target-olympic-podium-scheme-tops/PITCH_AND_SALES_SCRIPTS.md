## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good [morning/afternoon], I'm calling from [Your Firm Name]. We specialize in helping elite athletes and their support teams access government high-performance funding like the Target Olympic Podium Scheme (TOPS). I know your focus is on achieving peak performance for Olympic success, and I wanted to explore whether TOPS could provide the customized financial backing you need for foreign training, competition exposure, and support staff—without any upfront cost to you.

### Discovery Questions
- What is your current international competition level—have you achieved top-8 finishes in World Championships/Olympics or top-3 in Asian Games/Commonwealth Games in individual events?
- Are you part of a team that has won medals in international competitions, or are you an individual athlete showing consistent improvement with Olympic medal potential?
- What are your estimated annual expenses for foreign training, international competition participation, equipment, and hiring foreign coaches or support staff?
- Do you have an Annual Training Plan (ATP) approved by the TOPS Mission Directorate, or would you need support in developing one?
- Are you currently receiving any government or private funding for your training and competition expenses?
- What documentation do you have ready regarding your performance records, utilization certificates, and medical/fitness reports?
- How do you currently monitor and evaluate your performance progress, and are you open to structured tracking through a government-backed program?
- Are you affiliated with a National Sports Federation (NSF) or the Sports Authority of India (SAI), which typically identifies athletes for TOPS consideration?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Athlete has achieved top-8 finish in World Championships/Olympics (individual event)
- [ ] Athlete has achieved top-3 finish in Asian Games/Commonwealth Games (individual event)
- [ ] Athlete has medal-winning performance in team events at international level
- [ ] Athlete shows consistent improvement and potential (for developmental group consideration)
- [ ] Athlete is an Indian national competing in Olympic disciplines
- [ ] Athlete is identified by National Sports Federation (NSF) or Sports Authority of India (SAI)
- [ ] Athlete requires financial support for foreign training, competition, equipment, or support staff
- [ ] Athlete is willing to adhere to Annual Training Plan (ATP) and submit progress/utilization reports

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears you may meet the eligibility criteria for the Target Olympic Podium Scheme (TOPS), which provides customized financial assistance—including foreign training, competition exposure, equipment, support staff, and a monthly stipend—for elite athletes like yourself who are Olympic medal prospects. Let me share how TOPS works and how we’ve helped similar athletes secure this support.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Elite athletes aiming for Olympic medals face significant financial barriers in India often struggle to cover the high costs of foreign training, international competition exposure, specialized equipment, and hiring world-class coaches and support staff—expenses that are critical for performance but difficult to sustain without reliable, long-term funding.

**Scheme intro:**
The Target Olympic Podium Scheme (TOPS), administered by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India, is a grant-based initiative that provides customized financial assistance to elite athletes with medal-winning potential. Support includes funding for foreign training, international competition participation, equipment, sports science and medical support, hiring of foreign coaches and support staff, and a monthly out-of-pocket allowance—amounts tailored to individual athlete needs and approved by the TOPS Mission Directorate.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You have achieved top-8 finishes in World Championships/Olympics or top-3 in Asian Games/Commonwealth Games in individual events, or medal-winning performance in team events
- You are identified by your National Sports Federation (NSF) or the Sports Authority of India (SAI) as an athlete with Olympic medal prospects
- You require financial support for foreign training, competition exposure, equipment, or hiring foreign coaches and support staff
- You are committed to following an Annual Training Plan (ATP) and submitting required progress and utilization reports
