### Target Olympic Podium Scheme (TOPS)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://yas.nic.in/scheme/target-olympic-podium-scheme-tops
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Individual athletes (not applicable to businesses)
- Age of business: Not applicable
- Sector: Sports (elite athletic disciplines)
- Revenue cap: Not applicable
- Employee count: Not applicable
- Other: Athletes must have achieved top-8 finishes in World Championships/Olympics or top-3 finishes in Asian Games/Commonwealth Games in individual events, or medal-winning performance in team events; developmental athletes showing consistent improvement and potential may also be considered

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Customized financial assistance covering foreign training, competition exposure, equipment, support staff, and monthly stipend (quantum determined by TOPS Mission Directorate based on individual needs)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (upon submission of utilization certificates and progress reports)
- Duration of benefit: Annual (subject to renewal based on performance and compliance)

#### Documents Required
1. Performance records and certificates from national/international competitions
2. Annual Training Plan (ATP) approved by the TOPS Mission Directorate
3. Utilization certificates for previous fund disbursements
4. Progress reports and medical/fitness certificates
5. Details of foreign training/competition exposure proposed

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identification of potential athletes by National Sports Federations (NSFs) and the Sports Authority of India (SAI)
2. Evaluation by the TOPS Mission Directorate based on performance benchmarks and potential
3. Approval of inclusion in the TOPS core or development group
4. Formulation of a customized annual training plan (ATP) for each athlete
5. Disbursement of funds as per the approved ATP upon submission of utilization certificates and progress reports
