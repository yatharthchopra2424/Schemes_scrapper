## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Target Olympic Podium Scheme (TOPS)** is a flagship initiative by the **Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India**, designed to identify and support athletes with medal-winning potential for the Olympic Games. It operates on a **rolling basis**, allowing athletes to be considered for inclusion throughout the year based on performance and potential. The scheme is implemented pan-India and falls under the **grant** category.

### Objectives
- Identify and support athletes with medal-winning potential for the Olympic Games  
- Provide financial assistance for customized training and foreign exposure  
- Ensure access to high-performance support staff and sports science services  
- Supply necessary equipment and infrastructure for optimal preparation  
- Monitor and evaluate athlete progress through structured performance tracking

> **Note**: Inclusion in TOPS does **not** guarantee Olympic selection, which remains the sole prerogative of respective National Sports Federations (NSFs). Continued support is contingent on satisfactory performance, adherence to the Annual Training Plan (ATP), and timely submission of required reports.

### Benefits & Financial Support
TOPS provides **customized financial assistance** tailored to individual athlete needs. There is no fixed upper limit; the quantum is determined by the **TOPS Mission Directorate** based on requirements and performance potential.

### Application Process
The application is **not athlete-initiated** but driven by National Sports Federations (NSFs) and the Sports Authority of India (SAI). Athletes cannot apply directly; they must be nominated through their NSF or SAI center.

#### Step-by-Step Breakdown:
1. **Identification**: NSFs and SAI identify athletes meeting eligibility criteria (top-8 in Worlds/Olympics, top-3 in Asian/CWG, or medal in team events) or showing developmental promise.
2. **Evaluation**: TOPS Mission Directorate assesses performance, potential, and training needs.
3. **Approval**: Athlete is inducted into either the **TOPS Core Group** (medal prospects) or **TOPS Development Group** (future prospects).
4. **ATP Formulation**: A personalized Annual Training Plan is created, detailing foreign training, competitions, equipment, support staff, and stipend requirements.
5. **Fund Disbursement**: Money is released in tranches **only after** the athlete submits:
   - Utilization certificates for previous funds
   - Progress reports
   - Medical/fitness certificates
   - Details of proposed foreign training/competition

### Required Documents
Athletes (via their NSF/SAI) must submit:
1. Performance records and certificates from national/international competitions  
2. Annual Training Plan (ATP) approved by the TOPS Mission Directorate  
3. Utilization certificates for previous fund disbursements  
4. Progress reports and medical/fitness certificates  
5. Details of foreign training/competition exposure proposed  

### Key Caveats
> - Continued support depends on **satisfactory performance**, **adherence to ATP**, and **timely submission of reports**  
> - Funding may be **withdrawn or reduced** for non-compliance with training plans or **anti-doping violations**  
> - TOPS inclusion **does not guarantee Olympic team selection** — this remains with NSFs  
> - The scheme operates on a **rolling basis** with **no fixed deadlines**; evaluations occur throughout the year  

### Application Portal & Sources
- **Official Information Source**: [Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports - TOPS Page](https://yas.nic.in/sports/tops/)  
- **Note**: There is no direct public application portal; engagement is mediated through NSFs and SAI.
