## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is an individual athlete, coach, sports person, or sports institution  
- [ ] Applicant is recognized by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports or respective National Sports Federation  
- [ ] Application is recommended by the concerned National Sports Federation or State Sports Council  
- [ ] Activity is for training, equipment, participation in national/international competitions, exposure trips, or sports science support  
- [ ] Activity is not commercial or profit-oriented  
- [ ] Sport discipline is given priority if it is Olympic, Asian Games, or Commonwealth Games (though not mandatory)  
- [ ] Applicant is based in India (Pan-India scope)  
- [ ] No fixed turnover, employee count, or age of business thresholds apply (scheme is based on merit and recognition)  

> **Note**: Eligibility is based on recognition and recommendation, not financial or operational thresholds. Verify recognition status with the Ministry or federation.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- A formal Detailed Project Report (DPR) is **not explicitly required**, but a **detailed training/competition plan** and **itemized budget estimate** are mandatory.  
- If the nodal officer requests a more comprehensive plan, include:  
  - Executive summary  
  - Objectives and expected outcomes  
  - Activity schedule (timeline)  
  - Budget breakdown (equipment, travel, coaching, etc.)  
  - Monitoring and evaluation plan  
  - Sustainability statement (if applicable)  
- **Verify with nodal officer** whether a full DPR is needed for high-value requests.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
- ✅ Download the official NSDF application form from the Ministry’s portal or obtain it from your National Sports Federation  
- ✅ Fill all fields in block letters; do not leave any mandatory field blank  
- ✅ Ensure the applicant name matches exactly across all documents (PAN, bank, recommendation letter)  
- ✅ Clearly state the sport discipline, level of competition (national/international), and specific purpose of funds  
- ✅ Attach the recommendation letter as Annexure 1; training plan as Annexure 2; budget as Annexure 3; past performance as Annexure 4  
- ✅ Declare that the activity is non-commercial and not for profit-oriented purposes  
- ✅ Confirm that the applicant is recognized by the Ministry or federation  
- ✅ Double-check bank details (account number, IFSC, branch) for fund transfer  
- ✅ Save a filled copy before submission; do not submit handwritten forms  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All sections of the application form are completed  
- [ ] Recommendation letter is attached and valid (signed, stamped, on letterhead)  
- [ ] Training/competition plan is detailed and realistic  
- [ ] Budget estimate is itemized, justified, and matches the amount requested  
- [ ] Past performance records are relevant and verifiable  
- [ ] Bank details are correct and match applicant name  
- [ ] Declarations are signed and dated  
- [ ] No commercial or profit-making activity is indicated  
- [ ] All documents are in PDF, clearly named (e.g., “Annexure_1_Recommendation_Letter.pdf”)

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application **through your National Sports Federation or State Sports Council** (as per process)  
- Obtain a submission receipt or diary number from the federation/council  
- Take a screenshot of the submitted application (if online) or photocopy of hard copy  
- Store the reference number provided by the federation/council  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days after submission to check status  
- If forwarded to Ministry, track the application via the NSDF Council review process
