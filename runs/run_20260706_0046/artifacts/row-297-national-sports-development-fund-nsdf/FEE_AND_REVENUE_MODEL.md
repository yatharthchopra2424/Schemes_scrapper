## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual grant amount disbursed under NSDF, which varies based on merit, sport, competition level, and fund availability — ensuring clients pay only for realized value.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Retainers**: Retainers reflect the effort required to navigate NSDF’s multi-step approval process (federation recommendation → Ministry review → NSDF Council approval) and the uncertainty of fund availability.
3. **No Upfront Guarantees**: Given that NSDF assistance is subject to fund availability and prioritization of Olympic/Asian/Commonwealth Games disciplines, success fees are contingent on actual disbursement.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The NSDF operates on a rolling basis with no fixed upper limit on grants, but processing involves multiple review stages and reliance on federation recommendations. This creates moderate revenue lag risk, which is mitigated through retainer coverage during the 3–6 month approval cycle.
