## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping athletes, coaches, and sports institutions access government funding for training, equipment, and competition participation. The National Sports Development Fund (NSDF) provides financial support based on merit and need—no fixed upper limit—for those recognised by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports or National Sports Federations. I’d like to understand your current goals in sports development to see if this could be a fit.

### Discovery Questions
- Are you an individual athlete, coach, or representing a sports institution recognised by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports or a National Sports Federation?
- Has your application been or will it be recommended by your National Sports Federation or State Sports Council?
- What specific sport discipline are you involved in, and is it an Olympic, Asian Games, or Commonwealth Games priority discipline?
- Do you have a clear training or competition plan (national/international) for which you seek financial support?
- What are your estimated costs for equipment, travel, or sports science support needed?
- Do you have past performance records (e.g., competition results, rankings) available to support your application?
- Is your proposed activity purely for sports development, or does it involve any commercial or profit-oriented elements?
- Are you seeking support for infrastructure, equipment, athlete training, coach upskilling, or exposure trips?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is an individual athlete, coach, sports person, or recognised sports institution
- [ ] Recognised by Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports or respective National Sports Federation
- [ ] Application will be/recommended by National Sports Federation or State Sports Council
- [ ] Sport discipline is Olympic, Asian Games, or Commonwealth Games priority (higher chance)
- [ ] Proposed use: training, equipment, competition participation, exposure trips, or sports science support
- [ ] Not for commercial or profit-oriented sports activities
- [ ] Has details of training/competition plan and budget estimate
- [ ] Has past performance records of athlete/coach/institution
- [ ] Has bank account details for fund transfer
- [ ] Willing to route application through federation/council (not direct)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re aligned with the NSDF’s focus on supporting recognised athletes and institutions for priority sports development. The National Sports Development Fund provides merit-based grants—with no fixed upper limit—for exactly the kind of training, equipment, and competition support you’re describing. Let me walk you through how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Athletes, coaches, and sports institutions often struggle to cover the high costs of training, equipment, and international competition participation—especially when relying on personal or limited institutional funds. This financial gap can hinder performance, limit exposure, and delay career progression, particularly in Olympic-priority disciplines where sustained investment is critical.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Sports Development Fund (NSDF), administered by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, provides merit-based financial grants to individual athletes, coaches, and recognised sports institutions. There is no fixed upper limit on assistance—amounts vary based on sport, competition level, and specific needs. Support covers training, equipment, national/international competition participation, exposure trips, and sports science support. Non-financial benefits include facilitation of access to training facilities and coordination with sports authorities.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are an athlete/coach/institution recognised by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports or a National Sports Federation  
- Your application will be recommended by your National Sports Federation or State Sports Council (a mandatory requirement)  
- Your sport is an Olympic, Asian Games, or Commonwealth Games discipline (which receives priority consideration)  
- You seek funding for training, equipment, or competition—eligible uses under the NSDF
