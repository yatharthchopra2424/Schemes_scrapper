### National Sports Development Fund (NSDF)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports
- **Portal / Application URL:** [https://yas.nic.in/schemes/national-sports-development-fund](https://yas.nic.in/schemes/national-sports-development-fund)
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Individual athletes, coaches, sports persons, and sports institutions
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Sports
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be recognized by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports or respective National Sports Federations; applications must be recommended by the concerned sports federation or state sports council

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Grants based on merit and fund availability; quantum varies by sport, competition level, and applicant needs; no fixed upper limit
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit after approval by NSDF Council
- Duration of benefit: One-time per approved application

#### Documents Required
1. Application form
2. Recommendation letter from National Sports Federation or State Sports Council
3. Details of training/competition plan
4. Budget estimate for the proposed activity
5. Past performance records of the athlete/coach/institution
6. Bank account details for fund transfer

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Submit application through the respective National Sports Federation or State Sports Council
2. Obtain recommendation from the federation/council
3. Forward the application to the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports
4. Application is reviewed by the NSDF Council
5. Upon approval, funds are disbursed to the beneficiary
