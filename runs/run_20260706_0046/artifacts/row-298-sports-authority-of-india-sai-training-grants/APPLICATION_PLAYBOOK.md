## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Beneficiary type: Individual athlete, coach, or sports institution  
- [ ] Athlete: Registered with Sports Authority of India (SAI) or recommended by National Sports Federation (NSF)  
- [ ] Coach: Possesses recognized qualifications (as per SAI/NSF standards)  
- [ ] Institution: SAI-accredited training center or recognized sports academy  
- [ ] Sport discipline: Priority given to Olympic, Paralympic, Asian Games, and Commonwealth Games disciplines (verify eligibility for other disciplines)  
- [ ] Training plan and budget estimate prepared  
- [ ] Bank account details available for fund transfer  
- [ ] Previous utilization certificates submitted (if applicable for renewal)  
- [ ] Application submitted to concerned SAI office (regional or national)  
- [ ] Rolling basis: No fixed deadline; applications accepted throughout the year based on training cycles and competition schedules

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A formal Detailed Project Report (DPR) is **not explicitly required**, but a **training plan and budget estimate** is mandatory as per scheme guidelines. This should include:  
- Objectives of the training program  
- Timeline (start/end dates, phases)  
- Itemized budget (equipment, coaching fees, travel, stipends, etc.)  
- Expected outcomes (performance targets, competition participation)  
- Monitoring and evaluation mechanism  

> **Note**: Verify with the concerned SAI office or nodal officer if additional project report formatting is required for specific grant categories.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use the **official application form prescribed by SAI** — do not substitute with generic templates  
2. Fill all fields in **block letters** to avoid misinterpretation  
3. Ensure **beneficiary type** (athlete/coach/institution) is clearly selected  
4. Attach **self-attested copies** of all documents; originals only if requested  
5. Enter **correct bank account details** — any mismatch will delay fund transfer  
6. Specify **sport discipline and level** (sub-junior/junior/senior) accurately  
7. Declare **no prior misuse of government funds** and confirm accuracy of information  
8. Sign and date the form; get it countersigned by institution head (if applicable)  
9. Retain a **photocopy of the submitted application** for records  
10. Confirm submission method (in-person/email) with the respective SAI office  

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts – Required Documents, Application Process

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify **approval/sanction letter** from SAI specifying sanctioned amount, duration, and installment schedule  
- [ ] Confirm **fund disbursement** in installments as per approved training plan and submission of utilization certificates  
- [ ] Ensure **utilization certificates (UCs)** are submitted after each installment as per SAI format  
- [ ] Maintain records of **expenditure invoices, bills, and vouchers** for audit purposes  
- [ ] Submit **progress reports** on training outcomes and competition participation as required  
- [ ] Verify that funds are used **only for approved purposes** (training, equipment, travel, coaching, stipends)  
- [ ] Prepare for **success fee invoicing** to client upon successful fund utilization and closure  
- [ ] Retain all documents (approval letter, UCs, progress reports) for minimum 5 years for compliance
