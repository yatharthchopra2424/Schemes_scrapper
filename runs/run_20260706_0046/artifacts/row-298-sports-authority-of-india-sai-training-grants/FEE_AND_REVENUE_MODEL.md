## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual grant amount awarded, ensuring clients pay only for realized benefits.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the rolling application basis and dependency on SAI expert committee approvals (with no fixed timelines), success fees reflect moderate uncertainty in approval timing and quantum determination.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainer structures are scaled to client capacity, with Starter/Growth tiers typically unsuitable due to the scheme’s focus on athletes, coaches, and institutions requiring structured training plans and NSF recommendations.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover document preparation, NSF liaison, and application submission, while success fees are contingent on fund disbursement.

*Note: The scheme’s processing time varies with training cycles and competition schedules, and grant amounts are determined case-by-case by SAI committees, introducing variability in both timing and value.*
