## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping athletes, coaches, and sports institutions access government funding for training and development. The Sports Authority of India (SAI) Training Grants provide financial support for training expenses, equipment, competition travel, and coaching fees — particularly for those in Olympic, Paralympic, Asian Games, and Commonwealth Games disciplines. I’d like to understand your current training setup and goals to see if this scheme could support your next phase of development.

### Discovery Questions
- What sport discipline do you or your athletes specialise in, and is it recognised under Olympic, Paralympic, Asian Games, or Commonwealth Games categories?
- Are you currently registered with the Sports Authority of India (SAI) or recommended by a National Sports Federation (NSF)?
- What is your current level of participation — sub-junior, junior, senior, or elite/international?
- Do you have a documented training plan and budget estimate for the upcoming season or competition cycle?
- Are you affiliated with an SAI-accredited training center or a recognised sports academy?
- Have you previously received government sports funding, and if so, did you submit utilisation certificates and progress reports?
- What are your primary training expenses — coaching fees, equipment, travel, or stipends — that you’re seeking support for?
- Are you able to provide proof of age, identity, and sports achievement certificates as part of the application?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Athletes, coaches, and sports institutions often struggle to cover the high costs of training, equipment, competition travel, and coaching — especially when aiming for national or international excellence. Without financial support, many talented individuals are unable to access consistent, high-quality training or participate in key developmental competitions.

**Scheme intro:**
The Sports Authority of India (SAI) Training Grants, administered by the Sports Authority of India under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, provide financial assistance for training expenses, sports equipment, competition travel, coaching fees, and stipends. The quantum of support varies based on athlete level, sport discipline, and training requirements, as determined by SAI’s expert committees. Funds are disbursed directly to beneficiaries or institutions after approval of training plans and expenditure proposals. Non-financial benefits include access to SAI facilities, expert coaching, sports science support, and exposure to national/international competitions.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are registered with SAI or recommended by a National Sports Federation (NSF)  
- Your sport discipline is prioritised by SAI (Olympic, Paralympic, Asian Games, or Commonwealth Games)  
- You are an athlete, coach, or sports institution at sub-junior, junior, or senior level  
- You have a training plan and budget estimate ready for submission
