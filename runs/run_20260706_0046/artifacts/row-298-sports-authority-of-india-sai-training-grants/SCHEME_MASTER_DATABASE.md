### Sports Authority of India (SAI) Training Grants
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Sports Authority of India (SAI), Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports
- **Portal / Application URL:** [https://www.sai.gov.in](https://www.sai.gov.in)
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Individual athletes, coaches, and sports institutions
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Sports
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Athletes must be registered with SAI or recommended by National Sports Federations (NSFs); coaches must possess recognized qualifications; institutions must be SAI-accredited training centers or recognized sports academies

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Varies based on athlete's level, sport discipline, and training requirements; determined by SAI's expert committees
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit to beneficiaries or institutions
- Duration of benefit: One-time (per approved training plan), released in installments

#### Documents Required
1. Application form (as prescribed by SAI)
2. Recommendation letter from National Sports Federation (NSF) or SAI regional center
3. Proof of age and identity (Aadhaar, birth certificate, etc.)
4. Sports achievement certificates and performance records
5. Training plan and budget estimate
6. Bank account details for fund transfer
7. Previous utilization certificates (if applicable)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the appropriate SAI training grant scheme based on athlete level and sport
2. Obtain recommendation from the respective National Sports Federation (NSF) or SAI regional center
3. Submit application along with required documents to the concerned SAI office
4. Application is reviewed by SAI's expert committee
5. Upon approval, sanction letter is issued
6. Funds are released in installments as per the approved training plan and utilization certificates
