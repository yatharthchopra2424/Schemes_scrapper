## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The Sports Authority of India (SAI) Training Grants scheme is a **grant-based** initiative implemented by the **Sports Authority of India (SAI)** under the **Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports**. It operates on a **rolling basis** with applications accepted throughout the year based on training cycles and competition schedules. The scheme provides financial and non-financial support to athletes, coaches, and sports institutions across India to promote sports excellence at sub-junior, junior, and senior levels.

### Objectives
The scheme aims to:
- Provide financial support for training and development of athletes  
- Assist in procurement of sports equipment and kit  
- Support participation in national and international competitions  
- Enhance coaching standards through training and development programs  
- Promote sports excellence at sub-junior, junior, and senior levels  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Beneficiary Type | Eligibility Criteria | Registration/Recognition Requirement |
|------------------|----------------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Individual Athletes** | Must be registered with SAI or recommended by National Sports Federations (NSFs) | Registration with SAI or NSF recommendation |
| **Coaches** | Must possess recognized qualifications | Qualification recognized by SAI or NSFs |
| **Sports Institutions** | Must be SAI-accredited training centers or recognized sports academies | SAI accreditation or recognition by NSFs |

**Priority Disciplines**: Olympic, Paralympic, Asian Games, and Commonwealth Games disciplines receive preferential consideration.

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Support Type | Details |
|--------------|---------|
| **Financial Assistance** | Covers training expenses, sports equipment, competition travel, coaching fees, and stipends. Quantum varies based on athlete level, sport discipline, and training requirements, determined by SAI's expert committees. |
| **Disbursement Method** | Funds released in installments directly to beneficiaries or institutions after approval of training plans and expenditure proposals, subject to submission of utilization certificates. |
| **Non-Financial Benefits** | Access to SAI facilities, expert coaching, sports science support, and exposure to national/international competitions. |

> **Key Financial Caveats**  
> - Grants are subject to availability of funds and SAI's annual budget allocation  
> - Support may be withdrawn for non-compliance with training plans or submission of false information  
> - Beneficiaries must submit utilization certificates and progress reports
