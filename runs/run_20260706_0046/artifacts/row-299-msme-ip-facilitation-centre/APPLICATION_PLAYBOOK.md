## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Business is registered as an MSME under the MSME Act, 2006  
- [ ] Valid Udyam Registration Certificate is available  
- [ ] Business operates in India  
- [ ] Business is engaged in manufacturing or service activities  
- [ ] Business has not availed similar IP facilitation support under this scheme in the last 3 years  
- [ ] Business is willing to adopt IP management practices and file for IP rights (patents, designs, trademarks, GI)  
- [ ] Business falls under the definition of Micro, Small, or Medium Enterprise as per investment in plant & machinery or equipment and annual turnover  

> **Note**: Specific thresholds for turnover, employee count, or age of business are not explicitly defined in the available evidence. Verify current eligibility parameters with the nodal office or official guidelines before proceeding.

- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- Bank statement (last 6 months)  
- Income Tax Return (ITR) of last 2 financial years

- Project proposal outlining IP needs and objectives (e.g., patent filing, design registration, trademark strategy)  
- Details of innovations, designs, or creations intended for IP protection  
- Declaration on letterhead stating no prior benefit availed under this scheme in the last 3 years  
- Brief profile of the business including products/services and manufacturing/service process  

### Document Quality Check

- File size for each document should not exceed 5 MB

- All documents must be in English or Hindi; if in regional language, attach certified translation  
- Signatures and stamps must be visible and valid

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is **not explicitly required** for this scheme based on available evidence. However, a structured project proposal outlining IP objectives, expected outcomes, and budget for facilitation services is required.  
If the nodal officer requests a DPR, prepare it with the following standard sections:  
- Executive Summary  
- Business Profile  
- IP Landscape Analysis  
- Proposed IP Protection Strategy (Patents/Designs/Trademarks/GI)  
- Implementation Plan & Timeline  
- Budget Utilization Plan for Facilitation Services  
- Expected Outcomes and Commercialization Roadmap  
> **Action**: Verify DPR requirement with the nodal officer during eligibility check.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application portal at [https://ipindia.gov.in/msme-ip](https://ipindia.gov.in/msme-ip) — verify functionality; if unavailable, contact nodal officer for alternate submission method (email/offline).  
2. Fill all fields in English using block letters or as per portal format.  
3. Ensure Udyam Registration Number is entered correctly and matches the certificate.  
4. Select the type of IP support sought (patent, design, trademark, GI) from the dropdown menu.  
5. Provide accurate business turnover and investment details as per latest audited financials.  
6. Upload documents in the specified order and format as per portal instructions.  
7. Review all entries before final submission; once submitted, edits may not be allowed.  
8. Save a draft periodically to avoid data loss due to session timeout.  
9. Do not leave mandatory fields blank — if a field is not applicable, enter “NA” or as instructed.  
10. Confirm that the authorized signatory has reviewed and agreed to the declaration before applying digital/physical signature.
