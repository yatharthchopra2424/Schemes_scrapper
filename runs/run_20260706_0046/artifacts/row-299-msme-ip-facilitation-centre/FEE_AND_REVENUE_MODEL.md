## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-aligned pricing**: Fees are structured as a percentage of the financial benefit secured, ensuring consultant incentives align with client outcomes.
2. **Risk-adjusted retainers**: Retainers reflect the effort required to navigate the scheme’s application process, which, based on evidence, involves IP facilitation services with moderate procedural complexity.
3. **Transparency**: All fee structures are disclosed upfront, with clear definitions of what constitutes a "success" (i.e., formal approval of IP facilitation support under the scheme).
4. **Scheme-specific note**: The MSME IP Facilitation Centre scheme has a **moderate risk profile** due to its reliance on government IP offices for processing; while the scheme is designed to reduce IP costs for MSMEs, evidence indicates variable processing times and dependency on regional IP facilitation centre efficiency, which may affect approval lag and success certainty.
