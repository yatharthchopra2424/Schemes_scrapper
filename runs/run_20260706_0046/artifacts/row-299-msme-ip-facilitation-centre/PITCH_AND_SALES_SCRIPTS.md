## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because I specialise in helping MSMEs protect their innovations through government-backed IP support. The MSME IP Facilitation Centre offers structured assistance with patent, trademark, and design filings—critical for businesses looking to safeguard their intellectual property without bearing the full cost. I’d like to understand your current IP activities to see if this scheme could support your growth.

### Discovery Questions
- When was your business incorporated, and are you registered as an MSME under the MSMED Act, 2006?  
- Have you filed any patents, trademarks, or industrial designs in the past 24 months?  
- Are you currently developing a product, process, or brand that you believe has innovative or unique elements?  
- What challenges have you faced in accessing IP protection due to cost, complexity, or lack of expertise?  
- Do you have in-house IP expertise, or do you rely on external agents for filings and prosecution?  
- Are you looking to expand into new markets where IP protection would be essential?  
- Have you previously applied for any government IP-related schemes or incentives?  
- What is your annual turnover, and how many employees do you currently have?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is registered as an MSME (Micro, Small, or Medium Enterprise) under the MSMED Act, 2006  
- [ ] Engaged in manufacturing or service sector  
- [ ] Has innovative product, process, or brand eligible for IP protection (patent, trademark, design)  
- [ ] Seeking support for IP filing, prosecution, or awareness  
- [ ] Not currently under litigation or dispute that would disqualify IP application  
- [ ] Willing to engage with facilitation centre for guidance and documentation  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your business is actively innovating and could benefit from formal IP protection—especially given the cost and complexity involved. The MSME IP Facilitation Centre is designed specifically for businesses like yours, offering subsidised support for patents, trademarks, and designs. Let me explain how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Problem framing:
Many MSMEs struggle to protect their intellectual property due to high filing costs, procedural complexity, and lack of awareness. This leaves innovations vulnerable to imitation and limits their ability to attract investment or expand into new markets—especially when competitors can freely copy unprotected ideas.

### Scheme intro:
The MSME IP Facilitation Centre, administered by the Office of the Controller General of Patents, Designs & Trademarks (CGPDTM) under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, provides facilitation and financial support for MSMEs seeking to file patents, trademarks, and industrial designs. Benefits include reduced official fees, expedited processing, and access to IP professionals for guidance on filing and prosecution.

### Why they qualify (personalised):
- You are a registered MSME under the MSMED Act, 2006  
- Your business operates in the manufacturing or services sector  
- You have developed or are developing an innovation eligible for patent, trademark, or design protection  
- You are seeking cost-effective ways to secure IP rights

If you fall within these thresholds, you’re eligible—size alone isn’t a barrier if you meet the MSME criteria. |
| "I don't have the documents ready" | The three most commonly missing documents are: proof of MSME registration (Udyam certificate), detailed description of the invention/brand/design with drawings or specimens, and proof of prior art search or novelty assessment.
