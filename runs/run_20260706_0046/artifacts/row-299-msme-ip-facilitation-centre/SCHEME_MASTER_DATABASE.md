### MSME IP Facilitation Centre
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Intellectual Property India (Office of the Controller General of Patents, Designs & Trade Marks)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://ipindia.gov.in/msme-ip
- **Current Status:** Closed
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: MSME (Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises)
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Not specified
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be a registered MSME under the MSMED Act, 2006

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Facilitation services for IP filing (patents, designs, trademarks, GI) including awareness, search, drafting, filing, and prosecution support
- Disbursement mode: Service-based support (not direct monetary disbursement)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing facilitation during engagement period

#### Documents Required
1. MSME Registration Certificate (Udyam Registration)
2. Proof of business address
3. Identity proof of authorized signatory
4. Details of invention/creation (for patent/design) or brand/logo (for trademark)
5. Prior art search report (if available)
6. Authorization letter for IP attorney/agent (if applicable)
7. Any additional documents as specified by the facilitation centre

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the official MSME IP Facilitation Centre portal at https://ipindia.gov.in/msme-ip
2. Confirm eligibility as a registered MSME
3. Fill out the online application form with enterprise and innovation details
4. Upload required documents including MSME certificate and technical details
5. Submit the application for preliminary screening
6. Await assignment of an IP facilitator or attorney
7. Engage with the assigned facilitator for prior art search, drafting, and filing support
8. Proceed with IP application filing through the facilitator’s assistance
9. Receive ongoing support during prosecution and registration process
10. Complete the facilitation process upon grant or abandonment of IP application
