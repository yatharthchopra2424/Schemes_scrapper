## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, documentation preparation, and application submission.
2. Success fee is contingent upon approval of financial aid under the scheme.
3. Fees are structured to align with client outcomes, ensuring payment only upon successful benefit realization.
4. Scheme-specific note: MMAPUY provides a tailored package of measures with a target of reaching a minimum economic threshold of ₹1 lakh initially and ₹1.80 lakh per annum subsequently. The scheme’s processing time is not explicitly stated in the evidence, but as an umbrella mission coordinating multiple welfare schemes, it may involve moderate to high coordination risk, affecting revenue lag assumptions.
