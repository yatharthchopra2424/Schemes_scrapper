# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because the Mukhya Mantri Antyodaya Parivar Utthan Yojana (MMAPUY) is Haryana’s flagship initiative to uplift the poorest of the poor families through tailored support like skill development, job placements, financial aid, and entrepreneurial support. I’d like to understand your family’s situation to see if this scheme could help you achieve sustainable livelihoods and reach the target income threshold of ₹1 lakh initially and ₹1.80 lakh per annum subsequently.

### Discovery Questions
1. Which district in Haryana do you currently reside in?  
2. How many members are in your household, and what are their primary sources of income?  
3. Has your family been identified as part of the "poorest of the poor" under any state welfare database or survey?  
4. What is your approximate annual household income currently?  
5. Are you or any family member currently enrolled in any skill development, wage employment, or self-employment programs?  
6. Do you face challenges in accessing stable employment or starting a small business due to lack of training, capital, or guidance?  
7. Are you interested in receiving a customized package of measures—such as training, job placement, or financial aid—to improve your livelihood?  
8. Would you be open to a free eligibility check to see if MMAPUY can tailor support for your family’s specific needs?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Family resides in Haryana  
- [ ] Family is identified as "poorest of the poor" in Haryana  
- [ ] Household income is below the threshold requiring upliftment (target: ₹1 lakh initially, ₹1.80 lakh per annum)  
- [ ] Family requires interventions like skill development, job placement, financial aid, or entrepreneurial support  
- [ ] Family is economically disadvantaged and seeks sustainable livelihood  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your family may benefit from MMAPUY’s tailored package of measures. Let me explain how this scheme can help you reach a sustainable income level through skill development, job placements, and financial support—all designed to uplift the poorest families in Haryana.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many economically disadvantaged families in Haryana struggle to break the cycle of poverty due to lack of access to skill training, job opportunities, financial resources, and entrepreneurial guidance, preventing them from achieving a dignified quality of life and sustainable livelihoods.

**Scheme intro:**
The Mukhya Mantri Antyodaya Parivar Utthan Yojana (MMAPUY), launched in July 2021 by the Department of Information Technology Electronics & Communication, Haryana, is an umbrella mission that identifies the poorest of the poor families and tailors a package of measures—including skill development, wage employment, self-employment, and financial aid—to ensure economic upliftment and help families reach a minimum economic threshold of ₹1 lakh initially and ₹1.80 lakh per annum subsequently.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your family resides in Haryana, meeting the geographic scope of the scheme  
- You’ve indicated financial challenges and interest in livelihood improvement, aligning with the target of economically disadvantaged families  
- MMAPUY tailors support per family, so if identified as "poorest of the poor," you’d receive a customized intervention plan  
- The scheme aims to raise income levels to ₹1.80 lakh per annum, directly addressing your stated need for sustainable income growth

## 3. Objection Handlers

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I've heard these schemes never get approved" | MMAPUY is actively implemented by the Haryana government with clear objectives to identify and uplift the poorest families. Since its launch in July 2021, it has been operationalized through district-level identification and tailored intervention plans—approval is based on verification of economic status, not a competitive application. |
| "How much do you charge?" | [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT] |
| "I'll try it myself first" | Common mistakes include: not being correctly identified in the "poorest of the poor" database, and failing to engage with the tailored package of measures (like skill training or job placement) that are assigned based on individual family assessment. |
| "We're too small / too big" | MMAPUY targets families, not businesses. Eligibility is based on being identified as one of the poorest of the poor families in Haryana, regardless of size—there are no revenue or employee thresholds; it’s purely economic and social deprivation-based. |
| "I don't have the documents ready" | The three most commonly needed documents are: proof of residence in Haryana, income certificate or BPL/AAY card, and family identification details (such as ration card or Aadhaar-linked family data) for verification as "poorest of the poor." |
| "We already work with a CA" | Great — we work alongside CAs. They handle your accounts; we handle the scheme strategy and applications. |
