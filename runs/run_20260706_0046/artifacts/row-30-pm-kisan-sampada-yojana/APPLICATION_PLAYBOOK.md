## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Business is registered as a legal entity (Company, LLP, Partnership Firm, Proprietorship, Cooperative Society, or Self-Help Group)  
- [ ] Annual turnover does not exceed ₹500 crore (as per MSME classification for eligibility under PM Kisan Sampada Yojana)  
- [ ] Employee count is within MSME limits (micro, small, or medium enterprise as per MSME Act, 2006)  
- [ ] Business operates in the food processing sector (including fruits & vegetables, meat & poultry, marine products, dairy, grain milling, consumer foods like bakery, snacks, beverages, etc.)  
- [ ] Unit has been in operation for at least 1 year (existing unit) OR is a new project proposed for setting up  
- [ ] Applicant has not availed similar subsidy for the same component from any other Central/State Government scheme  
- [ ] For infrastructure components (e.g., cold chain, packaging center), land is in possession (owned or leased for minimum 10 years)  
- [ ] Applicant possesses necessary statutory clearances (FSSAI license, pollution control board consent, fire NOC, etc.) relevant to food processing  

> **Sources**: PM Kisan Sampada Yojana guidelines (Ministry of Food Processing Industries), eligibility criteria for infrastructure development and individual units under components like Cold Chain, Value Addition, and Agro-Processing Clusters.

- Detailed Project Report (DPR) with technical feasibility, financial viability, and employment generation projections  
- Land documents (Sale Deed, Lease Agreement for minimum 10 years, or Allotment Letter)  
- Architectural Plan and Layout Approval from local authority  
- Term Loan Sanction Letter from Bank/Financial Institution (if availing loan)  
- NOC from Pollution Control Board (if applicable)  
- Fire Safety Certificate from concerned authority  
- FSSAI License (Central/State, as applicable)  
- Quotations from suppliers for plant and machinery (minimum 3)  
- Affidavit regarding non-availing of similar subsidy from other Central/State schemes

- [ ] Bank statements are stamped and signed by the bank authority

- [ ] Affidavits are notarized on non-judicial stamp paper of appropriate value

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Required**: Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is mandatory for all components under PM Kisan Sampada Yojana (e.g., Cold Chain, Value Addition, Infrastructure).  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Promoter’s Background and Experience  
- Project Location and Land Details  
- Product Mix and Process Flow Diagram  
- Machinery and Equipment Details (with quotations)  
- Utilities Requirement (Power, Water, Fuel)  
- Manpower Plan  
- Pollution Control and Waste Management  
- Marketing Strategy and Tie-ups  
- Financial Projections (5-year):  
- Term Loan Details and Equity Contribution  
- Break-Even Analysis, IRR, NPV  
- Employment Generation (Direct and Indirect)  
- Implementation Schedule (Gantt Chart)  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
- Annexures: Land documents, Quotations, Approvals, Auditor’s Certificate on project cost  

> **Note**: DPR must be certified by a Chartered Engineer and countersigned by the applicant. Financial projections must be validated by a Chartered Accountant.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official SAMPADA portal: https://mofpi.nic.in/sampada  
2. Register/create login using Udyam-linked mobile number and email  
3. Select “Apply Online” → Choose relevant component (e.g., Cold Chain, Value Addition, Agro-Processing Cluster)  
4. Fill applicant details exactly as per Udyam and PAN (no abbreviations)  
5. Enter project cost component-wise (land, building, plant & machinery, misc. fixed assets, margin money for working capital)  
6. Upload all documents in PDF format, ensuring file names are clear (e.g., “APLL_Udyam_Certificate.pdf”)  
7. Declare that no similar subsidy has been availed for the same project from any Central/State scheme  
8. Preview the entire application before final submission — no edits allowed after submission  
9. Submit only after receiving OTP verification on registered mobile/email  
10. Download and save the submission receipt with Application Reference Number (ARN)  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All fields in the application form are filled (no blanks or “NA” without justification)  
- [ ] Uploaded documents match the checklist and are legible  
- [ ] DPR is complete, signed, and certified  
- [ ] Financials in DPR match ITRs and bank statements  
- [ ] Declarations (non-duplication of subsidy, land possession, etc.) are signed  
- [ ] ARN will be generated upon submission — confirm portal shows “Application Submitted Successfully”  
- [ ] Application submitted before the closing date/time of the current EOI/RFP window
