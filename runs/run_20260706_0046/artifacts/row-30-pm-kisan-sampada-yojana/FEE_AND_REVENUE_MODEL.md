## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit delivered under PM Kisan Sampada Yojana, ensuring clients pay only for realized outcomes.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s moderate processing time and documented approvals for food processing units (evidenced by Ministry of Food Processing Industries), success fees reflect implementation complexity and fund disbursement timelines.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainer structures are designed to match client scale, with lower entry barriers for MSMEs and startups—the primary beneficiaries of PM Kisan Sampada Yojana’s credit-linked and grant components.
4. **Transparency & Predictability**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover initial eligibility assessment and DPR preparation, while success fees are contingent on final approval and fund release.
