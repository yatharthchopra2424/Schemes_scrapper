## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping food processing and agro-industrial businesses access government support for infrastructure and technology upgrades. The PM Kisan Sampada Yojana is designed specifically to strengthen the food processing value chain by supporting projects that reduce post-harvest losses and increase processing capacity. I’d like to understand your current operations to see if this scheme aligns with your growth plans.

### Discovery Questions
- What is the date of incorporation or commencement of your food processing or agro-industrial unit?
- Are you currently operating a food processing unit, or are you in the process of setting up a new one?
- What is the primary sector of your food processing activity (e.g., fruits & vegetables, dairy, meat & poultry, fisheries, grain milling, etc.)?
- What is the estimated project cost for your proposed food processing infrastructure or technology upgrade?
- Are you seeking support for setting up a new unit, expanding an existing one, or modernising current facilities?
- Do you have clear title or lease rights to the land where the project will be located?
- Are you applying as an individual entrepreneur, a partnership, a company, a cooperative, or an NGO?
- Have you already availed of any other central or state government subsidy for the same project components?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity is a food processing unit (including fruits & vegetables, dairy, meat & poultry, fisheries, grain milling, etc.)
- [ ] Unit is either new or existing (expansion/modernisation eligible)
- [ ] Applicant is eligible: individual entrepreneur, partnership, company, cooperative, NGO, or FPO
- [ ] Project involves setting up/expanding/modernising food processing infrastructure or preservation facilities
- [ ] Applicant has clear land title or lease rights for the project site
- [ ] Project cost falls within the scheme’s financial limits (as per guidelines)
- [ ] No duplication of central government subsidy for same project components
- [ ] Unit is engaged in value addition to agricultural produce

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your project may align well with the PM Kisan Sampada Yojana, which offers grant support for food processing infrastructure projects. Let me explain how this scheme works and what it could mean for your expansion or modernisation plans.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Problem framing:
Food processing businesses in India often face high capital costs for setting up or upgrading infrastructure, limited access to modern technology, and significant post-harvest losses due to inadequate processing and preservation facilities. These challenges hinder scalability, reduce competitiveness, and limit farmers’ access to value chains.

### Scheme intro:
The **PM Kisan Sampada Yojana** (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Sampada Yojana), administered by the **Ministry of Food Processing Industries**, provides **grant-in-aid** for setting up, expanding, or modernising food processing units. The scheme offers financial support ranging from **30% to 50% of project cost**, with a maximum grant of **₹5 crore to ₹10 crore** depending on the component (e.g., infrastructure, cold chain, agro-processing clusters), subject to guidelines.

### Why they qualify (personalised):
- You are engaged in **food processing** (e.g., fruits & vegetables, dairy, meat, fisheries, or grain milling), which is a core eligibility criterion.
- Your project involves **setting up, expanding, or modernising** a food processing unit, which falls under the scheme’s supported activities.
- You have **clear land title or lease rights** for the project site, a mandatory requirement for approval.
- Your entity type (individual, partnership, company, cooperative, NGO, or FPO) is **explicitly eligible** under the scheme.
