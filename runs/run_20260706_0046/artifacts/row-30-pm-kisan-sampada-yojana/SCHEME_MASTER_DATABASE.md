### PM Kisan Sampada Yojana
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Food Processing Industries
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://mofpi.nic.in/pmkisan-sampada-yojana
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Rolling

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Food processing units, agro-processing clusters, cold chain infrastructure, packaging centers, value addition centers, and irradiation facilities
- Age of business: Not specified in evidence
- Sector: Food processing and agro-processing
- Revenue cap: Not specified in evidence
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: Must be engaged in setting up or expanding food processing infrastructure; proposals must align with scheme objectives of reducing post-harvest losses and increasing processing levels

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Grants-in-aid ranging from 35% to 75% of project cost (general areas: 35%; difficult areas: 50%; NE states/hilly areas/island territories: 75%)
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement
- Duration of benefit: One-time grant upon project completion and verification

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
2. Land documents (ownership/lease agreement)
3. Cost estimates from chartered engineer
4. Bank appraisal note sanctioning term loan
5. Certificate of incorporation/registration
6. PAN and GST registration
7. Audited financial statements of last 3 years (if applicable)
8. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from Pollution Control Board
9. NOC from Fire Department
10. Affidavit regarding no default with any bank/financial institution
11. Undertaking to complete project within stipulated timeline
12. Environmental clearance (if applicable)
13. Proof of contribution of promoter's equity
14. Quotations for major plant and machinery

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Submit online application through the Ministry of Food Processing Industries portal
2. Upload Detailed Project Report (DPR) and all supporting documents
3. Application undergoes preliminary scrutiny by the Ministry
4. Project is appraised by Technical Committee (if required)
5. Application placed before Inter-Ministerial Approval Committee (IMAC) for approval
6. Upon approval, sanction letter is issued
7. Implement project as per approved DPR and timeline
8. Submit claims for reimbursement with expenditure documents and chartered engineer's certificate
9. Ministry verifies claims and releases grant-in-aid in installments
10. Submit project completion report and final utilization certificate
