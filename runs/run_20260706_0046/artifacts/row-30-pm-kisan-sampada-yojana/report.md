### Objectives
The PM Kisan Sampada Yojana (PMKSY) is a central sector scheme launched by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI) with the following objectives:
- To create modern infrastructure for food processing along the value chain from farm to market.
- To reduce post-harvest losses of horticultural and non-horticultural produce.
- To provide better prices to farmers and create employment opportunities, especially in rural areas.
- To boost the export of processed foods and increase the share of processed food in the agricultural basket.
- To ensure availability of safe and hygienic processed food to consumers.

> **Important Notes on Financial Support:**
> - Grants are released in installments tied to verification of milestones (e.g., land acquisition, civil work completion, plant installation).
> - The balance of the project cost must be funded through term loans from banks/financial institutions and promoter's equity.
> - Projects must be completed within 18-24 months from the date of approval, depending on the component.
> - All assisted units must remain operational for a minimum of 3 years post-completion; failure may lead to clawback of grant.
> - Difficult areas include: North Eastern States (including Sikkim), Himalayan States (Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh), ITDP (Integrated Tribal Development Programme) areas, and LWE (Left Wing Extremism) affected districts.

> **Critical Warnings:**
> - Applications must be submitted strictly through the online SAMPADA portal; offline applications are not accepted.
> - The Detailed Project Report (DPR) must be prepared by a certified consultant or chartered engineer; self-prepared DPRs often lead to rejection on technical grounds.
> - Bank loan appraisal is mandatory before submitting the application; the loan must be sanctioned or in advanced stages of processing.
> - Promoter's contribution must be brought in upfront; grant is released only after verification of promoter's equity contribution.
> - Any change in project scope, location, or promoters after approval requires prior permission from MoFPI; unauthorized changes lead to cancellation of approval.
