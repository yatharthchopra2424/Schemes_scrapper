## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Client is a patent office (national or regional intellectual property office)
- [ ] Client has initiated formal discussions with the TKDL Unit regarding access
- [ ] Client is prepared to negotiate and sign a formal access agreement with TKDL
- [ ] Client understands that access is granted only upon execution of a formal access agreement
- [ ] Client acknowledges that TKDL does not provide financial support or direct benefits
- [ ] Client confirms intended use is for prior art search in patent examination related to traditional knowledge
- [ ] Client is not an individual, researcher, indigenous community, or government body seeking direct beneficiary support (note: while these groups may benefit indirectly, they are not eligible for direct access)
- [ ] Client operates within the jurisdiction of a patent office that has not yet signed an access agreement (if applicable for new access)

> **Note**: TKDL is not a beneficiary-oriented scheme. Eligibility is limited to patent offices that have signed formal access agreements. Access is granted to authorized patent examiners within those offices. Individuals, researchers, and communities do not receive direct access or financial support.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- **DPR Required?** No formal Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required for TKDL access.  
- **Note**: The access process is agreement-based, not project-funded. However, a **Statement of Purpose** (scheme-specific document) is required, which should include:  
  - Background of the patent office and its examination workload  
  - Rationale for seeking TKDL access (e.g., volume of traditional knowledge-related patent applications)  
  - Intended use of TKDL in prior art search and examination  
  - Commitment to data confidentiality and proper use  
  - Expected outcomes (e.g., reduction in erroneous patent grants)  
- **Action**: Verify with the TKDL Unit if any additional project-like documentation is preferred during negotiations.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
Since TKDL access is granted via a formal access agreement (not a standard application form), the following protocol applies to agreement drafting and internal preparation:  

1. **Confirm Negotiation Channel**: All communication must be directed to the TKDL Unit at CSIR (tkdl@csir.res.in) or through the AYUSH Ministry’s designated liaison.  
2. **Use Official Templates**: Request the TKDL Unit’s standard access agreement template to ensure compliance with their legal and security frameworks.  
3. **Define Scope Clearly**: Specify the number of authorized users, access duration, permitted purposes (prior art search only), and data usage restrictions.  
4. **Address Confidentiality**: Include robust clauses on data protection, non-downloading, non-sharing, and use strictly for patent examination.  
5. **Align with International Norms**: Ensure the agreement reflects TKDL’s existing arrangements with EPO, USPTO, JPO, UKIPO, and CIPO for consistency.  
6. **Internal Legal Review**: Have your office’s legal team review the agreement for liability, data compliance, and sovereign immunity considerations (if applicable).  
7. **Designate Point of Contact**: Clearly name the coordinator responsible for managing access, user credentials, and audit trails.  
8. **Plan for Training**: Outline how authorized examiners will be trained on TKDL search protocols and ethical use.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents are complete and signed  
- [ ] Statement of Purpose clearly aligns with TKDL’s objectives (preventing erroneous patents, prior art search)  
- [ ] List of authorized examiners is verified and limited to bona fide patent examiners  
- [ ] Information security undertaking includes confidentiality, no-download, and use-restriction clauses  
- [ ] Draft access agreement has been reviewed by legal and IP teams  
- [ ] All documents are correctly named, formatted, and free of errors  
- [ ] Internal approval obtained from head of patent office or designated authority  
- [ ] Confirmation that no financial expectation is held from TKDL (manages client expectations)
