## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with client benefit realization  
2. Transparent fee structures with no hidden charges  
3. Risk-sharing model where success fees are contingent on outcomes  
4. Customized solutions for different client segments based on scale and needs  

**Scheme-specific note:** The Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL) scheme does not provide direct financial benefits or grants to individuals or entities. As a defensive prior art database managed by CSIR and AYUSH, its value lies in preventing erroneous patent grants and supporting legal opposition rather than disbursing funds. Therefore, traditional success-fee models based on grant amounts are not applicable. Consulting engagements would focus on access facilitation, agreement negotiation, and utilization support, with fees structured around service delivery rather than financial outcomes.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description:** Retainer for Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL) access facilitation services, including advisory on access agreement prerequisites, documentation support, and liaison with CSIR/AYUSH units.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description:** Success fee for successful negotiation and execution of TKDL access agreement with the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) and Department of Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homoeopathy (AYUSH), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.  
**Note:** As TKDL does not provide financial grants, success fees are tied to service milestones (e.g., agreement signing) rather than financial benefit realization.
