## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
India’s traditional knowledge, especially from Ayurveda, Yoga, Unani, Siddha, and Homoeopathy, is vulnerable to erroneous patent grants due to lack of accessible prior art in formats understandable to international patent examiners. This leads to biopiracy, where commercial entities patent knowledge that has been in public use for centuries, undermining indigenous rights and innovation incentives.

**Scheme intro:**
The Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL) is a pioneering initiative by the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) and the Department of Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homoeopathy (AYUSH), under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. It is a secure, digitised repository that transcribes traditional knowledge from ancient Indian texts into multiple languages (including English, Japanese, Spanish, German, and French) to serve as prior art for patent examiners. Access is granted to patent offices that have signed formal access agreements — including the EPO, USPTO, JPO, UKIPO, and CIPO — enabling them to reject patent applications lacking novelty or inventive step based on TKDL evidence.
