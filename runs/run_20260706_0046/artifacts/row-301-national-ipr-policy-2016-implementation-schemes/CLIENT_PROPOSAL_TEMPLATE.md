## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified National IPR Policy 2016 Implementation Schemes — a recognition scheme administered by Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is primarily through fee concessions rather than direct grants or loans, with startups and small entities receiving reduced official fees for patent filings (e.g., ₹800 for natural persons/startups/small entities vs. ₹4000 for others under Form 1).

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment based on applicant category (natural person, startup, small entity, etc.)  
2. Guidance on determining the type of IP protection required (patent, design, trademark, GI, copyright)  
3. Document preparation support for required forms (e.g., Form 1, Form 2, Form 3, Form 5, etc.)  
4. Assistance with application filing via the e-filing portal at https://ipindia.gov.in  
5. Tracking application status through the online portal  
6. Support in responding to examination reports or objections within stipulated time  
7. Coordination for hearings if scheduled and final registration/certification
