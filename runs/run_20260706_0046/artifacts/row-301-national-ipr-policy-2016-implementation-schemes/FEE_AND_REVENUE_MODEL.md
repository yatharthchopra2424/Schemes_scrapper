## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, document preparation, and application filing support.
2. Success fee is contingent upon successful registration or grant of IP rights, aligning consultant incentives with client outcomes.
3. Tiered pricing reflects the scheme’s focus on startups, MSMEs, and individual innovators, with lower fees for smaller entities.
4. Scheme-specific note: The National IPR Policy 2016 Implementation Schemes offer procedural support and fee concessions rather than direct financial grants. Success is measured by IP registration, which typically takes 12–24 months for patents and 6–12 months for trademarks/designs based on evidence from IP India workflows. This creates a moderate revenue lag, but high success rates for properly filed applications (evidenced by steady grant volumes in crawled data) reduce risk.

**Description:** Success fee for National IPR Policy 2016 Implementation Schemes, payable upon successful registration or grant of intellectual property rights (patent, design, trademark, GI, or copyright) by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
