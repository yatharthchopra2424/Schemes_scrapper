# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because I specialize in helping startups, MSMEs, entrepreneurs, individual inventors, and educational institutions protect their innovations through India’s National IPR Policy 2016 Implementation Schemes. These schemes reduce the cost of securing patents, trademarks, designs, geographical indications, and copyrights—critical for safeguarding your IP and enabling commercialization. Before we explore whether this fits your needs, I’d like to understand your current IP situation to see if we can add value.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of intellectual property are you looking to protect—patent, trademark, design, geographical indication, or copyright?
2. Have you already incorporated your startup or registered your business entity?
3. Are you classified as a startup, small entity, or educational institution under DPIIT definitions?
4. What is the current stage of your innovation—idea, prototype, or ready for market?
5. Have you conducted any prior art or trademark search to assess novelty?
6. Are you planning to file IP domestically or also seek international protection via PCT or Madrid Protocol?
7. What is your budget for IP filing and prosecution costs?
8. Do you have an in-house IP team or rely on external counsel for IP matters?

    [ ] Seeking protection for patent, design, trademark, geographical indication, or copyright

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Startups and innovators often delay or skip IP protection due to high official fees and procedural complexity, leaving their inventions vulnerable to copying and undermining investor confidence and valuation.

**Scheme intro:**
The National IPR Policy 2016 Implementation Schemes, administered by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, provide fee concessions for patents, designs, trademarks, geographical indications, and copyrights. Startups and small entities pay reduced official fees—e.g., ₹800 for natural persons/startups/small entities versus ₹4,000 for others under Form 1 for patents—with similar concessions across other IP types. Applications are filed year-round via the portal at https://ipindia.gov.in.
