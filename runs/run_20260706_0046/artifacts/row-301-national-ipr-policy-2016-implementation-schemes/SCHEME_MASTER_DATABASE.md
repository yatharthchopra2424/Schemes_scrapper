### National IPR Policy 2016 Implementation Schemes
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://ipindia.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Natural persons, startups, small entities, educational institutions, and other legal entities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Not specified
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Eligibility varies by IP type; specific fee concessions available for startups and small entities as per the First Schedule of the Patents Rules 2003 and analogous provisions in other IP statutes

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Fee concessions (e.g., ₹800 for natural persons/startups/small entities vs. ₹4000 for others under Form 1 for patents; similar concessions across other IP types)
- Disbursement mode: Procedural and cost-reduction based (fee concessions)
- Duration of benefit: One-time per application

#### Documents Required
1. Form 1 (Application for Grant of Patent)
2. Form 2 (Provisional/Complete Specification)
3. Form 3 (Statement and Undertaking under Section 8)
4. Form 5 (Declaration of Inventorship)
5. Power of Attorney (if applicable)
6. Priority documents (if claiming priority)
7. Sequence listing (if applicable)
8. Drawings (for patents and designs)
9. Statement of Case (for Geographical Indications)
10. Affidavit (for Geographical Indications)
11. Geographical maps (for Geographical Indications)
12. Trademark representation (for trademarks)
13. Description of goods/services (for trademarks)
14. User affidavit (for trademarks, if applicable)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Determine the type of IP protection required (patent, design, trademark, GI, copyright)
2. Confirm eligibility and applicable fee structure based on applicant category (natural person, startup, small entity, etc.)
3. Prepare required documents including forms, specifications, representations, and affidavits as per guidelines
4. File application via the e-filing portal or physical submission with prescribed fees
5. Track application status through the online portal
6. Respond to examination reports or objections within stipulated time
7. Attend hearings if scheduled
8. Upon compliance, receive registration certificate and publication in the respective journal
