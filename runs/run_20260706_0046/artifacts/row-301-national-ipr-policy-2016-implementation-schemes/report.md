# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **National IPR Policy 2016 Implementation Schemes** (Scheme ID: row-301) is a recognition-type scheme administered by the **Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT)**, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, with pan-India geographic scope. The scheme operates on a **rolling basis** with no fixed deadline—applications are accepted year-round via the official portal: **https://ipindia.gov.in**. Last updated in 2026, the scheme focuses on intellectual property rights (IPR) awareness, creation, filing, prosecution, and commercialization through procedural guidance and fee concessions rather than direct financial grants.

### Objectives
- Promote IPR awareness and creation across sectors  
- Facilitate filing and prosecution of IP applications  
- Provide procedural and fee-related guidance for IP protection  
- Support innovation through IP asset creation and commercialization  
- Strengthen institutional capacity for IP administration  

### Eligibility Matrix
Eligibility varies by IP type but generally includes natural persons, startups, small entities, educational institutions, and other legal entities applying for patents, designs, trademarks, geographical indications (GIs), or copyrights under respective Acts and Rules. Specific fee concessions are available for startups and small entities as per the First Schedule of the Patents Rules 2003 and analogous provisions in other IP statutes.

| **Applicant Category**       | **Eligible For**                                                                 | **Fee Concession Basis**                                                                 |
|------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Natural Person               | Patents, Designs, Trademarks, GIs, Copyrights                                    | First Schedule of Patents Rules 2003; analogous in Designs, Trademarks, GI Rules       |
| Startup                      | Patents, Designs, Trademarks, GIs, Copyrights                                    | DPIIT-recognized startups; Form 28 submission required                                 |
| Small Entity                 | Patents, Designs, Trademarks, GIs, Copyrights                                    | Investment in plant & machinery < ₹10 crore; turnover < ₹50 crore                      |
| Educational Institution      | Patents, Designs, Trademarks, GIs, Copyrights                                    | Recognized universities, colleges, research institutes                                 |
| Other Legal Entities         | Patents, Designs, Trademarks, GIs, Copyrights                                    | Companies, LLPs, partnerships, societies, trusts                                       |
| Foreign Applicants           | Patents, Designs, Trademarks, GIs (via PCT/Madrid)                               | Must appoint Indian agent; provide address for service in India                        |

> **Key Caveat**: Fee concessions for startups and small entities require proper declaration and eligibility verification. Applications may be rejected for incomplete forms, insufficient fees, or lack of novelty/originality.

### Application Process
The application process varies slightly by IP type but follows a standardized workflow: determine IP type → confirm eligibility and fee structure → prepare documents → file via e-filing or physical submission → track status → respond to examination reports → attend hearings → receive certificate and publication.

#### Mermaid.js Flowchart: Unified IP Application Workflow
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Start: Determine IP Type<br/>(Patent/Design/Trademark/GI/Copyright)] --> B{Confirm Eligibility<br/>& Fee Structure}
    B -->|Natural Person/Startup/<br/>Small Entity/Edu Inst| C[Apply for Fee Concession<br/>e.g., Form 28 for Patents]
    B -->|Other Entity| D[Pay Standard Fee]
    C --> E[Prepare Required Documents<br/>See Required Documents Section]
    D --> E
    E --> F[File Application<br/>Via e-Filing Portal<br/>or Physical Submission]
    F --> G[Track Application Status<br/>Online Portal]
    G --> H{Examination Report/Issued?}
    H -->|Yes| I[Respond to Objections<br/>Within Stipulated Time]
    H -->|No| J[Proceed to Registration]
    I --> J
    J --> K{Attend Hearing?}
    K -->|Yes| L[Attend Scheduled Hearing]
    K -->|No| M[Receive Registration Certificate<br/>& Publication in Journal]
    L --> M
    M --> N[End: IP Rights Granted]
```

#### Step-by-Step Breakdown
1. **Determine IP Type**: Identify whether protection is needed for patent, design, trademark, geographical indication, or copyright.  
2. **Confirm Eligibility & Fee Structure**: Verify applicant category (natural person, startup, small entity, etc.) and applicable fee concessions.  
3. **Prepare Documents**: Compile all required forms, specifications, representations, affidavits, and supporting evidence as per IP type.  
4. **File Application**: Submit via the **e-filing portal (https://ipindia.gov.in)** or physically with prescribed fees.  
5. **Track Status**: Monitor application progress through the online portal using application number.  
6. **Respond to Examination**: Address examination reports or objections within stipulated time (typically 6–12 months).  
7. **Attend Hearings**: Participate in hearings if scheduled by the Controller.  
8. **Registration & Publication**: Upon compliance, receive registration certificate and publication in the respective journal (Patent Office Journal, Design Journal, Trade Marks Journal, Geographical Indication Journal, Copyright Journal).

**Specific Scenarios**:
