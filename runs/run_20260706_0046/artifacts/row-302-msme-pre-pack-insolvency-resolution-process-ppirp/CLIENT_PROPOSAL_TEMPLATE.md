## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified MSME Pre-Pack Insolvency Resolution Process (PPIRP) — a resolution scheme administered by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is structured as part of the resolution plan, where the successful resolution applicant infuses funds to settle claims of operational and financial creditors, pay CIRP costs, and provide performance guarantees (typically 10% of the resolution plan value). The quantum of financial support varies case-by-case based on the resolution plan approved by the Committee of Creditors and the Adjudicating Authority.

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment against MSME classification and creditor consent requirements.
2. Preparation of resolution plan, information memorandum, and evaluation matrix.
3. Obtaining declaration under Section 29A of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016.
4. Securing proof of consent of financial creditors (not less than 66% by value).
5. Compilation of required documents: certificate of incorporation, latest audited financial statements, details of claims, valuation reports.
6. Portal registration and application filing on https://ibbi.gov.in/intimation-applications/apply-iaaa.
7. Liaison with Adjudicating Authority (NCLT) for application examination and approval.

2. Point of contact for coordination and queries.
3. 2-day response SLA for document submission and feedback.
