## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, document preparation, and application filing.
2. Success fee is contingent on successful PPIRP approval and implementation of the resolution plan.
3. Fees are aligned with the scheme’s benefit structure, which involves private infusion of funds by the resolution applicant rather than direct government grants.
4. Scheme-specific note: PPIRP has a defined timeline (90 days, extendable to 150 days) and high success likelihood when eligibility and creditor consent (≥66% by value) are met, reducing execution risk for consultants.
