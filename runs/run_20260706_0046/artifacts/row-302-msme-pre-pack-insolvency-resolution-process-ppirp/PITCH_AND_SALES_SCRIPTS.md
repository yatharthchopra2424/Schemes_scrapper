## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) navigate insolvency resolution through faster, cost-effective processes like the MSME Pre-Pack Insolvency Resolution Process (PPIRP). Many MSMEs facing financial distress struggle with prolonged Corporate Insolvency Resolution Processes (CIRP) that drain value and disrupt operations. I’d like to understand your situation to see if PPIRP could be a viable path for your business.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your company incorporated, and are you currently classified as an MSME under the MSMED Act, 2006?
2. Are you facing insolvency proceedings, or is there a risk of insolvency being initiated against your company?
3. Have you or your financial creditors discussed a resolution plan to address outstanding debts?
4. Do you have the consent of at least 66% of your financial creditors by value to proceed with a resolution plan?
5. Is there any pending insolvency application under Sections 7, 9, or 10 of the IBC, 2016, or has a liquidation order been passed against your company?
6. Has your company undergone a PPIRP in the last three years?
7. Are you looking to retain management control if a resolution plan is approved?
8. Do you have access to the necessary documents such as your certificate of incorporation, latest audited financial statements, and details of creditor claims?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Corporate debtor is classified as an MSME under the MSMED Act, 2006
- [ ] Consent of not less than 66% of financial creditors by value obtained
- [ ] No pending application for insolvency resolution under Section 7, 9, or 10 of IBC, 2016
- [ ] No order for liquidation has been passed
- [ ] No PPIRP undergone in the last three years
- [ ] Resolution plan is feasible and viable
- [ ] Successful resolution applicant is eligible under Section 29A of IBC, 2016
- [ ] Resolution plan complies with Section 30(2) of IBC, including payment of CIRP costs and operational creditor dues in priority

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your situation may align with the eligibility criteria for the MSME Pre-Pack Insolvency Resolution Process (PPIRP). This scheme enables faster resolution—typically within 90 days—compared to the standard CIRP, reducing procedural costs and helping preserve business continuity. Would you like me to explain how PPIRP works and whether it could fit your needs?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
MSMEs in financial distress often face prolonged Corporate Debtor faces:**
MSMEs undergoing insolvency often face delays of 180–330 days under the standard Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP), leading to high procedural costs, erosion of going concern value, disruption to operations, and risk of liquidation. The public invitation of resolution plans and e-auctions further increase costs and uncertainty, making it difficult for existing management to retain control or preserve employment.

**Scheme intro:**
The MSME Pre-Pack Insolvency Resolution Process (PPIRP), administered by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI), is a faster insolvency resolution mechanism designed specifically for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises. It allows the corporate debtor to initiate resolution with creditor consent, avoiding public invitation of plans. The process must be completed within 90 days (extendable to 150 days), significantly reducing time and cost. Financial support comes from private infusion by the resolution applicant as part of the resolution plan, which may include fresh equity, quasi-equity, or internal accruals, and typically includes a performance guarantee of 10% of the resolution plan value.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your company is classified as an MSME under the MSMED Act, 2006  
- You have or can obtain consent from not less than 66% of financial creditors by value  
- There is no pending insolvency application under Sections 7, 9, or 10 of IBC, 2016, and no liquidation order has been passed  
- Your company has not undergone PPIRP in the last three years
