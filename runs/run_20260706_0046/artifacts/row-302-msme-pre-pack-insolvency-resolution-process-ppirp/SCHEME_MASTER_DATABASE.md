### MSME Pre-Pack Insolvency Resolution Process (PPIRP)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://ibbi.gov.in/intimation-applications/apply-iaaa
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Corporate debtor classified as Micro, Small, or Medium Enterprise as per the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act, 2006
- Age of business: Not specified in evidence
- Sector: Not specified in evidence
- Revenue cap: Not specified in evidence
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: 
  - Consent of not less than sixty-six percent of the financial creditors by value
  - No pending application for insolvency resolution under section 7, 9 or 10 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016
  - No order for liquidation has been passed
  - The corporate debtor must not have undergone a PPIRP in the last three years

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: No direct financial support from government; financial support structured as part of resolution plan where successful resolution applicant infuses funds (quantum varies case-by-case based on resolution plan approved by Committee of Creditors and Adjudicating Authority)
- Disbursement mode: Private infusion of funds by resolution applicant (fresh equity, quasi-equity, or internal accruals)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (per resolution process)

#### Documents Required
1. Resolution Plan
2. Information Memorandum
3. Evaluation Matrix
4. Declaration under Section 29A of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016
5. Proof of consent of financial creditors (not less than 66% by value)
6. Certificate of incorporation of the corporate debtor
7. Latest audited financial statements
8. Details of claims of financial and operational creditors
9. Valuation reports (if any)
10. Performance guarantee (as per regulation 36B(4A) of CIRP Regulations)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. The corporate debtor, in consultation with its financial creditors, prepares a resolution plan.
2. The resolution plan is approved by the Committee of Creditors (CoC) with a vote of not less than sixty-six percent of financial creditors by value.
3. The corporate debtor files an application for initiation of PPIRP before the Adjudicating Authority (National Company Law Tribunal) under section 30(6) of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016.
4. The application is accompanied by the resolution plan, information memorandum, evaluation matrix, and a declaration under section 29A of the Code.
5. The Adjudicating Authority examines the application for compliance with section 30(2) and 29A of the Code.
6. If satisfied, the Adjudicating Authority approves the resolution plan, making it binding on all stakeholders.
7. The successful resolution applicant then implements the plan, including infusion of funds and payment of performance guarantee.
