## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are tied to measurable outcomes such as time saved, approvals secured, or compliance burdens reduced via the BizReform App.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s facilitative nature (no direct financial grant) and dependency on departmental approvals, success fees reflect implementation risk and processing variability.
3. **Tiered Access**: Pricing scales with client complexity — startups and MSMEs benefit from standardized packages, while enterprises receive custom solutions for multi-state integrations.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The BizReform App does not provide direct financial support; therefore, revenue models must emphasize efficiency gains, cost avoidance, and process acceleration rather than grant-based incentives. Success is contingent on user engagement and inter-departmental coordination, with typical processing times varying by service type and state readiness.
