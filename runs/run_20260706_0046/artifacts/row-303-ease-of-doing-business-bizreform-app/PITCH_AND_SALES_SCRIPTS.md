## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I’m reaching out because many startups, MSMEs, and entrepreneurs we work with are spending too much time navigating fragmented government approvals — jumping between portals, chasing updates, and dealing with delays. The Ease of Doing Business – BizReform App, launched by DPIIT under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, is designed to solve exactly that by offering a single-window digital platform for regulatory services across India. I’d like to understand your current experience with government approvals to see if this could help streamline your processes.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of business entity are you operating (e.g., private limited, LLP, proprietorship), and when was it incorporated or registered?
- Which government approvals, registrations, or clearances are you currently seeking or anticipate needing in the next 6–12 months (e.g., GST, FSSAI, pollution clearance, factory licence)?
- How many different departments or portals do you currently interact with for these approvals, and how much time does your team typically spend on each application?
- Have you faced delays, rejections, or difficulties tracking application status in the past due to incomplete documentation or procedural complexity?
- Are you currently using any digital tools to manage compliance or government filings, or is the process largely manual?
- What would saving even 20–30% of time and cost on regulatory approvals mean for your ability to focus on core business growth?
- Are you based in a state where integration with central portals (like MCA, GSTN, or customs) is important for your operations?
- Do you have an authorized signatory with valid identity and address proof ready to act on behalf of the business for digital submissions?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business entity is a startup, MSME, or entrepreneur (any legal form: proprietorship, partnership, LLP, private/public limited, etc.)
- [ ] Seeking one or more government approvals, registrations, or clearances (e.g., tax, labour, environmental, sector-specific licences)
- [ ] Has valid PAN of the business entity
- [ ] Possesses Certificate of Incorporation or Registration
- [ ] Has GSTIN (if applicable based on turnover)
- [ ] Can provide bank account details
- [ ] Can furnish authorization letter from authorized signatory
- [ ] Has identity and address proof of the applicant (e.g., Aadhaar, passport, driver’s licence)
- [ ] Operates anywhere in India (Pan-India eligibility)
- [ ] Wants to reduce time, cost, and complexity in accessing government services

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like the Ease of Doing Business – BizReform App could help you consolidate your government interactions into one platform, reduce procedural delays, and gain real-time tracking — all without any cost to use. Let me show you how it works and how businesses like yours are already benefiting.
