## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the scheme’s core benefit: digitized compliance processes and reduced regulatory burden for employers under the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 and Social Security Code, 2020.
2. Tiered retainer structure reflects client size and complexity of labour law firm’s capacity to deliver end-to-end registration and compliance support via the Shram Suvidha Portal.
3. Success fee range calibrated to the scheme’s non-financial nature—fees are earned for facilitating access to compliance benefits (e.g., Labour Identification Number, online returns, self-certification) rather than direct financial outlays.
4. Scheme-specific note: The Shram Suvidha Portal involves no direct financial grants or subsidies; revenue is derived from service fees for registration, licensing, and return filing. Processing is immediate upon submission (certificate auto-generated), enabling near-term revenue recognition with minimal lag. Success is evidenced by high-volume usage (over 5.2 million LINs generated) and mandatory registration timelines (60 days for new establishments, 6 months for updates), creating predictable demand.
