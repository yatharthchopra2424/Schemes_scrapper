# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialise in helping employers and establishments navigate India’s new labour compliance framework under the Shram Suvidha Portal. Given your role as an employer or contractor, I wanted to understand your current experience with registration, licensing, and returns under the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 and the Social Security Code, 2020—especially since the Shram Suvidha Portal is now the mandatory one-stop solution for all these compliances. This will help me see if we can support you in avoiding penalties and streamlining your labour law adherence.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your establishment incorporated or registered? (To check if you fall under the 60-day registration window for new establishments or the 6-month update window for existing ones.)
2. Are you currently registered under any previous labour laws (e.g., Factories Act, Shops & Establishments Act)? If yes, have you updated your registration on the Shram Suvidha Portal since the Rules were notified on 8th May 2026?
3. Do you employ Inter-State migrant workers? (This is a specific data point required in Form-I for registration.)
4. Have you obtained your Labour Identification Number (LIN) yet? The portal auto-generates this upon successful registration.
5. Are you filing your Unified Annual Return (Form-XVII) electronically by 1st February each year as required under the new Codes?
6. Have you faced any challenges with manual processes for licence applications, returns submission, or inspection notices under the old system?
7. Are you aware that failure to register or update registration within the stipulated timelines may lead to directions from the registering officer to comply within a specified timeframe?
8. Would you like a free, no-obligation eligibility check to confirm your compliance status under the Shram Suvidha Portal?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Establishment came into existence after commencement of OSH & WC Code, 2020 and SS Code, 2020 → must register within 60 days via Form-I on Shram Suvidha Portal.
- [ ] Establishment already registered under other labour laws → must update registration within six months from 8th May 2026 (date of commencement of Rules).
- [ ] Employer has proof of identity (e.g., Aadhaar, passport, voter ID).
- [ ] Employer has PAN/TAN.
- [ ] Employer has address proof.
- [ ] Information on Inter-State migrant worker employment is available (if applicable).
- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation/Registration is available (for new establishments).
- [ ] Existing registration details are available (for establishments updating registration).
- [ ] Establishment falls under target beneficiaries: startups, MSMEs, employers, contractors.

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like the Shram Suvidha Portal – Labour Compliance scheme is directly relevant to your situation. This is the official Government of India platform that digitises registration, licensing, returns, and inspection processes under the new Labour Codes—helping employers like you avoid penalties and reduce compliance burden. Let me explain how it works and how we’ve helped similar businesses get registered and compliant.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Employers face significant challenges in complying with fragmented labour laws—managing separate registrations, licences, and returns across multiple portals and departments. This leads to duplication, missed deadlines, and risk of penalties. The Shram Suvidha Portal solves this by providing a unified, digital platform for all labour law compliances under the new Codes.

**Scheme intro:**
Shram Suvidha Portal – Labour Compliance, administered by the Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India, is a recognition-type scheme offering a one-stop solution for registration, licence, returns, inspection notices, and enforcement under the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020; the Code on Wages, 2019; the Industrial Relations Code, 2020; and the Code on Social Security, 2020. While it does not provide direct financial support, it facilitates ease of doing business by digitising processes and issuing the Labour Identification Number (LIN) immediately upon successful Form-I submission.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are an employer of an establishment that either came into existence after the commencement of the Labour Codes (requiring registration within 60 days) or is already registered under older laws (requiring update within six months from 8th May 2026).
- You possess or can obtain the required documents: proof of identity, PAN/TAN, address proof, and Certificate of Incorporation/Registration (if new).
- You need to provide information on Inter-State migrant worker employment as part of Form-I.
- You fall under the scheme’s target beneficiaries: startups, MSMEs, employers, or contractors.
