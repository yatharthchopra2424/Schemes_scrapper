### Shram Suvidha Portal – Labour Compliance
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://shramsuvidha.gov.in/register
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Registration must be completed within 60 days from the date of applicability of the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 and the Social Security Code, 2020. Existing establishments must update registration within six months from the date of commencement of these Rules.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Every employer of any establishment
- Age of business: New establishments must register within 60 days of coming into existence; existing establishments must update within six months of Rules commencement
- Sector: All sectors (no restriction mentioned)
- Revenue cap: Not mentioned
- Employee count: Not mentioned
- Other: Must apply electronically through Shram Suvidha Portal in Form-I; establishments already registered under other labour laws must update registration details within six months from the date of commencement of these Rules

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: No direct financial support; provides digitized processes (grants, loans, subsidies); offers compliance facilitation, Labour Identification Number (LIN), self-certification for startups, online submission of returns, risk-based inspection system
- Disbursement mode: Not applicable (non-financial scheme)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing (as long as registration is maintained and updated as required)

#### Documents Required
1. Proof of identity of the employer
2. PAN/TAN of the employer
3. Address of the employer
4. Information relating to employment of Inter-State migrant workers
5. Certificate of Incorporation or Registration (for new establishments)
6. Existing registration details (for establishments updating registration)
7. Any other supporting documents as specified in Form-I

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the Shram Suvidha Portal at https://shramsuvidha.gov.in
2. Click on 'Sign up' to register as a user by providing name, email ID, mobile number, and captcha code
3. Verify registration via the link sent to the registered email ID
4. Set user ID and password after OTP verification
5. Log in to the portal using OTP or user ID/password
6. Navigate to the Registration module
7. Select 'Apply for New/Existing/Offline Registration'
8. Choose registration type (e.g., 'New Registration' or 'Existing with LIN')
9. Fill Form-I with establishment details, proof of identity, PAN/TAN, address, and Inter-State migrant worker information
10. Upload supporting documents
11. Apply e-sign using Aadhaar-based OTP verification
12. Submit the application
13. Certificate of Registration (Form-III) is auto-generated and issued immediately upon successful submission
