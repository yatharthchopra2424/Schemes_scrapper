# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Shram Suvidha Portal – Labour Compliance** (Scheme ID: row-304) is a **recognition-type** scheme implemented pan-India by the **Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India**. It serves as a **one-stop solution** for labour law compliance under the new Labour Codes, facilitating **Ease of Compliance and Ease of Doing Business**. The portal digitizes registration, licence, returns, inspection notices, and enforcement processes, integrating with key agencies like EPFO, ESIC, DGMS, DGFASLI, and CLC. It does **not** provide direct financial support (grants, loans, subsidies); any financial aspects relate to user-borne fees for services like licence issuance or security deposits.

**Application Portal**: https://shramsuvidha.gov.in/register  
**Status / Deadlines**: Registration must be completed within **60 days** from the date of applicability of the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 and the Social Security Code, 2020. Existing establishments must update registration within **six months** from the date of commencement of these Rules.  
**Last Updated**: 2026  
**Confidence**: High

### Objectives
- Facilitate Ease of Compliance and Ease of Doing Business as per provisions of Labour codes  
- Enable effective monitoring and evaluation of labour compliances by digitizing processes of Registration, Licence, Returns, Inspection Notices, and Enforcement  
- Provide a one-stop solution for labour law compliance through integration with EPFO, ESIC, DGMS, DGFASLI, and CLC  
- Support self-certification for startups under labour laws to reduce regulatory burden  
- Ensure transparency, accountability, and uniform implementation of labour laws across Central and State spheres

### Application Process (Mermaid Flowchart)
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Start: Visit Shram Suvidha Portal] --> B[Click 'Sign up' to register as user]
    B --> C[Provide name, email ID, mobile number, captcha code]
    C --> D[Verify registration via link sent to email]
    D --> E[Set user ID and password after OTP verification]
    E --> F[Log in using OTP or user ID/password]
    F --> G[Navigate to Registration module]
    G --> H[Select 'Apply for New/Existing/Offline Registration']
    H --> I[Choose registration type: 'New Registration' or 'Existing with LIN']
    I --> J[Fill Form-I: establishment details, proof of identity, PAN/TAN, address, Inter-State migrant worker info]
    J --> K[Upload supporting documents]
    K --> L[Apply e-sign using Aadhaar-based OTP verification]
    L --> M[Submit application]
    M --> N[Certificate of Registration (Form-III) auto-generated and issued immediately]
    N --> End[End: Registration Complete]
```

### Key Caveats & Compliance Requirements
> **Warning**: Failure to comply with registration provisions may lead to direction by the registering officer to comply within a specified timeframe.  
> **Warning**: Certificate of registration may be cancelled if obtained by providing wrong information, after giving an opportunity to show cause within 30 days.  
> **Warning**: Any change in ownership, management, or registration details must be updated within **thirty days** via amendment on the portal.  
> **Note**: Registration number must be quoted on all documents, records, and correspondence related to the Code or Rules.  
> **Note**: Electronic registration is standard; temporary relaxation for non-electronic submission may be notified in exceptional circumstances.
