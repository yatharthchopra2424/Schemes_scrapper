# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The matter falls under company law cases transferred from Hon’ble High Courts or the erstwhile Company Law Tribunal  
- [ ] The matter relates to insolvency cases under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016  
- [ ] The matter involves oppression and mismanagement, class action suits, reduction of share capital, rectification of register of members, amalgamations, mergers, restoration of company name, winding up, or other functions under the Companies Act  
- [ ] The matter involves commencement and adjudication of Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) cases  
- [ ] The matter involves Insolvency Resolution Process for Personal Guarantors to Corporate Debtors  
- [ ] The applicant has identified the appropriate NCLT bench based on geographical jurisdiction (refer to NCLT Benches jurisdiction details)  
- [ ] The applicant is an MSME, corporate, creditor, shareholder, employee, or personal guarantor (as per target beneficiaries)  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration  
- PAN Card  
- ITR (last 2–3 years)  
- GST Registration Certificate  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Memorandum and Articles of Association (MOA/AOA)  
- Board Resolution authorizing the filing  
- Statement of Accounts and Audit Reports  
- Details of creditors and dues owed  
- Evidence of default (for IBC cases)  
- Proposed Resolution Plan (for CIRP)  
- Affidavit verifying the contents of the petition  
- Vakalatnama (if represented by counsel)  
- Proof of payment of court fees  
- Any other document as directed by the Tribunal  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (preferably OCR-enabled)  
- File names must clearly indicate document type and case reference (e.g., "MOA_ABC_Pvt_Ltd.pdf")  
- Affidavits and board resolutions must be notarized and signed  
- Financial statements must be audited and certified by a Chartered Accountant  
- Vakalatnama must be signed by advocate and client  
- Proof of court fee payment must be attached (challan or receipt)  
- Documents must be in English or accompanied by certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required. The NCLT process does not mandate a Detailed Project Report (DPR). However, for CIRP cases, a **Resolution Plan** is required (see Scheme-Specific Documents). Verify with the nodal officer if additional project details are needed for specific bench requirements.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Identify the correct NCLT bench based on the company’s registered office or location of assets (refer to jurisdiction table in evidence).  
2. Select the correct petition type: Company Petition (under Companies Act), Insolvency Petition (under IBC Section 7, 9, or 10), or Company Appeal, as applicable.  
3. Ensure all fields in the e-filing form are completed accurately; leave no mandatory field blank.  
4. Attach all scheme-specific documents in the order listed in the checklist.  
5. Verify that the affidavit is sworn and notarized before attachment.  
6. Confirm that Vakalatnama is attached if represented by legal counsel.  
7. Pay the prescribed court fees online via the portal and upload the receipt.  
8. Double-check for OCR readability, proper indexing, and absence of unsigned pages to avoid objections.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All eligibility criteria verified and documented  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents collected and quality-checked  
- [ ] Application form filled completely with correct case type and bench selection  
- [ ] All documents attached in correct order and format  
- [ ] Affidavit, board resolution, and Vakalatnama (if applicable) are signed and notarized  
- [ ] Proof of court fee payment uploaded  
- [ ] No procedural defects (incorrect case type, missing documents, improper indexing, lack of OCR, unsigned pages)

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the NCLT e-filing portal at https://nclt.gov.in  
- Take a screenshot of the successful submission confirmation page  
- Save the application/reference number generated by the system  
- Store all submitted documents and receipts in a dedicated case folder (digital and physical)  
- Set calendar reminders for follow-up at 7, 15, and 30 days post-submission

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and securely store the approval/order copy from the NCLT bench  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount or terms of the resolution plan (if applicable)  
- [ ] Monitor compliance with timelines prescribed under IBC for insolvency proceedings  
- [ ] Prepare utilization certificate if funds are involved in the resolution  
- [ ] Coordinate with the client for success fee invoicing upon successful resolution or asset recovery  
- [ ] Ensure adherence to post-order directives such as creditor payments, asset transfers, or liquidation supervision  
- [ ] Archive all case documents for future reference and audit compliance
