## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified NCLT Fast Track Insolvency for MSMEs — a judicial adjudication scheme administered by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India — for which your business appears eligible. The scheme does not provide direct financial support but facilitates legally binding resolutions under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, with outcomes such as debt restructuring or asset realization determined by the Tribunal.

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 and Companies Act, 2013  
2. Preparation and review of petition (Company Petition, Insolvency Petition, etc.)  
3. Compilation and verification of required documents as per NCLT checklist  
4. Jurisdictional bench determination based on geographical and subject-matter criteria  
5. Filing of application at the appropriate NCLT bench or via e-filing portal at https://nclt.gov.in  
6. Payment of prescribed court fees and generation of payment proof  
7. Service of notice to opposite party as per NCLT rules  
8. Representation at hearings and coordination with Tribunal for timely adjudication  
9. Monitoring of case status, interim orders, and judgment delivery  
10. Assistance in implementation of NCLT orders (e.g., resolution plan, repayment schedule)  
11. Post-order compliance support and reporting  
12. Scheme-specific item: Portal registration and e-filing support via https://nclt.gov.in; DPR/project report not required as this is a judicial adjudication process, not a grant or loan scheme  

## 4. What We Need From You
1. Documents as per the scheme's document list: Certificate of Incorporation, MOA/AOA, Board Resolution authorizing filing, Statement of Accounts and Audit Reports, Details of creditors and dues owed, Evidence of default (for IBC cases), Proposed Resolution Plan (for CIRP), Affidavit verifying petition contents, Vakalatnama (if represented by counsel), Proof of payment of court fees, and any other document as directed by the Tribunal  
2. Designated point of contact for coordination and document submission  
3. Response to queries within 2 business days to avoid procedural delays
