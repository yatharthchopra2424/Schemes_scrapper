## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-based pricing**: Fees aligned with the financial outcome achieved for the client (e.g., debt recovery, asset realization via NCLT orders under IBC).
2. **Risk-adjusted success fee**: Success fee reflects the judicial nature of outcomes — no guaranteed payout, dependent on Tribunal discretion and case merits.
3. **Transparency in retainer**: Covers initial case assessment, documentation, filing, and representation up to admission stage.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: NCLT does not disburse funds; financial outcomes arise from court-ordered resolutions (debt restructuring, liquidation proceeds). Processing time for IBC cases averages 600–700 days (per Annual Report 2022-23), creating revenue lag and requiring phased billing aligned with milestones (admission, resolution plan approval).
