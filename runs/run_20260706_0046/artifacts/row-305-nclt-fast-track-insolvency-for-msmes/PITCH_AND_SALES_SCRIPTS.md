## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping businesses navigate legal and regulatory frameworks, particularly around insolvency and company law matters under the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT). Many MSMEs and corporates face financial distress or legal disputes that require timely adjudication—whether it's for debt restructuring, winding up, or resolving oppression and mismanagement. I’d like to understand your current situation to see if NCLT proceedings could be a viable path forward for your business.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is the date of incorporation or registration of your company?  
2. Are you currently facing any financial distress, such as defaults on debt or inability to pay creditors?  
3. Has any creditor already issued a demand notice or filed a petition against your company?  
4. Are you a shareholder, creditor, employee, or personal guarantor seeking relief under company law or insolvency provisions?  
5. Are you dealing with issues like oppression and mismanagement, or considering a merger, amalgamation, or reduction of share capital?  
6. Do you have your Certificate of Incorporation, MOA/AOA, and recent audited financial statements ready?  
7. Are you located in a state or union territory that falls under the jurisdiction of a specific NCLT bench (e.g., Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, etc.)?  
8. Are you considering initiating or responding to a Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity is a corporate body, partnership firm, or individual (including personal guarantor)  
- [ ] Matter falls under Companies Act, 2013 or Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016  
- [ ] Case involves oppression and mismanagement, CIRP, merger/amalgamation, winding up, or similar  
- [ ] Applicant has or can obtain: Certificate of Incorporation, MOA/AOA, board resolution, audited accounts, creditor details, evidence of default (if IBC), affidavit, vakalatnama, court fee proof  
- [ ] Jurisdiction aligns with the territorial limits of an NCLT bench (based on company’s registered office)  
- [ ] Matter is not time-barred under limitation laws  
- [ ] No procedural defects such as incorrect case type, missing documents, or lack of OCR in filings  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it appears your situation may fall under the jurisdiction of the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), which handles insolvency and company law matters with binding judicial outcomes. While NCLT doesn’t provide direct financial aid, its rulings can lead to debt restructuring, asset recovery, or legally enforceable resolutions—critical for MSMEs and distressed companies. Shall I explain how we can assist you in navigating this process effectively?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many MSMEs and corporates face prolonged legal disputes, creditor pressure, or internal mismanagement that drains resources and stalls operations. Without a specialized forum, resolution through regular courts can take years, increasing costs and reducing chances of revival. Delays in insolvency proceedings often lead to asset depreciation and lost opportunities for restructuring.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), established under Section 408 of the Companies Act, 2013 and designated as the Adjudicating Authority under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, is a quasi-judicial body that provides a unified forum for adjudicating company law disputes, corporate insolvency (CIRP), and insolvency of personal guarantors. It operates through benches across India (including Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, etc.) and delivers legally binding orders on debt restructuring, liquidation, or revival plans. While NCLT does not disburse funds, its judgments enable court-supervised resolutions that can unlock value, settle dues, and restore financial viability.

- Your registered office falls within the territorial jurisdiction of an NCLT bench (e.g., Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, etc.)  
- You possess or can prepare key documents: MOA/AOA, audited financial statements, creditor details, board resolution, and affidavit
