### NCLT Fast Track Insolvency for MSMEs
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Recognition
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nclt.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: MSME; corporates; creditors; shareholders; employees; personal guarantors
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Not specified
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must fall under jurisdiction of NCLT for company law cases transferred from Hon’ble High Courts and the erstwhile Company Law Tribunal, and insolvency cases under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016; adjudicates matters relating to oppression and mismanagement, class action suits, reduction of share capital, rectification of register of members, amalgamations, mergers, restoration of company name, winding up, and other functions under the Companies Act; has exclusive jurisdiction to commence and adjudicate Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) cases and Insolvency Resolution Process for Personal Guarantors to Corporate Debtors

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: NCLT does not provide direct financial support such as grants, loans, or subsidies. It is a judicial tribunal that adjudicates insolvency and company law cases. Financial outcomes arise from court-ordered resolutions, such as debt restructuring, liquidation proceeds, or repayment plans under IBC, but NCLT itself does not disburse funds.
- Disbursement mode: Not applicable (judicial adjudication only)
- Duration of benefit: Case-dependent (varies based on proceedings under IBC or Companies Act)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. Memorandum and Articles of Association (MOA/AOA)
3. Board Resolution authorizing the filing
4. Statement of Accounts and Audit Reports
5. Details of creditors and dues owed
6. Evidence of default (for IBC cases)
7. Proposed Resolution Plan (for CIRP)
8. Affidavit verifying the contents of the petition
9. Vakalatnama (if represented by counsel)
10. Proof of payment of court fees
11. Any other document as directed by the Tribunal

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the appropriate NCLT bench based on geographical jurisdiction.
2. File the relevant petition or application (e.g., Company Petition, Company Appeal, Insolvency Petition) under the applicable provision of Companies Act, 2013 or Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016.
3. Submit the application along with required documents at the NCLT registry or through e-filing portal.
4. Pay the prescribed court fees.
5. Serve notice to the opposite party as per rules.
6. Attend hearings as scheduled by the Tribunal.
7. Await judgment or order from the NCLT bench.
