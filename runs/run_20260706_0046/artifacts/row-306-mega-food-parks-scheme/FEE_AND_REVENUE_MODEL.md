## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the substantial financial benefit delivered (grant amounts up to ₹10 Cr for Mega Food Park SPVs), ensuring client ROI exceeds consulting costs.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Fees**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s discontinuation status (effective 01.04.2021) and reliance on committed liabilities for ongoing projects only, increasing engagement risk.
3. **Transparency**: All fees and payment terms are disclosed upfront, with retainers covering initial SPV formation and guideline compliance work.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Mega Food Parks Scheme has a high risk profile due to its discontinuation and complex SPV-dependent implementation; success is contingent on securing committed liabilities for ongoing projects, which requires deep expertise in navigating legacy government provisions.
