# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping food processing entrepreneurs and agri-businesses access government grants for infrastructure development. The Mega Food Parks Scheme, administered by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries, is designed to link agricultural production to the market by creating state-of-the-art infrastructure like collection centers, cold chains, and developed plots for food processing units—maximizing value addition and reducing wastage. I’d like to understand your current operations to see if this scheme aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is the legal structure of your entity—are you operating as a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) registered under the Companies Act, or are you a State Government entity, State Government undertaking, or Cooperative?
2. What is the primary focus of your agri/horticultural zone—are you looking to establish infrastructure in a defined agricultural or horticultural cluster?
3. Do you have plans to develop core infrastructure such as collection centers, primary processing centers, central processing centers, and cold chain facilities?
4. Are you aiming to provide 25–30 fully developed plots for entrepreneurs to set up food processing units within your proposed park?
5. What is your current stage in securing land and obtaining statutory approvals for the Mega Food Park project?
6. Have you begun engaging with farmers, processors, and retailers to establish a backward and forward linkage model?
7. Are you seeking financial assistance for committed liabilities under ongoing projects, given the scheme’s discontinuation effective April 1, 2021?
8. Do you have access to the Scheme Guidelines and understand the conditions for fund release to the SPV?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity is a Body Corporate registered under the Companies Act (or exempt as State Govt./Govt. entity/Cooperative)
- [ ] Project is located in a well-defined agri/horticultural zone
- [ ] Plans include development of supply chain infrastructure: collection centers, primary processing centers, central processing centers, cold chain
- [ ] Intends to provide 25–30 fully developed plots for food processing units
- [ ] Aims to link farmers, processors, and retailers to maximize value addition and minimize wastage
- [ ] Seeking funds for committed liabilities of ongoing projects (scheme discontinued w.e.f. 01.04.2021)
- [ ] Willing to fulfill Scheme Guidelines conditions for fund release
- [ ] Located in India (Pan-India scope)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your project aligns with the Mega Food Parks Scheme’s objective of creating integrated agri-processing infrastructure. While the scheme is no longer accepting new applications, committed liabilities for ongoing projects are still being processed. If you’re managing an ongoing Mega Food Park project, we can help you navigate the approval and fund release process under this scheme.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Agri-entrepreneurs and processors face fragmented supply chains, high post-harvest losses, and lack of access to modern infrastructure like cold chains and processing centers. This limits value addition, reduces farmers’ income, and hinders rural employment growth—especially in horticulture and food processing clusters.

**Scheme intro:**
The Mega Food Parks Scheme, administered by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries, is a central sector grant scheme that provides financial assistance to Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs) for developing state-of-the-art agri-processing infrastructure. Although the scheme was discontinued effective April 1, 2021, it continues to support committed liabilities for ongoing projects. The scheme supports the development of collection centers, primary processing centers, central processing centers, cold chain facilities, and approximately 25–30 fully developed plots for entrepreneurs to set up food processing units, thereby linking farm production to market and maximizing value addition.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your entity is structured as a Body Corporate under the Companies Act (or qualifies for SPV exemption as a State Govt./Cooperative entity)  
- You are developing core infrastructure including collection centers, processing centers, and cold chain in a defined agri/horticultural zone  
- Your project includes plans to provide 25–30 developed plots for food processing unit setup  
- You are seeking to establish backward and forward linkages between farmers, processors, and retailers

| "I don't have the documents ready" | The three most commonly missing documents are: (1) SPV incorporation certificate under the Companies Act, (2) Detailed Project Report (DPR) showing infrastructure layout and linkage model, and (3) land possession/lease documents for the agri/horticultural zone. |
