### Mega Food Parks Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Food Processing Industries
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://mofpi.gov.in/schemes/mega-food-parks
- **Current Status:** Closed
- **Window:** Discontinued with effect from 01.04.2021 (provision for committed liabilities for ongoing projects only)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) which is a Body Corporate registered under the Companies Act
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Food processing
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: State Government, State Government entities and Cooperatives are not required to form a separate SPV for implementation

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Funds released to the Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) subject to fulfillment of the conditions of the Scheme Guidelines
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit to SPV
- Duration of benefit: One-time (funds released upon approval)

#### Documents Required
1. Proposal for financial assistance
2. Certificate of incorporation of SPV (Body Corporate registered under Companies Act)
3. Scheme Guidelines compliance documents
4. State Government/Cooperative exemption documentation (if applicable)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Form a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) as a Body Corporate registered under the Companies Act (State Government, State Government entities and Cooperatives are exempt from forming a separate SPV)
2. Ensure the SPV meets the conditions of the Scheme Guidelines
3. Submit proposal for financial assistance
4. Upon approval, funds are released to the SPV
5. Develop the Mega Food Park with supply chain infrastructure including collection centers, primary processing centers, central processing centers, cold chain, and 25-30 fully developed plots for food processing units
