# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
The **Mega Food Parks Scheme** is a central sector scheme implemented by the **Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI)**, Government of India. It aims to provide a mechanism to link agricultural production to the market by bringing together farmers, processors, and retailers to maximize value addition, minimize wastage, increase farmers' income, and create employment opportunities, particularly in the rural sector.

**Scheme ID:** row-306  
**Ministry / Category:** Food Processing  
**Scheme Type:** Grant  
**Geographic Scope:** Pan-India  
**Implementing Agency:** Ministry of Food Processing Industries  
**Status / Deadlines:** The scheme has been discontinued by the Government with effect from 01.04.2021 with provision for committed liabilities for ongoing projects only.  
**Last Updated:** 2024  
**Application Portal URL:** https://mofpi.gov.in/schemes/mega-food-parks  
**Contact Details:** Shri Pankaj Kumar, Director, Ministry of Food Processing Industries, Panchsheel Bhawan, August Kranti Marg, New Delhi-110049 | Tel: 011-26406510 | Email: pankaj[dot]kumar1975[at]nic[dot]in  

### Objectives
- Link agricultural production to the market by bringing together farmers, processors, and retailers  
- Maximize value addition and minimize wastage  
- Increase farmers' income  
- Create employment opportunities, particularly in the rural sector  
- Create state-of-the-art support infrastructure in a well-defined agri/horticultural zone  
- Provide well-developed plots for entrepreneurs to set up food processing units  
- Establish a well-established supply chain

> **> Key Takeaway:** The scheme does not provide direct subsidies to individual units but funds the SPV for developing common infrastructure. Individual food processing units set up in the plots are expected to be self-sustaining or seek other schemes (e.g., PMFME) for unit-level support.

### Application Process
The application process for the Mega Food Parks Scheme involves forming an SPV, ensuring compliance with guidelines, and submitting a proposal for financial assistance. Since the scheme is discontinued, this process is only relevant for ongoing projects with committed liabilities.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Start: Identify Agri/Horticultural Zone] --> B[Form Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) as Body Corporate under Companies Act]
    B --> C{Is Applicant State Govt/Govt Entity/Cooperative?}
    C -->|Yes| D[Exempt from forming separate SPV]
    C -->|No| B
    D --> E[Ensure SPV meets Scheme Guidelines Conditions]
    E --> F[Submit Proposal for Financial Assistance to MoFPI]
    F --> G{Approval by Inter-Ministerial Approval Committee?}
    G -->|Yes| H[Funds Released to SPV]
    G -->|No| I[Revise and Resubmit Proposal]
    H --> J[Develop Mega Food Park Infrastructure]
    J --> K[Develop Supply Chain: Collection Centers, Primary Processing, Central Processing, Cold Chain]
    K --> L[Allocate 25-30 Fully Developed Plots to Entrepreneurs]
    L --> M[End: Operational Mega Food Park with Established Supply Chain]
```

> **> Important Exemption:** State Government, State Government entities, and Cooperatives are **not required** to form a separate SPV for implementation of the Mega Food Park project.  

### Supporting Evidence Summary
- The scheme operates on a **cluster approach**, creating state-of-the-art support infrastructure in a well-defined agri/horticultural zone.  
- Funds are released to the SPV in installments based on fulfillment of conditions in the Scheme Guidelines.  
- The SPV develops infrastructure (collection centers, primary/central processing centers, cold chain) and allocates 25-30 developed plots for food processing units.  
- The scheme has funded **41 Mega Food Parks** in total, with 25 currently operational.  
- The implementing agency is the **Ministry of Food Processing Industries**, with Shri Pankaj Kumar as the point of contact.  
- All guidelines, circulars, and application templates are available for download on the MoFPI website under the Mega Food Park section.
