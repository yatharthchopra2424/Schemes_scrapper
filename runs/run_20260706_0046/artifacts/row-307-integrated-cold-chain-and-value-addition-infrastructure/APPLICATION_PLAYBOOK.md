# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a prospective entrepreneur, SC/ST category applicant, or NER region applicant (as per EOI notices)
- [ ] Applicant is a University, IIT, Central/State Government Institution, Government funded Organization, R&D laboratory, or CSIR recognized R&D unit in private sector (for R&D component)
- [ ] Applicant is engaged in setting up infrastructure projects in the food processing sector (cold chain, value addition infrastructure, irradiation units, backward-forward linkages)
- [ ] Project involves creation of modern infrastructure with efficient supply chain management from farm gate to retail outlet
- [ ] Project aims to reduce wastage of agricultural produce and increase processing level of perishable commodities
- [ ] For registered leasehold land, duration of lease is not less than 15 years
- [ ] Applicant has not been blacklisted/debarred for misuse of grant-in-aid (e.g., M/s Paras Spices, M/s Vision Fresh and Frozen)
- [ ] Applicant is prepared to comply with approval letter terms and conditions for fund release
- [ ] Applicant understands that Surety Bond is not required for Central/State Government Departments

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation/Registration Certificate
- PAN Card
- ITR (last 3 years)
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Acceptance Letter (Annexure-VI)
- Geo-tagged photographs of the project from various angles and locations
- Building plan/Blue Print/Lay-out/photo of the building
- Final term loan sanction letter from the Bank
- Confirmation from bank that 50% of term loan has been disbursed
- Utilization certificate of grant availed earlier from MoFPI (if any)
- Copies of Bank statements giving details of payment made to the suppliers
- Detailed list of approved equipment procured and installed indicating approved and actual cost as per Annexure-VIII(a)
- Details of Technical Civil Work and Furniture and Fixture purchased/done as per Annexure-VIII(b)
- Self-attested English/Hindi version of land document in support of land title
- Notarised Surety Bond in prescribed format (Annexure-IX) on non-judicial stamp paper of not less than Rs. 100/-
- Utilization Certificate generated through EAT Module of PFMS for grants-in-aid released as 1st instalment
- Certificate as per Annexure-VII indicating 100% expenditure of equity, term loan, and grants-in-aid
- Detailed Status of NABL accreditation for the facility created
- Confirmation from bank that 100% term loan has been disbursed

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and in English or Hindi
- File size for each document should not exceed 5 MB
- File names must match the document description (e.g., "Acceptance_Letter_Annexure-VI.pdf")
- Geo-tagged photographs must include latitude/longitude coordinates and timestamps
- Building plans must be approved by competent authority
- Bank statements must be stamped and signed by the bank
- Surety Bond must be on non-judicial stamp paper of Rs. 100/- or above and notarized
- Land documents must be self-attested and clearly show ownership or leasehold rights
- Utilization Certificates must be generated through the EAT Module of PFMS
- All certificates must be in the prescribed format as per annexures

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required for this scheme. Standard sections to include:
- Project At a Glance
- Introduction and Product Description
- Process Flow Chart
- Economics of the Project (Basis & Presumptions, Capacity Utilization, Production & Output, Premises/Infrastructure, Machinery & Equipment, Misc. Fixed Assets, Total Cost, Means of Finance)
- Term Loan Repayment & Interest Schedule
- Working Capital Calculations
- Salary & Wages
- Power Requirement
- Depreciation Calculation
- Repairs & Maintenance
- Projections of Profitability Analysis
- Break Even Point Analysis
- Projected Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow Statement
- Debt-Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
- Conclusion and Annexures

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Begin with submitting an Expression of Interest (EOI) as per notices issued under the scheme on the portal: https://mofpi.gov.in/en/Schemes/about-pmksy-scheme
2. Attend pre-bid meetings as scheduled in the EOI notice
3. Submit detailed proposals in response to EOI or RFP for engagement of PMA (if applicable)
4. Ensure all fields in the application form are filled accurately; leave no blank sections
5. Upload documents in the prescribed format and naming convention as specified in the Document Quality Check
6. Confirm that the project aligns with scheme objectives: reducing wastage, enhancing export, boosting farmer income, creating rural employment
7. Verify that financial projections show viable DSCR (>1.5) and reasonable payback period
8. Double-check that bank loan terms match the scheme’s requirement for 50% and 100% disbursement milestones

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All eligibility criteria verified and documented
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents collected and quality-checked
- [ ] DPR completed with all standard sections and realistic financials
- [ ] Application form filled completely on the portal with no errors
- [ ] All documents uploaded and accessible via the portal
- [ ] Declarations and undertakings signed by authorized signatory
- [ ] Geo-tagged photographs verified for authenticity
- [ ] Bank documents confirm loan sanction and staged disbursement compliance
- [ ] Surety Bond prepared as per Annexure-IX (if applicable)
- [ ] Land document confirms minimum 15-year leasehold (if leasehold)
- [ ] NABL accreditation status included (if food testing lab is part of project)
- [ ] Reference to latest EOI notice included in application remarks

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Take a screenshot of the final submission confirmation page showing the application/reference number
- Store the reference number securely in the case file
- Set calendar reminders for follow-up at 15, 30, and 45 days post-submission
- Monitor the portal regularly for any queries or requests for additional information
- Save all correspondence with the scheme authorities in the case folder

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and review the approval letter from Inter-Ministerial Approval Committee (IMAC)
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned grant-in-aid amount in the approval letter
- [ ] Fulfill compliance requirements for first instalment:
  - Submit Acceptance Letter (Annexure-VI)
  - Provide geo-tagged photographs of the project
  - Submit building plan/blue print
  - Confirm 50% equity and term loan expenditure (via bank statement and CA certificate)
- [ ] Ensure utilization of first instalment and generate Utilization Certificate through EAT Module of PFMS
- [ ] Submit Utilization Certificate and related documents for second instalment
- [ ] Confirm 100% expenditure of equity, term loan, and grants-in-aid (Certificate as per Annexure-VII)
- [ ] Obtain NABL accreditation for food testing laboratories (if applicable)
- [ ] Submit proof of project completion and commercialization
- [ ] Track utilization of funds against approved project cost
- [ ] Prepare for success fee invoicing as per consultancy agreement (typically 8-12% of sanctioned grant upon second instalment release)
