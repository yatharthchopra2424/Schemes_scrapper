## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the substantial financial benefit delivered (grant up to 35% of project cost, max ₹10 lakhs under PMFME, but for Integrated Cold Chain, grants-in-aid are released in instalments based on compliance, with fund size of ₹6000 Cr).
2. **Risk Sharing**: Success fees are contingent on successful grant approval and fund disbursement, which requires strict compliance with approval letter terms, utilization certificates, and project completion proof — moderate risk due to documentation intensity and phased release.
3. **Market Alignment**: Retainer fees reflect the effort required for proposal preparation, compliance documentation (e.g., geo-tagged photos, bank statements, NABL accreditation), and liaison with MoFPI/PMA/PMC.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: This scheme has a moderate risk profile due to its rolling EOI basis, strict compliance requirements for tranche releases (50% upon acceptance, 50% upon completion), and dependency on technical civil work completion and NABL accreditation where applicable. Processing time averages 6–12 months from EOI to first instalment, based on evidence of periodic EOI notices and compliance steps.
