# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping food processing entrepreneurs and MSMEs access government grants for infrastructure projects like cold chains, value addition facilities, and irradiation units. The Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure scheme under PMKSY is designed to boost your sector by supporting modern infrastructure from farm gate to retail. I’d like to understand your current setup to see if this aligns with your growth plans.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of food processing infrastructure are you planning to set up or upgrade (e.g., cold storage, irradiation unit, processing line)?
2. What is your current or planned project location, and is the land owned or on lease (if lease, duration)?
3. Have you secured or applied for term loan financing from a bank for this project?
4. What percentage of the project cost have you or plan to contribute as equity from your own funds?
5. Are you an entrepreneur, MSME, agri-startup, or part of an SC/ST or NER category applicant?
6. Do you plan to create backward and forward linkages with farmers or retailers?
7. Is your project aimed at reducing wastage, increasing processing levels, or enhancing exports of perishable commodities?
8. Have you previously applied for or received any grant from MoFPI under PMKSY or related schemes?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is an entrepreneur, MSME, agri-startup, or food processing unit
- [ ] Project involves cold chain, value addition infrastructure, irradiation units, or backward-forward linkages
- [ ] Land title is clear; if leasehold, duration is not less than 15 years
- [ ] Applicant has or can secure term loan from a bank
- [ ] Applicant can contribute minimum 50% equity (first tranche) and 100% equity + term loan (second tranche)
- [ ] Project is located in India (Pan-India eligibility)
- [ ] Applicant is not blacklisted/debarred for misuse of grant-in-aid
- [ ] For SC/ST/NER applicants: eligibility confirmed via EOI notices

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, your project appears aligned with the **Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure** scheme under PMKSY, which offers grants-in-aid released in two instalments (50% each) upon compliance. Let me walk you through how it works and how we’ve helped similar food processing businesses secure this support.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Food processing businesses struggle with high wastage of perishable produce, limited processing levels, and weak farm-to-retail supply chains due to inadequate cold chain and value addition infrastructure. This leads to lower farmer incomes, reduced export potential, and missed opportunities for employment generation in rural areas.

**Scheme intro:**
The **Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure** scheme, administered by the **Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI)** under **Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana (PMKSY)**, provides **grants-in-aid** released in two instalments. The first instalment (50% of grant) is released upon acceptance letter and compliance with enclosures (geo-tagged photos, building plans, 50% equity and term loan expenditure). The second instalment (50%) is released upon submission of utilization certificate, 100% expenditure of equity, term loan, and grant, NABL accreditation (if applicable), and proof of project completion and commercialization. Funds are released via the EAT module of PFMS.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are an entrepreneur/MSME/agri-startup/food processing unit planning infrastructure in the food processing sector  
- Your project includes cold chain, value addition, irradiation unit, or backward-forward linkages  
- You have or can secure a term loan and contribute equity as per scheme requirements  
- Your land is owned or on leasehold with duration ≥15 years (if applicable)
