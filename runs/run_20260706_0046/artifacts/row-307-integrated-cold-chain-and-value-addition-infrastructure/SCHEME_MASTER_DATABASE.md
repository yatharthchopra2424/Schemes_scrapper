### Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Food Processing Industries
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://mofpi.gov.in/en/Schemes/about-pmksy-scheme
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Rolling basis — EOIs are invited periodically with specific closing dates (e.g., EoI dated 20.05.2026 for irradiation units, EoI dated 27.05.2025 for multiproduct food irradiation units)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Prospective entrepreneurs, SC/ST category applicants, NER region applicants, MSME, agri-startups, food processing units
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Food processing sector
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: All Universities, IITs, Central/State Government Institutions, Government funded Organizations, R&D laboratories and CSIR recognized R&D units in private sector are eligible under the Research & Development scheme; for the Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure component, eligibility criteria are specified in the scheme guidelines and include prospective entrepreneurs, SC/ST category applicants, and NER region applicants as per EOI notices

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance is provided as grants-in-aid released in instalments. The first instalment (50% of grants-in-aid) is released upon acceptance letter and compliance with enclosures, including geo-tagged photographs, building plans, and confirmation of 50% equity and term loan expenditure. The second instalment (50%) is released upon submission of utilization certificate, confirmation of 100% expenditure of equity, term loan, and grants-in-aid, NABL accreditation status, and proof of project completion and commercialization.
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (Funds are released through the EAT module of PFMS)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (grants-in-aid released in instalments based on compliance)

#### Documents Required
1. Acceptance Letter (Annexure-VI)
2. Geo-tagged photographs of the project from various angles and locations
3. Building plan/Blue Print/Lay-out/photo of the building
4. Final term loan sanction letter from the Bank
5. Confirmation from bank that 50% of term loan has been disbursed
6. Utilization certificate of grant availed earlier from MoFPI (if any)
7. Copies of Bank statements giving details of payment made to the suppliers
8. Detailed list of approved equipment procured and installed indicating approved and actual cost as per Annexure-VIII(a)
9. Details of Technical Civil Work and Furniture and Fixture purchased/done as per Annexure-VIII(b)
10. Self-attested English/Hindi version of land document in support of land title
11. Notarised Surety Bond in prescribed format (Annexure-IX) on non-judicial stamp paper of not less than Rs. 100/-
12. Utilization Certificate generated through EAT Module of PFMS for grants-in-aid released as 1st instalment
13. Certificate as per Annexure-VII indicating 100% expenditure of equity, term loan, and grants-in-aid
14. Detailed Status of NABL accreditation for the facility created
15. Confirmation from bank that 100% term loan has been disbursed

#### Application Process
1. Submit Expression of Interest (EOI) as per notices issued under the scheme.
2. Attend pre-bid meetings as scheduled.
3. Submit detailed proposals in response to EOI or RFP for engagement of PMA.
4. Undergo technical and project approval committee review.
5. Receive approval letter from Inter-Ministerial Approval Committee (IMAC).
6. Fulfill compliance requirements for first instalment: submit acceptance letter, compliance documents, geo-tagged photographs, building plan, and proof of 50% equity and term loan expenditure.
7. Submit utilization certificate and related documents for second instalment.
8. Obtain NABL accreditation for food testing laboratories where applicable.
9. Submit proof of project completion and commercialization.
