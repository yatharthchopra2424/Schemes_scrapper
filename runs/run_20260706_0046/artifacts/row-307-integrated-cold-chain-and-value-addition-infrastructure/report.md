# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
The **Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure** scheme (Scheme ID: row-307) is a **grant-type** initiative under the **Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI)**, Government of India. It operates on a **Pan-India** geographic scope and is implemented directly by MoFPI. The scheme is currently active with a **rolling basis** application process, where Expressions of Interest (EOIs) are invited periodically with specific closing dates (e.g., EoI dated 20.05.2026 for irradiation units, EoI dated 27.05.2025 for multiproduct food irradiation units). The scheme has a **fund size of ₹6,000 Crore** and was last updated in 2025.

**Application Portal**: https://mofpi.gov.in/en/Schemes/about-pmksy-scheme

### Objectives
The scheme aims to:
- Create modern infrastructure with efficient supply chain management from farm gate to retail outlet
- Provide a big boost to the growth of the food processing sector in the country
- Help in providing better returns to farmers and double farmers' income
- Create huge employment opportunities, especially in rural areas
- Reduce wastage of agricultural produce
- Increase the processing level of perishable commodities
- Enhance the export of processed foods

### Eligibility Matrix
Eligibility varies by scheme component. For the **Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure** component, eligibility is specified in scheme guidelines and EOI notices. For the **Research & Development** component under the same scheme, the following are eligible:

| Eligible Entity Type | Details |
|----------------------|---------|
| Universities | All recognized universities |
| IITs | All Indian Institutes of Technology |
| Central/State Government Institutions | All government-funded institutions |
| Government Funded Organizations | Organizations receiving government funding |
| R&D Laboratories | Recognized R&D labs |
| CSIR Recognized R&D Units in Private Sector | Private sector R&D units recognized by CSIR |

**Target Beneficiaries**: Entrepreneurs, MSMEs, agri-startups, food processing units  
**Special Categories**: Prospective entrepreneurs, SC/ST category applicants, NER region applicants (as per EOI notices)

> **Important Caveat**: The scheme has been discontinued for some components (Mega Food Park, Creation of Backward & Forward Linkages, Human Resource & Institution - Promotional Activities, Skill Development & HACCP) in the 15th Finance Commission cycle, with provision for committed liabilities only.

### Benefits & Financial Support
Financial assistance is provided as **grants-in-aid** released in instalments:

| Instalment | Percentage | Release Conditions |
|------------|------------|---------------------|
| **First Instalment** | 50% of grants-in-aid | Upon acceptance letter and compliance with enclosures, including:<br>- Geo-tagged photographs<br>- Building plans<br>- Confirmation of 50% equity and term loan expenditure |
| **Second Instalment** | 50% of grants-in-aid | Upon submission of:<br>- Utilization certificate<br>- Confirmation of 100% expenditure of equity, term loan, and grants-in-aid<br>- NABL accreditation status<br>- Proof of project completion and commercialization |

**Funds Release Mechanism**: Through the **EAT module of PFMS** (Public Financial Management System)

**Key Benefits**:
- Support for cold chain, value addition infrastructure, irradiation units, and backward-forward linkages
- Facilitates release of funds in instalments based on compliance with approval letters, utilization certificates, and technical civil work completion
- Supports NABL accreditation for food testing laboratories
- Provides impetus to employment generation, farmer income growth, and export enhancement

### Required Documents
Applicants must submit the following documents:

1. Acceptance Letter (Annexure-VI)
2. Geo-tagged photographs of the project from various angles and locations
3. Building plan/Blue Print/Lay-out/photo of the building
4. Final term loan sanction letter from the Bank
5. Confirmation from bank that 50% of term loan has been disbursed
6. Utilization certificate of grant availed earlier from MoFPI (if any)
7. Copies of Bank statements giving details of payment made to the suppliers
8. Detailed list of approved equipment procured and installed indicating approved and actual cost as per Annexure-VIII(a)
9. Details of Technical Civil Work and Furniture and Fixture purchased/done as per Annexure-VIII(b)
10. Self-attested English/Hindi version of land document in support of land title
11. Notarised Surety Bond in prescribed format (Annexure-IX) on non-judicial stamp paper of not less than Rs. 100/-
12. Utilization Certificate generated through EAT Module of PFMS for grants-in-aid released as 1st instalment
13. Certificate as per Annexure-VII indicating 100% expenditure of equity, term loan, and grants-in-aid
14. Detailed Status of NABL accreditation for the facility created
15. Confirmation from bank that 100% term loan has been disbursed

> **Note**: Surety Bond is **not required** to be executed by Central/State Government Departments.  
> **Leasehold Land**: In case of registered leasehold land, duration of lease should not be less than 15 years.  
> **Blacklisting**: May occur for misuse of grant-in-aid (e.g., M/s Paras Spices, M/s Vision Fresh and Frozen).

### Application Process
The application process involves the following stages:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Submit Expression of Interest (EOI) as per notices issued under the scheme] --> B[Attend pre-bid meetings as scheduled]
    B --> C[Submit detailed proposals in response to EOI or RFP for engagement of PMA]
    C --> D[Undergo technical and project approval committee review]
    D --> E[Receive approval letter from Inter-Ministerial Approval Committee (IMAC)]
    E --> F[Fulfill compliance requirements for first instalment:<br/>- Submit acceptance letter<br/>- Submit compliance documents<br/>- Submit geo-tagged photographs<br/>- Submit building plan<br/>- Provide proof of 50% equity and term loan expenditure]
    F --> G[Submit utilization certificate and related documents for second instalment]
    G --> H[Obtain NABL accreditation for food testing laboratories where applicable]
    H --> I[Submit proof of project completion and commercialization]
```

**Key Process Notes**:
- EOIs are invited periodically with specific closing dates
- Pre-bid meetings are scheduled as per EOI notices
- Technical and project approval committee review precedes IMAC approval
- Funds are released **only upon strict compliance** with approval letter terms and conditions
- NABL accreditation is required for food testing laboratories where applicable
