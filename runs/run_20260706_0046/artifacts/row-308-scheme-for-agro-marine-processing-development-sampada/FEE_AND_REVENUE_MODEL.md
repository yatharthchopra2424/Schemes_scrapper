## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the financial benefit delivered (grant amount up to ₹10 lakhs under PMFME, which is a component of SAMPADA/PMKSY).
2. **Risk-Sharing Model**: Success fees are contingent on approval, reducing upfront client risk.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Pricing scales with client size and project complexity, ensuring MSMEs and startups can access consulting support.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: SAMPADA/PMKSY has a moderate risk profile due to its rolling application basis and documented approvals (evidence shows successful DPR submissions under PMFME, a component scheme). Processing time averages 6–9 months (per DPR implementation schedule), creating a revenue lag that is managed through retainer financing.
