# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping food processing entrepreneurs, MSMEs, agri-startups, farmers, and food processing units access government grants for infrastructure development under the Scheme for Agro-Marine Processing & Development (SAMPADA). I’d like to understand your current operations and see if this scheme aligns with your growth plans—no obligation, just a diagnostic conversation.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of food processing activity are you currently engaged in or planning to start (e.g., milling, baking, dairy, fruit/vegetable processing, meat/poultry, marine products)?
2. When was your business incorporated or registered? (SAMPADA supports entrepreneurs, MSMEs, agri-startups, farmers, and food processing units.)
3. Are you seeking financial assistance for setting up new food processing infrastructure, upgrading existing facilities, or creating backward/forward linkages?
4. Do you have ownership or a registered leasehold for the land where the project will be located? (Lease duration must be ≥15 years if applicable.)
5. What is the approximate scale of your proposed project in terms of investment or capacity (e.g., MT/annum, number of units)?
6. Have you prepared a detailed project report (DPR) or feasibility study for your food processing initiative?
7. Are you compliant with basic statutory requirements such as FSSAI registration, GST, and local municipal clearances?
8. Do you have access to promoter’s contribution (minimum 20% of project cost) and/or term loan financing to complement the grant?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Food processing entrepreneurs in India often face high upfront capital costs for setting up or upgrading processing units, including machinery, civil works, and technical civil work. Limited access to affordable finance hinders value addition, post-harvest loss reduction, and market linkage development—especially for MSMEs and agri-startups.

**Scheme intro:**
The **Scheme for Agro-Marine Processing & Development (SAMPADA)**, implemented by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI), provides **grants-in-aid** under the Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana (PMKSY). Financial support is released in two instalments: 50% upon acceptance letter and compliance, and the remaining 50% upon submission of utilization certificate and verification of expenditure. The scheme leverages a total investment of Rs. 11,095.93 crore against a fund size of Rs. 4,600 crore, with benefits including access to modern infrastructure, improved supply chain, reduced post-harvest losses, increased value addition, and market linkages.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are an entrepreneur/MSME/agri-startup/farmer/food processing unit seeking financial assistance for food processing infrastructure.
- You have or can secure land ownership or a registered leasehold (minimum 15 years if leasehold).
- You can arrange promoter’s contribution (minimum 20%) and term loan financing to complement the grant.
- Your project involves setting up or expanding food processing capacity, which aligns with SAMPADA’s focus on infrastructure creation from farm gate to retail outlet.
