### Scheme for Agro-Marine Processing & Development (SAMPADA)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://mofpi.gov.in/en/Schemes/about-pmksy-scheme
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Entrepreneurs, MSMEs, agri-startups, farmers, food processing units
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Food processing
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Eligibility varies by component scheme; general eligibility includes entrepreneurs, MSMEs, agri-startups, farmers, and food processing units seeking financial assistance for infrastructure projects under the seven component schemes of PMKSY

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Grants-in-aid under various component schemes of PMKSY; financial support pattern includes two instalments: 50% upon acceptance letter and compliance, and remaining 50% upon submission of utilization certificate and verification of expenditure
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit through PFMS EAT module
- Duration of benefit: One-time (disbursed in two instalments)

#### Documents Required
1. Project report
2. Land documents (ownership/registered leasehold)
3. Bank account details
4. Financial statements
5. Incorporation/registration certificate
6. PAN of the entity
7. Details of promoters/directors
8. Quotations for plant and machinery
9. NOC from Pollution Control Board (if applicable)
10. Architectural plan/layout of the project
11. Details of technical civil work and furniture/fixture
12. Self-attested English/Hindi version of land document
13. Notarised Surety Bond (if applicable)
14. Utilization certificate of earlier grants (if any)
15. Bank statements showing payments to suppliers

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the PMKSY portal or relevant component scheme portal
2. Check eligibility and guidelines for the specific component scheme
3. Prepare required documents including project report, land documents, financial statements, etc.
4. Submit Expression of Interest (EOI) or proposal through the online portal
5. Await scrutiny and evaluation by Technical Committee and Project Approval Committee
6. Upon approval, submit detailed project report and bank details
7. Receive approval letter and fulfill pre-disbursement compliances
8. Receive first instalment (50%) after submission of acceptance letter and compliance documents
9. Complete project and submit utilization certificate, bank statements, and other required documents
10. Receive second instalment (50%) after verification of utilization and completion
