# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Scheme for Agro-Marine Processing & Development (SAMPADA)**, also known as **Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana (PMKSY)**, is a central sector scheme launched by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI), Government of India. It aims to create modern infrastructure for food processing, reduce post-harvest losses, increase value addition, and boost farmers' income. The scheme operates on a **rolling basis** with no fixed deadline, accepting applications year-round as per EOI notices and scheme guidelines. The application portal is: **https://mofpi.gov.in/en/Schemes/about-pmksy-scheme**.

### Objectives
- Creation of modern infrastructure with efficient supply chain management from farm gate to retail outlet  
- Provide a big boost to the growth of food processing sector in the country  
- Help in providing better returns to farmers  
- Double farmers' income  
- Create huge employment opportunities especially in rural areas  
- Reduce wastage of agricultural produce  
- Increase the processing level of agricultural produce  
- Enhance the export of processed foods  

### Financial Support & Fund Size
- **Total Fund Size**: ₹4,600 crore (extended till 31.03.2026)  
- **Leveraged Investment**: ₹11,095.93 crore  
- **Financial Support Type**: Grants-in-aid under various component schemes of PMKSY  
- **Pattern of Release of Funds**:  
  - 50% upon acceptance letter and compliance  
  - Remaining 50% upon submission of utilization certificate and verification of expenditure  
- **Funds Released Through**: PFMS EAT module  

### Benefits
Financial assistance in the form of grants-in-aid for:  
- Setting up food processing infrastructure  
- Technical support  
- Capacity building  
- Skill development  
- Research and development support  
- Promotional activities  
- Operational guidelines for implementation  

Non-financial benefits include:  
- Access to modern infrastructure  
- Improved supply chain  
- Reduced post-harvest losses  
- Increased value addition  
- Market linkages  

### Eligibility Criteria
Eligibility varies by component scheme. General eligibility includes:  
- Entrepreneurs  
- MSMEs  
- Agri-startups  
- Farmers  
- Food processing units  

**Target Beneficiaries**: entrepreneurs; MSME; agri-startups; farmers; food processing units  

Specific criteria are detailed in individual scheme guidelines for the seven component schemes of PMKSY.

### Required Documents
1. Project report  
2. Land documents (ownership/registered leasehold)  
3. Bank account details  
4. Financial statements  
5. Incorporation/registration certificate  
6. PAN of the entity  
7. Details of promoters/directors  
8. Quotations for plant and machinery  
9. NOC from Pollution Control Board (if applicable)  
10. Architectural plan/layout of the project  
11. Details of technical civil work and furniture/fixture  
12. Self-attested English/Hindi version of land document  
13. Notarised Surety Bond (if applicable)  
14. Utilization certificate of earlier grants (if any)  
15. Bank statements showing payments to suppliers  

### Key Caveats
- The scheme was extended till **31.03.2026** with an allocation of **₹4,600 crore**  
- Component schemes of **Mega Food Park, Creation of Backward & Forward Linkages, Human Resource & Institution - Promotional Activities, Skill Development & HACCP** have been discontinued in the 15th Finance Commission cycle with provision of committed liability  
- **Surety Bond is not required** for Central/State Government Departments  
- Lease duration for registered leasehold land should not be less than **15 years**  
- Financial assistance is subject to compliance with terms and conditions in the approval letter  

### Application Process
1. Visit the PMKSY portal or relevant component scheme portal  
2. Check eligibility and guidelines for the specific component scheme  
3. Prepare required documents including project report, land documents, financial statements, etc.  
4. Submit Expression of Interest (EOI) or proposal through the online portal  
5. Await scrutiny and evaluation by Technical Committee and Project Approval Committee  
6. Upon approval, submit detailed project report and bank details  
7. Receive approval letter and fulfill pre-disbursement compliances  
8. Receive first instalment (50%) after submission of acceptance letter and compliance documents  
9. Complete project and submit utilization certificate, bank statements, and other required documents  
10. Receive second instalment (50%) after verification of utilization and completion
