# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] **Service Identification**: Client has identified the specific FSSAI service required (e.g., standard formulation, NSF & FI approval, vegan logo endorsement, FSMP approval, import NOC/NCR, food laboratory recognition, Food Analyst examination, FBO training via FoSTaC, RTI application, CPGRAMS grievance).
- [ ] **Scientific Rationale (Standard Formulation)**: For standard formulation service, client has prepared a scientific rationale for the proposal.
- [ ] **Form-I Submission (NSF & FI Approval)**: For NSF & FI approval, client confirms the food product/ingredient is covered under FSS (NSF & FI) Regulation, 2017 and has prepared Form-I of sub-regulation (1) of regulation 4.
- [ ] **Vegan Logo Documents**: For vegan logo endorsement, client has source of ingredients and Certificate of Analysis for absence of cholesterol, lactose, and animal DNA from a NABL-accredited laboratory.
- [ ] **FSMP Data Submission**: For Foods for Special Medical Purpose (FSMP) approval, client has scientific, clinical, and epidemiological data for dietary management of a specific disease/disorder/medical condition.
- [ ] **Import Compliance (NOC/NCR)**: For import NOC/NCR, client confirms the consignment complies with FSS Act, 2006, Rules, and FSS (Import) Regulations, 2017.
- [ ] **Laboratory Accreditation (Recognition)**: For food laboratory recognition, client confirms the laboratory has accreditation under FSSAI-NABL Integrated Assessment.
- [ ] **Food Analyst Eligibility**: For Food Analyst examination, client possesses a Bachelor’s/Master’s/Doctorate in specified fields (Chemistry, Biochemistry, Microbiology, Dairy Chemistry, Agriculture Science, Animal Science, Fisheries Science, Biotechnology, Food Safety, Food Technology, Food and Nutrition, Dairy Technology, Oil Technology, Veterinary Sciences) and has not less than three years’ experience in food analysis at recognized institutions/research laboratories. Experience is calculated from date of graduation to closing date of application; experience gained before/after this period or during academic programmes (Post Graduate, Doctoral) is not considered. No upper age limit applies.
- [ ] **FBO Training Eligibility**: For FBO training via FoSTaC, client has Aadhaar Card, Mobile Number, and documents to prove eligibility/experience (as per FoSTaC portal links).
- [ ] **RTI Eligibility**: For RTI application, client is a citizen of India and has written application, application fee (₹10), and proof of identity/address.
- [ ] **CPGRAMS Eligibility**: For CPGRAMS grievance, client is a citizen of India and has personal details, grievance details, and supporting documents.
- [ ] **Registration on CPGRAMS Portal**: For CPGRAMS grievance, client has completed registration on the CPGRAMS portal.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- PAN Card
- Aadhaar Card
- Bank Statements (last 3 months)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable)
- Business Registration Certificate
- GST Registration Certificate
- ITR (last 2 years)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Scientific rationale of the proposal (for standard formulation)
- Form-I of the sub-regulation (1) of regulation 4 of FSS (NSF & FI) Reg. 2017 (for NSF & FI approval)
- Source of ingredients, Certificate of Analysis for absence of cholesterol, lactose, animal DNA from NABL accredited laboratory (for vegan logo)
- Submission of scientific, clinical and epidemiological data (for FSMP approval)
- Consignment documents complying with FSS Act, 2006, Rules, and FSS (Import) Regulations, 2017 (for import NOC/NCR)
- Scanned/pdf format of requisite documents, duly filled FORM A, and fee receipt of Rs. 1000 (for food laboratory recognition)
- Scanned copies of original degree certificates or provisional degree certificates (for Food Analyst examination)
- Proof of identity and address (for RTI application)
- Personal details, grievance details, and supporting documents (for CPGRAMS grievance)
- Aadhaar Card, Mobile Number, and documents to prove eligibility/experience (for FBOs under FoSTaC)
- Written application, application fee (₹10), and proof of identity/address (for RTI)
- Registration on CPGRAMS portal and online form submission (for CPGRAMS grievance)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be in PDF or scanned format, clearly legible.
- File names must match the document description (e.g., "Form-I_NSF_FI_Approval.pdf").
- File size must not exceed 5 MB per document unless specified otherwise.
- All certificates (degree, NABL, etc.) must be valid and not expired.
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, a certified translation must be attached.
- Declarations and forms must be signed and dated where required.
- Fee receipts must be generated from the FSSAI ePayment portal (https://epay.fssai.gov.in/) and clearly show transaction details.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required for most FSSAI services. However, for Foods for Special Medical Purpose (FSMP) approval, a detailed dossier including scientific, clinical, and epidemiological data is required. For other services like standard formulation or NSF & FI approval, a project report is not explicitly mandated but scientific rationale or Form-I serves as the core submission. **Verify with nodal officer if a formal DPR is expected for your specific service.**

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Identify Correct Form**: Use Form-I for NSF & FI approval, Form-A for food laboratory recognition, Annexure for claims, specific format for vegan logo, dossier for FSMP, or online form for Food Analyst/FoSTaC/RTI/CPGRAMS.
2. **Accuracy**: Ensure all entries match supporting documents exactly (e.g., name, address, registration numbers).
3. **Completeness**: Fill every mandatory field; leave no blanks unless marked optional.
4. **Discipline Selection (Food Analyst)**: For Food Analyst CBT/Practical, select Chemical or Biological discipline and **do not change it** until examination completion.
5. **Experience Calculation**: For Food Analyst, calculate experience strictly from date of graduation to application closing date; exclude pre/post-graduation and academic programme experience.
6. **Fee Payment**: Pay applicable fees exclusively via FSSAI ePayment portal (https://epay.fssai.gov.in/) under the correct fee category (e.g., Sample Testing Lab Recognition for lab recognition).
7. **Document Attachment**: Attach all required documents in the specified format (scanned/pdf) and label them clearly.
8. **Declaration**: Sign and date all declarations; for online forms, use digital signature or OTP verification as prompted.
9. **Portal-Specific**: For FoSTaC, register on https://fostac.fssai.gov.in; for RTI, use the designated PIO portal; for CPGRAMS, register on https://cpgramss.gov.in.
10. **Review Before Submit**: Double-check all entries and attachments before final submission.
