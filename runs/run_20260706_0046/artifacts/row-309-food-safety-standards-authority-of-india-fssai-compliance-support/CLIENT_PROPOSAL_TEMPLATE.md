## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment for the desired FSSAI service (e.g., NSF & FI approval, vegan logo, Food Analyst exam, lab recognition).
2. Document preparation and review as per FSSAI requirements (e.g., scientific rationale, Form-I, NABL-accredited test reports, educational qualifications).
3. Portal registration and application submission via the official FSSAI portal: https://fssai.gov.in.
4. Fee payment facilitation through the FSSAI ePayment portal (https://epay.fssai.gov.in).
5. Application tracking and liaison with FSSAI divisions (e.g., Science and Standards, Training, Quality Assurance).
6. Response to queries or additional information requests from FSSAI officials.
7. Post-approval support, including certificate collection and compliance guidance.

*Note: A DPR/project report is not required for most FSSAI services; however, a scientific dossier is required for FSMP approval and standard formulation requests.*

## 4. What We Need From You

Documents as per the attached checklist, specific to the service sought (e.g., for NSF & FI approval: Form-I of sub-regulation (1) of regulation 4 of FSS (NSF & FI) Reg. 2017; for vegan logo: source of ingredients, Certificate of Analysis for absence of cholesterol, lactose, animal DNA from NABL-accredited lab; for Food Analyst exam: scanned degree certificates, proof of 3 years’ experience in food analysis).
