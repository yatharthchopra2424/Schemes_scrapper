## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the non-financial benefits delivered (e.g., regulatory approvals, certifications, laboratory recognition) rather than direct financial outlays, as the FSSAI Compliance Support scheme does not provide direct grants or subsidies to food businesses.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the moderate risk profile of this scheme, given processing times ranging from 2–10 days (import NOC/NCR) to 01 year (Food Analyst certification) and variable success rates dependent on technical documentation quality and regulatory scrutiny.
3. **Transparency & Predictability**: Retainer fees cover advisory, documentation, and submission support; success fees are contingent only upon successful service delivery (e.g., approval, certification, NOC issuance) as evidenced by defined timelines in the Citizen’s Charter.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Unlike subsidy-based schemes, revenue is derived from facilitating access to FSSAI’s fee-based services (e.g., NSF & FI approval at ₹50,000+GST, Food Analyst exam at ₹2,500–5,000+GST), making success fees applicable to the consultant’s service fee, not the scheme’s financial benefit.
