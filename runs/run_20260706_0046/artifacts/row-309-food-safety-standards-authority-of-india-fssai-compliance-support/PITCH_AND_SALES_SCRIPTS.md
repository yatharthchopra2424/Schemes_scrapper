# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good [Your Name] from [Your Firm]. We specialize in helping food businesses and regulatory professionals navigate FSSAI compliance pathways—whether you're seeking approval for a new food ingredient, vegan logo endorsement, Food Analyst certification, or laboratory recognition. Our goal today is to understand your specific needs and see if FSSAI's support services align with your objectives.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of FSSAI service are you currently exploring—such as NSF & FI approval, vegan logo, Food Analyst exam, lab recognition, or import NOC?
2. Are you a food business operator, regulatory official, laboratory, or consumer seeking assistance?
3. For product approvals: Does your food product or ingredient fall under the FSS (NSF & FI) Regulation, 2017, or are you seeking vegan logo or FSMP approval?
4. For laboratory recognition: Is your lab accredited under the FSSAI-NABL Integrated Assessment?
5. For Food Analyst certification: Do you hold a relevant degree (B.Sc./M.Sc./Ph.D. in food science, chemistry, microbiology, etc.) and have at least three years of food analysis experience?
6. For FBO training: Are you looking to upskill your team via FoSTaC, and do you have Aadhaar and mobile number ready for registration?
7. What is your timeline for completing this process—are you aiming for approval within the typical 4–6 month window for NSF & FI or vegan logo?
8. Have you begun gathering documents like scientific rationale, ingredient source proof, or NABL-accredited test reports?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Target beneficiary matches: food business operator, regulatory official, consumer, laboratory, or street food vendor
- [ ] For NSF & FI approval: Product/ingredient covered under FSS (NSF & FI) Regulation, 2017
- [ ] For vegan logo: Source of ingredients, COA for absence of cholesterol/lactose/animal DNA from NABL lab ready
- [ ] For FSMP: Scientific, clinical, and epidemiological data for dietary management of specific condition available
- [ ] For import NOC/NCR: Consignment complies with FSS Act, 2006, Rules, and FSS (Import) Regulations, 2017
- [ ] For lab recognition: FSSAI-NABL Integrated Assessment accreditation in place
- [ ] For Food Analyst: Bachelor’s/Master’s/Doctorate in specified field + ≥3 years food analysis experience (experience during academic programs not counted)
- [ ] For FBO training (FoSTaC): Aadhaar, mobile number, and proof of eligibility/experience available
- [ ] For RTI/CPGRAMS: Citizen of India
- [ ] No conflict with FSSAI’s caveats (e.g., experience timing for Food Analyst, discipline selection lock-in)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like you're pursuing [specific service, e.g., vegan logo endorsement or Food Analyst certification], which FSSAI supports through structured pathways. The FSSAI Compliance Support scheme provides end-to-end guidance on eligibility, documentation, and application for services like yours—helping you avoid delays and ensure first-time approval. Shall I walk you through how we assist clients like you through this process?
