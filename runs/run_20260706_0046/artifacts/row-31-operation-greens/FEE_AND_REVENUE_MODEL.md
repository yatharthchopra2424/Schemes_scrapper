## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client under Operation Greens.
2. Transparent fee structure with clear milestones for retainer and success fee payment.
3. Risk-adjusted pricing reflecting the scheme’s moderate processing time and dependency on timely submission of complete documentation.
4. Scalable model accommodating FPOs, agri-logistics, and processing facilities across different turnover brackets.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description:** Retainer fee for consulting services related to Operation Greens scheme application preparation and submission.  
**Scheme Name:** Operation Greens  
**Implementing Agency:** Ministry of Food Processing Industries  
**Scope:** Includes eligibility assessment, document preparation, application submission, and follow-up until acknowledgment.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description:** Success fee payable upon approval of subsidy under Operation Greens scheme.  
**Scheme Name:** Operation Greens  
**Implementing Agency:** Ministry of Food Processing Industries  
**Trigger:** Disbursement of funds or formal approval letter from the Ministry.
