# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping Farmer Producers Organizations (FPOs), agri-logistics providers, and food processing facilities access government support for Tomato, Onion, and Potato (TOP) value chain development. Many of our clients in the agriculture processing sector are looking to stabilize supply and prices while reducing post-harvest losses—exactly what Operation Greens is designed to support. I’d like to understand your current operations and see if this scheme aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you currently operating as a Farmer Producer Organization (FPO), agri-logistics provider, or food processing facility focused on Tomato, Onion, or Potato (TOP) crops?
2. What is your incorporation or registration date? (Operation Greens supports initiatives implemented since November 2018 for TOP value chain development.)
3. Are you planning or already engaged in activities related to integrated value chain development, such as setting up processing facilities, agri-logistics infrastructure, or professional management systems for TOP crops?
4. What is your annual turnover or operational scale? (While there’s no strict revenue cap, the scheme targets viable projects with clear business models.)
5. Do you have access to land, infrastructure, or partnerships necessary for setting up TOP crop processing or storage facilities?
6. Are you seeking financial support to reduce post-harvest losses or stabilize market prices for TOP crops in your region?
7. Have you previously applied for any Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI) schemes, such as PMKSY or PMFME?
8. What is your timeline for launching or scaling your TOP value chain initiative?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Engaged in Tomato, Onion, or Potato (TOP) value chain activities  
- [ ] Operates as an FPO, agri-logistics entity, or processing facility  
- [ ] Initiative launched or planned after November 2018  
- [ ] Focused on long-term integrated value chain development or short-term interventions  
- [ ] Demonstrates potential to stabilize supply/prices or reduce post-harvest losses  
- [ ] Has viable project concept with clear implementation plan  
- [ ] Located in India (Pan-India scheme)  
- [ ] Not excluded under any specific ineligibility clause (none explicitly stated beyond sector focus)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like your work in the TOP value chain—particularly around [mention specific area from discovery, e.g., setting up processing facilities or agri-logistics—could be well-aligned with Operation Greens. This scheme, launched by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries with a Rs.500 crore outlay, supports exactly the kind of integrated value chain development and short-term interventions you’re pursuing. Would you like me to walk you through how it works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Farmers and agri-businesses involved in Tomato, Onion, and Potato (TOP) production face volatile prices, supply gluts or shortages, and high post-harvest losses due to inadequate processing, storage, and logistics infrastructure. This undermines farmer incomes and creates inefficiencies in the supply chain—especially for perishable TOP crops that lack coordinated value chain support.

**Scheme intro:**
Operation Greens, launched by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI) in November 2018 with a total outlay of Rs.500 crore, is a Pan-India scheme designed to promote Farmer Producers Organizations (FPOs), develop agri-logistics, establish processing facilities, and encourage professional management for the Tomato, Onion, and Potato (TOP) value chain. It operates through two components: (I) Long Term Intervention—Integrated Value Chain Development Projects and (II) Short-Term Interventions, aiming to stabilize supply and prices while reducing post-harvest losses.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in the **Tomato, Onion, or Potato (TOP) value chain**, which is the explicit focus of the scheme since November 2018.  
- Your role as an **FPO, agri-logistics provider, or processing facility** matches the **target beneficiaries** defined under the scheme.  
- Your initiative supports **integrated value chain development** (e.g., processing, storage, logistics) or **short-term market interventions**, aligning with both components of Operation Greens.  
- Your goal to **stabilize supply/prices or reduce post-harvest losses** directly corresponds to the scheme’s core objectives.
