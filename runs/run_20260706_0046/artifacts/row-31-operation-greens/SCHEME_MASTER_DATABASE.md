### Operation Greens
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Food Processing Industries
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://mofpi.gov.in/operation-greens
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Farmer Producers Organizations (FPOs); agri-logistics; processing facilities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Agriculture processing (Tomato, Onion and Potato - TOP value chain)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be engaged in development of Tomato, Onion and Potato (TOP) value chain; scheme has two components: (I) Long Term Intervention-Integrated Value Chain Development Projects and (II) Short-Term Interventions

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Rs.500 crore outlay announced in Union Budget 2018-19
- Disbursement mode: Not specified in evidence
- Duration of benefit: Not specified in evidence

#### Documents Required
1. Application Template
2. List of Crops/Clusters/Trigger Prices
3. Statement of Subsidy released
4. Guidelines (various versions)
5. Circulars (various versions)
6. Help Desk (PDF - 385 Kb)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit Portal to Claim Subsidy
2. Download Guidelines
3. Download Circulars
4. Access Help Desk (PDF - 385 Kb)
5. Review Implementation Decision of Mission Operation Greens
6. Review Decision of Technical Committee
7. Access Application Template
8. Refer to List of Crops/Clusters/Trigger Prices
9. Check Statement of Subsidy released
10. Submit Proposal via MIEWS MIS Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana Grant Release Online
