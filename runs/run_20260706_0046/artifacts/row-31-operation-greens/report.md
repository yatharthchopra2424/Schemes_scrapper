# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
Operation Greens is a subsidy scheme announced in the Union Budget 2018-19 with an outlay of Rs.500 crore to promote Farmer Producers Organizations (FPOs), agri-logistics, processing facilities, and professional management. The scheme has been implemented since November 2018 by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI) for the development of the Tomato, Onion, and Potato (TOP) value chain across Pan-India.

### Objectives
- Promote Farmer Producers Organizations (FPOs)
- Develop agri-logistics
- Develop processing facilities
- Promote professional management
- Stabilize supply and prices of Tomato, Onion, and Potato (TOP) crops
- Reduce post-harvest losses

### Application Process Flowchart
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Start: Identify TOP Value Chain Project] --> B{Check Eligibility}
    B -->|FPO/Agri-logistics/Processing Facility| C[Prepare Detailed Project Report (DPR)]
    B -->|Ineligible| Z[End: Not Eligible]
    C --> D[Submit Application via MoFPI Portal]
    D --> E[Application Review by Technical Committee]
    E --> F{Approved?}
    F -->|Yes| G[Receive Approval Letter]
    G --> H[Submit Bank Guarantee & Other Documents]
    H --> I[Disbursement of Subsidy in Installments]
    I --> J[Project Implementation & Monitoring]
    J --> K[Submit Utilization & Progress Reports]
    K --> L[Final Settlement & Closure]
    F -->|No| M[Receive Rejection Letter with Feedback]
    M --> N[Revise DPR & Resubmit]
    N --> E
    L --> M1[End: Project Completed Successfully]
```

> **Important Notes:**
> - The scheme operates under two components: Long Term Intervention (Integrated Value Chain Development Projects) and Short-Term Interventions.
> - Short Term Interventions include market intervention via subsidy for transportation and storage of TOP crops during glut situations.
> - Long Term Interventions focus on creating infrastructure like farm gate processing, agri-logistics, and consumption points.
> - Applicants must refer to the latest guidelines available at https://mofpi.gov.in/en/Schemes/operation-greens/download-guidelines-9
> - Application templates and trigger prices are available at https://mofpi.gov.in/en/Schemes/operation-greens/download-circulars-9
> - The scheme is part of the broader Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana (PMKSY) framework.
