# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a Farmer Producer Organization (FPO)
- [ ] Applicant is a Private company
- [ ] Applicant is an Individual entrepreneur
- [ ] Applicant is a Section 8 company
- [ ] Applicant is a Micro, Small, or Medium Enterprise (MSME)
- [ ] Applicant is NOT a Government entity or cooperative
- [ ] Project is for establishment of new units or strengthening of existing units in eligible sectors:
  - Dairy processing and value addition infrastructure
  - Meat processing and value addition infrastructure
  - Animal feed plant
  - Breed improvement technology and breed multiplication farm
  - Animal waste to wealth management (Agri waste management)
  - Setting up of veterinary vaccine and drugs manufacturing facilities
- [ ] Project does NOT involve procurement of land, working capital, old machineries, or vehicle for personal use
- [ ] Applicant has obtained necessary statutory clearances (as per Annexure I of Implementation Guidelines 2.0)
- [ ] Applicant has prepared a Detailed Project Report (DPR) with component-wise cost breakup, viability, and required annexures
- [ ] Applicant is not defaulter on any loan repayment
- [ ] Applicant’s account is not classified as NPA by any bank

> **Sources**: Eligibility criteria from KEY FACTS and scheme-instruction.html; excluded entities from Key Caveats; eligible activities from scheme-instruction.html and scheme-benefits.html.

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate / Registration Certificate
- PAN Card of Applicant/Promoters
- ITR (Last 3 years)
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank Statements (Last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs)
- Aadhaar Card of Promoters/Directors
- Photograph of Applicant/Promoters

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) with component-wise cost breakup, total cost, recurring cost, net income, and project viability
- Supporting documents:
  - Proof of Address
  - Copy of PAN/TIN/Aadhaar card
  - MSME certificate (if applicable)
  - Proof of land holding (ownership or lease, conversion)
  - Education certificate
  - Photocopies of training certificate
  - Income proof
  - Bank statement for last six months relevant for the project
- Change of Land Use (CLU)
- Site Plan of the project
- List of Machinery and Equipment
- Layout plan (both Civil and Machinery) of the processing facility certified by a registered architect
- All Statutory clearances:
  - Clearances from Local Administration
  - Trade Licenses
  - Consent of Establishment
  - Consent to Operate from State Pollution Control Board
  - FSSAI License required for the project
- Roadmap to ensure Quality Management of the product, product promotion, and market development

### Document Quality Check
- File size: Each document < 5 MB
- File naming: Clear and descriptive (e.g., "PAN_Card_Applicant.pdf", "Bank_Statement_Jan-Jun_2024.pdf")
- All documents must be legible and in PDF or JPEG format
- Name on documents must match exactly with application form
- Certifications must be self-attested or notarized where required
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in vernacular language, provide translated (to English or Hindi) notarized copy
- DPR must include GIS location, geo-tagging, and site map with photo

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required.**  
Standard sections to include:
- Executive Summary
- Project Description and Objectives
- Location Details (with GIS tagging, site map, and photographs)
- Land Details (ownership/lease, Change of Land Use)
- Project Components and Activities (aligned with AHIDF-eligible activities)
- Component-wise Cost Breakup (Civil, Plant & Machinery, Utilities, Pre-operative, Contingency)
- Means of Finance (Loan, Beneficiary Contribution, etc.)
- Project Viability Assessment (Technical, Financial, Market)
- Employment Generation Details
- Raw Material Sourcing Plan
- Quality Management and Product Promotion Roadmap
- Market Development Plan
- Statutory Clearances Obtained/Applied For
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan
- Implementation Schedule (Timeline)
- Annexures: Land documents, promoter details, machinery list, architect-certified layout, statutory clearances

> **Source**: Implementation Guidelines 2.0, Section 16 (Formulation of Detailed Project Report); Annexure II (Online Application Form); applicantusermanual.pdf.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Open https://ahidf.udyamimitra.in/ and click “Apply for Loan”
2. Enter applicant’s mobile number, tick “I am not a Robot”, and click “Request OTP”
3. Enter the 6-digit OTP received and click “Verify OTP” to onboard
4. Fill in Applicant Information, Promoters/Management Details, and Project Information accurately
5. Validate all required fields on the “Fill Application” tab before saving/submitting
6. Ensure mobile number and email are active and accessible for OTP and communication
7. Double-check spelling of names, PAN, Aadhaar, and bank details against uploaded documents
8. Select the correct “Category of Infrastructure” from the dropdown (Dairy Processing, Meat Processing, etc.)
9. Select correct “Type of Eligible Entity” (FPO, Private Company, Individual Entrepreneur, Section 8, MSME)
10. Save the application after validation; do not proceed to document upload until all fields are complete and verified
11. Note: The portal does not allow editing after submission to the marketplace/lender; ensure accuracy before final submit

> **Source**: Application Process from KEY FACTS; applicantusermanual.pdf (Steps 1–5); scheme-instruction.html (Online Loan Application steps).

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- After document upload, click “Submit Application to Marketplace” or “Submit Application to Preferred Lender” as per client’s choice
- Click “Submit” to check Application Status and Application Number
- Upon successful submission, application status becomes “Logged”
- Take a screenshot of the confirmation screen showing Application Number and status
- Store the Application Number securely in the case file
- Set a follow-up reminder for 7 days to check application status on the portal
- If status remains “Logged” beyond 7 days, initiate follow-up with nodal officer or helpdesk

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from the portal or lender
- [ ] Confirm sanctioned loan amount (should be up to 90% of project cost)
- [ ] Verify interest subvention approval marked on the portal by DAHD
- [ ] Ensure credit guarantee cover is applied (if MSME and loan ≤ ₹2 crore via CGTMSE, or via NABSanrakshan for 25% cover)
- [ ] Confirm disbursement of loan amount by the lender
- [ ] Verify that interest subvention is released to the lending bank (check portal for “Interest Subvention Approved” status)
- [ ] Obtain Utilization Certificate format from the bank or DAHD for interest subvention
- [ ] Track quarterly progress reports (physical and financial) to be submitted to DAHD via portal
- [ ] Ensure installation of signboard at project site: “This project is funded under AHIDF scheme of GoI”
- [ ] Mark Date of Commencement of Commercial Operation on the portal (when functionality is available)
- [ ] Prepare for success fee invoicing as per consultancy agreement

> **Sources**: Disbursement and interest subvention process from scheme-instruction.html; revised-sop-for-ahidf.pdf (Sections 6f, 6g, 6h, 7, 11); minutes of bankers meet (Nov 29, 2023) for disbursement marking and signboard; applicantusermanual.pdf (Step 9: View application details).
