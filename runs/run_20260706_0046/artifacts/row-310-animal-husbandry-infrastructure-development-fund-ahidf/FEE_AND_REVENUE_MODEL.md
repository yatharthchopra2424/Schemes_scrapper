## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the actual financial benefit derived from the scheme (loan up to 90% of project cost, 3% interest subvention, credit guarantee up to 25–85%).
2. Tiered pricing reflects client capacity and scheme accessibility — MSMEs and startups are primary beneficiaries per eligibility criteria.
3. Success fees are contingent on loan sanction and interest subvention approval, reducing client risk.
4. Scheme-specific note: Moderate risk profile due to 3–6 month processing lag for interest subvention approval (per evidence), but high success rate for viable projects once bank-sanctioned, supported by 104 sanctioned applications and ₹19,520.36 Cr sanctioned loan amount.

**Invoice For:** Success Fee – Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund (AHIDF)  
**Description:** Success fee contingent upon loan sanction and interest subvention approval under the AHIDF scheme, implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying.  
**Scheme Reference:** Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund (AHIDF), Application Portal: https://ahidf.udyamimitra.in/
