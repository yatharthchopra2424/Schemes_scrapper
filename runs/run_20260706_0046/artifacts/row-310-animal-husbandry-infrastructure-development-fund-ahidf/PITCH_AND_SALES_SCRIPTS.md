# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping animal husbandry \- \-related businesses access government funding for infrastructure projects like dairy processing, meat processing, animal feed plants, and veterinary vaccine facilities. The Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund (AHIDF) is designed specifically for entrepreneurs and enterprises in this sector to scale up operations with subsidized loans. I’d like to understand your current plans and see if AHIDF could support your goals—no obligation, just a diagnostic conversation.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of animal husbandry infrastructure project are you planning or considering? (e.g., dairy processing unit, meat processing facility, animal feed plant, IVF centre, veterinary vaccine manufacturing unit, or agri-waste management system)  
2. Have you already incorporated your business, and if so, when was it established? (AHIDF supports individual entrepreneurs, private companies, MSMEs, FPOs, and Section 8 companies)  
3. What is the estimated total project cost for your infrastructure development? (AHIDF offers loans up to 90% of project cost with no minimum or maximum limit)  
4. Are you classified as a Micro, Small, or Medium Enterprise (MSME) under government definitions? This affects beneficiary contribution (10% for Micro/Small, 15% for Medium) and credit guarantee eligibility  
5. Do you have land already secured for the project, or will you need to acquire it? (Note: AHIDF does not fund land purchase, but land must be arranged by the beneficiary)  
6. What statutory clearances have you initiated or obtained so far? (e.g., FSSAI license, pollution control consent, local authority clearances—these are required for application)  
7. Are you currently in default on any existing loan repayments? (Interest subvention is not available if the entity is a defaulter or classified as NPA)  
8. What is your expected repayment capacity? (AHIDF allows up to 10 years repayment including 2 years moratorium, with interest subvention available for max 8 years from first disbursement)

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business type: Individual entrepreneur, private company, MSME, FPO, or Section 8 company  
- [ ] Project falls under eligible activities: dairy processing, meat processing, animal feed plant, breed improvement technology, animal waste to wealth management, or veterinary vaccine/drugs manufacturing  
- [ ] No land purchase component in loan request (AHIDF excludes land, working capital, old machinery, personal vehicles)  
- [ ] Beneficiary not a defaulter on any loan and not currently NPA with any bank  
- [ ] Project is viable and not yet commercially operationalized (if pre-sanctioned loan exists)  
- [ ] Beneficiary contribution capacity: 10% (Micro/Small), 15% (Medium), or up to 25% (other enterprises) as per MSME ceilings  
- [ ] Willingness to obtain required statutory clearances (FSSAI, pollution control, trade license, etc.)  
- [ ] Mobile number accessible for OTP-based registration on https://ahidf.udyamimitra.in  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like your [mention specific project type, e.g., dairy processing unit with value-added products] aligns well with what AHIDF supports. The scheme offers loans up to 90% of project cost, a 3% interest subvention for up to 8 years, and credit guarantee coverage—all administered by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying. Shall I walk you through how this could work for your specific case?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**  
Entrepreneurs in animal husbandry infrastructure face high upfront capital costs for setting up or upgrading facilities like dairy processing units, meat plants, or animal feed mills. Access to affordable, long-term financing is limited, especially for MSMEs and individual entrepreneurs, hindering scalability, quality improvement, and market access for rural producers.

**Scheme intro:**  
The Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund (AHIDF), administered by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying under the Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, is a central sector loan scheme under the Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan. It provides term loans up to 90% of project cost from Scheduled Banks or NCDC, with a 3% interest subvention released through the lending bank for up to 8 years from first disbursement. Credit guarantee cover is available up to 25% of loan amount via NABSanrakshan Trustee Co. Pvt. Ltd. for MSME units, and up to 85% for loans up to ₹2.0 crore via CGTMSE for micro and small enterprises. There is no minimum or maximum project cost limit.

- You have [or are arranging] land and are pursuing statutory clearances like FSSAI and pollution control consent—mandatory for application  
- You are not currently in default on any loan and have repayment capacity, meeting the NPA and defaulter exclusion criteria
