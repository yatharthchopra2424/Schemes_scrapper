### Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund (AHIDF)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://ahidf.udyamimitra.in/
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Farmer Producer Organization (FPO), Private companies, Individual entrepreneurs, Section 8 companies, Micro Small and Medium Enterprises
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Dairy processing and value addition, Meat processing and value addition, Animal Feed Plant, Breed Improvement Technology and Breed Multiplication Farm, Animal Waste to Wealth Management (Agri Waste Management), Setting up of Veterinary Vaccine and Drugs Manufacturing facilities
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Government entities and cooperatives are not eligible; Farming loans are not eligible

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Loan up to 90% of estimated/actual project cost; 3% interest subvention; Credit guarantee cover up to 25% of loan amount from NABSanrakshan Trustee Co. Pvt. Ltd. for MSME units; up to 85% guarantee cover for loan amount up to Rs. 2.0 crore from CGTMSE for micro and small enterprises
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (loan disbursed by Scheduled Bank or NCDC; interest subvention released through lending bank)
- Duration of benefit: Interest subvention available for a maximum of 8 years from the date of first disbursement

#### Documents Required
1. Project Documents
2. Documents related to Applicant/Promoters

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Open https://ahidf.udyamimitra.in/
2. Enter mobile number of Applicant. Select the checkbox of “I am not a Robot” and click on “Request OTP” button.
3. Enter 6 digits OTP received on Applicants mobile number and click on ‘Verify OTP’ button. Upon verification of OTP, User will be successfully on boarded.
4. Fill Applicant information, Promoters/ Management details and Project Information.
5. Validate all required fields on Fill Application tab and save/ Submit application.
6. On Successful submission on Application details, User will navigate to Upload Documents Page. On this screen user need to upload Project Documents and Documents related to Applicant/ Promoters.
7. Submit Application to marketplace or submit application to preferred lender.
8. Click on Submit to check Application Status and Application No.
9. View application status. Once application submitted, Application status become Logged. User can view application details or download application from this screen. In case of Application withdrawal click on Withdraw button so that application will get Withdraw and User need to apply again.
