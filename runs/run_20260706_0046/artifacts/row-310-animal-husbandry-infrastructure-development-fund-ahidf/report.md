# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund (AHIDF) is a central sector scheme under the Prime Minister's AtmaNirbhar Bharat Abhiyan stimulus package with a fund size of ₹15,000 crore. It is designed to incentivized investments by individual entrepreneurs,000 crore. Implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, the scheme operates on a rolling basis across all States and Union Territories of India. The application portal is https://ahidf.udyamimitra.in/.

### Objectives
The scheme aims to:
- Increase milk and meat processing capacity and product diversification to provide greater access for unorganized rural producers to organized markets
- Increase price realization for producers
- Make available quality milk and meat products for domestic consumers
- Fulfill protein-enriched quality food requirements for the growing population and prevent malnutrition
- Develop entrepreneurship and generate employment
- Promote exports and increase export contribution in the milk and meat sector
- Make available quality concentrated animal feed at affordable prices

**Note:** Government entities and cooperatives are **not eligible** under AHIDF.

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Benefit Type | Details | Limits/Conditions |
|--------------|---------|-------------------|
| **Loan Amount** | Up to 90% of estimated/actual project cost | No minimum or maximum project cost limit; no term loan limit |
| **Beneficiary Contribution** | Margin money required | 10% for Micro/Small units, 15% for Medium Enterprises, up to 25% for other categories |
| **Interest Subvention** | 3% provided by Department | Available for max 8 years from first disbursement; released through lending bank; not allowed for land, working capital, old machinery, personal vehicle loans |
| **Credit Guarantee (NABSanrakshan)** | For MSME-covered viable projects | Up to 25% of credit facility; managed by NABSanrakshan Trustee Co. Pvt. Ltd. |
| **Credit Guarantee (CGTMSE)** | For micro/small enterprises | Up to 85% of loan amount for loans up to ₹2.00 crore |

> **> Important Caveats:**
> > - Interest subvention will not be allowed for loan sanctioned for procurement of land, working capital, old machineries, and vehicle for personal use.
> > - Interest subvention will not be provided if the beneficiary is a defaulter in loan repayment in any given year.
> > - Interest subvention will not be provided during the period an account is classified as NPA, even if the beneficiary returns to standard category.
> > - Interest subvention is provided only up to 8 years of repayment period from the date of first disbursement.
> > - Farming loans are not eligible under the scheme.
> > - The Department has authority to recall interest subvention along with interest (10% per annum) within 30 days if:
> >   - Project not completed within approved period (including extensions)
> >   - Assisted project stops commercial operations within 7 years from commencement of commercial production
> >   - Interest subvention availed by manipulation/concealment of facts or used for other purposes
> >   - Beneficiary rescinds/expires/abandons the project

### Application Process Detailed Steps
1. **Registration**: Open portal → Enter mobile number → Verify OTP
2. **Applicant Details**: Fill applicant information, promoter/management details, project information
3. **Document Upload**: Upload project documents and applicant/promoter documents after application submission
4. **Submission**: Submit to marketplace (visible to all lenders) or preferred lender
5. **Status Check**: View application status (becomes "Logged" after submission); download details or withdraw if needed
