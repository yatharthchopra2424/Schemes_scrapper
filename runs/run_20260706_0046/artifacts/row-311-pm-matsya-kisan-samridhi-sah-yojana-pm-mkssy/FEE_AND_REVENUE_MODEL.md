## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial project scoping, eligibility assessment, and proposal preparation.
2. Success fee is contingent on approval of financial assistance under PM-MKSSY.
3. Tiered pricing aligns with client scale and expected scheme value range.
4. Scheme-specific note: PM-MKSSY has a medium confidence level in evidence and a fixed implementation period (FY 2023-24 to FY 2026-27), which introduces moderate execution risk due to state/UT readiness and fund allocation dependencies; however, the central sector nature and World Bank/AFD backing support predictability in funding availability.
