# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (PM-MKSSY supports fish farmers, aquaculture entrepreneurs, and coastal communities with financial assistance for sustainable fisheries projects. I’d like to understand your work in the fisheries sector to see if this scheme aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of fisheries or aquaculture activity are you currently engaged in (e.g., fish farming, hatchery, seaweed cultivation, ornamental fisheries)?
- How long have you been operating your fisheries-related venture?
- Are you based in a specific state or union territory where you plan to undertake fisheries development activities?
- Do you have a project proposal in mind for infrastructure development, technology infusion, or value chain enhancement in fisheries?
- Are you seeking financial support for sustainable aquaculture practices, post-harvest management, or livelihood support during fishing bans/lean periods?
- Are you part of a self-help group, cooperative, or aquaculture unit requiring financial and technical support for sectoral reforms?
- Have you previously applied for any central or state fisheries schemes like PMMSY or FIDF?
- What is the scale of your operation in terms of land/water area used for fisheries or aquaculture?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Engaged in fisheries and aquaculture activities (fish farmer, fisherman, aquaculture entrepreneur, coastal community, self-help group)
- [ ] Located in a state/UT implementing PM-MKSSY (FY 2023-24 to FY 2026-27)
- [ ] Undertaking or planning fisheries development activities requiring financial/technical support
- [ ] Aligns with scheme objectives: sustainability, value chain enhancement, infrastructure development, livelihood support
- [ ] Not excluded by state/UT-specific beneficiary criteria (to be confirmed with local fisheries department)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, PM-MKSSY—a central sector sub-scheme under PMMSY with a ₹6000 crore outlay—could provide the financial support you need for your fisheries project. Let me explain how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Fish farmers and aquaculture entrepreneurs often lack access to finance for modern infrastructure, technology adoption (like RAS or biofloc), and value chain development, limiting productivity and income growth, especially during lean periods or fishing bans.

**Scheme intro:**
Pradhan Mantri Matsya Kisan Samridhi Sah-Yojana (PM-MKSSY), implemented by the Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India, is a central sector sub-scheme under PMMSY with a total outlay of ₹6000 crore (supported by World Bank and AFD). It provides financial assistance in the form of subsidies and grants for approved fisheries projects from FY 2023-24 to FY 2026-27.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are engaged in fisheries/aquaculture activities (e.g., fish farming, hatchery, seaweed cultivation) as per your disclosure
- Your project aligns with scheme focus areas: infrastructure development, technology infusion, value chain enhancement, or livelihood support
- You operate in a state/UT where PM-MKSSY is being implemented (pan-India, subject to state readiness)
- You seek financial and technical support for sectoral reforms or sustainable practices
