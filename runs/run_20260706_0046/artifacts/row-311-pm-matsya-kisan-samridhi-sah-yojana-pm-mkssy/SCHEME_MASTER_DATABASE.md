### PM Matsya Kisan Samridhi Sah-Yojana (PM-MKSSY)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://pmmsy.dof.gov.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** FY 2023-24 to FY 2026-27

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Fish farmers, fishermen, aquaculture entrepreneurs, coastal communities, self-help groups
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Fisheries and aquaculture
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Open to all states and union territories for implementation from FY 2023-24 to FY 2026-27; specific beneficiary eligibility criteria determined at state/UT level based on notified guidelines under PMMSY framework, focusing on those engaged in fisheries and aquaculture activities requiring financial and technical support for sectoral reforms

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance in the form of subsidies and grants for approved fisheries projects; total outlay of Rs. 6000 crore
- Disbursement mode: Subsidies and grants (as per central sector scheme norms under PMMSY)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (project-based)

#### Documents Required
1. Project proposal
2. Land ownership/lease documents
3. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
4. Bank account details
5. Identity proof of applicant
6. Address proof
7. Business registration documents (if applicable)
8. Technical feasibility report
9. Environmental clearance (if required)
10. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from local authorities

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Consult the respective District Fisheries Officer of the district in the state/UT where fisheries development activities are intended
2. Prepare project proposal following guidelines issued under PMMSY and PM-MKSSY framework
3. Submit proposal through the state/UT fisheries department
4. Await approval by state/UT level committees
5. Upon approval, receive financial assistance as per sanctioned amount
