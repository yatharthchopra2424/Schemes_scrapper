# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
**Scheme Name:** PM Matsya Kisan Samridhi Sah-Yojana (PM-MKSSY)  
**Scheme ID:** row-311  
**Ministry / Category:** Animal Husbandry  
**Scheme Type:** subsidy  
**Geographic Scope:** Pan-India  
**Implementing Agency:** Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India  
**Status / Deadlines:** The scheme is implemented from FY 2023-24 to FY 2026-27. Application windows and specific deadlines are determined by state/UT fisheries departments as per annual action plans and notified guidelines.  
**Last Updated:** 2026  
**Fund Size (Cr):** 6000  
**Application Portal URL:** https://pmmsy.dof.gov.in  

### Objectives
- Address inherent weaknesses in the fisheries sector through financial and technical interventions  
- Enhance long-term sustainability and resilience of the fisheries sector  
- Facilitate sectoral reforms for improved fisheries management  
- Promote private sector participation and entrepreneurship in fisheries  
- Support development of fisheries value chain including infrastructure and technology adoption  
- Strengthen socio-economic conditions of fishers and fish farmers  
- Enhance fish production and productivity through modern aquaculture practices  
- Improve post-harvest management and reduce losses  

### Financial Support & Benefits
| Benefit Category | Details |
|------------------|---------|
| Financial Support | Financial assistance in the form of subsidies and grants for approved fisheries projects under its framework, with funding pattern following the central sector scheme norms under PMMSY. Total outlay is Rs. 6000 crore, supported by the World Bank and AFD, to be utilized over four years (FY 2023-24 to FY 2026-27) for activities including infrastructure development, technology infusion, value chain enhancement, and sectoral reforms. |
| Benefits | Financial support for fisheries projects, technical assistance for sectoral reforms, promotion of sustainable aquaculture practices, development of fisheries infrastructure, enhancement of fish production and productivity, support for value addition and post-harvest management, livelihood and nutritional support during fishing bans/lean periods, employment generation in fisheries and allied activities, facilitation of private investment and entrepreneurship in the sector. |

### Eligibility Matrix
| Eligibility Criteria | Details |
|----------------------|---------|
| Geographic Eligibility | Open to all states and union territories for implementation from FY 2023-24 to FY 2026-27 |
| Target Beneficiaries | Fish farmers; fishermen; aquaculture entrepreneurs; coastal communities; self-help groups |
| Specific Beneficiary Criteria | Determined at the state/UT level based on notified guidelines under PMMSY framework, focusing on those engaged in fisheries and aquaculture activities requiring financial and technical support for sectoral reforms |
| Implementation Condition | Subject to state/UT readiness and fund allocation |
| Benefit Contingency | Benefits are contingent upon approval of project proposals by state/UT level committees |
| Funding Pattern | Financial support follows PMMSY funding patterns and norms |

### Application Process
Stakeholders (intended beneficiaries) are required to consult the respective District Fisheries Officer of the district in the state/UT where they intend to undertake fisheries development activities. Proposals are to be submitted through the state/UT fisheries department following the guidelines issued under PMMSY and PM-MKSSY framework.

### Key Caveats
> - Implementation is subject to state/UT readiness and fund allocation  
> - Benefits are contingent upon approval of project proposals by state/UT level committees  
> - The scheme period is fixed from FY 2023-24 to FY 2026-27  
> - Financial support follows PMMSY funding patterns and norms
