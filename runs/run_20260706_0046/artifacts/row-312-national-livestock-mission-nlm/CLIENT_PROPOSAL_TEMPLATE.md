## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified the National Livestock Mission (NLM) — a centrally sponsored subsidy scheme administered by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit is up to 90% of project cost for specific components like fodder block making and silage production, or up to 50% for general livestock units, with higher rates for SC/ST beneficiaries.

## 3. Our Scope of Work
- Scheme eligibility assessment and component selection (e.g., poultry, piggery, fodder, dairy)  
- Preparation of Detailed Project Report (DPR) as required by the scheme
