## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to be tied to actual subsidy disbursement, reflecting the scheme’s installment-based release structure and verification requirements.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Retainers**: Retainers reflect the effort required for DPR preparation, land verification, and multi-agency coordination, which varies by beneficiary type and state-specific guidelines.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Pricing tiers align with beneficiary categories — MSMEs, SHGs, cooperatives, and agri-startups are primary targets under NLM, making Growth and Scale tiers most relevant.
4. **Scheme-Specific Risk Note**: The National Livestock Mission has a medium confidence rating due to fund availability constraints and state-level allocation variability. Processing times are not fixed, with approvals subject to field verification and fund release cycles, increasing revenue lag risk compared to centrally processed schemes.
