## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping livestock farmers, rearers, and agri-entrepreneurs access government support for sustainable livestock development under schemes like the National Livestock Mission (NLM). Many of our clients in poultry, dairy, goat/sheep rearing, and fodder production have successfully secured subsidies to scale their operations. I’d like to understand your current livestock activities and see if this scheme could support your growth — no obligation, just a diagnostic conversation.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of livestock activity are you currently engaged in (e.g., dairy, poultry, piggery, goat/sheep rearing, fodder production)?
- How long have you been operating your livestock farm or agri-enterprise?
- Are you an individual farmer, part of a self-help group (SHG), cooperative, or registered as defined under NLM guidelines?
- Do you have valid land ownership documents or a lease agreement for the land where livestock activities are conducted?
- Have you previously applied for or received subsidies under any central or state livestock or agriculture scheme?
- Are you from a SC/ST community, or are you a woman entrepreneur in livestock farming?
- What is the approximate scale of your current livestock unit (e.g., number of animals, birds, or fodder production capacity)?
- Are you planning to expand, upgrade breeds, set up a fodder unit, or start a new livestock enterprise in the near future?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Beneficiary is an individual farmer, livestock rearer, SHG, JLG, cooperative, producer company, NGO, or MSME engaged in animal husbandry
- [ ] Involved in eligible components: poultry, piggery, goat/sheep rearing, dairy, breed improvement, fodder production (block making/silage), or livestock insurance
- [ ] Has valid land records or lease agreement where applicable
- [ ] Not availed similar benefits for the same component from other central/state schemes
- [ ] Project complies with state-specific implementation guidelines
- [ ] Priority given if: small/marginal farmer, SC/ST, woman entrepreneur, or engaged in breed improvement/fodder/poultry/piggery/goat-sheep/dairy
- [ ] Willing to complete project within stipulated time and submit utilization certificates

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like your [specific activity, e.g., dairy farming] aligns well with the National Livestock Mission (NLM), which offers subsidies of up to 50% (or up to 75%/90% for eligible categories) for setting up livestock farms, breed improvement, and fodder units. Would you like me to walk you through how it works and whether you’d qualify?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Livestock farmers and entrepreneurs often struggle with high upfront costs for setting up or expanding farms, acquiring improved breeds, establishing fodder units, or managing disease risks — limiting productivity and income growth despite strong market demand for milk, meat, and eggs.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Livestock Mission (NLM), implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying under the Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, is a centrally sponsored scheme providing financial subsidies for livestock development. It offers up to 50% subsidy on project cost for poultry, piggery, goat/sheep rearing, and dairy units; up to 75% for SC/ST beneficiaries; and up to 90% for specific components like fodder block making and silage production. Support is released in installments based on progress verification and includes technical guidance, training, market linkages, and livestock insurance.
