### National Livestock Mission (NLM)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Individual farmers, livestock rearers, livestock rearers, self-help groups (SHGs), joint liability groups (JLGs), cooperatives, producer companies, NGOs, MSMEs engaged in animal husbandry activities
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Animal husbandry, livestock farming, dairy, poultry, piggery, goat and sheep rearing, fodder production, breed improvement
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Priority to small and marginal farmers, SC/ST communities, women entrepreneurs; valid land records or lease agreements required; compliance with state-specific implementation guidelines; must not have availed similar benefits for the same component from other central or state schemes

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Subsidy up to 50% of project cost for poultry, piggery, goat and sheep rearing, and dairy units; up to 75% for SC/ST beneficiaries; up to 90% for specific components like fodder block making and silage production under Feed and Fodder Development Sub-Mission
- Disbursement mode: Installment-based (released upon verification of progress and utilization of previous funds)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (per approved project, disbursed in installments)

#### Documents Required
1. Application form (as prescribed by State Implementing Agency)
2. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
3. Proof of identity (Aadhaar, Voter ID, etc.)
4. Proof of address
5. Land ownership documents or lease agreement
6. Bank account details (passbook or cancelled cheque)
7. Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC, if applicable)
8. Passport-sized photographs
9. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from Pollution Control Board (if required)
10. Affidavit regarding correctness of information

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify the relevant component under the National Livestock Mission (e.g., poultry, piggery, fodder, breed improvement).
2. Contact the State Implementing Agency (SIA) or District Animal Husbandry Office for guidance and application forms.
3. Prepare a detailed project report including cost estimates, land details, and beneficiary information.
4. Submit the application along with required documents to the concerned District Animal Husbandry Officer or State Nodal Agency.
5. Application undergoes scrutiny and field verification by the concerned authorities.
6. Upon approval, sanction letter is issued and funds are released in installments as per progress.
7. Beneficiary must complete the project within the stipulated time and submit utilization certificates and completion reports.
