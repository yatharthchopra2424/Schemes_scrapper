## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview   Livestock Mission (NLM) is a centrally sponsored scheme implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying under the Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying. It operates on a pan-India geographic scope with rolling basis applications accepted throughout the year, subject to fund availability and state-specific allocation windows. The scheme aims to ensure sustainable development and growth of the livestock sector through financial and non-financial support.

 Objectives
The National Livestock Mission has the following core objectives:
- Ensure sustainable development and growth of the livestock sector
- Increase availability of fodder and feed to reduce scarcity
- Promote entrepreneurship and breed improvement in livestock
- Transfer technology and build capacity of stakeholders
- Provide risk management and disease control support
- Enhance production and productivity of livestock and poultry
- Support development of small ruminants, pigs, and poultry in rural areas
- Strengthen extension services and skill development in animal husbandry

 Key Caveats & Warnings
> **Subsidy Availability**: Financial support is subject to availability of funds and state-wise allocation under the mission. Applications may be delayed or rejected if state allocation is exhausted.
> 
> **Double Benefit Prohibition**: Beneficiaries must not have availed similar benefits for the same component from other central or state schemes. Duplicate claims will lead to rejection and potential recovery.
> 
> **Project Timelines**: Projects must be completed within the stipulated time frame; delays may lead to cancellation of sanction and recovery of funds already disbursed.
> 
> **Utilization Certificates**: Submission of utilization certificates and completion reports is mandatory for release of subsequent installments. Non-submission halts further funding.
> 
> **Commercial Farming Limits**: Benefits are not applicable for commercial livestock farming exceeding prescribed unit sizes under the scheme guidelines. Verify unit limits with SIA.
> 
> **Pollution Clearance**: For components like piggery, dairy, or fodder units, a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from Pollution Control Board may be required if applicable under state environmental laws.
> 
> **Land Documentation**: Applicants must have valid land records or lease agreements where applicable. Informal or disputed land holdings may lead to rejection.
> 
> **State-Specific Guidelines**: While NLM is centrally sponsored, implementation varies by state. Always refer to the respective State Implementing Agency for precise procedures, formats, and deadlines.
