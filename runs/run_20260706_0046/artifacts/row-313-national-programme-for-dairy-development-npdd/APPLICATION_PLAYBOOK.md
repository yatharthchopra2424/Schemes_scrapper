## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a State Government, Milk Union, Milk Producer Company, Dairy Cooperative, or Private Dairy Enterprise  
- [ ] Project involves setting up new dairy infrastructure or strengthening existing dairy infrastructure  
- [ ] Project relates to milk production, procurement, processing, value addition, packaging, or marketing  
- [ ] Land for the project is in possession of the implementing agency (owned or leased)  
- [ ] Project complies with technical standards and guidelines issued by DAHD  
- [ ] Financial appraisal report from a bank/financial institution is available or can be obtained  
- [ ] Environmental clearance is obtained (if applicable based on project scale and location)  
- [ ] Undertaking on compliance with scheme guidelines can be provided  
- [ ] Proposal is submitted via the official portal: https://dahd.nic.in/npdd  
- [ ] Funds are available under the scheme (subject to annual allocation and PSC approval)  

> **Note**: There is no specified turnover threshold, employee count, or age of business in the eligibility criteria. Focus is on entity type and project nature.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
**Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is mandatory** for NPDD applications as per the scheme guidelines.  

**Standard DPR Sections to Include**:  
- Executive Summary  
- Introduction and Background of the Applicant Entity  
- Project Objectives aligned with NPDD goals (quality enhancement, organized procurement, infrastructure upgrade, value addition, market access, entrepreneurship)  
- Detailed Project Description (location, layout, technology, capacity)  
- Milk Procurement Plan (source, volume, quality standards)  
- Processing and Value Addition Details (products, capacity, packaging)  
- Marketing and Distribution Strategy  
- Technical Specifications and Compliance with DAHD/BIS Standards  
- Implementation Schedule (Gantt chart)  
- Organizational Structure and Manpower Plan  
- Financial Analysis:  
  - Total Project Cost  
  - Means of Financing (equity, loan, subsidy sought)  
  - 5-year Profitability Projections  
  - Break-even Analysis  
  - Sensitivity Analysis  
- Environmental and Social Impact Assessment (if applicable)  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
- Utilization Plan for NPDD Funds (installment-wise)  
- Annexures: Land documents, NOCs, quotes, financial appraisal, etc.  

> **Note**: Confirm DPR format requirements with the nodal officer or PSC guidelines if available.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official portal: **https://dahd.nic.in/npdd** and navigate to the proposal submission section.  
2. Register or log in using the entity’s official email and mobile number linked to PAN/GST.  
3. Select “New Proposal” under NPDD and enter the scheme name exactly as: **National Programme for Dairy Development (NPDD)**.  
4. Fill in applicant details exactly as per Certificate of Incorporation and PAN (name, address, CIN, PAN, GSTIN).  
5. Provide clear, concise project title and location (village, taluka, district, state).  
6. Enter total project cost and amount of financial assistance sought (central share) as per financial appraisal.  
7. Attach all documents in PDF format, ensuring file names match the checklist and are under 5 MB each.  
8. Tick the declaration box confirming compliance with scheme guidelines and accuracy of information.  
9. Preview the entire application before final submission to avoid errors.  
10. **Do not refresh or navigate away** during final submission — wait for confirmation message.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the **sanction letter** from DAHD specifying:  
  - Approved project cost  
  - Amount of financial assistance (central share)  
  - Terms and conditions of release  
  - Installment schedule  
- [ ] Confirm that the sanctioned amount aligns with the proposal and financial appraisal  
- [ ] Prepare for first installment release by ensuring:  
  - Land is in possession (if not already verified)  
  - Required NOCs and clearances are in place  
  - Bank account for fund transfer is active and verified  
- [ ] Utilization Certificate (UC) format: Obtain prescribed GFR 12-A form or as specified by DAHD  
- [ ] Plan for physical progress reporting aligned with installment release schedule  
- [ ] Coordinate with client for timely submission of UCs and progress reports to avoid delays in subsequent installments  
- [ ] Track milestone completion against implementation schedule in DPR  
- [ ] Prepare for third-party evaluation or site inspection by DAHD or its appointed agency  
- [ ] Upon project completion, submit final UC, completion report, and asset verification documents  
- [ ] Follow up for release of final installment and closure of sanction  
- [ ] Invoice success fee as per consultancy agreement (typically 1–2% of sanctioned amount or milestone-based)  

> **Note**: Financial assistance is released in installments against utilization certificates and physical progress reports. Ensure strict compliance with reporting timelines.
