## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-based pricing**: Fees aligned with the substantial financial support available under NPDD (central government grants up to 60% of project cost, 90% for hilly and northeastern states).
2. **Risk-adjusted success fees**: Success fees reflect moderate risk due to rolling application basis and fund availability dependencies, though strong government backing reduces uncertainty.
3. **Tiered retainer structure**: Retainers scaled to client capacity for managing complex dairy infrastructure projects requiring Detailed Project Reports (DPRs), land documents, and financial appraisals.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: NPDD involves moderate processing risk due to dependency on Project Sanctioning Committee (PSC) approval and fund availability, but offers high success potential for eligible dairy enterprises with strong project proposals, as evidenced by the scheme’s ₹1,790 Cr fund size and structured installment release mechanism.
