## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping dairy enterprises and cooperatives access government funding for infrastructure and value chain development. The National Programme for Dairy Development (NPDD) supports projects like milk chilling, processing, and marketing upgrades with grants covering up to 60% of costs (90% in hilly and northeastern states). I’d like to understand your current dairy operations to see if this could be a fit.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of dairy entity are you (e.g., milk union, producer company, cooperative, private enterprise, or state body)?
- Are you planning to set up a new dairy project or strengthen an existing one (e.g., processing, chilling, packaging, or value addition)?
- What is the current stage of your project planning — have you prepared a Detailed Project Report (DPR)?
- Do you have land in possession for the proposed project, or do you have ownership/lease documents ready?
- Have you secured a financial appraisal from a bank or financial institution for the project cost?
- Are you located in a hilly or northeastern state, which may qualify for higher central funding (up to 90%)?
- What is your timeline for submitting a proposal, given that NPDD accepts applications on a rolling basis subject to fund availability?
- Are you able to commit to submitting utilization certificates and progress reports for installment-based fund release?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Problem framing:
Dairy enterprises often struggle with high capital costs for setting up or upgrading infrastructure like milk chilling units, processing plants, packaging lines, and value-added product facilities. Without financial support, many projects are delayed or scaled down, limiting milk quality, organized procurement, and market access for producers.

### Scheme intro:
The National Programme for Dairy Development (NPDD), administered by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD) under the Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, provides grants-in-aid and subsidies for dairy infrastructure projects. The central government contributes up to 60% of project cost (90% for hilly and northeastern states), with the balance borne by the state government or implementing agency. Funds are released in installments based on progress and utilization certificates.

### Why they qualify (personalised):

- You have or can prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR) — a mandatory document
