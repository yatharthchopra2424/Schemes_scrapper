### National Programme for Dairy Development (NPDD)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD), Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dahd.nic.in/npdd
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (subject to fund availability and PSC approval)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: State governments, milk unions, milk producer companies, dairy cooperatives, private dairy enterprises
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Dairy (milk production, procurement, processing, value addition, packaging, marketing)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Land must be in possession of the implementing agency before project approval; projects must comply with technical standards and guidelines issued by DAHD

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Central government contributes up to 60% of project cost (90% for hilly and northeastern states); total fund size ₹1,790 Cr
- Disbursement mode: Installments based on project progress and utilization certificates
- Duration of benefit: Released in installments across project lifecycle (not one-time)

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
2. Land ownership or lease documents
3. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from concerned authorities
4. Financial appraisal report from bank/financial institution
5. Environmental clearance (if applicable)
6. Undertaking on compliance with scheme guidelines
7. Utilization certificates and progress reports (for installment release)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. State governments or eligible entities submit project proposals to the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD)
2. Proposals are appraised by the Project Sanctioning Committee (PSC) under DAHD
3. Upon approval, sanction letters are issued specifying the financial assistance and terms
4. Funds are released in installments against utilization certificates and physical progress reports
5. Projects are monitored through periodic inspections and third-party evaluations
