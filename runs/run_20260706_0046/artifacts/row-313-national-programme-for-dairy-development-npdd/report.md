## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **National Programme for Dairy Development (NPDD)** is a centrally sponsored **subsidy scheme** implemented pan-India by the **Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD)**, under the Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying. The scheme aims to strengthen the entire dairy value chain through financial and technical support for infrastructure development, quality enhancement, and market access. With a total fund size of **₹1,790 crore**, NPDD operates on a **rolling basis**, allowing proposals to be submitted throughout the year subject to fund availability and approval by the Project Sanctioning Committee (PSC).

### Objectives
The scheme pursues the following key objectives:
- Enhance the quality of milk and milk products  
- Increase the share of organized milk procurement  
- Upgrade dairy processing infrastructure  
- Strengthen value-added milk product manufacturing  
- Improve access to markets for milk producers  
- Promote dairy entrepreneurship and employment generation  

### Eligibility Matrix
The following entities are eligible to apply for financial assistance under NPDD:

| **Eligible Entity Type**       | ** | **Description** |
|----------------------------|-----------------|
| State Governments          | Eligible to submit proposals for state-level dairy infrastructure projects |
| Milk Unions                | Cooperatives formed by milk producers at the district level |
| Milk Producer Companies    | Producer-owned companies registered under the Companies Act |
| Dairy Cooperatives         | Dairy-based cooperative societies at any level (village, district, state) |
| Private Dairy Enterprises  | Private companies engaged in milk procurement, processing, or marketing |

> **Note**: All applicants must be involved in activities related to milk production, procurement, processing, or marketing to qualify.

### Financial Support & Benefits
NPDD provides financial assistance in the form of **grants-in-aid and subsidies**. The funding pattern is structured as follows:

| **Beneficiary Type**               | **Central Government Share** | **State/Implementing Agency Share** | **Notes** |
|------------------------------------|------------------------------|-------------------------------------|---------|
| General Category States            | Up to 60% of project cost    | Minimum 40%                         | Standard funding pattern |
| Hilly and Northeastern States      | Up to 90% of project cost    | Minimum 10%                         | Enhanced central support due to geographical challenges |
| Union Territories (without legislature) | 100% of project cost       | 0%                                  | Fully funded by central government |

**Assistance is released in installments** based on:
- Submission of utilization certificates (UCs)
- Physical progress reports
- Verification by DAHD or its designated agencies

#### Key Benefits Covered
Financial support is available for:
- Setting up new dairy projects  
- Strengthening existing dairy infrastructure  
- Infrastructure for:
  - Milk chilling  
  - Processing  
  - Value addition (e.g., cheese, yogurt, ice cream)  
  - Packaging  
  - Marketing  
- Technical support for:
  - Quality improvement  
  - Training  
  - Capacity building  
- Creation of forward and backward linkages in the dairy value chain

#### Step-by-Step Breakdown:
1. **Proposal Preparation**: Eligible entities must prepare a **Detailed Project Report (DPR)** covering technical, financial, and operational aspects.
2. **Submission**: Proposals are submitted online via the official portal: [https://dahd.nic.in/npdd](https://dahd.nic.in/npdd)
3. **Appraisal**: The **Project Sanctioning Committee (PSC)** under DAHD evaluates proposals based on feasibility, alignment with scheme objectives, and compliance with guidelines.
4. **Sanction**: Upon approval, a **sanction letter** is issued specifying the quantum of financial assistance, terms, and conditions.
5. **Fund Release**: Funds are disbursed in **installments** contingent upon:
   - Submission of utilization certificates (UCs)
   - Physical progress reports verified by DAHD or state agencies
6. **Monitoring**: Projects undergo periodic inspections and third-party evaluations to ensure compliance with technical standards and timely execution.
7. **Closure**: Final utilization certificate and project completion report are submitted for scheme closure.

### Required Documents
Applicants must submit the following documents with their proposal:

| **Document** | **Purpose** | **Notes** |
|-------------|-----------|---------|
| Detailed Project Report (DPR) | Technical, financial, and operational blueprint of the project | Must include cost estimates, timelines, and expected outcomes |
| Land ownership or lease documents | Proof of land possession | **Mandatory**: Land must be in possession of the implementing agency before approval |
| No Objection Certificate (NOC) | From concerned authorities (e.g., pollution control, local body) | Required if applicable |
| Financial appraisal report | From bank/financial institution | Assesses financial viability and creditworthiness |
| Environmental clearance | If applicable under EPA, 1986 | Required for projects with potential environmental impact |
| Undertaking on compliance | With scheme guidelines | Legal commitment to follow NPDD rules |
| Utilization certificates & progress reports | For installment release (post-sanction) | Must be submitted periodically |

### Key Caveats & Conditions
> **Financial assistance is subject to availability of funds** under the scheme. Proposals are approved only if funds are available in the current financial year.

> **Projects must comply with technical standards and guidelines** issued by DAHD from time to time. Non-compliance may lead to withholding of funds or project termination.

> **Beneficiaries must submit utilization certificates and progress reports** for subsequent installment releases. Delays in submission will delay fund disbursement.

> **Land must be in possession of the implementing agency** before project approval. Proposals without clear land title or lease will be rejected.

> The scheme operates on a **rolling basis** — there is no fixed deadline, but approvals depend on PSC meetings and fund availability.

### Application Portal
All proposals must be submitted through the official portal:  
**https://dahd.nic.in/npdd**

This portal hosts guidelines, application forms, and status tracking tools.
