## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the financial benefit delivered under DIDF, which provides term loans up to ₹10 Crore per eligible Producer-Owned Institution (POI) for dairy processing infrastructure projects, with interest subvention support from DAHD.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: The scheme involves moderate risk due to its 10-year interest subvention commitment (2018-19 to 2030-31) and reliance on NABARD’s lending to NDDB/NCDC, which then on-lends to end borrowers. Processing timelines are not explicitly defined in evidence, but multi-year project implementation (e.g., infrastructure setup) suggests a longer approval and disbursement cycle, warranting mid-tier success fees.
3. **Client Capacity Matching**: The scheme targets financially viable end borrowers such as cooperative milk unions, state cooperative dairy federations, multi-state milk cooperatives, milk producer companies, and NDDB subsidiaries — entities typically falling within the Scale and Enterprise tiers due to their operational scale and asset base.
4. **Success Fee Contingency**: Success fees are contingent on successful loan sanction and disbursement under DIDF, aligning consultant incentives with client outcomes in accessing subsidized long-term financing for dairy infrastructure modernization.
