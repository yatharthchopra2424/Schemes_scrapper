# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because I specialize in helping dairy cooperatives, milk unions, and producer-owned institutions access government funding for infrastructure modernization. The Dairy Processing & Infrastructure Development Fund (DIDF) is designed specifically for entities like yours—cooperative milk unions, state cooperative dairy federations, multi-state milk cooperatives, milk producer companies, and NDDB subsidiaries—to upgrade processing facilities, add value to milk, and improve returns for farmer members. Before we explore whether this fits your goals, I’d like to understand your current operations and challenges.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of dairy institution are you (e.g., milk union, federation, producer company, NDDB subsidiary)?  
2. When was your organization incorporated or registered?  
3. Are you currently financially viable with positive net worth and up-to-date audits without adverse observations?  
4. Do you have any outstanding defaults to banks or financial institutions?  
5. Are your dues to producer members current, or do they exceed four payment periods?  
6. Are you planning to modernize milk processing facilities, create value-added product units, or upgrade milk chilling infrastructure?  
7. Do you have a detailed project report (DPR) or clear plan for the infrastructure you wish to develop?  
8. Are you willing to contribute your share (typically 20% of project cost) as end borrowers’ contribution under the scheme?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is a cooperative milk union, state cooperative dairy federation, multi-state milk cooperative, milk producer company, or NDDB subsidiary  
- [ ] Organization is financially viable and willing to avail funds  
- [ ] Positive net worth  
- [ ] No defaults to banks/financial institutions  
- [ ] Audit of accounts is up-to-date with no adverse observations or disclaimers  
- [ ] Outstanding dues to producer members do not exceed four payment periods  
- [ ] Project involves modernization of milk processing, value-added products, milk chilling, electronic milk testing, or related dairy sector activities  
- [ ] End borrower’s contribution (20%) can be arranged  
- [ ] Project aligns with DIDF objectives: optimizing value realization for milk producers, improving quality milk supply, strengthening producer-owned institutions  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your organization meets the core eligibility for the Dairy Processing & Infrastructure Development Fund (DIDF), which offers long-term loans at concessional interest rates for dairy infrastructure upgrades. Let me walk you through how this scheme works and the specific benefits it could deliver for your modernization plans.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Dairy cooperatives and producer-owned institutions often struggle with outdated processing facilities, limited capacity for value-added products, and inefficient milk chilling infrastructure. This leads to lower realization for milk producers, inconsistent quality supply to consumers, and weakened market position in the organized dairy sector—especially when competing with private players who have better access to capital for modernization.

**Scheme intro:**
The Dairy Processing & Infrastructure Development Fund (DIDF), administered by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD), Government of India, provides financial assistance in the form of loans for modernization and creation of new milk processing facilities, manufacturing units for value-added products, milk chilling infrastructure, electronic milk testing equipment, and related dairy sector activities. The scheme has a total outlay of ₹11,184 crore, with NABARD extending loans to NDDB and NCDC at 6% per annum, which is then on-lent to eligible end borrowers at up to 6.5% per annum. DAHD provides interest subvention of ₹1,167 crore over 10 years (2018-19 to 2030-31) to reduce the effective cost of borrowing.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a cooperative milk union, state cooperative dairy federation, multi-state milk cooperative, milk producer company, or NDDB subsidiary — all explicitly listed as eligible end borrowers  
- Your audits are up-to-date with no adverse observations, meeting the financial viability criterion  
- You have no defaults to financial institutions and producer dues are within four payment periods  
- Your project aims to modernize processing or add value-added capacity, aligning with DIDF’s investment activities
