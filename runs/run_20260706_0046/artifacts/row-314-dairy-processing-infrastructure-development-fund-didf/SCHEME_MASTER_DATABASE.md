### Dairy Processing & Infrastructure Development Fund (DIDF)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD), Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nddb.coop/services/dairy-development/didf
- **Current Status:** Closed
- **Window:** 2018-19 to 2030-31 with spill over to first quarter of 2031-32

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Co-operative Milk Unions, State Cooperative Dairy Federations, Multi-State Milk Cooperatives, Milk Producer Companies, NDDB subsidiaries
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Dairy sector
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Financially viable, willing to avail funds, fulfill eligibility criteria as per Operation guidelines of DIDF; positive net worth; no defaults to banks/financial institutions; up-to-date audit without adverse observations; outstanding dues to producer members not exceeding four payment periods

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Total outlay of Rs 11184 Crore during 2020-21, of which Rs 8004 crore is loan from NABARD to NDDB and NCDC for end borrowers
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (NABARD extends loan to NDDB and NCDC at 6% per annum repayable at quarterly rests; interest rate to end borrower up to 6.5% per annum)
- Duration of benefit: Loan repayment period with interest subvention for 10 years (2018-19 to 2030-31 with spill over to first quarter of 2031-32)

#### Documents Required
1. Sanction letter of the Working Capital Loan along with terms and conditions
2. Disbursement letter
3. Copy of loan agreement
4. Application form (prescribed format)
5. Interest Certificate from the Bank/Financing Institution
6. Relevant Bank Statement
7. Stock Statement of the conserved commodities
8. Request for release of interest subvention (monthly)
9. Proof of payment of procurement price to farmers
10. Monthly reports with day-wise details on opening, addition, reduction and closing balance of conserved commodities and milk processing/operations reports
11. Audited annual accounts for the last three financial years
12. Month-wise details of milk procurement, liquid milk sale and milk disposed in various products
13. Month-wise projected details including projected procurement for the financial year of the working capital loan drawl and repayment

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. End borrowers submit proposals through NDDB or NCDC after meeting eligibility criteria
2. Application submitted to NDDB in prescribed format for availing interest subvention within 45 days from the date of sanction
3. Submit sanction letter issued by the Bank/FI of Working Capital Loan and copy of loan agreement along with the application
4. NDDB verifies from the concerned Bank/FI and approves the proposal for interest subvention
5. For claiming interest subvention, submit “Request for release of interest subvention” to NDDB every month in prescribed format, enclosing Interest Certificate from Bank/Financing Institution, relevant Bank Statement and Stock Statement of conserved commodities
6. Outstanding loan reconciled by Bank/Financing Institution with stock of eligible conserved commodities
7. NDDB releases eligible interest subvention on back ended basis every month to FI for onward transfer to working capital loan account of PA
8. For prompt and timely repayment, additional 2% per annum interest subvention payable at end of loan repayment period
