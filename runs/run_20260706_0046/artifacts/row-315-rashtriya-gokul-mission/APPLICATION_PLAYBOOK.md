## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Beneficiary type: Individual farmer, livestock rearer, dairy cooperative, self-help group, gaushala, NGO, or state livestock development board  
- [ ] Engaged in bovine breeding and development activities  
- [ ] Working with indigenous bovine breeds (preference for Gir, Sahiwal, Red Sindhi, Tharparkar, Rathi, Kankrej, Hariana, Ongole, or others)  
- [ ] Submitting a detailed project proposal outlining breed improvement, infrastructure development, and training components  
- [ ] Possessing valid land documents or lease agreement for the project site  
- [ ] Having a certificate of registration (if applicable: for NGOs, cooperatives, gaushalas)  
- [ ] Providing Aadhaar and PAN of the beneficiary  
- [ ] Maintaining a valid bank account for fund transfer  
- [ ] Submitting past performance report (if applicable)  
- [ ] Project focused on conservation and development of indigenous bovine breeds, enhancement of milk production, upgradation of nondescript population, distribution of disease-free bulls, establishment of Gokul Grams, promotion of professional farm management, organization of milk yield competitions, or strengthening of bull mother farms  

> **Note**: The scheme operates on a rolling basis with proposals accepted throughout the year based on state annual action plans. No turnover, employee count, or age-of-business thresholds are specified in the evidence.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a **detailed project proposal** is required (as per evidence under "Required Documents" and "Application Process"). It must include:  
- Objectives aligned with scheme goals (conservation of indigenous breeds, milk yield enhancement, etc.)  
- Details of indigenous bovine breeds to be used  
- Infrastructure plan (e.g., for Gokul Grams, semen stations, training centers)  
- Budget estimate and financial plan  
- Expected outcomes (progeny numbers, milk yield improvement, training sessions conducted)  
- Implementation timeline  
- Monitoring and evaluation plan  

> **Note**: While not explicitly called a "DPR", the project proposal must be detailed and technically sound. Verify format requirements with the State Implementing Agency (SIA) or nodal officer.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the official application form or proposal template from the State Implementing Agency (SIA) portal, as the central portal (dahd.nic.in) routes applications through SIAs.  
2. Fill all fields in block letters; avoid overwriting or correction fluid.  
3. Ensure the project title clearly reflects indigenous breed development (e.g., “Establishment of Gokul Gram for Gir Breed Conservation”).  
4. In the beneficiary details section, enter Aadhaar and PAN exactly as per documents.  
5. Specify the indigenous breed(s) in the livestock details table with quantity and purpose (breeding, milk production, etc.).  
6. Attach all scheme-specific and universal documents as per the checklist.  
7. Declarations must be signed by the authorized signatory with date and official stamp (if applicable).  
8. Verify that the budget matches the financial plan and does not exceed 50% subsidy limit for eligible components (e.g., indigenous bovine procurement, Gokul Gram setup).  

> **Portal Note**: Applications are submitted to the **State Implementing Agency (SIA)** or **State Livestock Development Board**, which forwards them to DAHD for appraisal. The central portal (https://dahd.nic.in/rashtriya-gokul-mission) provides guidelines and status tracking.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All sections of the project proposal completed: objectives, breed details, infrastructure, budget, outcomes, timeline  
- [ ] Indigenous breed(s) clearly specified and eligible (Gir, Sahiwal, etc.; exotic/crossbred excluded)  
- [ ] Budget estimate realistic and aligned with 50% subsidy norm for eligible items  
- [ ] Land documents validate project site availability  
- [ ] Registration certificate attached for NGOs, cooperatives, gaushalas  
- [ ] Aadhaar and PAN match across all documents  
- [ ] Bank details correct and active  
- [ ] Declarations signed and dated  
- [ ] File names consistent and PDF format  
- [ ] Total file size of attachment set < 25 MB (if uploading via portal)  
- [ ] Two senior consultants have reviewed for completeness and accuracy

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify approval letter from DAHD via SIA, specifying sanctioned amount and installment schedule  
- [ ] Confirm that subsidy does not exceed 50% of project cost for eligible components (indigenous bovine procurement, Gokul Gram setup, etc.)  
- [ ] Utilize funds strictly as per approved budget and project components  
- [ ] Submit progress reports and utilization certificates (UCs) after each installment as per schedule  
- [ ] Maintain records of bovine progeny, milk yield, and training activities for monitoring  
- [ ] Ensure exotic/crossbred animals are not used for breed procurement under subsidy  
- [ ] Complete project within stipulated timeline as per administrative approval  
- [ ] Submit final completion report and UC for final fund release  
- [ ] Verify receipt of sanctioned amount in bank account  
- [ ] Prepare utilization certificate and success fee invoice for client  
- [ ] Archive all approvals, reports, and financial records for audit compliance  

> **Note**: Funds are released in installments based on submission of utilization certificates and progress reports. Final release is contingent on verification of completion and outcomes.
