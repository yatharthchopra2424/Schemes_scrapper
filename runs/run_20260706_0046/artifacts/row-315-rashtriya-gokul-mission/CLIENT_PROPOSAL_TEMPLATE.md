## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified Rashtriya Gokul Mission — a subsidy scheme administered by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying — for which your business appears eligible.
