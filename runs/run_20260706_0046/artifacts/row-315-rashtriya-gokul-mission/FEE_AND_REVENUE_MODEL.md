## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the financial benefit delivered, with success fees calibrated to the scheme’s subsidy intensity (up to 50% of project cost for key components like indigenous bovine procurement and Gokul Gram establishment).
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees account for moderate implementation risk due to the requirement for utilization certificates, progress reports, and adherence to indigenous breed eligibility — non-compliance can delay or claw back funds.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: Retainer models are scaled to client capacity; smaller beneficiaries (e.g., individual farmers, SHGs) may opt for success-only engagements, while cooperatives and gaushalas, NGOs, and state boards typically engage via retainer for proposal development and compliance management.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Rashtriya Gokul Mission has a rolling application basis with no fixed deadlines, but fund release is contingent on state annual action plans and utilization certificate submission. Processing timelines typically range from 6–12 months from proposal approval to final disbursement, introducing revenue lag. Success is well-documented for eligible indigenous breed projects, supporting moderate-to-high confidence in fee realization upon compliance.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee contingent upon approval and release of financial assistance under the Rashtriya Gokul Mission for eligible components including procurement of indigenous bovines (up to 50% subsidy), establishment of Gokul Grams (up to 50% of project cost), training programs, and infertility/fertility camps.  
**Scheme**: Rashtriya Gokul Mission  
**Implementing Agency**: Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying  
**Application Portal**: https://dahd.nic.in/rashtriya-gokul-mission
