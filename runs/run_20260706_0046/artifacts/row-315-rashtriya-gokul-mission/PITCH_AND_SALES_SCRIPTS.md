## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping livestock farmers, dairy cooperatives, and gaushalas access government support for indigenous bovine breed development. The Rashtriya Gokul Mission is designed specifically to strengthen India’s native cattle wealth — whether you're rearing Gir, Sahiwal, or Tharparkar, or looking to upgrade your herd with elite germplasm. I’d like to understand your current breeding activities to see if this scheme could support your goals.

### Discovery Questions
- What indigenous bovine breeds are you currently working with (e.g., Gir, Sahiwal, Red Sindhi, Tharparkar, Rathi, Kankrej, Hariana, Ongole)?
- Are you an individual farmer, livestock rearer, dairy cooperative, self-help group, gaushala, NGO, or state livestock development board?
- Do you have a clear plan for breed improvement, such as establishing a Gokul Gram, procuring indigenous bovines, or organizing fertility/infertility camps?
- Do you possess land documents or a lease agreement for the proposed project site?
- Have you prepared a detailed project proposal outlining objectives, breed details, infrastructure, budget, and expected outcomes?
- Are you able to provide Aadhaar, PAN, bank account details, and registration certificate (if applicable)?
- Have you previously utilised any government livestock schemes, and do you have past performance reports available?
- What is your expected timeline for project completion, and are you prepared to submit utilisation certificates and progress reports for fund release?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many farmers and livestock rearers struggle to afford the high costs of setting up breed improvement infrastructure, procuring elite indigenous bovines, or organizing fertility camps — despite having the expertise and land. This limits genetic progress and milk productivity, especially when working with native breeds that are vital to India’s dairy heritage.

**Scheme intro:**
The Rashtriya Gokul Mission, implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying under the Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, provides financial assistance in the form of grants-in-aid and subsidies. It supports establishment of Gokul Grams (up to 50% of project cost), procurement of indigenous bovines (up to 50% subsidy), training programs, and infertility/fertility camps. The scheme has a fund size of ₹2,400 crore and accepts proposals on a rolling basis throughout the year via state implementing agencies.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You work with eligible indigenous breeds such as Gir, Sahiwal, Red Sindhi, Tharparkar, Rathi, Kankrej, Hariana, or Ongole  
- You are engaged in bovine breeding and development activities as an individual farmer, cooperative, gaushala, NGO, or similar entity  
- You can submit a detailed project proposal including breed details, infrastructure plan, budget, and expected outcomes  
- You have or can arrange land documents and essential KYC (Aadhaar, PAN, bank details) and registration certificates
