### Rashtriya Gokul Mission
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** SubsCategory:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://dahd.nic.in/rashtriya-gokul-mission
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (proposals can be submitted throughout the year based on annual action plans of states)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Individual farmers, livestock rearers, dairy cooperatives, self-help groups, gaushalas, NGOs, state livestock development boards
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Animal husbandry, dairy, bovine breeding and development
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be engaged in bovine breeding and development activities; preference for those working with indigenous breeds (Gir, Sahiwal, Red Sindhi, Tharparkar, Rathi, Kankrej, Hariana, Ongole, etc.); must submit a detailed project proposal outlining breed improvement, infrastructure development, and training components

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to 50% subsidy on project cost for establishment of Gokul Grams; up to 50% subsidy for procurement of indigenous bovines; financial assistance for training programs, infertility/fertility camps, semen stations, IVF labs, embryo transfer technology, and genomic selection
- Disbursement mode: Released through state implementing agencies based on approved annual action plans and utilization certificates (reimbursement-based)
- Duration of benefit: One-time per approved project (funds released in installments upon milestones)

#### Documents Required
1. Project proposal with objectives and implementation plan
2. Details of indigenous bovine breeds to be used
3. Land documents or lease agreement for project site
4. Budget estimate and financial plan
5. Certificate of registration (for NGOs, cooperatives, gaushalas)
6. Aadhaar and PAN of the beneficiary
7. Bank account details
8. Past performance report (if applicable)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Prepare a detailed project proposal including objectives, breed details, infrastructure plan, budget, and expected outcomes.
2. Submit the proposal to the State Implementing Agency (SIA) or State Livestock Development Board.
3. The SIA forwards the proposal to the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD) for appraisal.
4. Upon approval by DAHD, funds are released in installments to the SIA.
5. The beneficiary implements the project and submits progress reports and utilization certificates.
6. Final release is made after verification of completion and outcomes.
