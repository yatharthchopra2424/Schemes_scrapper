## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Rashtriya Gokul Mission (RGM)** is a central sector scheme launched by the Government of India under the **Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD)**, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying. It is designed to conserve and develop indigenous bovine breeds through financial and technical support. The scheme operates on a **pan-India** basis with **rolling applications** throughout the year, aligned with state annual action plans. The total fund size is **₹2,400 crore**, and the scheme is implemented via **State Implementing Agencies (SIAs)**.

**Application Portal**: [https://dahd.nic.in/rashtriya-gokul-mission](https://dahd.nic.in/rashtriya-gokul-mission)  
**Contact**: Email: dahd@nic.in | Helpline: 011-23382401  
**Last Updated**: 2024  
**Scheme ID**: row-315  
**Status**: Active, rolling basis

### Objectives
The Rashtriya Gokul Mission aims to achieve the following objectives:

- Conservation and development of indigenous bovine breeds  
- Enhancement of milk production and productivity of bovines  
- Upgradation of nondescript bovine population using elite indigenous breeds  
- Distribution of disease-free high genetic merit bulls for natural service  
- Establishment of integrated indigenous cattle development centres (Gokul Grams)  
- Promotion of indigenous bovine breeding through professional farm management and superior nutrition  
- Organization of milk yield competitions and bovine fairs to motivate farmers  
- Strengthening of bull mother farms to conserve indigenous breeds  

> **Key Takeaway**: The mission focuses on **indigenous breed conservation** and **productivity enhancement** through integrated interventions in breeding, infrastructure, training, and market linkages.

---

### Eligibility Matrix
The scheme is open to a wide range of entities engaged in bovine breeding and development. Preference is given to those working with recognized indigenous breeds.

| **Eligible Beneficiary** | **Preference Criteria** | **Notes** |
|--------------------------|-------------------------|---------|
| Individual farmers | Working with indigenous breeds (Gir, Sahiwal, Red Sindhi, Tharparkar, Rathi, Kankrej, Hariana, Ongole, etc.) | Must submit project proposal |
| Livestock rearers | Engaged in breeding and rearing of indigenous bovines | Priority to breed conservation activities |
| Dairy cooperatives | Involved in milk production and breed improvement | Must include training and infrastructure components |
| Self-help groups (SHGs) | Working on livestock-based livelihoods | Preference for women-led SHGs |
| Gaushalas | Engaged in shelter and breeding of indigenous cattle | Must have land and basic infrastructure |
| NGOs | Registered entities working in animal husbandry | Must submit registration certificate |
| State Livestock Development Boards | Government bodies overseeing livestock development | Act as intermediaries for fund disbursement |

> **Critical Caveat**: Subsidy is **limited to indigenous breeds only**. Exotic or crossbred animals are **not eligible** for breed procurement support under this scheme.

### Financial Support & Benefits
Financial assistance is provided as **grants-in-aid and subsidies** for specific components. Funds are released in installments through State Implementing Agencies (SIAs) based on approved annual action plans and utilization certificates.

| **Component** | **Financial Support** | **Details** |
|---------------|------------------------|-----------|
| Establishment of Gokul Grams | Up to **50% of project cost** | Integrated indigenous cattle development centres |
| Procurement of indigenous bovines | Up to **50% subsidy** | Limited to recognized indigenous breeds |
| Training programs | Full or partial support | Capacity building of farmers and technicians |
| Infertility/fertility camps | Financial assistance | Organization and execution support |
| Milk yield competitions | Support for organization | Incentivizing farmers through rewards |
| Semen stations | Financial assistance | For production and distribution of semen |
| In-vitro fertilization (IVF) labs | Support for setup | Advanced breeding technologies |
| Embryo transfer technology | Financial assistance | For rapid breed multiplication |
| Genomic selection | Support for implementation | Precision breeding using DNA markers |

#### Non-Financial Benefits
- Technical guidance and breed improvement advisory  
- Access to superior germplasm (semen, embryos, live animals)  
- Training and capacity building  
- Market linkages through fairs and competitions  
- Monitoring and evaluation support  

> **Important**: Assistance is **released only after submission of utilization certificates and progress reports**. Final disbursement is contingent on verification of project completion and outcomes.

### Required Documents
Applicants must submit a comprehensive dossier to the State Implementing Agency (SIA). All documents must be self-attested where applicable.

| **Document** | **Purpose** | **Notes** |
|-------------|-----------|---------|
| 1. Project proposal with objectives and implementation plan | Core application document | Must include breed details, infrastructure, budget, timeline, expected outcomes |
| 2. Details of indigenous bovine breeds to be used | Breed specification | Must specify recognized breeds (e.g., Gir, Sahiwal) |
| 3. Land documents or lease agreement for project site | Site validation | Ownership or legal possession proof |
| 4. Budget estimate and financial plan | Cost justification | Must break down capital and recurring costs |
| 5. Certificate of registration (for NGOs, cooperatives, gaushalas) | Legal entity proof | NGOs: 12A/80G; Cooperatives: bylaws; Gaushalas: trust/society regn. |
| 6. Aadhaar and PAN of the beneficiary | Identity and tax verification | Mandatory for all individual beneficiaries |
| 7. Bank account details | Fund transfer mechanism | Must be in name of beneficiary/entity |
| 8. Past performance report (if applicable) | Track record proof | For repeat applicants or expanding projects |

> **Warning**: Incomplete documentation will lead to **rejection or delay** in appraisal. Ensure all documents are current and legible.

### Application Process Flow
The application follows a structured workflow from preparation to final fund release. The process involves coordination between the beneficiary, State Implementing Agency (SIA), and the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD).

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Start: Prepare Detailed Project Proposal] --> B[Include objectives, breed details, infrastructure, budget, outcomes]
    B --> C[Submit Proposal to State Implementing Agency (SIA) or State Livestock Development Board]
    C --> D[SIA forwards proposal to DAHD for appraisal]
    D --> E{DAHD Appraisal}
    E -->|Approved| F[Funds released in installments to SIA]
    E -->|Not Approved| G[Revise and resubmit based on feedback]
    G --> C
    F --> H[Beneficiary implements project]
    H --> I[Submit progress reports and utilization certificates]
    I --> J{Verification by SIA/DAHD}
    J -->|Satisfactory| K[Next installment released]
    J -->|Unsatisfactory| L[Withhold funds; request resubmission]
    L --> H
    K --> M[Project completion]
    M --> N[Submit final report and utilization certificate]
    N --> O{Final verification}
    O -->|Approved| P[Final fund release]
    O -->|Not Approved| Q[Revise report; resubmit]
    Q --> N
    P --> End[Scheme Closed]
```

> **Process Notes**:
> - Applications are accepted on a **rolling basis** throughout the year.
> - Proposals must align with the **state’s annual action plan**.
> - DAHD appraisal includes technical, financial, and feasibility review.
> - Fund release is **tranche-based**, tied to milestone completion and UC submission.
> - Beneficiaries must maintain records of **bovine progeny and milk yield** for monitoring.

---

### Key Caveats and Warnings
> - **Subsidy is limited to indigenous breeds only**; exotic/crossbred animals ineligible for procurement support.  
> - **Funds released only after submission of utilization certificates and progress reports** — no advance without UC.  
> - Projects must be completed within the **stipulated timeline** as per administrative approval.  
> - Beneficiaries must maintain records of **bovine progeny and milk yield** for mandatory monitoring.  
> - Preference is given to proposals with clear **breed improvement, infrastructure, and training components**.  
> - Delay in UC submission can lead to **withholding of subsequent installments**.  
> - All beneficiaries must register on the **DAHD portal** or route through SIA for tracking.
