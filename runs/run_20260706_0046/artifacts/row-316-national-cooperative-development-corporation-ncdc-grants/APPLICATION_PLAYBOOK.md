# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Cooperative registered or deemed to be registered under the Cooperative Societies Act, 1912 or under the Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 1984 or under any other law with respect to cooperative societies for the time being in force in any State
- [ ] Engaged in any of the activities mandated to NCDC (including but not limited to: agricultural marketing, inputs, processing, storage, cold chain, marketing of agriculture produce, supply of seeds, fertilizers, other agricultural inputs, dairy, livestock, handlooms, sericulture, poultry, fishery, scheduled caste & scheduled tribes, women cooperatives, FPO, FFPO, PACS, etc.)
- [ ] For direct funding: cooperative should have been in operation for not less than 3 years
- [ ] For direct funding: cooperative should have positive net worth
- [ ] For direct funding: not less than 100% paid up share capital (i.e., no erosion in paid-up share capital)
- [ ] For direct funding: positive net worth
- [ ] For direct funding: no erosion in paid-up share capital

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
- PAN of the entity
- ITR (Income Tax Returns)
- GST registration certificate
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable)
- Audited financial statements (last 2 years)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Project report / Detailed Project Report (DPR)
- Board resolution for loan application
- List of promoters/directors
- Proof of address
- Net worth certificate
- Shareholding pattern
- No objection certificate from concerned authority (if applicable)
- Land documents / property papers (if applicable)
- Quotations for machinery/equipment (if applicable)
- Technical feasibility report (if applicable)
- Environmental clearance (if applicable)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and in PDF format
- File size for each document should not exceed 5 MB
- Document names must match the entity's legal name as per PAN and registration certificate
- Scanned copies must be clear and certified where required
- All documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, provide certified translation
- Bank statements must show the account holder name matching the cooperative's name
- PAN card must be valid and not expired
- Audited financial statements must be signed and stamped by a Chartered Accountant

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is required for this scheme as per the evidence.  
Standard sections to include:
- Executive Summary
- Project Description and Objectives
- Background of the Cooperative
- Technical Details (process flow, technology used)
- Market Analysis and Demand Assessment
- Financial Projections (cost estimates, revenue model, profitability)
- Implementation Schedule
- Management and Organizational Structure
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan
- Environmental and Social Impact Assessment (if applicable)
- Annexures (quotations, approvals, land documents, etc.)

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the common loan application form from the NCDC website: https://www.ncdc.in
2. Select the appropriate activity/purpose and loan type (Term Loan, Investment Loan, or Working Capital Loan) as per the project
3. Fill all fields in the form completely; leave no blank entries
4. Attach all required supporting documents as per the checklist
5. Ensure the cooperative’s name, PAN, and address match exactly across all documents
6. Clearly state the loan amount requested and the purpose of funds
7. Declarations must be signed by authorized signatories (Chairman/Secretary) with date and place
8. Verify that the form is filled in English or Hindi only

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All fields in the application form are complete and accurate
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents are attached and legible
- [ ] Universal documents (PAN, ITR, GST, bank statements, Udyam) are present and valid
- [ ] Declarations are signed and dated
- [ ] Bank account details are correct and match the beneficiary name
- [ ] Net worth certificate is recent (not older than 6 months)
- [ ] Shareholding pattern reflects current ownership
- [ ] No duplicate or missing documents
- [ ] File sizes are within limits and naming convention followed

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed application form along with all documents to the NCDC office (regional or head office as per jurisdiction)
- Obtain a receipt or acknowledgment of submission (physical or digital)
- Take a screenshot of the submission confirmation if submitted online
- Store the application reference number securely in the case file
- Set follow-up reminders for Day 25 and Day 35

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from NCDC
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned amount matches the requested amount
- [ ] Verify if subsidy component is included (subject to GOI availability; otherwise equivalent loan in lieu of subsidy)
- [ ] Prepare and submit utilization certificate as per NCDC guidelines
- [ ] Ensure timely utilization of funds for the approved purpose
- [ ] Maintain records of all invoices and expenditure related to the project
- [ ] Coordinate with NCDC for any post-approval compliance or reporting requirements
- [ ] Follow up on success fee invoice as per consultancy agreement (if applicable)
