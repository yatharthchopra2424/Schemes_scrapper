## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client.
2. Transparent fee structure with no hidden charges.
3. Retainer covers initial assessment, documentation, and application submission.
4. Success fee is contingent on approval and disbursement of NCDC financial assistance.
   - Scheme-specific note: NCDC grants involve credit-linked subsidies and loans with liberal assistance up to 90-95% of project cost. Processing time is typically 30–60 days for working capital loans and longer for term loans, introducing moderate revenue lag risk. Success is highly dependent on cooperative eligibility (3+ years operation, positive net worth) and subsidy availability from GOI, which affects predictability.
