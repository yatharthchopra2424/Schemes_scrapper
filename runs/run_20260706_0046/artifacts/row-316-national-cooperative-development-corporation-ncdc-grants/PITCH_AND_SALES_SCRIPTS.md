# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping cooperative societies access government financial support. The National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC) Grants provide term loans, investment loans, working capital loans, and credit-linked subsidies specifically for cooperatives engaged in agriculture, dairy, fisheries, handlooms, and other allied activities. I’d like to understand your cooperative’s current activities and funding needs to see if this scheme aligns with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of cooperative society are you registered as (e.g., dairy, fishery, handloom, PACS, FPO, tribal, women’s)?  
2. Under which Act is your cooperative registered (Cooperative Societies Act, 1912; Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 1984; or State-specific Act)?  
3. How long has your cooperative been in operation?  
4. What is your current net worth status—is it positive and equal to or greater than 100% of paid-up share capital?  
5. What specific activity or project are you seeking funding for (e.g., infrastructure, working capital, equipment purchase)?  
6. Do you have a detailed project report (DPR) prepared for this initiative?  
7. Are your audited financial statements up to date?  
8. Have you previously availed of any government loan or subsidy for cooperative development?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Registered under Cooperative Societies Act, 1912 or Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 1984 or State cooperative law  
- [ ] Engaged in an activity mandated to NCDC (agriculture, dairy, fishery, handloom, etc.)  
- [ ] In operation for not less than 3 years (for direct funding)  
- [ ] Positive net worth, not less than 100% paid-up share capital (no erosion in paid-up capital)  
- [ ] Has a detailed project report (DPR)  
- [ ] Audited financial statements available  
- [ ] Bank account details ready  
- [ ] Board resolution for loan application can be obtained  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your cooperative meets several key criteria for the NCDC Grants scheme—particularly your operational history, net worth position, and project focus. The NCDC Grants offer financial assistance of up to 90-95% of project cost, including a subsidy component of 15-25% under certain schemes. Shall I take five minutes to explain how this could work for your specific project?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many cooperative societies struggle to access sufficient financing for infrastructure, working capital, or business expansion due to stringent bank requirements, lack of collateral, or limited internal accruals. This often delays or prevents critical projects in agriculture, dairy, fisheries, and allied sectors that could boost productivity and rural incomes.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC) Grants, administered by the Ministry of Cooperation, Government of India, provide financial assistance in the form of Term Loan, Investment Loan, and Working Capital Loan, along with credit-linked Subsidy. Assistance is delivered under the Central Sector Integrated Scheme on Agricultural Cooperation (CSISAC), other Central Sector Schemes (CSS), and NCDC Sponsored Schemes. Liberal support covers up to 90-95% of project cost, with a subsidy element of 15-25% where available from the Government of India.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your cooperative is registered under a valid cooperative Act (as confirmed)

- You have been in operation for over 3 years and maintain positive net worth (no erosion in paid-up share capital)  
- You have or can prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR), a key requirement for application
