## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, document preparation, and application submission.
2. Success fee is contingent on loan sanction and disbursement by NCDC.
3. Fees are calibrated to the scheme’s maximum per-entity benefit of ₹20.00 crore, with success fee percentages decreasing for larger clients due to economies of scale.
4. **Scheme-specific note**: This is a working capital loan product with a one-year tenure and rolling basis processing. Evidence indicates no processing fees are charged by NCDC, but legal fees for security documentation are borne by the borrower. The interest rate is case-to-case and decided by NCDC’s Empowered Committee. The scheme has high confidence in eligibility criteria and disbursement process, resulting in a moderate risk profile for consultants — primarily dependent on timely submission of complete documentation and borrower’s ability to meet security requirements (e.g., mortgage of assets 1.5x loan value, FD pledge 1.1x, or government bond assignment 1.2x). Processing time is not explicitly stated in evidence, but the rolling basis and procedural guidelines suggest a standard timeline of 30–60 working days for sanction upon complete submission, per NCDC’s Citizen Charter (Working Capital Loan up to ₹300 Cr: 15 days; above ₹300 Cr: 30 days). However, since the scheme’s max is ₹20 Cr (<₹300 Cr), the line-item timeline applies, supporting a 3-month lag assumption in revenue projections.
