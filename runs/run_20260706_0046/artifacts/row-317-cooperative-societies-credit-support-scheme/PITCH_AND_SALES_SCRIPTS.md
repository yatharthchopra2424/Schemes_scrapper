# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping credit cooperatives access working capital finance through government-backed schemes. I understand your cooperative plays a vital role in providing financial services to members, and I’d like to explore whether the **Cooperative Societies Credit Support Scheme** **Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC)** could support your working capital needs.

### Discovery Questions
1. How long has your credit cooperative been in operation?  
2. What is your current paid-up share capital, and has there been any erosion in it over time?  
3. What is your cooperative’s net worth position — is it positive and at least equal to your paid-up share capital?  
4. Are you currently seeking working capital finance for activities like lending to members, operational expenses, or liquidity management?  
5. Do you have access to collateral such as government guarantees, bank guarantees, fixed assets, fixed deposits, or government securities to secure a loan?  
6. Are you applying for funding directly, or through your State Government/UT administration?  
7. What is the approximate amount of working capital you are looking to access?  
8. Have you previously availed of any NCDC loans or similar cooperative finance schemes?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Cooperative has been in operation for not less than 3 years  
- [ ] Positive net worth, not less than 100% paid-up share capital (no erosion in paid-up share capital)  
- [ ] Applicant is a Credit Cooperative  
- [ ] Loan purpose is for working capital (e.g., CC limit)  
- [ ] Loan amount requested does not exceed Rs.20.00 crore per borrower  
- [ ] Security can be provided via: State/Central Govt guarantee, Scheduled/Nationalized Bank guarantee, mortgage of fixed assets (≥1.5x loan), pledge of FDRs (≥1.1x loan), or hypothecation of Govt securities (≥1.2x loan)  
- [ ] Willing to intimate 90 days prior to sanction validity for renewal/closure  
- [ ] Agrees to monthly/quarterly/semi-annual interest servicing as agreed  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like your cooperative meets the core eligibility criteria for the **Cooperative Societies Credit Support Scheme** offered by NCDC — specifically, your operational history, net worth position, and need for working capital. This scheme provides a cash credit (CC) limit of up to **Rs.20.00 crore** with flexible drawal and repayment terms, which could directly support your liquidity and lending operations.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**  
Credit cooperatives often face challenges in accessing timely and flexible working capital to meet member loan demands, manage operational liquidity, or seize lending opportunities. Traditional banking channels may impose rigid terms, high processing fees, or lengthy approval cycles, limiting the cooperative’s ability to serve its members effectively.

**Scheme intro:**  
The **Cooperative Societies Credit Support Scheme** is a working capital loan product launched by the **National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC)**, under the Ministry of Cooperation. It offers a cash credit (CC) limit of up to **Rs.20.00 crore per borrower**, sanctioned for one year and repayable on demand. The interest rate is decided case-to-case by NCDC’s Empowered Committee and remains fixed for the entire tenure. There are **no processing fees** for sanction, and borrowerest is calculated on daily closing balance and compounded monthly. Legal fees for security documentation, if any, are borne by the borrower.

- Your net worth is positive and not eroded relative to paid-up share capital, satisfying the financial health criterion  
- As a credit cooperative, you fall under the **Target Beneficiaries** explicitly defined in the scheme  
- You intend to use the funds for working capital purposes, which aligns with the scheme’s objective
