### Cooperative Societies Credit Support Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://ncdc.in/index.jsp?page=common-application%3Den
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Credit Cooperatives
- Age of business: Not less than 3 years in operation
- Sector: Credit Cooperatives
- Revenue cap: Not specified in evidence
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: Must have positive net worth, not less than 100% paid up share capital (no erosion in paid-up share capital)

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Rs.20.00 crore per borrower (maximum)
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (working capital loan with CC limit)
- Duration of benefit: One year (repayable on demand)

#### Documents Required
1. Common Loan Application Form
2. Supporting documents

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Choose activity/purpose and loan type from the common loan application form.
2. Download the form from NCDC website.
3. Fill the form and submit it along with supporting documents to NCDC office.
4. Submit the application for sanction.
