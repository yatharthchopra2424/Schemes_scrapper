# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **Cooperative Societies Credit Support Scheme** (Scheme ID: row-317) is a **loan**-type scheme implemented by the **National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC)** under the **Cooperative Sector** with **Pan-India** geographic scope. It operates on a **rolling basis** with no fixed deadline, last updated in 2025. The scheme is designed to provide working capital loans to eligible credit cooperatives either directly or through State Government/UT administration.

### Objectives
- To further the mandate of development of the cooperative sector  
- To meet the requirements of various cooperative organizations  
- To launch NCDC cash credit product on the lines of cash credit limit as offered by commercial banks  
- To provide working capital loans to eligible credit cooperatives  
- To support cooperative societies through direct funding or via State Government/UT administration

### Security Requirements (Caveats)
Loan must be secured by one or more of the following:
- Guarantee by State/Central Government  
- Guarantee from Scheduled Banks/Nationalized Banks  
- Mortgage of fixed assets of borrower cooperative (value ≥ 1.50× loan amount)  
- Pledge of Fixed Deposit Receipts (face value ≥ 1.10× loan amount)  
- Hypothecation and assignment of Government bonds/securities (extent ≥ 1.2× NCDC loan)  

Additional Caveats:
- NCDC reserves right to recall entire amount due with applicable interest and foreclose loan without assigning reasons  
- For renewal after one year, NCDC informs status within 90 days  
- No processing fees; legal fees for security documentation borne by borrower
