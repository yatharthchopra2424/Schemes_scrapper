## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is a Primary Agricultural Cooperative Society (PACS) registered under the relevant State Cooperative Societies Act or Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002  
- [ ] The PACS is functionally active and currently engaged in agricultural credit, savings, input distribution, or related farmer services  
- [ ] The PACS has a minimum membership base (as per state-specific guidelines; verify with nodal officer/DCCB)  
- [ ] The PACS demonstrates viability for sustained operations post-computerisation (based on audited financial statements and operational history)  
- [ ] The PACS is willing to adopt computerisation for its operations and commit to proper maintenance and utilization of systems  
- [ ] The PACS has not previously availed subsidy under this scheme for the same purpose (verify with DCCB/SCB)  
- [ ] The PACS is located in a Pan-India geographic area (no state-level restrictions, though implementation guidelines may vary)  
- [ ] The PACS is prepared to bear its share of project cost (as per state-specific cost-sharing norms)  

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts (Eligibility, Target Beneficiaries, Application Process, Key Caveats)  
> **Portal**: [NABARD PACS Computerisation Scheme](https://www.nabard.org) (Refer to NABARD’s official website for latest guidelines and state-specific circulars)

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- [ ] Incorporation/Registration Certificate of the PACS  
- [ ] PAN Card of the PACS  
- [ ] Audited Financial Statements (ITR) for the last 2–3 years  
- [ ] GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)  
- [ ] Bank Statements (last 6 months) of the PACS’s operative account  
- [ ] Udyam Registration (if applicable, though not mandatory for PACS; verify exemption)  
- [ ] Cancelled Cheque or Bank Passbook copy for NEFT/RTGS details  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- [ ] Certificate of Registration of the PACS (from Registrar of Cooperative Societies)  
- [ ] Latest audited financial statements (Balance Sheet, P&L, Audit Report)  
- [ ] List of members and share capital details (certified by Secretary/Chairman)  
- [ ] Project report detailing computerisation requirements (hardware, software, networking, UPS, training plan)  
- [ ] Board resolution approving the computerisation project and authorising application submission  
- [ ] Quotations from at least two authorized vendors for hardware and software (itemised, with GST details)  
- [ ] Undertaking regarding maintenance, sustainability, and proper utilisation of computer systems (on stamp paper, notarised if required)  

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible, scanned in PDF format (minimum 300 DPI)  
- [ ] File names follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_PACSName_Date.pdf` (e.g., `PACS_Computerisation_ProjectReport_ABCPACS_20240515.pdf`)  
- [ ] Financial statements are audited and signed by a Chartered Accountant with UDIN  
- [ ] Board resolution is on letterhead, signed by Chairman and Secretary, and sealed  
- [ ] Undertaking is on non-judicial stamp paper of appropriate value (as per state stamp act) and notarised if mandated  
- [ ] Vendor quotations are on official letterhead, valid for at least 30 days, and include warranty/AMC details  
- [ ] All documents are in English or Hindi; if in regional language, provide certified translation  
- [ ] No overwriting or tampering; corrections, if any, are authenticated by authorised signatory

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
✅ **Required**  
The Project Report (DPR) is mandatory as per the Scheme Key Facts under "Required Documents".  

**Standard Sections to Include**:  
1. Executive Summary  
2. About the PACS (history, membership, share capital, current operations)  
3. Need for Computerisation (pain points in manual systems)  
4. Objectives of the Project  
5. Scope of Work (hardware, software, networking, UPS, training)  
6. Technical Specifications (itemised bill of quantities)  
7. Implementation Plan (timeline, milestones, vendor roles)  
8. Cost Breakdown and Funding Pattern (subsidy vs. beneficiary share)  
9. Sustainability Plan (maintenance, AMC, staff training)  
10. Expected Benefits (efficiency, transparency, service delivery, MIS)  
11. Risk Assessment and Mitigation  
12. Annexures (vendor quotes, board resolution, member list, financials)  

> **Note**: Align with NABARD’s guidelines for cooperative projects; verify state-specific templates with DCCB/SCB if available.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Use the official format** prescribed by the DCCB/SCB or NABARD (if available); otherwise, draft a formal proposal letter  
2. **Clearly state the PACS name, registration number, and registered address** in the header  
3. **Specify the total project cost** and **subsidy amount sought** (as per central/state cost-sharing ratio)  
4. **Attach all required documents** as per checklist; reference them in the application (e.g., “Annexure I: Audited Financials”)  
5. **Ensure the undertaking on maintenance and sustainability** is signed by authorised signatories and included  
6. **Mention the proposed vendor(s)** and confirm they are authorised/suppliers of genuine equipment  
7. **Highlight training plan for PACS staff** (duration, trainer, curriculum)  
8. **Declare that the PACS has not availed subsidy for same purpose earlier** (if applicable)  
9. **Submit through the correct channel**: DCCB or SCB (not directly to NABARD unless authorised)  
10. **Keep a copy of the submitted application** and obtain an acknowledgement receipt  

> **Portal Note**: Applications are submitted physically or via designated portal of DCCB/SCB; verify mode with nodal officer. NABARD does not typically accept direct applications from PACS.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and scan the **approval letter/sanction order** from NABARD (via DCCB/SCB)  
- [ ] Verify the **sanctioned amount**, subsidy percentage, and release schedule (installments)  
- [ ] Confirm **utilisation timeline** and conditions for fund release (e.g., after procurement proof)  
- [ ] Ensure **utilisation certificate (UC)** format is understood and prepared in advance  
- [ ] Track **vendor delivery and installation** against approved specifications  
- [ ] Conduct **staff training** as per plan and obtain completion certificates  
- [ ] Submit **utilisation certificate** along with bills, installation report, and training proof to DCCB/SCB → NABARD  
- [ ] Monitor **post-implementation review** by NABARD or financing agency  
- [ ] Ensure **proper use of systems** for intended purposes (loan management, savings, MIS) to avoid subsidy recovery  
- [ ] Prepare **success fee invoice** for consulting services as per engagement letter  
- [ ] Maintain all records (approvals, UC, bills, correspondence) for audit and future reference  

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts (Application Process: “funds released in installments based on progress”; Key Caveats: “failure to use systems may lead to recovery”; Benefits: “improved record-keeping… enhanced service delivery”)  
> **Portal**: [NABARD – Cooperatives](https://www.nabard.org/en/cooperatives.aspx) for guidelines on UC and monitoring
