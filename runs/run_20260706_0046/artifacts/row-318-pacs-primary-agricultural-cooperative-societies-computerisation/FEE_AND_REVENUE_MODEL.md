## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are tied to the actual subsidy disbursed under the PACS Computerisation scheme, ensuring clients pay only for realized benefits.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the rolling basis of approvals and dependency on annual budget allocations (per evidence), success fees reflect moderate implementation risk; retainers cover initial proposal development and agency coordination.
3. **Transparency**: All fee structures are disclosed upfront, with retainers covering advisory services and success fees payable only upon fund disbursement by NABARD.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The PACS Computerisation scheme has a moderate risk profile due to its rolling application process and dependence on budget availability. Processing typically involves multiple stages (DCCB/SCB scrutiny, NABARD sanction, installment-based release), which may extend timelines. However, once approved, fund disbursement is structured and trackable, supporting predictable success fee realization.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Advisory retainer for PACS Computerisation scheme proposal preparation, eligibility assessment, and coordination with District Central Cooperative Bank (DCCB) or State Cooperative Bank (SCB) for submission to NABARD.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for securing subsidy under the PACS Computerisation scheme implemented by the National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD), payable upon disbursement of funds to the Primary Agricultural Cooperative Society (PACS).
