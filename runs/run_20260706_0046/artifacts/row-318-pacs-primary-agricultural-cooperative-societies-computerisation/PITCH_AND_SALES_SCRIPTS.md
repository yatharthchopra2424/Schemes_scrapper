## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I'm reaching out because I specialise in helping Primary Agricultural Cooperative Societies (PACS) access government support for technological upgradation. Many PACS like yours are looking to improve record-keeping, loan disbursement, and service delivery to farmers through computerisation—but navigating the application process can be complex. I’d like to understand your society’s current operations and see if the PACS Computerisation scheme, implemented by NABARD, could be a good fit for your needs.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your PACS registered, and what is your current membership base?
2. Are you currently using any computer systems for accounting, loan management, or member services?
3. What challenges do you face in record-keeping, financial management, or service delivery to farmers?
4. Is your PACS functionally active and willing to adopt computerisation for sustained operations?
5. Have you discussed computerisation plans with your District Central Cooperative Bank (DCCB) or State Cooperative Bank (SCB)?
6. Do you have a clear idea of the hardware, software, and networking equipment needed for your operations?
7. Is your PACS prepared to bear its share of the project cost, as per state-specific guidelines?
8. Have you begun preparing documents such as your registration certificate, audited financial statements, or member list?

- [ ] The applicant is a Primary Agricultural Cooperative Society (PACS)

- [ ] The PACS has a minimum membership base (as per state guidelines)

- [ ] The PACS is prepared to bear its share of the project cost (state-dependent)
- [ ] The PACS has approached or is willing to approach DCCB/SCB for proposal submission

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many PACS still rely on manual or semi-digital systems, leading to delays in loan disbursement, errors in record-keeping, limited transparency, and difficulty in accessing real-time financial data. This hampers their ability to serve farmers efficiently and meet supervisory requirements from higher financing agencies.

**Scheme intro:**
The **PACS Computerisation scheme**, administered by the **National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD)**, provides a **subsidy for the procurement of computer systems and related infrastructure**. The central government contributes a fixed percentage of the total project cost, while the remaining amount is borne by the state government or the PACS itself, depending on state-specific implementation guidelines. The scheme supports computer hardware, software, UPS, networking equipment, staff training, and post-implementation review.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your PACS is functionally active and willing to adopt computerisation for operations  
- You have a minimum membership base and demonstrate viability for sustained operations post-computerisation  
- You are prepared to engage with your DCCB or SCB to submit a project proposal  
- You are willing to bear your share of the project cost as per state guidelines
