### PACS (Primary Agricultural Cooperative Societies) Computerisation
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD)
- **Portal / Application URL:** [https://www.nabard.org](https://www.nabard.org)
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open (subject to availability of funds and annual budget allocations)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Primary Agricultural Cooperative Societies (PACS)
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Agriculture / Cooperatives
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be functionally active and willing to adopt computerisation; minimum membership base; demonstrate viability for sustained operations post-computerisation

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Subsidy for procurement of computer systems and related infrastructure (central government contributes a fixed percentage of total project cost; remainder borne by state government or PACS)
- Disbursement mode: Installment-based release upon progress verification
- Duration of benefit: One-time (project-based)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Registration of the PACS
2. Latest audited financial statements
3. List of members and share capital details
4. Project report detailing computerisation requirements
5. Board resolution approving the computerisation project
6. Quotations from vendors for hardware and software
7. Undertaking regarding maintenance and sustainability

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. PACS submits a project proposal for computerisation to the District Central Cooperative Bank (DCCB) or State Cooperative Bank (SCB)
2. The proposal is forwarded to the National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) for scrutiny and sanction
3. Upon approval, funds are released in installments based on progress
4. PACS procures the approved hardware and software through authorized vendors
5. Installation and commissioning are carried out followed by staff training
6. Post-implementation review is conducted to assess utilization and impact
