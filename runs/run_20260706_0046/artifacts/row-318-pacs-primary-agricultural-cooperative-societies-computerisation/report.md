## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **PACS (Primary Agricultural Cooperative Societies) Computerisation** scheme is a **subsidy**-type initiative implemented by the **National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD)** under the **Cooperative Sector** with **Pan-India** geographic scope. The scheme operates on a **rolling basis**, subject to the availability of funds and annual budget allocations. Its primary objective is to modernize PACS operations through technological upgradation to improve service delivery to farmers.

### Objectives
The scheme aims to achieve the following objectives:
- Computerize the operations of Primary Agricultural Cooperative Societies (PACS)
- Improve the efficiency and effectiveness of PACS in serving farmers
- Enable real-time data management and financial accounting in cooperatives
- Enhance access to credit and other services for rural populations
- Strengthen the cooperative movement through technological upgradation
- Ensure transparency and accountability in PACS operations

The scheme provides financial assistance as a **subsidy** with the following cost-sharing pattern:
- **Central Government**: Contributes a fixed percentage of the total project cost (exact percentage not specified in evidence)
- **State Government / PACS**: Bears the remaining amount, depending on state-specific implementation guidelines

> **Important Caveat**: The exact subsidy percentage and cost-sharing ratio vary by state and are subject to the annual budget allocations and cooperative policies of individual states. Applicants must consult the latest NABARD circulars or state cooperative department guidelines for precise figures.

### Required Documents
Applicants must submit the following documents as part of their project proposal:
1. Certificate of Registration of the PACS
2. Latest audited financial statements
3. List of members and share capital details
4. Project report detailing computerisation requirements
5. Board resolution approving the computerisation project
6. Quotations from vendors for hardware and software
7. Undertaking regarding maintenance and sustainability

### Application Process
The application process involves the following sequential steps:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[PACS identifies need for computerisation and prepares project proposal] --> B[PACS submits proposal to District Central Cooperative Bank (DCCB) or State Cooperative Bank (SCB)]
    B --> C[DCCB/SCB forwards proposal to NABARD for scrutiny and sanction]
    C --> D{NABARD reviews proposal}
    D -->|Approved| E[NABARD sanctions project and approves subsidy]
    D -->|Rejected| F[PACS revises proposal based on feedback and resubmits]
    E --> G[Funds released in installments based on project progress]
    G --> H[PACS procures approved hardware and software through authorized vendors]
    H --> I[Installation and commissioning of systems]
    I --> J[Staff training on system usage]
    J --> K[Post-implementation review to assess utilization and impact]
    K --> L[Ongoing maintenance and sustainability responsibility of PACS]
```

### Key Caveats and Considerations
> **Warning**: Funding under this scheme is **subject to availability of budgetary allocations**. Approval does not guarantee disbursement if funds are exhausted in a given financial year.

> **Critical Compliance Note**: Beneficiary PACS must ensure proper maintenance and utilization of the computer systems. **Failure to use the systems for intended purposes may lead to recovery of the full subsidy amount** disbursed under the scheme.

> **State Variability Alert**: Scheme implementation, including subsidy ratios, eligibility thresholds, and procedural details, **may vary significantly across states** based on their respective cooperative policies and state government directives. Always verify state-specific guidelines before initiating an application.

> **Sustainability Requirement**: PACS must provide an undertaking regarding long-term maintenance and sustainability of the computerised systems as a mandatory part of the application.
