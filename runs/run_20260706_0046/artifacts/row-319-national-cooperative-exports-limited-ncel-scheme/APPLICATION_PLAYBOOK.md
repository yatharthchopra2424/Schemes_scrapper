# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is a Farmer Producer Organization (FPO), cooperative society, or agricultural producer collective registered under relevant Acts (e.g., Companies Act Part IXA, Co-operative Societies Act).
- [ ] The entity is engaged in production and processing of agricultural commodities for export.
- [ ] The entity has valid documentation (registration certificate, PAN, GST, bank details).
- [ ] The entity complies with quality and food safety standards (e.g., FSSAI, phytosanitary, international quality standards).
- [ ] The entity is willing to actively participate in NAFED-led FPO and cooperative development programs.
- [ ] The entity has product details and quality certification ready for submission.
- [ ] The entity has export licence (if applicable for the commodity/destination).
- [ ] The entity has board resolution authorizing export activity.
- [ ] The entity has details of production capacity and raw material sourcing.

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Registration certificate of the entity (under Companies Act or Co-operative Societies Act)
- PAN card of the entity
- GST registration certificate
- Bank account details (cancelled cheque or bank statement)
- ITR of the entity (last 2–3 years)
- Udyam Registration (if applicable as MSME)
- Audited financial statements (balance sheet, P&L)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Product details and quality certification (e.g., FSSAI, AGMARK, organic, GlobalGAP)
- Export licence (if applicable for the commodity/destination)
- Board resolution authorizing export activity
- Details of production capacity and raw material sourcing

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format, and clearly named (e.g., "PAN_Card.pdf", "GST_Certificate.pdf").
- File size for each document must not exceed 5 MB.
- Names on documents must exactly match the entity’s legal name as per registration.
- Certifications must be valid and issued by recognized authorities (e.g., FSSAI, APEDA, DGFT).
- Documents must be in English or accompanied by a certified translation.
- Declarations and undertakings must be signed and stamped by authorized signatory.

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
- DPR is **not explicitly required** for the NCEL scheme as per evidence.  
- However, applicants must submit product details, quality compliance documents, and production capacity details.  
- **Verify with nodal officer** whether a Detailed Project Report (DPR) is needed for specific commodities or export destinations.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Ensure all fields in the application form are filled accurately; leave no blank unless marked optional.  
2. Use the exact legal name of the entity as per registration certificate throughout the form.  
3. Attach all scheme-specific and universal documents as per the checklist in Stage 2.  
4. Verify that the board resolution explicitly authorizes export activity and is signed by authorized directors.  
5. Confirm that product details include HS codes, quality grades, and target export markets.  
6. Ensure GST and PAN numbers are correctly entered and match the uploaded documents.  
7. Declare compliance with international quality and phytosanitary standards in the declaration section.  
8. Double-check that the application is submitted via the correct portal: **https://nafex.in/#/** (NAFED’s export portal).  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All eligibility criteria verified and documented.  
- [ ] Universal documents collected and validated.  
- [ ] Scheme-specific documents collected and validated.  
- [ ] Application form filled completely and accurately.  
- [ ] All documents attached in correct format (PDF, password-protected if required).  
- [ ] Declarations signed and stamped by authorized signatory.  
- [ ] Application reference number saved after submission.  
- [ ] Follow-up reminders set for Day 18 and Day 30.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed application via email to **mdcell@nafed-india.com** with the subject line: **“NCEL Scheme Application – [Entity Name] – [Case ID]”**.  
- Attach all documents as separate password-protected PDF files (password to be shared separately via SMS or secure channel).  
- Take a screenshot of the sent email and save it in the case folder.  
- Save the email timestamp and message ID as the provisional reference.  
- Set a follow-up reminder for Day 21 (3 days post-submission) and Day 30 (12 days post-submission).

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and save the approval letter from NAFED (via email or portal).  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned benefits (e.g., access to international markets, technical support, branding assistance, logistics support) as communicated in the approval.  
- [ ] Utilize convergence benefits with linked schemes (Formation and Promotion of 10,000 FPOs, MIDH, PSF) for infrastructure, equity, or certification support.  
- [ ] Maintain records of export activities, market linkages, and utilization of NAFED’s international trade network (e-Samridhi, NAFEX).  
- [ ] Prepare utilization certificate (if required for any grant component) and submit to NAFED as per timelines.  
- [ ] Track invoice and success fee (if applicable) as per consultancy agreement.  
- [ ] Monitor compliance with international quality and phytosanitary standards for ongoing eligibility.  
- [ ] Ensure active participation in NAFED-led FPO and cooperative development programs to continue receiving benefits.
