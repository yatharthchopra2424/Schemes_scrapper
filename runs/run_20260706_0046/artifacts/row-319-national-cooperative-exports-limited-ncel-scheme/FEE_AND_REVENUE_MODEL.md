## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client through access to international markets, technical support, branding, logistics, and convergence with schemes like FPO promotion and MIDH.
2. Transparent fee structure with clear retainer for advisory work and success fee contingent on measurable outcomes such as export facilitation or market linkage establishment.
3. Tiered pricing reflects client capacity: FPOs and cooperatives (typically ₹10L–₹1Cr turnover) fall under Growth tier; larger cooperatives or federations may qualify for Scale or Enterprise.
4. Scheme-specific note: The NCEL scheme has a medium confidence rating and indirect financial support via convergence (no direct grant quantum specified), resulting in moderate risk due to reliance on NAFED-led programs and compliance with international standards; success fees reflect outcome dependency on quality certification and active participation in NAFED initiatives.
