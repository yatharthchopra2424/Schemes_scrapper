# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), cooperative societies, and agricultural producer collectives access government export promotion schemes. Given your work in agricultural production and processing for export, I wanted to explore whether the National Cooperative Exports Limited (NCEL) Scheme—implemented by NAFED—might support your goals of accessing international markets and strengthening value chains.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is the legal structure of your organization—are you registered as an FPO, cooperative society, or agricultural producer collective under the relevant State or Central Act?
2. Which agricultural commodities are you currently producing or processing with the intent to export?
3. Do you have valid quality and food safety certifications for your export-oriented produce (e.g., GlobalGAP, FSSAI, organic certification)?
4. Are you currently engaged in or planning to participate in NAFED-led FPO or cooperative development programs?
5. What is your current production capacity and primary source of raw materials for your export products?
6. Do you hold a valid Export Licence, GST registration, and PAN for your entity?
7. Has your board passed a resolution authorizing export activities?
8. Are you seeking support for branding, packaging, logistics, or market linkage to facilitate exports?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Registered as FPO, cooperative society, or agricultural producer collective under relevant Act  
- [ ] Engaged in production and processing of agricultural commodities for export  
- [ ] Possesses valid documentation (registration certificate, PAN, bank details, GST)  
- [ ] Has quality and food safety compliance certificates  
- [ ] Holds Export Licence (if applicable)  
- [ ] Board resolution authorizing export activity is in place  
- [ ] Details of production capacity and raw material sourcing available  
- [ ] Willing to engage with NAFED as National Implementing Agency  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your organization aligns well with the NCEL Scheme’s eligibility criteria. This scheme, implemented by NAFED, provides convergence benefits—including access to international markets, technical support for quality compliance, branding and packaging assistance, and logistics support—through linkage with existing schemes like FPO promotion and MIDH. Shall I walk you through how it works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
FPOs and cooperatives producing export-oriented agricultural commodities often face barriers in accessing international markets due to lack of quality certification, branding support, logistics linkages, and knowledge of export procedures—despite having production capacity and market potential.

**Scheme intro:**
The National Cooperative Exports Limited (NCEL) Scheme, implemented by the National Agricultural Cooperative Marketing Federation of India Ltd. (NAFED), promotes cooperative exports through Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) by providing convergence benefits such as access to international markets, technical support for quality compliance, branding and packaging assistance, export documentation facilitation, logistics support, market intelligence, and linkage with schemes like the Formation and Promotion of 10,000 FPOs, MIDH, and PSF. While specific financial quantum is not detailed, support is routed through converging schemes including matching equity, infrastructure grants, and certification subsidies.
