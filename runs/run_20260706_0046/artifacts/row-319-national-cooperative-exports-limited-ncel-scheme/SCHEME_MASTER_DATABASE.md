### National Cooperative Exports Limited (NCEL) Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Agricultural Cooperative Marketing Federation of India Ltd. (NAFED)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nafex.in/#/
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), cooperative societies, and agricultural producer collectives registered under relevant Acts
- Age of business: Not specified in evidence
- Sector: Agricultural commodities for export
- Revenue cap: Not specified in evidence
- Employee count: Not specified in evidence
- Other: Engaged in production and processing of agricultural commodities for export, with valid documentation and compliance with quality and food safety standards

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support through convergence with existing schemes (Formation and Promotion of 10,000 FPOs, MIDH, PSF) including matching equity, grants for infrastructure, and subsidy for certification and branding; specific quantum under NCEL not detailed
- Disbursement mode: Through converging schemes (not direct grants under NCEL)
- Duration of benefit: Not specified in evidence

#### Documents Required
1. Registration certificate of FPO/cooperative society
2. PAN of the entity
3. Bank account details
4. Product details and quality certification
5. Export licence (if applicable)
6. GST registration
7. Board resolution authorizing export activity
8. Details of production capacity and raw material sourcing

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register as an FPO or cooperative society under the relevant Act.
2. Engage with NAFED as the National Implementing Agency for export promotion.
3. Submit product details and quality compliance documents.
4. Undergo capacity building and training on export procedures.
5. Receive support for branding, packaging, and market linkage.
6. Export facilitation through NAFED’s international trade network and portals like e-Samridhi and NAFEX.
