# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Prospective entrepreneur (including SC/ST Category and NER Region)  
- [ ] Unit proposed under Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure scheme  
- [ ] Application submitted through Expression of Interest (EoI) for setting up of multi-product food irradiation units or cold chain projects  
- [ ] Land on registered leasehold must have a lease duration of not less than 15 years  
- [ ] Surety Bond not required for Central/State Government Departments  
- [ ] Original Surety Bond must be submitted to the Ministry through speed post  
- [ ] Not blacklisted/debarred for misuse of grant-in-aid (e.g., M/s Paras Spices, M/s Vision Fresh and Frozen)  
- [ ] Proposal not in ineligible lists published for non-compliant proposals (e.g., Ineligible list of 20 applications under Cold Chain Scheme)  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate  
- PAN Card  
- ITR (last 3 years)  
- GST Registration  
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Acceptance Letter (Annexure-VI)  
- Land document (ownership/registered leasehold) in name of PIA  
- Details of three bank accounts (for private PIAs) or two bank accounts (for Govt PIAs)  
- Term loan sanction letter from the Bank  
- Building plan/Blue Print/Lay-out/photo of the building  
- Utilization certificate of grant availed earlier from MoFPI (if any)  
- Copies of Bank statements showing payments to suppliers  
- Detailed list of approved equipment procured and installed (Annexure-VIII a and b)  
- Self-attested English/Hindi version of land document  
- Notarised Surety Bond (Annexure-IX) on non-judicial stamp paper of not less than Rs. 100/-  
- Self-declaration of project completion and commercialization (Annexure-X)  
- Proof of prominent display stating 'Project is assisted by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries, Government of India'  

### Document Quality Check
- File size: PDFs under 10 MB, images under 5 MB  
- File names must match document type (e.g., "Acceptance_Letter.pdf")  
- All documents must be self-attested or notarized where required  
- Documents must be in English or Hindi  
- Land document must show lease duration ≥15 years for leasehold  
- Surety Bond must be on non-judicial stamp paper of not less than Rs. 100/-

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not explicitly mentioned as required for this scheme in the evidence. Flag: **verify with nodal officer.**

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Submit Expression of Interest (EoI) for setting up of multi-product food irradiation units or cold chain projects under the scheme.  
2. Await pre-bid meeting notices and participate in clarification sessions.  
3. Respond to RFPs for engagement of Programme Management Agency (PMA) if applicable.  
4. Submit detailed proposals through the designated portal/link.  
5. Ensure all fields are filled accurately, especially land documents, bank account details, and surety bond information.  
6. Attach all scheme-specific documents as per the checklist.  
7. Double-check that the Surety Bond is on non-judicial stamp paper of not less than Rs. 100/- and notarized.  
8. Confirm that land lease duration is ≥15 years for leasehold land.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All application form fields complete  
- [ ] All universal documents attached  
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents attached and verified  
- [ ] Declarations signed and self-attested/notarized where required  
- [ ] Land document verified for lease duration ≥15 years (if leasehold)  
- [ ] Surety Bond verified for stamp value and notarization  
- [ ] Bank account details match the requirement (3 for private, 2 for Govt PIAs)  
- [ ] Term loan sanction letter is valid and recent  
- [ ] Utilization certificate attached if prior grant availed  
- [ ] Proof of prominent display statement ready

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Take screenshot of submission confirmation  
- Store application reference number securely  
- Set follow-up reminders for 7, 15, and 30 days post-submission

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify approval letter  
- [ ] Verify sanctioned amount (50% upon compliance with initial conditions, remaining 50% upon submission of utilization certificate and verification of expenditure)  
- [ ] Ensure funds are released through the PFMS EAT module  
- [ ] Submit utilization certificate and expenditure details for second instalment (50%)  
- [ ] Maintain records for invoice and success fee processing  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with bank account, surety bond, and land document requirements  
- [ ] Display prominent signage stating 'Project is assisted by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries, Government of India'  
- [ ] Avoid misuse of grant-in-aid to prevent blacklisting/debarring (e.g., as seen with M/s Paras Spices, M/s Vision Fresh and Frozen)
