## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees are aligned with the substantial financial benefit delivered—grants-in-aid under the Cold Chain Infrastructure Scheme can reach several crores for infrastructure projects, justifying tiered success fees.
2. **Risk Sharing**: Success fees are contingent on grant approval, reducing upfront client burden; the scheme’s two-installment release (50% on compliance, 50% on utilization certificate) introduces moderate disbursement risk, mitigated by structured milestone billing.
3. **Market Competitiveness**: Retainer and success fee ranges reflect standard rates for MSME-focused government grant consulting in India’s food processing sector, adjusted for the scheme’s pan-India scope and Ministry of Food Processing Industries implementation.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: The Cold Chain Infrastructure Scheme involves technical and compliance complexity (land documents, surety bonds, utilization certificates, PFMS processing), leading to a moderate risk profile. Processing time varies with EoI cycles and committee approvals (Technical Committee, Project Approval Committee, IMAC), typically spanning 6–12 months from proposal to first instalment, supporting a 3-month lag assumption in revenue projections.
