# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping food processing entrepreneurs and agri-startups access government grants for cold chain infrastructure. The Cold Chain Infrastructure Scheme from the Ministry of Food Processing Industries is designed to reduce post-harvest losses and boost farmers' income through modern preservation facilities. I’d like to understand your current setup to see if this scheme aligns with your expansion plans.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of food processing or preservation activity are you currently engaged in or planning to start?
2. When was your business incorporated or registered?
3. Do you own or lease the land where your facility will be located? If leased, what is the remaining lease duration?
4. Are you targeting SC/ST, NER region, or general category benefits under this scheme?
5. Have you secured a term loan sanction from a bank for this project?
6. What is the estimated project cost for your cold chain or irradiation unit setup?
7. Do you have land documents, building plans, and details of required bank accounts ready?
8. Are you aware of the two-installment grant release pattern (50% upfront, 50% post-verification)?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Prospective entrepreneur (including SC/ST or NER Region)
- [ ] Unit proposed under Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure scheme
- [ ] Applying via Expression of Interest (EoI) for multi-product food irradiation or cold chain project
- [ ] Land document (ownership/registered leasehold) in name of PIA available
- [ ] Lease duration ≥15 years if leasehold
- [ ] Details of three bank accounts (private PIA) or two (Govt PIA) available
- [ ] Term loan sanction letter from bank obtained
- [ ] Building plan/blueprint/layout/photo of building ready
- [ ] Utilization certificate of earlier grant (if any) available
- [ ] Bank statements showing payments to suppliers accessible
- [ ] Detailed list of approved equipment procured and installed (Annexure-VIII a/b) preparable
- [ ] Self-attested English/Hindi version of land document ready
- [ ] Notarised Surety Bond (Annexure-IX) on ≥Rs. 100/- non-judicial stamp paper arrangable
- [ ] Self-declaration of project completion and commercialization (Annexure-X) draftable
- [ ] Proof of prominent display stating 'Project assisted by MoFPI, GOI' plannable

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, the Cold Chain Infrastructure Scheme appears to be a strong fit—it offers grants-in-aid for food processing infrastructure, with 50% released upfront upon compliance. Let me walk you through how it works and why you may qualify.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Food processing businesses face high post-harvest losses, limited market linkages, and short product shelf life due to inadequate cold chain and preservation infrastructure, especially in rural and agri-startup settings.

**Scheme intro:**
The Cold Chain Infrastructure Scheme, administered by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI), provides grants-in-aid for setting up infrastructure projects in the food processing sector. Financial assistance is released in two instalments: 50% upon compliance (acceptance letter, land documents, term loan details), and the remaining 50% upon submission of utilization certificate and expenditure verification. Funds are disbursed via PFMS EAT module.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a prospective entrepreneur, eligible under PMKSY sub-schemes including Integrated Cold Chain
- You have or can secure land documents (ownership/leasehold ≥15 years if leased)
- You have or can obtain a term loan sanction letter from a bank
- You fall under target beneficiaries: entrepreneurs, MSME, agri-startups, SC/ST, or NER Region
