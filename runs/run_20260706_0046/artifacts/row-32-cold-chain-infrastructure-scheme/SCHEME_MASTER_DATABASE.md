### Cold Chain Infrastructure Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Food Processing Industries
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://mofpi.gov.in/coldchain
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Rolling basis — EoIs and proposals are invited periodically with specific closing dates (e.g., EoI dated 27.05.2025 for irradiation units closing 28.02.2026; EoI dated 20.05.2026 for irradiation units)

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Prospective entrepreneurs, including those from SC/ST Category and NER Region
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Food Processing Sector (including cold chain, value addition facilities, irradiation units)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Eligibility also extends to units proposed under the Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure scheme; applications invited through Expression of Interest (EoI) for setting up of multi-product food irradiation units and cold chain projects; specific eligibility criteria for R&D and other sub-schemes may apply as per respective guidelines

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial assistance as grants-in-aid for infrastructure projects under the Food Processing Sector; released in two installments: 50% upon compliance with initial conditions, and remaining 50% upon submission of utilization certificate and verification of expenditure
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit through PFMS EAT module
- Duration of benefit: One-time grant released in two installments

#### Documents Required
1. Acceptance Letter (Annexure-VI)
2. Land document (ownership/registered leasehold) in name of PIA
3. Details of three bank accounts (for private PIAs) or two bank accounts (for Govt PIAs)
4. Term loan sanction letter from the Bank
5. Building plan/Blue Print/Lay-out/photo of the building
6. Utilization certificate of grant availed earlier from MoFPI (if any)
7. Copies of Bank statements showing payments to suppliers
8. Detailed list of approved equipment procured and installed (Annexure-VIII a and b)
9. Self-attested English/Hindi version of land document
10. Notarised Surety Bond (Annexure-IX) on non-judicial stamp paper of not less than Rs. 100/-
11. Self-declaration of project completion and commercialization (Annexure-X)
12. Proof of prominent display stating 'Project is assisted by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries, Government of India'

#### Application Process
1. Submit Expression of Interest (EoI) for setting up of multi-product food irradiation units or cold chain projects under the scheme
2. Await pre-bid meeting notices and participate in clarification sessions
3. Respond to RFPs for engagement of Programme Management Agency (PMA) if applicable
4. Submit detailed proposals through the designated portal/link
5. Undergo evaluation by Technical Committee and Project Approval Committee
6. Receive approval from Inter-Ministerial Approval Committee (IMAC)
7. Fulfill compliance requirements including land documents, bank account details, and surety bond
8. Receive first instalment of grant (50%) upon submission of acceptance letter and compliance
9. Submit utilization certificate and expenditure details for second instalment (50%)
