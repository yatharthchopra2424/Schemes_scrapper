# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
**Scheme Name:** Cold Chain Infrastructure Scheme  
**Scheme ID:** row-32  
**Ministry / Category:** Agriculture Processing  
**Scheme Type:** Grant  
**Geographic Scope:** Pan-India  
**Implementing Agency:** Ministry of Food Processing Industries  
**Status / Deadlines:** Rolling basis — EoIs and proposals are invited periodically with specific closing dates (e.g., EoI dated 27.05.2025 for irradiation units closing 28.02.2026; EoI dated 20.05.2026 for irradiation units).  
**Last Updated:** 2026  
**Application Portal URL:** https://mofpi.gov.in/en/notifications/office-order-and-circulars  

### Objectives
- Reduce post-harvest losses of horticultural and non-horticultural produce  
- Enhance processing and value addition of agricultural products  
- Provide seamless cold chain and preservation infrastructure from farm gate to consumer  
- Boost exports of processed and perishable food products  
- Create modern infrastructure with efficient supply chain management  
- Generate employment opportunities in rural areas  
- Increase farmers' income through better returns and reduced wastage  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Eligibility Criteria | Details |
|----------------------|---------|
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Prospective entrepreneurs, including those from SC/ST Category and NER Region; MSME; agri-startups |
| **Eligible Entities Eligible | Units proposed under the Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure scheme; applicants for multi-product food irradiation units and cold chain projects |
| **Ineligible Cases** | Proposals blacklisted/debarred for misuse of grant-in-aid (e.g., M/s Paras Spices, M/s Vision Fresh and Frozen); ineligible lists published for non-compliant proposals (e.g., Ineligible list of 20 applications under Cold Chain Scheme) |
| **Special Provisions** | Specific eligibility criteria for R&D and other sub-schemes may apply as per respective guidelines |

### Benefits & Financial Support
**Financial Assistance:** Grants-in-aid for infrastructure projects under the Food Processing Sector  
**Pattern of Assistance:** Two-installment release:  
- **First Installment (50%):** Upon compliance with initial conditions (acceptance letter, land documents, term loan details)  
- **Second Installment (50%):** Upon submission of utilization certificate and verification of expenditure  
**Fund Release Mechanism:** Through PFMS EAT module  
**Additional Support:** Requirements for bank accounts, surety bonds, and utilization certificates; technical guidance; access to cold chain infrastructure, value addition facilities, and irradiation units; leverage investment, improve market linkages, enhance product shelf life and safety  

#### Financial Support Table
| Installment | Percentage | Conditions for Release |
|-------------|------------|------------------------|
| First | 50% | Acceptance letter, land documents (ownership/registered leasehold), term loan sanction letter |
| Second | 50% | Utilization certificate, verification of expenditure, detailed list of approved equipment procured and installed |

### Required Documents
1. Acceptance Letter (Annexure-VI)  
2. Land document (ownership/registered leasehold) in name of PIA  
3. Details of three bank accounts (for private PIAs) or two bank accounts (for Govt PIAs)  
4. Term loan sanction letter from the Bank  
5. Building plan/Blue Print/Lay-out/photo of the building  
6. Utilization certificate of grant availed earlier from MoFPI (if any)  
7. Copies of Bank statements showing payments to suppliers  
8. Detailed list of approved equipment procured and installed (Annexure-VIII a and b)  
9. Self-attested English/Hindi version of land document  
10. Notarised Surety Bond (Annexure-IX) on non-judicial stamp paper of not less than Rs. 100/-  
11. Self-declaration of project completion and commercialization (Annexure-X)  
12. Proof of prominent display stating 'Project is assisted by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries, Government of India'  

### Key Caveats
> - Land on registered leasehold must have a lease duration of not less than 15 years  
> - Surety Bond is not required for Central/State Government Departments  
> - Original Surety Bond must be submitted to the Ministry through speed post  
> - Blacklisting/debarring may occur for misuse of grant-in-aid (e.g., M/s Paras Spices, M/s Vision Fresh and Frozen)  
> - Extension of timelines may be granted for submission of proposals/EOIs  
> - Ineligible lists are published for non-compliant proposals (e.g., Ineligible list of 20 applications under Cold Chain Scheme)  

### Application Process Flowchart
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Submit Expression of Interest (EoI)] --> B[Await pre-bid meeting notices and participate in clarification sessions]
    B --> C[Respond to RFPs for engagement of Programme Management Agency (PMA) if applicable]
    C --> D[Submit detailed proposals through the designated portal/link]
    D --> E[Undergo evaluation by Technical Committee and Project Approval Committee]
    E --> F[Receive approval from Inter-Ministerial Approval Committee (IMAC)]
    F --> G[Fulfill compliance requirements including land documents, bank account details, and surety bond]
    G --> H[Receive first instalment of grant (50%) upon submission of acceptance letter and compliance]
    H --> I[Submit utilization certificate and expenditure details for second instalment (50%)]
    I --> J[Project completion and commercialization]
```
