## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is a multi-state cooperative society registered under the Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002  
- [ ] The society has a viable project proposal with clear developmental objectives  
- [ ] The society demonstrates repayment capacity for the proposed loan  
- [ ] The project is aligned with priority sectors (agriculture, dairy, fisheries, handicrafts, rural industries) or other developmental objectives of the fund  
- [ ] The society has no outstanding dues to NCDC or other government agencies (if applicable, verify during due diligence)  
- [ ] The society is prepared to contribute minimum promoter's equity as per NCDC norms  
- [ ] The project proposal includes utilization of funds strictly for the approved project purpose  

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts (Eligibility, Target Beneficiaries, Key Caveats, Objectives)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
✅ **Required** – A detailed project report with technical, financial, and economic feasibility analysis is explicitly mandated under the Required Documents and Application Process.

**Standard Sections to Include**:
- Executive Summary  
- Profile of the Applicant Society  
- Project Description and Objectives  
- Technical Feasibility (technology, equipment, site suitability)  
- Financial Feasibility (cost estimates, means of finance, profitability projections)  
- Economic Feasibility (employment generation, rural impact, backward linkages)  
- Project Implementation Schedule  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan  
- Environmental and Social Considerations (if applicable)  
- Conclusion and Recommendations  

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts (Required Documents: #4 Detailed project report; Application Process: Step 2)

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the official application form from the NCDC website or obtain it directly from the NCDC regional office.  
2. Fill all fields in block letters; avoid overwriting or use of correction fluid.  
3. Ensure the society’s name, registration number, and address match exactly across all documents and the form.  
4. Clearly state the loan amount sought, purpose, and repayment period proposed.  
5. Attach a checklist of enclosed documents as per the Required Documents list.  
6. Have the form signed and sealed by the authorized signatory (Chairperson or Secretary) as per the Board resolution.  
7. Verify that all declarations regarding utilization of funds, no outstanding dues, and promoter contribution are signed.  
8. Retain a photocopy of the submitted application for office records.  

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts (Application Process: Step 3; Required Documents); General government application best practices (no portal-specific form noted in evidence)

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed application along with all documents to the **National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC)** either:  
  - In person at the NCDC Headquarters or nearest regional office, **or**  
  - Via post/courier to the address specified by NCDC for scheme applications  
- Obtain a dated acknowledgment receipt (physical or digital) with an inward number  
- Take a clear screenshot or scan of the acknowledgment for case records  
- Store the application reference number (if provided) and set follow-up reminders for Day 25 and Day 35  
- Maintain a submission log with date, mode, recipient name, and reference  

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts (Application Process: Step 3: "Submit the application along with required documents to the National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC)")

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the sanction letter from NCDC outlining loan amount, interest rate, moratorium, and repayment schedule  
- [ ] Confirm that the sanctioned amount aligns with the project proposal and society’s repayment capacity  
- [ ] Execute loan documentation (agreement, hypothecation, etc.) as advised by the society  
- [ ] Ensure first disbursement is received only after fulfillment of pre-disbursement conditions  
- [ ] Utilize funds strictly for the approved project purpose as per the sanction letter  
- [ ] Maintain proper books of accounts and vouchers for all expenditures  
- [ ] Submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates as required by NCDC  
- [ ] Prepare for submission of final utilization certificate and audit report upon project completion  
- [ ] Coordinate with the client to ensure timely repayment as per the agreed schedule  
- [ ] Upon successful repayment and closure, obtain and store the No Dues Certificate from NCDC  
- [ ] Discuss success fee invoicing with the client per consultancy agreement  

> **Sources**: Scheme Key Facts (Application Process: Steps 6–8; Benefits; Key Caveats: "Utilization of funds must be strictly for the approved project purpose", "Regular monitoring and reporting are mandatory")
