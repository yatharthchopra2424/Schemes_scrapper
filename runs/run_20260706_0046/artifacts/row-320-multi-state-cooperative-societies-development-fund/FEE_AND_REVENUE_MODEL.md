## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial assistance available (loan-based support with concessional terms), ensuring consultant compensation aligns with the scale of benefit delivered.
2. **Risk Mitigation**: Success fees are contingent on loan sanction and disbursement, reducing client risk; processing typically takes 4–6 months based on NCDC’s evaluation and field verification steps, justifying a 3-month lag in revenue recognition.
3. **Transparency**: All fees and payment terms are disclosed upfront; retainers cover initial project assessment, documentation, and application submission, while success fees are triggered only upon formal loan approval.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: This scheme carries moderate risk due to its repayable loan nature (non-grant) and dependency on project viability assessment by NCDC. However, evidence shows consistent approvals for viable projects in priority sectors (agriculture, dairy, etc.), supporting a predictable success rate for well-prepared applications. Processing time averages 4–6 months, affecting cash flow timing but not ultimate revenue potential.
