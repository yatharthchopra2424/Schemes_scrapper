## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping multi-state cooperative societies access government-backed financial support for development and modernisation. The Multi State Cooperative Societies Development Fund, administered by the National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC), provides concessional loans for infrastructure, technology upgradation, and working capital needs. I’d like to understand your society’s current projects and goals to see if this fund could support your next phase of growth.

### Discovery Questions
- Is your cooperative society registered under the Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002, and operating in more than one state?
- What is the primary sector your society operates in (e.g., agriculture, dairy, fisheries, handicrafts, rural industries)?
- Do you have a viable project proposal for infrastructure development (e.g., godowns, processing units, retail outlets), technology upgradation, or working capital support?
- Have you prepared audited financial statements for the last three years?
- Do you have a detailed project report including technical and financial feasibility for the proposed initiative?
- What is your society’s current promoter contribution capacity for the project, as NCDC requires beneficiary equity contribution?
- Are there any outstanding dues to NCDC or other government agencies that might affect eligibility?
- Is your project aligned with priority given the rolling application basis, are you planning to apply in the near term to take advantage of current fund availability?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Registered under the Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002
- [ ] Operates in more than one state (multi-state)
- [ ] Has a viable project with clear developmental objectives
- [ ] Demonstrates repayment capacity
- [ ] Operates in a priority sector (agriculture, dairy, fisheries, handicrafts, rural industries) or underserved region
- [ ] Can provide audited financial statements for last 3 years
- [ ] Able to prepare detailed project report with technical and financial feasibility
- [ ] Can arrange promoter contribution as per NCDC norms
- [ ] No outstanding dues to NCDC or other government agencies

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your society meets several key criteria for the Multi State Cooperative Societies Development Fund. Let me outline how it works and how we can support your application.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Multi-state cooperative societies often face challenges in accessing affordable finance for infrastructure development, technology upgradation, and working capital needs. High interest rates and rigid repayment terms from conventional lenders can hinder modernisation and expansion, especially in priority sectors like agriculture, dairy, and rural industries.

**Scheme intro:**
The Multi State Cooperative Societies Development Fund, administered by the National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC) under the Ministry of Cooperation, provides term loans and working capital assistance to eligible multi-state cooperative societies. Financial support is determined based on project viability and scale, subject to fund availability and NCDC’s lending norms. Assistance is extended as a loan with concessional interest rates, flexible repayment schedules, and moratorium periods assessed case-to-case. The fund supports infrastructure (godowns, processing units, retail outlets), technology upgradation, automation, HR development, training, and working capital requirements.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Registered under the Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002 and operating across multiple states
- Engaged in a priority sector such as agriculture, dairy, fisheries, handicrafts, or rural industries
- Possesses audited financial statements for the last three years
- Capable of providing promoter contribution as per NCDC norms and has no outstanding dues to NCDC or government agencies
