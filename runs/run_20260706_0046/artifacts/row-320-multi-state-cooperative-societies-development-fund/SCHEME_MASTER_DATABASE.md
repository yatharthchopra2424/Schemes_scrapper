### Multi State Cooperative Societies Development Fund
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC), Ministry of Cooperation
- **Portal / Application URL:** [https://www.ncdc.in](https://www.ncdc.in)
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Multi-state cooperative societies
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Priority sectors include agriculture, dairy, fisheries, handicrafts, and rural industries
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be registered under the Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002; must demonstrate viable project proposals with clear developmental objectives and repayment capacity; must contribute minimum promoter's equity as per NCDC norms; societies with outstanding dues to NCDC or other government agencies may be ineligible

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Quantum of financial support determined based on viability and scale of the proposed project; includes term loans and working capital assistance at concessional interest rates with flexible repayment schedules
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (disbursement in stages as per project progress)
- Duration of benefit: Case-to-case basis (repayment duration assessed per project)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Registration under Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002
2. Memorandum of Association and Bye-laws of the society
3. Audited financial statements for the last 3 years
4. Detailed project report with technical and financial feasibility
5. Land documents or lease agreement for project site (if applicable)
6. Cost estimates and quotations for major equipment and civil works
7. Resolution of the Board of Directors approving the project and loan application
8. No Objection Certificate from concerned regulatory authorities (if required)
9. Promoter contribution details and means of finance
10. Project implementation schedule and timeline

#### Application Process
1. Identify a viable project proposal aligned with the developmental objectives of the fund
2. Prepare a detailed project report including technical, financial, and economic feasibility analysis
3. Submit the application along with required documents to the National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC)
4. NCDC undertakes preliminary scrutiny and field verification of the proposal
5. The proposal is evaluated by the competent authority within NCDC for approval
6. Upon approval, a sanction letter is issued outlining the terms and conditions of financial assistance
7. Loan documentation is executed and disbursement is made in stages as per project progress
8. Post-disbursement monitoring is conducted to ensure proper utilization of funds
