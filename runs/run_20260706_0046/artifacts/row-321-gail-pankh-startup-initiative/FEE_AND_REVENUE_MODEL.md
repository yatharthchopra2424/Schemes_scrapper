## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit delivered under the GAIL Pankh Startup Initiative, ensuring clients pay only for realized outcomes.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s low confidence rating and limited public evidence of disbursement or success rates, success fees are set at the lower end of industry ranges to reflect higher execution risk.
3. **Accessibility for Early-Stage Firms**: The pricing structure supports startups and MSMEs — the scheme’s explicit target — by avoiding prohibitive upfront costs and tying consultant compensation to performance.
4. **Transparency and Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover baseline advisory work, while success fees are contingent on verified benefit realization, reducing client financial risk.

*Note: The scheme’s processing time and success evidence are not fully documented in public sources, increasing uncertainty. Consultants should conduct enhanced due diligence and consider milestone-based billing if pilot engagements reveal delays.*
