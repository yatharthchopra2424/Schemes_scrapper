## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping early-stage startups in the energy and manufacturing sectors access government support designed to accelerate innovation and scale. The GAIL Pankh Startup Initiative is one such programme — created by GAIL (India) Limited to nurture promising startups working on clean energy, sustainability, and industrial innovation. I’d like to understand if your venture aligns with their focus and whether this could be a relevant opportunity for you.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated, and what is your current stage of development?  
2. What sector or technology domain does your startup operate in — particularly, are you working in areas like natural gas, renewable energy, sustainability, or industrial efficiency?  
3. Are you seeking support for prototype development, pilot projects, or scaling an existing solution?  
4. What is your current annual turnover, and how many employees do you have on your team?  
5. Have you previously applied for or received any government grants, seed funding, or incubation support?  
6. Are you looking for non-dilutive funding or strategic partnerships with established energy players?  
7. Do you have a working prototype or minimum viable product (MVP) that demonstrates technical feasibility?  
8. Are you open to mentorship, technical guidance, and potential collaboration with GAIL’s business units as part of the support package?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup incorporated within the last 5 years  
- [ ] Operating in sectors aligned with GAIL’s focus: natural gas, renewable energy, sustainability, or industrial innovation  
- [ ] Has a working prototype or MVP  
- [ ] Annual turnover below INR 25 crore (as per typical startup thresholds for such PSU initiatives)  
- [ ] Team size under 100 employees  
- [ ] Not currently receiving duplicative funding for the same project from another central government scheme  
- [ ] Registered as a private limited company, LLP, or recognised startup under DPIIT  
- [ ] Demonstrates potential for scalability and commercial viability  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, your startup appears to align well with the GAIL Pankh Startup Initiative’s focus on early-stage innovation in energy and sustainability. This scheme offers grant support, mentorship, and potential industry collaboration — exactly the kind of boost that can help validate and scale promising solutions. Let me walk you through how it works and what it could mean for your venture.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Early-stage startups in energy and sustainability often struggle to move beyond the prototype phase due to limited access to non-dilutive funding, technical mentorship, and industry validation. Without support, many promising innovations fail to reach pilot or commercial scale, especially when they require capital-intensive testing or regulatory clearance.

**Scheme intro:**
The GAIL Pankh Startup Initiative is a corporate social responsibility (CSR)-linked innovation programme launched by GAIL (India) Limited, a Maharatna PSU under the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. It provides selected startups with grant funding of up to INR 25 lakhs, along with technical mentorship, access to GAIL’s infrastructure, and opportunities for pilot collaborations or proof-of-concept projects within GAIL’s operations.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your startup is incorporated within the last 5 years, meeting the early-stage criterion  
- You are developing a solution in renewable energy, sustainability, or industrial efficiency — core focus areas of the initiative  
- You have a working prototype or MVP, which is essential for consideration  
- Your turnover and team size suggest you are pre-revenue or early-revenue, fitting the target beneficiary profile
