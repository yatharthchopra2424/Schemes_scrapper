# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Startup is a registered entity (Certificate of Incorporation/Registration available)
- [ ] Startup works on innovative solutions in domains relevant to IndianOil's business (energy, petrochemicals, sustainability, digital technologies)
- [ ] Startup has a scalable business model with potential for pilot integration with IOCL operations
- [ ] Startup possesses a detailed business proposal or pitch deck outlining innovation, relevance to IndianOil's domains, and expected outcomes
- [ ] Startup has provided details of funding received (if any)
- [ ] Startup has submitted an authorization letter from an authorized signatory
- [ ] Startup has PAN of the entity

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
- PAN of the entity
- Business proposal or pitch deck
- Details of funding received (if any)
- Authorization letter from authorized signatory

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Business proposal or pitch deck (specifically outlining innovation relevance to IndianOil's domains: energy, petrochemicals, sustainability, digital technologies)
- Details of funding received (if any)
- Authorization letter from authorized signatory

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and in English or accompanied by certified translation
- File names must match the document type (e.g., "Certificate_of_Incorporation.pdf")
- PDF files preferred; scanned copies must be clear and high-resolution
- Authorization letter must be on company letterhead and signed by an authorized signatory
- Business proposal/pitch deck must include problem statement, solution, market potential, and alignment with IndianOil's focus areas

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required. The scheme requires a business proposal or pitch deck instead of a detailed project report. Verify with nodal officer if any specific format is mandated for the proposal.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the portal at https://iocl.com/startup and complete startup registration
2. Ensure all mandatory fields in the proposal submission form are filled accurately
3. Upload documents in the specified format (PDF preferred) with clear, legible scans
4. Clearly articulate how the innovation aligns with IndianOil's business domains (energy, petrochemicals, sustainability, digital technologies)
5. Highlight scalability and potential for pilot integration with IOCL operations in the proposal
6. Include quantifiable outcomes and milestones where possible
7. Double-check that the authorization letter is signed and dated
8. Save a copy of the submitted proposal and reference number for tracking

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All required documents are attached and clearly legible
- [ ] Business proposal/pitch deck explicitly addresses relevance to IndianOil's domains
- [ ] Authorization letter is present and signed by authorized signatory
- [ ] PAN and Certificate of Incorporation are valid and match the entity name
- [ ] No blank mandatory fields in the application form
- [ ] File sizes are within portal limits (if specified; otherwise keep under 10MB per file)
- [ ] Application reference number has been recorded post-submission

---

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via https://iocl.com/startup after completing registration
- Capture a screenshot of the confirmation page showing submission success
- Store the application reference number securely in the case file
- Set a follow-up reminder for 30 days post-submission to check status

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and review the approval letter or collaboration agreement from IOCL
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned non-financial support (access to resources, mentorship, infrastructure, etc.) as outlined in the agreement
- [ ] Define clear milestones and deliverables for the pilot/project phase
- [ ] Prepare utilization reports or progress updates as required by IOCL
- [ ] Track success metrics for potential commercial collaboration or scaling
- [ ] Invoice success fee (if applicable per engagement terms) upon achievement of agreed milestones
- [ ] Ensure IP rights are clarified in separate agreements as per scheme caveats
- [ ] Maintain records of all interactions, approvals, and deliverables for audit and future reference
