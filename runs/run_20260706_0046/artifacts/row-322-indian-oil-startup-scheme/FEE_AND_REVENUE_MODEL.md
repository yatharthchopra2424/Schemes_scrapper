## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the non-financial support value (access to IOCL’s infrastructure, technical expertise, mentorship, and collaboration opportunities) rather than direct grants, as the scheme specifies no fixed financial support.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s rolling application basis and dependency on mutual agreement for pilot projects; lower success fee tiers apply due to uncertain financial outcomes and reliance on negotiated terms.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The Growth tier (MSME/Startup) aligns with the scheme’s eligibility for startups in energy, petrochemicals, sustainability, and digital tech domains, ensuring affordability for early-stage ventures.
4. **Outcome-Linked Revenue**: Success fees are contingent on securing tangible outcomes (e.g., pilot agreements, commercial collaboration), mitigating client risk while tying consultant compensation to measurable scheme-derived benefits.
