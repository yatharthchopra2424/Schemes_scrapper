# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialise in helping innovative startups in the energy, petrochemicals, sustainability, and digital technology sectors connect with PSU support programmes like the Indian Oil Startup Scheme. I’d love to understand your current innovation stage and explore whether this scheme could be a strategic fit for your growth.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated or registered?
2. What specific domain or technology is your innovation focused on (e.g., energy, petrochemicals, sustainability, digital tech)?
3. Do you have a working prototype or proof of concept ready for pilot testing?
4. Are you seeking collaboration opportunities with established PSUs for scaling or validation?
5. What stage of funding have you reached so far (bootstrapped, angel, seed, etc.)?
6. Are you open to non-financial support such as mentorship, infrastructure access, and technical guidance?
7. Do you have a clear business proposal or pitch deck outlining your innovation’s relevance to IndianOil’s operations?
8. Are you authorised to sign agreements and represent the entity in discussions with IndianOil?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup is a registered entity (Certificate of Incorporation/Registration available)
- [ ] Innovation is in a domain relevant to IndianOil’s business (energy, petrochemicals, sustainability, digital technologies, etc.)
- [ ] Startup has a scalable model with potential for pilot integration with IOCL operations
- [ ] Applicant has submitted or can submit a detailed business proposal/pitch deck
- [ ] Authorised signatory is available to provide authorization letter
- [ ] PAN of the entity is available
- [ ] Details of any prior funding are documented (if applicable)
- [ ] Startup is based in India (Pan-India eligibility)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, your startup appears to align well with the Indian Oil Startup Scheme’s focus on innovative, scalable solutions in energy and allied sectors. The scheme offers access to IndianOil’s technical expertise, infrastructure for pilot testing, mentorship, and potential commercial collaboration—without requiring upfront financial commitment. Would you like me to walk you through how it works and what the next steps could be?

Startups in energy, sustainability, and related deep-tech sectors often struggle to access corporate-grade infrastructure, technical mentorship, and validation opportunities needed to de-risk their innovations and scale. Without PSU partnerships, pilot testing and commercialisation can be delayed or under-resourced.

**Scheme intro:**
The Indian Oil Startup Scheme, administered by Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL), is a pan-India incubation initiative that supports startups with innovative solutions in energy, petrochemicals, sustainability, and digital technologies. It does not offer direct financial grants but provides non-financial support including access to IOCL’s technical expertise, infrastructure for pilot testing, mentorship from industry experts, and opportunities for commercial collaboration and visibility within IOCL’s innovation network.
