### Indian Oil Startup Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Incubation
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://iocl.com/startup
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Startups
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Energy, petrochemicals, sustainability, digital technologies, and other domains relevant to IndianOil's business
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be registered entities working on innovative solutions; preference given to scalable models with potential for pilot integration with IOCL operations

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Non-financial support (access to technical expertise, infrastructure for pilot testing, mentorship, potential for commercial collaboration, visibility through IOCL's innovation network); financial engagement based on mutually agreed terms for pilot projects or joint development
- Disbursement mode: Not applicable (non-financial support); financial terms mutually agreed
- Duration of benefit: Not specified (ongoing collaboration based on agreement)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. Business proposal or pitch deck
4. Details of funding received (if any)
5. Authorization letter from authorized signatory

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the official IndianOil startup portal at https://iocl.com/startup
2. Register as a startup on the platform
3. Submit a detailed proposal outlining the innovation, its relevance to IndianOil's domains, and expected outcomes
4. Await review by the IndianOil innovation team
5. If shortlisted, participate in discussions for potential pilot or collaboration
6. Upon agreement, proceed with defined milestones and deliverables
