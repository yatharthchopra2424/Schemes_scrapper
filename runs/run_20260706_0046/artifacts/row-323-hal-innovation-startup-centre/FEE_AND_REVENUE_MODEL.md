## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the non-financial value delivered (access to HAL infrastructure, technical expertise, testing facilities, and supply chain integration potential), as no direct monetary grant is specified in the scheme.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the moderate risk profile due to HAL’s internal evaluation process, lack of guaranteed funding or orders, and case-by-case IP negotiations, despite strong alignment with Make-in-India and defence innovation goals.
3. **Tiered Access**: Pricing scales with client maturity and capacity to engage with a PSU like HAL, ensuring accessibility for early-stage startups while capturing value from those nearing pilot or integration stages.
4. **Outcome Orientation**: Success fees are triggered only upon tangible outcomes such as pilot project approval, facility access grant, or supply chain integration — not mere application submission.
