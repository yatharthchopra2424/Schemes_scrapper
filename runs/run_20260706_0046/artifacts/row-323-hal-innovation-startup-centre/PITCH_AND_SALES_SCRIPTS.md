## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping aerospace and defence startups access strategic partnerships with PSUs like Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL). The HAL Innovation & Startup Centre is designed specifically for early-stage companies working on technologies that align with HAL’s operational needs in areas like avionics, materials, MRO, and aerospace systems. I’d like to understand if your startup’s focus and stage make this a relevant opportunity for you.

### Discovery Questions
- What specific aerospace, defence, avionics, materials, or MRO technology is your startup developing?
- When was your company incorporated, and how long have you been operating?
- Does your technology address any known challenges or requirements in HAL’s current product lines (e.g., LCA, HTT-40, Dhruv, or SU-30 MKI systems)?
- Are you seeking opportunities for prototype testing, validation, or potential integration into a defence PSU’s supply chain?
- Have you previously engaged with any PSU or defence organisation for collaboration, testing, or pilot projects?
- Is your startup incorporated in India and operating as a legal entity?
- Are you open to negotiating intellectual property terms on a case-by-case basis with a PSU partner?
- Does your innovation support Make-in-India initiatives in the aerospace or defence sector?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup is working on innovative technology in aerospace, defence, avionics, materials, MRO, or related domains
- [ ] Technology aligns with HAL’s technological requirements and Make-in-India initiatives
- [ ] Startup is incorporated and operational in India
- [ ] No direct financial grant or loan is expected — value is in access to infrastructure, expertise, and collaboration
- [ ] Startup understands participation is subject to HAL’s internal evaluation and roadmap alignment
- [ ] Startup accepts that there is no guarantee of funding, order placement, or long-term collaboration
- [ ] Startup is prepared to negotiate IP terms case-by-case with HAL

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like your work in [specific domain mentioned] could align well with what HAL is seeking through its Innovation & Startup Centre. This isn’t a funding grant, but rather a pathway to access HAL’s infrastructure, technical expertise, and potential for pilot projects or supply chain integration — exactly the kind of strategic validation that early aerospace and defence startups need. Let me explain how it works.
