## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-aligned pricing**: Fees are calibrated to the actual financial benefit delivered, which for this scheme is determined case-by-case for proof-of-concept validation and pilot projects, with no standardized quantum.
2. **Risk-adjusted success fees**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s risk profile—financial support is not guaranteed and depends on mutual agreement and fund availability, with processing involving proposal review, presentations, and final selection by SAIL’s innovation committee.
3. **Transparency**: Clients are informed that while non-financial benefits (access to manufacturing units, technical mentorship, leadership exposure) are guaranteed for selected startups, financial support is discretionary and subject to negotiation.
4. **Scalability**: The model accommodates the scheme’s rolling, year-round application process with no fixed deadline, enabling continuous client intake.
