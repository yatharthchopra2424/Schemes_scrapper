# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping innovative startups in sectors like steel processing, automation, AI/IoT, sustainability, and waste management access government-backed support programs. I understand you’re working on an innovative product or technology in one of these domains—could you share a bit about what you’re building and where you are in your journey?

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated, and do you have a valid DPIIT recognition certificate?
2. What stage is your product or technology at—do you have a prototype or minimum viable product (MVP) ready for pilot testing?
3. Which specific domain does your innovation address: steel processing, automation, AI/IoT, sustainability, waste management, or another related industrial application?
4. Are you seeking opportunities to test your solution in a real industrial environment, such as a manufacturing plant?
5. What kind of support are you looking for—technical mentorship, access to infrastructure, pilot testing facilities, or potential long-term collaboration?
6. Have you previously engaged with any PSUs or large industrial entities for pilot projects or collaborations?
7. What are your near-term goals for scaling or validating your solution?
8. Are you open to exploring structured pathways for integration into a larger value chain, such as SAIL’s operations?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup registered in India with a valid DPIIT recognition certificate  
- [ ] Working on innovative products, services, or technologies in domains such as steel processing, automation, AI/IoT, sustainability, waste management, or related industrial applications  
- [ ] Has a prototype or minimum viable product (MVP) ready for pilot testing  
- [ ] Target beneficiary is a startup (as defined by DPIIT)  
- [ ] Seeking access to manufacturing units, technical mentorship, or collaboration opportunities  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your startup aligns well with the SAIL Startup & Innovation Policy—a program that offers selected startups access to SAIL’s manufacturing units for pilot testing, technical mentorship from SAIL experts, and potential long-term collaboration. Since you have an MVP ready and are working in a relevant domain, this could be a strong fit. Would you like me to walk you through how it works?

**Problem framing:**
Startups with innovative prototypes often struggle to access real-world industrial environments for testing, lack domain-specific mentorship from established players, and face challenges in validating their solutions at scale—especially in capital-intensive sectors like steel and manufacturing.

**Scheme intro:**
