### SAIL Startup & Innovation Policy
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Steel Authority of India Limited (SAIL)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://sail.co.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Startups registered in India
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Innovative products, services, or technologies in domains such as steel processing, automation, AI/IoT, sustainability, waste management, or related industrial applications
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must have a valid DPIIT recognition certificate and a prototype or minimum viable product (MVP) ready for pilot testing

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support determined on a case-by-case basis for proof-of-concept validation and pilot projects; access to SAIL's manufacturing units for pilot testing, technical mentorship from SAIL experts, opportunities to present solutions to SAIL's leadership, potential for long-term collaboration or integration into SAIL's operations, and visibility through SAIL's innovation network
- Disbursement mode: Not specified (case-by-case mechanism)
- Duration of benefit: One-time (for pilot projects and proof-of-concept validation)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. DPIIT Startup Recognition Certificate
3. PAN of the entity
4. Audited financial statements (if available)
5. Detailed project proposal or pitch deck
6. Proof of concept or prototype details
7. Team details and resumes of key founders
8. Any existing partnerships or pilot project reports

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the SAIL official website and navigate to the Startup & Innovation Policy section
2. Review the eligibility criteria and focus areas
3. Prepare a detailed proposal including startup profile, problem statement, solution description, stage of development, and expected outcomes
4. Submit the application via the designated portal or email address provided on the SAIL website
5. Applications are reviewed by SAIL's innovation committee
6. Shortlisted startups are invited for presentations or discussions
7. Final selection is made based on innovation, feasibility, and alignment with SAIL's objectives
8. Selected startups are notified and guided through the next steps for collaboration
