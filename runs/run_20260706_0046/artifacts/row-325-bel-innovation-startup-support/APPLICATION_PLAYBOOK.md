# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a Company (including MSMEs and Start-ups) or R&D Institution (comprising Academia, Research Institutes, R&D organizations and Companies working in niche technologies) in India or abroad
- [ ] Applicant is an Expert/Consultant working independently or associated with R&D Institutions and having requisite expertise for collaborative R&D/Contract Research/Product and Subsystem development programs
- [ ] For individual experts/consultants: Possess qualifications, demonstrated experience, patents granted, papers published, awards received, and number of projects completed in the relevant field
- [ ] For R&D institutions and companies: Type of institution, number of years of operation, available manpower expertise, papers published, patents granted, number of projects/processes licensed and commercialized, total number of projects completed in the relevant field
- [ ] Applicant seeks to engage in collaborative R&D/Contract Research/Product and Subsystem development programs in the areas of BEL’s operations
- [ ] Applicant is willing to submit the completed Response Format (A-1 for institutions or A-2 for individual consultants) along with required documents via email to collab [at]bel[dot]co[dot]in

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate
- PAN Card
- ITR (last 2-3 years)
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (for MSMEs/Start-ups)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Completed Response Format for R&D Institution Empanelment (A-1) or Individual Consultant Empanelment (A-2)
- Details of qualifications and demonstrated experience
- Information on patents granted
- List of papers published
- Records of awards received
- Details of number of projects completed in the relevant field
- For institutions: Type of institution, number of years of operation, available manpower expertise
- For institutions: Details of papers published, patents granted, number of projects/processes licensed and commercialized
- For institutions: Total number of projects completed in the relevant field

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and in PDF or image format (JPEG/PNG)
- File size should not exceed 5 MB per document
- Document names must match the applicant’s name or entity name exactly
- Certificates must be self-attested where required
- Documents must be in English or accompanied by certified translation
- Photographs must be recent passport-size with light background (not older than 3 months)
- Signatures must be scanned on white paper with black ink pen only

### Client Communication Template
Subject: Application Invitation – BEL Innovation & Startup Support (Collaborative R&D) – Rolling Basis

Dear [Client Name],

This is to inform you about the BEL Innovation & Startup Support scheme (Collaborative R&D initiative) offered by Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL), Ministry of Defence, Government of India. The scheme invites Expression of Interest from Companies (including MSMEs and Start-ups) and R&D Institutions (academia, research institutes, etc.) and Experts/Consultants for collaborative R&D, contract research, and product/subsystem development in BEL’s operational areas.

**Key Details:**
- **Scheme Name:** BEL Innovation & Startup Support  
- **Application Portal:** https://bel-india.in/collaborative-rd/  
- **Status:** Rolling basis — applications accepted year-round, no fixed deadline

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required. The scheme does not mandate a Detailed Project Report (DPR). Applicants must complete the Response Format (A-1 or A-2) as the primary application document. Verify with nodal officer if any project-specific DPR is needed post-empanelment.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Download the correct form: A-1 for R&D Institutions or A-2 for Individual Consultants from https://bel-india.in/collaborative-rd/
2. Fill all fields in CAPITAL LETTERS only
3. Do not leave any field blank; if not applicable, write “N/A”
4. Attach self-attested copies of all supporting documents as specified
5. Ensure qualifications, experience, patents, papers, awards, and project details are accurately reflected
6. For institutions, provide complete details on type, years of operation, manpower, publications, patents, licensed projects, and total relevant projects
7. Do not alter the category or any pre-filled section once entered in the form
8. Sign the declaration and attach the scanned signature (black ink on white paper only)

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Application form (A-1 or A-2) fully completed in CAPITAL LETTERS
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents attached and clearly labeled
- [ ] Universal documents (PAN, ITR, GST, Bank Statements, Udyam) included
- [ ] Photograph and signature scanned as per guidelines (recent, light background, black ink)
- [ ] Declarations signed and dated
- [ ] No false or misleading information provided
- [ ] File sizes under 5 MB per document
- [ ] Email ready for submission to collab [at]bel[dot]co[dot]in

---

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed application form (A-1 or A-2) along with all required documents via email to collab [at]bel[dot]co[dot]in
- Take a screenshot of the sent email showing recipient, subject, and attachments
- Save the email reference or sent confirmation
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days post-submission if no acknowledgment received

### Escalation Path
- Level 1: Portal helpdesk – Email: collab [at]bel[dot]co[dot]in (same as submission email)
- Level 2: Nodal Officer – Head (Technology Planning), Bharat Electronics Limited, Registered & Corporate Office, Outer Ring Road, Nagavara, Bangalore – 560 045, Email: collab [at]bel[dot]co[dot]in

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and save the empanelment confirmation email or letter from BEL
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned terms (scope of work, time frame, remuneration, deliverables, IPR ownership) as outlined in the specific agreement
- [ ] Prepare for potential NDA execution if required before project commencement
- [ ] Ensure all statutory compliances (e.g., taxes) are met for any material transactions
- [ ] Track project milestones and deliverables as per the agreement
- [ ] Utilization certificate not applicable (no grant; payment based on delivered work)
- [ ] Invoice BEL for services rendered as per agreed terms and payment schedule
- [ ] Follow up on payment receipt and maintain records of all transactions
- [ ] Explore long-term collaboration opportunities (MoU/MoA) based on performance
