## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment, document preparation, and application submission for BEL Collaborative R&D empanelment.  
2. Success fee is payable only upon formal empanelment and execution of a specific R&D project agreement or MoU with BEL.  
3. Fees are structured to reflect the non-grant nature of the scheme — revenue is tied to project-based remuneration, not direct financial disbursement.  
4. Scheme-specific note: This scheme involves technical collaboration and project-based payments (not grants), resulting in medium risk due to variable project allocation post-empanelment and dependence on BEL’s technical requirements and approval timelines.
