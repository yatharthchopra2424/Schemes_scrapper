# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL), a Navratna PSU under the Ministry of Defence, Government of India, is actively seeking MSMEs and start-ups to collaborate on R&D projects in defence electronics and related sectors. Their Collaborative R&D initiative is designed to build stronger partnerships, improve delivery timelines, and support sustainable business growth—especially for companies working in niche technology areas. I’d like to explore if your organisation might be a good fit for this opportunity.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your organisation type (e.g., private limited, LLP) was your company incorporated or established?
2. What is the primary technology domain or niche area your company specialises in (e.g., AI, IoT, cybersecurity, embedded systems, etc.)?
3. How many years has your company been operational?
4. Do you have any granted patents or published research papers in your field?
5. Have you completed any R&D projects or developed products/systems that have been licensed or commercialised?
6. What is the size of your technical team or available manpower expertise?
7. Have you received any awards or recognitions for innovation or technical excellence?
8. Are you currently engaged in any collaborative R&D or contract research work with government or defence organisations?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Company is a startup or MSME (as per BEL’s definition)
- [ ] Operational for a demonstrable period (years of operation considered in empanelment)
- [ ] Has qualified technical manpower or expertise
- [ ] Has published papers or granted patents (evidence of innovation)
- [ ] Has completed relevant projects in the technology domain
- [ ] Capable of providing documentation: qualifications, experience, patents, papers, awards, project history
- [ ] Willing to sign MoU/MoA and comply with statutory procedures (e.g., taxes, NDAs)
- [ ] Located in India or abroad (BEL accepts global applicants)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your organisation aligns well with BEL’s Collaborative R&D initiative—particularly given your focus on [specific domain from answers] and your track record in [patents/projects/papers]. BEL is currently empanelling partners for joint R&D work, technical consultancy, and long-term collaboration via MoUs, with opportunities to access their R&D ecosystem and technology modules. Would you like me to outline how this works and what benefits it could bring to your organisation?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many startups and MSMEs in deep-tech sectors struggle to access defence-grade R&D infrastructure, validate their technologies through government-backed testing, or secure long-term collaboration opportunities with established PSUs like BEL—despite having innovative solutions that align with national goals like Aatmanirbhar Bharat.

**Scheme intro:**
The **BEL Innovation & Startup Support** scheme, administered by Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL) under the Ministry of Defence, Government of India, facilitates collaborative R&D and outsourcing mechanisms. Instead of direct financial grants, BEL engages empaneled MSMEs and start-ups through joint R&D work (on assignment or long-term basis via MoUs), technical consultancy, design vetting, verification, testing, characterisation, specialised engineering software, modelling, and expert guidance on an annual retainership basis. Empanelled partners gain access to BEL’s R&D ecosystem, technology modules, subsystems, and support for product development—promoting indigenisation and cost reduction in alignment with Aatmanirbhar Bharat.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your organisation is a startup/MSME operating in [specific domain], which falls within BEL’s areas of operations (e.g., Radar & Fire Control Systems, Communication Systems, Electronic Warfare, etc.)
- You have demonstrated experience through [number] completed projects, [number] patents granted, and [number] papers published—key criteria BEL uses for empanelment
- Your technical team and years of operation ([X] years) meet BEL’s evaluation benchmarks for R&D institutions and companies
- You are prepared to provide the required documentation: qualifications, experience, patents, papers, awards, and project history
