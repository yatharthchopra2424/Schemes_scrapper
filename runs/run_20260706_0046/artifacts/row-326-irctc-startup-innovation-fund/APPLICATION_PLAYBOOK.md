# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Startup incorporated in India (Certificate of Incorporation/Registration required)
- [ ] Working on innovative products, services, or processes in domains such as railway catering, tourism, digital ticketing, passenger experience, logistics, or allied sectors
- [ ] Focus on scalability and social impact
- [ ] Preference may be given to startups led by women, SC/ST, or differently-abled entrepreneurs (verify if applicable)
- [ ] No explicit turnover, employee count, or age of business thresholds mentioned in evidence — verify with nodal officer if such implicit criteria apply

> **Note**: Eligibility criteria are based on the scheme's focus on innovation in IRCTC-aligned sectors. No hard thresholds (turnover, employees, years) are specified in the evidence. Confirm any unstated requirements with the IRCTC innovation committee.

**Sources**:  
- Scheme Key Facts: Eligibility section  
- Application Process: Step 3 (submit application detailing innovation, team, business model, impact)

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration  
- PAN of the entity  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration (if applicable for MSME benefits)  
- ITR of the entity (last 2 years, if available)  
- GST registration certificate (if applicable)  
- Founder profiles and KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN of founders)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Pitch deck or business description  
- Details of funding received (if any)  
- Technical prototype or MVP details (if applicable)  

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are legible, scanned in PDF format (max file size: 5 MB per document)  
- [ ] File names follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_VersionDate.pdf` (e.g., `IRCTC_InnovationFund_PitchDeck_20240515.pdf`)  
- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation and PAN match the legal name of the entity  
- [ ] Founder KYC documents (Aadhaar/PAN) are valid and not expired  
- [ ] Pitch deck includes problem statement, solution, market size, business model, team, traction, and financials  
- [ ] Technical prototype/MVP details include screenshots, demo video link, or description of functionality  
- [ ] All documents are in English or accompanied by certified English translation  
- [ ] No handwritten documents unless explicitly permitted (none indicated in evidence)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not explicitly required per evidence. The application process emphasizes a pitch deck and business description rather than a formal DPR.  
**Flag**: Verify with nodal officer if a detailed project report is expected alongside the pitch deck.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official IRCTC website (https://irctc.co.in) or designated innovation portal to register as a startup and create a profile.  
2. Fill in all mandatory fields in the application form, including startup details, innovation description, team information, and business model.  
3. Upload documents in the specified order: Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, pitch deck, funding details, technical prototype/MVP, founder KYC.  
4. Ensure the innovation description clearly aligns with IRCTC’s strategic goals: railway catering, tourism, digital ticketing, passenger experience, logistics, or allied sectors.  
5. Highlight scalability and social impact potential in the application narrative.  
6. If applicable, mention any preference factors (women-led, SC/ST, differently-abled founder) in the relevant section.  
7. Double-check that all uploaded files are under 5 MB and in PDF format before final submission.  
8. Do not submit the application until all documents are attached and declarations are signed; incomplete applications may not be screened.

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Application form fully completed with no blank mandatory fields  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents attached and correctly named  
- [ ] Pitch deck includes problem, solution, market, model, team, traction, and ask  
- [ ] Innovation clearly maps to IRCTC sectors (railway catering, tourism, ticketing, etc.)  
- [ ] Founder profiles and KYC documents are valid and match application details  
- [ ] Declarations and consent checkboxes are signed/accepted  
- [ ] Application saved as draft for internal review before final submission  
- [ ] Reference number or submission ID will be captured post-submission

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the official IRCTC website or designated innovation portal (https://irctc.co.in).  
- Immediately after submission, take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing the application reference number and timestamp.  
- Store the screenshot and reference number in the case folder with naming convention: `CaseID_IRCTC_InnovationFund_Submission_YYYYMMDD.png`.  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 15 days post-submission to check status if no update is received.  
- Mark calendar for potential pitch/presentation invitation (typically within 3–4 weeks of submission, per evidence).

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and store the approval letter from IRCTC specifying sanctioned grant/seed funding amount and terms  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount against the application and due diligence outcomes  
- [ ] Review and sign any agreement related to fund disbursement, IP rights, data security, and milestone commitments  
- [ ] Track milestone achievement as per agreement for tranche-based disbursement (funds released upon milestone completion and due diligence)  
- [ ] Prepare utilization certificate (UC) for each tranche as per IRCTC’s format and timeline  
- [ ] Ensure compliance with IRCTC’s data security and privacy policies during pilot/testing phase  
- [ ] Monitor for potential integration of solution into IRCTC platforms and branding opportunities  
- [ ] Confirm invoice for success fee (if applicable per firm’s engagement model) post-approval  
- [ ] Maintain records of mentorship sessions and access provided to IRCTC’s user base for pilot testing  

**Note**: Financial support is disbursed upon milestone achievement and due diligence, with exact quantum determined case-by-case. Confirm disbursement schedule with IRCTC post-approval.
