**Data Handling Policy:**

1. **Storage:** All client data is stored in encrypted cloud storage with AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit.
2. **Access Control:** Access to client data is restricted to authorized personnel only, enforced via role-based access controls (RBAC).
3. **Authentication:** Multi-factor authentication (2FA) is required for all system access.
4. **Device Policy:** No personal devices are permitted for accessing or processing client data; all work must be conducted on company-issued, secured devices.
5. **Retention:** Client data is retained for a period of two (2) years post case closure, after which it is securely deleted unless otherwise required by law or scheme-specific obligations.
6. **Breach Notification:** In the event of a data breach, affected parties and relevant authorities will be notified within 24 hours of discovery.
7. **Scheme-specific note:** All documents submitted to Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation Limited (IRCTC) under IRCTC Startup Innovation Fund must be retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit purposes.
8. **Disposal:** Secure deletion methods (e.g., cryptographic erasure) are used for data disposal to ensure no recovery is possible.
9. **Compliance:** This policy aligns with the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 under the IT Act, 2000, and other applicable data protection regulations in India.
