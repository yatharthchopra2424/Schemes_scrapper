## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the transformative potential of the IRCTC Startup Innovation Fund, which offers non-dilutive grants, mentorship, and market access — justifying success fees in the 6–8% range for eligible startups.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: The scheme has a **medium risk profile** due to case-by-case funding determination, milestone-based disbursement, and dependency on IRCTC’s internal approval processes (evidence: low confidence in key facts, continued support contingent on performance). Success fees are set at the mid-range (6–8%) to balance effort against uncertainty in approval timing and amount.
3. **Effort Proportionality**: Retainer covers profile optimization, pitch deck refinement, document preparation (incorporation, PAN, MVP details, founder KYC), and application submission — efforts scale with client maturity but are standardized for startup applicants.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Processing time is not fixed; approvals depend on screening, pitch presentations, and due diligence. Based on evidence of multi-stage screening (application → screening → pitch → final selection), a **3-month lag** from application to approval is realistic for revenue modeling.

## Invoice Templates

### Retainer Invoice
**Description**: Retainer fee for IRCTC Startup Innovation Fund application support — including startup profiling, innovation positioning, document compilation (incorporation, PAN, pitch deck, MVP details, founder KYC), application submission, and liaison with IRCTC innovation committee.

### Success Fee Invoice
**Description**: Success fee for securing funding under the IRCTC Startup Innovation Fund, implemented by Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation Limited (IRCTC), upon disbursement of grant or seed funding to the startup.
