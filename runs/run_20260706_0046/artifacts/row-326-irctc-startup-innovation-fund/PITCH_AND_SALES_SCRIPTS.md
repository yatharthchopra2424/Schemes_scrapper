# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping innovative startups in the railway, tourism, and digital ticketing sectors access government-backed funding and support. The IRCTC Startup Innovation Fund is designed specifically for early-stage ventures like yours that are solving real problems in railway catering, tourism, passenger experience, or logistics. I’d love to learn about your innovation and see if this opportunity and explore whether this fund could be the right catalyst for your growth.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated in India?  
2. What specific problem are you solving in the railway, tourism, digital ticketing, passenger experience, logistics, or allied sectors?  
3. Do you have a working prototype, MVP, or pilot version of your product or service?  
4. Have you received any external funding so far, and if yes, at what stage and from whom?  
5. Can you share a brief overview of your founding team and their relevant expertise?  
6. Is your solution designed with scalability and social impact in mind?  
7. Are any of the founders women, from SC/ST communities, or differently-abled?  
8. Have you considered how your innovation could integrate with or be piloted within IRCTC’s operations?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup incorporated in India  
- [ ] Working on an innovative product, service, or process  
- [ ] Focused on domains: railway catering, tourism, digital ticketing, passenger experience, logistics, or allied sectors  
- [ ] Demonstrates scalability and social impact  
- [ ] Has a pitch deck or business description ready  
- [ ] Can provide Certificate of Incorporation and PAN  
- [ ] Founder profiles and KYC documents available  
- [ ] Technical prototype or MVP details available (if applicable)  
- [ ] Details of any prior funding available (if applicable)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, your work in [mention specific domain, e.g., digital ticketing or passenger experience] aligns closely with what the IRCTC Startup Innovation Fund seeks to support. This fund offers grants, mentorship, and direct access to IRCTC’s vast user base — exactly the kind of support that could help you validate, scale, and potentially integrate your solution into one of India’s largest transportation and tourism platforms.

**Problem framing:**
