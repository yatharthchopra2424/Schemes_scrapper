### IRCTC Startup Innovation Fund
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation Limited (IRCTC)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://irctc.co.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Startups incorporated in India
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Railway catering, tourism, digital ticketing, passenger experience, logistics, or allied sectors
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Focus on scalability and social impact; preference may be given to startups led by women, SC/ST, or differently-abled entrepreneurs

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support through grants or seed funding (quantum determined case-by-case); mentorship from IRCTC experts; access to IRCTC's user base for pilot testing; potential integration into IRCTC platforms; branding and visibility through IRCTC channels; guidance on regulatory and compliance matters
- Disbursement mode: Upon milestone achievement and due diligence
- Duration of benefit: One-time (subject to performance and milestone achievement)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. Pitch deck or business description
4. Details of funding received (if any)
5. Technical prototype or MVP details (if applicable)
6. Founder profiles and KYC documents

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the official IRCTC website or designated innovation portal.
2. Register as a startup and create a profile.
3. Submit an application detailing the innovation, team, business model, and potential impact.
4. Upload required documents including incorporation proof, PAN, pitch deck, and technical details.
5. Await screening by the IRCTC innovation committee.
6. Shortlisted startups may be invited for a pitch or presentation.
7. Final selection and disbursement of funds post due diligence and agreement signing.
