## Pricing Principles
- Fees are structured to reflect the value delivered, with retainers covering initial advisory and documentation efforts, and success fees tied to measurable outcomes such as empanelment and project acquisition.
- Success fee percentages decrease with client size and scheme value to align with decreasing marginal effort and increasing deal complexity.
- The RailTel Innovation Challenge operates on an empanelment model with no direct financial grants; revenue for clients comes from revenue-sharing or service-based projects post-empanelment, making success contingent on post-selection business development.
- Processing time for empanelment is not fixed (rolling basis), but typical scrutiny and LOE issuance takes 2–3 months, supporting a 3-month lag assumption in revenue projections.
