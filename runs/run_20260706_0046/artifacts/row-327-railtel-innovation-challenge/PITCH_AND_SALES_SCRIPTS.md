# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping technology providers, MSMEs, and startups gain strategic access to large PSU customers through structured empanelment programs. RailTel’s Innovation Challenge is designed specifically for firms like yours that have innovative ICT or telecom solutions and are looking to scale via a Mini Ratna PSU’s nationwide infrastructure and customer base. Before we explore fit, I’d like to understand your current business stage and goals to see if this aligns.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your firm incorporated or registered? (Must be at least 6 months old)
2. What type of legal entity are you operating as? (Limited, Pvt. Ltd., LLP, Startup, or Proprietary)
3. Are you offering technology solutions in areas like telepresence, data center services, cloud, AI/ML, IoT, or smart city projects?
4. Do you have experience delivering projects to government, PSU, or enterprise clients?
5. Are you open to revenue-sharing or service-based partnership models rather than upfront grants?
6. Can you provide a CA certificate with UDIN, HR certificate, and power of attorney as part of your application?
7. Have you previously applied for empanelment with any PSU or government entity?
8. Are you using credentials from a parent, subsidiary, or holding company for qualification? (If yes, Annexure-7 applies)

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Incorporation/registration date is at least 6 months old as of application date  
- [ ] Firm type: Limited, Pvt. Limited, LLP, Startup, or Proprietary  
- [ ] Willing to pay non-refundable empanelment fee (Rs 2,00,000 for established/proprietary; Rs 40,000 for startups)  
- [ ] Able to submit Covering Letter (Annexure-1)  
- [ ] Able to submit Empanelment Fee payment proof (UTR details)  
- [ ] Able to submit Supplier and Customer Form (Annexure-2a and 2b) with Excel sheet and cancelled cheque  
- [ ] Able to submit Power of Attorney (Annexure-3)  
- [ ] Able to submit Backlisting Certificate (Annexure-4)  
- [ ] Able to submit CA Certificate with valid UDIN (Annexure-5)  
- [ ] Able to submit HR Certificate (Annexure-6)  
- [ ] If using parent/subsidiary/holding company credentials: Annexure-7 completed  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like your firm meets the core eligibility for RailTel’s Innovation Challenge — particularly your incorporation timeline and focus on innovative tech solutions. This scheme doesn’t offer grants but provides empanelment with RailTel, giving you access to their nationwide infrastructure, customers across sectors, and co-development opportunities on a revenue-sharing basis. Let me walk you through how it works and the value it could bring to your growth plans.
