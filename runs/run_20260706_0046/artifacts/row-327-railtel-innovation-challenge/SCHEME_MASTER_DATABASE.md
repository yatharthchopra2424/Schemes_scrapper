### RailTel Innovation Challenge
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** RailTel Corporation of India Limited
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://railtelindia.com
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Any Firm (Limited/Pvt. Limited/LLP/Startup/Proprietary)
- Age of business: Incorporation/registration date of at least 6 months old as on the date of application submission
- Sector: Technology providers, startups, MSME, enterprises
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Applicants may use credentials of Parent/Subsidiary/Holding Company subject to undertaking as per Annexure-7

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Empanelment model with revenue-sharing or service-based collaboration; empanelment fees: Rs 2,00,000 for established and proprietary firms, Rs 40,000 for start-up firms
- Disbursement mode: Revenue-sharing or service-based model
- Duration of benefit: Empanelment valid for 3 years from Letter of Empanelment (LOE) issuance date

#### Documents Required
1. Covering Letter (as per Annexure-1)
2. Empanelment Fee payment proof (UTR details)
3. Supplier and Customer Form (as per Annexure-2a and 2b) with Excel sheet and cancelled cheque copy
4. Power of Attorney (as per Annexure-3)
5. Backlisting Certificate (as per Annexure-4)
6. CA Certificate with valid UDIN (as per Annexure-5)
7. HR Certificate (as per Annexure-6)
8. Annexure-7 (if using credentials of Parent/Subsidiary/Holding Company)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Prepare application with all required documents as per EOI requirements.
2. Pay empanelment fee via RTGS and attach UTR details.
3. Submit the complete application (single PDF for documents, Excel sheet for Supplier/Customer form, payment details) to baapplications@railtelindia.com.
4. Application undergoes scrutiny for eligibility and compliance.
5. Shortlisted applicants may be contacted for clarification or additional documentation.
6. Final selection leads to issuance of Letter of Empanelment (LOE).
7. Empanelment is valid for 3 years from LOE issuance date.
