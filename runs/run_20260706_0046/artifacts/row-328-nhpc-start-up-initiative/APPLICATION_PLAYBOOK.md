## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] The applicant is a registered Start-Up under the Startup India initiative (DPIIT-recognized)
- [ ] The Start-Up is incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Registered Partnership Firm
- [ ] The Start-Up is not more than 10 years old from the date of incorporation/registration
- [ ] The Start-Up has an annual turnover not exceeding INR 100 Crore in any of the previous financial years
- [ ] The Start-Up is engaged in innovation, development, or improvement of products, processes, or services, or has a scalable business model with high potential for employment generation or wealth creation
- [ ] The Start-Up is not formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business
- [ ] The Start-Up is working towards innovation in sectors aligned with NHPC’s core areas (hydropower, renewable energy, power systems, or related technologies) – *verify specific sector alignment with nodal officer*
- [ ] The Start-Up has a valid Udyam Registration Certificate
- [ ] The Start-Up has a valid GST Registration (if applicable)
- [ ] The Start-Up has a PAN and TAN (if applicable)
- [ ] The Start-Up has a bank account in the name of the entity

> **Note**: Eligibility criteria are inferred from standard Startup India and PSU innovation scheme frameworks, as specific NHPC Start-Up Initiative criteria were not explicitly detailed in the provided evidence. ## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Partnership Deed / LLP Agreement
- PAN Card of the entity
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)
- Udyam Registration Certificate
- Bank Statement (last 6 months) in the name of the entity
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the entity for the last 2 financial years (if applicable)
- Audited Financial Statements (Balance Sheet, P&L) for the last 2 financial years (if applicable)
- Cancelled cheque or bank verification letter
- Authorized Signatory’s PAN and Aadhaar
- Board Resolution authorizing application (for Companies/LLPs)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- DPIIT Start-Up Recognition Certificate (mandatory proof of eligibility)
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining innovation, technology, scalability, and alignment with NHPC’s domains (hydropower, renewable energy, power systems)
- Proof of concept / prototype / MVP demonstration (if applicable)
- Intellectual Property (IP) details (patents filed/granted, copyrights, trademarks) if relevant to the innovation
- Team profile with qualifications and roles of key founders/technical staff
- Letter of intent or endorsement from a recognized incubator, accelerator, or academic institution (if available)
- Declaration that the Start-Up is not formed by splitting or reconstructing an existing business
- Undertaking on utilization of funds strictly for the proposed innovation/project

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format (minimum 300 DPI)
- File names must follow format: `SchemeName_DocumentType_ClientName_Date.pdf` (e.g., `NHPC_Startup_Initiative_DPR_ABC_Tech_20240515.pdf`)
- No password protection or encryption on submitted files
- All certificates must be valid and not expired (check issue/expiry dates)
- Documents in regional languages must be accompanied by a certified English/Hindi translation
- Financial documents must be certified by a Chartered Accountant (CA)
- DPR must be signed and stamped by the authorized signatory and CA (if financials included)
- Maximum file size per document: 10 MB; total submission not to exceed 25 MB unless portal allows more

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
✅ **Required** – A Detailed Project Report (DPR) is mandatory for the NHPC Start-Up Initiative based on the scheme’s focus on innovation and technology alignment.  
**Standard Sections to Include**:
1. Executive Summary
2. Problem Statement and Innovation Proposition
3. Technology / Solution Description
4. Alignment with NHPC’s Core Sectors (Hydropower, Renewable Energy, Power Systems, Grid Management)
5. Market Analysis and Scalability Potential
6. Business Model and Revenue Projections (3–5 years)
7. Go-to-Market Strategy
8. Technical Feasibility and Development Roadmap
9. IP Status (Patents Filed/Granted, etc.)
10. Team Background and Expertise
11. Financial Plan: Fund Utilization, Break-even Analysis, ROI
12. Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan
13. Expected Outcomes: Employment Generation, Technology Adoption, Societal Impact
14. Annexures: Prototypes, Test Results, Letters of Support

> **Note**: Confirm exact DPR format and length requirements with the NHPC nodal officer, as specific guidelines may vary.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official NHPC Start-Up Initiative application portal – verify URL from NHPC’s website or nodal officer.
2. Register using the authorized signatory’s email and mobile number (OTP verification required).
3. Fill in all fields accurately; do not leave any mandatory field blank.
4. Enter the Start-Up’s official name exactly as per Certificate of Incorporation.
5. Ensure DPIIT recognition number is entered correctly in the designated field.
6. Select the correct sector(s) aligned with NHPC’s domains (e.g., Renewable Energy, Power Technology).
7. Upload documents in the specified order and format as per portal instructions.
8. Declare that the Start-Up is not formed by splitting or reconstructing an existing business.
9. Provide honest and realistic financial projections; avoid overstatement.
10. Double-check all uploaded files for correctness, naming, and size before final submission.
11. Review the declaration section carefully and ensure digital signature is applied where required.
12. Take a screenshot of the confirmation page after submission and save the application reference number.
13. Do not use special characters or symbols in text fields unless explicitly allowed.
14. Save draft periodically to avoid data loss due to session timeout.
