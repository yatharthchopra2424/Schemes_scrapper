## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are calibrated to the actual grant amount disbursed under the NHPC Start-Up Initiative, ensuring consultant compensation scales with client benefit.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s low confidence rating and limited public evidence of disbursements, success fees reflect the uncertainty in approval likelihood and potential delays in fund release.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The Growth tier pricing is designed for early-stage startups and MSMEs, aligning with the scheme’s focus on nurturing innovation in emerging enterprises.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover initial eligibility assessment and application preparation, while success fees are contingent only on verified fund disbursement.
