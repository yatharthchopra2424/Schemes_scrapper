</think># Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I’m reaching out because I specialise in helping early-stage startups in the energy and infrastructure sectors access government support designed specifically for innovative ventures. The NHPC Start-Up Initiative is a PSU-backed programme that provides funding and mentorship to startups working on solutions aligned with hydropower, renewable energy, and sustainable infrastructure — areas where NHPC has deep expertise. I’d like to understand if your venture might be a fit.

### Discovery Questions
- When was your company incorporated, and is it less than 10 years old?
- Are you working on a technology, product, or service related to hydropower, renewable energy, energy efficiency, or sustainable infrastructure?
- Is your startup registered in India and currently operational?
- Do you have a working prototype or minimum viable product (MVP), or are you in the early stages of product development?
- Are you seeking funding, mentorship, or technical validation to scale your solution?
- Are you looking to collaborate with or gain access to PSU resources, testing facilities, or industry networks?
- Have you previously applied for any government startup schemes or grants?
- Are you open to participating in a structured mentorship or pilot programme with a PSU like NHPC?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Company incorporated less than 10 years ago  
- [ ] Operating in hydropower, renewable energy, energy efficiency, or sustainable infrastructure  
- [ ] Registered and operational in India  
- [ ] Has a prototype, MVP, or defined product/service in development  
- [ ] Seeking funding, mentorship, or technical validation  
- [ ] Open to PSU collaboration or access to NHPC resources  
- [ ] Not currently receiving duplicate funding for the same innovation from another central government scheme  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your venture aligns well with the NHPC Start-Up Initiative’s focus on early-stage innovation in energy and infrastructure. This scheme offers selected startups grant funding, mentorship, and access to NHPC’s technical expertise — potentially up to several lakhs in support — to help validate and scale solutions. Would it be okay if I took five minutes to explain how it works and whether you might qualify?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Problem framing:
Many early-stage startups in the energy and infrastructure space struggle to access patient capital, technical validation, and industry connections needed to move from prototype to market. Traditional funding sources often hesitate due to long development cycles and regulatory complexity, leaving innovative solutions under-supported despite their national importance.

### Scheme intro:
The NHPC Start-Up Initiative is a programme administered by NHPC Limited, a Mini Ratna PSU under the Ministry of Power, designed to support innovative startups working in hydropower, renewable energy, energy efficiency, and sustainable infrastructure. It provides selected startups with financial assistance (grant-based support), mentorship from NHPC experts, and opportunities for pilot testing or collaboration — typically ranging from ₹5 lakhs to ₹25 lakhs per venture, depending on the scope and stage of innovation.

### Why they qualify (personalised):
- Your company is incorporated within the last 10 years  
- You are developing a technology or solution in hydropower, renewable energy, or sustainable infrastructure  
- You are based in India and have a working prototype or MVP  
- You are seeking non-dilutive support to validate or scale your innovation
