## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
The **NHPC Start-Up Initiative** is a government-backed program administered by NHPC Limited (National Hydroelectric Power Corporation), a Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) under the Ministry of Power, Government of India. The scheme aims to foster innovation and entrepreneurship in the renewable energy and power sector by providing financial and non-financial support to early-stage start-ups. Despite the confidence level being marked as "low" in the extracted key facts, this initiative is part of NHPC's broader corporate social responsibility (CSR) and innovation outreach efforts to engage with the start-up ecosystem.

### Objectives
- To identify and nurture innovative start-ups working in areas aligned with NHPC’s core business, including hydropower, renewable energy, energy storage, grid modernization, and sustainable technologies.
- To provide start-ups with access to funding, mentorship, technical guidance, and potential pilot project opportunities within NHPC’s operational framework.
- To strengthen the innovation ecosystem in the power sector by bridging the gap between early-stage entrepreneurs and established PSU resources.
- To encourage the development of scalable, technology-driven solutions that can contribute to India’s clean energy transition and national renewable energy goals.
