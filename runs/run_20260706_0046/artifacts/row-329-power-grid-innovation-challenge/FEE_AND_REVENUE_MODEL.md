## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are tied to the actual grant or benefit awarded under the Power Grid Innovation Challenge, ensuring consultant compensation scales with client outcomes.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s low confidence rating and PSU Support category (indicating potential bureaucratic complexity and longer review cycles), success fees are set at the higher end of typical ranges to reflect application uncertainty and effort required.
3. **Tiered Accessibility**: The retainer structure allows early-stage innovators (MSME/startup tier) to access expert guidance without prohibitive upfront costs, aligning with the scheme’s focus on grassroots innovation in power grid technologies.
4. **Transparency & Trust**: All fees are disclosed upfront; retainers cover initial eligibility assessment and proposal drafting, while success fees are payable only upon formal award confirmation from the implementing agency.
