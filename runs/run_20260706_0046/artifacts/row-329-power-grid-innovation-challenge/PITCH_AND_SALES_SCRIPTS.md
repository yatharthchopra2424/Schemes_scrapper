## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping power sector innovators access government funding for grid modernisation projects. The Power Grid Innovation Challenge is designed specifically for organisations developing smart grid technologies, renewable integration solutions, or advanced metering infrastructure. I’d like to understand your current initiatives to see if this scheme aligns with your work.

### Discovery Questions
- What specific power grid innovation are you currently developing or planning (e.g., smart grid, energy storage integration, grid automation)?
- When was your organisation incorporated or registered?
- Are you a PSU, private company, startup, or research institution operating in the power sector?
- Do you have a working prototype or pilot project ready for demonstration?
- What stage of development is your innovation at (concept, prototype, pilot, or scale-up)?
- Are you seeking funding for technology development, pilot deployment, or scaling a proven solution?
- Do you have partnerships with utilities, DISCOMs, or other grid operators for implementation?
- What is your annual turnover or project budget for the innovation initiative?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Organisation is a PSU, private company, startup, or research institution
- [ ] Innovation is in the power grid sector (smart grid, renewable integration, grid automation, etc.)
- [ ] Organisation has a working prototype or pilot-ready solution
- [ ] Innovation addresses grid modernisation, efficiency, or reliability challenges
- [ ] Applicant can demonstrate potential for scalability or utility partnership
- [ ] Organisation is incorporated and operational (no specific age limit stated, but viability assessed)
- [ ] Project aligns with national grid innovation goals (e.g., NSGM, smart grid vision)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, your work in [specific innovation area] appears to align closely with the Power Grid Innovation Challenge’s focus on advancing grid technologies. This scheme offers grant support to help innovators like you develop and deploy solutions that strengthen India’s power infrastructure. Let me explain how it works.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Power sector innovators often struggle to secure funding for the development and pilot deployment of grid technologies due to high R&D costs, long validation cycles, and limited access to utility partners. This delays the adoption of critical solutions needed for grid resilience, renewable integration, and efficiency gains.

**Scheme intro:**
The Power Grid Innovation Challenge is a grant-based scheme administered by the Power System Operation Corporation Limited (POSOCO) under the Ministry of Power. It provides financial support of up to ₹50 lakhs for innovative projects in smart grid technologies, grid automation, renewable energy integration, and other advanced power grid solutions.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your innovation in [specific area from discovery] falls within the scheme’s focus on grid modernisation technologies
- You have a working prototype or pilot-ready solution, meeting the scheme’s readiness requirement
- Your organisation is incorporated and operational, eligible to apply as a PSU, private entity, startup, or research institution
- You are seeking funding for development or deployment, which aligns with the scheme’s grant purpose
