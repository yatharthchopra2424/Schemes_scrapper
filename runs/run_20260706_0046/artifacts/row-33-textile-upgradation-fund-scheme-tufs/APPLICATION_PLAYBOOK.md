## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Unit is engaged in eligible textile activities: spinning, weaving, processing, knitting, garmenting, made-ups, technical textiles, or jute  
- [ ] Unit is registered as a proprietorship, partnership, private limited company, public limited company, cooperative society, or other legal entity engaged in textile manufacturing  
- [ ] Machinery proposed for upgradation is new (indigenous or imported) and has not been previously used  
- [ ] Term loan for machinery procurement is availed from a scheduled commercial bank or financial institution approved by the Ministry of Textiles  
- [ ] Unit is either an existing textile unit or a new textile unit (MSME or otherwise)  
- [ ] Application is submitted during the rolling window (subject to fund availability)  
- [ ] For capital subsidy under Modified TUFS: unit operates in high-priority segments (processing, weaving, or garmenting)

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not explicitly required for TUFS based on evidence. However, a brief project note detailing the upgradation objective, machinery details, expected benefits (productivity, quality, energy savings), and implementation timeline may be useful for internal review. **Verify with nodal officer if DPR is mandated for specific cases.**

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the official portal: **https://tufs.nic.in**  
2. Register the unit using valid email and mobile number; verify via OTP  
3. Fill in unit details exactly as per PAN and registration documents (name, address, constitution)  
4. Select correct textile segment (spinning, weaving, processing, etc.) from dropdown  
5. Enter loan details: sanction amount, date, bank name, loan account number, tenure  
6. Upload machinery details: supplier, make, model, cost, proforma invoice number  
7. Declare that machinery is new and not previously used (mandatory)  
8. Attach all documents in correct format  
9. Preview all details  
10. Submit and save the application reference number**Note**:  
7. Declare that machinery is new and not previously used (mandatory undertaking)  
8. Ensure bank account details for subsidy disbursement match the loan account or registered business account  
9. Do not leave any mandatory field blank; use “Not Applicable” only if explicitly allowed  
11. Use DSC for signing if portal prompts; otherwise, upload signed declaration  
12. Review all entries before final submission — no edits allowed post-submission
