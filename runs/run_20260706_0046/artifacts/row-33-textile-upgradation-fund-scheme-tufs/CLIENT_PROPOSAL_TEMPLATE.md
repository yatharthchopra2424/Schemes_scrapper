## 1. Executive Summary
Based on our initial review of [Client]'s business profile, we have identified the Textile Upgradation Fund Scheme (TUFS) — a subsidy scheme administered by the Office of the Textile Commissioner, Ministry of Textiles, Government of India — for which your business appears eligible. The potential benefit includes interest reimbursement of up to 5% per annum on term loans for 5 to 7 years, or a capital subsidy of up to 10% (capped at ₹30 lakhs) for high-priority segments such as processing, weaving, and garmenting.

## 3. Our Scope of Work
1. Initial eligibility assessment and scheme suitability analysis  
2. Documentation review and gap identification  
3. Assistance with loan application preparation for submission to scheduled commercial banks  
4. Portal registration on [https://tufs.nic.in](https://tufs.nic.in) and submission of TUFS application  
5. Coordination with nodal agency (Office of the Textile Commissioner) for application Commissioner) for verification and approval  
6. Support in submitting installation certificates, machinery invoices, and quarterly interest payment certificates  
7. Follow-up for disbursement of interest reimbursement or capital subsidy  

*Note: A detailed project report (DPR) is not mandatory under TUFS; however, machinery quotations, proforma invoices, and installation certificates are required as per the scheme guidelines.*
