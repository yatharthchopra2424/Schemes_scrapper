## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Fees**: Success fees are tied to the actual financial benefit realized by the client (interest reimbursement or capital subsidy), ensuring our compensation reflects the value delivered.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s rolling application basis and dependency on fund availability, success fees reflect moderate execution risk; retainers cover upfront due diligence and documentation effort.
3. **Transparency**: All fees are disclosed upfront; clients receive itemized invoices linking retainer to documentation support and success fee to verified disbursement agency confirmation.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: TUFS has a processing time of 5–7 years for interest reimbursement disbursement (per evidence), creating a long revenue lag. Success fees are therefore structured to be paid only upon actual receipt of funds by the client, reducing client risk and aligning consultant incentives with verified outcomes.
