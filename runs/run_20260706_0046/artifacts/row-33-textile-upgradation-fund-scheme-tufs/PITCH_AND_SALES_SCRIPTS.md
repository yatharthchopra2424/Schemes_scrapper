## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping textile units access government support for technological upgradation. The Textile Upgradation Fund Scheme (TUFS) is designed specifically for businesses like yours in spinning, weaving, processing, knitting, garmenting, made-ups, technical textiles, or jute manufacturing to reduce the cost of modernising machinery. I’d like to understand your current operations and see if this scheme could support your growth plans.

### Discovery Questions
- What type of textile manufacturing activity is your unit primarily engaged in (e.g., spinning, weaving, processing, garmenting, technical textiles)?
- When was your unit incorporated or registered, and what is your current legal structure (proprietorship, partnership, private limited, etc.)?
- Are you planning to procure new machinery for upgradation, expansion, or modernisation, and if so, what type and estimated value?
- Have you already applied for or sanctioned a term loan from a scheduled commercial bank or financial institution for this machinery?
- What is the approximate annual turnover or investment size of your unit, and do you classify as an MSME?
- Are you looking to improve productivity, reduce energy consumption, or enhance product quality through technology adoption?
- Have you previously applied for any government subsidies or schemes for textile machinery upgradation?
- What is your timeline for installing and commissioning the new machinery?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Unit is engaged in eligible textile activities: spinning, weaving, processing, knitting, garmenting, made-ups, technical textiles, or jute
- [ ] Unit is registered as a proprietorship, partnership, private limited, public limited company, cooperative society, or other legal entity
- [ ] Machinery proposed for upgradation is new (indigenous or imported) and not previously used
- [ ] Term loan has been or will be availed from a scheduled commercial bank or financial institution approved by the Ministry of Textiles
- [ ] Unit is an existing or new textile unit (MSME focus but open to all eligible units)
- [ ] Machinery falls under eligible segments for capital subsidy (processing, weaving, garmenting) if seeking 10% subsidy
- [ ] Unit has not previously claimed benefits for the same machinery under TUFS
- [ ] Unit is prepared to submit required documents: loan sanction letter, incorporation certificate, machinery quotations, installation certificate, invoices, bank statements, interest payment certificates, undertaking on newness, and machinery details

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your unit may be eligible for the Textile Upgradation Fund Scheme (TUFS), which offers interest reimbursement of up to 5% or a capital subsidy of up to 10% (capped at INR 30 lakhs) on eligible machinery. This could significantly reduce your effective cost of capital for technological upgradation. Let me explain how it works and how we’ve helped similar textile units access this support.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Textile units face high capital costs when upgrading to modern machinery, which limits their ability to improve productivity, reduce energy consumption, and stay competitive. Accessing affordable financing for such upgradation remains a key challenge, especially for MSMEs in the textile value chain.

**Scheme intro:**
The Textile Upgradation Fund Scheme (TUFS), administered by the Office of the Textile Commissioner under the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, provides financial assistance in the form of interest reimbursement of up to 5% per annum on term loans for 5–7 years, or a capital subsidy of up to 10% (with a ceiling of INR 30 lakhs) for specific high-priority segments like processing, weaving, and garmenting. The scheme supports both existing and new textile units across India. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis via the portal: https://tufs.nic.in.
