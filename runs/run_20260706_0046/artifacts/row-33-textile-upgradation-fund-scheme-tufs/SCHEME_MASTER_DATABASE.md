### Textile Upgradation Fund Scheme (TUFS)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Office of the Textile Commissioner, Ministry of Textiles, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://tufs.nic.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Proprietorship, partnership, private limited company, public limited company, cooperative society, or any other legal entity engaged in textile manufacturing
- Age of business: Existing and new textile units
- Sector: Textile manufacturing (spinning, weaving, processing, knitting, garmenting, made-ups, technical textiles, and jute)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Machinery proposed for upgradation must be new and indigenous or imported, and must not have been used previously

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Interest reimbursement of up to 5% on term loans for 5 to 7 years; capital subsidy of up to 10% (ceiling of INR 30 lakhs) for specific segments (processing, weaving, garmenting) under Modified TUFS
- Disbursement mode: Reimbursement (through nodal agencies after verification of loan documents and installation of machinery)
- Duration of benefit: 5 to 7 years (interest reimbursement); one-time (capital subsidy)

#### Documents Required
1. Loan sanction letter from bank/financial institution
2. Certificate of Incorporation/Registration of the unit
3. Machinery quotations and proforma invoices
4. Installation certificate of machinery
5. Machinery invoices and payment proof
6. Bank statements showing loan disbursement and interest payments
7. Quarterly interest payment certificates
8. Undertaking regarding newness of machinery
9. Details of existing and proposed machinery

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify eligible machinery for upgradation and obtain quotations from suppliers
2. Apply for a term loan from a scheduled commercial bank or financial institution for the procurement of machinery
3. Submit the loan sanction letter and machinery details to the nodal agency (Office of the Textile Commissioner) for registration under TUFS
4. Install the machinery and submit installation certificate along with invoices and bank statements
5. Submit quarterly interest payment certificates to claim interest reimbursement
6. The nodal agency verifies the documents and disburses the interest reimbursement or capital subsidy as applicable
