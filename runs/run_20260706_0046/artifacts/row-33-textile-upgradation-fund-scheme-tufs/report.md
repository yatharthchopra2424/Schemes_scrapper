## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The Textile Upgradation Fund Scheme (TUFS) is a subsidy-based initiative launched by the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, aimed at facilitating technological upgradation across the textile value chain. Administered by the Office of the Textile Commissioner, the scheme operates on a rolling basis with applications accepted throughout the year subject to fund availability. It targets both existing and new textile units engaged in various textile manufacturing activities, providing financial support through interest reimbursement and capital subsidy mechanisms.

### Objectives
- Facilitate technological upgradation of the textile industry  
- Enhance productivity, quality, and competitiveness of textile units  
- Promote investment in modernization and expansion of textile machinery  
- Support the textile value chain from fibre to garment  
- Reduce energy consumption and improve environmental performance  
- Encourage adoption of state-of-the-art technology in textile processing  

### Eligibility Matrix
| **Criteria**               | **Details**                                                                                                                                                               |
|----------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Unit Type**              | Existing and new textile units                                                                                                                                            |
| **Eligible Activities**    | Spinning, weaving, processing, knitting, garmenting, made-ups, technical textiles, jute                                                                                   |
| **Legal Entity**           | Proprietorship, partnership, private limited company, public limited company, cooperative society, or any other legal entity engaged in textile manufacturing             |
| **Machinery Requirements** | Must be new (indigenous or imported); second-hand or used machinery is **not eligible**                                                                                   |
| **Geographic Scope**       | Pan-India                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Target Beneficiaries**   | MSMEs, textile units (existing and new)                                                                                                                                   |

> **Key Caveat**: Only new machinery (indigenous or imported) is eligible; second-hand or used machinery is explicitly excluded from coverage.

### Benefits & Financial Support
| **Support Type**           | **Details**                                                                                                                                                               | **Limits & Conditions**                                                                                                                               |
|----------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Interest Reimbursement** | Reimbursement of interest paid on term loans for eligible machinery                                                                                                       | Up to **5% per annum** for a period of **5 to 7 years**, depending on segment and machinery type; capped at loan tenure or 5–7 years, whichever is less |
| **Capital Subsidy**        | Available under Modified TUFS for high-priority segments                                                                                                                  | Up to **10%** of machinery cost, with a ceiling of **INR 30 lakhs**; applicable only for processing, weaving, and garmenting segments                  |
| **Disbursement Mechanism** | Through nodal agencies (Office of the Textile Commissioner) after verification of loan documents and installation                                                         | Funds released post-installation and submission of required documentation                                                                             |
| **Non-Financial Benefits** | Improved productivity, product quality, energy efficiency, and market competitiveness                                                                                     | Indirect outcomes from technological upgradation                                                                                                      |

> **Important Note**: Financial support is disbursed **only after** successful installation and verification of machinery. Interest reimbursement claims require quarterly interest payment certificates.

### Required Documents
1. Loan sanction letter from bank/financial institution  
2. Certificate of Incorporation/Registration of the unit  
3. Machinery quotations and proforma invoices  
4. Installation certificate of machinery  
5. Machinery invoices and payment proof  
6. Bank statements showing loan disbursement and interest payments  
7. Quarterly interest payment certificates  
8. Undertaking regarding newness of machinery  
9. Details of existing and proposed machinery
