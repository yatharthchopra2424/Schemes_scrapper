## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A formal Detailed Project Report (DPR) is **not explicitly required** by the scheme. However, a **Project Proposal** is mandatory (see Scheme-Specific Documents). This should include:  
- Technology of interest (from CSIR portfolio)  
- Objectives for adoption (commercialization, process improvement, etc.)  
- Implementation plan and timeline  
- Required infrastructure and technical readiness  
- Expected benefits (output, efficiency, market expansion)  
- Commercialization strategy and revenue projections  
- IPR and licensing expectations  

> **Note**: Verify with the concerned CSIR laboratory or Technology Business Development Group (TBDG) whether a DPR is needed for specific technology negotiations.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
There is no standardized online application form for this scheme. Instead, the process is engagement-driven. Follow these rules:  
1. **Identify technology first**: Use the CSIR technology portal or contact CSIR laboratories/TBDG to find relevant technologies.  
2. **Initiate contact**: Reach out to the concerned CSIR laboratory or Technology Business Development Group (TBDG) before drafting any proposal.  
3. **Align proposal with technology**: Ensure the Project Proposal directly addresses the selected CSIR technology and its intended use.  
4. **Mutual discussion**: Be prepared to discuss technology suitability, terms of transfer, and collaboration scope during initial talks.  
5. **Document readiness**: Have all documents (especially Company Profile, Financials, and Infrastructure details) ready for sharing during discussions.  
6. **Authorization in place**: Ensure the signatory is authorized to negotiate and sign MoUs or technology transfer agreements.  
7. **Clarify financial terms**: Understand that financial support is case-specific (subsidized licensing, shared R&D costs, etc.) and not standardized — negotiate based on evaluation.  
8. **Follow CSIR’s process**: Adhere to the 5-step application process: identify → contact → discuss → negotiate → implement.

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
There is no online form submission. Instead:  
1. After internal review, share the complete document package with the **concerned CSIR laboratory or Technology Business Development Group (TBDG)** via email.  
2. Use the subject line: `Technology Transfer Inquiry – [Client Name] – MSME – [Technology of Interest]`  
3. Attach all documents (universal and scheme-specific) as per naming convention.  
4. **Screenshot the sent email** and store it in the case folder.  
5. Save the email thread as the primary reference; note the date of first contact as the submission date.  
6. Set a follow-up reminder for **7 days** after initial contact if no response.
