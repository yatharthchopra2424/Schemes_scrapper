## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the actual financial and operational benefit derived from technology access, which varies by technology and agreement terms.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the moderate risk profile due to case-specific financial support, negotiation dependency, and technology availability — not guaranteed grants.
3. **Transparency & Customization**: Retainers are customized based on client complexity and technology scouting effort; success fees are only charged upon signed agreement and technology transfer.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Unlike fixed-grant schemes, CSIR Technologies for MSMEs involves negotiated financial support (e.g., subsidized licensing, shared R&D costs), making outcomes highly variable. Processing time is not standardized and depends on mutual agreement, lab priorities, and technology readiness — introducing revenue lag uncertainty. Success is contingent on both technical evaluation and commercial viability assessment by CSIR laboratories.
