## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon, I'm calling from [Your Firm Name]. We specialise in helping MSMEs access government innovation support, particularly schemes like the CSIR Technologies for MSMEs programme that connects businesses with CSIR-developed technologies for commercialisation and process improvement. I'd like to understand your current technology needs and see if this could be a fit for your growth plans.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your business incorporated, and are you currently registered as an MSME under the MSMED Act, 2006 (Udyam Registration)?
2. What specific technologies or processes are you looking to adopt or improve in your operations currently?
3. Have you explored any CSIR-developed technologies or research outcomes relevant to your sector?
4. What is your current technical infrastructure and capability to absorb and scale new technologies?
5. Are you interested in licensing, joint R&D, or technology transfer arrangements for commercialisation?
6. What are your goals for scaling up lab-scale processes to industrial scale in the next 12-18 months?
7. Do you have a project proposal or clear use case in mind for adopting external technology?
8. Are you open to discussing IPR-related matters and negotiation terms as part of a technology agreement?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is a registered MSME under MSMED Act, 2006 (Udyam Registration Certificate available)
- [ ] Interest in adopting CSIR-developed technologies for commercialisation or process improvement
- [ ] Demonstrated absorptive capacity and technical capability to implement technology
- [ ] Clear commercialization potential or process improvement use case identified
- [ ] Willingness to engage in negotiation for technology transfer agreement or MoU
- [ ] Ability to provide company profile, audited financial statements, and project proposal
- [ ] Authorization letter from signatory can be arranged
- [ ] Located in India (Pan-India scheme)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you've shared, it sounds like you're actively looking to enhance your technological capabilities through external innovation — exactly what the CSIR Technologies for MSMEs scheme is designed to support. This programme facilitates access to CSIR-developed technologies with financial and technical support tailored to your needs. Would it be helpful if I outlined how it works and what benefits it could deliver for your business?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many MSMEs struggle to access cutting-edge research and technologies due to limited R&D budgets, lack of direct links to research institutions, and challenges in scaling lab-scale innovations. This gap hinders innovation, competitiveness, and the ability to modernise processes or commercialise new products effectively.

**Scheme intro:**
The CSIR Technologies for MSMEs scheme, administered by the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR), facilitates the transfer of CSIR-developed technologies to eligible MSMEs. It provides access to technologies through licensing, technical guidance, support for technology absorption, assistance in scaling up lab-scale processes to industrial scale, joint R&D opportunities, and IPR-related facilitation. Financial support varies case-by-case and may include subsidized licensing fees, shared R&D costs, or project-based funding, subject to technical and commercial evaluation.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a registered MSME under the MSMED Act, 2006 (Udyam Registration)
- You have expressed interest in adopting CSIR-developed technologies for commercialisation or process improvement
- You possess or can develop the technical infrastructure and capability needed for technology absorption
- You have a clear project proposal outlining the intended use of the technology

**Proof:**
"We've helped [N] similar businesses get approved for this. [TO BE FILLED BY CONSULTANT]"

**Next step:**
We offer a free, no-obligation eligibility check to assess your fit with the scheme and identify relevant CSIR technologies. If there's a potential match, we can support you through the entire process — from technology identification and laboratory engagement to agreement negotiation and implementation support. Our fee is success-based, so you only pay if we help you secure access to the technology and support.
