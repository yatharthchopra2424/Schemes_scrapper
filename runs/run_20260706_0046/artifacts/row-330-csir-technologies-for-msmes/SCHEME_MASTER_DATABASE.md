### CSIR Technologies for MSMEs
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.csir.res.in
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the year based on technology availability and mutual agreement.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) as defined under the MSMED Act, 2006
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Not specified (open to all sectors interested in CSIR-developed technologies)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be interested in adopting CSIR-developed technologies for commercialization or process improvement; must demonstrate adequate absorptive capacity and commercialization potential

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Financial support varies based on technology and agreement terms; may include subsidized licensing fees, shared R&D costs, or project-based funding as mutually agreed between CSIR laboratories and the MSME
- Disbursement mode: Not specified (subject to mutual agreement; likely project-based or reimbursement)
- Duration of benefit: Not specified (case-specific, based on agreement terms)

#### Documents Required
1. MSME Registration Certificate (Udyam Registration)
2. Company Profile and Audited Financial Statements
3. Project Proposal outlining intended use of technology
4. Authorization Letter from Signatory
5. Details of Existing Infrastructure and Technical Capability

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify relevant CSIR technology through the CSIR technology portal or CSIR laboratories.
2. Contact the concerned CSIR laboratory or Technology Business Development Group (TBDG).
3. Discuss technology suitability, terms of transfer, and potential collaboration.
4. Negotiate and sign a technology transfer agreement or MoU.
5. Implement the technology with technical support from CSIR as per the agreement.
