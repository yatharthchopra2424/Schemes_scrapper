## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
**Scheme Name:** CSIR Technologies for MSMEs  
**Scheme ID:** row-330  
**Ministry / Category:** Innovation Grants  
**Scheme Type:** Other  
**Geographic Scope:** Pan-India  
**Implementing Agency:** Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR)  
**Application Portal:** [https://www.csir.res.in](https://www.csir.res.in)  
**Status / Deadlines:** Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the year based on technology availability and mutual agreement.  
**Confidence Level:** Medium  

### Objectives
- Facilitate transfer of CSIR-developed technologies to MSMEs  
- Enhance technological capabilities and competitiveness of MSMEs  
- Promote industrial application of CSIR R&D outcomes  
- Support innovation and value addition in MSME sector  
- Strengthen industry-science linkages for sustainable growth  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Criterion | Requirement | Source |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| Entity Type | Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) as defined under the MSMED Act, 2006 | Key Facts |
| Interest Area | Must be interested in adopting CSIR-developed technologies for commercialization or process improvement | Key Facts |
| Geographic Scope | Pan-India (no regional restrictions) | Key Facts |
| Absorptive Capacity | Must demonstrate adequate absorptive capacity and commercialization potential | Key Caveats |

> **> Warning:** Eligibility is not automatic; MSMEs must actively seek out relevant technologies and negotiate terms with CSIR laboratories. Simply being an MSME does not guarantee access to funding or technology transfer.

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Benefit Category | Specific Benefits | Details |
|------------------|-------------------|---------|
| Technology Access | Access to CSIR-developed technologies through licensing | Subject to negotiation and IPR terms |
| Technical Guidance | Technical support for technology absorption | Provided by CSIR laboratories as per agreement |
| Scale-up Assistance | Assistance in scaling up lab-scale processes to industrial scale | Case-specific support |
| Collaboration Opportunities | Joint R&D opportunities | Mutually agreed between CSIR labs and MSME |
| IPR Facilitation | Facilitation of IPR-related matters | Includes patent filing, licensing, and technology transfer agreements |

### Required Documents
1. MSME Registration Certificate (Udyam Registration)  
2. Company Profile and Audited Financial Statements  
3. Project Proposal outlining intended use of technology  
4. Authorization Letter from Signatory  
5. Details of Existing Infrastructure and Technical Capability  

### Key Caveats
- Technology transfer is subject to mutual agreement and IPR terms negotiated with CSIR laboratories  
- Availability of specific technologies depends on ongoing R&D and lab priorities  
- Financial and technical support is case-specific and not standardized  
- MSMEs must demonstrate adequate absorptive capacity and commercialization potential  
- Applications are processed on a rolling basis; no fixed deadlines, but subject to technology availability
