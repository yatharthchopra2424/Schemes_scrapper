# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is an Indian research institute or organisation  
- [ ] Research team has cutting-edge biomedical research experience  
- [ ] Team is led by a senior Principal Investigator (PI)  
- [ ] Team has an excellent track record in health research with impactful publications  
- [ ] Demonstrated collaboration with or contribution to ICMR  
- [ ] Willingness to contribute to ICMR’s objectives and activities on an honorary basis (e.g., adjunct faculty for capacity development, assisting with proposal development workshops)  
- [ ] Willingness to work with ICMR on national priorities related to public health  
- [ ] Research team falls into one of the following categories: basic research, translational research, clinical research, laboratory-based research, or population-based research  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation certificate  
- PAN card  
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of last 3 years  
- GST registration certificate  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam registration (if applicable)  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Two-page CV: Name, academic/professional qualifications and affiliation of PI and the other members of the team (PI + maximum of 3 other investigators)  
- Experience in undertaking large projects on child growth/body composition in community settings (experience of the PI and a maximum of 3 other investigators should be included)  
- Research experience and publications of the team as a whole summarized within one page, including:  
  a) Up to five most relevant previous research grants with the main objectives of the research studies  
  b) Up to five most relevant previous publications with brief summary of each paper  
- Two-page EoI Document: The EoI should explicitly state:  
  a) The choice of the sites (states, districts, pre-schools/schools/pre-schools) where the study will be implemented and the well-founded rationale for the selection  
  b) The implementation strategy (one page) including selection of sample population, enrolment strategy, agreement with the existing criteria/suggestions for additional inclusion/exclusion criteria, quality assurance for anthropometric measurements, blood sampling, laboratory procedures, other data collection procedure, expected challenges and mitigations  

### Document Quality Check
- File size: Each document must be under 5 MB  
- File naming: Use format `SchemeName_DocumentType_Version.pdf` (e.g., `ICMR_CCoE_EoI_v1.pdf`)  
- All documents must be legible, scanned in color if originally colored, and text must be selectable (not image-only)  
- CVs and EoI must be signed and dated by the PI  
- Publications list must include full citations (journal name, year, volume, pages)  
- All documents must be in English or accompanied by a certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not required. The scheme requires only an Expression of Interest (EoI), not a detailed project report. However, verify with the nodal officer if any additional project details are needed during submission.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Register/login as PI at https://epms.icmr.org.in/userLogin  
2. Verify email ID and complete/update PI profile before proceeding  
3. Navigate to Proposal Submission → Click on “Apply Proposal”  
4. Select **‘Request for Expression of Interest for ICMR Collaborating Centre of Excellence (ICMR-CCoE) – 2026’** from the dropdown  
5. Fill the form step by step, ensuring all mandatory fields are completed  
6. Upload documents in the prescribed order and format as specified in the document checklist  
7. Do not include any identifying information in Part-A of the proposal (as per ICMR guidelines)  
8. Save draft frequently and review all entries before final submission  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All form fields are complete and accurate  
- [ ] All scheme-specific documents are uploaded and clearly labeled  
- [ ] File sizes are within limits (<5 MB each)  
- [ ] PI profile is verified and updated  
- [ ] Declarations and undertakings (if any) are signed  
- [ ] No identifying information in Part-A of the form  
- [ ] Final review conducted by senior consultant before submission

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from ICMR  
- [ ] Confirm that no financial support is provided (as per scheme guidelines: “No special financial support will be provided to ICMR-CCoE”)  
- [ ] Note that recognition is honorary and includes access to ICMR’s research infrastructure, mentorship, collaboration opportunities, visibility in national research networks, and support via portals like MedTech Mitra, Patent Mitra, and Biostatistics Helpline  
- [ ] Utilization certificate is not applicable (no funds disbursed)  
- [ ] Invoice success fee only after formal recognition letter is received and verified  
- [ ] Guide client on leveraging the recognition for future collaborations and funding opportunities
