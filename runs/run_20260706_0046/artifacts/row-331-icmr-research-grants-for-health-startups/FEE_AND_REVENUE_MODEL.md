## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the non-financial benefits of the ICMR-CCoE scheme (recognition, infrastructure access, mentorship, collaboration opportunities), which are critical for health startups seeking validation and scaling.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees reflect the scheme’s honorary nature (no financial support) and competitive selection based on research excellence; lower success fee tiers apply due to non-monetary outcomes and longer processing timelines.
3. **Transparency & Trust**: Clear disclosure of retainer and success fee structures aligns with ICMR’s emphasis on ethical conduct and accountability in research collaborations.
4. **Scalability & Access**: Tiered pricing ensures accessibility for early-stage health startups while capturing value from established entities seeking ICMR collaboration, in line with the scheme’s objective to promote innovation in biomedical research.

*Note on risk profile*: The scheme involves no direct financial grant, reducing fiscal risk but increasing reliance on non-tangible benefits. Processing time from evidence (Call released July 1, 2026; submission deadline July 31, 2026; results declared September 15, 2026) indicates a ~2.5-month evaluation cycle, supporting a 3-month lag assumption for success fee realization.
