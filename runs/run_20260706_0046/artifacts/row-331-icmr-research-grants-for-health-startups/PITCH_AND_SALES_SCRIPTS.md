# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping health startups and research institutions gain strategic recognition and support from India’s apex medical research body—the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR). Many organisations in the biomedical and public health space miss out on ICMR’s Collaborating Centre of Excellence designation because they don’t know how to position their research excellence and collaboration potential. Today, I’d like to understand your work and see if the ICMR Research Grants for Health Startups scheme—specifically the ICMR Collaborating Centre of Excellence (ICMR-CCoE) 2026 call—is a fit for your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is the primary focus of your research or innovation—basic, translational, clinical, laboratory-based, or population-based health research?  
2. How long has your organisation been active in health research, and what is your track record in terms of impactful publications or prior grants?  
3. Have you previously collaborated with ICMR or contributed to its objectives, such as through workshops, advisory roles, or joint projects?  
4. Is your team led by a senior Principal Investigator with a strong publication history in biomedical or public health research?  
5. Are you willing to contribute to ICMR’s national health priorities on an honorary basis, such as serving as adjunct faculty or supporting capacity-building initiatives?  
6. Which research domains does your team work in—e.g., diagnostics, therapeutics, preventive strategies, or implementation research for national health challenges?  
7. Do you have experience managing large-scale community-based studies, particularly in areas like child growth or body composition?  
8. Are you prepared to submit a two-page Expression of Interest (EoI) detailing your proposed study sites, implementation strategy, and rationale for site selection?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Organisation is an Indian research institute or organisation  
- [ ] Research team led by a senior PI with cutting-edge biomedical research experience  
- [ ] Excellent track record in health research with impactful publications  
- [ ] Demonstrated collaboration with or contribution to ICMR  
- [ ] Willingness to contribute to ICMR’s objectives on an honorary basis (e.g., adjunct faculty, workshop support)  
- [ ] Willingness to work with ICMR on national public health priorities  
- [ ] Team falls into one of the categories: basic, translational, clinical, laboratory-based, or population-based research  
- [ ] Ability to submit required documents: two-page CVs (PI + max 3 investigators), proof of large project experience in child growth/body composition, one-page research experience/publications summary, two-page EoI with site selection and implementation strategy  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your organisation aligns well with the ICMR Collaborating Centre of Excellence (ICMR-CCoE) 2026 scheme. This recognition offers access to ICMR’s research infrastructure, mentorship from its scientists, and visibility in national health networks—without requiring financial support from ICMR. Shall I walk you through how this works and what the next steps look like?

Health startups and research institutions often struggle to gain credibility, access to advanced research infrastructure, and collaboration opportunities with India’s top medical research body. Despite strong innovation potential, many lack the endorsement and strategic linkages needed to scale their work nationally or secure follow-on funding.

The ICMR Collaborating Centre of Excellence (ICMR-CCoE) – 2026, administered by the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR), is a prestigious recognition programme that designates eligible Indian research organisations as official ICMR Collaborating Centres of Excellence.
