### ICMR Research Grants for Health Startups
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://epms.icmr.org.in
- **Current Status:** Seasonal
- **Window:** Release of Call July 01, 2026; Last date of Submission of proposal July 31, 2026 (17:00 hrs); Declaration of Result September 15, 2026

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Indian research institutes or organisations
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Biomedical research (basic, translational, clinical, laboratory-based, population-based)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Research team with cutting-edge biomedical research experience led by a senior PI; excellent track record in health research with impactful publications; demonstrated collaboration with or contribution to ICMR; willingness to contribute to ICMR’s objectives on an honorary basis (e.g., adjunct faculty, assisting with proposal development workshops); willingness to work with ICMR on national public health priorities

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Recognition as ICMR Collaborating Centre of Excellence (ICMR-CCoE), access to ICMR's research infrastructure, mentorship from ICMR scientists, opportunities for collaboration on national health projects, visibility in national research networks, support for proposal development and technical guidance through ICMR's portals and helpdesks (MedTech Mitra, Patent Mitra, Biostatistics Helpline)
- Disbursement mode: Non-financial (recognition and support services)
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing (recognition is honorary and independent of financial support)

#### Documents Required
1. Two-page CV: Name, academic/professional qualifications and affiliation of PI and the other members of the team (PI + maximum of 3 other investigators)
2. Experience in undertaking large projects on child growth/body composition in community settings (experience of the PI and a maximum of 3 other investigators should be included)
3. Research experience and publications of the team as a whole summarized within one page, including: a) Up to five most relevant previous research grants with the main objectives of the research studies b) Up to five most relevant previous publications with brief summary of each paper
4. Two-page EoI Document: The EoI should explicitly state: a) The choice of the sites (states, districts, pre-schools/schools) where the study will be implemented and the well-founded rationale for the selection. b) The implementation strategy (one page) including selection of sample population, enrolment strategy, agreement with existing criteria/suggestions for additional inclusion/exclusion criteria, quality assurance for anthropometric measurements, blood sampling, laboratory procedures, other data collection procedure, expected challenges and mitigations

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Submission portal will open from 1st July 2026.
2. Visit https://epms.icmr.org.in. The user manual (under Guidelines → e-PMS manual) is available on the portal.
3. The proposal submission process has three steps:
   - Step 1: PI registration/login https://epms.icmr.org.in/userLogin
   - Step 2: Verify email ID and complete/update PI profile
   - Step 3: Proposal submission
4. After completing the mandatory sections of the PI profile:
   - Go to Proposal Submission
   - Click on Apply Proposal
   - Select ‘Request for Expression of Interest for ICMR Collaborating Centre of Excellence (ICMR-CCoE) – 2026’ from the dropdown – Apply New Proposal
   - Fill the form step by step.
5. For queries, contact: po.epms@icmr.gov.in
