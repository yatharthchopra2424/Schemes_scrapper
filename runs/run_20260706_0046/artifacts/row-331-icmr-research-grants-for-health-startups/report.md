# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Scheme Overview
**Scheme Name:** ICMR Research Grants for Health Startups  
**Scheme ID:** row-331  
**Ministry / Category:** Innovation Grants  
**Scheme Type:** grant  
**Geographic Scope:** Pan-India  
**Implementing Agency:** Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR)  
**Application Portal:** https://epms.icmr.org.in  
**Status / Deadlines:** Release of Call July 01, 2026; Last date of Submission of proposal July 31, 2026 (17:00 hrs); Declaration of Result September 15, 2026  
**Last Updated:** 2026  
**Confidence:** medium  

### Objectives
- Promote innovation in biomedical research and health technologies  
- Support startups in developing solutions for national health priorities  
- Strengthen the ecosystem for translational research in medicine and public health  
- Encourage collaboration between startups and ICMR institutes  
- Facilitate early-stage validation and scaling of health innovations  
- Enhance capacity for research in diagnostics, treatment, and preventive healthcare  

### Eligibility Matrix
| Eligibility Criteria | Details |
|----------------------|---------|
| **Applicant Type** | Indian research institutes or organisations |
| **Research Team Requirements** | A research team of an institute with cutting-edge biomedical research experience, led by a senior PI |
| **Track Record** | Excellent track record in health research in terms of impactful publications |
| **Collaboration History** | Demonstrated collaboration with or contribution to ICMR |
| **Willingness to Contribute** | Willingness to contribute to ICMR’s objectives and activities on an honorary basis (e.g., adjunct faculty for capacity development, assisting with proposal development workshops) |
| **National Priorities** | Willingness to work with ICMR on national priorities related to public health |
| **Research Categories** | Basic research, translational research, clinical research, laboratory-based research, population-based research |
| **Target Beneficiaries** | Startups; health startups; biomedical startups; MSME; research institutions |

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Benefit Type | Details |
|--------------|---------|
| **Recognition** | Recognition as ICMR Collaborating Centre of Excellence (ICMR-CCoE) |
| **Access to Infrastructure** | Access to ICMR's research infrastructure |
| **Mentorship** | Mentorship from ICMR scientists |
| **Collaboration Opportunities** | Opportunities for collaboration on national health projects |
| **Visibility** | Visibility in national research networks |
| **Technical Guidance** | Support for proposal development and technical guidance through ICMR's portals and helpdesks such as MedTech Mitra, Patent Mitra, and Biostatistics Helpline |
| **Financial Support** | No Financial Support: Recognition as ICMR-CCoE is independent of any financial support being given to the institution by ICMR. No special financial support will be provided to ICMR-CCoE. |

### Key Caveats
> - No special financial support will be provided to ICMR-CCoE  
> - Recognition is honorary and does not include funding  
> - Applicants must contribute to ICMR’s objectives on an honorary basis  
> - Selection is based on excellence in research and collaboration potential  
> - The EoI can be submitted through ONLINE MODE ONLY

### Required Documents
1. **Two-page CV:** Name, academic/professional qualifications and affiliation of PI and the other members of the team (PI + maximum of 3 other investigators)  
2. **Experience in undertaking large projects on child growth/body composition in community settings:** Experience of the PI and a maximum of 3 other investigators should be included  
3. **Research experience and publications of the team as a whole summarized within one page:**  
   a) Up to five most relevant previous research grants with the main objectives of the research studies  
   b) Up to five most relevant previous publications with brief summary of each paper  
4. **Two-page EoI Document:**  
   a) The choice of the sites (states, districts, pre-schools/schools) where the study will be implemented and the well-founded rationale for the selection  
   b) The implementation strategy (one page) including selection of sample population, enrolment strategy, agreement with the existing criteria/suggestions for additional inclusion/exclusion criteria, quality assurance for anthropometric measurements, blood sampling, laboratory procedures, other data collection procedure, expected challenges and mitigations
