## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the grant amount disbursed under the ICAR – Agri Startup Grants scheme.
2. Tiered retainer structure to accommodate varying client capacities and engagement scopes.
3. Success fee capped at a percentage of the actual grant received, ensuring consultant incentives align with client outcomes.
4. Scheme-specific note: The ICAR – Agri Startup Grants scheme has a moderate risk profile due to its technical feasibility assessment by ICAR and milestone-based disbursement, which may extend processing time; however, evidence of successful grantees and clear eligibility criteria support a predictable success rate for eligible agri-startups.
