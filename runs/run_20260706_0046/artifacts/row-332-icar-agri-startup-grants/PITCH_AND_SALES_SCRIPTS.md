# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping agri-startups access government innovation grants—particularly those working on crop improvement, animal husbandry, fisheries, or agri-processing with innovative prototypes. The ICAR – Agri Startup Grants scheme is designed exactly for ventures like yours, offering grant funding and access to ICAR’s research network. Before we explore fit, I’d like to understand your startup’s current stage and innovation focus to see if this aligns.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated, and are you DPIIT-recognized?  
2. What specific agriculture/allied sector are you working in (e.g., crop improvement, animal husbandry, fisheries, natural resource management, agri-processing)?  
3. Do you have a working prototype or proof of concept ready for field trials or market validation?  
4. Have you received funding from other government sources, and if so, what is the approximate amount?  
5. Is your startup led by a woman, SC/ST entrepreneur, or based in an aspirational district?  
6. Have you been recommended by an ICAR institute or KVK, or are you seeking such endorsement?  
7. What are your immediate needs for prototype development, field testing, or IPR support?  
8. Are you looking for non-dilutive grant funding to avoid equity dilution at this early stage?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Startup incorporated in India  
- [ ] Working in agriculture and allied sectors (crop improvement, animal husbandry, fisheries, natural resource management, or agri-processing)  
- [ ] Has innovative idea or prototype  
- [ ] DPIIT-recognized OR recommended by an ICAR institute/KVK  
- [ ] Has not exceeded specified limit of funding from other government sources  
- [ ] Preference: women-led, SC/ST-led, or aspirational district-based (not mandatory but strengthens case)

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it appears your startup aligns well with the ICAR – Agri Startup Grants scheme’s focus on early-stage agri-innovators. This scheme provides grant-based funding for prototype development, field trials, and market validation—along with access to ICAR’s labs and mentorship—without requiring equity. Shall I walk you through how it works?

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**  
Agri-startups often struggle to validate innovations due to high R&D costs, limited access to research infrastructure, and difficulty in conducting field trials or securing IPR support—especially at the prototype stage when traditional funding is scarce.

**Scheme intro:**  
The ICAR – Agri Startup Grants scheme, administered by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) under the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, provides grant-based financial support to agri-startups for prototype development, field trials, and market validation. Funds are disbursed in milestones upon achievement of predefined deliverables, with amounts tailored to the project proposal and innovation stage.
