### ICAR – Agri Startup Grants
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Grant
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR), Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://icar.org.in
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Startups incorporated in India
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Agriculture and allied sectors (crop improvement, animal husbandry, fisheries, natural resource management, agri-processing)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Must be recognized by DPIIT or recommended by an ICAR institute; should not have received more than a specified limit of funding from other government sources; preference given to startups led by women, SC/ST, or those working in aspirational districts

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Grant-based financial support determined by project proposal and stage of innovation; disbursed in milestones upon achievement of predefined deliverables
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit to startup’s account after due appraisal and approval by ICAR screening committee
- Duration of benefit: One-time (disbursed in installments as per milestones)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. DPIIT startup recognition certificate (if available)
3. Detailed project proposal
4. Budget breakdown and utilization plan
5. Proof of concept or prototype details
6. PAN card of the startup
7. Bank account details
8. Authorization letter from the signatory
9. Any prior funding or award details
10. Recommendation letter from an ICAR institute or KVK (if applicable)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the official ICAR website or designated portal for agri-startup grants.
2. Check eligibility and ensure the startup is DPIIT-recognized or endorsed by an ICAR institute.
3. Prepare a detailed project proposal including innovation description, objectives, methodology, budget, and expected outcomes.
4. Submit the application online along with required documents.
5. Applications are screened by an ICAR committee for technical feasibility and innovation quotient.
6. Shortlisted startups may be invited for a presentation or interview.
7. Final selection is made based on merit, and approved grantees receive a sanction letter.
8. Funds are disbursed in installments as per milestones.
9. Grantees are required to submit progress reports and utilization certificates.
