**Policy Statement:**  
[Firm Name] is committed to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of client
data. This policy outlines the minimum standards for handling sensitive information throughout the
engagement lifecycle.

**Data Storage:**  
All client data shall be stored in encrypted cloud storage environments with access controls
enforced via role-based permissions. Storage providers must be ISO 27001 certified or equivalent.

**Access Control:**  
Access to client data is restricted to authorized personnel only. Multi-factor authentication (2FA)
is mandatory for all system access. No personal devices may be used to access or store client data
without explicit security approval and mobile device management (MDM) enforcement.

**Data Minimization:**  
Only data necessary for the specific engagement purpose shall be collected and retained.  
Anonymous or aggregated data shall be used wherever possible for internal analysis.

**Retention:**  
Client data shall be retained for a period of two (2) years post case closure, unless otherwise
required by law, regulation, or scheme-specific guidelines.  
*Scheme-specific note: All documents submitted to National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) under NABARD RIDF (Rural Infrastructure Development Fund) must be retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit purposes.*

**Breach Notification:**  
In the event of a confirmed data breach involving client data, [Firm Name] shall notify the
affected client within 24 hours of discovery. Notification will include the nature of the breach,
types of data involved, steps taken to mitigate harm, and recommended actions for the client.

**Training & Compliance:**  
All consultants and staff handling client data shall undergo annual data protection and security
training. Compliance with this policy is monitored via internal audits and subject to disciplinary
action for violations.

**Review:**  
This policy shall be reviewed annually or upon significant changes in data protection regulations
or scheme guidelines, whichever comes first.
