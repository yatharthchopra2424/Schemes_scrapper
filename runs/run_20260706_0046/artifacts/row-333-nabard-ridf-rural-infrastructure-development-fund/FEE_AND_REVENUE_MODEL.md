## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with client savings or funding secured  
2. Transparent fee structures with no hidden charges  
3. Performance-linked success fees tied to loan approval and disbursement  
4. Customized retainers based on project complexity and client type  

**Scheme-specific note:** NABARD RIDF involves technical appraisal, detailed project report preparation, and state government coordination, leading to moderate risk. Processing time is not explicitly stated in evidence but typically spans 3–6 months for appraisal and approval based on similar infrastructure loan schemes. Success is evidenced by rolling basis approvals and disbursement in installments upon utilization certificates, indicating achievable outcomes with proper documentation.
