### NABARD RIDF (Rural Infrastructure Development Fund)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://nabard.org/ridf
- **Current Status:** Open
- **Window:** Rolling basis — proposals can be submitted throughout the year based on annual allocations

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: State governments, state-owned corporations, local self-governments, NGOs, and cooperatives
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Rural infrastructure (agriculture and allied sectors, irrigation, soil conservation, drinking water, rural roads, and bridges)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Projects must be technically feasible, economically viable, socially acceptable, and from the approved list of RIDF activities; loan available only for ongoing projects (not for new land acquisition); state government contribution mandatory as per prescribed ratio

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Loan amount up to 95% of project cost for special category states and 80-85% for general states
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit in installments based on project progress and utilization certificates
- Duration of benefit: Long-term financing (specific tenure not stated in evidence)

#### Documents Required
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
2. Land ownership or lease documents
3. Clearances from relevant authorities (e.g., irrigation, forest, pollution)
4. Financial viability and cost estimates
5. Implementation schedule
6. Undertaking from state government or implementing agency
7. Previous utilization certificates (for ongoing projects)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify eligible rural infrastructure project under approved RIDF activities
2. Prepare detailed project report (DPR) including technical, financial, and economic aspects
3. Submit application through the state government nodal officer to NABARD
4. NABARD appraises the project for technical feasibility and financial viability
5. Upon approval, loan agreement is executed
6. Funds are released in stages based on project progress and utilization certificates
