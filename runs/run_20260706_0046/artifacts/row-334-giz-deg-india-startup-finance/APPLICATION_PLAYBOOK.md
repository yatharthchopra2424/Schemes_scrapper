# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Legally registered Indian startup entity (Certificate of Incorporation/Registration)
- [ ] Innovative business model with potential for scalability and impact
- [ ] Operates in sectors aligned with Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
- [ ] Preference given to startups with social impact or green technology focus
- [ ] Not at idea stage; must have minimum viable product (MVP) or traction
- [ ] Demonstrates potential for job creation and economic development
- [ ] Willing to undergo due diligence and comply with GIZ/DEG environmental and social safeguards
- [ ] Understands financing is debt/quasi-equity, not a grant, and must be repaid per agreed terms

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
- PAN Card
- Audited Financial Statements (last 2 years)
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) (last 2 years)
- GST Registration Certificate
- Bank Statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration (MSME certificate)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Business plan and pitch deck
- Financial projections (3–5 years)
- Founders’ profiles and team details (CVs, roles, experience)
- Impact assessment or sustainability metrics (aligned with SDGs)
- Details of any existing funding or investment (cap table, term sheets)
- Bank account details (for disbursement)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format
- File names must match document type (e.g., "Certificate_of_Incorporation.pdf")
- Financial statements must be certified by a Chartered Accountant
- Impact metrics must be quantifiable and verifiable
- Documents must be in English or accompanied by certified translation
- Maximum file size per document: 10 MB
- All IDs (PAN, Udyam, GST) must match the legal entity name

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Not explicitly required as a formal DPR. However, a detailed **business plan and pitch deck** are mandatory, covering business model, innovation, scalability, impact metrics, financial projections, and team capability. If unsure, verify with nodal officer whether a formal DPR is expected alongside the business plan.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. **Initiate contact via GIZ/DEG India office** – There is no public online form; applications begin with an inquiry through partner channels or DEG’s India office.  
2. **Clearly articulate innovation and SDG alignment** – Explicitly link your business model to specific Sustainable Development Goals (e.g., SDG 7, 9, 13).  
3. **Quantify impact metrics** – Use measurable indicators (e.g., tons of CO₂ reduced, jobs created, beneficiaries reached).  
4. **Show traction** – Demonstrate MVP, customer validation, revenue, or pilot results; idea-stage startups are ineligible.  
5. **Detail fund utilization** – Specify how tranche-based financing will be used for milestones (e.g., product development, market expansion).  
6. **Highlight team capability** – Include founders’ relevant experience and commitment.  
7. **Disclose existing funding** – Transparently list all current investors, grants, or loans.  
8. **Prepare for due diligence** – Anticipate deep dives into financials, IP, compliance, and ESG safeguards.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All eligibility criteria met and documented  
- [ ] Business plan clearly outlines innovation, scalability, and SDG impact  
- [ ] Financial projections are realistic and assumption-driven  
- [ ] Founders’ profiles highlight relevant expertise  
- [ ] Impact metrics are specific, measurable, and verifiable  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached  
- [ ] Declarations signed (if any)  
- [ ] No red flags in compliance, ESG, or fund utilization

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit initial inquiry via email to DEG’s India office or through GIZ partner channels (e.g., GIZ India office, accredited intermediaries)  
- Await confirmation of receipt and next steps (typically within 5–7 working days)  
- Upon request, submit detailed business plan, financials, and impact metrics via secure channel (email or portal)  
- Save all email correspondence and document submission receipts  
- Note any reference number or application ID provided by GIZ/DEG  
- Set calendar reminders for follow-up at 10, 20, and 30 days if no response

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and review term sheet from DEG/GIZ  
- [ ] Negotiate financing terms (interest rate, tenure, covenants, quasi-equity kicker if applicable)  
- [ ] Sign financing agreement upon mutual agreement  
- [ ] Fulfill pre-disbursement conditions (e.g., legal opinions, security creation, ESG compliance)  
- [ ] Receive first tranche upon milestone verification (as per agreement)  
- [ ] Utilize funds strictly for approved purposes (e.g., scaling operations, market expansion)  
- [ ] Submit periodic progress and financial reports as required  
- [ ] Prepare for subsequent tranches based on milestone achievement  
- [ ] Maintain records for utilization certificate and audit readiness  
- [ ] Track repayment schedule per agreed terms  
- [ ] Invoice success fee to client upon first disbursement (per firm policy)
