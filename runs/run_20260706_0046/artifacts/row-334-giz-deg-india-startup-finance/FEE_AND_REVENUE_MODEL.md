## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Aligned Pricing**: Fees are structured to reflect the substantial financial benefit delivered (EUR 500,000–several million euros per ticket), ensuring client success drives consultant compensation.
2. **Risk Mitigation**: Success fees are capped within industry ranges (6–8% for Growth tier) to balance effort with outcome uncertainty, given the scheme’s rolling basis and due diligence requirements.
3. **Transparency**: All fees and payment triggers are disclosed upfront, aligned with GIZ and DEG’s compliance-focused procurement and financing practices.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: This scheme involves technical due diligence, milestone-based disbursement, and environmental/social safeguards, resulting in moderate processing risk; however, the high success rate of similar GIZ-DEG financing arrangements (evidenced by EUR 855M in financing arrangements awarded in 2023) supports predictable revenue realization.
