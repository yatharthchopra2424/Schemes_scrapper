# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialise in helping Indian startups access growth capital through international development partnerships. The GIZ–DEG India Startup Finance scheme is designed specifically for innovative, scalable startups with social or green impact—exactly the kind of ventures driving India’s sustainable future. I’d like to understand your startup’s stage and impact goals to see if this EUR 500,000+ financing opportunity aligns with your needs.

### Discovery Questions
1. When was your startup incorporated, and what is your current legal structure (e.g., Private Limited, LLP)?
2. What sector or industry do you operate in, and how does your business model align with sustainable development goals (e.g., green tech, social impact, clean energy)?
3. Do you have a minimum viable product (MVP) or early traction (customers, revenue, or pilot projects)?
4. What is your current stage of funding (bootstrapped, angel, seed, Series A), and how much capital are you seeking to scale?
5. Can you share your startup’s impact metrics or sustainability goals (e.g., jobs created, carbon reduced, communities served)?
6. Are your financial statements and projections up to date, and do you have a detailed business plan and pitch deck ready?
7. Have you previously applied for international financing or grants, and what was the outcome?
8. What are your primary growth milestones for the next 12–18 months (e.g., market expansion, product development, team hiring)?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Legally registered Indian startup (Certificate of Incorporation/Registration)
- [ ] Innovative business model with scalability potential
- [ ] Operates in sectors aligned with SDGs (preference for social impact/green tech)
- [ ] Has MVP or traction (not idea-stage)
- [ ] Demonstrates clear development impact or sustainability metrics
- [ ] Seeking debt/quasi-equity financing (EUR 500,000 to several million)
- [ ] Prepared to undergo due diligence and milestone-based disbursement
- [ ] Has founders’ profiles, team details, bank account, PAN, and existing funding documents

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like your startup meets several key criteria for the GIZ–DEG India Startup Finance scheme—particularly your incorporation date, sector focus on [mention sector from discovery], and traction milestones. This scheme offers EUR 500,000 to several million in debt or quasi-equity financing, disbursed in tranches tied to milestones, along with GIZ’s technical support. Let me walk you through how it works and why it could be a strong fit.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Indian startups with high impact potential often struggle to access growth capital that matches their scale and sustainability goals. Traditional lenders may lack appetite for early-stage ventures, while equity dilution can be costly. Founders need financing that supports long-term sustainability, includes technical guidance, and aligns with impact measurement—without requiring immediate profitability or excessive collateral.

**Scheme intro:**
The GIZ–DEG India Startup Finance scheme, implemented by Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ) GmbH in partnership with DEG (Deutsche Investitions- und Entwicklungsgesellschaft), provides debt or quasi-equity financing to eligible Indian startups. Ticket sizes typically range from EUR 500,000 to several million euros, disbursed in tranches based on milestone achievement. The financing is accompanied by GIZ’s technical support for capacity building and impact assessment.
