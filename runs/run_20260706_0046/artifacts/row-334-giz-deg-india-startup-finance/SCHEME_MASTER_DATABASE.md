### GIZ – DEG India Startup Finance
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** India
- **Administering Body:** Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ) GmbH in partnership with DEG (Deutsche Investitions- und Entwicklungsgesellschaft)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://giz.de/en/worldwide/369.html
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Indian startups that are legally registered entities
- Age of business: Not specified (covers startups with MVP/traction, excludes idea-stage)
- Sector: Sectors aligned with sustainable development goals; preference for social impact or green technology
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Demonstrates innovative business models, potential for scalability and impact; preference for startups with clear development impact, innovation, and scalability; must have minimum viable product or traction (not idea-stage)

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Ticket sizes typically ranging from EUR 500,000 to several million euros
- Disbursement mode: Tranches based on milestone achievement
- Duration of benefit: Not specified (structured as debt/quasi-equity financing with repayment terms)

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. Business plan and pitch deck
3. Financial statements and projections
4. Founders’ profiles and team details
5. Impact assessment or sustainability metrics
6. Bank account details and PAN
7. Any existing funding or investment documents

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Submit an initial inquiry or proposal through GIZ’s partner channels or DEG’s India office.
2. Undergo preliminary screening based on eligibility and innovation criteria.
3. Submit detailed business plan, financial projections, and impact metrics.
4. Participate in due diligence meetings with GIZ and DEG representatives.
5. Receive term sheet and negotiate financing terms.
6. Sign financing agreement and fulfill pre-disbursement conditions.
7. Receive funds in tranches upon milestone verification.
