# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is an Indian Citizen  
- [ ] Business plan is for a non-farm income generating activity (manufacturing, processing, trading, service, or agri-allied sector)  
- [ ] Credit need is up to ₹20 lakh (for Shishu category: up to ₹50,000)  
- [ ] Applicant can approach a Bank, MFI, or NBFC for availing of MUDRA loans under PMMY  
- [ ] Applicant must follow the usual terms and conditions of the lending agency  
- [ ] Business activity must be non-corporate and non-farm small/micro enterprise  
- [ ] Applicant must not be availing loans through agents or middlemen engaged by MUDRA (as MUDRA does not engage any agents)  
- [ ] Applicant must be targeting income-generating activities under manufacturing, processing, trading, service, or agri-allied sectors  

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration  
- PAN of the entity  
- ITR (Income Tax Returns)  
- GST registration certificate  
- Bank statements (last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration Certificate  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Pitch deck or business description  
- Authorization letter from authorized signatory  
- Details of funding received (if any)  

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible and in PDF or clear scanned format  
- File names must match the document type and applicant name (e.g., PAN_ApplicantName.pdf)  
- File size must not exceed 5 MB per document  
- All documents must be self-attested or certified by a gazetted officer/notary if required by the lending institution  
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, a certified translation must be attached  
- Authorization letter must be on company letterhead and signed by an authorized signatory

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
A formal Detailed Project Report (DPR) is not explicitly mandated for Mudra Shishu Loans. However, a pitch deck or business description is required as per scheme-specific documents. Applicants should verify with the nodal officer or lending institution whether a DPR is needed based on loan amount and institutional guidelines.  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the Udyamimitra portal at https://www.udyamimitra.in to begin the online application.  
2. Select “Apply for MUDRA Loan” and choose the Shishu category (for loans up to ₹50,000).  
3. Fill in all personal and business details accurately as per supporting documents.  
4. Upload all required documents in the specified format (PDF, max 5 MB per file).  
5. Ensure the authorization letter is uploaded and signed by an authorized signatory.  
6. Double-check that the business activity is classified under non-farm income generating activities (manufacturing, processing, trading, service, or agri-allied).  
7. Confirm that no agent or middleman is involved in the application process, as MUDRA does not engage any intermediaries.  
8. Review the application thoroughly before final submission to avoid errors or delays.  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] All fields in the application form are complete and accurate  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents are attached and clearly legible  
- [ ] Document file names follow the prescribed format  
- [ ] Authorization letter is signed and on letterhead  
- [ ] Business activity is confirmed as non-farm and income generating  
- [ ] Loan amount requested is within Shishu limit (up to ₹50,000)  
- [ ] No mention of agents or middlemen in the application  
- [ ] Application is submitted via the official Udyamimitra portal (https://www.udyamimitra.in)

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the application via the Udyamimitra portal (https://www.udyamimitra.in)  
- Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing the application reference number  
- Store the reference number securely in the case file  
- Set a follow-up reminder for 7 days after submission to check application status  
- If no update is received, initiate follow-up via the nodal officer or portal helpdesk

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Receive and verify the approval letter from the lending institution or via Udyamimitra portal  
- [ ] Confirm the sanctioned amount matches the requested loan (up to ₹50,000 for Shishu)  
- [ ] Ensure loan is disbursed to the designated bank account  
- [ ] Monitor utilization of funds for the intended income-generating activity  
- [ ] Prepare Utilization Certificate (UC) as per lending institution guidelines  
- [ ] Track invoice and success fee as per consultancy agreement  
- [ ] Advise client to maintain records of all expenditures for audit and compliance purposes  
- [ ] Remind client that MUDRA does not engage agents, and any future refinance or top-up loans must be applied through official channels only
