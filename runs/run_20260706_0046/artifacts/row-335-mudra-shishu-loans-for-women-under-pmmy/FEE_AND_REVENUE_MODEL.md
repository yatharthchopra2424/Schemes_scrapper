## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the actual financial benefit delivered (loan amount up to ₹20 lakh under PMMY, with Shishu category covering up to ₹50,000).
2. Transparent fee structure with no hidden charges; all fees disclosed upfront.
3. Risk-adjusted pricing reflecting the scheme’s processing time (typically 15–30 days for approval via Udyamimitra portal) and high success rate evidenced by over 6.22 crore beneficiaries in FY 2019-20.
4. Scheme-specific note: This scheme has a low to moderate risk profile due to its government-backed nature, high disbursement volume (`10,749.59 lakh crore as of June 2026), and standardized application process via Udyamimitra portal, reducing variability in outcomes.
