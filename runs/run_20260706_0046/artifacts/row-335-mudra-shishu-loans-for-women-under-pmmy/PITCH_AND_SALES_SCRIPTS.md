# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping women entrepreneurs access collateral-free financing through government schemes like the Mudra Shishu Loans for Women under PMMY. Many women-led micro businesses struggle to get formal credit due to lack of collateral or credit history. I’d like to understand your business goals and see if this scheme can support your growth without requiring asset security.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of income-generating activity are you currently running or planning to start? (e.g., manufacturing, trading, service, agri-allied)
2. When was your business incorporated or registered? (Shishu category supports units up to ₹50,000 loan need)
3. Do you have a clear business plan outlining how the loan will be used for income generation?
4. Are you seeking working capital or term loans, or do you need funds for equipment or inventory?
5. Have you approached banks or MFIs before for financing, and what challenges did you face?
6. Are you looking for a loan amount up to ₹50,000 specifically under the Shishu category?
7. Are you comfortable applying through a bank, NBFC, or MFI, or would you prefer the online Udyamimitra portal?
8. Do you have basic documents like identity proof, business address proof, and a simple project report ready?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Indian citizen
- [ ] Woman entrepreneur
- [ ] Business plan for non-farm income generating activity (manufacturing, processing, trading, service, or agri-allied)
- [ ] Credit requirement up to ₹20 lakh (Shishu covers up to ₹50,000)
- [ ] Not engaged in crop farming or land development activities
- [ ] Willing to approach a bank, MFI, or NBFC, or apply via Udyamimitra portal
- [ ] Has or can prepare basic documents: ID proof, business proof, business description

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like your venture aligns well with the Mudra Shishu Loans for Women under PMMY — especially since you're seeking a small, collateral-free loan to start or grow your business. Let me explain how this scheme works and how we can help you access up to ₹50,000 through trusted lending partners.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Women entrepreneurs often struggle to access formal credit due to lack of collateral, limited credit history, or reliance on informal lenders with high interest rates. This limits their ability to start or scale income-generating activities in trading, services, or small manufacturing.

**Scheme intro:**
The Mudra Shishu Loans for Women under Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY), implemented by Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency Ltd. (MUDRA), provides collateral-free loans up to ₹50,000 through banks, NBFCs, and MFIs. Applications can be made online via the Udyamimitra portal (https://www.udyamimitra.in), with no agents or middlemen involved.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a woman entrepreneur running a non-farm income-generating activity (e.g., trading, service, or manufacturing)
- Your credit need is within the Shishu category limit of up to ₹50,000
- You are willing to apply through a bank, MFI, NBFC, or the Udyamimitra portal
