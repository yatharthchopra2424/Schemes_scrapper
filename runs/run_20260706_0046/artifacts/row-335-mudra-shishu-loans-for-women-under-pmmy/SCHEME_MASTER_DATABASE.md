### Mudra Shishu Loans for Women (Under PMMY)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Loan
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency Ltd. (MUDRA)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://www.udyamimitra.in
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Any Indian Citizen who has a business plan for a non-farm income generating activity such as manufacturing, processing, trading or service sector
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Manufacturing, processing, trading, service, and agri-allied sectors
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Credit need up to ₹20 lakh; Shishu category covers loans up to ₹50,000 only

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Up to ₹50,000 under Shishu category; MUDRA provides refinance support to Banks/NBFCs/MFIs for lending to micro units having loan requirements up to ₹20 lakh
- Disbursement mode: Direct credit (through lending institutions)
- Duration of benefit: One-time

#### Documents Required
1. Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
2. PAN of the entity
3. Pitch deck or business description
4. Authorization letter from authorized signatory
5. Details of funding received (if any)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Approach any of the lending institutions (Commercial Banks, RRBs, Small Finance Banks, MFIs, NBFCs) mentioned above.
2. Alternatively, apply online through the Udyamimitra portal (www.udyamimitra.in).
3. Submit the required documents as per the lending institution's guidelines.
4. Await approval and disbursement from the lending institution.
