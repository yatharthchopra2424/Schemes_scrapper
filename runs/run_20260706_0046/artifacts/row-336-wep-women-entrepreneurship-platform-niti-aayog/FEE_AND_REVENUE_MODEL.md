## Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing aligned with the financial benefit delivered to the client.
2. Transparent fee structure with clear milestones for retainer and success fees.
3. Risk-adjusted pricing reflecting the scheme’s processing time and success likelihood.
4. Customizable tiers to accommodate diverse client profiles from micro to enterprise.

**Scheme-specific note:** WEP does not directly provide financial support but facilitates access to financial resources, grants, and schemes tailored for women-led businesses. The risk profile is low to moderate due to the platform’s role as an aggregator and facilitator rather than a direct funder. Success depends on the client’s ability to meet eligibility criteria of third-party schemes accessed via WEP’s ‘Access to Finance’ section. Processing time varies by the specific scheme being pursued, but WEP’s platform access and profile completion are immediate upon registration, reducing initial barriers. Evidence shows high user engagement (112,832+ WEP family members, 380+ schemes curated) and strong outcomes in financial inclusion support, indicating a favorable success rate for clients who actively engage with the platform.
