# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping women entrepreneurs across India access government resources, mentorship, and financial support through the Women Entrepreneurship Platform (WEP) by NITI Aayog. Before we explore how WEP can support your journey, I’d like to understand your current stage and goals to see if this platform aligns with your needs.

### Discovery Questions
1. Are you a woman entrepreneur based in India looking to start, grow, or scale your venture?
2. What stage is your business at—idea, early-stage, or established—and what are your immediate goals (e.g., funding, mentorship, market access)?
3. Have you previously accessed any government schemes or incubators for women-led businesses?
4. Are you seeking mentorship from industry experts or guidance on financial inclusion and skill development?
5. Do you want to explore curated funding opportunities, grants, or schemes tailored for women-led businesses through WEP’s ‘Access to Finance’ section?
6. Are you interested in networking with over 112,000+ women entrepreneurs or participating in workshops and skill-building sessions?
7. Would personalized partner recommendations via WEP’s Smart Match mentorship program be valuable for your growth?
8. Are you open to registering on a government-backed platform to access over 300+ resources, including 835+ government initiatives and 576+ incubators & accelerators?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Woman entrepreneur based in India
- [ ] Seeking to start, grow, or scale a venture
- [ ] Interested in mentorship, networking, or skill development
- [ ] Looking for access to government schemes, financial resources, or incubators
- [ ] Willing to complete OTP verification and profile registration on wep.gov.in
- [ ] Open to exploring personalized recommendations via Smart Match
- [ ] Interested in financial inclusion support or workshop participation
- [ ] Aims to benefit from recognition through WEP awards or outreach programs

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like the Women Entrepreneurship Platform (WEP) by NITI Aayog could be a strong fit—especially since it offers free access to mentorship, government schemes, and financial resources tailored for women like you. Let me share how WEP works and the specific benefits it provides.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Women entrepreneurs in India often struggle to find reliable, centralized access to government schemes, mentorship, funding opportunities, and peer networks—leading to missed growth opportunities and isolation in their entrepreneurial journey.

**Scheme intro:**
The Women Entrepreneurship Platform (WEP), launched by NITI Aayog, is a pan-India incubation-type initiative that provides free access to over 300+ resources, including mentorship from 944+ experts, 835+ government initiatives, 576+ incubators & accelerators, networking with 112,832+ WEP family members, personalized partner recommendations via Smart Match, financial inclusion support, skill development through articles and videos, workshop participation, and recognition through WEP awards and outreach programs. Access the platform at: https://wep.gov.in/register?page=register
