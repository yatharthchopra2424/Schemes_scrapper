### WEP – Women Entrepreneurship Platform (NITI Aayog)
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Incubation
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** NITI Aayog
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://wep.gov.in/register?page=register
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Women entrepreneurs in India
- Age of business: No specific criteria mentioned
- Sector: All sectors (start, grow, or scale ventures)
- Revenue cap: Not mentioned
- Employee count: Not mentioned
- Other: Registration requires OTP verification and profile completion to access full resources

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Access to over 300+ resources including government schemes, mentorship from 944+ experts, 835+ government initiatives, 576+ incubators & accelerators, networking with 112,832+ WEP family members, personalized partner recommendations via Smart Match, mentorship guidance, financial inclusion support, skill development through articles and videos, workshop participation, and recognition through WEP awards and outreach programs
- Disbursement mode: Direct access to resources and referrals
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing (as long as registered and active)

#### Documents Required
1. OTP verification
2. Profile completion

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the WEP portal at wep.gov.in
2. Click 'Register' or 'Start your entrepreneurial journey'
3. Enter OTP for verification
4. Complete registration and set a new password
5. Complete your profile after registration
6. Use Smart Match to get personalized partner recommendations
7. Explore mentors, government initiatives, incubators, accelerators, and schemes
8. Access financial resources via the 'Access to Finance' section
9. Join workshops, view articles and videos for skill development
10. Engage with the WEP community and partners for networking and support
