## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees reflect the tangible benefit delivered to the client, calibrated to the scheme’s actual outcomes.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Success fees are structured to reflect the effort-to-outcome ratio; this scheme offers no direct financial benefit, so success fees are not applicable.
3. **Transparency & Predictability**: Clients receive clear, upfront pricing with no hidden costs; retainer covers access facilitation and guidance.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Since *She Means Business – Digital Skills for Women* provides **no direct financial support, grants, loans, or subsidies** (per evidence), the scheme’s financial benefit amount is **₹0**. Therefore, success-based fees are not applicable. Revenue is derived solely from retainer services for program navigation, training access support, and application guidance. Processing time is immediate (rolling basis, year-round access), eliminating revenue lag.
