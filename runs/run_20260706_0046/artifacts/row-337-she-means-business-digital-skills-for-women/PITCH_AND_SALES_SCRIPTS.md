</think># Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialise in helping women entrepreneurs access free digital skills training and mentorship through government and industry-backed initiatives. Given your focus on growing your business online, I wanted to see if you might benefit from a program like **She Means Business – Digital Skills for Women**, which is designed specifically to support women like you in building digital capabilities at no cost.

### Discovery Questions
- Are you currently running a business or planning to start one as a woman entrepreneur?
- How long have you been operating your business, or when do you plan to launch?
- What digital platforms are you currently using (or planning to use) for your business, such as Facebook, Instagram, or WhatsApp?
- Have you accessed any formal training or mentorship to improve your digital marketing or online sales skills?
- Are you looking to grow your customer base, improve online visibility, or sell more through social media?
- Do you have reliable internet access and a Facebook or Meta account to participate in online learning?
- Are you interested in connecting with other women entrepreneurs for peer support and networking?
- Would you be open to free, self-paced online courses that teach practical skills like creating ads, managing a Facebook Page, or using Instagram for business?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant identifies as a woman entrepreneur, aspiring business owner, or runs a women-led small business  
- [ ] Seeking to enhance digital capabilities for business growth  
- [ ] Has access to internet and a Facebook/Meta account  
- [ ] Located anywhere in India (Pan-India scope)  
- [ ] Interested in free training, mentorship, or community networking (no financial support expected)  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like you’re actively looking to grow your business using digital tools — which is exactly what **She Means Business – Digital Skills for Women** is designed to support. Let me share how this free program from Meta can help you build the skills and confidence to succeed online.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many women entrepreneurs struggle to grow their businesses online due to limited access to practical digital training, mentorship, and peer networks — especially when balancing business with other responsibilities. Without the right skills, it’s hard to effectively use social media for marketing, sales, or customer engagement.

**Scheme intro:**
**She Means Business – Digital Skills for Women** is a free initiative by Meta (Facebook) that provides women entrepreneurs across India with access to online learning modules, mentorship, community networking, and tools to promote their businesses on Meta platforms. The program offers no direct financial support but focuses entirely on building digital capacity through free, self-paced training and guidance on leveraging Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp for business growth.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are a woman entrepreneur or aspiring business owner seeking to grow your venture  
- You want to enhance your digital capabilities using online platforms  
- You have internet access and a Facebook/Meta account (or are willing to create one)  
- You are based in India, making you eligible for this Pan-India program

Let’s do a quick, no-obligation eligibility check — it takes just 2 minutes. If you qualify, we’ll guide you through registration and help you make the most of the training. Our support is completely free to you; we only charge a success fee if we help you secure any future financial schemes — but for this one, there’s no cost at all since it’s already free to join.
