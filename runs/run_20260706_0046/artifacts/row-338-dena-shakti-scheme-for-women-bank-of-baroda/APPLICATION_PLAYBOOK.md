# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is a woman entrepreneur (individual or entity)
- [ ] Business is registered as proprietorship, partnership, private limited company, or LLP
- [ ] Business is engaged in manufacturing, services, or trading activities
- [ ] Business has been operational for at least 1 year
- [ ] Annual turnover does not exceed Rs. 10 lakhs (for micro enterprises) or Rs. 2 crores (for small enterprises) as per MSME classification
- [ ] Employee count is within MSME limits: not more than 10 for micro, not more than 20 for small enterprises
- [ ] Business operates in any sector eligible under Bank of Baroda's lending policies for women entrepreneurs
- [ ] Applicant has a satisfactory credit history (CIBIL score of 701 or above, as per general Bank of Baroda lending norms)
- [ ] Applicant is an Indian resident
- [ ] No existing default with Bank of Baroda or other financial institutions

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Completed application form
- PAN card of applicant and business
- Aadhaar card of applicant
- Business registration certificate (Proprietorship deed, Partnership deed, Certificate of Incorporation, or LLP agreement)
- GST registration certificate (if applicable)
- Bank statements of business account for last 6 months
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of business for last 2 years (if applicable)
- Udyam Registration Certificate (MSME registration)
- Passport-sized photographs of applicant

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Project report detailing business plan, utilization of funds, and repayment capacity
- Quotations for machinery/equipment or assets to be financed
- Proof of ownership or lease agreement for business premises
- Women entrepreneur declaration/affidavit (self-certification of being a woman-owned enterprise)

### Document Quality Check
- All documents must be legible, scanned in PDF format
- File size for each document should not exceed 5 MB
- Names on documents must match exactly as per PAN and Aadhaar
- Self-attested copies are acceptable; gazetted officer attestation preferred for affidavits
- Documents must be in English or Hindi; vernacular documents require notarized translation
- Photographs must be recent (within last 3 months) with white background

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a detailed project report is required. It should include:  
- Executive summary  
- Promoter details (background, experience)  
- Business description and objectives  
- Market analysis  
- Operational plan  
- Financial projections (profitability, break-even, cash flow)  
- Fund requirement and utilization schedule  
- Repayment plan  
- Risk analysis and mitigation  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Use the official Bank of Baroda loan application form for MSME/women entrepreneurs  
2. Fill all fields in block letters; avoid overwriting or correction fluid  
3. Ensure applicant name matches exactly as per PAN and Aadhaar  
4. Clearly mention "Dena Shakti Scheme for Women" in the scheme/type of loan field  
5. Provide accurate business address and contact details  
6. Declare all existing loans and liabilities truthfully  
7. Specify loan amount requested and purpose (e.g., machinery, working capital)  
8. Attach all required documents as per checklist  
9. Sign and date the form; co-applicant/guarantor must sign if applicable  
10. Retain a photocopy of the submitted form for records  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Application form completely filled and signed  
- [ ] All universal documents attached and verified  
- [ ] Scheme-specific documents (project report, quotations, etc.) attached  
- [ ] Declarations signed (including women entrepreneur self-certification)  
- [ ] No discrepancies in names, addresses, or figures across documents  
- [ ] Credit score checked (if available) and meets minimum threshold (CIBIL ≥ 701)  
- [ ] Loan amount within scheme limits (verify with nodal officer for exact ceiling)  
- [ ] Utilization plan in project report is realistic and bank-compliant

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit application and documents at the designated Bank of Baroda branch or via the bank’s online portal (if available)  
- Obtain system-generated acknowledgment with application/reference number  
- Take screenshot of online submission or photocopy of physical acknowledgment  
- Store reference number securely and set follow-up reminders for Day 25 and Day 35  
- Confirm receipt of application via SMS/email from bank
