## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are calibrated to the scheme’s benefit range (₹10L–₹1Cr), ensuring consultant compensation reflects the tangible uplift delivered to women entrepreneurs accessing collateral-free loans up to ₹7.5 lakhs under the Dena Shakti Scheme.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: The scheme’s moderate processing time and reliance on bank-specific eligibility (e.g., women proprietors/partners, MSME classification) support mid-tier success fees (6–8%); no evidence of high rejection rates or complex documentation delays justifies lower fees.
3. **Transparency & Trust**: Retainer covers initial eligibility assessment and application preparation; success fee is contingent on loan sanction, aligning incentives with client outcomes.
4. **Scalability & Access**: Tiered pricing enables broad access—from individual women entrepreneurs (Growth tier) to MSMEs—while reserving custom Enterprise pricing for conglomerates or group applications, though the scheme’s individual focus limits large-scale applicability.
