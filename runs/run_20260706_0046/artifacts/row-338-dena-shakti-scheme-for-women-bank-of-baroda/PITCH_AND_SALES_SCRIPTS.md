# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping women entrepreneurs access government-backed financial support. The Dena Shakti Scheme for Women by Bank of Baroda is designed specifically to empower women-led businesses with collateral-free loans and interest subsidies. I’d love to learn about your venture and see if this scheme aligns with your growth goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What year was your business incorporated or registered?
2. Are you the sole proprietor, partner, or director of a woman-owned enterprise?
3. Is your business engaged in manufacturing, services, or agro-processing?
4. Do you have a Udyam Registration certificate for your MSME?
5. What is your current annual turnover or projected revenue for the next financial year?
6. Are you seeking funding for working capital, machinery purchase, or business expansion?
7. Have you previously applied for any government loan schemes or subsidies?
8. Do you maintain audited financial statements and bank account statements for the last 6 months?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Business is woman-owned (sole proprietorship, partnership, or private limited with ≥51% women stake)
- [ ] Business is registered as MSME under Udyam
- [ ] Business operates in manufacturing, services, or agro-processing sector
- [ ] Applicant is an Indian national and resident
- [ ] Business has been operational for at least 1 year (implied from scheme context)
- [ ] Loan purpose aligns with permissible uses (working capital, machinery, etc.)
- [ ] Applicant can provide KYC, business registration, and financial documents

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it looks like your woman-led MSME in the {sector} space could be an excellent fit for the Dena Shakti Scheme for Women by Bank of Baroda, which offers collateral-free loans up to ₹20 lakhs with interest rate concessions. Let me share how this scheme works and how we’ve helped similar businesses secure funding.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Women entrepreneurs often struggle to access formal credit due to lack of collateral, limited credit history, and perceived higher risk by lenders, which hinders business scaling and working capital management.

**Scheme intro:**
The Dena Shakti Scheme for Women, administered by Bank of Baroda, is a government-supported loan initiative offering collateral-free credit up to ₹20 lakhs for women-owned MSMEs in manufacturing, services, and agro-processing sectors, with interest rate concessions and simplified documentation.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- Your business is woman-owned and registered as an MSME (per Udyam)
- Operates in an eligible sector: manufacturing, services, or agro-processing
- Seeking funds for permissible purposes like working capital or machinery
- Can provide required KYC and financial documents (to be verified)
