# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Applicant is an Indian citizen woman entrepreneur
- [ ] Applicant wishes to start or expand a business venture
- [ ] Applicant is a sole proprietor, partner in a partnership firm, or director of a private limited company where women hold majority stake
- [ ] Business activity is viable and falls under permissible sectors as per bank guidelines
- [ ] Business is engaged in manufacturing, services, or trading activities (as per scheme benefits)
- [ ] No minimum turnover, employee count, or age of business specified in evidence — verify with nodal officer if internal thresholds apply

> **Note**: Eligibility is based on applicant profile and business viability rather than fixed thresholds. Confirm sector permissibility and women-majority ownership for companies.

---

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Proof of identity (PAN card)
- Proof of address (Aadhaar card, utility bill, or passport)
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Udyam Registration Certificate (if applicable)
- Income Tax Returns (last 2 years, if applicable)
- Business registration proof (sole proprietorship/partnership deed/incorporation certificate)

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Business plan or project report (detailing viability, cost, and repayment capacity)
- Proof of business ownership/partnership/directorship (showing women hold majority stake in case of company/partnership)
- Quotations for machinery/equipment (if applicable for the proposed business activity)
- Licenses and permits related to the specific business activity
- Collateral documents (only if loan amount exceeds the collateral-free limit as per bank's policy)

### Document Quality Check
- [ ] All documents are clear, legible scans or certified copies
- [ ] Name on all documents matches exactly as per PAN and Aadhaar
- [ ] Photographs are recent (within last 6 months) and passport-sized where required
- [ ] Bank statements are stamped and signed by the bank authority
- [ ] Business plan/project report is detailed and includes financial projections
- [ ] All documents are self-attested by the applicant
- [ ] File size for each document does not exceed 5 MB
- [ ] Documents are in PDF or JPEG format only
- [ ] Language: English or Hindi; if regional language, attach certified translation

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
Yes, a **business plan or project report** is required as per the scheme evidence.  
**Standard sections to include**:  
- Executive summary  
- Promoter details (highlighting women entrepreneurship)  
- Business description and objectives  
- Market analysis and viability assessment  
- Project cost breakdown (land, building, machinery, working capital)  
- Means of funding (loan amount sought vs. promoter contribution)  
- Projected profitability (profit & loss statement for 3–5 years)  
- Repayment schedule (based on cash flow)  
- Risk analysis and mitigation plan  
- Conclusion and loan request  

> **Note**: Verify exact format and depth requirements with the nodal officer at Canara Bank, as the scheme assesses viability on a case-by-case basis.

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Access the application form via [https://www.canarabank.com](https://www.canarabank.com) or obtain it from the nearest Canara Bank branch  
2. Select “Synd Mahila Shakti Scheme” explicitly in the loan product dropdown (if applying online)  
3. Fill all applicant details exactly as per PAN and Aadhaar (no abbreviations or nicknames)  
4. Clearly state the loan purpose: business setup, expansion, or working capital under the scheme name  
5. In the project details section, attach or summarize the business plan/project report  
6. Declare women-majority ownership/partnership/directorship where applicable  
7. Disclose any existing loans or liabilities accurately in the liability statement  
8. Sign the declaration form and attach self-attested copies of all required documents  
9. Review thoroughly before submission — incomplete forms are liable for rejection  

### Internal Review Gate
- [ ] Applicant eligibility confirmed (woman entrepreneur, Indian citizen, viable business)  
- [ ] All universal and scheme-specific documents collected and quality-checked  
- [ ] Business plan/project report completed with financial projections  
- [ ] Application form filled accurately with no blank mandatory fields  
- [ ] Declarations signed and dated by the applicant  
- [ ] Documents attached in correct format and size  
- [ ] Reference number noted if applying online (screenshot saved)  
- [ ] Final review completed by senior consultant before submission

## Stage 4: Submission & Follow-Up (Day 18–30)

### Submission Protocol
- Submit the completed application either:  
  - **Online**: Through [https://www.canarabank.com](https://www.canarabank.com) → Loan Application → Select Synd Mahila Shakti Scheme  
  - **Offline**: At the nearest Canara Bank branch (request acknowledgment slip)  
- Immediately after submission:  
  - Take a screenshot of the confirmation page (online) or scan the acknowledgment slip (offline)  
  - Save the application reference number in the case file  
  - Store all submitted documents in a dedicated folder labeled: `CaseID_SyndMahilaShakti_Submission`  
- Set follow-up reminders for:  
  - Day 25: Initial status check  
  - Day 30: Escalation if no update received

- [ ] Maintain all approval documents in case file for audit and renewal purposes
