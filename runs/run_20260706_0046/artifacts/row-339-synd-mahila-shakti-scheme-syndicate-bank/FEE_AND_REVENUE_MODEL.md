## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Fees are structured to reflect the financial benefit delivered, with success fees calibrated to the actual loan amounts accessed under the Synd Mahila Shakti Scheme.
2. **Risk-Adjusted Pricing**: Given the scheme’s rolling basis approval and variable loan amounts (assessed case-by-case), success fees are set at moderate levels to reflect processing uncertainty and credit assessment dependencies.
3. **Accessibility for Target Beneficiaries**: The Starter and Growth tiers are designed to serve women entrepreneurs (sole proprietors, partners, or directors in women-majority private limited companies), aligning with the scheme’s eligibility criteria.
4. **Transparency and Predictability**: Retainer fees cover advisory workload (documentation, eligibility checks, application tracking), while success fees are contingent on loan sanction, ensuring client interests are prioritized.
