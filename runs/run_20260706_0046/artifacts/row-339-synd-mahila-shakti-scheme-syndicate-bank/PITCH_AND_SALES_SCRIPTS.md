# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon! I specialize in helping women entrepreneurs access government-backed financing to start or grow their businesses. The Synd Mahila Shakti Scheme from Canara Bank is designed specifically for women like you who are looking to launch or expand a venture—whether in manufacturing, services, or trading—with supportive loan terms and simplified processes. I’d love to learn about your business goals and see if this could be a good fit.

### Discovery Questions
- Are you an Indian citizen woman looking to start a new business or expand an existing one?
- What is your current business structure—are you a sole proprietor, in a partnership, or a director of a private limited company where women hold the majority stake?
- When was your business incorporated or registered (if applicable)?
- What sector does your business operate in (e.g., manufacturing, services, trading), and is it aligned with permissible activities under bank guidelines?
- Do you have a clear business plan or project report outlining your funding needs for setup, expansion, or working capital?
- Are you seeking financial support without wanting to pledge collateral, especially for smaller loan amounts?
- Have you explored loan options before, and what challenges have you faced in accessing finance?
- What is your approximate funding requirement, and how do you plan to use the loan amount?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Applicant is an Indian citizen woman
- [ ] Applicant is a sole proprietor, partner in a partnership firm, or director of a private limited company with women holding majority stake
- [ ] Business activity is viable and falls under permissible sectors as per bank guidelines (manufacturing, services, trading)
- [ ] Business is either proposed for setup or already operational and seeking expansion/working capital
- [ ] Applicant intends to use funds for legitimate business purposes (setup, expansion, working capital)
- [ ] Applicant is willing to submit required documents (identity, address, business proof, plan, etc.)
- [ ] Loan purpose aligns with scheme objectives: promoting women entrepreneurship and MSME support

### Transition to Pitch
Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like you’re aiming to [start/grow] your [business type] venture and need accessible financing—exactly what the Synd Mahila Shakti Scheme is designed to support. Let me walk you through how this Canara Bank scheme can help women entrepreneurs like you secure term loans or working capital finance with concessional rates and simplified documentation.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Many women entrepreneurs struggle to access timely and affordable finance due to stringent collateral requirements, complex documentation, and high interest rates—especially when launching or scaling a business. This often delays growth plans or forces reliance on costly informal credit.

**Scheme intro:**
The Synd Mahila Shakti Scheme, offered by Canara Bank (following the merger with Syndicate Bank), provides term loans and working capital finance to women entrepreneurs across India. The loan amount, interest rate, and repayment tenure are determined case-by-case based on project cost, viability, and repayment capacity. Key benefits include concessional interest rates compared to standard loans, simplified documentation, quick approval and disbursal, and no collateral requirement for loans up to a certain limit (as per bank policy).

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are an Indian woman entrepreneur seeking to start or expand a business venture  
- Your business activity falls under permissible sectors such as manufacturing, services, or trading  
- You are prepared to submit a business plan and proof of ownership (sole proprietorship, partnership, or directorship with women majority stake)  
- You are looking for financial support without immediate collateral pressure for eligible loan amounts
