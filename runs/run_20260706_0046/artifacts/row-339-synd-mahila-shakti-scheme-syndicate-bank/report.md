# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview

Mahila Shakti Scheme (Syndicate Bank)** is a **scheme specifically to promote entrepreneurship among women** across India. Originally Syndicate Bank Scheme Now Administered by Canara Bank
The **Synd Mahila Shakti Scheme** was originally launched by Syndicate Bank to promote women entrepreneurship. Following the merger of Syndicate Bank with Canara Bank effective April 1, 2020, the scheme is now administered by **Canara Bank** under its women-focused lending initiatives. The scheme retains its original name and objectives while being integrated into Canara Bank's product suite.

### Scheme Overview
- **Scheme Name**: Synd Mahila Shakti Scheme (Syndicate Bank)
- **Scheme ID**: row-339
- **Ministry / Category**: Women Entrepreneurship
- **Scheme Type**: Loan (Term Loan & Working Capital Finance)
- **Geographic Scope**: Pan-India
- **Implementing Agency**: Canara Bank (post-merger with Syndicate Bank)
- **Application Portal**: [https://www.canarabank.com](https://www.canarabank.com)
- **Status / Deadlines**: Rolling basis (no fixed deadline)
- **Last Updated**: 2026
- **Confidence Level**: Medium (based on available evidence)

### Objectives
The scheme aims to:
- Promote entrepreneurship among women
- Provide financial assistance for setting up and expanding women-owned businesses
- Enhance economic empowerment and self-reliance of women
- Support women-led micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs)
- Encourage women's participation in economic development

### Eligibility Criteria
The scheme is available to women entrepreneurs who meet the following criteria:

| Eligibility Factor | Requirement | Details |
|--------------------|-------------|---------|
| **Gender** | Female | Must be a woman entrepreneur |
| **Citizenship** | Indian Citizen | Applicant must be a citizen of India |
| **Business Structure** | Sole Proprietor / Partner / Director | Applicant must be:<br>- Sole proprietor of a business<br>- Partner in a partnership firm<br>- Director of a private limited company where women hold majority stake |
| **Business Activity** | Viable & Permissible Sectors | Business must be viable and fall under sectors permitted by bank guidelines (manufacturing, services, trading allowed) |
| **Project Viability** | Assessed by Bank | Business plan/project report must demonstrate feasibility and repayment capacity |

> **Key Takeaway**: The scheme targets women who are not only citizens but also have controlling ownership or leadership in their ventures, ensuring direct benefit to female entrepreneurs.

### Benefits & Financial Support
The scheme provides financial assistance with terms customized to individual projects:

| Benefit Category | Details |
|------------------|---------|
| **Loan Types** | Term loans and working capital finance |
| **Loan Amount** | Determined case-by-case based on project cost, viability, and repayment capacity (no fixed minimum/maximum specified in evidence) |
| **Interest Rate** | Concessional rates compared to standard loans; exact rate depends on credit score, loan amount, and bank policy (may be fixed or floating) |
| **Repayment Tenure** | Based on project viability and cash flow (assessed case-by-case) |
| **Collateral Requirement** | No collateral required for loans up to a certain limit (as per bank's internal policy); collateral may be required for higher amounts |
| **Documentation** | Simplified documentation process compared to standard commercial loans |
| **Approval & Disbursal** | Quick loan approval and disbursal upon successful verification |
| **Supported Activities** | Manufacturing, services, and trading businesses |

> **Important Caveat**: Loan approval is subject to credit assessment and project viability. Interest rates may vary based on credit score and loan amount. The scheme is subject to Canara Bank's internal policies and RBI guidelines. Post-merger integration may have led to product adjustments.

### Required Documents
Applicants must submit the following documents:

| Document Type | Examples / Details |
|---------------|--------------------|
| **Proof of Identity** | Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport |
| **Proof of Address** | Aadhaar Card, utility bill (electricity/water/gas), Passport |
| **Business Plan/Project Report** | Detailed report outlining business model, market analysis, financial projections |
| **Proof of Business Ownership** | Proprietorship deed, partnership deed, board resolution & shareholding pattern (for PLCs showing women majority stake) |
| **Bank Statements** | Statements of existing business/personal account (if applicable) |
| **Quotations for Machinery/Equipment** | Required if loan includes capital expenditure for equipment/machinery |
| **Licenses & Permits** | Business registration, GST certificate, industry-specific licenses (e.g., FSSAI for food, trade license) |
| **Income Tax Returns** | Personal/business IT returns (if applicable based on turnover/profit) |
| **Collateral Documents** | Property papers, asset details (only if loan exceeds collateral-free limit set by bank) |
