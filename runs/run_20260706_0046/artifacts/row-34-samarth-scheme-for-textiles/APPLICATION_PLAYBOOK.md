# Application Playbook

## How to Use This File
For each new client case, assign it a Case ID, pick the target scheme, and follow the steps below.

---

## Stage 1: Eligibility Verification (Day 1–3)

### Checklist
- [ ] Organization is a Textile Industry/Association registered under Central or State Government/Chambers of Commerce of Central or State Government  
- [ ] Organization is an Institution/Organization of the Ministry of Textiles/State Governments having training infrastructure and placement tie-ups with textile industry  
- [ ] Organization is a Reputed training institution/NGO/Society/Trust/Organization/Company/Start Up/Entrepreneur active in textile sector having placement tie-ups with textile industry  
- [ ] Organization has training infrastructure and placement tie-ups with textile industry  
- [ ] Organization is active in the textile sector  
- [ ] Organization is not engaged in Spinning and Weaving in the organized sector (excluded)  
- [ ] Organization agrees to implement skilling programmes through textile industry, institutions/organizations of Ministry of Textiles/State Governments, and reputed training institutions/NGOs/Societies/Trusts/Organizations/Companies/Start Ups/Entrepreneurs active in textile sector  
- [ ] Organization agrees to adopt strategy including Aadhaar enabled biometric attendance, training by certified trainers, CCTV recording, third party assessment, placement linked skilling with mandatory wage employment, random physical verification, NSQF aligned courses, robust MIS, call centre, mobile app, preference for marginalized groups and aspirational districts, public grievance redressal, internal complaints committee under Sexual Harassment Act, funding as per MSDE norms, common branding as per MSDE guidelines, concessional credit under PM MUDRA Yojana for self-employment  
- [ ] Organization agrees to provide demand-driven, placement oriented NSQF compliant skilling programmes  
- [ ] Organization agrees to promote skilling and skill up-gradation in traditional sectors of handlooms, handicrafts, sericulture and jute  
- [ ] Organization agrees to enable provision of sustainable livelihood by wage or self-employment to all sections of society across the country  
- [ ] Organization agrees to target training of 10.00 lakh persons (9 lakhs in organised & 1 lakh in traditional sector)  
- [ ] Organization agrees to implement skilling programmes through textile industry, institutions/organizations of Ministry of Textiles/State Governments, and reputed training institutions/NGOs/Societies/Trusts/Organizations/Companies/Start Ups/Entrepreneurs active in textile sector

## Stage 2: Document Collection (Day 3–10)

### Universal Documents (always required)
- Incorporation Certificate  
- PAN Card  
- ITR (Last 3 years)  
- GST Registration Certificate  
- Bank Statements (Last 6 months)  
- Udyam Registration  

### Scheme-Specific Documents
- Letter of Proposal Submission  
- Authorized Signatory Letter  
- Request Letter  
- Identity Proof document  
- Identity number  

### Document Quality Check
- File size: Each document must be less than 5 MB  
- File name: Must match the document type (e.g., "Letter_of_Proposal_Submission.pdf")  
- Certification: All documents must be self-attested by the authorized signatory  
- Language: Documents must be in English or Hindi; if in another language, a certified translation must be attached  
- Validity: Identity proof and PAN must be valid (not expired)  
- Clarity: Scanned copies must be legible with no blurring or cut-off text

## Stage 3: Application Drafting (Day 10–18)

### Project Report / DPR (if required)
DPR is required for this scheme. Standard sections include:  
- Executive Summary  
- Organization Profile  
- Project Objectives aligned with SAMARTH Scheme  
- Training Infrastructure Details  
- Course Details (NSQF-aligned, from RSA-approved list)  
- Mobilization and Selection Plan for Trainees  
- Trainer Details (ToT certified)  
- Placement and Post-Placement Tracking Plan  
- Budget Breakdown as per MSDE Common Norms  
- Utilization Plan linked to physical progress in MIS  
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation  
- Monitoring and Evaluation Framework  
- Annexures: Supporting documents, course syllabi, trainer certificates  

### Application Form Filling Protocol
1. Register for Empanelment under 'Samarth' (SCBTS) by clicking on the registration link on the portal.  
2. Enter Organization Name, Address details, Phone, Email id (must be unique), and Captcha.  
3. Click on Register button to register empanelment; verification link sent to registered mail id.  
4. Click on verification link (highlighted with blue colour) for verification of registered mail id.  
5. Click on Go to home page button to redirect to Home page.  
6. Click on Login Here button to redirect to Login page.  
7. Enter Valid Login Credential (Email id and password), enter captcha, then click on login button.  
8. Ensure Email id is unique and valid throughout the process.  
9. Fill all mandatory fields marked with * in Organization Credential, Training Infrastructure, and Industrial Tie-Up sections.  
10. Upload documents in JPEG, JPG, PNG, or PDF format only.  
11. Select Organization Type as appropriate (Textile Industry/Association, Institution/Organization of Ministry of Textiles/State Governments, or Reputed training institution/NGO/Society/Trust/Organization/Company/Start Up/Entrepreneur).  
12. For Training Infrastructure, fill all sections: Training Center name, contact person details, course details, and Human Resources/Trainer Details.  
13. In Industrial Tie-Up section, enter details of prospective employer (Name, State/UT, Sector, Number of trainees, Letter of Reference).  
14. Before final submission, review all entered data for accuracy and completeness.  
15. Keep a copy of the submitted application and acknowledgment receipt.

## Stage 5: Post-Approval (Day 30–90+)

### Approval Actions
- [ ] Obtain and verify the approval letter from the Ministry of Textiles  
- [ ] Verify the sanctioned amount against the approved project cost  
- [ ] Ensure funds are released linked to physical progress updated on the MIS  
- [ ] Submit Utilization Certificate as per GFR procedure  
- [ ] Submit Expenditure Statement and other requisite documents (Valuation Certificate, Project Progress Report)  
- [ ] Implement PFMS [Expenditure Advance & Transfer (EAT) Module] for financial support  
- [ ] Furnish Performance Security for an amount of 7.5% of the total project cost (valid for sixty days beyond project completion)  
- [ ] Open a dedicated Escrow Bank Account and map on PFMS  
- [ ] Appoint an independent Auditor for conducting quarterly, annual, and final audit  
- [ ] Upload Auditor details on MIS within one month of Sanction Order  
- [ ] Submit Audit Report and audited Financial Statements to Ministry/PMU  
- [ ] Ensure third party assessment and certification as per RSA protocol  
- [ ] Maintain CCTV recording of entire training programme for minimum one year  
- [ ] Ensure minimum 80% attendance for trainees to be eligible for assessment  
- [ ] Achieve mandatory wage employment: 70% in organized sector, 50% in traditional sector  
- [ ] Conduct post-placement tracking for one year  
- [ ] Verify that trainers are ToT certified by RSA/SSCs  
- [ ] Confirm Aadhaar enabled biometric attendance system is functional  
- [ ] Ensure public grievance redressal mechanism is active with designated officer  
- [ ] Constitute internal complaints committee under Sexual Harassment Act  
- [ ] Follow common branding as per MSDE guidelines  
- [ ] Facilitate concessional credit under Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana for self-employment beneficiaries  
- [ ] Calculate and remit success fee as per agreement with client
