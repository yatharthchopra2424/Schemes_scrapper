## Pricing Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Fees aligned with the scheme’s average benefit per trainee (₹10,380–₹12,120) and total project value (₹1300 Cr fund size).
2. **Risk-Adjusted Success Fees**: Lower success fee tiers (4–6%) for mid-market/large clients due to longer processing cycles (funds released linked to physical progress on MIS, with 30%/50%/20% tranche release tied to milestones).
3. **Retainer for Compliance Work**: Covers MIS registration, proposal preparation, and documentation (e.g., Letter of Proposal Submission, Authorized Signatory Letter) required for empanelment.
4. **Scheme-Specific Note**: Moderate risk profile due to mandatory CCTV recording, 80% attendance, and third-party assessment; however, high placement rates (70% in organized sector, 50% in traditional) and robust MIS reduce implementation risk for experienced consultants.
