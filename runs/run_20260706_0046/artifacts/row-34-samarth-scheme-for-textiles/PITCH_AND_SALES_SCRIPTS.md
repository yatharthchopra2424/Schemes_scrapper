# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I specialize in helping textile industry players, training institutions, and NGOs access government skilling subsidies like the SAMARTH Scheme for Textiles, which funds demand-driven, placement-oriented training programs for unemployed youth across India—especially in marginalized communities and aspirational districts. Before we explore whether this fits your organization, I’d like to understand your current work in the textile skilling space to see if we can align this opportunity with your goals.

### Discovery Questions
1. What type of organization are you—textile industry association, training institution, NGO, or startup—and are you registered under Central/State Government or a Chamber of Commerce?
2. Do you have existing training infrastructure and placement tie-ups with textile industry employers for wage employment or self-employment pathways?
3. Are you currently working in the organized textile sector (excluding spinning and weaving) or traditional sectors like handloom, handicraft, sericulture, or jute?
4. What is your target beneficiary group—are you focusing on unemployed youth, marginalized social groups, or candidates from 115 aspirational districts?
5. Can you commit to mandatory requirements like Aadhaar-enabled biometric attendance (80% minimum), CCTV recording for entire training duration, and third-party assessment via RSA-empaneled agencies?
6. Do your trainers hold ToT certification from RSA/SSCs, or are you planning to get them certified?
7. Are you prepared to maintain batch sizes between 10 and 30 candidates, with candidates aged 14 years or older?
8. For traditional sector training, are you aiming for at least 50% wage employment (vs. 70% in organized sector)?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Organization is Textile Industry/Association registered under Central/State Government/Chambers of Commerce  
- [ ] OR Institution/Organization of Ministry of Textiles/State Government with training infrastructure and textile industry placement tie-ups  
- [ ] OR Reputed training institution/NGO/Society/Trust/Organization/Company/Startup/Entrepreneur active in textile sector with textile industry placement tie-ups  
- [ ] Target beneficiaries include unemployed youth, marginalized social groups, or persons from 115 aspirational districts  
- [ ] Willing to exclude spinning and weaving in organized sector (if applicable)  
- [ ] For traditional sector: commitment to ≥50% wage employment  
- [ ] For organized sector: commitment to ≥70% wage employment  
- [ ] Minimum 80% Aadhaar-enabled biometric attendance for trainees  
- [ ] CCTV recording for entire training duration (records retained 1+ year)  
- [ ] Batch size: 10–30 candidates  
- [ ] Candidate age: minimum 14 years  
- [ ] Trainers to be ToT-certified by RSA/SSCs  
- [ ] Third-party assessment and certification as per RSA protocol  
- [ ] Post-placement tracking for one year  
- [ ] Preference for marginalized groups and aspirational districts (if applicable)  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, if you meet the core criteria—especially having textile industry placement ties and readiness to implement the mandatory skilling framework—I’d like to outline how the **SAMARTH Scheme for Textiles** can fund your training programs with up to ₹10,380 per trainee (level 2 courses) or ₹15,950 for higher-level courses, released in tranches linked to physical progress on the MIS.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Textile employers face critical skill gaps, especially in organized sectors (excluding spinning/weaving) and traditional sectors like handloom and handicraft, while unemployed youth—particularly from marginalized groups and aspirational districts—lack access to NSQF-compliant, placement-linked skilling with assured wage or self-employment outcomes.

**Scheme intro:**
The **SAMARTH Scheme for Textiles** (Scheme for Capacity Building in Textile Sector, SCBTS), implemented by the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, is a flagship subsidy program with a ₹1,300 crore outlay. It funds demand-driven, placement-oriented NSQF-compliant skilling programmes, targeting 10 lakh persons (9 lakh in organized sector, 1 lakh in traditional sector). Financial support is released in tranches linked to physical progress on the MIS, with concessional credit under Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana available for self-employment beneficiaries.

- You operate in the textile value chain (excluding spinning and weaving in organized sector) or traditional sectors (handloom, handicraft, etc.)  
- You can target unemployed youth, marginalized social groups, or aspirational districts
