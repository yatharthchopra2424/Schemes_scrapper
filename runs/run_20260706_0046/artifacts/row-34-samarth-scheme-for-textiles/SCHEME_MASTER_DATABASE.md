### SAMARTH Scheme for Textiles
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Pan-India
- **Administering Body:** Ministry of Textiles, Government of India
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://samarth-textiles.gov.in/
- **Current Status:** Closed
- **Window:** Please upload action plan (project wise) in MIS till 29-01-2024 under tab 'Notice'.

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Textiles Industry/Associations registered under Central or State Government/Chambers of Commerce of Central or State Government; Institutions/Organization of the Ministry of Textiles/State Governments having training infrastructure and placement tie-ups with textile industry; Reputed training institutions/NGOs/Societies/Trusts/Organizations/Companies/Start Ups/Entrepreneurs active in textile sector having placement tie-ups with textile industry
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Textiles (entire value chain except Spinning and Weaving in organized sector; traditional sectors: handlooms, handicrafts, sericulture and jute)
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Preference given to marginalized social groups and 115 aspirational districts; For self-employment, concessional credit under Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana will be provided for beneficiaries

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Fund size of ₹1300 Crore; Financial support as per common norms of MSDE; For self-employment, concessional credit under Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana
- Disbursement mode: Funds released linked to physical progress updated on the MIS
- Duration of benefit: One-time (linked to project completion and milestones)

#### Documents Required
1. Letter of Proposal Submission
2. Authorized Signatory Letter
3. Request Letter
4. Identity Proof document
5. Identity number

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Register for Empanelment under 'Samarth' (SCBTS) by clicking on Register for Empanelment under 'Samarth' (SCBTS) for new Registration.
2. Enter Organization Name, Address details, Phone, Email id (must be unique), and Captcha.
3. Click on Register button to register empanelment; verification link sent to registered mail id.
4. Click on verification link (highlighted with blue colour) for verification of registered mail id.
5. Click on Go to home page button to redirect to Home page.
6. Click on Login Here button to redirect to Login page.
7. Enter Valid Login Credential (Email id and password), enter captcha, then click on login button.
