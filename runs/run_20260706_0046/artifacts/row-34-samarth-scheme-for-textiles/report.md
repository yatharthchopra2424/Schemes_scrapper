# Comprehensive Scheme Masterclass & File Guide

## Scheme Deep Dive

### Overview
The **SAMARTH Scheme for Textiles** (Scheme for Capacity Building in Textile Sector - SCBTS) is a flagship skill development scheme approved by the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, under the Skill India Mission. It aims to provide demand-driven, placement-oriented NSQF-compliant skilling programmes to incentivize industry efforts in job creation, promote skilling in traditional sectors, and enable sustainable livelihoods through wage or self-employment across the country. The scheme targets training 10.00 lakh persons (9 lakhs in organized sector & 1 lakh in traditional sector) over three years (2017-20) with an estimated budget of ₹1300 crore. It excludes Spinning and Weaving in the organized sector and covers the entire textile value chain.

**Application Portal**: https://samarth-textiles.gov.in/  
**Implementing Agency**: Ministry of Textiles, Government of India  
**Geographic Scope**: Pan-India  
**Scheme Type**: Subsidy  
**Last Updated**: 2024  
**Fund Size**: ₹1300 crore  
**Status / Deadlines**: Please upload action plan (project wise) in MIS till 29-01-2024 under tab 'Notice'.

### Objectives
- Provide demand-driven, placement-oriented NSQF-compliant skilling programmes to incentivize and supplement industry efforts in creating jobs in organized textile and related sectors.
- Promote skilling and skill up-gradation in traditional sectors of handlooms, handicrafts, sericulture, and jute.
- Enable provision of sustainable livelihood by wage or self-employment to all sections of society across the country.
- Target training of 10.00 lakh persons (9 lakhs in organised & 1 lakh in traditional sector).
- Implement skilling programmes through textile industry, institutions/organizations of Ministry of Textiles/State Governments, and reputed training institutions/NGOs/Societies/Trusts/Organizations/Companies/Start Ups/Entrepreneurs active in textile sector.
- Adopt strategy including Aadhaar-enabled biometric attendance, training by certified trainers, CCTV recording, third-party assessment, placement-linked skilling with mandatory wage employment, random physical verification, NSQF-aligned courses, robust MIS, call centre, mobile app, preference for marginalized groups and aspirational districts, public grievance redressal, internal complaints committee under Sexual Harassment Act, funding as per MSDE norms, common branding as per MSDE guidelines, concessional credit under PM MUDRA Yojana for self-employment.

### Eligibility Matrix
| Eligible Entity Type | Specific Requirements |
|----------------------|------------------------|

| Reputed training institutions/NGOs/Societies/Trusts/Organizations/Companies/Start Ups/Entrepreneurs | Must be active in textile sector and have placement tie-ups with textile industry |

**Target Beneficiaries**: Unemployed youth; persons from all sections of society; marginalized social groups; beneficiaries in 115 aspirational districts.

### Benefits & Financial Support
| Benefit Category | Details |
|------------------|---------|

> **> Important Caveats**  
> - The scheme excludes Spinning and Weaving in the organized sector  
> - In traditional sector (Handloom, Handicraft, Silk & Jute), at least 50% should be wage employment  
> - CCTV recording is mandatory for entire training duration; recordings to be kept by Implementing Partners for minimum one year  
> - Minimum of 80% attendance is mandatory for a trainee to be eligible for assessment  
> - Batch size should be minimum 10 candidates and maximum 30 candidates  
> - Age of candidate should be minimum 14 years  
> - It is mandatory to get the trainer ToT certified  
> - Third Party Assessment and Certification as per assessment protocol laid down by RSA is mandatory for all eligible trainees  

### Application Process
The application process involves registration for empanelment under 'Samarth' (SCBTS) followed by login and submission of organizational details. Below is the step-by-step flowchart:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Start: Visit Application Portal] --> B[Click 'Register for Empanelment under Samarth (SCBTS)' for new Registration]
    B --> C[Enter Organization Name, Address details, Phone, Email id (must be unique), and Captcha]
    C --> D[Click Register button to register empanelment; verification link sent to registered mail id]
    D --> E[Click verification link (highlighted with blue colour) for verification of registered mail id]
    E --> F[Click Go to home page button to redirect to Home page]
    F --> G[Click Login Here button to redirect to Login page]
    G --> H[Enter Valid Login Credential (Email id and password), enter captcha, then click on login button]
    H --> I[Access Organization Details section]
    I --> J[Select Organization Type as per eligibility (e.g., State Government, Textile Industry, etc.)]
    J --> K[Fill Organization Credential details]
    K --> L[Upload supporting documents: Letter of Proposal Submission, Authorized Signatory Letter, Request Letter, Identity Proof document, Identity number]
    L --> M[Add organization member details (Name, Designation, Email, Mobile, Identity Proof, Identity number)]
    M --> N[Add Training Center details (name, contact person, course details, HR/Trainer details)]
    N --> O[Industrial Tie-Up: Enter Prospective Employer details (Name, State/UT, Sector, Number of trainees, Letter of Reference)]
    O --> P[Submit for Empanelment Acknowledgement]
    P --> Q[Download and submit Empaneling Acknowledgement]
    Q --> R[End: Await approval from Ministry of Textiles]
```

**Key Notes**:
- Email ID must be unique during registration.
- Verification link is sent to the registered email ID; clicking it verifies the email.
- After login, users proceed to fill Organization Details, Training Infrastructure, Industrial Tie-Up, and submit for acknowledgment.
- The portal supports document uploads in JPEG, JPG, PNG, PDF formats.
- Helpline is available in eight languages for assistance.
