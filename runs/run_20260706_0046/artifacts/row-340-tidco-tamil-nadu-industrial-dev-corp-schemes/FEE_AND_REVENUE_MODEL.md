## Pricing Principles
1. Retainer covers initial due diligence, proposal structuring, and liaison with TIDCO for joint venture identification and submission.
2. Success fee is contingent on successful execution of a joint venture agreement with TIDCO, aligning incentives with client outcomes.
3. Fees are calibrated to the scheme’s high-value nature: facilitating equity participation in large industrial and infrastructure projects, where benefits are strategic and long-term rather than direct cash grants.
4. Scheme-specific note: This scheme involves complex negotiations, due diligence, and approvals with TIDCO as a nodal agency, with processing times typically extending beyond 6 months due to multi-stakeholder evaluation; success fees reflect the effort-intensive nature of facilitating joint ventures in sectors like infrastructure, manufacturing, and industrial corridors.
