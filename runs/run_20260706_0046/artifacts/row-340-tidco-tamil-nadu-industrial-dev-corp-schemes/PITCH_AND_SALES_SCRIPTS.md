# Pitch & Sales Scripts

## Philosophy
We are advisors, not salespeople. We diagnose first. We only pitch a scheme if it fits the client. We never oversell success rates.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Script (First Contact — 20 min)

### Opening (2 min)
Good morning/afternoon. I'm calling from [Your Firm Name], and we specialize in helping medium and large industries, joint venture partners, and infrastructure developers in Tamil Nadu access government-backed joint venture opportunities through TIDCO – Tamil Nadu Industrial Development Corporation Limited. TIDCO promotes balanced industrial growth by facilitating equity participation in joint ventures across sectors like manufacturing, infrastructure, and agro-processing. Before we explore whether this is a fit for your goals, I’d like to understand your current expansion or partnership plans in Tamil Nadu to see if TIDCO’s joint venture framework aligns with your strategy.

### Discovery Questions
1. What is your company’s incorporation date, and are you currently operating or planning to operate in Tamil Nadu?  
2. Are you seeking a joint venture partner for an industrial or infrastructure project in Tamil Nadu, and if so, what sector are you targeting (e.g., Chemicals, Pharmaceuticals, Textiles, Auto Components, IT/ITES Parks, Bio-Tech Parks, SEZs, Road Development, or Agri Export Zones)?  
3. What level of equity participation are you considering with a TIDCO could offer in a joint venture—are you looking at Joint Sector (>11% to 50%), Associate Sector (>2% to 11%), Escort Sector (1% equity), or Escort Services (no equity)?  
4. What is the estimated investment size of your proposed project, and does it fall within the range of large-scale industrial or infrastructure projects TIDCO typically facilitates?  
5. Do you have a specific project proposal ready, or are you in the early stages of identifying a joint venture opportunity with TIDCO?  
6. Are you looking for TIDCO to act as a nodal agency for industrial corridor projects (e.g., Chennai Bengaluru Industrial Corridor, Chennai Kanyakumari Industrial Corridor) or other infrastructure initiatives?  
7. What is your timeline for evaluating and executing a joint venture agreement, and are you prepared to undergo TIDCO’s evaluation process upon proposal submission?  
8. Beyond equity participation, are you interested in accessing TIDCO’s network of existing joint venture partners and projects across sectors like Engineering, Petroleum and Petrochemicals, Floriculture, or Food & Agro?

### Qualification Checklist (internal — don't read aloud)
- [ ] Entity is interested in joint sector (equity participation above 11% and up to 50%)  
- [ ] Entity is interested in associate sector (equity participation above 2% and up to 11%)  
- [ ] Entity is interested in escort sector (1% equity participation)  
- [ ] Entity is interested in escort services (no equity participation)  
- [ ] Entity is a medium or large industry, joint venture partner, or infrastructure developer  
- [ ] Entity seeks industrial development in Tamil Nadu  
- [ ] Entity is willing to identify a suitable joint venture opportunity with TIDCO  
- [ ] Entity is prepared to submit a proposal and undergo evaluation by TIDCO  
- [ ] Entity accepts that financial support is structured through equity participation in joint venture projects  

### Transition to Pitch
Based on your responses, it sounds like you’re exploring a joint venture opportunity in Tamil Nadu with a specific equity participation level—TIDCO’s framework could be a strong fit. Let me walk you through how TIDCO – Tamil Nadu Industrial Development Corporation Limited facilitates joint ventures, the sectors involved, and the equity participation tiers they offer, so you can see exactly how this aligns with your goals.

## 2. Scheme Pitch (5 min)

### Structure: Problem → Scheme → Value → Proof → Next Step

**Problem framing:**
Medium and large industries, infrastructure developers, and joint venture partners often struggle to find credible government partners for large-scale industrial and infrastructure projects in Tamil Nadu, face challenges in accessing sector-specific networks (e.g., Chemicals, Pharmaceuticals, IT/ITES Parks, SEZs), and lack a structured pathway to negotiate equity participation that matches their risk appetite and investment capacity—whether they seek active joint venture roles (>11% equity) or passive collaboration (1% or no equity).

**Scheme intro:**
TIDCO – Tamil Nadu Industrial Development Corporation Limited, a premier industrial development agency of the Government of Tamil Nadu established in 1965, facilitates joint ventures through equity participation ranging from 1% to 50% depending on the sector and project type. TIDCO acts as a nodal agency for industrial corridor projects (e.g., Chennai Bengaluru Industrial Corridor, Chennai Kanyakumari Industrial Corridor) and promotes balanced industrial growth by enabling investment in large industrial and infrastructure projects across sectors such as Chemicals, Fertilizers, Pharmaceuticals, Textiles, Iron and Steel, Auto Components, Food & Agro, Floriculture, Engineering, Petroleum and Petrochemicals, IT/ITES Parks, Bio-Tech Parks, SEZs, Road Development Projects, and Agri Export Zones.

**Why they qualify (personalised):**
- You are seeking a joint venture partner for industrial development in Tamil Nadu, which matches TIDCO’s core mandate.  
- You are open to equity participation structures—TIDCO offers Joint Sector (>11% to 50%), Associate Sector (>2% to 11%), Escort Sector (1% equity), or Escort Services (no equity), allowing flexibility based on your investment strategy.  
- Your target sector (e.g., [Client’s sector from discovery]) is among those TIDCO actively promotes joint ventures in, including infrastructure projects like IT/ITES Parks, Bio-Tech Parks, or SEZs.  
- You are prepared to submit a proposal and undergo TIDCO’s evaluation process, which is the standard pathway for joint venture approval.
