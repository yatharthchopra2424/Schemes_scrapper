### TIDCO – Tamil Nadu Industrial Dev. Corp Schemes
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Equity
- **Geography:** Tamil Nadu only
- **Administering Body:** Tamil Nadu Industrial Development Corporation Limited (TIDCO)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://tidco.com
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Entities interested in joint sector, associate sector, escort sector, or escort services
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Chemicals, Fertilizers, Pharmaceuticals, Textiles, Iron and Steel, Auto Components, Food & Agro, Floriculture, Engineering, Petroleum and Petrochemicals, infrastructure projects like IT/ITES Parks, Bio-Tech Parks, SEZs, Road Development Projects, and Agri Export Zones
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Entities interested in joint sector (equity participation above 11% and up to 50%), associate sector (equity participation above 2% and up to 11%), escort sector (1% equity), or escort services (no equity participation) with TIDCO for industrial development in Tamil Nadu

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Equity participation ranging from 1% to 50% depending on the sector type
- Disbursement mode: Equity participation in joint venture projects
- Duration of benefit: Ongoing (as long as the joint venture agreement is in effect)

#### Documents Required
1. Proposal for joint venture project
2. Evaluation documents by TIDCO
3. Joint venture agreement (upon approval)

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Identify suitable joint venture opportunity with TIDCO
2. Determine equity participation category (Joint Sector: >11% to 50%, Associate Sector: >2% to 11%, Escort Sector: 1% equity, Escort Services: no equity)
3. Submit proposal for joint venture project
4. Undergo evaluation by TIDCO
5. Execute joint venture agreement upon approval
