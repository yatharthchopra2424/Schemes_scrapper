### TANSIDCO MSME Support Scheme
- **Scheme ID:** SCH-001
- **Category:** Subsidy
- **Geography:** Tamil Nadu
- **Administering Body:** Tamil Nadu Small Industries Development Corporation Limited (TANSIDCO)
- **Portal / Application URL:** https://tansidco.org/Applications/index
- **Current Status:** Rolling
- **Window:** Always Open

#### Eligibility Criteria
- Business type: Any individual, entrepreneur, MSME, or industrial unit
- Age of business: Not specified
- Sector: Manufacturing units; service units
- Revenue cap: Not specified
- Employee count: Not specified
- Other: Applicants must visit the industrial estate physically and upload geo-tagged photos as part of the application; Udyam/UAM registration is encouraged but not explicitly mandatory for application initiation

#### What It Offers
- Amount / Benefit: Access to developed industrial plots, sheds, and plug-and-play modules with basic infrastructure (power, water, approach roads); options for outright purchase, 99-year lease, or rental basis; costs vary by location, extent, and type (examples: Plug & Play modules in Guindy from Rs. 69,05,600 to Rs. 1,34,44,340; in Ambattur from Rs. 74,88,300 to Rs. 1,49,90,340; outright sale plots in Ariyalur from Rs. 4,69,780 to Rs. 18,69,018; Chengalpattu Alathur Phase-II up to Rs. 3,72,44,782)
- Disbursement mode: Not applicable (indirect support via subsidized or market-linked allotment; no direct grants or loans)
- Duration of benefit: One-time allotment with options for outright sale, 99-year lease, or rental basis

#### Documents Required
1. Udyam / Udyam Registration Certificate (recommended)
2. Geo-tagged photograph of the industrial estate/plot/shed/module
3. Identity proof of applicant
4. Address proof
5. Business constitution documents (if applicable)
6. Bank details for payment/refund

#### Application Process (numbered steps)
1. Visit the TANSIDCO website and navigate to the vacancy details for plots/sheds/modules.
2. Select the desired industrial estate and property type (outright sale, lease, rental, plug-and-play).
3. Physically visit the industrial estate for verification.
4. Upload geo-tagged photos of the proposed plot/shed/module.
5. Fill out the online application form with plot/shed/module details.
6. Submit the application and proceed to payment via SBI Collect or other modes.
7. Track application status using the application number.
8. Upon approval, proceed with allotment documentation and possession.
